NPP-controlled Soranathota PS budget defeated

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - The 2027 budget of the National People's Power (NPP)-controlled Soranathota Pradeshiya Sabha was defeated at its second reading today, with a majority of four votes.

At the council session chaired by Chairman Jayalath Edirimanna, the budget proposals received six votes in favour and 10 votes against.

All six NPP members voted in support of the budget, while members of the opposition parties united to defeat the proposals.

Those who voted against the budget included four members of the Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB), one from the United National Party (UNP), two from the Sri Lanka Freedom Party (SLFP), two from the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna and one from Sarvajana Balaya.

The budget was therefore defeated by a majority of four votes.
