Customers can now check transformer capacity for rooftop solar online

Electricity Distribution Lanka (Pvt) Ltd has introduced an online facility allowing customers to check whether the nearest electricity transformer has sufficient capacity to accommodate rooftop solar power systems before submitting an application.

Previously, customers could determine whether the transformer serving their area had sufficient capacity only after submitting an application, causing inconvenience and delays.

Acting on the directives of the Ministry of Energy, Electricity Distribution Lanka (Pvt) Ltd, a successor entity to the Ceylon Electricity Board (CEB), has introduced the online system to address this issue.

Under the new facility, customers can check the remaining capacity of their nearest transformer from their homes before proceeding with an application to install rooftop solar panels.

However, the facility is currently available only for transformers equipped with meters.

A project to install meters in the remaining transformers is also underway, with the aim of expanding the service to cover more areas.
