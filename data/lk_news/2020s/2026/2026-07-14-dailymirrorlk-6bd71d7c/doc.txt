Sri Lanka marks Dalai Lama's 91st birthday

Senior Buddhist monks participate in the Tri-Nikaya Maha Sangha Forum at Sri Bodhirukkharama Maha Viharaya in Biyagama, held to commemorate the 91st birthday of His Holiness the 14th Dalai Lama. The event, presided over by the Most Venerable Dr. Malwane Pannasara Mahanayake Thero, brought together senior monks of the three Nikayas, dignitaries and devotees to celebrate Buddhist unity, compassion and support for the preservation of Tibetan Buddhist heritage.
