Teachers, principals to launch island-wide strike on November 30

Colombo, Nov. 22 (Daily Mirror) - Several teachers’ and principals’ trade unions have decided to launch an island-wide one-day strike on November 30, accusing the Government of failing to address their longstanding grievances through the 2027 Budget.

The Ceylon Teachers’ Union said teachers and principals would report sick as part of the trade union action.

The unions said the Budget had failed to provide solutions to salary-related issues and other demands, while rising living costs continued to erode their purchasing power.

The strike has been scheduled a day before the parliamentary debate on the Education Ministry’s budgetary allocations on December 1.

They warned that school activities across the country could be disrupted and urged the Government to address their grievances during the remaining stages of the Budget process.
