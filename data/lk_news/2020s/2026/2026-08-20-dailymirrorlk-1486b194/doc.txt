Amana Bank celebrates 15 years with 15% savings at 150+ merchant outlets

From left:  Muhara Razak - Product Manager- Card offers and Marketing , Azim Rali- Assistant Vice President - Marketing & Corporate Communications , Arshad Jamaldeen - Assistant Vice President - Digital Banking & Cards, Siddeeque Akbar - Vice President Retail Banking & Marketing

Amana Bank, in celebration of its 15th anniversary, has partnered with over 150 merchant outlets to offer its customers with a 15 percent saving on their Debit Card spend. The promotion offers customers savings across a wide range of categories, including fashion, retail, dining, healthcare, household essentials, children’s products, and hospitality.

Apart from the discount offers, as part of the anniversary celebration, the Bank has also launched a 15-day 15 percent cashback challenge, where the first 150 customers to swipe their card on 15 separate days during August 2026 with a minimum cumulative spend of Rs. 50,000 will be rewarded with a 15 percent cashback up to Rs. 15,000.

The promotion brings together an extensive portfolio of merchant including leading apparel and fashion brands such as Cool Planet, Hameedia, Signature, Envoy Mansion, Crocodile, SWX, Avirate, Jezza, TFS, Stripes and Checks, Be Desi and The Factory Outlet; family, children’s and household retailers including The Kids Warehouse, House of Gifts and Elegant Charms; popular dining and culinary destinations such as Sheraton Colombo, Courtyard by Marriott, Mandarina Colombo, Arabian Knights, Indian Summer Restaurant, Khalid’s Biriyani, KFC, Chambers, Carnival Ice Cream, Sponge, The Cake Factory and Hill Food Street; and Retail Supermarket including Softlogic Glomark.

Customers can also enjoy savings at healthcare and wellness providers, including Durdans Hospital,  Ninewells Hospitals and Vision Care as well as leisure and hospitality partners such as SpeedBay, EckoHotels, Browns Group Hotels,  Araliya Group Hotels, among many other participating merchants.

Commenting on the anniversary campaign, Amana Bank’s Vice President – Retail Banking, Siddeeque Akbar said: “Celebrating 15 years is a significant milestone for Amana Bank, and we wanted to mark this occasion by giving back to our customers in a meaningful way. Our 15 percent anniversary Debit Card promotion is designed to help customers enjoy greater value on their everyday purchases while encouraging smart and responsible spending through debit cards further complemented with our 15 day 15 percent cashback challenge”.
