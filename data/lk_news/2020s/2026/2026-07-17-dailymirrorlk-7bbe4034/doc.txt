SLT-Mobitel powers HelaGo drivers with NextGen connectivity solutions

Sudharshana Geeganage - Chief Operating Officer of Mobitel (Pvt) Ltd. exchanging the MoU with Dhanika Perera - Chief Executive Officer of HelaGo, accompanied by SLT-Mobitel officials

Reinforcing its commitment to powering Sri Lanka’s digital economy through innovative connectivity solutions, SLT-Mobitel Mobile has partnered with HelaGo, the country’s emerging ride-hailing platform, to introduce exclusive mobile packages designed to enhance the connectivity needs of HelaGo drivers nationwide.

The collaboration was formalised at an event held recently at the Mobitel Headquarters in the presence of HelaGo and SLT-Mobitel leadership teams.

Through this partnership, SLT-Mobitel aims to empower Sri Lanka’s ride-hailing community while accelerating the nation’s digital transformation. By leveraging its state-of-the-art 5G network with the widest nationwide coverage, SLT-Mobitel enables drivers to stay seamlessly connected, more productive, and highly responsive on the move, ensuring a superior network experience.

The new lineup features three tailored plans to fit different driver schedules and budgets. The premier Rs. 596 monthly package is valid for 30 days and includes 750 minutes, 750 SMS, 5GB of anytime data, alongside a generous 15GB of dedicated app data for the HelaGo app, WhatsApp, and Google Maps. For shorter-term needs, a highly affordable Rs. 196 weekly package offers 150 minutes, 150 SMS, 1GB of anytime data, and 3.5GB of dedicated app data.

Additionally, for drivers seeking a budget-friendly, short-term option, the Rs. 46 daily package provides 1-day validity and includes 20 minutes, 20 SMS, 125MB of anytime data, and 512MB of app data.

Together, these bundles are specifically tailored to the daily operational needs of HelaGo drivers, guaranteeing them uninterrupted communication, efficient navigation, and seamless access to the HelaGo platform.

Supporting drivers through reliable connectivity, SLT-Mobitel continues to play a key role in advancing Sri Lanka’s digital transformation journey. By investing in next-generation network infrastructure and fostering strategic partnerships, the company is enabling greater digital inclusion and creating opportunities for innovation across industries.
