Indices close in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Hayleys, Central Finance Company and LOLC Holdings with the turnover crossing Rs. 972 Mn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Diesel & Motor Engineering, Access Engineering and Sarvodaya Development Finance. Mixed interest was observed in Sampath Bank, Royal Ceramics and Overseas Realty whilst retail interest was noted in Lanka Credit and Business Finance, LOLC Finance and Merchant Bank of Sri Lanka & Finance. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

Capital Goods sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Access Engineering, John Keells Holdings and Royal Ceramics) whilst the sector index lost 0.20%. The share price of Access Engineering closed flat at Rs 79.90. The share price of John Keells Holdings closed flat at Rs 19.50. The share price of Royal Ceramics closed flat at Rs 49.10.

Diversified Financials sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover whilst the sector index decreased by 0.64%.

Diesel & Motor Engineering and Sampath Bank were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Diesel & Motor Engineering closed flat at Rs 1,449.75. The share price of Sampath Bank moved down by Rs 0.25 (0.18%) to close at Rs 139.00.
