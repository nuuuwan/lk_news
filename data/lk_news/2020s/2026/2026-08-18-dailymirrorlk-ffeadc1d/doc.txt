Cabinet approves Inter-Ministerial Committee on Human Rights

Colombo, Aug. 18 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet has approved the establishment of an Inter-Ministerial Standing Committee on Human Rights to strengthen Sri Lanka’s national mechanism for monitoring and reporting on human rights.

Sri Lanka, as a member of the United Nations, is a state party to nine core human rights treaties and five optional protocols. It also participates in the Universal Periodic Review (UPR), through which countries voluntarily report on progress made in promoting and protecting human rights.

Sri Lanka underwent its fourth UPR cycle in 2023 and has been given the opportunity to submit a voluntary mid-term report on progress in implementing recommendations received from other countries.

The new committee will provide a stable national framework involving relevant ministries, departments and institutions to coordinate follow-up action and reporting on human rights-related activities.

The Cabinet approved the joint proposal submitted by the Ministers of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism, and Justice and National Integration.
