Central Bank to promote digital payments in Jaffna

The Central Bank has organised a Digital Payments Promotional Programme in Jaffna on August 7 and 8, 2026, at Cargills Square, Jaffna, from 8:30 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., with the objective of raising awareness of digital payment methods among the government officials, business community and members of the public.

Northern Province Governor Nagalingam Vethanayahan will grace the inaugural session as the chief guest. The programme will also be attended by the senior officials of the Central Bank, District Secretary Jaffna M. Piiratheepan, officials of banks, non-bank financial institutions and LankaPay (Pvt.) Ltd (LPPL), the operator of the national retail payment system.

This event is part of the nationwide campaign launched by the Central Bank, under the theme ‘Shaping the future through digital payments’, in collaboration with the financial institutions and other stakeholders.

Similar promotional events were successfully held in Hambantota, Nuwara Eliya, Dambulla, Kurunegala and Trincomalee, creating widespread awareness and enabling the businesses and members of the public to experience the convenience and benefits of digital payments.

At this event, the public will be able to receive personalised assistance from the financial institutions, including the banks, finance companies and e-money service providers, to register and effectively use a wide range of digital payment services.

Additionally, the financial institutions together with LPPL will conduct a merchant engagement initiative in the Jaffna town area, for the business entities to obtain the LANKAQR payment acceptance facility.

The Central Bank invites the members of the public and business community of Jaffna to take advantage of this opportunity to enhance their awareness and experience the conveniences of digital payments, with the guidance and personalised assistance provided by the financial institutions.
