Kadigamuwa embankment collapses again, train halted at Rambukkana

Colombo, Oct. 14 (Daily Mirror) - Part of an earth embankment near the Kadigamuwa railway station has collapsed again, sending soil onto the railway track and disrupting train services.

Railway workers have arrived at the site and are currently clearing the soil from the track.

A train that departed from Colombo Fort at 5.15 p.m. for Peradeniya has been halted at Rambukkana railway station due to the obstruction.

Authorities have assured that the obstruction will be cleared promptly to enable the train to resume its journey.
