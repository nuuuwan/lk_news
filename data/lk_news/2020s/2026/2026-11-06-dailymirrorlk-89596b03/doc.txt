Sri Lanka named ‘Luxury Food Destination of the Year’ at UK Travel & Taste Awards

Sri Lanka has been named ‘Luxury Food Destination of the Year’ at the Travel & Taste Awards 2026 in the United Kingdom, with judges citing the wave of new boutique hotel groups redefining luxury on the island among the reasons, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism said.

The award was presented by Travel & Taste Awards Chief Organiser Alex Crosley at Sri Lanka Tourism’s promotion stand during the World Travel Market exhibition in London.

The awards, which celebrate food-centric travel experiences and destinations across 17 categories, were judged by a panel of food and travel experts that included spirits specialist Millie Milliken-Holdsham, food travel content creator Verna Gao, and hospitality connoisseurs Gina Jackson and Millie Kenny-Ryder.

Besides the new boutique hotel groups, the judges placed particular emphasis on the island’s vibrant and dynamic culinary scene and how  Sri Lanka showcases its indigenous natural produce, according to the ministry.

The ministry noted that the recognition, which acknowledges the island’s culinary diversity and high-quality dining experiences, strengthens Sri Lanka’s position as an emerging luxury food and travel destination and “adds further momentum to the country’s efforts to attract quality-conscious and experience-driven tourists”.
