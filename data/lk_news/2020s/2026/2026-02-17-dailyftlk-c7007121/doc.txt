Yanika Amarasekera joins Vallibel One Board

Vallibel One PLC has appointed Yanika Amarasekera to its Board as an Independent Non-Executive Director.

Amarasekera holds an MSc in Entrepreneurship, Innovation and Management from Imperial College Business School, London, and dual Bachelor of Arts degrees in Political Economy and Mass Communication (with a focus on Digital Marketing) from the University of California, Berkeley.

She is the Founder and Chair of Silver Aisle – Sri Lanka’s first online wedding registry and luxury gifting platform for all occasions, launched in 2019. She currently serves as Chairperson and Chief Executive Officer of Silver Aisle Ltd.

She previously served as an Independent Non-Executive Director of LB Finance PLC and as a Non-Executive Director of Multi Finance PLC and Greener Water Ltd.

She was named to the Forbes 30-Under-30 Asia 2025 list within the retail and e-commerce segment in recognition of her work as Founder of Silver Aisle.
