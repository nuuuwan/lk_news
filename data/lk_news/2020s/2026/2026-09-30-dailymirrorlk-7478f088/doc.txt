Ranil Wickremesinghe’s case to be called on November 11

Colombo, Sep. 30 (Daily Mirror) - Colombo Fort Magistrate Pasan Amarasena today ordered that the case against former President Ranil Wickremesinghe and former Presidential Secretary Saman Ekanayake, who have been produced before court over allegations of misusing state funds, be called on November 11 for the Attorney General’s advice on the legal action to be taken against them.
