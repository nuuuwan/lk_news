GRI wins Silver for cleaner production

Global Rubber Industries (GRI) has won the Silver award in the Tyre Industry Sector – Manufacturing Large Category at the National Cleaner Production Awards 2026, organized by the National Cleaner Production Centre Sri Lanka.

The awards recognize organizations demonstrating excellence in Resource-Efficient and Cleaner Production (RECP) practices. Assessment covers energy and water efficiency, waste minimization, pollution prevention, resource conservation, circular economy practices and sustainable product development - areas that place the practical decisions behind production in the spotlight.

For GRI, the Silver Award marks a milestone in its journey. It highlights the importance of looking beyond the finished tyre to the processes, people and everyday decisions behind it.
