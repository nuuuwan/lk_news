Two excise officials arrested in bribery probe

Colombo, Nov 06 (Daily Mirror) - The Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) has arrested a retired Deputy Excise Commissioner and an Excise Corporal over allegations of accepting bribes.

According to the Commission, the arrests were made yesterday (05) at around 9.05 a.m. and 9.15 a.m., respectively.

The suspects have been identified as Channa Jayanath Alwis Weerakkody, a retired Deputy Excise Commissioner, and Polgampalage Sampath Ranasinghe, an Excise officer attached to the Matara Excise Station.

The retired Deputy Excise Commissioner is accused of accepting cheques issued from the accounts of a legally licensed company under his supervision while serving as the Excise Superintendent of Kalutara and the Acting Excise Superintendent (Distilleries) in Aluthgama.

Meanwhile, the other Excise officer is accused of accepting the same cheques as bribes, despite having no official authority to receive them in his capacity as an Excise officer attached to the company's distillery.

The two suspects are due to be produced before the Colombo Chief Magistrate's Court.
