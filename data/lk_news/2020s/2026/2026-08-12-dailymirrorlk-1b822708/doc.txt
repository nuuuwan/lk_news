Government trying to disable Judiciary and create dictatorship: Sajith

Colombo, Aug. 12 (Daily Mirror) - The Government is out to disable the Judiciary and create a dictatorship, Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa said today.

Speaking at a gathering in Galle, the Opposition Leader said the 22nd Amendment to the Constitution would weaken the Judiciary.

“The Government is trying to disable the Judiciary and then create a dictatorship,” he said.
