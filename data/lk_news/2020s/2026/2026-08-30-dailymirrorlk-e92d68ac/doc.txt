Fuel prices slashed

Colombo, Aug. 30 (Daily Mirror) - The Ceylon Petroleum Corporation (Ceypetco) has announced a reduction in the prices of Petrol 92 and Petrol 95 Octane, effective from midnight today (30).

The price of Petrol 92 Octane has been reduced by Rs. 15 per litre, from Rs. 414 to Rs. 399, while Petrol 95 Octane has been reduced by Rs. 20, from Rs. 495 to Rs. 475 per litre.

There will be no change in the prices of Auto Diesel, Super Diesel and Kerosene.

Accordingly, the new island-wide Ceypetco retail prices are Rs. 399 per litre for Petrol 92, Rs. 475 for Petrol 95, Rs. 382 for Auto Diesel, Rs. 478 for Super Diesel and Rs. 285 for Kerosene.
