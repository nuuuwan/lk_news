Three Sinha Industries named Sustainability Business Leader of the Year

Three Sinha Industries (Pvt) Ltd has been recognised as the Sustainability Business Leader of the Year at the prestigious Sri Lankan Sustainability Awards 2026, organised in conjunction with the 14th World Construction Symposium, reaffirming the company’s long-standing commitment to sustainable innovation and responsible manufacturing within Sri Lanka’s construction industry. The recognition was presented on 11 July 2026 at the Hilton Colombo, where industry leaders, policymakers, academics, and construction professionals gathered to celebrate organisations that have demonstrated outstanding leadership in advancing sustainability across the built environment. The award recognises Three Sinha Industries for its significant contribution to promoting sustainable business practices, innovation, and positive environmental and social impact. As one of Sri Lanka’s most trusted engineering and construction solution providers, the company has consistently invested in technologies and manufacturing processes that support energy efficiency, durability, and environmentally responsible development.
