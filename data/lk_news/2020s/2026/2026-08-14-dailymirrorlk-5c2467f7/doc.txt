Synapseus Technologies launches TapID

Synapseus Technologies launched TapID, a Sri Lankan-built digital business card and relationship management platform that goes beyond a typical NFC business card and is designed to transform the way the professionals network, connect and build robust relationships.

TapID helps individuals and challenges circumvent the challenges that arise with networking by going beyond simply exchanging traditional business cards and contact details.

Each introduction is transformed into meaningful connections as people can use their NFC card to tap and share a polished digital profile and save contacts instantly.

Further, they are prompted with notes, reminders and follow-up actions to build on these introductions.

TapID also offers searchable, exportable networking history as well as team analytics for organisations to make networking smart and efficient.

Designed for diverse users and with a special focus on Sri Lanka’s workforce, the platform currently supports English, Sinhala, Tamil and Arabic and has additional language support in the pipeline.

“Every business opportunity begins with a simple conversation but too many valuable connections are lost in the noise – TapID was inspired by this observation and is intended to bridge this gap and ease the networking process. We envision helping individuals build stronger professional networks while enabling the businesses to retain valuable customer relationships.

TapID combines multiple networking tools in one seamless platform and demonstrates that we are equipped to build world-class technology in Sri Lanka that can compete on a global stage,” said Synapseus Technologies Founder and Managing Director Milinda Lokuge.
