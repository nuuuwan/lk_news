World Odyssey brings 524 students to Colombo

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - The passenger vessel 'WORLD ODYSSEY', carrying 524 university students and faculty members, has arrived at the Colombo Port, bringing an opportunity for local tourism and related businesses.

The vessel, which sails under the flag of the Bahamas, also has a crew of 181 members on board.

Following its arrival, several groups of students have travelled to Kandy and tourist destinations in the Southern Province, while others are exploring Colombo.

A distinctive feature of the vessel is that it functions as a floating academic campus, with faculty members conducting lectures and educational activities for students while the ship is at sea.

The vessel will remain at the Colombo Port until November 23, during which arrangements have been made for groups of students to visit several universities in Sri Lanka.

The Ministry of Ports and Civil Aviation said the visit was expected to generate foreign revenue as the passengers had arrived on tourist visas.

The visit is also expected to benefit several sectors of the tourism industry, including hotels and transport service providers.
