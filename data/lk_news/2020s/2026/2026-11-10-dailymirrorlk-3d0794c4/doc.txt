Mahanayake requests prison date for Gnanasara Thera’s re-robing ceremony

Colombo, Nov. 10 (Daily Mirror) - The Mahanayake of the Kotte Chapter of the Siam Maha Nikaya, Most Ven. Dr. Ittapana Dhammalankara Thera, has requested the Commissioner General of Prisons to arrange a date and time for the re-robing ceremony of Ven. Galagoda Aththe Gnanasara Thera.

In a letter dated October 30, 2026, addressed to the Commissioner General of Prisons, the Mahanayake said the re-robing ceremony should be conducted in accordance with the Dhamma and Vinaya (Buddhist teachings and disciplinary rules).

He requested the Prisons Department to provide a suitable date and time as soon as possible to facilitate the ceremony.

The Mahanayake also informed the Commissioner General that a delegation of 10 members of the Kotte Chapter's Sangha Sabha, including himself, would visit the prison for the occasion.

He requested the authorities to make the necessary arrangements for the visit and inform him of the scheduled date and time.

A copy of the letter was also sent to the Superintendent of Welikada Prison.
