Sri Lanka men’s cricket team to tour Pakistan in October

Colombo, Sep 10 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka National Men’s Cricket Team is set to tour Pakistan in October and November 2026 for a multi-format series featuring T20 Internationals, an ODI Tri-Nation Series and two Test matches.

The tour will begin with a three-match T20I series against Pakistan at the Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium. The matches are scheduled for October 9, 11 and 13.

Sri Lanka will then join Pakistan and England in an ODI Tri-Nation Series. Sri Lanka will face Pakistan in Rawalpindi on October 18 and in Lahore on October 27, while the matches against England are scheduled for October 22 in Rawalpindi and October 29 in Lahore. The final is scheduled to be played on October 31.

The tour will conclude with the Test series against Pakistan. Sri Lanka will first play a three-day practice match against the Patrons XI in Islamabad from November 4 to 6.

The first Test against Pakistan is scheduled to be played in Rawalpindi from November 9 to 13, followed by the second Test in Lahore from November 17 to 21.
