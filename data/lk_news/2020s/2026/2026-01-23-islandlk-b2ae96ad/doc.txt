CSE donates LKR 25 million to post-Ditwah “Rebuilding Sri Lanka” fund

The Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE) recently made a significant donation of LKR 25 million to support the “Rebuilding Sri Lanka” Fund in support of post-cyclone Ditwah reconstruction.

The donation was formally presented at the Presidential Secretariat, where CSE Chairman, Dimuthu Abeyesekera, Chief Executive Officer, . Rajeeva Bandaranaike, and Senior Vice President – Finance and Administration, Kusal Nissanka, handed over the cheque to Dr. Nandika Sanath Kumanayake, Secretary to the President.
