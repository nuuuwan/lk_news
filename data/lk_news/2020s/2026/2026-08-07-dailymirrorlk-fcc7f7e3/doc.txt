Sentiment turns mixed, yield curve holds firm

The strong buying momentum and elevated trading volumes seen on Wednesday subsided yesterday, as investor sentiment turned mixed and market activity moderated.Consequently, the yield curve remained largely unchanged.

At the short end of the yield curve, 15.02.2028 and 15.03.2028 traded between 10.45% to 10.50% while further along the curve 01.07.2030, 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 were seen changing hands within the 11.31%-11.38% range.

In the 2031 segment 01.02.2031, 15.03.2031 and 15.05.2031 all traded between 11.38% to 11.58%. Moving ahead, 15.01.2033 and 01.11.2033 changed hands at 11.92% and 12.00% respectively while 15.10.2034 traded higher at 12.12%. Finally, at the long end of the curve 01.07.2037 was seen trading at 12.67%.

On the external front, the LKR appreciatedagainst the USD, standing at Rs. 335.60/USD, compared to Rs. 335.73/USD seen earlier.Overnight liquidity in the banking system expanded to Rs.218.0bn from Rs. 165.38bn recorded previously.
