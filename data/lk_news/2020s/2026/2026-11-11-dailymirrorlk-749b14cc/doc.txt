ComBank partners with Daraz 11.11

From left: Daraz Sri Lanka Country Payment Head Ashani Lidya, Brand Manager Safwan Yaseen, Managing Director Rukshan Xavier, Commercial Bank Chief Manager Card Centre Seevali Wickramasinghe, Senior Manager Merchant Solutions and Partnership Management Kalinga Divaratne and Merchant Solutions and Partnership Management Prabath Gangabada

Flat 25 percent discount during Brand Rush Hour and 12-month instalment plans for eligible cardholders

Demonstrating the bank’s commitment to offering the customers the best value throughout the year, Commercial Bank of Ceylon has partnered with Daraz Sri Lanka to kick off the festive season with Daraz 11.11, the platform’s flagship annual shopping campaign.

Driving exceptional value during the mega sale, the collaboration offers Commercial Bank credit and debit cardholders a flat 25 percent discount during the highly anticipated ‘Brand Rush Hour’, which launches at 8:00 p.m. on November 10 and concludes on November 21, 2026.

The eligible Commercial Bank credit cardholders may also opt for 12-month instalment plans on purchases made during the campaign, enabling them to manage their spending with greater flexibility.

In its 10th consecutive year in Sri Lanka, the highly anticipated campaign continues to scale, offering the Commercial Bank cardholders exclusive deals across more than 120 top-tier brands. The customers can maximise their savings across a comprehensive range of categories, from electronics and home appliances to fashion, beauty, groceries and lifestyle.

Commercial Bank Chief Manager Card Centre Seevali Wickramasinghe said, “Commercial Bank remains committed to delivering exceptional value to our cardholders through strategic partnerships with leading brands. Our collaboration with Daraz for the 11.11 campaign brings together attractive savings, exclusive privileges and flexible payment options, further enhancing the everyday value of our cards. Beyond providing rewarding shopping experiences, partnerships of this nature play an important role in accelerating the adoption of secure, convenient digital payments and supporting the continued growth of Sri Lanka’s digital economy.”

Daraz Sri Lanka Managing Director Rukshan Xavier said, “As Daraz 11.11 marks its 10th year in Sri Lanka, our focus remains on delivering greater value to customers. Our partnership with Commercial Bank enables us to extend exclusive cardholder benefits and flexible payment solutions, making online shopping more accessible.”

The collaboration reflects the shared commitment of both organisations to advancing e-commerce and encouraging secure, convenient digital payments in Sri Lanka.

The customers may also benefit from other campaign offers, including Big Boss Mega Deals, Big Boss Killer Deals and Daraz VMAX vouchers offering an additional 15 percent discount. The customers are encouraged to browse offers and collect vouchers on the Daraz app ahead of the sale.
