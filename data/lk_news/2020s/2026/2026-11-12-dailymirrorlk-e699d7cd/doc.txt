AIA Insurance Lanka recognised among Asia’s best workplaces

AIA Insurance Lanka has once again been recognised for its outstanding workplace culture, securing five prestigious accolades at the Great Place to Work Awards 2026. Reaffirming its commitment to creating an exceptional employee experience and fostering a people-first culture, these achievements further strengthen AIA’s reputation as one of Sri Lanka’s most sought-after employers.

AIA Group was ranked #15 on Asia’s Best Workplaces2026, a remarkable achievement earned through the collective efforts of participating business units. The ranking represents a significant rise from the previous year and places AIA among the very best employers in the region.

At the national level, AIA Sri Lanka was named one of the Best Workplaces in Sri Lanka 2026 – Large Category, achieving an impressive #4 ranking and securing its recognition for the 14th consecutive year. AIA Sri Lanka also earned the prestigious Cube Award for Best in Bold Acts of Leadership, recognising the organisation’s commitment to people-centred leadership while navigating an evolving business environment.

Further, AIA Sri Lanka received the Industry Excellence Award for Workplace Culture in the Financial Services & Insurance Sector for the second consecutive year;an accolade that recognises organisations that set the benchmark for employee engagement and organisational integrity within their industries. Demonstrating its continued investment in future talent, AIA Sri Lanka was recognised as one of the Best Workplaces for Young Talent for the third consecutive year.

These sustained achievements reflect the organisation’s unwavering focus on employee wellbeing, leadership excellence, inclusivity, and a culture built on trust and purpose.

Commenting on these significant recognitions, Director, Human Resources of AIA Sri Lanka, Thushari Perera, said, “Our people are our greatest strength. We are proud to cultivate a workplace where talent is nurtured, achievements are celebrated, and individuals are empowered to build meaningful and rewarding careers.”
