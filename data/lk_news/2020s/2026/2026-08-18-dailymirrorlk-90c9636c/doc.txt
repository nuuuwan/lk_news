Ven. Rambukana Siddhartha Thero passes away

Colombo, Aug. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Veteran Buddhist monk, renowned lyricist and author Ven. Rambukana Siddhartha Thero has passed away at the age of 76.

He died while undergoing treatment at a hospital, where he had been admitted following an illness.

Siddhartha Thero made a notable contribution to Sri Lanka’s music and literary fields, writing lyrics for numerous popular songs that gained widespread appreciation among music lovers.
