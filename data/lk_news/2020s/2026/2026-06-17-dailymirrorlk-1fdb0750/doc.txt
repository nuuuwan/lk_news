When you’re that rich, you can speak that low...” Trump jokes with UAE president

June 17 (Daily Mirror) - US President Donald Trump shared a light-hearted moment with UAE President Sheikh Mohammed bin Zayed Al Nahyan during the G7 Summit in France, drawing laughter with remarks about the Emirati leader's soft-spoken style.

After Sheikh Mohammed addressed the gathering in a calm tone, Trump quipped: "When you're that rich, you can speak that low."

As those present laughed, the US president continued: "I was just wondering, can anybody hear that?"

Trump then elaborated on the joke, saying: "But when you're so rich, you have such confidence that you don't have to do any strain to the voice."
