Sri Lankan Navy officer earns US Master’s with honours, wins two awards

Colombo, Oct 27 (Daily Mirror) - US Ambassador to Sri Lanka Eric Meyer today presented a master’s degree from the US Naval Postgraduate School (NPS) to Lieutenant Commander Piyumi Kathriarachchi of the Sri Lanka Navy at a ceremony held at the American Center in Colombo.

The ceremony was attended by Rear Admiral Rohan Joseph, Director General of Training, and Commander C.R.P. Walakuluge, Director of Media of the Sri Lanka Navy.

Lieutenant Commander Kathriarachchi graduated near the top of her class after completing the Defense Logistics Management programme at the NPS in Monterey, California, with honours and a grade point average of 3.98 out of 4.0.

She also received the NPS Outstanding Thesis Award and the Kiwanis Club of Monterey Outstanding International Student Award, which is presented to only two international students at the institution each year.

Her studies were funded through the US International Military Education and Training (IMET) programme, which supports the professional development of Sri Lankan military personnel and strengthens defence cooperation between the two countries.

Her award-winning research focused on spare parts procurement challenges faced by the Sri Lanka Navy, with findings aimed at addressing practical issues affecting naval logistics and readiness.

US Ambassador Eric Meyer said Lieutenant Commander Kathriarachchi’s achievements reflected her dedication and the value of American military education in developing the expertise of Sri Lankan defence personnel.

He said the knowledge and skills she had gained through US-funded training would help strengthen the Sri Lanka Navy and contribute to defence cooperation and security in the Indian Ocean region.

Following the ceremony, Lieutenant Commander Kathriarachchi met Sri Lankan youth at the American Center and shared her experiences of studying in the United States, encouraging them to pursue educational opportunities and develop skills that could contribute to the country’s future.

She said her studies in the United States had provided her with new tools to address practical challenges facing the Navy and expressed her intention to apply that knowledge in service of Sri Lanka.

A former student of Taxila Central College in Horana, Lieutenant Commander Kathriarachchi became the first female midshipman in Sri Lanka Navy history to receive the Sword of Honour in 2013.
