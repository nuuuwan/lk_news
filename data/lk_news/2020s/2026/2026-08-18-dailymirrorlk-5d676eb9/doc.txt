Education Council to be established in Sri Lanka

Colombo, Aug. 18 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet of Ministers has approved the preparation of a Bill to establish an Education Council to standardize, register, license, assure quality and accredit professionals in the general education sector.

The proposed council will cover professional categories including teachers, teacher advisors, principals, teacher educators and educational administrators.

A committee chaired by Senior Professor O. G. Dayaratne was appointed by the Secretary to the Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education to study the matter and make recommendations.

The committee submitted its report on October 3, 2025, which was subsequently presented to the Sub-Committee on the Establishment of the Education Council under the Ministerial Consultative Committee on Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education of Parliament.

Following public consultations on the recommendations contained in the report, a revised report was also submitted to the sub-committee.

Accordingly, the Cabinet, considering a proposal submitted by Prime Minister and Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education, approved instructing the Legal Draftsman to prepare a Bill for the establishment of the Education Council.

The drafting process is to be carried out in coordination with institutions already vested with relevant powers under the Constitution and other laws, ensuring that the proposed council’s functions do not conflict with existing legal mandates.
