Nuwara Eliya records lowest 12.5°C, Mannar highest 24.9°C

Colombo, Nov. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Nuwara Eliya recorded the lowest minimum temperature in the country at 12.5°C today (10), the  Meteorology Department said.

The temperature recorded in Nuwara Eliya was the lowest among the weather stations monitored by the Department.

Meanwhile, Bandarawela recorded a minimum temperature of 17.2°C, while Badulla recorded 19.2°C. Katugastota recorded 21.7°C and Monaragala 22.7°C.

Several other areas recorded minimum temperatures between 23°C and 25°C.

The readings included 24.2°C in Jaffna, 24.5°C in Mullaitivu, 24.9°C in Mannar, 24.1°C in Vavuniya, 24.4°C in Trincomalee, 24.5°C in Anuradhapura and Puttalam, and 24.2°C in Batticaloa.

In the Western Province, Colombo recorded 24.7°C, Katunayake 23.9°C and Rathmalana 24.5°C.

Galle recorded 24.6°C, Hambantota 24.8°C and Matara (Matlla in the chart) 24.0°C.

The figures are based on minimum temperatures reported by the Department of Meteorology on November 10, 2026.
