Colpetty circular Metro bus service to be suspended from December 1

Lanka Metro Transit (LMT) has announced the temporary suspension of its CM06 Colpetty circular bus service with effect from December 1.

In a notice, the company said the service would be temporarily discontinued from the specified date, while expressing regret over any inconvenience the decision may cause passengers.

However, the company has not disclosed the reason for suspending the service or indicated when operations are expected to resume.
