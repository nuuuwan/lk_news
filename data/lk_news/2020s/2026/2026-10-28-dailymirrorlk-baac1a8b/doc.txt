GPS Health-Ceylinco Life introduce digital cashless hospitalisation service

GPS Health has signed a strategic memorandum of understanding (MoU) with Ceylinco Life Insurance PLC, Sri Lanka’s No. 1 life insurance company, to introduce a next-generation cashless hospitalisation service powered by GPS Health’s Insurtech platform.

The MoU was signed by GPS Health Chairman Paranitharan Nadarajah and Ceylinco Life Insurance Chairman R. Renganathan.

Ceylinco Life selected GPS Health as its preferred technology and third-party service partner.

The collaboration connects insurers, hospitals and policyholders on one digital platform, delivering faster approvals, greater transparency and a smoother experience during medical emergencies.

GPS Health continues to set the benchmark for innovation and digital transformation in Sri Lanka’s insurance industry.
