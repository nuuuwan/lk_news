Secondary market stays lacklustre with selling pressure

The secondary market witnessed a sluggish trading day, amidst the moderate volumes and market activity.

The selling interest dominated the market, while the 2030 segment saw approximately 5-10bps increase in yields.

At the short end of the yield curve, 15.10.2029 changed hands at 10.45 percent. Over the 2030 segment, 01.03.2030 traded between 10.37 percent-10.51 percent, 01.08.2030 traded at 10.80 percent and 15.10.2030 changed hands within 10.80 percent-10.85 percent.

Moving ahead, 01.02.2031 traded at 10.92 percent, while 01.12.2031 traded between 11.05 percent-11.10 percent.

Towards the belly end of the yield curve, 01.06.2033, 01.11.2033 and 15.10.2034 traded at 11.50 percent, 11.65 percent and 11.80 percent, respectively.

Finally, at the long end, the 15.08.2036 maturity was dealt higher at 11.91 percent.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee marginally appreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.328.34/US dollar, compared to Rs.328.36/US dollar seen earlier.

Market liquidity in the banking system expanded to Rs.362.14 billion, from Rs.355.30 billion recorded previously.
