President Anura Kumara Dissanayake turns 58 today

Colombo, Nov 24 (Daily Mirror)- President Anura Kumara Dissanayake celebrates his 58th birthday today (November 24).

Born on November 24, 1968, in Dewahuwa, Matale, Dissanayake is Sri Lanka’s ninth Executive President.

He received his primary education at Thambuththegama Primary School and his secondary education at Thambuththegama Central College before entering the University of Kelaniya for higher studies.

His political career began in 1987 through the Socialist Students' Union, where he later became National Organiser in 1997.

He entered Parliament for the first time in 2000 and served as Minister of Agriculture in 2004.

In 2014, he was appointed leader of the Janatha Vimukthi Peramuna (JVP) and later played a key role in establishing the National People's Power (NPP) in 2019.

Dissanayake contested the 2019 Presidential Election, securing 3.16% of the votes, before winning the 2024 Presidential Election.

He was elected Sri Lanka's ninth Executive President on September 22, 2024, following decades in politics marked by electoral defeats and victories.
