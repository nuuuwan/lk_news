Three bullet fragments found in tusker Kavantissa’s abdomen

Colombo, Nov. 12 (Daily Mirror) - Three bullet fragments were found in the abdomen of the Kavantissa tusker, according to an interim post-mortem report submitted to the Mahawa Magistrate's Court today.

Meanwhile, Mahawa Magistrate Anuruddika Seneviratne ordered that the investigation into the shooting of the elephant be handed over to the Criminal Investigation Department (CID).

The Magistrate also directed the two suspects in custody to present evidence under the Public Property Act on the next court date, stating that the court had no authority to grant them bail under the Act.

The interim post-mortem report was submitted by Professor P.P.M.A. Wijesundara of the University of Peradeniya.
