Sri Lanka tests passenger drones for first time

Sri Lanka today conducted its first official trial of an electric-powered passenger drone service at Colombo Port City, using a Chinese-made EHang E216 electric vertical take-off and landing (eVTOL) aircraft capable of carrying two passengers without requiring a runway. The trial marks an initial step towards introducing passenger drone services in the country, with the Government currently working on regulations governing their future commercial operation. Pix by Mayurathy Pushparasa
