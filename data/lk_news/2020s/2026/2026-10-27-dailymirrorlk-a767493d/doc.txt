Mixed tones mark week’s start

Trading in the secondary market opened the week on a mixed footing, with the buying sentiment observed at the short end, while the longer tenures attracted selling interest, primarily from foreign investors.

Similar to the previous week, trading activity was largely confined to mid tenures, starting off with the 2030 bracket, within which maturities dated 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 traded from 11.50 percent to 11.55 percent.

Moving along the belly, the 01.12.2031 bond traded at 11.85 percent, followed by the 15.12.2032 maturity at 12.00 percent. Within the 2033 segment, activity was noted in the 01.06.2033 and 01.11.2033 maturities, both of which traded from 12.30 percent to 12.36 percent.

Finally, within the 2034 segment, the 15.06.2034 and 15.10.2034 maturities traded from 12.45 percent to 12.50 percent.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee depreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.332.23/US dollar, compared to Rs.332.03/US dollar seen earlier. Market liquidity in the banking system contracted to Rs.378.8 billion, compared to Rs.384.90 billion recorded previously.
