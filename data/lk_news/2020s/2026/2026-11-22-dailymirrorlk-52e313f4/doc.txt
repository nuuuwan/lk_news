Jeep driver arrested over assault on pedestrian in Borella

Colombo, Nov. 22 (Daily Mirror) - A 37-year-old jeep driver has been arrested in connection with an alleged assault on a pedestrian at a pedestrian crossing near the Kanatta Roundabout in Borella.

Police said the Borella Police had launched an investigation following a complaint regarding the incident, which reportedly took place on November 20 at around 3.30 p.m.

According to the complaint, two pedestrians were crossing the road at the designated crossing while the traffic signal was red. An argument allegedly broke out between the pedestrians and the driver of a jeep travelling from Rajagiriya towards the Kanatta Roundabout.

During the altercation, the driver is alleged to have stepped out of his vehicle and assaulted one of the pedestrians.

A team of officers from the Borella Police arrested the suspected driver yesterday afternoon. The suspect is a 37-year-old resident of the Borella area.

Following a medical examination conducted by the Colombo Chief Judicial Medical Officer, it was reported that the suspect was addicted to cannabis.

The suspect is scheduled to be produced before Magistrate's Court No. 02 in Aluthkade, Colombo, today (November 22).
