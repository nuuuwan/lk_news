NPP only government to professionally conclude IMF Programme: Bimal Rathnayake

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - The National People's Power (NPP) administration is the only government in Sri Lanka’s history to successfully conclude an International Monetary Fund (IMF) programme in a professional and dignified manner, Leader of the House and Minister Bimal Rathnayake said in Parliament today.

Addressing the House, the Minister asserted that the NPP government’s ability to work effectively with the IMF stems directly from the party's strict financial discipline.

"When we assumed office, the Opposition claimed our team lacked the capability to negotiate with the IMF. Instead, we deployed a strong, competent team to handle the talks. As a result, we are the only government to conclude an IMF programme in a professional and gentlemanly manner," Minister Rathnayake said.

He further noted that leaders from other leftist nations have inquired how Sri Lanka managed to stay on track with the IMF programme while simultaneously raising public sector wages.

"We successfully continued the IMF programme while increasing state-sector salaries, expanding welfare benefits, and refraining from selling off state-owned enterprises. We achieved this balance with the IMF purely through our financial discipline," he added.
