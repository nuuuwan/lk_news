Entrepreneurship, financial literacy to enter Grade 6 curriculum in 2027

Colombo, November 11 (Daily Mirror) - Entrepreneurship and financial literacy will be introduced into the Grade 6 curriculum from 2027, Prime Minister and Education Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya said.

The move is aimed at improving financial literacy among the younger generation, protecting them from financial fraud and encouraging greater public participation in the country's productive economic activities.

The Prime Minister made the announcement yesterday (10) at an event held at Isurupaya, the Ministry of Education premises, to recognise the winners of the first All-Island Commerce Knowledge and Entrepreneurial Skills Competition and launch activity-based learning centres for commerce education.

Speaking at the event, she said commercial education, which had previously been largely limited to students following the Commerce stream at the G.C.E. Ordinary Level and Advanced Level, would be introduced as a broader life skill across the school system from Grade 6.

She stressed the importance of improving financial literacy at national level to help people make informed financial decisions and protect themselves from financial scams.

The Prime Minister said the Government did not intend to turn everyone into entrepreneurs. However, she said it was essential to encourage a larger section of the population to actively participate in economic activities to strengthen the country's production-based economy.

She also emphasised the need to develop entrepreneurs who go beyond profit-making and demonstrate environmental awareness, humane values and social responsibility.

The Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education organised the all-island competition for the first time to assess students' commercial knowledge and entrepreneurial skills. The winning students received awards and certificates from the Prime Minister.

Meanwhile, a programme to establish regional learning centres was launched to make commerce education more practical and activity-based.

A total of 100 centres are planned across the country in three phases from 2026 to 2028. The first phase, scheduled for this year, will establish 25 centres, covering all 25 districts.

Several supplementary educational resources covering basic commerce-related subjects, along with a collection of modules introducing new teaching and learning methods, were also launched.
