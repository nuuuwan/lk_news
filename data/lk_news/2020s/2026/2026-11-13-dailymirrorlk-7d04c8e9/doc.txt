Three Chinese food businesses fined Rs. 444,000

Colombo, Nov. 13 (Daily Mirror) - Three Chinese food businesses operating in Colombo have been fined a total of Rs. 444,000 over 148 violations of food safety regulations.

The fines were imposed by the Maligakanda Magistrate’s Court on Friday (13), following legal action initiated by the Colombo Municipal Council (CMC) based on inspections carried out by its Public Health Inspectors (PHIs).

The businesses include two Chinese restaurants operating inside a popular shopping mall in Colombo and an ice cream company.

The two restaurants were fined a combined Rs. 360,000 after being found guilty of 120 charges relating to food safety violations.

During surprise inspections, PHIs discovered imported food products being sold and used for consumption without proper labels. They also found stocks of expired food items at the establishments.

Following the discoveries, the CMC initiated legal proceedings against the companies operating the restaurants.

The Maligakanda Magistrate’s Court subsequently imposed fines totalling Rs. 360,000 on the two businesses.
