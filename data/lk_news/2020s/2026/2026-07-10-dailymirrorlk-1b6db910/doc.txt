Avissawella hospital wins five 5S awards

Representatives of the Avissawella District General Hospital receive five 5S Certification Awards during the 10th Anniversary Celebration and 5S Certification Award Ceremony held at the Galle Face Hotel in Colombo yesterday (9). Organized by the National Productivity Secretariat under the Ministry of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development, the event recognized institutions for excellence in workplace organization, productivity, and quality management. The ceremony was held under the patronage of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development Minister Sunil Handunhetti and attended by Ministry Secretary Thilaka Jayasundara. Pix by Pradeep Pathirana.
