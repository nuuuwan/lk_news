Vallibel Finance surpasses Rs.200bn in total assets

Vallibel Finance PLC yesterday disclosed its total asset base has exceeded Rs.200 billion, marking a key milestone for the listed finance company as it continues to expand its balance sheet. In a disclosure to the Colombo Stock Exchange, the company said its total assets had surpassed the Rs.200 billion mark as at July 31, 2026, based on the unaudited management accounts. The company did not provide a breakdown of the asset growth or indicate the factors that contributed to the increase.
