All liquor shops to remain closed tomorrow

Colombo, Oct. 2 (Daily Mirror) - All licensed liquor outlets across Sri Lanka will remain closed tomorrow (03) in view of World Temperance Day, the Excise Department announced.

Accordingly, all liquor stores islandwide will be closed on Saturday (Oct. 3) to mark the annual observance.

World Temperance Day is observed on October 3 each year to raise awareness about temperance, promote moderation and highlight the importance of abstaining from alcohol consumption.

The annual observance also draws attention to the health and social concerns associated with harmful alcohol use.

Meanwhile, global health estimates indicate that alcohol consumption is linked to nearly three million deaths annually, underscoring the continuing public health concerns associated with alcohol use.
