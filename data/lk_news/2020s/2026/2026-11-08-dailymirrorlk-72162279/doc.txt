Poppy Day commemorated in Colombo

Poppy Day, also known as Remembrance Day or Armistice Day, was commemorated in Sri Lanka on November 8, ahead of the international observance on November 11. A ceremony was held at the Armed Forces Memorial Cenotaph in Viharamahadevi Park, Colombo, under the patronage of Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya.. Organised by the Sri Lanka Armed Forces Veterans' Association in collaboration with the three armed forces, the event honoured military personnel who sacrificed their lives in past and present conflicts.
