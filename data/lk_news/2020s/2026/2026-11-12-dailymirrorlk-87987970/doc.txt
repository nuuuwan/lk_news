Rupee weakens slightly against US Dollar

Colombo, Nov. 12 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lankan Rupee depreciated marginally against the US Dollar today (12), with the Central Bank of Sri Lanka reporting slight increases in both the buying and selling exchange rates compared to Wednesday.

According to the Central Bank's latest exchange rates, the US Dollar buying rate increased to Rs. 327.91 from Rs. 327.84 recorded on Wednesday.

Meanwhile, the selling rate rose to Rs. 336.82 from Rs. 336.79.
