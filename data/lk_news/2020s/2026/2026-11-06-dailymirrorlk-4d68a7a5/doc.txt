Xiaomi introduces Redmi Note 17 series

Xiaomi, a global consumer electronics and smart manufacturing brand, unveiled the Redmi Note 17 series in Sri Lanka, alongside an expanded Xiaomi ecosystem spanning entertainment, smart home technology, personal care, health and fitness, kitchen appliances and lifestyle products.

The Redmi Note 17 series comprises four models: Redmi Note 17 Pro Max 5G, Redmi Note 17 Pro 5G, Redmi Note 17 5G and Redmi Note 17. The lineup brings together long-lasting battery performance, immersive displays, enhanced durability and AI-powered capabilities, offering the consumers a range of options to suit their needs.

Leading the series, Redmi Note 17 Pro Max 5G features a 10,000mAh battery with 100W HyperCharge, designed to deliver up to three days of use. Its 6.83-inch 1.5K CrystalRes AMOLED display supports a refresh rate of up to 120Hz, while a 50MP main camera with optical image stabilisation (OIS), Snapdragon 6 Gen 5 processor and Xiaomi HyperAI with Google Gemini support an enhanced everyday smartphone experience.

Redmi Note 17 Pro 5G combines an 8,340mAh battery with 67W HyperCharge, while both Redmi Note 17 models feature 7,700mAh batteries and 45W charging. Across the range, features include high-refresh-rate AMOLED displays, capable camera systems and AI-assisted tools. The Pro models also offer IP66, IP68, IP69 and IP69K dust and water resistance, alongside certified drop resistance of up to three metres.

Xiaomi Sri Lanka Country Head Kain Wang said, “With Redmi Note 17 Series, we are delivering a combination of performance, durability, battery endurance and intelligent features that meet the evolving expectations of consumers.”

Beyond smartphones, Xiaomi introduced a broader portfolio of ecosystem products across multiple categories. The entertainment range includes televisions and sound speakers, while smart home offerings extend to security cameras, smart locks, LED bulbs, robot vacuum cleaners, air purifiers and tower fans.

The ecosystem also encompasses earbuds, watches, smart bands, headphones and audio glasses, alongside personal care products such as hair dryers, straightener brushes, trimmers and shavers. Smart scales, body composition scales and massage guns expand the health and fitness category, while blenders, air fryers, microwave ovens and water dispensers bring technology into the kitchen. Xiaomi Tags, power banks and scooters further extend the portfolio into everyday mobility and lifestyle needs.

The event featured product presentations by Wang and Xiaomi Sri Lanka Head of Marketing Sandani Gajadeera, giving the attendees an overview of the new smartphone range, pricing and launch offers as well as the wider ecosystem.

The consumers purchasing eligible Redmi Note 17 series devices from November 02 to 18 can benefit from an 18-month instalment plan, with the first month’s instalment free up to Rs.12,000, an 18-month warranty and a one-time screen replacement within the first year. The buyers of eligible 5G devices will also receive 275GB of free data, subject to applicable terms and conditions.
