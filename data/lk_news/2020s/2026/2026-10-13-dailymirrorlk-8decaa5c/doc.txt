Dharmaraja primary section gets Rs. 4 mn upgrade

Dharmaraja College, Kandy, officially handed over the “Abhiman Project – 2026,” an electrical and public address system upgrade worth over Rs. 4 million, to the College’s Primary Section on October 9. Led by the 2005/2008 Old Boys’ Group, with support from the main Old Boys’ Union, the project aims to provide safer electrical facilities and clearer public address services for students and teachers. The handover ceremony was attended by the College Principal Namal Chandana Kumara, Primary Section Principal Anoma Nawarathne, past pupils, teachers, parents and students.
