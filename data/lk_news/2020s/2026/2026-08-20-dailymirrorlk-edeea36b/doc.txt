Secondary market sees mixed activity amid modest volumes

The secondary market saw mixed activity yesterday, with modest volumes and slight movements across the yield curve.

At the short end of the curve, 01.05.2028 traded at 10.05 percent. In the 2029 segment, 15.10.2029 and 15.12.2029 traded between 10.50 percent and 10.55 percent. In the 2030 segment, 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 traded between 10.80 percent and 10.90 percent.

In the belly of the curve, 15.12.2032 traded at 11.25 percent. In the 2033 segment, 15.01.2033 traded at 11.50 percent while 01.11.2033 traded at 11.60 percent.  The 15.10.2034 maturity traded at 11.85 percent. Finally, at the long end of the curve, 01.07.2037 attracted foreign interest and traded between 12.15 percent and 12.10 percent.

Moreover, the PDMO concluded its weekly T-bill auction, successfully raising the full initial offer of Rs.140.0 billion. The three-month, six-month and 12-month bills raised Rs.55.0 billion, Rs.45.0 billion and Rs.40.0 billion, respectively, in line with the amounts initially offered.

The weighted average yields on the three-month and six-month bills declined by 22bps and 18bps, respectively, to 9.22 percent and 9.60 percent.  Meanwhile, the 12-month T-bill yield declined by 10bps, moving into single-digit territory after May 2026 and settling at 9.91 percent.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee appreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.331.83/US dollar, compared to Rs.332.29/US dollar seen earlier. Market liquidity in the banking system contracted to Rs.288.98 billion from Rs.292.85 billion recorded previously.
