Committee appointed to resolve issues concerning Catholic schools

A committee headed by Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education Ministry Secretary Nalaka Kaluwewa has been appointed to expedite the resolution of issues concerning Catholic schools, including their administration, education and the recruitment of teachers for Catholic religious instruction.

The decision was taken at a meeting held at the Presidential Secretariat between representatives of the Catholic Bishops’ Conference, Minister of Science and Technology Prof. Chrishantha Abeysena and Secretary to the President Dr. Nandika Sanath Kumanayake.

The meeting reviewed progress on agreements reached previously and measures taken to address issues relating to the administration of Catholic schools taken over by the Government, education-related matters and the recruitment of teachers for Catholic religious instruction.

Archbishop of Colombo Malcolm Cardinal Ranjith, President of the Catholic Bishops’ Conference Most Rev. Dr. Harold Anthony Perera and other members of the Conference attended the meeting.
