Shift Group celebrates 8 wins at The Dragons

Shift Group has marked another significant milestone in its creative journey, securing eight wins, including two Golds and a Bronze, at The Dragons of Sri Lanka 2026, one of the country’s most recognised platforms for advertising excellence.

Leading the accolades was ‘Fill Her Glass’, created for American Premium Water Systems for the World Water Day. The campaign secured Golds for Creative Excellence and Cause, Environment or Sustainability, while winning a Black Dragon for Small Budget.

For Commercial Credit & Finance Sri Lanka, Shift Group’s ‘Let Her Write Her Own Story’ challenged another deeply rooted social narrative: the belief that women need a saviour. The campaign won Bronze for Creative Excellence and Black for Cause, Environment or Sustainability.

Shift Group also brought an unconventional approach to corporate awareness with JXG (Janashakthi Group) Sri Lanka’s ‘We’re Sure You’re Not Aware of Us’. The approach successfully turned attention into awareness, earning two Black Dragons for Innovative Idea or Concept and Social Media.

Meanwhile, ‘Dengeez 2.0’ for Softlogic Life continued the brand’s unconventional approach to dengue awareness. The campaign earned a Black Dragon for Innovative Idea or Concept.

Shift Group CEO Sujeewa Samarasekera and Managing Director Chrishani Kotalawela said, “These wins are a reflection of what happens when strong client partnerships meet courageous thinking and an exceptional team.”

Managing Partner and Chief Creative Officer Sandun Lakmal said, “These campaigns allowed us to use creativity to challenge social norms, shine a light on important issues and find unexpected ways to engage people.”

Shift Solutions CEO Charitha Waidyaratne added, “The eight wins are a tremendous achievement for the team and a testament to their passion, resilience and willingness to push beyond the obvious.”
