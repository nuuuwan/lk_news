S-lon Roofing expands portfolio

S-lon, one of Sri Lanka’s most trusted names in the construction industry, has strengthened its roofing portfolio with the introduction of two new roofing profiles, bringing its range to three popular profiles designed to meet the evolving market needs.

The expanded range offers greater choice and flexibility, catering to diverse architectural and functional requirements across the residential, commercial, hospitality and industrial sectors, including houses, hotels, commercial buildings, warehouses and industrial facilities.

Driven by a deep understanding of customer preferences and changing market demands, this strategic expansion reflects S-lon Roofing’s continued commitment to product innovation, manufacturing excellence and delivering reliable roofing solutions.

It also strengthens the brand’s ability to pursue new opportunities in international markets, taking the quality and reliability of Sri Lankan-manufactured roofing solutions beyond its shores.
