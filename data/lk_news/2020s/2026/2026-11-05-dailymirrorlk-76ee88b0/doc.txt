Drug-laden trawler intercepted off South

Deputy Minister of Defence Major General Aruna Jayasekara (Retd.), accompanied by Navy Commander Vice Admiral Damian Fernando and senior Navy and Police Narcotics Bureau officials, inspects the 189 kilogrammes of Ice and heroin seized from a fishing trawler in deep seas off Sri Lanka's southern coast. The consignment has an estimated street value exceeding Rs. 4.2 billion.
