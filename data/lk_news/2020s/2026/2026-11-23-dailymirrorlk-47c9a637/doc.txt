Sri Lanka hopeful of securing RCEP membership: Foreign Minister

Colombo, Nov. 23 (Daily Mirror) - Foreign Minister Vijitha Herath expressed confidence in Parliament today that Sri Lanka will secure membership in the Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership (RCEP) in due course.

Addressing the House, the Minister noted that Sri Lanka has formally applied to join the regional trade bloc and revealed that RCEP has already appointed a dedicated committee to evaluate the country’s application.

Turning to broader trade policy, Minister Herath explained that the government had established a committee to review previously signed Free Trade Agreements (FTAs) due to certain concerns regarding their content. Based on that committee's findings, several sub-committees have now been appointed to determine the next steps regarding these FTAs.

The Minister added that measures will be taken to implement the revised agreements by the beginning of next year.
