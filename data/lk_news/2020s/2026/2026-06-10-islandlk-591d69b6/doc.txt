Meridian Talent launches to counter AI-led hiring with human-first recruitment model

As hiring becomes increasingly speed-driven and automated, businesses are being pushed to treat hiring as a race to fill roles rather than a decision about long-term fit and performance. Meridian Talent, the specialist recruitment division of Meridian Creative Solutions led by Az Da Rosa, argues that hiring accuracy should be treated as a commercial priority, not just a recruitment target.

Technology leaders are under mounting pressure to hire quickly, but the commercial risk of getting a hire wrong is substantial. Global research frequently places the cost of a bad hire at 30% of first-year salary at minimum, rising materially for senior roles. At the same time, specialised technology roles commonly take weeks to fill, increasing delivery risk, team strain, and opportunity cost. Meridian Talent’s model is built to reduce those risks through alignment-first, human-led assessment, where technology supports judgment rather than replacing it.

“AI tools can be useful at handling volume, but they cannot reliably measure the things that determine whether someone will thrive in a technical team,” said Az Da Rosa, Co-Founder and CEO of Meridian Talent. “In my experience, the hires that fail are rarely just a skills problem. They’re an alignment problem: working style, communication under pressure, delivery mindset, and cultural fit. Our job is to surface that early, so hiring feels less like a gamble and more like a partnership.”

Viraj Adhihetty, Founder and Director of Meridian Creative Solutions, said the goal is not to reject automation, but to restore context to a process that has become overly keyword-driven. “Automation and AI absolutely have a place in modern hiring,” he said. “But technology should support judgment, not replace it. When recruitment optimises only for speed, companies pay for it later in attrition, lost momentum, and mis-hires. Meridian Talent exists to help employers hire with clarity, not just urgency.”

Meridian Talent’s approach is built on three core principles: real specialist understanding of roles and team context; chemistry and culture fit alongside capability; and a respect-driven process that treats candidates as partners. Rather than relying solely on job descriptions, the firm spends time understanding how a business actually functions: delivery pressures, team dynamics, role expectations, and future direction.
