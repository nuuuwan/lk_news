CWIT propels Colombo Port to new heights with region’s tallest electric cranes

Colombo West International Terminal (Private) Limited (CWIT) has officially commenced its Phase 2 capacity development, marking a significant stride in its mission to redefine regional maritime infrastructure.

The terminal recently welcomed the arrival of two Ship-to-Shore (STS) electric cranes and seven Cantilever Rail Mounted Gantry Cranes (CRMGs). Distinguished as the tallest of their kind in the region, these cutting-edge assets represent a substantial capital investment aimed at delivering faster, smarter, and more sustainable cargo handling operations.

By introducing this future-ready infrastructure, CWIT reinforces its commitment to schedule integrity and reliable connectivity for global shipping lines. This major infrastructure milestone comes on the heels of a historic inaugural year of operations. Moving a record-breaking one million twenty-foot equivalent units (TEUs), CWIT has become the fastest terminal in the history of the Port of Colombo to achieve the one-million-TEU milestone within its first year.
