Leading corporates come on board for ‘IN GOOD TASTE 2026’

Leading corporates and brands including Singer, Luxury Brands, The Wijeya Group, Derana and Ada Derana News Channel, among others, are coming on board for IN GOOD TASTE 2026, an exclusive two-day lifestyle festival celebrating food, creativity, culture and community, taking over The Shoppes at City of Dreams on 7 and 8 November.

Presented by The Shoppes at City of Dreams, with extensive media support from the Wijeya Group and the Derana Media Network, IN GOOD TASTE 2026 is designed not only as a fun-filled family event, but also as a platform with a strong corporate focus.

“For participating brands, IN GOOD TASTE offers a unique opportunity to connect with a discerning audience in a premium environment while benefiting from a comprehensive communications campaign spanning print, television, radio, digital and social media platforms,” said Senior Vice President, Colombo Hotels & General Manager, Cinnamon Life at City of Dreams, Sri Lanka, Kamal Munasinghe.

“More than an event, IN GOOD TASTE represents the beginning of a new chapter for Colombo’s lifestyle and retail landscape. Designed as a festival of flavour, fun and fabulous experiences, the event will feature celebrity chefs, culinary competitions, fashion showcases, art installations, live entertainment and curated lifestyle experiences, attracting a highly engaged audience of consumers, tastemakers, media and industry leaders.”

Munasinghe welcomed the participation of the festival’s wide cross-section of partners, while Chairman, Headlines PR, Anusha David said, “This unique festival brings together food, art, fashion and lifestyle in celebration of Sri Lankan creativity, talent and community.

Hosted at  The Shoppes at City of Dreams, it promises to be one of Colombo’s most exciting lifestyle events of the year, and we guarantee everyone who attends an unforgettable weekend.”
