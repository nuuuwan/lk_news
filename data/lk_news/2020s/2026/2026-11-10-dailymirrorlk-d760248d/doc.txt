Four Chinese nationals arrested for collecting wildlife species in Sinharaja

Colombo, Nov. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Four Chinese nationals, including a woman, have been arrested by wildlife officers for allegedly collecting insects and reptile species from the Sinharaja Forest Reserve.

The suspects were arrested during a raid conducted by wildlife officers following information received by the Panahaduwa Wildlife Range Office of Udawalawe National Park.

More than 250 specimens belonging to nearly 40 insect and animal species were recovered from their possession, the Department of Wildlife Conservation said.

Preliminary investigations have revealed that the suspects had visited Sri Lanka on previous occasions as well.

The suspects, along with the recovered specimens, were produced before the Kalawana Magistrate's Court, which ordered that they be remanded until November 23, 2026.

The Magistrate also ordered that the living and non-living specimens recovered from the suspects be handed over to the National Museum today (10) for identification and arrangements to release the living specimens into their natural habitats.
