’’Profood Propack & Agbiz 2026’’

The exhibition "Profood Propack & Agbiz 2026" for the food processing, packaging, and agriculture sectors will take place from August 21 to 23 at the BMICH. The MEGA trade fair will provide an opportunity for all businesses and individuals involved in the processed food, beverages, packaging and agriculture sectors to showcase their products and services to a targeted audience.Pix by Kithsiri De Mel
