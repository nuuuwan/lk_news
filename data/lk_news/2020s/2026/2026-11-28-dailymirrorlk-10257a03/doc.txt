Murder suspect hospitalised after colliding with wild elephant while fleeing

A man suspected of murdering a 45-year-old woman in Mahailluppallama, Eppawala, was hospitalised after colliding with a wild elephant while fleeing the scene of the crime, police said.

The murder took place in the early hours of Friday (27), reportedly following a financial dispute between the suspect and the victim.

According to preliminary investigations, the suspect, who was closely associated with the woman, had allegedly assaulted her using a sharp weapon.

He had fled the area following the attack but was later admitted to Eppawala Hospital after colliding with a wild elephant.

Police investigations have also revealed that the suspect had consumed poison.

The victim was a 45-year-old resident of Mahailluppallama. Police have not yet disclosed the identity or age of the suspect.
