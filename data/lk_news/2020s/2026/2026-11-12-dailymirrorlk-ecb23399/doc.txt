Special Commodity Levy revised on essential food imports from November 13

Colombo, Nov. 12 (Daily Mirror) - The Special Commodity Levy (SCL) on several essential food imports, including sprats, Maldive fish, dried chillies and sugar, will be revised with effect from November 13, 2026, under the 2027 Budget proposals.

Accordingly, the levy on imported sprats and Maldive fish will be reduced to Rs. 10 per kilogram.

The SCL on both powdered and unpowdered dried chillies will be reduced by Rs. 50 per kilogram, while the levy on imported sugar will be increased by Rs. 10 per kilogram.
