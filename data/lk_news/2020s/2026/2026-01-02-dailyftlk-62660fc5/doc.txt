Estate workers donate to ‘Rebuilding Sri Lanka’ Fund

Estate employees from the N.C. Division of the Kotiyagala Estate in Bogawantalawa have contributed a day’s wages to the ‘Rebuilding Sri Lanka’ Fund, backing the country’s recovery efforts.

The contribution, amounting to Rs. 108,000, was formally handed over recently to National People’s Power (NPP) Nuwara Eliya District MPs Manjula Suraweera Arachchi and Krishnan Kalaichelvi.
