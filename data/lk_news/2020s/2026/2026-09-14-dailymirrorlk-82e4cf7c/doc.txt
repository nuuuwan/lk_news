Oil price surge weighs on CSE as ASPI slips below 21,350

The Colombo Stock Exchange opened the week lower on 14 September 2026, as the sharp rise in global oil prices weighed on investor sentiment and triggered broad-based selling across the market.

The ASPI declined 68.82 points, or 0.32%, to close at 21,313.92, while the S&P SL20 fell 8.14 points, or 0.14%, to 5,994.33. Selling pressure intensified after midday as investors assessed the potential impact of higher oil prices on inflation, corporate costs and the broader economy.

Market turnover stood at LKR 1.55 billion, with 56.23 million shares traded. The Capital Goods sector led activity with LKR 477.97 million in turnover, while John Keells Holdings recorded the highest individual counter turnover at LKR 254.81 million.

Crossing activity was notable, accounting for around 44% of total turnover. Sampath Bank led crossings with approximately LKR 213.75 million, followed by Lanka IOC with LKR 203.00 million and John Keells Holdings with LKR 148.61 million.

Market breadth remained firmly negative, with only 66 gainers against 141 decliners, while foreign investors recorded a net outflow of LKR 94.71 million.

Ceylinco Insurance was the largest negative contributor to the ASPI, followed by Lion Brewery, Dialog Axiata, Aitken Spence and John Keells Holdings. Carson Cumberbatch provided the strongest positive contribution.

The combination of elevated oil prices, weak market breadth and continued foreign selling kept sentiment firmly cautious. With nearly half of turnover generated through crossings, underlying market participation remained relatively soft, leaving the ASPI vulnerable unless broader buying interest returns.
