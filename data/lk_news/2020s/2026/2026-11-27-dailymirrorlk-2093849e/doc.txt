Navy seizes suspected drug-smuggling vessel in international waters south of Dondra

Colombo, Nov. 27 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Navy has apprehended seven suspects along with a flagless vessel suspected of transporting heroin and crystal methamphetamine (Ice) during a special operation in international waters south of Dondra.

The operation was conducted beyond Sri Lanka’s Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ), following intelligence shared by local and foreign intelligence agencies and maritime partners, the Navy said.

The vessel and the seven suspects are currently being escorted to shore to be handed over to the Police Narcotics Bureau (PNB) for further investigations and legal proceedings.

The Navy said its coordination with regional and international maritime partners had strengthened efforts to combat drug trafficking and ensure freedom of navigation along Indian Ocean shipping lanes.

It also highlighted the role of Sri Lanka’s membership in the Combined Maritime Forces (CMF) and the Information Fusion Centre (IFC) in Colombo in improving Maritime Domain Awareness through real-time intelligence, satellite surveillance and information sharing.

Joint aerial reconnaissance and surveillance operations conducted with the Sri Lanka Air Force have further strengthened the monitoring of maritime activities, the Navy added.

The seizure was carried out as part of the Government’s *Ratama Ekata* (A Nation United) national campaign, launched in October 2025 to eradicate the drug menace.

Since the launch of the campaign, the Navy has seized narcotics with an estimated gross street value exceeding Rs. 116 billion.

Meanwhile, since January 2026, a total of 66 vessels, including four foreign vessels, and 379 suspects, including 18 foreign nationals, have been brought before the law through Navy operations.
