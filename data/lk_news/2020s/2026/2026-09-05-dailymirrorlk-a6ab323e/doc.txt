Lions District 306D4 installs new Cabinet

Lions Clubs International District 306D4 held its Cabinet Installation and the first Cabinet meeting for the Lionistic year 2026/27, in grand scale at the Cinnamon Lake side, chaired by District Governor Lion Diluka Mahawatte PMJF, PMAF.

LCIF Trustee and Past International Director Lion Mahesh Pasqual PMJF, PMAF and Lion Dr. Manel Pasqual MJF, were the Chief Guests at this occasion, where a Cabinet of 157 Lion members were installed.

The District Governor’s theme “Empower to Elevate” encourages every Lion to strengthen Leadership, enhance service, and elevate impact of Lionism throughout our country.

Lions and Leos make a difference every day, everywhere they serve. With the support of the international association and the global foundation, they are changing lives, communities and the world we share.

Among the large gathering of Lions, Lion Ladies and Leos were the Past District Governors, and many other distinguished invitees.
