The Editors’ Guild of Sri Lanka elects new office bearers

The Annual General Meeting of The Editors’ Guild of Sri Lanka (TEGSL) was held on Tuesday and elected new office bearers for the year 2026-2027.

Apex body: Manik De Silva, Sinha Ratnatunga, Siri Ranasinghe, Prabhath Sahabandu

Executive Committee: Nisthar Cassim (Daily FT), Mahinda Illeperuma (Sathiaga Aruna), Kesara Abeywardena (Daily News), Vindya Amaranayaka (Ceylon Today), Sivaraja Ramasamy (Thamilan)
