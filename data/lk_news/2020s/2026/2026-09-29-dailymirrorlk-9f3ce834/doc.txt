Flood warnings lifted for four river basins

Colombo, Sep. 29 (Daily Mirror) - The Department of Irrigation has lifted the flood warnings issued for four river basins following a return to normal water levels.

The Hydrology and Disaster Management Division said the warnings issued under notices No. DI/Hydro/103(V), along with flood warnings issued for the relevant river basins, had been withdrawn as water levels had returned to normal.
