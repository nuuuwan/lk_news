Public officers to receive training in Azerbaijan under new MoU

Nov. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Public officers are to receive training programmes and internship opportunities in Azerbaijan under a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) signed between Sri Lanka's Ministry of Public Administration and Azerbaijan's ASAN Service.

The agreement was signed on November 7 by Minister of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government Prof. Chandana Abayarathna, on behalf of Sri Lanka.

The MoU aims to modernise public administration and improve the efficiency of public service delivery in Sri Lanka, with particular emphasis on developing the skills of public officers involved in service delivery.

Under the agreement, Sri Lankan trainers in relevant fields will receive training in Azerbaijan. They will subsequently develop and conduct training programmes in Sri Lanka, sharing the knowledge and expertise gained.

The agreement is expected to contribute to significant improvements in Sri Lanka's public administration and public service delivery over the next three years.

It was signed on the sidelines of the United Nations Governance Innovation Summit, held on November 5 and 6 at Gulustan Palace in Baku, Azerbaijan, under the theme "Advancing Future-Ready Institutions".

Addressing a panel discussion during the summit's second session, Minister Abayarathna stressed the importance of public service reforms, digitalisation and strengthening public trust.

He also highlighted the need to ensure that public services are accessible, simple and efficient for all sections of society, particularly rural communities and vulnerable groups.

The summit was jointly organised by the United Nations Department of Economic and Social Affairs (UN DESA) and Azerbaijan's State Agency for Public Service and Social Innovations under the President.

Ministers and senior public officials from more than 100 countries, along with representatives of international organisations, development partners, the private sector and academia, attended the summit.
