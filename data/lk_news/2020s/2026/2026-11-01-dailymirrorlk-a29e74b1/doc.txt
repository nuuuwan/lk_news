Vijitha urges media to counter misinformation

Colombo, Nov. 1 (Daily Mirror) - Responsible journalism plays a vital role in combating misinformation, strengthening public trust and promoting peace and understanding among communities, Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Minister Vijitha Herath said.

Addressing the welcome session of the 63rd General Assembly of the Asia-Pacific Broadcasting Union (ABU) at Cinnamon Life, City of Dreams, Colombo, today (November 1), the Minister said the rapid advancement of technology had made information more accessible while making it increasingly difficult to distinguish between truth and falsehood.

He stressed that broadcasting institutions had a responsibility to deliver accurate information and promote greater understanding among the public.

Referring to Sri Lanka's diverse society, Herath highlighted the importance of the media in encouraging dialogue, mutual respect and understanding among different communities.

He also emphasised that technological innovation should serve humanity and ensure wider access to knowledge.

"Peace cannot be sustained by diplomacy alone," the Minister said, stressing that understanding among people was equally important and that responsible broadcasting could play a significant role in bringing societies closer together.

President Anura Kumara Dissanayake and the Mayor of the Colombo Municipal Council also attended the event.

Held under the theme "Media for Peace and Humanity: Truth, Trust, and Transformation", the five-day ABU General Assembly and associated meetings are taking place from October 30 to November 3, 2026.

Hosted by MTV Sri Lanka, the event brings together broadcasters, media professionals and industry leaders from across the Asia-Pacific region and beyond.

Established in 1964, the Asia-Pacific Broadcasting Union is a regional organisation representing broadcasting and media organisations across 66 countries, providing a platform for cooperation, knowledge-sharing and professional development.
