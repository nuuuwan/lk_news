CSE extends rally as ASPI climbs above 21,500

The Colombo Stock Exchange extended its recovery for a fifth consecutive session on 9 November 2026, with both benchmark indices advancing strongly as buying momentum remained supportive across the market.

The ASPI gained 291.72 points, or 1.37%, to close at 21,531.37, while the S&P SL20 advanced 97.77 points, or 1.62%, to 6,143.15. The ASPI moved towards 21,600 during afternoon trading before mild profit-taking trimmed some of the gains towards the close.

Market turnover reached LKR 3.33 billion, with 144.43 million shares traded. The Banks sector led activity with LKR 667.76 million in turnover, while Dipped Products recorded the highest individual counter turnover at approximately LKR 322.56 million.

Crossings accounted for around 8% of total equity turnover, with notable blocks recorded in Hemas Holdings, Prime Lands Residencies, Hatton National Bank, Ceylon Tobacco and Commercial Bank.

Market breadth remained strongly positive, with 178 gainers against 63 decliners, producing an advance-decline ratio of 2.83. Foreign investors, however, remained net sellers with an outflow of approximately LKR 63.13 million.

Commercial Bank was the strongest positive contributor to the ASPI, followed by Hatton National Bank, Dialog Axiata, Asiri Hospital Holdings and Distilleries. Carson Cumberbatch recorded the largest negative contribution.

The fifth consecutive gain, strong market breadth and turnover approaching the YTD daily average indicate that the recovery remains well supported. While some profit-taking emerged near the intraday high, the ASPI’s move above 21,500 strengthens short-term momentum, making this level an important near-term support area.
