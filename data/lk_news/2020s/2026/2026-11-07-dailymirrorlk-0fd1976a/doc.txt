Deduru Oya residents alerted as 4,100 cusecs released

Deduru Oya reservoir releases 4,100 cusecs of water; downstream residents urged to remain vigilant

Six sluice gates of the Deduru Oya Reservoir are currently being opened by one foot each, releasing approximately 4,100 cubic feet of water per second into the Deduru Oya, the Irrigation Department said.

The measure has been taken to control the volume of water entering the reservoir.

The Director of Irrigation (Water Management) urged people living downstream of the reservoir, particularly residents along the Deduru Oya, to remain vigilant and closely monitor changes in river water levels.

Residents have also been advised to follow announcements and safety instructions issued by the Irrigation Department and Disaster Management authorities.

The department warned that water inflows into the reservoir and rainfall could change, potentially affecting the volume of water released downstream.
