CMA Sri Lanka to conduct two-day workshop on digital banking challenges and AI impact

The Institute of Certified Management Accountants of Sri Lanka (CMA Sri Lanka) will conduct a specialised two-day professional workshop on “Forensic Investigation, AI & Digital Fraud Risk Management for Banks and Financial Institutions”, addressing the growing challenges of digital banking fraud and the emerging role of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in detecting, investigating and preventing financial misconduct on the 26th and 27th November 2026 from 9.00 am-5.00 pm at the Taj Samudra, Colombo.

The programme is designed primarily for banking and financial institutions professionals and will also benefit professionals in public-sector finance & investigation, internal and external audit, risk management, compliance, fraud control and investigation. It aims to strengthen participants’ understanding of emerging digital fraud threats while equipping them with practical skills and techniques to identify suspicious transactions, analyse financial data and conduct structured forensic investigations.

The programme will be conducted by CA Dr. Durgesh Pandey, Managing Partner of DKMS & Associates, India, and Honorary Professor at the University of Portsmouth, UK.

Dr. Pandey has conducted over 750 professional training sessions, reaching chartered accountants, senior bankers, regulators, police and law enforcement officers across more than 35 countries. His international training experience includes Sri Lanka, UAE, Bahrain, Saudi Arabia, Kenya, the UK and several other countries across the Middle East and Asia.

He is a National-Level Resource Person and regular faculty member for the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) Forensic Accounting and Fraud Detection (FAFD) course, having conducted hundreds of professional sessions for ICAI across India. He has also contributed to the development of FAIS, ISAS, DPDP-related guidance and other ICAI technical publications.

The two-day programme will cover eight key sessions, beginning with the fundamentals of forensic investigation and the anatomy of digital banking fraud, followed by forensic analytics, investigation methodologies, digital evidence reconstruction and the practical application of AI in forensic investigations. The final sessions will focus on continuous fraud monitoring, strengthening internal controls, fraud risk governance and presenting investigation findings to management and Audit Committees.

A major highlight of the workshop is its 70 percent practical and 30 percent theoretical learning approach, designed to provide participants with hands-on experience in investigating realistic banking fraud scenarios.

Participants will work on four specially developed banking fraud case studies using simulated transaction datasets and supporting documentation.
