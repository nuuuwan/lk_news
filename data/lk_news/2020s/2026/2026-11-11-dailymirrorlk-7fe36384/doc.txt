Traffic disrupted on Badulla–Colombo road due to earth slip at Uduwara

Colombo, Nov. 11 (Daily Mirror) - Vehicular movement between Badulla and Bandarawela along the Badulla–Colombo main road has come to a complete standstill following a major earth slip at the Uduwara 6th Mile Post area.

The Road Development Authority (RDA) advises motorists traveling from Badulla toward Colombo, Ella, Wellawaya, and Bandarawela to use the Badulla–Hali-Ela–Attampitiya–Bandarawela road as an alternative route until clearing operations are completed.
