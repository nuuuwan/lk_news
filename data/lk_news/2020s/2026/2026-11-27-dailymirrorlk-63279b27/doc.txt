Non availability of sanitary pads in local prisons could turn into a major health hazard soon: MP

Colombo, Nov. 27 (Daily Mirror) - Non availability of sanitary pads for female prisoners in Sri Lanka could be a major health hazard soon if it is not addressed, an MP told Parliament yesterday.

MP Faizer Mustapha who raised the issue during the committee stage debate on the budget in the House proposed setting up vending machines for female inmates.

“An NGO is engaged in providing sanitary pads to inmates but it is not sufficient,” he said.

“We have addressed this issue before long as it could become a major health hazard if it is neglected,” he added.

The MP said the theme or the motto ‘All prisoners are humans’ is given lip service as nothing has been done to rehabilitate prison inmates.

However, he said the government’s decision to introduce house arrest as a solution to the congestion in prisons is commendable

Minister of Justice Harshana Nanayakkara who responded said the prison authorities have held a number of exhibitions to promote various items that are produced by inmates. “ I would like to inform the House that certain inmates are engaged in producing microchips. They are producing for a well-known brand,” the Minister said.
