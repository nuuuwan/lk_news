Chinese Embassy upbeat about passenger drone flights in SL

Says: Flying directly to the airport might be closer than you think!

Touts development as step towards making green and smart low-altitude mobility reality

The Chinese Embassy in Colombo has highlighted the successful demonstration flight of China’s self-developed autonomous passenger-carrying aircraft at Colombo Port City, presenting the technology as a potential new avenue for Sri Lanka’s tourism, connectivity and urban transport.

In a post on X, the Embassy said the EH216-S autonomous aerial vehicle had completed a successful demonstration flight at Port City, describing the development as a step towards making green and smart low-altitude mobility a reality.

“From vision to reality, green and smart low-altitude mobility is unlocking exciting new possibilities for Sri Lanka’s tourism, connectivity, and modern urban transport. Flying directly to the airport might be closer than you think!” it said.

The EH216-S is developed by Guangzhou-based EHang Holdings, a Chinese autonomous aviation technology company specialising in electric vertical take-off and landing (eVTOL) aircraft. The two-passenger aircraft is designed to operate autonomously using battery-powered propulsion, without an onboard pilot.

The demonstration comes as Sri Lanka explores the introduction of electric air transport, with passenger drone flights being tested at Port City ahead of an official demonstration scheduled for 11 November. The initiative is also expected to showcase potential applications beyond passenger transport, including tourism and logistics. The Government is yet to announce regulations.
