Modern Pack Lanka clinches gold at CNCI Achiever Awards 2026

The Gold Award received by Chief Executive Officer Lahiru Wijerathna, along with members of the Modern Pack Lanka team

Modern Pack Lanka (Pvt) Ltd has been honoured with the National Level Gold Award in the Extra Large category at the CNCI Achiever Awards 2026, organised by the Ceylon National Chamber of Industries (CNCI), Sri Lanka’s oldest industrial chamber.

This recognition marks another significant milestone in the company’s journey of excellence, innovation and continuous improvement in Sri Lanka’s manufacturing sector.

Established in 1993 by Sheamalee Wickramasinghe and Nishka Wickramasinghe as a joint venture with Modern Pack Singapore, the company began operations by manufacturing biscuit trays for the CBL Group. Today, Modern Pack Lanka has evolved into a leading packaging manufacturer, serving prominent local and multinational brands across diverse industries.

Recognised as a leader in Sri Lanka’s thermoforming industry, the company has expanded its capabilities into injection moulding, blow moulding, plastic sheet extrusion, aluminium foil printing and precision mould manufacturing through its dedicated MPL Engineering Division.
