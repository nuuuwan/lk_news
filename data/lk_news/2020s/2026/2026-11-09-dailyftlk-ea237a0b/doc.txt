FTZ Toastmasters Club presents Moulding the Mindset to Mastery on 12 November

Facilitator Toastmasters World Champion of Public Speaking 2026 Dirceu da Silva

In today’s rapidly evolving business environment, professional success requires more than technical knowledge and managerial experience. Organisations need leaders who can think strategically, communicate with confidence, inspire teams and navigate change. Developing this mindset is becoming an essential investment for businesses seeking sustainable growth.

Recognising this need, FTZ Toastmasters Club presents “Moulding the Mindset to Mastery”, an exclusive leadership development event featuring Toastmasters World Champion of Public Speaking 2026 Dirceu da Silva.

Scheduled for Thursday, 12 November 2026, at the Colombo International Airport Hotel, Katunayake, the program is designed specifically for mid-level managers and C-suite executives working in Free Trade Zone companies.

The event will feature two dedicated sessions. The Leadership Masterclass, from 3 p.m. to 5 p.m., will focus on developing leadership capabilities among mid-level managers. The evening program, from 6 p.m., to 10 p.m.will bring together senior corporate leaders for the CXO Forum, creating an opportunity to explore leadership perspectives at the highest levels of organisational responsibility.

Having a world champion of public speaking lead this experience offers participants an opportunity to gain insights into the power of communication, influence and personal mastery. In an increasingly competitive corporate landscape, the ability to communicate a vision, engage people and lead with clarity can make a significant difference to organisational performance.

Beyond the learning sessions, the event will also provide an opportunity for professional networking. High tea will be served, offering participants a relaxed setting to connect, exchange ideas and build relationships with fellow business professionals.

The initiative reflects the growing importance of investing in people as a foundation for business excellence. By bringing together international expertise and Sri Lanka’s corporate community, FTZ Toastmasters Club hopes to encourage a culture in which leadership development is viewed not as an occasional activity, but as a continuous organisational priority.

Tickets can be booked through PickMe by scanning the QR code on the event flyer. Corporate group bookings and further inquiries can be directed to 077 357 6077.

Bookings close on Tuesday 10 November 2026. As seating is limited, early reservations are encouraged.

Ultimately, Moulding the Mindset to Mastery is an invitation to look beyond job titles and positions—to recognise that the future of every successful organisation depends on the quality of its leaders and the mindset they bring to the table.
