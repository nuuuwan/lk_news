Chrissworld appoints Shenouk Joseph Graetian Perera to Board

Chrissworld PLC has appointed Shenouk Joseph Graetian Perera to its Board as a Non-Executive Non-Independent Director.

A business and investment professional with experience t, investment oversight, corporate restructuring and business development, his experience includes industrial park operations, tenant and lease management, facilities management, capital-market investments, cash-flow and liquidity oversight, and the development and management of property and plantation-related businesses.
