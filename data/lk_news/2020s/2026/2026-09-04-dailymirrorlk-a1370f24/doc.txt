Bribery Commission arrests Namal after five-hour questioning

Colombo, Sep. 4 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) National Organiser and Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa was arrested by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) following more than five hours of questioning today over the Airbus deal.

Rajapaksa arrived at the CIABOC this morning after being instructed to appear before the Commission at 9 a.m. to provide a statement in connection with the ongoing investigation.

He was questioned by CIABOC officials for more than five hours before being arrested.
