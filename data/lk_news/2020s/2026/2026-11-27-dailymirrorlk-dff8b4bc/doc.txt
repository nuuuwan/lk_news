Sri Lanka allocates Rs. 950 million for landmine clearance in 2027

Colombo, Nov. 27 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka has allocated Rs. 950 million from the national budget for its landmine clearance programme in 2027, as the Government seeks continued international support to achieve a mine-free country.

Foreign Minister Vijitha Herath highlighted the importance of sustained international partnerships during a meeting with members of the Diplomatic Corps in Colombo on November 25 to discuss Sri Lanka’s Mine Action Programme.

Deputy Minister of Housing, Construction and Water Supply T.B. Sarath also participated in the meeting.

Ambassadors, High Commissioners and senior diplomatic officials representing 19 countries, along with representatives of the European Union, UNDP and the UN Resident Coordinator’s Office, attended the discussion. Representatives of six countries accredited to Sri Lanka from New Delhi joined online.

The meeting reviewed progress made in mine clearance, future plans and operational challenges facing the programme.

Diplomatic representatives commended Sri Lanka’s progress and expressed their continued support, while discussions also focused on exploring new funding mechanisms to sustain demining operations.

The meeting also recognised the contribution of women, who account for approximately 40% of the demining workforce.

Minister Herath thanked countries and organisations that have supported the programme and invited potential donors to join efforts towards achieving a mine-free Sri Lanka.

The Army Commander, Secretary to the Ministry of Housing, Construction and Water Supply, senior officials from the Foreign Affairs and Finance ministries, the National Mine Action Centre and the Sri Lanka Police also attended.

Representatives of the four mine clearance organisations operating in Sri Lanka — MAG, The HALO Trust, SHARP and DASH — participated in the meeting.
