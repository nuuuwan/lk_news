Trade unions step up protests over 2027 Budget

Several trade unions representing public sector employees have launched or announced trade union action and protests over the failure to address their professional demands and provide promised allowances and relief in the 2027 Budget proposals.

The Irrigation Technical Assistants Association launched a trade union action today (16), with more than 450 technical assistants attached to the Irrigation Department taking part.

Association media spokesperson Madura Prasanna said the authorities had been repeatedly informed of the difficulties faced by technical assistants in carrying out their duties and their professional concerns. However, the 2027 Budget proposals had failed to provide adequate solutions.

Meanwhile, the All Ceylon Disaster Relief Services Association has decided to withdraw its members from all disaster relief duties from today, citing the failure to address their demands in the Budget.

Association Chairman Palitha Kumara said the decision was taken because even the basic assurances given during recent discussions with the Secretary to the Ministry of Defence regarding their professional demands had not been fulfilled.

The Public Service Engineers Association is also scheduled to stage a protest today over the failure to allocate field participation and transport allowances in this year's Budget.

The engineers had earlier launched a trade union action on the 13th by reporting sick leave in protest against the issue.

Similarly, the Government Veterinary Officers' Association has decided to report sick leave tomorrow (17) in protest against the Budget proposals.

Meanwhile, several trade unions held a joint discussion yesterday under the theme 'How is the Budget? What are the future measures?' to discuss the Budget proposals and decide on their future course of action.
