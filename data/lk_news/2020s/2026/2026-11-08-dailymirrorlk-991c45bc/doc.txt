Deepavali’s message of light over darkness vital due to economic hardships: Sajith

Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa has said the philosophical message of Deepavali, symbolising the victory of good over evil and light over darkness, is particularly relevant to Sri Lanka amid its current socio-economic challenges.

Issuing a message to mark Deepavali, he said the festival carried a deeper meaning of standing against oppression and injustice, adding that its philosophy should guide efforts to protect people's rights, uphold the rule of law and ensure justice.

He alleged that several legislative measures, either passed or proposed, had undermined people's sovereignty, increased their economic burden and threatened their right to live freely.

“What we are experiencing is a darkness engulfing society. It is the growth of hardship rather than well-being in the lives of the people,” he said.

Premadasa said the philosophy of Deepavali should inspire people to unite against wrongdoing and work towards bringing society from darkness to light and from evil to good.

He also called on the public to draw strength and courage from the festival to build a prosperous nation based on religious, ethnic and linguistic diversity.

Extending his Deepavali greetings, he wished everyone the wisdom and determination to contribute towards building a better country.
