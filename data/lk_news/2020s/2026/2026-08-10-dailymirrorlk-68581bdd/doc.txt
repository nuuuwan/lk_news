Sri Lanka’s landmine clearance programme a great success: Rosamund Pike

Colombo, Aug. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Award-winning Hollywood actress and International Ambassador for Landmine Clearance Rosamund Pike has described Sri Lanka’s landmine clearance programme as a “great success.”

Pike made the remarks during a meeting with Minister of Housing, Construction and Water Supply Dr. Susil Ranasinghe at the Ministry in Battaramulla today (10).

During the meeting, Pike inquired about the progress of landmine clearance in Sri Lanka and commended the work being carried out in accordance with international standards.

According to the Ministry, around 90% of Sri Lanka’s landmine-contaminated areas have now been cleared.

The remaining 20.8 square kilometres are considered highly complex and difficult areas, with Sri Lanka expected to become completely landmine-free within the next four years.

As part of the effort, 500 specially trained Sri Lanka Army personnel are to be deployed for demining operations, with the Army aiming to clear 1.5 square kilometres annually, in addition to work carried out by other sectors.

The Government has also doubled the funds allocated for the programme compared to the previous year.

The Ministry said the demining programme goes beyond removing landmines, describing it as a national effort supporting development in the North and East, promoting tourism and strengthening trust and reconciliation among Sinhalese, Tamils and Muslims.
