Accor marks a new milestone in Sri Lanka

Celebrates brand’s debut with arrival of Pullman Colombo Downtown and Pullman Bentota Resort & Spa

Pullman, the Accor brand built around exchange, marked a major milestone in Sri Lanka, with an exclusive gathering at Vistas Rooftop Bar, Pullman Colombo Downtown, recently.

The evening celebrated the brand’s debut in the country with the introduction of two landmark properties – Pullman Colombo Downtown and Pullman Bentota Resort & Spa.

The occasion brought together Accor South Asia CEO Ranju Alex, property owners, leadership teams from both hotels, distinguished dignitaries, including Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Minister Vijitha Herath and prominent members of Sri Lanka’s media fraternity to celebrate this exciting new chapter for Pullman in the country.

The introduction of Pullman strengthens Accor’s presence across two of Sri Lanka’s most compelling business and leisure destinations. Bringing together the energy of Colombo’s commercial landscape and the natural beauty of Bentota’s coastline, the two hotels extend Pullman’s contemporary hospitality to both corporate and leisure travellers.

More than a celebration, the evening offered a platform for meaningful exchange with Sri Lanka’s corporate leadership and key industry stakeholders, reflecting Accor’s commitment to building lasting relationships as it continues to grow in the market.

The guests also experienced first-hand Pullman’s approach to contemporary hospitality, where business, leisure and social moments come together.

Alex said, “Sri Lanka is an important market in Accor’s South Asia growth strategy and the introduction of Pullman represents a significant step in strengthening our presence in the country. With two properties in Colombo and Bentota, we are bringing the brand’s contemporary hospitality experience to both business and leisure destinations. Our focus is not only on expanding our portfolio but also on building lasting partnerships and contributing to the continued growth of Sri Lanka’s tourism industry.”

Softlogic Group and Softlogic Holdings Chairman and Managing Director Ashok Pathirage said, “Celebrating the arrival of Pullman in Sri Lanka alongside our partners at Accor is a proud moment for Softlogic Holdings. Pullman Colombo Downtown and Pullman Bentota Resort & Spa reflect our long-term confidence in Sri Lanka’s tourism potential and we believe they will set new benchmarks for premium hospitality in the country.”

The arrival of Pullman through its Colombo and Bentota properties marks an important chapter in Accor’s continued growth in Sri Lanka. Together, the two hotels strengthen the group’s presence across complementary business and leisure destinations, reinforcing its commitment to developing meaningful industry partnerships and supporting Sri Lanka’s continued growth as an international travel destination.

Pullman Hotels & Resorts is a premium global hospitality brand designed for the modern, connected traveller. The brand brings together contemporary design, intuitive service, distinctive culinary experiences, wellness and vibrant social spaces to create hotels where the guests can work, connect, recharge and explore.
