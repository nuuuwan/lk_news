CSE extends decline as cautious investors await earnings season

The Colombo Stock Exchange continued its downward movement on Tuesday, with both benchmark indices closing lower as investors remained cautious ahead of the upcoming quarterly earnings season. The market opened on a negative note and gradually moved lower throughout the session, with subdued turnover and share volumes reflecting limited investor participation and a lack of strong market conviction.

Market activity remained relatively muted despite a marginal improvement compared to the previous session, as both retail and institutional investors continued to adopt a wait-and-see approach. Investors appeared focused on upcoming corporate earnings announcements to assess financial performance and identify potential investment opportunities, resulting in selective participation rather than broad-based buying activity.

The ASPI declined by 118.35 points, or 0.54%, to close at 21,962.30, while the S&P SL20 Index fell by 31.55 points, or 0.51%, to settle at 6,135.50. Total market turnover stood at LKR 1.53 Bn, with 57.73 Mn shares traded, indicating relatively subdued trading conditions.

The Capital Goods sector emerged as the largest contributor to market turnover, generating LKR 0.48 Bn with 19.13 Mn shares traded. JKH.N led activity within the sector, recording LKR 0.30 Bn in turnover with 15.33 Mn shares traded. Crossing transactions accounted for LKR 0.55 Bn, representing 35% of total market turnover, with the largest crossing recorded in JKH.N amounting to LKR 0.26 Bn involving 13.20 Mn shares.

Market breadth remained weak, with 154 decliners outweighing 48 gainers, reflecting broad-based selling pressure across the market. PKME.N emerged as the largest positive contributor to the ASPI, adding 2.41 points, followed by RCL.N, HAYC.N, SLTL.N, and SDB.N. On the downside, DIAL.N exerted the largest drag on the index, reducing it by 12.62 points, while MELS.N, JKH.N, CARS.N, and NDB.N also weighed on overall market performance.

Among notable trades, SMOT.N recorded significant activity with a turnover of LKR 0.12 Bn on 0.10 Mn shares traded. However, the counter declined by 4.25% during the session, reflecting the cautious sentiment and selective profit-taking observed across the market. Overall, investors remained positioned cautiously as market participants awaited stronger earnings-driven signals to determine the next direction of the equity market.
