MTI strategises industrial development for Zimbabwe’s Sovereign Fund

Zimbabwe Mutapa Investment Fund CEO Dr. John Mangudya (left) and MTI Consulting CEO Hilmy Cader

MTI Consulting was engaged to conduct a performance review of the Industrial Development Corporation of Zimbabwe and to develop turnaround strategies, which have now been successfully concluded.

Mutapa Investment Fund CEO Dr. John Mangudya said, “The assignment benefitted from the active participation and strategic guidance of the leadership of the Mutapa Investment Fund and SERA (State Enterprises Restructuring Agency). We are pleased with the outcome and approach of MTI Consulting.”

SERA Executive Director Onesimo Musi said, “In developing these strategies, MTI’s robust approach included stakeholder feedback from Zimbabwe’s industrial and non‑bank financial institution (NBFI) sectors, along with best‑fit practices drawn from the region’s top‑performing Industrial Development Corporations.”

The Mutapa Investment Fund is Zimbabwe’s sovereign wealth fund, managing a portfolio of 66 companies across natural resources, energy, infrastructure, industrials, financial services, and real estate. SERA (State Enterprises Restructuring Agency) is the Government body responsible for the reform of state enterprises and parastatals.
