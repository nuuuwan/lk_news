Taj Samudra Colombo commemorated India’s 77th Republic Day on its premise on 26 January 2026. The Indian Flag was hoisted by Taj Sri Lanka and Maldives Area Director Samrat Datta, in a ceremony attended by team members and resident guests. The celebration concluded with warm greetings and the enjoyment of delectable refreshments, reflecting the true spirit of unity and togetherness.
