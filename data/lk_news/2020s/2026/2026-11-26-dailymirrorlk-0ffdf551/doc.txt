Dialog launches ‘Sayuru AI’ Fisheries Advisory Voice Assistant

From left: Nushan Rajapaksha, Associate Architect, AI Solutions, Dialog Axiata PLC; Tharusha Wanigasekara, Manager, Digital Innovation and Inclusion, Group Risk, Compliance & Sustainability, Dialog Axiata PLC; Asanga Priyadarshana, Group Chief Corporate Officer, Dialog Axiata PLC; Stepni Fernando, Member of Parliament, Gampaha District; Malith Fernando, Meteorologist, Department of Meteorology of Sri Lanka; Harshanath Jayasinghe, Manager, Negombo Fishery Harbour

Dialog Axiata PLC has launched ‘Sayuru AI’, Sri Lanka’s first AI-powered fisheries advisory voice service, enabling fishing communities and other stakeholders to access essential fisheries information simply by dialling 828 from any mobile phone.

Available in Sinhala, Tamil and English, the service reflects the company’s commitment to enriching lives through technology by harnessing artificial intelligence to support safer fishing practices, informed decision-making, and greater digital inclusion across Sri Lanka’s fisheries sector.

Developed in partnership with the Department of Fisheries, Aquatic and Ocean Resources and the Department of Meteorology, Aquatic and Ocean Resources, Sayuru AI was launched on 21 November to mark World Fisheries Day.

The newly introduced AI-powered voice assistant enhances Dialog’s existing Sayuru Interactive Voice Response (IVR) service, enabling users to access fisheries-related information through natural voice interactions rather than navigating conventional menu-based systems. Available around the clock, the service provides information on marine weather forecasts, safety instructions at sea, fish market prices, and guidance on ornamental fisheries.

Accessible through a simple voice call without requiring a smartphone or an internet connection, Sayuru AI helps address critical information gaps within the fisheries sector. The new AI-powered assistant is being introduced through Dialog’s existing Sayuru IVR service, while the Sayuru mobile application continues to offer its existing features, enabling broader access to essential information through a familiar and inclusive channel.

A Fisheries Ministry spokesperson said, “The fisheries sector plays a vital role in Sri Lanka’s economy and in the livelihoods of communities across the country. Ensuring that fishing communities have access to timely and relevant information is important in supporting livelihoods and the sustainable development of the sector. Through initiatives such as Sayuru AI, collaboration between the government and the private sector can help extend the reach of essential services, strengthen support for fishing communities, and ensure that the benefits of technological advancement contribute to national development.

This initiative also marks an important step towards a new era of transformation in Sri Lanka’s fisheries sector, moving beyond traditional practices towards a modern, technology enabled and knowledge driven industry. This transformation is expected to strengthen the resilience and the productivity of fisheries sector.” A.L.K. Wijemannage, Director General of the Department of Meteorology said, “Timely and reliable weather information is critical to the safety and livelihoods of Sri Lanka’s fishing communities.”

Commenting on the launch, Asanga Priyadarshana, Group Chief Corporate Officer of Dialog Axiata PLC said, “By combining trusted sector knowledge with the accessibility of voice and the power of AI, we are helping make critical information more readily available to the communities that depend on it every day. As Sri Lanka advances its digital transformation agenda, we remain committed to applying innovation in ways that strengthen industries, uplift communities, and contribute to national progress.”
