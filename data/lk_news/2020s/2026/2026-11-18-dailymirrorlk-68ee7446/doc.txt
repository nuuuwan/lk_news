JXG reports Rs. 13bn revenue for 1H FY27 despite market volatility

Janashakthi PLC (JXG) reported consolidated revenue of Rs. 13.0 billion for the six months ended 30 September 2026 (1H FY27), compared to Rs. 14.5 billion in the corresponding period overthe previous financial year.

The first half of FY27 unfolded against a markedly adverse operating environment from that of theprevious financial year, withthe continuedgeopolitical tensions and outbreak of hostilities in the Middle East having triggered heightened volatility across global and domestic financial markets. Because of the global volatility through the first half of the financial year and the upward movement in interest rates, JXG reported a loss after tax of Rs. 1.5 billion for the period, compared to a profit of Rs. 3.1 billion in the corresponding period last year. The impact was most acutely felt during the first quarter, with the second quarter marking an improvement in revenue growth and Quarter-on-Quarterlosses narrowed. The Group’s insurance and finance businesses generated profits during the quarter, though these were offset by the mark-to-market losses at First Capital Holdings.

Commenting on the performance, Ramesh Schaffter, Managing Director/Group CEO of Janashakthi PLC (JXG) said, “We undertook our IPO and commenced our journey as a listed diversified financial services group during a period that subsequently evolved into one of heightened global uncertainty. While current market conditions have not been conducive to reporting the level of earnings we have delivered historically, we remain confident in the strength of our underlying businesses, and continue with implementing our long-term strategy.”

Commenting on the performance, Chandan de Silva, Group Chairman of Janashakthi PLC (JXG), said, “The performance in the first half of FY27 reflects adverse global conditions that have persisted during the first six months of this financial year. While these conditions have weighed on our reported earnings, our focus remains on disciplined capital allocation, prudent risk management, strong governance and the continued execution of our long-term strategy.”

Looking ahead, JXG remains focused on strengthening its core businesses while pursuing targeted opportunities that create sustainable long-term value.
