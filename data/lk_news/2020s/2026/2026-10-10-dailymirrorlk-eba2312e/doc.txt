Selling pressure continues in secondary market

The selling pressure continued in the secondary market yesterday as well, with thin volumes and limited activities.

Among the trades that executed, 15.10.2028 traded at 10.65 percent, while 15.10.2030 traded at 11.40 percent. Moving ahead, 01.06.2033 and 01.11.2033 changed hands at 11.95 percent. Finally, 15.10.2034 traded at 12.12 percent.

Moreover, the PDMO announced a T-bond auction to be held on October 13, 2026. Total of Rs.100.0 billion to be raised, whereas Rs.20.0 billion, Rs.30.0 billion and Rs.50.0 billion to be raised under the 15.09.2029, 01.12.2031 and 15.08.2036 maturities, while the coupon rates stand at 11.00 percent, 12.00 percent and 10.85 percent, respectively.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee marginally appreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.330.96/US dollar, compared to Rs.330.98/US dollar seen earlier.

The Sri Lankan rupee depreciated against other major currencies such as GBP, EUR, CNY and AUD, while it appreciated against JPY.  Market liquidity in the banking system expanded to Rs.399.14 billion, compared to Rs.396.71 billion recorded previously.
