Air quality deteriorates in several areas due to external wind currents

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - The Central Environmental Authority (CEA) has reported an increase in airborne particulate matter in several parts of the country due to wind currents coming from outside Sri Lanka.

According to air quality monitoring data, increased levels of suspended particulate matter, including PM10 and PM2.5, have been detected in Jaffna, Kilinochchi and Mannar districts, as well as in areas around Anuradhapura, Puttalam, Trincomalee, Colombo and Kalmunai.

The CEA said the situation was caused by wind currents originating outside the country and could affect the health of sensitive individuals, according to Sri Lanka's Air Quality Index.

The authority warned that the situation could continue over the coming days and advised sensitive individuals to wear N95 face masks as a precaution.

It also urged the public to follow health advice issued by the Ministry of Health, which is available on the National Air Quality Website (aq.cea.lk).

The CEA said it was continuously monitoring atmospheric conditions and would keep the public informed of any developments.

The authority monitors air quality under the provisions of the National Environmental Act and publishes real-time information through its online air quality platform.
