Asia Asset Finance’s Rs. 1.5bn rights issue oversubscribed by Rs. 433mn

Asia Asset Finance PLC’s Rs. 1.5 billion rights issue has closed fully subscribed, with applications exceeding the offer by Rs. 433.5 million, according to a filing with the Colombo Stock Exchange.

The Muthoot Group-backed finance company issued 45,162,012 ordinary voting shares at Rs. 33.30 each on a four-for-eleven basis, raising Rs. 1,503,894,999 in fresh capital. Applications were received for 58,179,825 shares against the offer, the filing showed, with demand for additional shares running well beyond the residue left after provisional entitlements were taken up.

The pricing left investors with a healthy cushion: the rights were struck at a sizeable discount to both the Rs. 52.40 the stock closed the financial year at in March and the Rs. 59.10 it fetched by end-June, a run that has seen the share more than double from a 2025 low of Rs. 25.00. The stock extended that momentum on Thursday, closing at Rs. 49.80, up 4.18 percent on the day.

The board approved the issue in June to strengthen Tier 1 capital in line with Central Bank requirements and to fund the company’s

expanding loan book, and the strong take-up affirms investor confidence in that growth trajectory. Profit for FY2025/26 rose 135.4 percent to Rs. 1.04 billion as total assets grew 44.9 percent to Rs. 53.78 billion, momentum that carried into the June quarter with after-tax profit up 137 percent year-on-year.

Muthoot Finance Limited, which held 72.92 percent of the company’s shares ahead of the issue, is the anchor shareholder of the A+(lka)-rated lender, which operated 120 branches as at end-June.
