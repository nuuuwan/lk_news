Metro weekend pass gives unlimited rides

Colombo, Aug. 6 (Daily Mirror) - Lanka Metro Transit (LMT) has introduced a new Metro Bus Weekend Pass, allowing passengers to enjoy unlimited travel on Metro Bus services throughout the weekend for a one-time fee of Rs. 799.

Announcing the initiative, Lanka Metro Transit said the pass is designed to offer commuters greater convenience and flexibility for weekend travel, whether meeting friends, travelling to university, exploring the city or enjoying family outings.

The Weekend Pass is valid from 12:00 a.m. on Saturday until 11:59 p.m. on Sunday, enabling unlimited travel on Metro Bus services during the two-day period.

LMT also said that children under the age of 12 can travel free under the offer.

The Weekend Pass is available exclusively through the LMT GO App, the company said.
