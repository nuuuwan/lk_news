72-hour detention order issued against drug trafficker Shiran Basik

Colombo, August 15 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Police have obtained a 72-hour detention order against 48-year-old Shiran Basik, identified by police as a major drug trafficker.

Basik, a resident of Dehiwala, was arrested in Dubai and brought back to Sri Lanka yesterday (14), according to Police.

Police said the suspect is facing multiple allegations involving drug trafficking, firearms, dangerous weapons and unlawful assembly.

The drug trafficking allegations are being investigated by the Police Narcotics Bureau as well as the Dehiwala and Mirihana Police Stations.

He is also wanted in connection with firearms-related offences investigated by the Kalutara and Dehiwala Police Stations, while the Piliyandala Police are investigating allegations relating to the possession of dangerous weapons.

In addition, the Colombo Crimes Division is investigating an allegation of unlawful assembly causing harm, Police said.
