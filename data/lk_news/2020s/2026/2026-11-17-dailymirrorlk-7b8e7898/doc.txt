Grade 6 school cut-off marks for 2027 released

Colombo, Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - The cut-off marks for the admission of students to Grade 6 in 2027, based on the 2026 Grade 5 Scholarship Examination results, have been released.

The Ministry of Education said the cut-off marks could be accessed through its official website, www.moe.gov.lk.

Parents can also check the school allocated to their children by visiting https://g6application.moe.gov.lk.
