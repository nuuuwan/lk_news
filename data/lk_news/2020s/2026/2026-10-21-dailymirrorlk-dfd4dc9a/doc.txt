Mireka Seascape begins main building construction

Representatives of Mireka Seascape and Sanken Constructions participate in the groundbreaking ceremony

Mireka Seascape, a luxury coastal residential development by Overseas Realty (Ceylon) PLC, marked an important milestone by celebrating the commencement of main building construction at a groundbreaking ceremony held on 15 October 2026 in Dodanduwa.

This follows the completion of piling works and obtaining all government regulatory approvals, including approvals from the Coast Conservation and Coastal Resource Management Department and the Urban Development Authority (UDA).

As part of this milestone, Sanken Construction was appointed as the main contractor for the development. The appointment reflects Mireka Seascape’s commitment to maintaining high construction standards and delivering quality residential developments.

“With Sanken Construction’s extensive experience, expertise, and resources, I am confident that the project will be completed by November 2028, in line with the anticipated timeline and the highest quality standards,” stated Pravir Samarasinghe, CEO/Director of Overseas Realty (Ceylon) PLC.

Launched in June 2025, Mireka Seascape comprises 168 beautifully designed and spacious luxury apartments and villas, offering scenic views of the Indian Ocean and Rathgama Lake. The development combines contemporary residences with resort-style amenities, providing an appealing choice for those seeking a holiday home, a second residence, or a high-yieldinginvestmentproperty. Strategically located in Dodanduwa, between the cultural heritage city of Galle and tourists hot spots of Hikkaduwa, it offers a distinctive coastal resort type lifestyle where residential comfort meets the appeal of a holiday destination.

Mireka Seascape features a range of recreation facilities and amenities, including adult and children’s swimming pools, a fully equipped fitness centre, sauna and steam rooms, a yoga deck, bistro bar, sky lounge, viewing deck, multi-function room, children’s play areas, a work-from-home lounge, concierge services, and many other high-end amenities. The development also offers access to a wide unpolluted and unspoiled beach suitable and safe for swimming and direct access to the sea, adding to its appeal as a coastal residential destination.
