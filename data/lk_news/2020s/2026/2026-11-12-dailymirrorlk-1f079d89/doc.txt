SCL on imported sugar increased by Rs. 10 per kilo to protect local industry

Colombo, Nov. 12 (Daily Mirror) - The Special Commodity Levy (SCL) on imported sugar will be increased by Rs. 10 per kilogram to protect the domestic sugar industry and reduce the tax disparity between locally produced and imported sugar.

The proposal, announced by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake in the 2027 Budget, aims to create a level playing field for domestic sugar producers and importers.

The Government said the increase is intended to ensure fair competition and more balanced tax treatment between locally produced and imported sugar.
