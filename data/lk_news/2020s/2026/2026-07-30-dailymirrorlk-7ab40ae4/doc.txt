NPP cannot take disciplinary action against me : Lal Wijenayake

Colombo, July 30 (Daily Mirror) - National Peoples Power (NPP) cannot take disciplinary action against me as I have not gone against any decision made by the party, NPP executive committee member Lal Wijenayake said yesterday.

" NPP had not discussed the proposal to extend the retirement age of Judges in any of its committee's and had not made a decision on it. Therefore the party cannot take any disciplinary action against me" lawyer Wijenayake told journalists.

" This is not a battle against the government but a struggle to safeguard the independence of the Judiciary. No lawyer could practice his or her profession in a situation where there is no independence of Judiciary. A lawyer would not be able to practice if he is aware of the outcome of any case" he added.

Wijenayake earlier said that the NPP leadership had asked him to be silent about the extending the retirement age of judges.

Wijenayake was among the lawyers who were present at yesterday 's special meeting of the Bar Association.
