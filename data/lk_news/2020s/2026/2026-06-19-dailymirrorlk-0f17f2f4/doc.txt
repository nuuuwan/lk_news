SJB has no issues with Dayasiri: MP

Colombo, June 19 (Daily Mirror) - The Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) has no issues with MP Dayasiri Jayasekera as he is not a member of that party, SJB MP Chathura Gallappathi said today.

"MP Jayasekera contested the general elections of 2024 under the Samagi Jana Sandanaya. He does not hold any position in the SJB and is also not a member of its working committee. Furthermore, he has a good rapport with Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa," MP Gallappathi told a media conference.

MP Jayasekera has been working with the Joint opposition which includes the UNP, SLFP, the United Republic Front and others since last year.
