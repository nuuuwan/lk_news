AB Securitas takes Gold at National Business Excellence Awards 2026

AB Securitas, Sri Lanka’s trusted leader in security and cash management solutions, has been awarded the Gold Award in the Security and Technical Services – Large Category at the National Business Excellence Awards (NBEA) 2026, organised by the National Chamber of Commerce of Sri Lanka.

With over 30 years of industry expertise, pioneering certifications, and a strong commitment to innovation, AB Securitas delivers integrated security and cash management solutions to many of Sri Lanka’s most trusted organisations across multiple industry sectors. Combining advanced technology, operational excellence, and a highly trained workforce, the company continues to set industry benchmarks while delivering unmatched safety, reliability, and customer confidence.

AB Securitas has consistently led the industry through its unwavering commitment to quality and excellence. It was the first Sri Lankan security company to obtain ISO certification and the first in the industry to receive the National Quality Award for Business Excellence in 2015. The company is also the only Sri Lankan security provider certified with ISO 45001:2018 for Occupational Health and Safety Management, reaffirming its commitment to employee wellbeing, operational excellence, and stakeholder confidence.

Further strengthening its leadership within the industry, AB Securitas has been a founding member of the Sri Lanka Security Services Providers Association (SLASSPA) since 2004 and an active member of the Industrial Security Foundation (ISF) since 2009, contributing to the advancement of professional standards within Sri Lanka’s private security sector.

The company operates through several Strategic Business Units (SBUs), including Static Security, Total Cash Management, Cash-in-Transit, ATM Management, Electronic Security, and the KNINE Sniffer Dog Force. The KNINE unit supports specialised security operations through the detection of improvised explosive substances and the deployment of professionally trained detector and guarding dogs under Detector Dogs Programmes (DDP).

The National Business Excellence Awards (NBEA), the flagship awards program of the National Chamber of Commerce of Sri Lanka, recognises organisations that demonstrate world-class standards of excellence in organisational performance, innovation, sustainability, and corporate governance while making a significant contribution to Sri Lanka’s socio-economic development. Winning the Gold Award at NBEA 2026 further strengthens AB Securitas’ reputation as Sri Lanka’s most trusted security partner, reflecting the company’s enduring commitment to delivering world-class security and cash management solutions through innovation, professionalism, operational excellence, and an uncompromising focus on customer confidence. As AB Securitas continues to set new benchmarks for the industry, this recognition stands as a testament to the trust and credibility built over three decades - and an open invitation to organisations seeking a security partner they can rely on.
