DFCC Bank relocates Peradeniya branch

New, more spacious premises strengthen access to personal and business banking services across the greater Kandy region

DFCC Bank has relocated its Peradeniya branch to No. 554, Peradeniya Road, Kandy, providing the customers with a more spacious and convenient environment in which to access personal and business banking services.

The relocated branch will continue to serve individuals, professionals, entrepreneurs and businesses from Peradeniya and the wider Kandy region.

The new premises enable the branch team to assist the customers across a range of banking requirements, including everyday banking, savings, lending and business banking solutions. The customers can also receive financial guidance suited to their personal goals or business requirements while continuing to use DFCC Bank’s digital channels for greater flexibility in managing their finances.

Peradeniya serves a diverse community shaped by residential neighbourhoods, educational institutions, commercial activity and enterprise. Maintaining a strong presence in the area allows DFCC Bank to remain close to the people and businesses contributing to the region’s continuing development.

The relocation forms part of DFCC Bank’s wider effort to improve its customer touchpoints and make banking more convenient across its branch network.

DFCC Bank welcomes the existing and new customers to its relocated Peradeniya branch.
