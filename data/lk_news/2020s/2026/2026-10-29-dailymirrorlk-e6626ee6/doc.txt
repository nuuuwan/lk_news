Japan grants Rs. 4.56 billion for Kelani River flood mitigation project

Colombo, Oct. 29 (Daily Mirror) - The Government of Japan has agreed to provide a grant of 2.179 billion Japanese yen, equivalent to approximately Rs. 4.56 billion, to Sri Lanka for a project to improve the pump station in the Kelani River area and mitigate flooding.

The grant assistance is being provided through the Japan International Cooperation Agency (JICA) under Japan’s Economic and Social Development Programme.

The Exchange of Notes and the Grant Agreement for the project were signed on October 28, 2026, at the Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development in Colombo.

Dr. Harshana Suriyapperuma, Secretary to the Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development, signed on behalf of the Sri Lankan Government, while Japanese Ambassador to Sri Lanka Akio Isomata and JICA Sri Lanka Office Chief Representative Shigeo Honzu signed on behalf of the Japanese Government and JICA, respectively.

The project aims to provide a long-term solution to flooding in the Mudun Ela sub-basin, which covers the Wattala and Peliyagoda areas.

Under the project, a new pumping station with a discharge capacity of 15 cubic metres per second will be installed, along with a modern Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) monitoring and control system. The infrastructure is expected to improve stormwater drainage, particularly when water levels in the Kelani River rise.

The project is expected to reduce flood risks, protect residents and businesses, safeguard transport infrastructure and support sustainable urban development in the affected areas. It will also contribute to strengthening Peliyagoda’s role as a commercial, logistics and transportation hub.

The Sri Lanka Land Development Corporation (SLLDC) will implement the project under the supervision of the Ministry of Transport, Highways and Urban Development.
