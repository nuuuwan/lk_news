Road safety council calls for mutual respect between drivers and pedestrians

Colombo, Nov. 4 (Daily Mirror) - The National Council for Road Safety has called on motorists and pedestrians to show greater mutual respect and patience to prevent road accidents and ensure safer journeys.

The Council said road safety should not be limited to following traffic rules, regulations and signals, but should also involve responsible behaviour and consideration for other road users.

It urged drivers to give way to pedestrians at crossings, maintain adequate space for other vehicles and avoid unnecessary honking.

Pedestrians were also advised to obey traffic rules and exercise caution when crossing roads.

The Council hoghlighted that roads were shared public spaces and said respecting the lives and rights of all road users could help prevent accidents and conflicts.
