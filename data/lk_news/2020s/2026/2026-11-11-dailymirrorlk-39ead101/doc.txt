Daily Mirror wins top honours at Journalism Awards for Excellence 2025

Colombo, November 11 (Daily Mirror) - Daily Mirror secured top honours at the Journalism Awards for Excellence 2025, organised by The Editors’ Guild of Sri Lanka and the Sri Lanka Press Institute, held last night.

Daily Mirror Deputy Features Editor Kamanthi Wickramasinghe won the prestigious Mervyn de Silva Journalist of the Year Award, one of the highest honours presented at the ceremony.

In the website category, a joint Merit Award was presented to www.dailymirror.lk and www.ft.lk in recognition of their journalistic work.
