Strict ban on passenger cigarette imports

Sri Lanka Customs yesterday reiterated that a strict ban is in place on passengers bringing cigarettes into the country, warning that any violations will be met with severe penalties under the law.

Customs destroyed 232.804 million foreign-manufactured cigarette sticks seized over the past four months at the Kerawalapitiya Waste-to-Energy Power Plant.

The seized stock was valued at Rs. 305 million, and Customs said its release into the domestic market would have resulted in an estimated Rs. 271 million loss in tax revenue to the Government.

The destruction was carried out under official supervision, with senior officers from Sri Lanka Customs and the security divisions of Bandaranaike International Airport present, including the Director of Customs and Media Spokesperson.

Customs urged all arriving passengers to strictly comply with the ban, stressing that failure to do so will result in legal action.
