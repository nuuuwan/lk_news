Peradeniya ’Black Bridge’ construction expedited

Colombo, Oct. 2 (Daily Mirror) - Construction work on the Peradeniya 'Black Bridge' is being expedited, with steps being taken to complete the project as soon as possible, the Minister of Transport and Highways said today.

Speaking after arriving at Peradeniya Railway Station on a special train from Colombo Fort, the Minister said construction of the bridge had been delayed due to various reasons.

He said necessary measures had now been taken to accelerate the work and complete it at the earliest possible opportunity.

The Minister said the Government was also taking steps to develop railway infrastructure and improve the quality of railway transport services.

He added that facilities required by railway employees were also being developed as part of the programme.

The Minister's remarks came as the Rambukkana-Peradeniya section of the Upcountry Railway Line was reopened for train traffic after being closed for around 10 months following the disaster situation.

Deputy Minister of Transport and Highways Dr. Prasanna Gunasena, Railway General Manager K. Ravindra Pathmapriya and other officials were present.
