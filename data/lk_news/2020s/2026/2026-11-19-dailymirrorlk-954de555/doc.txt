Litro Gas incurs Rs. 800 million monthly loss to keep domestic prices low: Trade Minister

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - State-owned Litro Gas Lanka Limited is incurring a monthly loss of Rs. 800 million by selling domestic LP gas below cost to shield consumers from price hikes, Trade Minister Wasantha Samarasinghe informed Parliament yesterday.

Addressing the House, the Minister noted that Litro Gas refills more than two million 12.5 kg domestic gas cylinders each month. He explained that the company had recorded a cumulative profit of Rs. 5,900 million by the end of October, and the benefit of those earnings is now being passed down to the public by absorbing losses rather than raising retail prices.

Minister Samarasinghe further stated that in response to supply disruptions caused by LAUGFS Gas PLC failing to distribute stocks, Litro has significantly ramped up its supply—importing 41,000 metric tonnes of gas this month, with an additional 40,000 metric tonnes scheduled for import next month.

Commenting on recent pricing disputes, the Minister described LAUGFS Gas's steep price hike following months of supply shortages as unfair, noting that the company has since agreed to reduce its prices by Rs. 745 following intervention by the government.
