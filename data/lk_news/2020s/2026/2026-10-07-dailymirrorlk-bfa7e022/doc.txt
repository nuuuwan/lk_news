US warship USS Tulsa arrives at Colombo Port

Colombo, Oct. 7 (Daily Mirror) - The USS Tulsa (LCS 16), an Independence-variant littoral combat ship of the United States Navy, arrived at the Port of Colombo today for a scheduled port visit, marking its second visit to Sri Lanka in two years.

The visit is aimed at strengthening the maritime partnership between the United States and Sri Lanka, while promoting cooperation between the two countries in support of peace, security and prosperity in the Indian Ocean region.

U.S. Ambassador to Sri Lanka Eric Meyer said visits by American naval vessels provide opportunities to strengthen relations between the two countries and build closer ties between their sailors.

The USS Tulsa last visited Colombo in August 2025. During its current visit, the vessel will refuel and resupply before continuing its mission in the Indo-Pacific.

The visit also builds on longstanding cooperation between the U.S. and Sri Lankan navies, including ship visits, professional exchanges, training programmes and exercises such as Cooperation Afloat Readiness and Training (CARAT).

These engagements are intended to strengthen interoperability and the ability of the two navies to work together.

The USS Tulsa is designed for maritime security, sea control and operations with partner nations across the Indo-Pacific. Built by Austal USA in Mobile, Alabama, the vessel features a trimaran hull and can reach speeds of more than 40 knots.

Its large flight deck and adaptable mission spaces enable the ship to operate helicopters, small boats and unmanned systems, while supporting personnel and equipment for a range of maritime missions.
