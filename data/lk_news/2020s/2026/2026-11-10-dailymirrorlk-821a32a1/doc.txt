Indices close in green

The ASPI closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Commercial Bank, Hatton National Bank and Asiri Hospital Holdings with the turnover crossing Rs. 3.3 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Dipped Products, Seylan Bank and Sunshine Holdings. Mixed interest was observed in Commercial Bank, Sampath Bank and Asiri Surgical Hospital whilst retail interest was noted in Tokyo Cement Company nonvoting, Nations Trust Bank and ACL Cables. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers. The Banking sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Commercial Bank, Seylan Bank and Sampath Bank) whilst the sector index gained 1.82%. The share price of Commercial Bank gained Rs 6.00 (2.86%) to close at Rs 216.00. The share price of Seylan Bank recorded a gain of Rs 2.65 (2.72%) to close at Rs 100.25. The share price of Sampath Bank appreciated by Rs 1.00 (0.66%) to close at Rs 153.00. Capital Goods sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover whilst the sector index increased by 2.16%. Dipped Products and Sunshine Holdings were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Dipped Products increased by Rs 4.10 (6.45%) to close at Rs 67.70. The share price of Sunshine Holdings moved up by Rs 0.30 (0.98%) to close at Rs 30.90.
