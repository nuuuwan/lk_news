First Capital retains its ‘buy’ recommendation on Sampath Bank

Sampath Bank’s first-quarter earnings came under pressure from a sharp rise in loan-loss provisions and a precautionary buffer against global uncertainties, but First Capital says the setback is temporary and has maintained its positive outlook on the stock.

In a research note titled “Temporary Headwinds, Enduring Fundamentals,” First Capital retained its ‘buy’ recommendation on Sampath Bank, forecasting a potential total return of 26% this year and 37% by 2027, including dividends.

The bank reported a net profit of Rs. 6.8 billion for the three months ended March 2026, down 23.5% from a year earlier and below First Capital’s expectations. The decline was mainly driven by impairment charges that rose to Rs. 4.4 billion, compared with a provision reversal of Rs. 312 million in the corresponding quarter of 2025. Sampath Bank also booked an additional Rs. 1.5 billion overlay provision as a safeguard against geopolitical and macroeconomic risks.

Despite the earnings decline, the bank’s core business remained resilient. Net interest income rose 6.7% year-on-year while net fee and commission income grew 29.3%, reflecting continued strength in lending and transaction-based activities.

A standout feature of the quarter was loan growth. Gross loans and advances expanded 47.4% year-on-year to Rs. 1.4 trillion, supported by strong demand for long-term credit, import financing and leasing facilities. Deposits also increased, reaching Rs. 1.7 trillion, up 11.4% from a year earlier.

First Capital expects lending growth to moderate in the coming months due to tighter regulations affecting vehicle financing and gold-backed loans. However, it notes that these segments account for less than one-fifth of Sampath’s loan portfolio and therefore are unlikely to materially alter the bank’s growth trajectory.
