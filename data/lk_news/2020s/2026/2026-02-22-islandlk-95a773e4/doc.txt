Huawei unveils Top 10 Smart PV & ESS Trends for 2026

Huawei Digital Power has released its Top 10 Trends of Smart PV & ESS 2026, outlining the future of renewable energy as the industry shifts from expansion to system-wide integration.

Presented by Eric Zhong, Vice President of Huawei’s Smart PV & ESS product line, the trends focus on transforming solar, wind, and storage into dependable primary energy sources. Key developments include grid-forming energy storage for grid stability, AI-driven generation-grid-load-storage coordination, and system-level battery management to enhance safety.

The trends emphasize quantified safety standards replacing sample-based checks, high-voltage architectures reducing electricity costs, and AI-native residential PV systems. Grid-forming technology is evolving from passive support to active grid architecture, while AI agents enable power plant automation through cloud-edge-device collaboration.

“All-scenario grid-forming, unleashing AI, and forging excellence guide our technological progress,” Huawei stated, calling on industry partners to accelerate renewable adoption and make green electricity accessible to every home and business.
