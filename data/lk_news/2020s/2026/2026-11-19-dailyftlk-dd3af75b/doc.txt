High Court suspends Namal Rajapaksa’s bail in Krrish case

The Colombo High Court yesterday issued an interim order suspending the bail granted to Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa by the Colombo Chief Magistrate’s Court in the Krrish transaction case, pending the outcome of legal proceedings.

The order follows a revision application by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC), challenging the Chief Magistrate’s Court’s decision of 3 November, 2026 to grant Rajapaksa bail. The Commission is seeking to overturn the bail order and have him remanded again.
