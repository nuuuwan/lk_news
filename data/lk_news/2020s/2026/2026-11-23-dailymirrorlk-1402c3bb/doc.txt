Diesel spill hits Colombo coast

Coast Guard personnel deploy an oil containment boom to prevent diesel from spreading into the sea following an oil spill near the Mutwal Fisheries Harbour in Colombo on November 22, 2026. The spill occurred on November 21 after a pipeline connected to a storage tank at the Jaya Container Terminal (JCT) in Bloemendhal, Colombo Port, ruptured, allowing diesel to flow into the sea through a drainage canal. Coast Guard teams launched an operation to contain the spill and remove the leaked oil from the coastal waters. Pix by Pradeep Pathirana
