Special task force to be established to respond to Colombo floods

Colombo, Nov 07 (Daily Mirror) - A special emergency response task force comprising the Tri-Forces, Police and relevant government institutions is to be established under the leadership of the Colombo Municipal Council (CMC) to ensure a swift response to emergency situations such as flash floods.

Secretary to the President Dr Nandika Sanath Kumanayake issued the directive at a meeting held at the Presidential Secretariat today (07) to examine the causes of the sudden flooding in Colombo yesterday (06) and identify immediate measures to prevent similar situations.

CMC officials informed the meeting that approximately 129 mm of rain had fallen within one hour yesterday, far exceeding the capacity of the existing drainage system, which was designed to accommodate rainfall of around 30 mm per hour.

The meeting also discussed short-, medium- and long-term measures to upgrade Colombo's drainage infrastructure to address the increasing risk of flooding.

Officials pointed out that indiscriminate waste disposal had obstructed several drains and canals, affecting the proper flow of rainwater. The urgent need to clear blocked waterways and take legal action against those responsible for improper waste disposal was also highlighted.

Unauthorised constructions within the CMC limits were identified as another factor obstructing drainage systems and canals. Discussions focused on preparing plans to remove such structures and relocate affected residents to suitable alternative locations.

The meeting also stressed the importance of raising public awareness about proper waste disposal and keeping waterways free of obstructions.

Colombo Mayor Vraie Cally Balthazaar, Deputy Mayor Hemantha Kumara, senior officials of the CMC, the Road Development Authority, Sri Lanka Land Development Corporation, Urban Development Authority, Tri-Forces, Police and other relevant institutions attended the meeting.
