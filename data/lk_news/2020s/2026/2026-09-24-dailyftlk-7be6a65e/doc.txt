Divolca Nova receives SLSI certificates

Divolca Electric Ltd., recently received SLSI 1000, SLSI 734 and SLSI 734-2 certificates for the Divolca Nova series of switches and switch sockets. The certificates were presented to Divolca Electric Sales and Marketing Director Senal Samarawickrama (left) by SLSI Director General Dr. Siddhika Senaratne.  Divolca Quality Control Officer Wasana Ratnayake, Manager Sajith Madhusankha, Head of Sales Chandana Gunasena and SLSI Senior Director A.P. Kandage were also present at the occasion
