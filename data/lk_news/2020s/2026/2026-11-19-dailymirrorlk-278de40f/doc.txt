Three Mannar restaurants fined over health violations

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - Three restaurants operating within the Mannar Urban Council area have been fined a total of Rs. 95,000 by court over multiple food safety and public health violations.

The fines were imposed following cases filed by Public Health Inspectors (PHIs) of the Mannar Urban Council. One restaurant owner was fined Rs. 35,000, while the other two were each fined Rs. 30,000.

The court also imposed suspended prison sentences of 24 months on each of the three defendants.

The charges included preparing food without valid business registration certificates and recognised medical certificates, and discharging untreated wastewater into the main drainage system.

Other violations included failing to control flies in food preparation areas, preparing food in environments contaminated with rat and cockroach droppings, and handling food without proper protective equipment.

The defendants were also charged with preparing food under unhygienic conditions and failing to use protective gear such as masks, gloves, hairnets and aprons.

Health authorities said legal action was taken following routine inspections that revealed shortcomings posing risks to public health.

They urged owners of food-handling establishments to strictly follow food safety regulations and health guidelines to ensure that food served to the public was safe and hygienic.
