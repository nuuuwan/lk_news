Preparations underway to implement education reforms in 2027

Colombo, Sep. 14 (Daily Mirror) - A parliamentary committee has revealed that preparations are well underway for the implementation of education reforms in 2027, with activity books, teacher guides and modules for Grades 2 and 6 already prepared and teacher training programmes currently in progress.

The progress of the General Education Reforms and Preschool Education Reforms was discussed at a recent meeting of the Subcommittee on Education Reforms, chaired by Prime Minister and Minister of Education Dr. Harini Amarasuriya.

According to officials, plans are in place to introduce seven subjects, including History and Aesthetic Studies, for the G.C.E. Ordinary Level examination, while vocational education will be introduced for students in Grades 10 and 11.

Modules covering social cohesion, law and ethics are also planned for students in Grades 12 and 13.

The meeting was informed that a series of awareness programmes would be conducted in the coming months to educate teachers, parents and other stakeholders about the proposed reforms.

Meanwhile, a National Preschool Education Policy has been formulated to formalise preschool education in the country. Work is currently underway on the registration of preschools, preparation of curricula and teacher guides, and implementation of teacher training programmes.

The government had earlier put the proposed Grade 6 education reforms on hold following strong protests over the inclusion of a reference to a sex website in a Grade 6 English language module.
