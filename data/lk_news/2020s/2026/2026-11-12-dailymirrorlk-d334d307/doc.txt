LAUGFS Gas to resume cylinder deliveries after arrival of 3,000+ MT LPG shipment

Colombo, Nov. 12 (Daily Mirror) - LAUGFS Gas PLC will resume LPG cylinder deliveries in phases following the arrival of a shipment carrying more than 3,000 metric tonnes of liquefied petroleum gas (LPG), the company announced today (12).

The vessel GAS GOODWILL arrived in Sri Lanka carrying the consignment, which is expected to help restore supplies following the recent disruption.

Once the cargo is unloaded, LAUGFS Gas will commence cylinder filling and dispatch supplies through its distributor and dealer network.

The company said availability would gradually improve as deliveries reached local outlets.

Acknowledging the difficulties customers faced in obtaining refills, LAUGFS Gas thanked them for their patience.

Customers have been advised to check stock availability with their nearest authorised LAUGFS Gas dealer or call 1345 before visiting an outlet.
