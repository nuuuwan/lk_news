TIN numbers : What ails the government?

I walked into a regional Inland Revenue Department more as a fact-finding mission than any other. At least 1500 people huddled from shoulder to shoulder spanning the three stories were searching for a TIN number. I asked why it was not available online. Reply was telephones are not even answered so they have to trek personally. Deadline given is 30th November hence the crushing human torpedo! Apart from a TIN a PIN number is required by the authorities. Half the people did not even have a smart phone.

Had the opportunity to speak to the Deputy Commissioner who assured me that apart from logging in online nothing can be done.

Digitalising is fine. But are our people equipped with the necessary equipment to meet the requirements?

I witnessed long queues of people who had no resort to expensive tax consultants.  What about the elders? They have no recourse to online placements.

It is time the government take stock of ground realities and take steps to alleviate the misery heaped on the common man.
