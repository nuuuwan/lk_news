Prime Land Residencies exits financial biz following sale of 400 million HNB Finance shares

The CSE recorded satisfactory turnover yesterday when Prime Land Residencies sold 400 million shares of HNB Finance for Rs 3.5 billion by way of a crossing or arranged transaction.

However, the actual buyer in this transaction was not revealed but Prime Lands Residencies exited the financial business after this divestiture.

Amid of those developments the day’s turnover stood at more than Rs 7 billion. The All Share Price Index went up by 127.75 points while S and P SL20 rose by 37.16 points. Turnover stood at Rs 7.38 billion with eight crossings.

Those crossings were; HNB Finance 400 million shares crossed for Rs 3 5 billion; its shares traded at Rs 8.80, Softlogic Life 10.8 million shares crossed for Rs 1 billion; its shares traded at Rs 92, TJ Lanka two million shares crossed for Rs 63 million; its shares traded at Rs 31.60, JKH 3.1 million shares crossed for Rs 62.9 million; its shares traded at Rs 20, Dialog Axiata 1.1 million shares crossed for Rs 48 million; its shares sold at Rs 41.80, CDB (Non-Voting) one million shares crossed for Rs 33.5 million; its shares sold at Rs 33.50 and CIP Holdings one million shares crossed for Rs 33 million; its shares sold at Rs 33.

In the retail market companies that mainly contributed to the turnover were; HNB Rs 374 million (945,000 shares traded), Commercial Bank Rs 270 million (1.3 million shares traded), HNB Finance Rs 161 million (16.4 million shares traded), Sampath Bank Rs 119 million (839,000 shares traded), Access Engineering Rs 111 million (1.4 million shares traded), JKH Rs 109 million (5.4 million shares traded) and Softlogic Life Rs 90 million (950,000 shares traded). During the day 516 million share volumes changed hands in 23906 transactions.

It is said that the financial sector performed well, especially HNB Finance and Softlogic Life insurance, while banking sector counters, especially Commercial Bank performed well. Further manufacturing sector counters, especially JKH performed satisfactorily at the floor.

Yesterday the rupee was quoted at Rs 328.00/330.00 to the US dollar in the spot market, from Rs 328.50/332.00 Wednesday, while bond yields dropped sharply on the news of an US-Iran truce and crude oil dropping to dollars 91 per barrel subsequently, and Sri Lanka remaining in budget surplus for Jan-March, dealers said.

The telegraphic transfer rate for Sri Lanka’s rupee against the US dollar was 325.0000 buying, 334.0000 selling.

A bond maturing on 01.08.2030 was quoted at 11.80/85 percent, down from 12.00/15 percent.

A bond maturing on 15.01.2033 was quoted at 12.00/30 percent down from 12.45/55 percent.

A bond maturing on 15.03.2035 was quoted at 12.75/85 percent, down from 13.00/15 percent.

Aitken Spence announced a dividend of Rs 4 a share, which will be tax exempted for shareholders. The share was trading at Rs.138.50, up 3.17 percent.

Prime Lands Residencies declared a dividend of 80 cents a share. The stock was up Rs 0.20 at Rs.46.60.
