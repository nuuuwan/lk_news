CEPAC President appointed to EDB’s Trade and Logistics Advisory Committee

The Courier Express Parcel Association of Ceylon (CEPAC) announced that its President Nishantha Rathmalvinna has been appointed as a member of the Trade Logistics and Hub Operations Advisory Committee of the Sri Lanka Export Development Board (EDB).

The Association views this appointment as an important opportunity to constructively contribute industry perspectives and deep insights at a national level, supporting Sri Lanka’s efforts to enhance its trade and logistics capabilities.

CEPAC said it remains committed to working collaboratively with stakeholders to drive positive change and contribute towards a more efficient, competitive and progressive trade and logistics sector in Sri Lanka.
