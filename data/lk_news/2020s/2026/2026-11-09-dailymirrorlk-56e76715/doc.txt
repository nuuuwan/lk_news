DoubleTree by Hilton Weerawila Rajawarna Resort earns triple honours

DoubleTree by Hilton Weerawila Rajawarna Resort is celebrating a significant international achievement, securing three prestigious titles at the 2026 World Luxury Awards.

With one national and two regional honours, the resort has earned recognition across three of its defining offerings: exceptional accommodation, family holidays and destination weddings.

The resort received the titles of Best Presidential Suite in Sri Lanka, Best Luxury Family Resort in South West Asia and Best Luxury Wedding Resort in South West Asia. Together, these accolades mark a proud milestone for the property, recognising its appeal to the guests travelling for private retreats, meaningful family time and life’s most important celebrations.

The two South West Asia titles give the achievement particular significance, extending the resort’s recognition beyond Sri Lanka to a regional stage. Success across three distinct categories demonstrates the breadth of the property’s offering and brings further visibility to Weerawila as a destination for leisure and celebrations in Sri Lanka’s deep south.

The award for Best Presidential Suite in Sri Lanka places one of the resort’s most distinctive accommodation experiences in the national spotlight. Spanning three stories, the Presidential Suite offers a spacious setting for guests seeking privacy, comfort and time together, making it a standout feature of the resort’s accommodation portfolio.

The title of Best Luxury Family Resort in South West Asia celebrates another central part of the resort’s identity, bringing generations together through shared experiences. Its natural surroundings and opportunities for recreation and discovery provide a setting where families can spend meaningful time together and create memories beyond their everyday routines.

Recognition as Best Luxury Wedding Resort in South West Asia further strengthens the resort’s standing as a destination for wedding celebrations. Combining distinctive surroundings with indoor and outdoor venues and personalised service, the property offers couples a setting in which to bring their wedding vision to life and welcome loved ones for an extended celebration.
