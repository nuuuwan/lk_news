Varuni Bogahawatta suspended following remand over unexplained vehicle payments

Colombo, Nov 08 (Daily Mirror) - Woman Chief Inspector Varuni Bogahawatta, who served as the Officer-in-Charge (OIC) of the Administration Unit at the Kotawila Police Station, has been suspended from service.

The suspension order was issued by Matara and Hambantota Districts Deputy Inspector General of Police (DIG) Chamika Wickramasinghe.

Bogahawatta was recently remanded after failing to disclose or verify the source of funds used to purchase a motor vehicle and settle its leasing instalments.

Earlier, officers from the Financial Crimes Investigation Division of the Criminal Investigation Department (CID) seized a Honda Vezel allegedly in her possession.

The seizure followed investigations which reportedly revealed that the vehicle's leasing instalments had been paid by an individual suspected of involvement in a financial fraud exceeding Rs. 1 billion at the Matara Cooperative Society.
