BASL seeks meeting with President over judges’ retirement age amendment

Colombo, July 3 (Daily Mirror) - The Bar Association of Sri Lanka (BASL) has formally requested a meeting with President Anura Kumara Dissanayake to discuss the Government’s proposed constitutional amendment seeking to increase the retirement age of judges.

The BASl has urged the President that no further steps be taken on the proposal until it has had an opportunity to present its views.

In a letter addressed to the President, the BASL said constitutional amendments affecting the Superior Judiciary should never be introduced in an ad hoc manner or in response to temporary circumstances. It stressed that reforms of such constitutional significance should only be undertaken where there is a clearly demonstrable institutional necessity, transparent public justification and broad stakeholder consultation.

The BASL further stated that public confidence in constitutional governance depends not only on the substance of an amendment but also on the process by which constitutional changes are introduced.

The BASL said it had previously conveyed its concerns to the President in writing but had not received any response or official observations. It noted that, despite the absence of a response, the Cabinet of Ministers had proceeded to consider and approve the proposal without first giving the Association an opportunity to present its concerns.

According to the letter, the BASL was subsequently granted an opportunity to present its initial submissions to Justice Minister Harshana Nanayakkara. During that meeting, the Minister assured the Association that its position would be conveyed to the President and that a request would be made for a meeting between the President and the BASL to discuss the matter.

The BASL also recalled that on April 9, 2026, the Justice Minister had written to the President’s Private Secretary requesting that such a meeting be arranged.

Reiterating its request, the BASL said it continued to look forward to meeting the President and respectfully urged that no further action be taken regarding the proposed constitutional amendment until it had been afforded an opportunity to present its considered views directly to the Head of State.

The BASL letter dated August 1, 2026 had been sent under the signatures of its President Rajeev Amarasuriya and Secretary Nalin De Silva
