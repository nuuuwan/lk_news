Why did Colombo go underwater in just two hours?

Colombo, Nov 11 (Daily Mirror) - Colombo’s drainage system, designed to handle around 30mm of rain an hour, was overwhelmed when nearly 100mm of rain fell within just two hours last Friday (06), causing flooding across several parts of the city.

Speaking to the Daily Mirror, Deputy Director of Drainage and Water Supply at the Colombo Municipal Council (CMC), Deepika Wijethilake, said the unusually heavy rainfall within a short period was the main reason for the flooding.

"Our drainage system is designed to handle around 30mm of rain within an hour. However, last week we experienced nearly 100mm within an hour. We have not seen rainfall of this intensity within such a short period in recent times," she said.

She explained that the situation could have been different if the rain had fallen over a longer period, as the drainage system would have had time to carry away the water.

Wijethilake said the existing drainage capacity was not sufficient to handle such heavy downpours, adding that steps were being taken to expand the system and improve the capacity of existing drains.

Responding to criticism over poor maintenance, she said the CMC carries out routine cleaning of drains every day despite a shortage of workers.

"Steps are also underway to recruit more workers, while the support of the Tri-Forces is expected to be sought to help clean the drainage network,"

Friday's downpour flooded several roads and damaged shops across Colombo. A section of the National Hospital was also inundated, affecting hospital services.
