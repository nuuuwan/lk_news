‘Sonduru Sarisara’ book launched in Colombo

The launch of Sonduru Sarisara: Indian Railpili Dige (Wandering with Pleasure along Indian Railway Tracks), authored by Saman Athaudahetti, was held today at the Sri Lanka Foundation Institute in Colombo. Former President Ranil Wickremesinghe attended the event as the chief guest. Religious leaders, politicians and other distinguished guests were also present. Pix by Pradeep Pathirana
