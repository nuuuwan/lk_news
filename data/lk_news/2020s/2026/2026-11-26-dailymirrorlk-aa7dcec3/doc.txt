Room charges in state hospital paying wards increased

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Room charges in the paying wards of state hospitals have recently been increased, putting the public in a difficult situation, Sri Lanka Podu Jana Peramuna (SLPP) MP D. V. Chanaka told Parliament yesterday.

Speaking during the committee-stage debate on the Budget, the MP said a room that previously cost Rs. 2,700 had been increased to Rs. 7,000, while a room that cost Rs. 3,300 had been increased to Rs. 9,500.

“It is not the super-rich who make use of paying wards in state hospitals. Mostly state workers in middle level positions and they are affected by the increase of room charges,” the MP said.

He also said medical test charges had also been increased, making them unaffordable.

He further said the government had not spent money on the proposed cardiology unit. “One should make a note that around 20,000 die of heart disease in Sri Lanka annually while 6200 die of cancer per year,” he added.

He said only 52 per cent of the total allocation for capital expenditure had been spent. Only 12 per cent of the capital expenditure allocation for 2026 had been spent so far. In that context, he said thalassaemia patients have not received the promised payment of Rs. 10,000 either.
