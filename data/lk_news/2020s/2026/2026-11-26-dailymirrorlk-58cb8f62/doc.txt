Seven injured in huge Bangkok oil depot blast, four in critical condition

cna - Seven people were injured, four of them critically, after a huge explosion rocked an oil facility in Bangkok on Thursday (Nov 26), according to a local emergency medical centre.

The Erawan Emergency Medical Centre, which is operated by city authorities, reported four "red" cases and three less severe "yellow" cases after the blast in the east of the Thai capital.

AFP reporters saw a large fireball and black smoke rising above the city following the explosion at site run by Thai energy conglomerate Bangchak Corporation. It is Thailand’s fifth-largest and is also the country’s only refinery located in an urban area.

The Bangkok Metropolitan Administration advised residents to avoid the area.

The fire, which broke out at 2.51pm (3.51pm Singapore time), was later extinguished and the situation was returning to normal, according to government updates.

Bangchak said in a statement that it shut down all production units when the fire broke out.

The cause of the incident is under investigation, the company added.

“Sitting at work, the building’s shaking, smoke for an hour already,” wrote X user JnKimnoeymi.

AFP reporters a few kilometres away said the blast shook their doors.

Flames rose high above the site, followed by black smoke that swept across the Thai capital's skyline, reported AFP.

The sirens of emergency vehicles could be heard heading to the scene.
