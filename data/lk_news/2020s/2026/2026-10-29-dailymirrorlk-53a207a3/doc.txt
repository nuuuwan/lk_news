People’s Bank explores banking opportunities for Sri Lankans in UAE

Sri Lankan Bankers Association United Arab Emirates (UAE) Vice President Kapila Karunaratne met with People’s Bank Chairman Chandana Guniyangoda and Chief Executive Officer and General Manager Clive Fonseka on October 23, 2026, to explore opportunities to expand the bank’s services among the Sri Lankan community in the UAE.

The discussions focused on strengthening engagement with Sri Lankan expatriates, increasing awareness of the bank’s financial solutions and improving access to banking services that cater to their personal, family and business needs.
