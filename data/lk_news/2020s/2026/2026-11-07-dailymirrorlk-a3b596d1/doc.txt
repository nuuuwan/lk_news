Chinese Dragon Café opens flagship outlet in Negombo

Chinese Dragon Café (CDC) has expanded its presence with the opening of its newest outlet in Negombo, marking another important step in the brand’s growth across the country.

Located at Chilaw Road, Negombo, just 400 metres from the Maris Stella College towards Kochchikade, the new restaurant becomes Chinese Dragon Café’s 13th outlet in Sri Lanka and 14th outlet worldwide, bringing the brand to one of the country’s most popular coastal cities.

Founded in 1942 by a Chinese couple in Bambalapitiya, Chinese Dragon Café has grown from a small neighbourhood restaurant into one of Sri Lanka’s most recognized Chinese restaurant brands.

Commenting on the opening, Lahiruwan Lakmal, Chief Operating Officer of Chinese Dragon Café said, “Negombo is a unique and growing city with a strong connection to seafood, tourism and a diverse customer community. We see great potential in this market and believe our Chinese cuisine will be well received by customers here.”
