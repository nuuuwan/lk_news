President orders faster development of Katunayake airport

Colombo, Oct. 28 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake has instructed officials to expedite projects aimed at increasing passenger-handling capacity and improving facilities at Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) in Katunayake.

He also highlighted the need to develop other domestic airports across the country alongside the expansion of Katunayake Airport to meet future aviation requirements.

The President made these remarks during a discussion held at the Presidential Secretariat yesterday (27) on the 2027 Budget proposals for the Ministry of Ports and Civil Aviation.

The meeting reviewed the progress of ongoing airport development projects, with particular attention to initiatives aimed at increasing passenger capacity and upgrading infrastructure to provide better facilities for air travellers.

Discussions also focused on the further expansion of supporting infrastructure, including highways, to facilitate the development of the aviation sector.

The meeting was attended by Minister of Ports and Civil Aviation and Minister of Energy Anura Karunathilaka; Minister of Labour and Deputy Minister of Finance and Planning Dr. Anil Jayantha Fernando; Deputy Minister of Ports and Civil Aviation Janith Ruwan Kodituwakku; President's Chief of Staff Prabath Chandrakeerthi; Senior Additional Secretary to the President and Secretary to the Ministry of Energy Russell Aponsu; Secretary to the Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development Dr. Harshana Suriyapperuma; Secretary to the Ministry of Ports and Civil Aviation W.W.S. Mangala; and Deputy Secretary to the Treasury A.K.

Officials from the relevant institutions and the Ministry of Finance, including Mr. Seneviratne and Director General of the Department of National Budget Jude Nilukshan, also participated in the discussion.
