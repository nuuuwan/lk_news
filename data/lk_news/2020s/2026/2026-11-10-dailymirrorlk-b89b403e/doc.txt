Sri Lanka’s Rumesh Tharanga among world’s top 10 athletes

Colombo, Oct. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lankan javelin thrower Rumesh Tharanga Pathirage has secured eighth place in the 2026 Men’s World Athlete of the Year rankings.

Pathirage was among 10 athletes nominated for the prestigious World Athletics award following an impressive season on the international stage.

He enjoyed a remarkable 2026 campaign, claiming gold medals at the Asian Games and Commonwealth Games, while also winning the Diamond League title and the World Athletics Ultimate Championship.

Pathirage also set a new Sri Lankan national record with a throw of 92.62 metres at the Diamond League meeting in Rome, becoming the second-best Asian javelin thrower in history.
