Singer Sri Lanka hosts Fashion Academy Awards 2026

Singer Sri Lanka hosted the Singer Fashion Academy Awards 2026 at the Bandaranaike Memorial International Conference Hall (BMICH) recently, recognising 520 students who had successfully completed professional qualifications in Dressmaking, Machine Embroidery and Fashion Design.

With over 60 years of experience in fashion and sewing education, Singer Fashion Academy has established an extensive learning network across Sri Lanka. It serves over 6,000 active students annually at more than 50 locations islandwide and through online classes via Zoom. Its portfolio of over 20 courses spans different areas of fashion and sewing, including newly introduced pattern-making courses.

Commenting on the occasion, Mahesh Wijewardene, Group Managing Director of Singer (Sri Lanka) PLC, said, “At Singer, we believe that practical education can open doors to new opportunities and contribute to economic empowerment.

The achievements of these students reflect the value of investing in professional training that enables individuals to translate their abilities into tangible outcomes, whether through employment, self-employment or entrepreneurship. Through Singer Fashion Academy, we remain committed to making vocational education accessible and helping people build more independent and fulfilling futures.”

The ceremony recognised students across five programmes: the Certificate in Fashion Design, Diploma in Fashion Design, Advanced Diploma in Fashion Design, Diploma in Dressmaking and Diploma in Machine Embroidery. Of the 520 students recognised, 160 received diplomas, while eight received Advanced Diplomas, reflecting their progression through specialised professional training.

The event also featured a fashion show, giving students the opportunity to present their designs and demonstrate the creativity developed through their training.
