40 buses fail inspection in Pettah

Colombo, Sep 3 (Daily Mirror) - Around 40 inter-provincial private and Sri Lanka Transport Board (SLTB) buses were found to have failed to meet the required standards during a special inspection in Pettah today (03).

The inspection was carried out at the Bastion Mawatha bus stop by the National Transport Commission (NTC), with support from officers of the Department of Motor Traffic.

The authorities inspected about 67 long-distance buses between 9.30 a.m. and 1.30 p.m.

The inspection covered vehicle emissions, passenger safety, comfort, engine condition and other issues that could affect passengers.

Two buses with serious defects were banned from operating. The owners of the other 38 buses were given 14 days to correct the defects and present the buses for another inspection at the Werahera office of the Department of Motor Traffic.

NTC Director General Nilan Miranda said similar inspections would be carried out across the country to ensure safe and comfortable public transport.

He urged bus owners and drivers to ensure that their vehicles meet the required standards before operating.

NTC Director Shereen Athukorala and Department of Motor Traffic Quality Inspector C. K. Edirimuni also participated in the inspection.
