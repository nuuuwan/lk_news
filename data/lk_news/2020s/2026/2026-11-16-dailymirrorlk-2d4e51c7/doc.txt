Vallibel Finance opens Dehiattakandiya, Karapitiya, Tissamaharama branches

Vallibel Finance has further strengthened its presence across Sri Lanka with the opening of two new branches in Karapitiya and Tissamaharama in the Southern province, reinforcing the company’s commitment to expanding access to financial services, promoting financial inclusion and supporting regional economic development.

These two branch openings follow the recent inauguration of Vallibel Finance’s Dehiattakandiya branch last month, further strengthening the company’s islandwide presence.

The new branches are expected to enhance convenience and accessibility for individuals, entrepreneurs and businesses across the Galle, Hambantota and Ampara districts by bringing a comprehensive range of financial solutions closer to the local communities. The expansion marks another milestone in Vallibel Finance’s strategic growth journey, reflecting its continued focus on reaching underserved markets and supporting the evolving financial needs of customers across the country.

All three branches were ceremonially inaugurated by Vallibel Finance Managing Director Jayantha Rangamuwa, in the presence of the members of the corporate management, employees, valued customers and well-wishers.

Addressing the gatherings, Rangamuwa highlighted the company’s commitment to strengthening its presence throughout the Southern province while delivering accessible, professional and customer-focused financial services that respect the unique cultural and social values of the communities it serves.

He also emphasised the potential of the new branches to support a diverse range of economic activities, including agriculture, entrepreneurship and local business development, through a comprehensive portfolio of financial solutions tailored to regional requirements.

Through its expanding branch network, Vallibel Finance continues to offer a broad range of financial products and services, including leasing, fixed deposits, savings accounts, gold loans and Auto Draft facilities.

Supported by modern technological infrastructure and customer-centric service standards, the company remains focused on delivering efficient, accessible and reliable financial solutions that respond to the changing needs of individuals and businesses.

The latest expansion builds on Vallibel Finance’s strong growth trajectory and financial performance. The company surpassed the Rs.200 billion asset milestone within 19 years of operations, reflecting its sustained business growth, financial strength and strategic resilience. This achievement follows another significant milestone in December 2024, when Vallibel Finance became the fastest finance company in Sri Lanka to exceed an asset base of Rs.100 billion, achieving the feat within 17 years of operations.

With the opening of its Dehiattakandiya, Karapitiya and Tissamaharama branches, Vallibel Finance continues to advance its islandwide expansion strategy, bringing financial services closer to communities while creating opportunities for individuals, entrepreneurs and businesses to pursue their ambitions. Through innovation, accessibility and a continued commitment to customer confidence, the company remains focused on contributing to sustainable economic growth across Sri Lanka.
