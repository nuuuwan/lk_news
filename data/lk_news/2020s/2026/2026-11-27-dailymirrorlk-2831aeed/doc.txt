Indices close in red

Indices closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Distilleries, Commercial Bank and Access Engineering with the turnover crossing Rs. 2.7bn.

High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in JF Packaging, Housing Development Finance Corporation and Commercial Bank. Mixed interest was observed in Janashakthi Insurance Company, Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka and John Keells Holdings whilst retail interest was noted in Lanka Credit and Business Finance, Browns Investments and Softlogic Finance.

Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net buyers. The Banking sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Housing Development Finance Corporation, Commercial Bank and Hatton National Bank nonvoting) whilst the sector index edged up by 0.09%. The share price of Housing Development Finance Corporation decreased by Rs. 0.10 (0.15%) to close at Rs. 67.00. The share price of Commercial Bank moved down by Rs. 1.00 (0.47%) to close at Rs. 212.00. The share price of Hatton National Bank nonvoting declined by Rs. 2.25 (0.65%) to close at Rs. 342.75.

Materials sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to JF Packaging Limited) whilst the sector index edged down by 0.06%. The share price of JF Packaging Limited closed flat at Rs. 18.00.

Janashakthi Insurance Company was also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Janashakthi Insurance Company recorded a gain of Rs. 9.50 (24.55%) to close at Rs. 48.20.
