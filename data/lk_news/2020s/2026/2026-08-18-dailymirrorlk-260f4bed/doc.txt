Kandy Kumbal Perahera parades...

The first Kumbal Procession of the Kandy Esala Festival paraded the streets a short while ago, with Sri Dalada Maligawa tusker Indirajah carrying the casket of sacred relics. He was flanked by fellow Maligawa tuskers Myanraja on the left and Kotteraja on the right. Large crowds gathered along the procession route. Pix by Shane Seneviratne
