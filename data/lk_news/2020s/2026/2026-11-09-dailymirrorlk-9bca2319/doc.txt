Sri Lankan-born New Yorker completes 50th anniversary NYC Marathon

Sri Lankan-born American citizen Chamal Rathnayake has proudly represented Sri Lanka at the 2026 TCS New York City Marathon, completing the 42.2-kilometre race in four hours and 38 minutes.

Rathnayake took part in the historic event, which marked the 50th anniversary of the marathon's five-borough course, attracting more than 60,000 runners from over 100 countries.

He joined the Parade of Nations on October 30, alongside athletes from around the world and several other Sri Lankans participating in the event.

Rathnayake wore an outfit designed by Lovi, sponsored by renowned designer Asanka De Mel, to showcase Sri Lankan identity and culture on the international stage.

Another Sri Lankan participant, Chamal Mediwaka, also represented the country in Lovi attire and had the honour of carrying the Sri Lankan national flag as the flag bearer.

On November 1, Rathnayake successfully completed the full 26.2-mile (42.2-kilometre) marathon, which takes runners through all five boroughs of New York City, from Staten Island to the finish line in Central Park.

Having taken up running as a regular activity only a couple of years ago, Rathnayake trained for an entire year while continuing to work full-time in the IT sector. The 2026 event marked his first New York City Marathon.

"Every step of those 26.2 miles was for my motherland. I am grateful to my wife Indu, Asanka De Mel and team Lovi, my family, friends, and everyone who cheered me on," Rathnayake said.

He hopes to participate in more international running events and marathons in the future.
