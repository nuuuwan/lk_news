Cabinet approves Rs. 104.9 bn central expressway road project

Colombo, Aug. 25 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet has approved a proposal to develop connecting roads linking Sections I, III and IV of the Central Expressway with the existing road network in the Western, Central and North-Western provinces.

The project is aimed at improving connectivity, accessibility and traffic flow between the Central Expressway and the existing road network.

Accordingly, a 117.68-kilometre connecting road network has been proposed for Section I of the Central Expressway.

A further 12 connecting roads covering 83.98 kilometres are planned for Section III, while nine connecting roads with a total length of 81.36 kilometres are proposed for Section IV.

The provisional total cost of the project has been estimated at Rs. 104.9 billion.

The Cabinet approved the proposal submitted by the Minister of Transport, Highways and Urban Development to implement the connecting road development project for Sections I, III and IV during the 2026–2030 period.
