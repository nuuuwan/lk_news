Dinemore joins the Dialog Pay Merchant ecosystem

Dialog Axiata PLC, Sri Lanka’s #1 connectivity provider, has expanded the Dialog Pay merchant ecosystem through a new partnership with Dinemore, bringing secure LankaQR payments to customers across its network of 12 restaurants. Customers can now use Dialog Pay when enjoying Dinemore’s wide range of meals, while receiving complimentary Dialog data on eligible LankaQR transactions.

From quick meals during a busy day to dining with family and friends, customers can use Dialog Pay for a secure and convenient way to pay at Dinemore restaurants. By adding another digital payment option, the partnership brings greater flexibility to the everyday dining experience while complementing Dinemore’s longstanding focus on serving accessible and enjoyable meals to customers.

Shaheem Sadikeen, Director of Dinemore, said, “For many years, Dinemore has been part of the everyday dining experiences of Sri Lankans, and staying relevant means continuing to evolve alongside our customers. Partnering with Dialog Pay is part of that journey, bringing greater digital convenience and added value to the way customers dine with us. We are pleased to work with Dialog in embracing solutions that enhance the experience our customers have come to know and enjoy.”
