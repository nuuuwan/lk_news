Anuradhapura crash: SLTB bus driver tested positive for cannabis and alcohol

Colombo, Nov. 13 (Daily Mirror) - The driver of the Sri Lanka Transport Board (SLTB) bus who died in the fatal three-bus collision in Rambewa, Anuradhapura, had allegedly consumed cannabis and alcohol at the time of the accident, preliminary police investigations and medical tests have revealed.

According to Police, a urine sample test conducted on the deceased driver reportedly confirmed the presence of both substances.

The accident occurred on November 10, near the Weli Oya Junction in Rambewa, along the Kandy-Jaffna (A9) Road.

Four people, including an SLTB bus driver and two women, were killed in the collision, while 76 passengers sustained injuries and were admitted to hospitals.

The accident occurred when an SLTB bus travelling from Janakapura to Colombo collided head-on with another SLTB bus travelling from Akkaraipattu towards Jaffna.

A private bus travelling from Vavuniya to Colombo also crashed into the rear of the SLTB bus travelling towards Colombo, resulting in the three-bus collision.
