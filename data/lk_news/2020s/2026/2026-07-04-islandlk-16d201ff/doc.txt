Royal clinch Western Province Hockey title in penalty shootout

Royal College, Colombo were crowned Western Province Hockey champions after edging defending champions St. Joseph’s College, Colombo in a dramatic penalty shootout following a 1-1 draw in the final at the Colombo International Astro Turf ground.

The tournament, organised by the Western Province Ministry of Education, was held from June 19 to 23.

Royal dominated possession for much of the contest with fluent passing and attacking play, taking the lead in the ninth minute when centre forward Kithmina Rathnayake converted a rebound from a penalty corner. St. Joseph’s fought back in the closing stages, with captain Mark Ruiz equalising after capitalising on a defensive lapse. The Josephians missed a late penalty corner opportunity that could have secured victory.

The initial five-player penalty shootout ended 3-3, forcing sudden death. Royal’s Rumal Jayasinghe converted his effort before goalkeeper Stefan Anthonypillai produced the decisive save against St. Joseph’s vice-captain Raghavan Gokul to seal the title.

The victory gave Royal their second major title within a month, having also won the All-Island Championship in May. Royal reached the final unbeaten, defeating Bandaranayake College Gampaha (6-0), Isipathana College (3-0) and Prince of Wales’ College, Moratuwa (3-1). Prince of Wales’ secured third place by beating St. Benedict’s College 2-0 in the consolation final.
