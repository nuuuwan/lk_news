FCID objects to bail for two suspects in Rs. 190 Bn case

Colombo, Nov. 5 (Daily Mirror) - Colombo Chief Magistrate Asanga S. Bodaragama today ordered the remand of two suspects, including Jiffry Mohamed, till November 12 over an alleged illegal transfer of Rs. 190 billion to foreign countries under the pretext of importing goods into Sri Lanka.

When the case was taken up before court, the first and eighth suspects were produced before the court by prison officials.

Officers of the Financial Crimes Investigation Division informed the court that objections to granting bail to the first and eighth suspects had been filed today.

The investigating officers further informed the court that steps were being taken to obtain the final assessment reports and other relevant documents required for the ongoing investigation into the import and export transactions and submit them to court.

They also stated that statements would be recorded from individuals who had contributed to establishing the companies concerned and laying their foundations, and that such statements would be submitted to court.

The officers further said that investigations were continuing into the involvement of the first suspect, Jiffry Mohamed and the eighth suspect.

The FCID officers also informed the court that further inquiries were being conducted to determine whether there were any links between bank officials and the companies under investigation, and that relevant statements would be submitted to court.

Having considered the submissions, the Magistrate stated that the order on bail would be delivered on November 12.

Accordingly, the two suspects were further remanded till November 12.
