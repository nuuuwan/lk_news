Leema Creations retains CNCI Industrial Excellence (Large Category) Award

Leema Creations, with an impeccable track record on service delivery in interior architecture, proved that they are among the best in the interior industry by winning the Industrial Excellence Award-2026 (Manufacturing Sector Large Category/Silver), at the awards ceremony last week organised by the Ceylon National Chamber of Industries.

By winning this Award, for the fifth consecutive time, Leema’s exposure and credibility has significantly increased in the marketplace. This significant accolade for the utmost level of creativity, innovation, originality and inspiring practices in the field of architecture, interiors and design signifies our status and reputation as among the best in the industry. It is also an independent endorsement of the company’s expertise and professionalism.

Leema Creations described it as the most celebrated interior solutions provider in Sri Lanka, recognised by the industry in Sri Lanka and overseas during the past decade has proven once again that they are at the pinnacle of excellence in the interior industry marking another major milestone in the company’s corporate journey. The latest award recognises its sustained leadership and excellence in Sri Lanka’s interior design and manufacturing industry, the Company added.
