Parliament to take up debate on prison clash today

Colombo, July 7 (Daily Mirror) - Parliament is to take up a debate on the Negombo prison clashes today from 3.30 pm to 6.30 pm, Speaker Jagath Wickramaratne announced today.

The debate was given in response to a motion submitted by the Opposition calling for an urgent debate.
