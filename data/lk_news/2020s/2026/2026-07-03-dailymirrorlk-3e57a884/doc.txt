Litro cuts domestic LP gas prices by up to Rs. 300

Colombo, July 03 (Daily Mirror) - Litro Gas Lanka today announced a reduction in the prices of domestic LP gas cylinders, with the revised rates taking effect from midnight.

According to the Litro Gas Chairman, the price of a 12.5kg domestic cylinder has been reduced by Rs. 300 to Rs. 4,465.

The price of the 5kg cylinder has been cut by Rs. 118 to Rs. 1,792, while the 2.3kg cylinder will now be sold at Rs. 835 following a reduction of Rs. 55.

The Chairman said the revised prices will come into effect from midnight today.
