Heavy rains disrupt Colombo life

Heavy rains disrupt the day-to-day lives of people in Colombo and its suburbs, affecting commuters and pedestrians as they go about their daily activities today (15). The Department of Meteorology has forecast showers or thundershowers across most parts of the island, with heavy rainfall exceeding 75 mm possible in some areas of the Sabaragamuwa, Central and North Central provinces, as well as the Trincomalee and Kurunegala districts.
