Market Research Society of Sri Lanka elects new committee

The newly elected MRSSL Executive Committee for 2026. Seated (from left): Suthaharan Perampalam (President - Sparkwinn), Panchalie Pothupitiya (decrypt), Bhawangi Jayawardena (Sparkwinn), Crishmila George (Breakthrough), and Chamalika Gunawardana (Treasurer - Clarascope). Standing (from left): Ahinsa Jayathunga (Assistant Secretary - Quantum), Thushari Karunathilake (Kantar), Anjalin Daniel (Quantum), Prajika Fernando (Secretary - decrypt), Deepal Nanayakkara (Kantar), and Liyamalie Manayake (Breakthrough)

The Market Research Society of Sri Lanka (MRSSL) elected its Executive Committee for the 2026 term at its 13th Annual General Meeting, with Suthaharan Perampalam being re-elected as President for a second consecutive term.

In his acceptance speech, Suthaharan expressed his gratitude to the membership and Executive Committee for their continued support, acknowledging their collective efforts in delivering a successful year for the Society.

He highlighted the growing importance of data, insights, and evidence-based decision-making in today’s business environment, noting that while technology continues to transform the industry, the role of market research remains critical in understanding people and capturing consumer perspectives.

“At this important juncture, we must continue to strengthen our profession by upholding high standards and creating meaningful value for all our members,” he said.

Looking ahead, Suthaharan outlined plans to deepen engagement with research agencies and industry stakeholders while enhancing knowledge-sharing and professional development initiatives to meet the evolving needs of the industry.

The Society also announced changes to its Board of Directors, with Neomal Marambe of Quantum Research and Suresh Jayaweera of Breakthrough Intelligence joining the Board. MRSSL extends its appreciation to outgoing Board Directors Roshani Fernando and Dilini Jayasuriya for their dedicated service and valuable contributions to the Society.
