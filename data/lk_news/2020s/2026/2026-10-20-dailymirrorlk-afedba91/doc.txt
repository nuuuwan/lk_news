Richard Pieris Finance expands presence opening 19th branch

From left: Chairman - Nalin Wijekoon; Director – Rasitha Gunawardana and Chief Executive Officer – Lohika Fonseka

Richard Pieris Finance marked another significant milestone with the opening of its 19th branch in Galle, further strengthening its presence in the Southern Province and bringing innovative, convenient, and accessible financial solutions closer to the community.

The opening ceremony was graced by Chairman Nalin Wijekoon, Chief Executive Officer Lohika Fonseka, members of the Board of Directors, and the management team of Richard Pieris Finance.

The newly opened branch offers a comprehensive range of financial services, including Fixed Deposits and Savings, Leasing, Device Financing, Gold Loans, Property Mortgage Loans, and Islamic Financial Solutions. These services reflect the company’s continued commitment to providing accessible, customer-focused financial solutions that cater to the diverse needs of individuals, families, and businesses.

With the opening of its 19th branch, Richard Pieris Finance continues to expand its reach across Sri Lanka, reinforcing its commitment to delivering innovative financial solutions, building lasting customer relationships, and empowering communities to achieve their financial aspirations.
