LAUGFS gas slashes domestic LP gas prices

Colombo, July 3 (Daily Mirror) - LAUGFS Gas has announced a significant reduction in domestic LP gas prices, effective from midnight today.

Accordingly, the price of a 12.5kg domestic LP gas cylinder has been reduced by Rs. 1,280, bringing the new retail price down to Rs. 4,965.

The price of the 5kg domestic LP gas cylinder has also been reduced by Rs. 512, with the new price set at Rs. 1,988.

The company said the revised prices will take effect from midnight today, providing relief to domestic consumers.
