BOC Rings the Bell for Sri Lanka’s Largest-Ever Listed Basel III Debenture Issue

The Bank of Ceylon (BOC) marked the listing of its historic Rs. 30 billion Listed Basel III Debenture Issue with a bell-ringing ceremony at the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE), formally recognizing the largest ever, listed debenture issuance in Sri Lankan history.

The ceremony was attended by BOC’s Chairman, Mr Kavinda de Zoysa, Board of Directors, General Manager/Chief Executive Officer, Mr Y.A Jayathilaka, and senior management, alongside the CEO of the CSE and key transaction stakeholders.Opened on 3 November 2026, the issue was oversubscribed and closed on the opening day after attracting over Rs. 39 billion in applications. Exercising its upsize options up to Rs. 30 billion, the transaction stands as a defining milestone for Sri Lanka’s domestic debt capital market.

BOC Chairman Mr. Kavinda de Zoysa stated: “This bell ringing marks a historic milestone as we list Sri Lanka’s largest-ever Basel III Compliant Listed debenture issue, being oversubscribed 3.9 times over the initial issue of Rs.10 billion on the opening day itself. This reflects strong investor confidence in BOC’s financial strength, governance, and eight-decade legacy at the heart of Sri Lanka’s financial ecosystem proving once again that BOC continues to lead and set new benchmarks for others to follow. We are deeply grateful to the Colombo Stock Exchange, our regulators, and our investors for their continued trust and participation in this landmark achievement.”

The CEO of the Colombo Stock Exchange, Mr Rajeeva Bandaranaike added: “We congratulate the Bank of Ceylon on completing Sri Lanka’s largest-ever listed debenture issue to date. Raising LKR 30 billion on the CSE demonstrates the growing capacity of our domestic debt market to support large-scale institutional funding. Issuances of this scale align with our efforts to expand market access and serve a broader investor base.”

Rated ‘AA’ (Stable) by Lanka Rating Agency Limited, the debentures were issued at Rs. 100/- each to strengthen BOC’s Tier 2 capital base and expand credit across sectors, including agriculture, tourism, export manufacturing, logistics, infrastructure, and digital services. BOC’s Investment Banking Division served as Manager/Registrar to the issue while BOC’s Legal Division acted as Lawyers to the Issue.

This milestone builds on BOC’s strong 2026 performance highlighted by the first 09 month profit before tax of Rs. 94.1 billion, recognition as Sri Lanka’s Most Valuable Brand by Brand Finance Lanka, and a Fitch Ratings upgrade to ‘AA+(lka)’ with a Stable Outlook, the highest national long-term rating among Sri Lankan banks.

Guided by purpose and trusted by over 16 million customers, BOC continues support national progress and the long term economic development of Sri Lanka.
