Sri Lanka stocks close down, turnover low at Rs666mn

ECONOYNEXT – Sri Lanka’s Colombo Stock Exchange closed lower on Monday, CSE data showed, with the benchmark All Share Price Index falling 0.23 percent.

The ASPI was down 44.35 points at 19,640.56, while the more liquid S&P SL20 was down 0.21 percent, or 11.76 points, at 5,601.87.

Market sentiment remained subdued amidst low turnover, with investors adopting a wait-and-see approach as geopolitical tensions escalate, NDB Securities Head of Research and Strategy Raynal Wickremeratne noted.

Positive contributors to the ASPI were Singer (Sri Lanka) (up 1.74 percent at 70.00 rupees), Ceylon Cold Stores (up 0.84 percent at 120.00 rupees), LOLC Finance (up 2.27 percent at 4.50 rupees), ACL Cables (up 1.16 percent at 87.00 rupees), and John Keells Holdings (up 0.57 percent at 17.70 rupees).

Commercial Bank of Ceylon (down 0.38 percent at 195.25 rupees), Distilleries Company of Sri Lanka (down 0.58 percent at 51.60 rupees), Hayleys (down 0.58 percent at 215.75 rupees), Malwatte Valley Plantations (down 4.76 percent at 54.00 rupees), and LOLC Holdings (down 1.01 percent at 392.50 rupees) were top negative contributors.

Market turnover was 666.29 million rupees. The Banking industry group led turnover with 185.67 million rupees.

Crossings were recorded in Access Engineering (74.5 million rupees) and Commercial Bank of Ceylon (59 million rupees). (Colombo/Oct26/2026)
