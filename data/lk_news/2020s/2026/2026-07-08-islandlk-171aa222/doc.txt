Little beauty powerhouse …

Yes, tomatoes are full of lycopene, vitamin C, and natural acids that help brighten and clear skin, and amazing for the scalp, too.

Here are some easy homemade beauty tips with tomatoes you can do right in your kitchen:

Mash the tomato, mix it with the honey and apply to clean face. Let it remain for 15 minutes, and then rinse with cool water.

: Tomato brightens, while honey hydrates. Your skin will look fresh even on grey days.

Rub a slice of tomato gently on lips for 01 minute, before bed, and let the juice remain overnight. Rinse in the morning.

Patch test first – if your skin is sensitive, test on your wrist.

Use fresh tomatoes – and don’t use if you have open cuts. The acid can sting.

You will need 01 big ripe tomato and 02 teaspoons of lemon juice.

Blend the tomato and strain the juice. Mix in the lemon juice and apply directly to scalp with your fingers. Massage for 05 minutes, let it remain for 20 minutes, and then shampoo as usual.

Kills dandruff-causing fungus and soothes itching. Do this once a week.

You will need 01 tomato, 01 egg yolk and 01 teaspoon castor oil.

Blend all together and apply to scalp and hair. Leave 30 minutes, then wash.

Protein from egg and vitamins from tomato help strengthen roots. Do this every 10 days.

Important: Don’t use on coloured hair – the acidity can fade colour a bit.

Always follow with conditioner – tomato can be drying if you skip this.

Smell tip: The tomato smell goes away after one wash. Add a few drops of rosemary or lavender oil if you want.
