Over 300 to receive ACSM Marketing Diplomas

More than 300 marketing professionals from various sectors across Sri Lanka are expected to receive their diplomas at the sixth annual convocation of the Asian College of Sales and Marketing (ACSM) on January 4, 2027.

The ceremony, marking the completion of the 2025–2026 Marketing Diploma programme, will be held at the Bandaranaike Memorial International Conference Hall (BMICH) in Colombo.

The graduates have successfully completed the diploma course for the 2025–2026 academic year.

ACSM Director and Learning Consultant Sugath Munasinghe said several distinguished academics and education experts were expected to attend the convocation as invited guests.
