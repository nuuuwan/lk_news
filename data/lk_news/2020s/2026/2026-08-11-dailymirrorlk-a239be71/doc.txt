Police on alert for underworld retaliation following Maligawatta shooting

Colombo, Aug. 11 (Daily Mirror) - Police are on alert over possible retaliatory underworld activity following yesterday’s fatal shooting on Sangharaja Mawatha in Maligawatta, in which a close relative of underworld figure Kanjipani Imran was shot dead.

According to police sources, the incident is suspected to be a turf war or rivalry-related issue between two opposing underworld factions.

The victim, who succumbed to his injuries after being shot, was identified as a hardware store owner and reportedly the uncle of organised crime figure Mohamed Najim Mohamed Imran, also known as "Kanjipani Imran."

Police Spokesman ASP F.U Wootler said that two special teams had been deployed to arrest the suspects.

With this latest incident, police said a total of 27 shootings have been reported so far this year, resulting in 14 deaths and 14 injuries.

Amidst these heightened tensions, three individuals suspected of involvement in organised crime, including "Ratmalana Saima," a key associate of Kanjipani Imran were brought back to Sri Lanka from Malaysia under a special security operation. The repatriation was executed through close cooperation between Interpol, the Malaysian Police, and a specialised Sri Lankan police team comprising Assistant Superintendent of Police (ASP) Rohan Olugala, Director of the Walana Anti-Corruption Force, and ASP Wasantha Pitiyakumbura of the Legal Division. Upon their arrival, the suspects are expected to be handed over directly to the Criminal Investigation Department (CID) for further legal proceedings and intensive interrogation.

Police said that Saima’s arrest has dealt a significant blow to the alleged organised crime and drug operations connected to Imran in Malaysia. The arrests were the result of a special operation launched several weeks ago after ASP Rohan Olugala and ASP Wasantha Pitiyakumbura travelled to Malaysia to assist in locating and apprehending the suspects.

Working through the Sri Lankan Embassy in Malaysia and with the assistance of Malaysian law enforcement authorities and Interpol, investigators reportedly tracked down and arrested Saima, who was described as one of Imran’s closest associates.
