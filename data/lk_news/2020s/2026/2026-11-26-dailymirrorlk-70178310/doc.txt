CSE slips back towards 21,200 as selling pressure returns

The Colombo Stock Exchange returned to negative territory on 26 November 2026, with both benchmark indices closing lower as early buying interest faded through the session.

The ASPI declined 70.93 points, or 0.33%, to 21,244.91, while the S&P SL20 fell 12.64 points, or 0.21%, to 6,050.18. The ASPI briefly approached 21,400 after the open before selling pressure strengthened, pushing the index below 21,300 and towards the 21,200 region by the close.

Market turnover improved to LKR 2.65 billion, although activity remained below the YTD daily average of LKR 3.43 billion. The Banks sector led turnover at approximately LKR 819.77 million, while HDFC recorded the highest individual counter turnover at LKR 370.90 million.

Crossings accounted for around 39% of total equity turnover, led by JF Packaging with approximately LKR 300.90 million, followed by HDFC, Commercial Bank and HNB non-voting shares.

Market breadth remained firmly negative, with 76 gainers against 131 decliners, resulting in an advance-decline ratio of 0.58. Foreign investors, however, returned to net buying with an inflow of approximately LKR 34.29 million.

Distilleries was the largest negative contributor to the ASPI, followed by Commercial Bank, Access Engineering, LOLC Holdings and Richard Pieris. Janashakthi Insurance provided the strongest positive contribution.

The weak close, negative breadth and high concentration of crossing activity indicate that underlying sentiment remains cautious despite the improvement in headline turnover. The return of modest foreign buying provides some support, while the 21,200 region remains the immediate area to watch for near-term market direction.
