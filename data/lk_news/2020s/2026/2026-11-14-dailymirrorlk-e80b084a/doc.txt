Rotary Club of Ja-Ela - Kandana marks 52nd installation

The Rotary Club of Ja-Ela – Kandana celebrated its 52nd Installation Ceremony on 11 October at Ramada by Wyndham Katunayake, marking another significant milestone in the club’s distinguished history of fellowship, leadership, and community service.

The occasion witnessed the formal installation of Rotarian Malitha Fernando as President for the Rotary year 2026–2027, ushering in a new chapter of leadership dedicated to strengthening community engagement, developing meaningful partnerships, and creating sustainable social impact.

Fernando expressed his gratitude to the club’s membership and acknowledged the invaluable contributions of past leaders who helped establish its enduring legacy.

Under President Fernando’s leadership, the club aims to strengthen its commitment to community development through initiatives focusing on healthcare, education, financial literacy, environmental sustainability, and support for vulnerable communities.
