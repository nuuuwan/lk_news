Motorcycle-only lanes planned on major roads

Colombo, August 4 (Daily Mirror) - The Government is set to introduce dedicated motorcycle lanes on major roads across Sri Lanka as part of a new road safety initiative aimed at reducing traffic fatalities, Minister of Transport, Highways and Urban Development Bimal Rathnayake said.

The first phase of the programme will be launched in Kaluwanchikudy, where a designated lane for motorcycles will be introduced on a trial basis.

Explaining the rationale behind the initiative, the Minister said motorcycles account for a significant proportion of road accidents and fatalities in Sri Lanka. He said that meaningful reductions in the country's road death toll would not be possible without addressing motorcycle safety and riding patterns.

According to the Minister, around 2,500 human lives are lost annually due to road traffic accidents in Sri Lanka, with motorcyclists accounting for the majority of these fatalities.

According to reports from the National Council for Road Safety and the Sri Lanka Police, motorcycle accidents resulted in 1,400 deaths in 2019, 1,250 deaths in 2020, 1,350 deaths in 2021, and 1,009 deaths in 2022. In both 2023 and 2024, more than 1,000 people died each year, while in 2025, total road fatalities across the country reached 2,550. Over the past decade alone, 9,000 citizens in this country have died in motorcycle accidents.

Under the pilot project in Kaluwanchikudy, a dedicated section of the main road will be reserved exclusively for motorcycles. The Minister said the lane will be separated from other traffic using physical barriers and dividers, rather than painted road markings, to improve compliance and enhance the safety of motorcyclists.
