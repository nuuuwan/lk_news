Sun Siyam Group promotes Deepak Booneady to GCEO

Sun Siyam Group, a leading privately-owned hospitality group in the Indian Ocean, has announced the promotion of Deepak Booneady to Group Chief Executive Officer.

Booneady previously served as CEO of Sun Siyam Resorts, where he played a pivotal role in strengthening brand positioning, enhancing guest experiences and advancing strategic initiatives across the group’s portfolio in the Maldives and Sri Lanka.

In his new role, he will oversee strategic growth, long-term brand development and expansion into new concepts and markets, while ensuring alignment with the group’s founding values of authenticity and heartfelt hospitality.

Chairman and Managing Director Ahmed Siyam Mohamed commented: “Deepak has been an important part of our journey, and his leadership has contributed meaningfully to many of the milestones we have achieved together. He leads with strategic clarity, integrity and genuine respect for our people and culture.”

Founded more than three decades ago, Sun Siyam Group operates resorts across its Luxury, Privé and Lifestyle collections, supported by global sales offices in Europe, Asia Pacific, the Middle East and the Americas.
