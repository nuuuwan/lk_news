Medihelp launches 20th branch, enters Southern Province

Medihelp Hospitals opened its 20th branch in Ambalangoda on January 29, marking its first entry into Sri Lanka’s Southern Province. The new facility offers emergency care, OPD, diagnostics, and specialist services, with inpatient care coming soon.

Managing Director of Medihelp Hospitals, Chandika Wijesiriwardana stated that the Ambalangoda branch represents not only the company’s 20th location but also a significant step towards realizing its broader vision of establishing a comprehensive national healthcare network. “Our network aims to provide trusted and accessible medical services to communities throughout Sri Lanka,” he said.
