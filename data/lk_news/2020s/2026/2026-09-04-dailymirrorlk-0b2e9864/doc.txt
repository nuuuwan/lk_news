Police turn to professional designers for new uniform

Colombo, Sep. 4 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Police to work with professional fashion designers to create a new modern uniform as part of efforts to upgrade and modernize the police service, President Anura Kumara Dissanayake said.

The President said the new uniform, expected to be introduced by the middle of next year, will be designed to accommodate modern operational equipment, including body-worn cameras, batons, flashlights and other essential gear.

He said instructions have already been given to the Inspector General of Police to work with professional fashion design firms to develop a uniform that is both contemporary and functional.
