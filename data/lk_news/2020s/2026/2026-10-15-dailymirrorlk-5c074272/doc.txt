Home Lands completes 100% piling work of Central Park Boulevard Port City Colombo ahead of time

Construction of Central Park Boulevard Port City Colombo, Home Lands Group’s ultra-luxury twin-tower development in the coveted Central Park District of Port City Colombo, is progressing well ahead of planned timelines, having already completed 100 percent of piling work in just 88 days since construction began, working day and night.

Three leading contractors, Access Engineering PLC, Sanken Construction’s San Piling (Pvt) Ltd., and D P Jayasinghe Piling Company (Pvt) Ltd., were handpicked by Home Lands to carry out high-quality piling work of this important project of international significance, conforming to the highest standards in record time.

The construction of 192 piles in total was successfully completed with the deployment of 9 piling machines supported by the contractors’ extensive fleets and expertise.

Construction now progresses on to the superstructure building stage, with thedelivery of Central Park Boulevard Port City Colombo expected in 36 months hereon, driven by Home Lands’ unwavering commitment to delivering projects on time upholding its impressive and unparalleled track record of having already delivered over 3,750 units across multiple large-scale developments. With 400 more units ready for delivery this year, Home Lands will surpass the milestone of 4,000 unitsdeliveredby the end of 2026. This will further reinforce the trust placed in Home Lands by its local and international clientele, as the developer will hold the impressively credible record of having delivered 80 percent of its 5,000 plus units sold worldwide. The trust and credulity earned by a Sri Lankan developer by way of Home Lands’ impressive delivery track record will significantly contribute to strengthening and elevating Sri Lanka’s image in international markets as the next global real estate destination.

The 40-acre Central Park comprising the scenic waterway, which seamlessly merges with Central Park Boulevard Port City Colombo is also being constructed. Construction of the park is expected to be completed in 24 months. Central Park Boulevard Port City Colombo will offer the unique combination of Central Park, Waterfront and Indian Ocean views along with the ability to live and experience the 40 acre lush green central parkland andwaterfront lifestyle.

The achievement completing 100 percent of piling work well ahead of time, reinforces the pace at which Central Park Boulevard is taking shape within Port City Colombo, following the project’s earlier construction milestone of 60 percent piling completion in September 2026, when progress was recorded ahead of the planned schedule. With piling now fully completed and constructionprogressing into the next stages of development, the vision of Central Park Boulevard is taking shape and getting closer to reality.

“For prospective buyers and investors, tangible construction progress provides an important measure of confidence” said Nalin Herath, Chairman & Managing Director, Home Lands Group of Companies. “Central Park Boulevard Port City Colombo is more than simply a vision for the future; its development is visibly advancing on site” he further stated.

Located within the prestigious Central Park District of Port City Colombo, Central Park Boulevard is designed as an ultra-luxury development combining elevated architecture, luxury living and nature. The development overlooks 40 acres of Central Park greenery and the scenic waterway, while offering sweeping views of the Indian Ocean and Colombo skyline.The project comprises a diverse collection of residences, including 1, 2 and 3bedroom apartments, Sky Villas, Penthouses and the exclusive Emerald Mansions, complemented by more than 50 international-standard amenities.

Central Park Boulevard represents Home Lands’ first residential development within Port City Colombo, establishing the Group’s presence within South Asia’s international business and lifestyle hub. Building on this momentum, Home Lands is now set to introduce its second residential development within Port City Colombo with a combined inventory of over 1,700 units, making Home Lands the developer of the largest 2 projects done by a single Sri Lankan developer at Port City Colombo. The new project will further expand Home Lands’ presence within the destination, creating another opportunity for buyers to own a premium residence within Sri Lanka’s City of the Future.

Through these developments, Home Lands is also helping make ownership within Port City’s emerging ultra-luxury residential landscape more accessible.
