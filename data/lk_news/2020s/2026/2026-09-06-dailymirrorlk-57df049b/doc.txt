NPP begins islandwide rally series to mark two years in Government

Colombo, Sep. 6 (Daily Mirror) - The National People’s Power (NPP) government is set to mark two years in office with a series of public rallies across the country, beginning in the Kalutara District.

President Anura Kumara Dissanayake is expected to take part in the opening rally, which is being organised under the theme “Our Government – Two Years: Good for the Nation.”

Health Minister Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa said the rallies will be used to outline the government’s work during its first two years in office. He highlighted the government’s efforts to stabilise the economy, tackle corruption and strengthen action against the illegal drug trade.

The first rally is scheduled to be held at the Beruwala Beach Grounds in Kalutara. Further meetings are planned in several districts, including Anuradhapura, Polonnaruwa, Matara and Gampaha, during September.

The government has pointed to the country’s economic recovery following the financial crisis as one of its main achievements. It has also highlighted measures taken against corruption and efforts to improve public institutions.
