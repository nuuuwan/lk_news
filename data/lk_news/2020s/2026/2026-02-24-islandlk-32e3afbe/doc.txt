The Smart Phone Shop unveils Sri Lanka’s largest mobile phone experience center

The Smart Phone Shop, one of Sri Lanka’s most trusted smartphone retailers, officially opened Sri Lanka’s Largest Mobile Phone Experience Center in Maharagama on 14th February 2026, marking a significant milestone in the company’s expansion journey.

Founded in 2012 by entrepreneur . Ashok Wickremasinghe, The Smart Phone Shop has evolved from a modest retail operation into a major player in Sri Lanka’s competitive mobile technology market. Over the past decade, the company has built a strong reputation for offering genuine smartphones at highly competitive prices, backed by transparent transactions, strict quality assurance standards, and reliable warranty coverage

The newly launched Experience Center, located in the high-traffic commercial hub of Maharagama, has been designed to redefine the smartphone purchasing journey. The modern retail space enables customers to explore leading global smartphone brands in an interactive environment, receive professional consultation from trained staff, and access comprehensive after-sales support under one roof. This concept moves beyond conventional retail by prioritising customer engagement, product immersion, and service excellence.

The company’s growth story is closely tied to the entrepreneurial journey of its founder. After completing his advanced level studies, Wickremasinghe spent seven years working in South Korea, saving diligently with the ambition of returning to Sri Lanka to establish his own business. In 2012, he invested his savings to launch The Smart Phone Shop, focusing on authenticity, affordability, and operational efficiency as core pillars of the brand.
