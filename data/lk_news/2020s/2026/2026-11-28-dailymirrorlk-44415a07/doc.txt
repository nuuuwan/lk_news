Over 40 organised crime suspects wanted under Red Notices identified overseas

Colombo, Nov. 28 (Daily Mirror) - A total of 43 organised crime suspects wanted under Interpol Red Notices have been traced to several foreign countries, Public Security and Parliamentary Affairs Minister Ananda Wijepala told Parliament today (28).

According to the Minister, 17 of the suspects are in Dubai, 14 in India, three in Japan, while the remaining nine are residing in nine other countries.

He said necessary measures were being taken to bring all 43 individuals back to Sri Lanka as soon as possible.

Wijepala also revealed that 818 individuals linked to organised crime in Sri Lanka had been arrested over the past two years.

He further disclosed that 233 Interpol Red Notices and 92 Blue Notices had been issued in connection with organised crime suspects believed to be residing overseas.

Meanwhile, 72 organised crime suspects have already been brought back to Sri Lanka from foreign countries, and investigations into their alleged activities are currently underway, the Minister added.
