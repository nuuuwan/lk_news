Government to explore land allocation for young families in Thenuwara

Colombo, Oct. 28 (Daily Mirror) - The government will explore the possibility of allocating suitable land to newly married young families in Thenuwara while addressing land-related concerns raised by residents, Minister of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government Prof. Chandana Abayarathna said.

The Minister made these remarks at the Thenuwara Divisional Council meeting held on October 26, where village representatives highlighted land issues affecting the farming community.

Prof. Abayarathna said a suitable programme would be formulated in consultation with the Divisional Secretary and Grama Niladhari after measures were taken to strengthen the electric fence protecting the village.

The proposed programme would examine the possibility of allocating a portion of suitable land bordering the village to newly married young families, he said.

However, the Minister stressed that land allocation must take into account the need to protect wildlife habitats and preserve the country's forest cover.

He said the government's policy objective was to increase Sri Lanka's forest cover to 30 per cent, adding that development initiatives should be implemented without undermining environmental conservation.

The meeting also focused on the human-elephant conflict affecting residents near wildlife reserves. Villagers said elephants entering settlements had damaged crops and threatened public safety, prompting calls for stronger electric fences and improved wildlife protection measures.

The Minister said the government would seek practical solutions that addressed the needs of rural communities while safeguarding the environment.

Karuwalagaswewa Pradeshiya Sabha Chairman Neil Navarathna, provincial council members, divisional council representatives and residents of Thenuwara attended the meeting.
