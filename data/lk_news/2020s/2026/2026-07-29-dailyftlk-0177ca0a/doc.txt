FLi Super expands islandwide presence with Karandeniya outlet

FLi Super, the supermarket chain of FLi Trading, a strategic business unit of Future Life Holdings, recently opened its newest outlet in Karandeniya.

The new outlet highlights the unique concept by bringing together FLi Super, a dedicated FLiCo electronics corner inside the supermarket, and the group’s flagship microfinance brand, Future Life Investments Ltd. (FLi), under one roof.

The ground floor houses the supermarket, offering a comprehensive range of groceries, fresh produce, household essentials, and everyday necessities alongside quality electrical and home appliances through the FLiCo Corner.

The brand offers an integrated ecosystem, bringing together retail, electronics, and inclusive finance under one roof. Customers can shop for their daily essentials and purchase selected products through exclusive interest-free five-instalment payment schemes, whilst also conveniently accessing FLi’s responsible financial solutions within the same location. This concept reflects the group’s commitment to making everyday living more affordable while creating greater financial inclusion for the communities it serves.

Located on the upper floor, the FLi branch extends the group’s commitment beyond retail by delivering accessible financial solutions to entrepreneurs, self-employed individuals, daily wage earners, salaried employees, and families seeking to improve their livelihoods. Through responsible lending, a culture of investment, and customer-focused financial guidance, FLi continues its mission of promoting financial inclusion and supporting sustainable economic growth.

FLi Trading Director – Operations Hansa Jayawardhana said: “Our vision has always been to create convenient, reliable, and customer-focused experiences wherever we operate. The opening of our 19th supermarket in Karandeniya reflects this commitment while bringing greater convenience and value to the community.”

Future Life Investments Director – Operations Janaka Kumara added: “This integrated concept reflects the Future Life vision of empowering communities beyond conventional retail or finance. Customers can fulfil their daily shopping needs, access flexible payment options, and receive responsible financial solutions under one roof. It is about creating opportunities, supporting entrepreneurs, and helping families build a stronger financial future.”

FLi Trading Head of Human Resources Indika Bandara said: “At FLi Super, we believe a supermarket is not only about the products we offer but the experience we create. Our teams are committed to welcoming every customer with care, understanding their needs, and delivering a service built on trust, convenience, and genuine value.”
