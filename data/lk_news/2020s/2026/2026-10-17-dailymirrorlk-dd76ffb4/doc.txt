Week ends in green

The ASPI closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Dialog Axiata, Distilleries and Ceylon Cold Stores with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.1 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in CIC Holdings, Seylan Bank and Distilleries. Mixed interest was observed in CIC Holdings non-voting, Commercial Bank and Hayleys, whilst retail interest was noted in SMB Leasing non-voting, Softlogic Finance and Waskaduwa Beach Resort. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

During the week, the ASPI and the S&P SL20 lost 0.6% and 0.9% respectively, whilst recording an average daily turnover of Rs. 1.38 Bn.

Materials sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to CIC Holdings nonvoting and CIC Holdings) whilst the sector index gained 1.49%. The share price of CIC Holdings nonvoting closed flat at Rs 22.10. The share price of CIC Holdings moved up by Rs 0.30 (1.15%) to close at Rs 26.40.

The Banking sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Commercial Bank and Seylan Bank ) whilst the sector index increased by 0.71%. The share price of Commercial Bank closed flat at Rs 198.00. The share price of Seylan Bank recorded a loss of Rs 0.50 (0.57%) to close at Rs 87.30.

Hayleys was also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Hayleys edged downRs 0.25 (0.11%) to close at Rs 221.75.
