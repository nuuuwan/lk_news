Health-tech innovator wins top entrepreneurship awards

Dr. Dhanushi Hettiarachchi, a medical doctor and health-tech entrepreneur, achieved a double victory at the prestigious WCIC Prathibhabhisheka Women Entrepreneur Awards 2025 in Colombo.

Her company, Ophtha Innovations (Pvt) Ltd, received the Gold Award in the Startup Category, and she was named Digital Entrepreneur of the Year. The startup focuses on using digital solutions to improve accessibility and efficiency in eye care.

The awards, organized by the Women’s Chamber of Industry & Commerce Sri Lanka (WCIC), recognize women building successful businesses. Dr. Hettiarachchi described the recognition as humbling and inspiring, reinforcing her commitment to developing meaningful healthcare technology and encouraging young women in STEM and entrepreneurship.

Her achievement highlights the growing impact of women-led technology startups in Sri Lanka.
