CSE rebounds as ASPI holds firmly above 22,000

The Colombo Stock Exchange returned to positive territory on 12 November 2026, with both benchmark indices recovering as buying interest strengthened through the session.

The ASPI gained 124.47 points, or 0.56%, to close at 22,157.40, while the S&P SL20 advanced 16.57 points, or 0.26%, to 6,365.57. After slipping towards 22,030 during the morning, the ASPI recovered steadily and approached 22,200 before mild late profit-taking moderated the gains.

Market turnover stood at LKR 2.98 billion, below the YTD daily average of LKR 3.44 billion. The Capital Goods sector led activity, while Dialog Axiata recorded the highest individual counter turnover at approximately LKR 458.87 million.

Crossings accounted for around 11% of total equity turnover, led by Dialog Axiata with approximately LKR 199.60 million, followed by ACL Cables, John Keells Holdings, Lanka Milk Foods and Vallibel One.

Market breadth improved to 133 gainers against 82 decliners, resulting in an advance-decline ratio of 1.62. Foreign investors, however, returned to net selling with an outflow of approximately LKR 111.49 million.

Sri Lanka Telecom was the strongest positive contributor to the ASPI, followed by Distilleries, Dialog Axiata, Tokyo Cement and LOLC Finance. Ceylon Cold Stores recorded the largest negative contribution.

The recovery from the morning lows, positive breadth and the ASPI holding comfortably above 22,000 indicate that short-term sentiment remains constructive. However, moderate turnover and renewed foreign selling suggest some caution, with the 22,200 region emerging as the immediate level to watch.
