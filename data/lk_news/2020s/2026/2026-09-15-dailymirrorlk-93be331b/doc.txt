Sept. 25-Oct. 1 declared National Children’s Day Week

Colombo, Sep. 15 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet has approved the declaration of September 25 to October 1, 2026 as ‘National Children’s Day Week - 2026’ to raise public awareness of children’s rights and strengthen community participation in child protection.

Children’s Day is celebrated annually in Sri Lanka on October 1, with the aim of promoting awareness of children’s rights, education, health and well-being.

The Government has observed that the challenges faced by children have expanded beyond traditional child protection concerns, with children increasingly exposed to risks associated with the Internet and digital technology, psychological and social pressures, neglect and various social and economic challenges.

The Government has also identified the need to improve awareness of children’s rights and protection among parents, teachers, government officials, service providers and members of the public who regularly interact with children.

Accordingly, the National Children’s Day Week will be conducted alongside the National Children’s Day celebration programme, with special programmes planned to increase public awareness, strengthen social sensitivity towards children and encourage greater community involvement in child protection.

The proposal to declare September 25 to October 1 as ‘National Children’s Day Week - 2026’ was submitted by the Minister of Women and Child Affairs and subsequently approved by the Cabinet.
