Child protection system to be strengthened through new laws, policies

Colombo, Oct. 1 (Daily Mirror) - The Government is taking steps to strengthen Sri Lanka’s child protection system by integrating relevant institutions and introducing necessary policies and laws, Deputy Minister of Women and Child Affairs Dr. Namal Sudarshana said.

In a message issued to mark World Children’s Day, the Deputy Minister said the Government’s primary objective was to protect children from all forms of abuse and violence while safeguarding their rights.

He said a stronger child protection mechanism was being developed by coordinating various institutions involved in preventing abuse and violence against children.

“We are taking the necessary legal steps to further strengthen these measures,” he said.

The Deputy Minister said the Government was translating its decisions into policies and laws aimed at protecting children and ensuring their rights.

However, he stressed that the objective should not simply be to increase the number of laws or programmes relating to children.

“The aim is to create a country where a child feels safe, a society where they feel their dreams have value, and a world where they can believe that opportunities exist for them, regardless of who they are,” he said.

He said providing children with quality education and proper nutrition from early childhood was also an important investment in their future.

Among the measures highlighted were the introduction of a National Early Childhood Development Curriculum Framework for preschools and increasing the allowance for children’s morning meals.

The Deputy Minister said children growing up free from fear, with the freedom to dream and the support of society when they face difficulties, would be better prepared to shape their own futures.

“Every step we take to uphold a child's rights is a step taken for the future of humanity,” he said.

He added that the Government’s vision for children’s safety, rights, education, nutrition and dignity was one that should transcend national borders.

“As I extend my Children's Day greetings, I declare that our sole vision is to transform society—with adults at the forefront—to create a bright future filled with love, safety, respect, and hope for every child in the world,” Dr. Sudarshana said.
