Rs. 32.5 billion Kandy transport terminal 55% complete

Colombo, Nov. 9 (Daily Mirror) - The construction of the Rs. 32.5 billion Kandy Multimodal Transport Terminal (KMTT) has reached 55% completion, with the Government expecting to open the facility to the public before the end of July 2027.

Deputy Minister of Transport and Highways Dr. Prasanna Gunasena inspected the project on November 7 to review its progress and discuss challenges affecting its implementation.

Funded through World Bank assistance and domestic resources, the project is aimed at modernising Kandy's public transport system while reducing traffic congestion in the city.

The terminal, which will cover around 10 acres, is designed to provide convenient connections between bus and railway services. It will also include facilities for persons with disabilities, modern transport management systems, CCTV security, pedestrian walkways and road interchange facilities.

During the inspection, the Deputy Minister discussed implementation-related challenges with officials and examined the coordination and interventions needed to resolve them.

Meanwhile, plans are also underway to establish three additional transport terminals in Katugastota, Thennekumbura and Peradeniya.

The wider development programme includes improving pedestrian facilities within Kandy city, constructing a new overpass across the Kandy-Matale railway line and upgrading the city's internal road network.

The Government expects the project to play a major role in improving public transport, easing traffic congestion and providing safer and more efficient transport facilities for commuters in Kandy.
