‘What kind of thinking is this?’ President questions pin-sticking protests

President Anura Kumara Dissanayake has questioned the thinking behind certain opposition groups resorting to unusual protest methods, including inserting pins into clay figures.

Addressing the National Convention of the National Intellectuals Organization (NIO) on Saturday (31), the President said groups that had benefited from the previous political culture were struggling to accept the changes introduced by the National People’s Power (NPP) government.

He accused these groups of responding to the government’s reforms with hateful statements, fabricated claims and what he described as bizarre protest activities.

Referring to the practice of inserting pins into clay figures, President Dissanayake questioned how anyone could believe that harming a clay figure would cause pain to another person.

He criticised such actions, questioning the mindset behind attempts to use these methods as a form of political protest.
