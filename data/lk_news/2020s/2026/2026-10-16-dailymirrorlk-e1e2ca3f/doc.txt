Over 300 Rajarata University students to receive free spectacles

Colombo, Oct. 16 (Daily Mirror) - A total of 316 students at Rajarata University will receive free spectacles tomorrow (17) under a programme implemented by the President’s Fund to provide vision care to university students.

The initiative aims to provide spectacles to all university students who require them, with Rajarata University selected for the first phase of the programme.

Eye examinations for students at the university were conducted on September 19, while the second phase of the programme was carried out at the University of Ruhuna on October 10.

Implemented with the support of the Stroud Rotary Club in the United Kingdom and the Wish Foundation, the programme will provide spectacles to the 316 Rajarata University students who underwent eye examinations.

The spectacles will be presented to the students at the university tomorrow, according to the President’s Media Division.
