SJB MP questions Namal Rajapaksa’s continued remand, cites Bail Act

Colombo, Nov 21 ( Daily Mirror): SJB MP Chithral Fernando has questioned the continued remand of Opposition MP Namal Rajapaksa, arguing that the Bail Act No. 30 of 1997 establishes the granting of bail as the rule and its refusal as the exception.

Speaking during a parliamentary debate, Fernando said he was raising concerns about the matter in his capacity as a lawyer, despite Rajapaksa not belonging to his political camp.

“Today, Namal Rajapaksa is not a politician from my political camp. However, as lawyers, we are beginning to feel concerned about what is happening,” he said.

Referring to the Bail Act, Fernando stressed that the legislation was based on the principle that bail should ordinarily be granted, while refusal should be treated as an exception.

He said he respected the independence of the judiciary and acknowledged that Parliament could not use its privileges to challenge court decisions on the guilt or innocence of individuals.

However, he questioned the prolonged detention of Rajapaksa and the circumstances surrounding the refusal of bail, saying that concerns over the application of established legal principles should not be ignored.

Fernando warned that the issue should not be viewed solely through political affiliations, noting that similar circumstances could affect members of other political groups in the future.

He also stressed that lawyers had a responsibility to speak out when they believed that legal provisions governing personal liberty were not being applied appropriately.

“This is not about interfering with the independence of the judiciary or questioning the authority of courts, but about ensuring that the principles established under the Bail Act are respected,” he said.
