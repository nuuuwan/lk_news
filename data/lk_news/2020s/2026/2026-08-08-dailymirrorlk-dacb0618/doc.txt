Lanka Sathosa reduces prices of 10 essential food items

Colombo, August 8 (Daily Mirror) - Lanka Sathosa has announced a reduction in the prices of 10 essential food items with effect from today.

The decision was taken following instructions issued by the Ministry of Trade, Commerce, Food Security and Cooperative Development, the state-owned retail chain said.

Accordingly, consumers will be able to purchase the selected essential items at reduced prices from all Lanka Sathosa outlets islandwide.

The official announcement containing details of the revised prices has also been issued.
