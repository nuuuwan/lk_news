Sri Lanka to take  high-tech industries and SMEs global in partnership with German biz

The Government is working to expand cooperation with Germany and other international partners in advanced manufacturing, investment and trade, with the aim of elevating Sri Lanka’s industrial sector to the international stage, Deputy Minister of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development Chathuranga Abeysinghe stated.

He made these remarks while attending the “Top German Brands 2026 – The Ball Edition” event, organised in Colombo by the German Chamber of Commerce and Industry in Sri Lanka (AHK Sri Lanka) and the German Embassy, as the Chief Guest.

The Deputy Minister emphasised that Sri Lanka has achieved economic stability following the economic challenges faced over the past few years, and that the Government’s objective is to implement reforms that will accelerate economic growth in the years ahead.

He also highlighted the importance of strengthening links with international markets to expand Sri Lanka’s export economy, while progressively removing existing trade barriers by 2029 and creating opportunities for new trade agreements.

He noted that cooperation with Germany would be particularly important in transforming Sri Lanka’s manufacturing sector from industries primarily based on low labour costs towards advanced technology and high value-added production. In this regard, the Government is focusing on infrastructure development, expanding port and airport capacity, and strengthening vocational skills.

The Deputy Minister further revealed that the Ministry is implementing a program to prepare between 300 and 400 Sri Lankan small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) to access global markets. He stated that the Government expects the support of the German business community and other international partners in providing these enterprises with access to technology, financial management expertise and international markets.

He also emphasised the need to further strengthen public-private partnerships to develop vocational skills among young people and address the human resource requirements of the industrial sector.

The event was attended by German Ambassador Dr. Felix Neumann, AHK Sri Lanka Head Martin Klose, Sri Lanka Ports Authority Chairman Dr. Parakrama Dissanayake along with diplomats, German and Sri Lankan business leaders and policymakers.
