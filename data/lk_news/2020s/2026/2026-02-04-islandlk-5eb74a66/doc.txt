Phew! The heat …

Hi! The awful heat, during the day, continues to worry most of us – if not all – especially where our skin is concerned, and so I thought of giving my readers a little bit of advice on how to overcome whatever problems that may crop up.

These are some essentials that will help you avoid having problems with your skin.

After showering, gently pat skin dry and immediately apply a moisturiser, or oil, to lock in water.

Switch to lighter, gel-based, or water-based moisturisers that contain glycerin or hyaluronic acid, as heavy creams may feel too greasy in the heat.

Keep a rosewater, or homemade DIY (do it yourself) mist in the fridge and spray it on your face throughout the day for an instant boost.

Use lukewarm or cool water for showers, as hot water strips natural oils.

Drink plenty of water (08–10 glasses) and consume hydrating foods like cucumber, watermelon, and coconut water.

Apply a broad-spectrum sunscreen with SPF 30 or higher, as sun damage exacerbates dryness.

Use a mild, natural scrub (like coffee or oats) once a week to remove dead, flaky skin, allowing moisture to penetrate better.

Run a humidifier at night, especially if you spend long hours in air-conditioned rooms, which drain moisture from the air.
