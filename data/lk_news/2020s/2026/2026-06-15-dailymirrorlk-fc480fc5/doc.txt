Sri Lankan Rupee strengthens against US Dollar

Colombo, June 15 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lankan Rupee strengthened against the US Dollar today (15) after several days of depreciation, according to the latest exchange rates released by the Central Bank of Sri Lanka.

The Central Bank said the buying rate of the US Dollar stood at Rs. 326.34, while the selling rate was Rs. 336.58 today.

On Friday (12), the buying rate of the US Dollar was recorded at Rs. 331.42, while the selling rate stood at Rs. 341.36.
