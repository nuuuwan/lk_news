Meghan Markle’s father arrested by the FBI for possession of child pornography

Daily Mail - Thomas Markle, Sr., the estranged father of Meghan, Duchess of Sussex, has reportedly been arrested by the Federal Bureau of Investigation, according to the Daily Mail.

Markle was arrested at Maravida Senior Living, a specialized elderly care facility in North Lakeland, Florida, over his alleged possession of child pornography, the Daily Mail reported Friday, Nov. 6.

FBI agents stormed into his home after allegedly discovering "troves of indecent materials" at properties associated with Markle in the Philippines and Mexico.

The arrest comes after police in the Philippines launched an investigation into the 82-year-old, who allegedly kept a stash of child sex images inside his home in Cebu. Meghan's half-brother, Thomas Markle Jr., reportedly discovered several boxes of material and passed them to authorities.

Markle has denied the allegations, calling the claims "sick lies." In an interview with the Daily Mail, he admitted to paying for sex in the Philippines but said he had never been intimate with anyone underage.
