Travelco Holidays strengthens regional presence and opens branch in Kandy

Travelco Holidays, a prominent provider of comprehensive travel solutions in Sri Lanka, has announced the opening of its newest branch in Kandy, marking a strategic expansion into the Central Province. The new facility, located at Peradeniya Road, represents a significant milestone in the company’s mission to make world-class travel experiences accessible beyond the capital city.

The expansion brings Travelco’s full suite of travel services to residents of the Hill Capital and surrounding areas. The company offers customized international tour packages to destinations in Asia, Africa, Europe, Australia and Americas alongside air ticketing, visa consultancy, honeymoon packages, family getaways, corporate incentive tours, and educational travel programs. Known for its end-to-end support model, Travelco maintains a presence throughout the entire travel journey, from initial booking to safe return home.

In conjunction with the expansion, Travelco has launched an innovative partnership with Hameedia, Sri Lanka’s leading menswear specialist. The collaboration features a special raffle draw that bridges premium lifestyle brands with global travel experiences, reflecting Travelco’s vision of travel as an essential component of contemporary living rather than merely a transactional service.

“Opening our Kandy branch is about bringing expert travel consultancy closer to communities across the Central Province,” said Zanfer Sheriff, Director of Travelco Holidays. “We’ve seen strong demand from travelers outside Colombo who want the same personalized service and seamless experiences. This expansion allows us to serve them better while staying true to our commitment to making world-class travel accessible and hassle-free for everyone.”
