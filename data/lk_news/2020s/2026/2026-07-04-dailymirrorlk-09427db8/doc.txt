Rhino Roofing concludes 2025–2026 Dealer Convention & Awards Ceremony

Rhino Roofing hosted its Dealer Convention and Awards Ceremony 2025–2026 at the ITC Ratnadipa, Colombo, bringing together a large gathering of dealer partners representing various regions across Sri Lanka.

The event marked another significant milestone in Rhino Roofing’s continued growth journey, serving as a platform to recognize and appreciate the outstanding achievements of its dealer network over the past year. Special awards were presented to high-performing dealers in recognition of their exceptional contribution to the brand’s success.

In addition, the convention focused on strengthening the company’s dealer network, identifying new market opportunities, and sharing strategic insights to support future growth and expansion. The gathering also provided an important opportunity for dialogue between management and dealer partners, encouraging collaboration and exchanging ideas for future development.

Held under the theme “United in Strength,” the event highlighted the importance of unity, trust, and collaboration in building strong and sustainable business relationships. The theme reinforced Rhino Roofing’s commitment to growing together with its dealer partners through shared success and mutual support.

Rhino Roofing continued to emphasize its commitment to working closely with its dealer network to further strengthen its position as a leading player in Sri Lanka’s construction and roofing industry, while delivering high-quality and reliable roofing and building  solutions across the country.
