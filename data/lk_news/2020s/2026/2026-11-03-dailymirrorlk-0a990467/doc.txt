CSE returns to positive territory as ASPI holds above 20,000

The Colombo Stock Exchange returned to positive territory on 3 November 2026, with both benchmark indices closing higher despite a volatile session.

The ASPI gained 40.09 points, or 0.20%, to 20,119.64, while the S&P SL20 advanced 20.71 points, or 0.36%, to 5,722.57. The ASPI dipped below 20,100 during early trading before recovering above 20,150 in the afternoon, although some late profit-taking moderated the gains.

Market turnover improved to LKR 1.52 billion, with 61.85 million shares traded. PickMe led individual counter activity with approximately LKR 245.77 million in turnover, followed by Overseas Realty and Nations Trust Bank.

Crossings accounted for around 14% of total equity turnover, led by Overseas Realty with approximately LKR 182.20 million, while John Keells Holdings recorded a LKR 36.59 million block.

Market breadth improved, with 120 gainers against 76 decliners, producing an advance-decline ratio of 1.58. Foreign investors, however, returned to significant net selling with an outflow of approximately LKR 242.48 million.

Dialog Axiata was the strongest positive contributor to the ASPI, followed by CIC Holdings, MAL.N, SMB Finance and Overseas Realty. Carson Cumberbatch recorded the largest negative contribution.

The positive close and stronger breadth indicate some improvement in short-term sentiment, while the ASPI continues to hold above the 20,000 level. However, the sizeable foreign outflow and late-session easing suggest that sustained buying will be needed for the market to build momentum towards the 20,200 to 20,300 region.
