Namal’s Airbus case to be taken up before Fort Magistrate’s Court

Colombo, Sep. 30 (Daily Mirror) - The Criminal Investigation Department has filed a case under the provisions of the Prevention of Money Laundering Act against Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa in connection with the alleged Airbus deal.

The case is scheduled to be taken up before the Colombo Fort Magistrate’s Court today.
