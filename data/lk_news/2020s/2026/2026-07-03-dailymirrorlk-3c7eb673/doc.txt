Court puts off hearing of Rakitha Rajapaksa’s case until 2 pm

The case against Attorney-at-Law Rakitha Nirmala Rajapaksa, Samagi Jana Balawegaya Horana organiser Charith Wasantha Kumara Abeysinghe and former Airport Director of the Civil Aviation Authority Aruna Sri Chathuranga, who were arrested by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) over the alleged solicitation and acceptance of a Rs. 120 million bribe from the wife of an alleged organised crime figure in exchange for providing him with favours, was today postponed until 2.00 p.m by Colombo Chief Magistrate Asanga S. Bodaragama.

Senior Counsel Ashoka Weerasuriya, appearing for the first suspect Charith Abeysinghe, informed court that he was engaged in proceedings before a superior court and requested that the matter be taken up later. The request was made through counsel Sanjaya Marambe.
