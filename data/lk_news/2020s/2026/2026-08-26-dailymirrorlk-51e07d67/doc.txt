13 motorcycles handed over to wildlife officers in Trincomalee

Colombo, Aug. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Thirteen motorcycles have been handed over to field officers of the Department of Wildlife Conservation in the Trincomalee District to strengthen wildlife protection efforts, curb forest-related offences and improve the response to the growing human-elephant conflict.

The motorcycles were officially handed over yesterday (25) at the Trincomalee Regional Wildlife Office under the patronage of Deputy Minister of Environment Anton Jayakody.

Trincomalee District MP Roshan Akmeemana, the District Secretary, officials of the Department of Wildlife Conservation and Ministry of Environment, and other state officials attended the ceremony.

The initiative is part of a broader master plan to strengthen wildlife protection and improve field-level operations in the district.

The additional mobility is expected to enable wildlife officers to respond more quickly to public complaints and take effective action against illegal logging, deforestation and other unauthorized activities in forest and wildlife areas.

Officials said the distribution of the 13 motorcycles is a phased measure to address transportation and infrastructure difficulties faced by field officers.

Further measures are planned to strengthen the district's electric fence network and provide additional vehicles and mechanical equipment to support wildlife protection and human-elephant conflict mitigation.

The Ministry of Environment and its affiliated institutions are also working towards strengthening conservation efforts and promoting sustainable coexistence between communities and elephants.
