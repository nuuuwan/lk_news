Philatelic Bureau honours Irangani Serasinghe to mark 100 years of Sri Lankan cinema

Marking a historic milestone in the nation’s cultural calendar, the Philatelic Bureau of the Department of Posts on Wednesday (21) issued a commemorative postage stamp and First Day Cover to celebrate “1925–2025: A Century of Sri Lankan Cinema,” honouring veteran actress and living legend Irangani Serasinghe for her unparalleled contribution to the art of film.

Featuring Serasinghe’s portrait, the special issue pays tribute to a career spanning more than six decades, during which she emerged as one of Sri Lanka’s most respected and influential performers, shaping the artistic depth and moral compass of national cinema.

The official ceremony was held at the Tharangani Theatre under the patronage of Minister Dr. Hiniduma Sunil Senevi, with senior officials from the Department of Posts, the National Film Corporation and leading figures of the film fraternity in attendance.

Serasinghe’s body of work across cinema, theatre and television has earned her widespread critical acclaim and numerous national honours, including Presidential Awards and lifetime achievement recognitions, affirming her status as a cultural icon whose influence spans generations.

Coinciding with the stamp launch, the National Film Corporation’s official website was re-launched, reflecting efforts to modernise access to Sri Lanka’s cinematic heritage and institutional services.

Delivering a special address, Dr. Sunil Wijesiriwardena traced the evolution of Sri Lankan cinema over the past century, observing that the choice of Irangani Serasinghe for the commemorative stamp symbolised artistic excellence, discipline and enduring relevance.

Officials said the stamp and First Day Cover were issued not only for philatelic collectors, but as a permanent national tribute to an industry that has shaped Sri Lanka’s cultural identity for a hundred years.

The commemorative stamp and First Day Cover are available through the Philatelic Bureau and selected post offices countrywide.
