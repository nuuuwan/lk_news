Verdict due on Hemasiri, Pujith over Easter Sunday attacks

Colombo, July 31 (Daily Mirror) - The Colombo Permanent High Court Trial-at-Bar is scheduled to deliver its verdict today (July 31) in the case against former Defence Secretary Hemasiri Fernando and former Inspector General of Police (IGP) Pujith Jayasundara.

The two former senior officials are facing charges, including criminal dereliction of duty, over allegations that they failed to act despite receiving prior intelligence warnings about the impending Easter Sunday terrorist attacks and did not take adequate measures to prevent the tragedy.

The case was instituted by the Attorney General and has been heard before a three-judge bench of the Colombo Permanent High Court Trial-at-Bar.

The trial concluded recently after the prosecution and defence completed their submissions, paving the way for today's highly anticipated judgment.
