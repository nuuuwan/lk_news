HNB Legacy Open returns for third edition

HNB PLC’s Private Banking division will host its third edition of the HNB Legacy Open at the Nuwara Eliya Golf Club on 21st and 22nd August, with InQube Global PVT LTD confirmed as the tournament’s Official Performance Apparel Partner. Now in its third consecutive year, the HNB Legacy Open has established itself as a signature annual fixture for the bank’s Private Banking clients, combining competitive golf with the curated hospitality that has defined the event since its inaugural edition in 2024.

The tournament was launched following HNB’s long-standing association with the Millennium Trophy at the Royal Colombo Golf Club, marking the bank’s move toward independently hosting a Private Banking event of this scale. Each edition has built on the last, and the 2026 edition introduces InQube’s performance apparel expertise onto the course for the first time, with the company designing and supplying performance wear for the participants.

“HNB Private Banking is built on the quality of the relationships we keep, and the Legacy Open is an extension of that philosophy. The partners we bring into this tournament say something about the standard we hold ourselves to. InQube has built a globally credible performance apparel operation from Sri Lanka, and that is exactly the kind of partner we want alongside us in this space.”
