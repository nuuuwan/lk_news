Bangladesh batter explains emotional century celebration

NOV.17 - Litton Das explains his celebration against South Africa as Marques Ackerman enjoys his own milestone moment.

Bangladesh veteran Litton Das has revealed the reasons behind his unique celebration when scoring his eighth Test century against South Africa in Johannesburg on Monday.

Das pulled a note out of his pocket when he brought up his century on the second day of the first ICC World Test Championship fixture against the Proteas at the Wanderers, with the right-hander dedicating his innings to his daughter Anayra who has just turned three.

The note read: 'Happy Birthday my Princess. This 100 for you', with Das providing more insight into his message at stumps on the second day after South Africa reached 287/4 at the close in reply to Bangladesh's first innings total of 400.

Das revealed he had a Bangladesh staff member write the message on the morning of the second day when he was on 92*, with the 32-year-old confident he was going to complete his milestone on Monday given he only needed eight more runs to reach triple figures.

"I miss her," Das said in relation to his note for his daughter Anayra.

"Whenever you think of the future, you will think about positive things. I definitely thought last night that I would do that and I did."

There was a century of a different kind later in the day in Johannesburg when South Africa opener Marques Ackerman brought up his maiden hundred on his way to a brilliant innings of 157 that helped swing the momentum back into the favour of the hosts.

Ackerman was asked if he considered a similar celebration to the one that Das performed earlier in the day, but the left hander kept things a bit more simple as he acknowledged the role of his family and his teammates as he went that little bit better then the superb 95 he stroked against Australia last month in what was just his second Test appearance.

"No, nothing fancy like that," Ackerman said when asked if he had similar celebration plans to Das.

"The last time I was in the press I said that it (innings of 95 against Australia) was motivation to try and get it the next time, so it was a big relief and a lot of emotion came out.

"It was a special feeling, especially that the family was also here."

While Ackerman's ton helped the Proteas work their way back into the contest, Das believes Bangladesh is still well placed for victory and a strong chance to keep their hopes alive of reaching next year's World Test Championship Final at The Oval.

"The game plan is quite simple now. We have to take wickets, bowl them out. We can't allow them a big lead," Das added.

“With the current playing conditions, I don't think we have too many runs on the board. 100-120. But still, if we can come early tomorrow and take two or three wickets, the scenario will be different,” said Litton. “But if we can't, then we will have to work a bit harder, and the game should go on a bit longer, and we shouldn't leak runs too quickly. That will be our target." - ICC
