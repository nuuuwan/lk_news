IQPM appoints Executive Council for 2026

The Institute of Quality and Productivity Management (IQPM) held its Extraordinary General Meeting on 20 January 2026 at the OPA to appoint its Executive Council for 2026. Standing from left: Indika Perera (Attorney-at-Law), Suranjith Swaris, Traver Wanniachy (Attorney-at-Law /Asst. Secretary), Punya De Silva, K. A. Susiru Lakdham, and Dr. Nimalashanthi Amarasekera. Seated from left: Priyantha Wickramarathna (Editor), Nishadi Rajapakshe (Secretary), Dr. Samantha Rathnayake (President-Elect), Upali Gamakumara (President), Priyangani Jayasuriya (Vice President), Jayanath Abeywardena (Treasurer), and Kasun Fernando (Asst. Treasurer).
