Terrorism Bill clauses inconsistent with Constitution

Colombo, Nov. 12 (Daily Mirror) - Some clauses of the Protection of the State from Terrorism Bill have been found to be inconsistent with the Constitution, Speaker Jagath Wickramaratne announced in Parliament today.

The Bill was challenged before the Supreme Court under Article 121(1) of the Constitution.

The Supreme Court has determined that the Bill can be passed by a simple majority in Parliament, provided that the necessary amendments are made at the Committee Stage.

The Court has specified several amendments, three of which have already been undertaken by the Additional Solicitor General.
