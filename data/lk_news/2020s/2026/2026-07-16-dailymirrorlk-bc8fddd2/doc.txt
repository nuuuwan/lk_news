Ceylon Chamber to host BCCS 2026 launch seminar on ESG Reporting and Strategy

Following the launch of the revamped Best Corporate Citizen Sustainability Awards (BCCS) 2026, the Ceylon Chamber of Commerce will host a launch seminar on 30 July at 2.00 p.m. at the Ceylon Chamber Auditorium, providing businesses with insights into the updated evaluation framework and submission requirements.

The seminar will help participants understand the key areas assessed under the new BCCS framework, including stakeholder engagement, materiality assessment, ESG strategy, measurable targets, performance tracking and long-term sustainability planning.

The seminar will also explain the approach taken to assess areas such as impact and financial materiality, the identification of key sustainability priorities, the setting of targets and the demonstration of progress over time.
