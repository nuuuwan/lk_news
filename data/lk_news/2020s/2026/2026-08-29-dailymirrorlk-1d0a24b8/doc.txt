Is a referendum on the cards? Opposition MP questions President’s rally plans

Colombo, August 29 (Daily Mirror) - Opposition MP Dayasiri Jayasekera has questioned the decision by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake to hold a series of rallies from September 5, raising speculation over whether a referendum could be on the cards in relation to the 22nd Amendment.

“We question why this series of rallies is being held. Perhaps the President is fully aware of the situation regarding the 22nd Amendment,” Jayasekera told journalists.

He also said the President could consider appointing several judges to fill the existing vacancies in the Supreme Court.
