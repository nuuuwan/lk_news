Balangoda Kassapa Thero granted bail following apology to court

Colombo, Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - Balangoda Kassapa Thero, who was remanded over his failure to appear in court in connection with the construction of a shrine room and the installation of a Buddha statue at the Sri Sambuddha Jayanthi Bodhiraja Temple premises in Trincomalee, was released on bail today (November 17).

The Trincomalee Magistrate's Court ordered his release after the Thero, through his attorney, tendered an apology for violating his previous conditional bail and making derogatory remarks against the Trincomalee Magistrate's Court and a judge in Anuradhapura.

The Thero had been remanded yesterday (November 16) after being produced before the court over his failure to appear at a previous hearing in the case.

The court had issued an arrest warrant after he failed to appear when the case was taken up on November 11.
