MullenLowe Sri Lanka appoints Harendra Uyanage as Chief Creative Officer

MullenLowe Group Sri Lanka has elevated Harendra Uyanage to Chief Creative Officer, reinforcing its creative leadership.

Uyanage, a widely decorated figure with over two decades of experience, is recognised for strategic creativity and filmmaking that drives commercial impact. His leadership has been pivotal in securing multiple Effie awards and steering brand revivals into long-term successes.

“Harendra’s journey exemplifies perseverance and uncompromising creative conviction,” said Thayalan Bartlett, Executive Chairman. “He has been central to building our reputation and inspiring talent.”

Uyanage, the first recipient of the industry’s Next Generation Leader Scholarship, stated his role carries both responsibility and purpose. “I look forward to mentoring the next generation and ensuring Sri Lanka is recognised for leadership in the global creative landscape.”

In his new role, he will lead the creative vision for Sri Lanka’s largest integrated brand communications company.
