Defence Ministry expenditure head approved by Parliament

Colombo, Nov 28 (Daily Mirror) - Parliament approved the Defence Ministry's expenditure head by a majority of 81 votes, with 85 MPs voting in favour and four against.

As many as 139 MPs, including members of the ruling party and the Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB), New Democratic Front (NDF), Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) and Illankai Tamil Arasu Kachchi (ITAK), were absent at the time of voting.
