CSE consolidates above 22,000 after six-session rally

The Colombo Stock Exchange ended its six-session winning streak on 11 November 2026, with both benchmark indices closing lower as profit-taking emerged following the recent sharp rally.

The ASPI declined 90.46 points, or 0.41%, to 22,032.93, while the S&P SL20 fell 19.12 points, or 0.30%, to 6,349.00. Despite the pullback, the ASPI remained above the key 22,000 psychological level.

Market turnover stood at LKR 4.82 billion, remaining above the YTD daily average of LKR 3.45 billion. The Banks sector led activity with approximately LKR 1.18 billion in turnover, while Dipped Products recorded the highest individual counter turnover at LKR 616.7 million.

Crossings accounted for around 9% of total turnover, led by Dipped Products with approximately LKR 91.2 million, followed by Hatton National Bank and DFCC Bank.

Market breadth turned firmly negative, with 83 gainers against 163 decliners, resulting in an advance-decline ratio of 0.51. Foreign investors, however, returned to net buying with an inflow of approximately LKR 46.4 million.

Hatton National Bank was the largest negative contributor to the ASPI, followed by Overseas Realty, Commercial Bank, Hayleys and Carson Cumberbatch. Dialog Axiata provided the strongest positive contribution.

The first negative close after six consecutive gains suggests the market entered a short-term consolidation phase following the recent rally. However, turnover remaining above the YTD average, renewed foreign buying and the ASPI holding above 22,000 provide some support, making this level the key near-term area to watch.
