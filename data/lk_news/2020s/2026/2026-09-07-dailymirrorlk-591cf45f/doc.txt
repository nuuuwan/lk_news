SJB seniors participate in UNP anniversary celebration

Colombo, Sep. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) seniors including its General Secretary Ranjith Madduma Bandara participated in a ceremony held to mark the 80th anniversary of the United National Party (UNP) yesterday indicating that there would be a political marriage between the two parties in the future.

MP Madduma Bandara told media that he and other seniors of the SJB participated in the UNP anniversary celebration on behalf of SJB Leader Sajith Premadasa. "Our leader could not participate in the event as he was busy," he said.

MP Harsha de Silva said he was happy to participate in the event at Sirikotha as the UNP is his mother party. "The UNP is our mother party. We came into politics from the UNP. Therefore, we are happy to participate in this event" he told media.

"The grassroots level members of both the UNP and the SJB have already joined together. It is the two leaders who should draw up a mechanism on how they could work together" he added.

UNP General Secretary Talatha Athukorala said the marriage between the two parties will take place soon. "Both parties will come together at the right time," she said.

MP Ravi Karunanayake said it is time that all opposition parties came together. "My mission is to bring all political parties together for the sake of the nation," he said.
