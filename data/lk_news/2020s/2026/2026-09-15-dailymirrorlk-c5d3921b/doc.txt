Rs. 2.5 bn additional funds for Paddy Marketing Board

Colombo, Sep. 15 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet has approved the allocation of an additional Rs. 2.5 billion to the Paddy Marketing Board (PMB) to continue purchasing paddy harvested during the 2026 Yala season.

The PMB has already been provided with Rs. 6 billion from allocations made to the Ministry of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation to implement the Yala season paddy purchasing programme.

However, the paddy harvest in the districts of Anuradhapura, Polonnaruwa, Puttalam, Mannar and Mullaitivu, as well as in the Mahaweli B, C and H zones, has not yet been completed.

The Government has observed that the funds currently allocated for paddy purchases in these areas are insufficient to meet the remaining requirement.

Accordingly, Cabinet approved a proposal by the Minister of Agriculture, Livestock, Lands and Irrigation to provide a further Rs. 2.5 billion to the PMB on a reimbursement basis.

The funds will be drawn from an additional Rs. 7.5 billion allocated to the Ministry to implement the 2026 Yala season paddy procurement programme.
