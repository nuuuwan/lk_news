Central Industries shines at National Business Excellence Awards 2026

Central Industries PLC has once again proven its industry dominance by winning an award at the National Business Excellence 2026 Awards.

The company received, the joint Runners-up award in the Construction Sector.

In addition to its award-winning performance, Central Industries PLC was the first to receive the SLS 147 and SLS 659 standard certificates for uPVC water-pipe production and uPVC accessories production, respectively.

Central Industries PLC’s diverse product portfolio includes solvent cement, rainwater management system, electrical conduits, PE water-pipes and fittings, PE water tanks, stainless steel water tanks, garden hoses, National PVC compact ball-valves, cable trunking, Greenwood brand valance boards, and Krypton electrical switches, sockets, and accessories. These products continue to meet high demand in the market, reinforcing the company’s reputation.

Operating under the theme ‘The first choice for quality and reliability,’ Central Industries PLC has been serving the nation for over four decades. Consumer segments in Sri Lanka trust Central Industries PLC’s brands for their commitment and service.
