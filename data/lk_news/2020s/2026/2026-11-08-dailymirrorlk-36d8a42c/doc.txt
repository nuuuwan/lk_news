Special dengue prevention week from tomorrow in 74 MOH divisions

The Health Ministry has declared a special National Dengue Prevention Week from November 9 to 14, covering 74 high-risk Medical Officer of Health (MOH) divisions across the country.

The programme will involve the National Dengue Control Unit, the Ministry of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government, the Clean Sri Lanka Secretariat, Sri Lanka Police, the Tri-Forces and voluntary organisations.

The initiative aims to prevent a possible dengue outbreak amid changing weather conditions, rising dengue risk indicators and trends observed during previous outbreaks, the Health Ministry said.

During the six-day programme, joint inspection teams will be deployed to identify and destroy mosquito breeding sites, particularly in unoccupied premises. Special cleaning programmes will also be conducted at schools, Pirivenas, universities and religious places.

The activities will be coordinated through relevant MOH offices, while a dedicated operations room will monitor the progress of the programme daily.

Acting Director of the National Dengue Control Unit Dr. Kapila Kannangara said the unit was continuously monitoring mosquito-related indicators and reported dengue cases to prevent a possible outbreak.

The special dengue prevention week was initially scheduled for November 2 to 7 but was postponed to November 9 to 14 due to adverse weather conditions, the ministry said.
