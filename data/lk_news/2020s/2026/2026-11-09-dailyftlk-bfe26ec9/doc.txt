Police search Panadura hotel over suspected online fraud, seize 23 computers

Police searched a hotel in the Nalloruwa area of Panadura on 6 and 7 November after information that a group of Chinese nationals staying there was involved in suspected online financial fraud and illegal business activity, and seized 23 computers and accessories.

The operation by the Panadura Divisional Crime Investigation Unit followed information received by the Senior Superintendent of Police in charge of the Panadura Division, and was carried out on a warrant obtained from the Panadura Magistrate’s Court.

Officers found 32 Chinese nationals at the hotel. The computers were taken to the Panadura South Police Station for examination to establish whether offences had been committed, the Police Media Division said.

The Criminal Investigation Department (CID) is conducting further investigations, supported by the Police Special Task Force, the Criminal Records Division, the Police Kennels Division, and the Department of Official Languages.

Police warned that several cases of financial fraud involving groups of foreign nationals operating from hotels, guesthouses, resorts, and other accommodation had been uncovered in recent months, and urged providers to stay vigilant. Information can be given on the hotlines 118 or 119, or at the nearest Police station.
