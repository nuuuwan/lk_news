Police open fire on van carrying robbery suspects in Eheliyagoda

Colombo, Nov. 28 (Daily Mirror)- Police opened fire on a van carrying a group of suspects allegedly involved in a robbery in Parakaduwa, Eheliyagoda, as they attempted to flee the area.

One of the suspects was injured in the shooting and subsequently taken into police custody, while several others managed to escape.

Police said investigations were underway to locate the absconding suspects and establish further details surrounding the incident.
