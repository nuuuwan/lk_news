MBA Kelaniya Alumni Association sets sights on making national impact

The University of Kelaniya MBA Alumni Association has embarked on an exciting new chapter, placing collective leadership, innovation and national contribution at the heart of its future.

This renewed direction was unveiled at its Annual General Meeting and Fellowship Night, held on 31 October at the Lotus Tower, Colombo.

The event was graced by Chief Guest Dr. S. C. Thushara, Dean of the Faculty of Commerce and Management Studies, together with Heads of Departments, Professors and other distinguished academic representatives of the University of Kelaniya.

A new 21-member Executive Committee was appointed for the 2026–2028 term, ushering in a fresh phase of leadership and organizational development. The Association also introduced its refreshed brand identity, symbolizing the strength of connection, diversity, resilience and shared heritage that unite Kelaniya MBA graduates.

Marking another significant step in its digital transformation, the Association launched its official website (www.mbakelaniya.lk), featuring a dedicated Members’ Portal and online voting facilities to strengthen member engagement, accessibility and transparent governance.

Addressing the gathering, the Association’s President Saranga Panapitiya said, “Our ambition is to build more than an alumni association. We want to create a powerful leadership network with over 2,000 members, that transforms shared knowledge and collective action into meaningful national impact. Through entrepreneurship, education, sustainability and community-focused initiatives, we aspire to contribute to a stronger future for our country and create a legacy that extends beyond generations”.

Looking ahead, the Association’s strategic priorities include the Founder’s Hub, entrepreneurship development programmes, the ALOKA CSR initiative supporting education, and distance-learning solutions connecting university professionals with schools in rural and resource-constrained communities. Sustainability, social responsibility and stronger collaboration between academia and industry will remain integral to this journey.

With renewed purpose, progressive leadership and a shared commitment to making a difference, the Association is positioning itself as a dynamic platform where Kelaniya MBA graduates connect, collaborate and lead change.

The University of Kelaniya Master of Business Administration programme that commenced in 2005, today boasts of an alumni of more than 2000 members holding key positions in various reputed entities both in Sri Lanka and overseas.
