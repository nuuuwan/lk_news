Budget speech on November 12

Colombo, Sep. 30 (Daily Mirror) - Party leaders have decided that the Second Reading of the Appropriation Bill for 2027 (Budget Speech/presentation of Budget proposals) will take place on November 12, 2026, followed by the Second Reading debate from November 13 to December 14.

Secretary General of Parliament Kushani Rohanadeera said the decision was approved at a meeting of party leaders chaired by Speaker Dr. Jagath Wickramaratne.

Accordingly, the Appropriation Bill is scheduled to be presented to Parliament for its First Reading on October 7, the Secretary General said.
