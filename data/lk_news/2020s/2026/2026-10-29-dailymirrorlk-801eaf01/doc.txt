CBSL declares Max App Media, affiliates a prohibited pyramid scheme

The Central Bank of Sri Lanka (CBSL) has determined that Max App Media Ltd, together with its affiliates Max LK (Pvt.) Ltd and Max Media Lanka (Pvt.) Ltd, engaged in, conducted and promoted a prohibited pyramid scheme.

In a public notice, the regulator said the determination followed an investigation under Section 83(C) of Banking Act, No. 30 of 1988, as amended.

The three companies were included in a list of companies, apps and related entities that the CBSL has determined to be prohibited schemes under the act.

The other names on the list include OnmaxDT, the MTFE app and associated groups, Qnet/Questnet, Tiens Lanka Health Care (Pvt.) Ltd, Best Life International (Pvt.) Ltd and Sport Chain app and Sport Chain ZS Society Sri Lanka.

The notice also lists SGO/sgomine.com, Infinity Rover (Pvt.) Ltd, TM App and I.C.A.N. Advertising (Pvt.) Ltd and its affiliated websites.
