Tri-forces step in to treat injured elephant calf amid wildlife vets’ strike

Colombo, Aug. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Veterinary personnel from the Army, Navy and Air Force have stepped in to treat an elephant calf that sustained a gunshot injury and was found in critical condition in the Herathgama Wildlife Zone.

The elephant calf was found near the upper reaches of Nikawewa and Thibbatuwawewa and was reportedly struggling between life and death due to the injury.

As wildlife veterinary officers continued their strike, veterinary personnel and other officers from the three armed forces arrived at the location on the evening of August 12 and carried out an emergency procedure.

The operation was conducted following instructions from the commanders of the three armed forces, with personnel from the Welisara Naval Base, Air Force bases in Katunayake and Sigiriya, and the Sri Lanka National Guard under the Army’s 57 Division taking part.

During the procedure, maggots and pus were removed from the wound on the calf’s right leg. Wildlife officials said an X-ray examination would be required to determine whether the injury had caused any internal damage.

The calf is expected to be taken to the Herathgama Wildlife Office for further treatment and protection.
