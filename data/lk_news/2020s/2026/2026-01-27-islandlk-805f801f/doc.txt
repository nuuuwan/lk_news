Fluctuating fortunes for CSE as capital goods sector takes centre stage

CSE trading kicked off on a positive note yesterday but in the middle of the session it turned negative due to low investor sentiment stemming from geopolitical issues. However later the market ended on a positive note. Capital goods sector counters performed notably well.

Both indices moved upwards. The All Share Price Index went up by 35.63 points, while the S and P SL20 rose by 22.19 points. Turnover stood at Rs 5.21 billion with eight crossings.

The top seven crossings were reported in JKH which crossed 7 million shares to the tune of Rs 173 million and its shares traded at Rs 22.70, LOLC Holdings 200,000 shares crossed for Rs 124 million; its shares traded at Rs 620, Hemas Holdings 2.9 million shares crossed for Rs 101 million and its shares sold at Rs 34.80, Tokyo Cement (Non-Voting) 600,000 shares crossed for Rs 57 million; its shares traded at Rs 95, Sinha Hospital three million shares crossed to the tune of Rs 42 million; its shares traded at Rs 40, Pan Asia Bank 400,000 shares crossed for Rs 24.4 million and its shares sold at Rs 61 and hSenid 950,000 shares crossed to the tune of Rs 21.2 million; its shares traded at Rs 22.30.

In the retail market top seven companies that mainly contributed to the turnover were; Colombo Dockyard Rs 630 million (4.15 million shares traded), EML Consultants Rs 214 million (22 million shares traded), Asia Siyaka Rs 155 million (249,000 shares traded), LOLC Holdings Rs 153 million (249,000 shares traded), JKH Rs 137 million (6.1 million shares traded), York Arcade Rs 114 million (5.7 million shares traded) and Sampath Bank Rs 95.3 million (611,000 shares traded). During the day 272.5 million share volumes changed hands in 48721 transactions.

It is said that the manufacturing sector, especially JKH, performed well while the capital goods sector, especially Colombo Dockyard, excelled as well.

Yesterday the rupee was quoted at 309.55/60 to the US dollar in the spot market relatively unchanged from 309.50/60 the previous day, having depreciated in recent weeks, dealers said, while bond yields were broadly steady.
