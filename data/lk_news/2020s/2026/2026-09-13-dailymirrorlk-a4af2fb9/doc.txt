Dense fog causes traffic disruptions in Central Hills

Colombo, September 13 (Daily Mirror)- Traffic disruptions have been reported along the western slopes of the Central Hills due to dense fog that has persisted since the early hours of today (13).

The fog has significantly reduced visibility along the Hatton–Nuwara Eliya and Hatton–Colombo main roads, as well as several surrounding by-roads.

Hatton Police have urged motorists travelling in the affected areas to exercise caution, reduce their speed and keep their headlights switched on to ensure greater visibility and road safety.
