Siyapatha Finance-Toyota enhance value-added services in vehicle leasing

Strategic partnership aims to deliver flexible financing solutions, enhanced customer convenience and comprehensive vehicle ownership support

Siyapatha Finance PLC has entered into a strategic partnership with Toyota Lanka (Private) Limited through the signing of a memorandum of understanding (MoU), marking a significant milestone in the companies’ shared commitment to enhancing value-added services and elevating the vehicle leasing experience in Sri Lanka.

The partnership brings together Siyapatha Finance PLC’s financial expertise and Toyota Lanka’s automotive leadership to deliver accessible, convenient and flexible vehicle financing solutions, complemented by services designed to address the evolving needs and expectations of the leasing customers.

Through this collaboration, both organisations aim to create greater value for the customers by streamlining the leasing journey and strengthening support throughout the vehicle ownership lifecycle.

The MoU was formally signed by Siyapatha Finance Chief Operating Officer Rajeev De Silva and Chief Marketing Officer Hisham Ziard and Toyota Lanka Chairman Masaaki Kawabata and Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer Manohara Atukorala.

The signing ceremony was attended by the senior management teams of both organisations, reflecting their shared commitment to establishing a strong and enduring business relationship.

Under this strategic alliance, the customers can look forward to a more streamlined and convenient leasing experience, supported by flexible financing solutions, long-term leasing options, enhanced service delivery, comprehensive protection through trusted insurance partners and access to a range of reliable vehicles from a globally recognised automotive brand.

The collaboration aims to strengthen customer confidence by bringing financing and automotive services together through a more integrated and customer-focused approach.

A key strength of the partnership is Toyota Lanka’s islandwide network, which provides the customers with convenient access to vehicle sales and comprehensive after-sales support.

With trained technicians, Toyota Genuine Spare Parts and dedicated service facilities, Toyota Lanka offers a broad range of services, including routine maintenance, mechanical repairs and body and paint services. These capabilities help the customers preserve vehicle quality, safety, reliability and long-term value throughout their ownership journey.
