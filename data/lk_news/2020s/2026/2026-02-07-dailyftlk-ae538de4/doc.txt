Sri Lanka Press Association launches special academic book on mass communication ‘Pathrakala Prasadini’ at Russian Cultural Centre, Colombo

Sri Lanka Press Association President Kurulu Koojana Kariyakarawana presented a copy of ‘Pathrakala Prasadini’ to Russian Cultural Centre Director Mariya Popova, during the launch of the book at the Russian Cultural Centre in Colombo on Saturday. Also pictured are International Media Support in Denmark Regional Adviser – Asia and Africa Dr. Ranga Kalansooriya, SLPA Secretary General Tilak Senanayake, and Vice President P.M. Senaratne
