Ceylinco Life’s Manjula among 3 global finalists

Ceylinco Life has announced that A.I.P. Manjula of the company’s Ambalantota branch was among the three global finalists for the prestigious Insurance Agent of the Year award at the 11th Asia Trusted Life Agents & Advisers Awards (ATLAA) 2026, bringing recognition for Sri Lankan insurance excellence to one of the region’s most respected platforms for life insurance and financial advisory professionals.

For Ceylinco Life, the recognition reflects the calibre and customer-focused approach of its agency force, while for Manjula it represents a significant personal achievement on the regional stage, with acknowledgment of his dedication, professionalism and commitment to putting customers first, the company said.

The ATLAA programme recognises high-performing professionals who demonstrate exceptional standards of competence, client trust and commitment to the interests of policyholders.

ATLAA 2026 attracted nominations from across South Asia, Southeast Asia, East Asia and the broader Asia-Pacific region, including markets such as Sri Lanka, India, Bangladesh, Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, the Philippines, Vietnam, Hong Kong, Taiwan, China, Macau, Japan and South Korea.

“Manjula’s achievement in being selected one of the three finalists represents a significant recognition of Sri Lankan insurance professionalism within a highly competitive regional field and reinforces the value of placing customers and their long-term interests at the heart of insurance advisory,” the company added.
