Home Lands delivers 3,500 resort apartments and villas

The Home Lands Group has reached a landmark milestone: the successful delivery of 3,500 residential units across its completed projects. This achievement reinforces the Group’s position as the market leader, celebrated for its credibility, innovation, and commitment to creating lifestyle-focused developments.

Since its inception, Home Lands has been at the forefront of modern living in Sri Lanka, pioneering resort-style, themed residential developments that seamlessly blend thoughtful design, ultimate comfort, and vibrant community living. Guided by a philosophy rooted in trust, integrity, and timely delivery, the Group has consistently elevated the homeownership experience, ensuring transparency, reliability, and exceptional service at every stage of the journey.

Home Lands currently has over 6,000 residential units under management, of which 3,500 units have already been delivered and over 2,500 units under construction to be completed and delivered within the next 30 months across multiple themed resort lifestyle projects, which will be completed and delivered in 2026, 2027, and 2028. The company continues to expand its footprint while maintaining its commitment to timely delivery and unmatched quality.

A cornerstone of Home Lands’ success is its fully integrated business model. The Group operates as a conglomerate of 14 companies, including Heyraa Construction, which holds the prestigious CS2 grading from the Construction Industry Development Authority (CIDA) of Sri Lanka, a distinction reserved for a select few construction companies nationally. This integration provides customers with both developer and contractor assurance, ensuring confidence and long-term reliability.

Home Lands Group Chairman Nalin Herath said: “This milestone reflects years of dedication, vision, and innovation. We are delighted to welcome Mr. and Mrs. Nishan Mendis as our 3,500th homeowners and celebrate this momentous occasion with them.”

In honour of this milestone, Home Lands invited Mr. and Mrs. Mendis to celebrate and officially hand over the keys to their new home at Greendale Retirement Resort Apartments and Residences, Sri Lanka’s first international-standard retirement leisure village. Featuring over 50 world-class amenities, Greendale showcases the Group’s commitment to resort-style lifestyle-focused developments.

Reflecting on the experience, Mendis said: “When you think of retirement, peace of mind is essential. Greendale offers excellent facilities, a serene environment, and strong infrastructure. We are thrilled to be part of this milestone.”

Home Lands’ leadership and excellence have been consistently recognised through prestigious national and international accolades. The Group has secured multiple wins at the PropertyGuru Asia Property Awards, including Best Developer – Sri Lanka in 2021, 2024, and 2025, becoming the only Sri Lankan developer to achieve this title for three consecutive years. In a historic milestone, Home Lands also became the first Sri Lankan developer to win Best Lifestyle Developer – Asia 2025 at the PropertyGuru Asia Property Awards Grand Finale. Further reinforcing its market leadership, the 2025 RIU Brand Health Survey named Home Lands the Overall Real Estate Winner, while the brand also achieved Superbrands status, becoming the only real estate Superbrand in Sri Lanka. These recognitions reflect Home Lands’ credibility, quality, and strong reputation, making it Sri Lanka’s number one, most trusted and most innovative real estate brand.

In a market where credibility and timely delivery are critical to buyer confidence, Home Lands’ unwavering dedication ensures it remains at the forefront of Sri Lanka’s real estate sector, continuously elevating lifestyles through trust, innovation, and excellence.
