Three arrested with firearms, hunting gear in ‘Operation Hasthi Sena’ raid

Colombo, Nov. 5 (Daily Mirror) - Three suspects have been arrested with firearms, hunting equipment and animal parts during a special search operation conducted under ‘Operation Hasthi Sena’ in the Galkiriyagama area.

The operation was jointly carried out by the Environment Ministry, the Wildlife Conservation Department and the Sri Lanka Army to prevent wildlife crimes and protect wild elephants.

The search covered 10 Grama Niladhari divisions within the Galkiriyagama Wildlife Range, which have been identified as areas vulnerable to wildlife crimes.

Ten teams, each consisting of nine members, were deployed for the operation. Each team was led by a wildlife officer and included two Civil Security Department personnel and six Army personnel.

During the operation, three suspects were arrested in the Nikkiniya Grama Niladhari Division in the Kekirawa Divisional Secretariat Division for possessing equipment allegedly used in wildlife crimes.

The items recovered included spring-loaded traps used to harm wild elephants, wires, cables, snares, explosives and parts of dead animals.

The seized items included six sets of deer antlers, one locally made muzzle-loading firearm, two knives and a sword with deer-antler handles, and a fragment of an elephant's jawbone.

Officers also recovered lead pellets, pieces of lead, an iron rod used to load gunpowder into firearms, fragments of percussion caps, coils of wire and 650 grams of meat.

In addition, two 12-bore firearms, a spring-loaded trap and seven rounds of 12-bore SG ammunition were seized.

The Environment Ministry and the Wildlife Conservation Department said such joint operations would continue to prevent wildlife crimes, protect wild animals and safeguard the country's wildlife resources.
