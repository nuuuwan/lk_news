Colombo Book fair, a book lovers’ paradise

It is amazing to see many people interested in the Colombo International Book Fair despite hefty taxes imposed on books. Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya, who declared open the book fair this year, said the Government is focusing on revising the Value-added Tax (VAT) imposed on books through the upcoming Budget, adding that the Government recognises the value of books. Even thought successive governments made this promise, they failed to do so.

Book readers argue that books sold across Indian subcontinent are sold at five times the rate than what is being sold in India. Gone are the days when people bought stacks and stacks of books written by their favorite authors. Purchasing books, even children’s storybooks for that matter,  has now become a dream for many.

The 20% deduction on prices of all books is a welcome move to encourage people to purchase books. Even though there are many digital reading platforms such as Kindle, there are many who still want to feel a book when they read it. Therefore the government should encourage more opportunities of this nature to encourage the reading habit among all Sri Lankans.
