Cabinet clears $ 5.12 m procurement of cardiac and diabetes medicines

China’s Shenzhen Techdow Pharmaceutical Co. Ltd., gets 920,000 units of Enoxaparin Sodium Injection contract at $ 828,000

India’s M.J. Biopharm Ltd., gets 2.7 m vials contract for Biphasic Isophane Insulin at $ 4.293 m

The Cabinet of Ministers has approved the procurement of two essential medicines for cardiac and diabetic patients at a combined cost of around $ 5.12 million, following international competitive bidding.

The approvals cover 920,000 pre-filled syringes/vials of Enoxaparin Sodium Injection, used in the treatment of cardiac patients, and 2.7 million vials of Biphasic Isophane Insulin Injection for diabetic patients.

The Enoxaparin procurement has been awarded to China’s Shenzhen Techdow Pharmaceutical Co. Ltd., for $ 828,000, including Cost and Freight (C&F), after seven bids were received and evaluated. The company was identified as the substantially responsive lowest bidder.

The insulin procurement has been awarded to India’s M.J. Biopharm Ltd., for $ 4.293 million, including C&F, following the receipt of two bids. The company was the only substantially responsive bidder.

Both awards were approved on the recommendation of the High-Level Standing Procurement Committee, following evaluation of the bids, the Government said.

The proposals to this effect were submitted by the Health Minister Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa.
