Week ends in green

Indices closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Melstacorp, Sampath Bank and DialogAxiata with the turnover crossing Rs. 2.2 Bn. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in R I L Property, Commercial Bank nonvoting, and Ceylon Cold Stores. Mixed interest was observed in Melstacorp, Sampath Bank and Sierra Cables whilst retail interest was noted in SMB Leasing, Agstar and Softlogic Capital.

During the week, the ASPI and the S&P SL20 gained 1.2% and 1.3% respectively, whilst recording an average daily turnover of Rs. 2.5 Bn.

Retailing sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to RIL Property PLC) whilst the sector index gained 1.01%. The share price of RIL Property PLC increased by Rs 0.10 (0.39%) to close at Rs 25.90.

Food, Beverage & Tobacco sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Melstacorp and Ceylon Cold Stores) whilst the sector index increased by 1.11%. The share price of Melstacorp gained Rs 4.50 (2.42%) to close at Rs 190.75. The share price of Ceylon Cold Stores recorded a loss of Rs 0.75 (0.59%) to close at Rs 126.00.

Commercial Bank nonvoting and Sampath Bank were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Commercial Bank nonvoting moved down by Rs 0.50 (0.27%) to close at Rs 184.50. The share price of Sampath Bank appreciated by Rs 2.25 (1.63%) to close at Rs 140.00.
