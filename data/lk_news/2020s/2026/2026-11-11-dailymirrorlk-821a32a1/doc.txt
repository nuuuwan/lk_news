Indices close in green

Indices closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Commercial Bank, Dialog Axiata and Hatton National Bank with the turnover crossing Rs. 7.9bn.

A similar behavior was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Hayleys, Commercial Bank and Sampath Bank.

Furthermore, foreigners closed as net sellers. The Banking sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Commercial Bank, Hatton National Bank and Sampath Bank) whilst the sector index gained 3.69%. The share price of Commercial Bank gained Rs. 9.25 (4.28%) to close at Rs. 225.25. The share price of Hatton National Bank moved up by Rs. 15.25 (3.81%) to close at Rs. 415.00. The share price of Sampath Bank recorded a gain of Rs. 5.00 (3.27%) to close at Rs. 158.00. Telecommunication Services sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Dialog Axiata) whilst the sector index increased by 4.82%. The share price of Dialog Axiata increased by Rs. 2.30 (4.88%) to close at Rs. 49.40. Dipped Products was also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Dipped Products appreciated by Rs. 1.10 (1.62%) to close at Rs. 68.80.
