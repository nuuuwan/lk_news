Family thanks President for securing Anojan’s release

Colombo, Nov. 12 (Daily Mirror) - Family members of Anojan Sivarasa, who had been sentenced to death in Saudi Arabia, have thanked President Anura Kumara Dissanayake for his intervention in securing his release and acquittal on all charges.

Sivarasa’s family met the President at the Presidential Secretariat this morning (12) and expressed their gratitude for the efforts made to save his life and bring him back from the death sentence.

During the meeting, President Dissanayake said it was his responsibility to stand up for Sri Lankan citizens wherever they were in the world.

He also acknowledged the support extended by the Government of Saudi Arabia and thanked all those who had worked towards securing Sivarasa’s release, regardless of differences in ethnicity, religion or political affiliation.

Sivarasa’s parents informed the President that their son was currently in good health.

His mother expressed particular appreciation to the President for intervening from the beginning to secure her son’s release from the death sentence and clear him of all charges.

She also thanked Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Minister Vijitha Herath, Deputy Minister Arun Hemachandra, Sri Lanka’s Ambassador to Saudi Arabia O.L. Ameer Ajwad and embassy officials, as well as the lawyers who travelled to Saudi Arabia to provide legal assistance.

She further appreciated all Sri Lankans who contributed to the efforts to secure her son’s freedom.

Social activist Rajkumar Rajeevkanth also thanked the President for fulfilling the assurance he had given during their first meeting regarding efforts to secure Sivarasa’s release.

Rajeevkanth revealed that Sivarasa’s parents had suffered severe psychological distress during the same period following the execution of a Bangladeshi couple who had also been sentenced to death.
