Colombo Dockyard returns to profit as ship repair earnings surge

Colombo Dockyard PLC swung to a profit in the three months through June as stronger ship repair earnings and lower finance costs outweighed a drop in revenue.

The group posted a net profit of Rs.80.2 million, reversing a Rs.813.4 million loss a year earlier, according to interim financial statements filed with the Colombo Stock Exchange.

Revenue fell 6.8 percent to Rs.5.84 billion, but cost of sales declined 21.8 percent to Rs.4.63 billion. That lifted gross profit to Rs.1.21 billion from Rs.345.4 million and widened the gross profit margin to 20.7 percent from 5.5 percent.

Ship repairing was the strongest-performing business during the quarter. Revenue from the segment climbed 48.4 percent to Rs.3.95 billion, while its result at gross profit level more than doubled to Rs.1.46 billion.

That helped counter continued weakness in shipbuilding, where revenue declined 61.6 percent to Rs.1.05 billion. The segment remained loss-making at gross profit level, though its loss narrowed to Rs.378.8 million from Rs.899.6 million.

Heavy engineering revenue fell 11.7 percent to Rs.703.6 million, while its segment result dropped to Rs.30.4 million from Rs.480.7 million.

The company also benefited from a 63.8 percent reduction in net finance expenses to Rs.188.2 million. Administrative expenses, however, rose 18.2 percent to Rs.1.03 billion, while other income declined 57.7 percent to Rs.108.5 million.

Profit attributable to owners was Rs.86.3 million, compared with a loss of Rs.808.4 million in the corresponding quarter. Earnings per share were 22 cents, against a restated loss of Rs.3.51.

The return to profit did not translate into positive operating cash flow. The group used Rs.678.1 million in operating activities, as trade and other receivables increased by Rs.3.43 billion during the quarter. Trade and other payables rose by Rs.2.35 billion.

Colombo Dockyard ended June with Rs.8.48 billion in cash and cash equivalents. Interest-bearing borrowings stood at Rs.14.79 billion, while bank overdrafts amounted to Rs.1.70 billion.

Total group equity was Rs.15.59 billion, compared with Rs.4.07 billion a year earlier. Retained losses stood at Rs.9.23 billion at the end of the quarter.

The company changed its financial year-end from December to March to align its reporting period with parent Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Limited. The results are subject to audit.
