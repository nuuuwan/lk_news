Sri Lanka Insurance General unveils country’s first-ever Virtual Motor Claim Office

Sri Lanka Insurance Corporation General Ltd (SLICGL) has unveiled the country’s first-ever Virtual Motor Claim Office, a mobile-accessible web platform, which delivers the entire post-accident claims journey directly to the customers’ smartphones.

The motor insurance policyholders islandwide can now bypass physical branch visits when filing vehicle claims and connect with SLICGL anytime and anywhere.

Dealing with a vehicle accident means navigating stressful, paperwork-heavy processes and making repeated trips to physical branches. Built on over 64 years of government-backed trust and reliability, SLICGL has launched the digital platform to replace traditional delays with speed, transparency and convenience.

The new workflow is straightforward and puts full control in the driver’s hands. Once an SLICGL technical officer completes the initial vehicle inspection, the policyholder receives a dedicated, secure web link. Through this portal, the customers can easily complete their claim forms, upload supporting documents and enter their bank details for direct financial settlements without ever stepping foot inside an office.

In addition to submissions, the platform offers complete visibility and direct support throughout the repair process. The policyholders can review their uploaded files, track approval stages and monitor payment settlements in real time. Whenever questions come up, the customers can message their assigned claim officers directly through a built-in online chat during office hours. For the eligible claims within specified value limits, even the final post-repair inspection can be completed online.

As Sri Lanka’s leading general insurer, SLICGL continues to modernise the local insurance landscape and part of its long-term digital transformation strategy is pairing six decades of dependable service with practical mobile technology. In removing rigid, time-consuming hurdles, the company reinforces its commitment to delivering responsive support to drivers islandwide.
