India team to arrive in Sri Lanka on August 4 for Test series

Colombo, August 1 (Daily Mirror) - The Indian cricket team is scheduled to arrive in Sri Lanka on August 4 ahead of their upcoming Test series against Sri Lanka.

India will play a three-day warm-up match instead of the initially planned four-day fixture, with the game set to commence on August 7 at the NCC Grounds in Colombo.

The first Test of the series will be played at the Galle International Cricket Stadium from August 15 to 19, while the second Test is scheduled to be held at the SSC Ground in Colombo from August 23 to 27.
