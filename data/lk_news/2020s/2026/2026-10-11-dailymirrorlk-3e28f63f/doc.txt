Sri Lanka retains Tier 2 status in US Trafficking Report

Colombo, Oct. 11 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka has retained Tier 2 status in the United States Department of State’s 2026 Trafficking in Persons (TIP) Report for the fifth consecutive year, reflecting the country’s continued efforts to combat human trafficking.

The National Anti-Human Trafficking Task Force (NAHTTF), chaired by the Secretary to the Ministry of Defence, continues to coordinate efforts among government institutions involved in prevention, victim protection, law enforcement, prosecution, migration management and victim assistance.

A key development during the reporting period was the launch of the National Strategic Action Plan to Monitor and Combat Human Trafficking 2026-2030, which sets out a framework for coordinated action over the next five years.

Sri Lanka has also expanded District Anti-Trafficking Forums to strengthen cooperation among local authorities, law enforcement agencies and other relevant institutions. These forums aim to improve the early identification of trafficking victims, referrals and assistance at the district level.

Other initiatives include awareness and capacity-building programmes for frontline officers, cooperation with international and regional partners, and efforts to address emerging forms of trafficking, including technology-facilitated recruitment, online exploitation and forced criminality.

While the continued Tier 2 classification recognises the country's efforts to address human trafficking, further improvements are needed to strengthen prevention, victim protection, investigations, prosecutions and coordination among relevant institutions.

The NAHTTF said it remained committed to addressing outstanding challenges and strengthening Sri Lanka's national response to human trafficking through sustained cooperation among government institutions and other stakeholders.
