27-acre land near Colombo proposed for Arts and Cultural Village

Colombo, Nov. 27 (Daily Mirror) - A 27-acre plot of land located close to Colombo has been proposed for acquisition to establish an Arts and Cultural Village under the Ministry of Buddha Sasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs.

The proposal was discussed at a recent meeting of the Ministerial Consultative Committee on Buddha Sasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs, held at the Parliament Complex under the chairmanship of Minister Dr. Hiniduma Sunil Senevi.

The proposed village is expected to accommodate institutions dedicated to cinema, arts and cultural activities, as well as meditation and the study of Buddhist philosophy.

The Minister informed the Committee that a joint Cabinet Memorandum had been submitted seeking approval for the acquisition of the land.

The Committee was also briefed on the use of modern technology in archaeological investigations, including Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR), which enables the examination of underground archaeological remains without excavation.

Meanwhile, discussions were held on coordinating the examination calendars of the Dhamma School Final Examination and the G.C.E. Ordinary Level Examination to avoid scheduling conflicts that cause difficulties for students and parents.

The Committee discussed working with the Ministry of Education and the Department of Examinations to prepare a coordinated examination calendar and issue instructions to prevent such conflicts at provincial and zonal levels.

The conservation of the damaged Rajya Sabha Mandapaya (Royal Assembly Hall) in Kurunegala was another matter discussed at the meeting.

It was decided that the Department of Archaeology, the Central Cultural Fund, the Kurunegala Municipal Council and the Urban Development Authority would hold a joint discussion to resolve ownership and administrative issues concerning the site and reach a final decision as soon as possible.

The Minister also said arrangements would be made to ensure the fair and transparent distribution of dates donated by foreign countries during the Ramadan season among 2,700 mosques across the country.

The Committee further discussed establishing a properly documented distribution mechanism to ensure transparency in the process.

Deputy Ministers Gamagedara Dissanayake and Muneer Mulaffar, several Members of Parliament and officials from the Ministry and institutions under its purview attended the meeting.
