Muted trading keeps yield curve anchored

The secondary market started the week on a lethargic footing, with both market participation and trading activity remaining constrained, while the yield curve stayed broadly unchanged.

Noteworthy trades were limited to a handful of transactions, with activity concentrated mainly across the short to mid-end of the curve.

At the short end of the curve 15.02.2028 and 15.03.2028 both traded at 10.00 percent while 01.05.2028 traded modestly higher at 10.10 percent.

Further along the yield curve 01.08.2030 changed hands at 10.80 percent while 15.12.2032 traded between 11.30 percent to 11.36 percent.

Finally, 01.11.2033 and 15.10.2034 were seen trading at 11.50 percent and 11.75 percent, respectively.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee appreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.333.09/US dollar, compared to Rs.333.60/US dollar seen earlier.

Market liquidity in the banking system expanded to Rs.301.35 billion, from Rs.290.69 billion recorded previously.
