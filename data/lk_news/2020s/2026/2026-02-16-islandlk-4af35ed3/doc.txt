Unavoidable circumstances …

The ‘ROCK meets REGGAE’ concert, scheduled for 28th February, has been rescheduled, due to unavoidable circumstances, as mentioned by Rohitha Jayalath, of JAYASRI fame, on social media: “We’ve rescheduled the event! New date coming soon. Refunds available ….”

Rock and reggae music fans were looking forward to this event as the lineup included Sri Lanka’s rock star Chitral Somapala, together with Black Majesty (from Australia) and the band JAYASRI.

The last ROCK meets REGGAE event was held in Colombo in 2019; in general, rock and reggae music fans have very little to look forward to in the scene here.

According to reports, Austria’s biggest Reggae Festival is coming up in August, this year – 7th and 8th August – with a very strong lineup, and JAYASRI is in the scene, as well.

Alpha Blondy, who did his thing in Colombo, last year, is also a part of the programme.
