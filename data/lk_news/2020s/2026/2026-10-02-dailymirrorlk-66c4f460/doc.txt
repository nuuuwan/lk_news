LOLC Life Assurance appoints COO Jayantha Kalinga as Principal Officer

LOLC Life Assurance Ltd. has officially announced the appointment of its Chief Operating Officer (COO), Jayantha Kalinga, as the company’s Principal Officer, in addition to his current executive responsibilities.

The appointment, made pursuant to formal approval from the Insurance Regulatory Commission of Sri Lanka (IRCSL), further reinforces the Company’s leadership structure and governance framework as it advances its growth trajectory within Sri Lanka’s life insurance sector.

A distinguished industry veteran with over 35 years of comprehensive experience spanning both the Life and General Insurance sectors, Kalinga brings extensive expertise in sales management, distribution network expansion, business development and strategic direction to his expanded portfolio. Throughout his career across leading insurance institutions in Sri Lanka, he has established a proven track record of driving sustained business performance, cultivating high-performing teams, and building customer-centric insurance propositions.

In his ongoing capacity as Chief Operating Officer of LOLC Life Assurance Ltd., Kalinga plays a pivotal role in steering the Company’s operational and commercial strategies while accelerating its market development. His appointment as Principal Officer underscores the Board’s confidence in his leadership acumen, operational depth, and acute understanding of the evolving dynamics within the regional insurance landscape.

Complementing his industry experience, Kalinga holds a Master of Business Administration (MBA) from the University of Northampton, UK, alongside a solid academic foundation in Business Management. His professional credentials include the Life Underwriter Training Council (LUTC) designation awarded by LIMRA, USA, and the Licentiate Examination from the Insurance Institute of India. His career is further backed by participation in premier international insurance forums, providing him with a global perspective on modern insurance practices and emerging market trends.

Kalinga’s responsibilities come at a strategic juncture for LOLC Life Assurance, as the organisation continues to scale its market footprint, enhance stakeholder value and deliver tailored financial protection solutions to individuals and families across Sri Lanka.
