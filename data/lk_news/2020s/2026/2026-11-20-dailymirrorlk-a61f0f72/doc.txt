‘Builders Gateway’ opens at former residence of Hema Premadasa

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - A dedicated facilitation unit named ‘Builders Gateway’ has been established to help contractors resolve issues related to construction projects through direct intervention with government institutions and banking authorities.

The unit was officially opened today (20) at the former official residence of Hema Premadasa, the spouse of late former President Ranasinghe Premadasa.

Operating as an affiliate of the National Business Facilitation Centre, Builders Gateway is intended to provide contractors with a central point to seek assistance and resolve operational difficulties encountered in implementing construction projects.

The unit will intervene in matters involving state institutions and banking institutions to help contractors obtain necessary approvals, support and solutions to issues affecting their projects.

The opening ceremony was attended by Minister of Housing, Construction and Water Supply Susil Ranasinghe, Deputy Minister T. B. Sarath, Secretary to the President Dr. Nandika Sanath Kumanayake and several other officials.

Officials said the establishment of the unit was in response to a longstanding request by government contractors for a national-level mechanism to address their operational challenges.
