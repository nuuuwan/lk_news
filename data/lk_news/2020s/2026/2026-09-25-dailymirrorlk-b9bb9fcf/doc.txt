Jetwing Travels adopts new-energy mobility solution with Hayleys tie-up

Mohan Pandithage, Chairman & Chief Executive of Hayleys PLC, and Shiromal Cooray, Chairman and Managing Director of Jetwing Travels (Private) Limited, together with representatives of Hayleys Mobility, Jetwing Travels and Jetwing Journeys, at the handover of 21 JAECOO J7 Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles

As Sri Lanka strengthens its position as a responsible tourism destination, Hayleys Mobility Limited and Jetwing Travels are advancing a more sustainable approach to travel across the island through the adoption of new-energy mobility solutions.

The initiative reflects a shared commitment to reducing the environmental impact of ground transportation, an essential yet often overlooked part of the tourism experience, while maintaining the comfort, reliability and service standards expected by local and international travellers.

Shiromal Cooray, Chairman and Managing Director of Jetwing Travels stated, “This investment is part of our continued effort to make responsible travel more tangible across every stage of the guest journey. By introducing plug-in hybrid technology to our fleet, we are taking a practical step towards reducing the environmental impact of ground transportation, while continuing to deliver the comfort, reliability and flexibility our guests expect when travelling across

As part of this transition, Hayleys Mobility has delivered 21 JAECOO J7 Plug-in Hybrid Electric Vehicles (PHEVs) to Jetwing Travels. The vehicles will be deployed across Jetwing Travels and Jetwing Journeys for airport transfers, excursions and extended tours throughout the island.

Hayleys Mobility is backed by its parent entity, Hayleys Fentons Limited, the energy, mobility and projects vertical of Hayleys PLC, Sri Lanka’s largest and most diversified conglomerate. This heritage provides the engineering expertise, nationwide capabilities and institutional strength required to support Sri Lanka’s evolving mobility needs.

Hasith Prematillake, Managing Director of Hayleys Fentons Limited said, “Sustainable mobility must deliver both environmental and commercial value if it is to be adopted at scale. With approximately 90 kilometres of electric-only driving, a combined range of up to 1,200 kilometres and an estimated saving of around Rs. 15 per kilometre compared with petrol-powered driving, the JAECOO J7 PHEV presents a strong proposition for high-mileage fleet operators.”

Rajieve Fernando, Chief Executive Officer of Hayleys Mobility Limited added, “Hayleys Mobility supports the transition towards more sustainable mobility through a comprehensive mobility ecosystem, bringing together vehicles, charging infrastructure, technical expertise and dependable after-sales care to provide customers with a seamless mobility experience.”
