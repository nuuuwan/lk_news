JAECOO J5 HEV launched

The all-new JAECOO J5 HEV was launched by Hayleys Mobility Limited in Colombo on June 4, 2026. The hybrid SUV expands Sri Lanka’s premium new-energy vehicle segment, combining intelligent hybrid performance, advanced safety technologies and premium comfort for both urban and long-distance driving. Hayleys Mobility introduced the model to meet growing demand for sustainable and high-performance mobility solutions in the country. Pix by Pradeep Pathirana
