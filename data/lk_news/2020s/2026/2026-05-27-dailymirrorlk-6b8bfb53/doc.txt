Hilton Colombo earns global recognition at 2026 Haute Grandeur Global Excellence Awards

Hilton Colombo has been recognized on the global stage at the 2026 Haute Grandeur Global Excellence Awards, receiving four distinguished accolades that celebrate the hotel’s excellence in hospitality, culinary experiences, and guest service.

The hotel was named Leading Culinary Hotel in Asia, Best Guest Service in Sri Lanka, Best Luxury Hotel in Sri Lanka, and Most Unique Guest Experience in Sri Lanka, marking a significant milestone for Hilton Colombo, and further strengthening its reputation as one of the country’s most celebrated hospitality destinations.

Recognized globally as one of the hospitality industry’s most respected award platforms, the Haute Grandeur Global Excellence Awards honor outstanding establishments that consistently deliver exceptional guest experiences. Based entirely on guest feedback, the awards reflect Hilton Colombo’s continued commitment to world-class hospitality and meaningful service.

Commenting on the achievement, Manesh Fernando, Area General Manager for Hilton Sri Lanka and General Manager of Hilton Colombo said, “To be recognized across multiple categories on a global platform is incredibly meaningful, particularly because the recognition is driven by guest experiences. These awards are a proud reflection of the passion, consistency, and dedication demonstrated by our team every day. We remain committed to delivering exceptional hospitality while continuing to elevate Colombo’s position on the global travel and culinary landscape.”

From award-winning dining concepts and curated culinary showcases to memorable stays and personalized experiences, Hilton Colombo continues to redefine luxury hospitality in the heart of Colombo. The recognition also highlights the dedication and passion of the hotel’s team members, whose commitment to excellence remains at the core of every guest journey.
