S&P assigns Hayleys ‘B+’ foreign currency rating with stable outlook

S&P Global Ratings has assigned Hayleys PLC a ‘B+’ long-term foreign currency rating, with a stable outlook, providing the diversified conglomerate with an internationally comparable credit benchmark as it explores overseas funding opportunities.

Hayleys said the rating could broaden its access to foreign currency capital from international institutional investors, sovereign wealth funds and global lenders. The actual cost and availability of such funding would, however, depend on the prevailing market conditions and the terms of any prospective issuance.

The assessment was supported by the group’s export-oriented operations and diversified business portfolio, according to a statement issued by Hayleys. About 50 percent of group earnings are generated in foreign currency, reducing its reliance on the domestic market and providing a natural hedge against some Sri Lanka-related risks. Hayleys operates across 17 sectors and more than 20 countries, with production, sales and marketing operations spanning five continents. Its businesses range from export manufacturing and agriculture to logistics, consumer products and renewable energy.

“This rating is the outcome of a business model built deliberately for resilience, with approximately 50 percent of our earnings in foreign currency, a portfolio diversified across 17 sectors and an operating discipline that has delivered record results during tough conditions,” Hayleys Executive Director Sarath Ganegoda said.

The group reported revenue of Rs.585.02 billion and pre-tax profit of Rs.38.04 billion for the financial year ended March 2026. It also said its operations generated about US $ 800 million in foreign exchange during the year.

Hayleys has identified international capital raising, expansion of its export-oriented businesses, retail and renewable energy as strategic priorities. The rating does not represent a fundraising transaction and the company did not disclose the details of any planned foreign currency borrowing or debt issuance.
