Multiple explosions heard in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia

MNA– Several explosions reportedly heard in Saudi capital Riyadh on Sunday. Reports indicate that a fire has broken out at Saudi Aramco oil facilities in the Abqaiq area, located in the country's Eastern Province.

Following early-morning attacks by Yemeni armed forces on strategic targets within Saudi Arabia, reports indicate that a fire has broken out at Saudi Aramco oil facilities in the Abqaiq area, located in the country's Eastern Province.

Earlier on Sunday, Yemen's health minister said Saudi attacks and military operations since July 13 have martyred or wounded 1,039 people, with rescue teams still pulling bodies from the rubble after a strike on homes in Sanaa province.

The minister stated 1,039 people have been martyred or wounded in Saudi attacks and military operations since the escalation against the country began on July 13.
