Sri Lanka Insurance Life in Anuradhapura to assist Poson pilgrims

Sri Lanka Insurance Life will be in Anuradhapura from the 28th to the 30th of June 2026, extending round the clock support to thousands of pilgrims visiting the sacred city to commemorate Poson Poya.

Continuing a programme that has been conducted for more than three decades, Sri Lanka Insurance Life, in collaboration with the Anuradhapura District Secretariat, will operate a special service to assist in locating lost individuals and belongings across key pilgrimage sites.

The main coordination centre will be located at Pethmaga, supported by four sub centres strategically placed at Ruwanweli Maha Seya, Sinha Kuluna, Mirisavetiya and Thuparama.
