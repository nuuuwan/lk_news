Keheliya Rambukwella released on bail

Colombo, Aug. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Former Minister Keheliya Rambukwella was today (18) ordered to be released on bail by the Colombo Magistrate’s Court in connection with a corruption case filed against him by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC).

Colombo Chief Magistrate Asanga S. Bodaragama issued the order after considering submissions made by both the Bribery Commission and attorneys representing the former Minister.

The case is one of several legal proceedings filed against Rambukwella in connection with alleged corruption.
