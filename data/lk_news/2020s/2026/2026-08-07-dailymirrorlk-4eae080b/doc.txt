Elite Joes brought down by Maliyadeva

AUG.07 (Mirror Sports) - After the completion of the league matches of the Under 18 Elite Rugby Sevens tournament at the Royal Sports Complex ground in Reid Avenue, the eight teams to qualify for the Cup quarter finals were found after the fancied St. Joseph’s College went down to the unfancied Maliyadeva College.

The eight teams to make it to the knockout stage were Ananda, St Peter’s. Isipathana, Trinity, Zahira, Kingswood, Maliyadeva and DS Senanayake.

The highest score of the tournament on the first day was achieved by Vidyartha who recorded a 63-0 win over Sri Sumangala Panadura who underwent another big defeat of -0-52 to Trinity. Four teams Rahula MV Katugastota, Science, St Sylvester’s and St Aloysius Galle pulled out of the tournament at the last minute.

Quarter Final Schedule: Cup - Ananda vs Isipathana. Maliyadeva vs St Peter’s. Kingswood vs Zahira. Trinity vs DSS
