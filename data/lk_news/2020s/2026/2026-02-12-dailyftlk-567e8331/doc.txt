Siddhalepa awarded   Superbrands Sri Lanka status

Reaffirms its position as a trusted heritage brand rooted in Ayurveda

Siddhalepa, Sri Lanka’s most trusted Ayurvedic healthcare brand, has been awarded Superbrands Sri Lanka status, reaffirming its long-standing position as one of the nation’s most respected heritage brands with deep roots in Ayurveda. The recognition celebrates Siddhalepa’s enduring commitment to authenticity, holistic wellbeing, and consistent brand excellence spanning over nine decades.

Founded in 1934, Siddhalepa has become synonymous with natural healing in Sri Lanka, particularly in ache, pain, and cold relief, while steadily expanding into a broad portfolio of holistic wellness solutions. Built on time-tested indigenous knowledge and strengthened by modern scientific expertise, the brand continues to blend tradition with innovation to meet the evolving needs of contemporary consumers, both locally and globally.

This latest accolade reflects nearly two decades of consistent Superbrands recognition, having first received Superbrands status in 2006. Siddhalepa also stands proud as the only Ayurvedic brand in Sri Lanka to be recognised with Superbrands status, a distinction that underscores the deep trust earned across generations.

stated: “Being awarded Superbrands Sri Lanka status for the second time is a profound honour for all of us at Siddhalepa. This recognition reflects the trust that generations of Sri Lankans have placed in our brand and reaffirms our purpose of delivering authentic, high-quality Ayurvedic solutions that improve everyday life. As we move forward, we remain committed to preserving our heritage while innovating responsibly to make Ayurveda relevant and accessible to modern lifestyles.”

Superbrands Sri Lanka, the independent authority on branding excellence, identifies and honours brands that demonstrate outstanding quality, reliability, and consumer trust—making this recognition a powerful endorsement of Siddhalepa’s values and performance.

Beyond products, Siddhalepa continues to play a responsible social role through sustainability initiatives, ethical sourcing, and community wellbeing programmes, reinforcing its position as a proud symbol of Sri Lankan heritage.

With this latest Superbrands recognition, Siddhalepa once again demonstrates that a heritage brand, grounded in authenticity and guided by innovation, can remain relevant, trusted, and admired across generations.
