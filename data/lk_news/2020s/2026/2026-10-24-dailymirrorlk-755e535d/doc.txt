Lanka IOC profit slides despite 38% revenue growth

Lanka IOC PLC’s net profit fell 67 percent to Rs. 962.5 million in the September quarter as sharply lower gross margins pushed the fuel retailer into an operating loss, despite a 38 percent rise in revenue.

The company reported earnings of Rs. 1.81 per share for the second quarter of its 2026/27 financial year, down from Rs. 5.55 a year earlier, according to its unaudited financial statements filed with the Colombo Stock Exchange.

Revenue increased to Rs. 97.31 billion from Rs. 70.36 billion. However, cost of sales rose faster, reaching Rs. 94.84 billion and reducing gross profit by 57 percent to Rs. 2.47 billion.

Gross profit margin narrowed to 2.5 percent from 8.1 percent a year earlier, leaving less income to cover operating expenses.

Lanka IOC consequently recorded an operating loss of Rs. 296 million, compared with an operating profit of Rs. 2.50 billion in the corresponding quarter.

The deterioration came despite selling and distribution expenses falling to Rs. 2.10 billion from Rs. 2.48 billion. Administrative expenses were broadly unchanged at Rs. 800.5 million.

Finance income helped keep the company profitable. It increased to Rs. 1.76 billion from Rs. 1.31 billion, while finance expenses declined to Rs. 83.6 million from Rs. 119.5 million.

Pre-tax profit nevertheless fell to Rs. 1.38 billion from Rs. 3.69 billion.

For the six months ended September 30, net profit declined 53 percent to Rs. 2.49 billion from Rs. 5.28 billion, despite revenue rising 43 percent to Rs. 189.42 billion.

Half-year earnings per share fell to Rs. 4.68 from Rs. 9.92.

Gross profit for the six months dropped to Rs. 6.26 billion from Rs. 10.53 billion, while operating profit declined to Rs. 798 million from Rs. 4.37 billion. Finance income increased to Rs. 2.82 billion from Rs. 2.45 billion.

The balance sheet showed higher borrowings and receivables compared with the financial year-end. Interest-bearing borrowings rose to Rs. 5.90 billion at September 30 from Rs. 1.52 billion at March 31, while trade and other receivables increased to Rs. 23.01 billion from Rs. 15.05 billion.

Inventories declined to Rs. 29.47 billion from Rs. 37.62 billion. Cash and bank balances increased to Rs. 4.91 billion from Rs. 1.75 billion.

Net assets per share stood at Rs. 172.53, compared with Rs. 172.86 at March 31. Indian Oil Corporation held 75.12 percent stake in the company.
