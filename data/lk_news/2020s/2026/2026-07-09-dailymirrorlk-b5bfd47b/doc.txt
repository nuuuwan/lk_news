Archchuna threatens to remove clothes after Govt MP questions his attire

Colombo, July 9 (Daily Mirror) - Independent MP Ramanathan Archchuna in Parliament today threatened to remove his clothes after ruling party MP M. Jeganeeswaran  questioned his attire.

MP Jeganeeswaran questioned about the vaitee which MP Archchuna was clad in.

"There is nothing wrong in wearing a vaitee. I can remove it if the MPs want. It seems that the ruling party MP is suffering from some disease. He can come to me for treatment later," MP Archchuna said.
