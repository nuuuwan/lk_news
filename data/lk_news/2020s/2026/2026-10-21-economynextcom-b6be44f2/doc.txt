Fitch Ratings recalibrates Sri Lanka’s national rating scale

Fitch Ratings-Singapore-21 October 2026: Fitch Ratings has recalibrated its Sri Lanka National Rating scale to reflect changes in the relative creditworthiness among Sri Lankan issuers.

This follows Fitch’s upgrade of Sri Lankas’s sovereign Long-Term Local-Currency Issuer Default Rating (IDR) to ‘B-‌’/Stable from ‘CCC+’‌ on 22 September 2026.

The recalibration may result in rating revisions for some issuers with Sri Lankan national ratings.

National scale ratings are a risk ranking of issuers in a particular market designed to help local investors differentiate risk.

Sri Lanka’s national ratings are denoted by the unique identifier ‘(lka)’.

Fitch adds this identifier to reflect the unique nature of the Sri Lankan national scale.

National scales are not comparable with Fitch’s international ratings scales or with other countries’ national rating scales.
