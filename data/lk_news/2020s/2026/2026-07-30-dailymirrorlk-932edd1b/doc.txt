AMW opens Kandy Suzuki showroom

Associated Motorways (Private) Limited (AMW), an Al-Futtaim company and the sole authorised distributor for the Suzuki vehicles in Sri Lanka, has further strengthened its nationwide presence with the opening of its new dedicated Suzuki showroom in Kandy.

Located at No. 448, Katugasthota Road, Kandy, the new facility marks a significant milestone for the brand as the second dedicated Suzuki showroom in Sri Lanka, following the flagship Suzuki showroom in Borella.

The expansion reflects AMW’s continued commitment to making Suzuki’s globally renowned vehicles and customer-centric services more accessible to the customers across the Central province.

The ceremonial opening was attended by AMW Managing Director Jawahar Ganesh, AMW Sales Director Prasanna De Siva, members of the AMW management team, valued customers, business partners and other distinguished guests.

The new showroom showcases Suzuki’s popular passenger vehicle line-up, including Grand Vitara, Jimny, Fronx, Celerio and Alto K10, providing the customers with the opportunity to explore the latest models and experience Suzuki’s innovation, quality and reliability firsthand. The customers purchasing through AMW Suzuki benefit from a comprehensive ownership experience backed by a three-year or 100,000 km bumper-to-bumper warranty, access to genuine Suzuki parts and accessories and expert after-sales care delivered by Suzuki-trained technicians.

To further enhance customer convenience in the Central province, the owners can also access comprehensive after-sales support at AMW Pitstop – Kandy, located at No. 334/1, Yatiwawala, Katugastota, Kandy, offering vehicle servicing, repairs and body shop facilities.

Ganesh said, “The opening of our dedicated Suzuki showroom in Kandy marks another important milestone in our journey to bring the Suzuki brand closer to the customers across Sri Lanka. As the demand for Suzuki vehicles continues to grow, we remain committed to expanding our network while delivering an exceptional customer experience that extends well beyond the point of purchase. Backed by our trusted after-sales expertise, genuine parts and accessories and manufacturer-backed warranty, we are proud to offer the customers the confidence and peace of mind that come with owning a Suzuki from AMW.”
