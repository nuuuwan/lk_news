Week ends in red

Indices closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Dialog Axiata, Commercial Bank and Hatton National Bank with the turnover crossing Rs. 2.3 Bn. High

net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Nawaloka Hospitals, Hatton National Bank Non- Voting and Ceylon Cold Stores. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers. During the week, the ASPI and the S&P SL20 lost 3.0% and 3.5% respectively, whilst recording an average daily turnover of Rs. 4.04 Bn.

Health Care Equipment & Services sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Nawaloka Hospitals) whilst the sector index lost 1.97%. The share price of Nawaloka Hospitals increased by Rs 0.40 (3.48%) to close at Rs 11.90. The Banking sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Hatton National Bank nonvoting) whilst the sector index decreased by 0.83%. The share price of Hatton National Bank nonvoting lost Rs 2.50 (0.73%) to close at Rs 341.75. Ceylon Cold Stores, Paragon Ceylon and Dipped Products were also included amongst the top turnover contributors.

The share price of Ceylon Cold Stores closed flat at Rs 124.00. The share price of Paragon Ceylon recorded a gain of Rs 65.50 (14.26%) to close at Rs 524.75. The share price of Dipped Products appreciated by Rs 0.10 (0.14%) to close at Rs 69.80.
