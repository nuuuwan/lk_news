New Chairman and members appointed to Public Service Commission

Colombo, July 9 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake has appointed S. A. Nimal Saranatissa as the new Chairman of the Public Service Commission.

The newly appointed members of the Commission are B. Sanath Poojitha, E. R. Weerakoon, R. Ketheeswaran, J. M. R. Jayasundara, E. A. P. N. Edirisinghe, Dr. S. A. A. N. Jayasekara and M. H. Mohammed Sameel.

The letters of appointment were presented to the Chairman and members by Secretary to the President Dr. Nandika Sanath Kumanayake during a ceremony held at the Presidential Secretariat on Wednesday (9).

The appointments were made to fill vacancies created following the expiry of the previous term of the Public Service Commission.
