Suthar six-fer seals India’s 165-run win in Galle

AUG.19 (Mirror Sports) - India sealed a 165-run victory over Sri Lanka in the first Test at Galle, taking a 1-0 lead in the two-match series despite a brave fight from Sonal Dinusha.

Chasing 372 for victory, Sri Lanka were bowled out for 206, with Dinusha once again leading the resistance. The left-hander followed his first-innings century with a determined 84, but found little support as India closed out the match.

Manav Suthar was the key figure for India, finishing with 6 for 55 to break Sri Lanka’s lower-order resistance and complete the victory.

Sri Lanka had earlier been set a daunting target after India made 462 in the first innings and added 193 in their second. Devdutt Padikkal’s 167 and KL Rahul’s 82 formed the backbone of India’s first-innings total.

Sri Lanka responded with 284, with Dinusha making a century, but India retained a 178-run lead before setting up the fourth-innings chase.

Dhananjaya de Silva added 59 in Sri Lanka’s final innings, but the hosts could not build enough partnerships around Dinusha to keep the chase alive.

Once Dinusha departed for 84, Sri Lanka’s resistance quickly faded, allowing India to secure victory and move ahead in the series.

The second and final Test will be played at the SSC in Colombo from August 23 to 27, with Sri Lanka needing a win to level the series.

For Sri Lanka, Dinusha’s century and 84 were the biggest positives from a disappointing result.
