Teachers, principals to report sick leave on November 30

Colombo, Nov. 22 (Daily Mirror) - All teachers and principals across the country are expected to report sick leave on November 30 in protest against the Government's failure to address several long-standing salary and service-related issues through the 2027 Budget.

The Teachers' and Principals' Trade Union Alliance has informed the Secretary to the Ministry of Education of its decision to launch the islandwide trade union action.

The alliance said the action was being taken over the failure to resolve salary anomalies affecting officers of the Sri Lanka Teachers' Service and the Sri Lanka Principals' Service.

Among its key demands are the immediate payment of the remaining two-thirds of the salary increase due to teachers and principals and the payment of a Rs. 25,000 allowance until the outstanding salary discrepancy is fully settled.

The alliance has also called for the payment of the Principals' Allowance to all principal-grade teachers, a telephone allowance for principals and an increase in difficult and extremely difficult service allowances.

Other demands include allocating 6 per cent of the country's Gross Domestic Product (GDP) to education, increasing the number of students covered by the school nutrition programme and its funding, and removing additional burdens imposed on teachers, including the collection of green fees.
