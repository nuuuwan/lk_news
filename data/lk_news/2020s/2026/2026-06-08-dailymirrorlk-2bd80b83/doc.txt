Continental Cars launches Volkswagen Taigun and Virtus in Sri Lanka

New era of German engineering, innovation and premium mobility begins in Sri Lanka

Continental Cars & Commercials (Pvt.) Ltd, the official authorised importer for Volkswagen passenger vehicles in Sri Lanka, ceremonially launched the all-new Volkswagen Taigun and Virtus, at the prestigious Bandaranaike Memorial International Conference Hall recently.

The highly anticipated launch marks a historic milestone for Volkswagen in Sri Lanka, reinforcing the brand’s long-term commitment to the local market while introducing a new generation of premium German-engineered mobility solutions designed specifically for modern Sri Lankan consumers.

The prestigious event brought together senior representatives from the Volkswagen group, diplomats, government officials, corporate leaders, members of the business community, media representatives, industry stakeholders and automotive enthusiasts for an evening celebrating Volkswagen’s global heritage, innovation and future vision for mobility in Sri Lanka.

The launch event featured an immersive unveiling experience combining advanced digital presentations, cultural performances, entertainment segments, executive presentations and dynamic product showcases that highlighted Volkswagen’s globally recognised standards in engineering, technology, safety and design excellence.

The launch was conducted in collaboration with Skoda Auto Volkswagen India (Pvt.) Ltd, the regional operational and manufacturing hub for the Volkswagen group brands in India. Skoda Auto Volkswagen India oversees the Indian operations of globally renowned automotive brands including Volkswagen, Škoda, Audi, Porsche, Lamborghini and Bentley, while driving innovation, localisation, advanced engineering, manufacturing excellence and customer-focused mobility solutions across the South Asian region.

The partnership with Continental Cars & Commercials reflects the Volkswagen group’s broader strategic vision to strengthen its footprint in Sri Lanka by delivering globally benchmarked products supported by world-class after-sales infrastructure, advanced technical expertise and superior customer experiences.

The all-new Volkswagen Taigun enters the Sri Lankan market as a bold and progressive SUV developed for customers seeking versatility, performance, advanced technology and premium comfort. Built on Volkswagen’s internationally acclaimed engineering platform, Taigun combines sporty styling with intelligent innovation, offering a confident and engaging driving experience for both urban and long-distance travel.

The all-new Volkswagen Virtus redefines premium sedan mobility with its combination of elegant styling, refined comfort, advanced technology and exhilarating performance. Designed to deliver an elevated driving experience, Virtus combines sophisticated aesthetics with Volkswagen’s signature German engineering standards, offering customers a sedan that balances luxury, practicality, innovation and performance.

Skoda Auto Volkswagen India MD and CEO Piyush Arora stated, “The introduction of Taigun and Virtus in Sri Lanka marks an important milestone in Volkswagen’s regional growth journey.”

Continental Cars & Commercials Chairman Heinz Reuter stated, “Through the launch of Taigun and Virtus, we are committed to delivering an exceptional ownership experience supported by premium after-sales service, genuine spare parts, internationally trained technical expertise and modern facilities built to global standards.”
