GMOA to join islandwide strike on November 30

Colombo, Nov. 27 (Daily Mirror) - The Government Medical Officers Association (GMOA) has decided to join the islandwide trade union action scheduled for November 30.

The decision was taken today (27) following a meeting of the association’s Central Committee.

The special meeting was convened to make a final decision on whether the GMOA would participate in the planned islandwide strike.
