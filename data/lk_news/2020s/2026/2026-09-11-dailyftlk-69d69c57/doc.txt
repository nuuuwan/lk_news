Lankatiles expands design footprint with 61st showroom in Mount Lavinia

Lankatiles Managing Director Priyantha Talwatte, Senior Archt. Indika Balasooriya, Archt. Chana Samarasinghe, and Chief Guest and Archt. Jayanath Silva

LANKATILES marked another significant milestone in its journey with the opening of its 61st showroom in Mount Lavinia, bringing its distinctive portfolio of design-led surface solutions to one of Colombo’s most celebrated coastal destinations. The new showroom reflects the brand’s commitment to helping customers translate the natural beauty, relaxed character and timeless elegance of coastal living into contemporary interior and exterior spaces.

The opening ceremony was graced by Chief Guest, Architect Jayanath Silva, together with Lankatiles Managing Director Priyantha Talwatte, senior management representatives and distinguished guests from Sri Lanka’s architecture, construction, design and business communities.

Commenting on the opening, Talwatte said: “Mount Lavinia has a unique identity where heritage, coastal living and contemporary urban lifestyles come together. Our 61st showroom has been created to respond to that character by giving homeowners, architects and designers greater access to collections that can transform both indoor and outdoor spaces. As Lankatiles continues to grow, our focus remains on bringing inspiring design, innovation and trusted quality closer to our customers while enabling them to create spaces that reflect the way they want to live.”

The showroom features an extensive selection of Lankatiles wall and floor tiles, including contemporary collections, premium Double Fired wall tiles, Majestica large-format collections, Mosaics by Lankatiles and the brand’s latest innovation, Cool Rooftop Tile, alongside a diverse range of designs for residential and commercial applications.
