Govt refutes claims of barring Russian Prosecutor General from visiting AG’s Office, CIABOC

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Justice Minister Harshana Nanayakkara refuted allegations that the government prevented the Prosecutor General of the Russian Federation, Alexander Gutsan, from visiting the Attorney General’s Department and the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC).

Addressing Parliament, the Minister clarified that the government had made no such decision. Instead, according to information available to him, the move was decided independently by the Russian Prosecutor General’s own security detail after assessing the security situation.

Minister Nanayakkara made this clarification in response to remarks by Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) MP

Chithral Fernando, who cited media reports claiming that Gutsan had been restricted from visiting the two state institutions.
