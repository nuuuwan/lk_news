LOLC General Insurance delivers promise with immediate EV replacements following Cyclone Ditwah

LOLC General Insurance has reinforced its commitment to policyholders by delivering on its promises in the aftermath of recent natural disasters, setting new benchmarks for customer excellence within Sri Lanka’s insurance industry.

As part of a synergistic collaboration with Browns EV, LOLC General Insurance has initiated the replacement of brand-new EV vehicles for its policyholders whose Browns EVs were declared a total loss due to the recent unprecedented disaster caused by Cyclone Ditwah.

This step reflects the company’s customer-first philosophy, ensuring continuity of lifestyle, minimising financial disruption, and providing timely support, reassurance, and peace of mind during challenging times. It forms part of a broader set of measures proactively implemented by the company to assist policyholders and maintain a seamless and transparent claims experience following the disaster.

“At LOLC General Insurance, our promise goes beyond policy wording. It is about standing by our customers during their most challenging moments,” said Kithsiri Gunawardena, Chairman of LOLC General Insurance PLC. “Building on our strong financial position and the backing of the LOLC Group, this initiative, carried out in collaboration with Browns EV, demonstrates our commitment to turning promises into action and delivering real value to our policyholders when it matters most. I also extend my sincere appreciation to Browns EV for their collaboration and support during this critical initiative.”
