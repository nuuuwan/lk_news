Fcode Labs invests in Germany-based coffee startup Bunamo

Bunamo Co-Founder Phill Kopp, Fcode Labs Co-CEO Tharindu Malawaraarachchi, Bunamo Co-Founder Dr. Torben Gräber, and Fcode Labs Co-CEO Buddhishan Manamperi

Fcode Labs, a global product innovation and engineering company, has made a strategic investment in Bunamo, a Europe-based coffee-centric startup building a digital platform for coffee enthusiasts.

Alongside the investment, Fcode Labs partnered with Bunamo to design and engineer a scalable product that brings together home baristas, professional roasters, and coffee brands in one connected experience.

Through the collaboration, Fcode Labs worked closely with Bunamo’s team to translate the startup’s concept into a production-ready platform. The engagement focused on product design, engineering and go-to-market strategies ensuring the platform was built and launched to support content-driven interactions and future expansion across Europe and beyond.

Fcode Labs Co-CEO Buddhishan Manamperi said: “Our decision to invest in Bunamo was driven by the passion of the team and we see a fast-growing coffee community not only across Europe but also in the rest of the world. That opportunity gave us confidence to move forward.”

Founded by Dr. Torben Gräber and Phill Kopp in Germany, Bunamo was created as a dedicated platform for coffee lovers to explore brewing recipes, discover roasters and brands, and engage with a wider coffee community.

Working from a user-centred design approach, Fcode Labs shaped an interface that prioritises clarity and ease of navigation for both casual users and experienced professionals. On the engineering side, Fcode Labs built a scalable architecture capable of supporting content-driven interactions such as recipe sharing and brand exploration.

The platform was developed with a cloud-ready foundation to ensure reliability and performance as the user base grows. Fcode Labs continues to invest in and support product-led startups by combining capital with deep expertise in product design, engineering and go-to-market support, helping founders turn clearly defined ideas into scaling digital platforms.
