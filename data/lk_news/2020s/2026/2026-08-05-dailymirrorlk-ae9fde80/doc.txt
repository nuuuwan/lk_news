Rains to intensify from tomorrow

Colombo, Aug. 5 (Daily Mirror) - The Meteorology Department said the prevailing rainy and windy conditions over the South-Western parts of the island are expected to intensify from tomorrow due to the active South-West monsoon and continue for the next few days.

The Department said heavy rains above 100 mm are likely at some places in the Western and Sabaragamuwa Provinces and in the Kandy, Nuwara Eliya and Galle districts.

It said showers or thundershowers will occur at times in the Western, Sabaragamuwa and North-Western Provinces and the Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Galle and Matara districts while cloudy skies will prevail over these areas.

Showers or thundershowers can be expected at a few places in the Ampara, Batticaloa and Moneragala districts after 2.00 p.m, the Department said.

It said strong winds of about 40 to 50 kmph can be expected at times over the North-Central, Sabaragamuwa, Southern, Central and North-Western Provinces.
