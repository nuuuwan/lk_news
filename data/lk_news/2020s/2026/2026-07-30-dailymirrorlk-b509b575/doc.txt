Colombo High Court drops PTA charges against ’Harak Kata’

Colombo, July 30 (Daily Mirror) - The Colombo High Court has ruled that charges brought under the Prevention of Terrorism Act (PTA) against notorious organized crime figure Nadun Chinthaka Wickramaratne, popularly known as "Harak Kata," and four other defendants cannot be maintained.

The PTA charges had been filed against the suspects over an alleged conspiracy to escape from custody while being held under detention orders at the Criminal Investigation Department (CID).

Delivering the ruling, Colombo High Court Judge Buddhika C. Ragala noted that while the charges under the Prevention of Terrorism Act cannot proceed, there is no legal barrier to maintaining the prosecution against the accused under the Penal Code, given the nature of the alleged offenses.
