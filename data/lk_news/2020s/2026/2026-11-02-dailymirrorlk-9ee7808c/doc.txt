Soysapura shooting: Police release CCTV images of wanted suspect

Colombo, Nov. 2 (Daily Mirror) - Police are seeking public assistance to identify and apprehend a suspect wanted in connection with the shooting and killing of a restaurant owner in the Soysapura area on October 20, 2026.

The Mount Lavinia Division Crime Investigation Bureau is conducting investigations into the incident, which occurred within the Mount Lavinia Police Division.

According to police, photographs and video footage of the suspect, obtained through CCTV recordings, have been released to assist in identifying him.
