India grants Rs. 600 million for Puttalam Hospital upgrade

The Government of India has agreed to provide a grant of Rs. 600 million for the construction of a new building at the Puttalam Base Hospital, with the Government planning to upgrade the hospital under the Central Government.

The Cabinet of Ministers has approved a proposal to construct a six-storey building with a total floor area of 8,640 square metres within four years.

The proposed building will house an Accident and Emergency Unit, clinics, a short-stay inpatient treatment unit, a blood bank, and diagnostic and laboratory facilities.

The Indian grant will cover the construction of the first three floors, where the Accident and Emergency Unit is to be established.

The Cabinet approval also covers entering into a Memorandum of Understanding with the Government of India to obtain the grant.

Puttalam Base Hospital, which previously functioned under the North Western Provincial Council, was approved for takeover by the Central Government at the Cabinet meeting held on March 3, 2026, with the concurrence of the Provincial Governor.

The move is aimed at improving the hospital's infrastructure and ensuring quality and uninterrupted medical services to patients.
