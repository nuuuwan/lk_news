Rice supplier fined Rs. 1 mn for selling Red Nadu above MRP

A rice supplier has been fined Rs. 1 million by the Polonnaruwa Magistrate’s Court after being found guilty of selling Red Nadu rice above the government-imposed Maximum Retail Price (MRP), the Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA) said.

The CAA said legal action was instituted after the supplier was found selling Red Nadu rice at Rs. 225 per kilogram, exceeding the government-approved maximum retail price of Rs. 220 per kilogram introduced in 2022.

The case was heard before the Polonnaruwa Magistrate’s Court on Thursday (August 6), where the court found the supplier guilty and imposed a Rs. 1 million fine.
