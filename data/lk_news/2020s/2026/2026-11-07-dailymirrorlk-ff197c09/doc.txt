Prime Minister returns after CHOGM 2026

Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya returned to Sri Lanka today (7) following her participation in the 28th Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting (CHOGM 2026) held in Antigua and Barbuda.

She arrived at the Bandaranaike International Airport in Katunayake following the conclusion of her official visit.

During the summit, the Prime Minister joined Commonwealth leaders in discussions on key global economic and development priorities, focusing on strengthening cooperation and partnerships among member countries to address shared challenges.

She also held bilateral meetings with leaders and representatives of several Commonwealth member states to explore opportunities to strengthen bilateral relations and cooperation in areas of mutual interest.
