‘The Youthquake’ book launched at BMICH

Dr. Ranga Kalansuriya’s latest book, The Youthquake, based on his experiences in Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Nepal, Myanmar and Indonesia, was launched at the BMICH in Colombo on September 7, 2026. The first copy of the book was presented to Venerable Thrikunamale Ananda Maha Nayaka Thero. The event was attended by Indigenous leader Uruwarige Wanniyala Wanniyala Aththo, members of the Maha Sangha, former Presidents Maithripala Sirisena and Ranil Wickremesinghe, political leaders and other distinguished guests.
