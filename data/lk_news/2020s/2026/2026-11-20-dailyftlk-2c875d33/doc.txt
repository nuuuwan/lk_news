Retired Air Force Officers  Association annual dinner dance

The Retired Air Force Officers Association (RAFOA) held its annual dinner dance at Cinnamon Lakeside on 14 November. Defence Ministry Secretary A.V.M. (Retd.)Sampath Thuiyakontha was the Chief Guest and Air Force Commander Air Marshal Bandu Edirisinghe was the Guest of Honour. Now in its 14th year, the RAFOA dinner dance is a much-anticipated event for retired officers and their families.
