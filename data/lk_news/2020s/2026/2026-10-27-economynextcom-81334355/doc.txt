Sri Lanka Cabinet clears $100mn ADB loan for economic recovery

ECONOMYNEXT – Sri Lanka’s Cabinet has approved a 100 million US dollar loan agreement with the Asian Development Bank to support a macro-recovery programme aimed at strengthening economic resilience and expanding inclusive economic opportunities, minister Nalinda Jayatissa said.

The financing forms part of a larger 620 million US dollar policy-based lending package approved earlier to support Sri Lanka’s reform agenda under the International Monetary Fund’s Extended Fund Facility programme.

The latest tranche consists of a 50 million US dollar policy-based loan and a 50 million US dollar investment loan from the ADB’s ordinary capital resources.

The Department of External Resources has completed formal loan negotiations with the ADB, after which Cabinet approved a proposal submitted by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake in his capacity as Finance Minister to sign the agreement, Jayatssa said.

The Macro-Recovery Sector Development Programme was approved by Cabinet in September 2026 using part of the broader ADB funding package and is intended to support reforms designed to improve economic stability, resilience and inclusive growth. (Colombo/Oct27/2026)
