Adhitya Ayurveda takes traditional Sri Lankan Ayurveda expertise to South Korea

Adhitya Ayurveda, a premier holistic healing organization from Sri Lanka, has expanded its international presence by conducting a wellness knowledge-sharing program in South Korea, in partnership with Shriveda Ayurveda Korea. Led by Dr. Kasun Gunawardhana, the session aimed to educate participants on the core principles of authentic Sri Lankan Ayurveda and its application to modern health issues.

The presentation covered essential topics such as detoxification, digestive health, Panchakarma therapies, and pulse diagnosis, while emphasizing practical daily routines for sustained wellbeing. Dr. Gunawardhana explained that Ayurveda defines health as a harmonious balance of body, mind, and soul, not merely the absence of illness. Participants learned about the concept of “Ama” (toxin accumulation) and the significance of digestive strength. The program highlighted how ancient, traditional wisdom can offer simple yet effective solutions to restore balance in today’s fast-paced world, successfully bridging the gap between Sri Lankan heritage and Korean interest in holistic health.
