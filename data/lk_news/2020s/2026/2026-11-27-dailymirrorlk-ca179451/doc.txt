Sri Lanka seeks New Zealand’s intervention to bring stranded SL woman from Cook Islands

Colombo, Nov. 27 (Daily Mirror) - Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs and Foreign Employment Arun Hemachandra has sought the intervention of New Zealand's Associate Minister of Immigration, Cameron Brewer, to facilitate the safe return of a Sri Lankan woman stranded in the Cook Islands.

Responding to a media query regarding Ruchira Sewwandi Ranasinghe, the Deputy Minister said he had formally written to Brewer seeking his personal intervention and exceptional humanitarian consideration to facilitate her voluntary repatriation to Sri Lanka.

He said the Sri Lankan High Commission in Wellington had been actively involved in the matter from the outset.

While respecting New Zealand's immigration laws and regulatory framework, Hemachandra said the Government hoped to reach a practical and compassionate solution through diplomatic engagement, in keeping with the longstanding friendship between the two countries.

He added that the Government remained committed to protecting the welfare and dignity of Sri Lankan nationals overseas and would continue to pursue diplomatic and consular channels to facilitate her safe return.
