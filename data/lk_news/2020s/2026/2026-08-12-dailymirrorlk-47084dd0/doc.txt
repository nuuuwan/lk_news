Death penalty for drug offences only a proposal: Govt.

Colombo, Aug. 12 (Daily Mirror) - The possible imposition of the death penalty for drug-related offences remains only a proposal, with no final decision having been taken by the Government, Cabinet Spokesman Minister Nalinda Jayatissa said yesterday (11).

Speaking at the Cabinet press briefing, Minister Jayatissa said the proposal had been put forward by certain stakeholders during discussions on the country’s escalating drug problem.

“It is only a proposal made by certain stakeholders. A final decision has not been taken,” he said.

He added that the Government had yet to determine how such a measure, if approved, would be implemented.

This was raised during a meeting of the “Ratama Ekata” (Country Together) National Operation Council held at the Presidential Secretariat on Monday (10).

The meeting, chaired by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake, focused on the escalating drug crisis and its impact on Sri Lanka’s economy and society, according to the President’s Media Division.

Officials pointed out that illicit wealth generated through drug trafficking had contributed to the growth of a criminal ecosystem, posing a serious threat to the national economy, tourism industry and social values.

However, the Government has not yet made a final decision on introducing the death penalty for drug-related offences, with the proposal remaining under consideration.
