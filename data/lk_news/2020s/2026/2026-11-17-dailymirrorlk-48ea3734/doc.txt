Egg prices rise as festive season demand increases

Egg prices in the local market have increased with the onset of the festive season, with eggs currently selling at between Rs. 42 and Rs. 45, the All Ceylon Egg Producers’ Association said.

Association Secretary Anurasiri Marasinghe said the price increase was mainly due to rising production costs and increased demand.

He said heavy rainfall had affected climatic conditions, reducing the productivity of laying hens and lowering production capacity at poultry farms from around 80% to between 50% and 60%.

Marasinghe also pointed out that the rising prices of animal feed ingredients, including maize, had further increased production costs.

Meanwhile, demand for eggs has increased as bakeries step up preparations for the upcoming festive season, he said.

"We believe the price increase is due to supply and demand rather than any artificial price hike," he said.

Meanwhile, All Island Poultry Association President Ajith Gunasekara said egg prices could remain high until January next year.
