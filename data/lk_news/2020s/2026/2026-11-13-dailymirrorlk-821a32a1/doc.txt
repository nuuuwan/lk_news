Indices close in green

The ASPI closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Sri Lanka Telecom, Distilleries and Dialog Axiata with the turnover crossing Rs. 3.0bn.

A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Cargo Boat Development Company, Dialog Axiata and ACL Cables.

Mixed interest was observed in John Keells Holdings, Dipped Products and Tokyo Cement Company nonvoting whilst retail interest was noted in UB Finance Company, Browns Investments and Softlogic Finance.

Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

Capital Goods sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to John Keells Holdings) whilst the sector index gained 0.56%. The share price of John Keells Holdings closed flat at Rs. 19.70. Telecommunication Services sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Dialog Axiata) whilst the sector index increased by 1.11%. The share price of Dialog Axiata increased by Rs. 0.30 (0.60%) to close at Rs. 50.00.

Cargo Boat Development Company, Dipped Products and Tokyo Cement Company nonvoting were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Cargo Boat Development Company gained Rs. 35.50 (17.64%) to close at Rs. 236.75. The share price of Dipped Products recorded a gain of Rs. 0.20 (0.28%) to close at Rs. 71.10. The share price of Tokyo Cement Company nonvoting appreciated by Rs. 5.80 (8.67%) to close at Rs. 72.70
