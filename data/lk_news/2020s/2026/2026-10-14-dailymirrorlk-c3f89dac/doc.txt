Sangeeth Cooray to lead Sri Lanka at Hong Kong Sixes 2026

Colombo, Oct. 14 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Cricket Selection Committee has selected a seven-member squad for the Hong Kong Sixes 2026, scheduled to be held from October 30 to November 1 at the Tin Kwong Road Recreation Ground in Kowloon, Hong Kong.

Sangeeth Cooray has been appointed captain of the Sri Lankan team for the 12-team tournament.

The squad comprises Sangeeth Cooray (Captain), Sachitha Jayatilake, Movin Subasinghe, Ravindu Fernando, Vishen Halambage, Muditha Lakshan and Chamindu Wijesinghe.
