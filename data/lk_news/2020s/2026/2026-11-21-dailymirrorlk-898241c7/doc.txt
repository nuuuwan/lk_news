New apartment complexes planned for Colombo by 2027

The Government plans to address housing and settlement problems in Colombo and other urban areas by 2027 through a programme to construct new apartment complexes in collaboration with the private sector.

The National Housing Development Authority (NHDA) has joined hands with the private sector to develop new apartment complexes to address the shortage of housing in Colombo and its suburbs.

As part of the initiative, Deputy Minister of Housing, Construction and Water Supply T.B. Sarath conducted an inspection tour of several lands owned by the NHDA in Colombo yesterday (20) to assess their suitability for apartment development.

During the visit, the Deputy Minister and officials discussed the current condition of the lands and properties, as well as legal, technical and practical challenges affecting their development. The housing and infrastructure issues faced by residents in the areas were also discussed.

Special attention was given to the problems at the Jana Sevana Housing Complex on Aramaya Road, Dematagoda. The Deputy Minister instructed officials to take immediate steps to address the issues faced by residents.
