No tsunami threat to Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka's coastal areas have been declared safe following a 6.8 magnitude earthquake in the Indian Ocean, the Department of Meteorology said.

The National Tsunami Early Warning Centre issued a no-tsunami-threat bulletin at 3.30 p.m. on November 21, confirming that the earthquake posed no tsunami threat to the country.

According to the preliminary information, the earthquake occurred at 3.12 p.m. at a depth of 156 kilometres, with its epicentre located near Banda Aceh.

The department said the announcement was issued in consultation with relevant national and international agencies.

People living in coastal areas can continue their normal activities, the department added.
