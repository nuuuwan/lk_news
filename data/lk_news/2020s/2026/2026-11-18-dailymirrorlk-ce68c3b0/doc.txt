Pathirana, Hasaranga axed as IPL franchises dump six Sri Lankans

Colombo, Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Six Sri Lankan cricketers, including fast bowler Matheesha Pathirana and star all-rounder Wanindu Hasaranga, have been released by their respective franchises ahead of the Indian Premier League (IPL) 2027 season, following the retention deadline on November 17.

While six players will return to the auction pool, fast bowlers Nuwan Thushara and Eshan Malinga, along with all-rounder Dasun Shanaka, have retained their places for the upcoming season.

A total of nine Sri Lankan players featured in the IPL 2026 season, although Hasaranga, who was picked by Lucknow Super Giants (LSG), was ruled out due to injury.

Among the players released, Pathirana's departure from Kolkata Knight Riders (KKR) is one of the major surprises. The fast bowler endured a disappointing 2026 campaign after suffering an injury in his first match, limiting him to just two appearances.

Hasaranga was also released by LSG as part of a major squad restructuring, with the franchise looking to free up overseas player slots and funds ahead of the mini-auction.

Sunrisers Hyderabad (SRH) have released all-rounder Kamindu Mendis and fast bowler Dilshan Madushanka, while Delhi Capitals have parted ways with fast bowler Dushmantha Chameera and top-order batter Pathum Nissanka.

Meanwhile, defending champions Royal Challengers Bengaluru (RCB) have retained Nuwan Thushara, while Eshan Malinga will continue with SRH following his performances with the ball.

All-rounder Dasun Shanaka has also retained his place with Rajasthan Royals, keeping him in the franchise's plans for the 2027 season.

The IPL 2027 mini-auction is scheduled to be held in Goa on December 16, 2026, where the six released Sri Lankan players will have another opportunity to secure franchise contracts for the upcoming season.
