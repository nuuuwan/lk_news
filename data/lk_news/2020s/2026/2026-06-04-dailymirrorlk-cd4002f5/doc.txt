Eagles’ golf tournament set for June 13-14

The Eagles’ Quadrangle Cup Trophy is unveiled at Air Force Headquarters ahead of the 5th Eagles’ Quadrangle Cup Golf Tournament 2026, set to take place at Eagles’ Citadel Golf Course in Sigiriya on 13 and 14 June.

Air Vice Marshal Gihan Seneviratne ceremonially unveils the Eagles’ Quadrangle Cup Trophy during the media briefing.

The press briefing was attended by the Chairman of SLAF Golf, Air Commodore Erandika Gunewardene, Director Media of the SLAF, Group Captain Nalin Wewakumbura, Secretary Air Force Sports Council, Group Captain Eranda Geeganage, Secretary Air Force Golf, Wing Commander Thushan Fernando, Executive Director of JAT Holdings (Pvt) Ltd, Mr. Aelian Gunawardena, Chief Executive Officer of Browns EV (Pvt) Ltd and LOLC Finance, Mrs. Pavithra Jayasekara, Director – Marketing and Development of Jetwing Hotels Limited, Mr. Hashan Coorey, Chairman of NCG Holdings, Mr. Nikitha Grero and General Manager of TVS Lanka (Pvt) Ltd, Mr. Malaka Vehalla.
