SriLankan Airlines reclaims top inflight Entertainment Award in Asia

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - SriLankan Airlines has reclaimed the APEX Best Entertainment in Central and Southern Asia title while retaining its APEX Four-Star Major Airline rating for the eighth consecutive year at the 2027 APEX FTE EXPO held recently in Singapore.

The dual recognition reflects the airline's continued efforts to enhance its onboard passenger experience, based on feedback and ratings provided directly by travellers.

The APEX Awards are based on more than one million verified passenger votes collected through TripIt by Concur, a travel-organising application. The awards evaluate more than 600 airlines worldwide, recognising excellence in key aspects of the passenger experience, including seat comfort, cabin service, food and beverages, inflight entertainment and Wi-Fi.

Commenting on the achievement, SriLankan Airlines Senior Manager Product Development Maria Sathasivam said the recognition was particularly meaningful as it reflected passengers' actual travel experiences.

She said retaining the Four-Star Major Airline rating for eight consecutive years reaffirmed the airline's commitment to maintaining a consistent passenger experience.

Sathasivam also thanked passengers for their feedback and recognition, while acknowledging the contribution of the airline's employees towards the achievement.

SriLankan Airlines said it had enhanced its inflight entertainment offering with particular attention to the preferences of its South Asian passenger base, which accounts for more than 50 per cent of its global weekly operations across Sri Lanka, India, Pakistan, Nepal, Bangladesh and the Maldives.

The enhancements include an expanded selection of Sinhala entertainment programmes, teledramas presented in box-set format and documentaries covering travel, culture, industry and current affairs.

The airline has also introduced exclusive podcasts and interviews featuring leading Sri Lankan publications, alongside music videos and curated audio collections featuring seasonal playlists, tributes and emerging local artists.

Its regional entertainment portfolio has also been expanded to include a wider selection of Tamil, Hindi and other South Asian films, as well as contemporary regional music across multiple genres.

SriLankan Airlines connects passengers to 130 destinations in 63 countries through its own operations and codeshare partnerships, with direct services to 33 destinations worldwide. Its network covers Europe, Australia, the Middle East, the Indian Subcontinent, Southeast Asia and the Far East.
