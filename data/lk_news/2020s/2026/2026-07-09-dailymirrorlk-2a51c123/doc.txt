World Cup Quarter-finals Set! Eight Teams, Four Blockbuster Battles

- Eight teams remain. Four matches await. Only four will reach the FIFA World Cup 2026 semi-finals.-

The FIFA World Cup 2026 enters its decisive stage with only eight teams left standing. France, Morocco, Spain, Belgium, Norway, England, Argentina and Switzerland will battle across four blockbuster quarter-finals as the race for football's biggest prize reaches its climax.

Morocco get another shot at France after their heartbreaking defeat in the 2022 World Cup semi-finals. The Atlas Lions have impressed with disciplined defending and lethal counterattacks, but Kylian Mbappe and France remain among the tournament favourites in this high-stakes rematch.

Belgium's remarkable run faces its toughest challenge yet against an unbeaten Spain side that has yet to concede a goal. La Roja's defensive dominance will be tested by Romelu Lukaku and a confident Belgian attack.

Erling Haaland and Harry Kane go head-to-head in a blockbuster clash that could shape the Golden Boot race. With both strikers in outstanding form, this quarter-final promises goals and fireworks.

Defending champions Argentina will look to avoid another dramatic escape after surviving two thrilling knockout matches. Switzerland, meanwhile, arrive full of confidence after reaching their first World Cup quarter-final since 1954.

Four matches. Eight teams. Just four places in the FIFA World Cup 2026 semi-finals are up for grabs.
