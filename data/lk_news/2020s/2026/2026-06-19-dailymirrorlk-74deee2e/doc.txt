Calico Colors launches EV initiative and carbon-free vision

Calico Colors Chairman Prabath Gunasekara (in middle) accepts keys of vehicles from Hayleys Chairman and Chief Executive Mohan Pandithage in the presence of Calico Colors Managing Director Hadjireen Rasiff (left)

Calico Colors (Pvt.) Ltd, one of Sri Lanka’s leading apparel trim manufacturers serving globally renowned brands, has reaffirmed its commitment to sustainable manufacturing by launching a comprehensive environmental initiative that includes the introduction of electric vehicles (EVs) for its workforce and an ambitious vision to achieve carbon-free manufacturing by 2030.

As part of its sustainability roadmap, Calico Colors has unveiled its flagship environmental project ‘Green Village in Green Valley’ (Haritha Nimnayee Soduru Gammanaya), which aims to set new benchmarks in environmentally responsible manufacturing while promoting a culture of sustainable living among its employees

In a significant step towards reducing its carbon footprint, Calico Colors has commenced the transition of junior staff vehicles to EVs. The initiative, undertaken in partnership with Hayleys Mobility, forms part of a broader strategy to adopt cleaner and greener transportation solutions while reducing dependence

The company has announced plans to progressively expand the programme with the ultimate objective of transitioning all management and staff vehicles to environmentally friendly EVs, thereby creating a fully sustainable transportation network

Calico Colors Chairman Prabath Gunasekara stated, “Sustainability is no longer an option; it is a responsibility. Our vision is to create a manufacturing environment that delivers excellence while safeguarding the planet for future generations. The adoption of EVs is another important milestone in our journey towards a carbon-free future.”

Calico Colors Managing Director Rasiff Hadjireen noted, “The success of our sustainability agenda depends on the collective efforts of every member of the Calico family. Through innovation, commitment and responsible action, we believe we can inspire positive changes within the industry and beyond. Our goal is not only to manufacture world-class products but also to create a workplace and a business model that exemplifies sustainable living and environmental responsibility.”
