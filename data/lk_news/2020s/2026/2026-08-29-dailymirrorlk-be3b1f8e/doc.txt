Wheat flour prices increased by Rs. 17

Colombo, August 29 (Daily Mirror) - The price of wheat flour has been increased by Rs. 17 per kilogram by Prima Ceylon (Pvt) Ltd and Serendib Flour Mills (Pvt) Ltd, with the new prices taking effect from today.

Meanwhile, the Bakery Owners’ Association is scheduled to meet on Monday to discuss the impact of the flour price hike and decide on possible changes to the prices of bread and other bakery products.
