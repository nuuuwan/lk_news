Huge response to Lanka Business TV’s “Vyavasayaka” Business and Investment Summit in Kalutara

Lanka Business TV’s highly acclaimed “Vyavasayaka” Business and Investment Summit was held with great success recently at the Jie Jie Beach Hotel in Panadura, with the participation of over 250 attendees, including entrepreneurs and those eager to gain economic knowledge.

Speaking at the event, Lanka Business TV Founder and CEO Jayantha Kovilagodage stated that the organisation, which was established with the objective of providing economic knowledge to the people of the country, has already held eight such summits in various districts across the island. He noted that the high level of public enthusiasm and participation is a clear indication of the growing interest in these initiatives.

The Keynote Address of the summit was delivered by Senior Psychological Counselor and Psychotherapist, Dr. Chaminda Weerasiriwardena. The Guest Lecture was delivered by Wasana Bakers Group Managing Director Dayananda Bombuwala. Additionally, valuable presentations were conducted by an expert panel including, NDB Wealth Management Chief Executive Officer Ruwan Perera,

Sri Lanka Computer Emergency Readiness Team (Sri Lanka CERT) Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) Nirosh Ananda and John Keells Properties Head - Asset Management and John Keells Group Vice President Rusiru Abeyasinghe.

Participants at the summit had the opportunity to win valuable prizes, including a chance to win a return air ticket to Malaysia and a holiday at the RIU Ahungalla Hotel.

Lanka Business TV is already making arrangements to hold two such summits in Kandy and Kurunegala for the year 2026.
