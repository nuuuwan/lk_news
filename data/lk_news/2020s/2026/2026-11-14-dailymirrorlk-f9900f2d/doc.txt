Sri Lanka, Pakistan continue winning runs at Emerging Nations Trophy

Nov.14 (Mirror Sports) - Sri Lanka and Pakistan maintained their unbeaten starts to the ICC Women's Emerging Nations Trophy 2026, recording their second consecutive victories on Day 2 in Ajman. Sri Lanka defeated Scotland by four wickets, while Pakistan overcame Thailand by 24 runs to continue their strong campaigns.

Vishmi Gunaratne played a key role in Sri Lanka's successful chase, scoring 49 as her side recovered from a middle-order setback to reach their target with 11 balls remaining.

Batting first, Scotland posted 111 for eight in 20 overs, with Darcey Carter top-scoring with 62. Sugandika Kumari and Sathya Sandeepani claimed two wickets each for Sri Lanka.

Sri Lanka suffered an early setback when Hasini Perera was dismissed for a duck, but Gunaratne and captain Harshitha Samarawickrama, who contributed 24, helped rebuild the innings.

Although Abtaha Maqsood picked up three wickets to put Scotland back in contention, Gunaratne kept Sri Lanka on course before Kaushini Nuthyangana (16 not out) guided the team to 115 for six in 18.1 overs.

Pakistan also secured their second successive victory, defeating Thailand by 24 runs after posting an impressive 180 for two.

Ayesha Zafar led the batting effort with an unbeaten 59 off just 26 deliveries, supported by Shawaal Zulfiqar (42) and Sadaf Shamas (37). Captain Muneeba Ali remained unbeaten on 37 as Pakistan finished strongly.

Thailand responded with an aggressive start, led by Natthakan Chantham (43) and Phannita Maya (33), who shared a 74-run opening partnership. However, Tasmia Rubab turned the match in Pakistan's favour, claiming four wickets for 18 runs, including three in the final over, as Thailand finished on 156 for eight.

Elsewhere, Ireland registered their first victory of the tournament with a convincing 61-run win over hosts UAE. Captain Gaby Lewis struck 71 off 40 balls to help Ireland post 171 for five before restricting the UAE to 110 for eight.

Bangladesh also bounced back from their opening-day defeat, recording a nine-wicket victory over Zimbabwe. Dilara Akter led the chase with an unbeaten 68, while Shathi Rani contributed 45 as Bangladesh reached 132 for one in 17.3 overs, chasing Zimbabwe's 131 for seven.
