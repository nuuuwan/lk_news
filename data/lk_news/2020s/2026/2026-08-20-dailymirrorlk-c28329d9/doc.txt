Retirement declarations of assets and liabilities introduced

Colombo, Aug. 20 (Daily Mirror) - The Anti-Corruption Amendment Bill which was presented to Parliament yesterday provides provisions to make post retirement declaration of assets and liabilities legal. The amendment of Section 90 of the Anti-Corruption Act of 2023 allows a person to declare his or her assets and liabilities even after retirement.

Section 88 of the Act is to be amended to make clarity to declaration of assets and liabilities and protects the privacy of the declaration of assets and liabilities. Accordingly, any person who has obtained a redacted version of a declaration of assets and liabilities of any other person shall not use such declaration for any other purpose than submitting such declaration to any officer or any institution under section 86 of the Bill. A person who uses a redacted version of declaration of assets and liabilities of a person in violation of such a person’s privacy, commits an offence and shall of conviction after a summary trial by a magistrate will be liable to a fine not exceeding hundred thousand rupees or to a term of imprisonment not exceeding one year or to both such fine and imprisonment.

The section 84 of the Anti-Corruption Act of 2023 is amended to provide provisions for the investigation on matters pertaining to conflicts of interest.

Another salient feature of the Bill is the applying of the laws in the Anti-Corruption Act to trade unions with more than one thousand members. The laws will also apply to proprietors , Chairpersons and directors of media companies registered at the Ministry of the Minister assigned the subject of mass media and licensed under section 22 of the Sri Lanka Telecommunications Act No 25 of 1991.
