First Capital Holdings donates Rs. 15 m to ‘Rebuilding Sri Lanka’ fund

First Capital Holdings PLC recently donated Rs. 15 million to the ‘Rebuilding Sri Lanka’ fund, established to provide relief to communities affected by the cyclone Ditwah.

The cheque was presented by First Capital Holdings PLC Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer Dilshan Weerasekara to Secretary to the President Dr. Nandika Sanath Kumanayake at the Presidential Secretariat.

First Capital Holdings PLC Board of Directors Randinith Madanayake and Diluni Dhanushika were also present at the occasion.
