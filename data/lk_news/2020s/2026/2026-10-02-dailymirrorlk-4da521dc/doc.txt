Lal Kantha critical of two private media channels

Colombo, Oct. 2 (Daily Mirror) - Agriculture Minister K. D. Lal Kantha accused two private media channels of deliberately distorting statements made by Government representatives instead of reporting national policy matters accurately.

Speaking at a public event, Lal Kantha alleged that the two media institutions had repeatedly taken his remarks out of context and portrayed them in a manner that invited criticism of himself, the Ministry Secretary and the Government.

The Minister’s remarks followed criticism over his recent statement that Sri Lanka does not need to increase the extent of land currently used for paddy cultivation.

Lal Kantha said his position was based on scientific assessments and the country’s actual agricultural requirements. However, he alleged that the two media channels had presented his statement as unreasonable rather than examining the facts behind it.

He accused the institutions of focusing on sensationalising and distorting statements instead of informing the public about broader agricultural policies and national priorities.

The Minister also criticised what he described as the conduct of the two media institutions, comparing their approach to that of thugs.

He further claimed that the public was becoming increasingly aware of the manner in which the two institutions reported on statements made by Government representatives.
