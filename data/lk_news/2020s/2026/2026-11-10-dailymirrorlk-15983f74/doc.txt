Seylan Bank partners Kedalla Construction Expo 2026

Seylan Bank has joined hands with Kedalla Construction Expo 2026 as its Title & Official Banking Partner for the 15th consecutive year.

Recognised as the largest lifestyle exhibition in the country, the Expo will be held at the BMICH from 13th to 15th November, bringing homemakers face to face with the products and services needed to build, furnish and refresh a home.

Visitors to the Seylan Bank stall will find the home financing journey made simpler. The Bank’s ‘Home Loan Specialists’ will be on hand to guide customers through every stage, from completing the loan application to the final granting of the facility, at a competitive interest rate.

Visitors to the Seylan Bank stall during the Expo can enjoy a range of exclusive benefits, starting with a free CRIB credit report for both existing and potential customers. They can also receive a free home loan evaluation, followed by on-the-spot approvals for eligible salaried customers. Seylan Home Loan Specialists will be available to provide end-to-end assistance throughout the process, while visitors can also benefit from a special discount on the processing fee.

Commenting on the Bank’s enduring association with the Expo, Eugene Seneviratne, Deputy General Manager – Retail Banking atSeylan Bank PLC said, “Returning to Kedalla is a reflection of our partnership and the value it brings homebuyers, builders, and renovators by uniting them together under one roof, giving us the opportunity to meet them with financial solutions that are straightforward, supportive, and built around their plans for the future.”

Organised by Asia Exhibition and Conventions Pvt Ltd for the 20th consecutive year, Kedalla Construction Expo 2026 will host over 200 trade stalls presenting a diverse range of lifestyle products and services. Visitors can browse household appliances, home decoration, housing and real estate, landscaping, furniture, tiles and granite, bathroom fittings, kitchenware, electrical goods, paints, wall designs, ceiling decorations, lighting and home drapery, alongside banks and insurance companies, making it an ideal destination for homemakers seeking inspiration and choice in one place.
