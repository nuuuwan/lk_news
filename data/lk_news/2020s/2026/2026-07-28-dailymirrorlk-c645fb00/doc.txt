Businessman arrested in Gampola over Rs. 2 Mn bribery scheme for hospital tender

Colombo, July 28 (Daily Mirror) - Officers from the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) have arrested a businessman and another individual in Gampola for allegedly soliciting, accepting, and aiding in a bribe of Rs. 2,000,000.

The case stems from the bidding process to supply food ingredients for the Peradeniya Teaching Hospital's kitchen for the year 2027. According to investigators, the suspect had obtained official tender application forms under the names of his father and uncle. He offered to withhold these competing bids so that the complainant could secure the supply contract.

During negotiations, the main suspect initially demanded a bribe of Rs. 3 million, which was later reduced to Rs. 2.5 million, before eventually settling on Rs. 2 million.

Following a raid on July 27, 2026, CIABOC investigation officers arrested the suspects at Ratgala, near the Gampola Post Office. The businessman, identified as Arunagiri Nadar Logendran, was taken into custody at around 9:13 PM, while his accomplice, Logendran Sivaraj, was arrested shortly after at around 9:43 PM.

Both suspects face charges of soliciting, receiving, and aiding and abetting a bribe, and are scheduled to be produced before the Gampola Magistrate's Court for further legal proceedings.
