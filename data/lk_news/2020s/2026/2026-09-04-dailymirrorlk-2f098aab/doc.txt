Asiri Medical Hospital refurbishes Ward 3B

Asiri Medical Hospital unveiled its newly refurbished Ward 3B, now ready to welcome patients and visitors.

The ward features modern amenities, aimed at improving patient privacy, comfort and overall well-being, underscoring the hospital’s commitment to delivering high-quality healthcare.

This investment reflects the hospital’s ongoing commitment to creating an environment where clinical expertise is complemented by comfort, reassurance and compassionate care.

The opening ceremony was attended by Asiri Hospital Holdings Chairman Ashok Pathirage and Dr. Manjula Karunaratne.

By continuously enhancing its infrastructure, Asiri Medical Hospital aims to ensure that every stage of the patient journey, from admission through treatment and recovery, is supported by facilities designed around patient needs.

Located on the third floor of Asiri Medical Hospital, the refurbished Ward 3B has been thoughtfully redesigned to provide patients with a modern, welcoming and healing environment throughout their stay.

Every aspect of the refurbishment has been planned with the patient experience in mind, creating spaces that promote comfort, privacy and recovery while supporting the delivery of high-quality clinical care.

Asiri Medical Hospital Director Operations Dr. Kushan Wijeweera said, “The refurbishment of Ward 3B reflects our ongoing commitment to delivering both clinical excellence and a truly patient-centred experience. Asiri Medical Hospital continues to invest in advanced infrastructure that enhances patient comfort while supporting our healthcare professionals in delivering the highest standards of care. This is part of our broader vision to continuously strengthen our facilities in line with the evolving needs of our patients.”

Looking ahead, the hospital plans to upgrade all rooms with advanced facilities. Construction of a new state-of-the-art operating theatre complex is also set to commence soon, further expanding its existing surgical facilities.
