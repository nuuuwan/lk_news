BOI unveils ‘Ready to Invest’ platform to drive next wave of foreign investment

In a bold move aimed at accelerating foreign direct investment and positioning Sri Lanka as a leading investment destination in the Indian Ocean region, the Board of Investment of Sri Lanka (BOI) has launched its flagship digital investment platform, “Ready to Invest,” offering global investors direct access to 30 structured, project-ready ventures across some of the country’s most strategic sectors.

The platform, now live at readytoinvestsl.com⁠, is expected to serve as a one-stop gateway for international investors seeking immediate entry into Sri Lanka’s rapidly recovering economy.

The projects featured on the platform span eight high-potential sectors — Tourism and Leisure, Manufacturing, Transport and Logistics, ICT, Innovation and Tech Park Development, Agriculture and Agro Processing, Urban, Residential and Mixed-use Development, Aviation, Ports and Maritime Infrastructure, and Renewable Energy and Sustainability.

The launch comes amid growing international confidence in Sri Lanka’s economic recovery following sweeping fiscal and structural reforms undertaken in recent years.

Acting Chairman of the BOI, Dr. Sulakshana Jayawardena, described the initiative as a transformational step in Sri Lanka’s investment promotion strategy, noting that the country was now moving decisively from recovery to growth.

He said the “Ready to Invest” initiative was designed to eliminate traditional investment bottlenecks by presenting investors with bankable, implementation-ready projects backed by policy support and institutional facilitation.

Investment officials said Sri Lanka’s economic fundamentals have shown remarkable resilience, with the country recording 5.0 percent GDP growth in both 2024 and 2025, signalling sustained recovery momentum.

Foreign Direct Investment inflows surged to USD 1.06 billion in 2025 — a 73 percent increase over the previous year — reflecting renewed investor confidence in Sri Lanka’s macroeconomic stability and reform agenda.

The BOI also disclosed that nearly USD 1.9 billion worth of BOI-approved projects are now ready to break ground, potentially generating thousands of jobs and stimulating economic activity across multiple sectors.

Sri Lanka’s investor-friendly regulatory framework continues to be a major attraction, with 100 percent foreign ownership permitted in most sectors and full repatriation of capital and profits guaranteed.

Officials further highlighted Sri Lanka’s strategic location along one of the world’s busiest maritime trade routes, its highly literate and English-speaking workforce, and expanding infrastructure network as key advantages for global businesses seeking regional operational hubs.

Economic observers say the launch of the “Ready to Invest” platform signals a new phase in Sri Lanka’s economic diplomacy, with the country aggressively courting high-quality foreign investments capable of driving exports, technology transfer, innovation, and sustainable development.

Analysts also noted that the initiative reflects a growing urgency within policymakers to convert economic stabilisation into tangible growth, employment creation, and long-term investor partnerships.

With Sri Lanka now actively showcasing ready-to-launch projects rather than merely promoting potential opportunities, the BOI’s latest initiative is widely viewed as a strategic attempt to reposition the country as a competitive and credible destination for global capital.
