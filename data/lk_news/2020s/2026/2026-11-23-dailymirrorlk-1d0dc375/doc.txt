Renovated Galadari unveils new Radisson Blu identity

Colombo, Nov. 23 (Daily Mirror) - The Galadari Hotel in Colombo Fort has been rebranded as the ‘Radisson Blu Hotel Galadari Colombo’ following an extensive renovation and upgrading programme.

The hotel's new brand was officially unveiled yesterday under the patronage of Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Vijitha Herath.

The renovated hotel features outdoor swimming pools, a spa, fitness facilities, conference and banquet halls, restaurants and bars.

Listed on the Colombo Stock Exchange, the hotel said the unveiling of its new brand marked a significant milestone in its transformation process.

The hotel is scheduled to become fully operational by December.

A large gathering of officials and representatives from the hospitality and tourism sectors attended the brand launch ceremony.
