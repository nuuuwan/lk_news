Concessions announced for teachers’ efficiency bar examinations

The Public Service Commission has approved several concessions for teachers serving in national and provincial schools following the introduction of a new efficiency bar examination system under the amended Teachers’ Service Minute.

The concessions have been introduced as teachers are no longer able to complete the efficiency bar requirements under the previous module system following the amendments published in Extraordinary Gazette No. 2444/21 dated July 8, 2025.

According to the Education Ministry, the Public Service Commission informed the Ministry of its decision on September 7, 2026.

Under the decision, teachers who were promoted to Grade I of the Teachers’ Service between October 23, 2022 and June 30, 2025 will be exempted from the requirement to complete Efficiency Bar III.

The Commission has also decided to exempt officers who have already been promoted from completing the efficiency bar examination required before the grade to which they were promoted, provided that they complete the efficiency bar examination required before their next promotion.

In addition, officers promoted between June 30, 2025 and the date of the efficiency bar examination applicable to their respective grade will be granted grade promotions without considering the completion of the relevant module or efficiency bar examination.

The provision applies to the first efficiency bar examination in accordance with Gazette No. 2494 dated June 19, 2026, the second efficiency bar examination in accordance with Gazette No. 2498 dated July 17, 2026, and the third efficiency bar examination in accordance with Gazette No. 2503 dated August 21, 2026.

The Education Ministry said an educational circular would be issued in due course to inform all relevant parties of the decision.
