Supreme Court upholds Shashi Weerawansa’s two-year prison sentence

Colombo, Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - The Supreme Court has upheld the two-year prison sentence imposed on Shashi Weerawansa, wife of former Minister Wimal Weerawansa, after dismissing her appeal against her conviction for possessing an irregular diplomatic passport.

Shashi Weerawansa was initially sentenced to two years in prison and fined Rs. 100,000 by the Colombo Magistrate’s Court on May 27, 2022, after being found guilty of the offence.

Challenging the conviction, she subsequently appealed to the Colombo High Court. However, the High Court dismissed her appeal on June 11, 2026, affirming the Magistrate’s Court ruling and the sentence imposed.

She then approached the Supreme Court, seeking to overturn the High Court decision.

After considering her appeal, the Supreme Court dismissed it, thereby upholding her conviction and the two-year prison sentence.
