CSE tests 21,200 as trading activity remains subdued

The Colombo Stock Exchange extended its decline for a second consecutive session on 27 November 2026, with both benchmark indices closing lower as selling pressure persisted through most of the day.

The ASPI declined 46.67 points, or 0.22%, to 21,198.24, while the S&P SL20 fell 11.17 points, or 0.18%, to 6,039.02. The ASPI remained volatile during early trading before gradually weakening and closing below the 21,200 level.

Market turnover remained subdued at LKR 1.43 billion, well below the YTD daily average of LKR 3.42 billion, while share volume stood at just 39.50 million shares. C T Holdings recorded the highest individual turnover at approximately LKR 306.90 million.

Crossings accounted for around 26.5% of total equity turnover, led by two C T Holdings blocks totaling approximately LKR 306.90 million. HNB.N, Singer Sri Lanka and Watawala Plantations also recorded notable crossings.

Market breadth remained slightly negative, with 93 gainers against 109 decliners, resulting in an advance-decline ratio of 0.85. Foreign investors, however, remained net buyers with an inflow of approximately LKR 20.47 million.

Commercial Bank was the largest negative contributor to the ASPI, followed by Singer Industries, LOLC Holdings, Hatton National Bank and Laugfs Gas. Carson Cumberbatch provided the strongest positive contribution.

The combination of weak participation, subdued turnover and the ASPI closing below 21,200 indicates that buying conviction remains limited despite continued foreign inflows. The 21,100 to 21,200 region now becomes the immediate support area, while a recovery above 21,300 would be needed to improve short-term momentum.
