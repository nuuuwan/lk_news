UK care leader praises IIHS for building a globally ready health workforce

Professor Martin Green OBE, Chief Executive of Care England, commended Sri Lanka’s International Institute of Health Sciences (IIHS) for preparing compassionate, skilled professionals aligned with UK care standards. Following a campus visit, Green highlighted IIHS’s blend of academic rigor and practical training – epitomized by its Certificate in Health and Social Care Assistance – as vital for meeting rising global demand amid aging populations. With over 20 years in healthcare education, IIHS strengthens international links, fosters cultural adaptability, and equips graduates for careers both locally and overseas, while stressing that employment paths depend on host-country regulations.

According to IIHS, Professor Green’s engagement with IIHS further strengthens the institution’s links with the global healthcare community and enables students to gain valuable insight into international professional standards, workplace expectations and best practices.
