2008 murder case: Eight former EPDP members sentenced to death

Batticaloa, Nov 19 (Daily Mirror) - The Batticaloa High Court today (Nov. 19) sentenced eight former members of the Eelam People's Democratic Party (EPDP) to death over the abduction and murder of a man in the Eravur Police Division in 2008.

High Court Judge T.J. Prabhakaran also ordered the acquittal and release of the ninth accused, who had been indicted in connection with the incident.

The court was told that on June 19, 2008, a group of EPDP members stationed at the party's Chenkalady camp abducted Devadas Suresh Kumar in a van, murdered him and buried his body before planting a banana sapling over the burial site.

Following investigations, Eravur Police arrested nine EPDP members and initiated legal proceedings before the Batticaloa Magistrate's Court.

After the preliminary magisterial inquiry concluded, the case was referred to the Batticaloa High Court in 2016 for trial.

Following a lengthy trial, during which the van allegedly used in the abduction was also recovered, the High Court found eight of the accused guilty and imposed the death sentence.
