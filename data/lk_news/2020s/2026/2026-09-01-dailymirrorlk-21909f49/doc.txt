Janashakthi Finance relocates Kandy branch

Janashakthi Finance PLC has relocated its Kandy branch to a more accessible and customer-friendly location at No. 40, Cross Street, Kandy, as part of its continued efforts to elevate customer convenience and deliver a better service experience.

The new branch premises provide improved accessibility and enhanced facilities, creating a more comfortable and efficient environment for the customers to access Janashakthi Finance’s range of financial solutions. The relocation reflects the company’s focus on bringing its services closer to the customers while strengthening the overall branch experience.

The Kandy branch continues to offer a comprehensive portfolio of financial solutions, including deposits, savings accounts, leasing, gold loans, alternative finance solutions, corporate and SME financing and other tailored financial services designed to support both individual and business needs.

With the new location, Janashakthi Finance continues to strengthen its presence in Kandy while focusing on delivering greater convenience, responsive service and a customer experience aligned with the evolving needs of its customers.
