Government allocates Rs. 749 Billion for education in 2027 Budget: PM

Colombo, Nov. 13 (Daily Mirror) - The government has allocated Rs. 749 billion for the education sector in the 2027 Budget, including a record Rs. 104 billion for capital expenditure, Prime Minister and Education Minister Harini Amarasuriya announced today.

Speaking during the Budget debate, she said the allocation accounts for 12.12 percent of total government expenditure.

Responding to Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa’s allegation that the government had failed to provide even a single smart board to schools, Amarasuriya rejected the claim, stating that 4,570 smart boards had already been distributed to schools across the country.

She further said that steps were underway to provide internet connections to secondary schools, with 6,000 connections expected to be established by December this year.
