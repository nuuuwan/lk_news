WindForce secures Letter of Award for 50 MW Mullikulam Wind Power Project – Lot 1

WindForce PLC has been awarded the official Letter of Award by the Ceylon Electricity Board (CEB) for Lot 1 of the 50 MW Mullikulam Wind Power Project, confirming WindForce’s position as the Lowest Cost Bidder for one of Sri Lanka’s most strategically significant renewable energy developments.

The award follows the successful completion of all statutory and regulatory approvals, including clearance from the Cabinet Appointed Negotiating Committee (CANC), the Cabinet of Ministers, and the Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka (PUCSL)—underscoring the robustness, transparency, and credibility of the national procurement process.
