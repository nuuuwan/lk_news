London scene for Rajiv

Rajiv Sebastian is billed to perform in London, at ‘Funky Bash 70s’, a dinner dance organised by the UK Old Girls Association of Ananda Balika Vidyalaya.

It will be held at the Double Tree by Hilton, on 28th February, and Rajiv says he is eagerly looking forward to this prestigious event.

He will have for company the UK-based band Hasthi who will provide the music for Rajiv to move into action.

Rajiv also mentioned that he was involved in Corrine Almeida’s latest release ‘Valentine’ (‘Malsara‘), created for Valentine’s Day, and which is now available on YouTube, Spotify and other digital platforms.

Rajiv, in fact, was also responsible for Corrine’s big hit  ‘Mage Konde Nethath.’
