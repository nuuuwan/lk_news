President accepts invitation to visit Bangladesh early next year

Colombo, Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - State Minister for Foreign Affairs of Bangladesh, Humaiun Kobir, who called on President Anura Kumara Dissanayake extended an invitation to visit Bangladesh early next year.

The meeting took place at the Presidential Secretariat in Colombo.

During the meeting, the formal invitation of the Prime Minister of Bangladesh  Tarique Rahman was handed over.

The meeting also provided an opportunity to exchange views on bilateral cooperation, trade and investment, regional connectivity and shared development aspirations.
