Sri Lankan Rupee appreciates slightly against USD

Colombo, Nov. 23 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lankan Rupee has appreciated slightly against the US Dollar today (23), compared to last Friday, with both the buying and selling rates declining, according to the Central Bank of Sri Lanka.

The Central Bank reported that the US Dollar buying rate had decreased from Rs. 328.35 to Rs. 328.23, while the selling rate had fallen from Rs. 337.28 to Rs. 337.08.
