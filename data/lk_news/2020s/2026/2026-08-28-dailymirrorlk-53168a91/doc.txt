Central Highlands Protection Authority Bill to be presented to Parliament soon: President

Colombo, Aug. 28 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake said today that the Bill to establish the Central Highlands Protection Authority is expected to be presented to Parliament for approval by the end of this year or early next year.

Speaking at a ceremony held in Bandarawela to hand over houses under the “Navodaya Housing Project” at Punagala Kabaragala Mountain, the President said the Authority would be established with the assistance of the Asian Development Bank (ADB).

He said the proposed Authority would be vested with full powers to oversee and address matters relating to the protection and conservation of the Central Highlands.

The President acknowledged that some people could be affected by measures taken to protect the Highlands, including the possible loss of houses or plantation lands.

However, he stressed that such measures should be implemented while considering the protection of the Central Highlands as a national responsibility.

President Dissanayake said the damage already caused to the Central Highlands could take decades to reverse, emphasizing the need to prevent further destruction immediately.
