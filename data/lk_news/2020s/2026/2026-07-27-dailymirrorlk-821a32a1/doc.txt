Indices close in green

The ASPI edged up as a result of price gains in counters such as John Keells Holdings, Dialog Axiata and Aitken Spence with the turnover crossing Rs. 2.0 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. Crossings were witnessed in John Keells Holdings and Hatton National Bank, Hayleys accounting for 50.3% of the turnover.

Mixed interest was observed in Sampath Bank, Nations Trust Bank and Ceylon Cold Stores whilst retail interest was noted in Industrial Asphalts, LVL Energy Fund and LOLC Finance. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net buyers.

Capital Goods sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to John Keells Holdings and Hayleys) whilst the sector index gained 0.77%. The share price of John Keells Holdings increased by Rs 0.20 (1.02%) to close at Rs 19.80. The share price of Hayleys closed flat at Rs 225.00.

The Banking sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Sampath Bank, Hatton

National Bank and Nations Trust Bank ) whilst the sector index edged down by 0.03%. The share price of Sampath Bank lost Rs 0.25 (0.18%) to close at Rs 136.75. The share price of Hatton National Bank moved up by Rs 1.00 (0.26%) to close at Rs 386.00. The share price of Nations Trust Bank edged downRs 0.25 (0.08%) to close at Rs 305.00.
