Kandy Esala Perahera kicks off today

Colombo, Aug. 18 (Daily Mirror) - The Kandy Sri Dalada Maligawa Esala Perahera will parade the streets tonight.

The processions of the four Devalas will set off at 6.28 pm and arrive at the Sri Dalada Maligawa, and the main procession carrying the Casket of Sacred Relics will set off at the auspicious hour of 6.52 pm and parade the streets.

It will proceed along Dalada Weediya, D.S.Senanayaka Weediya, Deva Weediya, Colombo Street, Yatinuwara Weediya and return to the Sri Dalada Maligawa along Raja Weediya.

Crowds had thickened from last morning and taken position along the streets to watch the grand pageant in the hill capital of Kandy.

They had reserved space by spreading mats and polythene sheets by the roadsides along the route taken by the procession. The streets, house verandahs, and upstairs are already occupied by the spectators.

It is said that the elders take a particular interest in providing the opportunity to the children to catch a glimpse of the grand cultural pageant to dispel the evil planetary influence on them, if any.

However, a senior police official said the individuals occupying space by spreading mats and polythene hours before the procession do so in the hope of selling them for exorbitant prices, and it would be investigated.
