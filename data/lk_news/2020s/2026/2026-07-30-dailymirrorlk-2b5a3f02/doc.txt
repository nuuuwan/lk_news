Two killed after cab crashes against tree in Vavuniya

Colombo, July 30 (Daily Mirror) - Two persons were killed after a cab transporting vegetables crashed against a tree in the Kanagarayankulam area along the Jaffna–Kandy main road in Vavuniya early today, police said.

Police said the accident occurred when the cab, which was travelling from Dambulla to Jaffna carrying a load of vegetables, veered off the road and collided against a roadside tree.

Preliminary investigations suggest that the driver may have fallen asleep at the wheel, causing the vehicle to lose control.

The injured victims were admitted to the Mankulam Hospital, where they later succumbed to their injuries.

The bodies have been transferred to the Vavuniya Hospital mortuary, where post-mortem examinations are scheduled to be conducted.
