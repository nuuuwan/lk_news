Sri Lanka, Türkiye sign MoU to boost agricultural cooperation

Sri Lanka and Turkiye this week signed a Memorandum of Understanding in Ankara to strengthen cooperation in agriculture.

The signatories were Foreign Affairs and Foreign Employment Deputy Minister Arun Hemachandra and Turkiye Deputy Minister of Agriculture and Forestry Dr. Ahmet Bagcı.

The MoU marks an important step towards strengthening bilateral cooperation in agriculture, providing a framework for technical collaboration, knowledge sharing, capacity building and the exchange of agricultural expertise.

The instrument is expected to facilitate practical cooperation between the relevant institutions of the two countries, contributing to agricultural development, productivity and food security.
