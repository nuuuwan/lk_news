OMICRON launches CPX 200 ecosystem in Colombo, strengthening commitment to Sri Lanka’s power sector

Participants at the CPX 200 ecosystem launch held at the Galaradi Hotel in Colombo

OMICRON recently officially launched the CPX 200 ecosystem, a compact multifunctional test set for commissioning, maintenance, and condition assessment of medium- and high-voltage assets, at a special event held at the Galadari Hotel, in collaboration with its local partner, Olex Projects Ltd.

The event brought together representatives from the Ceylon Electricity Board (CEB), Lanka Electricity Company (LECO), and many other leading organisations within Sri Lanka’s power and industrial sectors.

OMICRON has maintained a strong presence in Sri Lanka since the early 2000s, playing a vital role in transforming testing practices across substations and power plants. Its advanced testing solutions have supported utilities, engineers, and service providers in improving system reliability, ensuring accurate commissioning, and enhancing maintenance standards across the national grid. Over the past two decades, OMICRON’s technologies have contributed significantly to modernising electrical testing procedures and strengthening the stability and performance of Sri Lanka’s power infrastructure.

As OMICRON’s trusted local partner, Olex Projects Ltd. has been instrumental in introducing these advanced technologies to the Sri Lankan market. Through close collaboration with utilities, contractors, and industrial clients, Olex Projects has actively supported the adoption of modern testing solutions, provided technical expertise, and contributed to improving the quality, reliability, and efficiency of electrical networks across the country.

This launch further reinforces OMICRON’s long-term commitment to Sri Lanka and highlights the continued partnership between OMICRON and Olex Projects in supporting the nation’s evolving energy sector with reliable, future-ready solutions.
