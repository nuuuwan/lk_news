Fly A World Consultants and Tourism recognised for excellence in travel consultancy

Fly A World Consultants and Tourism (Pvt) Ltd has been honoured with the Excellence in Travel Consultancy Services Award in the Small Scale Services Sector at the Honor of Business Talent 2026, recognising the company’s outstanding contribution to Sri Lanka’s travel and tourism industry. The award celebrates organisations that demonstrate excellence in customer service, business performance, and professional standards. It highlights Fly A World’s commitment to providing reliable, personalised, and high-quality travel solutions for both leisure and business travellers. Founded by M. C. M. Shahood, Founder and Director, Fly A World Consultants and Tourism has established itself as a trusted name in the travel industry. The company is a Civil Aviation Authority of Sri Lanka licensed travel agency (Air Transport Licence – Group 6(A), Passenger Transport, Licence No. A-1642), a registered member of the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority (SLTDA), and a member of the Travel Agents Association of Sri Lanka (TAASL), demonstrating its commitment to maintaining the highest industry standards.
