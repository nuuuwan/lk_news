Representative-designate assumes duties in State of Palestine

Representative-designate to the State of Palestine M.F. Mohamed Fawzer assumed duties on 6 May at the Representative Office of Sri Lanka in Ramallah.

Upon assuming duties, he addressed the office staff and emphasised the importance of strengthening bilateral relations between Sri Lanka and Palestine, including in the political, economic, and cultural spheres, as well as enhancing services to the Sri Lankan community in Palestine.

He expressed his sincere appreciation to the staff for their dedicated service and commitment during the recent challenging and uncertain period in Palestine.
