Walk-in services suspended at DMT Head Office from Nov. 23

All public services at the Narahenpita Head Office of the Department of Motor Traffic (DMT) will be provided strictly through an advance appointment system from November 23.

The department said it had decided to introduce the system to improve service delivery and reduce unnecessary queues at its head office.

To accommodate the public, the DMT has more than doubled the number of daily appointment slots compared to the number available before November 23, allowing more people to obtain services each day.

Members of the public can book appointments by calling 011 2 117 116 or through the online portal at https://dmtappointments.dmt.gov.lk.

Commissioner General of Motor Traffic Thushara Fernando requested the public to avoid arriving at the Narahenpita office without prior appointments and to use the advance booking system to save time and obtain services conveniently.

The department said the new arrangement would apply to all public services provided by its Narahenpita Head Office.
