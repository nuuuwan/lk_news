Innox Holdings opens new showroom

Innox Holdings, a supplier of architectural glass solutions, opened its new showroom recently.

Located at Kandawatta Road, Nugegoda, the new showroom allows customers to get a first-hand experience of the company’s entire product range. On display are solar control glass, low-E glass, stained glass and ceramic-designed glass, alongside Vercon smart mirrors and specialised shower cubicle displays.

The showroom also features the complete range of DOWSIL sealants and adhesives, as well as a LandVac thermal showcase that demonstrates the insulating performance of vacuum insulated glass. Customers can test the strength of each type of glass, learn more about the products, and get one-on-one consultations with the team of experts at the showroom.

Vicknesh Sivakumar – Managing Director of Innox Holdings said, “Opening our new showroom is a significant step in the company’s goal to better connect with our clientele. It provides them with a one-stop shop to experience our growing portfolio of world-class products, including Saint-Gobain Glass, DOWSIL, EnduroShield, Vercon Smart Mirrors, Protectapeel and Vetrotech. We invite all customers to drop in and see our products. Our product experts are always available to guide them through each product and its application.”

Innox Holdings was founded with a singular, disruptive mission: to democratize access to world-class construction materials by offering superior quality at prices the Sri Lankan market can actually afford. Over the past few years, the company has turned this vision into reality by securing exclusive partnerships with global industry giants, serving as the trusted distributor for Saint-Gobain Glass and EnduroShield, the world’s premier easy-clean coating provider.

Earlier this year, the company was appointed as the Exclusive Authorised Distributor for DOWSIL - the world’s leading brand in speciality construction sealants and adhesives - in Sri Lanka, marking a significant milestone for the country’s construction and architectural materials sector.

2026 has been a landmark year for the company. Innox Holdings made its mark at the Architect 2026 exhibition earlier this year, winning the award for Innovation on the strength of its new partnership with LandVac, introducing vacuum insulated glass to the Sri Lankan market.
