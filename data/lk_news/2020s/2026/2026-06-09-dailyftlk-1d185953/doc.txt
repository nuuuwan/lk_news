Peterson Solutions Sri Lanka opens new corporate office at Orion City

​Embassy of the Kingdom of the Netherlands in Sri Lanka Deputy Head of Mission / Chargé d’Affaires a.i. Iwan Rutjens cuts the ribbon to inaugurate the new Peterson Solutions corporate office at Orion City. Looking on is Peterson Solutions - Asia Pacific Regional Manager Dr. Rukshan Gunatilaka

Peterson Solutions Sri Lanka officially opens its new corporate office at Orion City, Colombo, on 5 June 2026, marking an important milestone in the company’s growth journey and long-term commitment to supporting businesses across Sri Lanka and the wider region.

The opening ceremony was attended by distinguished guests from the diplomatic and development community, including Embassy of the Kingdom of the Netherlands in Sri Lanka Deputy Head of Mission / Chargé d’Affaires a.i. Iwan Rutjens, who officiated the ribbon-cutting ceremony. The event also brought together representatives from the European Union, development organisations including the United Nations Industrial Development Organization (UNIDO) and Market Development Facility (MDF) Australia, industry partners, clients and other key stakeholders.

Peterson Solutions is the consultancy and advisory arm of the globally recognised Peterson and Control Union Group, a multinational organization with roots dating back to 1920. Over the past century, the group has grown into a trusted international partner, operating in more than 80 countries, undertaking projects in over 120 countries, and serving a global client base of more than 40,000 through a network of over 6,500 professionals.

Built on a legacy of integrity, technical expertise and practical business solutions, Peterson Solutions supports organizations in navigating increasingly complex sustainability, compliance, sourcing, certification and technology-related challenges.

Today, the company delivers a diverse portfolio of services including: Certification support services, sustainable sourcing solutions, sustainability and esg services, technology and digital solutions, training and capacity building through Peterson Academy, and international crop consultancy services through Delphy, a Peterson Solutions company.

These services help organisations strengthen compliance, improve operational performance, enhance sustainability outcomes, manage risk and create long-term business value.

Peterson Solutions serves a broad range of industries including agriculture, food and beverage, textiles, fisheries, tourism, manufacturing, retail, information technology, logistics, and export-oriented sectors. Its multidisciplinary approach allows businesses to access technical expertise, sustainability knowledge, digital innovation and assurance services through a single integrated platform.

Across the Asia-Pacific region, Peterson Solutions currently operates through a network of 14 offices, covering Sri Lanka, India, Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia, Japan, South Korea, China, Taiwan, Papua New Guinea, Pakistan, Australia, and Singapore. This regional footprint enables the organisation to combine local market understanding with international expertise and best practices.

In Sri Lanka, Peterson Solutions commenced operations in 2023, initially operating from its office in Colombo 03. In just a few years, the company has established itself as a trusted partner to more than 170 clients across Sri Lanka while also supporting projects in the Maldives, Cambodia, and Myanmar.

The move to Orion City reflects the company’s continued growth, expansion of its consulting team and the increasing demand for value-added services across the region. In addition to its established sustainability and certification offerings, Peterson Solutions has further expanded its capabilities in areas such as crop consultancy, sustainable agriculture, digital solutions, data-driven sustainability management and professional training and capacity development.

The company continues to work with a growing portfolio of leading organizations including Barista, Pelwatte Dairy Industries, Ethimale Plantations, Sysco LABS, INSEE Ecocycle, Ceylon Tobacco Company, Prima and Ansell, among many others.

Speaking at the event, company representatives noted that the new office represents more than a change of location. It reflects the organization’s broader vision of supporting businesses as they respond to evolving market expectations, sustainability requirements, regulatory obligations and technological transformation.

As organizations across the region increasingly focus on responsible growth, supply chain resilience, ESG performance, traceability and digital innovation, Peterson Solutions remains committed to serving as a trusted partner that helps organizations move from compliance to competitiveness and from strategy to measurable impact.

The opening of the new corporate office at Orion City marked the beginning of the next phase of Peterson Solutions’ journey in Sri Lanka; one built on collaboration, innovation, and a continued commitment to delivering practical, value-driven solutions for businesses across Sri Lanka and beyond.
