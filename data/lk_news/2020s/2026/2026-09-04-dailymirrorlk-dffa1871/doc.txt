Royal College Archers Shine at Wayamba Youth and Junior Archery Championship, 2026

Colombo, Sep. 4 (Daily Mirror) - Royal College produced a strong overall performance at the Wayamba Youth and Junior Archery Championship 2026, securing third place in the team standings with a total of 72 points. The achievement reflected the growing strength and depth of archery at Royal, with the young Archers recording notable results across several age categories.

The most outstanding Royal College performance came in the Under-12 Boys 18m category, where Ikitha Devapriya claimed the Championship with an impressive score of 316 points. He finished 23 points ahead of second-placed Jithum Jithsara Bandara of Lyceum International School, Kurunegala. Royal College also secured further places in the event, with Suhas Arachchige taking third place with 288 points, followed by Riyon Jayasinghe in fourth with 267 points, Sithira Samarasinghe fifth with 265 points and Yuvein Kulatilake sixth with 259 points.

Royal College continued its strong showing in the Under-14 Boys 18m event. K. Kulana Hettiarachchi emerged as the winner with 322 points, overcoming a competitive field. Yuvein Samaraweera finished third with 302 points, while Ikitha Devapriya placed fifth with 299 points. Thamindu Abeyrathne was eighth with 263 points, and Ziyan Nizar completed the Royal College representation in tenth place with 204 points.

In the Under-18 Boys 30m category, Royal College had a sizeable representation. Caiu

Perera was the leading Royal College archer, finishing fifth with 283 points. Tathsilu Jayasena placed seventh with 253 points, followed by Luqmaan Aneeq in eighth with 247 points. Yesara Dolawatta finished tenth with 228 points, while Sakith Dehigahalanda, Bimeth Karunathilake, Ramiru Wattage, Lakindu Elvitigala and Chanmira Athukorala also represented the school.

Royal College archers also competed in the Under-21 Boys 30m event, where Jevindu Weerawardana finished sixth with 227 points and Ovindu Kaluarachchi seventh with 194 points.

Another significant result came in the Cadet 60m Boys category. Caiu Perera, representing the Royal College Archery team, secured fourth place with a combined score of 305 points, recording 144 points in the first detail and 161 in the second.

The results underline the breadth of Royal College's participation and the promising development of its young archers. With a championship-winning performance in the Under-12 and Under-14 categories and strong representation across the older age groups, Royal College demonstrated that its archery programme possesses both emerging talent and a solid competitive foundation for future championships.

While congratulating the young Archers of Royal College, mention must be made of the

Coaches, Parents, Teachers-in-Charge and the Royal College Archery Management Committee, for their time, effort and commitment, without which these results would not have been possible.
