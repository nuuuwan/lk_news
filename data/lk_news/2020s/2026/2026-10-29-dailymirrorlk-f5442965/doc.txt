TVS Lanka opens Knowledge Centre in Ragama

TVS Lanka (Pvt) Ltd is stepping up its investment in technical skills development with the opening of its second TVS Knowledge Center in Ragama, expanding access to specialised training for motorcycle and three-wheeler technicians, garage mechanics and aspiring automotive professionals across Sri Lanka.

The new facility aims to conduct more than 20 training programmes and train over 450 participants each month, strengthening the industry’s capacity to meet the growing demand for skilled technicians, advanced diagnostic expertise and higher customer service standards.

Speaking at the opening, Geethal Anthony, Chief Executive Officer of TVS Lanka (Pvt) Ltd, said the Knowledge Centers are designed to strengthen technical capabilities and professional competencies across the industry, while improving service delivery across sales, servicing and spare parts operations.

“Our main TVS Knowledge Center in Colombo 14, which is approved by the Tertiary and Vocational Education Commission (TVEC), already provides structured training for technicians, sales and service personnel, as well as young people aspiring to enter the industry. With the opening of the Ragama centre, we are extending these opportunities to three-wheeler garage mechanics as well, broadening our contribution to skills development across the sector,” he said.

According to Ranganath Vidanapathirana, Senior Manager – Training & Development at TVS Lanka, the Ragama facility marks a further expansion of the company’s training reach across both two-wheeler and three-wheeler segments.

A significant milestone in the TVS Knowledge Center’s development has been its TVEC approval to offer NVQ Level 4 qualifications, marking what the centre identifies as the first recognised NVQ Level 4 programme introduced by a company operating in Sri Lanka’s motorcycle and three-wheeler industry.
