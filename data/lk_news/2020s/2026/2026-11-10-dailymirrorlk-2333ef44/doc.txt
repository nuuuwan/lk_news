Several injured in three-bus collision on A9 Road in Rambewa

Colombo, Nov. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Several people have been injured in a collision involving three buses in the Rambewa area along the A9 road in Anuradhapura.

The accident occurred when two Sri Lanka Transport Board (SLTB) buses and a private passenger bus collided, police said.

The injured are being rushed to the Anuradhapura Teaching Hospital for treatment, hospital sources said.

The Mihintale Police have launched an investigation into the accident.
