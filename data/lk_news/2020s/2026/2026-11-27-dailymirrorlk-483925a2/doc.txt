Suspect arrested over Rs. 1 Mn bank fraud using fake website

Colombo, Nov. 27 (Daily Mirror) - A 25-year-old man has been arrested over the alleged theft of Rs. 1 million from a bank account using a fraudulent website designed to resemble the official website of a private bank.

Police said the suspect had allegedly obtained the victim’s bank account login credentials through the fake website and subsequently transferred the money from the account.

An investigation was launched following a complaint lodged with the Wariyapola Computer Crime Investigation Division.

Based on information uncovered during the investigation, the suspect was arrested yesterday afternoon (November 26).

The suspect, who is employed as a data entry operator at a private institution, is a resident of Ragama.

The Wariyapola Computer Crime Investigation Division is conducting further investigations into the incident.
