SLID-Frankfurt School of Finance & Management executive programme on Dec. 1

Recognising the importance of strengthening risk oversight at board level, the Sri Lanka Institute of Directors (SLID), in collaboration with Frankfurt School of Finance & Management, will present ‘Beyond the Obvious: Risk Management’, an executive programme for directors and senior professionals.

The programme will take place on December 1, 2026, from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., at Ivy Room, Cinnamon Grand Colombo.

It will be led by Dr. Joachim Bald, Frankfurt School’s Senior International Advisor and Lead Consultant at its Competence Centre in Risk Management.

Marking an inaugural initiative in the SLID’s collaboration with Frankfurt School of Finance & Management, this programme establishes a partnership with one of Germany’s leading business schools, bringing international expertise and perspectives in risk management to Sri Lanka’s corporate and professional community.

With extensive advisory experience across Europe, Asia, Africa and the Middle East, Dr. Bald has worked with leading financial institutions and development organisations, including the European Investment Bank, European Bank for Reconstruction and Development, KfW, Development Bank Ghana, RawBank, Alex Bank and Banque Misr.

He also serves on the Supervisory Boards of KMF Bank in Kazakhstan and Bailyk Finance in Kyrgyzstan.

At Frankfurt School, Dr. Bald is Academic Director of the Certified Expert programmes in Risk Management, Treasury and Markets and Climate Risk Management. His expertise spans quantitative analytics, machine learning and generative AI, supported by his background in software development and statistics, a doctorate in economics, a German MBA and the US Certified Management Accountant qualification.

The programme will explore how the directors and senior executives can move beyond conventional approaches to risk identification and incorporate analytical insights into strategic decision-making.

As organisations face economic uncertainty, technological developments and evolving regulatory and sustainability requirements, boards must be equipped to assess potential exposures, recognise early warning signals and evaluate the implications of emerging risks.

Key areas of focus will include quantitative risk assessment and management, risk analytics, key performance indicators, scenario analysis, risk dashboards and early warning indicators.

The programme will also address sustainability-related risks and the requirements of IFRS S1 and IFRS S2, alongside board risk oversight, reporting and accountability. Practical case studies and applications will connect these concepts with the challenges faced by organisations and their boards.

Through this inaugural collaboration, the SLID and Frankfurt School of Finance & Management aim to establish a platform for continued knowledge exchange and international engagement, bringing global expertise and practical insights to Sri Lanka’s corporate sector.

By strengthening participants’ understanding of risk analysis and oversight, the programme seeks to support more informed decision-making, stronger governance practices and greater organisational resilience.
