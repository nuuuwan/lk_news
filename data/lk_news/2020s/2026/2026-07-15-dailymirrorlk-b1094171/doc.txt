‘Abhisari’ street photo exhibition opens in Colombo

Visitors walk past the ‘Abhisari’ street photo exhibition displayed along Green Path in front of the Colombo Public Library in Colombo. Organized by the Stand Up Movement Lanka social action collective, the exhibition was launched as part of the promotional campaign for director Chinthana Dharmadasa’s upcoming film Abhisari, which is scheduled for release on July 24. The exhibition features photographs highlighting the lives and experiences of women in Colombo. Pix by Pradeep Pathirana
