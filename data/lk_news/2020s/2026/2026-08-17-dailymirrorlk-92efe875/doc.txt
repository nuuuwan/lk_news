Sri Lanka to be transformed into a country like Australia, Handunneththi

Colombo, Aug. 17 (Daily Mirror) - Minister of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development Sunil Handunneththi has pledged to transform Sri Lanka into a country offering freedom, equality before the law and opportunities similar to those available in Australia.

Addressing overseas Sri Lankan entrepreneurs at the Sri Lankan Entrepreneurs’ Night 2026 in Melbourne, Handunneththi said many Sri Lankans had left the country for education, business opportunities or a life offering greater freedom and independence.

“A majority of those here are Sri Lankans. Despite the language we speak, we are from the same country, living at a 10-hour distance,” he said.

“You might have left for Australia for education, business, or for a life with freedom and independence. I promise today, alongside you and the 18 million people who voted for us, that within a short period we will transform Sri Lanka into a country like Australia, where future generations can study and live with freedom, with equal laws, and where you would want to reside,” he said.

Handunneththi also stressed the importance of overseas Sri Lankan entrepreneurs and investors in building a strong, production-based economy capable of overcoming the country’s economic challenges.

He said a new economic environment based on transparency, free from corruption and conducive to investment had already been established.

The Minister urged Sri Lankan entrepreneurs, both in the country and overseas, to play a leading role in advancing the country’s industrial and technological sectors and contributing to the creation of a stronger economy.
