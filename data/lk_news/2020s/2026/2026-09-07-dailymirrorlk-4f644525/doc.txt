Pinnawala Zoo unveils three leopard cubs on World Cat Day

Colombo, Sep. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Pinnawala Zoo marked World Cat Day with a special educational programme featuring the public debut of three leopard cubs born at the zoo.

The programme, held on August 17, 2026, focused on promoting awareness and conservation of Sri Lanka’s wild cat species, particularly among children and young people.

A major highlight was the first public appearance of the three leopard cubs born at Pinnawala Zoo. The public was also given an opportunity to suggest names for the cubs, with nearly 500 entries received from across the country.

Following consideration by a committee, the names Thiyo, Kylo and Heela were selected for the three cubs. Sakura Wickramasinghe of Kelaniya, Bathiya Rathnayake of Kandy and Nuzair of Orugodawatte were selected as the respective winners, and prizes were presented to them during the programme.

The programme also featured a special lecture on Sri Lanka’s small cat species conducted by Professor Nihal Dayawansa of the University of Colombo.

Students of Sujatha College, Rambukkana, members of the Wildlife Exploration Team of Pinnawala National School, members of the Young Zoologists Association of the National Zoological Parks Department and Pinnawala Zoo staff participated in the educational session.

Organisers said the programme aimed to increase knowledge and conservation awareness of Sri Lanka’s lesser-known small wild cats among children and young people.

The event was organised with the approval of the Director General of the National Zoological Parks Department, with support from Director of Operations Anoma Priyadarshani, Deputy Director of Pinnawala Zoo Navod Abeysinghe, Professor Nihal Dayawansa and zoo staff.

Prizes for the naming competition were sponsored by the Manager of Central Finance in Kegalle and the Media Travelers Institute.

Pinnawala Zoo said such programmes would contribute to strengthening public awareness about wildlife conservation and encouraging younger generations to appreciate and protect Sri Lanka’s wild cat species.
