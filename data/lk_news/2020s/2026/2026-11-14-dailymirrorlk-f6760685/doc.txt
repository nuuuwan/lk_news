NEO Technologies hosts ‘NEO Hard Talk 2026’

NEO Technologies (Pvt) Ltd hosted its premier ‘NEO Hard Talk 2026’ aboard the elegant 8 Degrees Boat at Cinnamon Lakeside, Colombo recently, bringing together diplomats, and professionals for an evening of intellectual engagement, inspiration and meaningful dialogue.

The event featured thought-provoking insights into Sri Lanka’s extraordinary biodiversity, particularly its unique cloud forests and biodiversity hotspots, followed by an inspiring presentation titled “The Sweet Spot of Management” by Major (Retd.) Tissa Aluwihare, founder of NEO Technologies. Drawing upon his remarkable entrepreneurial journey, Aluwihare shared valuable perspectives on leadership, organisational transformation and the importance of empowering individuals to achieve collective success.

The highlight of the evening was the unveiling of NEO’s ‘Black Box Book’, a working manual documenting the company’s indigenous management systems, concepts and organisational practices, reflecting its commitment to institutional excellence and sustainable growth.
