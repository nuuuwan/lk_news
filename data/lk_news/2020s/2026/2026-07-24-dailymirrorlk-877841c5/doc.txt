A silent wrap-up to week

The secondary market yield curve witnessed a subdued trading session, with low activity and muted volumes, while the yield curve remained broadly unchanged.

Among the traded maturities, the 15.10.2028, 15.07.2029 and 15.09.2029 maturities changed hands at the rates of 10.75 percent, 11.15 percent and 11.20 percent, respectively.

Meanwhile, the 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 maturities traded at the rate of 11.65 percent, while the 01.06.2033 and 01.11.2033 maturities traded at the rate of 12.20 percent.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee slightly appreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.336.26/US dollar, compared to Rs.336.42/US dollar seen earlier. Overnight liquidity in the banking system expanded to Rs.163.07 billion, from Rs.156.54 billion recorded previously.
