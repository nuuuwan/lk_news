Fitch lifts ratings of People’s Leasing, MBSL and CBC Finance on parent bank upgrades

People’s Leasing & Finance PLC and Merchant Bank of Sri Lanka & Finance PLC (MBSL) have been lifted two notches to ‘AA-(lka)’, while CBC Finance PLC moved a notch higher to ‘A+(lka)’, as last week’s upgrades of their parent banks strengthened the parents’ ability to support them, Fitch Ratings said.

The action follows Fitch’s 30 October upgrades of People’s Bank and Bank of Ceylon to ‘AA+(lka)’ and Commercial Bank of Ceylon PLC to ‘AA(lka)’, which came after the agency recalibrated its national rating scale following Sri Lanka’s sovereign rating upgrade to ‘B-’ from ‘CCC+’ in September. All three finance companies carry Stable Outlooks.

The upgrades “reflect our view of the parents’ improved ability to support their subsidiaries,” Fitch noted, adding that high ownership stakes, shared branding in some cases and management oversight raise the reputational risk to the parents should a subsidiary fail. People’s Bank owns 75 percent of People’s Leasing and Bank of Ceylon 84.4 percent of MBSL, while Commercial Bank wholly owns CBC Finance.

However, Fitch drew distinctions in each parent’s willingness to step in. It said People’s Leasing’s “longstanding and largely successful role” as the People’s Bank group’s leasing arm strengthened its parent’s propensity to provide support, though this was balanced against the company’s significant balance sheet size relative to that of People’s Bank.

Meanwhile, MBSL plays a more limited role within the Bank of Ceylon group, and its record of high growth and variable profitability has put pressure on its regulatory capitalisation and leverage, the agency observed, adding that this may weigh on the parent’s willingness to support it. Even so, Fitch expects Bank of Ceylon, which has provided shareholder support when needed in the past, to keep meeting MBSL’s growth and regulatory capital needs over the near to medium term.

CBC Finance’s modest group role and variable performance could limit Commercial Bank’s propensity to back it, Fitch cautioned, although the company’s recent earnings and asset quality have improved. With each company rated two notches below its parent, CBC Finance, the only wholly owned subsidiary of the three, ranks lowest as Commercial Bank’s own rating sits a notch beneath those of the two state-owned banks.

Fitch also upgraded the companies’ rupee-denominated subordinated debentures, which sit two notches below the issuers’ ratings, to ‘A(lka)’ for People’s Leasing and MBSL and to ‘A-(lka)’ for CBC Finance, from ‘BBB+(lka)’. Ratings on proposed subordinated debenture issues by MBSL and CBC Finance moved up in line.
