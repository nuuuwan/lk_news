Dialog introduces Blackview 5G Smartphone and Tablet to advance digital inclusion

Dialog Axiata PLC, Sri Lanka’s #1 connectivity provider, has announced the introduction of two new affordable Blackview 5G devices to expand access to next-generation technology, reinforcing its commitment to democratising digital connectivity and accelerating digital transformation by enabling more Sri Lankans to experience the capabilities of Dialog 5G Ultra, Sri Lanka’s largest 5G network.

Building on the momentum created with Dialog’s recent launch of affordable devices, the new Blackview Shark 6 5G features a 6.88-inch HD+ IPS display with a 120Hz refresh rate and an octa-core 5G Unisoc T8100 processor. It also offers 4GB + 128GB memory with 8GB RAM expansion, a 5000mAh battery with 18W fast charging, 8MP front and 16MP rear cameras, an 18-month warranty, a Dialog data bundle and availability across 600+ outlets.

Complementing the smartphone, the Blackview Zeno 10 5G tablet delivers a robust and versatile user experience with its 11-inch FHD+ IPS display featuring a 90Hz refresh rate, UNISOC T8200 5G-ready chipset, 6GB RAM with up to 12GB virtual RAM, 128GB storage expandable to 2TB, 8,800mAh battery, 16MP rear and 13MP front cameras, and Android 15 with DokeOS 4.0. The tablet comes bundled with a case, screen protector, wireless keyboard, earphones and stylus, and includes dual-SIM & 5G connectivity, 18-month warranty and a Dialog data bundle.
