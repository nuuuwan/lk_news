Knuckles incident: Girl to receive monthly allowance and counselling support

Colombo, Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - The Women and Child Affairs Ministry has decided to provide a monthly allowance to the 15-year-old girl involved in the Knuckles incident to ensure her welfare and continued education.

The allowance will be provided under the Government's 'Artha' programme until she completes the compulsory education age or proceeds to higher education or vocational training, according to her interests.

The girl has registered for the G.C.E. Ordinary Level examination. Minister of Women and Child Affairs Saroja Savithri Paulraj has also instructed the Ministry to arrange psychosocial counselling through the National Child Protection Authority to support her examination preparations.

The 'Artha' programme provides financial assistance to children in institutional care, those under protective guardianship and street children.

The programme is jointly implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Affairs and the Department of Probation and Child Care Services, benefiting around 9,000 children every month.
