Ceylinco Life dominates NAFLIA 2026 winning 12 top honours

Ceylinco Life has secured an outstanding 12 awards at the 2026 edition of the National Forum for Life Insurance Advisors (NAFLIA), including five National Awards that recognised its professionals as the best in the country.

The awards event saw Ceylinco Life’s sales professionals excel at both the Large Company and National levels, with the company claiming top honours across the Advisor, Supervisor and Branch Manager categories in both levels, while also producing two winners in the Large Company level in the Fast Starter Advisor category.

In the Advisor category, A. P. S. Wijayakumar secured first place at National and Large Company Level, while A. I. P. Manjula was placed second in both levels, giving Ceylinco Life four awards across the two tiers of recognition in this category.

The company’s strength was equally evident in the Supervisor category, where R. P. Edirisinghe emerged as the first-place winner and also secured the National Award for the category, securing two more awards for his company.

Ceylinco Life also dominated the Branch Manager category, with A. S. Samarasinghe taking first place and J. Meera securing second place at both the National level and Large Company level, adding another four awards to the company’s tally.

The company’s emerging talent was recognised in the Fast Starter Advisor category at Large Company level, with S. Sivakumar taking first place and D. N. Shantha securing third place.

Commenting on the achievement, Ceylinco Life Chairman R. Renganathan said, “This success reflects talent, discipline, professionalism and an unwavering commitment to excellence, and I warmly congratulate every one of our winners.”
