Seven railway services cancelled tonight

Colombo, Sep. 16 (Daily Mirror) - The Railways Department has cancelled seven scheduled train services, including six night mail trains and one additional service.

Accordingly, the cancellations affect night mail trains operating between Colombo Fort and Kankesanthurai, Trincomalee and Batticaloa in both directions.

The 11.00 p.m. train from Colombo Fort to Rambukkana has also been cancelled.
