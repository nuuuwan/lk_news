Commercial Bank tops Fitch’s local ratings for Sri Lankan private banks

Fitch Ratings upgraded Commercial Bank of Ceylon PLC’s national long-term rating by one notch to AA(lka), from AA-(lka), effective October 30, after revising its Sri Lankan rating scale following the September sovereign upgrade.

The move makes Commercial Bank the highest-rated private-sector bank in Sri Lanka on Fitch’s national scale, the lender said in a statement.

National ratings compare the creditworthiness among the issuers within Sri Lanka.

The sovereign upgrade prompted Fitch to recalibrate its local scale, giving the agency room to distinguish Commercial Bank’s credit profile more clearly from those of other large private lenders, according to the statement.

Fitch pointed to Commercial Bank’s position as Sri Lanka’s largest private-sector bank and its ability to raise funds at a lower cost than other large private lenders. It also cited the bank’s ability to generate business as a factor supporting its credit profile.

The bank’s asset quality, profitability and capital coverage of impaired loans after provisions have generally been among the stronger levels recorded by the Sri Lankan banks rated by Fitch, the lender said, citing the agency’s assessment.
