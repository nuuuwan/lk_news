Major reservoirs at 33.6% storage amid dry conditions

Colombo, Sep. 14 (Daily Mirror) - The combined storage level of Sri Lanka’s 74 major reservoirs stood at 33.6% of their total capacity as of September 13, the Irrigation Department’s Water Management Branch said.

The 74 reservoirs have a combined gross capacity of 3,114,407 acre-feet, with the present storage recorded at 33.6%.

Among the 13 irrigation ranges, Kurunegala recorded the highest storage level at 71.7%, followed by Galle at 63.2%, Kandy at 60.5% and Puttalam at 60.2%.

Monaragala reservoirs were at 55.1% of capacity, while Anuradhapura stood at 43.6%, Hambantota at 36.7% and Polonnaruwa at 35.7%.

Lower storage levels were recorded in Ampara (21.5%), Batticaloa (24.6%), Trincomalee (25.8%) and Mannar (27.7%).

Among individual reservoirs, Ambewela in Badulla was at 98.9% of effective storage, while Vakaneri in Batticaloa was recorded at 100%. Rajangana in Anuradhapura was at 83.2% and Deduru Oya in Kurunegala at 90.6%.

Meanwhile, Senanayaka Samudraya, the country’s largest reservoir in terms of gross capacity among those listed, was at 17.6% storage despite receiving 23 mm of rainfall.

No spilling was reported from any of the 74 major reservoirs monitored by the Irrigation Department as of September 13.

The department said rainfall varied considerably across the reservoir regions, with several reservoirs recording no rainfall while Senanayaka Samudraya and Mahadivul Wewa recorded 23 mm and 21.5 mm respectively.
