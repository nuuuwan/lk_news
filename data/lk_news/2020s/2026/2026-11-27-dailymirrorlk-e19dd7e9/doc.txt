Singapore businesses explore Sri Lanka for expansion at Port City Colombo Investor Forum

Representatives from the High Commission of Sri Lanka in Singapore, SLSTE and CHEC  at the investor forum

The High Commission of Sri Lanka in Singapore and the Sri Lanka Singapore Tech Exco (SLSTE), in collaboration with Port City Colombo, hosted the Port City Colombo Investor Forum in Singapore on 20 November.

The evening forum was Port City Colombo’s second in the country this year, following an earlier forum in January, and was designed to introduce Port City Colombo’s investment and business setup proposition to businesses, particularly in the technology sector, that are looking to expand their operations or establish a presence in another jurisdiction.

Delivering the opening address, Senarath Dissanayake, High Commissioner of Sri Lanka in Singapore, spoke to the strength of the relationship between the two countries and the opportunities for Singapore’s business community in Sri Lanka. “Singapore and Sri Lanka share strong economic and people-to-people ties, and Singapore’s technology community is among the most dynamic in the region,” he said. “Port City Colombo offers businesses a modern, well-regulated base in Sri Lanka, and we are pleased to see it return to Singapore to share that opportunity directly with this community.”

Mahesh Kavinda, President of SLSTE, opened the evening with welcoming remarks. “Sri Lankan tech professionals in Singapore, and the businesses they work with, are well placed to see what Port City Colombo can offer,” he said. “For companies thinking about where to expand or set up next, this forum gives a clear look at the options, and SLSTE is pleased to help connect the two communities.”

The Port City Colombo presentation was delivered by Nuwan Jayawardene and Kasun Badaturuge, Senior Managers, Business Development at CHEC Port City Colombo (Pvt) Ltd. They introduced Port City Colombo as a master-planned special economic zone governed by a dedicated regulatory authority, and set out how technology and services businesses can use it, from regional headquarters and engineering and R&D teams to fintech operations and business continuity centres. They drew on business case studies such as IGT1, a subsidiary of IFS and one of the many early movers to establish under the Port City Colombo framework, and MillenniumIT ESP, which has also shared its experience of operating within the Port City Colombo ecosystem. Features designed for internationally mobile businesses were also highlighted, including a single-window regulatory authority, 100 percent foreign ownership, operations in 16 designated foreign currencies, and the ability to repatriate capital and profits. The presentation was followed by an interactive audience Q&A session.

The forum drew representatives of Deloitte, Maybank, NCINGA, Reap Investments and BDO Singapore, among others, reflecting interest in Port City Colombo from across Singapore’s business community.

Delivering the vote of thanks, Bai Xiaping, Assistant Managing Director of CHEC Port City Colombo (Pvt) Ltd., thanked the High Commission and SLSTE for their support and the guests for their participation.
