Fire breaks out at scrap metal shop in Pannipitiya

Colombo, November 21 (Daily Mirror)- A fire broke out at a scrap metal shop in Depanama, Pannipitiya, this morning, police said.

Three fire engines from the Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte Municipal Council have been dispatched to the scene to bring the blaze under control.

Police confirmed that no casualties have been reported so far.

The cause of the fire remains unknown, while Maharagama Police are conducting further investigations into the incident.
