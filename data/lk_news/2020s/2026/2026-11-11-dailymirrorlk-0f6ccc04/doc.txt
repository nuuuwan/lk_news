Colombo-Kandy road reopens after landslide risk assessment

The Colombo-Kandy main road, which was temporarily closed due to the risk of landslides and falling rocks, was reopened to traffic from 6.00 a.m. today (11), police said.

The road was closed last night near Dawson Tower in the Kadugannawa Police Division and Ganethenna Junction in the Mawanella Police Division following falling rocks, earth slips and the threat of landslides caused by heavy rainfall.

Kandy District Secretary Indika Udawatta said a decision had been taken to keep one lane of the Kadugannawa-Ganethenna section open to traffic following on-site inspections to assess the risk.

However, he said the road would be closed during the night if heavy rainfall continues, due to safety concerns.

Motorists have been advised to use alternative routes wherever possible and exercise caution when travelling along the affected section.
