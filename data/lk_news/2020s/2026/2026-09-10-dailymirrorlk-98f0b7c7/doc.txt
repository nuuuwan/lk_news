Nolimit opens 31st store at One Galle Face Mall

Nolimit, one of Sri Lanka’s leading fashion retail brands, has strengthened its presence in Colombo, with the opening of its 31st store at Level 2 of One Galle Face Mall, marking another milestone in the company’s continued expansion and commitment to enhancing the modern retail experience.

Spanning over 12,000 square feet, the new outlet brings Nolimit’s extensive fashion and lifestyle portfolio to one of Colombo’s prominent retail and lifestyle destinations.  The store offers menswear, womenswear, kids-wear, footwear, accessories, homeware, toys, travel and luggage products and cosmetics, bringing a broad range of fashion and lifestyle needs together under one roof.

Designed to complement the contemporary environment of One Galle Face Mall, the outlet features a spacious layout, modern design and tech-based interior, including self-checkout facilities that enhance convenience and contribute to a seamless shopping experience. Nolimit Managing Director Hafiz Mubarack stated, “The opening of our 31st store at One Galle Face Mall marks an exciting milestone in Nolimit’s continued growth. With its strategic location and diverse audience, One Galle Face Mall provides an ideal platform to bring our extensive fashion and lifestyle offering to Sri Lanka. This over 12,000-square-foot store reflects our commitment to creating contemporary, accessible and world-class retail experiences for our customers.” Nolimit Assistant General Manager Raneez Sheriff added, “The One Galle Face Mall outlet brings together a modern store environment with a tech-based interior and self-checkout facilities, creating a more seamless and engaging shopping experience. As we continue to expand, we remain focused on embracing innovation while delivering the variety, value and service our customers expect from Nolimit.” Strategically positioned within One Galle Face Mall, the outlet enables Nolimit to connect with young professionals, families, fashion-conscious consumers, local residents, tourists and visitors to Colombo, further strengthening the brand’s relevance within the city’s evolving fashion and lifestyle landscape.
