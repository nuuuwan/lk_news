SJB accuses Justice Minister of putting showpiece on social media

Colombo, August 14 (Daily Mirror)- A video of Justice Minister Harshana Nanayakkara driving his vehicle came under fire by Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) MP Harshana Rajakaruna today.

"A Minister driving a vehicle on his own is not anything new. Many of them and even MPs have been driving their vehicles in the past. It is just a showpiece by Minister Nanayakkara," MP Rajakaruna told a media briefing.
