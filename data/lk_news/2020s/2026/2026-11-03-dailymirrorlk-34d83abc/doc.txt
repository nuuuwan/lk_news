Cyclone Murasu makes landfall on India’s eastern coast

Reuters - Cyclone Murasu made landfall ​along India's eastern coast on Tuesday, the country's ‌weather department said, the second storm to hit the region in a week as authorities evacuated thousands of people to safety.

The ​India Meteorological Department said the severe cyclonic storm ​crossed the coast of the south-eastern state of ⁠Andhra Pradesh between 03:30 and 05:30 local time, with ​wind speeds of 100-110 kph (62 to 68 mph) and gusts ​of up to 120 kph.

No deaths or injuries have been reported so far, although some trees and electric poles were uprooted, ​Prakhar Jain, Managing Director of the Andhra Pradesh State ​Disaster Management Authority, said.

Authorities began evacuating thousands of people on Monday ‌and ⁠planned to move some 30,000 to safety in Andhra Pradesh, which is in the path of the cyclone.

Cyclone Murasu formed over the Bay of Bengal, a major region for ​tropical cyclones ​that has ⁠spawned deadly storms on India's east coast.

IMD forecasts said the states of Andhra Pradesh and ​Tamil Nadu to its south would be ​worst ⁠hit.

At least six people were killed in Cyclone Arnab, which was classified by IMD as a very severe cyclonic storm and ⁠made ​landfall in Andhra Pradesh on Wednesday.
