Pakistan and Sri Lanka share points after Rawalpindi test draw as WTC race heats up

Nov.13 (Mirror Sports) - The first Test between Pakistan and Sri Lanka ended in a draw in Rawalpindi after five days of competitive cricket, with both teams taking valuable points in the ICC World Test Championship 2025–27.

The result leaves both sides looking to strengthen their positions in the ongoing championship, while Pakistan will be disappointed not to have converted their efforts into a victory on home soil.

Elsewhere, West Indies secured a 1-0 series victory over Bangladesh after the final Test ended in a draw, earning crucial World Test Championship points from their away series.

South Africa also made a significant move in the standings, climbing into second place after securing a 2-1 home series victory over Australia.

New Zealand, meanwhile, moved to the top of the standings, pushing Australia down as the battle for a place in the World Test Championship final continues to intensify.

With the championship entering another important phase, teams will be aiming to collect maximum points from their remaining fixtures as the race for the top two positions gathers momentum.
