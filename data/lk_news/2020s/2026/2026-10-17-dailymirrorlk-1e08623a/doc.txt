Ceylinco Life awards Rs.23.4mn in Pranama scholarships at 25th presentation

Recipients of Ceylinco Life Pranama scholarships for their performances at GCE Advanced Level examination

Life insurance leader’s cumulative investment in education of children of policyholders tops Rs.263.4mn

Ceylinco Life has marked the 25th edition of its flagship Pranama Scholarships programme by awarding 147 scholarships worth Rs.23.4 million to recognise the academic excellence and outstanding extracurricular achievements among the children of its policyholders, taking the programme’s cumulative contribution to young Sri Lankans to Rs.263.4 million.

The latest presentation brings the total number of scholarships awarded since the programme’s inception in 2002 to 3,613, reflecting a quarter-century of sustained investment in educational achievement and the development of young talent across the country.

The milestone event featured addresses by Prof. Prasad Sethunga of the University of Peradeniya, the chief guest, Ceylinco Life Executive Director and Deputy CEO Ranga Abeynayake and Dr. S.K.A.B. Dilhara, a former Pranama scholar, whose own journey illustrates the opportunities that can follow from academic achievement and support at an important stage of a young person’s life.
