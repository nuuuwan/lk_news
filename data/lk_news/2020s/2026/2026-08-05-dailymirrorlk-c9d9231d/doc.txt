Asian Paints Causeway unveils Royale Infinity

Asian Paints Causeway has officially launched another market first, with the introduction of Royale Infinity - a pinnacle of luxury interior emulsion, offering ‘Diamond Perfection with Nano Technology’.

The grand unveiling took place during a prestigious event hosted at Heritance in Negombo.

Crafted specifically based on extensive local consumer research, Royale Infinity targets a league of its own. The product breaks new ground by delivering an exceptionally smooth wall finish that is five times smoother to touch than anything the local market has experienced before, creating an unprecedented sense of visual harmony and tactile elegance.

Asian Paints International Regional Head South Asia and South Pacific Islands Budhaditya Mukherjee stated, “The elite consumer segment here demands absolute perfection and with Royale Infinity, we are combining cutting-edge nano technology with global standards to deliver an unparalleled experience that elevates the value we offer to our dedicated dealers and customers.”

Asian Paints Causeway Country Head Vaithilingam Kiritharan added, “Royale Infinity isn’t just another paint; it’s a meticulously engineered lifestyle solution.”

To cater to diverse aesthetic preferences, Royale Infinity is offered in over 3,000 shades across two distinct, ultra-premium variants. The Luxe Pearl Finish provides a subtle, sophisticated shimmer, while the Soft Matt Finish delivers a smooth, non-glossy, contemporary elegance.

Beyond aesthetics, Royale Infinity is heavily anchored in health, safety and environmental responsibility, boasting rigorous performance certifications from the globally recognised Wimpey Laboratories LLC.

Reflecting absolute confidence in its durability, the product comes backed by a seven-year performance warranty, ensuring long-lasting assurance for elite homeowners.
