Bargain-hunters to the fore as some stocks diminish in value

The CSE yesterday kicked off on a strong positive note because of an acute buying interest by bargain hunters following the decline of certain valuable stocks.

Amid those developments both indices moved upwards. The All Share Price Index went up by 44.72 points while S and P SL20 rose by 17.32 points. Turnover stood at Rs 2.15 billion with three crossings.

Those crossings were: s HNB 750,000 shares crossed to the tune of Rs 300 million and its shares price traded at Rs 400, CCS 400,000 shares crossed for Rs 49.5 million; its shares traded at Rs 123.75, Janashakthi 3.5 million shares crossed to the tune of Rs 46 million; its share sold at Rs 13.

In the retail market top seven companies that mainly contributed to the turnover were; HNB Rs 87 million (217,000 shares traded), ACL Cables Rs 85 million (857,000 shares traded), Softlogic Holdings Rs 53.3 million (4.1 million shares traded), JKH Rs 53 million (3.9 million shares traded ), Agstar Rs 51 million (3.9 million shares traded), HNB (Non-Voting) Rs 50.8 million (155,000 shares traded) and Ceylon Land Equity Rs 50 million (4.9 million shares traded). During the day 76.6 million shares volumes changed hands in 25855 transactions.

It is said that banking sector counters, especially HNB, led the market while manufacturing sector counters, especially JKH and ACL Cables, performed well. Other sectors performed moderately.

Yesterday the telegraphic transfer rate for Sri Lanka’s rupee against the dollar was Rs 343.5000 buying, Rs 352.5000 selling, while bond yields were up, dealers said.
