Four million Sri Lankan adults yet to obtain TIN numbers

Colombo, Nov. 9 (Daily Mirror) - Around four million Sri Lankans aged 18 and above have yet to obtain a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN), despite a legal requirement for all adults to register with the Inland Revenue Department (IRD).

IRD Commissioner Nandana Kumara said approximately 13 million people had already registered with the department and obtained TIN numbers.

He said Sri Lanka has an estimated 17 million people aged 18 and above, leaving around four million yet to complete the registration process.

Kumara explained that TIN registration was previously required only for specific purposes. However, under the current law, every individual who has reached the age of 18 is required to obtain a TIN.
