Update: Backhoe Saman’s mother killed in shooting

Colombo, Aug. 12 (Daily Mirror) - A woman who was reportedly the mother of underworld figure Backhoe Saman has died after being shot in the Ulhitiya area of Middeniya this afternoon (12).

The woman was initially injured in the shooting and admitted to hospital for treatment, where she later succumbed to her injuries.

Backhoe Saman is a notorious Sri Lankan organized crime figure who has previously been linked to drug trafficking and contract killings. His name has also emerged in investigations into a triple murder in Middeniya.
