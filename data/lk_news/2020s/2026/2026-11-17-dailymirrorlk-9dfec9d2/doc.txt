Janashakthi Finance records 40.5% increase in PAT for 1H 2026/27

From left: Janashakthi Finance Chairman Rajendra Theagarajah and Chief Executive Officer Sithambaram Sri Ganendran

Janashakthi Finance PLC recorded an unaudited profit after tax (PAT) of Rs.198.6 million for the six-month period ended September 30, 2026, up 40.5 percent from Rs.141.3 million in the corresponding period last year.

The company recorded a year-on-year growth in its lending and deposit portfolios, together with an increase in the net operating income. It is focused on building on this progress through prudent growth, sustainable profitability and continued attention to customer needs.

Profit before tax (PBT) increased by 1.9 percent to Rs.245.6 million, while the net operating income grew by 11.0 percent to Rs.1,591.7 million.

As at September 30, 2026, the loans and receivables stood at Rs.32.94 billion, an increase of 23.5 percent compared with September 30, 2025. The deposits increased by 14.7 percent over the same period to Rs.19.36 billion.

For the second quarter of FY 2026/27, Janashakthi Finance recorded PAT of Rs.108.2 million, an increase of 19.7 percent from Rs.90.4 million in the preceding quarter.

The net operating income decreased by 10.0 percent to Rs.754.0 million, while PBT declined by 22.3 percent to Rs.107.3 million.

Janashakthi Finance Chairman Rajendra Theagarajah said, “These results highlight the company’s focus on maintaining responsible growth. Our priority is to build a strong and sustainable financial institution that responds to customer needs and creates long-term value for all our stakeholders.”

Janashakthi Finance Chief Executive Officer Sithambaram Sri Ganendran said, “Janashakthi Finance’s half-year performance reflects the continued expansion of our lending and deposit base. Our focus is on translating this growth into sustainable earnings through disciplined business execution, operational efficiency and prudent risk management, while continuing to meet the evolving needs of our customers.”
