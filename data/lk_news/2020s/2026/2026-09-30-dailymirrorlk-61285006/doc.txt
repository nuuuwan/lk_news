INSEE honours suppliers and partners at ‘Supplier Excellence Awards’

INSEE recently hosted the ‘INSEE Supplier Excellence Awards 2026’ at Grandeeza Hotel, Negombo, under the theme ‘Partnership Beyond Boundaries.’

The event recognised and celebrated the company’s valued suppliers and business partners for their outstanding performance, contributions, and continued partnership with INSEE.

Bringing together suppliers, business partners, and INSEE employees, the event celebrated excellence, collaboration, and shared success. Awards were presented across the large, medium, and small supplier categories, covering production services, general administration services, logistics, transportation, and material handling.

A total of 25 supplier awards and six employee awards were presented. Suppliers received Gold, Silver, and Bronze Performance Excellence Awards, while Special Excellence Awards recognised achievements in innovation, value creation, safety, and sustainability. INSEE employees were also recognised through the Best Supervisor and Best Custodian awards.

Eng. Thusith Gunawarnasuriya, CEO of INSEE Lanka said, “The Supplier Excellence Awards provide an important platform to recognise the partners who support and sustain our operations every day. Strong and trusted supplier relationships are fundamental to the reliability and quality we deliver to our customers and to building a more resilient and responsible construction industry in Sri Lanka. This year’s theme, ‘Partnership Beyond Boundaries,’ reflects the enduring value we create together, not only through products and services, but also through collaboration and a shared commitment to excellence.”
