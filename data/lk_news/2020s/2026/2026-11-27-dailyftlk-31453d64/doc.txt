GRI Executive Director Ananda Caldera receives PRISL Lifetime Achievement Award

The Plastics & Rubber Institute of Sri Lanka (PRISL) on 17 November presented its Lifetime Achievement Award to Global Rubber Industries (GRI) Executive Director Ananda Caldera.

A former President of the Institute, Caldera is only the fourth person to receive the distinction, which recognises more than four decades of service to Sri Lanka’s rubber industry.

A qualified engineer, Caldera served as Director of Manufacturing before joining GRI. During that time, he established a custom rubber-mixing facility and introduced hot-process retreading for off-the-road tires in Sri Lanka, giving these heavy-duty tires a second working life.

Since joining GRI on 1 January 2012, Caldera has helped lead the expansion of its specialty tire manufacturing operations in Sri Lanka. Under his guidance, GRI has strengthened its international presence, bringing Sri Lankan-made agricultural and industrial tires to farms and workplaces around the world and showcasing the expertise of the country’s rubber manufacturers.

Caldera has brought the voice of Sri Lanka’s rubber manufacturers and exporters to national industry discussions. He chaired the Sri Lanka Association of Manufacturers and Exporters of Rubber Products and the Ministry of Industries’ Rubber Sector Advisory Board. In 2018, the President of Sri Lanka appointed him to the Industrialisation Commission. Today, he serves on advisory committees of the Export Development Board, the Ministry of Industry and the University of Moratuwa, where he earned his MBA in Management of Technology.

At GRI, we are proud to work alongside Caldera and to be among the generations of rubber professionals who have learned from him. For those of us who continue to benefit from his guidance, it means a great deal to see the Institute he once led honor his contribution to Sri Lanka’s rubber industry and its people.
