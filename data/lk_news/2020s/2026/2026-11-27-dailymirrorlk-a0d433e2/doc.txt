Climate change, marine pollution need collective action, says Minister

Colombo, Nov. 27 (Daily Mirror) - Climate change, marine pollution and maritime safety cannot be addressed through individual efforts and require sustained collective action by Indian Ocean countries, Foreign Minister Vijitha Herath said.

Addressing the 25th Indian Ocean Rim Association (IORA) Council of Ministers meeting in New Delhi, India, on November 27, Herath stressed the need for stronger regional cooperation to tackle challenges facing the Indian Ocean.

He said cooperation should continue beyond individual chairmanships, highlighting the importance of the IORA Troika in maintaining continuity in the organisation's work.

Speaking as Sri Lanka's Immediate Past Chair, the Minister said the country's experience during its 2023–2025 chairmanship had highlighted the importance of partnerships, practical cooperation and collective efforts.

He also recalled Sri Lanka's initiatives during its chairmanship, including strengthening ties with regional organisations such as BIMSTEC and hosting the sixth IORA Blue Carbon Hub Think Tank.

Herath said Sri Lanka would continue to support IORA's work and strengthen regional partnerships to ensure that agreed initiatives were implemented.
