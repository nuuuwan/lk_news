Intex–InMac 2026 opens

Industry Minister Sunil Handunnetti inaugurates ‘Intex – InMac Sri Lanka 2026’ at BMICH, highlighting the importance of innovation, technology and investment to strengthen Sri Lanka’s textile and apparel sector, the country’s leading export-earning industry. The three-day exhibition features over 250 exhibitors from more than 15 countries. Pix by Kithsiri De Mel
