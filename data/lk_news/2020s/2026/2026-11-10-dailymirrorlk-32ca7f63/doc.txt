CSE rally accelerates as ASPI breaks above 22,000

The Colombo Stock Exchange extended its strong recovery for a sixth consecutive session on 10 November 2026, with both benchmark indices advancing sharply as broad-based buying momentum continued.

The ASPI surged 592.02 points, or 2.75%, to close at 22,123.39, while the S&P SL20 gained 224.97 points, or 3.66%, to 6,368.12. The ASPI moved above 22,100 during afternoon trading and approached 22,200 before mild profit-taking emerged near the close.

Market turnover strengthened significantly to LKR 7.96 billion, with 253.92 million shares traded. The Banks sector led activity with approximately LKR 2.03 billion in turnover, while Dialog Axiata recorded the highest individual counter turnover at LKR 1.42 billion.

Crossings accounted for around 22% of total equity turnover, led by Dialog Axiata with approximately LKR 815.48 million, followed by Commercial Bank with LKR 309.57 million. John Keells Holdings and Swisstek also recorded notable block transactions.

Market breadth remained strongly positive, with 206 gainers against 45 decliners, resulting in an advance-decline ratio of 4.58. Foreign investors, however, recorded a significant net outflow of approximately LKR 1.20 billion.

Commercial Bank was the strongest positive contributor to the ASPI, followed by Dialog Axiata, Hatton National Bank, Hayleys and Vallibel One. Carson Cumberbatch recorded the largest negative contribution.

The sixth consecutive gain, turnover more than double the YTD daily average and strong market breadth indicate that the current rally has gained considerable strength. However, the sizeable foreign outflow remains a key point to monitor, while the ASPI’s move firmly above 22,000 makes this level an important near-term support area.
