Sampath Bank launches SL’s 1st community-powered book discovery platform

Sampath GPay customers can enjoy 25% cash back on Book Fair purchases

Sampath Bank PLC has launched Sampath Book Finder, Sri Lanka’s first community-powered book discovery platform, giving the visitors to the Colombo International Book Fair a faster and more seamless way to find the books they are looking for across the fair’s 150+ stalls, while also opening up new opportunities to discover titles recommended by fellow readers.

Introduced on the opening day of the Colombo International Book Fair on September 25, Sampath Book Finder brings together digital innovation and the collective knowledge of readers to address a familiar challenge at one of Sri Lanka’s largest literary gatherings, where the scale of the fair can make locating a particular title a time-consuming exercise.

When a book proves difficult to locate, the visitors can access bookfairtracker.com or scan the designated QR code and submit a request to the community, allowing the readers who have already spotted the title to share its location and help others find it without having to search stall after stall.

Sampath Bank Chief Marketing Officer Milinda Weerasinghe said, “At Sampath Bank, we believe meaningful innovation begins by identifying everyday challenges and creating solutions that make a real difference. Sampath Book Finder brings this thinking to the Colombo International Book Fair by turning the collective knowledge of readers into a real-time digital solution, empowering them to find what they are looking for, discover something new and help others do the same, while making the overall experience more convenient and connected.”

The platform also turns book discovery into a shared experience, allowing the visitors to recommend books they encounter by sharing the title, publisher, hall and stall number, which enables fellow readers to discover sought-after titles and recommendations while making it easier to locate them within the fair.

Sampath Bank’s long-standing association with the Colombo International Book Fair also continues in 2026, marking more than 20 years of partnership, with the bank serving as the Official Banking Partner for this year’s fair.

As part of its presence at the event, SampathCards offer 25 percent cashback for Sampath GPay customers on their purchases and three months zero percent instalment plans for book purchases via Sampath credit cards, while the visitors can also access banking services through the bank’s physical presence at the fair.

The customers registering to open selected fixed deposits on the premises will receive an additional 0.25 percent, adding further value and convenience for the visitors.

The initiative reflects Sampath Bank’s continued commitment to developing future-ready solutions that make everyday experiences more convenient, accessible and meaningful, while demonstrating how digital innovation can be applied to address practical needs beyond traditional financial services. Bringing this approach to a space centred on reading, learning and discovery, Sampath Book Finder further reinforces the bank’s broader commitment to empowering communities through technology and creating meaningful experiences for its stakeholders.

The Colombo International Book Fair, organised by the Sri Lanka Book Publishers’ Association, continues at the BMICH until October 4, from 9:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. daily.
