Ananda Palitha arrested over CPC overtime payment fraud

Colombo, Nov. 16 (Daily Mirror) - Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) Executive Committee member and Samagi Joint Trade Union Alliance Convener Ananda Palitha has been arrested over an alleged fraud involving employee overtime payments at the Ceylon Petroleum Corporation (CPC) between 2015 and 2019.

He was arrested by officers of the Colombo Central Criminal Investigation Bureau in connection with the alleged financial irregularities.
