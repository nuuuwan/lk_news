Bus driver involved in fatal Rambewa accident found to have been under influence of drugs

Colombo, Nov. 14 (Daily Mirror) - Mihintale Police have confirmed that the driver of the bus involved in the recent accident, which killed four people and injured 76 others, had been under the influence of drugs, according to the post-mortem examination.

Police said reckless driving by the driver had caused the accident. The ill-fated bus belonged to the Kebithigollewa depot. The driver has been identified as Lanka Jayanath, a resident of Ataweeragollewa, Hirallugama, Kebithigollewa.

Earlier, Transport Minister Bimal Rathnayake also confirmed that the driver had been under the influence of drugs at the time of the accident.

The accident occurred when a Colombo-bound bus travelling from Janakapura collided head-on with a Jaffna-bound bus travelling from Akkaraipattu. Meanwhile, another Colombo-bound private bus travelling from Vavuniya crashed into the two buses from behind.

The accident occurred on the morning of November 10 near the Rambewa–Weli Oya junction on the A9 Kandy–Jaffna main road in Anuradhapura. Four people were killed and 76 others were injured when three buses collided.
