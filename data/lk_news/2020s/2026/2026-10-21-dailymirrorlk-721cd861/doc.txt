Defence Deputy Minister reviews army operations and restructuring plans

Colombo, Oct. 21 (Daily Mirror) - Deputy Minister of Defence Major General Aruna Jayasekara (Retd) visited Army Headquarters, where he was welcomed by Commander of the Army Lieutenant General Nilantha Premaratne.

During the visit, the Deputy Minister toured the Army Headquarters Operations Room and received briefings on the Sri Lanka Army’s current operational activities, future plans and ongoing restructuring process.

Addressing officers at Army Headquarters, Major General Jayasekara commended the dedication, professionalism and commitment of Army personnel in safeguarding the country’s sovereignty and territorial integrity while ensuring national security.

The Deputy Minister also met with a cross-section of Army other ranks and civilian employees, providing an opportunity to engage with personnel serving in various capacities and acknowledge their contributions to the organisation.
