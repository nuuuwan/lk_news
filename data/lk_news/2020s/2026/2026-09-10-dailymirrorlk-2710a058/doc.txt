ACJU calls for justice for Easter Sunday attack victims

Colombo, Sep. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Representatives of the All Ceylon Jamiyyathul Ulama (ACJU) called for justice for the victims of the Easter Sunday attacks and urged that information about those responsible be revealed.

The matter was raised when ACJU representatives met President Anura Kumara Dissanayake at the Presidential Secretariat today (10).

The representatives also drew the President's attention to propaganda targeting the Muslim community both before and after the Easter Sunday attacks.

They said the victims of the attacks deserved justice and expressed their expectation that information concerning those involved would be disclosed.

The ACJU representatives also pledged their support for efforts to uncover the truth and ensure justice.

The meeting further discussed the need to prevent the spread of ethnic and religious hatred and strengthen measures against hate speech, particularly on social media.
