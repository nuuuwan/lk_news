China’s 77th National Day celebrated in Colombo

Minister Krishantha Abesena, the chief guest, attended an event marking the 77th National Day of the People’s Republic of China and the 45th anniversary of the Sri Lanka-China Association at BMICH in Colombo. Deputy Minister of Tourism Professor Ruwan Ranasinghe, Chinese Embassy Cultural Counsellor Ji Lili, Sri Lanka-China Association President Jinith de Silva and other distinguished guests also attended the event.
