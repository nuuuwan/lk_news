Importers, traders called for talks to prevent market shortages

Colombo, Nov. 2 (Daily Mirror) - The Government has taken steps to ensure an uninterrupted supply of essential goods to the local market during the upcoming festive season and prevent possible shortages, Trade Minister Wasantha Samarasinghe said.

The Minister said discussions were underway with key stakeholders, including the Cooperative Wholesale Establishment (CWE), Sathosa, cooperative societies, Pettah-based importer associations and local manufacturers to maintain a steady supply of goods.

He said these coordinated efforts were aimed at ensuring essential commodities remained readily available in the open market throughout the festive period.

Acknowledging that minor price fluctuations could occur during the season, the Minister said such changes were not unique to Sri Lanka.

However, he said that the Government was engaging with importers and manufacturers to maintain market stability and minimise the financial burden on consumers.
