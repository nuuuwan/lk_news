Kakkaitivu Harbour reconstruction begins

Sep. 11 (Daily Mirror) - Reconstruction work on the Kakkaitivu Savarkattu Fishery Association Harbour in Jaffna, which was severely damaged by Cyclone ‘Ditwah’, officially commenced on September 10 under the direction of Fisheries, Aquatic and Ocean Resources Minister Ramalingam Chandrasekar.

The project follows longstanding requests from the Savarkattu fishing community. Following the required approvals from relevant authorities, including the Coast Conservation Department, the Ministry of Fisheries has allocated an initial Rs. 2.1 million for the first phase of the redevelopment.

The initial work will focus on clearing moss and algae, land filling and levelling the harbour premises, with the aim of enabling local fishermen to resume their daily activities without further disruption.

The inauguration was attended by Jaffna District Secretary M. Pradeepan, the Divisional Secretary, Assistant Director of the Department of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Akilan, and officials from several government institutions.

Jaffna Municipal Council Member and Jaffna District Coordinating Committee Coordinator Sundaramurthy Kapilan, representatives of the Coast Conservation Department, Pradeshiya Sabha members, fisheries association representatives and a large number of local fishermen also participated in the event.
