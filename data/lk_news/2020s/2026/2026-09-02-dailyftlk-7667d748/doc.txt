Arctek Innovations achieves International Standards Certification, strengthening commitment to quality and food safety

Arctek Innovations has achieved a significant milestone with the successful implementation of internationally recognised management systems covering quality, food safety, good manufacturing practices, and hazard control

Arctek Innovations Ltd., a Sri Lankan manufacturer of food-grade adhesive solutions, has achieved compliance with several internationally recognised standards, including ISO 9001:2015 for Quality Management Systems, ISO 22000:2018 for Food Safety Management Systems, Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP), and Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Point (HACCP).

The certifications cover the company’s operations relating to the manufacturing, packaging, storage, and dispatch of food-grade polymer-based (PVA) and starch-based adhesives for commercial applications. The achievement represents an important step in Arctek Innovations’ ongoing efforts to strengthen its manufacturing capabilities and provide customers with products that consistently meet stringent quality and food safety requirements.

The achievement reflects the strategic vision and leadership of Sumedha Abeyrathna, Nayomi Rathnayaka, Sanjaya Seneratne, Charitha Perera, whose collective commitment has driven the organisation’s transformation towards greater operational excellence and continual improvement.

The certification process involved a comprehensive review and strengthening of the company’s operational systems. Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) were established and enhanced, Good Manufacturing Practices were strengthened, HACCP principles were incorporated, and a risk-based approach was introduced across key operational processes.

In parallel, Arctek Innovations strengthened employee training, internal auditing, supplier management, preventive maintenance, product traceability, and customer satisfaction monitoring. These initiatives have contributed to the development of an integrated management system designed to support consistent performance and continuous improvement.

Commenting on the achievement, the management of Arctek Innovations said, “Achieving these internationally recognised standards is a proud milestone for our organisation. It demonstrates the commitment, teamwork, and professionalism of every member of the Arctek family. More importantly, it reinforces our promise to provide safe, reliable, and high-quality adhesive solutions that our customers can trust. We view certification not as the finish line, but as the foundation for continuous improvement and sustainable growth.”

The achievement comes at a time when the demand for reliable food-grade adhesive solutions continues to grow across food packaging and related manufacturing sectors. As manufacturers and packaging companies place increasing emphasis on product safety, traceability, regulatory compliance, and consistent quality, the adoption of internationally recognised management systems has become an important competitive advantage.

Arctek Innovations continues to invest in modern manufacturing practices, process improvements, employee development, and customer-focused innovation. The company believes that maintaining robust management systems will further strengthen its ability to serve both local and international markets while supporting compliance with applicable regulatory and industry requirements.

The implementation of ISO 9001, ISO 22000, GMP, and HACCP demonstrates Arctek Innovations’ commitment to embedding quality and food safety into its day-to-day operations rather than treating certification as a standalone achievement.

By integrating risk management, process controls, employee competency, supplier evaluation, traceability, and continual improvement into its operating culture, the company aims to provide customers with greater confidence in the consistency and reliability of its products.

For Arctek Innovations, the certification milestone is therefore not simply a recognition of systems already established, but a foundation for further development. The company intends to continue improving its processes, investing in innovation, and strengthening its capabilities as it works towards sustainable growth.

As Arctek Innovations moves forward, the company remains focused on innovation, quality, food safety, operational excellence, and sustainable business growth. Its internationally aligned management systems provide a structured platform for achieving these objectives while supporting the company’s ambition to build long-term partnerships based on trust, consistency, and performance.

With its latest certification achievements, Arctek Innovations is reinforcing its position as a Sri Lankan manufacturer committed to responsible manufacturing and the delivery of dependable adhesive solutions for commercial applications.
