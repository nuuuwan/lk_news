Special dengue prevention week from November 9 to 14 in 74 high-risk areas

Colombo, Nov. 3 (Daily Mirror) - A special National Dengue Control Week will be conducted from November 9 to 14, covering 74 high-risk Medical Officer of Health (MOH) divisions across the country.

The programme has been launched in response to the trend of dengue cases reported during previous outbreaks, dengue mosquito breeding indicators and the increased risk of an outbreak due to changing weather conditions.

The National Dengue Control Unit, together with the Ministry of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government, the Clean Sri Lanka Secretariat, Sri Lanka Police, the Tri-Forces and voluntary organisations, will coordinate dengue prevention activities during the six-day programme.

The activities will include deploying joint inspection teams, identifying and destroying mosquito breeding sites, and cleaning unoccupied premises.

Special cleaning programmes will also be conducted at schools, Pirivenas, universities and religious places to prevent the spread of dengue.

An operations room will monitor the activities carried out in the selected MOH divisions on a daily basis.

Acting Director of the National Dengue Control Unit Dr. Kapila Kannangara said the unit was continuously monitoring mosquito breeding indicators and reported dengue cases to prevent a possible outbreak.
