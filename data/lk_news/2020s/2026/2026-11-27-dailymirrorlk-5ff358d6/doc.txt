Bus-truck collision in Ratnapura leaves sixteen injured

Colombo, Nov. 27 (Daily Mirror) - Sixteen people were injured, five of them seriously, in a head-on collision between a passenger bus and a tipper truck in Ratnapura this morning (27).

The accident occurred along the Ratnapura–Avissawella road when a bus travelling from Avissawella towards Ratnapura collided with a tipper truck approaching from the opposite direction, Police said.

All 16 injured persons were rushed to the Ratnapura Teaching Hospital for treatment.

Police said five of them were in serious condition, while the other 11 had sustained minor injuries.
