Taste of Shangri-La brings a world of flavour to Colombo, Hambantota

This October, Shangri-La Colombo and Shangri-La Hambantota invite diners to experience the Taste of Shangri-La, a month-long global celebration of culinary artistry taking place from 1 to 31 October 2026.

Bringing together 25 restaurants and bars across 11 Shangri-La hotels in Europe, Canada, India, Sri Lanka, the Indian Ocean and the Middle East, Taste of Shangri-La presents a collection of specially curated menus inspired by the individual character of each restaurant and destination.

From Cantonese craftsmanship in Paris and contemporary British-Asian cuisine high above London to Northern Italian flavours in Toronto and the culinary traditions of India, the campaign offers diners a journey around the world through the kitchens of Shangri-La.

Sri Lanka joins this international celebration with four experiences spanning two strikingly different settings: the cosmopolitan energy of Colombo and the open landscapes of the island’s southern coast. At Shangri-La Colombo, Shang Palace and Capital Bar & Grill explore the meeting point between tradition and contemporary technique. At Shangri-La Hambantota, Ulpatha and Sera draw inspiration from the sea, the land and the vibrant flavours of Southeast Asia.

At Shangri-La Colombo, the journey begins at Capital Bar & Grill, where the elemental drama of fire shapes a menu defined by smoke, char and confident flavour.

The Taste of Shangri-La tasting menu opens with choices including orange-cured salmon and yellowfin tuna before moving through a selection of thoughtfully composed mains. An adobo-glazed smoked chicken leg is paired with garlic crémeux, sweetcorn croquette and confit pepper sauce, while harissa lends warmth to a barramundi fillet.

A leek and organic mushroom Wellington provides an equally considered meat-free alternative. Each plate reflects the restaurant’s character: generous yet refined, grounded in familiar flavours but sharpened through contemporary technique.

A few steps away, Shang Palace turns to the elegance and precision of Cantonese cuisine. Here, tradition is not simply preserved; it is interpreted with an assured sense of theatre.

The four-course tasting menu begins with delicately crafted dim sum, including a seafood dumpling shaped to resemble a sea cucumber. Mixed mushrooms are deepened with truffle, while crispy braised beef ribs bring together star anise, Ceylon cinnamon and Hua Diao wine in a dish of aromatic richness. Garden-fresh vegetables wok-tossed with hearty red kidney beans.

The result is a menu that honours ancient Chinese culinary wisdom while allowing each dish to speak in a distinctly contemporary voice.

At Shangri-La Hambantota, Taste of Shangri-La moves beyond the city and follows the rhythm of the southern coast, where the landscape itself becomes part of the dining experience.

Overlooking the resort’s golf course, Ulpatha offers a relaxed setting for a four-course menu that draws together seafood, contemporary favourites and subtle Sri Lankan influences. Pan-seared yellowfin tuna and cinnamon-infused white onion soup lead into a choice of mains, including a dill-marinated grilled barramundi mosaic accompanied by cuttlefish and leek compote and Sri Lankan-style tempered lagoon prawns.

Other selections include cheese and spinach-stuffed baked chicken breast, beef medallion and ricotta-spinach ravioli, before the menu closes with rich chocolate fudge and mango-passionfruit ice cream. It is an experience shaped by contrast: delicacy and depth, land and sea, familiar ingredients and unexpected combinations.

At Sera, the journey travels further east. Inspired by the lively food cultures of Southeast Asia, its family-style menu is designed for the table rather than the individual—generous, colourful and made to be shared.

Satay ayam with raw papaya salad begins the experience, followed by the bright heat of tom yum goong. Hot garlic prawns, steamed freshwater fish with chilli-coriander sauce, Singapore-style vegetable noodles and Sichuan chilli chicken with cashew bring together layers of spice, fragrance and texture. Thai sticky rice pudding with mango-coconut sauce provides a cooling, gently sweet finale.

Taste of Shangri-La reflects a culinary philosophy that has long been central to the brand: that a restaurant can be a destination in its own right, and that a memorable meal is capable of connecting diners with cultures and places far beyond the table before them.

Across Colombo and Hambantota, this philosophy takes on two distinct expressions. One is urbane, polished and international in outlook; the other is shaped by the warmth, generosity and natural abundance of Sri Lanka’s southern coast. Together, they offer diners four different ways to experience the imagination, craftsmanship and sense of place that define Shangri-La’s global culinary identity.
