CMA Sri Lanka recognises outstanding professional accountants

From left: Kaushika. N. Jayalath, Dr. D. B. S. Chamara Bandara, Suren Rajakarier, Gayathri Manathunga, Sanjeewa Samaranayake, T. Chaaminda D. Kumarasiri, Mohammed Humayun Kabir, Prof. Dale Pinto, Prof. Lakshman R. Watawala, W. L. Badrajith Siriwardana, R. Aloysius, J. Felix Fernandopulle, Rodney L. R. Balasingham and H. D. H. Karunasekara

At the CMA Sri Lanka National Management Accounting Conference 2026 where the Chief Guest was Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya, held on 20 and 21 July 2026 at Cinnamon Life, the “CMA Business Excellence Awards 2026” were presented to distinguished accounting professionals in recognition of their outstanding achievements in the profession, exemplary leadership, and significant contributions to their respective fields.

The prestigious “Business Excellence Awards 2026” were presented to: Oracle Australia Principal Solutions Consultant Kaushika N Jayalath; AAT Sri Lanka President Dr. D. B. S. Chamara Bandara, KPMG Sri Lanka & Maldives Managing Partner Suren Rajakarier;  EY Sri Lanka & Maldives Country Managing Partner Gayathri Manathunga; SLT PLC Chief Financial Officer Sanjeewa Samaranayake; The Lanka Hospitals Corporation PLC Non-Executive Director T. Chaaminda D. Kumarasiri; Kings Hospital Colombo CEO W. L. Badrajith Siriwardana; The Autodrome PLC Joint Managing Director R. Aloysius; Mahaweli Coconut Plantations PLC Managing Director J. Felix Fernandopulle; Kreston MNS & Co Partner Rodeney L. R. Balasingham; Kelani Cables PLC Chief Financial Officer H. D. H. Karunasekara.

The awards were presented by SAFA President Mohammed Humayun Kabir, CPA Australia President Prof Dale Pinto, CMA President Prof. Lakshman R. Watawala, in recognition of the recipients’ excellence, visionary leadership, and valuable contributions to the advancement of the accounting profession and the outstanding contribution to the corporate sector.
