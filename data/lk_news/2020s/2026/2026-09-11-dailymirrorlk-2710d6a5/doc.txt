NFF to rally with SLPP on Sept. 12

Colombo, Sep. 11 (Daily Mirror) - The National Freedom Front (NFF) has decided to participate in a rally organised by the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) on September 12.

NFF leader Wimal Weerawansa said the party’s Political Council had agreed to accept the SLPP’s invitation and support the event.

Weerawansa said the decision was aimed at encouraging greater cooperation among opposition groups around issues of national importance.

He said the public had elected the present Government with high expectations, particularly following the country’s economic crisis and calls for action against corruption.

However, he criticised the Government, alleging that it had failed to meet some of the expectations placed on it and had not delivered adequately on its promises to address corruption.
