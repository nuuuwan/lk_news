National Project Management Excellence Awards 2026 FOUNDATION.LK’s Hithawathi Teens Hub Expansion Project bags Gold

FOUNDATION.LK, the social empowerment arm of LK Domain Registry, has achieved a significant milestone with its Hithawathi Teens Hub Expansion Project receiving the Gold award at National Project Management Excellence Awards (NPMEA) 2026, organised by the PMI Colombo, Sri Lanka Chapter.

The project was awarded under Category 13 – Best Managed Project in Community Development/NGO, recognising excellence in project management and its contribution to creating safer digital environments for young people.

The award is a significant achievement for FOUNDATION.LK and Hithawathi, reaffirming their commitment to promoting cyber safety and digital empowerment among Sri Lankan communities.

“This recognition is a proud moment for FOUNDATION.LK and the entire Hithawathi team. It also recognises the collective efforts of our partners, education authorities, teachers and students who contributed to making the Teens Hub initiative a success,” stated FOUNDATION.LK CEO Niroja Jayawickrama.

The Hithawathi Teens Hub initiative was initially introduced as a pilot project to establish school-based spaces where the students could learn about cyber safety, understand responsible digital behaviour and share their knowledge with their peers.

Following the successful pilot, the initiative was expanded with the sponsorship of LK Domain Registry, following a request by LK Domain Registry Domain Registrar and CEO Prof. Gihan Dias to extend the programme to more schools.
