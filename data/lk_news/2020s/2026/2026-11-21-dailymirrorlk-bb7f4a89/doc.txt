Chatham Luxury-IWC Schaffhausen host watchmaking workshop

Chatham Luxury and IWC Schaffhausen hosted an exclusive watchmaking workshop recently.

The event offered the guests a hands-on opportunity to explore the art of Swiss watchmaking, gaining a deeper appreciation of the precision, craftsmanship and technical expertise behind IWC Schaffhausen’s timepieces.

The workshop reflects Chatham Luxury’s commitment to bringing distinctive Swiss watchmaking experiences to Sri Lankan enthusiasts.
