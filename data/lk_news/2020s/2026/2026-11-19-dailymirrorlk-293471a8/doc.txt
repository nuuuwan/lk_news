Public Sector pay hikes not yet proportionate to cost of living: Lal Kantha

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - Agriculture Minister K. D. Lal Kantha acknowledged in Parliament today that public sector salary increases granted by the government have not yet been proportionate to the rising cost of living, while emphasizing the necessity of achieving that balance.

Addressing the House, the Minister noted that all trade unions—including those affiliated with the Janatha Vimukthi Peramuna (JVP)—have long championed two primary demands: the incorporation of allowances into the basic salary, and wage adjustments aligned with the cost of living.

Minister Lal Kantha pointed out that while the government has raised basic salaries across its three budgets, it has not yet been able to scale those increments to match living expenses.

He further informed Parliament that all trade unions have been requested to submit their salary-related grievances to the Public Service Salaries and Pensions Commission before December 31, assuring that appropriate measures will be taken once the commission submits its recommendations.
