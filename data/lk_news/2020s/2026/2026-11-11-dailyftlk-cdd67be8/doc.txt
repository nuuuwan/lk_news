Cabinet approves MoU between University of Jaffna and Southern Illinois University

The Cabinet of Ministers has approved the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between the University of Jaffna and the Board of Trustees of Southern Illinois University, United States, to remain in effect for five years from the date of signing.

Addressing the weekly post-Caninet meeting media briefing yesterday, Cabinet Spokesman and Minister Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa said the agreement aims to achieve excellence in academic activities and further develop the friendly relationship between the two countries, while enhancing and disseminating knowledge.

Under the MoU, the two institutions are expected to cooperate on joint research and academic endeavours, and to promote international experience through the exchange of academic staff for research, lecturing and discussions across all academic sectors.

He said the partnership will also encourage undergraduate and postgraduate students in their studies, research and internship training. The two universities will organise joint summits, conferences and seminars, and link their websites to facilitate the exchange of information.

The proposal to this effect was submitted by Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya  in her capacity as the Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education Minister.
