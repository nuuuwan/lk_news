13 arrested after allegedly attempting to travel to India

Colombo, Sep. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Thirteen individuals, including a woman, have been arrested after allegedly attempting to travel to India by sea, police said.

The group had reportedly attempted to reach India by sea but was turned back by Indian customs officials.

Following their return to Sri Lanka, the suspects were taken into custody by officers of the Department of Immigration and Emigration and handed over to Kankesanthurai Police for further investigations yesterday (06).

The suspects are residents of Karunkodithivu, Mampitiya, Medawachchiya, Haldummulla, Wellawaya, Monaragala, Mavadichenai, Balangoda, Trincomalee, Badulla and Pottuvil.

They are currently being detained at the Kankesanthurai Police Station and are scheduled to be produced before court today (07).
