People’s Bank wins Overall Gold at National Business Excellence Awards 2026

People’s Bank has won the Overall Gold Award at the National Business Excellence Awards (NBEA) 2026 organised by the National Chamber of Commerce of Sri Lanka (NCCSL), recognising its performance in business leadership, governance, innovation, customer service and sustainable practices.

The bank also secured three other awards, winning the Excellence in the Extra-Large Category, Local Market Reach Award and Banking Sector Award.

People’s Bank Chairman Prof. Narada Fernando said the recognition reflected the commitment of employees across the country and the trust placed in the bank by customers over more than six decades.

Chief Executive Officer/General Manager Clive Fonseka said the award recognised the professionalism and teamwork of the bank’s employees while reaffirming its commitment to improving services, advancing digital transformation and strengthening its role in Sri Lanka’s banking sector.

The NBEA recognition highlights People’s Bank’s achievements in corporate governance, operational excellence, customer-focused services, innovation and sustainable business practices.

With consolidated assets exceeding Rs. 3.7 trillion, People’s Bank serves more than 15.2 million customers through a network of 746 branches and service centres, supported by over 8,100 employees.

The bank also operates more than 300 Self Banking Units, providing customers with round-the-clock access to banking services.
