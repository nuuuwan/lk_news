Sri Lanka, Thailand exchange visa-free travel agreement

Colombo, Nov 23 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake met Thai Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Foreign Affairs Sihasak Phuangketkeow at the Presidential Secretariat yesterday (22), with the two countries exchanging an agreement on visa exemption for holders of ordinary passports.

Phuangketkeow is visiting Sri Lanka as the Royal Representative of Thai King Maha Vajiralongkorn to attend the Royal Kathina Robe Offering Ceremony at the Asgiri Maha Viharaya in Kandy.

During the meeting, the two leaders discussed ways to further strengthen bilateral relations, recalling the longstanding cultural and religious ties between Sri Lanka and Thailand.

President Dissanayake said diplomatic visits of this nature would further reinforce the historic religious and cultural relations between the two countries.

The Thai Deputy Prime Minister said the Thai Prime Minister was keen to visit Sri Lanka. In response, President Dissanayake said he would invite the Thai Prime Minister to visit Sri Lanka to witness the historic Kandy Esala Perahera scheduled to be held in August next year.

The two sides also discussed expanding cooperation in trade, investment, tourism, agriculture and foreign employment.

Meanwhile, the agreement on visa exemption for holders of ordinary passports was exchanged between Thai Deputy Prime Minister Sihasak Phuangketkeow and Sri Lankan Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Vijitha Herath.

Phuangketkeow said the agreement would significantly contribute to promoting tourism between the two countries and strengthening business ties.

He also said Thailand's ready to provide technical assistance to Sri Lanka, particularly in the agricultural sector, when needed.

The meeting also discussed the welfare of Sri Lankan workers employed in Thailand, with the Thai Deputy Prime Minister assuring that foreign workers were treated equally to Thai citizens and that their safety and welfare would be ensured.

Phuangketkeow also invited President Dissanayake to undertake an official visit to Thailand in the near future.

The meeting was attended by senior Sri Lankan and Thai government officials, including Secretary to the President Dr Nandika Sanath Kumanayake and Thai Ambassador to Sri Lanka Paitoon Mahapannaporn.
