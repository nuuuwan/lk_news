Health Ministry sets up new dengue control centre

Colombo, November 11 (Daily Mirror) - The Ministry of Health has established a new Dengue Control Operations Office to coordinate and strengthen dengue prevention and control activities amid rising mosquito density and concerns over reported cases.

The new office was established yesterday (9) within the Ministry's Disaster Preparedness and Response Division (DPRD).

The move comes as the Ministry has declared a special Dengue Control Week from November 9 to 14, covering 74 Medical Officer of Health (MOH) divisions across 13 districts identified as being at risk of dengue.

The newly established office will oversee and coordinate activities carried out during the special control week to ensure that operations are implemented systematically and without delay.

Following its establishment, a special meeting was held yesterday under the chairmanship of Health and Mass Media Minister Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa to discuss the activities planned for the week and the measures required to strengthen dengue control efforts.

Health Ministry Secretary Dr. Anil Jasinghe, Additional Secretary (Medical Services) W.K. Wickramasinghe, Additional Secretary (Public Health Services) Dr. S. Mahendra Arnold, National Dengue Control Unit Director Dr. Kapila Kannangara and Head of the Disaster Preparedness and Response Unit Dr. Asanka Wedamulla attended the meeting.

Consultant Physician at the Dengue Control Unit Dr. Prashila Samaraweera, representatives of the Clean Sri Lanka Secretariat, Sri Lanka Police, Armed Forces and several voluntary organisations also participated.
