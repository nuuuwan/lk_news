Healthguard opens 17th outlet in Mount Lavinia

Healthguard opened of its 17th outlet in Mount Lavinia recently.

The new outlet was declared open by Sunshine Holdings PLC Group Chief Executive Officer Shyam Sathasivam and Group Chief People and Corporate Communications Officer Michelle Senanayake, in the presence of Healthguard management and outlet staff.

Chief Operations Officer for Healthguard Pharmacy chain, Farman Nizar said, “Our expansion into Mount Lavinia reflects our continued commitment to making quality healthcare more accessible to communities. Through our customer-focused approach and signature Purple Service, we aim to deliver a trusted and personalised pharmacy experience at every Healthguard outlet.”
