Bourse sags under persisting global economic uncertainty

The CSE yesterday was trending down due to prevailing global economic uncertainty but the local capital goods sector pushed turnover to a somewhat respectable level, market analysts said.The All Share Price Index went down by 422.67 points, while the S and P SL20 declined by 91.20 points. Turnover stood at Rs 3.26 billion with seven crossings.

Those crossings were: CIC Holdings 6.7 million shares crossed to the tune of Rs 205 million; its shares traded at Rs 30.50, HNB (Non-Voting) 250,000 shares crossed for Rs 86.2 million; its shares sold at Rs 345, LMF 500,000 shares crossed to the tune of Rs 41.5 million; its shares traded at Rs 83, Lanka IOC 200,000 shares crossed for Rs 28.2 million; its shares traded at Rs 141, RIL Properties one million shares crossed to the tune of Rs 27.2 million; its shares sold at Rs 27.20, NTB 73000 shares crossed for Rs 21.5 million; its shares traded at Rs 295 and Digital Mobility Solutions 140,000 shares crossed for Rs 20 million; its shares traded at Rs 143.

In the retail market top, companies that mainly contributed to the turnover were; Acme Printers Rs 339 million (51.7 million shares traded), Jetwing Symphony Rs 249 million (18.2 million shares traded), CIC Holdings Rs 186 million (6.1 million shares traded), Colombo Dockyard Rs 109 million (1.3 million shares traded), Softlogic Rs 103 million (7.1 million shares traded) and Lanka IOC Rs 88.5 million (603,000 shares traded). During the day 176 million share volumes changed hands in 29787 transactions.

It is said that the manufacturing, banking and real estate sectors and the energy supply sectors performed well.

Yesterday the rupee was quoted at Rs 313.90/314.25 to the US dollar in the spot market, depreciating from Rs 313.80/314.50 the previous day, dealers said, while bond yields opened up.
