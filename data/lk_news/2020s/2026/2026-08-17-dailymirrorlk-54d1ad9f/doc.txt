Elephant takes a peaceful stroll through Kandalama hotel lobby

Colombo, Aug. 17 (Daily Mirror) - Guests and staff at a Kandalama hotel were given an unexpected surprise when a wild elephant calmly wandered into the hotel lobby before making its way back outside.

A video circulating on social media shows the elephant walking peacefully through the hotel premises as a staff member films the unusual encounter.

Rather than causing panic or damaging property, the elephant appears remarkably calm as it moves through the lobby, passing by furniture and other hotel facilities without disturbing anything.

According to the video, the elephant eventually walked out of the hotel on its own, leaving without harming any employees or damaging hotel property.

The unusual encounter has since attracted attention online, with many viewers fascinated by the sight of an elephant taking what appeared to be a quiet stroll through the hotel.
