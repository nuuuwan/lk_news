Prime Lands hands over 43 by the Sea

Prime Lands Residencies PLC recently announced the official handover of 43 by the Sea, welcoming its homeowners to one of Colombo’s most distinctive residential addresses on Marine Drive.

Construction commenced in 2023 and the development has now been brought to life in full, marking the fulfilment of a commitment made to every buyer who placed his or her trust in the project from the very beginning.

The handover ceremony that was held recently carried the weight of a promise delivered. The homeowners gathered at 43 by the Sea for an evening of fellowship during which Prime Residences officially handed over the project, setting the foundation for a community by the ocean to begin taking shape.

For those who had chosen this address years earlier, drawn by the prospect of ocean views, city connectivity and the quality that Prime Lands Residencies is known for, the day confirmed that what had been envisioned had been realised without compromise.

The development has been conceived around the defining idea of where the city meets the shore, a location where proximity to the ocean is matched in equal measure by access to Colombo’s leading schools, hospitals, commercial centres and cultural destinations. Furthermore, the project was developed in accordance with all the requisite approvals. All the transfer deeds are ready to be executed and the project is completely sold out.

Rising ground plus 12 floors on Marine Drive, Colombo, 43 by the Sea offers an exclusive collection of three-bedroom and four-bedroom apartments set against one of the city’s most commanding coastal addresses. Every residence has been designed to draw fully from this setting, with layouts, orientations and finishes that reflect the standard expected of an address of this nature. The result is a development that places its residents at the intersection of two of Colombo’s most compelling qualities, its energy as a city and its openness to the sea.

The completion of 43 by the Sea reflects Prime Lands Residencies PLC’s approach to development, one grounded in the belief that a promise made to a buyer is a commitment that must be honoured in full. Construction is complete, the building has been officially handed over and residents are being welcomed home. That sequence, from vision through to delivery, represents the standard that Prime Lands Residencies holds itself to across every project it undertakes.
