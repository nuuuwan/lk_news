Clean Colombo drive focuses on drainage, waste management

Colombo,Oct. 9 (Daily Mirror) - The progress of the Clean Sri Lanka initiative’s ‘Clean Colombo’ programme was reviewed at a meeting held at the Presidential Secretariat this morning (09), under the chairmanship of Secretary to the President Dr. Nandika Sanath Kumanayake.

The programme focuses on improving Colombo’s urban environment through the rehabilitation of sewerage networks and drainage canals, environmental beautification and improvements to waste management.

During the meeting, officials held extensive discussions on ongoing projects and action plans being implemented by the relevant institutions, while reviewing progress made so far.

The Colombo Municipal Council, Sri Lanka Land Development Corporation, Urban Development Authority, Road Development Authority, Department of Coast Conservation and Coastal Resource Management, and Sri Lanka Railways are the main institutions involved in implementing the programme.

The meeting also stressed the importance of integrating other Clean Sri Lanka initiatives being implemented across the country that are relevant to Colombo into the Clean Colombo programme.

These initiatives aim to promote social well-being, environmental sustainability and an ethical revival by encouraging positive changes in public behaviour.
