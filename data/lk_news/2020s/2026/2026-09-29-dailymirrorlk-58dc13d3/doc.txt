Former NTC Chairman Renuka Perera released on bail

Colombo, Sep. 29 (Daily Mirror) - Former Chairman of the National Transport Commission, Renuka Perera, who was arrested by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption in connection with a corruption allegation, was released on bail by Colombo Chief Magistrate Asanga S. Bodaragama.

He was ordered to be released on two surties of Rs. 5 million each.
