Joint emergency response mechanism established at Jaffna Prison

A joint coordination mechanism involving state agencies has been established at Jaffna Prison to strengthen regional responses to emergencies and improve coordination during incidents within the prison system.

The initiative was launched under the patronage of Minister of Justice and National Integration, Attorney-at-Law Harshana Nanayakkara. The programme is aimed at streamlining emergency response and enhancing prisoner welfare through closer coordination among state institutions at the local level. Similar mechanisms are expected to be introduced at prisons across the country.

Addressing state officials at the event, Minister Nanayakkara outlined the key objectives of establishing the unified coordination network and stressed the importance of effective inter-agency cooperation during prison emergencies.

The event was attended by Jaffna District Members of Parliament K. Ilankumaran and S. Bhavanandarajah, Jaffna District Secretary M. Pradeepan, Jaffna Prison Superintendent S. Indrakumar and Jaffna Deputy Inspector General of Police G.H. Marapana. Officials representing the Water Supply Board, Department of Prisons, Sri Lanka Navy, Sri Lanka Army, Police, Jaffna Divisional Secretariat and the Medical Officer of Health’s Office also participated.

Following discussions on the implementation of the mechanism, the Justice Minister conducted an inspection of the Jaffna Prison to assess the conditions and welfare needs of inmates. He inspected several areas, including the prison kitchen, women’s section and remand housing facilities.
