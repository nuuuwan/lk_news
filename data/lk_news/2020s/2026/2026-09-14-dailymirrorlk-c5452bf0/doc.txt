Yala Blocks 1 & 2 closed for a month due to severe drought

Colombo, Sep. 14 (Daily Mirror) - The Wildlife and Forest Conservation Department has temporarily closed Blocks 1 and 2 of the Yala National Park to tourists from today (14) due to the prevailing dry weather conditions.

The closure will remain in effect for one month, until October 15, as authorities seek to minimise disturbances to wildlife struggling to find food and water amid the severe drought.

Yala National Park is home to 44 species of mammals, 215 bird species, 47 reptile species and 21 fish species. The park generally attracts the highest number of tourists between August and March. However, this year’s tourist season has been severely affected by the El Niño weather phenomenon.

The prolonged hot and dry conditions have caused several water sources inside the park to dry up, creating a serious shortage of drinking water for wildlife. Grasslands and other pasture areas have also dried up, resulting in a scarcity of food.

Wildlife officials are currently taking emergency measures to provide water to animals. Water from four locations along the Menik Ganga is being pumped using solar-powered motors and transported through underground pipelines to ponds inside the park.

In addition, wildlife officials are transporting around 200,000 litres of water daily by bowser to fill both artificial and natural ponds within the park.

Authorities said the temporary closure of the two zones was necessary to reduce disturbances caused to animals as they move in search of food and water under the prevailing harsh weather conditions.

The measure is aimed at giving wildlife greater freedom to access the limited food and water resources available within the park during the drought.
