TECNO launches CAMON 50 Pro

TECNO and John Keells Office Automation officials and team members at TECNO CAMON 50 PRO launch event

TECNO has officially introduced the CAMON 50 Pro smartphone to Sri Lanka, ushering a new era of smartphone photography with powerful innovations like SuperZoom FlashSnap, AI 60X Super Zoom, 50MP Sony Ultra Night Camera and advanced underwater photography.

Designed for young users who want to capture every moment with clarity and creativity, CAMON 50 Pro solidifies TECNO’s leadership in camera innovation and premium design.

At the core of TECNO CAMON 50 Pro is its breakthrough SuperZoom FlashSnap technology, built to capture fast-moving subjects with precision. Whether it is action shots, street moments or everyday memories, SuperZoom FlashSnap ensures sharp, blur-free images in real time. Users can also zoom into fast-moving moments up to 3X with upgraded SuperZoom FlashSnap.

Powered by intelligent AI, the system includes AI Auto Zoom, which automatically detects and tracks subjects like people and pets, while Best Moment 2.0 selects the perfect frame instantly. Together, these features make it easier than ever to capture every moment effortlessly.

Moreover, AI 60X Super Zoom in the TECNO CAMON 50 Pro smartphone allows users to capture distant subjects with impressive clarity, making it ideal for travel, events and outdoor photography. From cityscapes to landscapes, every detail remains sharp even at extreme zoom levels.

Adding to its versatility, CAMON 50 Pro introduces advanced underwater photography, enabling users to capture stunning photos and videos even in challenging conditions with Aqua Mode. Combined with IP68/IP69K Water and Dust Resistance, the device is built to perform wherever life takes you.

Speaking at the launch, TECNO Sri Lanka CEO Muhammad Shahzeb Ather said, “CAMON 50 Pro reflects our commitment to empowering Sri Lanka’s new generation to see further, create with confidence and capture every moment without limits. By combining advanced camera innovation, intelligent AI experiences and premium design, we are making meaningful technology more accessible to consumers who want to express their world in new ways.”

TECNO CAMON 50 Pro also features a powerful 50MP Sony LYT-700C Ultra Night Camera, designed to deliver exceptional performance in low light. With a large 1/1.56” sensor and f/1.8 aperture, users can capture brighter, clearer images even at night.

Enhanced with Optical Image Stabilisation and advanced pixel technology, the camera ensures stable, detailed shots across both photography and videography. A 50MP telephoto lens enables professional portrait shots, while an 8MP Ultra-wide Angle camera allows users to capture more in every frame.

TECNO CAMON 50 Pro delivers an immersive experience with its 6.78-inch curved AMOLED display, featuring 1.5K resolution and a 144Hz refresh rate for vibrant, ultra-smooth visuals. Sleek and lightweight in design, it is available in Moonshadow Black, Nebula Titanium and Malachite Green.

Powered by the MediaTek Helio G200 Ultimate processor with up to 16GB RAM (including extended RAM) and 256GB storage, the device ensures fast, seamless performance, while a 6500mAh battery with 45W fast charging supports all-day use.

Enhanced by TECNO AI, the smartphone brings intelligent photography, creative tools and everyday convenience together in one powerful, easy-to-use experience.

TECNO CAMON 50 Pro is now available across Sri Lanka through authorised TECNO retailers and leading mobile stores islandwide. Priced at Rs.159,999, the smartphone brings together advanced camera capabilities, AI-powered features and a premium design experience for consumers.
