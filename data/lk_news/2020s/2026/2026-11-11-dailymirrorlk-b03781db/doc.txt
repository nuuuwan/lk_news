President puts final touches to 2027 Budget

Colombo, Nov 11 (Daily Mirror) - The final document containing the 2027 Budget proposals was submitted to President Anura Kumara Dissanayake for review at the Presidential Secretariat today (11).

Secretary to the Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development Dr. Harshana Suriyapperuma, was also present during the review.

According to the President's Media Division, despite facing domestic and external challenges, including the Ditwah disaster and the Middle East crisis, the Government has made progress across several key economic sectors through sound economic management and strict fiscal discipline.

The 2027 Budget is expected to focus on moving beyond economic stabilisation towards economic expansion and transformative development.

President Dissanayake, in his capacity as Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development, is scheduled to present the 2027 Budget to Parliament tomorrow (12) afternoon.
