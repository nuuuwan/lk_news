Grama Niladhari officers call off TU action

Colombo, Aug. 17 (Daily Mirror) - Grama Niladhari (GN) officers have decided to call off their ongoing trade union (TU) action following discussions held today (17) with Minister of Public Administration Professor Chandana Abeyratne and the Secretary to the Ministry.

Representatives of the GN officers’ trade unions said President Anura Kumara Dissanayake also joined the discussions by telephone and assured them that steps would be taken to address their demands through the upcoming Budget.

Following the discussions and the assurances given, the unions decided to end the trade union action.
