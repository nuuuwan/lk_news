Who is ‘Rani Sri Lanki’? Foreign Ministry seeks public help

Colombo, Oct 15 (Daily Mirror) - The Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism has sought public assistance to identify a deceased woman believed to be a Sri Lankan national who reportedly lived in Lebanon for more than 42 years.

According to a public notice issued by the Ministry, the woman was reportedly known by the name “Rani Sri Lanki”.

The Ministry has urged anyone with information that could help establish her identity or trace her family members and known contacts to come forward.

Information can be provided to the Consular Affairs Division of the Ministry at the 16th Floor of ‘Suhurupaya’, Battaramulla.

The public can also contact the Ministry via its hotline on +94 11 227 5525 or email [email protected].

The Ministry has requested that relevant information be provided within 14 days of the notice, adding that all information received would be treated with strict confidentiality.
