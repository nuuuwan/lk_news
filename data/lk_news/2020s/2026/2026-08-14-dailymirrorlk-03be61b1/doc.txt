Woman in Colombo arrested over Rs. 57M drug-linked assets

Colombo, August 14 (Daily Mirror) - A 53-year-old woman has been arrested by the Illegal Assets Investigation Division over properties and assets allegedly acquired through drug trafficking.

Police said the investigation was launched after the Inspector General of Police (IGP) received information regarding a drug trafficker residing on Ferguson Road in Colombo 14, who had allegedly accumulated substantial assets through the illegal trade.

Following reports submitted to court by the division, the assets and properties were frozen under the provisions of the Prevention of Money Laundering Act. The freezing orders were subsequently extended by the High Courts of Negombo and Colombo.

The frozen assets include an 8.66-perch plot of land in Ja-Ela valued at Rs. 1.4 million, Rs. 5.9 million deposited in six bank accounts, three shop rooms in Colombo 14 valued at Rs. 22.5 million, and another shop room in Colombo 14 valued at Rs. 7.5 million.

The assets also include a plot of land with a three-storey house in Colombo 14, valued at Rs. 20 million.

Police said investigations had revealed that the properties and assets were allegedly acquired through drug trafficking. The suspect, a 53-year-old woman and resident of Colombo 14, was arrested by officers of the Illegal Assets Investigation Division on August 13, 2026.
