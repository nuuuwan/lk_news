University staff protest over Budget demands

Members of the University Trade Union Joint Committee (UTUJC) stage a protest outside the University Grants Commission in Colombo today (18), demanding that the Government address their outstanding concerns, which they say were overlooked in the 2027 Budget. University non-academic staff, excluding those at the University of Ruhuna, launched a one-day token strike in support of their demands. Pix by Pradeep Pathirana
