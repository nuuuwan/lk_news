No tsunami threat to Sri Lanka after 6.6 magnitude quake off Indonesia

Anadolu Ajansı - A magnitude 6.6 earthquake struck off the coast of Indonesia on Monday, the United States Geological Survey (USGS) reported.

The earthquake occurred around 148 kilometres (92 miles) west-southwest of Tual, at a depth of 68 kilometres (42 miles).

There were no immediate reports of casualties or damage, while information on possible tsunami warnings was not immediately available.

Meanwhile, Sri Lanka's National Tsunami Early Warning Centre said there was no tsunami threat to the country following the earthquake.

The Centre assured that coastal areas of Sri Lanka remained safe.
