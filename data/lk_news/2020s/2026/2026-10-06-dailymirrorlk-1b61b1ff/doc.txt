Suresh Salley’s hospital stay ends, returns to CID custody

Colombo, Oct. 6 (Daily Mirror) - A ten-member medical committee of doctors from the National Hospital Colombo informed Colombo Fort Magistrate Pasan Amarasena that former Director of the State Intelligence Service, retired Major General Suresh Salley, could be discharged from the hospital.

Accordingly, Additional Solicitor General Dileepa Peiris informed the court that Suresh Salley would be taken back into the custody of the Criminal Investigation Department.
