Sun Match Company’s Soorya brand shines as Silver Sponsor of JITF 2026

The Sun Match team handing over the Silver sponsorship for the Jaffna International Trade Fair 2026

Forty two‑year‑old heritage firm from the Central Province, Sun Match Company, has announced that is brand Soorya was made Silver Sponsor of the Jaffna International Trade Fair 2026. The three‑day event will be held in Jaffna from 23–25 January 2026.

Popularly known as the Jaffna International Trade Fair (JITF) the annual event is one of the Northern region’s most anticipated commercial gatherings, bringing together businesses, entrepreneurs and consumers to showcase products, services and innovations while strengthening regional economic ties across Sri Lanka. Soorya, a trusted household brand and a familiar presence in Jaffna homes for generations, has long been synonymous with quality and everyday convenience. Sun Match Company’s sponsorship underscores the brand’s commitment to reinforcing its regional engagement and supporting local communities. “Soorya is built on quality, reliability and everyday convenience,” said Sun Match Company Executive Director Gowri Rajan. “Our participation in the Jaffna International Trade Fair (JITF) reflects our vision to connect with communities across Sri Lanka, strengthen regional engagement and continue delivering products that meet the evolving needs of our customers.”

Visitors to the trade fair are invited visit the Soorya Stall Nos. 18 and 115 to explore the brand’s full product range and experience the company’s focus on customer‑focused innovation and practical everyday solutions. Through its support of the Jaffna International Trade Fair 2026, Sun Match Company reaffirms its mission to be a brand for all Sri Lankans — committed to lighting up homes, livelihoods and communities today and for generations to come.
