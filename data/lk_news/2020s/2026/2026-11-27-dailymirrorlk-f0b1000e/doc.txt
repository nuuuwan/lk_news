Samay Raina hits world record, becomes first YouTube creator to cross 1Mn channel members

Comedian and YouTuber Samay Raina hit headlines when he landed himself in a massive controversy over his show, India's Got Latent, in 2025.

He removed all episodes from his YouTube channel and later made his comeback this year with stand-up comedy, Still Alive.

On the other hand, India's Got Latent returned for season 2, partnering with Netflix. Amid this, Raina has achieved a new milestone, becoming the first YouTube creator to cross one million channel members worldwide.

Google announced the news in a blog post, crediting the comedian's growing community of fans for helping him reach the landmark.
