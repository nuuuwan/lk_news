Army seizes firearm and hunting equipment in ‘Hasthi Sena’ raid

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - A firearm, elephant tusk fragments, hunting equipment and other items allegedly used for illegal hunting have been seized during a special operation conducted under the Sri Lanka Army-led national ‘Hasthi Sena’ operation in the Kebithigollewa area.

The operation was carried out in the Yaan Oya-Wahalkada area, which is frequently visited by a wild elephant known as ‘Gajabahu’.

During the operation, security personnel searched forest areas and residential premises, recovering a muzzle-loading firearm, several cartridges, metal rods used for hunting wild animals, snares set up inside the forest, prepared snares and traps, three elephant tusk fragments, wild animal meat and several pointed knives allegedly used to kill wild animals.

The national ‘Hasthi Sena’ operation is being implemented by the Sri Lanka Army on the instructions of Defence Secretary Air Vice Marshal Sampath Thuyacontha (Retd.), with the aim of protecting wild elephants, reducing threats to wildlife and mitigating human-elephant conflict while safeguarding human lives and property.

The operation is being conducted jointly by the Sri Lanka Army and the Department of Wildlife Conservation under the guidance of the Ministry of Defence and the Ministry of Environment.
