BYD with JKCG Auto opens Jaffna 3S facility

BYD, the number one brand in the brand-new new energy vehicle (NEV) segment in Sri Lanka and the world’s leading NEV manufacturer, together with its authorised distributor in Sri Lanka John Keells CG Auto (JKCG Auto) has announced the launch of its Jaffna 3S facility

BYD is the only NEV brand with a 3S facility in the Northern province, offering sales, service and spare parts under one roof.

Located at the official BYD Jaffna showroom, No. 7, Ariyalai East Road, Ariyalai, Jaffna, the facility brings customers in the North the complete BYD ownership experience close to home.

It features a showroom displaying BYD’s NEV lineup, including ATTO 1, ATTO 2, DOLPHIN, SEALION 6, SEALION 5, SEALION 8, RACCO, SHARK 6, a fully equipped service centre and a dedicated genuine spare parts inventory.

The BYD Jaffna showroom is established in partnership with A9 Motors. Led by Director Kuwait Theivendran, A9 Motors brings experience and an established presence in Jaffna’s automotive industry, which will strengthen BYD’s customer reach and engagement across the Northern province.

Theivendran said, “Jaffna’s customers are increasingly looking at new energy vehicles and they want the assurance of a brand with sales, service and genuine parts in their own city. As a Jaffna-based dealer, we take that responsibility seriously. Our aim is to make owning a BYD simple and worry-free for every family and business in the North and to be a partner they can rely on for years to come.”

The opening is another significant milestone in JKCG Auto’s journey of expanding BYD’s islandwide presence, ensuring that the customers in the North can buy, service and maintain their vehicles with the same standards of quality and care as customers anywhere else in the country.

JKCG Auto Chief Executive Officer Charith Panditharathne said, “This is a remarkable milestone for BYD and for JKCG Auto. Jaffna and the wider North are communities with growing aspirations and they deserve a brand that is present and dependable on their doorstep. Our 3S facility gives the local customers a dedicated place to experience, own and care for their BYD and it marks the start of a lasting relationship with the people of Jaffna. As more families and businesses in the North move towards new energy mobility, we will be here to support them at every step.”
