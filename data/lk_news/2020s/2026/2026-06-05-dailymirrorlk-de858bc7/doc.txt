Massive raid nets 19 firearms, Ice and 100,000 pregabalin pills

Colombo, June 5 (Daily Mirror) - Police have seized 19 firearms, T-56 ammunition, crystal methamphetamine (Ice), and more than 100,000 Pregabalin pills following a two-week investigation that uncovered a suspected drug and weapons trafficking network operating between the Southern and Western provinces.

The haul was recovered from a house in Dematagoda on Wednesday by officers of the Western Province North Crimes Division.

According to Police, the operation was launched following a series of narcotics investigations carried out in May. On May 12, officers arrested a suspect in Ragama in possession of crystal methamphetamine and heroin.

A second suspect was taken into custody in Peliyagoda on May 18 with more than 1.5 kilograms of crystal methamphetamine.

Further investigations led detectives to a major drug cache in Sri Dharmarama Mawatha, Dematagoda, on May 22, where nearly 46 kilograms of crystal methamphetamine and more than seven kilograms of Kerala cannabis were recovered.

No suspects were present at the location at the time of the raid.

Police said intelligence gathered during the investigation subsequently led officers to the Dematagoda residence, where the latest cache of firearms, ammunition and narcotics was discovered.
