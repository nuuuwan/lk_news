New scene for Benjy …

Benjamin Ranabahu, better known as Benjy, has always moved into action with a band, but that era now seems over for this dynamic bassist, whose first contract performance was as a member of the group Pioneers, in 1972.

Benjy did have two outfits of his own – Aquarius and Inner Vision. While Aquarius was a total success, with female vocalists from the Philippines, and the Maldives, the band Inner Vision, says Benjy, didn’t come up to his expectation.

Inner Vision made its debut at a nightspot in Mount Lavinia, but couldn’t break into the scene the way Sri Lanka’s most respected bassist expected it to happen.

Aquarius was extremely popular in the international scene, performing in Europe and the Middle East.

However, the electrifying bassist, who’s been a fixture on Sri Lanka’s live music scene for years, is, probably, best known as the bassist for Mirage, one of the country’s top bands.

He made his name with Mirage, playing high-energy gigs in the music scene and has a reputation for strong stage presence, groove, and audience connection.

Benjy has a history of working with top local bands, including Amazing Grace, Desmond & The Clan, Soundwaves, Lotus, with Priyanthi and Raja Jalaldeen, and Orpheus.

In fact, he has been the pulse behind some of Sri Lanka’s most electrifying live performances for years.

Known as the powerhouse bassist of Mirage, he built a reputation for groove, energy, and an uncanny ability to connect with any audience, whether in Colombo or overseas.

His fingers have driven countless nights of rock, pop, and fusion, earning him recognition as one of the country’s top bassists.

After anchoring bands and shaping Sri Lanka’s live music scene, his next phase promises something fresh: the same fire, but with Benjy firmly in the spotlight.

However, for a short while he plans to collaborate with some of our reputed artistes for guest sessions, and now moves into action with Manilal and Derek.
