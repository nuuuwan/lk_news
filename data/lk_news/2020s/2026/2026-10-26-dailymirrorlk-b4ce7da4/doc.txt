HNB FINANCE-Toyota Lanka expand vehicle-leasing solutions

From left: Toyota Lanka Managing Director and CEO Manohara Atukorala and Chairman Masaaki Kawabata followed by HNB FINANCE Managing Director and CEO Chaminda Prabhath, Chief Credit Officer Aruna Somasiri, Head of Channel Management Pathum Sampath, Head of Leasing Saman Premarathna, Head of Marketing Communications and Digital Business Madura Wijeratne and Senior Marketing Manager Ruwan Indika

HNB FINANCE PLC and Toyota Lanka (Private) Limited have entered into a strategic partnership to offer competitive leasing solutions for the customers purchasing brand-new Toyota vehicles and Toyota Sure Certified Used Vehicles.

The partnership was recently formalised through a memorandum of understanding (MoU), reinforcing the commitment of both parties to collaborate on this initiative.

Under the agreement, HNB FINANCE will provide leasing facilities for eligible Toyota customers, combining access to formal financing with a package of after-sales and ownership benefits offered by Toyota Lanka.

HNB FINANCE Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer Chaminda Prabhath said, “Reliable access to vehicle finance is important not only for individual mobility but also for business activity and broader economic participation. Through this partnership with Toyota Lanka, we are looking to offer the customers competitive financing options together with the assurance of a globally recognised automotive brand and strong after-sales support.”

The customers purchasing eligible Toyota vehicles under the partnership will receive free vehicle registration from Toyota Lanka, along with a comprehensive warranty covering the engine and gearbox for three years or 100,000 kilometres, whichever comes first.

The Toyota forklift customers will receive a warranty of two years or 6,000 operating hours, whichever occurs first.

The partnership also offers three complimentary services for Toyota vehicles and three complimentary labour services for Toyota forklifts.

The MoU was exchanged in the presence of senior representatives of both companies.

Toyota Lanka was represented by Chairman Masaaki Kawabata and Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer Manohara Atukorala.

HNB FINANCE was represented by Managing Director and CEO Chaminda Prabhath, Deputy General Manager and Chief Credit Officer Aruna Somasiri, Assistant General Manager and Head of Channel Management Pathum Sampath, Head of Leasing Saman Premarathna, Chief Manager and Head of Marketing Communications and Digital Business Madura Wijeratne and Senior Manager Marketing Ruwan Indika.
