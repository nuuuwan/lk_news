Yield curve dips marginally on the back of modest buying

Continued optimism in the geopolitical front and declining global oil prices ignited buying interest in the secondary market. As a result, the yield curve underwent a modest downward adjustment across the board while trading volumes appeared moderate yesterday.

Within the 2030 segment, 01.03.2030, 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 traded from11.05% to 10.95%. Moving ahead, 01.12.2031 traded between11.20% to 11.10%, while15.12.2032 changed hands from 11.55% to 11.50%.

At the belly of the yield curve, 15.01.2033, 01.06.2033 and01.11.2033 traded from 11.80% to 11.65%. Further along the curve, 15.06.2034, 15.09.2034 and15.10.2034 changed hands from 12.00% to 11.95%. Finally, 01.03.2035 was seen trading at 12.10% while 15.08.2036 traded from 12.25% to 12.20%.

On the external front, the LKR remained flat against the greenback, standing at LKR 332.56/USD. Market liquidity in the banking system expanded to LKR 455.19bn compared to LKR 429.10bn seen previously.
