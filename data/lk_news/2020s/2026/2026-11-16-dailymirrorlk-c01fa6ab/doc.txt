Sajith questions delay in appointing Select Committee on tusker Kawantissa’s death

Colombo, Nov 16 (Daily Mirror) - Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa today questioned Speaker Jagath Wickramaratne over the failure to appoint a Parliamentary Select Committee to investigate the death of tusker Kawantissa, saying no decision had been taken on a request made by the Opposition.

“A Select Committee was appointed to look into the July 1983 communal riots, but our request has not received the same consideration,” Mr Premadasa told Parliament.

Chief Government Whip Nalinda Jayatissa, responding to the Opposition Leader, said the matter would be considered by party leaders after the Budget.
