Teacher training and professional development key to education reforms: PM

Colombo, Nov. 27 (Daily Mirror) - Prime Minister and Education Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya has stressed that strengthening teacher education and ensuring continuous professional development are key components of the ongoing education reform process.

She made these remarks while attending the Third International Research Symposium on Education 2026, organised by the Research and Development Department of the National Institute of Education (NIE) in Maharagama on November 26.

The two-day symposium, held on November 25 and 26, provided a platform for researchers and education professionals to exchange knowledge and discuss emerging challenges, trends and policy directions in the education sector.

It featured keynote speeches, technical sessions and panel discussions aimed at promoting research, academic dialogue and collaboration.

During the event, the Prime Minister also officially launched the new website of the NIE's Research and Development Department.

Following the symposium, she observed a teacher trainer training programme conducted as part of the new education reforms. The programme focused on preparing trainers for the teaching of Grade 6 Health and Physical Education during the second and third terms of 2027.

Addressing the gathering, Dr. Amarasuriya said education should not be viewed merely as a process of preparing individuals for the job market, but also as a national responsibility to produce responsible citizens.

She said the proposed reforms would cover the entire education system rather than being limited to changes in textbooks and syllabuses.

The 13-year school education curriculum will be reformed systematically and introduced in stages, she added.

NIE Director General Senior Professor O.G. Dayaratne Banda, University of Engineering and Management, Jaipur, India, Vice Chancellor Professor Dr. Biswajoy Chatterjee, NIE Deputy Directors and teacher trainers were among those present.
