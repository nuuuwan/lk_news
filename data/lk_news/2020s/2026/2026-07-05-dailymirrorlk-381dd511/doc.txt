ASUS strengthens retail strategy launching 3rd exclusive store in Galle

Janaka Karunathilaka - Outstation Sales Manager - ASUS Sri Lanka; Sameera Bopage - Chief Executive Office - Sell-X Computers; Ishkey Irshad - Country Channel Manager - ASUS Sri Lanka; and Lochana Bandara - Channel Marketing Manager - ASUS Sri Lanka

As a step towards strengthening the brand’s retail footprint across the country, ASUS Sri Lanka, a Taiwanese tech giant announced the launch of its third exclusive store in the country, now opened in Galle.

The new store is set up to host an extensive range of laptop and computer hardware, including the ASUS flagship consumer products such as Republic of Gamers (ROG), TUF Gaming, ProArt, Zenbook and Vivobook laptops. Additionally, customers will also benefit from the ASUS 4A Guarantee which promises a superior ownership experience through; Assured Quality of reliable ASUS products, Always-on Support backed by dedicated customer service, All-around Coverage with international warranty and Added-value Experience. This is the brand’s 1stexclusive store located in the South Province of Sri Lanka.

Talking about the expansion, Ishkey Irshard, Country Channel Manager – Consumer PC and Gaming Business, ASUS Sri Lanka said, “We are delighted to announce the expansion of our retail footprint in Sri Lanka. Galle, being a vital market for us, the inauguration of this new brand store in the Southern Province marks a pivotal step towards empowering consumers across the region with a unique experience of our latest laptops and innovation. With a strategic retail expansion approach, we will continue to create greater interaction and new touch points for our users”
