CSE opens week mixed as large-cap strength offsets broad selling pressure

The Colombo Stock Exchange started the week on a mixed note on Monday, 17 August 2026, with the All Share Price Index edging lower while the S&P Sri Lanka 20 Index extended its positive momentum, highlighting continued strength among selected large-cap counters.

The ASPI declined marginally by 6.29 points, or 0.03%, to close at 21,616.88, while the S&P SL20 gained 18.82 points, or 0.31%, to finish at 6,084.54.

The ASPI traded firmly around the 21,670 level during early trading before selling pressure intensified towards midday, pulling the index close to 21,600. Buying interest emerged during the afternoon and supported a recovery towards 21,650, although renewed weakness towards the close pushed the index back into negative territory.

Total market turnover stood at LKR 1.89 billion, with approximately 45.51 million shares changing hands during the session.

The Food, Beverage and Tobacco sector dominated market activity, generating LKR 677.19 million in turnover from approximately 6.96 million shares. Melstacorp accounted for the majority of sector activity, recording LKR 599.10 million in turnover.

Crossing transactions contributed approximately LKR 665.59 million, representing around 35% of total equity turnover. Melstacorp recorded the largest combined crossing activity, with four crossings valued at approximately LKR 565.84 million. This included a 2.00 million-share block executed at LKR 190.00, valued at LKR 380.00 million.

Foreign investors continued to remain net sellers, recording a net foreign outflow of LKR 473.53 million during the session.

Broader market participation remained weak, with only 75 securities advancing against 132 decliners, resulting in an advance-to-decline ratio of 0.57. The negative breadth indicated relatively widespread selling pressure despite gains among selected heavyweight counters.

Sampath Bank was the strongest positive contributor to the ASPI, adding 13.75 points to the index. Dialog Axiata, Bukit Darah, Commercial Bank and Melstacorp also provided support.

Lion Brewery recorded the largest negative contribution, reducing the ASPI by 8.81 points. Richard Pieris, RIL Property, Colombo Dockyard and Citizens Development Business Finance also weighed on the index.

Among notable trades, Melstacorp recorded the highest individual counter turnover at LKR 599.10 million, followed by Dialog Axiata with LKR 93.22 million and Sampath Bank with LKR 81.54 million.

Overall, the session reflected a divergence between broader market sentiment and large-cap performance. While the S&P SL20 continued to advance, weak market breadth, persistent foreign selling and the marginal decline in the ASPI indicated a more cautious start to the week.
