CID launches probe into SLPP General Secretary’s remarks on IGP

Colombo, August 4 (Daily Mirror) - The Acting Inspector General of Police (IGP) has instructed the Central Criminal Investigation Bureau (CCIB) to launch an investigation into a statement made by Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) General Secretary Attorney-at-Law Sagara Kariyawasam concerning the IGP.

Accordingly, the IGP has directed the bureau to investigate the remarks made by Kariyawasam during a media briefing held yesterday.

Addressing the media, Kariyawasam levelled strong allegations against both the government and the IGP over the state of the police service. He claimed that the current government had placed the police in a “highly unfortunate position” and alleged that three former IGPs had faced extremely distressing circumstances under the present administration.

He further urged the incumbent IGP to stop serving the government and instead serve the people.

During the briefing, Kariyawasam also made a controversial statement, claiming that if the IGP failed to do so, he could become “the 37th Inspector General of Police to go down in history after being assaulted to death by members of the public on a main road.”

Following the remarks, the IGP instructed the Central Criminal Investigation Bureau to initiate an investigation into the statement.
