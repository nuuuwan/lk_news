Two inmates killed, two injured in clash at Kuruwita Prison

Colombo, Aug. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Two inmates were killed and two others injured following a clash among a group of inmates at Kuruwita Prison on Friday morning, according to the Kuruwita Police.

The unrest broke out inside the prison earlier today, resulting in four inmates sustaining injuries. Police said two of the injured later succumbed to their injuries.

Prison officials fired tear gas to bring the situation under control.
