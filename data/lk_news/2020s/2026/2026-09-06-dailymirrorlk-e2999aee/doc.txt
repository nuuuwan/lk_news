41 arrested following raid on Facebook party in Teldeniya

Kandy, Sep 06 (Daily Mirror) - Police have arrested 36 male and five female suspects following a raid on a Facebook party held in the Victoria area of Teldeniya. The operation was carried out by a team of officers from the Teldeniya Police Station based on a tip-off.

During the search, police seized a variety of illicit substances and alcohol in the suspects' possession, including 947 mg of cannabis, 3.657 g of hashish, 1.276 g of "Ice" (crystal methamphetamine), 18 ecstasy pills, and 21 illegal cigarettes. Officers also confiscated 35 cans of beer and 13 bottles of liquor.

The arrested men, aged between 21 and 40, are residents of Geli Oya, Aluthwatta, Warakapola, Peradeniya, Ampitiya, Kandy, and Hanguranketha. The female suspects, aged between 22 and 43, reside in Pallekele, Peradeniya, Hanguranketha, Dehiwala, and Katugastota. Teldeniya Police are conducting further investigations.
