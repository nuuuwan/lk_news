Mahara Prison unrest brought under control; Curfew remains in force

Ragama, August 2 (Daily Mirror) - The unrest at Mahara Prison has been brought under control, with all inmates now confined to their cells, Police said.

However, the police curfew imposed in several Grama Niladhari divisions in the Mahara area remains in effect until further notice.

The unrest erupted on Friday evening (01) following a dispute between inmates and prison officers, resulting in a tense situation inside the prison.

Seven inmates were injured during the incident and admitted to the Ragama Teaching Hospital for treatment. One of the injured inmates later died.

To restore order, authorities deployed personnel from the Sri Lanka Police, the Special Task Force (STF), and the Sri Lanka Army. The Sri Lanka Air Force also deployed a Bell 412 helicopter and drones to monitor the situation from the air.

According to reports, inmates set fire to the office of the Chief Jailer and the prison's disciplinary division during the unrest.

Meanwhile, relatives who had arrived at the prison to visit inmates were denied entry and later gathered outside the prison premises, where tensions flared between the crowd and police.

After several loud noises resembling gunfire were heard from inside the prison, some relatives and nearby residents reportedly attempted to breach police barricades. Security personnel intervened to bring the situation under control, while some individuals were also reported to have hurled stones at security forces.

Police have imposed a curfew in the Grama Niladhari divisions of 246 Kendaliyadda Road West, 246C Kendaliyadda Road North, and 181G Dambuwa, with the restrictions remaining in force until further notice.
