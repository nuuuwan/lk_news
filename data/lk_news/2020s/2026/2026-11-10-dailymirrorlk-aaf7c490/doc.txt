Over 900 candidates shortlisted for Court Clerk Service interviews

Colombo, Nov. 10 (Daily Mirror) - The Judicial Service Commission (JSC) has released the results of the Open Competitive Examination for Recruitment to Grade III of the Court Clerk Service (Court Management Assistants’ Service) for 2026.

More than 900 candidates who sat the competitive examination held on October 17, 2026, have been summoned for interviews, JSC Secretary Prasanna Alwis said.

The interviews are scheduled to be held on November 22, 2026, commencing at 9.00 a.m. at the Colombo High Court premises.

The examination results and details of the candidates selected for the interviews have been published on the official website of the Judicial Service Commission, www.jsc.gov.lk.
