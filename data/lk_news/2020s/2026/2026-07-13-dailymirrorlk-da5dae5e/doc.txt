AKD once called MR ’a culture of this country’: C.B. Ratnayake

Colombo, July 13 (Daily Mirror) - Former Nuwara Eliya District MP C.B. Ratnayake alleged that President Anura Kumara Dissanayake (AKD) had once described former President Mahinda Rajapaksa as "a culture of this country", but later came to power by campaigning against him.

Addressing a Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) divisional representatives' meeting for the Maskeliya electorate at the Ginigathhena Cooperative Hall on Saturday, Ratnayake claimed that the Janatha Vimukthi Peramuna (JVP) had accused Rajapaksa of being a thief during its election campaign.

He alleged that the current National People's Power (NPP) government was attempting to extend the terms of service of several judges, including the Chief Justice, claiming the move was aimed at retaining judges loyal to the government. Ratnayake questioned whether such actions were consistent with democratic principles.

Ratnayake also accused the government of irregularities in the procurement of coal for the Norochcholai power plant and alleged that hackers had stolen funds from the Central Bank. He further claimed that commissions had been appointed to shield those responsible, adding that members of the previous administration would have faced imprisonment had they committed similar acts.

Walapane Electoral Organizer Abheetha Ratnayake, Ambagamuwa Pradeshiya Sabha member Indika Dorakumbura and several SLPP representatives attended the meeting.
