WindForce pulls plug on Rs. 4bn green bond issue

WindForce PLC yesterday announced it has shelved its planned Rs. 4 billion green bond issue, due to adverse market conditions, nearly six months after unveiling the proposed capital raising.

In a filing to the Colombo Stock Exchange, the renewable energy company said it would not proceed with the proposed listed, rated, senior, unsecured and redeemable green bond issue and has withdrawn its initial listing application.

The move marks a reversal from April, when the WindForce Board resolved to issue up to 40 million green bonds with a total value of up to Rs. 4 billion, subject to necessary regulatory and other approvals.

When the fundraising was first announced on 2 April, WindForce had left the tenure, interest rates and other terms of the proposed bonds open, saying these would be notified in due course.

WindForce did not specify the particular market factors that prompted the withdrawal or indicate whether it intends to revisit the green bond issue when conditions improve.

The company also did not disclose an alternative funding plan in the latest announcement.
