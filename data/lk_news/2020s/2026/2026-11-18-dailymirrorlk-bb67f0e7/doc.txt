Nuwara Eliya records lowest temperature; Mullaitivu highest

Colombo, Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Nuwara Eliya recorded the lowest minimum temperature in the country at 12.4 degrees Celsius, while Mullaitivu recorded the highest minimum temperature at 26.6°C yesterday (18), according to the Department of Meteorology.

Bandarawela recorded the second-lowest temperature at 16.8°C, followed by Badulla at 19.8°C and Maha Illuppallama at 20.7°C.

Among other areas, Katugastota recorded 22.1°C, Polonnaruwa 22.3°C, Puttalam and Vavuniya 22.4°C each, Anuradhapura and Monaragala 22.6°C each, and Jaffna 22.8°C.

Trincomalee recorded 23°C, Kurunegala 23.4°C, Batticaloa 24°C, Katunayake 24.2°C, Ratnapura 24.5°C, while Colombo and Ratmalana recorded 25.4°C each.

Meanwhile, Hambantota recorded 25.5°C, Galle 25.8°C, Mannar 25.9°C and Pottuvil 25.1°C.

The figures were released in the Department of Meteorology's minimum temperature report for November 18, 2026.
