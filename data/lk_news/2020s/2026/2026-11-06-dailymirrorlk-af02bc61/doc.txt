Raid Amazones returns to Sri Lanka after two-year break

Sri Lanka is hosting the world-renowned women-only adventure event, Raid Amazones, after a two-year break, with around 300 French participants taking part in a series of sporting activities in the country's southern region.

The event, which began on November 2, will continue until November 10, with Kalutara selected as the main destination for the 2026 edition. It aims to promote Sri Lanka's cultural heritage, beaches, natural landscapes and community life among international visitors.

The programme includes trail running, canoeing, cycling, running and treasure hunts, testing participants' endurance and physical strength.

A canoeing competition was held on November 5, starting and ending at Malli's Seafood Restaurant. Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Vijitha Herath attended the event and flagged off the competition, encouraging the participants.

The 2026 edition brings together around 300 French female contestants and 55 international staff members. Connaissance de Ceylan (Pvt) Ltd is the official ground-handling partner.

The delegation arrived at Bandaranaike International Airport on November 2 and was welcomed by officials of the Sri Lanka Tourism Information Centre with traditional dance performances. The organisers also highlighted that the flight carrying the delegation was operated entirely by female staff, reflecting the event's focus on women's empowerment.

Raid Amazones is a women-only multi-sport adventure challenge founded in 2001 by French entrepreneur Alexandre Debanne of ZBO France. It brings together teams of women who compete in activities such as orienteering, mountain biking, canoeing, horse riding and running, while experiencing the culture and natural attractions of the host country.

Sri Lanka has previously hosted the event in 2004, 2009, 2018, 2019, 2022 and 2023, with each edition attracting more than 250 international participants.

Sri Lanka Tourism said the event provides an opportunity to strengthen the country's international tourism profile, particularly in European markets. Participants are mainly expected from France, Belgium, Switzerland, Luxembourg and other European countries.

The event is also expected to benefit the local tourism industry by attracting high-spending travellers who may extend their stays to explore the country's cultural and natural attractions.

The 2023 edition, organised with the support of Sri Lanka Tourism and SriLankan Airlines, generated an estimated USD 50 million in publicity value, according to the organisers. It also attracted more than 300 international press articles, reached an audience of around one million people and recorded over 65,000 engagements and 650,000 video views.

Sri Lanka Tourism expects the 2026 edition to generate further international media exposure and strengthen the country's position as a tourism destination.

The event is also expected to support the development of sports tourism, an emerging segment of the industry that could bring additional international visitors and tourism revenue to Sri Lanka.
