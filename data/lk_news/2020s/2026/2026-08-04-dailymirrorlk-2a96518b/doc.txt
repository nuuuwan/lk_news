Youth arrested for defrauding woman of Rs. 6.6M

Colombo, August 4 (Daily Mirror) - A 21-year-old suspect has been arrested by the North Western Province Unit of the Computer Crime Investigation Division for allegedly defrauding a woman of more than Rs. 6.6 million by falsely promising to secure foreign employment.

Police said the victim, a 28-year-old resident of the Kurukupane area in Bangadeniya, was persuaded to transfer Rs. 6,365,000 to the suspect through online banking transactions. The total financial loss suffered by the victim amounted to Rs. 6,644,000.

The investigation was launched following a complaint lodged on June 27, 2025, regarding the alleged online fraud. After conducting extensive inquiries, investigators identified the suspect as a resident of the Ampilanthurai area.

The suspect was produced before the Chilaw Magistrate's Court today.
