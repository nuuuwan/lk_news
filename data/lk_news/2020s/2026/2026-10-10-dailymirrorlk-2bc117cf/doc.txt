BOI sets timetable for 2 northern investment zones

Paranthan land reservations to open by end-November; infrastructure work at both zones to begin in January 2027

BOI proposes single-point coordination mechanism to resolve approval delays and land allocation bottlenecks

Northern investment push includes workforce mapping and expansion of seaweed and sea cucumber exports

The Board of Investment (BOI) has set dates to open land reservations in Paranthan and begin infrastructure work at two proposed northern export processing zones, as it seeks to draw investors to the region and address the administrative delays holding up projects.

The land reservations at the proposed Paranthan Export Processing Zone will open to the investors by end-November 2026, while the internal infrastructure development at both Paranthan and Kankasanthurai (KKS) is scheduled to begin in early January 2027, the BOI said this week.

The commitments followed a visit to the Northern Province by BOI Chairman Duminda Hulangamuwa and Director General Dr. Sulakshana Jayawardena, who held discussions with the regional administrators, infrastructure agencies and existing and prospective investors.

The BOI will also organise a familiarisation and investment promotion visit in November for leading investors and investor associations, showcasing the opportunities in the province, including the two proposed zones.

Alongside the infrastructure push, the visit brought the investor concerns over the land allocation and approval procedures into focus.

The investors highlighted the need for clearer and more consistent procedures for land valuation and allocation, faster approvals and clearances from the line agencies and better coordination across the government institutions, according to the BOI’s release.

In response, Hulangamuwa proposed a single-point coordination mechanism to follow the investor issues through to resolution. The mechanism would work with the relevant government institutions to expedite the outstanding approvals and address the implementation bottlenecks, drawing on political and administrative support where required.

Hulangamuwa also sought Northern Province Governor Nagalingam Vethanayahan’s support to establish a regional Single-Point Investor Facilitation Centre, giving the investors one point of contact for approvals and services from the regional and national government agencies.

The BOI delegation briefed Vethanayahan on the proposed zones, their implementation schedules and target completion timelines. However, the release did not disclose the completion dates, infrastructure costs or confirmed investment commitments.

At Paranthan, the discussions involved the Kilinochchi District Secretary, Paranthan Divisional Secretary, a coordinating secretary to Minister Bimal Rathnayaka and the officials responsible for roads, railways and electricity distribution.

A separate meeting at the Tellippalai Divisional Secretariat brought together the Jaffna district and divisional officials, Cement Corporation chairman, infrastructure agencies and KKS police officer-in-charge. The participants agreed that the internal infrastructure development at KKS would also begin in early January 2027.

The investment drive also includes a proposal to map the Northern province’s workforce, allowing the prospective investors to assess the availability of workers and their qualifications and skills.

Hulangamuwa proposed a Regional Manpower Information Database or Data Centre to capture this information, recognising the workforce readiness as a factor in investment decisions alongside land and infrastructure.

Beyond the industrial zones, the delegation visited the sea cucumber and seaweed cultivation projects in Ariyalai in Jaffna, where the discussions focused on expanding production and overcoming the challenges facing the industries.

The BOI identified the potential to scale up and commercialise these activities as export-oriented enterprises capable of generating employment and income in the province. The release did not provide production figures, export earnings or investment targets for either sector.
