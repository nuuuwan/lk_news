Bourse trading characterized by highs and lows but turnover hits Rs 9 billion

CSE trading kicked off on a positive note yesterday during the first trading session but later profit- takings were noted due to dropping of investor sentiment stemming from external and internal environmental factors.

Amid those developments both indices moved downward. The All Share Price Index went down by 85.58 points, while the S and P SL20 declined by 8.83 points.

Turnover stood at Rs 9.91 billion with ten crossings. Those crossings were reported in HNB, which crossed 4.9 million shares crossed to the tune of Rs 2.1 billion; its shares traded at Rs 415, Colombo Dockyard 6.5 million shares crossed for Rs 995 million; its shares traded at Rs 154, Hemas Holdings five million shares crossed for Rs 173 million; its shares traded at Rs 43.60, Cargills Ceylon 1 million shares crossed for Rs 880; its shares traded at Rs 88, Sampath Bank 200,000 shares crossed for Rs 31 million; its shares traded at Rs 155.25 and VallibelOne 200,000 shares crossed for Rs 22 million; its shares traded at Rs 110.

In the retail market top seven companies that mainly contributed to the turnover were; Colombo Dockyard Rs 1.68 billion (10.9 million shares traded), Hemas Holdings Rs 298 million (8.6 million shares traded ), ACL Cables Rs 274 million (2.7 million shares traded), Softlogic Capital Rs 258 million (35.8 million shares traded), Prime Lands Residencies Rs 170.3 million (3.67 million shares traded), Sampath Bank Rs 150 million (955,500 shares traded) and Central Finance Rs 148 million (528,000 shares traded). During the day 211.6 million share volumes changed hands in 41044 transactions.

It is said that mixed reactions were noted throughout the day. The Capital goods sector performed well. Banking and financial sector performed well too, especially HNB. The real estate sector also impressed, especially Prime Lands Residencies.

Yesterday the rupee was quoted at Rs 309.10/20 to the US dollar in the spot market, improving from Rs 309.58/63 the previous day, having depreciated in recent weeks, dealers said, while bond yields were broadly steady.

A bond maturing on 15.12.2029 was quoted at 9.59/64 percent, down from 9.65/70 percent.

A bond maturing on 01.03.2030 was quoted at 9.68/72 percent.

A bond maturing on 01.07.2030 was quoted at 9.70/80 percent, down from 9.75/80 percent.

A bond maturing on 15.12.2032 was quoted at 10.30/35 percent, down from 10.28/38 percent.

A bond maturing on 15.06.2034 was quoted at 10.80/90 percent.
