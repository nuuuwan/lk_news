No fraud allegations made against me, says Rohitha Rajapaksa after FCID statement

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Rohitha Rajapaksa, the youngest son of former President Mahinda Rajapaksa, said no allegations of fraud had been made against him when he appeared before the Financial Crimes Investigation Division (FCID) today (26) to provide a statement over the ongoing investigation into the SupremeSAT satellite project.

Speaking to journalists outside the FCID premises after giving his statement, Rajapaksa said investigators were carrying out their duties by gathering information to conclude an open investigation file.

He said he was willing to cooperate fully with the authorities whenever required to assist in bringing the matter to a conclusion.

Responding to questions about his political future, Rajapaksa said he had no intention of entering active politics.

Asked about his elder brother, Namal Rajapaksa, who is currently in custody, he rejected suggestions that he was disillusioned and expressed confidence that his brother would emerge from the situation more mature and stronger.

He said whether the public had expectations regarding his brother's political future was a matter best answered by the people.

Responding to questions about his low-key appearance at the FCID without security escorts or a crowd of supporters, Rajapaksa said he had never personally used or owned a Defender vehicle.

He added that his current routine was more convenient and normal.
