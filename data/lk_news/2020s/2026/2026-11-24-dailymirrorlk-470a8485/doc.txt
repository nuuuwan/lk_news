HighVoltagePR leads influencer rollout for Daraz 11.11 Big Boss Sale

From left; Sanjula Peiris, Safwan Yaseen, Daraz Sri Lanka Managing Director Rukshan Xavier, HighVoltagePR Managing Director Supun Dias and Mafaz Mohideen

Boutique public relations and influencer marketing agency HighVoltagePR is spearheading the ongoing influencer and affiliate marketing campaign for the flagship Daraz Sri Lanka 11.11 Big Boss Sale, held in a landmark year as Daraz marks its 10th anniversary.

Managing a diverse network of over 130 content creators and affiliates, the agency launched with a high-impact teaser phase before transitioning into the main 11.11 rollout – driving sustained digital engagement, consumer discovery, and conversion momentum throughout the sale period, which runs until 21 November 2026. To maximise reach across Sri Lanka’s digital landscape, HighVoltagePR structured a multi-tiered creator strategy combining high-reach lifestyle figures, niche category reviewers, and performance-driven affiliates. By aligning creator storytelling with real-time flash deals and platform promotions, the campaign maintains continuous visibility across major social platforms while guiding shoppers directly to the Daraz app.

HighVoltagePR Managing Director Supun Dias said, “Executing an influencer campaign at this scale requires more than just volume – it demands precision, alignment, and real-time agility. Managing over 130 creators and affiliates simultaneously for Sri Lanka’s largest e-commerce event demonstrates how far our digital capabilities have evolved over the past decade. By pairing authentic creator voices with data-backed campaign management, we are delivering measurable visibility and strong consumer engagement at every stage of the 11.11 sale.”

Dias also extended his appreciation to the Daraz leadership team, adding, “We are grateful to Daraz Sri Lanka for trusting HighVoltagePR with a campaign of this magnitude, especially during a milestone year marking Daraz’s 10th anniversary. Working alongside Rukshan Xavier, whose 11 years of experience in the e-commerce space continue to shape the industry, has brought invaluable strategic direction to this collaboration.”

Daraz Sri Lanka Managing Director Rukshan Xavier added, “11.11 is the defining moment of the year for online retail in Sri Lanka, and connecting with shoppers through trusted, relatable voices is central to its success. As we celebrate a decade of Daraz, HighVoltagePR has brought structure, scale, and speed to our creator ecosystem this season, helping us translate widespread social buzz into meaningful customer activity across the platform.”
