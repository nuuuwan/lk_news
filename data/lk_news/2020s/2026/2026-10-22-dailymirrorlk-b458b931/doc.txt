HNB-IDEAL Motors offer vehicle leasing solutions

HNB Senior Vice President and Head of Retail Banking Kanchana Karunagama and IDEAL Motors Director Legal and Corporate Affairs Nimisha Welgama share MoU From left: HNB Bank Associate Chamal Siriwardena, Assistant Manager Leasing Chamindra Mallikaarachchi, Manager Leasing Mahesh Ratnayake, Vice President Leasing Udara Pathinayake, IDEAL Motors General Manager Commercial Vehicles Kasun Fernando, Head of Marketing Harsha Perera and Business Development Manager Dinesh Manage

HNB PLC has partnered with IDEAL Motors (Pvt.) Limited to provide the customers with attractive leasing solutions for brand-new vehicles and generators.

The partnership introduces a range of benefits designed to support individuals, entrepreneurs and businesses seeking reliable vehicle and power solutions. Through this collaboration, the customers can access structured lease facilities, special interest rates, free life insurance coverage and attractive discounts through HNB General Insurance.

HNB Senior Vice President and Head of Retail Banking Kanchana Karunagama said, “At HNB, we believe that the right financial support can help people and businesses move forward with greater confidence. This partnership with IDEAL Motors shows our commitment to creating meaningful solutions that make vehicle and power investments more accessible, while helping our customers achieve their personal and business aspirations. By combining HNB’s financial expertise with IDEAL Motors’ industry capabilities, we are creating opportunities that support the ambitions of Sri Lankans and contribute towards a stronger, more connected economy.”

The customers purchasing vehicles through IDEAL Motors will also benefit from exclusive offers including free registration and discounts on Bolero Maxx Pik-Up, Alfa Plus Load Carrier, Mahindra Pik-Up Single Cab, Mahindra Pik Up Double Cab, XUV 3XO vehicles, creating greater value throughout their ownership journey.

IDEAL Motors Director Legal and Corporate Affairs Nimesha Welgama said, “Our collaboration with HNB represents a shared commitment to supporting Sri Lankans in achieving greater financial independence through accessible ownership solutions. By combining IDEAL Motors’ mobility and power solutions with HNB’s financial expertise, we aim to empower individuals and businesses with opportunities that contribute to economic progress and community development. This partnership highlights our vision of creating lasting value by enabling the customers to confidently invest in their future.”
