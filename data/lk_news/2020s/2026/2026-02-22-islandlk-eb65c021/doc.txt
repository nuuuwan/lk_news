Fcode Labs invests in German coffee startup Bunamo

Fcode Labs, the global product innovation and engineering company, has made a strategic investment in Bunamo, a Germany-based startup building a digital platform for the coffee community.

Alongside the investment, Fcode Labs partnered with Bunamo to design and engineer a scalable, production-ready platform. The goal is to create a connected experience that brings together home baristas, professional roasters, and coffee brands through content-driven interactions like recipe sharing.

Founded by Dr. Torben Gräber and Phill Kopp, Bunamo serves as a dedicated space for coffee lovers to explore brewing methods and discover new roasters.

“Our decision to invest was driven by the passion of the team and we see a fast-growing coffee community not only across Europe but also in the rest of the world,” said Buddhishan Manamperi, Co-CEO of Fcode Labs.

Fcode Labs built the platform with a user-centered design and a scalable, cloud-ready architecture to support future expansion. The collaboration highlights Fcode’s model of combining capital with deep technical expertise to help founders turn ideas into scalable digital platforms.
