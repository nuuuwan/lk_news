Death compensation for migrant workers increased to Rs.2 million

Colombo, Sep. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Minister Vijitha Herath said compensation for Sri Lankan migrant workers who die while employed overseas has been increased from Rs.600,000 to Rs.2 million.

He said the increase was one of the benefits promised to Sri Lankans working abroad after the present Government came to power.

The Minister made these remarks today (10) while participating in an event at the Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment (SLBFE) to provide compensation to the families of migrant workers who died while working overseas.

He said every person who leaves the country for employment does so with dreams of improving their lives and those of their families, while the loss of a worker abroad could place a severe financial burden on their dependants.

“Previously, workers who lost their lives while trying to fulfil those dreams were provided Rs.600,000 through the free insurance scheme provided by the Bureau. We announced when we came to power that this amount was insufficient and that it should be increased to Rs.2 million. Today, we have been able to do so,” he said.

The Minister said around 300,000 Sri Lankans leave the country for employment each year, while approximately 250 migrant workers die annually due to various unfortunate circumstances.

He said the Government had proposed awareness and other programmes to educate migrant workers about the laws of the countries where they work and to strengthen their mental preparedness.
