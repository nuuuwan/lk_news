Women’s leadership a condition for global survival, PM says

Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya has said women’s leadership is no longer merely an equality target but a condition for global survival.

She made these remarks while addressing the opening plenary session of the Commonwealth Women’s Forum (CWF) on November 2, 2026, under the theme “Advancing Gender Equality: Investment in a Prosperous and Resilient Commonwealth”.

Highlighting growing challenges such as geopolitical instability, gender-based violence, technology-facilitated abuse and climate change, the Prime Minister stressed the need for responses that consider the different experiences of women, particularly in the Global South.

She called for the removal of structural barriers that prevent women from accessing positions of power, financial resources and security.

Referring to the impact of climate change on women, particularly in vulnerable island nations, Dr. Amarasuriya stressed the importance of climate justice. She called for direct climate investments without increasing debt burdens to support community-led initiatives.

She also said achieving equality requires more than minor policy changes, stressing the need for a united effort involving governments, civil society, the private sector, men and boys.

The Prime Minister announced that Sri Lanka will host the 14th Commonwealth Women’s Affairs Ministers Meeting (WAMM), reaffirming the country's commitment to promoting gender equality and strengthening cooperation among Commonwealth countries.

Concluding her address, she called on Commonwealth members to turn their commitments into meaningful action to ensure prosperity, autonomy and resilience for every woman and girl.

The session was attended by Queen Camilla, the Queen Consort of the United Kingdom, the Commonwealth Deputy Secretary-General and several Commonwealth leaders, dignitaries and delegates.
