CSE holds near 21,700 as foreign buying strengthens

The Colombo Stock Exchange closed lower for a third consecutive session on 17 November 2026, although the pace of decline moderated as strong foreign buying provided some underlying support.

The ASPI declined 31.30 points, or 0.14%, to 21,701.07, while the S&P SL20 fell 15.37 points, or 0.25%, to 6,184.47. Early buying interest faded during the session, leaving the ASPI below the key 22,000 psychological level.

Market turnover remained healthy at LKR 3.73 billion, with 117.33 million shares traded. Activity was heavily concentrated in Sierra Cables and JAT Holdings, which together accounted for around 66% of total turnover.

Crossings amounted to approximately LKR 1.00 billion, or around 27% of total equity turnover, led by Sierra Cables with approximately LKR 828.00 million in block transactions.

A key positive development was the strong return of foreign buying. Foreign purchases reached approximately LKR 1.10 billion against sales of just LKR 15.31 million, resulting in a net foreign inflow of around LKR 1.09 billion.

Capital Goods led sector turnover at approximately LKR 1.68 billion, largely supported by Sierra Cables, followed by Materials at LKR 1.27 billion.

The session reflected a more measured decline compared with the previous broad sell-off, while the sizeable foreign inflow provided a notable positive signal. However, with the ASPI recording a third consecutive decline and remaining below 22,000, short-term momentum remains cautious, with the 21,700 area now an important near-term level to watch.
