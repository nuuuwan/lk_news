Police in disarray with the recent controversial transfers - Ajith P. Perera

Colombo, Oct. 5 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Police appeared to be in disarray, with the decision to transfer top-ranking police officers being subsequently cancelled, Opposition MP Ajith P. Perera alleged yesterday.

Speaking at a media briefing, Perera said the cancellation of the transfers of Senior Deputy Inspector General of Police (SDIG) Ranmal Kodituwakku and Deputy Inspector General of Police (DIG) of the Police Narcotics Bureau Ashoka Dharmasena was an indication of a clear lack of coordination within the police.

The National Police Commission recently cancelled, with immediate effect, the transfers of several police officers, including SDIG Kodituwakku and DIG Dharmasena. The transfers had been ordered by the Inspector General of Police (IGP).

“Senior DIG Kodituwakku should have been presented with an award after he managed to seize a container carrying narcotics worth Rs. 10 billion. One wonders who decided on his transfer. The police appear to be in disarray when transfers are first ordered and subsequently cancelled. Such a situation does not augur well for the country,” Perera said.

He also said the Government appeared to be facing divisions on several issues. “It was Minister Bimal Rathnayake who said there were divisions within the Government over the 22nd Amendment to the Constitution,” he added.

Asked about the non-participation of some opposition parties in the recent protest organized against the 22nd Amendment, Perera said all opposition parties were opposed to the amendment, although some had not participated in the protest.
