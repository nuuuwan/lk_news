Megha Nadha unveils ‘new concept’ for live music in Sri Lanka

MEGHA NADHA, a premium signature live concert experience, was officially launched at Waters Edge, Colombo, setting a fresh benchmark in Sri Lanka’s entertainment scene. The concept offers an immersive musical journey blending timeless artistry with contemporary energy, featuring two fully live bands.

The concert brings together a curated lineup of six artistes: two legendary performers, two iconic 1990s stars, and two viral contemporary acts – uniting nostalgia with modern musical expression.

Developed by award-winning event manager H.P. Kasun Eranga Gunawardena – recently honoured as “Global Icon of the Year – Event Manager (Sri Lanka)” at the Global Icon Awards 2025—MEGHA NADHA is produced by CCC Events (Pvt) Ltd. Designed for both indoor and outdoor venues, the experience promises to connect deeply with audiences as Sri Lanka’s live music scene continues to evolve.
