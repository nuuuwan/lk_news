Citrus Group CEO Nirekshe Perera appointed to related-party company Boards

Related companies Citrus Leisure PLC, Hikkaduwa Beach Resort PLC, and Waskaduwa Beach Resort PLC has appointed Nirekshe Perera to their Boards as an Executive Director.

Perera is a multifaceted hospitality executive with over 25 years of leadership experience spanning luxury hotels, integrated leisure complexes, MICE operations and banking. He brings a strong track record in building and positioning premium brands, driving revenue growth, optimising costs, and delivering operational excellence in challenging business environments.

His exposure in the leisure industry includes Group Chief Executive Officer of Monarch Imperial, senior leadership roles at Shangri-La Hotel Colombo, Waters Edge Limited, and Hilton Colombo Residences. He holds a Bachelor of Commerce from the University of Western Sydney, Australia, and is a Member of the Chartered Institute of Marketing (CIM). Presently, he serves as the Group Chief Executive Officer of Citrus Group.
