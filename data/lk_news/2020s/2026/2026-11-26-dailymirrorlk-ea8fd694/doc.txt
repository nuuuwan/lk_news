Migration of Doctors drops sharply: Deputy Minister

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Deputy Minister of Health Dr. Hansaka Wijemuni yesterday dismissed allegations of a continuing exodus of doctors, saying the migration of doctors had declined significantly over the past two years, with only 30 doctors leaving the country in 2025 and six in 2026 so far.

Addressing Parliament, the Deputy Minister said some individuals making such allegations even counted doctors travelling overseas for training as those permanently migrating from the country.

According to actual figures maintained by the Health Ministry, 28 doctors left the country in 2020, 58 in 2021, 153 in 2022, 120 in 2023, 117 in 2024, 30 in 2025 and six in 2026 so far, he said.

Wijemuni also pointed to an increasing trend of doctors returning to Sri Lanka after completing overseas training, saying that 312 doctors had returned to the country in 2026, marking a record number in recent years.

He further said that 5,712 doctors would receive new appointments by the middle of next year.
