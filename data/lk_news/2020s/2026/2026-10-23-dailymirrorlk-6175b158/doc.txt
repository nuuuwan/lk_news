Fire erupts at machinery store in Nittambuwa

Colombo, Oct. 23 (Daily Mirror) - A fire broke out at a machinery and electrical appliance store in Nittambuwa town this afternoon (23), prompting the deployment of two fire engines.

Nittambuwa Police said two fire trucks were sent to the scene to extinguish the blaze.

The cause of the fire and the value of the damage caused are yet to be ascertained.
