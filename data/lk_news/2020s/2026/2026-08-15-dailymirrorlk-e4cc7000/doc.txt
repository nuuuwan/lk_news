Indonesia Flores earthquake kills two, tsunami warning lifted after panic

(INDIA TODAY) - A powerful earthquake struck off Flores, collapsing buildings and prompting evacuations. The brief tsunami alert was lifted, but widespread damage and panic kept authorities on edge.

A 7.7-magnitude earthquake struck off Indonesia’s Flores region early on Saturday, collapsing homes and buildings, killing at least two people, injuring two others and causing panic. Authorities issued a tsunami warning after the quake, but later withdrew it after Indonesia’s Meteorology, Climatology and Geophysics Agency said monitoring showed no significant sea-level changes that would threaten coastal communities.

The US Geological Survey said the earthquake struck at a depth of 10 km at 5:58 am local time. Its epicentre was 68 km north-northwest of Ende in East Nusa Tenggara province. Several aftershocks followed. Preliminary reports from the Maumere Search and Rescue Agency, cited by Indonesia’s disaster management agency, said the quake caused severe damage and brought down buildings.
