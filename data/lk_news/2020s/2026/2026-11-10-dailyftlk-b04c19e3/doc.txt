SLID and Life Insurance Corporation to host CEO and Chairpersons Forum

The Sri Lanka Institute of Directors (SLID), in collaboration with Life Insurance Corporation (Lanka) Ltd., will host its upcoming CEO and Chairpersons Forum on “Emerging Trends and Challenges in Financial Services” tomorrow (11) at the Atrium, Cinnamon Grand Colombo, from 6 p.m. to 7 p.m., followed by cocktails and networking.

The forum is an exclusive event for SLID members, providing a platform for senior business leaders to exchange perspectives on developments shaping the financial services industry and explore the opportunities arising from India’s economic momentum and the evolving regional financial landscape.

The keynote address will be delivered by Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) Chief Executive Officer and Managing Director R. Doraiswamy. His address will explore India’s financial services landscape and the potential implications of its economic development for Sri Lanka and the wider region. The forum will also feature Janashakthi Life and Janashakthi General Founder/Director Chandra Schaffter, as a panelist, bringing his industry experience and insights into the development of Sri Lanka’s insurance sector.

The fireside chat will be moderated by Platinum Realty Investments Managing Director and Indian CEO Forum President Kishore Reddy. The discussion will focus on emerging trends, evolving business opportunities, and the strategic considerations facing financial services leaders in an increasingly interconnected regional economy. Through this exclusive engagement, SLID continues to strengthen its role as a platform for boardroom leaders to exchange knowledge, engage with industry experts, and gain insights relevant to strategic decision-making and sustainable business growth. The forum is intended for SLID members serving as Chairpersons/Chairmen, CEOs/Managing Directors, C-suite Executives or Board Directors.

The CEO and Chairpersons Forum reflects SLID’s commitment to promoting excellence in corporate governance and leadership by facilitating meaningful dialogue among senior business leaders on issues shaping the future of business and the economy.
