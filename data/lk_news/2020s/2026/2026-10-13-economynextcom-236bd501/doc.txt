Sri Lanka Cabinet approves $100mn ADB loan for water sector reforms

ECONOMYNEXT – Sri Lanka’s cabinet has approved a proposal to enter into an agreement with the Asian Development Bank to obtain a 100 million dollar loan for water sector improvements, minister Nalinda Jayatissa said.

Of this, 50 million US dollars will be obtained as a policy-based loan, and 50 million dollars will be from the concessional ordinary capital resources of the ADB.

The funds will be used to implement the Water Supply and Sanitation Reform Program – Sub-program 2.

The program is expected to support Sri Lanka in addressing weaknesses in water resource management, operational and financial inefficiencies, exposure to climate risks, shortcomings in the inclusion of all segments of society, and lack of proper coordination among stakeholders.

This USD 100 million is part of a much larger 620 million dollar total loan package from the Asian Development Bank aimed at financing Sri Lanka’s broader economic reform agenda. (Colombo/Oct13/2026)
