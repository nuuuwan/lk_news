Five Chinese nationals wanted by China deported

Colombo, Nov. 28 (Daily Mirror) - Five Chinese nationals wanted by the Chinese Government have been arrested in different parts of Sri Lanka and deported to China following a request from the Chinese authorities.

The five suspects were arrested by officers of the Investigation Unit of the Department of Immigration and Emigration and detained at the Welisara Detention Centre before being deported on the night of November 26.

They had been staying in Sri Lanka in violation of the provisions of the Immigration and Emigration Act.

The five Chinese nationals were deported on two separate flights to China under the escort of eight officers from the Department of Immigration and Emigration.

The first group of three Chinese nationals, accompanied by five Sri Lankan immigration officers, left Bandaranaike International Airport at Katunayake at 8.50 p.m. on November 26 aboard China Eastern Airlines flight MU 6018, bound for Shanghai.

The remaining two Chinese nationals, accompanied by three immigration officers, were deported on Air China flight CA 426, which left Katunayake at 10.25 p.m. on the same day for Chengdu, China.

The five deportees have since been handed over to Chinese immigration authorities by Sri Lankan immigration officials.

The Chinese Government had borne the cost of the air tickets for their deportation.
