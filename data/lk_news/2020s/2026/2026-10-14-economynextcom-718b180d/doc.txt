Sri Lanka seeks to ease forex regulatory hurdles for electronics exporters

ECONOMYNEXT – Sri Lanka’s central bank and export board have met electronics exporters to discuss easing forex regulatory hurdles for the sector as the country targets 1 billion dollars in exports by 2028.

“To achieve this target, it is essential that Foreign Exchange Rules, regulations, Monitory and Fiscal Policies are export friendly,” the Export Development Board said.

Over 35 senior management representatives from the Electronic and Electrical sector participated in the seminar.

“The progarmme aimed to support the country’s Electronics and Electrical Sector by leveraging the expertise available within the Central Bank of Sri Lanka to share knowledge and to resolve issues in the areas of Foreign Exchange Rules, Inward and Outward Transactional Regulations, and Forex Repatriation Regulations.”

The industry, with over 100 companies and 37,000 employees, is the fifth largest merchandise export revenue earner of the country and earned 440 million dollars in 2025, the EDB said.

Central bank officials advised them on foreign exchange rules, transactional and working capital regulations, and forex repatriation regulations relevant to them.

“The event provided a platform for Electronics and Electrical Sector and Central Bank of Sri Lanka to connect, share knowledge, and discuss the Regulatory and Operational Issues Faced by Electronic and Electrical Manufacturing Companies and how to resolve them.” (Colombo/Oct14/2026)
