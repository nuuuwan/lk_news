Browns Hotels & Resorts triumphs with Gold and Silver at SATA 2026

From left: Gangadaran Velasamy - General Manager, Newburgh Ella – The Tea Factory Resort; Eksath Wijeratne - Director/CEO, Browns Hotels & Resorts; Rohan Gamage - Cluster General Manager, Thaala Bentota and The Eden Beruwala; and Manoj Perera - General Manager, Club Hotel Dolphin

Browns Hotels & Resorts has secured five accolades at the South Asian Travel Awards (SATA) 2026, held in the Maldives, earning two Gold and three Silver awards across five properties in its portfolio.

The achievement celebrates the group’s diverse hospitality offering, spanning Sri Lanka’s tea heritage, luxury wellness, all-inclusive holidays, beachfront resorts and boutique experiences, strengthening its presence on the international tourism stage.

Leading the Gold wins, Newburgh Ella – The Tea Factory Resort clinched South Asia’s Best New Hotel, while Ayugiri Ayurveda Wellness Resort secured South Asia’s Leading Wellness Resort.

As the newest addition to the Browns Hotels & Resorts portfolio, Newburgh Ella – The Tea Factory Resort, under the leadership of General Manager Gangadara Velasamy, is a former tea factory dating back to 1903, transformed into a contemporary hospitality experience.

Winning Gold at the South Asian Travel Awards 2026 as South Asia’s Leading Wellness Resort, Ayugiri Ayurveda Wellness Resort in Sigiriya, led by General Manager Sujeewa de Zoysa, offers a considered approach to luxury wellness. Set across 44 acres in Sigiriya, the resort combines personalised wellbeing with Sri Lanka’s rich cultural heritage and abundant wildlife, including opportunities for elephant spotting.

At Club Hotel Dolphin in Negombo, General Manager Manoj Perera and his team clinched South Asia’s Leading All-Inclusive Resort for the third consecutive time, reinforcing the property’s established reputation for energetic, experience-led family holidays, with an extensive range of dining, leisure and entertainment activities for guests of all ages.

Cluster General Manager Rohan Gamage and the team at The Eden Beruwala secured the Silver award for South Asia’s Leading Beach Resort for the second consecutive time. Located along Sri Lanka’s renowned Golden Mile, this five-star beachfront resort combines coastal surroundings with varied dining experiences, including beach dining, alongside a range of water sports and leisure activities.

Lantern Boutique Hotel by Reveal the Collection, part of Browns Hotels & Resorts, clinched the Silver award for South Asia’s Leading Boutique Hotel, celebrating its approach to boutique hospitality. Led by General Manager Sharmen Perera, the property is located in Mirissa and offers an intimate, design-led stay shaped by its coastal surroundings.

Commenting on the achievement, Eksath Wijeratne, Director/Chief Executive Officer of Browns Hotels & Resorts, said, “These accolades belong to the people behind every guest experience, whose dedication and passion make the difference every day. We look forward to welcoming more travellers to discover our properties, experience Sri Lanka in different ways and create stories of their own.”
