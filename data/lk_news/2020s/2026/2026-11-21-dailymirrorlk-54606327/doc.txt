Vietjet launches new Colombo-Maldives flights

VietJet is set to expand its regional connectivity with the launch of new flights between Colombo and the Maldives, commencing December 5, 2026, offering the travellers greater convenience and enhanced connectivity between the two destinations.

The new service will operate three times weekly, on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays, providing an additional travel option for the passengers travelling between Sri Lanka and the Maldives, while further strengthening Colombo’s position as an important regional aviation hub.

The introduction of this service reflects VietJet’s continued commitment to expanding its regional network and providing the travellers with greater accessibility, competitive travel options and convenient connections across Asia.

VietJet Sri Lanka General Sales Agent Mahen Kariyawasan said, “We are delighted to announce the introduction of VietJet’s new flights connecting Colombo with the Maldives.

This is an exciting development for Sri Lanka’s aviation and tourism sectors, creating new opportunities for travel, tourism, trade and regional cooperation.”

“The Maldives is an important tourism destination and the introduction of this service will offer greater convenience to travellers while also creating opportunities for Sri Lanka to strengthen its position as a regional gateway. We believe this new connection will benefit both leisure travellers and the wider travel and tourism industry, while further enhancing the connectivity between Sri Lanka, the Maldives and VietJet’s broader network.”

The three weekly flights, operating on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays from December 5, 2026, are expected to provide an attractive travel option for the holidaymakers, business travellers and passengers seeking convenient regional connections.

The launch further reinforces VietJet’s growing presence in the South Asian market and its commitment to bringing destinations closer through affordable and accessible air travel.
