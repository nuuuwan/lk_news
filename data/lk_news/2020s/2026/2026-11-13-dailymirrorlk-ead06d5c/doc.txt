Negligence in drain cleaning contributed to recent Colombo floods: Minister

Colombo, Nov. 13 (Daily Mirror) - Negligence in maintaining and cleaning drainage systems in Colombo City was a major factor behind the recent flooding, Minister of Public Administration and Local Government A. H. M. Abeyrathana told Parliament today.

Responding to an oral question raised by MP Mujibur Rahman, the Minister also identified unauthorised structures as another contributing factor to the flooding.
