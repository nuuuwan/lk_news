HNB invests Rs.3.2 million in vocational training

HNB and SOS Children’s Villages Sri Lanka reaffirmed their enduring commitment towards elevating vocational training and career development at SOS Vocational Training Centres in Jaffna and Malpotha with a contribution of Rs.3.2 million from HNB

Empowers 80 young people through strengthened partnership with SOS Children’s Villages Sri Lanka

Hatton National Bank (HNB) has strengthened its long-standing partnership with SOS Children’s Villages Sri Lanka, committing Rs.3.2 million towards the vocational training and career development of 80 young people at the SOS Vocational Training Centres in Jaffna and Malpotha.

Recognising the importance of vocational skills in helping local youth transition into employment opportunities and independent adulthood, SOS Children’s Villages Sri Lanka operates Vocational Training Centres that provide practical, job-oriented training, career development and workplace readiness. Building on this commitment, the partnership with HNB will further strengthen these opportunities for young people, equipping them with the skills and confidence needed to pursue meaningful employment, achieve greater financial independence and build sustainable livelihoods. SOS Children’s Villages Sri Lanka National Director Divakar Ratnadurai said, “Vocational training offers holistic avenues of growth for young people with more than technical skills. It gives them the confidence, workplace readiness and practical capabilities needed to take meaningful steps towards employment and independent living. We value our long-standing partnership with HNB and its continued commitment to creating opportunities that enable young people to build stronger and more sustainable futures.” The initiative builds on the broader role of SOS Children’s Villages Sri Lanka in supporting young people as they develop the skills and capabilities required for independent adulthood.  Since 1981, SOS Children’s Villages Sri Lanka has provided long-term, family-like care for children who have lost parental care, while also supporting children who are at risk of losing parental care through its Family Strengthening Programmes. Today, the organisation operates six Children’s Villages, Family Strengthening Programmes across 11 locations and four Vocational Training Centres in Anuradhapura, Monaragala, Jaffna and Malpotha, supporting children and young people with care, security and opportunities to develop skills for their future.

Through this partnership, HNB and SOS Children’s Villages Sri Lanka are investing in skills development as a pathway to greater independence, employability and sustainable livelihoods for young people, while building skills to break the cycle of poverty.
