Daily Mirror report comes to limelight in Parliament

A Daily Mirror report published on July 31 exposing the fate of the Central Expressway came to limelight in Parliament today, with Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) MP S. M. Marikkar questioning the government over losses incurred as a result of limited competition during the tender process.

Quoting the Daily Mirror report, the MP said the cost of Section 3 of the expressway had increased to Rs. 117.5 billion from the original estimate of Rs. 94.6 billion.

He said competitiveness had been restricted due to changes in the tender procedure. “The ultimate result is that a mafia is being created when only a handful of contractors are allowed to bid. Another issue is that contractors awarded the contracts then subcontract the work to others. They keep a markup for themselves when awarding subcontracts,” the MP said.

At the same time, he said the report had created a positive outcome, as an advertisement had subsequently appeared in the media calling for contractors for other road development projects.
