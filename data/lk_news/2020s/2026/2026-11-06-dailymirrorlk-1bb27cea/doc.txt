Namal Rajapaksa produced before Colombo Magistrate’s Court

Colombo, Nov 06 (Daily Mirror) - MP Namal Rajapaksa, who is currently in remand custody, was produced before the Colombo Magistrate's Court today in connection with the ongoing proceedings in the Krrish transaction case.

Although the court had previously granted him bail in connection with the Krrish transaction case, he remains in remand custody following an order issued by a three-member High Court bench in relation to the Airbus bribery case.
