Taj Samudra welcomes Defence Minister of India

A warm welcome to Sri Lanka and Taj Samudra for Shri Rajnath Singh Defence Minister of India

Defence Minister Shri Rajnath Singh (left) with Taj Maldives and Sri Lanka Area Director Samrat Datta and Indian High Commissioner Santosh Jha

Taj Samudra, Colombo was honoured to welcome Defence Minister of India Shri Rajnath Singh to the hotel.

Taj Area Director – Maldives and Sri Lanka and Taj Samudra Colombo General Manager Samrat Datta warmly welcomed Shri Rajnath Singh on his arrival.
