FINEX unveils SL’s 1st facial tissue canister

FINEX Tissues, owned by Global Consumer Products (Pvt.) Ltd, marked its third consecutive appearance at Hotel Show Colombo 2026 by introducing a genuine first to the local market: the FINEX facial tissue canister holder.

The brand unveiled what it describes as Sri Lanka’s first locally introduced facial tissue canister, designed to provide a convenient tissue solution for a wide range of settings, including homes, offices, vehicles, hotels and other commercial spaces.

Global Consumer Products Managing Director I.L. Dawood said, “ We wanted to give the hospitality buyers and our end consumers something genuinely new and the number of conversations we had over three days told us that’s exactly what they were looking for.”
