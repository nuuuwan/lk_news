Committee begins review of prison regulations following talks with Maha Sangha

The first meeting of a special committee appointed to review regulations and standing orders under the Prisons Ordinance was held at the Presidential Secretariat yesterday (9).

The committee was appointed following discussions between President Anura Kumara Dissanayake and members of the Maha Sangha representing the three Nikayas on October 8.

The meeting was attended by Committee Chairman and Secretary to the Ministry of Buddhasasana, Religious and Cultural Affairs Prince Senadheera, Additional Secretary to the Ministry of Justice and National Integration Piyumanthi Peiris, Additional Secretary to the Ministry of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government Wasantha Ariyarathna, Commissioner-General of Buddhist Affairs R.M.G. Senarathna and Prisons Commissioner A.C. Gajanayake.

Minister of Justice and National Integration Harshana Nanayakkara provided policy guidance to the committee.

According to the President’s Media Division, the committee has been tasked with updating the regulations and standing orders made under the Prisons Ordinance.
