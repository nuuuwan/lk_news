Government mulls one-year retirement extension for Police officers

Colombo, August 4 (Daily Mirror)- The government is considering extending the retirement age of police officers with unblemished service records by one year beyond the current retirement age of 60, Public Security Minister Ananda Wijepala told Parliament today.

Responding to a question raised by SJB MP Ranjith Madduma Bandara, the Minister said there are a large number of vacancies in the Police Department and that steps are being taken to fill these vacancies.

He said the government plans to recruit 7,800 personnel to the police service before the end of this year as part of efforts to strengthen the force.

Minister Wijepala also said steps have been taken to transfer 10,000 personnel from the Civil Security Department to the police service, adding that 6,000 have already assumed duties.

In addition, he said 200 Sub Inspectors (SIs) are currently undergoing training at Police Training Colleges, while interviews are in progress to recruit more officers.
