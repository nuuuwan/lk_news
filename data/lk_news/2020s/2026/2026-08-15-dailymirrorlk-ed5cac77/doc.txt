Did Justice Minister record a livestream while driving?

Colombo, August 15 (Daily Mirror) - A video of Justice and National Integration Minister Harshana Nanayakkara driving has been falsely presented on social media as footage of him livestreaming while driving on August 13, 2026.

A fact-check by Fact Crescendo Sri Lanka found that the video is from August 13, 2023, when Nanayakkara was serving as the National People’s Power (NPP) organiser for the Moratuwa electorate.

The footage resurfaced this week with claims that Nanayakkara had recorded or livestreamed himself while driving on August 13 this year.

The date mentioned by Nanayakkara in the video appears to have contributed to the misleading claim.

However, a reverse image search showed that the footage had been published online on August 13, 2023.

In the original video, Nanayakkara is seen driving and speaking to the camera while inviting supporters to an NPP electorate meeting in Moratuwa. The event was attended by then-NPP leader Anura Kumara Dissanayake and MP Harini Amarasuriya.

The fact-check found that the camera was being operated by another person seated in the front passenger seat, rather than by Nanayakkara.

The video was recorded before Nanayakkara became a Cabinet Minister and does not show him livestreaming while driving on August 13, 2026.
