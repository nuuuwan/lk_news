Indices close in green

Indices closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Commercial Bank, Dialog Axiata and Hatton National Bank with the turnover crossing Rs. 2.2 Bn. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Melstacorp, LB Finance and Hemas Holdings. Mixed interest was observed in Dialog Axiata, CIC Holdings and Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka whilst retail interest was noted in Browns Investments, ACME Printing & Packaging and HNB Finance. Furthermore, foreigners remained active closing as net sellers.

Telecommunication Services sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Dialog Axiata) whilst the sector index gained 2.82%. The share price of Dialog Axiata increased by Rs 1.30 (3.08%) to close at Rs 43.50.

Food, Beverage & Tobacco sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Melstacorp) whilst the sector index increased by 1.03%. The share price of Melstacorp gained Rs 0.50 (0.28%) to close at Rs 177.00.

CIC Holdings, LB Finance and Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of CIC Holdings moved up by

Rs 0.90 (3.61%) to close at Rs 25.80. The share price of LB Finance closed flat at Rs 139.75. The share price of Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka closed flat at Rs 150.00.
