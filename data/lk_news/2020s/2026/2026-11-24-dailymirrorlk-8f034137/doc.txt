Saroja wins Global Business Excellence Award

Managing Director of S G Actuarial Consultancy (Pvt) Ltd, Saroja Gunatilleke, has won the Woman of Excellence in Actuarial and Insurance Leadership award at the Global Business Excellence Awards 2026.

Presented by London Business Consultancy (UK), the awards programme carries institutional recognition through the DEC under Sri Lanka’s Ministry of Industry.

“I am deeply honoured to receive the Global Business Excellence (GBE) Award, which recognises the contributions of outstanding individuals and organisations. I see this recognition as an acknowledgement of my ongoing efforts to advance the actuarial profession and contribute to Sri Lanka’s development,” Saroja said.

The award adds to the Global CEO Forum’s 45 Under 45 Leadership Excellence Platinum Award she received in 2024, and acknowledges her continuing contribution to the actuarial and insurance sectors.

With 15 years of experience spanning actuarial science, risk management, regulation and insurance, Saroja has built a career distinguished by academic excellence and professional leadership. She holds a B.Sc. (Hons) in Mathematics, along with a Graduate Diploma and an M.Sc. in Actuarial Science, from UK universities, all achieved with First Class or Distinction passes.

In 2010, aged 26, she qualified as an Associate of the Institute and Faculty of Actuaries (IFoA), UK, becoming the youngest person and the only woman in Sri Lanka to hold the qualification at the time. Two years later, she achieved Fellowship of the institute and was then the only Sri Lankan Fellow Actuary of the IFoA working in the country.

Today, Saroja leads S G Actuarial Consultancy, which provides tailored consultancy services in areas including risk management, strategic planning, gratuity valuation and regulatory compliance, as well as training for non-actuarial professionals and students.

Her work reflects a broader ambition to raise awareness of the actuarial profession, inspire the next generation of professionals and strengthen the practice of risk management in Sri Lanka. At a time when actuarial careers and their wider applications remain little understood, she is committed to creating greater opportunities for aspiring actuaries while helping organisations across industries recognise the value of sound risk management and informed decision-making. Through these efforts, she hopes to contribute not only to the growth of the profession but also to Sri Lanka’s broader economic and professional development.
