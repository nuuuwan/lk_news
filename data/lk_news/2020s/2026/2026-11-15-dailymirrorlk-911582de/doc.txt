Sajith Premadasa urges ‘Red Notice’ and rapid deployment force to protect tuskers

Nov 15 (Daily Mirror) - Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa urged the government to issue a domestic ‘Red Notice’ and establish a dedicated rapid deployment force to safeguard Sri Lanka’s dwindling tusker population, according to a statement issued by the Opposition Leader’s Office.

Making a special statement ahead of the one-month remembrance of the late iconic tusker Kavantissa, Premadasa warned that the country’s tuskers—who account for only 6 to 10 percent of the male elephant population—face grave danger.

He noted that current government conservation efforts lack a structured national plan and criticised authorities for relying on the advice of a single organisation rather than consulting all environmentalists and stakeholders when compiling a registry of tuskers.

Highlighting severe resource shortages within the Department of Wildlife Conservation, including a deficit of veterinary surgeons with only 17 serving out of the required 33, Premadasa called for mobile veterinary ambulance services in human-elephant conflict zones.

He further proposed training a unit of the security forces in wildlife conservation, similar to models in India and Africa, to protect small, medium, and large tuskers alike, pledging the Samagi Jana Balawegaya’s (SJB) full support for a concrete action plan.

Meanwhile, a Dhamma sermon and an alms-giving (Dana) will be held at the Buduruwakanda Rajamaha Viharaya in Galgamuwa on November 16 and 17 to invoke merit on the late tusker Kavantissa.
