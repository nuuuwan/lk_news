18-day capacity-building program in India  for Sri Lankan gem and jewellery sector

A 29-member delegation of professionals from Sri Lanka’s Gem and Jewellery sector visited India from 6–23 July 2026 to participate in a specialised Training and Capacity Building Program.

It was organised pursuant to the announcement made by Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi, during his visit to Sri Lanka in April 2025, under which India committed to offering 700 customised training slots annually for Sri Lankan professionals as part of ongoing bilateral capacity-building cooperation.

The 18-day training program was conducted by the Government of India at the Indian Institute of Gem and Jewellery, Jaipur, Rajasthan. The curriculum comprised a comprehensive set of technical and thematic sessions covering the entire Gem and Jewellery value chain. The program focused on practical tools, editing, and 3D print-ready outputs, building a strong base for advanced skills.

As part of the experiential learning component, the participants undertook site visits to leading gemstone manufacturing units namely Valentine Silver International, Jaipur, gaining first-hand exposure to contemporary production technologies, design development processes, and modern retail practices within India’s Gem and Jewellery ecosystem.

The specialised training program contributed meaningfully to strengthening professional competencies, promoting knowledge exchange, and deepening institutional and industry linkages in the Gem and Jewellery sector between India and Sri Lanka, reflecting the continued commitment of both countries to capacity building and people-centric economic cooperation.
