AG clears draft bill to extend judges’ retirement age

Colombo, Aug. 2 (Daily Mirror) - The Attorney General has granted legal clearance for the draft bill seeking to extend the retirement age of judges by two years, paving the way for the legislation to move to the next stage.

According to the Attorney General's Department, the legal review of the draft bill has been completed and the necessary approval has been formally conveyed to the Ministry of Justice.

Following the Attorney General's clearance, the Ministry of Justice is expected to finalize the draft legislation before it is published in the Government Gazette.

The move follows the recent approval by the Cabinet of Ministers for a proposal submitted by the Ministry of Justice to increase the retirement age of judges by two years.
