Two children among four killed in Kaduwela blaze

Colombo, Nov 19 ( Daily Mirror)- Four members of the same family, including two young children, have died after a fire broke out at an electrical appliances store in Kaduwela town this morning (19).

The victims were identified as the father, mother and their two children, aged two and six.

The electrical appliances store was located on the ground floor of a two-storey building, while the family was reportedly residing on the upper floor.

Three fire engines from the Kotte Fire Brigade were deployed to the scene to bring the blaze under control.

The cause of the fire has not yet been established, while further details surrounding the incident are yet to be disclosed.
