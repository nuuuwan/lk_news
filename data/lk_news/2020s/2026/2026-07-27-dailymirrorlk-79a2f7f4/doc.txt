Three-wheeler driver killed in Gothatuwa road accident

Colombo, July 27 (Daily Mirror) - A three-wheeler driver was killed in a road accident in the Gothatuwa area this morning (27), Police said.

The accident occurred when a three-wheeler travelling from Kolonnawa towards Gothatuwa collided with a private bus.

The three-wheeler driver died at the scene, while another person who sustained injuries was admitted to the Colombo National Hospital for treatment.
