Nearly 450kg of suspected smuggled beedi leaves found on Chilaw coast

Nearly 450 kilogrammes of beedi leaves suspected to have been smuggled into the country by sea were found abandoned along the Chilaw coastline during a joint operation by the Sri Lanka Coast Guard and the Navy early today (24).

The operation was launched around 5 a.m. following information received by personnel at the Chilaw Coast Guard Station.

The operation involved the crew of patrol vessel CG 105, which was conducting night patrols, and personnel from the Thoduwawa Naval Task Force.

During the operation, officers found 15 sacks containing beedi leaves abandoned on the beach. Each sack was estimated to weigh around 30 kilogrammes.

Officials suspect that those involved in the smuggling had abandoned the consignment and fled when Coast Guard personnel arrived at the scene.

The seized stock is to be handed over to the Excise Department in Chilaw for further legal action.
