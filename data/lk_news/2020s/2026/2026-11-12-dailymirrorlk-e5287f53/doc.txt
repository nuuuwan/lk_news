Kareena Kapoor served legal notice over Bhopal royal pendant with Quranic verse

INDIA TODAY - Actor Kareena Kapoor Khan's Diwali look has landed at the centre of a controversy following allegations of hurting religious sentiments. Madhya Pradesh Congress spokesperson Abbas Hafeez has served a legal notice to Kareena Kapoor Khan and her stylist, Rhea Kapoor, over a pendant the actor wore which featues a Quranic verse.

According to India Today, Hafeez has demanded that the photographs be removed from social media and sought a public apology.
