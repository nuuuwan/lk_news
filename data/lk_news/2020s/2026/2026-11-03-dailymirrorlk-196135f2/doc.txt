Parliamentary Committee discusses legal reforms, alternatives to ease prison overcrowding

Colombo, Nov. 3 (Daily Mirror) - The Sectoral Oversight Committee on Governance, Justice and Civil Protection discussed measures to ease prison overcrowding, including amendments to the Prisons Ordinance, referring drug addicts for rehabilitation and expediting the issuance of Government Analyst's reports.

These matters were taken up when the Committee met at Parliament recently (23) under the chairmanship of MP Dr. Najith Indika.

The Committee has been holding a series of discussions with relevant stakeholders to find solutions to prison overcrowding, with the latest meeting focusing on proposed measures to address challenges within the prison system.

Among the proposals discussed were legislative amendments, the establishment of open prisons and work camps, conditional release of inmates and directing prisoners with good conduct towards employment opportunities.

The Committee also considered strengthening the mechanism for referring people with drug addiction to rehabilitation centres instead of sending them to prison.

Measures to clear the backlog of reports at the Government Analyst's Department were also reviewed. These included increasing staff capacity, improving transport facilities and expanding overtime services.

The discussions further focused on the legal and administrative reforms required to address prison overcrowding, with particular attention to coordination between the Ministry of Justice and National Integration and the Department of Prisons.
