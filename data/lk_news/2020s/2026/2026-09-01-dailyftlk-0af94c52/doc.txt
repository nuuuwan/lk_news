GovPay transactions cross Rs. 5 b mark

Transactions processed through GovPay, Sri Lanka’s digital Government payment platform, have surpassed Rs. 5 billion, with more than 300 institutions now using the system, the Digital Economy Ministry said.

The Ministry announced the milestone in a post highlighting the expanding adoption of GovPay as part of efforts to digitise payments to Government institutions.

GovPay enables the public to make payments to participating Government institutions digitally, providing an alternative to physical payment processes.
