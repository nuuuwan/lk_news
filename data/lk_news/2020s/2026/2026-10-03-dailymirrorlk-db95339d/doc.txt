IRD revenue tops Rs.2,040bn in 9 months

The Inland Revenue Department (IRD) has collected more than Rs.2,040 billion in tax revenue during the first nine months of 2026, recording a 24 percent increase compared to the corresponding period last year.

According to the IRD, the revenue collection as of September 30, 2026, stood at Rs.2,040 billion, compared to Rs.1,642 billion collected during the same period in 2025.

The IRD said it has already achieved 85 percent of its annual revenue target for 2026 by the end of the third quarter.
