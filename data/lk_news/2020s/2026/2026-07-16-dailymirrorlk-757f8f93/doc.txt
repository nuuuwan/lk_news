FACETS 2027 Set for January

Officials and organisers attend the launch of the 34th edition of FACETS Sri Lanka 2027, organised by the Sri Lanka Gem & Jewellery Association (SLGJA). The international gem and jewellery exhibition will be held from January 4 to 6, 2027, at Cinnamon Life at City of Dreams Colombo, bringing together over 100 exhibitors and buyers from the USA, Europe, the Middle East, India, China, Thailand and other key markets. The exhibition will showcase Sri Lanka's world-renowned sapphires, gemstones and luxury jewellery, while featuring events such as Sapphire Night, Story Corner, Power Breakfast, Mine Tour and Port City Tour. During the media briefing, the organisers also unveiled the FACETS 2027 theme and teaser video and announced the exhibition dates for 2028, 2029 and 2030, reaffirming FACETS' position as Sri Lanka's premier international gem and jewellery exhibition.
