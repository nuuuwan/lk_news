65 youths benefit from Dreams Registrations CSR

Colombo, Oct. 07 (Daily Mirror) - A youth empowerment programme aimed at inspiring young people to recognize their potential and pursue their future aspirations was held at Diyagala Boys’ Town in Ragama, benefiting 65 boys aged between 14 and 19.

The initiative was organized by Dreams Registrations (Pvt) Ltd as part of its corporate social responsibility efforts, under the theme “Dream Big, Discover Yourself – Dream, Believe, Lead Achieve.”

The programme focused on personal growth and the development of essential life skills, with sessions covering goal setting, communication, teamwork, leadership, positive thinking, personality development and grooming.

Through interactive activities, discussions and practical exercises, participants were encouraged to identify their strengths, set goals and build greater confidence in themselves and their abilities.

The programme also highlighted the importance of consistent effort, resilience, responsible leadership and effective communication, while encouraging the young participants to approach their future with confidence and determination.

Dreams Registrations said the initiative was intended to remind participants that their future is shaped by the choices they make today, and encouraged them to overcome challenges and become responsible individuals who can make a positive contribution to society.

The company reaffirmed its commitment to supporting community development and creating meaningful opportunities for young people, noting that investment in youth is an investment in the future of the country. The company also expressed its appreciation to Diyagala Boys’ Town, Ragama, for facilitating the programme and welcoming the initiative.

Special appreciation was extended to Dreams Registrations Chief Operating Officer Ms. Nilanka Anthony and Senior Consultant – Corporate Compliance & People Strategy Ms. Sandika Alahakoon for their contribution and efforts in facilitating and delivering the programme. The company said it remains committed to supporting initiatives that empower young people, strengthen communities and create lasting positive social impact.
