Mihintalen Handak YouTube Channel defamation case postponed to December4

Colombo, Sep. 29 (Daily Mirror) - The case against Administrator of Mihintalen Handak YouTube Channel Ajith Edirisinghe on the charge of contempt of court was taken up yesterday before Anuradhapura District Judge Sampath Hewawasam.

He is alleged to have violated the court order to refrain from telecasting any programme bringing disrepute to the Chief Incumbent of the Mihintale Rajamaha Vihara Ven. Walawahengunawewe Dhammarathana Thera.

The Ven. Thera has filed a defamation case against the respondent for telecasting programmes on the Mihintalen HandakYouTube Channel as the court had issued a restraining order on further telecast of any programme related to the Mihintale temple or Ven. Dhammarathana Thera, pending the case filed by the Ven. Thera.

When the case was taken up, the charges relating to the violation of the Court order, which amounted to contempt of court were read over and explained to the suspect. However, he pleaded not guilty.

Lawyers appearing for Ven. Dhammarathana Thera told Court that the Administrator of the YouTube Channel Mihintalen Handak is the respondent in the defamation case filed by Ven.Thera for the telecast of derogatory programmes against the temple and the Ven. Thera. They submitted the respondent has violated the restraining order on any further programmes pending the case. The lawyers informed Court that the respondent continued to telecast programmes bringing disrepute to the Ven. Thera regardless of the restraining order.

Attorney at Law Suraj Walgama instructed by Attorney at Law Jayamuditha Jayasuriya appeared for Ven. Dhammarathana Thera while Attorney at Law Bhagya Sri Herath appeared for the respondent.
