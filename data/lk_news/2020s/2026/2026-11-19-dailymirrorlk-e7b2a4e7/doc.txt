2026 O/L admission card corrections open until November 30

The Examinations Department has opened an online revision facility for admission cards issued to candidates sitting the 2026 G.C.E. Ordinary Level (O/L) Examination, warning that the deadline for corrections will not be extended.

Candidates and school principals have been given until midnight on November 30 to make any necessary amendments to admission cards.

The examination is scheduled to be held from December 8 to 17 at 3,519 examination centres islandwide.

The Department said admission cards of school candidates had been sent to the relevant Zonal Education Offices, while those of private candidates had been posted to their personal addresses.

School principals have been instructed to log into the Department's website using their respective usernames and passwords to correct any errors in the admission details of school candidates, including first-, second- and third-time applicants.

Private candidates who need to correct their names, addresses, subjects, examination medium or gender must visit the Department's website and enter their National Identity Card numbers to make the necessary changes.

The online correction facility will remain open from November 19 until midnight on November 30. The Department has stressed that the deadline will not be extended.

In a special notice to both school and private candidates, the Department emphasised that any corrections to candidates' names must be made online within the stipulated period.

It warned that no name corrections would be permitted after the examination under any circumstances.

Candidates have also been instructed to ensure that their full names are correctly entered in English.

Candidates seeking further information can contact the Department through its 1911 hotline, telephone numbers 0112784208 and 0112784537, or email [email protected].

The notice was issued by Commissioner General of Examinations A.K.S. Indika Kumari Liyanage.
