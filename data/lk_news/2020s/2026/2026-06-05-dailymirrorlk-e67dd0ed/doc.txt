Shashel Lanka Manpower clinches Platinum Excellence Award

Shehani Hettiarachchi and Shenal Dananjana (Recruitment Manager) receiving the award

Shashel Lanka Manpower, a licensed foreign employment agency operating under Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment (SLBFE), was honoured with the ‘Platinum Excellence in Ethical Overseas Recruitment & Workforce Solutions’ award at the Platinum Excellence Awards 2026 ceremony held recently at Cinnamon Life – City of Dreams, Colombo.

The recognition highlights the company’s growing reputation as a trusted and ethical manpower recruitment agency committed to connecting skilled Sri Lankan workers with international employment opportunities while maintaining high standards of professionalism, transparency, and compliance.

Shashel Lanka Manpower has received the award in recognition of its contribution towards ethical overseas recruitment practices and reliable workforce solutions for global markets.

Based in Negombo, Shashel Lanka Manpower has steadily expanded its operations by supplying skilled, semi-skilled, and unskilled workers to several overseas destinations, particularly within Europe.

The company currently supports sectors including construction and engineering, hospitality and catering, healthcare and elder care, security services, manufacturing and general labour, domestic and cleaning services, agriculture, garments and textiles, automobile services, and air conditioning services.

Licensee Shehani Hettiarachchi noted that the company remains focused on creating trustworthy overseas employment opportunities for Sri Lankan workers while building strong international partnerships based on integrity and mutual growth.

“At Shashel Lanka Manpower, we believe that the strength of every successful organisation lies in the quality of its people. This recognition encourages us to continue delivering reliable and ethical recruitment solutions that benefit both employers and employees.”
