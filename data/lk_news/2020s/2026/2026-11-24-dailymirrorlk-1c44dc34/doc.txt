Sri Lanka temporarily allows travelers to board flights without an ETA

Due to the temporary unavailability of its Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) system, Sri Lanka is allowing travelers of most nationalities to board flights without obtaining prior approval until further notice.

Eligible passengers will be able to complete their entry formalities upon arrival in the country.

In a circular dated November 23, 2026, bearing reference number DIE/VISA/01/121/ETA Resto, Sri Lanka’s Department of Immigration and Emigration announced that its ETA application portal was temporarily unavailable due to ongoing system maintenance.

The announcement was shared on Tuesday, November 24, 2026, by several Sri Lankan diplomatic missions around the world, including the embassies in the Philippines, Japan, Sweden, and Türkiye, as well as the high commissions in Australia and Singapore.

To minimize disruption for international travelers, Sri Lankan authorities have temporarily adjusted the country’s entry procedures.

Authorities have confirmed that eligible travelers are temporarily permitted to board flights to Sri Lanka without obtaining prior ETA approval.

Upon arrival at Colombo’s Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) or another relevant port of entry, passengers will be able to obtain their Electronic Travel Authorization or Visit Visa directly from immigration authorities.

Airlines and their ground handling agents have been asked to make the necessary arrangements to facilitate boarding for passengers covered by this temporary measure.

Citizens of the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, and European Union countries are among those eligible for the temporary relaxation of entry requirements.

Since May 25, 2026, Sri Lanka has been offering free tourist ETAs to citizens of 40 countries. However, the notices issued by Sri Lankan diplomatic missions on Tuesday do not specify whether this fee waiver will also apply to authorizations issued upon arrival under the temporary arrangements.
