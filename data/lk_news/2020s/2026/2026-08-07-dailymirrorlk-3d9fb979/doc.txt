14 injured after van plunges into precipice in Bulathsinhala

Colombo, Aug. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Fourteen people were injured after a van veered off the road and fell into a precipice in the Udugalakanda area of Bulathsinhala this morning (7).

The injured passengers were admitted to hospital for treatment, with 12 of them reported to be women.

It was reported that  the accident occurred after the vehicle’s brakes failed, causing the driver to lose control.
