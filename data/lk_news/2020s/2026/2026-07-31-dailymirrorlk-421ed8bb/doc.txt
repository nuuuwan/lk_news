Week ends in red

The ASPI edged down as a result of price losses in counters such as Hatton National Bank, Central Finance Company and DFCC Bank with the turnover crossing Rs. 4.3 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P

SL20. Crossings were witnessed in United Motors Lanka, Dialog Axiata and Hayleys accounting for 57.8% of the turnover. Mixed interest was observed in Commercial Credit & Finance, Haycarb and Nations Trust Bank whilst retail interest was noted in Softlogic Finance, LOLC Finance and Browns Investments. Furthermore, foreigners closed as net buyers.

During the month, the ASPI and the S&P SL20 lost 5.1% and 4.3% respectively, whilst recording an average daily turnover of Rs. 1.85 Bn.

Capital Goods sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Hayleys and Access Engineering) whilst the sector index gained 0.46%. The share price of Hayleys decreased by Rs 1.00 (0.44%) to close at Rs 224.00. The share price of Access Engineering recorded a gain of Rs 0.20 (0.26%) to close at Rs 75.90.

Retailing sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to United Motors Lanka) whilst the sector index decreased by 0.24%. The share price of United Motors Lanka lost Rs 0.20 (0.74%) to close at Rs 26.80.

Dialog Axiata and Commercial Credit & Finance were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Dialog Axiata closed flat at Rs 44.20. The share price of Commercial Credit & Finance appreciated by Rs 2.50 (2.27%) to close at Rs 112.50.
