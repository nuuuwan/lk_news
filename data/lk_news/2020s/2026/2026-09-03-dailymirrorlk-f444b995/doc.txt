Govt. focuses on unlocking US$ 3bn gem potential: Minister

Unlocking the estimated US$3 billion potential of Sri Lanka’s gem and jewellery sector is a key priority of the government, with plans underway to transform Ratnapura into a major international hub for the industry, Deputy Minister of Industry Chathuranga Abeysinghe said.

He said the government aims to strengthen the entire gem and jewellery value chain, from mining and value addition to manufacturing and exports, while enhancing Sri Lanka’s position in the global market.

Abeysinghe made these remarks while addressing the closing ceremony of the Gem City Ratnapura International Gem and Jewellery Exhibition 2026, held from August 31 to September 2 at the Silver Ray Hotel in Ratnapura.

He said the significant increase in participation compared to the previous year reflected a renewed revival in Sri Lanka’s gem and jewellery industry.

The Deputy Minister said steps were being taken to strengthen traceability and build greater international confidence in Sri Lankan gems and jewellery, while simplifying import and export procedures and providing the necessary regulatory framework and facilities.

He also highlighted the importance of developing skills among the younger generation and expanding research and knowledge within the industry.

International entrepreneurs from India, Pakistan, Thailand, the United States, China, Japan and Poland, among other countries participated in this year’s exhibition.

The event also provided local entrepreneurs with an opportunity to showcase their products and establish links with international markets.
