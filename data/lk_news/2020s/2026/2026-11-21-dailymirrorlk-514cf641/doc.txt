Four Deduru Oya reservoir gates opened due to heavy rains

Four sluice gates of the Deduru Oya reservoir have been opened by one foot each, releasing 2,800 cubic feet of water per second into the Deduru Oya due to prevailing rainy weather conditions.

Director of Irrigation (Water Management) said the gates had been opened to control the water inflow into the reservoir amid continued rainfall.

People living downstream, particularly along the Deduru Oya, have been urged to remain vigilant and follow announcements issued by the Irrigation Department and Disaster Management authorities.

The public has also been warned to closely monitor changes in river water levels, as the volume of water entering the reservoir and the number of gates opened could change depending on rainfall conditions.
