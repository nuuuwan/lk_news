Sri Lankan, Indian Foreign Ministers hold talks in Delhi

Colombo, Nov. 27 (Daily Mirror) - Foreign Minister Vijitha Herath met Indian External Affairs Minister Dr. S. Jaishankar in New Delhi yesterday, ahead of the 25th Indian Ocean Rim Association (IORA) Council of Ministers Meeting scheduled for today (November 27).

The discussions focused on Sri Lanka-India bilateral relations, the regional situation and matters related to the proceedings of the IORA Council of Ministers Meeting.

Minister Herath arrived in New Delhi yesterday to participate in the ministerial meeting.
