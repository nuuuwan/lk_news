CSE opens trading floor to public as investor forum showcases 360% market growth

The Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE), in collaboration with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Sri Lanka (SEC), is launching a series of country-wide investor forums aimed at demystifying the capital market and raising financial literacy among the public.

The inaugural forum will be held on the CSE trading floor at the World Trade Centre in Colombo on 25th June 2026, running from 9.00 am to 5.00 pm. In a significant move to enhance accessibility, the Exchange is opening its trading floor to prospective investors, with representatives from stockbrokering firms and unit trust associations on hand to facilitate account opening. The first 200 participants to open accounts will be eligible for a raffle draw offering an investment coupon.

The forum comes against the backdrop of a remarkable six-year performance by the capital market. The All-Share Price Index (ASPI) has surged from 4,846 points in May 2020 to 22,310.80 points by May 2026, representing a staggering 360% growth with a compound annual growth rate of approximately 28.98% with capital gains remaining entirely tax-free.

Free of charge to participants, the forum will feature an interactive panel discussion with members of the SEC, CSE, Central Bank of Sri Lanka, leading stockbroker firms, and unit trust associations. Sessions will provide comprehensive insights into Sri Lanka’s capital market, macro-economic outlook, sectoral performance, and market outlook, alongside a detailed overview of unit trusts.
