Rienzie Arseculeratne PC to come forward for BASL Presidency - 2027/28

Colombo, Nov 08 (Daily Mirror) - President’s Counsel Rienzie Arseculeratne, the present Deputy President of the Bar Association of Sri Lanka (BASL), will come forward for the office of President of the Association for the year 2027/28. He has consented to do so at the request of a large number of senior and junior members of the Bar.

The term of the incumbent President, Attorney-at-Law Rajeev Amarasuriya, who was re-elected uncontested for 2026/27, concludes in 2027.

Members who urged Arseculeratne to contest point to a career that spans both sides of the courtroom: nearly three decades as a prosecutor and senior Law Officer of the State, followed by over two decades at the unofficial Bar. They say that breadth of experience, together with his record of leadership in the association and as one who has upheld a high standard as a legal practitioner, equips him to credibly lead a united and independent Bar, at a time when questions of the Rule of Law, the independence of the judiciary and proposed legislation affecting fundamental rights are before the country.

Arseculeratne commenced his career in the Attorney General’s Department, where he rose to the rank of Additional Solicitor General and the head of the criminal branch. He was appointed President’s Counsel in 1998. During this period, he also served as Director General of the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption. Upon his retirement in 2003, he has been in active practice in criminal law and public law. From 2015 to 2018 he was Chairman of Hatton National Bank PLC.

As Deputy President of the Bar Association of Sri Lanka since 2025 he has been closely involved in the Association’s public positions during the current term. He would bring to the Presidency both the wealth of his long experience and a commitment to the welfare of junior members of the profession.
