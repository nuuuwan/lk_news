Rupee strengthens further as dollar selling rates decline

Colombo, May 26 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lankan Rupee appreciated further against the US Dollar today (26), according to the latest exchange rates released by the Central Bank of Sri Lanka (CBSL).

CBSL reported the buying rate of the US Dollar at Rs. 318 and the selling rate at Rs. 328.

Commercial banks also recorded a decline in dollar selling rates, with some banks offering the currency at nearly Rs. 326.
