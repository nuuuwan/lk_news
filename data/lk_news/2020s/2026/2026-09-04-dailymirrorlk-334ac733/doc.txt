IMF team to visit Sri Lanka for key economic review in September

Colombo, Sep. 4 (Daily Mirror) - An International Monetary Fund (IMF) staff team is scheduled to visit Sri Lanka from September 10 to 23, 2026, for discussions with government authorities as the country prepares for another key assessment of its ongoing economic reform program.

The visit was announced by Evan Papageorgiou, IMF Mission Chief for Sri Lanka. He said the discussions will focus on the combined Seventh Review of Sri Lanka’s reform program supported under the IMF’s Extended Fund Facility (EFF) arrangement and the 2026 Article IV Consultation.

Papageorgiou said the IMF team will communicate its findings at the conclusion of the mission.

The upcoming discussions are expected to assess Sri Lanka’s progress in implementing economic reforms and examine recent developments in the country’s macroeconomic situation, as authorities continue efforts to maintain economic stability and strengthen the recovery.

The September mission follows an earlier IMF visit led by Papageorgiou, which took place in Colombo from June 24 to 30, 2026. During that mission, IMF officials reviewed recent macroeconomic developments and held discussions with Sri Lankan authorities on progress under the EFF-supported reform program.
