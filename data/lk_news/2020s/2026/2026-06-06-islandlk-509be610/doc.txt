XPeng G6 launched in Lanka, marking push into premium EV segment

Evolution Auto Private Limited, the authorised distributor for XPeng in Sri Lanka, has launched the all-new XPeng G6 electric SUV, signalling a new phase in the country’s premium electric mobility market.

The vehicle was unveiled at Evolution Auto’s multi-brand showroom in Colombo 05 on May 22, 2026, at an event attended by media representatives, industry stakeholders and invited guests. The programme featured a formal reveal, live demonstrations and test drives.

Officials said the introduction of the G6 comes at a pivotal moment for Sri Lanka’s electric vehicle sector, which continues to face challenges such as range anxiety, limited premium offerings and aftersales concerns.

Positioned in the mid-to-upper EV segment, the XPeng G6 offers a WLTP-certified driving range of up to 480 kilometres and is powered by an electric motor producing 110 kW and 440 Nm of torque.

A key feature of the model is XPILOT, XPeng’s Level 2 advanced driver assistance system, which includes adaptive cruise control, lane centering and automated parking functions.

The SUV also features a 15.6-inch infotainment display, an 18-speaker premium audio system, ventilated and massaging seats, and noise-reduction technologies aimed at enhancing cabin comfort.

Speaking at the launch, Evolution Auto Head of Marketing Ashan Wickramasinghe said the company was introducing “a new standard in technology and ownership experience” for Sri Lankan consumers.

XPeng, a China-based technology company listed on the New York Stock Exchange, has global partnerships including with Volkswagen Group and is expanding into AI, robotics and advanced mobility systems.

Evolution Auto said it will also strengthen aftersales support and service infrastructure as part of the brand’s entry into the local market.
