MoU to formalise Public-Private Partnership on e-waste management

Colombo, August 1 (Daily Mirror) - The Environment Ministry has announced that the Central Environmental Authority (CEA) and Dialog Axiata PLC will sign a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on Monday, August 3, to formalise a partnership for a nationwide electronic waste (e-waste) management programme.

The signing ceremony will be held at the Central Environmental Authority Head Office.

The initiative is expected to establish an island-wide e-waste collection network, providing the public with a structured and environmentally responsible mechanism to dispose of discarded electrical and electronic equipment.

Officials said the programme is intended to encourage responsible e-waste disposal, reduce environmental pollution caused by improper dumping, and improve public awareness of sustainable waste management practices.

The partnership forms part of the government's broader efforts to implement national environmental policies through collaboration between the public and private sectors while promoting greater public participation in environmental conservation.
