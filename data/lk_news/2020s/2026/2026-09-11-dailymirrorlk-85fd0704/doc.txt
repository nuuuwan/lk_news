University recruitment process to be made more efficient: PM

Colombo, Sep. 11 (Daily Mirror) - The recruitment process for academic and non-academic staff at state universities should be made more efficient, Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya instructed officials.

The Prime Minister made the remarks when the Subcommittee on the Higher Education Sector met on September 10 at the Parliament premises.

Officials of the University Grants Commission (UGC) presented a report on the progress of recruiting academic and non-academic staff to universities during 2025 and 2026.

The Prime Minister inquired into the progress of the ongoing recruitment process at each university and instructed officials to adopt a more efficient mechanism to expedite recruitments.

Attention was also drawn to conducting a second round of recruitment at universities that have made significant progress in their current recruitment programmes.

Deputy Minister of Education and Higher Education Dr. Madura Seneviratne, Secretary to the Ministry Nalaka Kaluwewa, UGC Chairman Senior Professor Kapila Seneviratne and several university vice-chancellors participated in the meeting.
