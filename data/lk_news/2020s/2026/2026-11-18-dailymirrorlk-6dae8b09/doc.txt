Sugeeshwara Bandara granted bail

Colombo, Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Sugeeshwara Bandara, the former Private Secretary to ex-President Gotabaya Rajapaksa, has been granted bail by the Colombo Fort Magistrate’s Court.

The court had earlier scheduled November 18 to consider his bail application following allegations that he had received salaries simultaneously for two positions under the Presidential Expenditure Head.
