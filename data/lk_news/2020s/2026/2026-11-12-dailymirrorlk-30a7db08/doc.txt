Rs. 2,500 monthly allowance for pensioners retiring between 2020 and 2024

Colombo, Nov. 12 (Daily Mirror) - Pensioners who retired between 2020 and 2024 will receive a monthly interim allowance of Rs. 2,500 from July 1, 2027, until the proposed pension revision under the 2025 basic salary structure is implemented, President Anura Kumara Dissanayake announced in his 2027 Budget.

The Government is implementing a phased pension revision programme to address disparities in pensions received by retired public servants who retired at different times.

Under the first Budget presented in 2025, the Government introduced a three-phase programme to convert the pensions of public servants who retired before 2020 to the 2020 basic salary scale. The first two phases have already been completed, while the final phase is scheduled for 2027.

The Government also plans to gradually address disparities between pensioners who retired before 2025 and those who retired in or after 2025 following the introduction of the new basic salary structure in 2025.

The next pension revision, based on the 2025 basic salary structure, is expected to begin with the 2028 Budget and will be implemented in several phases.

Once the revised pensions are implemented and payments commence, the proposed monthly interim allowance of Rs. 2,500 for pensioners who retired between 2020 and 2024 will be discontinued.

These measures will also apply to public sector pensioners whose salaries were determined under Department of Management Services Circulars 02/2025, 03/2025, 04/2025 and 05/2025.

The Government said the measures aim to gradually reduce pension disparities while ensuring a fair and financially sustainable pension system.
