Save Kavantissa the tusker!

Photos of tusker Kavantissa in water are now viral on social media. Even though authorities treated its wound,  that won’t be the end of this issue. It shows that this iconic tusker has now become a target of individuals who either perceive it as a threat or wildlife smugglers aiming to get hold of its tusks. Whatever the reason it may be, the gunshot wound is an alarm call for authorities to monitor this magnificent tusker.

A special team has already been deployed to monitor changes in the elephant’s movements, including at night.

The Minister has instructed officials to promptly provide the necessary medical facilities, equipment and other resources, with future treatment decisions to be based on the elephant’s changing health condition and behavioural patterns.

While these decisions are welcome,  it is important to note that Kavantissa doesn’t deserve to face a similar plight like Agbo or Barana. Sri Lanka has already lost many of her tuskers and this paradise isle cannot afford to lose anymore!
