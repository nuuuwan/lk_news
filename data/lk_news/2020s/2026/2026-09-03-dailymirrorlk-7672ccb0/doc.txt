Sumanthiran’s post on natural justice sparks social media interest

Colombo, September 03 (Daily Mirror) - Former TNA Parliamentarian and President’s Counsel M.A. Sumanthiran has drawn public attention on social media after posting two Latin legal maxims relating to the fundamental principles of natural justice: that no one should be a judge in their own case and that the other side should also be heard.

Sumanthiran, in a post uploaded today on his official Facebook page, referred to the maxims Nemo judex in causa sua and Audi alteram partem.

Nemo judex in causa sua, which translates as “no one should be a judge in their own case”, embodies the rule against bias. Audi alteram partem, meaning “let the other side be heard as well”, reflects the right to a fair hearing.

The two maxims are widely recognised as fundamental principles of natural justice, requiring decision-makers to remain impartial and ensuring that parties to proceedings are given a fair opportunity to present their case.

The post has been widely shared by lawyers on social media, with its publication coinciding with the Supreme Court hearings on Special Determination petitions challenging the constitutionality of the proposed Twenty-Second Amendment Bill.

The post also comes in the wake of the decision by the Bar Association of Sri Lanka (BASL) not to tender its post-hearing written submissions in relation to its Special Determination petitions.

The BASL has cited, among other circumstances, the Supreme Court’s decision to refuse petitioners an oral right of reply following the oral submissions made by the Attorney General.
