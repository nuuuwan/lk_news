200 Pakistani pigeons seized at Kalpitiya sea

Colombo, Sep. 7 (Daily Mirror) - A consignment of Pakistani pigeons smuggled through India was seized and four suspects were arrested by the STF during a raid off Kalpitiya jetty on Saturday evening (05)

The suspects were from Mawanella and Kalpitiya areas who had smuggled the consignment of pigeons by sea. The racketeers had transferred the stock of pigeons from Indian trawlers into their boats in the Sri Lankan sea belt.

Police said the stock contained 200 pigeons in plastic cages. The suspects under interrogation revealed that the 200 Pakistani pigeons were valued more than Rs. 10 million.

During the raid, the STF had seized the boat along with the engine, and a van waiting near the jetty to transport the stock of pigeons.

A police team under OIC STF Special Raids Unit SI S. S. Fernando had carried out the raid on the instructions of SSP Puttalam Division Kapila Premadasa.
