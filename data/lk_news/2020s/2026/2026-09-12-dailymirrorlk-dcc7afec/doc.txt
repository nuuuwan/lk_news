OTS 1 Intake celebrates 46 years of friendship, camaraderie

The OTS 1 Intake celebrated its 46th anniversary at the Pigeon Island Beach Hotel, Nilaveli recently.

The intake, which over the years produced close to ten Generals, most of whom played key roles in operational areas, marked the special occasion with around 23 officers participating in a weekend together. The celebrations included a beach barbecue, music by the Navy band, and a friendly cricket match.

A special feature of the event was the inauguration of the OTS 1 Intake Song, which added a nostalgic and memorable touch to the celebrations. The pictures depict Major Tissa Aluwihare initiating the Intake Song together with his batchmates, as well as the group photograph taken during the anniversary gathering.

The event was a fitting celebration of 46 years of friendship, camaraderie and shared memories.
