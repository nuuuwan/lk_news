Lightning alert for Eastern areas

Colombo, Oct. 28 (Daily Mirror) - The Meteorology Department has warned of thundershowers accompanied by severe lightning in several areas of Monaragala, Ampara and Batticaloa districts.

Temporary localised strong winds may also occur during thundershowers, the department said, urging the public to take adequate precautions to minimise damage caused by lightning.

The public has been advised to seek shelter indoors and avoid standing under trees during thunderstorms. People should also stay away from open areas such as paddy fields, tea plantations and water bodies.

The department further warned against using wired telephones and electrical appliances connected to power outlets during thunderstorms. The public has also been advised to avoid open vehicles, including bicycles, tractors and boats.

People are urged to remain alert to fallen trees and power lines and contact local disaster management authorities for emergency assistance.
