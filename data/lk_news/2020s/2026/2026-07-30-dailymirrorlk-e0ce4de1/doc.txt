Dengue cases surpass 84,000

Colombo, July 30 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka has recorded 84,091 dengue cases so far this year, with the dengue-related death toll reaching 61, according to the National Dengue Control Unit (NDCU).

The NDCU said the highest number of dengue cases has been recorded from the Gampaha, Colombo, Kandy, Matara, Kalutara, and Galle districts.

In July alone, 28,717 dengue cases have been reported to date, highlighting a significant rise in infections.

Meanwhile, the NDCU said 175 high-risk dengue zones remain across the country.

Authorities have urged the public to keep their homes and surroundings clean to prevent mosquito breeding.

The NDCU also advised those experiencing fever to avoid taking medication other than paracetamol and to seek immediate medical attention if symptoms persist or worsen.
