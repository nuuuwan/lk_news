Print Expo 2026

The Sri Lanka Association of Printers (SLAP) today announced that Print Expo 2026, regarded as the country’s largest trade exhibition for the printing, packaging, labelling, signage and graphic communications industries, will be held from June 19 to 21 at the Bandaranaike Memorial International Conference Hall (BMICH) in Colombo. Secretary to the Ministry of Industries and Enterprise Development, Ms. Thilaka Jayasundara, attended the event as the Chief Guest. Pix by Kithsiri de Mel
