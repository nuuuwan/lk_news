Sri Lankan conglomerates leading the charge in Port City’s economic surge

In April 2026, the Cabinet of Sri Lanka approved 77 Businesses of Strategic Importance (BSIs) under the Colombo Port City Economic Commission Act, marking a major milestone for the Colombo Port City Special Economic Zone (SEZ) as a rising regional hub for investment and innovation.

Among the approved entities, three Primary BSIs are leading significant real estate developments: Prime Melwa Port City and Marina Hotel Holdings are developing a marina-front residential and marina project, while Home Lands Port City is building a mixed-use complex adjacent to Central Park. These developments represent combined land and construction costs of approximately Rs. 125 billion, including around US$262 million in foreign inflows over the next five years.

Additionally, the approved Secondary BSIs are projected to generate roughly US$364 million in foreign exchange inflows and create over 10,000 direct jobs within the same period.

The BSIs span diverse sectors; maritime, IT, digital economy, regional headquarters, tourism, and real estate and qualify for targeted fiscal and non-fiscal incentives. The Colombo Port City Economic Commission (CPCEC) operates a Single Window Investment Facilitation framework for efficient approvals.
