Cake Date brings people together at The Shoppes at City of Dreams Sri Lanka

The Shoppes at City of Dreams Sri Lanka continues to shape itself as a vibrant meeting place for Colombo, bringing together retail, dining, lifestyle and community experiences under one destination.

In its latest community-led activation, The Shoppes will host the upcoming edition of Cake Date, a social gathering that celebrates connection through the simple joy of cake.

Taking place on June 27, at The Shoppes at City of Dreams, Cake Date will bring its much-loved format into a new setting, creating a welcoming space for people to gather, share and connect.  Centred around the act of bringing and enjoying cake together, the experience is designed to turn a simple shared moment into something memorable.

Inspired by the global cake picnic movement, Cake Date has grown in Sri Lanka as a warm social concept that brings people together through creativity, conversation and a shared love for cake. This edition will be especially significant as it takes place within South Asia’s first and largest fully integrated lifestyle destination, City of Dreams Sri Lanka, opening up the experience to a fresh environment where community, lifestyle and discovery naturally come together.

The Shoppes will serve as the setting for the gathering, with retailers at The Shoppes also joining the experience to add their own touch to the day. Their participation will help create a more engaging and layered event, giving the visitors more to explore while adding to the overall atmosphere of the activation.

Cake Date forms part of The Shoppes’ growing calendar of community-driven experiences, designed to bring people together through shared interests and meaningful moments. Following the recent Mat to Matcha 2.0 session in collaboration with Soul Theory, The Shoppes continues to introduce experiences that move beyond the traditional idea of a retail destination and create new reasons for people to connect.

Through activations such as Cake Date, The Shoppes at City of Dreams continues to build its identity as a place where communities can gather, ideas can be shared and memorable experiences can take shape. With more activities planned in the months ahead, including Lanka Collector Con in July, The Shoppes is set to further strengthen its role as a destination where Colombo comes together.
