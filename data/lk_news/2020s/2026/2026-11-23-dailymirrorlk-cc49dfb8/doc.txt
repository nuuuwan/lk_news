India-funded KKS Harbour, Jaffna airport projects stalled, claims Indian MP

Colombo, Nov. 23 (Daily Mirror) - Indian MP Dr. Thol. Thirumavalavan has accused the Sri Lankan Government of failing to make progress on the development of Kankesanthurai (KKS) Harbour and Jaffna International Airport, despite India’s willingness to fund the projects.

The leader of the Viduthalai Chiruthaigal Katchi (VCK) made the allegation at a media briefing held at the Jaffna Press Club on Sunday (22), claiming that the Government was reluctant to develop the two facilities as it could boost economic activity in the Northern and Eastern Provinces.

He alleged that Sri Lanka’s development approach was centred on the interests of the South, with commercial connectivity being channelled through Colombo rather than allowing the North and East to benefit from direct international links.

Thirumavalavan said the KKS Harbour and Jaffna International Airport could play a significant role in the economic development of the North and East, adding that India had offered assistance to take over and develop both facilities.

He also raised concerns over disruptions to flights between Jaffna and Chennai in recent days due to fog and air pollution, which had reduced visibility and prevented aircraft from landing.

The MP said he was shocked to learn that runway lights had not been installed at Jaffna International Airport, claiming that the lack of basic infrastructure was affecting connectivity and discouraging Indian businesspeople and potential investors from travelling directly to Jaffna.

He alleged that investors were instead compelled to enter Sri Lanka through Colombo, limiting opportunities for the Northern region.

Thirumavalavan said he would raise the matter with the Indian Government and lodge a complaint with India’s Minister of External Affairs over the alleged delays in developing the harbour and airport.
