Navy, Air Force join efforts to extinguish fire at Galle Fisheries Harbour

Galle, Nov 08 (Daily Mirror): The Sri Lanka Navy, together with the Air Force and local firefighting teams, has extinguished a fire that broke out at a boat maintenance yard at the Galle Fisheries Harbour this afternoon (08).

The fire was detected aboard a boat within the harbour premises by SLNS Dakshina, following which a firefighting team from the Southern Naval Command was immediately deployed to the scene.

Navy personnel, working alongside Sri Lanka Air Force firefighters, the Galle Municipal Firefighting Unit and local fishermen, managed to bring the blaze under control and prevent it from spreading to nearby boats and harbour infrastructure.
