Former Minister Jagath Pushpakumara’s wife passes away

Colombo, Aug. 19 (Daily Mirror) - The wife of former Cabinet Minister and Monaragala District MP Jagath Pushpakumara has passed away following an accident.

She reportedly fell from the second floor of a newly constructed building belonging to the family.

She was admitted to Karapitiya Teaching Hospital for treatment following the incident but passed away this morning (19).
