Sri Lanka’s Ransilu Jayathilake wins gold at World Powerlifting Championship

Colombo, Nov. 23 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lankan powerlifter Ransilu Jayathilake has won a gold medal in the 140kg weight category at the 2026 IPL Powerlifting World Championship held in Las Vegas, United States.

Representing Sri Lanka at the international championship, Jayathilake secured first place in every event he competed in, demonstrating an outstanding performance.
