Lanka Hospitals makes history with third consecutive Gold at TAGS Awards

Lanka Hospitals PLC has secured the Gold Award in the Health Sector at the TAGS Awards 2025, marking its third consecutive win and a historic achievement coinciding with the 60th “Diamond Chapter” anniversary of the awards organised by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Sri Lanka.

The awards, themed “Diamond Chapter: The Grand Honour of Excellence,” recognise excellence in Transparency, Accountability, Governance, and Sustainability. This year’s evaluation integrated advanced AI-driven criteria to assess the quality and credibility of annual reports.

“Three years. Three Golds. One unwavering standard. This is the result of a collective commitment to leadership and transparency,” said Group Managing Director Chaaminda Kumarasiri. “Excellence is never accidental; it is structured, measured, and delivered.”

Group Chief Financial Officer M. B. G Wickramasiri added that the win affirms the rigour applied to financial stewardship amid a complex economic environment.

The award was received at a ceremony held at Shangri-La, Colombo. Lanka Hospitals holds Joint Commission International accreditation and is the first medical institution in Sri Lanka to be reaccredited by JCI.
