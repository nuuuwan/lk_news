Road blocked, bus crew attacked by motorcycle gang

Colombo, Oct. 28 (Daily Mirror) - Kalutara North Police have launched an investigation to identify and arrest a group of youths who allegedly assaulted the driver and conductor of a luxury bus operating from Colombo to Kalutara.

Police said that a formal complaint has been lodged regarding the incident, which occurred on the afternoon of October 27.

According to reports, the incident followed an incident in Moratuwa, where a passenger attempting to board the bus was refused entry by the conductor. The bus subsequently continued its journey to Kalutara without the passenger.

After completing the trip and dropping off passengers in Kalutara, the driver was taking the bus to a residential location for parking when the vehicle was intercepted at Nagas Junction, within the Kalutara North Police Division.

CCTV footage from inside the bus reportedly shows six individuals arriving on three motorcycles blocking the road and physically assaulting the driver and conductor.

The injured conductor was admitted to Nagoda Teaching Hospital in Kalutara.
