Heavy rain pushes Castlereagh, Maussakelle to spill levels

Colombo, Aug. 10(Daily Mirror) -The Castlereagh and Maussakelle reservoirs have risen close to their spill levels following heavy rainfall in their respective catchment areas, engineers in charge of the reservoirs said today (10).

The water level of the Castlereagh Reservoir has risen to within 1.2 feet of its spill level, with 25 mm of rainfall recorded in its catchment area over the past 24 hours.

Meanwhile, the Maussakelle Reservoir is now 3.4 feet below its spill level after receiving 29 mm of rainfall during the same period.

As water levels continue to rise, power generation at the Canyon, Laxapana, New Laxapana, Polpitiya and Wimalasurendra power stations is being carried out at maximum capacity, engineers said.
