Lanka IOC, Sinopec hike Octane 95 petrol price by Rs20

ECONOMYNEXT – Private fuel operators in Sri Lanka, Lanka IOC and Sinopec, have raised the retail price of a liter of Octane 95 petrol by 20 rupees, moving from 475 rupees to 495 rupees.

The prices of other fuel varieties, Octane 92 petrol, Auto Diesel, and Super Diesel, remain unchanged currently.

The revised pricing was to be implemented from midnight on October 25.

State-owned Ceylon Petroleum is selling Octane 95 petrol at 475 rupees.

Fuel queues were seen at some petrol sheds, and consumers told reporters that petrol was not available at some sheds.

Private fuel operators in Sri Lanka have been restricting supply to the domestic market because they ware incurring losses under the government’s regulated pricing structure, the energy minister told parliament earlier this month.

“All those companies are stating that they cannot sell at the prevailing market price in Sri Lanka. However, we do not have the ability to increase prices proportionately to that. In light of this, they have restricted the fuel released to the market,” Anura Karunatilaka told parliament. (Colombo/Oct26/2026)
