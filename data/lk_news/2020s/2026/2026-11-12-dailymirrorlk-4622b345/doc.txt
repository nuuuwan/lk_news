Dutch Embassy warns of fake job scams targeting Sri Lankans

Colombo, Nov. 12 (Daily Mirror) - The Embassy of the Kingdom of the Netherlands has warned the public about fraudulent employment offers promising jobs in the Netherlands, saying several people have already fallen victim to such scams.

In a media statement, the Embassy said certain individuals were misleading job seekers with fake employment opportunities and circulating forged letters purportedly issued by the Dutch Immigration Services.

The Embassy clarified that the Netherlands does not generally issue work visas for most types of employment, adding that genuine opportunities may exist only in exceptional fields, such as highly skilled high-tech jobs.

It also stressed that the Embassy does not facilitate recruitment for employment in the Netherlands.

The public has been advised to verify job offers directly with prospective employers before making any financial commitments.

Those who have fallen victim to such scams have also been urged to seek immediate legal assistance from Sri Lankan law enforcement authorities.
