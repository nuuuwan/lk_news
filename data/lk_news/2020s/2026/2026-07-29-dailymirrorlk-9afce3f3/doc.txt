BASL to hold special General Meeting on proposed judicial retirement age increase

Colombo, July 29 (Daily MIrror) - The Bar Association of Sri Lanka (BASL) will convene a Special General Meeting in Colombo today (29) to discuss the government's proposed move to increase the retirement age of judges.

BASL President, Attorney-at-Law Rajeev Amarasuriya, said the meeting, scheduled to begin at 11:00 a.m. at the BASL premises, will focus on determining the association's response to the proposal.

Amarasuriya said that the matter is not related to any individual judge but concerns the independence of the judiciary, constitutional principles, and public confidence in the justice system. He said that any constitutional amendment of this nature should only be introduced following broad public and stakeholder consultation, with full transparency and due process.

He also called on all members of the Bar Association of Sri Lanka to attend the meeting and take part in discussions on what he described as an important national issue.
