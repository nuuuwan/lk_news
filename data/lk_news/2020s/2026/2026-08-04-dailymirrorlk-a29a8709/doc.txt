Steps initiated to develop Delft Island as sustainable tourism hub

Coolombo, August 4 (Daily Mirror) - The Government has initiated steps to develop Delft Island in the Northern Province as a sustainable tourism destination, with the aim of attracting more visitors by promoting underutilised tourist locations.

Deputy Minister of Tourism Prof. Ruwan Ranasinghe recently participated in a special inspection tour of Delft Island and discussions with government officials, with Jaffna District as the base, as part of a programme to accelerate tourism development in the Northern Province.

The initiative focuses on transforming Delft Island, known for its Dutch-era historical monuments and population of wild horses, into a sustainable tourism hub. During the visit, officials assessed existing requirements and identified areas needing development.

Plans are underway to secure financial allocations through the upcoming Budget and the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority (SLTDA) to improve infrastructure facilities, including drinking water supplies, road networks, transport services and tourism-related skills among local communities.

The inspection tour and discussions were attended by Jaffna District MPs Ilankumaran Karunanathan, Dr. Sri Bhavanadaraja Shanmuganathan and Rajeevan Jeyachandramurthy, officials from the SLTDA and Sri Lanka Tourism Promotion Bureau, the Delft Divisional Secretary, regional authorities, government officials, residents, and representatives from the Sri Lanka Navy and Sri Lanka Police.

Following the visit, a discussion was held at the Jaffna District Secretariat involving the District Secretary, state officials, security forces and police representatives to review tourism development plans, future initiatives and progress in the Jaffna District.
