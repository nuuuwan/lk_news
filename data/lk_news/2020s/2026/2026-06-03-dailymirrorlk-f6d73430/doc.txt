Reports claiming suspension of Suwa Seriya ambulance service false

Colombo, June 3 (Daily Mirror) - Reports circulating on social media claiming that the Suwa Seriya ambulance service would cease operations from 8.00 p.m. today are false, informed sources confirmed.

The sources said that the emergency ambulance service will continue to operate normally across the island and that there are no plans to suspend services.

They further clarified that while certain concerns have been raised by a section of employees, these issues will not affect the functioning of the ambulance network or the provision of emergency medical services to the public.

The public has been urged not to be misled by unverified reports and to continue using the Suwa Seriya service as usual.
