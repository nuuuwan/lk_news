Election Commission invites applications for political party recognition in 2026

The Election Commission has invited applications from political parties seeking official recognition for 2026.

The Commission said applications will be accepted from 28 January to 28 February. Submissions must be made using the prescribed application form and supported by the required documentation, signed by the party Secretary.

Application forms are available through the Election Commission’s official website, district election offices, and the Office of the Election Secretary.

The Commission said recognised political parties will be required to specify their approved symbol in line with relevant Gazette notifications.

Applicants must submit supporting documents including the party constitution, a list of office bearers, details of female office bearers during the past four years, audited financial statements, the party’s current policy statement, and evidence of continuous political activity over the previous four years.

Completed applications must be submitted either by post or by hand to the Election Commission on or before 3 p.m. on 28 February. Submissions must be clearly marked ‘Application for Recognition of Political Party – 2026.’
