No final decision on exit from IMF programme: Nalinda

Colombo, Sep. 29 (Daily Mirror) - Amid claims that Sri Lanka will exit the current International Monetary Fund (IMF) programme in 2027, Cabinet Spokesman, Minister Nalinda Jayatissa said today that the Government has not taken a final decision on whether to continue or end the programme.

He said the decision would be made based on the global economic situation and Sri Lanka’s economic conditions at the time.

Responding to a question at the weekly Cabinet decision briefing, Jayatissa expressed confidence that Sri Lanka would receive the seventh and eighth tranches under the IMF’s Extended Fund Facility (EFF) programme.

He said Sri Lanka had already achieved several targets under the programme, while work was continuing to meet others.

“There are still several months available for us to achieve the remaining targets,” he said.

Meanwhile, an IMF staff team recently concluded a visit to Sri Lanka after discussions on recent economic developments, progress under the EFF programme and policy recommendations under the IMF’s 2026 Article IV consultation.

The IMF said discussions with the Sri Lankan authorities would continue with the aim of reaching a staff-level agreement in the near term, paving the way for the timely completion of the seventh review under the programme.
