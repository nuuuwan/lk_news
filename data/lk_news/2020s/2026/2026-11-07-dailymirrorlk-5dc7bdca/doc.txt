Rainforest Ecolodge - A new chapter at the edge of Sinharaja

Managed by Aitken Spence Hotels, The Rainforest Ecolodge is entering a new chapter, placing the Sinharaja Forest Reserve, its biodiversity, and the communities living alongside it at the heart of the guest experience. This renewed direction strengthens the property’s role as the hospitality gateway to one of Sri Lanka’s most significant natural landscapes, bringing together responsible hospitality, conservation, and the spirit of discovery.

Set within the Enselwatte Tea Estate and adjoining the Sinharaja Forest Reserve, the ecolodge occupies one of Sri Lanka’s most extraordinary natural settings and holds a LEED Platinum certification from the US Green Building Council, reflecting the principles of sustainability embedded in its design and construction. A UNESCO World Heritage Site and a Man and the Biosphere Reserve, the Sinharaja is Sri Lanka’s last viable area of primary tropical rainforest, supporting an exceptional concentration of endemic plants, birds, mammals, reptiles, amphibians, invertebrates, and aquatic life.

The relaunch positions The Rainforest Ecolodge as the hospitality gateway to this living ecosystem: a place for travellers who want to do more than simply stay near nature. It is designed for those who want to enter the forest with curiosity, understand what they encounter, immerse themselves in the rainforest, and leave with a stronger connection to the natural world and self.”

“The Rainforest Ecolodge gives us an opportunity to bring people closer to one of Sri Lanka’s most remarkable natural treasures, while ensuring that conservation and local communities remain an integral part of that experience. I strongly believe that’s how we create meaningful travel experiences. This is very much in keeping with how we, at Aitken Spence, see the future of tourism; thoughtful, responsible, and deeply connected to place,” commented Stasshani Jayawardena, Chairman/Chairperson – Aitken Spence Hotel Holdings PLC.

“We want the ecolodge to be more than just a place to stay; we want it to become a meaningful gateway to understanding one of Sri Lanka’s most vital and vibrant ecosystems,” said Susith Jayawickrama, Managing Director – Aitken Spence Hotel Holdings PLC.

The ecolodge’s approach has received recognition across sustainability and hospitality platforms. In 2026, The Rainforest Ecolodge was named Silver Winner in the ‘Nature Positive’ category at the ICRT Indian Subcontinent Awards, recognising its work in biodiversity conservation, responsible nature-based experiences, community benefit, and conservation education. It was also named Sri Lanka’s Leading Lodge 2026 at the World Travel Awards, reinforcing its position as one of the country’s most distinctive nature-led stays.
