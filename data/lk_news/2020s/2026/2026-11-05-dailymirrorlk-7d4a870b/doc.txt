CSA ‘Class of 2026’ graduation today

Today (November 5), graduates, families, educators, and distinguished guests will convene at the Bandaranaike Memorial International Conference Hall to celebrate a dual milestone: the graduation of the Class of 2026 and the 40th anniversary of The City School of Architecture (CSA).

Over the past 40 years, CSA has produced over 1,500 graduates. Behind this significant figure are countless individual journeys—years of dedicated study and creative exploration supported by mentors, families, and peers. These graduates represent the promise of architecture as a catalyst for thoughtful change, improving how people live, work, and congregate.

By bridging a gap in Sri Lanka’s higher education sector, CSA has consistently opened doors for deserving students to pursue careers that once seemed out of reach. As the school celebrates its 40th anniversary, this graduation ceremony honors not only the qualifications earned but also four decades of dedication to excellence. Congratulations to the Class of 2026 and to The City School of Architecture for 40 years of nurturing talent and shaping the future.

For four decades, CSA has been instrumental in shaping architects who define the future of Sri Lanka’s built environment. As the nation’s first private-sector higher education institute for architecture, CSA has created a vital pathway for aspiring young professionals, grounding their learning in Sri Lanka while connecting them to global standards.

CSA offers several programmes, including undergraduate and postgraduate level diplomas, as well as Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees in Architecture in partnership with the University of the West of England, Bristol. Its programmes hold prestigious validation from the Sri Lanka Institute of Architects (SLIA) and the Royal Institute of British Architects (RIBA). At its core, the school’s mission remains persistent: to nurture competent architects who are professionally qualified and deeply committed to social and environmental responsibility.
