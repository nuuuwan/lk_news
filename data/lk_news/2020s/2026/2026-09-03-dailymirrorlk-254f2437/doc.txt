Siyapatha Finance records exceptional financial performance for 1H2026

Siyapatha Finance PLC delivered an exceptional financial performance for the six months ended June 30, 2026, reflecting the company’s continued strategic growth initiatives, resilient asset quality and unwavering commitment to sustainable value creation.

The company recorded a profit after tax of Rs.1,007 million, a robust 43 percent increase from Rs.706 million in the corresponding period of 2025, while profit before taxes grew 38 percent to Rs.2,334 million, from Rs.1,689 million, demonstrating sustained market and customer confidence in the company’s core operations.

“Our performance in the first half of 2026 is a clear reflection of Siyapatha Finance’s strategic foresight and our unwavering commitment to sustainable growth,” said Siyapatha Finance CEO Mathisha Hewawitharana.

“Surpassing the Rs.104 billion mark in total assets while significantly improving our asset quality underscores the strength of our core operations and the deep trust our customers place in us.”

The company’s core business operations continued to yield strong returns, with total interest income growing to Rs.7,719 million, from Rs.5,272 million a year earlier, driving the net interest income up to Rs.3,487 million, from Rs.2,629 million. Other income strengthened to Rs.1,054 million, from Rs.826 million. The cost-to-income ratio improved to 49 percent, from 54 percent.

The gross stage three loans ratio improved to 4 percent, from 8 percent a year earlier, while the net stage three loans ratio declined to 2 percent, from 3 percent.

The company’s balance sheet witnessed robust expansion, with total assets crossing the landmark Rs.100 billion mark to reach Rs.104 billion as at June 30, 2026, up from Rs.74 billion in the previous year. The loan book grew by 44 percent to Rs.99 billion.

Customer deposits, a key indicator of public trust in the company, grew to Rs.42 billion, from Rs.34 billion, while bank borrowings rose to Rs.28 billion, from Rs.12 billion.

Siyapatha Finance continued to maintain a strong capital and liquidity position, well above the regulatory thresholds mandated by the Central Bank of Sri Lanka, with a Tier 1 capital adequacy ratio of 11.88 percent, against a required minimum of 8.50 percent and a total capital adequacy ratio of 16.93 percent against a required 12.50 percent.

The company’s available liquid assets to required liquid assets ratio stood at a healthy 148.05 percent, well above the regulatory minimum of 100 percent. The net asset value per share appreciated to Rs.109.63, from Rs.91.07 a year earlier.

Looking ahead, Siyapatha Finance aims to leverage its newly achieved milestone of surpassing Rs.100 billion in total assets as a powerful launchpad for the second half of 2026.
