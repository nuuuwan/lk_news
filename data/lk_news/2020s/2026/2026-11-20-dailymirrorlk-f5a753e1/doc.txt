Appeal Court sets 13-year jail term for Hindu Poosari over rape of war-displaced student

Colombo, Nov. 21 (Daily Mirror) - The Court of Appeal yesterday dismissed the appeal and affirmed the conviction of a Hindu temple priest (Poosari) who was found guilty of raping a 17-year-old war-displaced student staying at his residence in Mullaitivu in 2011.

While upholding the conviction, the two-judge bench reduced his prison sentence from 17 years to 13 years of rigorous imprisonment.

The judgement was delivered by Justice Dr. Sumudu Premachandra, with Justice Amal Ranaraja, in an appeal filed by Nithiyananthan Mohan Raj against his conviction by the Vavuniya High Court.

According to the judgement, the victim, a 17-year-old internally displaced person (IDP) living at a displaced camp, moved into the residence of the accused in August 2011 to attend G.C.E. Advanced Level classes.

On or about November 11, 2011, the accused forcibly had sexual intercourse with the teenager. Following an attempted second assault the next day, the accused threatened her, warning that her life would be in danger if she told anyone.

The crime only came to light six months later when camp health workers noticed she was pregnant. A subsequent medical examination confirmed her six-month pregnancy, prompting a formal police complaint and the arrest of the accused. DNA testing later established that the accused was the biological father of the child. During her pregnancy, the victim was cared for at a convent, and the child was placed for adoption following birth.

The High Court acquitted the accused of the attempted rape charge due to insufficient evidence of a separate attempt, but convicted him of rape, sentencing him to 17 years of rigorous imprisonment, a Rs. 5,000 fine, and Rs. 300,000 in compensation to the victim.

"The accused, as a poosari, used his position and force to overpower her will," Justice Premachandra stated, finding no fault with the Vavuniya High Court's conclusion that the victim was a credible witness.

Although the Court affirmed the conviction, noting that the rape of a girl under 18 carries a mandatory minimum sentence of 10 years and up to 20 years under Section 364(2) of the Penal Code, the bench took into account that the appellant is a first-time offender and a father of five children.

Consequently, the Court reduced his sentence from 17 years to 13 years of rigorous imprisonment, effective from the date of his conviction, while leaving the Rs. 5,000 fine, the Rs. 300,000 victim compensation order, and default prison terms intact.
