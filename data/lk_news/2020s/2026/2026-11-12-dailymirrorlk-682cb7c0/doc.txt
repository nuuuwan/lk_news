Budget 2027 spending: where Sri Lanka’s money goes

The 2027 budget sets aside Rs. 2,000 billion for public investment in 2027, or 5.12% of GDP, including Cyclone Ditwah rebuilding. Highways, expressways and railways receive Rs. 251 billion; social protection, including Aswesuma, Rs. 230 billion, and the first phase of suburban rail electrification Rs. 80 billion.

The speech also announces 73 new spending proposals worth Rs. 363 billion, with transport taking the largest share. A further Rs. 260 billion is set aside separately to finish rebuilding after the cyclone.
