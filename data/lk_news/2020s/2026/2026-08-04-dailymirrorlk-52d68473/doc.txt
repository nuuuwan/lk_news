No opposition from Mahanayake Theras to judicial reforms: Nalinda

Colombo, August 4 (Daily Mirror) - The Mahanayake Theras did not oppose the reforms in the judicial system aiming to create efficiency and clear the backlog of cases, Cabinet Spokesman and Minister Nalinda Jayatissa said today.

He told the weekly briefing to announce Cabinet decisions that he and Justice Minister Harshana Nanayakkara met with the Mahanayake Theras of the three Chapters and briefed them on the proposed 22nd Constitutional Amendment.

The Minister said the concern raised by the Chief Prelates was whether the amendment is targeting a particular judge and added that the Mahanayake theras were informed that the retirement age of all judges from Magistrate courts to the Supreme Court are extended through the amendment.

Responding to a question, the Minister said he is unaware of a letter said to have been sent to the President by the Mahanayake theras regarding the matter.
