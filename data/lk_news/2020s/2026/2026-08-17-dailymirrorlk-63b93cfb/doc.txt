Sainthamaruthu Acting OIC arrested over Rs. 20,000 bribe

Colombo, Aug. 17 (Daily Mirror) - The Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) has arrested the Acting Officer-in-Charge (OIC) of the Sainthamaruthu Police Station and a police constable over an alleged attempt to solicit and accept a bribe of Rs. 20,000.

The arrests follow an incident involving a suspect who was taken into custody by Sainthamaruthu police on August 14 while allegedly possessing 70 milligrams of heroin.

According to information provided to investigators, the suspect was due to be produced before court on August 15. The Acting OIC, a Police Inspector, had allegedly offered to refrain from submitting facts through a formal B-report and instead proceed with the case solely through a charge sheet concerning the alleged possession of heroin.

The Police Inspector had allegedly demanded Rs. 20,000 from the suspect in exchange for the arrangement.

The suspect agreed to the demand and arranged to hand over the money today (17) after the court proceedings. However, the Acting OIC had reportedly left the station on official duties by the time the money was to be collected.

Acting on the Police Inspector’s instructions, a police constable allegedly went to collect the money. CIABOC officers subsequently arrested the constable while he was allegedly accepting the Rs. 20,000.

Following further inquiries and information obtained during the investigation, CIABOC officials also arrested the Acting OIC.

Both officers, who had recently been assigned to the Sainthamaruthu Police Station, were taken to the Kalmunai Police Station under the custody of CIABOC investigators.
