SANASA General Insurance introduces One + Two concept

With the aim of increasing public awareness about protection beyond motor insurance, SANASA General Insurance has now introduced an innovative new concept under the theme ‘One + Two |රක්ෂණ වාසි දැන්  Double වෙලා.

This unique insurance initiative has been specially designed for comprehensive motor insurance policyholders, offering added protection for both individuals and their families.

Under the One + Two scheme, the customers who obtain a comprehensive motor insurance policy will receive:

Home insurance coverage of up to Rs.500,000 against fire and lightning damages

These additional benefits are provided free of charge, subject to terms and conditions.

The purpose of this initiative is to remind policyholders that protection should extend beyond the vehicle they drive.

SANASA General Insurance believes that the One + Two insurance scheme is not merely an insurance package but a meaningful investment in the safety, stability and peace of mind of every family.
