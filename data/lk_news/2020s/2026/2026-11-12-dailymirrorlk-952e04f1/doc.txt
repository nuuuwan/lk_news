Budget 2027: President Anura Kumara Dissanayake’s Full Budget Speech (Download)

Nov 12 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake presented Sri Lanka’s Budget 2027 in Parliament, outlining the Government’s economic priorities, proposed tax reforms, public sector salary increases and development plans for the year ahead.

The Budget includes several key proposals, including an increase in the personal income tax-free threshold, the removal of VAT on school exercise books and printed books, higher allowances for public sector employees and additional financial relief for pensioners.

The President also outlined plans for investments in infrastructure, education, healthcare, agriculture, tourism, digital transformation and the promotion of new economic opportunities.

Budget 2027 is the third Budget presented by the National People’s Power (NPP) Government since assuming office.
