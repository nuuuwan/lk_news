Rs. 100 million allocated to upgrade Sigiriya Domestic Airport

Colombo, Nov. 12 (Daily Mirror) - The Government has proposed an allocation of Rs. 100 million to improve facilities at the Sigiriya Domestic Airport, including its passenger terminal, to promote tourism in the area.

Presenting the 2027 Budget in Parliament, President Anura Kumara Dissanayake said the proposed development was aimed at strengthening tourism centred around Sigiriya.

Meanwhile, the China Bay Airport will also undergo facility improvements in 2027, with the necessary investment to be made by Airport and Aviation Services (Sri Lanka) Limited (AASL).

The developments form part of the Government's plans to improve airport infrastructure and support tourism and regional connectivity.
