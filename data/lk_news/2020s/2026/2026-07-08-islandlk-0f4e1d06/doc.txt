Perituza conducts insightful AI workshop for Sri Lanka’s PR and Ad agencies

Perituza, a Texas-based AI and application development consultancy firm, recently hosted an exclusive executive breakfast session titled “Applying AI in Agency Workflows” for senior leaders from Sri Lanka’s public relations, communications, advertising, marketing and digital sectors.

The informative session formed part of Perituza’s ongoing efforts to introduce practical AI capabilities, workflow automation solutions and digital transformation opportunities to organizations across the region. The initiative was supported by Solutions Ground in its capacity as a strategic business advisory and stakeholder engagement partner, helping convene senior leaders from across Sri Lanka’s communications, marketing and agency sectors to facilitate dialogue around the opportunities and implications of AI for the industry.

As client expectations continue to evolve, agencies are increasingly being asked to provide services that extend beyond traditional communications, marketing and creative execution. Organizations today are looking for support in areas such as workflow automation, AI implementation, data intelligence, analytics, customer-facing digital experiences and business process optimization. Perituza believes this presents a significant opportunity for agencies to expand their service offerings, unlock new revenue streams and strengthen client relationships without necessarily building large technology teams internally.
