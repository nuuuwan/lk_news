Concord Apparel and Concord Footwear win Presidential Export Awards

Two subsidiaries of Hong Kong-based Guston Limited, Concord Apparel and Concord Footwear, have secured top honours at the Presidential Export Awards 2024/2025.

Concord Apparel in Avissawella was named Best Exporter in the Apparel Sector (Medium Scale), while Concord Footwear in Ulapane won the award for the Footwear & Leather Products Sector. The awards ceremony organised by the Export Development Board at the BMICH, was graced by President Anura Kumara Dissanaya.

The Managing Director of Guston Limited, Prasanna Jayasinghe, received the awards on behalf of both factories. Concord Apparel is recognised for its high-quality workwear for international brands, and Concord Footwear for its innovative safety footwear.

Both facilities employ advanced technology, automation, and uphold strong sustainability and ethical standards, holding certifications including SA 8000, ISO, and LEED Gold. The dual award highlights their role in strengthening Sri Lanka’s reputation as a leading global manufacturing destination.
