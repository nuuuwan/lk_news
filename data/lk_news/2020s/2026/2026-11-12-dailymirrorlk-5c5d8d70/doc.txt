SDF to support SME investment in EV charging infrastructure

Sarvodaya Development Finance PLC (SDF) is strengthening its commitment to sustainable development by supporting small and medium enterprises (SMEs) and entrepreneurs seeking to invest in electric vehicle (EV) charging infrastructure across Sri Lanka.

The initiative forms part of SDF’s broader focus on promoting green financing, sustainable entrepreneurship and the country’s transition towards cleaner mobility.

Through this initiative, SDF aims to facilitate the expansion of EV charging infrastructure by exploring suitable financing solutions for SMEs interested in establishing and operating charging stations.

The programme seeks to encourage entrepreneurship within the emerging EV ecosystem while creating new opportunities for businesses to contribute towards environmentally responsible economic development.

As a practical demonstration of this commitment, SDF is introducing an EV fast-charging facility at its Head Office in Colombo 08, with Vdrive, which is a fully owned subsidiary of Vidulanka PLC serving as the charging service provider. Located on Dr. Danister De Silva Mawatha, the facility will operate 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, improving charging accessibility and convenience for EV users.

The facility is supported by a 70 kW solar power system, which also contributes to the SDF Head Office’s daily energy requirements. This integration demonstrates how renewable energy can support commercial operations while encouraging environmentally sustainable transport solutions.

Commenting on the initiative, Nilantha Jayanetti, Chief Executive Officer of Sarvodaya Development Finance PLC said, “At SDF, we recognize that the transition towards a greener economy presents significant opportunities for SMEs and entrepreneurs. Our objective is to make sustainable investments more accessible by supporting businesses that wish to enter emerging sectors such as EV charging infrastructure. Through this initiative, we aim to encourage entrepreneurship, promote renewable energy adoption and create opportunities for communities across Sri Lanka to participate in the country’s transition towards environmentally responsible development.”

The program also aligns with SDF’s development financing approach, which prioritizes SME growth, regional economic development and financial inclusion. By extending its sustainability agenda beyond urban centers, SDF seeks to encourage environmentally responsible investments among rural entrepreneurs and businesses.

Through its continued focus on green financing, renewable energy and sustainable enterprise development, SDF aims to strengthen Sri Lanka’s EV infrastructure while empowering SMEs to participate in the country’s transition towards a more sustainable economy.
