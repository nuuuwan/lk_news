Tour guide killed in elephant attack

Colombo, October 26 (Daily Mirror)- A tour guide was trampled to death by a temple tusker at Kande Viharaya in Aluthgama this afternoon.

The victim, Piyashantha de Silva (35), a resident of Middaramulla in Ahungalla, had visited the temple with a group of tourists when the elephant attacked him as he approached it to take a selfie.

The seriously injured tour guide was rushed to the Balapitiya Base Hospital and admitted to the Intensive Care Unit, where he later succumbed to his injuries.
