Indices close on a mixed note

The ASPI edged up as a result of price gains in counters such as Seylan Bank, Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka and Cargills with the turnover crossing Rs. 3.5 Bn. Meanwhile, the S&P SL20 closed in red. Crossings were witnessed in On’ally Holdings, Nations Trust Bank and Dialog Axiata accounting for 73.4% of the turnover. Mixed interest was observed in Sampath Bank, Commercial Bank and Haycarb whilst retail interest was noted in Cargills Bank, Merchant Bank of Sri Lanka & Finance and UB Finance Company. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

Real Estate sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to On’ally Holdings) whilst the sector index gained 1.03%. The share price of On’ally Holdings increased by Rs 1.80 (3.75%) to close at Rs 49.80.

The Banking sector was the second highest contributor tothe market turnover (due to Nations Trust Bank and Sampath Bank) whilst the sector index increased by 0.36%.

The share price of Nations Trust Bank lost Rs 0.50 (0.16%) to close at Rs 304.50. The share price of Sampath Bank recorded a loss of Rs 0.25 (0.18%) to close at Rs 136.75.

Dialog Axiata and Haycarb were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Dialog Axiata moved down by Rs 0.10 (0.23%) to close at Rs 43.70. The share price of Haycarb declined by Rs 1.25 (0.73%) to close at Rs 170.25.
