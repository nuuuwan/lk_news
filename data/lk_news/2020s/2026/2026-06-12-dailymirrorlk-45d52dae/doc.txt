Hayleys Solar completes GPV Lanka’s 1.7MWp installation

Hayleys Solar has successfully completed the installation of a 1.7MWp solar power system for GPV Lanka (Pvt.) Ltd in Negombo, further supporting the country’s transition towards sustainable and energy-efficient industrial operations.

GPV Lanka, a leading company in the electronics manufacturing sector, is strengthening its efforts to improve energy efficiency and reduce its environmental footprint through renewable energy. The solar installation supports the site’s operational energy requirements while contributing to a more stable and cost-efficient energy supply.

GPV Lanka Managing Director Chandana Dissanayake commented, “The installation is part of our ongoing efforts to integrate more renewable energy into our operations.”

Hayleys Fentons Managing Director Hasith Prematillake stated, “At Hayleys Solar, we remain committed to empowering Sri Lankan industries with reliable, future-ready renewable energy solutions that create long-term value for both businesses and the environment.”

Hayleys Fentons Deputy Managing Director Energy Roshane Perera stated, “We are proud to support GPV Lanka in advancing its renewable energy journey while contributing to the wider adoption of sustainable energy solutions across Sri Lanka.”
