Rise in online indirect fraud reported: SLCERT

Colombo, July 11 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Computer Emergency Readiness Team (SLCERT) has reported an increase in indirect fraud incidents occurring in cyberspace.

Chief Information Security Engineer of SLCERT Charuka Damunupola said that more than 100 complaints related to such incidents have been received over the past month.

He urged the public to remain vigilant when engaging in online activities and to be cautious of suspicious messages, links and requests for personal information.
