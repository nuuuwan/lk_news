VIASL urges higher LTV over import surcharge

Colombo, Aug. 17 (Daily Mirror) - The Vehicle Importers Association of Sri Lanka (VIASL) has urged the Government to increase the existing Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio for vehicle purchases if it is unable to remove the temporary 50% surcharge imposed on Customs Import Duty on selected motor vehicles.

Addressing the media, VIASL Vice President Arosha Rodrigo said the Government should consider increasing the current 40% LTV ratio to 50% or 60% to make it easier for people to purchase vehicles through financing.

He said the Government has extended the temporary 50% surcharge on Customs Import Duty applicable to selected motor vehicle imports until December 31, 2026.

The extension came into effect on August 15 under an order issued by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake in his capacity as Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development.

Rodrigo said that if the Government was unable to remove the surcharge due to concerns over foreign exchange and the dollar outflow, it should at least take steps to increase the LTV ratio.

He said such a move would provide greater access to vehicle financing and ease the financial burden on consumers seeking to purchase vehicles.

The 50% surcharge was initially introduced on May 16 as a measure to control foreign exchange outflows arising from vehicle imports. The Ministry of Finance initially imposed the surcharge for a period of three months before extending it.

The Government has now extended the measure until December 31, 2026, as part of its efforts to manage foreign exchange expenditure related to vehicle imports.
