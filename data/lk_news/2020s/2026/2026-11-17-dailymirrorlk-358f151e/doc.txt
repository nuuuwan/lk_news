US$200 Mn loan for climate-resilient water supply

Colombo, Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet of Ministers has approved the signing of loan agreements with the Asian Development Bank (ADB) to obtain US$200 million for the implementation of the Climate Resilient Sustainable Water Supply and Sanitation Project.

Formal negotiations with the ADB to secure the loan under its Regular General Capital Resources were held on September 7, 2026, and have now been concluded.

Following the completion of negotiations, the Cabinet approved a proposal submitted by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development to sign the relevant loan agreements with the ADB.
