Nawaloka Hospitals pioneers in AI-driven healthcare, announced as Platinum Sponsor of Medicare 2026

Medicare 2026 Organising Committee  Chairperson and Asia Wellness Forum President Dr. Amal Harsha de Silva, Nawaloka Hospitals PLC  Director/General Manager  Prof. Lal Chandrasena and Nawaloka Hospitals PLC Senior Manager Operations Kanishka Warusavitarana  at the sponsor handing over occasion

Nawaloka Hospitals PLC, a pioneer in Sri Lanka’s private healthcare sector, is taking a bold step into the future by integrating Artificial Intelligence (AI) into healthcare. Demonstrating its commitment to advancing medical innovation, the hospital group will proudly serve as the Platinum Sponsor of the 15th edition of the Medicare – International Healthcare Exhibition 2026, where it will unveil its latest AI-driven technologies.

The three-day exhibition, scheduled from 6 to 8 March 2026 at the Sirimavo Bandaranaike Memorial Exposition Centre (BMICH), Colombo, is widely recognised as the nation’s premier platform for healthcare advancement, knowledge exchange, and industry collaboration.

With Nawaloka Hospital Holdings PLC’s unwavering dedication to healthcare excellence, its role as a lead partner strengthens Medicare 2026’s vision of shaping a healthier future for Sri Lanka. Visitors will gain unique insights into the practical applications of AI in clinical practice and its transformative impact on healthcare delivery.

Since its inception, Nawaloka Hospital has led the introduction of advanced medical technology in Sri Lanka — from pioneering the nation’s first CT scanner to now embracing AI-driven healthcare solutions. The hospital is developing AI-powered CT, MRI, OCD, and cardiac scanners for early, non-invasive disease detection, delivering faster and more accurate diagnostics.

Jayantha Dharmadasa, recognised as a Lifetime Achiever in Private Healthcare, has consistently demonstrated excellence in driving innovation within Sri Lanka’s private healthcare sector. Under his leadership, Nawaloka Hospitals has significantly contributed to the nation’s medical advancements, successfully performing over 25,000 bypass surgeries and preventing medical outflows.

Nawaloka Hospital is also advancing toward AI-integrated workflows and data-less hospital environments, improving operational efficiency, data security, and patient care in alignment with global smart healthcare trends. With this continued innovation, the hospital reaffirms its mission to provide world-class, future-ready healthcare for the people of Sri Lanka.

As the Platinum Sponsor of Medicare 2026, Nawaloka Hospitals PLC is poised to inspire healthcare professionals, industry leaders, and policymakers to accelerate digital transformation and innovation in healthcare — building a stronger and healthier tomorrow for Sri Lanka.
