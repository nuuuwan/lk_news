IRD urges taxpayers to file returns by Nov. 30

Colombo, Oct. 21 (Daily Mirror) - The Inland Revenue Department (IRD) has urged taxpayers to submit their income tax returns for the year of assessment 2025/2026 on or before November 30.

Taxpayers can obtain further information by calling the department’s hotline on 1944 or visiting its official website, www.ird.gov.lk.

The department has highlighted the importance of tax compliance under its message, “Taxes for a Better Future.”
