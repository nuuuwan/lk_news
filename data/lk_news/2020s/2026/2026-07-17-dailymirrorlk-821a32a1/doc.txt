Indices close in green

The ASPI closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Dialog Axiata, Central Finance Company and John Keells Holdings with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.4bn.

A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in E B Creasy & Company, ACL Cables and John Keells Holdings.

Mixed interest was observed in ACL Cables, Sampath Bank and Haycarb whilst retail interest was noted in Lanka Credit and Business Finance, Softlogic Capital and Ceylon Land & Equity.

Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers. Capital Goods sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to E B Creasy & Company and ACL Cables) whilst the sector index gained 0.23%. The share price of E B Creasy & Company increased by Rs. 0.40 (1.01%) to close at Rs. 40.00.

The share price of ACL Cables gained Rs. 0.70 (0.73%) to close at Rs. 96.70. The Banking sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Sampath Bank and Commercial Bank) whilst the sector index edged down by 0.01%. The share price of Sampath Bank closed flat at Rs. 137.00. The share price of Commercial Bank declined by Rs. 0.25 (0.12%) to close at Rs. 201.25. Haycarb was also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Haycarb closed flat at Rs. 175.75.
