South Asia Democracy Conference opens in Colombo

Academics, legal experts and civil society representatives from across South Asia, including Britta Petersen, Resident Representative / Regional Director for South Asia of the Rosa Luxemburg Stiftung, Viranjana Herath - Chairperson - Media Law Forum and Prof. Deepika Udagama -Chair Professor of Law and Head, Department of Law, University of Peradeniya, participate in the opening session of the South Asia Democracy Conference 2026 in Colombo on November 3. The three-day conference focuses on strengthening democracy, addressing regional challenges and exploring South Asia's democratic traditions and future.
