Public warned against fake GovPay traffic fine SMS scam

Colombo, Nov. 9 (Daily Mirror) - The Ministry of Digital Economy has warned the public against fraudulent SMS and iMessage notifications falsely claiming to be from the Sri Lanka Police Traffic Division and demanding payment of traffic fines through links to the GovPay platform.

The Ministry said the messages, which reportedly claim that recipients have been fined for speeding violations, are fake and part of an organised cyber scam.

It clarified that neither the Ministry nor any other government institution had issued such messages requesting traffic fine payments through links provided in SMS or iMessage notifications.

The Ministry said that the official GovPay digital payment system operates through government-approved and secure channels and does not request payments through suspicious or foreign website links, including the fraudulent domain 'lygov.art'.

Warning that the scam could be aimed at stealing personal information and banking details, the Ministry urged the public not to respond to such messages or click on the links provided.

The public was also advised to access government services only through official government websites and refrain from sharing personal or financial information with unverified third parties.
