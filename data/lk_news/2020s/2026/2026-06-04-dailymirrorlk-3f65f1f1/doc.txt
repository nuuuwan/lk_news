Principal service grade III recruitment exam on June 7

Colombo, June 4 (Daily Mirror) - The limited competitive examination for recruitment to Grade III of the Sri Lanka Principal Service for the year 2025 is scheduled to be held on the June 7.

The Examinations Department said that admission cards for the examination can be downloaded online through its official website.

The exam was originally set to take place on April 26 but was postponed indefinitely by the Ministry of Education due to unavoidable circumstances.
