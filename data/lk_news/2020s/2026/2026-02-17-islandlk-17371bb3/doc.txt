Lankem Ceylon and Axalta Coating Systems strengthen partnership

Lankem Ceylon PLC, the authorized partner of Axalta Coating Systems in Sri Lanka, has announced the expansion of its collaboration with Axalta to strengthen access to high-performance industrial coating technologies in the country. The development follows the successful introduction of Axalta’s globally recognized automotive coating products to the Sri Lankan market.

Under the expanded partnership, Lankem will expand the availability of advanced industrial liquid, marine, and powder coating solutions tailored to meet the protection needs of key sectors including infrastructure, energy, agriculture, heavy equipment, rail, and steel structures. The initiative aims to address the growing demand for durable coating systems that enhance operational life while reducing maintenance frequency and associated costs.

Axalta Coating Systems brings more than 150 years of coatings expertise and serves over 100,000 customers across 130 countries. Its high-performance coatings are engineered to prevent corrosion, enhance durability, and improve productivity across a wide range of industrial and infrastructure applications.

A key feature of these coating systems is their ability to be applied directly onto clean, firmly adherent rust. Through stabilising corrosion rather than merely covering it, the coatings reduce the need for extensive blast cleaning and surface preparation. This capability minimizes downtime and allows critical infrastructure and industrial facilities to remain operational during maintenance activities.

Sri Lanka’s climatic conditions, characterized by high humidity, coastal exposure, and demanding industrial environments, accelerate corrosion and present ongoing challenges for infrastructure owners and operators. Axalta’s industrial coating technologies have been developed to perform in such aggressive environments and are used globally to protect steel structures, equipment, and assets where conventional coating systems may not deliver sustained performance.

“For the infrastructure owners, value is defined by long-term performance,” said Suren Goonewardene, Managing Director, Lankem Ceylon PLC. “As Axalta’s authorized partner in Sri Lanka, Lankem is focused on delivering globally proven coating technologies that extend coating life, reduce maintenance frequency, and support more sustainable and cost-effective infrastructure development.”
