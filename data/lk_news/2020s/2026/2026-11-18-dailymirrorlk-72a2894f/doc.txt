Former British High Commissioner joins George Steuart board

George Steuart & Company Ltd has announced the appointment of former British High Commissioner to Sri Lanka and the Maldives James Dauris as an Independent Non-Executive Director of its board, with effect from January 1, 2027.

Dauris brings over three decades of experience spanning international diplomacy, commercial relations and strategic advisory. His distinguished career includes serving as British High Commissioner to Sri Lanka and the Maldives from 2015 to 2019, British Ambassador to Peru and Panama and holding senior leadership positions within the UK’s Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office.

A graduate in Law from the University of Cambridge, he began his professional career as a solicitor in London, specialising in corporate and commercial law, before entering diplomatic service.

The appointment reinforces George Steuart & Company’s British heritage, reflected in its legacy of having British board directors for over 150 years, until 1988. Further, it is a timely and strategic appointment that aligns well with the group’s renewed vision and focus on expanding its global footprint.

Dauris becomes the fourth Independent Non-Executive Director on the board and his appointment reaffirms the company’s commitment to strong corporate governance. The increased representation of independent directors reflects George Steuart’s commitment to independent oversight, broader perspectives in board deliberations and sound decision-making in the interests of all its shareholders.

George Steuart & Company Group Chairman Sarva Ameresekere stated, “We are honoured to welcome James to the board. We believe his vast international experience and understanding of Sri Lanka will be valuable as we strive to explore opportunities beyond our shores. His addition to the board adds further depth and diversity of expertise to support the group’s growth aspirations.”

Dauris said, “I am delighted to be joining the George Steuart board at an exciting time for the group as it works to grow its businesses in and outside Sri Lanka. It is going to be an important period for each of the George Steuart companies and I am looking forward to helping the board to make decisions that will serve customers, employees and shareholders well going forwards.”

Founded in 1835 by Captain James Steuart, the group has evolved from Sri Lanka’s oldest mercantile establishment into a diversified group with interests spanning tea exports, healthcare, travel and leisure, financial services, consumer products and industrial solutions.

As it approaches its bicentenary, the group remains committed to building on its legacy, creating lasting value and shaping a stronger future for generations to come.
