Upcountry train services disrupted again by landslide

Train services on the upcountry railway line were disrupted again this morning (17) after a large amount of soil from a mound near Kadigamuwa Railway Station fell onto the tracks.

The incident delayed a train travelling from Polgahawela to Gampola, as well as train services operating towards Gampola and Colombo Fort.

Although a large amount of soil has been removed, railway workers are continuing to clear the remaining soil from the tracks.
