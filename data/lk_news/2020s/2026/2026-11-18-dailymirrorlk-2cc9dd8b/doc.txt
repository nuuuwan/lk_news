Speaker, MP engage in verbal clash

Colombo, Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Speaker Jagath Wickramaratne and Opposition MP Rohana Bandara engaged in a verbal clash in Parliament today over tge time taken to raise questions during an oral question session.

The clash broke out when the Speaker prevented the MP from raising a supplementary question.

"You cannot take too much time to raise questions. Can't you understand Sinhala?" the Speaker said.

"I would have finished if you did not jump the gun (oba thuma penne nethanam mama prashnaya ahala ewarai)," the MP said in Sinhalese.
