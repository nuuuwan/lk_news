First iPhone 18 Pro sold in Sri Lanka

Ramesh De Silva, Deputy General Manager of Futureworld Lanka (Pvt) Ltd., handed over the first iPhone 18 Pro to a customer at the launch of the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Duo in Colombo today (23). Futureworld Lanka is an Apple-authorised reseller and service provider in Sri Lanka. Pix by Pradeep Pathirana.
