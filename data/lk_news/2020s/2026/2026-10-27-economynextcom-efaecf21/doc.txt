Sri Lanka Cabinet approves $60mn OPEC Fund loan for Mahaweli project Phase 2

ECONOMYNEXT – Sri Lanka’s Cabinet of Ministers has approved a proposal to enter into an agreement with the OPEC Fund for International Development (OFID) to secure a 60 million US dollar loan facility for Phase 2 of the Mahaweli Water Security Investment Program (MWSIP).

The total estimated cost for Phase 2 of the program is 536.5 million dollars, which the government plans to finance through co-financing arrangements with multiple development partners. The Asian Development Bank (ADB) has already agreed to provide 200 million dollars toward the program.

“Phase 2 of the Mahaweli Water Security Investment Program is planned for implementation to achieve the Government’s long-term objectives: enhancing food security in the Dry Zone, strengthening water resource management, improving resilience to climate change, and alleviating poverty,” Minister Nalinda Jayatissa said.

Following prior cabinet approval on July 20, 2026, to begin negotiations with OFID, the development fund confirmed its willingness to provide the 60 million dollar facility in two components of 48 million dollars and 12 million dollars.

The proposal was submitted by the President in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning, and Economic Development.

Under Phase 2, key construction works include finishing the remaining section of the main tunnel with about seven kilometers of excavation, constructing 68 kilometers of open and covered canals, conduits, and short tunnels, and building an eight-kilometer canal section for the Ellapothana Irrigation Project.

The project will also establish commercial farming infrastructure, including around 13 pressurized distribution networks to supply reliable irrigation water for high-value and field crops.

“Transforming agricultural production systems by introducing climate-resilient value chains and expanding farmers’ access to financial services and agricultural project-related services.” (Colombo/Oct27/2026)
