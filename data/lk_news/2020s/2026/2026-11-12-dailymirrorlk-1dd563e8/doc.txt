Sri Lanka Budget 2027 explained: the key numbers at a glance

Sri Lanka’s Budget 2027 was presented to Parliament on 12 November 2026 by Anura Kumara Dissanayake as Minister of Finance. It is the Government’s third Budget, and its stated aim is to move the economy “from economic stability towards transformation”.

The Budget follows a year shaken by Cyclone Ditwah and fuel price rises linked to the Middle East war. The Government plans to collect Rs. 6,180 billion and spend Rs. 7,673 billion, leaving a deficit of 3.8% of GDP. That rises to 4.5% with the Rs. 260 billion Ditwah reconstruction estimate. Interest alone costs Rs. 2,643 billion.

The 2027 Budget comes with a separate Rs. 260 billion Supplementary Estimate to finish Cyclone Ditwah reconstruction. Switch views to include it.

Interest is the single biggest bill. Rs. 2,643 billion goes on interest in 2027. That is about Rs. 43 of every Rs. 100 the Government collects in revenue.

Taxes on goods and services, which include VAT, bring in more than half of all revenue. Interest takes the largest share of spending.

Shares are of gross spending items. Annexure III also lists a small net "Other" adjustment of Rs. -11 bn.

Spending has grown faster than revenue, so the deficit has widened from Rs. 745 billion in 2025 to Rs. 1,493 billion in the 2027 Budget.
