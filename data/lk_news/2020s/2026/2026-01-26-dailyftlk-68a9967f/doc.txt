R I L Property donates to post-Ditwah recovery

R I L Property PLC has made a financial contribution of Rs. 10 million to the Government’s ‘Rebuilding Sri Lanka’ Fund, established to provide relief to communities affected by Cyclone Ditwah.

The cheque was formally handed over at the Presidential Secretariat by R I L Property COO M.C. Wickramasinghe to Secretary to the President Dr. Nandika Sanath Kumanayake. R I L Property CFO W.A. Indunil Perera and Assistant Maintenance Engineer Kasun Anuranga were also present at the occasion.
