President pays homage to Sacred Tooth Relic

Colombo, August 27 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake, who is in Kandy to participate in the annual Esala Perahera celebrations, paid homage to the Sacred Tooth Relic at the historic Sri Dalada Maligawa this afternoon (27).

During his visit, the President also met thousands of devotees who had gathered at the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic to witness the Perahera celebrations and inquired about their well-being.

Central Province Governor Dr. Harsha Suranga was also present on the occasion.
