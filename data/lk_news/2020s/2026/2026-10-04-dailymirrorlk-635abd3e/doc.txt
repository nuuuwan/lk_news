Gnanasara Thera to be produced before court today

Venerable Galagoda Aththe Gnanasara Thera, who was arrested last night at the Thiniyawala Aranya Senasanaya in Kalutara while an open warrant was in effect against him, is due to be produced before court today (4).

The Court of Appeal had recently issued an open warrant for the arrest of Gnanasara Thera after he was sentenced to a term of imprisonment for contempt of court.

The court had also imposed a travel ban preventing him from leaving the country.

Despite the warrant, Gnanasara Thera was arrested last night at the Thiniyawala Aranya Senasanaya.

Following his arrest, he was brought to the Colombo Crime Division last night and is due to be produced before court today.
