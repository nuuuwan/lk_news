Bouquet from The British School

We had an appreciative note coming our way from The British School Colombo, referring to our story ‘Pure Christmas magic and joy at British School,’ appearing in The Island of 08th January, 2026.

The article is well received by the British School principal, students, admin and the media team. They have already shared the news on all their social media platforms and in their WhatsApp groups and I can ensure that it is getting wide coverage through the internet. Please continue with the good work. Thanks.
