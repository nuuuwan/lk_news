Joint Opposition Fears Government May Try to Postpone Elections

Colombo, Aug. 31 (Daily Mirror) - The Joint Opposition, at its seminar in the Galle District, raised concerns about whether the government was planning to postpone the presidential and general elections after the completion of their five-year terms.

Addressing the gathering, its convenor, Prof. G.L. Peiris, said the government had stated clearly that elections to the Provincial Councils would not be held.

“We fear that the government may try to do the same with the presidential and general elections. We are concerned because some government ministers claim that they will remain in power for another 20 or 25 years. They say they did not make this gain merely for five years,” he said.

Therefore, he said, the proposed move to extend the retirement age of superior court judges could not be taken lightly, despite attempts by some to belittle its seriousness.

He said the enactment of the Twenty-Second Amendment to the Constitution for this purpose would sound the death knell for the remaining semblance of democracy in the country.
