Pahala Kadugannawa road reopened after maintenance work

The Pahala Kadugannawa section of the Colombo–Kandy main road, which was closed last night (23) for maintenance work, was reopened this morning (24).

The road was closed from 11 p.m. yesterday until 6 a.m. today following a decision by the Disaster Management Centre to safely remove hazardous rocks in the area and carry out essential maintenance work.
