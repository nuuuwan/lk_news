SLAF rescues three hostages in Central African Republic after 8 months

Colombo, Nov. 1 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Air Force (SLAF) Aviation Unit serving under the United Nations Multidimensional Integrated Stabilization Mission in the Central African Republic (MINUSCA) has successfully rescued three hostages who had been held captive for more than eight months.

The rescue was carried out during a high-risk operation in the Central African Republic, with the SLAF personnel successfully securing the release of all three hostages.
