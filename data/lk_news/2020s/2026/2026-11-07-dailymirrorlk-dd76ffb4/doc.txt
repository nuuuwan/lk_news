Week ends in green

The ASPI closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Carson Cumberbatch, Commercial Bank and Hatton National Bank with the turnover crossing Rs. 2.9 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Hatton National Bank, Sierra Cables and Sunshine Holdings. Mixed interest was observed in Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka, Commercial Bank and Dipped Products whilst retail interest was noted in ACME Printing & Packaging, Lanka Credit and Business Finance and Waskaduwa Beach Resort. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

During the week, the ASPI and the S&P SL20 gained 5.4% and 5.2% respectively, whilst recording an average daily turnover of Rs. 3.0 Bn.

The Banking sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Hatton National Bank and Commercial Bank) whilst the sector index gained 1.00%. The share price of Hatton National Bank gained Rs 3.75 (0.96%) to close at Rs 393.75. The share price of Commercial Bank recorded a gain of Rs 3.75 (1.82%) to close at Rs 210.00.

Capital Goods sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Sierra Cables) whilst the sector index decreased by 0.32%. The share price of Sierra Cables increased by Rs 1.20 (3.34%) to close at Rs 37.10.

Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka and Dipped Products were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka moved down by Rs 1.25 (0.76%) to close at Rs 164.00. The share price of Dipped Products appreciated by Rs 0.50 (0.79%) to close at Rs 63.60.
