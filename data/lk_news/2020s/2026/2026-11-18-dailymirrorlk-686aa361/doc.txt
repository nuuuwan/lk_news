Capital Trust Holdings joins European Chamber of Commerce of Sri Lanka

From left: European Chamber of Commerce of Sri Lanka Vice President Mario Stubbs, President Ahamed ur Rahman, Capital Trust Properties Chairperson and CEO Minoli Wickramasinghe and Head of Business Development Aruni De Mel Ahkil

Strengthens investment links between Sri Lanka and Europe across financial and real estate sectors

Capital Trust Holdings, through its subsidiary Capital Trust Properties (Pvt.) Ltd, has joined the European Chamber of Commerce of Sri Lanka (ECCSL), strengthening its engagement with European businesses and investors while expanding opportunities across Sri Lanka’s financial and real estate sectors.

The membership provides a platform for the European investors to explore opportunities in Sri Lanka’s capital and equity markets, alongside residential and commercial real estate.

It also creates scope for greater collaboration between local businesses and international investors, supported by professional advisory services and access to market expertise.

As the first real estate transaction and wealth management advisory company to join the chamber, Capital Trust Holdings aims to facilitate cross-border investment, strengthen commercial relationships and encourage the exchange of international best practices.

The move comes as Sri Lanka seeks to attract greater international investment and deepen its commercial ties with overseas markets. Access to reliable market intelligence, transparent transaction processes and professional advice remain important for the investors assessing opportunities in emerging markets.

Capital Trust Properties Chairperson and CEO Minoli Wickramasinghe said, “Sri Lanka’s financial and real estate sectors offer considerable opportunities for international investors. However, realising this potential requires confidence, transparency and access to reliable market expertise. Our membership in the ECCSL provides an important platform to build stronger relationships with the European businesses and connect investors with opportunities across Sri Lanka’s capital markets and property sector.”

She added, “Our responsibility extends beyond facilitating transactions. We are committed to helping investors make informed decisions through professional advisory services, transparent practices and internationally benchmarked standards. Through this association, we hope to encourage greater collaboration, promote responsible investment and contribute to strengthening Sri Lanka’s position as an attractive investment destination.”

Capital Trust Holdings operates across a diversified portfolio of businesses, including stockbroking, real estate development, real estate transaction and advisory services, education, packaging and wealth management. This cross-sector presence enables the group to engage with the European companies seeking investment, expansion and commercial opportunities in Sri Lanka. For the businesses considering establishing or expanding operations locally, the association offers access to professional guidance on commercial property requirements, location selection and market conditions. The investors exploring residential and income-generating properties can also benefit from local expertise, market insights and structured transaction advisory services.

The membership further supports Capital Trust Properties’ commitment to strengthening professional standards within Sri Lanka’s real estate industry. The company places emphasis on ethical business practices, due diligence and transparent transactions, while advocating for greater professionalism and accountability across the sector.

Through its engagement with the ECCSL, Capital Trust Holdings also intends to promote knowledge sharing and collaboration across industries connected to real estate and investment, including banking, finance, construction, hospitality and professional services.

Looking ahead, the group aims to use its membership to build lasting commercial relationships, broaden international participation in Sri Lanka’s investment markets and support the country’s continued integration with the global business community. The association reflects Capital Trust Holdings’ long-term commitment to connecting international capital with local opportunities while contributing to a more competitive and professionally developed investment environment in Sri Lanka.
