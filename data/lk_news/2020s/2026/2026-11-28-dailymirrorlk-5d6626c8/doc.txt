O/L tuition classes, guess papers banned from Dec. 2

The Sri Lanka Examinations Department has prohibited the conducting of tuition classes, seminars, workshops and other examination-related programmes ahead of the 2026 G.C.E. Ordinary Level (O/L) examination.

Commissioner General of Examinations A.K.S. Indika Kumari Liyanage said the ban will come into effect from midnight on December 2 and remain in force until the conclusion of the examination, which will be held from December 8 to 17 at 3,519 examination centres islandwide.

The Commissioner General said the prohibition also covers conducting lectures and workshops for examination candidates, as well as printing and distributing question papers containing anticipated or guess questions relating to the examination.

Publishing or possessing posters, banners or handbills containing examination questions or similar questions through electronic or print media is also strictly prohibited during the period.

The regulations have been issued under Section 22 of the Public Examinations Act No. 25 of 1968, in accordance with Gazette Notification No. 1816 dated June 21, 2013.

Any individual, institution or party violating the regulations will be considered to have committed an offence under the Act.

The public has been urged to report any violations to the nearest police station, Police Headquarters or the Sri Lanka Examinations Department.
