ACT reaffirms commitment to advancing Sri Lanka’s container transport industry

The Association of Container Transporters (ACT), the apex representative body of Sri Lanka’s container transport industry, successfully concluded its 41st Annual General Meeting (AGM) and Fellowship on 26 June 2026 at the Lotus Tower, Colombo, reaffirming its commitment to strengthening the country’s logistics and supply chain sector through closer industry collaboration and constructive engagement with policymakers.

The AGM was attended by Anura Karunatilleka, Minister of Ports, Aviation and Energy, who graced the occasion as the Chief Guest, while Ruwan Kodituwakku, Deputy Minister, attended as the Guest of Honour. The event also brought together senior government officials, industry leaders, corporate members, and representatives from the ports, shipping, logistics and supply chain sectors.

As Sri Lanka’s pioneering association representing container transport operators, ACT has played a vital role in supporting the country’s import and export trade for over four decades.
