‘Go Anywhere, Get Anything’: Uber arrives in Sri Lanka’s gem capital

Uber, Sri Lanka’s leading ride-hailing and delivery platform, announced the launch of Uber Eats and Uber Rides in Ratnapura, further expanding its footprint across the island. Residents and visitors can now access food, groceries, everyday essentials, and reliable transportation through the Uber app, while local merchants, delivery partners, and drivers gain new opportunities to grow and earn.

With Uber Eats now live across a broad coverage area in the district, customers can order from iconic local favourites such as XYZ , alongside grocery and retail partners, all delivered at the tap of a button. At the same time, Uber Rides offers residents and visitors a convenient and affordable way to get around entire Ratnapura district through a range of two-, three-, and four-wheel options. Known as Sri Lanka’s gem capital, Ratnapura is home to a vibrant community of entrepreneurs, merchants, and small businesses, making it a natural next step in Uber’s expansion across Sri Lanka.

Varun Wijewardane, Country Manager – Delivery, Uber Sri Lanka, said: “Ratnapura is home to a vibrant community of local businesses and entrepreneurs, and we’re excited to bring Uber Eats to the district. By connecting local merchants with more customers and creating flexible earning opportunities for delivery partners, we hope to make everyday convenience more accessible while supporting local economic growth.”

Kaushalya Gunaratne, Country Manager – Mobility, Uber Sri Lanka, said: “We’re excited to bring Uber Rides to Ratnapura and make reliable, affordable mobility available to more people across the district. This launch creates new earning opportunities for local drivers while giving residents and visitors access to convenient transportation backed by technology designed to make every trip safer and more seamless.”
