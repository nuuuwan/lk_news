Supreme Court rules Prevention of Organised Crime Bill consistent with Constitution

Colombo, November 21 (Daily Mirror)- The Supreme Court has ruled that the Prevention of Organised Crime Bill is not inconsistent with the Constitution and can therefore be passed in Parliament with a simple majority, subject to certain amendments.

Announcing the Court’s determination in Parliament today, Speaker Jagath Wickramaratne said the Supreme Court had instructed that amendments proposed by the Acting Attorney General, along with those directed by the Court, be incorporated into the Bill at the Committee Stage.
