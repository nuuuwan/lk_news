70-year-olds who can’t care for themselves still behind bars

Colombo, Sep. 10 (Daily Mirror) - A number of prisoners aged over 70 who are unable to attend to even their basic personal needs remain behind bars, MP Chaminda Wijesiri told Parliament yesterday.

Speaking for the first time in Parliament since his release on bail, Wijesiri said there were also inmates who had spent decades behind bars despite being eligible for release on good behaviour.

“There are a number of inmates in prison who are well over 70 years of age. They cannot even relieve themselves on their own,” he said.

He said an inmate who had been sent to prison at the age of 18, more than 30 years ago, died while he was in prison.

“There are a large number of inmates who are eligible for release on good behaviour but they have been inside decades and decades,” Wijesiri said.

Wijesiri was sentenced to 18 months’ imprisonment on July 28 after being found guilty of charges relating to the obstruction and assault of two police officers. He was subsequently released on bail.

Reflecting on his experience in prison, he openly criticised both the Government and the Opposition over what he said were actions that had caused him to suffer losses during the recent past.

“I curse both those in the Government and in the Opposition whose actions caused me to lose what I have lost during the past,” he said.

The MP also said he had been looking for individuals allegedly involved in the Central Bank bond scam and people who had allegedly hidden money in Uganda.

“I also looked for the persons who hid their monies in Uganda. However, I did not find any of them in prison,” he said.
