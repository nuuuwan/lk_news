Payable to accelerate Sri Lanka’s digital payments landscape following acquisition by Short Circuit

Sri Lanka’s leading digital payment platform, Payable, has been acquired by regional tech firm Short Circuit to accelerate the country’s digital payments landscape. Since its founding in 2016, Payable has grown into Sri Lanka’s largest payments technology company, currently serving over 40,000 merchants.

With Short Circuit’s global expertise and integrated technology, Payable aims to expand its merchant network by an additional 100,000 merchants over the next four years, driving innovation and delivering more advanced payment solutions.

Payable’s CEO, Yohan Wijesiriwardane, called the acquisition a pivotal step to scale technology and deepen market reach. Chairman Rajendra Theagarajah noted the combination will offer a strong payments layer alongside Short Circuit’s ERP infrastructure. Short Circuit’s Group Managing Director, Bhanuka Harischandra, highlighted that the merger combines both companies’ strengths to accelerate fintech innovation and increase merchant adoption, positioning digital payments in Sri Lanka for long-term growth.
