Nearly 8,000 Police, tri-forces deployed for Kandy Esala Perahera security

A comprehensive security and traffic plan has been implemented for the 2026 Kandy Esala Perahera, with more than 7,900 security personnel to be deployed throughout the festival period from August 18 to August 28.

Police said the security operation will involve 6,200 police officers, 564 Special Task Force (STF) personnel, 1,200 tri-forces personnel, 29 civil security officers, and police tracker dogs.

Security measures will include roadblocks at 14 entry points into Kandy city, along with vehicle and individual inspections and arrangements to guide visitors.

The historic Kandy Esala Perahera will be held under state patronage from August 18 to August 28. The Kumbal Perahera will take place from August 18 to August 22, followed by the Randoli Perahera from August 23 to August 27. The final Randoli Perahera and the traditional water-cutting ceremony will be held on August 28.

To facilitate visitors, 13 designated parking areas have been allocated, including six parking areas for light vehicles and seven for heavy vehicles.

Police have also advised motorists to use alternative routes to avoid the Kandy city centre during the Perahera.

Vehicles travelling from Katugastota towards Colombo or Nuwara Eliya are advised to use the Katugastota Bridge and proceed along Polgolla Road towards Peradeniya.

Those travelling from Colombo through Kandy towards Kurunegala, Matale, or Mahiyanganaya are advised to use the Peradeniya–Polgolla Road towards Kurunegala or Matale, or travel via Madawala towards Mahiyanganaya.

Motorists travelling from Kandy towards Mahiyanganaya and Badulla are advised to use Mahaiyawa Junction, Dharmashoka Mawatha, and Thennekumbura.

Police have requested the public and motorists to respect national cultural traditions, maintain environmental cleanliness, and extend their cooperation to officers deployed for security and traffic duties throughout the 24-hour operation.
