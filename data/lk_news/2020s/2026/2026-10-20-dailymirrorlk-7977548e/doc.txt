Global Finance World’s Best Digital Bank & AI in Finance Awards 2026 ComBank crowned as Best Digital Bank in Sri Lanka

Commercial Bank of Ceylon has emphatically reinforced its position as Sri Lanka’s leading digital banking innovator, winning three prestigious accolades at Global Finance World’s Best Digital Bank & AI in Finance Awards 2026.

The bank won the awards for Best Consumer Digital Bank, Best Mobile Banking App and Most Innovative Digital Bank, at the ceremony held at Raffles Hotel, Singapore.

The awards honour excellence in digital banking among financial institutions worldwide, placing Commercial Bank alongside leading international players in an increasingly technology-driven industry.

Commercial Bank was represented at the awards event by Chief Digital Officer Randil Boteju, Digital Banking Unit Manager Ramendra Nonis and Senior Engineering Manager IT R&D Nadika Nagahawatte.

The Global Finance recognition reflects the bank’s sustained investment in digital transformation, customer-centric technology and advanced research and development, spearheaded by its Digital Banking Unit and IT research and development teams. It also underscores the strength of digital innovation in meeting global benchmarks for banking technology, functionality and customer experience, the bank said.

The bank’s recognition also places Sri Lanka’s digital banking capabilities on a prominent international platform. At the same ceremony, Singapore-based DBS Bank was named the World’s Best Digital Bank, highlighting the global calibre of institutions recognised by the programme.

According to Global Finance, the winners were selected on the strength of their strategies for attracting and serving digital customers, customer adoption and growth, the breadth of digital product offerings, tangible benefits delivered through digital initiatives and the design and functionality of their websites and mobile platforms. The winners in the sub-categories were assessed on the relative strength and success of their web products and services.

“Global Finance’s World’s Best Digital Banks 2026 are the institutions setting new standards for digital banking worldwide,” said Global Finance founder and Editorial Director Joseph Giarraputo.

Commercial Bank’s multi-category success comes as the industry continues to accelerate the adoption of digital technologies to deliver more efficient, responsive and secure financial services. The awards affirm the bank’s progress in developing a digital banking ecosystem designed to support the customers’ everyday financial needs while keeping pace with changing expectations and technological advances.
