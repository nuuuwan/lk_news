FIFA plans US$25 million support package to boost Sri Lankan football

Colombo, Oct. 27 (Daily Mirror) - FIFA is planning to provide approximately US$25 million in support for Sri Lankan football over the next four-year cycle, with infrastructure development, youth programmes and improvements to the national team among the key priorities discussed at a meeting in Baku, Azerbaijan.

FIFA President Gianni Infantino met Minister of Youth Affairs and Sports Sunil Kumara Gamage and Football Federation of Sri Lanka (FFSL) President Jaswar Umar to discuss a development roadmap aimed at making football the most popular sport in Sri Lanka within the next four years.

The discussions focused on expanding access to football across the country, modernising facilities and creating pathways for young players to progress to higher levels of the game.

Infantino said FIFA was ready to support Sri Lanka's football ambitions by working with the Government and the FFSL on infrastructure projects and development initiatives.

He also met National Sports Council Chairman Priyantha Ekanayake and High Performance Centre lead Prasanna Jayalath as part of the Sri Lankan delegation.

The proposed financial assistance is expected to support the national team, youth development, women's football, coaches and referees during the next four-year cycle.

Infrastructure development was another key area discussed, including proposals for an international-standard football stadium and the expansion of facilities across the island.

One of the proposals is to establish football grounds in every district, with work expected to begin next year. Separately, the FFSL plans to develop around 40 football grounds through its member leagues by the end of 2031, according to the programme outlined.

The discussions also covered the establishment of a National High-Performance Centre, with FIFA expected to provide technical expertise and infrastructure support in collaboration with the Sports Ministry and the FFSL. The proposed centre is intended to strengthen player development, coaching standards and professional opportunities for talented footballers.

Infantino also highlighted the role of youth competitions and festivals in identifying and developing talent, saying such events provide children with opportunities to improve their skills and experience representing their country.

Minister Gamage expressed the Government's appreciation for FIFA's continued support for the national team and youth football programmes, while reaffirming its commitment to accelerating the development of the sport.
