Suthar, Jadeja put India in command after Dinusha ton

AUG.17 (Mirror Sports) - Sri Lanka were bowled out for 284, leaving India with a 178-run first-innings lead in the rain-affected first Test after Sonal Dinusha's maiden Test century delayed the hosts' collapse.

Dinusha made 100 and Niroshan Dickwella scored 80 as Sri Lanka recovered from 90/5 with a 146-run sixth-wicket partnership.

Manav Suthar made an immediate impact on his Test debut, taking 4/76, while Ravindra Jadeja claimed 3/57 as India eventually broke through Sri Lanka's resistance.

India had earlier posted 462, led by Devdutt Padikkal's 167 and KL Rahul's 82.

Bad light prevented India from beginning their second innings late in the day, with only 13 overs lost, leaving the visitors in a strong position to push for victory if conditions allow.
