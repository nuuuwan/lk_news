Volkswagen Taigun, Virtus launched

Continental Cars & Commercials Pvt Ltd, the official authorized importer of Volkswagen passenger vehicles in Sri Lanka, ceremonially launched the all-new Volkswagen Taigun and Volkswagen Virtus at the Bandaranaike Memorial International Conference Hall (BMICH) this evening, marking a significant milestone for the brand’s presence in the country.

The high-profile launch event highlighted a new era of German engineering, innovation, and premium mobility tailored for modern Sri Lankan consumers, reinforcing Volkswagen’s long-term commitment to the local automotive market.

The event brought together senior representatives from the Volkswagen Group, diplomats, government officials, corporate leaders, business community members, media personnel, industry stakeholders, and automotive enthusiasts.

The evening featured an immersive unveiling experience with advanced digital presentations, cultural performances, executive addresses, entertainment segments, and dynamic product showcases, reflecting Volkswagen’s global standards in engineering excellence, safety, technology, and contemporary design.
