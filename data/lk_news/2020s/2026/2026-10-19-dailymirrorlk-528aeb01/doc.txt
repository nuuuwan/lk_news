Daily Mirror wins environment awards

Daily Mirror Deputy Features Editor Kamanthi Wickramasinghe (Gold) and Deputy Web Editor Chaturanga Pradeep Samarawickrama (Bronze) receive their awards at the Presidential Environment Awards 2026, in recognition of their contributions to environmental awareness and communication. The Gold Award was presented by Prime Minister Dr Harini Amarasuriya, while the Bronze Award was presented by Environment Ministry Secretary K. R. Uduwawala.

Meanwhile, Duminda Sanjeewa Balasooriya, Chief Editor of the Wijeya Children's Newspaper, received a Merit Award in the Newspaper category under the Media Sector Institution Category. The award was presented by Prof. Tilak Hewawasam, Senior Professor in Geology. Pix by Kithsiri De Mel
