Indices close in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Commercial Bank, Carson Cumberbatch and Hatton National Bank with the turnover crossing Rs. 3.7bn.

A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in JAT Holdings, Sierra Cables and Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka.

Mixed interest was observed in Access Engineering, Dipped Products and Commercial Bank whilst retail interest was noted in TAJ Rights, Softlogic Finance and York Arcade Holdings.

Furthermore, foreigners closed as net buyers. Capital Goods sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Sierra Cables and ACL Cables) whilst the sector index edged up by 0.08%. The share price of Sierra Cables increased by Rs. 0.40 (1.12%) to close at Rs. 36.20. The share price of ACL Cables closed flat at Rs. 101.00.

Materials sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Jat Holdings and Dipped Products) whilst the sector index increased by 0.12%.

The share price of JAT Holdings gained Rs. 1.40 (3.63%) to close at Rs. 40.00. The share price of Dipped Products recorded a loss of Rs. 0.40 (0.56%) to close at Rs. 71.60. Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka was also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka moved up by Rs. 0.50 (0.29%) to close at Rs. 172.00.
