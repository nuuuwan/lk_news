Indices close in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Melstacorp, Dialog Axiata and Cargills with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.5bn.

A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Sampath Bank, Commercial Bank and Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka. Mixed interest was observed in John Keells Holdings, Commercial Credit & Finance and Renuka Agri Foods whilst retail interest was noted in HNB Finance, ACME Printing & Packaging and Asia Siyaka Commodities.

Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net buyers.

The Banking sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Sampath Bank and Commercial Bank) whilst the sector index edged up by 0.05%. The share price of Sampath Bank increased by Rs. 0.50 (0.36%) to close at Rs. 139.50. The share price of Commercial Bank lost Rs. 0.25 (0.12%) to close at Rs. 205.00.

Capital Goods sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Royal Ceramics and John Keells Holdings) whilst the sector index decreased by 0.55%. The share price of Royal Ceramics moved down by Rs. 0.60 (1.22%) to close at Rs. 48.50. The share price of John Keells Holdings recorded a loss of Rs. 0.10 (0.51%) to close at Rs. 19.40. Overseas Realty was also included amongst the top turnover contributors.

The share price of Overseas Realty declined by Rs. 0.60 (1.12%) to close at Rs. 53.20.
