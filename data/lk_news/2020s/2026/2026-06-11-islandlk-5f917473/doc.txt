Treasury Bill rate hike taking toll on stock trading

CSE trading reported a further drop yesterday due to an increase in Treasury Bill rates apart from other negative factors, including the war situation in West Asia and the depreciation trends in the local rupee, market analysts said.

Amid those developments both indices moved downward. The All Share Price Index went down by 191.45 points, while the S and P SL20 declined by 46.11 points.

Turnover stood at Rs 1.66 billion with 7 crossings. Those crossings were; JKH crossed 12 million shares to the tune of Rs 235 million and its shares traded at Rs 19.50, Sunshine Holdings 2 million shares crossed to the tune of Rs 57 million; its shares traded at Rs 28.50, Digital Mobility Solutions 197,000 shares crossed for Rs 31 million; its shares sold at Rs 158.50.

Hemas Holdings 1 million shares crossed to the tune of Rs 31 million; its shares traded at Rs 30, EML 2.5 million shares crossed for Rs 30 million and its shares sold at Rs 9.50 and Dialog Axiata 500,000 shares crossed for Rs 24 million; its shares sold at Rs 44.

In the retail market top seven companies that mainly contributed to the turnover were; Sampath Bank Rs 198 million (1.4 million shares traded), JKH Rs 57 million (2.9 million shares traded), Commercial Bank Rs 57 million (285,000 shares traded), HNB Finance (Right Issue) Rs 54 million (70.2 million shares traded), Prime Lands Residencies Rs 38 million (987,000 shares traded), Brown’s Investments Rs 32 million(5.7 million shares traded) and HNB (Non-Voting) Rs 30 million (920,000 shares traded). During the day 86.4 million share volumes changed hands in 22534 transactions.

It is said that manufacturing sector counters, especially JKH, led the market while banking sector, especially Sampath, performed well. Further the real estate sector stocks, especially Prime Lands, also performed well.

Yesterday the rupee was quoted at Rs 333.00/335.00 to the US dollar in the spot market on Thursday, from 332.25/75 the previous day, dealers said, while the secondary bond market was quiet ahead of the auction.

An auction of Rs 150,000 million Treasury bonds is scheduled.

The telegraphic transfer rate for Sri Lanka’s rupee against the US dollar was Rs 328.00 buying, Rs 337.00 selling; the euro was Rs 376.4077 selling, Rs 390.3247 buying; and the pound was Rs 437.9428 buying, Rs 451.9884 selling.
