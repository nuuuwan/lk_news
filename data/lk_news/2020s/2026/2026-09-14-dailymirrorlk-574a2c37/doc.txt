Court of Appeal upholds 15-year sentence for father convicted of raping daughter

Colombo, Sep. 14 (Daily Mirror) - The Court of Appeal has dismissed an appeal filed by a man convicted of raping his biological daughter and affirmed the 15-year rigorous imprisonment sentence imposed on him by Hambantota High Court.

The Court of Appeal, comprising Justices Amal Ranaraja and Dr. Sumudu Premachandra, held that there was no merit in the appeal filed by an accused against his conviction for incestuous statutory rape.

The accused had been indicted over three alleged incidents of sexual abuse involving his biological daughter between October 1 and October 31, 2018.

Following a contested trial, the High Court of Hambantota convicted him on one count under Section 364(3) of the Penal Code on July 16, 2025, while acquitting and discharging him on the remaining two counts.

The High Court sentenced him to 15 years of rigorous imprisonment and imposed a fine of Rs. 10,000. The Court also ordered him to pay Rs. 400,000 as compensation to the victim, with an additional six months' rigorous imprisonment in default of payment of each amount.

The accused challenged the conviction on several grounds, including the alleged failure of the prosecution to establish the date or time of the offence, the purported improbability of the victim's testimony, the use of leading questions and the manner in which the trial judge had considered his unsworn dock statement.

Justice Dr. Sumudu Premachandra, delivering the judgment, held that the prosecution's version was probable and that there was no basis for the Court of Appeal to interfere with the findings of the trial judge.

Accordingly, the Court dismissed the appeal and affirmed both the conviction and the 15-year sentence.

The Court directed that the sentence should run from the date of conviction, July 16, 2025, while the fine and compensation awarded to the victim would remain unchanged.
