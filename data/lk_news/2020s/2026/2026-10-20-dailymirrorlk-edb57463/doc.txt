Selling pressure prompts an uptick in yields

The secondary market activity soared yesterday, with a notable increase in the volumes traded, as some selling pressure kicked in from both foreign and local investor groups.

This prompted an upward shift in the yield curve, with a sharper increment in yields noted towards the longer end.

Despite the prevailing selling pressure, some buying interest emerged, whilst investor sentiment continued to be shaped by the ongoing global uncertainties.

Amongst the trades that were observed yesterday, the 01.08.2030 maturity traded at 11.55 percent and this was followed by maturities within the 2031 segment, namely the 01.02.2031 maturity, which traded at 11.75 percent and the 01.12.2031 maturity, which traded at 11.90 percent.

Moving along the curve, the 15.01.2033 bond was seen changing hands at 12.25 percent. Lastly, within the 2034 bracket, the 15.06.2034, 15.09.2034 and 15.10.2034 maturities traded from 12.32 percent to 12.48 percent.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee marginally appreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.331.28/US dollar, compared to Rs.331.29/US dollar seen earlier.
