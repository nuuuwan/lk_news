Fairway Holdings unveils seventh luxury project in Colombo

Fairway Holdings launched its seventh luxury apartment development, “Fairway Latitude,” on January 20, 2026, at Cinnamon Life. Located on High Level Road in Kirulapone, Colombo 05, the project marks the group’s first residential venture within Colombo’s central business district.

The development emphasizes connectivity, situated near key schools, hospitals, and commercial hubs. Amenities include a rooftop infinity pool, a fully equipped gym, a function hall for 250, and a convenience store. Residences range from two to four bedrooms and feature European kitchens, smart-home provisions, and sustainable design elements.

Chairman Virath de Alwis stated the project continues Fairway’s commitment to high standards, with construction already underway. CEO Ashean Karthelis highlighted the project’s advanced progress, noting that six floors are already completed, which aims to offer earlier occupancy and a strong return on investment for buyers.

Scheduled for completion in 2028, Fairway Latitude expands Fairway Holdings’ portfolio of over 1,000 luxury apartments across Sri Lanka..
