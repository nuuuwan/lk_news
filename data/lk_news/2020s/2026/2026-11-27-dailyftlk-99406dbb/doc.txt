Indian High Commissioner backs SLUDI,  startups and AI at Digital Sri Lanka Summit 2026

CItes India’s population-scale Digital Public Infrastructure, built on inclusion, interoperability and openness, as a model India is willing to share with Sri Lanka in line with its development priorities

Welcomes Sarvam AI’s engagement with Sri Lankan entities on Sinhala language models

India is ready to move from sharing experiences to building digital capabilities with Sri Lanka, High Commissioner Santosh Jha said at the Digital Sri Lanka Summit 2026 on Thursday, pointing to around Rs. 25 billion in support for the national digital ID project (SLUDI), growing startup ties, disaster-tech cooperation after Cyclone Ditwah, and early work on Sinhala AI models.

The summit organised by WION and SAP, the High Commissioner underscored the emergence of technology and innovation as a key pillar of the India-Sri Lanka bilateral partnership.

Highlighting the scale and intensity of India’s digital transformation over the last decade, Jha spoke about India’s experience in developing population-scale Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI), guided by the principles of inclusion, interoperability and openness. He emphasised India’s approach towards leveraging technology to empower people and its readiness to share its experiences with Sri Lanka in line with its developmental priorities.

In his address, High Commissioner touched upon India’s support of around Rs. 25 billion for the Sri Lanka Unique Digital Identity (SLUDI) project. He also spoke about the growing linkages between the startup and innovation ecosystems of both countries through initiatives of NASSCOM Disha program, IIT Madras Pravartak capacity - building, India - Sri Lanka Innovation Forum and launch of tinkering lab under Indian Aral Innovation Mission at Nalanda college in Colombo. In the context of India’s $ 450 million assistance package for recovery and reconstruction following Cyclone Ditwah, he highlighted the ongoing collaboration on geospatial technology for disaster impact assessment and a mobile broadcast-based Early Warning System.

High Commissioner underscored the potential for bilateral collaboration in Artificial Intelligence and welcomed recent engagements between Sarvam AI and Sri Lankan digital entities, including towards development of Sinhala language models.

India and Sri Lanka are moving from sharing experiences to building capabilities together. India stands ready to partner with Sri Lanka in building an innovative, secure and inclusive digital future, responsive to the aspirations of the people of both countries.
