Schools to close on December 4, reopen on January 4

Colombo, Nov. 27 (Daily Mirror) - The Ministry of Education has announced that the third school term of all government and government-approved private schools will end on December 4, 2026.

Accordingly, schools will remain closed for the year-end vacation, with the first school term of 2027 scheduled to begin on January 4.

In a circular issued today (27), Education Ministry Secretary Nalaka Kaluwewa instructed Provincial Education Secretaries, Provincial and Zonal Education Directors and school principals to ensure that all academic activities and examinations scheduled for the third term are completed without interruption before the term ends.

The circular applies to all government and government-approved private schools across the country.
