EU, Sri Lanka discuss Trincomalee green energy hub, port development

Colombo, Oct. 14 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka and the European Union have discussed proposals to establish a green energy hub at Trincomalee Port, with the need for a feasibility study highlighted during talks on renewable energy and port development.

The discussions were held between a European Union delegation led by Peteris Ustubs, Director for the Asia-Pacific region, and Minister of Ports, Civil Aviation and Energy Anura Karunathilaka at the Ministry premises.

The EU delegation commended the Government’s initiatives in renewable energy generation and energy storage technologies, while expressing interest in exploring ways to support Sri Lanka’s transition towards green energy.

Minister Karunathilaka briefed the delegation on renewable energy projects currently being implemented by the Government. Discussions also focused on developing container terminals to enhance the capacity and efficiency of the Port of Colombo.

The proposed green energy hub at Trincomalee Port was another key area of discussion, with the Minister stressing the importance of conducting a feasibility study to assess the initiative.

The two sides also discussed potential European Union support for port development projects under the EU’s Global Gateway programme.

Deputy Minister Janitha Ruwan Kodithukku and Ministry Secretary W.W.S. Mangala also participated in the discussions.
