Rs. 80,000 bill puts Aluthgama station in the dark

Colombo, June 30 (Daily Mirror) - The electricity supply to Aluthgama Railway Station is set to be disconnected after the station reportedly failed to settle an outstanding electricity bill of nearly Rs. 80,000.

According to reports, the relevant authorities have decided to disconnect the power supply due to the non-payment of the bill.

The incident has raised concerns over administrative lapses within the Railway Department. In the past, telephone connections at several railway stations were disconnected due to similar administrative shortcomings.

If the electricity supply is cut off, public announcement systems at the station will be disrupted, making it difficult to inform passengers about train arrivals, departures, and delays. This is expected to cause inconvenience to commuters and further highlight ongoing management issues within the department.
