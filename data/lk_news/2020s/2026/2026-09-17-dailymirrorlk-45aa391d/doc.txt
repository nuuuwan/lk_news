CSE extends four-day slide as ASPI nears 21,000

The Colombo Stock Exchange closed lower for a fourth consecutive session on 17 September 2026, with both benchmark indices weakening amid sustained selling pressure and cautious investor sentiment.

The ASPI fell 109.98 points, or 0.52%, to 21,023.43, while the S&P SL20 declined 22.33 points, or 0.38%, to 5,924.96. The ASPI trended lower through most of the session and moved close to the 21,000 level as afternoon selling intensified.

Market turnover stood at LKR 1.33 billion, with 55.41 million shares traded. The Capital Goods sector led activity with LKR 282.21 million in turnover, while PickMe recorded the highest individual counter turnover at LKR 161.82 million.

Crossings accounted for around 33% of total turnover, led by a LKR 155.00 million block in PickMe. Other notable crossings were recorded in Hayleys, Commercial Bank, John Keells Holdings, Melstacorp and Sunshine Holdings.

Market breadth remained firmly negative, with only 50 gainers against 152 decliners, while foreign investors recorded a sizeable net outflow of LKR 213.08 million.

Melstacorp was the largest negative contributor to the ASPI, followed by Dialog Axiata, Hayleys, Lion Brewery and RIL Property. John Keells Holdings provided the strongest positive contribution.

Despite Brent crude easing to around USD 105.83 per barrel, energy prices remain elevated, while persistent foreign selling and weak market breadth continue to weigh on sentiment. With the ASPI now testing the 21,000 area after four consecutive declines, the market remains vulnerable unless stronger buying support emerges.
