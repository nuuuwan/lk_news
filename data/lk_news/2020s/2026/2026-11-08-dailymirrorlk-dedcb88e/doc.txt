32 Chinese nationals found at Panadura hotel in suspected online fraud probe

Panadura, Nov 08 (Daily Mirror) - Police have launched an investigation into suspected illegal online operations and financial fraud following a search of a hotel in Nalluruwa, Panadura, where 32 Chinese nationals were staying.

The search was conducted on November 6 and 7 by the Panadura Division Crimes Investigation Bureau under a warrant issued by the Panadura Magistrate's Court.

The operation followed intelligence received by the Senior Superintendent of Police in charge of the Panadura Division regarding suspicious online financial activities allegedly taking place at the premises.

The raid was carried out with the assistance of the Criminal Investigation Department (CID), Police Special Task Force (STF), Criminal Records Division, Police K9 Division and the Department of Official Languages.

During the search, police found 32 Chinese nationals at the hotel and seized 23 computers and related electronic equipment to determine whether any criminal offences had been committed. The seized items were handed over to the Panadura South Police.

The CID has taken over the investigation to conduct further inquiries into the suspected activities.
