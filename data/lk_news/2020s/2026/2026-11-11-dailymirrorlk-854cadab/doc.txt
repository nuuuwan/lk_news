Child dies after kidney surgery; both kidneys reportedly missing, Ministry faces questions

Colombo, November 11 (Daily Mirror) - Serious questions have been raised over the Ministry of Health’s handling of the death of a child at the Lady Ridgeway Hospital for Children in Borella, after post-mortem findings reportedly revealed that both kidneys had been removed during surgery, with hospital authorities yet to produce the organs.

Dr. Chamal Sanjeewa, President of the Medical Professionals' Alliance for Medical and Civil Rights, has accused the Ministry of failing to take adequate action over the incident, which occurred nearly three years ago.

The child died following kidney surgery at the hospital. Subsequent post-mortem examinations reportedly revealed that both kidneys were missing, an issue which had also been raised by the child's parents before court.

According to Dr. Sanjeewa, the child's kidneys were fused together and had been removed during the surgical procedure.

Dr. G. Wijesuriya, who was the hospital director at the time, had initially explained that the second kidney had also been removed because it was attached to the kidney that surgeons intended to remove.

However, the parents had disputed this explanation, claiming that medical examinations conducted before the child was admitted to the hospital had confirmed that the two kidneys were located separately within the abdominal cavity.

The child's death and the subsequent post-mortem findings led to serious concerns, particularly over the failure of hospital authorities to produce the removed kidneys.

The matter was also raised before court, while steps were reportedly taken at the time to suspend the Judicial Medical Officer who conducted the initial post-mortem examination.

Dr. Sanjeewa questioned why the Ministry of Health had allegedly failed to extend adequate cooperation to ensure that the law was enforced, despite nearly three years having passed since the incident and court directions having been issued.

He described the Ministry's conduct as suspicious and called on the Secretary to the Ministry of Health to initiate an internal investigation and submit all relevant investigation reports to court without further delay.

He also pointed out that court directions had previously been issued to produce the relevant health officials before it in connection with the case.

Meanwhile, a protest was held outside the Ministry of Health this morning by a group opposing the handling of the incident at the Lady Ridgeway Hospital for Children. The protesters demanded answers from the Ministry over its failure to properly address the matter.

The Daily Mirror made several attempts to contact the Director of the Lady Ridgeway Hospital for Children for comment, but was unable to reach him.

The Ministry of Health's response to the allegations was not immediately available.

It was also reported that Dr. G. Wijesuriya, who conducted the surgery, is currently overseas. However, it remains unclear whether his absence has affected the progress of the investigations or the implementation of court directions in connection with the case.
