Colombo to remove unauthorised structures obstructing drainage system

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - The Colombo Municipal Council (CMC) is taking steps to identify and remove unauthorised constructions obstructing the city’s drainage system, Colombo Mayor Vraie Cally Balthazaar said.

She said unauthorised structures remained a major obstacle to improving drainage in the city, as they restricted the flow of water and made it difficult for authorities to clean and maintain drainage canals.

The Mayor said the CMC was continuing efforts to identify such structures and remove them as part of a wider programme to improve Colombo’s drainage infrastructure.

Meanwhile, she welcomed the decision by several homeowners and companies to voluntarily remove unauthorised constructions on their properties, noting that such cooperation would support efforts to address the city’s drainage problems.
