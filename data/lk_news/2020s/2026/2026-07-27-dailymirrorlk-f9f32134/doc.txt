Roadside Garbage Dumping by Car Owners

Every morning, many people throw polythene garbage bags on roadside pavements and vacant plots while driving to work. It has become a serious health hazard and an eyesore for residents living along these roads.

This problem is not new. About 26 years ago, in Piliyandala town near the Kesbewa Urban Council, the UC had set up a designated garbage drop-off point with 3 separate containers:

These containers were regularly serviced by UC garbage collection staff. I am not sure if this system still exists as I no longer live there, but it worked well at the time.

I believe if Municipal Councils and Urban Councils across towns and suburbs implement a similar system — providing 3 segregated containers at designated points — the habit of surreptitiously dumping garbage bags on roadsides will stop. It gives car owners a proper place to dispose of waste instead of littering.

I urge the municipal authorities to consider reviving and expanding this simple, low-cost solution. Public awareness combined with convenient disposal points can make our roads cleaner and healthier.
