Kongahawatte named new Government Factories DG

Colombo, Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet of Ministers has approved the appointment of K. U. K. Kongahawatte as the new Director General of the Department of Government Factories with immediate effect.

Kongahawatte, a Grade I officer of the Sri Lanka Engineering Service, is currently serving as the Director (Works) of the Department.

He will fill the vacancy created following the retirement of former Director General S. N. B. M. Padmasiri, who retired from public service on October 31, 2026, upon reaching the age of 60.

The appointment was approved following a proposal submitted by the Minister of Housing, Construction and Water Supply.
