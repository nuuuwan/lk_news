Delmege Insurance Brokers retains No. 1 position

From left: Delmege Insurance Brokers Chief Executive Officer Sidantha Karunaratne, Vallible One Managing Director Dinusha Baskaran and Delmege Insurance Brokers Chief Operating Officer Chathura Kehelpannala

Delmege Insurance Brokers has reaffirmed its position as Sri Lanka’s No. 1 insurance broker for the third consecutive year, 2023, 2024 and 2025, according to the Insurance Regulatory Commission of Sri Lanka.

With a gross written premium of Rs.6.3 billion and a 9.37 percent overall market share, Delmege Insurance Brokers secured the leading position among 83 registered insurance brokers in Sri Lanka.

The achievement reflects the consistency, expertise and commitment of the Delmege team, together with the trust placed in the company by its clients, insurance and reinsurance partners.

Vallibel One PLC Managing Director Dinusha Bhaskaran said, “Achieving the No. 1 position is significant; sustaining it for three consecutive years is an even greater accomplishment.”

Delmege Insurance Brokers CEO Sidantha Karunaratne said, “Being ranked No. 1 for three consecutive years demonstrates consistency and the confidence our clients and partners place in us.”

The company’s professional standing has also been reflected in industry recognition. Chief Operating Officer Chathura Kehelpannala was named the Best Health Insurance Adviser at Global Insurance Excellence Awards 2025 for the Asia and MENA Region and received the Niranjan Nagendra Memorial Award for Insurance Broker Professional of the Year at Sri Lanka Insurance Brokers Association Awards 2024.

JAT Holdings PLC Group HR Manager Sudath Silva said, “Delmege Insurance Brokers has been a trusted partner in securing the most suitable staff medical insurance package for JAT Holdings PLC.”

GP Garments (Pvt.) Ltd Chief Finance and Compliance Officer Patrice Jermann said, “For more than a decade, we have relied on Delmege for professional guidance, responsive service and timely support whenever challenges arise. Congratulations to the entire Delmege team!”
