Indices lose 4.3% during the week

The ASPI closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as John Keells Holdings, Melstacorp and Richard Pieris & Company with the turnover crossing Rs. 2.1 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20.

High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in John Keells Holdings, Ceylon Hospitals and Ceylon Cold Stores. Mixed interest was observed in Hayleys Fabric, Sampath Bank and Melstacorp whilst retail interest was noted in Hela Apparel Holdings, Browns Investments and ACME Printing & Packaging. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

During the week, the ASPI and the S&P SL20 lost 4.3% and 3.6% respectively, whilst recording an average daily turnover of Rs. 3.07 Bn.

Capital Goods sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to John Keells Holdings) whilst the sector index gained 1.33%. The share price of John Keells Holdings increased by Rs 0.20 (1.01%) to close at Rs 20.00.

Food, Beverage & Tobacco sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover whilst the sector index increased by 1.59%.

Hayleys Fabric, CIC Holdings, Ceylon Hospitals and Sampath Bank were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Hayleys Fabric gained Rs 1.00 (3.31%) to close at Rs 31.20. The share price of CIC Holdings closed flat at Rs 32.10. The share price of Ceylon Hospitals recorded a gain of Rs 0.90 (1.50%) to close at Rs 60.90. The share price of Sampath Bank appreciated by Rs 1.00 (0.70%) to close at Rs 143.00.
