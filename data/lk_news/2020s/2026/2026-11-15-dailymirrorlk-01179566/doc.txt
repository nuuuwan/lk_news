Sri Lankan governmen to roll out ‘Alternative Care Policy’ in 2027 following Knuckles teenage childbirth incident

Nov 15 (Daily Mirror) - Following the shocking discovery of a 15-year- old girl and her newborn baby after an unsafe childbirth, Women and Child Affairs Minister Saroja Savithri Paulraj announced that the Ministry will implement a new ‘Alternative Care Policy’ starting in 2027 to protect vulnerable children.

Issuing a special statement on November 14, the Minister said it was a relief that both the underage mother and the newborn are currently safe. She added that relevant authorities had been instructed to ensure their immediate and ongoing physical and mental well-being.

Paulraj noted that economic, social, and political complexities—combined with a breakdown in communication between parents and children—are increasingly placing minors at risk.

She urged parents to foster open dialogue and build unwavering trust at home so children feel safe turning to their families.

The Minister also acknowledged that state institutions and frontline responders dealing with vulnerable individuals must become more accessible and trustworthy to the public.
