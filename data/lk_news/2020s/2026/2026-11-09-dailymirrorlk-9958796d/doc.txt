Special Dengue prevention week begins

Colombo, Nov. 9 (Daily Mirror) - A special National Dengue Prevention Week begins today (09), targeting 74 Medical Officer of Health (MOH) divisions identified as being at increased risk of a dengue outbreak.

The islandwide programme, organised by the National Dengue Control Unit, will run until November 14, with the participation of government institutions, security forces and voluntary organisations.

The initiative comes amid concerns over a possible rise in dengue infections, based on previous disease trends, mosquito breeding indices and changing weather conditions.

As part of the campaign, joint inspection teams will be deployed to identify and eliminate mosquito breeding sites, while abandoned properties will also be inspected and cleaned to prevent mosquito proliferation.

Special cleaning programmes will also be carried out at schools, Pirivenas, universities and religious institutions as authorities step up efforts to minimise the risk of a dengue outbreak.

The daily operations will be monitored through a dedicated operations centre to ensure the effective implementation of prevention activities.

Meanwhile, Sri Lanka has recorded 107,829 dengue cases and 81 deaths so far this year, according to the latest statistics released as of midnight on November 8.
