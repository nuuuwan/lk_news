Sri Lanka, India wrap up ‘Mitra Shakti – XII’

The 12th edition of the annual bilateral joint military exercise between the Sri Lanka Army and the Indian Army, ‘Mitra Shakti – XII’, successfully concluded on 26 November 2026 at the Army Training School, Maduru Oya, following two weeks of intensive joint training conducted from 13 to 26 November 2026.

The Sri Lanka Army was represented by a contingent of 17 Officers and 153 Other Ranks, while the Indian Army participated with 19 Officers and 150 Other Ranks. In addition, over 650 personnel, including exercise control and support staff, contributed to the successful conduct of the exercise, with the Sri Lanka Air Force extending its support throughout the programme.

The exercise included battalion-level counter-insurgency operations, integration of air support, urban and jungle warfare, live-firing drills, ambush operations, protection of military convoys, and operations in support of maintaining public order.

Furthermore, the participating troops had the opportunity to inspect Special Forces training facilities, familiarise themselves with modern combat training equipment, and engage in practical exercises designed to address a range of operational scenarios.

The final practical phase of the exercise, themed ‘Assault on Fortified Village’, was successfully conducted on 25 November 2026 at the Army Training School, Maduru Oya. The demonstration showcased the tactical capabilities, combat readiness, and coordinated operational effectiveness of the participating troops in executing a simulated military operation.

Major General K.J.N.M.P.K. Navarathne, Deputy Chief of Staff of the Sri Lanka Army, graced the occasion as the Chief Guest. The Defence Adviser and the Assistant Defence Adviser of the High Commission of India were also present, along with other distinguished invitees and senior military Officers.
