China’s former Vice Premier Liu Yandong has died

Reuters - Liu Yandong, China's former vice ​premier and a ‌former member of the elite Politburo, died in Beijing ​on Wednesday, state-run ​Xinhua news agency said.

Liu, who ⁠was 80 when she died, was widely ​regarded as the most ​prominent and highest-ranking female official in China during much ​of her tenure in ​the country's male-dominated political leadership.

She ‌was ⁠one of only a handful of women ever to serve as a ​full ​member ⁠of the Politburo since the founding ​of the People's ​Republic ⁠in 1949. The current 21 members of the ⁠Politburo ​are all ​men.
