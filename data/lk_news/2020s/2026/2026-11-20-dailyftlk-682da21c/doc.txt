Namal further remanded till 2 Dec.

Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) Leader and MP Namal Rajapaksa, an accused in the Airbus transaction case, was yesterday further remanded till 2 December, 2026 by the Colombo Permanent High Court Trial-at-Bar.

The order followed submissions by Deputy Solicitor General Janaka Bandara, appearing for the prosecution, that no exceptional circumstances had been presented to court to justify granting bail. President’s Counsel Romesh de Silva, appearing for the accused, had sought bail for Rajapaksa.

The case was heard before a three-judge bench comprising High Court Judges Priyantha Liyanage (President), Thilakaratne Bandara and Nuwan Dhammika Bandara.
