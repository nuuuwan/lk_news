Man arrested in Wattala with over 18kg of Ice and heroin

Colombo, Oct. 11 (Daily Mirror) - A 24-year-old man has been arrested in Mabola, Wattala, with more than 18 kilograms of crystal methamphetamine, commonly known as ‘Ice’, and heroin, police said.

The arrest was made by officers of the Colombo Crimes Division (CCD) following a tip-off.

Police recovered 9kg and 229g of Ice and 9kg and 264g of heroin from the suspect. A weighing scale believed to have been used to measure narcotics and a mobile phone were also seized.
