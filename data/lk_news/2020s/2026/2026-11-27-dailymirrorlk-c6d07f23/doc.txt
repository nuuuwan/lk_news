Hayleys Mobility delivers electric vans to Melwa

Hasith Prematillake handing over the SRM X30L electric vans to Arunasalam Abishake, Executive Senior Project Manager of Melwa Conglomerate

Hayleys Mobility Limited has handed over a fleet of SRM X30L electric passenger vans to Melwa Conglomerate, supporting the company’s transition towards electric mobility for its corporate transport requirements. Hasith Prematillake, Managing Director, Hayleys Fentons Limited said, “Electric mobility is now a strategic business decision, not a symbolic one, and fleet operators are discerning customers. Their endorsement is the clearest measure of the standard we hold ourselves to. We aim to be the partner, Sri Lankan enterprises turn to as the nation’s transport future takes shape, and we will continue to earn that standing with every fleet we deliver.”

Roshani Dharmaratne, Executive Director of Hayleys Mobility Limited added, “Backed by the engineering strength of Hayleys Fentons and the 148-year legacy of the Hayleys Group, we bring that assurance to every vehicle we deliver.”
