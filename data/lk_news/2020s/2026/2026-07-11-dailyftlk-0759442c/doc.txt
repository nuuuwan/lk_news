Dialog Enterprise, NCE renew strategic  partnership to accelerate digital transformation

From left: Dialog Enterprise Head of SME Products, Converged Connectivity Sameera Wijerathna, NCE Chief Operating Officer Inok Perera, Secretary General/CEO Shiham Marikar, Dialog Enterprise Group Chief Officer Navin Pieris and Vice President/Head of Innovation and Platform Business Viranga Seneviratne

Dialog Enterprise, the corporate solutions arm of Dialog Axiata PLC and Sri Lanka’s number one ICT solutions provider, has renewed its strategic partnership with the National Chamber of Exporters of Sri Lanka (NCE), reaffirming its commitment to empowering Sri Lankan exporters with advanced digital technologies, industry expertise, and innovation-driven solutions.

The renewed partnership builds on a successful collaboration established in 2025, under which Dialog Enterprise served as the official Digital Technology Partner of NCE. Through this partnership, both organisations have worked closely to enhance the digital capabilities of Sri Lankan exporters by facilitating access to cutting-edge ICT solutions, knowledge-sharing initiatives, and technology-led business transformation programs. As part of the renewed collaboration, Dialog Enterprise will continue to support NCE and its membership base with a comprehensive portfolio of Digital Transformation tools including converged connectivity, cloud services, cybersecurity, data analytics, artificial intelligence, and Internet of Things (IoT) technologies. The partnership will also facilitate educational forums, industry workshops, and expert-led sessions designed to help exporters navigate evolving global market demands and accelerate their Digital Transformation journeys specially focusing on the Aspiring Exporters who are Small and Medium Entrepreneurs (SME).

The partnership reflects the shared vision of Dialog Enterprise and NCE to strengthen Sri Lanka’s export sector through technology adoption, innovation, and digital readiness, enabling exporters to enhance operational efficiency, improve competitiveness, and unlock new opportunities in international markets.

Commenting on the partnership, NCE Secretary General/CEO Shiham Marikar noted that the renewed collaboration will further strengthen the chamber’s efforts to equip exporters with the knowledge, tools, and technologies required to thrive in an increasingly digital global economy.

Speaking on the partnership renewal, Dialog Enterprise Group Chief Officer Navin Pieris said that the continued collaboration with NCE underscores Dialog Enterprise’s commitment to empowering Sri Lankan businesses with future-ready digital solutions that drive sustainable growth and global competitiveness.

Through this renewed partnership, Dialogue Enterprise and NCE will continue to work together to foster innovation, technology adoption, and digital excellence across Sri Lanka’s export community, contributing towards the nation’s broader economic growth and Digital Transformation agenda.
