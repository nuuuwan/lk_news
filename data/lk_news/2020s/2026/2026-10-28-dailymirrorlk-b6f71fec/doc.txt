Krrish deal case: Namal Rajapaksa’s bail order on November 3

Colombo, Oct. 28 (Daily Mirror) - Colombo Chief Magistrate Asanga S. Bodaragama today fixed November 3 to deliver the order on the bail application filed on behalf of Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa, who is in remand custody over the Krrish deal.

The Magistrate made the order after considering submissions made by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption and the suspect’s counsel.

Namal Rajapaksa was remanded over allegations that he had accepted a bribe of Rs. 100 million in connection with facilitating the transfer of land situated at Transworks Square in Colombo Fort to the Krrish Group for a multi-purpose development project.
