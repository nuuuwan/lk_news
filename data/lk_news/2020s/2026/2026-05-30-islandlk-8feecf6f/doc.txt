Union Assurance agency premier league celebrates sport and fellowship

Union Assurance, the country’s longest-standing private life insurer, brought together its agency network in a celebration of sport and camaraderie with the staging of the Union Assurance Agency Premier League for the second consecutive year at the NCC Grounds.

The event drew more than 900 participants, including senior management of the agency business, and featured competition alongside community engagement under the banner of sport and fellowship.

Part of the company’s agent value proposition, the Premier League is designed to promote holistic well-being, professional development, internal community building and long-term engagement across its nationwide agency network.

Teams representing Union Assurance’s islandwide agency divisions competed in both male and female categories. This year’s edition was extended beyond the field of play, with agents joined by their families, transforming the tournament into a wider social gathering. A dedicated children’s carnival added colour to the event, providing entertainment for younger attendees.

Senior leadership from the agency division also took part, underscoring the organisation’s emphasis on agent recognition and engagement.

In the men’s category, Team Chilaw of the North Western 01 Zone emerged champions, while Victory Queens of the Uva-Central Zone secured the women’s title. Both teams were awarded the Union Assurance Agency Premier League trophy, a perpetual shield that will be defended annually as the tournament builds its legacy.

Chief Agency Officer Imtiyaz Aniff said the event reflected the camaraderie and unity within the agency force, noting that the inclusion of families added a deeper dimension to the gathering.
