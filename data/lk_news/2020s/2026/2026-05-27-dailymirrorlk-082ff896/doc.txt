Seela programme for Vesak Week

A Seela programme was held at Gampaha Sri Bodhi Primary School in conjunction with Vesak Week. Monks representing each class level attended the programme, while an afternoon alms-giving for 25 monks was organised at the school’s main hall. The programme was attended by the school’s Principal, Deputy Principal, Assistant Principal, academic and non-academic staff, as well as parents, students, development society members and representatives of the alumni association. Organisers said the event was conducted with the blessings and support of all stakeholders connected to the school community. Pix by Kushan Pathiraja
