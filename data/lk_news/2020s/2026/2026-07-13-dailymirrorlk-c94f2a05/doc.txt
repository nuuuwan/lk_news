Rotary Club of Colombo West welcomes new President

The Rotary Club of Colombo West installed Rtn. Harsha Rajan, PHT, as its 66th President at a ceremony held at the Mahogany Ballroom, Cinnamon Grand Colombo. The event was graced by District Governor RID 3220, Kumar Sundararaj, as the Chief Guest, while A. S. Venkesh, TRF Trustee (2026–2030) and RI Director (2021–2023), delivered the keynote address. The installation marked the beginning of a new Rotary year under the theme, "Create Lasting Impact." Pix by Kithsiri De Mel
