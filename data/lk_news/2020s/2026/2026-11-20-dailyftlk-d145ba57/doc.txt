People’s Bank and Justice Ministry partner to introduce digital gateway for Court payments

People’s Bank and the Justice and National Integration Ministry have signed an agreement to implement an Internet Payment Gateway (IPG) for accepting court-related payments electronically, marking another significant milestone in Sri Lanka’s judicial digital transformation journey.

Justice and National Integration Ministry Secretary Ayesha Jinasena PC and People’s Bank Chairman Chandana Guniyangoda exchanged the agreement recently at the Justice Ministry premises in Colombo 10. People’s Bank Payments and Cards Deputy General Manager I.K. Indika, along with Card Centre officials and representatives of the Justice Ministry, also participated in the event.

The partnership further strengthens the longstanding collaboration between the two institutions in introducing convenient, secure and efficient digital payment facilities across the country’s judicial system.

The Internet Payment Gateway will further expand these facilities by enabling court users to make applicable payments through online channels, reducing the need for physical visits to court payment counters or bank branches. This is expected to provide greater convenience to litigants, Attorneys-at-Law and other members of the public accessing judicial services.

The initiative is aligned with the Government’s broader digital transformation agenda and the E-Courts Project, which aims to modernise Sri Lanka’s judicial administration through technology-driven solutions, improve operational efficiency, enhance transparency and facilitate better access to justice.

“The digital transformation of the judicial system is an important priority in our efforts to improve the efficiency, accessibility and transparency of the administration of justice. The introduction of an Internet Payment Gateway for court-related payments is a significant step towards creating a more convenient and technology-enabled judicial environment. This initiative will reduce the administrative burden associated with traditional payment methods and provide greater convenience to the public, including litigants and legal practitioners. We appreciate the continued cooperation and support of People’s Bank in facilitating the digital transformation of the judicial sector,” Jinasena said.

“People’s Bank is proud to be a strategic banking partner in supporting the digital transformation of Sri Lanka’s judicial system. Our collaboration with the Justice Ministry has already demonstrated encouraging results. Today’s agreement represents an important next step in expanding digital payment acceptance through an Internet Payment Gateway,” Guniyangoda said.

“Our vision is to move beyond conventional payment methods and establish a comprehensive digital payment ecosystem that provides convenient, secure and accessible payment facilities to citizens, while conforming to the highest standards in governance, risk management and compliance. We also look forward to exploring opportunities to extend these facilities through mobile banking, internet banking, QR-based payments and other digital payment channels, in line with the Ministry’s requirements and the country’s broader digitalisation objectives. People’s Bank remains committed to supporting the Government’s efforts to build a digitally empowered Sri Lanka.”

The proposed Internet Payment Gateway will provide a foundation for further integration with the Ministry’s digital platforms, including relevant E-Courts services and electronic case-related processes. Subject to future requirements and technical integration, opportunities may also be explored to facilitate multiple payment channels, automated payment confirmations and electronic receipts, together with improved transaction monitoring and reconciliation facilities.

The partnership further reinforces People’s Bank’s commitment to supporting national digitalisation initiatives and providing innovative banking and payment solutions to Government institutions, businesses and the general public.

Through this collaboration, People’s Bank and the Justice and National Integration Ministry aim to contribute towards a more accessible, efficient and digitally enabled judicial system, ensuring that technology plays an increasingly important role in improving the delivery of public services in Sri Lanka.
