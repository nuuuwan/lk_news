Anna Weerakoon Karunatilleke as Sri Lanka’s ambassador for Maritime SheEO.

Anna Weerakoon Karunatilleke has spent her career breaking barriers in one of the world’s most traditionally male-dominated sectors. Having made history as Sri Lanka’s first female Marine Engineer, her recent appointment as an Ambassador for Maritime SheEO marks the latest chapter in a life dedicated to technical excellence and social progress.

Maritime SheEO is a global community dedicated to empowering women leaders across the maritime sector. Through high-impact conferences, a structured mentorship programme, and a community of over 800 members, the organisation provides tangible leadership pathways for everyone from students to CXOs. Notably, its dedicated research wing produces data-driven insights to inform policy, including a significant 2025 study on the inclusion of women in the Sri Lankan maritime industry. By offering a free, community-led platform, Maritime SheEO ensures that access to opportunity is never a barrier to driving systemic change.

Anna’s influence extends far beyond the shipyard; she is a published author and an international speaker who has consistently contributed to global conversations on maritime safety and gender inclusion.
