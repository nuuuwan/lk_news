Gallagher doubles  Sri Lanka office space;  signals further investment

Global insurance brokerage and risk management firm Gallagher has doubled its office space in Sri Lanka, with the opening of a new office at Orion City IT Park, reaffirming its commitment to expanding operations and investing in local talent.

The new office, located on the 10th floor of Orion City IT Park, is twice the size of the company’s previous premises on the 12th floor.

The expansion marks another milestone in Gallagher’s growth in Sri Lanka since entering the country in 2022. The company started with a small team of 12 technology professionals and has since expanded its workforce to more than 130 employees.

Speaking at the opening ceremony, Gallagher Chief Information Officer EMEA Brent Kruger said the expansion reflected the company’s confidence in Sri Lanka and its skilled workforce.

“I’m proud of the Gallagher team and what we have built here in Sri Lanka. The support the team provides is highly valuable to our global business,” he said.

He said the expansion demonstrated Gallagher’s long-term commitment to Sri Lanka and its intention to continue investing in local professionals.

“It shows our commitment to Sri Lanka and the fantastic skill sets that we have here. Gallagher plans to continue to invest in good people,” he said.

Founded in 1927, Gallagher is a global insurance brokerage, risk management and consulting services firm headquartered in the United States. It employs 73,000 people and operates in approximately 130 countries.

Gallagher Director Operations Malsha Jayamaha said the opening of the new office was a significant milestone for the company’s Sri Lankan operations.

“This is more than just opening a new office; it’s a reflection of how far we’ve come and the confidence Gallagher continues to place in Sri Lanka. For our employees, it’s about creating an environment that encourages collaboration, supports productivity and gives us room to grow. It’s a milestone our entire team can take pride in.

“Sri Lanka has a strong pool of skilled, adaptable and capable professionals who bring tremendous value to global organisations. Our talent, commitment and ability to work across international markets make Sri Lanka an attractive destination, with significant potential for further growth.

“To prospective talent, Gallagher offers an opportunity to grow, build meaningful careers and be part of a global organisation with an exciting future,” she said.
