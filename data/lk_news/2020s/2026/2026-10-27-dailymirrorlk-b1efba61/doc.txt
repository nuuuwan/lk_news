Rs. 5 million compensation for prison officers affected by Negombo clash

Colombo, Oct. 27 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet has approved special compensation of up to Rs. 5 million for prison officers who lost their lives or suffered disabilities during clashes at the Negombo Prison on July 5 and 6, 2026.

The decision was taken following recommendations submitted by a committee appointed at the Cabinet meeting held on August 3, 2026, under the leadership of the Secretary to the Ministry of Justice and National Integration.

The committee was tasked with making recommendations on compensation and relief for prison officers who died or sustained injuries while on duty during the clashes. The Minister of Justice and National Integration subsequently presented the committee’s report to the Cabinet, which approved the implementation of its recommendations.

Under the approved scheme, the families of officers who died in the clashes will receive Rs. 5 million for each deceased officer. Officers who have become completely disabled will also be eligible for Rs. 5 million in compensation.

Officers who suffer partial disabilities, with their earning capacity reduced by up to 100 per cent, will receive Rs. 4 million, while those who become temporarily partially disabled will be granted Rs. 3 million.

The special compensation will be provided in addition to payments made under Public Administration Circular No. 22/93.

The Cabinet has also approved measures to strengthen the Prison Tactical Force in the wake of the incident.

These include amending the Prisons Ordinance to provide the force with formal legal recognition, recruiting an adequate number of suitable officers, providing specialised training and modern equipment, and establishing a headquarters for the force at a suitable location.
