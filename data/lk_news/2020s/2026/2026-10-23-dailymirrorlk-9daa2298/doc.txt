Complaint lodged over Sri Lanka Police Facebook post

Colombo, Oct. 23 (Daily Mirror) - A complaint was lodged with the Human Rights Commission of Sri Lanka yesterday (22) over a Facebook post published on the official page of the Sri Lanka Police, which the complainants described as threatening.

The post, titled “Think twice before you open your mouth,” prompted representatives of ‘YOUTH FOR CHANGE’ to raise concerns with the Commission.

The complaint was lodged by the organization’s International Affairs Secretary Shakthi Makavita, National Committee member Udara Sandaruwan and Colombo District Committee member Terence Rodrigo.
