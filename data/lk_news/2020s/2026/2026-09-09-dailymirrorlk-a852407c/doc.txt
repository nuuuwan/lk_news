India, Sri Lanka to exchange three key defence agreements today

Colombo, September 09 (Daily Mirror) - Three agreements covering military training, defence education and the maintenance of air defence equipment are scheduled to be exchanged during Indian Defence Minister Rajnath Singh’s official visit to Sri Lanka, Foreign Affairs Minister Vijitha Herath said.

The Indian Defence Minister arrived in Sri Lanka yesterday (8) for a three-day official visit.

Speaking at a Cabinet press briefing, Herath said Singh’s three-day visit is aimed at strengthening broader bilateral relations and mutual trust between Sri Lanka and India, rather than focusing solely on defence or strategic matters.

“The Indian Defence Minister is a very mature political leader and Minister in Indian politics. He is visiting Sri Lanka primarily to further strengthen relations between Sri Lanka and India, and not solely for defence matters,” he said.

Singh is scheduled to hold bilateral discussions with President Anura Kumara Dissanayake this morning (September 9), during which the three agreements are expected to be exchanged.

The first agreement will establish cooperation between the Sri Lanka Cadet Corps and its Indian counterpart, facilitating mutual training, joint programmes and other collaborative exchanges.

The second agreement will establish formal cooperation between Sri Lanka’s National Defence College and the National Defence College in New Delhi. It is expected to facilitate knowledge sharing, the exchange of instructional staff and the enhancement of defence education programmes for officers.

The third agreement relates to maintenance and repair assistance for air defence equipment. Herath said India had previously donated 24 40mm L70 anti-aircraft guns to the Sri Lanka Air Force between 2000 and 2006.

“Out of the 24 air defence weapons provided to the Air Force, maintenance work for 18 units was carried out free of charge during the past period. These 24 air defence weapons were provided to us as grant aid, along with support and sponsorship for their repair and maintenance.

The third agreement will be signed for the maintenance of the remaining six units. Mumbai-based company Bharat Electronics Limited will be involved in carrying out the maintenance work” he said.

Following the meeting with the President, Minister Singh is scheduled to hold discussions with senior Sri Lankan officials at the Ministry of Defence before meeting Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya.

Responding to questions on regional maritime security, fishing disputes and reports of foreign research vessels entering Sri Lankan waters, Herath said the visit was primarily focused on strengthening goodwill, mutual understanding and high-level dialogue between the two countries.

He said further details of the bilateral discussions between Singh and Sri Lankan leaders would be shared following the conclusion of the scheduled meetings.
