South Asia’s first 4,000-seat Air Dome set to open next month

Colombo, Nov 25 (Daily Mirror) - South Asia’s first Air Dome structure has been established at the Colombo Port City, with the specialised indoor event venue expected to commence operations by the end of December.

Designed to accommodate up to 4,000 people, the facility is intended to host conferences, exhibitions, business meetings and other large-scale events. However, several construction works remain to be completed before it becomes fully operational.

CHEC Port City Colombo Executive Director and Deputy General Manager of Land Development and Marketing Shalaka Wijeratne said the venue had been developed based on similar facilities operating in international cities, including Dubai.

The Air Dome is expected to function as an interim event venue until the proposed permanent convention and exhibition centre at Colombo Port City is constructed.

According to the development plans, the permanent centre will feature an indoor multi-purpose hall with a capacity of 4,000 people and an outdoor event space capable of accommodating 5,000 people.

The Colombo Port City master plan also proposes the construction of a star-class hotel with between 400 and 450 rooms adjacent to the convention and exhibition centre.
