President says proposed organised crime detention period to be reduced to 48 hours

Colombo, Nov. 5 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake said that the proposed detention period for suspects under the draft Prevention of Organised Crimes Bill will be reduced from 72 hours to 48 hours.

Addressing an event  organised under the “Ratama Ekata” national operation, the President said the government was prepared to reconsider certain provisions of the proposed legislation following concerns raised by various parties.

He also said the provision relating to tattoos, which are used in some countries as a method of identifying members of organised criminal groups, could be removed if it was considered unnecessary in the Sri Lankan context

President Dissanayake said he and his political movement had consistently opposed the Prevention of Terrorism Act (PTA), adding that they had personally experienced the impact of repressive laws.

He stressed that the government had no intention of introducing legislation similar to the PTA to combat organised crime.

However, he explained that the proposed Prevention of Organised Crimes Bill had been presented to Parliament because of the genuine legal need to address organised criminal activities effectively.

The President reaffirmed that the government was willing to engage in discussions with human rights activists and other stakeholders regarding concerns and recommendations on the draft legislation.

He emphasised that the government had no intention of suppressing the public or violating people's fundamental rights.
