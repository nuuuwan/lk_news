Navy seizes drugs worth over Rs. 55 Billion this year

Colombo, Sep. 25 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Navy has seized illicit drugs estimated to be worth more than Rs. 55 billion through maritime and coastal anti-narcotics operations conducted so far this year, Navy Spokesman Commander Chaminda Walakuluge said.

According to the Navy, around 300 suspects and 50 vessels allegedly linked to local and international drug trafficking networks have been taken into custody during the operations.

Detailing the massive hauls recovered during these operations, the Navy Spokesman revealed that forces have intercepted over 1,080 kilograms of heroin, 590 kilograms of crystal methamphetamine (commonly known as Ice), and 256 kilograms of cocaine.

In addition to these highly addictive substances, the Navy confiscated over 4 million narcotic pills, 51 kilograms of hashish, and a staggering 55,000 kilograms (55 tonnes) of smuggled beedi leaves.

A recent operation in the Gurunagar lagoon area resulted in the detection of around 800 kilograms of cannabis.

Drug trafficking networks use different maritime routes to bring contraband into Sri Lanka. Cannabis and beedi leaves are mainly smuggled through the Northern Sea, while heroin, crystal methamphetamine and cocaine are primarily transported through the Arabian Sea.

The Navy has strengthened surveillance and interception operations in these areas to prevent illegal consignments from reaching the shore, he said.

Alongside maritime enforcement, the Navy is also conducting community awareness programmes aimed at preventing drug abuse, particularly among young people.

The Navy said its operations are focused on disrupting drug trafficking networks, supporting legal action against those involved in the trade and protecting communities from the impact of illicit drugs
