Another bag full of promises unveiled

President Dissanayake yesterday read out another ambitious budget for the people of Sri Lanka. What is noteworthy is that in almost all of these budget speeches the ground reality isn’t being captured properly. Or at least they disregard it as they fear they would become unpopular among masses. While the President read out allocations for the 2027 budget a minister people are finding it increasingly difficult to make ends meet. Promising budgets are welcome if people can be benefitted. There is limited consultation about the needs and wants of people and it is a doubt as to whether people’s needs are being covered in the national budget. Already fuel prices have soared to exorbitant levels and the cost of living is on the rise. The incumbent government did promise relief for people but it is easier said than done. Apart from high cost of living people have to face the tax burden as well. Taxing people unnecessarily is quite unrealistic in a country like ours. The bribery commission keeps extending tax declarations for people because there has to be some monitoring and streamlining of taxes to begin with. The government should look at providing relief to people while continuing on its projects and securing the treasury.
