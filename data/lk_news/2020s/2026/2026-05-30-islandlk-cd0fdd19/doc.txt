SLT-MOBITEL posts strong Q1 growth with 53% profit surge

SLT-MOBITEL has reported a strong start to 2026, posting double-digit revenue growth and sharp gains in profitability in the first quarter, driven largely by rising demand for broadband services and improved operational performance.

The Group’s consolidated revenue rose 10.6% year-on-year to LKR 30.8 billion in Q1 2026, up from LKR 27.9 billion in the corresponding period last year. Gross profit increased 14.1% to LKR 14.6 billion, supported by sustained growth in internet and data services.

Operating profit climbed 39.1% to LKR 5.1 billion, while profit before tax surged 55.4% to LKR 4.2 billion. Net profit rose 53.3% to LKR 3.1 billion, underscoring what the company described as a “decisive turnaround” in performance.

At company level, SLT PLC recorded revenue of LKR 19.7 billion, a 10.6% increase year-on-year. Operating profit rose 36% to LKR 3.5 billion, while net profit also grew 53.3% to LKR 2.1 billion.

Mobitel, the Group’s mobile arm, reported revenue growth of 9.9%, with operating profit up 54.7% and net profit increasing 63.7%. The performance was driven by a 28% rise in broadband revenue, alongside gains in value-added services, roaming, and solutions, despite a decline in voice and international termination revenues.

Group costs rose 7.6% amid higher electricity tariffs, fuel prices and currency pressures. However, revenue growth outpaced cost increases, while lower finance costs and foreign exchange gains, compared to losses in Q1 2025, further strengthened the bottom line.

Chairman Dr. Mothilal de Silva said the results reflected the Group’s resilience and strategic direction, adding that SLT-MOBITEL was committed to driving innovation, connectivity and sustainable value creation.

CEO Riyaaz Rasheed said the Group was well-positioned to expand digital services and advance AI and 5G-led innovation, noting continued momentum in broadband growth and subscriber gains across Mobitel’s operations.
