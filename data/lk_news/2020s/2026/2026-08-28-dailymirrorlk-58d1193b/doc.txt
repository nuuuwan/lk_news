Missing woman turns up at police station with marriage certificate

Colombo, Aug. 28 (Daily Mirror) - A young woman from Pidurupitiya in Monaragala, who had been reported missing from her home for five days, turned up at the Mahakalugolla Police Station and informed police that she had got married.

The woman’s father had lodged a complaint with police stating that his daughter had gone missing from home on Sunday (23).

During investigations, police found that the woman had eloped with her lover and subsequently married him. She later went to the Mahakalugolla Police Station and produced her marriage certificate to confirm that she had married.
