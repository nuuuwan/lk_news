LAUGFS Gas hikes cylinder prices by up to Rs. 1,775

LAUGFS Gas PLC has increased the prices of its 12.5kg and 5kg domestic LPG cylinders with effect from midnight today (13), citing higher international LPG procurement costs.

Accordingly, the price of a 12.5kg cylinder has been increased by Rs. 1,775 from Rs. 4,965 to Rs. 6,740, while the price of a 5kg cylinder has gone up by Rs. 213 from Rs. 1,775 to Rs. 1,988.

The company said rising LPG prices in the international market had increased the cost of securing shipments to Sri Lanka.

LAUGFS Gas said it would make every effort to ensure a continuous supply of LPG refills through its dealer network.

It added that domestic LPG prices would be adjusted in line with prevailing international market prices.

Customers can call 1345 for information on refill availability and district-wise prices.
