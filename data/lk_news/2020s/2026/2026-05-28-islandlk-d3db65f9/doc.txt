Dr. Binod Chaudhary champions Global Business Leadership

Dr. Binod Chaudhary, Chairman of CG Corp Global and Nepal’s preeminent billionaire, shared profound insights on navigating the complexities of global business leadership at Chairman &CEO Forum, organized by the Sri Lanka Institute of Directors (SLID) together with Union Bank and Port City. At the event as a symbolic gesture Dr. Chaudhary presented a signed copy of his book Made in Nepal, which chronicles his journey of building a global conglomerate from the Himalayas to Krishan Balendra, Chairperson of John Keells Holdings PLC.
