President signals further PAYE tax relief in 2027 Budget

Colombo, Nov 07 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake said the government expects to further increase the Pay-As-You-Earn (PAYE) tax threshold through the 2027 Budget, which is scheduled to be presented to Parliament next week.

Addressing the National Trade Union Convention in Colombo today (07), the President said the existing PAYE tax threshold had been increased from Rs. 100,000 to Rs. 150,000, and the government intends to raise it further to provide additional relief to salaried employees.

He said the government recognises the significant contribution made by the private sector to the national economy and is paying particular attention to the economic well-being of its employees.

The President said discussions had been held with private-sector institutions and business associations, following which steps were taken to increase employees' basic salaries.

However, he acknowledged that increasing basic salaries alone was insufficient to provide employees with meaningful financial relief, adding that the government had also considered supplementary allowances.

He said PAYE tax deductions on higher salaries remained an important concern and that a further adjustment to the tax threshold was expected in the upcoming Budget.

President Dissanayake stressed that the government had a responsibility to protect the interests and economic security of employees in all sectors, including the state, semi-government, plantation and private sectors.
