INSEE Sanstha Cement crowned Sri Lanka’s Most Awarded Cement Brand in 2025

-Showcases brand as symbol of trust, sustainability, and innovation in Sri Lanka’s construction industry-

As the only homegrown local brand, INSEE Sanstha Cement dominated industry awards in 2025, securing top honours for quality, sustainability, and innovation, and reinforcing its status as Sri Lanka’s leading and best‑performing cement brand.

At the SLIM Kantar People’s Awards, INSEE Sanstha was once again named ‘People’s Housing and Construction Brand of the Year’, an unprecedented 14th consecutive win. The recognition proves unmatched consistency for the brand. It positions INSEE Sanstha as the undisputed leader in Sri Lanka’s construction sector and the brand’s enduring trust among builders, contractors, engineers, masons, and homeowners throughout Sri Lanka.

Adding to the historic achievement, INSEE Sanstha was recognised as the ‘Sustainable Cement Brand of the Year’ by the Ceylon Institute of Builders (CIOB), affirming leadership in the brand’s ability to balance technical excellence with environmentally responsible construction solutions. The brand also received the coveted ‘People’s Pinnacle – Best Cement Brand of the Year 2025’, further cementing its reputation as the industry’s benchmark for performance and innovation.
