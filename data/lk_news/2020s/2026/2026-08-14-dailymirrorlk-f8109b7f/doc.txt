Lal Kantha says he will not contest next parliamentary election from Kandy

Agriculture Minister K.D. Lal Kantha has said he does not intend to contest the next parliamentary election from the Kandy District, while asserting that the National People’s Power (NPP) government will continue to remain in power.

Speaking to the media, Lal Kantha was responding to questions regarding recent remarks by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake that he had not assumed office with the intention of remaining in power indefinitely and was prepared to take political risks, even if they resulted in the loss of his position.

Lal Kantha said there was no contradiction between the President’s remarks and his own assertion that the current government would continue in power.

He explained that the continuation of a political movement or government and the continuation of an individual in office were two separate matters.

According to Lal Kantha, his decision not to contest another parliamentary election from Kandy should therefore not be interpreted as an indication that the NPP government would lose power.
