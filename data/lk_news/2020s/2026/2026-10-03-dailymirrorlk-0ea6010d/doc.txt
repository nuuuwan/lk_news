Yield curve holds steady as market activity stays muted

The secondary market reflected a quiet trading day with limited activity, while the yield curve stood broadly unchanged.

Over the 2030 segment, both the 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 maturities traded between 11.15 percent-11.20 percent. Moving ahead, 01.02.2031 and 15.12.2032 changed hands at 11.25 percent and 11.70 percent, respectively. Finally, at the belly-end, the 15.10.2034 bond traded at 12.00 percent.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee appreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.330.62/US dollar, compared to Rs.330.70/US dollar seen earlier. The Sri Lankan rupee appreciated against other major currencies such as GBP, EUR, CNY and AUD, while marginally depreciated against JPY. Market liquidity in the banking system expanded to Rs.360.11 billion, compared to Rs.355.13 billion recorded previously.
