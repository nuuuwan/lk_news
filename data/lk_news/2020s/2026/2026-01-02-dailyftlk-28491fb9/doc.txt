President pays homage to Sacred Tooth Relic

President Anura Kumara Dissanayake yesterday visited the historic Sri Dalada Maligawa in Kandy, where he paid homage to the Sacred Tooth Relic and received blessings for the New Year. Thereafter, the President called on the Mahanayake Theros of the Malwathu and Asgiri Chapters and received their blessings.
