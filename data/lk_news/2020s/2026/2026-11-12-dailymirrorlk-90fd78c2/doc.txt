Yoshitha Rajapaksa’s case postponed till November 26 for further evidence

Colombo, Nov. 12 (Daily Mirror) - Colombo High Court Judge Udesh Ranatunga today fixed November 26 for the resumption of further evidence in the case filed against Yoshitha Rajapaksa under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act over allegations of acquiring assets through unlawful means.

Although the case was scheduled for further evidence today, President’s Counsel Sampath Mendis, appearing for the accused, informed court that his client was suffering from a back-related medical condition and was therefore unable to appear in court today.

The President’s Counsel also submitted a medical certificate to court in support of his submission.

At that stage, Deputy Solicitor General Janaka Bandara, appearing for the prosecution, informed court that the prosecution had expected to conclude presenting its evidence today.

He further informed court that only three prosecution witnesses remained to be called and that the prosecution intended to summon all three witnesses on the next hearing date.

Having considered the submissions, the High Court Judge fixed November 26 for the resumption of the trial and directed the witnesses to appear before court on that date.

The Attorney General had filed the case against Yoshitha Rajapaksa, alleging that he had committed an offence under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act by spending over Rs. 730 million to purchase five parcels of land situated in Dehiwala and Ratmalana.
