Vallibel One lifts direct stake in Fortress Resorts to 49.93%

Vallibel One PLC raised its direct stake in The Fortress Resorts PLC to 49.93 percent after buying 34.7 million shares through market transactions, the company said yesterday.

The purchase on October 21 brought its combined holding with subsidiaries LB Finance PLC and Royal Ceramics Lanka PLC to 53.89 percent, Vallibel One said in a stock exchange filing.

The company acquired 34,747,249 shares from two parties acting in concert.

Its subsidiaries’ existing holdings account for the remaining 3.96 percentage points of the combined stake.
