Prime Group sweeps 3 of LMD’s most coveted distinctions

Named as Best of the Best Brand in the Real Estate Industry, Most Loved Corporate Brand and Most Loved Service Brand

Prime Group has swept three of LMD’s most coveted distinctions being named as Best of the Best Brand in the Real Estate Industry, Most Loved Corporate Brand and Most Loved Service Brand.

The triple recognition consolidates the company’s position as Sri Lanka’s true leader in modern real estate. With over 30 years of consistent delivery, the company continues to set the benchmark for customer trust, innovation and market leadership, a standing built on the confidence the customers place in the brand.

Prime Group CEO Ruminda Randeniya said, “To be named in three LMD categories is recognition we owe entirely to our customers. For over 30 years, families and investors have believed in us with some of the most important decisions of their lives and this honour reflects that confidence. Our task now is to keep sustaining it, delivering on every promise, adopting the right technology and carrying the Prime name into new markets at home and abroad.”

Prime Group, the real leader in modern real estate in Sri Lanka, carries a 30‑year legacy of contributing to the country’s urban development and economic progress. Over the past three decades, the group has delivered more than 70 housing and apartment developments and over 10,000 land developments in residential, commercial and lifestyle sectors, shaping modern living and a working environment throughout Sri Lanka.

Customer loyalty remains at the heart of Prime Group’s success. Repeat buyers, referrals and steady confidence in the brand highlight transparency and reliability which differentiates Prime from its competitors. Even during periods of economic uncertainty, the company has sustained strong sales momentum, a direct measure of enduring trust placed in the company by Sri Lankan families and investors.
