MBC Networks names Buddhi as CEO

The Capital Maharaja Group has announced the appointment of Buddhi Wickramanayake as the Chief Executive Officer of MBC Networks Ltd.

Having been an integral part of the organisation’s media operations over the years, Buddhi brings with him extensive industry experience, strong leadership, and a deep understanding of the evolving media landscape.

MBC Networks includes market giants like Sirasa FM, Shakthi FM, Yes FM, Y FM, and Legends 96.6.

The newly appointed CEO brings nearly three decades of unbroken, high-performance history within the Capital Maharaja Group, representing a rare blend of deep institutional loyalty and disruptive entrepreneurial drive.
