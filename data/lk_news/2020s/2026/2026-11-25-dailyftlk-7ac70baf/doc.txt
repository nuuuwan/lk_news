Cabinet approves UK-Sri Lanka Customs cooperation MoU

The Cabinet of Ministers has approved entering into a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between Sri Lanka Customs and the United Kingdom’s His Majesty’s Revenue and Customs (HMRC) to strengthen institutional capacity and support trade facilitation reforms.The proposed agreement aims to provide technical assistance and capacity building to Sri Lanka Customs for implementing the World Trade Organisation’s (WTO) Trade Facilitation Agreement.

The cooperation will include; technical assistance, specialised training, workshops, knowledge sharing and educational visits to support customs modernisation and trade-related reforms.

The proposal submitted by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake, in his capacity as the Finance, Planning and Economic Development Minister.
