Rs.30Bn owed by university dons over bond violations: COPE

Colombo, Nov. 10 (Daily Mirror) - As much as Rs.30 billion is believed to be recoverable from university lecturers who have violated agreements and bonds with the universities, the Committee on Public Enterprises (COPE) has revealed.

COPE has advised the relevant authorities responsible for administering the university system to pay special attention to the issue and establish a mechanism to recover the outstanding amounts.

It was also revealed that Rs.3,633,900 had been paid to a female lecturer who re-joined the service after resigning to contest the 2024 elections. COPE instructed the authorities to recover this amount from her.

Meanwhile, certain documents relating to the educational qualifications and promotions of some university officials have gone missing.

In another significant disclosure, 38 non-academic employees had avoided using the universities' attendance-scanning machines, while some employees had obtained leave exceeding their stipulated entitlements. While some had taken five additional days of leave, others had exceeded their entitlements by between five and 83.5 days.

As a result, an additional Rs.99,181,457 had been paid to these employees, but no action had been taken to recover the money.

COPE also examined the amounts recoverable from both retired university employees and those who continue to serve in the university system.

However, documents relating to these payments had gone missing. The authorities had attributed the loss of records to natural disasters, including floods that occurred from time to time, as well as the 2004 tsunami.

COPE Chairman MP Nishantha Samaraweera and committee members, however, rejected these explanations and stressed the need for the authorities to produce accurate records of the relevant information.
