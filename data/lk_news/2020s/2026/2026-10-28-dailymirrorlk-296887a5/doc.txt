Nuwara Eliya records lowest temperature of 15°C

Colombo, Oct. 28 (Daily Mirror) - Nuwara Eliya recorded the lowest minimum temperature in Sri Lanka at 15.0°C on Wednesday (October 28), according to the Department of Meteorology.

Badulla recorded a minimum temperature of 19.8°C, while Katugastota recorded 21.8°C and Rathnapura 23.8°C.

Vavuniya recorded a minimum temperature of 24.0°C, while Mullaitivu recorded 24.2°C. Maha Illuppallama and Kurunegala each recorded 24.6°C, while Polonnaruwa recorded 24.4°C.

Hambantota recorded 24.5°C, while Batticaloa and Matara (Mattala) recorded 24.9°C each. Anuradhapura recorded 25.2°C, Trincomalee 25.0°C and Pothuvil 25.7°C.

In the western and north-western parts of the country, Colombo and Katunayake recorded minimum temperatures of 25.1°C each, while Puttalam recorded 26.0°C. Rathmalana recorded 26.2°C, Jaffna 26.1°C and Mannar 27.0°C.

Galle recorded 25.8°C, while Monaragala recorded 23.4°C and Bandarawela 18.5°C.

The figures were issued by the Department of Meteorology as part of its minimum temperature observations for October 28, 2026.
