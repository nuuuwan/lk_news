Suranjith Wevita appointed SLTDA Chairman

The Ministry of Tourism yesterday confirmed the appointment of Suranjith Wevita as Chairman of the Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority (SLTDA), separating the leadership of the country’s key tourism agencies.

Wevita succeeds Buddhika Hewawasam, who will continue as Chairman of the Sri Lanka Tourism Promotion Bureau (SLTPB). The Tourism Ministry said the change was aimed at restructuring administrative responsibilities between the two institutions.

Wevita currently serves as Secretary of the All Island Tourism Service Providers Association and the Central Province Tourism Services Development Co-operative Society. He has also served on the Presidential Task Force on Tourism Development.

He previously spent nearly two decades in the financial services sector and is a licensed national and regional tourist guide lecturer.
