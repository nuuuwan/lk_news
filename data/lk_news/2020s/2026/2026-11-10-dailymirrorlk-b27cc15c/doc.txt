Japan grants Rs. 2.08 Bn to Sri Lanka Navy for 25 patrol boats

Colombo, Nov. 10 (Daily Mirror) - The Government of Japan has agreed to provide grant assistance of Japanese Yen 1 billion (approximately Rs. 2.08 billion) to strengthen the defence and maritime surveillance capabilities of the Sri Lanka Navy under its Official Security Assistance (OSA) Programme for 2026.

The Exchange of Notes relating to the grant was signed today (10) by Secretary to the Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development Dr. Harshana Suriyapperuma, on behalf of the Sri Lankan Government, and Japanese Ambassador to Sri Lanka ISOMATA Akio, representing the Japanese Government.

Under the project, 25 Rigid Hull Inflatable Boats (RHIBs) will be provided to the Sri Lanka Navy for deployment across the Northern, Eastern, North Western, Western and Southern Naval Commands.

The boats are expected to support coastal surveillance operations and maritime law enforcement activities.

The assistance comes amid increasing maritime activities in the Indian Ocean and emerging security challenges, including illegal activities at sea. It is expected to enhance the Navy's operational readiness to respond to both traditional and non-traditional maritime security threats, while also supporting humanitarian assistance and disaster response operations.

Japan previously provided OSA grant assistance amounting to Japanese Yen 500 million in 2025 for the procurement of long-range and short-range drones for the Sri Lanka Navy.
