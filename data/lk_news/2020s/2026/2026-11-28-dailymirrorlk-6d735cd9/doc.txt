Tri forces to be restructured by 2031: Minister

The tri forces, namely the Sri Lanka Army, Sri Lanka Navy and the Sri Lanka Air Force will be restructured by the year 2031, Deputy Minister Aruna Jayasekera told Parliament today.

He said the tri forces will be converted into professional forces which can handle modern warfare.

He also said that funds have been allocated to purchase five anti-drone systems while a Defence Review Committee comprising retired service commanders will also be set up.

Earlier, ITAK MP Shanakiyan Rasamanickam stressed the need to convert the Sri Lankan tri forces into technology-based forces from the current status of personnel-based forces, given the modern warfare tactics.
