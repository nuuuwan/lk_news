JAT Holdings 1Q profit stalls despite 34% revenue growth

JAT Holdings PLC recorded group revenue of Rs.3.04 billion for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, representing a 34 percent year-on-year increase, supported by growth across both its Sri Lankan and international operations.

Local revenue increased by 8 percent to Rs.1.83 billion, while revenue from the foreign operations increased by 110 percent to Rs.1.21 billion.

Gross profit increased by 29 percent to Rs.1.07 billion, while the gross profit margin moderated from 37 percent to 35 percent, reflecting the higher input costs associated with the increases in the global commodity prices.

The operating profit declined by 25 percent to Rs.174 million, primarily reflecting the consolidation of Mirotone’s expenses, alongside the higher administrative, selling and distribution expenses associated with the Volt software depreciation, increased transportation costs, following the fuel price increases and additional warehouse capacity established to support wider market reach.

The foreign exchange gains partly offset the finance costs, with profit before tax remaining broadly unchanged at Rs.167 million and profit after tax at Rs.150 million.

The wood coatings business maintained its 57 percent market share while achieving an 8 percent quarter-on-quarter (QoQ) revenue growth, despite the supplier price increases that reduced the net gross profit margin by 4 percent. The paint, chemicals and related products segment recorded an 85 percent QoQ revenue growth, with its share of the Brilliant White segment increasing to 11 percent.

Brushes and rollers delivered a 78 percent QoQ revenue growth, expanded its outlet reach by 12 percent to more than 3,000 outlets and further strengthened its market-leading position through a 3 percent increase in market share.

Although the furnishing business recorded a reported 7 percent decline in revenue, the total revenue value, including the LC components paid directly by customers, increased by 64 percent QoQ. The business secured key furnishing contracts for OPPEIN doors, while confirmed projects in the pipeline totalled approximately Rs.763 million. It also launched HM Circle, a first-of-its-kind loyalty programme for Herman Miller customers.

In Bangladesh, revenue increased by 33 percent in BDT terms, with the industrial segment growing 31 percent, the retail segment growing 40 percent, Coat-Ex sales increasing by 61 percent and ACL gross profit improving by one percent. The Maldives also recorded a strong growth during the quarter, with revenue increasing by 624 percent QoQ. Mirotone New Zealand recorded a 10 percent QoQ revenue growth in NZD terms.
