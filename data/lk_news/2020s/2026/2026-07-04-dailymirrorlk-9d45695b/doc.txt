Pregnancy kits donated at charitable event

Sri Lanka Thailand Society organised a charitable event  called “Gabini Matha Sathkaraya” on Poson poya day by donating Pregnancy kits to thirty pregnant women at MOH office, Mihintale,  in the presence of His Excellency Paitoon Mahapannaporn Ambassador of Thailand in Sri Lanka.

Dr. Muditha Amarasinghe (MOH), Erandhi Kalanika Herath (Midwife), Prof. Preethi Weerathunga (Rajarata University), K.M. Dimuthu Chaminda (Project Coordinator ) The President of Sri Lanka Thailand Society Deshabandu K. Jayakumar, and Exco Members  Champani Padmasekara, Tejitha Koralage and Amara Wanigaratne (Secretary to the Ambassador)   were present for the event.
