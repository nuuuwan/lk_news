Sampath Bank introduces one-stop housing loan hub with 10-day processing target

From left: Sampath Bank Chief Marketing Officer Milinda Weerasinghe, Deputy General Manager Consumer and SME Banking Asanka Liyanage, Chief Business Intelligence Officer Darshin Pathinayake, Senior Manager Retail Banking Suneth Hathurusinghe and Assistant General Manager Retail Banking Keshan Dabare at Sampath Sevena 360 Hub launch

Sampath Bank PLC introduced a new level of convenience to housing finance with the launch of Sampath Sevana 360 Hub at its Borella Super Branch.

Sri Lanka’s first dedicated housing loan hub, the initiative brings the entire housing loan journey under one roof, simplifying the process for the customers seeking to purchase or build a home.

Securing a housing loan often involves multiple stages, including document verification, legal coordination, property valuation and credit assessment. Sampath Sevana 360 Hub addresses these complexities through a one-stop service model, allowing the customers to initiate their application at a single location while the bank coordinates the process through to loan disbursement.

Speaking at the launch, Sampath Bank Deputy General Manager Consumer and SME Banking Asanka Liayanage said, “Sampath Sevana 360 Hub reflects our commitment to making home ownership a more straightforward experience. Buying or building a home is a significant milestone and the financing process should support that ambition rather than add to its difficulty. Our aim is to bring the entire journey together through a single point of contact, greater transparency and more efficient processing, with a targeted 10-working-day approval timeline, subject to complete documentation and applicable approval requirements.”

The initiative is designed to support a broad range of housing aspirations, offering a more convenient and streamlined financing experience for the customers looking to purchase, build or renovate their home/homes. Therefore, Sampath Bank aims to make housing finance more accessible and convenient, combining personalised assistance with greater process efficiency to support the customers pursuing their home ownership ambitions.

A dedicated relationship officer will guide each customer throughout the process, eliminating the need to navigate multiple departments and coordinate separately with bank officers, lawyers and property valuers. The services will cover application acceptance, document verification, legal coordination, property valuation, credit processing, customer progress updates and loan disbursement.

The Borella Super Branch will serve as the initial location for Sampath Sevana 360 Hub, introducing a dedicated housing finance experience that brings convenience, coordination and a clearer path towards homeownership under one roof.
