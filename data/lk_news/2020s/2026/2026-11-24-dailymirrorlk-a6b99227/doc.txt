Illegally assembled jeep seized in Bandarawela

Colombo, November 24 (Daily Mirror)- The Gampaha Division Criminal Investigation Bureau has arrested a suspect and seized a jeep allegedly imported into Sri Lanka without paying the required customs duties and later assembled locally.

The suspect was arrested by a team of officers from the Gampaha Division Criminal Investigation Bureau on November 19 in connection with the incident.

As part of the ongoing investigation, police yesterday took the jeep into custody in the Bandarawela area after uncovering further information about the suspect and the vehicle.

Further investigations into the incident are being carried out by the Gampaha Division Criminal Investigation Bureau.
