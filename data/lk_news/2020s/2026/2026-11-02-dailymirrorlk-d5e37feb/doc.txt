UNP-SJB electoral alliance does not spell victory: Ranil

Colombo, Nov. 2 (Daily Mirror) - A United National Party (UNP) and Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) alliance does not spell victory, former President Ranil Wickremesinghe said.

“Some anticipate that a UNP-SJB alliance would be a political success, as such an alliance would win future elections. Such an anticipation could be wrong, as politics has changed today,” Wickremesinghe told party members.

"One has to look into the thinking patterns of Millennial and Gen Z generations. I have had discussions with the members of these generations. They are of the opinion that the party activities should be carried out online. Also, the days of preparing election manifestos for five-year terms will soon be gone. Accordingly, one has to prepare policies for longer periods. Actually, policies should be prepared for 25 years. UNP is planning to adopt such changes," Wickremesinghe said.

"Millennial and Gen Z generations have no knowledge of political parties and politics. However, a party has to attract them if it is to win in an election in the future. A good number of members belonging to these generations could go abroad as the present government has failed to live up to their expectations. Members of these generations were involved in the Aragalaya in 2022. The same was witnessed in the uprising of Bangladesh," he also said.

Furthermore, Wickremesinghe said the UNP will be restructured to attract the new generation.
