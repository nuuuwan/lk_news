Belarus waives on-arrival visa fees for first-time Sri Lankan students

Colombo, Nov 15 (Daily Mirror) - The Government of Belarus has decided to waive on-arrival visa fees for Sri Lankan students travelling to the country for the first time to pursue higher education.

The decision follows Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Minister Vijitha Herath’s official visit to Belarus in May 2026.

Meanwhile, steps have been initiated to simplify visa application procedures for family members wishing to visit Sri Lankan students currently studying in Belarus.

The measures are expected to provide financial relief to Sri Lankan students seeking higher education in Belarus and make it easier for their families to visit them.
