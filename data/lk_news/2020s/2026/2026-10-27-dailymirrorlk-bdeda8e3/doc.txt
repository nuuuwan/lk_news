Illegal electric fence kills two young female elephants

Colombo, Oct. 27 (Daily Mirror) - Two young female elephants have died after coming into contact with an allegedly illegal electric fence in a home garden in Aluthgama village, within the Pinagama Grama Niladhari division, under the Pihimbiyagollewa Police jurisdiction.

The incident occurred early this morning (27), according to the Anuradhapura Wildlife Office.

The two elephants, estimated to be between 10 and 12 years old, were discovered dead in the garden by local residents, who alerted the Pihimbiyagollewa Police and wildlife officials.

Officials subsequently visited the scene and located the carcasses. A post-mortem examination was scheduled to be conducted today to determine the circumstances surrounding the deaths.

Police and wildlife officials have launched further investigations into the incident. However, the farmer suspected of installing the electric fence had not been arrested at the time of reporting.

The incident highlights the continuing threat posed to Sri Lanka’s wild elephants by illegal electric fences, which can cause fatal injuries to animals entering residential gardens and agricultural land.
