President to address farmers in Polonnaruwa on Sept. 12

Colombo, Sep. 11 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake is scheduled to address a National People’s Power (NPP) farmers’ rally in Polonnaruwa on Saturday (Sept. 12).

The rally, organised under the theme “People’s Rule – Two Years Strong, Progress for All,” is scheduled to begin at 4 p.m.

The event is part of a series of rallies being organised by the NPP to mark two years of its administration.

The Polonnaruwa rally will focus on farmers and the agriculture sector, according to promotional material released ahead of the event.
