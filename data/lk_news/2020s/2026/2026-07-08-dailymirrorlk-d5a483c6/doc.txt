HNB General Insurance among SL’s 50 Best Workplaces

Becomes only general insurer named among Sri Lanka’s 50 Best Workplaces 2026 by Great Place To Work

HNB General Insurance (HNBGI) has become the only general insurance company to make it to the exclusive list of 50 Best Workplaces in Sri Lanka for 2026.

Compiled by Great Place To Work, the renowned global authority on workplace culture, the prestigious list features 50 Sri Lankan organisations across all sizes, with HNBGI ranking 16th in the Large Sized Workplaces (>500 Employees) segment.

This recognition is based on comprehensive, confidential survey data assessing employee experiences of trust, innovation by all, meaningful organisational values and leadership effectiveness. Organisations were rigorously evaluated on how well they create a ‘For All’ workplace experience, where every single employee is welcomed, supported and engaged.

According to Great Place To Work, “A best workplace is one in which employees, no matter who they are and what they do for the organisation, are having a consistently positive experience of a ‘Great Place To Work For All’. It is an honour for HNB General Insurance to be recognised by their employees as an organisation that fosters a foundation of trust and meaningful values through effective leaders.”

HNBGI Executive Director and CEO Sithumina Jayasundara stated, “Transitioning from being Certified last year to becoming the only general insurance company to be recognised among Sri Lanka’s 50 Best Workplaces this year makes this achievement even more meaningful and reinforces our commitment to building a workplace where our people can thrive.”

HNBGI Vice President and Head of Human Resources Malsha Munasinghe said, “Earning a spot on this prestigious list validates our long-term commitment to nurturing an environment where talent meets opportunity, completely free of barriers.”

“We applaud HNBGI for seeking employee feedback and for the opportunity to recognise them as a Best Workplace in Sri Lanka,” said Great Place To Work Sri Lanka CEO and Director Kshanika Ratnayaka.
