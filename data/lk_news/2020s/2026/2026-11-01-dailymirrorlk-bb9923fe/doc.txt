South Korean national arrested with cocaine worth Rs. 49.4 million at BIA

Colombo, Nov. 1 (Daily Mirror) - A 64-year-old South Korean national was arrested by officers of the Airport Police Narcotics Bureau at the Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA), Katunayake, while attempting to collect a suitcase containing cocaine worth approximately Rs. 49.44 million, police said.

The suspect, a carpenter by profession, had arrived at the airport on the afternoon of November 1 to collect the missing suitcase containing the narcotics.

Police said the suspect travelled from Tabuk Airport in Saudi Arabia to Dubai on October 26 before arriving in Sri Lanka aboard Flydubai flight FZ-579.

The suitcase containing the cocaine had not been loaded onto the flight on which he travelled to Sri Lanka and was subsequently sent to Katunayake Airport on another flight.

Police said the Inspector General of Police (IGP) received intelligence that led to the arrest.
