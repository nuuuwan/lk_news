Is government printing money, asks Ravi K

The government should explain to the public as to how the inflation rate keeps increasing if there is no money printing going on, Opposition MP Ravi Karunanayake said today.

"Previous governments were blamed for printing money and one wonders as to how the inflation rate is increasing if there is no money printing going on," MP Karunanayake told a media briefing.

The Central Bank should also make correct projections and make plans to stabilise the economy, he added.
