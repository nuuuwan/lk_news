Sampath Bank enhances care at Lady Ridgeway Hospital with essential hospital upgrades

Sampath Bank Employees’ Association Treasurer Nalin Nishantha Liyanage, Secretary Chandana Weragama and President Kalpa Jayasinghe, Chief Operating Officer Deepal De Silva, Hospital Director Consultant Dr. Pradeep Rathnasekara, Hospital Deputy Director Dr. Prabath Amarasinghe, Hospital Committee Chairman Kusal De Silva and Hospital Committee Secretary Anura Thilakasiri

Recognising the importance of maintaining quality healthcare facilities for children, the Sampath Bank Employees’ Union, together with the bank’s Sustainability Unit, marked the union’s 35th anniversary by supporting a series of essential upgrades at the Lady Ridgeway Hospital for Children, Colombo.

As Sri Lanka’s national referral centre and largest free paediatric hospital, Lady Ridgeway Hospital provides specialised care to children from across the country, making continuous investment in its facilities vital to delivering timely and effective treatment.

The initiative included the refurbishment of a blood filtration machine required for patient care, the installation of air-conditioning for the Physiotherapy Unit, the erection of directional signboards across the hospital premises and the addition of child-friendly decorations to create a more welcoming environment for young patients and their families.

The event was attended by Sampath Bank Chief Operating Officer Deepal de Silva, the Sampath Bank Employees’ Union President Kalpa Jayasinghe, members of the bank’s staff and the hospital director together with the hospital staff.
