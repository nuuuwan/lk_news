ADB to provide Rs.6.7 billion for provincial health development in 2027

Colombo, Sep. 13 (Daily Mirror) - The Asian Development Bank (ADB) is set to provide Rs.6.7 billion in capital funding in 2027 to support health sector development programmes across Sri Lanka’s nine provinces.

The Provincial Annual Development Plans (PADPs) for 2027, prepared under the ADB’s financial assistance programme covering the period from 2025 to 2030, were reviewed yesterday (12) at Jetwing Lake, Dambulla.

The review was held under the patronage of Director General of Health Services Dr. Asela Gunawardena, with the participation of Finance Commission Secretary A.T.M.U.D.B. Tennakoon.

Under the programme, each of the nine provinces is scheduled to receive Rs.750 million in capital funding from the ADB in 2027, amounting to Rs.6.7 billion in total.

The relevant provincial development plans are required to be prepared and submitted to the Finance Commission by mid-November, enabling the funds to be allocated from January 2027 for the implementation of health development programmes.

The ADB has approved a US$100 million loan and a US$6.9 million foreign grant for the overall programme under its Results-Based Lending (RBL) mechanism for 2020-2030.

Under the results-based system, funding is released in phases following independent verification that agreed health targets or Disbursement-Linked Indicators (DLIs) have been achieved.

The health departments of all nine provinces, together with the central Ministry of Health, are required to work towards achieving the agreed targets.

The Finance Commission will continue to review the Provincial Annual Development Plans in consultation with provincial Chief Secretaries and health authorities to ensure the funds are used efficiently and the required targets are achieved.

A separate allocation will also be provided by the ADB to the Ministry of Mass Media, with the relevant plans already prepared and submitted to the Ministry of Finance.

ADB Team Leader Dai-Ling Chen, Senior Public Health Specialist Consultant Dr. Kumari Vinodani Navaratne, Provincial Health Directors, District Health Directors, Provincial Secretaries and other health officials also participated in the review.
