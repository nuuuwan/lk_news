Measles scare suspends inmate transfers to courts

Colombo, Nov 09 (Daily Mirror) - The Department of Prisons has temporarily suspended producing inmates before courts from the Colombo Remand Prison, Magazine Remand Prison and Welikada Prison after detecting several measles cases among inmates.

Prison Media Spokesperson Senaka Pallethanna said the relevant courts had been informed in writing of the decision.

Accordingly, no inmates from the three prisons were produced before courts today (November 9).

He said several cases of measles had been reported at the three prison facilities over the past few days, raising concerns about the possible spread of the disease among inmates.

A vaccination programme has also been launched within the prisons to contain the outbreak, he added.
