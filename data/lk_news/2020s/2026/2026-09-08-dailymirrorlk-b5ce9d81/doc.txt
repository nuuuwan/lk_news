Customs seizes Rs. 51.37 million worth of Kush and Hashish at BIA

Colombo, Sep. 8 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Customs Narcotics Control Unit officers have seized 5.137 kilograms of Kush cannabis and hashish worth an estimated Rs. 51.37 million from two Thai nationals at the Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA).

The seizure was made on September 7 during a special inspection of the luggage of two passengers who arrived in Sri Lanka on Thai Airways flight TG 307.

According to Sri Lanka Customs, a 39-year-old Thai national was found carrying 2,224 grams of Kush cannabis, valued at approximately Rs. 22.24 million. The drugs had been concealed in eight plastic bottles inside his suitcase.

A subsequent search of the luggage of a 23-year-old Thai national uncovered another 2,272 grams of Kush cannabis, with an estimated street value of Rs. 22.72 million. The drugs were similarly concealed in eight plastic bottles.

Customs officers also found 641 grams of hashish, valued at approximately Rs. 6.41 million, hidden inside a cardboard box in the 23-year-old suspect's suitcase.

The total haul consisted of 4,496 grams of Kush cannabis and 641 grams of hashish, bringing the total quantity seized to 5,137 grams, with an estimated street value of Rs. 51.37 million.

Sri Lanka Customs said the seizure highlights the effectiveness of the Narcotics Control Unit's continuous surveillance and risk-identification methods aimed at detecting drugs being illegally brought into the country through international passenger transport.

Following preliminary investigations, the two suspects and the seized drugs were handed over to the Police Narcotics Bureau (PNB) for further investigations and legal action.
