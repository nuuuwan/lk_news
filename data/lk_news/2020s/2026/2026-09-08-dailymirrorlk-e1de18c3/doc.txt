SLT-Mobitel shines at National Business Excellence Awards 2026

SLT-Mobitel has reinforced its commitment to business excellence by securing three accolades at the National Business Excellence Awards 2026, organised by the National Chamber of Commerce of Sri Lanka and held recently at Shangri-La Colombo.

SLT PLC secured two Runner-up awards in the Infrastructure & Utilities Sector Category and the Excellence in Performance Management Criteria, reaffirming its commitment to operational excellence, strategic leadership, innovation, and fostering a high-performance culture.

eChannelling PLC was also honoured with the Gold Award in the ICT Services Sector Category, recognising its continued commitment to innovation and its contribution to advancing digital healthcare in Sri Lanka. The recognition reflects the company’s ongoing efforts to harness technology to enhance healthcare accessibility, improve service delivery, and create greater value for patients, healthcare providers, and the wider community.

Collectively, these three accolades reflect the breadth of expertise and performance across SLT-Mobitel’s telecommunications, infrastructure, and digital services businesses. They further reinforce SLT-Mobitel’s ongoing transformation into a broader technology and digital solutions provider, driven by innovation, operational excellence, and sustainable value creation.

The National Business Excellence Awards follow a comprehensive evaluation process involving a multi-stage assessment conducted by an independent Technical and Financial Evaluation Committee, followed by a final review by an independent panel of judges.
