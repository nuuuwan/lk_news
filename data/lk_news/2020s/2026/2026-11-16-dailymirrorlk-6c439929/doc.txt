German national dies after drowning at Hikkaduwa Beach

Galle, Nov 16 (Daily Mirror) - A 71-year-old German national has died after being swept away by a strong current while bathing at Narigama Beach in Hikkaduwa.

Police said the foreign national had been caught in a current and swept out to sea while bathing at the beach.

Area residents rescued the man from the water and rushed him to Balapitiya Hospital, where he later succumbed to his injuries while receiving treatment.

The body has been placed at the Balapitiya Hospital mortuary pending a post-mortem examination.
