Warm weather advisory for Eastern Province, seven districts

Colombo, Aug. 18 (Daily Mirror) - The Meteorology Department has issued a Warm Weather Advisory for Wednesday (19), warning that the heat index could reach the ‘Caution’ level in parts of the Eastern Province and seven districts.

The advisory, issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre at 4.00 p.m. today (18), covers the Eastern Province and the districts of Jaffna, Kilinochchi, Vavuniya, Mullaitivu, Polonnaruwa and Monaragala.

According to the Department, the heat index—the temperature felt by the human body—is expected to increase to the ‘Caution’ level in some areas during the daytime.

The heat index is calculated using relative humidity and maximum temperature and does not represent the actual maximum air temperature.

The Department urged people to take precautions, including staying hydrated, limiting strenuous outdoor activities and taking frequent breaks in shaded areas.

People working outdoors were advised to wear lightweight, white or light-coloured clothing, while the public was urged to check on elderly and sick persons. Motorists were also warned never to leave children unattended inside vehicles.

Under the heat index scale, temperatures of 39–45°C are considered the ‘Caution’ level, at which prolonged exposure and activity may cause fatigue and continued activity could lead to heat cramps.

The advisory was issued in consultation with health authorities, and the public was also advised to follow any additional guidance issued by the Disaster Preparedness and Response Division of the Ministry of Health.
