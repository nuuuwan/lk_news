IGP orders criminal probe into Colombo Port drug seizure

Colombo, Oct 01 (Daily Mirror) - The Inspector General of Police (IGP) has directed the Criminal Investigation Department (CID) to conduct a formal criminal investigation into alleged negligence and delays by police officers in handling intelligence related to a major illicit drug consignment seized at the Colombo Port on August 31, 2026.

The decision follows an interim investigation by the Police Special Investigation Unit (SIU), which found that officers responsible for acting on an initial intelligence report had allegedly failed to take appropriate action.

According to the Police, Senior Deputy Inspector General of Police (SDIG) Ranmal Kodituwakku received intelligence on August 14 that two containers containing illegal drugs had been imported into Sri Lanka and were located at the Colombo International Container Terminals (CICT).

The information was forwarded to the Police Narcotic Bureau (PNB) on the same day. However, after it was found that appropriate action had not been taken, the IGP ordered the SIU to conduct an internal inquiry.

The SIU's interim findings indicated alleged negligence and dereliction of duty by officers assigned to act on the initial intelligence. The unit recommended that a comprehensive criminal investigation be conducted alongside the ongoing departmental inquiry.

The investigation also revealed that Sub-Inspector Ekanayake of the Central Crimes Investigation Bureau (CCIB) received separate information about the same consignment from a personal informant on August 22.

Police said SI Ekanayake acted immediately on the information. A team led by the Director of the CCIB went to the Colombo Port on August 31 and began scanning the containers in coordination with Customs officials.

PNB officers arrived at the location after the scanning operation was already underway, according to the police statement. The police therefore said the detection of the suspected drugs was primarily carried out by the CCIB team.

The CID investigation will determine whether the delay, alleged negligence and other omissions by police officers or officials of relevant agencies were intended to benefit an outside party.

The investigation will also determine whether there are sufficient grounds for criminal charges to be filed against any officers found responsible.
