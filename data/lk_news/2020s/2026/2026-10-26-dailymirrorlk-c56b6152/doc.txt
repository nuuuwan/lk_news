UNP calls for immediate presidential pardon for Gnanasara Thera

Colombo, October 26 (Daily Mirror)- The United National Party (UNP) today called for an immediate presidential pardon for Ven. Galagodaaththe Gnanasara Thera to resolve the ongoing crisis, with the intervention of the Maha Nayake Theros of the Three Chapters.

In a statement, the party said the Sathyagraha demanding the release of Gnanasara Thera had been suspended following a notification by the Maha Nayake Theros.

According to former Chief Justice Sarath N. Silva, the maximum prison sentence for contempt of court is 18 months. Gnanasara Thera has already served more than 10 months in prison for the offence, the party said.

The UNP said steps had previously been taken to secure a presidential pardon for the Thera at the request of the Maha Nayake Theros. It also claimed that the Supreme Court ruling had indicated that another presidential pardon could be considered.

The party further criticised the government for obtaining court orders against Buddhist monks who had not participated in the protest march and Sathyagraha demanding the Thera’s release, describing the move as a serious mistake.

It also called for an investigation into those responsible for ordering the removal of the Thera’s robes.

The UNP reiterated that an immediate presidential pardon, facilitated by the intervention of the Maha Nayake Theros of the Three Chapters, was necessary to resolve the crisis.
