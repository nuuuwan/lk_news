England Women to tour Sri Lanka for T20I and ODI series

Colombo, Oct. 21 (Daily Mirror) - The England Women’s National Team is scheduled to tour Sri Lanka in February and March 2027 to play a series of Twenty20 Internationals (T20Is) and One-Day Internationals (ODIs).

The English side will arrive in Sri Lanka in February for the three-match T20I series before returning in March, following the ICC Women’s Champions Trophy 2027, for the ODI leg of the tour.

The T20I series will begin on February 1, with the first match scheduled to be played at the Pallekele International Cricket Stadium (PICS) in Kandy. The second and third T20Is will take place at the same venue on February 3 and 5, respectively.

The three-match ODI series will be played at the Rangiri Dambulla International Cricket Stadium (RDICS) in Dambulla. The first ODI is scheduled for March 4, followed by the second on March 6 and the third on March 8.
