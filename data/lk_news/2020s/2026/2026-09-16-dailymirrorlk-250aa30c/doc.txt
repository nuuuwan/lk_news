High Court bail for drug cases involving less than 100g

Colombo, Sep. 16 (Daily Mirror) - The Government is taking steps to amend the law to once again allow suspects arrested with less than 100 grams of drugs (gross) to seek bail from the High Court, Minister of Justice and National Integration Harshana Nanayakkara said yesterday.

The Minister said the proposed amendment, which is to be submitted to Parliament for approval, was being considered in response to delays in obtaining forensic reports, which have contributed to suspects being held in remand custody for extended periods.

Speaking at an event at the Government Analyst’s Department in Battaramulla, Minister Nanayakkara said delays in issuing forensic reports had become a serious problem, particularly in drug-related cases.

He said that when the present Government assumed office, the Government Analyst’s Department had 118 vacancies, creating difficulties in issuing forensic reports efficiently.

Measures have since been taken to recruit around 70 personnel, while officers are working until about 10.00 p.m. to expedite the preparation of reports, he said.

The Minister added that steps had also been taken to ensure that forensic reports relating to drug cases are issued within three months.

Under the law enacted in 2022, suspects arrested with more than 10 grams of drugs were required to seek bail from the Court of Appeal, which resulted in delays in obtaining bail, according to the Minister.

He said the proposed amendment would restore the ability to seek bail from the High Court where the net weight of the drugs at the time of arrest is less than 100 grams.
