Sheraton Colombo elevates culinary landscape with authentic Mughlai heritage festival

A recent visit to Sheraton Colombo revealed a dining experience that is both a treat for the palate and a sophisticated cultural journey. The hotel, in a significant partnership with JW Marriott Bengaluru, is hosting ‘An Ode To Mughlai Heritage’ at its Colombo Kitchen restaurant.

Designed as a vibrant all-day dining destination inspired by a modern gourmet market, Colombo Kitchen is proving to be an ideal setting where food meets culture, providing a space for the diners to explore global flavours in a contemporary atmosphere.

This collaborative event, running from August 14 to 23, between 7:00 p.m. and 10:30 p.m., is a clear indication of Sheraton Colombo’s commitment to reviving Colombo’s dining scene.

The property has been actively transforming the city’s culinary landscape by bringing in specialised international expertise to stimulate the local market. This follows their highly successful ‘Spice Trails of Malaysia’ festival in June and July, where they collaborated with Le Méridien Kuala Lumpur and the High Commission of Malaysia to showcase the authentic regional gastronomy.

Alongside other recent initiatives like the Jaffna Food Festival, Sheraton Colombo is strategically positioning itself as a premier hub for diverse, world-class culinary events.

The Mughlai festival masterfully captures the essence of this historic cuisine, known for its rich flavours and intricate preparation. The centrepiece and perhaps the most exciting aspect for biryani aficionados, is the rotating selection of regional biryanis.

“We wanted to do something different and give our customers the opportunity to experience different types of biryani and flavours from across the region,” the Sheraton Colombo executive chef explained.

“Over the 10 days, we will rotate five varieties, including Lucknow, Hyderabadi, Mumbai, Donne from Karnataka and Delhi biryani.”

The chef’s vision goes beyond menu planning, serving as a strategic nod to regional unity through food.

“With the Independence Day celebrations of our neighbours, Pakistan and India, also falling during this period, we thought it was a good opportunity to bring some of those traditional flavours together here in Colombo,” he added.

This brings a thoughtful, timely layer to the entire experience, making it a culinary celebration of shared regional heritage.

The tasting session on a recent Friday proved that the execution easily matches the ambition. The flavours are deep, authentic and crafted with obvious expertise. Enhancing the diverse biryani offerings is an interactive live grill section featuring an array of fresh seafood and perfectly spiced kebabs, adding a dynamic and engaging element to the dinner service. Coupled with the soothing ambiance of live music, the collaboration with JW Marriott Bengaluru’s chefs delivers a truly premium dining experience.

For professionals and food enthusiasts alike, this event represents a valuable opportunity to indulge in exceptional cuisine that is often hard to find at this level of authenticity. With the festival concluding on August 23, there is a limited window to savour these historic flavours.
