Rains to ease by tomorrow afternoon: Met. Department

Colombo, Aug. 3 (Daily Mirror) - Director General of the Department of Meteorology, Ajith Wijemanne said the prevailing rainy weather will ease by tomorrow (4, Tuesday) afternoon while Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, the Sabaragamuwa Province and some parts of the Western Province will continue to receive rains tonight and early tomorrow morning.

He told a special media briefing that however, rains can be expected in the Kandy and Nuwara Eliya districts and in the Sabaragamuwa Province even after August 6 due to the influence of a typhoon over the Pacific Ocean south of Japan.

Responding to a question, he said there is less possibility of the upcoming A/L and Grade Five Scholarship examinations being affected due to the bad weather.

The Director General said a discussion is scheduled with the education authorities on August 5 to discuss matters concerning the examinations.
