Three-wheeler driver dies after Dematagoda shooting

Colombo, September 17 (Daily Mirror) - A three-wheeler driver who was injured in a shooting incident near the ‘Kalu Palama’ in Dematagoda this evening has succumbed to his injuries while receiving treatment in hospital, police said.

The shooting was reported earlier today (17), and the victim was rushed to hospital following the incident.

However, police later confirmed that the three-wheeler driver had died while undergoing treatment.

Further investigations are underway to identify the gunman and establish the motive behind the shooting.
