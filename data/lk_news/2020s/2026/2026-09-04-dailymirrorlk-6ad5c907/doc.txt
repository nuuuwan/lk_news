250 Japanese tourists attend farewell ceremony

A group of 250 Japanese tourists who recently visited Sri Lanka attend a farewell ceremony in Katunayake today, following the conclusion of their tour. The ceremony was organised under the leadership of Dr. Ruwan Perera, founder of the Little Japan Foundation, before the group departed from Bandaranaike International Airport.
