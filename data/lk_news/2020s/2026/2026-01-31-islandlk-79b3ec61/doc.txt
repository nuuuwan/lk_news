Ceylinco Life first to open eco-friendly branch on company land in Ambalantota

Life insurance market leader Ceylinco Life has strengthened its footprint in Sri Lanka’s deep south with the opening of a new purpose-built, eco-friendly branch in Ambalantota, becoming the first life insurer in the area to operate from its own company-owned building.

Located at No. 207G, Tangalle Road, Ambalantota, the two-storey building is the 35th such company-owned branch building of Ceylinco Life. Purpose-designed to prioritise customer comfort while minimising environmental impact, the new branch reflects Ceylinco Life’s long-term commitment to sustainable infrastructure development, the Company said.

The modern facility has a total gross floor area of 3,200 square feet, provides parking for 10 vehicles and was completed over a period of 11 months. The building has been constructed using precast concrete technology introduced by the National Engineering Research and Development Centre, offering significant advantages over traditional construction methods, including a 10 to 15 per cent cost saving, a 20 per cent reduction in construction time, high thermal comfort and lower long-term maintenance costs.

Designed in line with Ceylinco Life’s green building concept, the Ambalantota branch operates with a 15 kW off-grid solar power system, enabling a zero electricity bill and eliminating the need for a diesel generator. The building also incorporates a rainwater harvesting system and a waste-water recycling system that supplies treated water for toilet flushing and gardening, while ensuring minimal use of pipe-borne water.
