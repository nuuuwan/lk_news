Sri Lanka to implement national pharmaceutical policy 2026-2030

Colombo, Aug. 18 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet of Ministers has approved the expedited implementation of Sri Lanka’s National Pharmaceutical Policy for 2026-2030, aimed at ensuring access to safe, effective, quality and affordable essential medicines for the entire population.

The policy was developed in response to current health needs, changes in the global pharmaceutical market, challenges in the pharmaceutical supply chain and new requirements related to local pharmaceutical production.

Although preliminary work was initiated between 2020 and 2025 to update the National Pharmaceutical Policy introduced in 2005, the process had not been completed.

Taking into account the experience gained from implementing the existing policy, the Cabinet approved a proposal by Health and Mass Media Minister to prioritize and expedite the implementation of the updated policy for 2026-2030.

The policy aims to ensure sustainable and equitable access to quality and safe medicines that meet internationally recognized standards and are available at affordable prices.

It also seeks to improve the quality of healthcare and reduce wastage by promoting the scientifically sound and cost-effective use of medical products by healthcare professionals and consumers.

Another key objective is to promote efficient local production of essential medical products while ensuring their efficacy, safety and quality.
