Army Chief meets family of fallen war hero

Army Commander Lieutenant General Nilantha Premaratne and President of the Army Seva Vanitha Unit Mrs. Vindya Premaratne meet Mrs. Gayani Nandisiri, wife of late Captain U.G.A.S. Samaranayake of the 9th Battalion, Gemunu Watch, who was posthumously awarded the Parama Weera Vibhushana, the highest gallantry award of the Sri Lanka Armed Forces. The Army Commander inquired about the well-being of the family and assured them of the Army’s continued support and assistance in recognition of the late officer’s supreme sacrifice during the battles to liberate Pudukudiyiruppu on March 2, 2009.

Captain U.G.A.S. Samaranayake was a valiant officer who made the ultimate sacrifice of his life on March 2, 2009, during the battles to liberate Pudukudiyiruppu from LTTE terrorist control.
