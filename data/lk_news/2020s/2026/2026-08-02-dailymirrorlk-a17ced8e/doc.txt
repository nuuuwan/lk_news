Sri Lanka Women seal T20I series with six-wicket win over Pakistan

Colombo, Aug. 2 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Women clinched the three-match T20 International series against Pakistan Women with a match to spare after registering a convincing six-wicket victory in the second T20I at the Rangiri Dambulla International Stadium on Sunday.

Chasing a challenging target of 176, the hosts produced a composed batting display to reach 176 for 4 with 14 balls remaining, taking an unassailable 2-0 lead in the series.

Earlier, Pakistan Women, after being asked to bat, posted a competitive 175 for 5 in their 20 overs. Openers Shawal Zulfiqar and Muneeba Ali laid a solid platform with knocks of 48 and 45 respectively, while Sri Lanka's Chamudi Praboda claimed 2 for 27 to keep the visitors in check.

Sri Lanka's run chase was powered by captain Chamari Athapaththu, who gave the innings a flying start with a rapid 40 off just 15 balls. Debutant Sanjana Kavindi then impressed with a match-winning 46 from 24 deliveries, while Harshitha Samarawickrama remained unbeaten on 37 to guide the hosts over the line comfortably.

The victory follows Sri Lanka's win in the opening T20I on Friday, where Imesha Dulani struck a historic unbeaten century to lead the team to victory.

With the series already secured, Sri Lanka and Pakistan will meet in the third and final T20I on Tuesday, August 4, at the Rangiri Dambulla International Stadium.
