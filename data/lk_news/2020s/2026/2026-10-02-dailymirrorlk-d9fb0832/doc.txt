Kananathan congratulates Nigerian President on 66th Independence Day

Oct. 2 (Daily Mirror) - Former Ambassador  Kana  Kananathan extended his warm congratulations to His Excellency President Bola Ahmed Tinubu,and the people of the Federal Republic of Nigeria as they celebrated their nation's 66th Independence Day yesterday

Ambassador Kananathan commended Nigeria’s significant contribution to Africa’s economic development, regional integration and international diplomacy, highlighting its position as one of the continent’s leading economies.

Drawing on his extensive business experience across Africa, he emphasized the importance of strengthening economic cooperation, trade and investment between Nigeria and Sri Lanka.

“Nigeria remains a vital economic powerhouse and an influential voice in Africa. I extend my warmest wishes to President Tinubu and the Nigerian people for continued peace, prosperity and economic progress,” Kananathan stated.
