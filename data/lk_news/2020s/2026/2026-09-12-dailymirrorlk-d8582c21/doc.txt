Week ends on a mixed note

The ASPI edged up as a result of price gains in counters such as Haycarb, Cargills and Sampath Bank with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.1 Bn. Meanwhile, the S&P SL20 edged down. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in John Keells Holdings, Lanka IOC and Singer Finance. Mixed interest was observed in Haycarb, Ceylon Cold Stores and Renuka Agri Foods whilst retail interest was noted in Asia Siyaka Commodities, LVL Energy Fund and Asiri Surgical Hospital. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

During the week, the ASPI and the S&P SL20 lost 1.1% and 0.9% respectively, whilst recording an average daily turnover of Rs. 3.64 Bn.

Capital Goods sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to John Keells Holdings) whilst the sector index gained 0.15%. The share price of John Keells Holdings decreased by Rs 0.10 (0.52%) to close at Rs 19.30.

Materials sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Haycarb and JF Packaging Limited) whilst the sector index increased by 1.26%. The share price of Haycarb gained Rs 11.25 (5.63%) to close at Rs 211.25. The share price of JF Packaging Limited recorded a gain of Rs 0.30 (1.78%) to close at Rs 17.20. Lanka IOC and Singer Finance were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Lanka IOC moved up by Rs 1.00 (0.77%) to close at Rs 131.25. The share price of Singer Finance appreciated by Rs 1.00 (2.04%) to close at Rs 50.00.
