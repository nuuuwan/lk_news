Severe lightning warning issued for several areas today

Colombo, Oct. 2 (Daily Mirror) - The Meteorology Department has issued an advisory for severe lightning, warning that thundershowers accompanied by severe lightning are likely at several places in several provinces and districts today (Oct. 2).

The advisory, issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre at 11.30 a.m., will remain valid until 11.30 p.m. today.

The warning covers the North-Western, Central, Sabaragamuwa, Uva, Western and North-Central provinces, as well as the Galle, Matara and Mannar districts.

The Department said temporary localised strong winds may also occur during thundershowers and urged the public to take adequate precautions to minimise damage caused by lightning.

People have been advised to seek shelter, preferably indoors, and avoid taking shelter under trees during thunderstorms.

The public has also been warned to stay away from open areas such as paddy fields, tea plantations and open water bodies during thunderstorms.

The Department further advised against using wired telephones and electrical appliances connected to power supplies during thunderstorms, while people using open vehicles such as bicycles, tractors and boats have also been urged to exercise caution.

The public has been asked to remain alert for fallen trees and power lines and to contact local disaster management authorities for emergency assistance.
