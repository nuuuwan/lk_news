CEA recruits 17 management assistants to strengthen administration

Colombo, Nov. 12 (Daily Mirror) - The Central Environmental Authority (CEA) has recruited 17 new Management Assistant Officers to strengthen its administrative structure and improve public service delivery.

Appointment letters were handed over to the newly recruited officers today (12) at the CEA Head Office under the patronage of Deputy Environment Minister Anton Jayakody.

CEA Chairman Prof. Tilak Hewawasam, Director General Kapila Rajapaksha and other senior officials attended the event.

Addressing the gathering, Deputy Minister Jayakody said the officers had been selected through a transparent recruitment process to fill long-standing vacancies.

He asid that selecting qualified individuals based solely on merit, without political interference, was the change the public had long expected from the public service.

The new appointments are expected to strengthen the administrative capacity of the CEA Head Office and its provincial and district offices islandwide, while speeding up administrative work and improving the quality of services provided to the public.

The recruitment is also expected to support the CEA's efforts to protect and sustainably manage the country's environment.
