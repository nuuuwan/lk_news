Former Minister Rohitha Bogollagama arrested

Colombo, Oct. 7 (Daily Mirror) - The Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) has arrested former Minister Rohitha Bogollagama over an alleged corruption offence.

Bogollagama reportedly appeared before CIABOC investigators today (07) to provide a statement in connection with the allegation.

He was subsequently taken into custody following questioning by investigators, according to reports.

The former Minister is expected to be produced before the Colombo Chief Magistrate’s Court.
