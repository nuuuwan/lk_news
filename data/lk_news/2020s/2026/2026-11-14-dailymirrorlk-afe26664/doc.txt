Sunshine Holdings brings diabetes screening and awareness to 9 Healthguard outlets

From left: Suwa Diviya Medical Coordinator Dr. Chamari Pandithage, Consultant Physician Dr. Kayathri Periasamy and Consultant Endocrinologist and Sri Lanka Diabetes Federation Chairperson Dr. Maheshi Amarawardena

Sunshine Holdings PLC, through its community health arm Sunshine Foundation for Good, conducted a five-day diabetes screening and awareness programme across nine Healthguard outlets from November 09 to 13, 2026, ahead of the World Diabetes Day.

Conducted under the foundation’s Suwa Diviya initiative, the programme focused on encouraging early detection, improving awareness of diabetes risks and helping communities make informed decisions about their health.

The initiative came as diabetes continues to place a significant burden on Sri Lanka’s healthcare system. According to the International Diabetes Federation, an estimated 1.6 million Sri Lankan adults aged 20 to 79 were living with diabetes in 2024, with approximately 37.7 percent of cases remaining undiagnosed. These figures point to the importance of accessible screening, greater public awareness and timely medical advice.

This year’s programme aligned with the World Diabetes Day 2026 theme ‘Early action on diabetes’, which focused on early diagnosis, reducing risk and improving access to appropriate care. Through Suwa Diviya, Sunshine Holdings aims to bring these priorities closer to communities by creating opportunities for people to better understand diabetes and the importance of preventive healthcare.

Sunshine Holdings Group CEO Shyam Sathasivam said, “Last year’s response showed us that people value having a convenient place to ask questions and understand their health risks. This year, we wanted to build on that by giving communities more opportunities to take action early. Diabetes awareness should not be limited to a single day. Through Suwa Diviya, our focus is on helping people make informed choices about their health and encouraging habits that can make a difference over time.”
