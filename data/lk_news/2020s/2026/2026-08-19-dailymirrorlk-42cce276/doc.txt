HNB Life among Best Workplaces for 4th time

HNB Life PLC has been recognised by Great Place to Work Sri Lanka as one of the Top 50 Best Workplaces in Sri Lanka, further strengthening its reputation as an organisation that places its people at the heart of its success.

This marks the fourth time the company has received this prestigious recognition, reaffirming its continued commitment to building a workplace culture founded on trust, inclusivity, collaboration and growth.

The recognition is based on comprehensive feedback from the employees and an independent assessment of the company’s workplace culture, leadership practices and initiatives aimed at creating a positive and supportive environment for its people.

HNB Life Executive Director and CEO Lasitha Wimalaratne stated, “Being recognised as one of Sri Lanka’s Best Workplaces for the fourth time is a proud milestone for HNB Life. This recognition reflects the culture we have built together over the years and most importantly, the trust and confidence our people place in the organisation. As we celebrate our 25-year journey and step into a new chapter as HNB Life, we remain committed to creating a workplace where every employee feels valued, empowered and inspired to grow.”

Over the years, HNB Life has continued to strengthen its people-focused culture through initiatives centred around employee engagement, leadership development, diversity and inclusion, learning and career advancement. The company believes that nurturing a workplace where the employees can thrive is fundamental to delivering exceptional value to the customers and achieving sustainable growth.

EVP and Chief People Officer Navin Rupasinghe said, “Receiving this recognition for the fourth time is a testament to the culture we continue to build together. This achievement belongs to every member of the HNB Life family whose passion and contribution shape our workplace every day. We believe that creating a great workplace is an ongoing journey that requires us to continuously listen, learn and evolve. As we move forward, our focus remains on creating an environment where our people have the opportunity to grow, contribute and succeed.”
