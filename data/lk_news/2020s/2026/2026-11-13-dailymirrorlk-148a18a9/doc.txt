SriLankan Airlines increases Melbourne flights to 10 per week

Colombo, Nov. 13 (Daily Mirror) - SriLankan Airlines will operate three additional weekly flights to Melbourne from November 15, increasing its services to Australia to 14 flights a week amid rising travel demand between the two countries.

The additional flights will operate on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Sundays, adding to the airline’s existing daily Melbourne service.

Under the revised schedule, flight UL608 will depart Colombo at 2.15 p.m. and arrive in Melbourne at 5.40 a.m. the following day. The return flight, UL609, will depart Melbourne at 7.10 a.m. and arrive in Colombo at 12.10 p.m.

The existing daily service will continue, with flight UL604 departing Colombo at 12.35 a.m. and arriving in Melbourne at 4.00 p.m. Its return flight, UL605, will leave Melbourne at 5.30 p.m. and arrive in Colombo at 10.30 p.m.

In a statement, the national carrier said the expansion was in response to growing travel demand between Sri Lanka and Australia.

Australia has also emerged as one of Sri Lanka’s fastest-growing inbound tourism markets, supported by leisure travellers and the Sri Lankan diaspora.
