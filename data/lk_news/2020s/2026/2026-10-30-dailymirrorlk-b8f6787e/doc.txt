48 children murdered in Sri Lanka in three years

Colombo, October 30 (Daily Mirror)- A total of 1,664 murders were reported in Sri Lanka over the past three years, including 48 children among the victims, according to Sri Lanka Police.

According to the Police Performance Report for 2025, a total of 573 murders were reported in 2023, followed by 555 in 2024 and 536 in 2025.

The report states that the police have been able to solve approximately 93% of the total murder cases reported during the three-year period.

A total of 317 women were among those killed during the same period.

Accordingly, 105 women were murdered in 2023, while 104 were killed in 2024 and 108 in 2025.

Meanwhile, 48 children were murdered during the three-year period. According to the annual performance reports, 16 children were killed in 2023, 20 in 2024 and 12 in 2025.

Police Media Spokesman Assistant Superintendent of Police and Attorney-at-Law F. U. Wootler said the Police Performance Report for 2026 is expected to be released shortly.
