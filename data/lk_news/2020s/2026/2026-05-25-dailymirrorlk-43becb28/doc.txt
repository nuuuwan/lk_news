Pelwatte to invest Rs.1.86bn in liquid milk plant in Kurunegala

Pelwatte Dairy Industries said it would invest Rs.1.86 billion in a new liquid milk manufacturing facility in Kurunegala, as the company seeks to expand into value-added dairy products while supporting Sri Lanka’s push to strengthen local food security and nutrition.

The investment will be made through its fully-owned subsidiary Pelwatte Foods (Private) Limited, under an agreement signed with the Board of Investment (BOI) on May 21.

The Greenfield facility, expected to begin construction in July 2026, with commercial operations targeted by July 2027, will produce liquid milk and flavoured milk beverages including chocolate, vanilla, strawberry and iced coffee variants.

The move comes as Sri Lanka continues to grapple with persistent malnutrition concerns, particularly among the mothers and children, despite the improvements in macroeconomic stability.

According to global and national health estimates cited by the company, nearly one in six babies in Sri Lanka is born with low birth weight, while around 17 percent of children under five are stunted and over 15 percent suffer from wasting.

Pelwatte said the new plant, which would feature automated processing technology and modern manufacturing systems, is expected to strengthen the domestic dairy production capacity and reduce reliance on imported dairy products.

The investment also reflects a broader shift by the local dairy producers towards higher-value and ready-to-consume nutrition products as consumer demand evolves.

Although the project is expected to generate limited direct employment, due to its automated nature, the company said it would support the wider dairy ecosystem by increasing the demand for locally sourced raw milk.

Pelwatte, which has built its brand around full cream milk powder products, said the expansion into liquid milk is aimed at improving access to protein-rich nutritional products, particularly for the children and expecting mothers.

The agreement was signed by Pelwatte Dairy Chairman Ariyaseela Wickremanayake and Managing Director Akmal Wickramanayake in the presence of BOI Chairman Dr. Sulakshana Jayawardena.
