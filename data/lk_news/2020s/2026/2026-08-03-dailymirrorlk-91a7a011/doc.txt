Mahindra showcases future of electric mobility at EV Motor Show

Mahindra by Evolution Auto made a strong impact at the Colombo EV Motor Show, showcasing its growing range of electric vehicles and strengthening its position as a trusted mobility brand in Sri Lanka’s transition towards sustainable transportation.

Visitors had the opportunity to explore the Mahindra Treo Plus, an electric passenger three-wheeler designed for efficient urban mobility, the Mahindra Zor, a cargo electric three-wheeler built for last-mile delivery needs, and the Mahindra ZEO, a cargo four-wheeler developed to support modern commercial transport requirements.

Throughout the exhibition, the Mahindra stall attracted strong interest from customers who were keen to understand the practical benefits of shifting to electric mobility. The conversations went beyond product display, allowing visitors to learn about running cost savings, vehicle performance, charging convenience, cargo capacity, passenger comfort and long-term business value. For many potential customers, the event served as an important introduction to how Mahindra electric vehicles can support both daily transport and commercial operations.

The Treo Plus drew attention from passenger transport operators looking for a reliable and cost-effective alternative to traditional fuel-powered three-wheelers. Its electric platform, low operating cost and suitability for city travel made it a strong point of interest among visitors. The Zor also generated meaningful engagement from small business owners and delivery operators seeking a practical solution for urban cargo movement. Meanwhile, the ZEO highlighted Mahindra’s capability in the electric commercial vehicle space, offering a future-ready option for businesses that require greater cargo capacity and efficiency.
