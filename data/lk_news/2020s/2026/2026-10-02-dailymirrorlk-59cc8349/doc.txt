Bitumen shortage hampers road construction

Colombo, Oct. 3 (Daily Mirror) - A shortage of bitumen, used to surface roads, slowed road construction in Sri Lanka in August, according to the Central Bank’s latest construction survey.

Contractors said the continuing shortage was holding back road works and slowing growth across the construction industry.

“Many survey respondents highlighted the ongoing bitumen shortage, which continued to constrain road construction progress,” the Central Bank said.

The shortage comes as government-funded projects bring more work to construction companies. New orders grew at a faster pace in August, although overall construction activity expanded more slowly than in July.

Companies also reported shortages of skilled workers and other building materials.

Contractors expect activity to increase over the next three months as newly awarded projects get under way. However, the bitumen shortage and changes in material prices remain challenges.
