Are learner drivers barred from using expressways?

Colombo, Aug. 6 (Daily Mirror) - Vehicles displaying ‘L’ (Learner) plates and those driven by learner permit holders are not permitted to enter expressways, according to Police.

A learner’s permit is issued only for driving practice and does not authorise the holder to operate a vehicle on expressways, where a valid full driving licence is mandatory.

Police clarified that even if a fully licensed driver is seated alongside the learner, a vehicle displaying ‘L’ plates cannot be driven on an expressway.

"Vehicles driven by learner permit holders will be denied access at expressway entry points. If a learner-driven vehicle enters an expressway in violation of the law, the driver may face legal action, including fines or prosecution," Police said.

Police also warned that insurance claims arising from accidents involving vehicles being operated unlawfully may be rejected.
