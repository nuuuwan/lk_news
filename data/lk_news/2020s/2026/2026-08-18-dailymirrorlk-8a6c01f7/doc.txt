Health Ministry seeks solution to Muslim nurses’ uniform concerns

Colombo, Aug. 18 (Daily Mirror) - The Ministry of Health has taken steps to find an immediate and permanent solution to concerns over the official uniforms worn by Muslim female nursing officers at Kalmunai Hospital and other hospitals.

The issue was discussed at a special meeting chaired by the Health Minister at the Parliamentary Complex this morning, with Muslim and Tamil government MPs also participating.

The meeting focused on the sensitive issue, which has attracted attention in recent days. Participants stressed the need for a peaceful and practical solution that would ensure the smooth functioning of hospitals while safeguarding the rights of the nursing officers concerned and maintaining unity among all parties.

The Health Minister assured those present that immediate short-term measures would be introduced to address the current situation.
