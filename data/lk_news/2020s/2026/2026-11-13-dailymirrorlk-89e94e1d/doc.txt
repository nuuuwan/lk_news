Youth arrested for abducting and raping 12-year-old girl

Colombo, Nov. 14 (Daily Mirror) - The Buttala Police arrested a young person for allegedly abducting a 12-year-old girl (aged 12 years and 11 months) and raping her. The girl resided near the 'Dekahamaara' section of the Wellawaya-Ella Road.

The girl’s mother had filed a complaint with the police reporting her daughter missing. During the subsequent investigation, the girl and her boyfriend were discovered at the boyfriend's residence in the Gonagangara area of ​​Buttala and were taken into custody.

According to the girl’s statement, she contacted her boyfriend by phone, claiming she was ill, and asked him to meet her near a bus stop in the '06 Kanuwa' area so she could obtain medicine. The boyfriend arrived on a motorcycle, picked her up, and took her to his home.

The Buttala and Gonagangara Police stations are conducting a joint investigation.
