Spot On returns with 5 of  Sri Lanka’s standout brand stories

We often look to global markets for inspiration, while there is so much being created here that our own industry can learn from.

That is the thinking behind Spot On, a platform by The Faculty of Brands (TFOB) that shines a spotlight on noteworthy brand work from Sri Lanka.

It brings the people behind the work together to share the thinking, purpose, challenges and lessons behind it. An opportunity for the industry to learn from one another, build confidence in what we can create here, sharpen our craft and take our work to a global stage.

Following a successful inaugural edition that brought together more than 400 participants, Spot On returns on November 12, 2026, at Bay 7, Trace Expert City, Colombo, for its second edition.

This year, five new Sri Lankan brands will take the stage, offering a closer look at the thinking, decisions and work behind some of the country’s most noteworthy brand stories.

The line-up features Barista Coffee Lanka (Pvt.) Ltd Chief Executive Officer Dilupa Pathirana, who will share how Barista has helped shape Sri Lanka’s coffee landscape through years of sustained effort, with particularly exponential growth over the last two years. The story behind that growth is one of sharp execution, knowing exactly what the brand stands for and having the conviction to commit to it.

Representing Maliban, Maliban Group of Companies Group Head of Corporate Communications Positha Perera and Shuffl Studio co-founder Sagar Hiranand will explore what it takes to keep a heritage brand relevant to a generation that did not grow up with it. Their story looks at how a brand with decades of history can make its legacy meaningful to new audiences, balancing the weight of heritage with the courage to do things differently.

LOVI Ceylon founder Asanka de Mel brings a perspective that connects brand building with the bigger question of national development. Through the LOVI story and his vision for Sri Lanka in 2048, he challenges marketers and business builders to think beyond the growth of individual companies. His ambition is a Sri Lanka that is 10 times richer by 2048 and his message is that businesses, their leaders and the people who build brands have a role to play in making that future possible.

From Unilever Sri Lanka, Director Marketing, Personal Care, Beauty and Wellbeing and Media Shamara Perera will discuss how Sunsilk tapped into the cultural influence of cinema to connect with younger audiences. Drawing on the popularity of Korean dramas, Bollywood and Hollywood, the Hair Moves campaign recognised the role of hair in the worlds and stories young audiences already love, using cinema as a vehicle to reach them.

Completing the line-up, Lion Brewery (Ceylon) PLC Chief Sales and Marketing Officer Madhushanka Ranatunga will share the thinking behind building a brand in a category where traditional advertising opportunities are restricted. With a brand legacy spanning 139 years, Lion’s story explores how marketers can work within constraints, find different ways to reach audiences and continue building strong brands when conventional tools are not available.

Each presentation will take the audience behind the finished work, exploring the thinking, challenges, strategic choices and execution that shaped the brand’s journey. Together, the five stories offer a range of perspectives on what it takes to build, grow and sustain brands in the Sri Lankan market.

The event begins at 6:00 p.m. Tickets are available through PickMe Explore.
