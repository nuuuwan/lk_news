Sri Lanka getting hotter and hotter

Colombo, Aug. 17 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka is expected to experience its temperature rising further next year than previous averages, a climatologist said yesterday.

Department of Meteorology Climatologist Malith Fernando told Daily Mirror that Sri Lanka could experience its highest ever temperature by March 2027 along with the rest of the world . " It is predicted that the entire globe will experience the highest ever temperature in its history and Sri Lanka would also experience the same due to El-Nino phenomenon" he said while highlighting that the average temperature in Sri Lanka could rise close 40 c degrees. The last time the country's temperature had gone up to 40c degrees was April 10, 1983. He said areas such as Northern, North Central, Eastern and districts such as Hambamtota would be affected mostly.

He said Sri Lanka's average temperature- calculated based on data available for a 30 year period between 1991 and 2022, remained 33- 35 c.

"It can rise further. Recently, the temperature rose to 39.9 c on July 25 , 2026 in Trincomalee ," he said.

Commenting on the heat that is experiencing in Sri Lanka these days is due to the Southwest Monsoon. Maximum temperature in some areas almost touched 37c in some areas such as Pollonnaruwa, Matala, Mullathivu, Moneragala and Trincomalee districts. However Mr. Fernando said the current temperature hike is due to Southwest Monsoon and is a usual phenomenon.
