Indian Defence Minister Rajnath Singh departs Sri Lanka

Colombo, Sep. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Indian Defence Minister Rajnath Singh has left the island after concluding his official three-day visit to Sri Lanka.

He departed from Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) on board a special flight at around 1.00 p.m. today (10).

During his visit, the Indian Defence Minister met with President Anura Kumara Dissanayake, Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya, Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa and several other officials.
