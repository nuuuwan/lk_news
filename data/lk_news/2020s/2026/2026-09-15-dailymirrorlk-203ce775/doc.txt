TID takes over probe into alleged LTTE promotion at SLPP Rally

Colombo, Sep. 15 (Daily Mirror) - The complaint lodged with the Criminal Investigation Department (CID) over alleged promotion of the banned Liberation Tigers of Tamil Eelam (LTTE) at a Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) rally in Anuradhapura has been handed over to the Terrorism Investigation Division (TID) for investigation.

Police Spokesman ASP F.U. Wootler said the Inspector General of Police had directed the investigation to be handed over to the TID.

Yesterday, a group of civil society members identifying themselves as the “Joint Opposition” lodged a complaint following allegations that statements made during the rally amounted to promoting or supporting the LTTE, which remains a proscribed organisation in Sri Lanka.

On Sunday, the Opposition raised concerns over remarks reportedly made during the rally, which was attended by several political figures, including former Minister Wimal Weerawansa and Parliamentarian Archchuna Ramanathan.

Addressing the rally, MP Archchuna Ramanathan referred to himself as a “Tiger” and criticised the public for electing a “fox”, in an apparent reference to President Anura Kumara Dissanayake.

The remarks reportedly surprised even some SLPP supporters, who have long been critical of references to the LTTE. However, some supporters were seen cheering when the LTTE was mentioned on stage during the rally in Anuradhapura.
