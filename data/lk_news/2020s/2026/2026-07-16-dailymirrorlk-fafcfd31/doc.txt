Access Real Estate partners with Chaminda Vaas to drive nationwide expansion

Access Real Estate, a prominent subsidiary of the Access group, yesterday officially appointed legendary Sri Lankan cricketer Chaminda Vaas as its Brand Ambassador, aligning with the company’s landmark 15th anniversary celebrations.

The strategic partnership anchors a new era of growth for the property developer, which aims to aggressively expand its footprint into new geographical districts across Sri Lanka.

Since its inception in 2011, the company has grown into a major industry player, successfully delivering more than 155 developments spanning over 350 acres of land and securing a loyal customer base of over 4,500 landowners.

The collaboration is designed to spearhead the company’s next phase of business expansion, moving beyond its traditional strongholds into new regional markets.

Access Real Estate specialises in developing fully-serviced, ready-to-build residential and commercial plots, equipped with modern infrastructure, including three-phase electricity, pipe-borne water and wide internal roads situated near major economic hubs.

By aligning with Vaas, the company plans to scale its operations manifold, leveraging his massive public appeal and trusted reputation to reach the hearts of consumers in untapped markets across the island.

Reflecting on the milestone and the company’s forward-looking expansion strategy, Access Real Estate Managing Director and CEO Theo Fernando noted that the partnership reflects the core values of the institution.

“Reaching our 15-year milestone is a testament to the trust our clients place in us. Welcoming Chaminda Vaas to the Access Real Estate family is the perfect way to celebrate. Chaminda represents the pinnacle of Sri Lankan dedication, consistency and excellence, the exact pillars upon which we develop our projects,” Fernando stated, highlighting the company’s commitment to venturing into new pathways and achieving new heights.

Expressing his enthusiasm for the partnership and the company’s vision to elevate community living standards nationwide, the newly appointed Brand Ambassador Chaminda Vaas highlighted the strong foundations of the venture.

“Access Real Estate has proven itself to be a company worthy of respect and admiration, not just because of their impeccable professional standards but due to their unwavering commitment to the communities they serve,” Vaas said.

He further emphasised that trust, transparency and exceptional service remain the true pillars of a successful real estate journey as the company opens doors to a brighter future, one property at a time.
