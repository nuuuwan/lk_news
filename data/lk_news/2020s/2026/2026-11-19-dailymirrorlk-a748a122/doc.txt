Boats warned as Cyclone Akvan intensifies

Colombo, November 19 (Daily Mirror) - Naval and fishing communities have been warned not to venture into deep-sea areas of the west-central Bay of Bengal due to strong winds and rough seas caused by Severe Cyclonic Storm “Akvan”.

The Department of Meteorology said the storm had moved northwards at a speed of 17 kmph during the past six hours and was centred near latitude 15.4°N and longitude 85.2°E, approximately 830 km northeast of Kankesanthurai, at around 11.30 a.m. yesterday (November 19).

The storm is expected to continue moving north-northwestwards and cross the Andhra Pradesh coast between Narsapur and Puri in India around 11.30 a.m. tomorrow (November 20).

Accordingly, naval and fishing communities have been advised not to venture into deep-sea areas bounded by 12°N–20°N and 80°E–93°E until further notice.

Those already operating in the affected sea areas have been advised to move to safer locations.

The Department of Meteorology has also urged naval and fishing communities to remain alert to future weather forecasts and advisories.
