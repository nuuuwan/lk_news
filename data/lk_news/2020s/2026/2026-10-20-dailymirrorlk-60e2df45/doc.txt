Heavy showers of up to 100mm expected in several provinces

Colombo, Oct. 20 (Daily Mirror) - Rainfall is expected to increase across the island from this evening (20) due to the development of a low-level atmospheric disturbance near Sri Lanka, the Department of Meteorology said.

Showers or thundershowers are likely in many parts of the island after 1.00 p.m., while heavy rainfall exceeding 100mm is possible at some locations in the Northern, North Central, Central, Eastern, North Western and Uva provinces.

Morning showers are also likely in the Northern and North Western provinces, according to the Department.

Meanwhile, foggy conditions may prevail in some areas of the Western, Sabaragamuwa, Central and Uva provinces during the early morning hours.
