Some Opposition MPs keep away from tea party after the Budget

Colombo, Nov. 13 (Daily Mirror) - Some Opposition MPs kept away from the tea party hosted by the government after the budget speech yesterday.

A Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) MP who did not wish to be named said the Leader of Opposition Sajith Premadasa asked them not to participate in the tea party.

Chief Opposition Whip Gayantha Karunatilaka told Daily Mirror that a meeting of the SJB Parliamentary Group was fixed just after the budget. “We will have to attend this meeting and therefore we may not be able to focus on the tea party,” he said.

However, some Opposition MPs including Rauff Hakeem and Shanakiyan Rasamanickam were seen at the tea party according to eyewitnesses.

MP Dayasiri Jayasekara also confirmed that the Opposition leader did have a discussion with the MPs during the time the tea party was going on.
