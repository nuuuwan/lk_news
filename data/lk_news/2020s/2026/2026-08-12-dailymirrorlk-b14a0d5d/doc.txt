Hikkaduwa–Maradana train delayed after repeated technical issues

Colombo, August 12 (Daily Mirror) - Passengers travelling on the Hikkaduwa–Maradana railway line faced delays today after a train developed technical problems at two stations along the route.

The train encountered technical difficulties while stopping at Beruwala and Maggona railway stations, disrupting its scheduled journey towards Maradana.

Railway authorities said the technical fault had affected the Hikkaduwa–Maradana service, resulting in delays to passengers using the coastal railway line.
