Tea auction sees mixed demand as offerings edge above 5mnkg

Total Auction offerings barely surpassed 5.0 M/Kgs. Low Growns continued to meet with reasonably good demand, whilst the High & Medium Grown liquoring Leafy teas witnessed an easier trend and a sluggish market, particularly as the sale progressed.  Orthodox Rotovane teas sold well with the better teas continuing to attract a fair premium.

Ex-Estate offerings were limited to 0.6 M/Kgs, the lowest on offer since Sale No. 48 of 5/6 December 2023 (0.5 M/Kgs).

The limited availability of good liquoring Western BOP/BOPF’s continued to sell at a premium. Interestingly, in this category the BOP’s often secured a premium price vis-à-vis the corresponding BOPF’s. Teas in the Below Best and Plainer categories sold around last week’s levels.

Nuwara Eliya and Uda Pussellawa regions had hardly any offerings. Uva – BOP’s sold at a premium although limited in availability once again, whilst the corresponding BOPF’s were Rs. 20-40 per kg dearer.

High & Mid Grown CTC teas continued to maintain the previous week’s price levels, whilst the Low Growns continued to sell well with price fluctuations reflective of quality.

Less activity from shippers to the UK, the continent and South Africa, whilst there was fair activity from Japan, China together with the CIS and Middle East.

Low Growns totalled approximately 2.2 M/Kgs with all categories witnessing fair demand.

In the Leafy and Semi-Leafy catalogues, well-made BOP1’s together with teas at the lower end were firm, whilst the Below Best together with the bold varieties declined. OP1’s, in general, were firm. Select Best OP’s were firm to dearer, whilst the balance together with the OPA’s in general sold around last. Select Best and Best PEK/PEK1’s declined, whilst the balance were firm.

In the Tippy catalogue, a selection of high-priced FBOP’s maintained, whilst the balance together with the Best and Below Best were easier. Cleaner teas at the bottom were firm, whilst the balance were easier. A few Select Best FF1’s sold around last week’s levels, whilst the balance in general were lower.
