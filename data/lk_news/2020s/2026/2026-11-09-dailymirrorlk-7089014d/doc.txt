Fire in China’s Shanxi province apartment building kills 14

MACAU BUSINESS.COM - A fire that broke out in an apartment building in northern China killed at least 14 people, state media said Monday, raising the death toll from the previous night.

The blaze occurred in a building in Shanxi province’s Taiyuan city around 5:00 pm on Sunday, the official Xinhua news agency said that evening.

“A total of 38 people were sent to hospitals for treatment, among whom five have died,” Xinhua reported, raising the death toll to 14 on Monday morning.

The flames were extinguished on Sunday, with search and rescue efforts completed, it said.
