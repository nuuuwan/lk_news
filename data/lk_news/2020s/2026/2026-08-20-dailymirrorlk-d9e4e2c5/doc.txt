Three-day national mourning declared for late Nanda Malani

Colombo, August 20 (Daily Mirror) - The Government has declared August 21, 22 and 23 as national days of mourning following the passing of veteran singer Dr. Nanda Malani.

The Department of Government Information said further details on arrangements and observances connected with the national mourning period would be announced through the Ministry of Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government.
