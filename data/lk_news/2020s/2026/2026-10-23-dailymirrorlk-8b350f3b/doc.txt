Dayasiri sings Vas Kavi in Parliament

Colombo, Oct. 23 (Daily Mirror) - Opposition MP Dayasiri Jayasekara created a dramatic scene in Parliament by singing a Vas Kaviya (a traditional plea seeking divine intervention to punish wrongdoers).

He alleged that there was a move to arrest those who wrote and sang a Vas Kaviya that is being circulated on social media.

“Those in the Government are afraid of Vas Kavi,” he said before beginning to sing.
