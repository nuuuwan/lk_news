Lightning warning issued for several districts

The Department of Meteorology has issued an Amber-level lightning warning for several districts across the country, cautioning the public to remain vigilant until 11 p.m. today (24).

The warning, issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre at 11.57 a.m., said showers or thundershowers were likely in the affected areas during the evening or night.

The warning covers all Divisional Secretariat divisions in Badulla, Colombo, Galle, Gampaha, Kalutara, Kandy, Kegalle, Kurunegala, Mannar, Matale, Matara, Monaragala, Nuwara Eliya, Polonnaruwa, Puttalam and Ratnapura districts.

Several Divisional Secretariat divisions in Ampara, Anuradhapura, Batticaloa, Hambantota, Kilinochchi, Mullaitivu, Trincomalee and Vavuniya districts are also under the warning.

The Meteorology Department warned that thunderstorms and lightning strikes could cause serious injuries or deaths, particularly among people exposed outdoors.

It also cautioned that strong winds and heavy rainfall could accompany thunderstorms, potentially disrupting outdoor activities, transport, electricity and communication services.

The public has been advised to suspend outdoor activities, including sports, construction and agricultural work, when thunderstorms approach.

People have also been urged to move into substantial buildings or enclosed vehicles, avoid beaches and other water bodies, and refrain from taking shelter under isolated trees or in open sheds.

The department further advised the public to stay away from electrical appliances, plumbing and wired electrical systems during thunderstorms.
