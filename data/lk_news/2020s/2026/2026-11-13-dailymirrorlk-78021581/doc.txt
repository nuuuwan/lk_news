Two accused acquitted, Manamperi, Perera called to present defence

Colombo, Nov. 13 (Daily Mirror) - The Colombo High Court today ordered the acquittal of the first and second accused and directed the third accused, Sampath Manamperi and the fourth accused, Niranjan Perera, to call their defence in a case involving the alleged robbery of a lorry loaded with rice, cash and a mobile phone belonging to a Tamil businessman near the Narahenpita Junction.

The incident allegedly involved a group of police officers, including an individual who had served as a personal security officer to former President Mahinda Rajapaksa during his tenure.

The order was delivered when the case was taken up before Colombo High Court Judge Rasantha Godawela today.

The court made the order while delivering its ruling on an application made by the accused under Section 200(1) of the Code of Criminal Procedure, seeking their acquittal without being called upon to present their defence.

In his order, the Judge stated that there was insufficient evidence to prove the charges against the first and second accused beyond reasonable doubt. Accordingly, they were acquitted.

The two accused who were acquitted were the first accused, Indika Daladawatta and the second accused, Bandula Jayasinghe.

Meanwhile, the court decided to call for the defence of the remaining two accused, Sampath Manamperi and Niranjan Perera, having determined that there were insufficient grounds to acquit them at this stage.

Counsel appearing for the third and fourth accused informed court that they intended to call witnesses on behalf of their clients.

The Judge accordingly directed the defence to submit a list of witnesses to court within one week, if they intended to call witnesses.

The Attorney General had instituted the case against four accused, including Sampath Manamperi, on seven charges, including allegedly impersonating police officers, intimidating a businessman named Selvadurai Kolavennadan, who was transporting rice from the Eastern Province, and robbing him of a lorry loaded with rice and other belongings on August 12, 2009.
