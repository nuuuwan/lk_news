Multilac secures five Green Label Certifications at Green Building Awards 2026

Macksons Paint Lanka (Pvt) Ltd has reinforced its commitment to environmental sustainability, with five Multilac products receiving Green Label Certification from the Green Building Council of Sri Lanka (GBCSL) at the 15th Annual Green Building Awards 2026.

Multilac 3 in 1 MultiProtector, 3 in 1 Waterproofing Paint and 3 in 1 Speed Primer were awarded three-star ratings, while Multilac Premium Sheen Emulsion and Weatherguard Ultra received two-star ratings.

This recognition underscores the company’s ongoing efforts to incorporate sustainable practices into product development while delivering innovative, high-quality coating solutions that support environmentally responsible construction.

As Sri Lanka’s largest paint exporter, Multilac continues to prioritise innovation, product safety and environmental stewardship, reaffirming its commitment to contributing towards a greener, safer and more sustainable future.
