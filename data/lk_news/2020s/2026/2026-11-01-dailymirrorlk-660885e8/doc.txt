Welimada deputy principal who assaulted grade 7 schoolgirl surrenders

Nov. 1 (Daily Mirror) - A deputy principal of a school in the Welimada Education Zone, accused of assaulting a 12-year-old Grade 7 student with a broom handle, wooden sticks and her hand, surrendered to the Ambagasdowa Police yesterday (31) along with her husband.

The suspect was produced before the Welimada Magistrate’s Court and released on a personal bail of Rs. 200,000 by Acting Magistrate S.B. Samarakoon.

Police said the deputy principal had been avoiding the police for several days following the incident. She had reportedly received treatment at a private hospital, claiming that she was suffering from high blood pressure, before staying at a house in the Bandarawela area.

The incident had taken place on October 23, when the deputy principal entered a Grade 7 classroom during the third period as the regular teacher had been absent.

According to police, the deputy principal questioned the students about several pieces of leaves found on the classroom floor. When two students stood up, she allegedly took a broom kept near the classroom door and assaulted them with its handle.

She had allegedly struck the 12-year-old girl with the broom handle, poked her body with wooden sticks and slapped her on the ear.

The student later informed her mother and grandfather about the incident. She initially sought treatment at an Ayurvedic centre in the area but was subsequently admitted to the Welimada Base Hospital after experiencing pain in her hand.

Police said the incident came to light when hospital police officers recorded a statement from the girl and informed the Ambagasdowa Police.

The suspect had not reported to school following the incident and had allegedly gone into hiding while police were looking for her.

The girl, who received treatment at the Welimada Base Hospital, has since been discharged.

Further investigations are being conducted by the Ambagasdowa Police under the instructions of senior police officers in the Uva Province and Badulla District.
