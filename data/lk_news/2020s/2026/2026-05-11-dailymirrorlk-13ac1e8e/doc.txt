Water tariff revision due on June 30: Minister

Colombo, May 11 (Daily Mirror) - The next revision of water tariffs will take place on June 30, Minister of Housing, Construction and Water Supply Susil Ranasinghe said.

The Minister stated that water charges are reviewed twice a year in line with prevailing economic and operational conditions.

According to him, the upcoming tariff revision will be based on the situation as of June 30, with the revised rates expected to reflect current sector costs and operational requirements at the time of the review.
