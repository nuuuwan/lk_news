Uga Resorts earns three top rankings at 2026 Condé Nast Traveller Readers’ Choice Awards

Uga Resorts has earned notable recognition in the 2026 Condé Nast Traveller Readers’ Choice Awards, with three its properties ranked among the leading hotels and resorts in the Rest of Asia Category.

Uga Prāva claimed top honours as number one among the region’s best hotels with a score of 98.82, while Uga Haloowella took eighth place with a score of 91.24. Simultaneously, Uga Ulagalla was ranked sixth among the regions best resorts scoring 97.28.

Published in the November 2026 issue of Condé Nast Traveller, the annual Readers’ Choice Awards are designed to celebrate outstanding travel experiences as voted for by readers. The results are a testament to the diversity of Uga Resorts’ portfolio, which brings together Sri Lanka’s beaches, hill country heritage and cultural richness through distinctive hospitality experiences.

Locate along the coast in Tangalle, Uga Prāva was voted the number one hotel in the Rest of Asia category. Featuring seven suites, the property offers a cozy, intimate stay that combines contemporary minimalist design with the natural beauty of the Indian Ocean. Notable highlights include the infinity pool, considered wellness experiences and dining shaped by the daily catch and other local sourced ingredients.

The property also offers guests an opportunity to explore the country’s southern coastline, with experiences such as whale watching, snorkelling, visits to ancient temples, and encounters with the regions picturesque natural landscape. The top ranking is attributed to Uga Prāva’s distinctive approach to luxury, which is centred on thoughtful design, tranquillity and strong connection with its surroundings.

Uga Halloowella, which secured eighth position, is nestled in the hills of Hatton, overlooking the Castlereagh Reservoir. Established in 1880, the historic estate was restored by Uga in collaboration with reputed architect Channa Daswatte. Situated 1,444 metres above sea level, the colonial bungalow is surrounded by lush tea plantations and combines historic character with contemporary comforts.

The property features six luxurious suites, refined dining, expansive laws and an infinity pool, creating an intimate retreat amidst the enduring charm of the hill country. The recognition reflects Uga Hallowella’s preservation of its architectural heritage and its ability to provide distinct experiences for the modern traveller.

Uga Ulagalla secured sixth place in the Best Resorts in Rest of Asia category. This recognition is attributed to its distinct combination of heritage, seclusion and nature-led luxury. Set among 58 acres of paddy fields and ancient woodland in Sri Lanka’s Cultural Triangle, the property occupies an estate with deep historical roots. Featuring 25 standalone villas, each with a private plunge pool, Uga Ulagalla offers guests an intimate experience among landscape of reservoirs ponds and greenery.

The heart of the estate is the 150-year-old house, which was once the residence of the village chieftain. Today, the manor is the centre point for dining and gathering, combining historic architecture and contemporary hospitality. The resort also offers access to some of Sri Lanka’s most significant cultural attractions, including ancient ruins, monumental dagobas and UNESCO World Heritage sites, providing a distinctive base from which to explore the island’s history.

The three rankings represent a significant achievement for Uga Resorts and reinforce Sri Lanka’s presence on the international luxury travel landscape. From Uga Prāva’s intimate coastal setting to Uga Halloowella’s historic hill country retreat and Uga Ulagalla’s heritage-rich wilderness, each property offers a distinct interpretation of Sri Lankan hospitality.

Recognition across both hotel and resort categories highlights the breadth of experiences available to international travellers and the appeal of properties that draw on their natural surroundings, cultural identity and heritage to create memorable stays.
