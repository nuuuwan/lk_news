Several rivers rise as heavy rains trigger minor flooding and landslides

Colombo, Nov. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Water levels in several rivers across the country are rising due to the prevailing rainy weather conditions, with minor flooding reported in several areas, the Irrigation Department said.

The Kalu, Nilwala, Gin, Walawe and Kuda Ganga rivers, along with the Attanagalu Oya, have recorded rising water levels.

Minor flooding has been reported in the Baddegama, Thawalama, Nagoda and Welivitiya-Divithura areas due to the rising water levels of the Gin Ganga.

Director of the Water Management Division of the Irrigation Department L.S. Suriyabandara said nearly 160 mm of rainfall had been recorded in the upper Gin Ganga basin, extending towards the Dellawa area.

He said the water levels of the Gin and Nilwala rivers had risen significantly, with the Nilwala Ganga approaching minor flood levels in areas downstream of Akuressa.

The lower Thawalama areas and locations near Baddegama have also reached minor flood levels, while low-lying areas in Nagoda and Peravittiya North are at risk of inundation, he said.

The Kalu Ganga, particularly at Millakanda, has also reached levels close to minor flooding. Low-lying areas in Bulathsinhala and Palindanuwara are already at risk of being inundated.

Meanwhile, water levels at Dunamale on the Attanagalu Oya have also reached minor flood levels, he added.

Separately, around 40 families in the Nugeyaya Grama Niladhari Division have been affected following the opening of spill gates at the Alikotaara Reservoir due to heavy rainfall.

The Disaster Management Centre (DMC) said residents in the affected houses had been advised to move to safe locations.

Traffic along the Ella-Passara road has been disrupted following a landslide in the Maussagolla area.

The DMC also reported that traffic on the Hatton-Nuwara Eliya main road had been disrupted due to a landslide in the Kudagama area.

Meanwhile, the National Building Research Organisation (NBRO) has issued Level 1 landslide warnings for Badulla, Colombo, Galle, Kalutara, Kandy, Kegalle, Monaragala, Nuwara Eliya and Ratnapura districts.

Level 2 warnings have also been issued for several Divisional Secretariat divisions in Ratnapura, Nuwara Eliya, Kegalle, Galle, Kandy and Badulla districts.
