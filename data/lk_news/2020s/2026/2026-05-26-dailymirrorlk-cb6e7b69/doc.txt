Laugfs Power expands renewable energy portfolio with new mini hydro power project

Laugfs Power PLC, has recently strengthened its clean energy portfolio with the commissioning of the Kehelgamu Oya 2 MW Mini Hydro Power Project in Ginigathhena.

The project has been developed by PAMS Power (Pvt) Ltd, a project company under Laugfs Power PLC. Designed as a run-of-the-river mini hydropower facility, the plant generates clean and sustainable electricity by harnessing the natural flow of Kehelgamu Oya while supplying renewable energy to Sri Lanka’s national grid.

With an expected annual generation capacity of approximately 7–8 GWh, the project is expected to make a meaningful contribution to the country’s renewable energy sector. The initiative will support Sri Lanka’s long-term objectives of reducing dependence on fossil fuel-based power generation, improving energy security, and promoting environmentally responsible development.

The Kehelgamu Oya Mini Hydro Power Project has been equipped with state-of-the-art hydro mechanical machinery supplied by VS Hydro, together with high-quality generator systems and electrical panel boards sourced from Germany. The integration of advanced technology and internationally sourced equipment is expected to support efficient, reliable, and safe operation of the plant throughout its long-term service life.

Commenting on the commissioning of the project, Russell De Zilva, Managing Director / Chief Executive Officer of Laugfs Power PLC, stated, “The commissioning of the Kehelgamu Oya Mini Hydro Power Project marks another important milestone in our renewable energy journey. This project reflects our ongoing commitment to investing in sustainable infrastructure that supports Sri Lanka’s growing energy needs while contributing to a cleaner and more resilient energy future. We remain focused on expanding our renewable energy footprint through responsible development, operational excellence, and environmentally sustainable practices.”

The completion and commissioning of the project further demonstrates Laugfs Power PLC’s continued commitment to expanding Sri Lanka’s renewable energy capacity through sustainable investments and responsible project development. The company remains focused on engineering excellence, environmental stewardship, and long-term value creation as it continues to play an active role in supporting the country’s transition towards a sustainable energy future.
