Sampath Bank-GV Auto accelerate EV adoption

Gavinro International Managing Director Abeeth Dangalla (fourth from left) and Sampath Bank Chief Business Intelligence Officer Darshin Pathinayake (fourth from right) exchange the agreement in the presence of representatives from both organisations

Sampath Bank recently entered into a strategic partnership with GV Auto by Gavinro International (Pvt.) Ltd, expanding access to electric vehicle (EV) ownership through exclusive financing benefits for the customers.

Formalised through the signing of a memorandum of understanding at Sampath Bank’s head office, the collaboration enables the customers to enjoy a preferential interest rate, with a 0.5 percent reduction from the bank’s published vehicle loan rate when purchasing the eligible EVs from GV Auto.

As an emerging one-stop EV mobility partner, GV Auto offers a range of smart and reliable transport solutions, including the premium Skywell BE11 electric SUV and Keyton commercial electric vehicles.

The partnership is designed to make sustainable mobility more accessible while delivering greater value, affordability and long-term savings to the customers seeking environmentally responsible transport options.

Combining Sampath Bank’s customer-centric financing solutions with GV Auto’s growing EV portfolio and comprehensive after-sales support network, the partnership supports the wider adoption of electric mobility in Sri Lanka while helping the customers transition to cleaner and more cost-effective transportation.
