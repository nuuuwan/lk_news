Two grenades thrown at Ratmalana home

Ratmalana, August 05 (Daily Mirror) - A hand grenade attack was carried out on a house located on Sri Gnanendra Mawatha in Ratmalana, police said.

According to the police, two hand grenades were thrown at the residence during the incident. One of the grenades exploded, while the other failed to detonate.

Police said that no injuries or casualties were reported as a result of the explosion.
