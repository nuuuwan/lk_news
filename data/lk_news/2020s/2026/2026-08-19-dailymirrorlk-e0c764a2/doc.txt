Imperial Teas Group secures BestWeb.LK 2026 Gold

Imperial Teas Group has achieved a significant milestone in digital excellence, securing the Gold award in the Best Food and Beverage Website Category, at the 16th edition of prestigious BestWeb.LK Awards 2026, held at Monarch Imperial.

The competition evaluates websites through a comprehensive and impartial assessment conducted by industry experts. The key evaluation areas include technical functionality, design and creativity, content quality, user interaction, mobile optimisation, multilingual support, website accessibility and overall community value.

The award recognises the strength of its official digital platform www.imperialteasgroup.lk.

The website successfully brings together the heritage and identity of food and beverage market, with a contemporary digital experience, providing international buyers, business partners and consumers with access to comprehensive information about the group, its products, brands and global presence.

The recognition reflects the group’s commitment to maintaining a high standard of digital communication while strengthening its engagement with the customers and stakeholders around the world.

The Gold award marks another achievement in Imperial Teas Group’s continued journey of innovation and excellence. As a globally focused Sri Lankan exporter, the group continues to embrace technology and digital transformation to complement its strengths in product quality, manufacturing, international trade and customer service.
