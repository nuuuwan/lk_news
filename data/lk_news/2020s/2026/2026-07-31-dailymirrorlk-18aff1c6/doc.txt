Govt moves to confiscate illegal assets worth Rs. 3.8 bn seized since 2024

Colombo, July 31 (Daily Mirror) - The Government has initiated legal action to vest with the State illegal assets worth Rs. 3,811 million seized between 2024 and 2026 as part of the ongoing nationwide anti-drug operation, Police Spokesman Assistant Superintendent of Police (ASP) F.U. Wootler said.

Addressing a special media briefing on the progress of the “Ratama Ekata” national operation on Friday (31), ASP Wootler said authorities had taken steps to deactivate or confiscate illegal assets worth Rs. 1,490 million in 2024.

He said legal proceedings were initiated to vest Rs. 1,549 million worth of illegal assets with the State in 2025, while action has already commenced this year to confiscate assets valued at Rs. 772 million.

ASP Wootler also revealed that 239,384 suspects linked to drug-related offences have been arrested under the national operation since its launch.

He said cases had been filed against 995 major drug traffickers, while investigations into the assets of 378 suspects taken into police custody were currently underway.

He added that detention orders had been obtained against 3,287 suspects as part of the ongoing crackdown.
