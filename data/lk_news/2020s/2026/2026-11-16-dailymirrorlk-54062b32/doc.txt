LIC Chief meets Foreign Minister Herath

During his visit to Colombo, Shri R. Doraiswamy, CEO and Managing Director of the Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) and Shri Ramakrishnan Chander MD, met with Vijitha Herath, Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism.

He was accompanied by Umesh Gauthum and Dinesh Weerakkody Directors of LIC Lanka, and Premila Chittazhi Ramanadhan, CEO and Managing Director of LIC Lanka.

The meeting provided an opportunity to strengthen engagement and further enhance cooperation between India and Sri Lanka.
