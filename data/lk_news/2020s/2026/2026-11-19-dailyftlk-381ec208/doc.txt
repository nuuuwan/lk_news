Seven new envoys present credentials to President

New Ambassadors and High Commissioners from Republic of Korea, Switzerland, EU, Egypt, Lao PDR, Namibia and Singapore also accredited

Seven newly appointed Ambassadors and High Commissioners presented their Letters of Credence to President Anura Kumara Dissanayake at a ceremony held at the Presidential Secretariat on 17 November, the President’s Office said.

In order of precedence, the newly accredited envoys are Republic of Korea Ambassador Kim Youn-Jung, Switzerland Ambassador Daniel Hunn, European Union (EU) Ambassador Sylvia Hartleif and Arab Republic of Egypt Ambassador Ahmed El-Sayed Abdel Halim Amer Helal, all resident in Colombo.

They are followed by Lao People’s Democratic Republic Ambassador Vithaya Xayavong and Republic of Namibia High Commissioner Wing Commander Alex L. Tukuhupwele (Retd), both resident in New Delhi, and Republic of Singapore High Commissioner Patrick Daniel, who is resident in Singapore.

The envoys were appointed by their respective Governments, with the concurrence of the Government of Sri Lanka, to represent their countries in Sri Lanka. The EU Ambassador was appointed to represent the EU in Sri Lanka.

Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Minister Vijitha Herath and Secretary to the President Dr. Nandika Sanath Kumanayake were also present at the ceremony.
