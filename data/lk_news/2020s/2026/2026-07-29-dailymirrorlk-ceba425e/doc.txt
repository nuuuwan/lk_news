Three Sinha Industries wins Gold Award at INCO 2026

Three Sinha Industries (Pvt) Ltd was recognised with the  Gold Award for the ‘Best Stall with Locally Assembled Products’ at the INCO 2026 Premier Industrial Exhibition organised by the Institution of Incorporated Engineers, Sri Lanka (IIESL).

The award was presented in recognition of the company’s outstanding exhibition presence, commitment to local manufacturing, and contribution towards promoting Sri Lankan-made industrial and engineering products.

The INCO Exhibition, held annually at the Bandaranaike Memorial International Conference Hall (BMICH), is regarded as one of the country’s leading industrial exhibitions, bringing together manufacturers, engineers, technology providers, and industry stakeholders from across Sri Lanka.
