Swisstek strengthens integrated building solutions capability

As Sri Lanka’s construction sector continues to evolve, demand is increasingly shifting towards solutions that combine quality, technical reliability, performance and long-term value. Against this backdrop, Swisstek is strengthening its position as a diversified building solutions business with capabilities spanning construction materials, aluminium systems, roofing and cladding and premium flooring.

With a heritage dating back to 1967, Swisstek has developed from a specialist timber flooring manufacturer into a broader construction solutions group supported by manufacturing scale, engineering expertise and quality standards trusted by the customers across international markets.

Today, the business operates across four specialist platforms: construction materials, aluminium systems and solutions, integrated roofing and cladding solutions and premium flooring solutions. Together, these capabilities address requirements across residential, commercial, hospitality and mixed-use developments.

Swisstek (Ceylon) PLC and Swisstek Aluminium Ltd Group Managing Director Daminda Perera said, “We are no longer operating as a specialist manufacturer in a single category. We have built capabilities across multiple areas of the construction value chain and our opportunity now is to bring those capabilities together in a way that creates greater value for our customers. As we look ahead, our focus is on strengthening what we manufacture, deepening our technical capabilities and expanding the markets we serve, while maintaining the quality and discipline that have shaped Swisstek for nearly six decades.”

Manufacturing remains central to the group’s operations. Swisstek currently operates three manufacturing facilities with an annual production capacity of approximately 100,000 MT and a workforce of more than 1,300. Its manufacturing base supports product development, quality control, technical consistency and supply capability across the portfolio.

The group’s technical capabilities extend across aluminium extrusion, architectural systems, roofing, waterproofing, surface preparation, flooring and quality assurance, supported by relevant local and international certifications and standards. Swisstek’s solutions have been deployed across prominent developments across the island. The company also serves eight international markets, continuing an export heritage established through its premium timber flooring business.

Part of Vallibel One PLC, Swisstek continues to build on nearly six decades of manufacturing and technical expertise as it expands its role within Sri Lanka’s construction and built environment sectors.
