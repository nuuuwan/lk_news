Bay of Bengal system threatens heavy rain across Sri Lanka

Colombo, Nov. 15 (Daily Mirror) - Showers are expected across several parts of Sri Lanka today (15) under the influence of a low-pressure area over the Bay of Bengal, which is expected to develop into a depression, the Department of Meteorology said.

According to the department's weather forecast issued at 5.30 a.m. today, the system was located approximately 600 kilometres east of Trincomalee and is expected to move northwestwards, away from Sri Lanka's eastern coast.

Cloudy skies are expected to prevail across the island throughout the day, with showers occurring at times in the Northern, North Central and Eastern provinces.

Several spells of showers are also expected in the Western and North Western provinces and in the Galle and Matara districts.

Showers or thundershowers may occur at a few places in other parts of the country during the evening or night.

The Department of Meteorology has warned the public to take precautions against possible damage from temporary localised strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.

Misty conditions are also expected in some areas of the Western, Sabaragamuwa, Central and Uva provinces, as well as in the Galle, Matara and Kurunegala districts during the early hours of the morning.

Meanwhile, the developing weather system has prompted warnings to fishing and naval communities to remain alert and closely monitor official weather bulletins.

An earlier marine weather advisory warned of rough sea conditions and temporarily strong gusty winds during thundershowers in several sea areas around the island.

The public, particularly those in areas experiencing heavy rainfall, has been urged to follow updates issued by the Department of Meteorology and relevant authorities.
