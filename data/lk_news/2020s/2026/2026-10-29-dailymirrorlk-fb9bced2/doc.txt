Senior journalist Siri Heenpella dies in New York at 77

Senior journalist and writer Siri Heenpella, who spent decades in journalism and was widely known for his writings on international affairs, has died in New York at the age of 77 following a brief illness, family sources said.

Heenpella, a father of two sons, was well known among Sri Lankan readers for his articles on international developments, which were published in the Lankadeepa newspaper under the byline “Siri Heenpella from New York.”

His reports and commentary offered Sri Lankan readers insights into global affairs, helping him maintain a longstanding connection with audiences in his home country throughout his journalistic career.
