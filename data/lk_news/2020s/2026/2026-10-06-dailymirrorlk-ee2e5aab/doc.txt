New DG appointed for Botanic Gardens

Colombo, Oct. 6 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet has approved the appointment of M.M.D.J. Senaratne as the Director General of the Department of National Botanic Gardens on a full-time basis.

The appointment follows the retirement of H.C.P. Jayaweera, who served as Director General of the Department and retired from public service on September 14, 2026, upon reaching the age of 60.

Accordingly, the Cabinet approved the proposal submitted by the Minister of Environment to appoint Senaratne to the vacant post with immediate effect.

Senaratne is currently serving in the Department as the senior-most officer in Grade I of the Sri Lanka Scientific Service.
