Ladies’ Circle Sri Lanka celebrates nine years of service

Celebrating nearly a decade of dedicated community service and lasting friendships, Ladies’ Circle Sri Lanka (LCSL) marked another successful year at its Annual General Meeting, held at Cloud Wine, Cinnamon Life.

The AGM brought together members from across the country to reflect on a year of outstanding achievements in service, leadership, fellowship, and international representation. Built on the international motto “Friendship and Service,” Ladies’ Circle Sri Lanka continues to demonstrate that meaningful social change begins when passionate women come together with a shared purpose.

Chartered by Ladies’ Circle Great Britain & Ireland in 2017, Ladies’ Circle Sri Lanka has grown into a dynamic organisation of young women aged 18 to 45 committed to serving their communities while building lifelong friendships. As the sister organisation of Round Table Sri Lanka, the association believes that true leadership is demonstrated through service, collaboration, and the desire to leave every community better than it was found.

As the Sri Lankan affiliate of Ladies’ Circle International (LCI), a global movement founded in 1932 with approximately 13,000 members across more than 40 countries, LCSL provides members with opportunities to exchange ideas, share best practices, and participate in international service initiatives while fostering friendships across borders.

Throughout the past year, Ladies’ Circle Sri Lanka carried out numerous service projects supporting education, healthcare, women’s empowerment, and families in need. Members volunteered countless hours, raised funds for worthy causes, and worked alongside community partners to ensure that assistance reached those who needed it most. These projects are entirely member-driven, reflecting the passion and commitment of women who volunteer their time to create meaningful and sustainable change.

A highlight of the year has been Sri Lanka’s privilege of hosting the Ladies’ Circle International Service Project (2025–2027). Implemented in partnership with the HELP Schools Foundation, the initiative, “No Child Left Behind,” is transforming the lives of vulnerable school children by providing educational resources, improving learning environments, and supporting schools that require urgent assistance. As the international host nation, Ladies’ Circle Sri Lanka has taken on the responsibility of coordinating and driving a project that will leave a lasting legacy for children across the country.

Addressing the gathering, the President of Ladies’ Circle Sri Lanka reaffirmed the association’s commitment to purposeful service. “Every project we undertake reflects our commitment to improving lives, empowering communities, and ensuring that our efforts create sustainable change. We are proud to be part of a global movement of women who believe that even small acts of service can make an extraordinary difference.”
