Supreme Court to hear 26 petitions challenging Organised Crimes Bill

Colombo, Nov. 4 (Daily Mirror) - Twenty-six Special Determination petitions have so far been filed before the Supreme Court challenging the constitutionality of the proposed Prevention of Organised Crimes Bill.

Chief Justice Preethi Padman Surasena has nominated a three-member Supreme Court bench to hear the petitions today (04).

The bench, presided over by Chief Justice Preethi Padman Surasena, will comprise Justices Achala Wengappuli and Sampath Abayakoon.

The petitions are scheduled to be taken up for hearing at 10 a.m. today.

The Prevention of Organised Crimes Bill, published as a Gazette Supplement issued on October 6, 2026 and tabled in Parliament on October 20, aims to give effect to the United Nations Convention Against Transnational Organised Crime and facilitate national and international cooperation in preventing, investigating and prosecuting serious offences.
