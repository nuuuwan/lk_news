Woman found dead inside parked vehicle near Teldeniya hospital

Colombo, June 17 (Daily Mirror) - The body of a woman was found inside a vehicle parked near Teldeniya Hospital today.

According to police, the vehicle was discovered in the vicinity of the hospital with the woman’s body inside.

The identity of the deceased and the cause of death have not yet been established.

Police have launched an investigation to determine the circumstances surrounding the death, while further inquiries are underway.
