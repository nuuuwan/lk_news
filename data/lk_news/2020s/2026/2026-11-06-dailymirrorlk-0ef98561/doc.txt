“We are studying proposed anti-terrorism law” – Cardinal

The Catholic Church is examining the Government’s proposed anti-terrorism legislation to identify potential shortcomings and concerns before expressing its views on the new law, Archbishop of Colombo Cardinal Malcolm Ranjith has said.

Speaking to the media, Cardinal Ranjith said the proposed legislation, which is intended to replace the existing Prevention of Terrorism Act (PTA), was currently being reviewed with the assistance of legal experts.

He said the Church had begun examining the provisions of the proposed law following its presentation by the Government to the public.

“We are currently studying the proposed legislation. Our lawyers are examining its provisions to determine whether there are any shortcomings or issues that need to be addressed,” Cardinal Ranjith said.

He added that the Church was undertaking the review to identify any concerns within the proposed legal framework.

The review comes as the Government moves towards replacing the controversial PTA with new anti-terrorism legislation.
