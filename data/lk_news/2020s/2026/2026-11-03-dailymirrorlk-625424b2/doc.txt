Culinary skills vital to strengthening tourism industry: Herath

Colombo, Nov. 3 (Daily Mirror) - Investing in people and developing professional skills are essential to strengthening Sri Lanka’s tourism industry and creating better employment opportunities for young people, Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Minister Vijitha Herath said.

Addressing the awards ceremony of the Nestlé Golden Chef’s Hat Competition 2026 at Cinnamon Life – City of Dreams, Colombo, on November 1, the Minister said Sri Lanka’s culinary traditions could play an important role in attracting international tourists.

He said the country should promote its distinctive cuisine, local ingredients and cultural heritage internationally while preserving their authenticity and maintaining international standards.

The Minister highlighted the need to develop culinary and hospitality skills among young people and strengthen cooperation between the private sector, professional associations and educational institutions to produce skilled professionals for the industry.

He also said providing young people with opportunities to showcase their talents was important in developing future careers in the hospitality sector.

Commending the participation of more than 1,500 students in the Junior Nestlé Golden Chef’s Hat Competition, Herath said such initiatives helped nurture young talent and create opportunities in the tourism and hospitality industries.

He congratulated the winners, finalists and participants and appreciated Nestlé Lanka and its partners for their contribution to developing culinary talent in Sri Lanka.
