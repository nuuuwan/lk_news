Asia Siyaka achieves global carbon neutral first in tea logistics

Asia Siyaka Warehousing (ASW) has become the world’s first tea logistics company to earn ZeroCarbon certification. The achievement, verified by Sustainable Future Group, confirms the Sri Lankan firm’s full carbon neutrality across its operations.

The company’s dedicated warehouse is also the first globally to hold both ZeroCarbon and LEED environmental certifications, alongside ISO 22000 for food safety.

This milestone results from rigorous emissions measurement, reduction strategies, and support for verified offset programs. It provides tangible value to the tea industry’s supply chain, helping producers and exporters reduce their logistics-related carbon footprint and meet growing international demand for sustainable, traceable sourcing.

As a subsidiary of Asia Siyaka Commodities PLC, ASW’s certification reinforces Sri Lanka’s leadership in responsible tea logistics and strengthens the competitiveness of Ceylon Tea.
