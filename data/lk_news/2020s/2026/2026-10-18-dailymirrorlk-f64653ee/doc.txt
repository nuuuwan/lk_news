Heavy rain disrupts traffic in Nuwara Eliya

Colombo, Oct. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Heavy rainfall accompanied by lightning disrupted traffic in Nuwara Eliya town yesterday afternoon (17), with several roads inundated by rainwater.

Traffic disruptions were reported in the town centre as heavy rain lashed the area due to adverse weather conditions prevailing across the country.

The Nuwara Eliya–Badulla, Nuwara Eliya–Maha Kandy and Nuwara Eliya–Hatton roads were also flooded, affecting traffic movement.
