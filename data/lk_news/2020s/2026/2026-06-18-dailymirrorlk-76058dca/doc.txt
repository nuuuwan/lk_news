Cinnamon Grand Colombo invites to celebrate Father’s Day with heartfelt moments

This Father’s Day, Cinnamon Grand Colombo invites families to come together and celebrate the incredible fathers and father figures who make life special, with a series of memorable dining experiences and family moments from June 19 to 21.

On June 21, from 12 noon to 3:00 p.m., guests can enjoy a range of specially curated dining experiences across the hotel. At Plates, the Gentleman’s Brunch will take place, bringing together bold flavours and classic indulgences at Rs.9,200 nett per person.

The seafood lovers can indulge in the all-you-can-eat seafood buffet at Lagoon for Rs.8,500 nett per person, while Cheers presents a hearty Father’s Day Carvery Buffet at Rs.7,900 nett per person. Adding to the celebrations, Tea Lounge will host a Gentleman’s High Tea on June 20 and 21, from 3:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m. at Rs.5,500 nett per person, perfect for an elegant afternoon with loved ones.

Guests can also pick up specially themed Father’s Day cakes and sweet treats from Coffee Stop from June 19 to 21, available daily from 10:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.

The highlight of the celebrations will be the ‘Dad & Me Cook-Off Challenge’ taking place on June 21, from 10:00 a.m. onwards. Designed to bring fathers and children together through fun, creativity and delicious food, the challenge promises magical moments created from mystery ingredients and teamwork in the kitchen. Priced at ,15,000 nett, the experience also includes the Father’s Day Brunch Buffet. Early registration is encouraged as limited spaces are available.

Celebrate the joy of fatherhood, shared laughter and meaningful family memories this Father’s Day at Cinnamon Grand Colombo.
