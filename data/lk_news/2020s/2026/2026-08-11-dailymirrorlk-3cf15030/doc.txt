Muthurajawela to get three new fuel tanks

Colombo, Aug. 11 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet has approved the award of a contract for the construction of three new fuel storage tanks at the Muthurajawela Terminal, aimed at strengthening Sri Lanka’s fuel storage capacity.

The project involves the construction of two fuel storage tanks with a capacity of 15,000 cubic metres each and another tank with a capacity of 10,000 cubic metres.

The Cabinet had initially approved the project on June 2, 2025, with funding to be provided by the Ceylon Petroleum Storage Terminal Company.

At a subsequent Cabinet meeting on July 20, 2026, the Minister of Energy and the Minister of Environment were requested to consult relevant stakeholders and determine the measures required to meet environmental, legal and other requirements related to the project.

The two Ministers subsequently informed Cabinet that the recommendations and conditions stipulated by the Central Environmental Authority (CEA) had been incorporated into the procurement contract. They also confirmed that the project would be implemented in accordance with the provisions of the Wildlife and Plant Protection Ordinance.

Accordingly, Cabinet approved a proposal by the Minister of Energy to award the contract to the joint venture of Indo-East Engineering & Construction (Lanka) (Private) Limited and Ceylex Engineering (Private) Limited.

The joint venture was selected as the lowest bidder among the bids submitted under the International Competitive Bidding procedure.

The contract is valued at US$3.04 million and Rs. 2,796.14 million, excluding VAT.
