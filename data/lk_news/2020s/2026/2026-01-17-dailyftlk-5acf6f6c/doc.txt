Concord Apparel and Concord Footwear win Presidential Export Awards

Guston Ltd. Managing Director Prasanna Jayasinghe receiving the award at the Presidential Export Awards from Industry Minister Sunil Handunneththi

Two leading subsidiaries of Guston Ltd. Hong Kong, Concord Apparel Ltd. and Concord Footwear Ltd., have achieved a significant milestone by securing top honours at the Presidential Export Awards (PEA) 2024/2025. At this year’s awards, Concord Apparel was recognised as the Best Exporter – Apparel Sector (Medium Scale category), while Concord Footwear won the Best Exporter Award – Footwear and Leather Products Sector, highlighting the group’s excellence across two key export industries.

The prestigious awards ceremony, organised by the Export Development Board (EDB) of Sri Lanka, was held at the Bandaranaike Memorial International Conference Hall (BMICH), Colombo. The awards were received by Guston Ltd. Managing Director Prasanna Jayasinghe on behalf of both factories.

Concord Apparel Ltd. continues to set benchmarks in the apparel industry through its consistent delivery of high-quality, value-added work wear garments for international markets. Strong operational discipline, a skilled workforce, and a customer-centric approach have positioned the factory as a trusted partner to leading global brands. Concord Footwear has emerged as a key player in the leather and footwear sector, combining craftsmanship with modern production technologies to manufacture innovative, high-quality safety footwear that meets stringent international standards.  Both facilities are powered by advanced manufacturing technologies, automation, and digital systems, ensuring high productivity, precision, and continuous innovation. Sustainability and responsible business practices remain central to operations, with a strong emphasis on ethical employment, employee well-being, environmental stewardship, and community development. Supported by globally recognised certifications including SA 8000, ISO 9001, ISO 14001, LEED Gold, and STeP by OEKO-Tex, this dual recognition reflects the dedication of the workforce and the strategic vision of Guston Limited as it continues to strengthen Sri Lanka’s reputation as a reliable global manufacturing destination.
