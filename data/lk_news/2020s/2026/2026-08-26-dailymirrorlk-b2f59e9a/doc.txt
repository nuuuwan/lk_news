Nuwara Eliya records lowest minimum temperature at 14.6°C

Colombo, Aug. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Nuwara Eliya recorded Sri Lanka’s lowest minimum temperature at 14.6°C on today (26), the Department of Meteorology’s daily temperature report said.

The central highlands experienced significantly cooler conditions compared to other parts of the country, with Bandarawela recording 19.4°C and Badulla 21.7°C. Katugastota recorded a minimum temperature of 22.8°C.

In contrast, warmer nighttime temperatures were recorded across several northern, eastern and coastal areas.

Polonnaruwa recorded the island’s highest minimum temperature at 27.5°C. Jaffna, Mullaitivu and Puttalam each recorded 27.2°C, while Trincomalee and Rathmalana recorded 27.1°C. Mannar recorded 27.0°C.

Colombo recorded a minimum temperature of 26.8°C, while Anuradhapura recorded 26.7°C and Batticaloa 26.5°C. Vavuniya and Katunayake recorded 26.4°C and 26.2°C respectively.

Other recorded minimum temperatures included 25.6°C in Pothuvil, 25.4°C in Mattala, 25.2°C in Maha Illuppallama, 25.1°C in Monaragala, 24.9°C in Galle, 24.8°C in Hambantota, 24.5°C in Kurunegala and 24.4°C in Ratnapura.

The figures highlight the marked temperature difference between Sri Lanka’s cool central highlands and warmer lowland and coastal areas.
