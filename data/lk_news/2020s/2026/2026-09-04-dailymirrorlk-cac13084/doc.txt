Namal arrives at Bribery Commission

Colombo, Sep. 4 (Daily Mirror) - SLPP National Organiser and Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa arrived at the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) this morning to provide a statement regarding the Airbus deal.

Namal had been instructed to appear before the Commission at 9.00 a.m. today.
