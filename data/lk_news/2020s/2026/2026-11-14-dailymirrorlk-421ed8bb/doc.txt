Week ends in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Hatton National Bank, Richard Pieris & Company and LOLC Holdings with the turnover crossing Rs. 3.2 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Dialog Axiata, Dipped Products and Hemas Holdings. Mixed interest was observed in Access Engineering, Commercial Bank and Sierra Cables whilst retail interest was noted in TAJ Lanka Hotels Rights, Lanka Credit and Business Finance and Softlogic Finance. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net buyers.

During the week, the ASPI and the S&P SL20 gained 3.7% and 4.1% respectively, whilst recording an average daily turnover of Rs. 4.46 Bn.

Capital Goods sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Commercial Bank) whilst the sector index lost 0.83%. The share price of Commercial Bank closed flat at Rs 225.25.

Materials sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Sierra Cables and Dialog Axiata) whilst the sector index edged up by 0.10%. The share price of Sierra Cables recorded a loss of Rs 0.60 (1.61%) to close at Rs 36.70. The share price of Dialog Axiata appreciated by Rs 0.20 (0.40%) to close at Rs 50.20.

Dipped Products and Access Engineering were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Dipped Products increased by Rs 2.80 (3.94%) to close at Rs 73.90. The share price of Access Engineering gained Rs 0.60 (0.71%) to close at Rs 85.30.
