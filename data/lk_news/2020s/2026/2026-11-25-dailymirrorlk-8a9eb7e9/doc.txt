Sub-Inspector sentenced to 50 years rigorous imprisonment over abuse of minor girl

Nov. 25 (Daily Mirror) - A Sub-Inspector who faced four separate indictments before the Kurunegala High Court over abducting and abusing a schoolgirl was found guilty on all four counts and sentenced to 50 years rigorous imprisonment by Colombo High Court Judge Lal Ranasinghe Bandara, who previously served as a High Court judge in the North Western Province.

The judge ordered that the 50-year sentence of rigorous imprisonment be served within 15 years. The High Court Judge also ordered the accused to pay Rs. 1 million in compensation to the victim, imposing a further two years imprisonment in default of payment.

In addition, the judge ordered the accused to pay a fine of Rs. 50,000 separately for each of the four charges, with a further one year of imprisonment in default of paying the fines.

Delivering the judgment, the High Court Judge said that the prosecution had succeeded in proving all four indictments against the accused beyond reasonable doubt. The person sentenced was Sub-Inspector H. Ruwan Amarasinghe. The Attorney General had filed indictments against the accused Sub-Inspector before the Kurunegala High Court under Section 384 of the Penal Code and Section 364(2)(e) of the Penal Code (Amendment) Act No. 22 of 1995, alleging that he deceived the minor victim, took her from lawful guardianship, abducted and raped her. A group of witnesses, including the Judicial Medical Officer (JMO) of the Kurunegala Teaching Hospital, had been named in the case.
