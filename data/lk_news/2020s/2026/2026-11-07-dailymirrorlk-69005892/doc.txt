Colombo Mayor holds urgent talks to address city flooding

Colombo Mayor Vraie Cally Balthazaar is holding a special discussion with representatives of key government institutions to identify immediate solutions to the flooding experienced in the city following Friday evening’s heavy rainfall.

The discussion is focused on improving the city’s drainage systems and addressing issues related to Colombo’s canal network and road infrastructure.

The Mayor earlier said Colombo’s drainage system was designed to accommodate approximately 30mm of rainfall, whereas the city received 129mm within a few hours on Friday, resulting in widespread flooding and disruption.

The meeting is expected to focus on identifying urgent measures to improve the city’s capacity to cope with heavy rainfall and prevent similar flooding in the future.
