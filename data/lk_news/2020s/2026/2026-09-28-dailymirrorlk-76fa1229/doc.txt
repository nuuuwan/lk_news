Upcountry train services to Gampola resume from Oct. 2

Colombo, Sep. 28 (Daily Mirror) - Train services between Colombo Fort, Peradeniya and Gampola on the Upcountry Line will resume from October 2 following the completion of restoration work on sections of the railway track damaged by Cyclone “Ditwah”, Sri Lanka Railways announced today.

Sri Lanka Railways said 103 locations between Rambukkana and Gampola were damaged, including 36 major landslides and 67 earth slips and rock falls.

The restoration work had faced several challenges, including the steep gradient of the Rambukkana-Kadugannawa section, the steepest section in the railway network, lack of alternative road access to affected areas and adverse weather conditions.

Repair machinery and materials also had to be transported to the affected locations exclusively by rail, according to Sri Lanka Railways.

The Way and Works Department of Sri Lanka Railways, together with the National Building Research Organisation (NBRO), carried out the restoration work and restored the damaged track to a condition safe for train operations.

Accordingly, regular train services between Colombo Fort and Peradeniya/Gampola will resume from the afternoon of October 2.

Among the services resuming is Intercity Train No. 1029, which will depart Colombo Fort at 3.35 p.m. and reach Peradeniya at 5.56 p.m. daily from October 2.

Train No. 1035 will depart Colombo Fort at 4.35 p.m. and arrive in Gampola at 7.51 p.m., while Train No. 1037 will leave Colombo Fort at 5.15 p.m. and reach Peradeniya at 7.53 p.m.

Train No. 1030 will depart Peradeniya at 6.25 a.m. and arrive at Colombo Fort at 8.44 a.m.

Train No. 1040 will leave Gampola at 4.35 a.m. and reach Colombo Fort at 8.06 a.m., while Train No. 1038 will depart Peradeniya at 4.40 a.m. and arrive at Colombo Fort at 7.12 a.m.

Meanwhile, the Podi Menike Express (Train Nos. 1005 and 1006) will resume daily operations from October 3.

Train No. 1005 will leave Colombo Fort at 5.55 a.m., reaching Peradeniya at 8.34 a.m. and Gampola at 9.06 a.m.

Train No. 1006 will depart Gampola at 4.10 p.m., reach Peradeniya at 4.31 p.m. and arrive at Colombo Fort at 7.23 p.m.

Sri Lanka Railways said the resumption of services follows the successful completion of restoration work on the cyclone-damaged railway sections.
