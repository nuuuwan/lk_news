MAXXIS recognizes top dealers at Awards Night

The MAXXIS Annual Awards Night, themed ‘MAXXIS Champions of Champions 2025’, was held recently at the Shangri-La Hotel, Colombo.

Around 200 MAXXIS dealers and their families attended the event, where 60 dealers were evaluated for award presentations. The Winner of the Year 2025 was Wijaya Motors & Tyre House (Pvt) Ltd., Colombo 14; the trophy was presented to Managing Director  Sajith Sankalana by Kenny, Section Manager, MAXXIS International Taiwan jointly with Lesley Fonseka and  Sunil Fonseka of Tyre House Group.
