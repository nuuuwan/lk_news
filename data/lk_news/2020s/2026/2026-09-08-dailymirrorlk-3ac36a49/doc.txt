Turtle conservation programme held at Adam’s Bridge Marine National Park

Colombo, Sep. 8 (Daily Mirror) - A special awareness programme was held on September 3 to evaluate the progress of the “Sea Turtle Nest Observation Programme – 2026”, conducted as part of ongoing sea turtle conservation efforts at the Adam’s Bridge Marine National Park in Talaimannar.

The programme focused on reviewing conservation activities carried out during 2025–2026 in the Vavuniya Wildlife Zone, with particular attention to protecting sea turtle nesting grounds through the participation of local communities.

The initiative was conducted under the leadership of Chandraratne, Assistant Director of the Vavuniya Zonal Office of the Department of Wildlife Conservation, in collaboration with site security officers in the Vavuniya region, the Park Warden of Adam’s Bridge Marine National Park and the research team of Pelagikos (Pvt) Ltd.

Representatives of the Mannar Divisional Secretariat, Grama Niladhari officers, Pesalai Pradeshiya Sabha, Sri Lanka Police, Department of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources, Sri Lanka Navy, Department of Coast Conservation and Coastal Resource Management, as well as government and non-governmental organizations representing local fishing communities, participated in the programme.

The main objective was to assess the success of monitoring programmes implemented with the direct involvement of local communities to safeguard sea turtle populations and their nesting habitats.

The programme also provided an opportunity for stakeholders to discuss ways of strengthening community participation and improving future sea turtle conservation efforts in the area.
