Sticky and Jasmine Rice face higher import tax of Rs. 280 per kg

Colombo, Nov. 4 (Daily Mirror) - Sticky Rice and Jasmine Rice will now be subject to a combined import tax of Rs. 280 per kilogram, replacing the previous Special Commodity Levy of Rs. 65, following a government tax revision.

Accordingly, the government has removed the Special Commodity Levy of Rs. 65 per kilogram imposed on selected categories of imported raw and parboiled rice, with effect from November 3.

The change was introduced through a gazette notification issued by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development.

Under the revised tax structure, the affected rice varieties will be subject to a combination of applicable import taxes, including Customs Import Duty, Value Added Tax (VAT), the Social Security Contribution Levy and the CESS levy, bringing the total import tax to Rs. 280 per kilogram.

The revision applies specifically to HS codes 1006.30.19 (Raw Rice) and 1006.30.29 (Parboiled Rice).

Imports under these categories are permitted only in limited quantities and under strict conditions to meet the requirements of tourist hotels, specialised catering establishments and foreign clientele.

Meanwhile, the Ministry of Agriculture has confirmed that there is no necessity to import rice into the country and that the Government has not taken any decision to authorise rice imports.
