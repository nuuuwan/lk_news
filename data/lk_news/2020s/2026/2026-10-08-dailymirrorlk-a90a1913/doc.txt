Shiranthi held under special security at Welikada Prison

Colombo, Oct. 8 (Daily Mirror) - Former First Lady Shiranthi Rajapaksa, who was remanded until October 13, is being held under special security at the Welikada Magazine Prison, according to the Department of Prisons.

Prisons Commissioner and Media Spokesperson Senaka Palliyaguruge said arrangements have been made to accommodate her in a secure area within the women’s section of the prison. Authorities are also making arrangements for her to receive medical treatment in accordance with a court order.

Rajapaksa was arrested on October 7 by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) over allegations concerning the alleged misuse of Rs. 10 million obtained from a state-owned bank. The Colombo Chief Magistrate’s Court subsequently ordered that she be remanded until October 13.

The court also ordered that steps be taken to admit her to a private hospital in Colombo for medical treatment.
