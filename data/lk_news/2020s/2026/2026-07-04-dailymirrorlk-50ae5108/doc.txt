Secondary market eases after previous rally

The secondary market lost some of the strong momentum witnessed in the previous session, with overall trading activity moderating as market volumes remained subdued.

Activity at the short end of the curve was minimal, while the 2035 maturities attracted relatively stronger investor interest and accounted for the bulk of the day’s trades.

Among the traded maturities, the 15.10.2028 bond traded within a yield range of 10.50 percent-10.60 percent, while the 01.08.2030 maturity changed hands between 11.20 percent and 11.30 percent.

Further along the curve, the 01.10.2032 maturity traded between 11.50 percent and 11.60 percent, while the 15.01.2033 maturity traded within a range of 11.55 percent-11.65 percent.

Towards the longer end, the 15.06.2034 maturity traded between 11.60 percent and 11.70 percent. In the 2035 segment, both the 15.03.2035 and 15.06.2035 maturities saw comparatively active trading, with yields ranging between 11.70 percent and 11.85 percent.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee appreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.336.03/US dollar, compared to Rs.336.29/US dollar seen earlier. Liquidity in the banking system expanded to Rs.128.47 billion, from Rs.81.20 billion recorded previously.
