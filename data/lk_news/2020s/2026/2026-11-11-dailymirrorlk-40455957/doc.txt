India to provide 100 more ambulances to Sri Lanka under Rs. 1.7 billion grant

Colombo, Nov. 11 (Daily Mirror) - India and Sri Lanka have signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to provide 100 additional fully equipped ambulances to the 1990 Suwa Seriya National Emergency Ambulance Service under an Indian grant of Rs. 1,725.73 million.

The MoU was signed and exchanged yesterday (11) by Indian High Commissioner Santosh Jha and Secretary to the Ministry of Health and Mass Media Dr. Anil Jasinghe.

The exchange took place in the presence of India's Minister of Health and Family Welfare, Jagat Prakash Nadda, and Sri Lanka's Health and Mass Media Minister Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa during Nadda's visit to Colombo for the first BIMSTEC Health Ministers' Meeting.

The initiative follows a request from the Sri Lankan leadership and aims to strengthen the existing Suwa Seriya ambulance fleet and improve emergency response times to under 10 minutes.

The 1990 Suwa Seriya service was initially launched in July 2016 in the Western and Southern Provinces with an Indian grant of USD 7.5 million. The assistance covered 88 ambulances, their operational expenses for one year and the establishment of an emergency response centre.

In July 2018, the service was expanded island-wide by Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi and then Prime Minister Ranil Wickremesinghe, with a further Indian grant of USD 15.02 million. This covered 209 ambulances, operational expenses and training costs.

India's development assistance to Sri Lanka's health sector also includes the construction of Dickoya Hospital, the upgrading of Jaffna Teaching Hospital and hospitals in Kilinochchi and Vavuniya, and the construction of a new surgical unit at Batticaloa Teaching Hospital.

Ongoing Indian-supported projects in the health sector include the development of Accident and Emergency facilities at Mannar District General Hospital, a medical ward complex at Mullaitivu District General Hospital and the provision of medical equipment to Deniyaya Base Hospital.
