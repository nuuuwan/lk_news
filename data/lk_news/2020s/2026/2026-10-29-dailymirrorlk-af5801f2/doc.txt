Sixth edition of ‘BMPC Awards’ opens applications

The Institute of Chartered Professional Managers of Sri Lanka (CPM Sri Lanka) has opened applications for the Best Management Practices Company Awards (BMPC Awards) 2027, marking the sixth edition following five successful editions.

The awards recognise outstanding public and private sector organisations that demonstrate excellence in management practices, innovation, operational efficiency, sustainability and organisational growth.

The awards will culminate in a grand awards night on 26 February 2027 at Monarch Imperial, Sri Jayewardenepura Kotte, expecting over 1,200 corporate leaders, senior executives, entrepreneurs and distinguished guests to celebrate organisational excellence and continuous improvement.

As an added benefit, every organisation submitting a report for the BMPC Awards 2027 will receive three complimentary seats at the 14th Management Insights Summit 2027, scheduled for 25 February 2027.

A key highlight of the BMPC Awards 2027 is the opportunity for the top 10 selected companies to showcase their best management practice stories at the 14th Management Insights Summit on 25 February.

To facilitate participation, technical guidance and standardised templates will be provided to all registered applicant organisations, supporting them in preparing their reports and documenting their management practices and results.

Initial registration is open until 20 November 2026, while the deadline for submitting completed reports is 15 December 2026.

Organisations interested in participating can obtain comprehensive information on eligibility, applications and submission guidelines by visiting www.cpmawards.com.
