Allianz Lanka expands road safety campaign to Kurunegala and Kuliyapitiya

As road safety continues to be a growing concern across Sri Lanka, Allianz Lanka is strengthening its commitment to safer communities through the latest phase of its islandwide road safety awareness campaign.

Building on the initiative first launched in May 2026, Allianz Lanka recently carried out impactful activations in Kurunegala and Kuliyapitiya, further amplifying a simple yet powerful message: prioritise life and prevent accidents.

Recognising the critical role that the three-wheelers play in the daily lives of Sri Lankans, Allianz Lanka has leveraged this widely used mode of transport to take its road safety message directly into communities.

Today, more than 6,000 three-wheelers across the country proudly carry road safety messaging, helping to increase awareness among drivers, passengers, pedestrians and all road users while encouraging responsible and mindful behaviour on the road.

As part of the campaign, the company extended a one-year complimentary accidental death cover to the participating three-wheeler drivers, offering an added layer of protection and peace of mind for those who spend long hours on the road serving their communities.
