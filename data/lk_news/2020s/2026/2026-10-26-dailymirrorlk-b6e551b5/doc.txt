Lanka IOC, Sinopec increase Octane 95 petrol price by Rs. 20

Colombo, Oct 26 (Daily Mirror) - Lanka IOC and Sinopec have increased the price of a litre of Octane 95 petrol by Rs. 20, effective from midnight on October 25.

Following the revision, the price of a litre of Octane 95 petrol at both fuel providers has risen from Rs. 475 to Rs. 495.
