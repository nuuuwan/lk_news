Minister suspects outside influence in Mahara Prison unrest

Colombo, Aug. 2 (Daily Mirror) - Justice Minister Harshana Nanayakkara said that the unrest at Mahara Prison is fundamentally different from the incident reported at Negombo Prison, noting that the nature of the two incidents is not the same.

Speaking to the media after an event on Sunday (August 2), the Minister explained that the clash at Negombo occurred between inmates, whereas the unrest at Mahara involved inmates and the prison administration.

Minister Nanayakkara also said he suspects that certain groups may be orchestrating prison unrest from outside in an organised manner. He noted that such incidents had not previously emerged without apparent cause and said the authorities would investigate whether there was any external influence behind the disturbances.

Addressing concerns over prison conditions, the Minister acknowledged that overcrowding remains one of the factors contributing to tensions inside prisons. However, he pointed out that overcrowding had existed long before the current government assumed office and stressed that measures are now being taken to address the issue.

The Minister further condemned the damage caused to public property during the unrest, stating that an investigation would be carried out and that those responsible for vandalism would face strict legal action.
