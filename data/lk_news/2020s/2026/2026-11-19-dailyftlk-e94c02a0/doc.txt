CanCham Sri Lanka builds bridges for economic partnerships through mission to Canada

Led by Co-founders and Board Directors Mohamed Hameez and Priyantha Chandrasekara, the Canadian Chamber of Commerce in Sri Lanka (CanCham Sri Lanka) continued its Business Promotion Mission to Canada with a series of Government meetings, investment discussions, institutional visits and business networking activities across Toronto, Ottawa and Montréal, as part of its wider effort to advance and strengthen bilateral trade, investment and economic cooperation between Canada and Sri Lanka and the wider Indo-Pacific region.

The delegation met with Ontario Economic Development, Job Creation and Trade Minister Victor Fedeli. Both sides discussed opportunities for deeper economic partnerships, investment facilitation and cross-border collaboration between Ontario and Sri Lanka. Fedeli highlighted the importance of collaboration in technology, artificial intelligence (AI) and research and development (R&D) as key drivers of future economic growth and job creation. The meeting was attended by representatives of the CanCham business mission, including delegates from CHEC Colombo Port City, ECD Holidays, and Horizon College and Campus.

Discussions were held with Invest Ontario, the province's investment promotion agency, on opportunities for investment collaboration, business partnerships and greater connectivity between the two markets. These discussions formed part of CanCham Sri Lanka's broader efforts to introduce Sri Lankan investment opportunities to Canadian stakeholders and encourage greater private-sector engagement.

Ahangama Law Professional Corporation organised a business networking and roundtable session where representatives from Home Lands, Port City Colombo, Horizon Campus, Horizon College International, Thames International, Hutch Sri Lanka, ECD Global and Amma Mega Industry Ltd. were provided with an opportunity to discuss business interests and potential areas of collaboration between the two countries.

Another significant networking initiative was an HNI Business Networking Session organised by Marsa Business Solutions Inc. at the White Field Convention Center in Scarborough. The Sri Lankan delegation connected with institutional investors and business representatives from the United States, South Korea, India, Indonesia and the Philippines. Discussions covered investment opportunities in Port City Colombo, joint ventures involving educational institutions, and potential developments in retirement, wellness and resort projects, opening avenues for investment in Sri Lanka's tourism and health and wellness sectors.

The delegation also participated in networking sessions with members of the Sri Lankan diaspora, high-net-worth individuals, investors and the Lions Club of Scarborough. The sessions helped strengthen connections with the Canadian-based business community and opened opportunities to explore investment and commercial partnerships across the real estate and education sectors.

Devika Anthony hosted additional business networking sessions at the Grand Hyatt Toronto, while Caliber Mortgage Inc. Director and Mortgage Broker Kelum Hewage provided opportunities to connect with Canadian business professionals and explore collaboration in the sectors of financial services, property investment and real estate development.

The Toronto program also included diaspora-focused business networking with the participation of the World University Service of Canada (WUSC) Diaspora Development and Engagement Liaison. Discussions focused on the role of diaspora networks in connecting Canadian-based professionals, investors and entrepreneurs with business opportunities in Sri Lanka, as well as WUSC's Growth, Resilience, Investment and Training (GRIT) project, supported by Global Affairs Canada. The project, which works with diaspora professionals to strengthen women-led enterprises in Northern Sri Lanka through mentorship, skills development and improved access to international markets, demonstrates how diaspora expertise and investment can support entrepreneurship and regional economic development.

In Ottawa, the delegation attended a business forum with Sri Lanka's Acting High Commissioner to Canada Chathura Weerasekara. The Trade and Investment Discussion and Networking Event was jointly facilitated by the High Commission of Sri Lanka in Ottawa and CanCham Sri Lanka, with participation from members of the diplomatic corps, officials from Global Affairs Canada, Canadian business representatives and the visiting Sri Lankan delegation.

Weerasekara highlighted Sri Lanka Expo 2027 as a platform to showcase the country's export capabilities and emerging sectors. Hameez outlined CanCham Sri Lanka's role in promoting bilateral trade and investment, while Home Lands Group Chairman Nalin Herath presented the group's developments and business activities in Sri Lanka.

The delegation also met with officials of the High Commission of Sri Lanka in Ottawa, including Minister Counsellor Kiritharan Kumarasamy, during its visit to the mission. The program also included a visit to the Senate of Canada, organised by the High Commission of Sri Lanka, providing the delegation with an opportunity to observe Canada's parliamentary proceedings and learn more about its legislative system.

The delegation met with Global Affairs Canada Executive Director for Pakistan, Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka and Maldives Yannick Lamonde, and Trade Facilitation Office (TFO) Canada Executive Director Steve Tipman. Discussions focused on trade development and capacity building, including strengthening Sri Lankan exporters' readiness, improving access to Canadian market information and understanding market requirements and compliance standards.

In Montréal, the delegation attended a business networking session organised by Sri Lanka's Honorary Consul in Montréal Chanaka Kurera, which was attended by local businesses, business chambers, consular representatives and other Canadian stakeholders, including representatives from the City of St-Laurent, to build business relationships and explore trade, investment and collaboration opportunities between Sri Lanka and Montréal.

The Montréal leg of the mission coincided with Sri Lanka's participation in the International Tourism & Travel Show (SITV), held from 30 October to 1 November, 2026. During the exhibition, the CanCham Sri Lanka leadership met with Tourism Deputy Minister Prof. Ruwan Ranasinghe.

The CanCham team presented the Deputy Minister with a copy of its Tourism Plan, together with the Five-Year Action Plan, which outlined the proposed priorities for its tourism sector, and further briefed him on the proposed Global Health and Wellness Summit Sri Lanka 2027 (GHWSSL) and its potential to support the development of Sri Lanka as a health and wellness tourism destination.

As a follow-up to the mission, discussions are underway to organise an inward business delegation from Canada to Sri Lanka. The proposed visit would provide Canadian investors, prospective buyers, manufacturers and tourism stakeholders with an opportunity to experience Sri Lanka's business and investment landscape first-hand, explore potential partnerships and gain a better understanding of opportunities across key sectors. The mission has also opened avenues for continued engagement, with CanCham Sri Lanka receiving invitations to participate in future business and industry events in Canada.

Across Canada, the mission covered investment and partnership opportunities in real estate, education, tourism, healthcare and wellness, agriculture and AgriTech, research and development, green housing, and financial services. Through this mission, CanCham Sri Lanka continued its efforts to position Sri Lanka as a gateway to the Indo-Pacific region, connecting Canadian expertise, investment and business networks with local opportunities while strengthening bilateral trade, investment and private-sector partnerships.
