Trade Minister warns chili importers against creating artificial shortages to gain undue profits

Colombo, Nov. 23 (Daily Mirror) - Trade Minister Wasantha Samarasinghe has urged chili importers to maintain proper quality standards and warned them against exploiting the market by creating artificial shortages to gain undue profits.

Speaking to journalists yesterday, the Minister stressed that importers are responsible for ensuring that chili brought into the country meets the required standards. He said there was no issue with importing chili as long as it complied with the proper quality requirements.

"We suspect that the issue may have arisen because chili stored in freezers due to the El Niño phenomenon and other conditions may have been transported to Sri Lanka in ordinary containers," he said.

The Minister noted that Sri Lanka usually imports between 250 and 350 containers of chili, adding that a similar incident had never been reported in the past.

He said concerns had emerged over 14 containers imported in October and another 52 containers brought into the country in November.

Samarasinghe called on importers to ensure that all chili consignments meet the required standards rather than seeking short-term gains at the expense of consumers.

"Do not try to take undue advantage by creating an artificial shortage in the market," he warned.
