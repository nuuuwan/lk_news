Indices close on a positive note

The ASPI closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Melstacorp, Colombo Dockyard and ACL Cables with the turnover crossing Rs. 2.1bn.

Meanwhile, the S&P SL20 edged up. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Hatton National Bank, Agstar and Ceylon Cold Stores.

Mixed interest was observed in ACL Cables, Softlogic Holdings and John Keells Holdings whilst retail interest was noted in SMB Leasing, ACME Printing & Packaging and Citrus Leisure. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net buyers.

The Banking sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Hatton National Bank) whilst the sector index edged up by 0.07%. The share price of Hatton National Bank decreased by Rs. 0.50 (0.12%) to close at Rs. 400.00.

Capital Goods sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to ACL Cables and Softlogic Holdings) whilst the sector index increased by 0.87%. The share price of ACL Cables gained Rs. 1.80 (1.87%) to close at Rs. 98.00.

The share price of Softlogic Holdings appreciated by Rs. 0.60 (5.04%) to close at Rs. 12.50. Janashakthi Limited and Ceylon Cold Stores were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Janashakthi Limited closed flat at Rs. 12.90. The share price of Ceylon Cold Stores recorded a gain of Rs. 2.25 (1.86%) to close at Rs. 123.50.
