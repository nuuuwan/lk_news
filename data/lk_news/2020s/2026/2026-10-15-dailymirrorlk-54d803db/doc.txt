Eradicate ragging in universities

The Daily Mirror editorial on  October 08, 2026 about ragging in universities is commendable for highlighting this nationwide malaise which must be eradicated forthwith because it deters the morale of almost all future aspirants of university education.

Whenever an incident of ragging is reported, there arises a hullabaloo on the media with some focus on ways and means of curbing it,  but no solution has been offered by any government yet.

There are laws, penalties, security measures, punishments, for the offenders but the vicious circle of this barbaric practice continues unabated. The so called aims of ragging such as, socialising, bonding, fostering senior -junior camaraderie, solidarity, cultivating egalitarianism etc. are nonsense and have turned to a savage form and deteriorated to the brink of cruelty. Such aims can’t be achieved through sadistic violence.

Thousands of aspiring university candidates are disheartened from the frequent news of horrific ragging incidents,  and they either opt for private universities where this barbarity is under control or go abroad incurring a heavy burden of expenditure to the parents and loss of foreign exchange to the country.

The seniors who inflict ragging on juniors behave like the administrators of the university,  and if any anti-ragging action is taken by the authorities, they resort to protest rallies, surround the offices and hold authorities as hostages.

Therefore, a mandatory oath taking against ragging at registration must be introduced immediately and any violation or breach  must be dealt strictly without delay.
