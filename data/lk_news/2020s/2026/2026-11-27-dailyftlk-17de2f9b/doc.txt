Dialog launches Sayuru AI:  Sri Lanka’s first AI-powered fisheries advisory voice assistant

From left: Dialog Axiata PLC AI Solutions Associate Architect Nushan Rajapaksha, Group Risk, Compliance and Sustainability Digital Innovation and Inclusion Manager Tharusha Wanigasekara, Group Chief Corporate Officer Asanga Priyadarshana, Gampaha District MP Stepni Fernando, Department of Meteorology Meteorologist Malith Fernando and Negombo Fishery Harbour Manager Harshanath Jayasinghe

Dialog Axiata PLC has launched Sayuru AI, Sri Lanka’s first AI-powered fisheries advisory voice service, enabling fishing communities and other stakeholders to access essential fisheries information simply by dialling 828 from any mobile phone.

Available in Sinhala, Tamil and English, the service reflects the company’s commitment to enriching lives through technology by harnessing artificial intelligence to support safer fishing practices, informed decision-making and greater digital inclusion across Sri Lanka’s fisheries sector.

Developed in partnership with the Department of Fisheries, Aquatic and Ocean Resources and the Department of Meteorology, Aquatic and Ocean Resources, Sayuru AI was launched on 21 November to mark World Fisheries Day. The newly introduced AI-powered voice assistant enhances Dialog’s existing Sayuru Interactive Voice Response (IVR) service, enabling users to access fisheries-related information through natural voice interactions rather than navigating conventional menu-based systems. Available around the clock, the service provides information on marine weather forecasts, safety instructions at sea, fish market prices and guidance on ornamental fisheries. Accessible through a simple voice call without requiring a smartphone or an internet connection, Sayuru AI helps address critical information gaps within the fisheries sector. The new AI-powered assistant is being introduced through Dialog’s existing Sayuru IVR service, while the Sayuru mobile application continues to offer its existing features, enabling broader access to essential information through a familiar and inclusive channel.

The launch event brought together Government representatives, fisheries sector stakeholders and fishing community representatives to demonstrate how voice-based artificial intelligence can improve access to essential information and support more informed decision-making.

A Ministry of Fisheries, Aquatic and Ocean Resources spokesperson said: “The fisheries sector plays a vital role in Sri Lanka’s economy and in the livelihoods of communities across the country. Ensuring that fishing communities have access to timely and relevant information is important in supporting livelihoods and the sustainable development of the sector. Through initiatives such as Sayuru AI, collaboration between the Government and the private sector can help extend the reach of essential services, strengthen support for fishing communities and ensure that the benefits of technological advancement contribute to national development. This initiative also marks an important step towards a new era of transformation in Sri Lanka’s fisheries sector, moving beyond traditional practices towards a modern, technology-enabled and knowledge-driven industry. This transformation is expected to strengthen the resilience and productivity of the fisheries sector.”

Department of Meteorology Director General A.L.K. Wijemannage said: “Timely and reliable weather information is critical to the safety and livelihoods of Sri Lanka’s fishing communities. Through our collaboration on Sayuru, we have been able to extend important marine weather information and alerts directly to those who depend on them. With the integration of AI into the service, we now have an opportunity to serve fishing communities even better by making this information more accessible through a simple and familiar voice interaction. We will continue to support Sayuru AI with timely weather information and alerts, helping fishing communities make better-informed decisions and operate more safely at sea.”

Dialog Axiata PLC Group Chief Corporate Officer Asanga Priyadarshana said: “At Dialog, we believe the true value of AI lies in its ability to solve real-world challenges and create meaningful impact. Since launching Sayuru six years ago, we have continuously expanded the platform with new features and services to better serve Sri Lanka’s fishing communities, and its enhancement with AI marks the next significant step in that journey. By combining trusted sector knowledge with the accessibility of voice and the power of AI, we are helping make critical information more readily available to the communities that depend on it every day. As Sri Lanka advances its digital transformation agenda, we remain committed to applying innovation in ways that strengthen industries, uplift communities and contribute to national progress.”

As adoption grows across Sri Lanka’s fishing communities, Sayuru AI has the potential to strengthen access to critical information, support safer decision-making and contribute to a more resilient and digitally inclusive fisheries sector. To access Sayuru AI, dial 828 from any Dialog mobile connection and interact with the service in Sinhala, Tamil or English.
