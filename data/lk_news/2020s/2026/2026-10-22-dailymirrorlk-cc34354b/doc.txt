HNB Life recognised among Sri Lanka’s Top 10 Great Workplaces for Parents

HNB Life was recognised among Sri Lanka’s Top 10 Great Workplaces for Parents 2026 by Great Place to Work Sri Lanka, becoming the only insurance company to receive this distinction.

The recognition underscores the Company’s commitment to creating a workplace culture that empowers employees to achieve professional success while fostering meaningful family well-being.

The recognition celebrates organizations that have demonstrated a strong commitment to supporting employees through family-friendly workplace practices, inclusive policies, and a people-centric culture that enables parents to thrive both personally and professionally.

HNB Life’s people-first philosophy has been instrumental in fostering a workplace environment where employees are empowered to bring their best selves to work while balancing the demands of family life.

Commenting on the achievement, Executive Director / Chief Executive Officer of HNB Life, Lasitha Wimalaratne said, “At HNB Life, we believe that a truly successful organization is one that supports its people not only in their careers but also in their personal journeys. Being recognized among Sri Lanka’s Great Workplaces for Parents is a reflection of the culture we have intentionally built over the years, one that values empathy, inclusion, flexibility, and well-being.”

Sharing his thoughts, EVP / Chief People Officer of HNB Life, Navin Rupasinghe said, “At the heart of this recognition is our belief that when people feel supported, respected, and valued, they are empowered to perform at their best. We continuously strive to create a workplace that understands the evolving needs of working parents through meaningful policies, development opportunities, wellness initiatives, and a culture of care. This recognition reflects the collective efforts of our teams and leaders in shaping an environment where family and career aspirations can successfully coexist.”
