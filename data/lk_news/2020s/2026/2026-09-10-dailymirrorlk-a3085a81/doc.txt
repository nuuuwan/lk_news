Ceylinco Life launches 20th Family Savari

Some journeys are measured in miles; others are measured in memories. Ceylinco Life’s Family Savari, launched last week for the 20th year, has been creating the latter, bringing generations of policyholder families together for experiences to treasure long after the journey home. Sri Lanka’s biggest and longest-running life insurance customer promotion returns with another trio of popular destinations, China, Malaysia and Pearl Bay, continuing this two-decade tradition of rewarding customers not simply with prizes but with something far more enduring: time together and memories to share.

Ceylinco Life has announced that the landmark 20th edition of Family Savari will take five winning policyholder families, comprising 20 people to Beijing, another 10 families or 40 people to Malaysia, while 250 families, totalling 1,000 people, will enjoy a day of fun and excitement at Pearl Bay.

Both new and existing Ceylinco Life policyholders are eligible to participate, with the selection based on their life insurance policies and retirement plans.

The milestone edition also continues the much-loved tradition of having Ceylinco Life’s celebrity brand ambassadors Roshan Ranawana, his wife Kushlani and their son Minneth join the winning families, adding another dimension of fun and companionship to the Family Savari experience. “Reaching the 20th edition of Family Savari is a very special milestone for us because it represents much more than the longevity of a promotion. It represents two decades of a unique relationship with our customers,” Ceylinco Life Director and Chief Operating Officer Samitha Hemachandra said.
