Police probe alleged lapses in Colombo Port Ice bust

Colombo, Sep. 11 (Daily Mirror) - A Special Police Investigation Unit (SIU) has launched an internal inquiry into potential lapses and negligence surrounding an ongoing investigation into two containers carrying a stock of more than 471 kgs of crystal methamphetamine Ice.

Earlier, five suspects were arrested after authorities found over 471 kilograms of crystal methamphetamine, known as "Ice," hidden inside shipping containers at the Colombo Port.

The containers, which were imported from Pakistan, are currently held at the Colombo International Container Terminals (CICT) at the Colombo Port. The internal probe was initiated on the instructions of Inspector General of Police (IGP) Priyantha Weerasooriya following observed shortcomings in the operational process.

Opposition Parliamentarian Ajith P Perera yesterday (9) claimed that an investigation had been initiated into Police officers who carried out a major drug raid at the Colombo Port, including Senior Deputy Inspector General of Police Ranmal Kodithuwakku, warning that such action could discourage honest officials from performing their duties.

Speaking in Parliament, Perera questioned the decision to investigate the Police team responsible for the recent seizure of more than 471 kgs of crystal methamphetamine Ice at the Port, despite the Government and its Ministers repeatedly stressing their commitment to combating the drug trade.

The Police said that the internal disciplinary investigation will in no way disrupt or impede the primary criminal investigation into the seized contraband. The main probe into the illegal narcotics shipment is being conducted with full transparency by the Central Crime Investigation Bureau, police said.
