CEJ installs awareness boards in Anuradhapura to promote human-elephant coexistence

Colombo, Sep. 4 (Daily Mirror) - The Center for Environmental Justice (CEJ) has taken steps to install awareness boards in several key ecological areas in Anuradhapura to promote human-elephant coexistence and protect the country’s natural heritage.

The boards were installed in collaboration with the Department of Forest Conservation and the Department of Wildlife Conservation, covering proposed protected forest areas including Oyamaduwa, Pemaduwa and surrounding locations, as well as major wild elephant migration routes.

The initiative aims to educate the public on forest conservation regulations and encourage greater caution when travelling through areas frequented by wild elephants, while protecting both biodiversity and human lives.

CEJ acknowledged the support provided by Swallows, Finland, towards implementing the initiative.

The public has been urged to respect environmental laws and exercise caution when travelling through these areas.
