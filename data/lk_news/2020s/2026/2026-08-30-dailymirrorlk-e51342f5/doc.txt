Phoenix IT Lanka wins Check Point Best Performance  SMB Award again

Orin Corporation Chief Technology Officer Thilina Madawala, ⁠ Check Point Distribution Manager Kiran Kukrimath, Check Point Sri Lanka and Maldives Country Manager Ashok Baddage, Phoenix IT Lanka Chief Executive Officer Sudarika Warakapola, Orin Corporation Sales Manager Shenali Samarasekara, Phoenix IT Lanka Managing Director Prabath Amila Perera, Check Point South India and Sri Lanka Sales Head Ganapathy Cheppudira and Check Point APAC Head of Sales Wei See Wong

Phoenix IT Lanka (Pvt.) Ltd has strengthened its position in Sri Lanka’s cybersecurity sector by securing the Check Point Best Performance SMB Winner 2026 award for the second consecutive year, marking another significant milestone in the company’s five-year growth journey.

The recognition was presented at Check Point Award Night 2026, organised by South Asia Technologies (Pvt.) Ltd.

Established five years ago with a vision to contribute meaningfully to Sri Lanka’s technology and cybersecurity industry, Phoenix IT Lanka has evolved from a start-up into a trusted cybersecurity partner for businesses.

The company attributes its growth to a strong focus on customer trust, reliable cybersecurity solutions, technical expertise and long-term support.

As organisations face increasingly complex cyberthreats, technology risks and compliance requirements, Phoenix IT Lanka has positioned its services around helping businesses identify vulnerabilities, safeguard critical systems and strengthen their overall security posture.
