Shooting in Boralesgamuwa leaves 74-year-old man dead

Colombo, Sep. 4 (Daily Mirror) - A 74-year-old man was shot dead at his residence on Malani Bulathsinghala Mawatha in Boralesgamuwa a short while ago, police said.

According to police, two unidentified individuals who arrived on a motorcycle carried out the shooting while the victim was at his residence.

The assailants had used a pistol in the shooting, police said.

The deceased has been identified as a 74-year-old resident of the Boralesgamuwa area.

Police have launched an investigation to identify and arrest the suspects.
