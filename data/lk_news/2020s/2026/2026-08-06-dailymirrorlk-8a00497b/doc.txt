A/L and Grade 5 Scholarship exams to proceed as scheduled

Colombo, Agu. 6 (Daily Mirror) - The G.C.E. Advanced Level Examination and Grade 5 Scholarship Examination will be conducted as scheduled, authorities announced.

The decision was taken during a special meeting held yesterday involving officials from the Disaster Management Centre (DMC), Department of Examinations, National Building Research Organisation (NBRO), and other relevant institutions.

Officials said the examinations would proceed as current weather forecasts do not indicate any major adverse conditions expected during the examination period.

They added that the situation would continue to be monitored and necessary measures would be taken if weather conditions change.
