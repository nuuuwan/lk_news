Minor flood warning for Nilwala Ganga as several rivers remain on alert

A minor flood situation has been reported in the Nilwala Ganga at Panadugama, while water levels in four other river locations remain at alert levels, according to the Irrigation Department.

The department's latest islandwide water level and rainfall report, issued at 6.30 a.m. today (November 8), said the Nilwala Ganga at Panadugama had reached 6.05 metres, exceeding its minor flood level of 6.00 metres. The water level was reported to be rising.

Meanwhile, alert-level conditions have been reported in the following locations:

* Kuda Ganga – Kalawellawa (Millakanda): 5.14 metres and rising.

The department said water levels in the Kelani, Kalu, Gin, Kirindi, Menik, Kumbukkan, Heda, Maduru, Mahaweli, Yan, Maa, Malwathu, Mee, Deduru and Maha Oya river basins remained within normal levels at the time of the report.

Among the rainfall figures recorded during the preceding 21 hours, Glencourse in the Kelani Ganga basin received 72.9 mm, while Ratnapura recorded 79.2 mm in the Kalu Ganga basin.

Thawalama in the Gin Ganga basin recorded 80.8 mm, while Pitabeddara in the Nilwala Ganga basin received 72.5 mm.

The Irrigation Department urged people living in flood-prone areas to remain vigilant and follow safety instructions issued by relevant authorities as water levels continue to be monitored.
