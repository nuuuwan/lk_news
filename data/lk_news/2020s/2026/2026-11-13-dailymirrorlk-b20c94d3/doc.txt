Microsoft reports ‘encouraging recovery progress’ in global outage

Nov. 13 (Daily Mirror) - Microsoft just said it is seeing “encouraging recovery progress” at the same time as some social media users and CNN journalists report receiving emails in their inboxes again.

In a statement on X, the company reported “email delivery performance improving across a growing portion of affected Microsoft 356 infrastructure,” but warned some users may still “experience intermittent issues.”

While some social media users reported getting emails again, others said they were still unable to access them.
