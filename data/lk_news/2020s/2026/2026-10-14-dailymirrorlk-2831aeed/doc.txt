Indices close in red

Indices closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Dialog Axiata, Commercial Bank and Hatton National Bank with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.1bn.

High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Lanka Milk Foods, Aitken Spence and John Keells Holdings.

Mixed interest was observed in York Arcade Holdings, Lanka IOC and Colombo Dockyard whilst retail interest was noted in LVL Energy Fund, Ceylon Land & Equity and LOLC Finance. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net buyers.

Food, Beverage & Tobacco sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Lanka Milk Foods) whilst the sector index lost 0.31%. The share price of Lanka Milk Foods decreased by Rs. 0.40 (0.48%) to close at Rs. 82.60.

Capital Goods sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to John Keells Holdings and Aitken Spence) whilst the sector index decreased by 1.26%. The share price of John Keells Holdings lost Rs. 0.40 (2.16%) to close at Rs. 18.10. The share price of Aitken Spence declined by Rs. 1.75 (1.30%) to close at Rs. 132.50.

York Arcade Holdings and Lanka IOC were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of York Arcade Holdings moved down by Rs. 2.10 (24.71%) to close at Rs. 6.40. The share price of Lanka IOC recorded a loss of Rs. 1.00 (0.80%) to close at Rs. 124.50.
