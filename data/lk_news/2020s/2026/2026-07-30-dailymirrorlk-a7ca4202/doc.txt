Red warning issued for strong winds, rough seas

Colombo, July 30 (Daily Mirror) - The Department of Meteorology has issued a red warning for strong winds and rough seas off the coasts extending from Kankesanthurai to Kalpitiya via Mannar and from Hambantota to Pottuvil.

The Department said the sea areas off the coasts extending from Kankesanthurai to Kalpitiya via Mannar and from Hambantota to Pottuvil will be very rough at times with strong gusty winds up to 60 to 70 kmph.

Naval and fishing communities have been advised not to venture into these sea areas until further notice.

The sea areas off the coasts extending from Trincomalee to Kankesanthurai via Mullaittivu and Kalpitiya to Hambantota via Colombo and Galle will be rough at times with strong gusty winds up to 50 to 60 kmph.

The wave height is expected to increase about 2.0 to 2.5 metres in the sea areas off the coast extending from Mannar to Pottuvil via Colombo, Galle and Hambantota.

Meanwhile,the Meteorology Department said strong winds of about (40-50) kmph can be expected at times over Northern, North-central, North-western and Central provinces and in Rathnapura, Monaragala, Hambantota and Trincomalee districts due to the active south-west monsoonal condition.
