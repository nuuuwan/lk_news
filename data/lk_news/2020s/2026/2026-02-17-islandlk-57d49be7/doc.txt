Banking sector counters foster sanguinary sentiment in bourse

CSE trading began on a positive note yesterday but around the middle of the session, the All Share Price Index dipped due to selling pressure. However, at the end of the day investors became rather sanguine owing to positive results expected in banking sector counters.

Amid those developments both indices pointed to mixed reactions. The All Share Price Index went down by 17.29 points, while the S and P SL20 rose by 5.31 points.

Turnover stood at Rs 4.23 billion with seven crossings. Those crossings were reported in VallibelOne where 1.29 million shares crossed to the tune of Rs 140 million; its shares traded at Rs 108.50, Access

Engineering 800,000 shares crossed for Rs 59 million; its shares traded at Rs 74.50, HNB 100,000 shares crossed for Rs 45.2 million; its shares traded at Rs 452, JKH 2 million shares crossed for Rs 44 million; its shares sold at Rs 22.30.

United Motors one million shares crossed for Rs 37 million; its shares traded at Rs 37, Distilleries 500,000 shares crossed to the tune of Rs 29 million; its shares sold at Rs 58 and Samson International 400,000 shares crossed for Rs 22.4 million; its shares fetched Rs 560.

In the retail market top seven companies that mainly contributed to the turnover were; Softlogic Rs 273 million (34.2 million shares traded), Prime Lands Residencies Rs 245 million (4.8 million shares traded),

LMF Rs 239 million (2.7 million shares traded), Asia Siyaka Rs 192 million (13.3 million shares traded),

Pan Asia Bank Rs 174 million (2.9 million shares traded), Colombo Dockyard Rs 156.6 million (963593 shares traded) and Access Engineering Rs 150 million (1.6 million shares traded). During the day 158.8 million share volumes changed hands in 38685 transactions.

It is said that banking sector counters, especially HNB, performed well while the financial sector also performed well, especially Softlogic Capital. Real estate sector companies, such as Prime Lands Residencies, also performed well.

Yesterday the rupee was quoted at Rs 309.20/22 to the US dollar in the spot market, from Rs 309.20/25 the previous day, dealers said, while bond yields were broadly steady.
