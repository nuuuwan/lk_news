Cleantech becomes Sri Lanka’s first GP certified recycler

Cleantech (Pvt) Ltd has become Sri Lanka’s first recycler to achieve GP Certification, marking a historic step for the country’s plastic recycling industry. The certification was awarded by Ind-Expo Certification Ltd at a ceremony in Colombo on January 7, 2026.

After a rigorous audit of its operations, Cleantech, part of the Abans Group, now produces verified, high-quality recycled plastic pellets. This provides FMCG and packaging manufacturers with a reliable local source of ethically processed recyclate to meet sustainability goals and Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) mandates.

“This supports the rollout of EPR by giving brands a credible local compliance pathway,” said GP Certified Managing Director Chaminda Rajapakse. Cleantech’s Assistant General Manager Kasun Thennakoon stated the achievement reinforces their commitment to sustainability and cleaner cities.

The milestone is supported by the World Bank-funded PLEASE project, implemented by the South Asia Co-operative Environment Programme (SACEP). It aims to reduce Sri Lanka’s reliance on imported virgin plastic and strengthen its circular economy.
