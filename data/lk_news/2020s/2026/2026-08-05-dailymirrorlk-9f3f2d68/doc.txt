Pan Asia Bank appoints new Chairman, Deputy Chairperson

Pan Asia Bank this week announced the appointment of B.D.A. Perera as Chairman and Ayodhya Iddawela Perera as Deputy Chairperson with effect from 3 August 2026.

B.D.A. Perera, who joined the Board in April 2021, had served as Deputy Chairman and Senior Independent Director since November 2024. He succeeds Aravinda Perera, who chaired the Bank for nine years.

With over two decades of experience in leasing and finance, Perera currently serves as Executive Director of L.B. Finance PLC and its Myanmar subsidiary, L.B. Microfinance Myanmar Company Ltd. He previously held senior positions at Commercial Leasing Company Ltd, Lanka ORIX Leasing Company PLC and Merchant Bank Bangladesh.

An Associate Member of the Chartered Institute of Management Accountants (UK), Perera holds a BSc Special Degree in Business Administration from the University of Sri Jayewardenepura. He has also completed the High Potentials Leadership Programme at Harvard Business School.

Ayodhya Iddawela Perera joined Pan Asia Bank’s Board as an Independent Non-Executive Director in April 2026. She brings more than 36 years of local and international banking experience and served as Managing Director of Sampath Bank PLC from July 2023 to September 2025.

Her governance experience includes directorships at Lanka Financial Services Bureau Ltd, LankaPay (Pvt) Ltd, National Credit Guarantee Institution Ltd and Sampath Centre Ltd. She has also served on the boards of the Sri Lanka Banks’ Association and the Institute of Bankers of Sri Lanka.

She holds an MBA from the Postgraduate Institute of Management of the University of Sri Jayewardenepura and an Advanced Diploma in Management Accounting from CIMA, UK. She has completed her banking studies up to Associateship level and is a Senior Fellow of the Institute of Bankers of Sri Lanka.
