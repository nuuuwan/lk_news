Ceylinco Life partners with FTZMA as insurance service provider

Ceylinco Life has entered into a Memorandum of Understanding with FTZMA, a preeminent trade chamber representing manufacturing and export-oriented companies operating under the purview of BOI (Board of Investment of Sri Lanka) for a partnership as the Insurance service provider of FTZMA members to protect the health, reduce financial stress and secure the families’ future of their employees. This partnership further facilitates an exclusive customised insurance package that is tailor-made to all types of employees of FTZMA member organisations, including a retirement plan too.

Accordingly, FTZMA has recommended all their member companies to consider offering this insurance to their employees by the companies themself or by way of a contributory insurance scheme.

Further Ceylinco Life Insurance and Ceylinco General Insurance have come forward to partnering with FTZMA as the sole insurance sponsor of the AGMs of FTZMA under the gold category for 03 consecutive year period commencing from 2026.
