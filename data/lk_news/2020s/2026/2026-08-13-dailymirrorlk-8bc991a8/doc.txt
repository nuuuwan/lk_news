Cargills Food City’s ‘Shop & Win’ winner drives away in brand-new BAW E7 electric car

Cargills Food City’s ‘Shop & Win’ Promo came to an exciting finish, with one lucky shopper driving away in a brand-new BAW E7 electric car worth Rs. 5 million.

The grand prize was awarded to D.P. Medhavi Madhushika from Alubomulla, following the official draw.

A resident of Panadura, Madushika earned her winning entry while shopping at the Cargills Food City Matara 2 outlet during a family trip. The win underscores the nationwide accessibility of the promotion, giving customers across Sri Lanka the opportunity to shop and win at any Cargills Food City outlet.

Held from 1st to 30th June 2026, the promotion gave customers the chance to enter the grand prize draw by purchasing groceries worth Rs. 2,500 or more, including any three nominated brands, at any Cargills Food City outlet island wide.

The campaign attracted enthusiastic shoppers across the country, reflecting the trust they continue to place in Cargills Food City and its rewarding shopping experiences. As Sri Lanka’s leading supermarket chain, Cargills Food City remains committed to delivering exceptional value through quality products, everyday savings and rewarding promotions that make a meaningful difference in the lives of its customers.
