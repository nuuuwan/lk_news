Castlereagh and Maussakelle reservoirs reach overflow levels

Water levels in the Castlereagh and Maussakelle reservoirs have reached their respective overflow levels following intermittent rainfall in the surrounding catchment areas, officials said.

Engineers in charge of the reservoirs said both reservoirs had reached overflow levels by this morning (21).

The Castlereagh Reservoir had reached 0.6½ feet above its overflow level, while 4 millimetres of rainfall had been recorded in its catchment area during the past 24 hours.

The Maussakelle Reservoir had reached 3.7 feet above its overflow level, with 2.5 millimetres of rainfall recorded in the catchment area during the same period.

Engineers in charge of the power stations said electricity generation at the Canyon, Laxapana, New Laxapana, Polpitiya and Wimalasurendra power plants is currently being carried out at maximum capacity due to the increased water levels.

Meanwhile, the heavy rainfall in the catchment areas has also increased water flow at several waterfalls, including Gartmore, Mohini, St. Clair and Devon Falls.
