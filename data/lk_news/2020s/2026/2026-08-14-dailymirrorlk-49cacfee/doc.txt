“Shilpabhimani” Sales Fair

The “Shilpabhimani” Sales Fair 2026, organized by the Western Province Department of Industries to promote and market handicraft products, was launched today (14) and will continue until August 16 at Nugasewana Hall, BMICH, Colombo, from 9 a.m. to 8 p.m. Pix by Kithsiri De Mel
