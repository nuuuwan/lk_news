Sri Lanka expresses deep sorrow over loss of lives in Indonesian earthquake

Colombo, Aug. 16 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka has expressed deep sorrow over the the loss of lives, injuries and devastation caused by the earthquake and aftershocks that struck Flores Island, Indonesia.

Foreign Minister Vijitha Herath said Sri Lanka stands in solidarity with the Government and people of Indonesia during this difficult time.

"It is with deep sorrow that I note the loss of lives, injuries and devastation caused by the earthquake and aftershocks that struck Flores Island, Indonesia.

"My deepest condolences to the families of those who lost their loved ones, and wishes for a speedy recovery to the injured. My thoughts are also with those still missing and their families," he said in a post on X.

The Minister commended the dedicated efforts of the Indonesian authorities, rescue teams, humanitarian workers and all those working tirelessly in search, rescue and relief operations.
