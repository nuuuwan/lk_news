Access Motors receives award for ‘Most Professional Workshop’

Access Motors Private Limited, the sole authorized agent for Jaguar Land Rover in Sri Lanka, has been awarded the Most Professional Workshop in the After Sales Service sub-industry category at the Automobile Industry Awards 2026- a national ceremony held at Temple Trees, Colombo, on 12 June.

The Automobile Industry Awards are the foremost recognition of excellence and innovation across Sri Lanka’s automotive sector, honouring organizations that have demonstrated an unwavering commitment to quality, professionalism, and industry leadership. To be recognized in the After Sales Service category is a distinction that speaks directly to the daily standard of care that Access Motors delivers to every Jaguar Land Rover client.

“This recognition is a reflection of something we have always believed that the ownership experience does not end at handover. It continues every time a client brings their vehicle to us,” said Shiran Weerasinghe, General Manager After-Sales, Access Motors Private Limited.

The award is a testament to the consistently high standard of service, mechanical expertise, and vehicle repair delivered to Jaguar Land Rover clients across Sri Lanka. A team of qualified JLR-certified technicians handles every vehicle that passes through the Access Motors workshop. These individuals are trained and assessed to the same exacting standards as the global Jaguar Land Rover service network.

Access Motors operates the only Jaguar Land Rover certified Body Shop in Sri Lanka, a state-of-the-art, multi-brand repair facility equipped with the latest diagnostic technology and aluminium repair capabilities. From routine maintenance to complex structural repairs, the workshop operates to a single standard: uncompromising quality, every time.
