Heavy showers expected in several provinces

Showers and thundershowers are expected in several parts of the island, with fairly heavy rainfall exceeding 75mm likely in some areas, the Department of Meteorology said.

Accordingly, showers will occur at times in the Northern, North Central and Eastern provinces, while showers or thundershowers are expected at several places elsewhere in the country after 1.00 p.m.

Fairly heavy rainfall exceeding 75mm is likely at some places in the Western, Sabaragamuwa, Central, Uva and North Western provinces, as well as in the Galle and Matara districts.

Misty conditions may also prevail in the Western, Sabaragamuwa and Central provinces and in the Galle, Matara and Badulla districts during the early hours of the morning.

The public has been advised to take adequate precautions to minimise possible damage caused by temporary localised strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.
