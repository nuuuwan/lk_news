Shark fins for kendu leaves: Seven arrested in sea exchange off Chilaw

Colombo, Nov. 27 (Daily Mirror) - Four Sri Lankan fishermen and three Indian fishermen were arrested after the Sri Lanka Coast Guard and Navy intercepted an illegal exchange of shark fins and kendu leaves at sea off Chilaw on November 26.

The operation was launched after the Coast Guard vessel Samaraksha inspected a suspicious local dinghy on Thursday morning and discovered six sacks containing shark fins. Four Sri Lankan fishermen aboard the dinghy were taken into custody.

During questioning, the fishermen revealed that the shark fins were to be exchanged for kendu leaves with an Indian dhow at sea.

Acting on the information, the Coast Guard launched a follow-up operation in coordination with the Sri Lanka Navy.

When the Indian dhow approached the dinghy to carry out the exchange, the Coast Guard intercepted the operation and arrested three Indian fishermen aboard the vessel. A further 63 sacks containing kendu leaves were also seized.

The operation resulted in the seizure of one local dinghy, one Indian dhow, six sacks of shark fins and 63 sacks of kendu leaves.

Based on an estimated weight of 30 kilogrammes per sack, the seized consignment comprised approximately 180 kilogrammes of shark fins and 1,890 kilogrammes of kendu leaves.

The Coast Guard and Navy continue to work together to prevent illegal maritime activities and the trafficking of prohibited goods through Sri Lankan waters.
