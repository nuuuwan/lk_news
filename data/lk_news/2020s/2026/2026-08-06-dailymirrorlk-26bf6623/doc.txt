Privacy of politicians who publish their assets, liabilities on CIABOC site will not be restricted: Govt.

Colombo, Aug. 6 (Daily Mirror) - Privacy of politicians who publish their assets and liabilities with the Commission to Investigate Allegations on Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) will not be restricted under the amendment of the Anti Corruption Act, Leader of the House Bimal Ratnayake informed Parliament today.

Minister Ratnayake said the privacy of others will be restricted.

"Some forces, including some media organisations are engaged in misusing information uploaded on the CIABOC websites," he added.
