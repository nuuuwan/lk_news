Policeman sporting beard arrested in Kandy while taking pre wedding photos

Colombo, Nov. 25 (Daily Mirror) - A man who was walking around Kandy city wearing a police uniform and sporting a full beard while taking pre-wedding photographs with his fiancée has been arrested by officers of the Kandy Headquarters Police.

Police arrested the man after receiving information that an individual wearing a police traffic uniform and sporting a full beard was at Yatinuwara Street in Kandy. Kandy Headquarters Police Inspector Rasika Sampath subsequently arrived at the location and took the suspect into custody.

However, subsequent investigations revealed that the man was, in fact, a serving police officer. He was identified as a police constable attached to the Thalangama Police.

Further investigations revealed that the constable, who is a Hindu, is due to get married shortly and had obtained permission from the Inspector General of Police to grow a beard in keeping with Hindu religious customs ahead of his wedding.

When contacted regarding the incident, a senior police officer in Kandy stressed that although the constable had been granted permission to grow a beard for his wedding, he had not been authorised to wear the police uniform for the purpose.

The senior officer further said that a departmental disciplinary inquiry would be conducted against the constable in connection with the incident.
