Namal Rajapaksa produced before court in Krrish case

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - Remanded MP Namal Rajapaksa was brought before the Colombo Magistrate’s Court today (20) in connection with the Krrish transaction case.

The case was scheduled to be taken up before the Colombo Magistrate’s Court today, and Rajapaksa was escorted from remand custody to appear before the court for the proceedings.
