Rs. 500 million allocated for northern power transmission project

The Government has allocated Rs. 500 million for preliminary work on a 400-kilovolt (kV) transmission line from Kilinochchi to New Habarana as part of efforts to modernise and expand the country's electricity transmission network.

The project is expected to be completed by 2030, with plans to secure US$ 150 million in financial assistance from the Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB) for its construction.

The Budget has also proposed several other transmission line projects through private investment to improve the capacity and reliability of the national electricity grid.

They include a 220 kV transmission line from Norochcholai to Wariyapola, another 220 kV line from Monaragala to Hambantota, and a 400 kV line from New Habarana to Kirindiwela.

The proposed projects aim to strengthen the country's electricity transmission capacity to ensure the efficient distribution of power as electricity generation increases.
