Ex-cop wanted over T-56 rifle theft brought back from Dubai

Colombo, Nov 26 (Daily Mirror) - A former police officer wanted over the theft of his official T-56 assault rifle while on roadblock duty in Mount Lavinia has been arrested in Dubai and brought back to Sri Lanka today (26).

The suspect, identified as 26-year-old Jayakody Arachchige Kavindu Akash Jayakody, a resident of Halambagaswewa in Pemaduwa, Anuradhapura, is alleged to have stolen the firearm while serving at the Mount Lavinia Police and handed it over to members of an organised crime gang before fleeing the country.

He was arrested in a joint operation in Dubai involving officers of the Criminal Investigation Department (CID) and security personnel of SriLankan Airlines.

The suspect arrived at the Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) in Katunayake at around 5.20 a.m. today aboard SriLankan Airlines flight UL-226.

Upon his arrival, CID officers took him into custody to record a statement. He is scheduled to be handed over to the Western Province South Crimes Division for further investigations.
