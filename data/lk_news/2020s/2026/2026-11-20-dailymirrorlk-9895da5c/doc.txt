Court orders Namal Rajapaksa to be produced on December 1 if still in remand custody

Colombo, Nov 20 (Daily Mirror) - Colombo Chief Magistrate Asanga S. Bodaragama today directed prison authorities to produce Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa before court on December 1, if he remains in remand custody.

Rajapaksa had previously been granted bail in connection with the Krrish transaction case. However, the Colombo High Court issued an interim order on November 18 suspending the bail order.

Consequently, Rajapaksa remains in remand custody pending further court proceedings.
