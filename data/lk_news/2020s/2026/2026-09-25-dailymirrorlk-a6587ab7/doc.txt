Beyond Ordinary - Nations Business Advisory Nations Trust Bank advances business dialogue

Nations Trust Bank (NTB) continues to convene business leaders and entrepreneurs through Beyond Ordinary - Nations Business Advisory, a knowledge-sharing platform designed to provide timely economic insights, expert perspectives and meaningful business connections that support informed decision-making in a dynamic business environment.

The platform serves as a forum for engaging discussions on key economic and business developments, enabling organisations to better navigate emerging opportunities and challenges while fostering valuable connections within Sri Lanka’s business community.

The participants gained access to expert perspectives on economic trends, tax developments and factors shaping the business environment, alongside the opportunities to network with the industry peers and thought leaders.

Advocata Institute CEO Dhananath Fernando delivered the keynote address, sharing perspectives on economic trends, market dynamics and the evolving business landscape. The session was followed by a panel discussion featuring Moore Consulting Managing Director and CA Sri Lanka President Tishan Subasinghe, Fernando and NTB Senior Executive Vice President Commercial Banking Arosha Liyanaarachchi.

Liyanaarachchi said, “Beyond Ordinary - Nations Business Advisory was created to bring together business leaders, industry experts and our customers to engage in meaningful conversations around the factors shaping business success. For the past five years, we have continuously evolved and refined this platform, enhancing the experience and increasing the value it delivers to our customers.”

The Kandy edition also provided an opportunity for Trail Sri Lanka to share insights on the Karapitiya Cancer Hospital Project, led by Mahela Jayawardena, helping raise awareness among the business community and encouraging support for this significant national initiative.
