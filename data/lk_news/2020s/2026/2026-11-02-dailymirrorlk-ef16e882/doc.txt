‘Hasthi Sena’ operation seizes illegal firearms, elephant traps

Colombo, Nov. 2 (Daily Mirror) - A large number of illegal items, including unauthorised firearms, ammunition, animal traps and explosive-laden baits known as hakka-patas, were seized during a special operation launched in the Kala Wewa area today (November 2) to protect elephants and other wildlife.

The operation, named ‘Hasthi Sena’, was carried out through a joint effort by the Ministry of Environment, the Sri Lanka Army and the Department of Wildlife Conservation in the North-Western Wildlife Zone.

The seized items included firearms that could be used to kill elephants and other wild animals. They are to be produced before the Kekirawa Magistrate’s Court for further legal action.

Environment Minister Dhammika Patabendi led the initiative, which involved 60 military personnel deployed in 10 operational teams organised into six groups.

The operation was also attended by North Western Province Wildlife Director Pushpantha Wimaladasa, Ministry of Environment Adviser Ravinda Kariyawasam, Major General Kaminda Silva of the Army’s 21 Division, 212 Brigade Commander Brigadier Basnayake and Lieutenant Colonel Abeywickrama.

Authorities said raids would continue to identify and take action against those using illegal firearms, hakka-patas, unauthorised electric fences and animal traps that threaten the lives of elephants and other wildlife.

Those arrested and the seized items will be handed over to the relevant authorities for legal action under the Fauna and Flora Protection Ordinance.
