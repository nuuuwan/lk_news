AMW launches Yamaha XSR-155 and NMAX-155 at Colombo Motor Show 2026

Associated Motorways (Private) Limited (AMW), the authorized distributor for Yamaha in Sri Lanka, unveiled two new 155cc two-wheelers for the local market the retro-styled Yamaha XSR-155 motorcycle and the Yamaha NMAX-155 scooterat the Colombo Motor Show 2026 at BMICH.

With the dual launch, Yamaha’s Sri Lankan lineup now includes two distinct riding styles: a heritage-inspired manual motorcycle and a connected automatic scooter built for urban commuting.

Jawahar Ganesh, Group Managing Director of AMW said, “Sri Lankan riders are increasingly selecting two-wheelers according to their lifestyle, whether that means stress-free technology in everyday traffic in Colombo or weekend mechanical character. Backed by Yamaha’s global Japanese engineering and AMW’s nationwide after sales network, introducing the XSR-155 and NMAX-155 together at the Colombo Motor Show gives riders a clear choice between connected scooter practicality and retro motorcycle styling.”

Designed for riders who want classic motorcycle proportions supported by modern hardware, the Yamaha XSR-155 combines a 155cc, air-cooled, 4-stroke, SOHC, 4-valve fuel-injected engine with a round LED headlight, LED taillight, digital instrument cluster, and 10-litre fuel tank. Paired with a 6-speed constant mesh gearbox and a wet multiple-disc clutch, the powertrain generates 13.5 kW (18.4 PS) at 10,000 rpm and 14.2 Nm of torque at 7,500 rpm. The 134 kg motorcycle has a Deltabox frame with telescopic front forks, a link-type swingarm rear suspension, 17-inch tubeless tyres, an 810 mm of seat height, 170 mm of ground clearance, and front and rear hydraulic single disk brakes with dual-channel ABS.

Targeted at everyday city commuters looking for comfort and onboard connectivity, the Yamaha NMAX-155 is powered by a 155cc liquid-cooled, 4-stroke, SOHC, 4-valve fuel-injected engine delivering 11.1 kW at 8,000 rpm and 14.0 Nm of torque at 6,500 rpm. Equipped with a 7.1-litre fuel tank, and CO2 emissions of 57 g/km, it introduces Yamaha’s Electric Continuously Variable Transmission (YECVT) with selectable ride modes. Weighing 135 kg wet, the scooter features an accessible 770 mm seat height, 125 mm ground clearance, telescopic front forks, unit swing rear suspension, 13-inch tyres, front and rear hydraulic single disk brakes, and a 4.2-inch display with on-screen Garmin StreetCross navigation, MyRide smartphone connectivity, and a smart keyless ignition system.
