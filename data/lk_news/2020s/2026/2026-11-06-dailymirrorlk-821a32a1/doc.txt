Indices close in green

Indices closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Commercial Bank, Dialog Axiata and Hayleys with the turnover crossing Rs. 5.4bn.

High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Lanka Milk Foods, Cargills Bank and Royal Ceramics.

Mixed interest was observed in People’s Leasing & Finance, Hatton National Bank and Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka whilst retail interest was noted in Softlogic Finance, SMB Leasing and Softlogic Finance.

Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers. The Banking sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Hatton National Bank and Cargills Bank) whilst the sector index gained 3.73%.

The share price of Hatton National Bank moved up by Rs. 5.00 (1.30%) to close at Rs. 390.00. The share price of Cargills Bank appreciated by Rs. 0.10 (1.45%) to close at Rs. 7.00.

Materials sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Tokyo Cement Company nonvoting) whilst the sector index increased by 3.81%. The share price of Tokyo Cement Company nonvoting recorded a gain of Rs. 5.60 (9.62%) to close at Rs. 63.80.

Lanka Milk Foods and People’s Leasing & Finance were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Lanka Milk Foods increased by Rs. 4.60 (%) to close at Rs. 88.30. The share price of People’s Leasing & Finance gained Rs. 0.60 (5.50%) to close at Rs. 18.10.
