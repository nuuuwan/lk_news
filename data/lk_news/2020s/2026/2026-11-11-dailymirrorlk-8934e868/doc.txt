Atmospheric disturbance in Bay of Bengal could severely affect Sri Lanka

Colombo, November 11 (Daily Mirror) - Atmospheric disturbances developing over the South East section of the Bay of Bengal could have a severe effect on the Eastern part of Sri Lanka, the Department of Meteorology said yesterday.

Department of Meteorology Director General Ajith Wijemanne told the media that the department is closely monitoring the weather changes and will issue notices if and when the need arises. “We don’t have much details on the disturbance and will come up with notices within the coming days. However, it is likely that the Eastern part of Sri Lanka could experience heavy rains as the disturbance which could develop into a depression is expected to travel towards the North over the Eastern coast of Sri Lanka,” he said and added there could be heavy rains in Sri Lanka starting from November 15.

“Sri Lanka is currently experiencing the second inter-monsoon period for the year while there is the effect of El Nino as well. Inter-monsoon conditions could bring in evening showers to any part of the country,” he said.

He said naval and fishing communities should be attentive to the notices issued by the Department of Meteorology. “Also one should not be driving during heavy showers,” Wijemanne said.

The Department of Meteorology advised that all outdoor sports, construction, agricultural and recreational activities should be halted when thunderstorms approach, while those living in vulnerable areas should be prepared for evacuation if the need arises and be attentive to warnings issued by the Building Research Organization and Department of Irrigation.
