Child singer among four injured in Hatton road accident

Colombo, Nov. 16 (Daily Mirror) - Four members of the same family, including child singer Senumi Giwansa, were injured when their car crashed into a hotel boundary wall on the Hatton-Nuwara Eliya main road early today (16).

The accident occurred around 4.00 a.m. in the Commercial area of Kotagala, Dimbula Pathana Police said.

The child singer, her mother, the driver and another passenger were travelling to their home in Welimada after attending a musical concert in Galle when the car veered off the road and collided with the boundary wall of a tourist hotel.

All four injured occupants were admitted to the Dikoya Base Hospital for treatment.

Police said their injuries were not serious, although the car sustained extensive damage.

Preliminary investigations have revealed that the driver had fallen asleep at the wheel, causing the accident.
