DFCC Aloka empowers entrepreneurs as key sponsor of women’s awards

DFCC Bank’s women-focused initiative, DFCC Aloka, has reaffirmed its commitment to women entrepreneurs as the diamond sponsor and official banking partner of the Women’s Chamber of Industry and Commerce (WCIC) Prathibhabhisheka Awards 2025.

Marking its fourth consecutive year of support, DFCC Aloka backs this flagship platform that celebrates women-led businesses driving innovation, employment, and inclusive growth across Sri Lanka and the SAARC region.

Through tailored financial solutions, advisory support, and dedicated partnership, DFCC Aloka aims to empower women entrepreneurs to start, sustain, and scale their ventures. The bank’s leadership emphasised that the partnership reflects a shared mission to combine recognition with practical financial pathways, enabling meaningful economic participation and growth for women in business.
