Hatton–Colombo Main Road partially reopened after landslide

Colombo, August 15 (Daily Mirror) - One lane of the Hatton–Colombo main road at Kuilwatta, which was completely blocked following a landslide early this morning (15), has now been reopened to traffic.

A mound of earth reportedly collapsed onto the road at around 1 a.m., completely obstructing traffic in the area.

Police said one lane has now been cleared for traffic, but motorists have been urged to drive with caution due to the continued risk of further landslides.
