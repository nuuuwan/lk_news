Cement prices increased by Rs. 100 per bag

Cement companies have increased the price of a 50kg bag of cement by Rs. 100, citing rising production costs.

Following the revision, the price of a 50kg bag, which previously ranged between Rs. 2,100 and Rs. 2,250, has increased to between Rs. 2,200 and Rs. 2,350.

The companies said the price hike had become unavoidable due to the rising costs associated with cement production.

The increase is expected to add to construction costs, putting further pressure on consumers and the construction industry.
