Harischandra Mills delivers strong first-quarter earnings on revenue surge

Harischandra Mills PLC reported a sharp rise in first-quarter earnings, with profit after tax surging 147 percent year-on-year to Rs.91.9 million for the three months ended June 30, 2026, supported by a double-digit revenue growth and stronger operating profitability across its core businesses. Revenue rose 31 percent to Rs.1.93 billion, from Rs.1.47 billion a year earlier, while gross profit increased 35 percent to Rs.347.5 million, lifting the gross profit margin to 18 percent, from 17 percent in the corresponding period. Other income more than tripled to Rs.22.6 million, further boosting earnings.

The food products segment, Harischandra’s largest business, remained the key earnings driver, with revenue increasing to Rs.1.27 billion, from Rs.1.04 billion a year earlier, while the segment operating profit more than doubled to Rs.83.2 million. Fuel and lubricants also returned to profit, although the soap segment slipped into a small operating loss, despite the higher sales. The operating profit climbed 144 percent to Rs.105.4 million as the revenue growth outpaced the increases in administrative and selling expenses. Although the finance expenses rose nearly fourfold, largely offsetting the higher finance income, profit before tax still more than doubled to Rs.110.4 million. Basic earnings per share improved to Rs.47.86, from Rs.19.41 a year earlier. The company’s balance sheet remained largely equity-funded, with total equity increasing to Rs.2.05 billion at end-June, from Rs.1.96 billion three months earlier, reflecting the quarter’s earnings. Net asset value per share improved to Rs.1,068.83, from Rs.1,020.98 at end-March. During the quarter, Harischandra generated Rs.141.1 million in operating cash flow but stepped up the capital expenditure, investing Rs.162.4 million in property, plant and equipment. The cash balances rose, although the bank overdrafts remained elevated at Rs.175.1 million.
