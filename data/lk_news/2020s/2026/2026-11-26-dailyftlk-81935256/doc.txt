FACETS 2027 puts Sri Lankan jewellery in spotlight

FACETS Sri Lanka 2027 takes place on 4–6 January 2027 at Cinnamon Life – City of Dreams, Colombo

A grander pavilion, a “Bridges of Light” design competition, Sapphire Night and a new Gem Conversations morning headline the 34th edition

Sri Lanka’s gemstones have long dazzled the world. At FACETS 2027, the spotlight turns to the artistry that transforms them into jewellery. The Sri Lanka Gem & Jewellery Association (SLGJA) has announced the jewellery program for the 34th edition of FACETS Sri Lanka, to be held from 4 to 6 January 2027 at Cinnamon Life – City of Dreams, Colombo.

Recognised as South Asia’s premier gem and jewellery exhibition, FACETS celebrates the design excellence and manufacturing expertise that complement the country’s coloured gemstones.

SLGJA Vice President Shezard Careem, who leads the jewellery segment, said this year’s program is built around four focus areas.

A grander stage: With the exhibition moving to the larger main Lumina Ballroom, the Jewellery Pavilion will be positioned at the entrance, with new participants exhibiting alongside established jewellery houses to present fine jewellery collections and bespoke creations.

Bridges of Light: A dedicated area will showcase the Jewellery Design Competition, themed “Bridges of Light”, which celebrates peace, unity and human connection. Ten finalist designs are being made by some of Sri Lanka’s leading jewellery houses, and the completed pieces will be unveiled at the exhibition, where the winners will be announced. The competition aims to nurture emerging talent and raise design standards, while giving designers local and international exposure.

Sapphire Night: The industry’s most glamorous occasion returns, blending fashion, entertainment and celebration for exhibitors, invited guests and international buyers.

The Gem Conversations: Following the success of the Power Breakfast for Women, FACETS 2027 introduces The Gem Conversations – Stories That Sparkle, an intimate, invitation-only morning of gemstone stories, inspiring women and lively conversations about jewellery and creativity.

“Sri Lankan jewellery has come a long way, and this program gives it the stage it deserves,” said Shezard Careem.

FACETS 2027 Chairman Shezmin Mansoor said: “FACETS continues to evolve as a world-class platform. These initiatives showcase Sri Lankan talent and strengthen our position as a premier jewellery destination.”

With over 100 exhibitors expected and buyers attending from around the world, the exhibition will also offer opportunities for business, networking, education and international collaboration.
