‘Ethera Diriya’ home loan scheme for expatriates

Colombo, September 9 (Daily Mirror) - A new home loan scheme named ‘Ethera Diriya’ is set to be officially launched tomorrow (10), offering affordable housing loans to Sri Lankan expatriate workers ranging from Rs. 500,000 to Rs. 10 million.

Under the scheme, a portion of the interest payable on the loans will be reimbursed by the Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment (SLBFE), helping reduce the financial burden on expatriate workers seeking to build or purchase homes.

The loans will be offered with a repayment period of up to 10 years, while applications can be submitted through both public and private sector banks.

The initiative has been introduced as a housing support programme for Sri Lankan migrant workers, enabling them to invest their overseas earnings in secure and affordable housing.

The official launch of the ‘Ethera Diriya’ Home Loan Scheme is scheduled for September 10, 2026.
