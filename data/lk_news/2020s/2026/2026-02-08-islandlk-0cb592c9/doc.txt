Nyne Hotels launches luxury villa tier amid growing market

Nyne Hotels has unveiled NYNE LUXE, its new premium tier and Sri Lanka’s first integrated boutique villa concept. The launch targets the island’s high-end tourism sector, which Mordor Intelligence forecasts will grow at 8.11% annually through 2031.

The brand, founded by hospitality veteran Nayantara “Taru” Fonseka, structures its art-led properties across three tiers: ‘Essence,’ ‘Signature,’ and the newly introduced ‘LUXE’.

The flagship NYNE LUXE development in Bentota features two distinct properties; the beachfront Anugrah hotel and the all-inclusive Anuprit boutique retreat within a single destination. Shared amenities, branded Anubava, include leisure facilities and wellness zones. Dining options comprise the fusion-focused Table by Nyne and the casual Tuck Shop café.
