Gnanasara Thera remanded until tomorrow

Colombo, Oct 04 (Daily Mirror) - Bodu Bala Sena (BBS) General Secretary Ven. Galagoda Aththe Gnanasara Thera, who was arrested while an open warrant was in force against him, has been remanded until tomorrow (5) by the Colombo Magistrate’s Court.

The court also ordered that steps be taken to produce Gnanasara Thera before the Court of Appeal tomorrow.

He was produced before the Colombo Magistrate’s Court today (4) by officers of the Colombo Crimes Division following his arrest.
