Fire destroys e-waste warehouse in Divulapitiya

Colombo, Nov. 15 (Daily Mirror) - A fire that broke out at an electronic waste collection warehouse in the Wewagedara area of Divulapitiya at around 11.00 a.m. today (15) has completely destroyed the electronic waste stored at the facility.

Residents in the area, officers from the Divulapitiya Police Station and firefighters have been battling the blaze for nearly eight hours in an effort to bring it under control.

However, the fire had still not been completely extinguished at the time of reporting.

Authorities are yet to determine the extent of the damage caused by the fire.
