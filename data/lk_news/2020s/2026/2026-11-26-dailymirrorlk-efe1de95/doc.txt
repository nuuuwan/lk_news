Further evidence in Yoshitha Rajapaksa’s money laundering case to be heard on December 3

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - The hearing of further evidence in the money laundering case filed by the Attorney General against Yoshitha Rajapaksa under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act was postponed till December 3 by Colombo High Court Judge Udesh Ranatunga.

The Attorney General filed this case against Yoshitha Rajapaksa, alleging that he committed an offence under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act by purchasing five plots of land in the Dehiwala and Ratmalana areas for a sum exceeding Rs. 730 million.
