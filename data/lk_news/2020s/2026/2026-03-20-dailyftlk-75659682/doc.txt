US Special Representative for South and Central Asia meets President

The US Special Representative for South and Central Asia and US Ambassador to India, Sergio Gor, who is currently visiting Sri Lanka, met with President Anura Kumara Dissanayake yesterday (19) at the Presidential Secretariat.

During the meeting, discussions were held on further strengthening bilateral relations between the US and Sri Lanka.

The President also briefed the delegation on Sri Lanka’s position regarding the ongoing conflict situation in the Middle East, as well as the challenges faced by the country in light of these developments.

Secretary to the President Dr. Nandika Sanath Kumanayake, Treasury Secretary Dr. Harshana Suriyapperuma; Senior Economic Adviser to the President Duminda Hulangamuwa; Senior Additional Secretary to the President Roshan Gamage; along with members of Sergio Gor’s delegation were also present at the meeting.
