Overcrowded prisons: Prisoners’ living  conditions need urgent attention

The following unfortunate incidents have been reported during the last 20 months.Unrest at Matara prison on 22/23 April 2025, at Negombo Prison on  July 5/6 2026, at Mahara Prison on  August 1, 2026 and the suspension of the Commissioner General on  June 9 2025.

According to the Prison Statistics-2025, the anti- drug campaign has resulted in the increase of offenders from 9344 in 2021, to 19,094 in 2022, 29,192 in 2023 and to 31,314 in 2024. The campaigns were a success but their impact on the Courts, Analyst’s Department and the Prisons were drastic.

The backlog of cases are in the lower Courts. Ways and means of setting up more courthouses, recruiting lower court judges, expediting Analyst’s reports, increasing accommodation and staff in the Prisons, and reducing/ redistributing the prison population must be found.

Overcrowding has caused tremendous pressure on the prison authorities and inmates due to cramped conditions, poor health, sanitation and recreation facilities and poor quality of food. This is reflected in the  damage caused to property.

This situation calls for concerted and immediate action and is a herculean task. The Ministry of Justice could deal with the issues relating to the backlog of cases and delays in Analyst’s Reports and a separate Deputy Ministry of Prisons and  Prison Reforms could deal with issues in the prisons.
