NDB recognised as best women-friendly workplace

National Development Bank PLC (NDB) has been honoured as a Best Women-Friendly Workplace at the prestigious Great HR Awards 2025.

The award, presented by the Chartered Institute of Personnel Management Sri Lanka in collaboration with Mercer, recognises NDB’s long-standing commitment to empowering women and fostering an inclusive culture at all organisational levels.

This accolade reinforces the strategic priority NDB places on gender inclusivity, reflected in its leadership development and employee experience. The bank’s commitment is further validated by its EDGE Certification for gender equity, an international benchmark it has achieved for three consecutive years—the only local organisation to do so.

“At NDB, we believe that diversity strengthens us, inclusion defines us, and empowering women elevates us,” said Lasantha Dasanayaka, Vice President – Human Resources.

The award underscores NDB’s position as a leading employer committed to progressive people practices and holistic talent development in Sri Lanka.
