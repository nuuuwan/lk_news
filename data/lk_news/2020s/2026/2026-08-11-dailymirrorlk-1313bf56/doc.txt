World Bank approves US$100 mn for North, East development programme

Colombo, Aug. 11 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet has approved the signing of financial agreements to secure a US$100 million facility from the World Bank’s International Development Association (IDA) to implement an integrated regional development programme in Sri Lanka’s Northern and Eastern Provinces.

The funding is part of a broader commitment by the World Bank Group to provide US$1 billion to Sri Lanka between 2026 and 2031, with US$100 million earmarked for the Northern and Eastern Provinces Integrated Regional Development Programme.

Cabinet approval for negotiations on the loan facility was granted on February 9, 2026, and negotiations between the Government of Sri Lanka and the World Bank Group have since been concluded.

The programme is aimed at strengthening economic potential groups, supporting micro, small and medium-scale enterprises (MSMEs), expanding economic opportunities and improving regional connectivity.

It is also expected to enhance the livelihoods of communities through sustainable regional development while accelerating economic growth and improving the competitiveness of the Northern and Eastern Provinces.

Accordingly, Cabinet approved the proposal submitted by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake in his capacity as Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development, to enter into the necessary financial agreements with the World Bank’s IDA to obtain the US$100 million facility.
