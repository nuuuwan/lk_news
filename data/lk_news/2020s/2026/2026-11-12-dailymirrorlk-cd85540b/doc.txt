Peugeot and TotalEnergies extend 30-year global partnership to Sri Lanka

Peugeot and TotalEnergies Lubricants are extending their global partnership of more than 30 years to Sri Lanka, through a Marketing and Technical Support Agreement signed on 27 October 2026 between their local partners: Energy Core Lanka (Pvt) Ltd (ECL), the authorised distributor of TotalEnergies Lubricants, and Carmart (Pvt) Ltd, the authorised distributor of Peugeot, as well as sister brand, Leapmotor.

TotalEnergies Lubricants is Peugeot’s recommended lubricant brand worldwide. Engineers from both companies work together from the earliest stages of engine and transmission design, formulating the first-fill lubricants in every new Peugeot, developed for smooth running, longer engine life, and lower fuel consumption and CO₂ emissions.

The relationship extends from the road to the track and to the next generation of mobility. TotalEnergies is the official motorsport partner of Peugeot Sport, co-developing the battery and specialist fluids behind Peugeot’s hybrid Hypercar programme in the FIA World Endurance Championship, including the 24 Hours of Le Mans. The two companies are also working together on electric vehicles, batteries, low-carbon and bio-sourced fuels, and eco-designed lubricants.

Under the agreement, ECL will supply Carmart’s Peugeot service network with TotalEnergies lubricants, including Quartz Ineo 5W-30, sourced from France and fully complying with Peugeot’s stringent specifications. ECL will also train CARMART’s technicians, and the two companies will run joint campaigns for Peugeot customers.

For Peugeot owners, it means their vehicles are serviced with the same products their engines were developed with. Carmart’s Peugeot service and health check campaign, from 16 November to 18 December 2026 at Union Place, Colombo 2, offers fixed-price service packages using TotalEnergies lubricants.

Dimitri Sheriff, Managing Director of Energy Core Lanka (Pvt) Ltd said, “Working with the Carmartteam, we see this as far more than a supply agreement. It is a genuine collaboration, covering training and technical support for Carmart’sservice teams, joint marketing and customer education. TotalEnergies and Peugeot have built a partnership over thirty years globally, and together we intend to bring the full strength of that relationship to Sri Lanka.”

Yasendra Amerasinghe, Director/Chief Executive Officer of Carmart said, “From this month, every Peugeot serviced at Carmart uses the TotalEnergies brand oil its engine was developed with. That is the standard Peugeot owners should expect, and we are backing it with fixed-price service packages with the support of ECL and are working closely with our partners and principals to strive to reduce the cost of ownership.”
