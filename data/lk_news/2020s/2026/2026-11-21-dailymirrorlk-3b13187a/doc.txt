Two feared drowned while bathing in sea off Nintavur

Nov 21, ( Daily Mirror): Two youths have gone missing after reportedly drowning while bathing in the sea off Nintavur, police said.

The incident occurred when three youths went into the sea to bathe. Area residents managed to rescue one of them, who was subsequently admitted to Nintavur Hospital.

The two missing youths, aged 16 and 23, are residents of the Nintavur area.

Sri Lanka Navy diving teams, together with Nintavur Police and local residents, have launched a joint search operation to locate the missing youths.
