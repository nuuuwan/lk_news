Division 1A Season Kicks Off This Weekend

June.11 (Mirror Sports) - The 2026 Dialog Schools Under-19 Rugby League Tournament gets underway this weekend with an exciting slate of Division 1A fixtures across the country.

Several traditional rivals and school rugby powerhouses will be in action as teams look to make a strong start to their campaigns. Key opening-week clashes include St. Joseph’s College vs St. Anthony’s College, Royal College vs Trinity College, and Isipathana College vs St. Peter’s College, setting the stage for another thrilling season of schools rugby.
