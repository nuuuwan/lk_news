Court issues notice on National Hospital Director over failure to submit Suresh Sallay’s medical reports

Colombo, Sep. 15 (Daily Mirror) - Colombo Fort Magistrate Pasan Amarasekara today issued notice on the Director of the National Hospital, Colombo to appear before court on September 22 and show cause as to why the reports on the medical condition of Suresh Sallay, which were ordered by court on September 2, had not been submitted to court.

The Magistrate further directed the Hospital Director to explain why the reports requested by the five-member medical board had also not been submitted to the medical board.
