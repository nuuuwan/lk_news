‘Aiming to create generation of Sri Lankans with entrepreneurial mindset’

Sarvajana Balaya Leader and MP Dilith Jayaweera (right) addressing the audience at the launch of the the GLEC digital platform

Sarvajana Balaya Leader Dilith Jayaweera says at launch of GLEC digital platform

Sarvajana Balaya Leader and MP Dilith Jayaweera said that a generation of Sri Lankans with an entrepreneurial mindset would be nurtured both locally and internationally by instilling the concept of entrepreneurship in the minds of the people. He further stated that his objective was to generate wealth for Sri Lanka through this initiative.

Jayaweera made these remarks at the launch of the Global Lanka Entrepreneur Club (GLEC) digital platform in Colombo on 15 November. He invited everyone to join the GLEC digital platform, which was launched based on his own concept.

Jayaweera further stated: “Just a decade ago, the word ‘entrepreneurship’ was not familiar to us. However, over the past 10 years, we have played a pioneering role in popularising the concept of entrepreneurship within society.

“As a result, entrepreneurship has now become a key term on the lips of many people. Academics, professionals and university students are now talking about entrepreneurship. Not only that, politicians and diplomats have also begun using the term. This is a welcome development.

“This is something that can be transformed into a reality on the ground. Through this digital platform, we are seeking to provide opportunities to make that happen. Our objective is to facilitate the sharing of global knowledge and information on entrepreneurship through the digital space. This platform will also enable us to bring together some of the finest and brightest Sri Lankan entrepreneurs from around the world and share their experiences.

“We invite everyone to join the GLEC platform. What we want is to embed the concept of entrepreneurship in the minds of the people. Our goal is to create a generation of Sri Lankans with an entrepreneurial mindset, both within Sri Lanka and beyond its borders. Our aim is to generate wealth for our motherland through them.”
