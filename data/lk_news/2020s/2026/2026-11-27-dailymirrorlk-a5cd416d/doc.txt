Eastern Terminal to reach 50% capacity this month, Deputy Minister says

Colombo, Nov. 27 (Daily Mirror) - The Colombo Port’s Eastern Terminal is expected to begin operating at 50% of its capacity within this month, with automated yard operations now being introduced, Deputy Minister of Ports and Civil Aviation Janitha Ruwan Kodithuwakku said.

He made these remarks during an inspection visit to the Eastern Terminal today (27), to review the progress of its automated yard operations.

The Deputy Minister said the Sri Lanka Ports Authority had successfully overcome several challenges associated with the terminal's semi-automated yard, paving the way for the commencement of operations.

He said one lane of the yard had begun operating, while work was underway to activate the remaining 10 lanes within this month.

The terminal had also achieved its expected operational efficiency of handling between 20 and 30 containers per hour during the initial launch, he said.

The automated system uses Automated Rail Mounted Gantry (ARMG) cranes to handle container operations.

Kodithuwakku described the commencement of automated yard operations as a major achievement for the Government and the Sri Lanka Ports Authority, noting that the terminal had faced delays for a considerable period due to various challenges.

He said the Eastern Terminal was capable of handling some of the world's largest vessels and could accommodate import containers as well as re-export containers transferred through other terminals.

The automated yard employs approximately 100 workers and operates under international safety standards.

Sri Lanka Ports Authority Additional Managing Director (Operations) Prabath Jayantha Malavige, Director of Operations Pradeep Rohana, Director of Technology G.D.T. Nanayakkara and other senior officials were also present during the inspection.
