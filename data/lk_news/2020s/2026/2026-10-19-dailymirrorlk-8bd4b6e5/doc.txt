Mike Audio appointed as official distributor for Onkyo, Klipsch, Integra

Klipsch Reference loudspeakers and subwoofer paired with Onkyo AV receiver

Three world-renowned home audio names now available locally, backed by a Sri Lankan team with decades of audio experience

Mike Audio (Pvt.) Ltd, a sister company of Yamaha Music Centre Sri Lanka, has been appointed as the official distributor for Onkyo, Klipsch and Integra in Sri Lanka, with the full backing of the brands’ global parent company.

The appointment brings together some of the most respected names in home entertainment.

Klipsch, founded in the United States in 1946, is known worldwide for its horn-loaded loudspeakers and signature dynamic sound.

Onkyo, with a Japanese heritage going back to the same year, has long been a benchmark for AV receivers, home theatre and hi-fi. Integra, its sister brand, rounds out the line-up for custom installations.

The customers can look forward to a wide range, including Klipsch Reference and Reference Premiere loudspeakers, subwoofers, soundbars, tabletop Bluetooth speakers and in-ceiling and in-wall architectural speakers for Dolby Atmos setups, alongside Onkyo AV receivers from entry-level five-channel models up to flagship multi-channel units. For audiophiles, Onkyo’s range of stereo amplifiers and CD players will also be available.

“Sri Lankan music lovers and home cinema enthusiasts have waited a long time for genuine, fully supported access to these brands,” said Mike Audio Director Shane Joachim.

“Our aim is simple: the right product, fair pricing and proper after-sales care, from a team people already know and trust.”

The Onkyo, Klipsch and Integra products will be available through Mike Audio and online at welovemusic.lk. Mike Audio will also work closely with Sri Lanka’s leading audio system integrators to deliver custom-designed home cinemas and multi-room systems for homes, hospitality and commercial projects.

The customers are invited to experience the range at the Mike Audio showroom, 296 Bauddhaloka Mawatha, Colombo 7. Walk-ins are welcome and private demonstrations can also be booked.
