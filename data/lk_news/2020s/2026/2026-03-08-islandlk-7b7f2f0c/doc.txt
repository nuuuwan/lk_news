Maqara emerges as top Colombo dining spot

Maqara Sri Lankan Restaurant, located opposite the World Trade Center, has become a premier culinary destination for both tourists and locals. A subsidiary of the Chinese Dragon Cafe Group, the venue is recognized for its authentic Sri Lankan breakfast and diverse Western options.

Its signature lunch experience allows guests to customize rice and curry plates from over 10 traditional vegetable dishes. Dinner menus feature a blend of Sri Lankan and Chinese cuisine. The restaurant provides free delivery and caters bulk orders for corporate and family events, reinforcing its commitment to quality and convenience under the Chinese Dragon Cafe Group.
