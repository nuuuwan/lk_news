Port access expressway to allow only permitted vehicles from December 1

Colombo, Nov. 28 (Daily Mirror) - The Port Access Expressway will be open exclusively to vehicles holding valid permits from December 1, the Road Development Authority (RDA) announced.

Under the new arrangement, only vehicles authorised to enter the Port of Colombo will be permitted to use the expressway from that date.

The RDA said the restriction would come into effect on December 1.
