Fire brigade rescues dog trapped in drain for over a day in Kandy

Colombo, November 17 (Daily Mirror) - Officers of the Kandy Municipal Council Fire Brigade rescued a dog that had been trapped for more than a day in a narrow drain inside a building near the Channel Centre in Katukale, Kandy.

The rescue operation was carried out on November 16, 2026, after the dog became trapped in the drain running through the building.

Following the rescue, the dog was taken to the Peradeniya Veterinary Teaching Hospital for further treatment.

Appreciation was expressed to the fire brigade officers for their efforts to save the animal's life and to the building owner for allowing the rescue operation to proceed despite damage caused to the property during the operation.
