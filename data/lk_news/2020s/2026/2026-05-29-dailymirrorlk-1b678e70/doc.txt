Special trains to operate for Vesak pilgrims

Colombo, May 29 (Daily Mirror) -  The Railway Department has announced the operation of several special train services on May 30 and 31 to facilitate travel during the Vesak Full Moon Poya holidays.

The department said the special services are being introduced to ease transportation for devotees travelling to Anuradhapura and for members of the public attending the State Vesak Festival at the ancient Raja Maha Vihara in Thihagoda, Midellawela in Matara.

Railway officials said the additional train services will operate throughout the two-day Vesak period in view of the anticipated increase in passenger demand.
