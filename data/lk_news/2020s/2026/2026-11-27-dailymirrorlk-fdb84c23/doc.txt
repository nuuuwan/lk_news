Rupee weakens further against US Dollar

Colombo, Nov. 27 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lankan Rupee recorded a further depreciation against the US Dollar today (27), with the exchange rates moving higher compared to Thursday.

According to the Central Bank of Sri Lanka, the US Dollar buying rate increased to Rs. 328.51 from Rs. 328.25, while the selling rate rose to Rs. 337.44 from Rs. 337.16.
