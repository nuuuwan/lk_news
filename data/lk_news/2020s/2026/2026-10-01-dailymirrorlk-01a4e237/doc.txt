SLFUW Celebrates  85th Anniversary

SLFUW President Eng. Lakmini Nonis, Chief Guest Prof. Emeritus Niranjanie Ratnayake, Council Members & other guests at the anniversary celebrations

The festive commemorative cake was cut jointly by  chief guest Prof. Ratnayake and Dr. Premala Sivaprakasapillai  Sivasegaram, the first female engineer of Sri Lanka

The Sri Lanka Federation of University Women celebrated its historic 85th Anniversary on Saturday September 19, 2026, at its Headquarters on Kithulwatte Road, Colombo. The Auditorium was filled to capacity as SLFUW President

Eng. Lakmini Nonis, welcomed the assemblage, which included distinguished professionals and academics.

Subsequently, Prof. Emeritus Niranjanie Ratnayake, the chief guest, initiated proceedings by lighting the traditional oil lamp.

The festive commemorative cake was cut jointly by  chief guest Prof. Ratnayake and Dr. Premala Sivaprakasapillai  Sivasegaram, the first female engineer of Sri Lanka. Then followed the presentation of eight scholarships to female undergraduates, including two who were visually impaired.

In her address, President Nonis stated that the SLFUW  assisted not only university women, but also school education in  underprivileged areas, with special focus on the socially disadvantaged. Besides, it also helped alleviate poverty, and uplift the lot of the common people. She hoped that this legacy of patriotic service would be carried forward during the next 85 years as well. Finally, she paid tribute to the late Mrs. Clara Motwani, American educationist, who set up the SLFUW in 1941.

The day’s programme included a panel discussion on  ‘Honouring Traditions, and Building a Sustainable Future for the Next Era of the SLFUW.’ The panelists were, Eng. Premala Sivasegaram Ph.D, Ms. Dinali Fernando, Head, Dept. of English, Kelaniya University, and Snr. Prof. Kumudu Perera of the Wayamba University. Compere Ms. Ayeshah Bansajayah  served as Moderator.

Ms. Gnanakanthi Makalanda,  Chairperson of the Membership Committee, spoke on the modernisation of the SLFUW Membership database, and a token presentation of a few Membership Cards was done by Prof. Ratnayake.

After  lunch,  games and a sing-along followed, with gifts for everybody. The participants spent a relaxed, enjoyable day. Concluding the event, SLFUW Vice President Nirmali de Silva presented the Vote of Thanks.
