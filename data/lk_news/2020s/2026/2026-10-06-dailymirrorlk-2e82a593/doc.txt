Leading regional voices to headline Sri Lanka Economic & Investment Summit 2026

Senior business leaders, economists and international development experts, alongside Prime Minister of Sri Lanka Dr. Harini Amarasuriya, will headline Sri Lanka Economic & Investment Summit 2026, organised by The Ceylon Chamber of Commerce on October 12 and 13, 2026, at Shangri-La Colombo.

The summit will open with a keynote address by Reliance Consumer Products Ltd Chief Executive Officer T. Krishnakumar, who brings more than four decades of experience in the consumer brands and FMCG sectors.

Having previously served as President of Coca-Cola India and Southwest Asia, with extensive experience in building businesses and driving growth across emerging markets, Krishnakumar, more popularly known as Kk, now leads Reliance Consumer Products as it expands across categories and builds new customer, partnership and market opportunities.

Day 2 will bring together four international perspectives across discussions on economic resilience, regional connectivity, sector growth and digital transformation.

Asian Development Bank Senior Country Economist for Sri Lanka Lilia Aleksanyan will open the day’s discussions with a focus on Sri Lanka’s economic resilience.The summit will then turn to Sri Lanka’s connections with regional markets, with Standard Chartered Bank Chief Executive Officer India and South Asia P.D. Singh delivering the keynote for the session on trade corridors and value chains.

The Sector Deep Dives will bring international perspectives to discussions on healthcare and energy.

Zydus Lifesciences Ltd Chief Financial Officer Tushar Shroff will deliver the keynote for the healthcare discussion, drawing on more than three decades of experience in corporate finance, strategic investment and financial transformation.

For the energy-focused deep dive, International Finance Corporation Regional Infrastructure Industry Manager for Bangladesh, Sri Lanka and Nepal Edore Onomakpome will bring extensive experience in infrastructure investment and project finance across emerging markets.

The summit will conclude its keynote programme with Dr. Amarasuriya, who will deliver the keynote for ‘Nation Building in the Digital Age’.
