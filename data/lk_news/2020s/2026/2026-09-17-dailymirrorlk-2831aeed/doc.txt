Indices close in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as John Keells Holdings, Melstacorp and R I L Property with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.6 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Sunshine Holdings, John Keells Holdings and Ceylon Cold Stores. Mixed interest was observed in Commercial Bank,

R I L Property and Overseas Realty whilst retail interest was noted in LVL Energy Fund , Lanka Credit and Business Finance and Janashakthi Limited. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

Food, Beverage & Tobacco sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Sunshine Holdings and Ceylon Cold Stores) whilst the sector index edged down by 0.11%. The share price of Sunshine Holdings decreased by Rs 0.20 (0.68%) to close at Rs 29.00. The share price of Ceylon Cold Stores moved up by Rs 1.25 (1.05%) to close at Rs 120.25.

Capital Goods sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to John Keells Holdings) whilst the sector index decreased by 0.87%. The share price of John Keells Holdings lost Rs 0.20 (1.04%) to close at Rs 19.10.

Commercial Bank and John Keells Hotels were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Commercial Bank recorded a loss of Rs 0.50 (0.25%) to close at Rs 203.50. The share price of John Keells Hotels declined by Rs 0.40 (2.44%) to close at Rs 16.00.
