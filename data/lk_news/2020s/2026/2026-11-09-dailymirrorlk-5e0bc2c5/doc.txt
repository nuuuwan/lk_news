Vraie Balthazar should resign or should be removed : Marikkar

Colombo, Nov. 9 (Daily Mirror) - Whilst highlighting that Colombo Mayor Vraie Cally Balthazar has failed to carry out her duty of getting drains of Colombo City cleaned despite prior warnings of heavy rain, opposition MP S. M. Marikar yesterday said she should resign from the post or should be removed.

" Prior warnings were issued by the Department of Meteorology of heavy rains due to El Nino in the months of October or November. The mayor should have got the drains and culverts in Colombo City cleaned on time. Colombo City would not have been flooded on Friday if the drains were cleaned on time. Either the mayor should resign or President Anura Kumara Dissanayake should remove her from her post " MP Marikar who is also a member of the Disaster Management Council said.

" There has been heavier rainfall in the City of Colombo in the past according to the Department of Meteorology. Rainfall over 400mm had been recorded in 1992. However important establishments such as Colombo National Hospital was never affected by floods in the past " he added.

" Jawatte which is the ward represented by the Colombo Mayor was also flooded on Friday" he said.

He said the Colombo Municipal council has miserably failed to do justice to the jewellers in Colombo despite being one of the richest city councils in the region." Colombo Municipal Council did very much better during the time of Mayor Muhammed Uvais Emitiyas who was a three wheeler driver" he said.

Furthermore Marikar alleged that neither the opposition leader of CMC or any opposition member of the council had been invited for the post disaster discussions in Colombo.
