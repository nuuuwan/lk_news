Man swallows gemstone stolen from Ratnapura mine, ends up in hospital

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - A man who allegedly swallowed a gemstone secretly while mining at a gem pit in Ratnapura was admitted to the Kandy National Hospital after it became lodged in his throat.

His condition reportedly worsened, prompting doctors to arrange his transfer to Welisara Hospital to remove the gemstone, a spokesperson for the Kandy National Hospital said.

The man had travelled to Kandy secretly after failing in his attempts to retrieve the gemstone himself. He was admitted to the Kandy National Hospital, hoping that doctors would be able to remove the stone lodged in his throat.

However, the incident was subsequently reported to the hospital police.

Hospital police officers questioned the man and uncovered the circumstances surrounding the incident, including how he had swallowed the gemstone while working at the Ratnapura gem mine.
