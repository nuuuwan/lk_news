Election Commission urges SLFP to resolve general secretary dispute

Colombo, Nov. 10 (Daily Mirror) - The Election Commission has renewed its request for the Sri Lanka Freedom Party (SLFP) to resolve its dispute over the post of general secretary to secure official recognition.

The post of general secretary is key to a political party's official operations, including communicating with election authorities and submitting nomination papers. The SLFP continues to remain a recognised political party.
