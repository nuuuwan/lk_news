SLIM ushers in new leadership era for 2026/27

The Sri Lanka Institute of Marketing (SLIM), the country’s national body for marketing, successfully convened its Annual General Meeting (AGM) 2026 on April 8 at the Galle Face Hotel. The event marked a significant milestone as the Institute formally appointed a new Council of Management and Executive Committee to steer SLIM into its next phase of growth.

Building on a transformative 2025, the newly appointed leadership team consists of an accomplished group of marketing professionals entrusted with roles for the 2026/27 term. The incoming President, a seasoned professional with extensive international business experience, emphasized a strong commitment to strengthening professional standards, expanding global engagement, and equipping Sri Lanka’s marketing fraternity to compete effectively on an international stage.

The forum brought together institute members and industry leaders, underscoring SLIM’s ongoing mission to advance the marketing profession while navigating a rapidly evolving global and domestic market landscape.
