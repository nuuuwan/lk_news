Kusal Mendis elected first President of Sri Lanka Professional Cricketers’ Association

Colombo, Aug. 9 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka limited-overs captain Kusal Mendis has been elected the first President of the newly established Sri Lanka Professional Cricketers’ Association (SLPCA).

The appointment was confirmed at the association’s inaugural general meeting, which was attended by several prominent national cricketers, including Wanindu Hasaranga and Chamari Athapaththu.

The newly formed association aims to provide an independent and collective voice for Sri Lanka’s professional cricketers and advocate for their rights, welfare and professional interests.

The SLPCA is set to be affiliated with the Federation of International Cricketers’ Associations (FICA), the global body representing professional cricketers.

The establishment of the association brings Sri Lanka in line with several leading cricketing nations, including Australia, England and New Zealand, where professional players’ associations have long represented the interests of cricketers.
