Tension at Ruwanweli Maha Seya over attempted Satyagraha

A tense situation was reported near the Ruwanweli Maha Seya in Anuradhapura after a group, including several Buddhist monks, attempted to launch a Satyagraha campaign at the Dansal premises.

Tensions escalated when police officers arrived at the scene and removed the group, including the monks, from the premises.
