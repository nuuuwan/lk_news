New education reforms to introduce 40-minute subject periods

Colombo, Sep. 10 (Daily Mirror) - The new education reforms have proposed introducing 40-minute subject periods as part of changes to the general education system, according to the Education Ministry.

The proposal was discussed at a meeting of the Subcommittee on Education Reforms chaired by Prime Minister and Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya at Parliament on September 9.

The reforms are being implemented for Grade 1 this year, while new programmes for Grades 2 and 6 are scheduled to begin in 2027.

The National Curriculum Framework (NCF) for General Education in Sri Lanka has proposed a range of changes to the school curriculum and assessment system.

According to officials, the proposed framework includes 40-minute subject periods and changes to school-hour arrangements.

The Secretary to the Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education, Nalaka Kaluwewa, presented a progress report on the reforms and future plans at the meeting.

The Ministry has also received the National Curriculum Framework, the Concept Paper for General Education in Sri Lanka (2026), prepared by the National Education Commission, and the School Level Assessment and Certification Framework for General Education.

These documents are scheduled to be submitted to the Cabinet for approval.

The meeting was attended by Deputy Minister of Education and Higher Education Dr. Madura Seneviratne, Commissioner General of Examinations Indika Liyanage, MPs and senior Education Ministry officials.
