HNB Life delivers highest industry performance with 41% GWP growth

HNB Life PLC continued its strong growth trajectory during the nine months ended September 30, 2026, while maintaining a disciplined approach to profitability, investment management and long-term value creation.

The company recorded a gross written premium (GWP) of Rs.19.4 billion, representing a strong 41 percent year-on-year growth, compared with Rs.13.8 billion during the corresponding period of 2025. The net written premium increased by 42 percent to Rs.18.5 billion, reflecting the continued strong momentum of the life insurance business.

Reflecting this strong premium growth, HNB Life’s net income increased by 31 percent to Rs.24.9 billion, compared with Rs.19.0 billion in the corresponding period of 2025. The interest and dividend income also increased by approximately 14 percent to Rs.6.2 billion, contributing positively to the company’s overall financial performance.

HNB Life recorded profit before tax of Rs.862.6 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, while profit after tax stood at Rs.602.3 million, without any surplus transfer from the Life Insurance Fund, which usually happens after the actuarial valuation at the end of the year.

HNB Life continued to strengthen its investment base during the period, with financial investments increasing to Rs.70.2 billion as of September 30, 2026, representing a growth of approximately 12 percent from Rs.62.5 billion at end-2025. The company’s overall financial position continued to expand alongside business growth, with total assets reaching Rs.77.1 billion as of September 30, 2026, an increase of approximately 13 percent from Rs.68.4 billion at end-2025.

Significantly, the Life Insurance Fund expanded by 22 percent to Rs.59.8 billion, from Rs.48.9 billion as of e31 December 2025, further strengthening the company’s long-term financial position.

HNB Life PLC Chairman Stuart Chapman said, “Sri Lanka’s economy has continued to demonstrate resilience, although the inflationary pressures and global uncertainties remain important considerations for businesses and households. Against this backdrop, HNB Life’s performance during the first nine months of 2026 demonstrates the resilience of the company and the strong growth momentum. Our quality investment portfolio reinforces our ability to meet policyholder commitments through changing economic conditions. We remain focused on prudent stewardship and financial strength, positioning HNB Life to support customers’ long-term protection needs while creating sustainable value for the shareholders.”

The company maintained its growth momentum during the third quarter, recording life insurance GWP of Rs.7.0 billion for the three months ended September 30, 2026, an increase of 37 percent, compared with Rs.5.1 billion in the corresponding quarter of 2025.

HNB Life PLC Executive Director/Chief Executive Officer Lasitha Wimalaratne said, “I’m proud of the fact that the growth we’ve recorded in 3Q is the highest in the industry. Our ambition is to build HNB Life into a company that the customers choose not only for the protection we provide today but for the value we bring throughout their lives. Our strategy is centred on strengthening our distribution capabilities and keeping the customers’ evolving needs at the heart of how we grow. For us, success means translating business momentum into lasting customer relationships, with a disciplined focus on profitability rather than growth alone. As we look ahead, we remain committed to building the capabilities that will sustain this ambition and shape HNB Life’s next phase of growth.”

HNB Life has also continued its progress towards the implementation of SLFRS 17 and SLFRS 9, including completing its main actuarial models, finalising the related accounting policies, running the SLFRS 17 results in parallel with the current reporting basis and further strengthening governance, controls and data validation processes.
