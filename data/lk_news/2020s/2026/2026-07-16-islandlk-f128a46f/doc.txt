OPA holds 39th Annual Conference

Established in 1975, the Organisation of Professional Associations of Sri Lanka (OPA) stands as the apex representative body of Sri Lanka’s professional community, serving as a distinguished platform for professional excellence, interdisciplinary collaboration, and national advancement.

As the Organisation enters its second half-century of service, OPA continues to uphold its commitment to advancing professional standards, fostering cross-sectoral knowledge exchange, and strengthening the collective contribution of professionals towards shaping a resilient, innovative, forward-looking Sri Lanka through meaningful professional engagement and thought leadership.

Over the past 51 years, the OPA has established itself as Sri Lanka’s foremost collective platform for professional engagement, bringing together diverse disciplines to contribute to national dialogue and informed decision-making. Through the collective expertise of its member associations, the organisation has played an important role in promoting knowledge sharing, and advancing professional standards across multiple sectors.
