Cabinet approves MoU with India for 100 new Suwa Seriya ambulances

Colombo, Nov. 10 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet of Ministers has approved a proposal to sign a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the Government of India to obtain a grant for the procurement of 100 fully equipped ambulances for the 1990 Suwa Seriya National Emergency Ambulance Service.

The 1990 Suwa Seriya service was launched in 2016 with financial grant assistance from the Indian Government and was expanded nationwide by 2018.

The free emergency ambulance service operates around the clock across all districts, handling between 1,000 and 1,200 emergency patient transportations daily.

The existing fleet of 322 ambulances covers approximately nine million kilometres annually, resulting in increased mechanical defects and wear and tear, according to the Cabinet proposal.

In response to a special request by the Sri Lankan Government, India has agreed to provide financial grant assistance to procure 100 modern ambulances equipped with comprehensive medical facilities.

Accordingly, the Cabinet approved the proposal submitted by the Minister of Health and Mass Media to sign the MoU with the Indian Government to secure the grant assistance.
