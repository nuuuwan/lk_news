280 medical officers seek to migrate so far this year: Deputy Minister

Colombo, Aug. 6 (Daily Mirror) - Deputy Health Minister Dr. Hansaka Wijemuni said some 280 medical officers have applied for foreign no-pay leave so far this year.

The Deputy Minister told Parliament that a total of 1,429 medical officers and 140 Specialist Medical Officers are overseas on no-pay leave from 2020 until June 19, 2026.

Responding to a question by SJB MP Dayasiri Jayasekara, Dr. Wijemuni said that some 3,791 medical officers have migrated from 2020 to 2025.

The Minister said the majority of medical officers have migrated for postgraduate studies.
