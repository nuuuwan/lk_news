Three seawater desalination projects currently under NWSDB

Colombo, Sep. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Housing, Construction and Water Supply Minister Dr. Susil Ranasinghe said three seawater desalination projects are currently being implemented under the National Water Supply and Drainage Board (NWSDB).

He made the remarks in Parliament today (10) while outlining the Government's preparedness to address the prevailing dry weather conditions.

The Minister said the NWSDB currently treats and distributes approximately 3 million cubic metres of water daily across the country.

Of this, around 77,000 cubic metres are obtained from 59 groundwater sources located across the island, while another 712,000 cubic metres are supplied from 59 reservoirs.

The remaining approximately 2.2 million cubic metres required each day are obtained from freshwater sources, including rivers, streams and other sources, he said.

Dr. Ranasinghe said the desalination projects were among the measures being undertaken to strengthen the country's water supply capacity amid changing weather conditions.
