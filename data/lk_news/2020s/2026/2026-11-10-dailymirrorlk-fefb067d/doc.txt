Lorry swept away by floods found trapped in tree

Colombo, Nov. 10 (Daily Mirror) - A lorry swept away by flash floods following the overflow of the Hatton Oya was found trapped in a tree along the riverbank early this morning (10), according to the vehicle's owner.

The incident occurred yesterday afternoon (09) following heavy rainfall in the Hatton area, which caused the Hatton Oya, a major tributary of the Mahaweli River, to swell and sweep away the lorry parked in the area.

Meanwhile, the overflow of the Hatton Oya flooded nearly 50 houses in low-lying areas surrounding Rothes, affected residents said.
