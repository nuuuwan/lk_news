Sri Lankan contingent heads to Cannes Young Lions competition

Eight creative talents will represent Sri Lanka at the prestigious Young Lions Competition in Cannes, France, on June 22nd. Six of the eight team members are from Ogilvy Group Sri Lanka, marking the largest contingent ever fielded by a single agency from the country.

The Young Lions Competition is a globally recognised proving ground for creative talent under 30, bringing national winners from around the world to compete across PR, Media, Digital, Film, Print, and Design categories. Sri Lanka’s cohort will compete against the world’s top young creative minds in this high-pressure global competition.

Ogilvy’s teams will compete across three categories: Sachintha Gunawardena and Hasith Dilakshana in Media, Azraa Saldin and Nirmala Premachandra in PR, and Afridi Hussain and Maveena Manickam in Film. They earned their place following wins at the Young Lions Sri Lanka Competition held in April.

At the competition, teams receive a real-world brief from a non-profit or purpose-led organisation, typically centred on pressing social or environmental challenges. With just 24 hours to respond, they must develop a fully-fledged campaign—crafting insight-led media strategies, compelling PR narratives, or original 60-second films depending on their category.

The teams will work from the dedicated Young Lions Competition Hub at the Cannes Lions School before presenting their campaigns to a jury of senior creatives from leading global agencies. The competition runs alongside the Cannes Lions International Festival of Creativity, the world’s foremost gathering for the creative communications industry.
