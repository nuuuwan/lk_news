Udhayanidhi’s ’double-meaning’ remark allegedly on actor Trisha courts controversy

August 04 - Leader of Opposition in Tamil Nadu Assembly, Udhyanidhi Stalin courted controversy on Monday over his offensive remark, alluding to actor Trisha, Indian media reported.

Udhayanidhi took a jibe at Tamil Nadu Chief Minister C Joseph Vijay saying the that state has not received a single drop of Cauvery water while the CM is unconcerned.

During his speech, someone in the crowd chanted, "Trisha, Trisha", after which pausing his speech, Udhayanidhi with a smirk on his face responded with an "offensive double entendre", a remark that has since sparked severe political backlash, PTI reported.

Following his remarks, Tamil Nadu BJP chief spokesperson Narayanan Thirupathy said Udhayanidhi's "double-meaning" remark is "disgusting, obscene, vulgar and shameful".

"Truly, even a rogue or thief who has a mother, wife, and daughter would not speak so vulgarly. No mother, wife, or daughter would forgive those who speak like that. But it is shameful that former Chief Minister M. K. Stalin watches these Nonsense while these wicked people, who call themselves the “Dravidian Model,” speak this way in public. Arresting and imprisoning Udayanidhi Stalin for this low-grade speech would bring more credit to Tamil Nadu Chief Minister Joseph Vijay (TVK Vijay) than arresting Markandeyan. Let us see whether the courts punish this speech or grant bail," he wrote on X.

Additionally, MLA Rhevanth Charan also took to his X account and called out Udhayanidhi's remarks.

"Absolutely disgusting. Passing such disgraceful comments against anyone is completely unacceptable. It exposes the mindset, political culture, and declining standards of the Arivalayam clan. Your announced protest collapsed even before it could begin. Now, desperate for attention, you have resorted to cheap provocation and engagement farming," he wrote in a post.
