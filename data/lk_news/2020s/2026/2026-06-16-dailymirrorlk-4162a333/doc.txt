Deepal Sri Lanka unveils S05 REEV; announces Changan’s NEVO Brand arrival

Prime EV Automobiles (Pvt) Ltd, a member of the Senok Group and the authorized distributor of Deepal vehicles in Sri Lanka, unveiled the all-new Deepal S05 REEV at its flagship 3S showroom in Colombo recently.

The launch event was attended by senior officials of the Senok Group, valued customers, media representatives, banking and finance partners, and distinguished guests.

The launch marks another significant milestone in Sri Lanka’s rapidly evolving new energy vehicle landscape, introducing a revolutionary mobility solution that combines electric driving with exceptional long-distance capability.

The Deepal S05 REEV (Range Extended Electric Vehicle) represents the next generation of intelligent mobility. Designed to address one of the key concerns of Sri Lankan motorists—long-distance travel—the S05 REEV offers the benefits of electric vehicle driving while providing an impressive combined driving range of over 1,200 km, ensuring complete peace of mind for both urban and intercity journeys.

Powered by Changan Automobile’s advanced REEV technology, the vehicle delivers outstanding fuel efficiency, intelligent energy management, premium comfort, cutting-edge safety systems, and a futuristic driving experience that aligns perfectly with the needs of modern Sri Lankan consumers.

A special highlight of the event was the ceremonial handover of the first Deepal S05 REEV vehicles to pioneering customers, who became the first owners of this groundbreaking model in Sri Lanka.

Speaking at the event, company representatives highlighted that Sri Lankan consumers are increasingly embracing advanced new energy vehicle technologies that offer both sustainability and practicality. The Deepal S05 REEV has been specifically introduced to meet this growing demand by providing an ideal balance between electric mobility and extended travel freedom.

Prime EV Automobiles also announced that it has secured the rights to introduce NEVO, Changan’s latest new energy vehicle brand, to Sri Lanka. The company confirmed that the highly anticipated NEVO Q05 will be launched in the local market in the near future, further expanding the range of innovative mobility solutions available to Sri Lankan customers.

Backed by Senok Group, which has over 45 years of automotive industry experience in Sri Lanka, Prime EV Automobiles continues to set new benchmarks in customer satisfaction and after-sales excellence. During its first year of operations, Prime EV has earned widespread recognition for delivering exceptional after-sales support, professional technical expertise, genuine spare parts availability, and a customer-centric ownership experience.
