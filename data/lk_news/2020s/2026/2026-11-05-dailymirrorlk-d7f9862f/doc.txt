Pregnant women beyond 36 weeks from Jaffna’s islands advised to stay in urban areas

Colombo, Nov. 5 (Daily Mirror) - Pregnant women beyond 36 weeks of gestation residing in the island areas of Jaffna have been advised to stay in Jaffna town.

The advisory was issued during a preparedness meeting held yesterday at the District Secretariat, chaired by District Secretary Maruthalingam Pratheeban, to discuss measures to address potential disasters caused by the Northeast monsoon.

The meeting extensively discussed strategies to deal with monsoon-related disasters, reduce disaster risks, and protect the public.

With Jaffna expected to receive 331 mm of rainfall this month, the Regional Director of Health Services advised pregnant women beyond 36 weeks of gestation living in islands such as Delft, Analaitivu and Nainativu to stay with relatives in Jaffna town or at Kayts Hospital.

The recommendation was made in view of the risks associated with sea travel during adverse weather conditions, ensuring that these women can safely undergo childbirth during the disaster period.
