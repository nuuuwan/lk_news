President proposes unified defence research centre

Colombo, Aug. 17 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake has proposed establishing a single national research and development centre by integrating the existing research and development institutions operated by the Sri Lanka Army, Navy and Air Force, noting that such a move would enable the country to achieve its objectives more efficiently and effectively.

The President made these remarks while participating in the 2027 pre-budget discussion on the Ministry of Defence, held at the Presidential Secretariat this morning, the President’s Media Division (PMD) said.

During the meeting, extensive discussions were held on the need for the defence sector to rapidly adapt to modern technology and make greater use of advanced technological equipment to strengthen national security and address emerging global challenges.

The discussion also focused on increasing allocations for overseas training programmes to enhance the professional skills of defence personnel and provide them with opportunities to acquire new knowledge and expertise.

Attention was also drawn to the operations of the General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University Hospital and the planned commencement of operations at Neville Fernando Hospital. Discussions covered the allocation of necessary funds and future plans for the two institutions.

The meeting also focused on a programme aimed at encouraging defence personnel to participate in the construction sector through their labour contribution.
