NITF and CECB Sign MoU to Implement AGRAHARA Medical Insurance for CECB Staff

The National Insurance Trust Fund (NITF) and the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau (CECB) have formalized a significant partnership aimed at strengthening employee welfare, with the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to provide the semi-government AGRAHARA Medical Insurance Scheme to permanent staff members of CECB.

The MoU was exchanged between Samil Thushara, Assistant General Manager – Operations (NITF) and S. A. U. D. C. Siriwardhana, General Manager (CECB), in the presence of senior officials from both institutions. Representing NITF were Nuwan Dissanayake, Assistant Manager – Marketing, and Sajani Iddagoda, while CECB was represented by S. Wijesinghe, Corporate AGM (Projects), S. S. Aravinda Kalugaldeniya, Corporate AGM (Designs), and W. C. S. Gunarathne, Manager – Administration & Human Resources.

Under this agreement, permanent employees of CECB will be covered by the AGRAHARA Medical Insurance Scheme, one of Sri Lanka’s most trusted and widely recognized health insurance solutions for the public and semi-government sectors.
