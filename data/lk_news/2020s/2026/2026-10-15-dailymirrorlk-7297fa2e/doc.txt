Seventeen students, one teacher killed in school roof collapse in Pakistan

Reuters - At least 17 students, all ​of them below ‌the age of 13, and one teacher were ​killed in ​a school roof collapse ⁠in Pakistan's Peshawar ​city, a local ​hospital spokesperson said on Thursday.

The students and the ​teacher were ​brought dead to the Lady ‌Reading ⁠Hospital along with more than a dozen people who were injured ​in ​the ⁠incident, hospital spokesperson Mohammad Asim ​said.
