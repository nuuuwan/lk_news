Uber-Hayleys Mobility accelerate NEV adoption

Exclusive pricing and financing options on electric cars to help Uber drivers switch to cleaner, more affordable mobility

Uber and Hayleys Mobility announced an exclusive partnership to make electric vehicles (EVs) more accessible and affordable for the Uber drivers.

The collaboration brings together Uber’s ride-hailing technology and driver network with Hayleys Mobility’s new energy vehicle (NEV) portfolio and EV charging ecosystem, helping the drivers transition to electric mobility while reducing their everyday operating costs.

As part of the partnership, the eligible Uber drivers will have access to the Kaiyi eVibe e04electric car, at an exclusive discounted price, making the transition easier.

Hayleys Mobility will also facilitate leasing options through its network of over 20 financing partners, helping the drivers overcome one of the biggest barriers to EV ownership - the upfront cost of purchasing a vehicle.

The offer will be available through Uber’s driver app, allowing the eligible drivers to discover the vehicle and explore purchase and financing options directly through the Uber app.

Uber Sri Lanka Country Manager Mobility Kaushalya Gunaratne said, “For the drivers, the switch to EVs needs to make economic sense, not just environmental sense. By making EVs more affordable and accessible, our partnership with Hayleys Mobility can help the drivers lower their operating costs, retain more of their earnings and contribute to cleaner mobility in Sri Lanka. We are confident that this encourages more drivers to make the switch.”

Hayleys Fentons Managing Director Hasith Prematillake said, “The brand promise of Hayleys Mobility, ‘One Brand for Every Journey’ extends well beyond the vehicle. It is about being accessible to our customers, delivering dependable service and building relationships that last. As we welcome the Uber drivers to Hayleys Mobility, we want every interaction to give them confidence in the people and expertise behind their vehicle.”

Hayleys Mobility Executive Director Roshani Dharmaratne said, “Our islandwide dealer network brings us closer to our customers, from their first conversation with us to their after-sales needs. For the Uber drivers, time on the road matters, so responsive service, clear advice and convenient access to support are central to the experience we aim to deliver.”

The collaboration also brings together Hayleys Mobility’s growing EV portfolio and its StarCharge charging infrastructure ecosystem, supporting the development of a more accessible electric mobility network.
