Minister seeks report on death of three-month-old infant at Welikada Prison

Colombo, Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Minister of Women and Child Affairs Saroja Savithri Paulraj has called for a detailed report on the death of a three-month-old infant belonging to an inmate at the women’s section of Welikada Prison.

The infant reportedly died after breast milk entered the wrong airway, causing aspiration.

The Minister has taken steps to obtain the report through the Commissioner General of Prisons to examine the circumstances surrounding the incident.

Meanwhile, the Sri Lanka Women’s Bureau and the Department of Probation and Child Care Services have been instructed to intervene in the matter and take necessary action.

The Minister has also directed the relevant institutions to introduce measures to minimise the risk of similar incidents occurring in the future.
