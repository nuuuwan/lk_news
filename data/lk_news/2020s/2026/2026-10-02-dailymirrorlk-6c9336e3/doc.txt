HMD and Dialog Axiata partner to introduce new 4G Feature Phone range to Sri Lanka

HMD (Human Mobile Devices), the leading European mobile phone manufacturer and maker of Nokia phones, has partnered with Dialog Axiata PLC, to officially introduce a new range of affordable 4G feature phones to Sri Lanka, including the HMD 101 4G and HMD 102 4G.

Combining the legendary durability and trust of Nokia’s hardware with Dialog’s nationwide coverage, this strategic initiative supports the country’s transition from legacy 2G networks to modern 4G LTE while prioritizing an upgraded, superior connectivity experience for local consumers.

The feature phone segment remains a vital component of Sri Lanka’s mobile landscape, serving as an indispensable primary tool for daily communication in many households and a trusted, long-lasting secondary backup device for professionals and travellers seeking a digital detox.

While migrating users from 2G to 4G is a core network objective, the focus of this partnership centres directly on elevating what local consumers value most: clear HD voice through VoLTE call quality, stronger reception, modern USB Type-C charging convenience, rugged IP52 splash resistance, and a long battery life without requiring an expensive or complex smartphone. Leading the new 4G offering, the HMD 101 4G is engineered for maximum everyday reliability and communication efficiency. It features a clear 2.0-inch display, dual SIM 4G VoLTE connectivity, a modern USB Type-C charging port, an MP3 player, wireless FM radio, and a powerful dual LED torch.

Complementing this, the HMD 102 4G brings a touch of modern style to the segment, combining all core 4G features with a sleek rounded design, a smartphone-inspired camera module with rear camera and LED flash, auto call recording, and practical daily utilities making high-quality 4G hardware accessible across every budget.

Both devices feature a removable 1,000 mAh battery, offering up to 8 hours of talk time and up to 625 hours of standby time. Vivek Khandelwal, General Manager, Emerging Asia, HMD, said, “We intend to be in Sri Lanka for the long term, and that means competing at every price point rather than justthe ones that make headlines.

The 101 4G and 102 4G represent the threshold of the 4G feature phoneportfolio and are held to the same standard as everything above them. Furthermore, Dialog Axiata puts them within the reach of customers in every part of the country.” Gayan Wijethilaka, Country Head for Sri Lanka, Nepal, and Singapore at HMD, said, “Customers choose these phones for the same reasons they always have; they are simple, durable, and built to last.

With the addition of 4G, calls are crystal clear, ensuring the device remains reliable for years to come.” Lim Li San, Group Chief Operating Officer of Dialog Axiata PLC, said, “As Sri Lanka transitions to newer network technologies, we believe better connectivity should be within reach of customers from all walks of life.

Through our partnership with HMD, we are making 4G more affordable and accessible through smart device options, in line with our commitment to help more Sri Lankans enjoy clearer calls and reliable connectivity every day.”
