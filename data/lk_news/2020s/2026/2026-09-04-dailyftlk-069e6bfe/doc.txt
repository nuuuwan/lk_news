Namal summoned to appear before CIABOC today

Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) National Organiser and Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa has been summoned to appear before the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) today.

Rajapaksa’s Attorney Muditha R. Lokumudali said CIABOC had formally notified his client in writing to appear before the Commission at 9 a.m.

Lokumudali said Rajapaksa had agreed to comply with the summons and would appear before the Commission as requested.
