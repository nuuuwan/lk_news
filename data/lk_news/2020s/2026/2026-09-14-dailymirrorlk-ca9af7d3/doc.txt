Sri Lanka, Thailand sign landmark agreements to strengthen labour cooperation

Sri Lanka and Thailand today signed two landmark agreements aimed at strengthening labour cooperation and creating structured employment opportunities for Sri Lankan workers in Thailand.

The Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on Labour Cooperation and the Memorandum of Agreement (MoA) on the Employment of Sri Lankan Workers in Thailand were signed by Sri Lankan Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Minister Vijitha Herath and Thai Labour Minister Julapun Amornvivat.

The agreements establish a transparent and structured framework for the recruitment and employment of Sri Lankan workers while ensuring their rights, welfare and protection.

The Thai Cabinet has approved the recruitment of an initial 10,000 Sri Lankan workers under the new legal framework to help address labour shortages in Thailand.

The MoU provides for cooperation in areas including labour policies and practices, labour market information, skills development, labour productivity and labour protection, as well as measures to prevent illegal recruitment and human trafficking.

The MoA sets out practical arrangements covering recruitment procedures, employment contracts, legal protection, welfare, visas and work permits, and return and repatriation of workers.

Minister Herath welcomed the opportunity for Sri Lankan workers to seek employment in Thailand and stressed the importance of protecting their rights, welfare and social security.

He also called for the establishment of a Joint Working Group to monitor and review the implementation of the agreements. He further requested Thailand to consider expanding employment opportunities for Sri Lankan youth, particularly in the information technology and tourism sectors.

Minister Julapun said Sri Lankan workers recruited under the framework would enjoy equal rights and protection under Thai labour law, including legally mandated wages, benefits and healthcare.

The agreements also provide a basis for broader cooperation to strengthen the workforces of both countries in line with future labour market and economic developments.

The Sri Lankan Government considers Thailand and other Southeast Asian countries as emerging destinations for Sri Lankan workers as it seeks to diversify foreign employment markets.

The agreements are expected to expand opportunities for skilled workers and professionals, particularly youth, while further strengthening the longstanding relations between Sri Lanka and Thailand.
