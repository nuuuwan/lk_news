IT Gallery Computers excels at ‘Lenovo 360 Accelerate 2026’ regional summit

IT Gallery Computers (Pvt) Ltd.has been honoured with the ‘Distributor Excellence Award 2025–2026 | Gaming & Visual Sri Lanka’ at the Lenovo 360 Accelerate 2026, marking a significant milestone in its continued partnership with Lenovo and its contribution to the growth of the Gaming & Visual business segment in Sri Lanka.

The recognition underscores IT Gallery Computers (Pvt) Ltd. as a key driving force in expanding Lenovo’s presence in the local market, particularly within high-growth categories such as gaming, creator-focused devices, and visual computing solutions.

The award celebrates the company’s sustained efforts in strengthening channel partnerships, developing the market, and building long-term customer trust across Sri Lanka’s ICT ecosystem.

Over the years, IT Gallery Computers (Pvt) Ltd. has played a pivotal role in enabling wider access to premium Lenovo technologies through a strong nationwide reseller network. Its consistent investment in partner development, brand engagement activities, and customer-centric initiatives has helped accelerate category awareness and adoption across gaming enthusiasts, creative professionals, educational institutions, and corporate users.

IT Gallery Computers (Pvt) Ltd.  also continues to strengthen Lenovo’s Gaming & Visual portfolio in Sri Lanka through product lines such as Lenovo Legion and LOQ gaming devices, Lenovo Legion gaming monitors, and Lenovo ThinkVision monitors for creators and professionals.

For consumers, the company offers an extensive range of Lenovo monitors and display solutions, with sizes ranging from 16-inch to 49-inch models, including Full HD 1080P, 4K, OLED, and curved display options tailored to suit a variety of user needs and market segments.

Dilantha Perera, Founder and Chief Executive Officer, IT Gallery Computers (Pvt) Ltd, expressed gratitude and reaffirmed the company’s forward-looking vision he said, “This recognition is not only an award for IT Gallery, but a shared achievement with our valued partners, customers, and the Lenovo team. We remain committed to driving innovation, empowering the gaming and creator ecosystem, and bringing world-class technology experiences to Sri Lanka. Together, we will continue building the future of gaming, AI, and visual technologies in the country.”

Looking ahead, IT Gallery Computers (Pvt) Ltd. aims to further deepen its partner engagement, expand category growth, and support the development of immersive technology experiences for Sri Lankan consumers and businesses alike.
