SCL on imported sprats and Maldive fish slashed to Rs. 10 per kilo

Colombo, Nov. 12 (Daily Mirror) - The Special Commodity Levy (SCL) on imported sprats and Maldive fish will be reduced to Rs. 10 per kilogram from November 13, 2026, as part of measures to ease the cost-of-living burden on consumers, President Anura Kumara Dissanayake announced in the 2027 Budget.

Accordingly, the levy on imported sprats will be reduced from Rs. 100 to Rs. 10 per kilogram, while the levy on Maldive fish will be slashed from Rs. 302 to Rs. 10 per kilogram.

The SCL on both powdered and unpowdered dried chillies will also be reduced by Rs. 50 per kilogram.

The tax revisions are aimed at easing the impact of rising prices of essential food items on consumers and will take effect from November 13, 2026.
