Opposition launches budget tracking website

Colombo, Nov. 11 (Daily Mirror) - The Opposition has launched a new website, oversight.lk, aimed at keeping citizens informed by scrutinizing the government’s financial performance across its two previous budgets as well as the upcoming budget.

The newly unveiled platform features progress trackers for past budgets, detailed ministry-by-ministry analyses, audit query reports concerning state institutions, and analytical data breaking down government revenue sources and expenditure.

The launch comes just ahead of the President’s Budget Speech scheduled for tomorrow.
