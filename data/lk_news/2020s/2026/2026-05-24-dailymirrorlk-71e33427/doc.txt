E. B. Creasy – BIC and DENTA Factory wins Women-Led Sustainability Awards

E. B. Creasy & Co., PLC – BIC & DENTA Factory was recently recognised as a Winner at Women-Led Sustainability Projects Appreciation Awards 2026.

Presented under the Responsible Care Initiative at The Barnhouse, Panadura, the award celebrates the company’s ‘Awesi’ project, as one of the year’s top 10 women-led projects, distinguished for its proven impact on safety, innovation and workplace sustainability.

Led by Executive Quality Assurance Shanika Amarasekara and Executive Energy and Sustainability Thilakshi Navodya, the Awesi project was recognised for its role as a significant and replicable model for inclusive industrial development.

Organised by the Lanka Responsible Care Council, the awards recognise organisations that demonstrate safe, responsible and sustainable practices within the chemical sector.

This achievement further reaffirms E. B. Creasy & Co., PLC – BIC and Denta Factory’s continued commitment towards improving health, safety and workplace sustainability.
