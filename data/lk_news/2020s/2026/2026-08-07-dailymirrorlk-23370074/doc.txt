Badulla reservoirs spill, downstream warning issued

Colombo, Aug. 7 (Daily Mirror) - The Irrigation Department has warned people living in downstream areas of reservoirs and waterways to remain vigilant as two reservoirs in the Badulla district are currently spilling.

According to the Water Management Division of the Irrigation Department, as of 12.30 p.m. today (07), the Ambewela Reservoir in the Badulla division has reached spilling status, with two spill outlets opened, releasing water into the Mahaweli Ganga.

The Bomuruella Medium Reservoir, also located in the Badulla division, is also spilling, with three spill outlets opened.

The Irrigation Department urged residents and users of downstream areas to closely monitor changes in river water levels and strictly follow instructions issued by the department and local authorities regarding reservoir spilling and gate openings.

Meanwhile, the overall storage level of major reservoirs under the Irrigation Department stands at 38.7%, with 1,150,032 acre-feet of water currently stored across 73 reservoirs.
