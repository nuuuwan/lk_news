Sri Lanka, Bhutan discuss closer cooperation in education

Colombo, Sep. 4 (Daily Mirror) - Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya held bilateral talks with Bhutanese Minister for Education and Skills Development Yeezang De Thapa on September 3, focusing on strengthening cooperation between Sri Lanka and Bhutan in education and vocational training.

The meeting took place on the sidelines of the inaugural Global Conscious Food Systems Summit 2026, being held in Bhutan from August 31 to September 4.

The discussions focused on expanding Government-to-Government cooperation and creating new opportunities for students, educators and academic institutions in both countries.

Both sides agreed to initiate discussions towards finalising and signing a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) on cooperation in education and vocational training. They also agreed to consider establishing a Secretary-level Joint Working Group to oversee the implementation of the MoU, review progress annually and identify new areas of cooperation.

Areas discussed included university and institutional partnerships, academic and faculty exchanges, joint research and university programmes, teacher training and capacity building, and the sharing of expertise and best practices.

The two sides also discussed child protection and safeguarding, promoting ethical values among students, and measures to address violence against children while ensuring their safety and access to quality education.

Opportunities for Bhutanese students to pursue undergraduate studies in Sri Lanka, particularly in Dentistry, Medicine (MBBS) and allied health fields such as Physiotherapy, were also discussed. Opportunities for postgraduate studies in medical and health sciences were also explored.

The meeting was held with the aim of advancing long-term cooperation between Sri Lanka and Bhutan in education and vocational training.
