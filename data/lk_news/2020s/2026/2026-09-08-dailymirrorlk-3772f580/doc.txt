INS Udaygiri makes port call in Colombo

Colombo, Sep. 8 (Daily Mirror) - Indian Naval Ship (INS) Udaygiri arrived at the Port of Colombo today, September 8, 2026, on an official visit to Sri Lanka. The vessel was received by the Sri Lanka Navy in accordance with longstanding naval traditions.

The 149.02-metre-long frigate is commanded by Captain Vikas Sood. During the visit, Captain Sood is scheduled to call on Commander Western Naval Area Rear Admiral Harsha De Silva at the Western Naval Command Headquarters.

Captain Sood previously served as Defence Adviser at the High Commission of India in Sri Lanka from July 2020 to October 2023.

During its stay in Sri Lanka, the crew of INS Udaygiri will take part in a series of goodwill activities organised by the Sri Lanka Navy, aimed at strengthening camaraderie and enhancing relations between the two naval forces.

The visiting personnel are also scheduled to tour several prominent tourist and cultural sites across the island.
