Court of Appeal defers order on Sallay’s writ against PTA detention

The Court of Appeal yesterday deferred until 28 January, 2027 its decision on whether to proceed with a writ application filed by former State Intelligence Service (SIS) Director Major General (Retd.) Suresh Sallay, challenging his arrest and detention under the Prevention of Terrorism Act (PTA) in connection with investigations into the Easter Sunday attacks.

The application was scheduled to be taken up for order yesterday. However, Court of Appeal Justice Priyantha Fernando, a member of the bench, announced that the order would instead be delivered on 28 January.

Seeking urgent judicial intervention, Sallay is asking for interim relief to suspend the detention order issued against him under the PTA. He has also sought an order staying further proceedings before the Colombo Fort Magistrate’s Court in connection with the investigations.

The petition names several high-ranking officials as respondents, including Inspector General of Police Priyantha Weerasuriya, Criminal Investigation Department (CID) Director Shani Abeysekera, the Colombo Fort Magistrate and the Attorney General.
