SDB bank launches Fixed Deposit Campaign offering attractive rates

SDB bank announced the launch of its highly anticipated Fixed Deposit Campaign offering one of the best interest rates in the market, reaching up to 11.25 percent per annum for selected short to medium tenures. This campaign is a strategic initiative aimed at attracting new customers and enhancing deposit mobilization in the face of rising market interest rates and increased competition.

The FD Campaign is designed to strengthen SDB bank’s competitive position and provide existing and new customers with an opportunity to secure superior returns on their deposits. The fixed deposit tenures range from 120 to 390 days, with the campaign offering an attractive proposition for rate-sensitive customers, including salaried professionals, retirees, and business owners looking for stable investment opportunities.

The launch of this time-bound campaign reflects SDB bank’s commitment to providing customers with high-yield financial solutions in a dynamic market environment, extending an opportunity for customers to enjoy greater return.

Chitral De Silva, Chief Business Officer of SDB bank said, “As a bank that focuses on empowering progressive individuals, we are excited to offer this special FD rate. This initiative aligns with our focus on offering secure and high-return financial solutions that cater to the evolving needs of our customers. By providing these competitive rates, we aim to deliver more value to our customers and further strengthen our position as a trusted partner in their financial journey.”

SDB bank’s FD Campaign also provides customers with an opportunity to enhance their returns by locking in their deposits at an attractive rate.

SDB bank continues to remain committed to providing practical and accessible financial solutions to its customers, offering a reliable and regulated banking environment for those looking to secure their financial future.
