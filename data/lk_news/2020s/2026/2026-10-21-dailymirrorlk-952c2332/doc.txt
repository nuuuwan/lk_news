Displaying names on vehicle number plates

According to Daily Mirror report, the government is considering displaying vehicle owners› names on number plates. The proposal was discussed at the Consultative Committee on Public Transport. The objective is to increase government revenue and cater to the desires of vehicle owners through personalized number plates. The statement also notes that legal and security aspects are being considered.

There is a backlog of 958,651 in number plates and 673,000 in driving licenses due to card scarcity and lack of printing machines, which is expected to be sorted by mid-2027.

This proposal seems to be inspired by personalized plates in countries like the UK and Dubai, but implementing it in Sri Lanka without fixing the basic system first is putting the cart before the horse. While it can be a good revenue idea if done as an optional premium service, making it mandatory to display owners’ names is a serious mistake that risks privacy and security.

However there are various plus points to consider such as selling personalized plates at a premium price, easy identification and prestige and deterrence of theft and fraud. But it also raise concerns about serious privacy and security risks, loopholes in existing system would lead to more corruption, delays and backlog.

In short, as an “optional, paid, premium choice” it is okay, but as a mandatory rule it is dangerous and impractical.
