Rupee weakens slightly against US Dollar

Colombo, Nov.9 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lankan Rupee has depreciated slightly against the US Dollar today (09), compared to last Friday, according to the Central Bank of Sri Lanka.

The Central Bank's daily exchange rates show that the US Dollar's buying rate has increased from Rs. 328.05 to Rs. 328.09, while the selling rate has risen from Rs. 337.00 to Rs. 337.05.

At Commercial Bank, the buying rate increased from Rs. 326.38 to Rs. 326.58, while its selling rate has risen from Rs. 336.30 to Rs. 336.50.

At People’s Bank, the buying rate has increased from Rs. 328.35 to Rs. 328.48, while the selling rate has moved up from Rs. 336.49 to Rs. 336.59.
