Container carrying Rs. 600 million worth of illegal cigarettes seized in Sedawatta

Colombo, Nov. 11 (Daily Mirror) - Police have seized a container carrying approximately 5.7 million illegal cigarettes worth more than Rs. 600 million during a raid at a private container yard in Sedawatta.

The raid was carried out on November 10 by officers of the Western Province North Crimes Division.

The container, which had been imported from Dubai, was found to contain cigarettes of the Manchester brand, with an estimated market value exceeding Rs. 600 million.

Two suspects, residents of Ja-Ela and Katugastota, were arrested in connection with the incident. They were scheduled to be produced before the Maligakanda Magistrate’s Court yesterday (11).

The raid was conducted by Investigation Team II of the Western Province North Crimes Division, comprising Police Sergeant 73086 Anuradha and Police Constables 11713 Anuradha, 26536 Nimal, 100486 Nuwaranga and 9055 Sanjeewa.

The operation was carried out under the guidance of Division Director Assistant Superintendent of Police Indika Dharmaratne and the supervision of Police Inspector Pathum Anjana. Pic by Nimalsiri Edirisinhe
