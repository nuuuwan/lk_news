Mitsubishi seven-seater vehicle range places high emphasis on road safety and passenger protection

From 19 September 2026, every passenger travelling on Sri Lanka’s expressways is required to wear a seat belt, reinforcing the importance of passenger safety for motorists across the country.

Mitsubishi’s seven-seater vehicle range addresses this need by providing seven seats, each equipped with a three-point Emergency Locking Retractor (ELR) seat belt, allowing families to travel together without compromising passenger safety.

The Mitsubishi Xpander, Mitsubishi Xpander Cross and Mitsubishi Destinator are seven-seater MUVs and SUVs designed to accommodate the travel needs of Sri Lankan families, combining passenger protection with comfort and practicality.

Each of these models offers seven designated seating positions with individual three-point ELR seat belts, enabling passengers across all three rows to travel with appropriate restraint systems. This is particularly relevant under the expressway regulations, where seat belt compliance extends to every occupant, including those seated in the second and third rows.

Safety remains central to Mitsubishi’s engineering philosophy. Built on the renowned RISE (Reinforced Impact Safety Evolution) body structure, the vehicles incorporate a range of safety technologies, including ABS with EBD, Active Stability Control, Traction Control, Hill Start Assist, rear-view camera with parking sensors, ISOFIX child seat anchors and airbags, including side and curtain airbags on selected variants. Combined with high ground clearance of up to 225 mm, Mitsubishi’s seven-seater range is designed to support confident driving across varying Sri Lankan road conditions. As greater emphasis is placed on road safety and passenger protection, the availability of seven individual seats with dedicated seat belts offers a practical consideration for families selecting a vehicle. The Mitsubishi Xpander, Xpander Cross and Destinator reflect this focus, bringing together seating capacity, safety features and everyday functionality for family travel.

The Mitsubishi seven-seater range is available through United Motors Lanka PLC’s showroom network.
