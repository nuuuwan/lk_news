Hilton Colombo brings flavours of Việt  Nam to Graze Kitchen

This August, Hilton Colombo presents the second edition of its widely popular ‘Flavors of Việt Nam,’ a 10-day culinary showcase that will celebrate the vibrant gastronomic traditions of Việt Nam.

Taking place from 20 – 29 August in collaboration with Hilton Saigon, this immersive gourmet experience will encompass authentic Vietnamese recipes, live cooking stations, and regional specialties served alongside Graze Kitchen’s signature dinner buffet.

Led by guest chefs Nam and Van from Hilton Saigon, the event takes diners on a nightly journey through Vietnam’s diverse culinary regions, with dishes shaped by traditional techniques and bold, balanced flavours.

The showcase will feature Bánh Mì, layered with crisp pickled vegetables, fresh basil and coriander; steaming bowls of Vietnamese Pho, served with beef or chicken and accompanied by fresh herbs among other authentic dishes. Guests can also indulge in Bánh Xèo, a Vietnamese pancake, along with delicate fresh spring rolls and charcoal-grilled specialties. Traditional favorites such as fragrant Vietnamese coffee jelly and mango sticky rice bring a distinctly Vietnamese touch to the dessert selection.

Commenting on the collaboration, Manesh Fernando, General Manager of Hilton Colombo said, “Food has a unique way of bringing cultures together. Through our collaboration with Hilton Saigon, we are honored to share the flavors and traditions of Việt Nam with our guests, bringing a taste of this remarkable culinary destination to Colombo for the second time.”
