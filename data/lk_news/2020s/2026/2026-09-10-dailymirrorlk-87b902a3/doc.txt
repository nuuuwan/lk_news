Mixed sentiment persists; yield curve remains anchored

The investor sentiment remained mixed, prompting the yield curve to stay broadly anchored while trading volumes appeared moderate.

The PDMO concluded its weekly T-bill auction raising a total of Rs.80.0 billion, on par with the initial offer.

The weighted average yield of the three-month bill edged higher by 7bps and stood at 9.03 percent while that of the six-month and 12-month bills eased by 3bps and 4bps, respectively, to stand at 9.24 percent and 9.77 percent.

In the 2028 segment, 15.03.2028, 01.07.2028, 15.10.2028 and 15.12.2028 traded between 10.10 percent and 10.20 percent. Further along the curve 15.06.2029 and 15.12.2029 traded at 10.40 percent and 10.50 percent, respectively while 01.08.2030 changed hands at 10.70 percent.

Moreover, 01.02.3031 traded at 10.80 percent while 01.11.2033 was seen trading between 11.80 percent and 11.76 percent. Finally, 15.10.2034 traded at 11.90 percent while 15.08.2036 traded higher at 11.93 percent.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee depreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.328.77/US dollar, compared to Rs.328.21/US dollar seen earlier. Market liquidity in the banking system remained broadly stable at Rs.363.14 billion, compared to Rs.363.31 billion recorded previously.
