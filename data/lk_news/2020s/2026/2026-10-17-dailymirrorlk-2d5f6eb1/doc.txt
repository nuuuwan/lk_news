Seylan Developments hands over RO drinking water purification plant to Kalyanipura Vidyalaya

Rs.2.20mn CSR initiative provides sustainable access to safe drinking water for children and surrounding community

Seylan Developments PLC marked the World Children’s Day on October 1, 2026, by handing over a newly installed reverse osmosis (RO) drinking water purification plant, valued at approximately Rs.2.20 million, to Mu/Kalyanipura Vidyalaya in Welioya, Mullaitivu District.

Expected to reduce the school’s dependence on externally purchased drinking water, the new RO plant is set to provide a reliable and consistent source of purified water for students, staff and the surrounding community.

Resolving the difficulties faced by the school in obtaining a consistent supply of purified drinking water, the installation of the RO plant emphasises the right of consistent access to safe drinking water, the health and well-being.

Undertaken, in collaboration with Nayagara Water and Technology, Padaviya, with the approval and continued encouragement of the valued shareholders of Seylan Developments, the initiative is the fourth CSR project undertaken by Seylan Developments.

Seylan Developments CEO Anuja Ratnaayake said, “We believe that meaningful CSR initiatives like this should create practical, lasting value and impact for the communities we serve.”

Prior to the installation of the RO plant, the school had to navigate financial and logistical challenges in purchasing approximately 500 litres of purified drinking water each week from Ethawatunuwewa, located nearly 10 kilometres away. In establishing the RO plant, the initiative also resolves concerns related to chronic kidney disease by making access to safe drinking water easier.

Seylan Developments Chairman Ramesh Jayasekara, together with the board and management, aim to continue supporting initiatives that address essential community needs and create tangible and sustainable social value.

The handover ceremony was attended by Seylan Developments Directors Gillian Edwards and Ranil Dissanayake, together with Ratnaayake, and the staff members of the company.
