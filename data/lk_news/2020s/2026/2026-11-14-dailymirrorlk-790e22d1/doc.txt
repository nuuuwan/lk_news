SLASSCOM TechKids holds online safety awareness session

On October 1, 2026, SLASSCOM TechKids marked the Children’s Day with a free awareness session on cyberbullying, cyber harassment and online safety for children aged 12 to 16 and their parents.

The session helped the children recognise cyberbullying and online harassment, understand digital risks and learn how to respond to harmful online behaviour and seek appropriate support.

The session was led by Asela Waidyalankara, a cybersecurity expert with over a decade of experience in technology, digital strategy and policy. He is Managing Director of Talos Consulting and Vice President of the Digital Trust Alliance.

“Keeping children safe online is not only about teaching them what to avoid but also about giving them the confidence and support to navigate the digital world safely. Creating an environment where children feel comfortable speaking up and parents are equipped to support them is essential to building a safer digital environment,” he said.

A key focus of the session was the role parents play in protecting children online. The discussion highlighted the importance of open, non-judgemental conversations between parents and children as well as the shared responsibility of creating a safer digital environment.

The interactive format gave children the opportunity to engage directly with the speaker, ask questions and discuss online challenges. The session welcomed 30 participants, including children and their parents and reinforced the importance of seeking support when faced with unsafe online situations.

Through its annual Children’s Day initiative, SLASSCOM TechKids reinforces the importance of online safety and responsible digital behaviour as part of technology education.
