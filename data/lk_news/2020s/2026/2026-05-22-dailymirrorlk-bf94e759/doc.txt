Bingxue Sri Lanka opens first outlet in Dehiwala

ICETALK TIME (PVT) LTD Chairman Errol Weerasinghe, Executive Director Nivran Weerakoon and Director/General Manager Isaac Wangat the ceremonial ribbon-cutting

Bingxue Sri Lanka officially marked a significant milestone with the opening of its first local outlet in Dehiwala on 16 May, introducing the globally recognized dessert and beverage brand Bingxue to the Sri Lankan market for the very first time.

The launch represents a key step in the brand’s regional expansion and signals the beginning of a new chapter in Sri Lanka’s rapidly evolving dessert and café culture.

Located in the heart of Dehiwala, the newly opened outlet offers customers an immersive experience centered around Bingxue’s signature range of ice creams, bubble teas, and refreshing beverages. Designed with a modern lifestyle concept, the outlet combines vibrant aesthetics with a customer-focused environment, aiming to appeal particularly to young consumers and families seeking premium yet affordable dessert and beverage options.

The grand opening ceremony was attended by Errol Weerasinghe – Chairman of ICETALK TIME (PVT) LTD, Nivran Weerakoon – Executive Director of ICETALK TIME (PVT) LTD, and Isaac Wang – Director / General Manager of ICETALK TIME (PVT) LTD, together with invited guests, business partners, and well-wishers celebrating the official debut of Bingxue in Sri Lanka.

Bingxue’s global journey began in China in 2014 under Jinan Baodao Enterprise Management Consulting Co., Ltd., and has since expanded rapidly. Today, the brand operates in more than 14 countries and continues to grow as one of the most recognizable names in the new-generation dessert and beverage industry.

Speaking on the occasion, representatives highlighted that the Dehiwala launch is only the beginning of a wider expansion strategy in Sri Lanka. Plans are already underway to introduce additional outlets across key urban locations in the coming months, further strengthening Bingxue’s footprint in the country.

The Sri Lankan operations are led by ICETALK TIME (PVT) LTD, the official master franchisee responsible for bringing the international brand into the local market. Headquartered in Colombo, ICETALK TIME (PVT) LTD emphasized its mission to deliver a world-class bubble tea and ice cream experience across the island, combining global standards with local accessibility.
