Former Fisheries Corporation chairman remanded after failing to meet bail conditions

Colombo, Aug. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Former Chairman of the Ceylon Fisheries Corporation Mahil Senaratne, who was granted bail after being arrested by the Bribery Commission, was remanded today (18) after failing to fulfil the bail conditions imposed by court.

Senaratne was produced before the Colombo Chief Magistrate’s Court today, where he was initially granted bail.

The court ordered his release on two surety bails of Rs. 2.5 million each and imposed a travel ban preventing him from leaving the country.

However, as he failed to fulfil the bail conditions, the court subsequently ordered that he be remanded.
