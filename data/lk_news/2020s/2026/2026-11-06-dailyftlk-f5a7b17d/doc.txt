Sri Lanka’s CH17 takes proprietary SaaS technology beyond borders with Nepal expansion

CH17 Founder and CEO Jumar Preena (right) with CH17 Systems Ltd., Nepal Co-Founder Vivek Thakuri

Homegrown technology company seeks international recognition for Sri Lankan-developed white-label loyalty, customer intelligence and engagement solutions

Sri Lankan loyalty and customer engagement technology company CH17 has taken a significant step towards international expansion with the establishment of Channel17 Systems Ltd., in Nepal, marking its first overseas market operation under its own brand.

The development represents more than geographical expansion. It opens a new avenue for international recognition of Sri Lankan-developed proprietary Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) technology, particularly in the specialised fields of loyalty management, customer intelligence, digital engagement and customer value-added services.

Established in 2013, CH17 has spent more than a decade developing technology-driven solutions for organisations across Sri Lanka’s insurance, finance and leasing, apparel and retail industries.

Its experience has evolved into a proprietary technology ecosystem that enables businesses to introduce customised loyalty, rewards, membership management and customer engagement programmes under their own corporate identities.

CH17’s international ambitions are supported by practical experience in developing and managing white-label technology solutions for established organisations operating across diverse industries.

A distinguishing feature of its business model is its pioneering focus on closed and targeted customer communities, identified through demographic, firmographic and institutional relationships.

This approach enables organisations to engage specific customer groups more effectively, moving beyond conventional mass-market promotions towards relevant, measurable and sustained customer relationships.

CH17’s proprietary SaaS infrastructure supports digital membership identification, customer authentication, loyalty and rewards management, customer segmentation, promotional campaign administration, merchant privileges and customer intelligence reporting.

The technology is designed to accommodate different institutional requirements while allowing participating organisations to retain their individual brand identities.

Beyond technology development, CH17 has established expertise in introducing innovative, out-of-the-box marketing and customer engagement strategies to industries traditionally dependent on product-led promotions, conventional advertising and transactional customer relationships.

As customer expectations evolve, businesses increasingly require solutions that extend engagement beyond the initial sale, policy issuance, financial transaction or membership enrolment.

CH17 addresses this requirement by combining technology with practical marketing intelligence, structured customer recognition, year-round privileges and measurable engagement opportunities.

Its approach is particularly relevant to insurance, finance and leasing, and other established industries seeking to modernise customer relationships without necessarily replacing their existing core business systems.

The company’s proprietary solutions allow businesses to develop more meaningful customer relationships, encourage participation, strengthen retention strategies and create additional value through external merchant partnerships.

CH17’s technological and commercial capabilities have progressively evolved into its Customer Value as a Service (C-VAAS) framework.

The model integrates three complementary disciplines: technology infrastructure, customer intelligence and customer engagement.

However, the company’s proprietary SaaS capabilities extend beyond conventional loyalty programmes and customer value-added services.

Its technology architecture also offers applications in white-label membership management, digital verification, rewards exchange, institutional engagement, customer segmentation and campaign management.

This versatility creates opportunities for CH17’s technology to be deployed across multiple sectors and international markets, with configurations tailored to local business requirements.

The company’s approach combines Human Intelligence supported by Artificial Intelligence, recognising that effective customer engagement requires both technological capability and practical understanding of customer behaviour.

The establishment of Channel17 Systems Ltd., in Nepal on 30 October 2026 marks the beginning of CH17’s international market development strategy.

The Nepal operation has been established with local business partners to pursue opportunities across retail, financial services, insurance and other customer-oriented industries.

Under the proposed operating structure, the Nepal entity will focus on local business development, client relationships and market operations, while CH17’s Colombo operation will provide proprietary technology, platform licensing, technical support and agreed specialist services.

This asset-light operating model is intended to create opportunities for recurring technology and service export revenue originating from Sri Lanka.

Importantly, the expansion demonstrates how locally developed intellectual property can be introduced to overseas markets without the need to rebuild technology platforms for each country.

It also provides a potential foundation for evaluating further international market opportunities through suitably qualified local partners.

CH17 Founder and CEO Jumar Preena said: “Sri Lanka has established a strong reputation for software development and technology services. We believe the next opportunity lies in gaining international recognition for proprietary technology platforms, intellectual property and innovative business solutions developed within Sri Lanka.

Over the past thirteen years, we have gained valuable experience in understanding the customer engagement challenges faced by established industries. We have translated that experience into proprietary, white-label technology solutions supported by practical marketing intelligence.”

“Our entry into Nepal is not simply about taking a loyalty programme overseas. It is about introducing Sri Lankan-developed technology, specialised industry knowledge and innovative customer engagement solutions to international markets. We believe Sri Lanka has the expertise and intellectual capacity to create technology products that can be commercially deployed beyond our shores under our own brands. Our objective is to demonstrate that potential through sustainable international business development.”

CH17’s international expansion reflects a broader ambition to position Sri Lankan-developed SaaS solutions within the global marketplace for customer engagement and business technology.

With its proprietary technology platform, experience across multiple industries and focus on scalable white-label solutions, the company aims to establish a sustainable international presence while retaining its technology development and intellectual property base in Sri Lanka.

For Sri Lanka, it offers another example of how homegrown technology, industry expertise and innovative thinking can create opportunities for international recognition and technology exports.
