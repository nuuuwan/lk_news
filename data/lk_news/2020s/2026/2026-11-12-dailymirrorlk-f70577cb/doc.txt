CESS rates on 800 import categories to be reduced by 25%

Colombo, Nov. 12 (Daily Mirror) - The Government has proposed a 25% reduction in existing CESS rates applicable to 800 selected Harmonized System (HS) code lines as part of its programme to gradually reduce import tariffs under the 2027 Budget.

The move aims to simplify the import tax structure, reduce trade-related costs and improve the competitiveness of the economy.

Meanwhile, the 10% Ports and Airports Development Levy (PAL) imposed on imported newsprint classified under specified HS codes will be removed with effect from January 1, 2027.

The proposed changes are expected to reduce the tax burden on selected imports and ease costs for businesses.
