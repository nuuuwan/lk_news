13-year-old boy dies in fireworks explosion in Valachchenai

Batticaloa, Sep.12 (Daily Mirror) - A 13-year-old boy has died following a tragic fireworks explosion during an annual temple festival in the Valachchenai area of Batticaloa last night (11).

The victim, a resident of Kulakkadu in Kiran, was reportedly at the temple during the night celebrations when the incident occurred at around 11.00 p.m. A fireworks item that had failed to ignite properly was removed from the main display area after being considered unexploded.

The boy later approached the discarded firework to inspect it when it suddenly detonated, causing him serious injuries. He was immediately rushed to the Valachchenai Base Hospital for emergency treatment.

However, the boy succumbed to his injuries while receiving treatment at the hospital. Valachchenai Police have launched an investigation into the circumstances surrounding the explosion.
