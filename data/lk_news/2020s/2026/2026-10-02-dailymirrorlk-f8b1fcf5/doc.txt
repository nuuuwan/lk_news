New Police posts planned for Colombo housing schemes

Colombo, Oct. 3 (Daily Mirror) - Police and the Urban Development Authority (UDA) have launched a joint initiative to strengthen security and maintain law and order across 24 UDA-managed housing complexes in and around Colombo.

The high-level discussion was held at the Colombo Deputy Inspector General’s Office under the direction of Inspector General of Police Attorney Priyantha Weerasooriya and chaired by Colombo Division Deputy Inspector General Nishantha de Zoysa.

The initiative is aimed at protecting the lives and property of residents living under the Urban Regeneration Project through a comprehensive police security plan. Following proposals made by UDA Director General Eng. Damith Dissanayake, nearby police stations have been instructed to implement the necessary security measures.

Officials also discussed concerns over drug trafficking in certain housing complexes and its reported connection to vandalism and damage to public property. As part of the response, plans were agreed to establish new police posts, repair and improve existing police facilities, increase regular patrols and take strict legal action against offences involving public property.

Both the police and UDA emphasised the importance of continued cooperation to ensure the long-term safety and security of residents in high-density urban communities across Colombo.
