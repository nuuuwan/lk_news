BASL to brief MPs on failure to fill judges’ vacancies in SC and AC

Colombo, Aug. 19 (Daily Mirror) - A delegation of Bar Association Members will brief MPs on the failure to fill the existing judges' vacancies in the Supreme Court and the Appeal Court, Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa told Parliament today.

He urged the Speaker to make arrangements to enable ruling party MPs to participate in this discussion together with the Opposition MPs.
