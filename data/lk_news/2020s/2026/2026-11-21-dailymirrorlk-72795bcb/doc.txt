Thai Deputy Prime Minister arrives in Sri Lanka

Colombo, Nov 21 (Daily Mirror)- Deputy Prime Minister and Minister of Foreign Affairs of Thailand Sihasak Phuangketkeow arrived in Sri Lanka on November 20 for a four-day official visit.

He was welcomed upon arrival by Deputy Minister of Foreign Affairs and Foreign Employment Arun Hemachandra.

The Thai Deputy Prime Minister is visiting Sri Lanka as the Royal Representative of King Maha Vajiralongkorn to attend the Royal Kathina Ceremony at the Asgiriya Maha Viharaya in Kandy today (21).

During his visit, he is scheduled to meet President Anura Kumara Dissanayake and Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya. He is also expected to hold bilateral discussions with Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Vijitha Herath.

A key highlight of the visit is the proposed signing of an agreement on visa exemption for ordinary passport holders of the two countries.

The agreement is expected to facilitate travel and further strengthen people-to-people relations between Sri Lanka and Thailand.
