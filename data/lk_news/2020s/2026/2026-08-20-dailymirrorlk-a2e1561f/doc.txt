Final rites of late Nanda Malani to be held on Saturday

Colombo, Aug. 20 (Daily Mirror) - The final rites of the late veteran Sri Lankan singer Visharada Nanda Malani will be held on Saturday, August 22, at the Borella General Cemetery, according to Professor Sunil Ariyaratne.

Her remains will be kept at her residence on Wijayaba Mawatha, Nawala, from 10:00 a.m. tomorrow, allowing members of the public to pay their final respects.

Religious observances will be held at 3:30 p.m. on Saturday, after which the funeral procession will leave for the Borella General Cemetery.

Professor Sunil Ariyaratne said the family has decided to honor Nanda Malani’s final wishes by holding the final rites at 5:00 p.m. at Borella Cemetery, without an official or special state funeral ceremony.
