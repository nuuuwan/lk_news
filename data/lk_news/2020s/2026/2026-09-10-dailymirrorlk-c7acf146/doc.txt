Maliban Group clinches two Gold Dragons among seven accolades

The Maliban Group of Companies has been recognised for impactful brand communication at the Dragons of Sri Lanka 2026 Awards, securing a tally of two Gold Dragons, one Silver Dragon, two Bronze Dragons, and two Black Dragons across multiple categories.

The recognition highlights the Group’s continued commitment to developing purposeful campaigns that connect with consumers through creativity, innovation, and strategic execution.

Awarded campaigns spanned experiential marketing, sustainability, media, content creation, brand trials, sales generation, and brand activation and awareness, reflecting the Group’s ability to create meaningful brand experiences across its diverse business categories.

The Dragons of Sri Lanka Awards represent one of the country’s most respected platforms recognising excellence in marketing effectiveness and communication. As an official extension of the Dragons of Asia, the awards are part of a globally recognised network celebrating results-driven marketing excellence since 2000.

The Dragons of Asia is positioned alongside leading international marketing effectiveness platforms such as the Cannes Lions, Effie Awards, and IPA Effectiveness Awards, making recognition at the Dragons a significant benchmark for brands and agencies across the region.

For the Maliban Group of Companies, this achievement reflects the strength of campaigns developed through strategic insight, creative excellence, and purposeful brand engagement. The seven recognitions demonstrate the Group’s ability to create impactful communication that builds meaningful connections with consumers.

The “Maliban Chocolate Biscuits. Kids Will Do Anything for This Taste” campaign for Maliban Biscuit Manufactories Sri Lanka received a Gold Dragon in the Brand Activation and/or Awareness category, while “The Superhero That Replaced Child Advertising” campaign for Maliban Dairy & Agri Products Sri Lanka secured a Gold Dragon in the Content Creation category, recognising their ability to create engaging and impactful consumer experiences.

The “HumanTill. A Lived Experience of Giving” campaign for Maliban Biscuit Manufactories Sri Lanka secured a Silver Dragon in the Event or Experiential category and a Bronze Dragon in the Cause, Environment or Sustainability category. The “The One Breath Challenge” campaign received a Bronze Dragon in the Media category and a Black Dragon in the Creative Excellence category, while the “Maliban Kiri Consumer Promo” campaign for Maliban Milk Products Group Sri Lanka received a Black Dragon in the Brand Trial or Sales Generation category.
