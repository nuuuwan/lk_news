No entry to Nil Diya Pokuna without wildlife permit

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - Visits to Nil Diya Pokuna, located within the Ravana Ella Sanctuary in the Badulla District, have been temporarily suspended until the Department of Wildlife Conservation formally commences authorised tourism operations at the site.

The Department said entry to the site is strictly prohibited under the regulations introduced through Extraordinary Gazette Notification No. 2506/43 dated September 15, 2026.

The regulations establish a formal framework governing tourism activities at Nil Diya Pokuna, which falls under the administration of the Department of Wildlife Conservation.

The Department has observed that certain individuals are attempting to take tourists to the site without obtaining the required authorisation.

It emphasised that visitors will only be permitted to enter the area with a valid permit issued by the Department and accompanied by a tour guide registered with the Department.

Legal action will be taken against individuals who violate these regulations, the Department warned.

The Department has requested public cooperation in ensuring visitor safety and protecting the sensitive ecosystem surrounding Nil Diya Pokuna.
