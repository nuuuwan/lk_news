Parliament still searching for qualified head for proposed budget office: Harsha

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - Parliament has yet to find a suitably qualified candidate to head the proposed Parliamentary Budget Office, Committee on Public Finance (COPF) Chairman Dr. Harsha de Silva informed the House today.

Addressing Parliament, Dr. de Silva revealed that the latest proposal to attract qualified talent involves offering a remuneration package on par with that of a Central Bank Director.

"The establishment of the Parliamentary Budget Office cannot be delayed any further," he stressed.
