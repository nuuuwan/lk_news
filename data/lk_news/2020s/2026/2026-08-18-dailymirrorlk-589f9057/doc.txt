Government will not confront UN

Colombo, Aug. 18 (Daily Mirror) - The government does not intend to confront the United Nations (UN) over the comments made by its Special Rapporteur on the 20th Amendment, Leader of the House Bimal Ratnayake told Parliament today.

"The government does not have to accept the comments made by the UN Special Rapporteur," he also said at the same time.

He said the government of the late President J.R. Jayewardene did not allow 12 judges to remain in their posts when the 1978 Constitution was introduced.

"What our government intends to do is to extend the retirement age of all judges. We are not like the J.R. government," he said.
