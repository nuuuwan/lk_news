Allianz Lanka expands customer support with 24/7 roadside assistance

Allianz Lanka has launched ‘Allianz Road Care’, a 24/7 roadside assistance service designed to provide eligible policyholders with prompt and reliable support during unexpected roadside emergencies.

Available to eligible motor and non-motor policyholders, Allianz Road Care reflects Allianz Lanka’s belief that caring for customers goes beyond policy coverage. Whether faced with a flat tyre, a dead battery, a vehicle lockout, or a minor mechanical or electrical breakdown, customers can access professional assistance around the clock, ensuring help is always within reach when it matters most.

Eligible policyholders can enjoy Free 24/7 roadside assistance within Colombo City and a 10-kilometre radius, including jump-start assistance, puncture or spare wheel fitting support, basic mechanical and electrical breakdown assistance, key lockout support, and on-site assistance until towing or recovery services arrive.
