Major reservoirs hold 37.2% of effective storage capacity

Colombo, Aug. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka’s 74 major reservoirs held a total of 1.106 million acre-feet of effective water storage as of August 16, Director of Irrigation (Water Management), Eng. H.M.P.S.D. Herath said.

He said the total effective storage stood at 37.2% of the combined effective capacity of the major reservoirs, with 1,248,382 acre-feet of effective storage recorded against the total capacity.

Among the districts, Kurunegala recorded the highest effective storage level at 76.6%, followed by Puttalam at 67.8%, Kandy at 63.3% and Monaragala at 59.5%.

At the individual reservoir level, Deduru Oya recorded 98.2% of its effective storage capacity, while Wan Ela stood at 98.0% and Rajangana at 86.0%. Several reservoirs, however, remained at relatively low levels, including Manankattiya at 2.8%, Muruthawela at 2.4% and Ethimale at 5.7%.

The data covers the period from August 10 to 16 and was released by the Irrigation Department’s Water Management Branch. None of the 74 reservoirs were reported to be spilling during the period.
