CFA Society Sri Lanka-Deloitte to host programme on successful M&A

CFA Society Sri Lanka, in collaboration with Deloitte, will host a professional development programme titled ‘Demystifying Key Concepts to Successful Mergers and Acquisitions’ on Thursday, November 5, 2026, from 8:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., at Courtyard by Marriott, Colombo City Centre.

The programme aims to provide the participants with practical insights into the mergers and acquisitions (M&A) process, highlighting the critical factors that contribute to successful transactions.

With increasing emphasis on corporate consolidation, strategic restructuring and business growth, the programme will focus on effective deal structuring, comprehensive due diligence, risk management and value creation.

The participants will gain a better understanding of the key stages of an M&A transaction, with particular attention to tax, legal and financial due diligence, common transaction challenges and the importance of informed decision-making in achieving successful outcomes.

The programme will be facilitated by an experienced panel of industry professionals, including Deloitte Sri Lanka and Maldives Partner and Head of Tax Charmaine Tillekeratne, Deloitte Sri Lanka Associate Director Strategy, Risk and Transactions Buddhi Pathiraja, Sudath Perera Associates Partner and Head of Corporate and Commercial Law Dushyantha Perera and NDB Capital Holdings Limited Chief Executive Officer Senaka Kakiriwaragodage, CFA.

The event will kick start with a fireside chat hosted by Deloitte Sri Lanka and Maldives Head of Strategy, Risk and Transactions Ruvini Fernando, joined by Lasanga Abeysuriya - Valuation and Modelling Leader of Deloitte Sri Lanka and Maldives Strategy, Risk and Transaction Services and graced by the panelists.

Designed for corporate executives, finance and investment professionals, business leaders, legal and tax practitioners and others involved in corporate transactions, the programme offers an opportunity to strengthen practical knowledge and gain insights from experienced industry practitioners.
