Jailor’s evidence sensitive; don’t report in media: Magistrate

Colombo, Aug. 1 (Daily Mirror) - Negombo Magistrate Selani Perera ordered the media to refrain from covering any of the evidence given by the Head Jailor of the Negombo Prison who testified at the magisterial inquiry into the recent clashes, since it contained sensitive information that could seriously endanger national security if revealed in public.

Head Jailor Wickrema Jayantha Kumara testified before the magistrate for two hours when the magisterial inquiry into the incident was taken up before the Negombo Magistrate on July 30. He told court that he was the Head Jailor on duty in the prisons when the clashes erupted.

Led by the officials of the Criminal Investigations Department (CID), he testified from 1.00 pm to 3.00 pm.

The magistrate ordered the Registrar of the Court to keep the record of evidence made by the jailor that contained sensitive information affecting national security, well protected in the custody of the court.

The CID informed court that a formal investigation of the incident is being held under a team of four Asst. Superintendents of Police and that the Govt. Analyst’s reports on the case productions including firearms and the sharp weapons used in the clash were yet to be received.

The magistrate ordered CID to submit a progress report on the ongoing investigations.
