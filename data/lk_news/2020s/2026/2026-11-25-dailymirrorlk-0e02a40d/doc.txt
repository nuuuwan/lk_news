Heavy showers exceeding 100mm on the cards

Colombo, Nov. 25 (Daily Mirror) - Heavy showers exceeding 100mm are likely in several parts of Sri Lanka, with wet weather expected across much of the island, the Department of Meteorology warned.

According to the latest weather forecast, heavy falls above 100mm are likely at some places in the Western, Sabaragamuwa, Central, Uva and North-Western provinces, as well as in the Galle and Matara districts.

Showers will occur at times in the Northern, North-Central, Uva and Eastern provinces and in the Hambantota district.

Meanwhile, misty conditions may prevail in the Western, Sabaragamuwa and Central provinces, as well as in the Galle, Matara and Badulla districts during the early hours of the morning.
