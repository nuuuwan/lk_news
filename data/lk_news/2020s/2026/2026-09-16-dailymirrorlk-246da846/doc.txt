Court issues warrants for Aravinda de Silva and wife

Colombo, Sep. 16 (Daily Mirror) - Colombo Fort Magistrate Pasan Amarasena today issued warrants for the arrest of former cricketer Aravinda de Silva and his wife, Priyanga Anushka Wijenayake.

The order was made when the case against Kapila and his wife was taken up before the Fort Magistrate’s Court today, as Aravinda de Silva and his wife, who had stood as sureties for Kapila Chandrasena’s wife, failed to appear in court.

Counsel appearing for them submitted that they were unable to appear in court as Aravinda de Silva’s mother had passed away and her funeral was scheduled to be held today.

The Magistrate said that this fact should be brought to the court’s attention when the warrants are taken up for recall.
