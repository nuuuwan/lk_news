Ranil’s security officer hospitalised following alleged assault by video journalist

Colombo, Sep. 30 (Daily Mirror) - A security officer attached to the security detail of former President Ranil Wickremesinghe was admitted to the National Hospital in Colombo today after he was allegedly struck on the head with a camera by a television cameraman.

Sources from the former President’s office told Daily Mirror that the officer had lodged a complaint with the Slave Island Police before being admitted to hospital.

The incident allegedly occurred near the gate of the Colombo Fort Magistrate's Court as Wickremesinghe was leaving the court following a hearing in a case filed over allegations of misusing state funds.
