LAUGFS justifies price hike, cites rupee depreciation and rising costs

LAUGFS Gas PLC and LAUGFS Engineering Chief Executive Officer Nalinda Kurukulasooriya has justified the Rs. 1,775 increase in the price of a 12.5kg domestic gas cylinder, citing the depreciation of the rupee, taxes, financing costs and rising international procurement costs.

Explaining the factors behind the latest price revision, Kurukulasooriya said the depreciation of the rupee and its impact on the exchange rate had added Rs. 299 to the cost, while VAT accounted for a further Rs. 256.

The company had also included Rs. 90 in financing costs arising from bank borrowings and the opening of Letters of Credit (LCs), he said.

According to the cost breakdown provided by the company, these components account for approximately Rs. 1,768, against the total increase of Rs. 1,775 announced by LAUGFS Gas.

Kurukulasooriya said the company had considered several factors when revising its prices, adding that international market conditions and the closure of its Hambantota operation had also affected its cost structure.

Acknowledging that the revised price was high for consumers, he said the adjustment was necessary to cover the increased costs faced by the company.

The latest revision comes amid rising international LPG procurement costs and pressure on the domestic gas market, placing an additional burden on household consumers.
