Doctors to launch two-hour island-wide trade union action tomorrow

Colombo, Nov. 22 (Daily Mirror) - The Government Medical Officers’ Association (GMOA) will launch a two-hour island-wide trade union action tomorrow (23), protesting the failure of the 2027 Budget to address doctors’ longstanding grievances.

The GMOA said doctors would withdraw from routine duties from noon to 2 p.m. to attend emergency meetings of branch unions across the country.

The association said the action was prompted by the Government’s failure to address issues relating to doctors’ salaries, allowances, professional concerns and working conditions through the Budget.

It urged the Government to introduce solutions during the remaining stages of the Budget debate in Parliament.
