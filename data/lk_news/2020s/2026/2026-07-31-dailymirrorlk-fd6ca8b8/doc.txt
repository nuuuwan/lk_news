Sri Lanka reviews roadmap to achieve US$5 bn digital exports by 2030

Colombo, July 31 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka on Friday (31) reviewed a strategic roadmap aimed at increasing the country’s digital export earnings to US$5 billion by 2030, during a national workshop attended by key industry stakeholders.

The workshop, titled “The Road to US$5 Billion,” was held at Cinnamon Life, Colombo, to review the proposed strategy for accelerating the growth of Sri Lanka’s digital industry and to build consensus on its implementation.

Organised by the Ministry of Digital Economy and GovTech Sri Lanka with technical assistance from the World Bank Group, the event brought together representatives from the public and private sectors to discuss the future direction of the country’s digital economy.

Deputy Minister of Digital Economy Eranga Weeraratne, Senior Presidential Adviser on Digital Economy and Chairman of GovTech Sri Lanka Dr. Hans Wijayasuriya, and International Finance Corporation (IFC) Lead Industry Specialist Victor Antonypillai delivered the opening remarks.

During the workshop, stakeholders reviewed an assessment report on Sri Lanka’s information technology industry and a proposed strategic roadmap prepared by local and international consultants from KPMG with technical support from the World Bank Group. The roadmap was further developed with inputs from the Ministry of Digital Economy, GovTech Sri Lanka and the Industry Development Committee.

Participants also took part in thematic discussions focusing on developing future-ready digital skills, strengthening innovation and support for independent professionals, expanding global market access, and improving policies, investments and infrastructure needed for sustainable growth.

Discussions also covered measures to strengthen Sri Lanka’s competitiveness in the global digital marketplace, address the growing impact of artificial intelligence on businesses and employment, promote high-value digital exports based on intellectual property, and help local digital innovations reach international markets.

The Government said the roadmap is expected to provide an evidence-based framework to improve the international competitiveness of Sri Lanka’s digital industry, create quality employment and entrepreneurship opportunities, expand exports of locally developed digital products and services, and position the country as a leading digital economy in the region.

The roadmap will be further refined based on the recommendations made during the workshop, with the next phase focusing on the preparation of an integrated action plan outlining priority initiatives, responsible institutions, implementation timelines and progress indicators.
