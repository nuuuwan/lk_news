Thailand to recruit 10,000 Sri Lankan workers: Vijitha Herath

Colombo, Sep. 14 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka and Thailand signed landmark agreements today in Bangkok on Labour Cooperation and the Employment of Sri Lankan Workers in Thailand, Foreign Minister Vijitha Herath said.

The Minister said in a post on X that the framework will facilitate safe, regular and mutually beneficial labour mobility, while safeguarding workers’ rights, welfare and protection.

"Thailand has approved the initial recruitment of 10,000 Sri Lankan workers, with opportunities to expand skilled employment, particularly for youth in sectors such as IT and tourism. It is a significant milestone in diversifying employment opportunities for Sri Lankans and deepening Sri Lanka Thailand relations," he said.
