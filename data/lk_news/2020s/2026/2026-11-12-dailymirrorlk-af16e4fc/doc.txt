Clash erupts at Ruwanweli Maha Seya satyagraha site

Colombo, Nov. 12 (Daily Mirror) - Unrest erupted at a satyagraha site being held in front of the Ruwanweli Maha Seya in Anuradhapura today, following a heated confrontation between two groups of lay people.

The clash broke out when a group of lay people, including Ven. Balangoda Kassapa Thera, who was staging a fast unto death, arrived at the site and confronted another group led by Rajanganaya Saddharathana Thera, who has been expelled from the Buddhist order.

During the confrontation, Rajanganaya Saddharathana Thera sustained a head injury after being allegedly assaulted with a club by members of the group at the fasting site.
