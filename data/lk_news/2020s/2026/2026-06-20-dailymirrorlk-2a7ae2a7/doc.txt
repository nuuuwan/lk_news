Showers expected in several provinces

Colombo, June 20 (Daily Mirror) - Several spells of showers are expected in the Western, Sabaragamuwa and North-Western provinces, as well as in the Galle, Matara, Kandy and Nuwara Eliya districts, according to the Department of Meteorology.

The department also forecast showers or thundershowers at several places in the Uva and Eastern provinces and in the Mullaitivu district after 2.00 p.m.

As such, the general public has been advised to take adequate precautions to minimise damage caused by temporary localized strong winds and lightning associated with thundershower
