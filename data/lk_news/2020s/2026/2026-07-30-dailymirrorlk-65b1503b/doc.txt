Sri Lanka-US diplomatic ties stronger than ever: Mahinda Samarasinghe

July 30 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka's Ambassador to the United States, Mahinda Samarasinghe, said diplomatic cooperation between Sri Lanka and the United States has grown stronger than ever during his tenure, resulting in significant progress in the country's economic development.

He made the remarks during a visit to the Maryland International Buddhist Temple on July 28, where he received blessings from the Chief Judicial Sanghanayake of the United States, Most Venerable Katugastota Uparathana Nayaka Thero.

Addressing the gathering, Ambassador Samarasinghe said the United States now holds a very positive perception of Sri Lanka and has extended assistance in several areas supporting the country's economic development.

He highlighted the importance of maintaining and further strengthening the longstanding cooperation between the two countries.

Speaking at the event, Ven. Katugastota Uparathana Nayaka Thero praised Ambassador Samarasinghe's service, saying his contribution to Sri Lanka had gone beyond the traditional role of a diplomat.

The Thero further said that a stronger and more optimistic bridge had been built between Sri Lanka and the United States than ever before, adding that Ambassador Samarasinghe had played a leading role in fostering those ties.
