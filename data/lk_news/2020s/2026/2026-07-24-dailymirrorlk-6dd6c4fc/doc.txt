ESOL Premier Campus launches OCN London qualifications in Sri Lanka

Amali Jayasinghe, Director – ESOL Premier Campus; Himantha Karunarathne, Director – ESOL Premier Campus; Carlos Cubillo-Barsi, Chief Executive Officer – OCN London; Premila Paulraj, Regional Head – South Asia, OCN London; and Andrew Abinash, General Manager – ESOL Premier Campus, at the official launch of the exclusive partnership

Offering local students and professionals, access to world-renowned UK-accredited qualifications, ESOL Premier Campus has officially launched OCN London qualifications in Sri Lanka through an exclusive partnership with the UK awarding organisation.  In operation since the 1970s, OCN London is a recognised UK Awarding Organisation regulated by Ofqual UK. ESOL Premier Campus is an established education provider with a track record of delivering universally recognisedprogrammes.

This provides an opportunity to gain globally accepted, Ofqual-regulated qualifications in English, Business Management, Education and Training, and Digital Marketing without having to travel abroad, while enhancing academic and career prospects.

Elaborating further, Andrew Abinash, General Manager of ESOL Premier Campus said, “We are delighted to introduce OCN London qualifications to Sri Lanka through ESOL Premier Campus, creating new opportunities for learners to access internationally accepted UK qualifications without the need to travel overseas.”

The opportunity to gain OCN London qualifications in Sri Lanka provides immense benefits for school leavers seeking globally reputed qualifications; students who have completed their O/Ls and A/Ls and are looking for alternative higher education pathways; working professionals seeking career advancement; teachers and aspiring educators interested in professional development qualifications; parents seeking quality international education opportunities for their children; entrepreneurs and corporate employees looking to strengthen their management expertise; and individuals considering overseas university progression opportunities.

The OCN London portfolio offered through ESOL Premier Campus includes OCN London ESOL International qualifications at CEFR B1, B2, C1 and C2 levels, the OCN London Level 4 Certificate in Education and Training, the Level 3 Certificate in Principles of Business Administration, the Level 4 Diploma and Level 5 Diploma in Business Management, as well as the Level 4 Certificate and Level 4 Diploma in Digital Marketing. These qualifications have been developed in accordance with international academic and professional standards and are designed to support progression into higher education and employment opportunities.
