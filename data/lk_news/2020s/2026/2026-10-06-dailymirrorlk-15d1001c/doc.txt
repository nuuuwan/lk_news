Bill to combat organised crime to be gazetted

Colombo, Oct. 6 (Daily Mirror) - Steps have been taken to gazette the Prevention of Organised Crimes Bill, aimed at strengthening legal measures to prevent organised crime and tackle serious offences in Sri Lanka.

The proposed legislation seeks to provide the necessary legal provisions to prevent organised crime, enable more effective action against serious offences and protect society from threats posed by organised criminal groups.

The Bill also aims to strengthen national and international cooperation in preventing and combating serious crimes.

In addition, it is intended to establish the necessary legal framework to give effect to the United Nations Convention against Transnational Organized Crime, particularly in addressing organised criminal activities that extend beyond national borders.
