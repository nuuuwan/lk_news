Rescue operation expands as several fishing vessels report distress off Negombo

Colombo, August 1 (Daily Mirror) - Search and rescue teams have intensified operations off the coast of Negombo after several fishing vessels were reported to be in distress, with one vessel capsizing and leaving one person dead and two others missing.

The Sri Lanka Navy (SLN) and the Sri Lanka Air Force (SLAF) are jointly conducting rescue operations in the area, with one body recovered so far while efforts continue to locate the two missing fishermen.

A Bell 412 helicopter of the Sri Lanka Air Force has been deployed to assist the ongoing search operation, alongside naval assets engaged in the rescue mission.

Authorities said they have also received reports of several other fishing vessels experiencing difficulties in the same sea area, and rescue teams are continuing operations to provide assistance to those affected.
