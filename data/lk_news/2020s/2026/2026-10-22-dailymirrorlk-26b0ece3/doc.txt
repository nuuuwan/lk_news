Pursues fraud recoveries; amount and timetable undisclosed

NDB is pursuing recovery and legal action over its Rs. 13.64 billion fraud loss, although its latest results announcement gave shareholders no figure for funds recovered or a timetable for further recoveries.

Director and Chief Executive Officer Kelum Edirisinghe said the bank was also implementing changes to controls, technology and governance following the fraud identified in April.

“The Bank continues to pursue all available avenues for recovery, appropriate legal and internal action, and implement relevant controls, technology and governance enhancements, while keeping the CBSL updated on the measures being taken,” he said.

Edirisinghe said the final forensic report’s findings were generally consistent with matters identified through the bank’s internal investigations.

The announcement did not detail the legal proceedings, quantify expected recoveries or specify when the control improvements would be completed.

The final forensic assessment placed the fraud at approximately Rs. 13.64 billion, compared with Rs. 13.58 billion disclosed after the interim report.

Of that total, Rs. 2.6 billion was attributed to the first quarter of 2026 and included in the nine-month results. Comparative figures were also restated to reflect losses attributable to earlier periods.

Edirisinghe signalled a measured approach to expansion, saying the bank would remain disciplined in managing risk, capital, liquidity and costs.
