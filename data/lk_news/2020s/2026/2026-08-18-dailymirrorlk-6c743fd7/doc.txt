Special programme launched to distribute drinking water in drought-hit districts

Colombo, Aug. 18 (Daily Mirror) - A special programme has been launched to distribute drinking water to people in districts including Batticaloa, Monaragala and Ampara, which are currently experiencing dry weather conditions, the President’s Media Division (PMD) said.

The decision was taken yesterday (17) during the third meeting of the Special Ministerial Sub-Committee and Officials’ Committee appointed to take necessary measures to address the impacts of the El Niño climate phenomenon.

The meeting, chaired by Environment Minister Dr. Dammika Patabendi at the Presidential Secretariat, discussed action plans submitted by the plantation, agriculture, wildlife and water resources management sectors, which are expected to be directly affected by the prevailing conditions.

It was decided that the drinking water distribution programme would be implemented through District Secretaries, with relevant officials to be informed of the necessary arrangements.

The meeting also considered measures to be taken if dry conditions persist and groundwater levels decline further.

Attention was also given to forecasts issued by the Department of Meteorology on the El Niño phenomenon and programmes to protect sectors that could be affected by its impacts.

The committee further stressed the importance of keeping the public informed through the media about the latest developments related to El Niño and its potential impacts.
