Cordelia Empress docks at Colombo Port

The Cordelia Empress passenger cruise ship arrived at the Colombo Port this morning carrying 1,600 tourists and 588 crew members. Around 1,800 passengers and crew members went on excursions to Colombo city, the Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage and Galle. About 90 per cent of the tourists are Indian nationals. The 210-metre-long vessel has 796 passenger cabins across 11 decks and caters mainly to Indian and Southeast Asian markets. It is scheduled to make three more visits to Colombo on December 17, 24 and 31 during the 2026/2027 tourist season.
