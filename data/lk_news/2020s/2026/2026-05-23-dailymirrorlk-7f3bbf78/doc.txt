Dollar selling rate rises to Rs. 353

Colombo, May 23 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lankan Rupee continued to weaken against the US Dollar today, according to the latest exchange rates released by the Central Bank of Sri Lanka.

The Central Bank said the selling rate of the US Dollar increased to Rs. 353.1723, indicating a continued downward trend in the value of the local currency.

Meanwhile, the buying rate was reported at Rs. 342.6799, according to the latest official exchange rate update.
