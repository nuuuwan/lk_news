Balaji joins Lanka Ashok Leyland Board

Ashok Leyland Ltd., Deputy CEO K.M. Balaji has been appointed to the Board of Lanka Ashok Leyland PLC as a Non-Independent Non-Executive Director.

Balaji has held a distinguished career with Ashok Leyland (being his first employment) spanning more than 32 years.
