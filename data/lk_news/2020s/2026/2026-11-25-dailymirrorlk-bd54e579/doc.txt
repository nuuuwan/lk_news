US issues fresh security alert for Americans across Middle East over Iran, Houthi attack threat

Hindustan Times - The US State Department has issued a fresh security alert for American citizens across the Middle East. The department warned of an increased threat from Iranian-backed Houthi forces and possible attacks targeting US government personnel and facilities.

The advisory urged Americans to remain vigilant amid rising regional tensions that could trigger flight cancellations, airspace closures and broader travel disruptions.

The warning comes as the United States says Iran and its allied groups have expanded attacks against American interests and regional partners.

In security messages published through several US embassies in the region on Tuesday, the State Department advised US citizens to closely monitor local developments and prepare for rapidly changing security conditions.

“Americans in the Middle East should exercise heightened vigilance due to the increased threat of armed attacks by Iranian-supported Houthis and Iranian efforts to target U.S. government officials and facilities in the region,” the department said.

The advisory also urged travellers to remain aware of “potential alerts, flight cancellations, airspace closures and travel disruptions,” saying the regional security situation could deteriorate quickly.

“The hostilities have the potential to escalate rapidly,” the department warned.

The State Department said Iran had previously launched attacks targeting US assets in Bahrain and Kuwait. Meanwhile, Iranian-backed Houthis have continued military operations against Saudi Arabia, including strikes on civilian airports and key infrastructure.

The advisory follows a reported Houthi attack earlier on Tuesday that struck one of the towers at Riyadh’s Financial Centre in Saudi Arabia.

Saudi Arabia’s General Directorate of Civil Defence said 11 people were injured, including two Indian nationals, two Saudi citizens, three Nepalese nationals and four Egyptian nationals. Emergency responders treated the injured after the attack, authorities said.

Saudi officials described the strike as a “terrorist attack” carried out by the Houthi militia. “The Civil Defence responded to an incident in which one of the towers of the Financial Centre in the city of Riyadh was subjected to a terrorist attack by the terrorist Houthi militia, resulting in (11) injuries,” the General Directorate of Saudi Civil Defence wrote in an X post.
