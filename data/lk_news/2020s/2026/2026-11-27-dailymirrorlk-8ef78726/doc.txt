13-year-old schoolgirl dies after falling from milk lorry

Colombo, Nov. 27 (Daily Mirror) - A 13-year-old schoolgirl died after falling from a milk-collecting lorry while travelling home following a Christmas carol rehearsal in Norwood yesterday, police said.

The victim was identified as Wijekumar Harshani, a Grade 8 student of Norwood Tamil Maha Vidyalaya and a resident of Akkara 50 in the Norwood Venture Estate.

She had attended a Christmas carol rehearsal at her school and was returning home when she reportedly got onto the rear of a milk-collecting lorry.

At around 4.00 p.m., the girl fell from the moving vehicle near her home and sustained serious injuries.

She was rushed to Dickoya Base Hospital but died after being admitted.
