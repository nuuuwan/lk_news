University of Surrey launches Asia’s first Master’s in Digital Health at IIHS, Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka’s International Institute of Health Sciences (IIHS) has launched Asia’s first Master’s in Digital Health awarded by the University of Surrey, UK. The programme addresses the growing global demand for professionals who can integrate technology and data into healthcare delivery.

Delivered at IIHS in Colombo, the curriculum covers digital health systems, informatics, data analytics, and emerging technologies. It is designed for doctors, nurses, health administrators, IT graduates, and other professionals seeking leadership roles in digital health. No prior technical expertise is required.

Dr. Kithsiri Edirisinghe, CEO of IIHS, stated: “Digital Health is essential to the future of healthcare. By delivering Asia’s first University of Surrey Master’s in Digital Health, we are creating opportunities for graduates and professionals to access global careers.”

Graduates can pursue roles including Digital Health Specialist, Health Informatics Manager, and Telemedicine Lead. The launch reinforces Sri Lanka’s position as a hub for international healthcare education under the “Study Sri Lanka” initiative.
