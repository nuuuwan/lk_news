Sri Lanka on track to complete IMF programme by 2H27

Sri Lanka is on course to complete its four-year programme with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) in the second half of 2027, Central Bank Governor Dr. Nandalal Weerasinghe said, signalling confidence that the country can stay on track with the reform programme despite the heightened global uncertainty.

Dr. Weerasinghe said the next programme review is expected around November or December this year, as Sri Lanka moves towards the final stages of the Extended Fund Facility (EFF) arrangement.

“The next review is somewhere in November, December and we are expecting that is also to be completed,” Dr. Weerasinghe told Bloomberg Television’s The Asia Trade.

“Then next year, we will be completing the four-year EFF; fully completing the programme by the second half of next year,” he said.

The IMF Executive Board in May completed the combined fifth and sixth reviews under Sri Lanka’s EFF, providing access to about US $ 695 million and taking total disbursements under the programme to around US $ 2 billion. The programme has been central to Sri Lanka’s economic stabilisation, following the 2022 crisis, requiring a series of fiscal, monetary, governance and structural reforms alongside the restructuring of the country’s external debt.

Dr. Weerasinghe said implementation remained on track despite a challenging global environment and a series of external and domestic shocks faced by the economy.

Sri Lanka entered the IMF programme in March 2023, when the IMF approved a 48-month EFF arrangement of about US $ 3 billion, to support the country’s economic policies and reforms.
