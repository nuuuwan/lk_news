Sajith calls for amendment of Mosquito Breeding Act

Colombo, July 4 (Daily Mirror) - Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa today called for the amendment of the Mosquito Breeding Act no 11 of 1987 to meet the needs of today where the country is experiencing an outbreak of dengue.

Premadasa made this remark when he met a group of Larvicidal Scientists in his office.

He stressed the need for providing facilities such as motorbikes to Larvicidal Scientists who work in the field.
