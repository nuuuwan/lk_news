CSE begins week on positive note as buying interest supports market gains

The Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE) commenced the week on a positive note on 28th July 2026, with both benchmark indices closing higher as improved investor sentiment and selective buying in fundamentally strong counters supported the market. Favorable global and domestic developments helped sustain buying interest throughout the session, enabling the market to maintain its upward momentum despite moderate overall trading activity.

The All Share Price Index (ASPI) gained 41.92 points (+0.20%) to close at 21,229.14, while the S&P SL20 Index advanced 12.66 points (+0.21%) to 5,961.40. Total market turnover amounted to LKR 2.02 Bn, with 87.47 Mn shares changing hands during the session. Market breadth remained positive, recording 116 gainers against 78 decliners, reflecting healthy buying interest across the broader market.

The Capital Goods sector emerged as the largest contributor to market turnover, recording LKR 1.04 Bn with 43.11 Mn shares traded. RIL.N dominated sector activity, generating LKR 1.03 Bn in turnover with 42.91 Mn shares traded. Meanwhile, crossings accounted for LKR 1.19 Bn, representing 58.82% of total market turnover. The largest crossing was recorded in RIL.N, totaling LKR 1.02 Bn involving 42.50 Mn shares traded.

On the positive side, DOCK.N contributed the most to the ASPI, adding 7.45 points, followed by JKH.N, COMB.N, ACL.N, and NDB.N. On the downside, SAMP.N was the largest negative contributor, weighing on the index by 5.89 points, while GRAN.N, TKYO.N, WIND.N, and NTB.N also recorded declines.

Among notable trades, RIL.N attracted significant investor interest, supported by substantial turnover and trading volume, while closing the session with a 1.26% price gain. The strong participation in the counter, together with positive market breadth, suggests investors remain selectively optimistic as the market continues to respond to improving economic and corporate developments.
