Cinnamon Life to stage West End Musical ‘La Bamba!’ in Colombo

Cinnamon Life, in partnership with John Keells Foundation and Nations Trust Bank, announced it will host the West End-licensed musical La Bamba! The Song of Veracruz from 24–28 April 2026.=The vibrant production, celebrating Mexican heritage through music and dance, will feature seven performances over five days at Cinnamon Life’s state-of-the-art venue, The Forum. This marks the first large-scale international production at the complex since its opening.

In a collaborative effort, local auditions will integrate Sri Lankan performers with the 19-member international cast and crew. “This creates a performance that is dynamic, diverse, and exciting for audiences,” said Paul Morrissey of London-based Paul Morrissey Ltd, the creative partner.

Kamal Munasinghe, Senior Vice President of Cinnamon Life, stated the production reinforces Colombo’s position as a hub for world-class entertainment and commitment to local talent.

Presenting partner John Keells Foundation views the event as a platform to showcase Sri Lankan artistry on a global stage. Theatre veteran Jerome de Silva will provide associate direction for the local cast.

Tickets are available at www.cinnamonboxoffice.com, with a special discount for Nations Trust Bank cardholders.
