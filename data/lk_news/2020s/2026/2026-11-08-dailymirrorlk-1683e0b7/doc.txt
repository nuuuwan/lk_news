Customs seize 39 drones, 16 microphones from SJB MP

Customs officials have seized 39 drones and 16 microphones brought into the country by Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) MP Nalin Bandara without the required approvals, Customs Media Spokesperson Chandana Punchihewa said.

He said the items were seized at the Katunayake Airport on the morning of August 11.

The MP arrived from Singapore aboard SriLankan Airlines flight UL 307 at 11.23 a.m. when Customs officers detected the equipment.

According to the Customs spokesperson, the drones had been brought into the country without the required import licences, approval from the Telecommunications Regulatory Commission of Sri Lanka (TRCSL) or clearance from the Ministry of Defence.
