Indices close in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Hatton National Bank, Overseas Realty and Commercial Bank with the turnover crossing Rs. 4.8 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Dipped Products, Asiri Surgical Hospital and DFCC Bank. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net buyers.

The Banking sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to DFCC Bank, Commercial Bank and Sampath Bank) whilst the sector index lost 0.71%.

The share price of DFCC Bank moved up by Rs 4.75 (3.39%) to close at Rs 144.75. The share price of Commercial Bank recorded a loss of Rs 0.50 (0.22%) to close at Rs 224.75. The share price of Sampath Bank declined by Rs 1.00 (0.63%) to close at Rs 157.00. Materials sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Dipped Products) whilst the sector index increased by 0.67%. The share price of Dipped Products increased by Rs 2.10 (3.05%) to close at Rs 70.90. Asiri Surgical Hospital was also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Asiri Surgical Hospital lost Rs 0.90 (6.72%) to close at Rs 12.50.
