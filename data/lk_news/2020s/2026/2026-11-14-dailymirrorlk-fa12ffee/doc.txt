Sriyani Dress Point marks Colombo debut with new showroom

Sriyani Dress Point has entered Colombo’s fashion retail market with the opening of its new showroom in Wellawatte.

The ceremonial opening was graced by Indian actress Pooja Hegde, alongside cricketers, celebrities and other distinguished guests. The occasion marked the retailer’s arrival in Sri Lanka’s commercial capital and the beginning of a new phase of growth for the Sri Lankan brand.

The Colombo debut followed a teaser campaign under the tagline “Kolamba Ayata, Colombo Hayata”, which built curiosity around the brand’s plans before revealing the new showroom.

Established in 1993, Sriyani Dress Point built its presence through eight showrooms across six cities including Kegalle, Kurunegala, Kandy, Matale, Negombo and Ratnapura, with two each in Kurunegala and Kandy, before entering Colombo. The retailer was recognised as Most Popular Retailer at the Pinnacle Awards 2025.
