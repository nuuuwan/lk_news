Yellow House-Nanotek advance culture-tech sector

From left: Nanotek Computer Solutions Head of Marketing and Business Development Mohammed Zameer, Chief Operation Officer Zakir Zubair, Yellow House Productions co-founder and CEO Hasitha Jayathilaka and co-founder and CCO Prabha Hewage

Yellow House Productions (Pvt.) Ltd and Nanotek Computer Solutions (Pvt.) Ltd announced a formal technology partnership, bringing together Sri Lanka’s most advanced creative technology studio and the country’s leading hardware solutions provider.

Under the partnership, Nanotek Computer Solutions serves as the Official Technology Partner of Yellow House Productions, equipping the studio with professional-grade hardware that powers its creative and development pipeline.

Yellow House Productions was founded on the premise that Sri Lanka has the cultural depth, creative talent and institutional seriousness to build immersive cultural experiences for global audiences. The studio has pursued that premise on its own resources, bootstrapping years of research and development without external funding.

The National Innovation Agency of Sri Lanka has recognised Yellow House Productions for its contribution to the country’s creative technology sector and the studio holds trademarks and industrial design applications under NIPO Sri Lanka.

“We have spent years building something Sri Lanka has never had before: a studio creating research-led immersive cultural experiences for global audiences, from our own conviction and resources. Having Nanotek alongside us is a signal that Sri Lankan companies believe in what we are building,” Yellow House Productions co-founder and CEO Hasitha Jayathilaka said.

“What impressed us most about Yellow House Productions was not just what they are building but how they are building it, with rigour, with purpose and entirely on their own conviction,” said Nanotek Computer Solutions CEO Shazil Azeez.
