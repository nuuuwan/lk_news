Namal Rajapaksa indicted on 24 charges over Airbus transaction

Colombo, Oct. 27 (Daily Mirror) - The Attorney General has filed an indictment comprising 24 charges against Parliamentarian Namal Rajapaksa before the Colombo Permanent Trial-at-Bar in connection with the Airbus transaction, the Colombo Chief Magistrate’s Court was informed today.

Deputy Solicitor General Janaka Bandara informed Colombo Chief Magistrate Asanga S. Bodaragama that the indictment, which lists 59 witnesses, had been filed before the Colombo Permanent Trial-at-Bar.
