Naval, fishing communities urged to remain vigilant amid rough seas

Naval and fishing communities engaged in activities in the sea areas from Hambantota to Pottuvil have been urged to remain vigilant as rough sea conditions and strong winds are expected in the area.

According to the latest weather advisory, sea areas off the coast from Hambantota to Pottuvil may become rough at times, with strong gusty winds reaching 50 to 60 kmph.

The wave height in the sea areas extending from Chilaw to Pottuvil via Galle, Matara and Hambantota could increase to between 2.0 and 3.0 metres. However, authorities stressed that this refers to wave heights in the sea and not the height of waves reaching the shore.

There is also a possibility of seawater inundating low-lying coastal areas extending from Beruwala to Matara via Galle.

The naval and fishing communities engaged in fishing and other maritime activities in the affected sea areas have therefore been requested to exercise caution and remain vigilant.
