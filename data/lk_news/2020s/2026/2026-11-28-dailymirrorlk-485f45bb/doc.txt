Colombo Coffee Company reopens at One Galle Face

Colombo Coffee Company (CCC) has reopened its outlet at One Galle Face (OGF).

Situated on Level 05 of one of Colombo’s leading commercial and lifestyle destinations, the reopening reinforces the company’s long-term commitment to Sri Lanka’s growing coffee market.

The renewed outlet gives customers a place to explore CCC’s range of coffee varieties, home and commercial brewing equipment, and complementary solutions. It is designed to serve both the casual coffee enthusiast and the business owner looking for dependable equipment, with a team on hand to guide visitors through their options and share practical advice on preparing a quality cup.

The outlet also brings product discovery together with the everyday enjoyment of coffee. Visitors can pause, relax and savour a freshly brewed cup within the space, experiencing the brand first-hand before making a purchase.

Commenting on the reopening, Kushan Samararatne, Chief Executive Officer of Colombo Coffee Company said, “The reopening of our One Galle Face outlet reflects our confidence in the Sri Lankan coffee market and our commitment to making quality coffee and equipment more accessible to consumers. Interest in coffee culture and home brewing is steadily growing, and we want to be the partner customers trust, whether they are discovering coffee for the first time or equipping a home or business. We are pleased to return to this location and look forward to welcoming both returning patrons and new customers.”
