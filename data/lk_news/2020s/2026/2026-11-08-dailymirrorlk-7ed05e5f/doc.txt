President extends Deepavali greetings, calls for unity and compassion

President Anura Kumara Dissanayake has extended his Deepavali greetings to Hindu devotees in Sri Lanka and across the world, calling for greater unity, compassion and coexistence.

In his Deepavali message issued on November 6, the President said the festival symbolised the triumph of light over darkness and knowledge over ignorance.

Referring to the victory of Lord Krishna over the demon Narakasura, he said the festival reminded people of the importance of righteousness and the triumph of goodness over evil.

He said the true meaning of Deepavali went beyond lighting oil lamps in homes, stressing that it should also kindle love, compassion, brotherhood and humanity in people's hearts.

The President said the festival provided an opportunity to renew the commitment to building a more just and compassionate society by choosing kindness over hatred, coexistence over division and justice over injustice.

He reaffirmed the Government's commitment to transforming society, the economy and politics while building a nation that respects all religious and cultural identities and treats everyone with equality and dignity.

Expressing hope that Sri Lanka's journey towards becoming an "Island of Peace and Humanity" would be further illuminated by the Deepavali celebrations, he wished all Hindu devotees a peaceful, joyful and prosperous festival.
