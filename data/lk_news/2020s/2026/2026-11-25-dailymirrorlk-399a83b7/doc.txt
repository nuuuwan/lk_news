Pope Francis’ private notes reveal visions of the devil and a plan to allow married men to be priests

yahoo news - Pope Francis had three visions of the devil in his life. He described them in notes discovered by Argentine cardinal Víctor Manuel Fernández and published this Tuesday in a book entitled Il diario personale di Papa Francesco (Libreria vaticana edizioni).

"I recalled three definite episodes in which I do not doubt that he appeared physically", read the writings of Pope Francis, previewed by several Italian newspapers. "He stared at me with eyes of fire."

The book, which is being published in five languages in 94 countries, brings together diaries, notes and documents dated between 1992 and 2024, which were in a box found by the current Prefect of the Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith, who is also Argentine, like Pope Francis.

Pope Francis, born José Mario Bergoglio, had his first vision of the devil as a bishop in Buenos Aires claiming "I saw him from the car window. I do not forget his eyes, threatening and at the same time seductive".

A "look" Bergoglio said he would never forget. The second encounter, when he was still in Argentina, was "stronger than the previous one".

"He looked at me with eyes of fire. I do not forget that look", the description states. "They were like rays of fire. Then he tried to come closer and I ran away, clutching the Rosary and pleading with the Virgin [Mary, mother of Jesus]... Curiosity struggled against the Rosary. And the Virgin saved me."

The third encounter took place in January 2021, when he was already leading the Church.

"During a meeting, I saw him and heard his voice, triumphant, master of everything: "Now I have obtained everything I wanted", the pontiff noted.

"I cannot forget the powerful rasp of his voice. The Lord gave me the opportunity to leave that meeting and so I was freed."

The Pope's notes reveal he immediately fled from these apparitions so as not to enter into dialogue with absolute evil, which is why his descriptions are brief.

These were times of spiritual crisis for the Argentine pontiff, between 2021 and 2022, observes Cardinal Fernández who, although he has been one of the religious figures closest to the Pope for almost twenty years, never received confidences from him on the subject.

"The Pope perceives what he calls pagan seduction. The difficulty of managing power. The power a pope has is not only internal to the curia [administrative body of the Catholic Church]; it concerns international relations. And for him it represented a strong temptation", said the cardinal in an interview on Monday with the newspaper Corriere della Sera.

"There are no gestures of his that show a man obsessed with power, who mistreats people. It was something interior. The fear of reaching a demonic point. The need to remember that he was a simple man, a creature of God, someone in need", Fernández added, admitting that he had doubts about publishing these writings so as not to "upset anyone".

Among the visions experienced and described by Begoglio in fact, there is also one of hell. "I saw the gates of hell. Pagan seduction with all its force", he wrote in Spanish in the original collected by the prefect.

"I leaned forward to look a little and it terrified me. It was before sleeping", Francis recounted. "Into hell one slips as the most natural thing. It is not the first time in my life that I have had this experience, but this time it was of a devouring intensity. It is dangerous to approach the gates of hell because the flames can come out and burn you."

Fernández has tried to explain what the Pope's temptations might have been, whom he describes as a "mystic".

"In no way is it a hedonistic seduction. Bergoglio was completely uninterested in hedonism, eros, sensory pleasure. He was a very moderate man. Of absolute austerity. He never took a holiday in his life, he did not even have an ice cream", he told the newspaper.

"Pagan temptation is to reject the true God in order to worship idols, or worse still to worship oneself. The strongest temptation is power badly managed", the cardinal added, citing the pontiff.

"The temptation of power that places you above others, that induces you to subject others to your projects and to treat them like puppets, for him became terrible", Fernández explained.

Bergoglio was tormented by accusations he had received in Argentina of having been excessively harsh with subordinates and of having somehow abused his power during the ecclesiastical rise that led him to become provincial superior of the Jesuits (from 1973 to 1979) and Archbishop of Buenos Aires (from 1998 to 2013, when he was elected Pope).

A political power from which he also had to defend himself physically, according to the Argentine cardinal, who said that Francis never visited Argentina also because of intelligence information indicating that "the far right" would "organise something against him", since he was considered an "enemy".

Yet, tensions also existed within the Vatican and the Curia, against which he took several decisions while avoiding others. Among the innovations, partly moderated by his successor, Robert Prevost, the current Leo XIV, who said he was a "great shepherd and an irreproachable man", there was also the possibility of ordaining married men as priests.

"He wanted to take this decision. But he wanted to avoid internal battles, verbal violence. When we prepared the document to allow blessings for same-sex couples there was a very harsh reaction...", Fernández revealed.

The Pope wrote that he controlled temptations and doubts by entrusting himself to the the Virgin Mary, particularly during his trip to Fatima - a pilgrimage site in Portugal - in October 2023.
