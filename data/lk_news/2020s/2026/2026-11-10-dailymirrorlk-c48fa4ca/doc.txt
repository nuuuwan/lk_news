Government aims to make SriLankan Airlines profitable

Colombo, Nov. 10 (Daily Mirror) - The Government is determined to turn SriLankan Airlines into a profitable national carrier, despite the airline continuing to face mounting financial losses, Cabinet Spokesperson Minister Nalinda Jayatissa said.

He said some progress had already been made towards improving the airline’s financial position, adding that the Government hoped to make it financially sustainable as tourist arrivals to the country continued to increase.

Jayatissa said the Government was committed to bringing the airline to a position where it could operate without incurring losses. The financial allocation required for the initiative is expected to be outlined in the 2027 Budget, he added.
