JKH unveils 1st Wendy’s with Elephant House drinks on menu

From left: John Keells Group Chairperson Krishan Balendra, Ambassador of the United States of America to Sri Lanka and the Maldives Eric S. Meyer and Wendy’s International Senior Vice President and Managing Director APMEA Chris Conway

Chicken-heavy menu with local twists such as Lankan Fire Burger

John Keells Holdings (JKH) has pledged the full strength and resources of the group to making Wendy’s a success, as the US burger chain unveiled its first Sri Lankan restaurant at One Galle Face this week, ahead of its public opening, with the group’s own Elephant House soft drinks on its menu.

Addressing the ceremonial opening in Colombo, JKH Chairperson and CEO Krishan Balendra noted that the Group Executive Committee considered the franchise a strong fit when it was first proposed.

Wendy’s marks the conglomerate’s entry into quick service restaurants and the group already has a presence in food and beverages through the Elephant House soft drinks and ice cream, Keells processed meats and hot meals sold at the Keells supermarkets.

Elephant House Ginger Beer, Cream Soda and Necto are on the drinks menu alongside Coca-Cola, Coke Zero and Sprite, giving the group’s beverage arm a foothold in its new restaurant business from the outset.

“We saw an opportunity because we feel there is a vacuum in this space in the market in Sri Lanka. It will be an excellent addition to the offering to the Sri Lankan consumer,” Balendra remarked.

He added that the partnership would bring Wendy’s expertise, training, technology and modern ways of working “not just for the Wendy’s franchise but to Sri Lanka”.

“We will put our full weight behind making this business a success,” he assured the stakeholders.

Wendy’s arrives in a market that has been without McDonald’s since March 2024, when the US chain ended its franchise arrangement in Sri Lanka. Softlogic-run Burger King and Cargills-operated KFC remain among the most established global fast-food names on the island.

“One of my priorities as US Ambassador is to help create opportunities for the American companies here,” US Ambassador to Sri Lanka Eric S. Meyer told the gathering.

“It’s not just about bringing an American company to Sri Lanka. It’s about bringing American innovation, standards and quality as well,” he added.

Meyer also pointed out that the opening creates opportunities for Sri Lankan producers and suppliers and he thanked the John Keells group for the partnership.

He said he looked forward to seeing more Wendy’s outlets “open here in Sri Lanka, I think very soon”.

The menu leans heavily on chicken. The Classic and Spicy Chicken Burgers, the hand-breaded Chickenator, a Grilled Chicken Burger, Chicken Tenders, Chicken Bites and a value range of Chicken Tender Burgers sit alongside Wendy’s signature square beef burgers, which are built on a 120-gram patty and come in single, double and triple versions.

The local flavours come through in the Lankan Fire Burger, billed as the “Nai Miris” burger, as well as a Yellow Rice Bowl and a Paneer Double Decker. The brand’s signature Frosty dessert, made with real dairy, comes in vanilla and chocolate.

The Wendy’s Company Senior Vice President and Managing Director for Asia Pacific, Middle East and Africa Chris Conway explained that the Lankan Fire Burger was developed for the Sri Lankan market from the start.  He said this reflected the brand’s aim of being “globally great and locally loved”.

Conway recalled that around 18 months had passed since the two parties first spoke and that the restaurant had arrived nine months after the kick-off meeting.

“So, here’s to many, many more Wendy’s babies in the future,” he quipped.

Meanwhile, the JKH management told the investors at the group’s second-quarter investor webinar earlier this month that the group would “definitely look to open two outlets before the end of this financial year or maybe a third”.

The franchise also carries a minimum outlet commitment over five years, which the management is “quite confident” of meeting. At the first-quarter webinar in July, the management said the business could open “about eight to 10 stores annually. Maybe more”.

The restaurants are run by John Keells Restaurants (Private) Limited, a wholly-owned subsidiary set up in 2025. Wendy’s was founded in the United States in 1969 and has more than 7,000 restaurants worldwide.

To mark the launch, the restaurant will offer unlimited drinks and sauces through its opening month.
