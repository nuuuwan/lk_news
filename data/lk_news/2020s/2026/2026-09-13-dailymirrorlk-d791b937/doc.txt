India set Sri Lanka 184-run target in Asia Cup Final

SEP.13 (Mirror Sports) - India Women posted 183 for 5 against Sri Lanka Women in the Women’s Asia Cup 2026 final in Dubai, setting Sri Lanka a target of 184 runs for victory.

Shafali Verma led the way with a blistering 68 off 39 balls, while Smriti Mandhana added 59 off 39 as India made a strong start and finished with a formidable total.

India Women have won the toss and elected to bat first against Sri Lanka Women in the Women’s Asia Cup 2026 final at the Dubai International Cricket Stadium on Sunday.

The two-time finalists are meeting in a highly anticipated summit clash, with India looking to extend their record as the most successful side in Women’s Asia Cup history, while defending champions Sri Lanka are aiming to retain the title.

India have been in impressive form throughout the tournament, defeating Thailand, Hong Kong and Pakistan in the group stage before overcoming Bangladesh in the semi-final to book their place in the final.

Sri Lanka, meanwhile, secured their spot in the title clash after defeating Pakistan in their semi-final. Chamari Athapaththu’s side will now look to produce another strong performance and defend the crown they won in the previous edition.

With India choosing to bat first, Sri Lanka’s bowlers will have the first opportunity to put pressure on the Indian top order, while India will look to build a strong total on the Dubai surface.

India: Harmanpreet Kaur (c), Smriti Mandhana (vc), Shafali Verma, Bharti Fulmali, Deepti Sharma, Richa Ghosh (wk), G Kamalini (wk), Arundhati Reddy, Prema Rawat, Radha Yadav, Sree Charani, Renuka Thakur, Nandni Sharma, Kranti Gaud, Pratika Rawal.

Sri Lanka: Chamari Athapaththu (c), Mithali Ayodhya, Kavisha Dilhari, Imesha Dulani, Vishmi Gunaratne, Kawya Kavindi, Sanjana Kavindi, Sugandika Kumari, Nimasha Meepage, Kaushini Nuthyangana, Hasini Perera, Chamudi Praboda, Harshitha Samarawickrama, Nilakshika Silva, Chethana Vimukthi.
