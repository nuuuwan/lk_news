WhatsApp 119 service for hearing-impaired

Colombo, Sep. 9 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Police have launched a special WhatsApp video service to enable members of the hearing-impaired community to lodge emergency complaints with the Police Emergency Call Unit 119.

The new service was introduced as part of programmes marking the 160th anniversary of the Sri Lanka Police on September 3, 2026, under the patronage of the President. It will officially commence operations on September 10.

Until a dedicated short-code number is introduced, users can access the service by saving 011 280 9980 to their mobile phone contacts and making an initial audio call through WhatsApp.

The call will be automatically disconnected, after which a web link will be sent to the user's mobile phone. Users must open the link through WhatsApp, select “Join Browser” and then “Join Meeting” to connect to a live video call with police officials.

The Police Media Division said the service has been introduced to improve emergency access and safeguard the rights of the hearing-impaired community.
