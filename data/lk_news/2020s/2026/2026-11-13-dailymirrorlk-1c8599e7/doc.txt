SJB member helps pass NPP-controlled Thanamalvila Pradeshiya Sabha budget

Nov. 14 (Daily Mirror) - In what appeared to be a political somersault, the budget of National People's Power (NPP)-controlled Thanamalwila Pradeshiya Sabha was passed with the support of a Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) member.

As the vote resulted in a tie with 17 votes in favour and 17 against, the budget was passed through the use of the Chairman’s casting vote.

Notably, a female member representing the Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) voted in favour of the budget.

In a previous vote held on November 4, the budget had been defeated by a margin of three votes, with 14 members voting in favour and 17 against.

That outcome was influenced by the absence of two members from the governing side due to illness, and the absence of one opposition member. The NPP has secured 16 seats in the Thanamalwila Pradeshiya Sabha, while the opposition collectively obtained 18 seats.

During the budget debate, several heated arguments broke out among the council members.
