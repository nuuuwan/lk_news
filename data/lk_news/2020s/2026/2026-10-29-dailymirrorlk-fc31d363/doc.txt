Alliance Finance plans Rs.1bn bond issue with focus on women-led enterprises

Alliance Finance Company PLC plans to raise up to Rs.1 billion through a three-year bond issue, to fund the eligible lending activities, with the earmarked targets for women-led enterprises and female beneficiaries.

The company’s board approved the proposed issue on October 27, according to a filing with the Colombo Stock Exchange.

Alliance Finance intends to issue up to 10 million listed, rated, unsecured, redeemable senior bonds, at Rs.100 each.

The gender-responsive, “orange-labelled” bonds will carry a three-year tenure, with the principal repayable in a single payment at maturity.

The proceeds will be allocated to the eligible social project categories, including micro, small and medium enterprise (MSME) value chain financing, MSME climate financing and microfinance. The company said these categories would include ring-fenced targets for women-led businesses and female beneficiaries.

The proposed fundraising remains subject to the necessary regulatory approvals, including clearance from the rating agency, Central Bank of Sri Lanka and Colombo Stock Exchange and other conditions being acceptable to the company.
