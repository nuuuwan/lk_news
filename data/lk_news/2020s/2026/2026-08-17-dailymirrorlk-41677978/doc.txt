Seifert powers Super Giants to maiden Hundred title

AUG.17 (Mirror Sports) - Manchester Super Giants clinched their first men’s Hundred title with a five-wicket victory over Trent Rockets in a thrilling final at Lord’s.

Chasing 159 for victory, Super Giants appeared firmly in control as Tim Seifert smashed a brilliant 72 off 38 balls to put the chase on track.

Seifert’s explosive innings featured seven sixes and four fours before his dismissal briefly opened the door for the Rockets.

The Super Giants then suffered a late wobble, with Heinrich Klaasen and Michael Bracewell falling in quick succession to leave them needing three runs from the final three balls.

Liam Dawson held his nerve, smashing the next ball over mid-wicket for six to seal the title with two deliveries to spare.

Earlier, Trent Rockets posted 158 for 8, with Aneurin Donald leading their late charge with a rapid 38 off 19 balls. Lewis Gregory also contributed a quickfire 32.

However, Seifert’s stunning knock proved decisive as Manchester Super Giants held their nerve to complete the chase and lift the trophy.

Formerly known as Manchester Originals, Super Giants had lost their previous two men’s Hundred finals. This time, they finally ended their wait for the title in memorable fashion at Lord’s.

Manchester Super Giants 162-5 (98 balls): Seifert 72 (38); Amir 3-29
