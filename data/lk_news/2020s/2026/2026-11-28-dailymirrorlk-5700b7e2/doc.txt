SheTrades Sri Lanka Hub empowers women entrepreneurs through int’l business exposure

Two Sri Lankan women entrepreneurs supported by the SheTrades Sri Lanka Hub, hosted by the Sri Lanka Export Development Board (EDB), participated in an international capacity building programme in China aimed at strengthening the business capabilities of small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) from developing countries and enhancing their engagement in international trade.

The two entrepreneurs, representing Cinnomina Exports and Sinharaja Herbs (Pvt) Ltd, participated in the ‘Seminar of the Business Study Tour for Small and Medium Sized Enterprises of Belt and Road Developing Countries to China’, held from 28 October to 10 November.

The programme was organised through the collaboration between the International Trade Centre (ITC) and the Academy for International Business Officials (AIBO) under China’s foreign-aid training programme. It brought together 38 enterprise representatives from 15 developing countries, providing a valuable international platform for knowledge sharing, capacity development and business networking.

The programme aimed to enhance participants’ understanding of the Chinese business environment and strengthen their capacity to explore international market opportunities. It provided exposure to key aspects of international business, including Chinese market requirements, product quality standards, export pricing, delivery conditions, market trends and competitive positioning. Participants also had opportunities to exchange experiences with entrepreneurs from other developing countries and gain insights into different business practices and approaches to international trade.

An important component of the programme was the opportunity to gain practical exposure to the Chinese market through the China International Import Expo (CIIE) 2026, held in Shanghai. As part of their participation, the two Sri Lankan entrepreneurs also had the opportunity to display and promote their products at the ITC Pavilion, providing additional exposure to potential Chinese and international buyers and opportunities to establish business connections.

Participation in this programme represents a valuable learning and development opportunity for Sri Lankan women entrepreneurs, enabling them to strengthen their business knowledge, gain international exposure and explore opportunities for expanding their businesses beyond domestic markets.

The participation was facilitated through the SheTrades Sri Lanka Hub, hosted by the EDB, which works towards empowering women-led businesses through capacity development, international market exposure, networking and access to trade opportunities.

Through initiatives of this nature, the EDB continues to support the growth and internationalisation of women-led enterprises, strengthening their capacity to compete in global markets and contribute to Sri Lanka’s export development.
