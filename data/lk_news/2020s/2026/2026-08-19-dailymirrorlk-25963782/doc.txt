Yield curve holds firm despite modest buying

The secondary market saw modest buying interest yesterday, although the activity lacked sufficient momentum to push yields lower, while trading volumes remained moderate.

At the short end of the curve 15.02.2028 and 01.05.2028 traded at 10.00% and 10.07% respectively while 15.12.2029 changed hands at 10.60%. In the 2030 segment, 15.05.2030, 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 traded from 10.75% to 10.95%.

Further along the curve 01.02.2031 changed hands between 10.95% to 11.00% while 15.12.2032 was seen trading between 11.40% to 11.35%. Modest activity was evident across long tenors as well, with 15.08.2036 and 01.07.2037 trading at 12.07% and 12.12% respectively.

On the external front, the LKR appreciated against the USD, standing at Rs. 332.29/USD, compared to Rs. 333.09/USD seen earlier. Market liquidity in the banking system contracted to Rs.  292.85bn from Rs. 301.35bn recorded previously.
