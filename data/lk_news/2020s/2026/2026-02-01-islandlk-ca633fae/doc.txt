Bitget hits $18B in tokenised stock trading as institutions drive growth

A new Messari report reveals Bitget has processed $18 billion in tokenized stock futures volume, with institutional traders dominating its spot market.

The research highlights a structural shift on the platform, where 82% of spot trading volume now comes from professional firms. Tokenized equities, launched in July 2025, have become a fastest-growing segment, with $13.6 billion traded in November alone.

“Tokenized equities are moving from experimentation to real market structure,” said Bitget CEO Gracy Chen, noting demand for continuous, on-chain access to traditional assets.

Tesla led trading with over $6.3 billion in volume. East Asia accounts for 39.6% of activity, with growing interest from Latin America and Europe. The data underscores a global trend toward consolidated platforms for both digital and traditional assets.
