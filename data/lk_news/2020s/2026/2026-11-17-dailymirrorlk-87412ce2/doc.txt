Graduate teacher recruitment: School preference system opens today

Colombo, Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - The Ministry of Education has announced that the online system for submitting school preferences for graduate teacher recruits will be available from today (November 17).

The system is for graduates recruited to Class 3-1 (a) of the Sri Lanka Teachers' Service to fill vacancies for Sinhala, Tamil and English medium teachers in national and provincial schools islandwide.

Applicants can access the “Teacher Recruitment Application System” through the Ministry of Education's official website and submit their school preferences.

To log in, applicants must enter their National Identity Card (NIC) number or examination index number, along with the one-time password (OTP) provided in the letter sent with their interview call-up.

The Ministry said applicants who have not yet received their letters by post would not be able to access the system until they receive them.

However, it assured applicants that there was no need for concern over delays in receiving interview call letters, adding that the system would remain accessible until each applicant's scheduled interview date.

Those who have not received their letters by the interview date will be given an opportunity to submit their school preferences on the day of the interview.
