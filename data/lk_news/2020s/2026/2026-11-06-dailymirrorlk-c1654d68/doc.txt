Broad-based rally drives yields lower across the curve

Secondary market sentiment turned decisively bullish yesterday, supported by the continued moderation in oil prices and an improvement in global risk appetite following the US midterm elections.

Strong buying interest across the yield curve drove yields lower, accompanied by elevated trading volumes and robust investor participation. The broad-based rally signals an early improvement in investor confidence and a more favourable global risk environment in the aftermath of the midterm elections.

Within the 2030 segment, 01.03.2030, 15.05.2030, 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 traded from 11.40% to 11.00%. Further along the curve, 01.02.2031 and 01.12.2031 traded between 11.45% to 11.01% while 15.12.2032 was seen trading from 11.70% to 11.60%.

At the belly of the curve, 15.01.2033, 01.06.2033 and 01.11.2033 traded at 11.80%, 11.85% and 12.00% respectively. Moreover, 15.06.2034, 15.09.2034 and 15.10.2034 traded from 12.40% to 12.00% while 15.03.2035 and 15.06.2035 both traded between 12.20% to 12.15%. Finally, at the long end of the curve, 15.08.2036 changed hands from 12.65% to 12.25%.

On the external front, the LKR depreciated against the greenback, standing at LKR 332.79/USD compared to LKR 332.64/USD recorded earlier. Market liquidity in the banking system expanded to LKR 421.18bn compared to LKR 389.73bn seen previously.
