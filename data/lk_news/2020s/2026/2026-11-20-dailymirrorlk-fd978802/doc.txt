CBSL Governor warns inflation could rise to 9%

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka’s inflation rate could increase to around 9% by next month, Central Bank Governor Dr. Nandalal Weerasinghe said today (20), warning that the prolonged conflict in the Middle East and elevated global fuel prices were putting further pressure on domestic prices.

Speaking at the Central Bank’s Monetary Policy Review media briefing, Dr. Weerasinghe said inflation had reached 8.3% at the end of last month and was expected to rise further in the coming weeks.

He explained that when the Central Bank decided to increase interest rates in May 2026, its projections indicated that inflation would peak between 7% and 8% before gradually declining.

However, those projections had since changed due to the longer-than-anticipated conflict in the Middle East and its impact on global energy prices.

The Governor stressed that bringing inflation down to the targeted 5% level as quickly as possible remained a key priority, warning that persistently high inflation would adversely affect the national economy, people's living standards and their purchasing power.
