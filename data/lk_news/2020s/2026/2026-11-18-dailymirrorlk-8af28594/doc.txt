Colombo Needs More Than Drain/Canal Cleaning to Prevent Flooding

Colombo Mayoress  V. C. Balthazaar is being blamed  for  not cleaning the drains in advance as  per request  by   PM Dr. H. Amarasuriya   a couple of weeks  ago which resulted in a deluge in the city of Colombo.  I am of the opinion that the Colombo Municipal Council (CMC) must adopt a comprehensive, long-term flood-prevention strategy instead of relying predominantly on cleaning drains and canals whenever heavy rainfall is anticipated.

While regular maintenance is essential, it is inadequate to address flooding during intense rainfall exceeding 30 mm. Several practical measures should be considered. These include constructing additional and wider stormwater drains and also replacing undersised underground pipes. Underground rainwater retention tanks constructed beneath public parks and playgrounds could temporarily store excess water and release it gradually.

The CMC should also explore rainwater absorption pits to reduce surface runoff. Preventing unauthorised reclamation of marshlands, and constructing retention ponds would further strengthen Colombo’s natural flood-control capacity.

CMC must also coordinate with the Sri Lanka Land Development Corporation, Irrigation Department and other relevant agencies, as flooding cannot be tackled by the CMC alone.

The objective should be to make Colombo a flood-resilient city, not merely a city that cleans its drains before every rainy season.
