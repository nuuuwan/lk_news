Mobile Number Portability service will not launch in September

Colombo, Aug. 7 (Daily Mirror) - The end of September is the deadline for telecom companies to reach an agreement on implementing Mobile Number Portability (MNP), and not the date for launching the service, the Ministry of Digital Economy said.

The clarification was issued following reports that the MNP facility would be introduced in September, based on remarks made by Deputy Minister of Digital Economy Eranga Weeraratne in Parliament on August 6.

The Deputy Minister said discussions were ongoing between the Telecommunications Regulatory Commission of Sri Lanka (TRCSL), the Ministry and telephone operators to resolve issues related to implementing the service.

The Ministry said if the relevant companies fail to reach an agreement by the end of September, the TRCSL will take steps to issue a regulatory order under the Sri Lanka Telecommunications Act and operator licence conditions.

It added that if an agreement is not reached, the government would consider introducing Mobile Number Portability first, as it would benefit a larger number of subscribers, before implementing Fixed Number Portability.

The official launch date of the MNP service will be announced later, while the government remains committed to introducing the facility as soon as possible, the Ministry said.
