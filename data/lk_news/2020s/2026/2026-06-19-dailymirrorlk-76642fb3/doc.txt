Mid-year mega sale at BMICH

Shoppers can enjoy discounts of up to 70% at the Mid-Year Clearance Mega Sale, which is being held at the BMICH in Colombo from June 19 to 21.

The exhibition features a wide range of products, including textiles, footwear, home appliances, gift items and many other consumer goods, attracting bargain hunters from across the country. Pix by Kithsiri De Mel
