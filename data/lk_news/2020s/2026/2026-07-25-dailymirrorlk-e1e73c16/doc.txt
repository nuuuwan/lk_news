Cassilingham receives appointment as SLPP Jaffna District Organizer

Colombo, July 25 (Daily Mirror): Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) Politburo Member G. Cassilingham today officially received his letter of appointment as the party’s Jaffna District Organizer from SLPP National Organiser Namal Rajapaksa at the party headquarters on Nelum Mawatha in Colombo.

The appointment reflects the party leadership’s confidence in Cassilingham as the SLPP continues to strengthen its organisational network in the Northern Province.
