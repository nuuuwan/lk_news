‘Dialog Air Fibre powers a new era of Ultra Fast Home WiFi’

Dialog Axiata PLC, Sri Lanka’s #1 connectivity provider, announced the commercial launch of Dialog Air Fibre, the country’s first 5G-powered, ultra-fast wireless broadband service, following exceptional demand during its pre-order phase.

Powered by Dialog’s rapidly expanding 5G network, now spanning over 1,000 sites nationwide, Air Fibre delivers high-speed internet without the need for physical cables. Built for modern digital lifestyles, the service seamlessly supports streaming, gaming, video conferencing, and remote work across multiple devices, and includes a built-in battery offering up to six hours of connectivity during power outages.

Air Fibre is available in three plans: 100GB at LKR 2,795, 200GB at LKR 3,795, and 300GB at LKR 5,495 (tax inclusive). Dialog Air Fibre Customers also enjoy complimentary access to over 100 TV channels via Dialog Play, along with a range of digital entertainment benefits, bringing connectivity and content together in a single, compelling offering.

“Dialog Air Fibre redefines how Sri Lankan households access high-speed broadband,” said Tharindra Gooneratne, Head of Business, Fixed Broadband and Global, Dialog Axiata PLC. “By leveraging the power of our 5G network, we are delivering a flexible, home broadband solution that meets the evolving connectivity needs of our customers.”

Each Air Fibre connection undergoes a comprehensive onboarding and validation process, including a location assessment and installation within 48 hours of purchase at no additional cost. Customers who do not meet the required service standards following validation are eligible for a full refund, underscoring Dialog’s commitment to quality and transparency.
