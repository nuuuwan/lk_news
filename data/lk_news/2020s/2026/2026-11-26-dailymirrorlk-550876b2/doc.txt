Sri Lankan-born Professor knighted by King Charles meets PM

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya met with President and Vice-Chancellor of the University of Leicester, Professor Sir Nishan Canagarajah, who was recently knighted by King Charles III, at Parliament yesterday (25), to discuss strengthening cooperation between Sri Lanka and the United Kingdom in higher education and research.

Professor Sir Nishan was welcomed back to Sri Lanka by the Prime Minister, who congratulated him on the honour and acknowledged his achievements in the field.

Discussions focused on developing academic partnerships, joint research initiatives and knowledge exchange, while expanding educational opportunities for Sri Lankan students, including access to academic programmes and pathways to higher studies.

During the meeting, the Prime Minister outlined the Government's ongoing education reforms, highlighting the need to develop the vocational education sector and better integrate it into the national education system.

She stressed the importance of changing conventional perceptions of vocational education and ensuring that it is recognised as an equally valued pathway to further education and employment.

The Prime Minister said the reforms aimed to transform the education system and equip young people with the knowledge and skills needed to meet the demands of a changing world.

She also welcomed opportunities to establish research partnerships and academic collaborations in emerging and priority fields, including artificial intelligence, space engineering and other interdisciplinary areas that support Sri Lanka's development needs.

The Prime Minister further emphasised the importance of expanding access to quality higher education and research opportunities within Sri Lanka to reduce the need for students to seek such opportunities overseas.

Both sides expressed their willingness to explore further cooperation, enabling Sri Lankan students to benefit from academic programmes, research initiatives and international exposure through such partnerships.

The meeting was attended by Lady Thabithal Canagarajah, members of the University of Leicester delegation and Additional Secretary to the Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education Apsara Caldera.
