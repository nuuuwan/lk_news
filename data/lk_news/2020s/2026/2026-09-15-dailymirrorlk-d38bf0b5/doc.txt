Heavy rain above 100mm possible in several provinces

Colombo, Sep. 15 (Daily Mirror) - A low-level atmospheric disturbance persisting in the vicinity of Sri Lanka is expected to bring cloudy skies and showers or thundershowers to several parts of the country during the next 36 hours, the Meteorological Department said.

In a weather forecast issued at 4 p.m. today (15), the Department said showers or thundershowers could occur at times in the Western, Sabaragamuwa, Central, Uva, Eastern and Southern provinces.

Heavy rainfall exceeding 100 mm is likely at some locations, it said.

Showers or thundershowers are also expected at several places elsewhere in the island after 1 p.m.

The public has been advised to take adequate precautions to minimise possible damage caused by temporary localised strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.
