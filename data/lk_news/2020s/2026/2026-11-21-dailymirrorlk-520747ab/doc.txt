Ting debuts with new approach to how SL shops, sells, delivers

Ting is now available in Colombo, bringing food, groceries and everyday essentials together in one app, with multi-vendor ordering and built-in bill splitting

Sri Lankan-owned, innovation-first platform launched with free delivery for a limited time, connecting consumers, businesses and delivery riders through a locally built ecosystem

Sri Lankan technology company Edine Services (Pvt.) Ltd has launched ‘Ting’, a locally developed on-demand platform designed to bring consumers, merchants and delivery operations together within a single ecosystem.

Going beyond conventional delivery services, Ting aims to create a model that combines customer convenience with new commercial opportunities for businesses and a more integrated approach to delivery operations.

At a time when digital platforms are reshaping how people discover, purchase and receive goods, Ting is entering the market with a distinctly local perspective: that the future of delivery should work not only for customers but also for the businesses that serve them and the people who make each delivery possible. Its approach combines locally developed technology with dedicated delivery operations, direct customer support and a merchant model designed to create commercial opportunities beyond individual transactions.

Launching with more than 300 vendors across food, groceries and everyday essentials, Ting gives customers access to a broad selection of products through a single app. Features include Favourite lists for convenient repeat ordering and multi-vendor ordering with integrated bill splitting, supported by a dedicated customer support hotline.

For participating businesses, Ting offers an additional digital sales channel, a wider potential customer base and promotional opportunities to build visibility and generate orders. Its competitive commission structure has been designed with merchant economics in mind, while delivery coverage of up to 10 kilometres creates opportunities for businesses to reach customers beyond their immediate neighbourhoods.

The platform is also investing in the people behind the delivery experience. Through its dedicated rider operation, Ting is investing in facilities, training and support intended to provide delivery riders with a more structured working environment and greater financial stability. The company sees the long-term strength of a delivery ecosystem as dependent on the experience of everyone involved, not simply the convenience delivered to the end customer.

To mark its launch, Ting is offering free delivery on eligible orders for a limited time. The introductory programme also includes promotions from participating merchants and dedicated cardholder promotional days for Commercial Bank, Sampath Bank and HNB customers.

Fully Sri Lankan-owned and locally developed, Ting represents an investment in homegrown technology and the operational capabilities needed to support it. The business also has ambitions to develop connections across the wider supply chain, with the aim of building a more integrated ecosystem for consumers, merchants and delivery partners.

Operating under the brand promise ‘Ting – The one ting for everything’, the app is now available for download on Apple App Store and Google Play Store.
