IMF team to visit Sri Lanka for key economic review in September

An International Monetary Fund (IMF) staff team is scheduled to visit Sri Lanka from September 10 to 23, 2026, for discussions with the government authorities, as the country prepares for another key assessment of its ongoing economic reform programme.

The visit was announced by IMF Mission Chief for Sri Lanka Evan Papageorgiou.

He said the discussions would focus on the combined Seventh Review of Sri Lanka’s reform programme supported under the IMF’s Extended Fund Facility (EFF) arrangement and 2026 Article IV Consultation.

Papageorgiou said the IMF team would communicate its findings at the conclusion of the mission. The upcoming discussions are expected to assess Sri Lanka’s progress in implementing the economic reforms and examine the recent developments in the country’s macroeconomic situation, as the authorities continue efforts to maintain economic stability and strengthen the recovery. The September mission follows an earlier IMF visit led by Papageorgiou, which took place in Colombo from June 24 to 30, 2026. During that mission, the IMF officials reviewed the recent macroeconomic developments and held discussions with the Sri Lankan authorities on progress under the EFF-supported reform programme.
