Piotex Gloves donates to ‘Rebuilding Sri Lanka’ Fund

Piotex Gloves Ltd. together with its Employees’ Welfare Association donated over Rs. 2 million to the Government-established ‘Rebuilding Sri Lanka’ Fund.

The cheque was handed over Piotex Gloves’ Assistant Accountant Gamini Prasanna to the Secretary to the President Dr. Nandika Sanath Kumanayake.
