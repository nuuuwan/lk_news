Further evidence in Lalith-Kugan disappearance case fixed for Feb. 12

Colombo, Nov. 27 (Daily Mirror) - The Jaffna Magistrate’s Court today fixed February 12 for the recording of further evidence from former President Gotabaya Rajapaksa in connection with the case relating to the disappearance of political activists Lalith Kumar Weeraraj and Kugan Muruganandhan in Jaffna in 2011.

Rajapaksa gave evidence today from around 1.30 p.m. to 2.30 p.m. before the Colombo Chief Magistrate’s Court via video conferencing, with the evidence being recorded by the Jaffna Magistrate’s Court.

Former President Gotabaya Rajapaksa appeared before the Colombo Chief Magistrate’s Court online and testified in connection with the case.
