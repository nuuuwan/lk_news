Assetline Insurance Brokers-Asia Asset Finance strengthen integrated insurance solutions

David Pieris Group of Companies Group Chairman and Managing Director Rohana Dissanayake and Asia Asset Finance Director and Chief Operating Officer Roshan Gunasekara exchange partnership agreement in the presence of Assetline Insurance Brokers Chairman Dhanika De Silva, Managing Director/CEO/Principal Officer Nihal Handunge, Director Sales Mahesh De Alwis, Chief Operating Officer Anuradha Hettige, Asia Asset Finance General Manager Product Operations Jayantha Weerappulige, General Manager Branch Operations Sajith Athapaththu, Assistant General Manager Leasing Marketing Ayantha Sampath and Chief Manager Leasing Operations Priyantha Wijesinghe

Assetline Insurance Brokers (Private) Limited (AIBL), a subsidiary of David Pieris Group of Companies and one of Sri Lanka’s leading insurance brokerage firms, has entered into a strategic partnership with Asia Asset Finance PLC (AAF), reinforcing both organisations’ commitment to delivering enhanced financial and insurance solutions to customers across the country.

The partnership marks a significant step toward strengthening integrated service delivery by combining AAF’s extensive financial services reach with AIBL’s specialised insurance expertise. Through this collaboration, the customers of AAF will gain seamless access to comprehensive insurance solutions designed to complement their financial requirements.

Under the agreement, AIBL will provide end-to-end insurance services to the AAF customers, including policy placement, insurance advisory, claims assistance and customised risk management solutions. The collaboration is expected to simplify insurance access, improve service efficiency and ensure customers benefit from competitive coverage supported by professional expertise and strong insurer partnerships.

The management of AIBL stated that the collaboration aligns with the company’s broader strategic vision of expanding its presence within Sri Lanka’s financial services ecosystem while delivering customer-focused insurance solutions backed by technical expertise, trusted insurer relationships and superior service standards.
