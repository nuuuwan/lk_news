University non-academic staff to stage one-day strike tomorrow

Colombo, November 24 (Daily Mirror) - University non-academic staff have decided to launch a massive protest campaign tomorrow (25), accompanied by a one-day symbolic strike, alleging that their salaries and allowances have been reduced.

Mangala Devathilina Dabarera, Co-President of the University Trade Union Joint Committee (UTUJC), said the employees, who staged a symbolic strike last week over budget-related allowances, had decided to step up their trade union action.

"If these issues remain unresolved, we have decided to launch another protest campaign on December 1 and 2, accompanied by a two-day symbolic strike," he said.

He said that the issue was not simply about making new demands but about the reduction of allowances and privileges that university employees had enjoyed previously.
