Vickum of Santani-fame  joins RIL Property Board

RIL Property PLC has appointed Vickum Nawagamuwage to its Board as an Independent Non-Executive Director.

He is a Sri Lankan born entrepreneur and founder of the world-renowned, purpose-built wellness resort, Santani. He was educated at Harvard and the University of Maryland, and worked in strategy consulting both at Deloitte Consulting and KPMG in the US.
