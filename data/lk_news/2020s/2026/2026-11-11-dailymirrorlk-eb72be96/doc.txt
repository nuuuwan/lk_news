Indian Health Minister arrives in Sri Lanka

India’s Union Minister of Health and Family Welfare, Jagat Prakash Nadda, arrived in Colombo today (11) to attend the inaugural BIMSTEC Health Ministers’ Meeting, which brings together regional representatives to discuss cooperation in healthcare and shared public health challenges.

Nadda was received upon arrival by Indian High Commissioner to Sri Lanka Santosh Jha.

Sri Lanka is hosting the first BIMSTEC Health Ministers’ Meeting as the lead country for public health cooperation within the regional grouping.

The meeting is expected to provide a platform for member states of the Bay of Bengal Initiative for Multi-Sectoral Technical and Economic Cooperation (BIMSTEC) to strengthen collaboration, advance shared public health priorities and address common healthcare challenges.
