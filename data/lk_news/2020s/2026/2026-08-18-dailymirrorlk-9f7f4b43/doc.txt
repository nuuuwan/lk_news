Huge challenge before SL to avoid another economic crisis: Ranil

Colombo, Aug. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka is facing a huge challenge to prevent another economic crisis in 2027/28 as it has to find funds to start repaying its debts, former President Ranil Wickremesinghe said today.

"Sri Lanka will have to increase its reserves up to USD 15 billion and will have to issue bonds worth 3 billion in order to raise funds in order to start repaying its debts by 2028. This is a huge challenge before the country. The Central Bank has said Sri Lanka's foreign reserves will only increase up to USD 8 billion. How are we to find the remaining amount to begin repaying the debts?" he questioned at the launch of the book 'Yudda Dekakata Ura Dee' written by former Minister Ranjith Siyambalapitiya, in Colombo.
