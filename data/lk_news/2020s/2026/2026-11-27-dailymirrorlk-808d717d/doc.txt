Almost half a million people attend Indian mega-wedding with helipads, horses and 500 chefs

Daily Mail - An estimated 400,000 guests attended a wedding hosted by a politician in India's poorest state, with some outlets claiming as many as one million people showed up.

Anant Singh, a six-time lawmaker who faces dozens of criminal cases including murder charges, invited villagers, political allies and VIPs to his daughter's wedding in the eastern state of Bihar. Reports claimed the event cost £6.5million.

The scale was staggering even by the standards of India's famed extravagant weddings that last for days.

The current Guinness World Record for the largest wedding attendance is 150,000 guests, set at a wedding in India in 1995, meaning this celebration on Wednesday may well have surpassed it.

Some 500,000 traditional sweets and a vegetarian feast prepared by an army of 500 chefs and cooks were served to guests, Singh's confidant Munna Singh said.

The event in Anant Singh's native village of Nadawan drew such huge crowds that roads leading to the venue ground to a standstill.

One politician, unable to force his way through the traffic chaos, abandoned his car and rode a tractor across farm fields to reach the venue, the Times of India reported.

Images published by the Press Trust of India news agency showed thousands jostling for a glimpse of the venue, while others perched precariously on top of trees and electricity towers.

Organisers were forced to postpone a horse race as part of the celebrations after some 100,000 people turned up to watch the spectacle involving 200 horses.

Elaborate flower arrangements featuring blooms imported from China and a fireworks display also enthralled guests.

Videos circulating online show guests using drinking glasses as plates, scooping food straight out of cooking vessels with their hands and carrying it away in cardboard boxes.

One guest said in a viral video: 'Getting food here is like winning a war.'

Singh's daughter Shivangi married Rishikesh Kumar, the son of a commandant.

As a wedding present, he reportedly received a white Land Rover Defender, a 100-gram gold chain and an 80-gram gold bracelet.

The bride arrived in a luxury BMW, while the groom travelled in a convoy of around 200 cars.

More than 10 million weddings take place in India every year, feeding an industry estimated to be worth $40-$50 billion, according to KPMG.

Known as 'Chhote Sarkar' (Hindi for younger lord), the bride's father is a powerful but controversial leader.

In social media videos, Singh is often seen twirling his moustache.

Locals say he travels in a convoy of up to 25 cars, and on one occasion Singh reportedly chased away tax officials who visited him by turning up with snakes wrapped around his neck.

The politician has described himself as a social worker who has businesses and agricultural land, declaring that his combined wealth with his wife added up to 1bn rupees.

Meanwhile, more than two dozen criminal cases are pending against him. He is accused of serious crimes such as murder, attempted murder, extortion and rioting. He's reportedly done several stints in prison.
