Increased foreign remittances pep-up stock trading

CSE trading yesterday took on a positive trend with the recording of increased foreign remittances, market analysts said.

The All Share Price Index went up by 143.89 points, while the S and P SL20 rose by 45.27 points. Turnover stood at Rs 2.16 billion with five crossings.

Those crossings were; Hayleys 1.4 million shares crossed to the tune of Rs 312 million and its shares traded at Rs 226, Abans Electronics 260,500 shares crossed for Rs 281 million; its shares traded at Rs 1120, CCS 1.4 million shares crossed for Rs 191 million; its shares sold at Rs 136.25 , JKH 9.3 million shares crossed to the tune of Rs 184 million; its shares sold at Rs 19.70 and Dialog 760,000 shares crossed for Rs 33.4 million; its shares traded at Rs 44.

In the retail market companies that mainly contributed to the turnover were; Hayleys Rs 110 million (484,000 shares traded), Dialog Axiata Rs 81 million (1.9 million shares traded), Renuka Agri Rs 55 million (4.8 million shares traded), JKH Rs 53 million (2.7 million shares traded), Commercial Bank Rs 42.2 million (210,000 shares traded), Lanka IOC Rs 36 million (269,000 shares traded) and Sampath Bank Rs 35 million (252,000 shares traded). During the day 73 million shares volumes changed hands in 17864 transactions.

It is said that manufacturing sector counters, especially Hayleys and JKH, performed well while the banking sector, especially Commercial Bank, performed well. Petroleum sector, especially Lanka IOC, also actively participated at the floor.

Meanwhile, a trading suspension on Hela Apparel Holdings was announced, effective June 18, due to a disclaimer of audit opinion in the independent auditor’s report for the year ended March 31, 2025, unless the company rectifies the non-compliance on or before June 16.

Yesterday the rupee was quoted at Rs 338.00/10 to the US dollar in the spot market on, weaker from Rs 337.00/30 the previous day, dealers said, while bond yields were broadly steady.

The telegraphic transfer rate for Sri Lanka’s rupee against the US dollar was 332.50 buying, 341.50 selling; the euro was 381.2184 selling, 395.1354 buying; and the pound was 442.9204 buying, 456.9660 selling.
