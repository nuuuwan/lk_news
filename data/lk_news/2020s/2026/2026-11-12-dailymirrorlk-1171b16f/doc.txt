CMA Sri Lanka to host Budget Highlights 2027 Forum on Monday

The Institute of Certified Management Accountants of Sri Lanka (CMA Sri Lanka) will host its annual Budget Highlights 2027 forum under the theme ‘Beyond Stabilization: Building for Growth – Impact on Business, Economy and Society’ on 16 November from 2.00 p.m. to 6.00 p.m. at the Crystal Ballroom, Lower Level, Taj Samudra, Colombo.

The forum will bring together leading economists, tax professionals, industry experts, policymakers and business leaders to examine the key proposals of Budget 2027 and their implications for Sri Lanka’s economic recovery, business environment and future growth prospects.

The programme will commence with the welcome address by Prof. Lakshman R. Watawala, Founder President of CMA Sri Lanka. The keynote address will be delivered by Dr. W. A. Wijewardena, Former Deputy Governor of the Central Bank of Sri Lanka.

The technical presentations will cover several important areas, including Budget 2027 tax proposals, indirect taxation and VAT, the impact on pricing and cost of living, IMF reforms, economic recovery and debt repayment, achieving national export development plan, and the importance of cost accounting in improving industrial efficiency, productivity and competitiveness.

The forum will feature distinguished speakers, including Athula Ranaweera, Managing Partner of Ranaweera Associates; Suresh Perera, Principal – Tax & Regulatory, KPMG; Talal Rafi, Economist;  Mangala Wijesinghe, Chairman of the Sri Lanka Export Development Board; Mahendra Jayasekera, Chairman of the CMA Sri Lanka Cost and Management Accounting Standards Board; and Mr. Felix Fernando, Chairman of the Joint Apparel Association Forum Sri Lanka.

A special panel discussion moderated by Dinesh Weerakkody, Chairman of Union Bank of Colombo PLC and the Employers’ Federation of Ceylon, will bring together prominent business leaders to discuss the challenges and opportunities facing key sectors of the Sri Lankan economy.

The distinguished panel will comprise: Dudley Sirisena - Chairman Araliya Group of Companies, Samanthi Kariyawasam -Director Finance Hemas Hospitals & Laboratory PLC, Hasitha Premaratne - Group Managing Director Brandix Lanka (Pvt) Ltd, Ganesh Deivanayagam - Chairman Eswaran Brothers Exports (Pvt) Limited, Eng. Maj. Ranjith Gunatilleke - Chairman Sanken Gen (Pvt) Ltd and Managing Director / CEO Sanken Construction (Pvt) Ltd, K. Raghu - Managing Director Lanka IOC PLC, Asoka Hettigoda - President   Hotels Association of Sri Lanka (THASL), Damitha Kumarasinghe - Director General Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka (PUCSL), Sampath Jayasundera -  Chairman SLASSCOM.

The forum will provide an important platform for exchanging views, analysing policy directions and identifying practical strategies to support sustainable economic growth, strengthen business competitiveness and create opportunities for the country.
