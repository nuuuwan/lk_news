Sub-Inspector suspended over misconduct and aggressive behavior

Colombo, July 26 (Daily Mirror) - A Sub-Inspector attached to the Wathupitiwala Investment Zone Police Post has been suspended with immediate effect following an incident involving inappropriate conduct and threatening behavior toward members of the public.

The incident took place at around on July 23, 2026, in front of a private establishment near the Wathupitiwala Hospital. While in uniform, the officer allegedly engaged in a heated, threatening argument with bystanders, behaving in a manner deemed entirely unbecoming of a duty-bound officer.

Police said that the suspension was executed immediately in accordance with the provisions of the Establishments Code. Official statements emphasized that the officer's actions brought disrepute to both the Sri Lanka Police and the broader public service, set a poor precedent for fellow officers, and tarnished the integrity and dignity of the service.
