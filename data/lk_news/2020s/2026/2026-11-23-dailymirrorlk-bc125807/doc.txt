FitsAir resumes  direct flights  between Colombo  and Chennai

FitsAir, Sri Lanka’s first privately owned international airline, has announced the resumption of direct flights between Colombo and Chennai, effective December 15, 2026.

The service will operate daily, offering passengers greater convenience and flexibility when travelling between the two cities.

To mark the relaunch, FitsAir is offering introductory return fares from Rs.66,000, inclusive of a 20kg checked baggage allowance and 7kg of hand luggage.

The airline said the resumption of the Chennai service strengthens its presence in the Indian market while expanding regional connectivity. As a major commercial, healthcare and cultural hub in South India, Chennai plays a significant role in business, tourism and personal travel between the two countries.

The daily flights are expected to further support these links, giving travellers an affordable option while contributing to the growing economic and tourism ties between Sri Lanka and India.

Commenting on the relaunch, a senior FitsAir representative noted, “The resumption of our Chennai service reflects our commitment to building a stronger, more sustainable airline, with a network that responds to market demand while remaining true to our promise of delivering the right price and a dependable travel experience.”

FitsAir noted that the Airline continues to position itself as a practical and accessible choice for regional travellers, prioritising affordability, punctuality and hassle-free travel.
