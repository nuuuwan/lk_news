Würth Lanka receives Great Place to Work certification

Würth Lanka (Pvt.) Ltd, the leading automotive aftermarket, hardware, industry and construction solutions specialist, was recently awarded the Great Place to Work certification, affirming its status as an employer of choice in Sri Lanka’s corporate landscape.

The certification recognises Würth Lanka’s commitment to providing an outstanding employee experience while instilling trust, pride and camaraderie among its workforce comprising sales, customer service, administration and HR.

“We are delighted to have been awarded this prestigious certification, placing us amongst the handful of corporate heavyweights in Sri Lanka. The certification comes hot on the heels of our strategic management level adjustments and adds another milestone to our many significant achievements,” said Würth Lanka Managing Director Suranga Kekuluwalage.

The Great Place to Work certification helps Würth Lanka stand out as a top competitor and attract the young, capable talent it needs to pursue its medium- to long-term goals.

“As a fully-owned subsidiary of Würth GmbH of Germany, we have access to Würth’s vast portfolio of products and solutions spanning several industries such as vehicle aftermarket, construction, etc. Based on our strategic priorities, we have planned and budgeted for the expansion of our portfolio. We are confident that we are in a good position to attract, hire and retain the talent that we need to support our plans,” he added.
