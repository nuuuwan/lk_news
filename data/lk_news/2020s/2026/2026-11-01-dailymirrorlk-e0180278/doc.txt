Sajith plans life-size statue of tusker Kavantissa

Colombo, Nov. 1 (Daily Mirror) - Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa has initiated plans to establish a life-size statue of the late tusker Kavantissa, alongside a wild elephant conservation and research centre.

The project is to be set up on the estate premises of former President R. Premadasa at Kahambiliawetiyawatta in Ambanpola, close to an area frequented by the Kahalla-Pallekele elephant herd, which was led by Kavantissa.

The proposed centre will include a life-size statue of Kavantissa, as well as a research facility and library.

According to the project organisers, the centre aims to promote research on wild elephants, support practical measures to reduce human-elephant conflict and strengthen elephant conservation efforts for future generations.

The project is being carried out by the Kavantissa Elephant Conservation Task Force, headed by Sajith Premadasa.

Premadasa has invited environmentalists, conservationists and others interested in protecting Kavantissa's legacy and Sri Lanka's wild elephant population to join the initiative.
