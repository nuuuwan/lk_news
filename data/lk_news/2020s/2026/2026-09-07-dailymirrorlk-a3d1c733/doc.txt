Colombo hosts Global Brand Awards 2026, She for the Nation in boost for tourism sector

Sri Lanka hosted two international award ceremonies last month, drawing over 100 local and international delegates through Bandaranaike International Airport between August 25 and 29.

Global Brand Awards 2026 and She for the Nation – Season 2 culminated in a main ceremony held on August 29, at Temple Trees, the official residence of the prime minister, bringing together award recipients, business leaders, professionals and international brand representatives.

The event was organised by India’s Anyelp Groups Director Zakir Hussain. Former President Maithripala Sirisena attended as the chief guest, while Indian actor Garuda Ram, known for the KGF film series, took part as the international celebrity guest.

The Global Brand Awards recognised individuals, organisations and international brands for achievements in leadership, innovation and contributions to society, according to the organisers.

She for the Nation – Season 2, meanwhile, honoured women from India, Malaysia, the Maldives, Sri Lanka, the UK and US, with the organisers positioning it as distinct from a conventional beauty pageant, judging the participants instead on confidence, kindness, leadership, values, service and purpose. The programme was held under the theme ‘The real jewel of every woman is her smile’.

The organisers said the twin events created a platform for international relations, business networking and cultural exchange and pointed to the gathering as evidence of Sri Lanka’s capacity to attract and host global-scale events.
