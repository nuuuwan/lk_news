CCID obtains 90-day detention order for drug trafficker Shiran Basik

Colombo, Aug. 17 (Daily Mirror) - The Central Criminal Investigation Department (CCID) has obtained approval to detain 48-year-old drug trafficker Shiran Basik for 90 days.

Basik, a resident of Dehiwala, was arrested in Dubai and brought back to Sri Lanka on August 14, according to Police.

He was arrested by officers of the Criminal Investigation Department’s Airport Unit upon arrival at Bandaranaike International Airport and subsequently handed over to the CCID for further questioning.

According to reports, Basik told investigators that he was stopped and questioned by Dubai police while at a luxury shopping complex during a period when the emirate was experiencing continuous attacks from Iran.

He reportedly claimed that police examined his mobile phone and found images related to the Iranian strikes, which led to his arrest.

Basik further alleged that Dubai police questioned him daily until his deportation, mainly over possible links to Iran.
