Liquor shops in Kandy to close for 11 days during Esala Perahera

Colombo, Aug. 17 (Daily Mirror) - The Excise Department has taken steps to close liquor shops in several areas of Kandy for 11 days, from tomorrow (18) until August 28, coinciding with the Kandy Esala Perahera.

Accordingly, all liquor shops within the Kandy Kadawatha Satara and Gangawata Korala Divisional Secretariat Divisions will remain closed during this period.

In addition, liquor shops located up to the fuel station adjacent to the main road in the Nattharampota Grama Niladhari Division of the Kundasale Divisional Secretariat Division will also be closed.

The closures will take effect tomorrow (18), when the first Kumbal Perahera of the Kandy Esala Perahera takes to the streets, and will continue until August 28, when the Perahera concludes.
