National Chamber and NEDA conduct ‘Access to Finance Awareness Programme’

The National Chamber of Commerce of Sri Lanka (NCCSL) and the National Enterprise Development Authority (NEDA) jointly hosted an “Access to Finance” awareness programme on May 20, 2026, aimed at helping Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) secure concessionary loans. The event, held at the National Chamber Auditorium, featured Deputy Minister of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development, Chathuranga Abeysinghe, as the keynote speaker.

Two technical presentations followed: Kuloja G. Peiris (Acting Director, Department of Development Finance) outlined government concessionary support, while Jude T. Fernando (CEO, National Credit Guarantee Institution) discussed facilitating lending for SMEs lacking sufficient collateral. A panel discussion, moderated by NCCSL Secretary General Nilupul Chandrasena, explored key financing challenges and potential policy solutions. Panelists included representatives from NEDA, the Ministry of Finance, and Bank of Ceylon. The session concluded with an interactive Q&A.
