Digital IDs planned by next year

Colombo, Aug. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Digital Economy Deputy Minister Eranga Weeraratne said the Government plans to introduce digital National Identity Cards (NICs) within the next few years, eventually replacing the existing identity cards currently used by the public.

He said the first digital ID card is expected to be issued within the next few months as part of the Government’s ongoing digitalisation programme.

“We have given a big push to the digital economy. We are going to the point where the first digital ID will be issued within this year,” Weeraratne said.

He added that the Government expects to issue the full digital ID system by August next year and gradually bring all 17 million people in the country into the digital identification system within about two years thereafter.

The initiative is expected to replace the existing NIC system with a digital identification platform as part of Sri Lanka’s broader digital economy drive.
