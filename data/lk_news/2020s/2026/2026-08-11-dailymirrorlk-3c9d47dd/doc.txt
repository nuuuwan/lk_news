Sri Lankan Consulate to hold mobile service in Bern

Colombo, Aug. 11 (Daily Mirror) - The Consulate General of Sri Lanka in Switzerland will conduct a two-day Consular Mobile Service in Bern on August 29 and 30, 2026, to provide essential consular services to Sri Lankan expatriates living in Bern and surrounding areas.

The service will be held from 10.00 a.m. to 3.00 p.m. at Haus Der Religionen, Europaplatz 1, 3008 Bern.

The Consulate said that, due to the limited time available, Sri Lankans wishing to obtain consular services are encouraged to make appointments on or before August 27.

The mobile service is aimed at making essential consular services more accessible to the Sri Lankan community in Bern and nearby areas.

For further information and appointments, the Consulate can be contacted via [email protected] or on +41 77 990 6073 and +41 77 909 8969.
