Asian media leaders in Colombo for ‘Beyond Print’ digital conference

Colombo, Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - Journalists and digital managers from ten media organisations in Bangladesh, Indonesia, Nepal, Sri Lanka and Thailand are in Colombo this week for the closing conference of “Beyond Print.” The programme helps news publishers build digital businesses that can pay their own way.

The three-day conference opened yesterday (16) and ends tomorrow (18). It is run by Germany’s taz Panter Foundation, with the Sri Lanka Press Institute as project partner. Dr. Felix Neumann, German Ambassador to Sri Lanka, gave the opening remarks.

This morning the delegates visit Wijeya Newspapers Ltd, publisher of the Daily Mirror, and then Express Newspapers. At the Daily Mirror, they will spend time in the editorial department to see how the newsroom runs from day to day and to exchange ideas with its journalists and editors.

Twenty-seven media organisations applied to join the programme. Ten were chosen by a selection committee made up of Katrin Gottschalk, Co-Editor-in-Chief of the German daily taz; the Director of the taz Panter Foundation; Kumar Lopez, Chief Executive Officer of the Sri Lanka Press Institute; and Sven Recker, Project Manager of Beyond Print.

Wijeya Newspapers and Express Newspapers are the two Sri Lankan participants. The others are The Daily Star and Dhaka Stream from Bangladesh, Tempo Digital and Solopos Media from Indonesia, Ukeera.com and Sancharika Samuha from Nepal, and Bangkok Tribune and Thai News Pix from Thailand.
