CDB opens new branch in Kilinochchi

Citizens Development Business Finance PLC (CDB) has further strengthened its island wide presence with the opening of its 72nd branch in Kilinochchi.

Commenting on the branch opening, Chief Sales & Digital Business Officer Hasitha Dassanayake said, “We recognise the tremendous potential of Kilinochchi and the aspirations of its people, entrepreneurs and businesses. Our expanding network of physical and virtual touchpoints reflects our commitment to reaching more Sri Lankans with financial solutions that are relevant, accessible and convenient. Our presence in Kilinochchi goes beyond establishing a physical branch.”

With the addition of Kilinochchi to its growing network, CDB continues to advance its commitment to financial inclusion, bringing its services closer to more Sri Lankans while supporting the financial aspirations of communities across the island.
