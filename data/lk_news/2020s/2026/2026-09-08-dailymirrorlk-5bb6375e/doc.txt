Some clauses of Chartered Institute of Media Professionals Bill inconsistent with Constitution

Colombo, Sep. 8 (Daily Mirror) - The Supreme Court has held that some sections of the  Chartered Institute of Media Professionals of Sri Lanka is inconsistent with the Constitution and has to be approved by a special majority in Parliament, Speaker Jagath Wickramaratne said today.

He said the Bill could be approved by a simple majority if amendments recommended by the Supreme Courts are accommodated.
