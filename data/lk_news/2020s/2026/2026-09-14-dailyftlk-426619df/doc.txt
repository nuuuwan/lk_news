KEMCARE at 11: Celebrating a journey of innovation, empowering industry, shaping future

KEMCARE Technologies Ltd. marked its 11th anniversary with a grand celebration held on 4 September at Waters Edge, Battaramulla, bringing together distinguished Government officials, industry leaders, business partners, customers, manufacturers, and key stakeholders from across the country.

The celebration marked 11 years of KEMCARE’s journey, built on a strong foundation of innovation, quality, trust, and lasting relationships.

Since its inception, KEMCARE has continued to strengthen its presence in Sri Lanka’s rapidly evolving beauty and household cleaning products industry, supporting local manufacturers and creating opportunities for businesses operating within the sector.

The milestone was celebrated under the leadership of KEMCARE Technologies Director Sumedha Abayarathna, Directress Nayomi Rathnayaka, and KEMCARE Chief Executive Officer Charitha Perera, whose leadership and strategic direction have contributed to the company’s continued growth and development.

A key highlight of the evening was the gathering of prominent Government and industry leaders under one roof, creating a valuable platform for networking, knowledge sharing, and strengthening collaboration between the public and private sectors.

Among the distinguished guests were Sri Lanka Export Development Board Chairman and Chief Executive Officer Mangala Wijesinghe, National Enterprise Development Authority Chairman Lakshman Abeysekara, and Director General of Government Information Harsha Bandara, alongside a strong representation of industry stakeholders.

A particularly significant feature of the celebration was the participation of leading manufacturers from Sri Lanka’s beauty and household cleaning products sector. Bringing together such a wide representation of manufacturers highlighted the strong relationships KEMCARE has developed within the industry and its commitment to supporting the local manufacturing sector.

The anniversary also provided an opportunity to recognise and appreciate the contribution of KEMCARE’s employees, customers, suppliers, business partners, and other stakeholders who have been instrumental in the company’s growth throughout the past 11 years.

The KEMCARE management emphasised that the company’s success extends beyond business achievements. It has been built on trust, partnerships, innovation, and a shared commitment to delivering value to the industry and its customers.
