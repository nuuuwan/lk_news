Sri Lanka National Oceans and Fisheries Data Centre opens with Korean govt. support

Marking a major milestone in ocean science, digital innovation and sustainable fisheries management, the Sri Lanka National Oceans and Fisheries Data Centre (SOFDC) was officially inaugurated on 19 November at the National Aquatic Resources Research and Development Agency (NARA) Headquarters in Colombo 15.

Established with the support of the Government of the Republic of Korea and technical collaboration of the Korea Institute of Ocean Science and Technology (KIOST), the centre represents a significant advancement in Sri Lanka’s scientific and digital research capabilities.

The inauguration was attended by Youn-Jung Kim, Ambassador of the Republic of Korea to Sri Lanka, Dr. Kolitha Kamal Jinadasa, Secretary to the Ministry of Fisheries, Aquatic and Ocean Resources, and distinguished national and international representatives.

The centre aims to strengthen scientific fishing ground forecasting and fisheries resource management, oceanographic and fisheries data integration and analysis, marine environmental monitoring and climate change research, data-driven decision-making for sustainable fisheries, and digital infrastructure for marine scientific research.

Coinciding with World Fisheries Day on 21 November, the inauguration marks a significant achievement for NARA and an important step towards supporting Sri Lanka’s fishing communities through science and technology.

This landmark initiative further strengthens Sri Lanka - Korea scientific cooperation while advancing the country’s digital transformation and sustainable blue economy.
