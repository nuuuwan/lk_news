Navy board probes Angulana craft accident

Colombo, Aug. 16 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Navy has appointed a special board of inquiry to investigate the sinking of a Fast Attack Craft off the Angulana coast, as search operations continue for a missing naval officer and salvage efforts are underway to recover the vessel.

The incident occurred on August 15, 2026, when the Fast Attack Craft encountered difficulties off Angulana while returning from a routine patrol. The Navy said the expert panel will conduct a preliminary investigation to establish the circumstances and factors that led to the incident.

Meanwhile, search and rescue operations are continuing to locate the naval officer who remains missing following the accident. Navy personnel are conducting searches in the surrounding waters as efforts continue to trace the missing crew member.

The Navy also commenced specialised salvage operations today to recover the sunken craft. Naval divers have been deployed to the site and are working to raise the vessel from the seabed and secure it for recovery.
