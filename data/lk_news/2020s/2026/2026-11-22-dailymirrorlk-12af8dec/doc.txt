21 University of Jaffna students remanded over clash

Jaffna, Nov 22 (Daily Mirror) - Twenty-one students arrested in connection with a clash between two groups at the Faculty of Commerce and Management of the University of Jaffna have been remanded until November 26.

The order was issued by the Jaffna Magistrate’s Court today (22), after the suspects were produced before the court.

Meanwhile, Police Media Spokesperson F. U. Wootler said that two special police teams had been appointed to investigate the incident.

According to police, the clash erupted following an argument between two groups of second- and third-year students of the Faculty of Commerce and Management, which subsequently escalated into a confrontation.

Three students injured in the incident have been admitted to the Jaffna Teaching Hospital for treatment.
