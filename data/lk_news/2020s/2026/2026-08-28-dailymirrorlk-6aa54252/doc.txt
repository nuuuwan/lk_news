Family dispute: Husband murders wife in the wild

Aug. 29 (Daily Mirror) - Following a family dispute that had escalated, the husband allegedly deceived his wife yesterday (28) and took her to a forest reserve in the Korakahawwa area of Anuradhapura, where he attacked her with a sharp weapon, causing serious injuries and killing her. The Anuradhapura Police said that he subsequently stabbed himself with the same knife, sustaining injuries.

The deceased has been identified as 59-year-old Ashoka Kumari Rambukwella, a mother of three who resided in the Ulukkulama area of Anuradhapura.

Police said that the suspect husband had travelled with his wife by motorcycle to the Korakahawwa forest reserve yesterday morning, where the stabbing took place.

The suspect husband, who is a carpenter by profession, is currently receiving treatment at the hospital, and police said that he is due to be arrested.

The wife, who sustained serious injuries in the sharp-weapon attack, died at the scene. The suspect husband, who also sustained serious injuries after stabbing himself, is receiving treatment after being admitted to Anuradhapura Teaching Hospital.

A magistrate’s inquiry into the death was scheduled to be held, following which the body was to be taken to Anuradhapura Teaching Hospital for examination by the Judicial Medical Officer.

Further investigations into the incident are being conducted by a team of officers from the Criminal Investigation Division of the Anuradhapura Police.
