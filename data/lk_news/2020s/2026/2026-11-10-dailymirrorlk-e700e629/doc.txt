Former Water Board Chairman Nishantha Ranatunga arrested

Colombo, Nov. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Former Chairman of the National Water Supply and Drainage Board (NWSDB) Nishantha Ranatunga has been arrested by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC).

The arrest was made in connection with an ongoing investigation being conducted by the Bribery Commission.
