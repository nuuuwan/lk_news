CSE shifts gears to an energetic session, manufacturing sector performs well

The Colombo Stock Exchange witnessed heightened activity yesterday, underpinned by robust institutional participation. According to analysts, this uptick was further supported by falling T-Bill rates and a series of favorable macroeconomic indicators, which together fueled a more energetic trading session.

Amid those developments both indices moved upwards. All Share Price Index up by 29.77 points while S and P SL20 up by 17.45 points. Turnover stood at Rs 1.48 billion with six crossings. Those crossings were reported in E.B Creasy, which crossed 7.5 million shares to the tune of Rs 260 million and its share price traded at Rs 40,  ACL Cables 1.2 million shares crossed to the tune of Rs 114 million and its share price traded at Rs 96.10, JKH 1.5 million shares crossed to the tune of Rs 30 million and its share price traded at Rs 20, NDB 250,000 shares crossed to the tune of Rs 27 million and its share price traded at Rs 109,Pan Asia Bank 433,000 shares crossed to the tune of Rs 23 million and its share price traded at Rs 52 and Commercial Bank 100,000 shares crossed to the tune of Rs 20 million and its share price traded at Rs 20.

In the retail market top seven companies that have mainly contributed to the turnover were  E B Creasy Rs 150 million (3.8 million shares traded), Sampath Bank Rs 96 million (703,000 shares traded), Haycarb Rs 74 million (420,000 shares traded), Commercial Bank Rs 46 million (1.4 million shares traded), Access Engineering Rs 37 million (461,000 shares traded) and JKH Rs 36 million (1.8 million shares traded). During the day 43 million shares volumes changed hands in 13502 transactions. It was said that manufacturing sector counters especially EB Creasy and JKH performed well. Further banking sector counters especially Sampath Bank Commercial Bank also performed well.

Yesterday the Central Bank announced the US Dollar rate as against rupee.  The rupee was quoted at 336.30/45 to the US dollar in the spot market, from Rs 336.30/40 the previous day, while bond yields were broadly steady, dealers said.

The telegraphic transfer rate for the dollar was Rs 331.80 buying, Rs 340.80 selling; the euro was Rs  377.8715 buying, Rs 391.7885 selling; and the pound was Rs 447.7701 buying, 461.8157 selling.
