Icon V Talpe markets elite beachfront residences to global buyers

Icon V Talpe, a hotel-style oceanfront residential development by Odiliya Group Ltd, is attracting significant interest from overseas Sri Lankans and international investors. Located in Talpe near Galle, the project has already sold or reserved a substantial portion of its 227 fully furnished, sea-facing apartments.

The twin-tower development rises 17 levels, featuring a distinctive V-shaped design that maximizes panoramic Indian Ocean views. The residences offer contemporary coastal elegance, with amenities including an infinity pool, direct beach access, and resident lounges.

Catering to both lifestyle and investment objectives, Icon V Talpe provides a flexible ownership model suitable for permanent occupancy, holiday use, or rental income. The development is notably the only project of its kind complying with tourist board requirements, enhancing its rental potential. The project specifically targets expatriates and investors seeking premium coastal real estate near Sri Lanka’s key southern tourist destinations, presenting a consolidated opportunity for luxury living and asset growth.
