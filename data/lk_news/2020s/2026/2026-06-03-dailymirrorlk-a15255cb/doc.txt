Four arrested with 19 revolvers in Dematagoda

Colombo, June 3 (Daily Mirror) - The Police have arrested four suspects in possession of 19 revolver-type firearms. According to the police, the suspects and the sizable cache of weapons were uncovered during a special operation carried out by the Western Province Northern Crime Division in the Dematagoda area.

During the targeted raid, officers managed to seize 19 revolvers, all of which were confirmed to be locally manufactured.

The illegal firearms have been taken into police custody, and further investigations are underway to uncover the intended distribution network and motives behind stockpiling these weapons.
