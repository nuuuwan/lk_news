Fuel distributors back November 30 trade union action

The Petroleum Distributors Association (PDA) has announced its support for the proposed island-wide trade union action scheduled for November 30, citing mounting difficulties faced by fuel station owners across the country.

In a statement issued yesterday (27), the association said fuel station operators representing CEYPETCO, Lanka IOC, Sinopec and RM Parks were facing serious challenges in maintaining their businesses.

The association said two fuel distribution companies had been unfairly reducing the discounts offered to distributors since 2025, making it increasingly difficult for fuel station owners to sustain their operations.

It also raised concerns over what it described as illegal VAT collection, inadequate fuel supplies to privately owned stations and the failure to establish a proper regulatory mechanism for fuel distribution.

The PDA said authorities had failed to pay sufficient attention to the difficulties faced by fuel station owners and had not even provided an opportunity for discussions to resolve the issues.

The association said it had received requests from fuel distributors and employees across the country to support the November 30 trade union action.

Meanwhile, the PDA has scheduled a meeting in Colombo at 9 a.m. tomorrow (29) to discuss its response to the proposed trade union action.

Members of the association's executive committee, provincial association officials and regional representatives are expected to attend the meeting.

Association Chairman Shelton Fernando said the fuel distribution sector was facing increasing difficulties due to the lack of intervention by relevant authorities.
