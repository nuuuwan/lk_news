Women Parliamentarians’ Caucus to launch islandwide breast cancer detection programme

Colombo, Oct. 29 (Daily Mirror) - The Women Parliamentarians’ Caucus, in collaboration with the Ministry of Health, will launch an islandwide programme on November 2 to promote the early detection of breast cancer and raise public awareness of the disease.

The decision was taken at a recent meeting of the Caucus chaired by its Chairperson, Minister Saroja Savithri Paulraj.

Ministry of Health officials briefed the meeting on the prevalence of breast cancer in Sri Lanka and the importance of early detection. Around 6,000 new cases are reported annually, while approximately 1,000 women die from the disease each year.

Although breast cancer can be successfully treated when detected early, fear, embarrassment and a lack of awareness often discourage women from seeking medical attention promptly.

As part of the initiative, women MPs will organise health clinics and awareness programmes at electorate and district levels on November 2 and take an active role in these activities.

The programme will also highlight Breast Cancer Awareness Month through the use of pink ribbons, wristbands and clothing. Women MPs, schoolchildren and university students will be encouraged to participate in these awareness initiatives.

Special emphasis will be placed on encouraging women in rural communities, plantation areas, urban high-rise housing complexes and other underserved areas to attend Suwanari Clinics. With only around 50 per cent of women aged 35–45 currently attending these clinics, efforts will be made to increase participation and encourage greater use of designated cancer early-detection centres.

A comprehensive programme is also planned for next year to refer all women aged 35–45 for screening tests. The initiative will involve relevant stakeholders, including women’s societies operating at Grama Niladhari division level, to expand screening coverage and improve early detection.
