Prabhakaran speech excerpts at Maaveerar Day event raise concerns

Excerpts from a speech by former LTTE leader Velupillai Prabhakaran were incorporated into a song broadcast during Maaveerar Day commemorations at the Kanakapuram Maaveerar Cemetery in Kilinochchi yesterday, raising serious concerns as to how a terrorist leader’s speech was publicly played.

Thousands gathered at the cemetery on November 27 to commemorate fallen LTTE members, as Maaveerar Day events were held across the Northern and Eastern provinces.

Notably, portions of Prabhakaran’s speech were played midway through a song broadcast ahead of the ceremonial lighting of the Common Flame.

The inclusion of the former LTTE leader’s speech at a public event attended by thousands is a chargeable offense, as the LTTE remain a banned organization in Sri Lanka and many other countries.

While commemorating those who lost their lives during the conflict is distinct from promoting the LTTE’s political legacy, the use of Prabhakaran’s speech has raised questions about the nature and intent of the message being conveyed.
