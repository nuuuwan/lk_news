Govt denies claims of LTTE training camp operating in Pallai

Colombo, Aug. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Claims circulating on social media that an LTTE terrorist training camp is operating within the Pallai forest reserve are completely false, Cabinet Spokesman Minister Dr. Nalinda Jayatissa said today (18).

Responding to a question at the weekly Cabinet media briefing, Jayatissa said there was no LTTE camp or terrorist training facility at the location referred to in the social media reports.

He said the information gathered so far indicated that an individual had unauthorisedly cleared a section of the forest reserve and constructed a multi-storey building at the site.

According to the Minister, a garment manufacturing facility is currently being operated at the location, with people from the surrounding area involved in the operation.

He reiterated that there was no evidence of an LTTE training camp or terrorist-related activity at the site.
