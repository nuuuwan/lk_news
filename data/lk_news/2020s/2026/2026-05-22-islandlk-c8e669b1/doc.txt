Fairfirst continues its winning run in Leadership Excellence for sixth year

Fairfirst Insurance continued its focus on leadership development at the Great Manager Awards 2025, organised by the Colombo Leadership Academy. The company was also recognised as an organisation with Great Managers for the sixth consecutive year, reflecting its sustained commitment to shaping a workplace where individuals are empowered to grow, take ownership, and lead with purpose.

The Great Manager Awards recognise organisations that prioritise effective people management and cultivate environments where teams can perform, collaborate, and evolve. Fairfirst’s continued recognition highlights its belief that strong leadership is built through trust, accountability, and a shared sense of responsibility across teams.

Among this year’s award recipients, Mr Madhava Hettiarachchi, Head of Finance, was recognised as a Millennial Manager.
