Carefully planned system was put in place to avoid floods in Colombo by my Ministry: Patali

Colombo, Nov. 9 (Daily Mirror) - A carefully placed system was put in place during my tenure as Minister of Megapolis, former Minister Patali Champika Ranawaka said today.

He said pumping stations at Ambatale, Kolonnawa and Peliyagoda were set up during his tenure while two underground tunnels were also constructed to avoid flooding

"One tunnel was constructed from Noris Canal Road to Colombo North and the other from Independence Square to the canal near D.S. Senanayake College. The issue which led to the recent floods in Colombo was solely due to the Colombo Municipal Council (CMC) not carrying its duty of cleaning the drains and culverts, he added.
