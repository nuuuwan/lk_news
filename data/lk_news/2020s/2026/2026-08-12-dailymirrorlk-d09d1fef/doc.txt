Police warn against fake news following viral Kohuwala image

Colombo, Aug. 12 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Police have warned the public against spreading fake news and unverified information on social media following the circulation of a viral image purportedly showing a dead man on a motorcycle beneath the Kohuwala Bridge.

Police said the circulation of false information, misinformation and content capable of inciting violence can cause serious harm to society and may constitute a punishable offence under Sri Lankan law.

Authorities urged social media users to verify the authenticity of such content before sharing it and advised the public to follow three basic steps: 'Check, Verify and Share Responsibly'.

Users have been asked to check whether the original source is genuine and verify information through trusted and official news sources before sharing it with others.

Police warned that individuals who deliberately spread fake news with the intention of creating public unrest could face legal action under the Penal Code and the Computer Crimes Act.

The public has been urged not to share unverified information, particularly content relating to incidents that could cause fear or public alarm.
