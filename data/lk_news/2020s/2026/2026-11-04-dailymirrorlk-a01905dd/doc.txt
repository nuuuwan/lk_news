Landslide blocks Up-Country railway line, train services disrupted

Colombo, Nov. 4 (Daily Mirror) - Train services on the Up-Country railway line have been disrupted after a major landslide blocked the tracks between Bandarawela and Heel Oya early yesterday (4).

An official at the Nanu Oya Railway Station said a large mound of earth and rocks had collapsed onto the railway line near milepost 162/54 in the early hours of the day.

The official said it would take around six hours to clear the debris from the tracks.

As a result, the Podi Menike train travelling from Nanu Oya to Badulla was terminated at Bandarawela Railway Station yesterday.

All trains scheduled to operate from Badulla to Nanu Oya have also been suspended until the railway line is cleared and restored to normal operations, the official added.
