Tea export earnings fall 6% in October

Türkiye leads buyers through October, with shipments more than doubling to 38.83 mnkg

Ceylon tea export earnings fell about 6 percent in dollar terms in October as a sharp drop in packeted tea shipments outweighed a slight rise in average export prices, Sri Lanka Customs data compiled by Forbes & Walker Research showed.

Exports fell to 20.44 million kg from 21.89 million kg a year earlier. Based on shipment volumes and average free-on-board values, October receipts were around US$ 118.8 million, down from US$ 126.5 million. In rupee terms, the same figures put receipts at Rs. 39.3 billion, up 2.5 percent.

Packeted tea accounted for the biggest volume loss, with shipments down 19.1 percent to 8.43 million kg. Tea bag exports fell 9.0 percent to 2.33 million kg, while bulk tea shipments rose 3.7 percent to 8.58 million kg. Instant tea exports more than doubled and green tea shipments rose 54.5 percent, though the two categories together accounted for just over 5 percent of October volumes.

The average export value rose to Rs. 1,924.15 per kg from Rs. 1,752.65 a year earlier. In dollar terms, it edged up to US$ 5.81 from US$ 5.78. Tea bags and packeted tea fetched higher dollar prices per kg, while bulk, instant and green tea recorded declines.

Tea exports in the first 10 months of 2026 fell 4.7 percent to 209.85 million kg. The average export value also declined in dollar terms, to US$ 5.73 per kg from US$ 5.86, putting estimated earnings at US$ 1.20 billion, down nearly 7 percent from US$ 1.29 billion a year earlier.

Packeted tea accounted for about two-thirds of the 10.36 million kg decline in shipments. Bulk tea and tea bag volumes also fell, while instant and green tea recorded gains. Tea bags were the only category to fetch a higher average dollar price over the period.

Türkiye remained the largest buyer of Ceylon tea, taking 38.83 million kg, up 119 percent from 17.74 million kg a year earlier. Shipments to Iraq, the second-largest market, fell 41 percent to 20.07 million kg, while exports to third-ranked Russia rose 3.3 percent to 19.02 million kg. Azerbaijan took 9.53 million kg, up 26 percent, and China rounded out the top five at 7.92 million kg, down 9 percent.
