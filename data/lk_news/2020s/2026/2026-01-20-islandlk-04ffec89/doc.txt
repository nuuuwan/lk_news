Ceylon Cold Stores honoured at National Supply Chain Excellence Awards 2025

Ceylon Cold Stores PLC (CCS), a subsidiary of John Keells Holdings PLC and one of Sri Lanka’s most trusted and enduring corporates, has reinforced its leadership in operational excellence with a prestigious recognition at the National Supply Chain Excellence Awards 2025. The company was honored with the National Award of Excellence, Best Supply Chain Practicing Organization 2025 (Commendation Award) in the Extra Large, Manufacturing category at the ceremony held on 24th October at Monarch Imperial, in collaboration with the Ministry of Industries. This national accolade underscores CCS’s continued commitment to pioneering high-performance manufacturing and supply chain practices in an increasingly complex and dynamic operating environment.

Established in 1866, CCS stands as one of the country’s most respected institutions, known for its heritage of quality and innovation under the iconic Elephant House brand portfolio. Over its long history, the company has consistently embraced transformation to strengthen its value chain, from procurement and production to logistics, distribution, and end-customer engagement. Its sustained investments in supply chain digitization, process optimization, and sustainability-driven operations have positioned CCS among Sri Lanka’s benchmark organizations in modern manufacturing.

The award reflects CCS’s strategic focus on data-driven decision-making, supply chain agility, and the integration of environmentally responsible practices across all aspects of its operations. As the manufacturing landscape continues to evolve with shifting consumer expectations and economic pressures, CCS has demonstrated resilience, operational discipline, and a clear commitment to future-ready systems that drive national industry standards.

Daminda Gamlath, President, John Keells Consumer Foods Sector, said, “Ceylon Cold Stores has long believed that excellence in manufacturing stems from disciplined processes, empowered teams, and a willingness to innovate ahead of the industry. This recognition reaffirms that our strategic focus on technology-led transformation and supply chain modernization continues to create meaningful impact across the value chain.”
