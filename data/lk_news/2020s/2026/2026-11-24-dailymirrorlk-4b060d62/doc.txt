Falcon Valley Hydro rings market opening bell to mark Empower Board listing

From left: Rohan Senewiratne, Managing Director – Atarah Capital (Private) Limited; Air Vice Marshal Andrew Wijesuriya, Director – Falcon Valley Hydro Limited; Ruchit Kandage, Director – Falcon Valley Hydro Limited; Nilupa Perera, Chief Regulatory Officer – Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE); Air Chief Marshal Gagan Bulathsinghala (Retd.), Chairman – Falcon Valley Hydro Limited; Dimuthu Abeyesekera, Chairman – CSE; Bernard Tan, Director, Projects – Falcon Valley Hydro Limited; Thusitha Peiris, Chief Executive Officer – Falcon Valley Hydro Limited; Gerard Suares, Chief Financial Officer – Falcon Valley Hydro Limited.

Falcon Valley Hydro Limited (FVH) rang the market opening bell on the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE) trading floor last week to mark the listing of its 16,000,000 Ordinary Voting Shares on the Empower Board by way of introduction.

These shares, comprising the FVH’s entire issued and paid-up share capital, were listed at a Reference Price of Rs. 31.00 per share determined in consultation with Atarah Capital (Private) Limited, the Financial Advisor & Manager to the Introduction and Sponsor.

Incorporated in Sri Lanka, FVH operates the 2.4 MW Falcon Valley mini-hydro power plant in Medadumbara, Kandy, which was commissioned in 2012 and is fed by the Retiyagama Oya. FVH is wholly owned by Khen Energy Ltd through its subsidiaries, a company listed on the Catalist Board of the Singapore Exchange. Looking ahead, the Company seeksto explore new renewable energy projects across hydro and solar.

“This listing brings Falcon Valley Hydro into greater public view and strengthens our accountability and transparency in an established hydropower business. It holds us to the discipline that public markets demand,” said Air Chief Marshal Gagan Bulathsinghala (Retd.), Chairman of Falcon Valley Hydro Limited.

“As part of the Khen Energy Group, we make three commitments: governance and integrity, performance and sustainable growth, and responsibility to the communities and rivers we depend on. We will be judged not by the promises we make today, but by the quality of our governance, the strength of our performance, and the lasting value we create.”

Through a listing by introduction, FVH’s existing shares are admitted for trading on the secondary market without a public share issue or immediate capital raising, giving the Company greater visibility, stronger governance, and future access to capital markets.

“On behalf of the CSE, I congratulate Falcon Valley Hydro on its listing today,” remarked Nilupa Perera, Chief Regulatory Officer of the CSE, during her welcome address at the ceremony. “Listing on the CSE marks an important step in corporate governance, aligning the company with the high standards of disclosure and risk management we uphold to build lasting market confidence.”

FVH’s listing aligns with the CSE’s broader commitment to advancing Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) integration, sustainable finance across Sri Lanka’s capital market and the transition towards a green economy. Since introducing GSS+ bonds (Green, Social, Sustainability, and Sustainability linked bonds) in 2024, the Exchange has facilitated 12 issuances mobilising over Rs. 92 billion with three Sri Lankan institutions earning international recognition at the 2026 Environmental Finance Sustainable Debt Awards.
