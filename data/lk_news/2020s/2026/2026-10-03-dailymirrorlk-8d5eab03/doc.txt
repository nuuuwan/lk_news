ACHE celebrates 2026 graduates at Waters Edge

The American College of Higher Education (ACHE) marked another significant milestone in its journey of providing internationally recognised higher education opportunities to Sri Lankan students with its 2026 Graduation Ceremony, held on 22 September at the Waters Edge Hotel, Colombo.

The ceremony brought together graduating students, parents and family members, academics, university representatives, education-sector officials and well-wishers to celebrate the achievements of a new generation of graduates.

The Chief Guest was Professor Dr. Premkumar Rajagopal, President and Chief Executive Officer of the Malaysia University of Science and Technology (MUST). His presence carried added significance, as the ceremony also saw the signing of a new academic partnership between MUST and ACHE.

The graduates received a range of qualifications, including bachelor’s and master’s degrees, diplomas and other professional and academic awards, marking the culmination of years of dedication, hard work and perseverance.

ACHE Chief Executive Officer Dr. Sherryn Yaseen congratulated the graduates and highlighted the achievements of ACHE students on international platforms, noting in particular the success of graduates who have continued their education and built careers in the United States and other countries.

Dr. Yaseen also thanked the parents and families who supported the graduates throughout their educational journey, while encouraging the new graduates to carry forward the values, knowledge and confidence gained during their time at ACHE.

The ceremony was also attended by ACHE Director of Studies Dr. Padmini Sirimanne and Director of Operations Victor S. Patrick, together with representatives of partner universities and the Ministry of Higher Education.

A major highlight of the event was the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between MUST and ACHE. The agreement is expected to create opportunities for the joint delivery of several sought-after higher education qualifications in Sri Lanka, further strengthening ACHE’s growing network of international university partnerships.
