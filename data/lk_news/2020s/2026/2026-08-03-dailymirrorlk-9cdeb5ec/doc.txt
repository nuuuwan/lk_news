Roar Global named an OpenAI Select Partner

Roar Global, a venture builder in marketing technology and AI across the Asia-Pacific region, announced that it has been named an OpenAI Select Partner within the OpenAI Partner Network.

The OpenAI Partner Network is a global programme for partners to build, sell and deliver AI solutions with OpenAI. It brings together partners with deep industry expertise, delivery capabilities and customer relationships while equipping them with resources, enablement and support to help enterprises adopt OpenAI frontier models and products and turn them into measurable impact.

As an OpenAI Select Partner, Roar Global will continue working with OpenAI to help organisations build, deploy and scale AI solutions responsibly and effectively. This work will help organisations get more useful work from every token and stronger performance per dollar with GPT‑5.6, while using ChatGPT Work to turn ambitious goals into finished work.

Roar Global combines deep expertise across platform advertising, the creator economy and enterprise AI integration within a single group. From driving growth across the world’s leading digital platforms to building AI infrastructure, automation and enterprise solutions, the company helps businesses translate emerging technology into measurable commercial advantage.

“Being named an OpenAI Select Partner recognises the execution capability our teams already bring to clients across digital platforms, content and AI-led growth. It also marks an important step in our ambition to become a trusted infrastructure and integration partner for enterprises adopting AI across Sri Lanka and the wider Asia-Pacific region. This partnership strengthens our ability to turn that ambition into meaningful business outcomes,” said Roar Global Founder and Chief Executive Officer Mustafa Kassim.

Roar Global is a B2B technology and capability group supporting organisations across key industries, including retail, banking and financial services, automotive and hospitality. Operating across six markets in Asia, Australia and South Africa, the group delivers its capabilities through five specialised subsidiaries. Its work spans managed advertising services across Meta, TikTok and Google, AI visibility and Answer Engine Optimisation solutions for brand growth, enterprise AI automation and integration for Australian businesses and the operation of one of Australia’s fastest-growing UGC platforms.

Looking ahead, Roar Global plans to expand its OpenAI-related offerings, invest in talent and enablement, strengthen the infrastructure required for enterprise AI integration and deploy AI capabilities that help customers translate ambition into tangible business outcomes.
