Landslide warnings issued for three districts

Colombo, November 21 (Daily Mirror)- The National Building Research Institute (NBRI) has issued landslide warnings for vulnerable areas in three districts, effective until 5 a.m. tomorrow (22).

Amber-level warnings have been issued for Yatinuwara in Kandy district, Mawanella in Kegalle district while yellow-level warnining has been issued for Ambagamuwa in Nuwara Eliya district.

The NBRI has warned that persistent rainfall could trigger landslides, slope failures, rockfalls and ground subsidence in the affected areas.
