Budget 2027: Rs. 77 billion allocated for drinking water and sanitation projects

Nov 12 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake has announced an allocation of Rs. 77 billion to expand drinking water supply and sanitation facilities across the country under Budget 2027.

Presenting the Budget in Parliament today (12), the President said the Government aims to ensure an uninterrupted supply of clean drinking water while meeting the growing water requirements of households, businesses and industries.

The Government is also considering opportunities to attract private sector investment into the water supply sector.

As part of the programme, Rs. 5.19 billion has been allocated to commence 13 new water supply projects covering areas including Jaffna, Mannar, Mullaitivu, Vavuniya, Nagadeepa, Valachchenai and Kotte.

Among the major projects announced is the Greater Kilinochchi Water Supply Project, which will receive Rs. 1.8 billion in 2027. The project has an estimated total cost of Rs. 41.14 billion and will cover the Karachchi, Kandavalai, Poonakary and Pachchilaipalli Divisional Secretariat divisions.

The Greater Galle City Water Supply Project, estimated to cost Rs. 37.501 billion, will receive Rs. 3 billion to commence construction.

Meanwhile, Rs. 1.8 billion has been allocated to begin the Kundasale-Haragama Water Supply Project, which has an estimated cost of Rs. 34.368 billion.

A further Rs. 1 billion has been allocated to commence the Eheliyagoda Water Supply Project, estimated to cost Rs. 4.775 billion.

Other projects include the Haldummulla-Beragala Water Supply Project, which will receive Rs. 700 million towards its estimated cost of Rs. 3.262 billion, and the Yatimahana-Ma Oya Water Supply Project, for which Rs. 300 million has been allocated to begin work in 2027. The latter has an estimated total cost of Rs. 22.158 billion.

Rs. 1 billion to address drinking water issues in Jaffna and Vanni

The President also highlighted drinking water quality issues in Jaffna and the Vanni region, including seawater intrusion, excessive water hardness, high mineral salt content and bacterial contamination.

An allocation of Rs. 1 billion has been proposed to gradually address these issues and improve access to safe drinking water in the affected areas.

The Government has allocated a further Rs. 12 billion for projects aimed at increasing the capacity of existing drinking water schemes, expanding distribution networks and improving sanitation facilities.

In addition, Rs. 2 billion has been allocated to improve community-based water supply schemes across the country.

Another Rs. 250 million has been earmarked for feasibility studies to identify solutions to meet the water requirements of industrial and commercial activities in Kuliyapitiya, Pannala, Bingiriya, Udubaddawa, Trincomalee and Gampola-Jayamalapura.

Meanwhile, Rs. 1.74 billion has been allocated for wastewater management projects in Ella, Moratuwa and Ekala.

The projects aim to improve sanitation facilities and establish more systematic wastewater management services.

The proposals form part of the Government's wider investment programme under Budget 2027 to strengthen water infrastructure, improve access to safe drinking water and support future industrial and economic development.
