Supreme Court dismisses contempt petitions against Shani Abeysekara, Ravi Seneviratne, Fr. Cyril Gamini

Colombo, Sep. 28 (Daily Mirror) - The Supreme Court today dismissed at the inception two petitions filed against Deputy Inspector General of Police Shani Abeysekara, Public Security Ministry Secretary Ravi Seneviratne and Rev. Father Cyril Gamini, alleging that they had committed contempt of court.

The petitions were filed by retired Air Force officer Shantha Jayathilaka.

The petitions were taken up before a three-judge bench of the Supreme Court comprising Chief Justice Preethi Padman Surasena and Justices Achala Wengappuli and Arjuna Obeyesekere.

During the hearing, Chief Justice Preethi Padman Surasena inquired from the petitioner as to how the respondents had committed the alleged offence of contempt of court.

In response, the petitioner submitted that the contents of a complaint made by Rev. Father Cyril Gamini to the Criminal Investigation Department (CID) contained material capable of substantiating an allegation of contempt of court.

The petitioner further submitted that affidavits filed before the Supreme Court by Shani Abeysekara and Ravi Seneviratne also contained material which, according to him, constituted an offence of contempt of court.

However, the Court was informed that the matters referred to in the petitions had already been concluded.

Having considered the facts and circumstances, the Supreme Court observed that there was no legal basis to proceed with the petitions.
