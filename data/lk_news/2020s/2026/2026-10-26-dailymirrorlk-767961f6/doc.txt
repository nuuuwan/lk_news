Bus driver to face criminal action over Habaraduwa train collision

Colombo, Oct 26 (Daily Mirror) - Police are pursuing criminal charges against the bus driver involved in the collision between a bus carrying students and a train at a railway crossing in Habaraduwa, which left 13 students injured.

Deputy Inspector General of Traffic Control and Road Safety W.P.J. Senadheera said the accident occurred around 2.15 p.m. yesterday (25), when a group of 63 students, parents and teachers from a private educational institution in Kottawa was returning from a visit to a museum in Koggala.

Addressing a media briefing today (26), the DIG said the warning lights and bells at the railway crossing had been operating when the driver attempted to cross the tracks.

Despite students reportedly shouting at the driver to stop, he continued driving, resulting in the train striking the rear section of the bus, the DIG said.

Thirteen injured students were admitted to the Karapitiya Teaching Hospital. One student has since been discharged, while eight remain under medical care. The other injured students have received the necessary treatment.

Highlighting the seriousness of the incident, DIG Senadheera said it could have resulted in a major tragedy similar to the fatal train accident at Yangalmodara. However, no fatalities were reported in the Habaraduwa collision.

The driver is also being subjected to a drug test as part of the ongoing police investigation, the DIG said.
