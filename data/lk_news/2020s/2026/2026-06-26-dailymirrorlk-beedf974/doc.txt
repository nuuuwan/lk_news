Anti-drug awareness march in Colombo

Participants take part in an anti-drug awareness march organised under the "Ratama Ekata" national campaign to mark the International Day Against Drug Abuse and Illicit Trafficking in Colombo on June 26, 2026. Organised by the Bureau of Rehabilitation, the event brought together members of the security forces and representatives of government institutions to promote a drug-free society.
