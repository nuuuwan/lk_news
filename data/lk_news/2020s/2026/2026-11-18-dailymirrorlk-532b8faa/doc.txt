People’s Bank and Justice Ministry partner to digitize judicial payment system

People’s Bank and the Ministry of Justice and National Integration have signed an agreement to facilitate the implementation of an Internet Payment Gateway (IPG) for accepting court-related payments electronically, marking another significant milestone in Sri Lanka’s judicial digital transformation journey.

The agreement was exchanged between Ayesha Jinasena, PC, Secretary to the Ministry of Justice and National Integration, and Chandana Guniyangoda, Chairman of People’s Bank, recently at the Ministry of Justice premises in Colombo 10. I. K. Indika, Deputy General Manager – Payments & Cards, People’s Bank, along with Card Centre officials and representatives of the Ministry of Justice, also participated in the event.

This partnership further strengthens the longstanding collaboration between the two institutions in introducing convenient, secure and efficient digital payment facilities across the country’s judicial system.

The introduction of the Internet Payment Gateway will further expand these facilities by enabling court users to make applicable payments through online payment channels, reducing the need for physical visits to court payment counters or bank branches. This is expected to provide greater convenience to litigants, Attorneys-at-Law and other members of the public accessing judicial services.
