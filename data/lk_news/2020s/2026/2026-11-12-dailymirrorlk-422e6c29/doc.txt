Long-end yields see a modest pullback

Extending the positivity seen in the secondary market the previous day, the yields at the long-end of the yield curve slightly dropped yesterday.

The T-bill yields remained broadly stable amid the weekly auction conducted yesterday, while the overall market was characterised by moderate volumes.

Within the 2030 segment, 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 traded from 11.05 percent to 11.10 percent. Moving ahead, 01.12.2031 and 15.12.2032 traded at 11.15 percent and 11.55 percent, respectively.

At the belly end, 01.11.2033 and 15.10.2034 changed hands at 11.78 percent and 11.95 percent, respectively. Finally, the long-dated 15.08.2036 bond was dealt at 12.10 percent.

Yesterday, the PDMO concluded its weekly T-bill auction, raising Rs.79.1 billion, below the initial offer amount of Rs.90.0 billion. For the three-month T-bill, Rs.40.0 billion was initially offered, while Rs.54.1 billion was accepted.

The weighted average yield rate (WAYR) stood flat at 9.35 percent. For the six-month T-bill, the PDMO accepted only Rs.11.1 billion against an offered amount of Rs.30.0 billion, with the WAYR declining by 1bp to 9.43 percent.

For the 12-month T-bill, Rs.13.9 billion was accepted, which was below the offered amount, while the WAYR remained unchanged at 9.95 percent.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee appreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.332.35/US dollar, compared to Rs.332.56/US dollar seen previously.

The CBSL is yet to release the overnight liquidity data for November 11, 2026.
