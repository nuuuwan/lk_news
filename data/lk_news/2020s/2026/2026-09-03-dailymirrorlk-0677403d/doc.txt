Dayasiri urges Polls Commission  to intervene and stop SLFP event today

Colombo, Sep. 3 (Daily Mirror) - MP Dayasiri Jayasekera today urged the Elections Commission to intervene and stop an event which has been organised by the Nimal Siripala group to mark the 75th anniversary of the Sri Lanka Freedom Party (SLFP) today.

In a letter addressed to the Polls Chief, MP Jayasekera has said the group which has organised the event cannot legally function because of a stay order issued against them by the Colombo District Courts from functioning as office bearers of the SLFP and in accordance with the statement that had been issued by the Election Commission itself, declaring the SLFP as an inactive party that cannot contest elections.
