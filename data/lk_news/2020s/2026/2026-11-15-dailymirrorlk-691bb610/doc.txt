Wife of former Speaker Chamal Rajapaksa passes away

Colombo, Nov. 15 (Daily Mirror) - Chandra Malini Rajapaksa, the wife of former Speaker and former Minister Chamal Rajapaksa, has passed away.

She reportedly died while receiving treatment at the Karapitiya Teaching Hospital in Galle.

She was also the mother of former Uva Province Chief Minister Shasheendra Rajapaksa.

Chamal Rajapaksa is a senior politician who has served as Speaker of Parliament and held several ministerial portfolios.
