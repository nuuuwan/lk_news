Sri Lanka, Thailand expedite visa-free travel talks

Thailand, September 17 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka and Thailand have agreed to further strengthen bilateral relations, with both sides discussing steps to expedite arrangements for visa exemption for holders of ordinary passports.

The agreement was reached during a meeting between Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Minister Vijitha Herath and Thailand’s Acting Minister of Foreign Affairs Sarun Charoensuwan in Bangkok.

The proposed visa exemption is expected to facilitate travel, boost tourism and promote greater people-to-people exchanges between the two countries.

The two ministers also discussed expanding cooperation in trade, investment, tourism, labour mobility, education, maritime security and cultural exchanges.

The meeting reaffirmed the commitment of both countries to deepen Sri Lanka-Thailand relations and advance practical areas of cooperation for the mutual benefit of their people.
