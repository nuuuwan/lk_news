Chamber of Commerce Nuwara Eliya marks first anniversary

The Chamber of Commerce Nuwara Eliya marked its first year anniversary on Saturday (15) at a ceremonial event held at Hotel Araliya Red with leading entrepreneur and Chairman Araliya Group of Companies Dudley Sirisena as the chief guest.

The event was preceded with the participation of a gathering of distinguished guests including Nuwara Eliya District Secretary Thushari Tennekoon, Nuwara Eliya Mayor Upali Wangiasekara, and former Mayor Mahinda Dodampegama. Assistant Director Nuwara Eliya Small Enterprises Development Division P. G. N. Bandara and President CCNE and Nuwara Eliya District senior journalist Douglas Nanayakkara addressed the gathering.

The CCNE, which was formed in order to encourage and enhance exports by businessmen and industry representatives in the Nuwara Eliya District, was established in August 2025.

The CCNE Board of Directors led by President Douglas Nanayakkara, Senior Vice president Gamini Wickrama Singhe, Vice President G. Dinesh, Secretary M. S. Hussain, Treasure Irshad Razick and Consultants Asoka Abeywickrama and D. G. Nandana Bandara took part in a group photograph with the chief guest Dudley Sirisena.
