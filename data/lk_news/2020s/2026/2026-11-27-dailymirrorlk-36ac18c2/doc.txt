No ban on public taking mobile phones into police stations: Minister

Colombo, Nov. 27 (Daily Mirror) - Refuting allegations, Public Security Minister Ananda Wijepala clarified that the government has not issued any circular prohibiting members of the public from taking mobile phones into police stations.

Responding to a question raised by SJB MP Suranga Rathnayake in Parliament, the Minister dismissed media reports claiming that such a restriction had been imposed.

He stated that members of the public are free to carry their mobile phones when visiting police stations.

However, the Minister noted that restrictions may apply to suspects held in police cells regarding the use of mobile phones.
