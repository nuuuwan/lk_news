Diamond Engineers wins Platinum Award

Diamond Engineers (Pvt) Ltd was recently honoured with a Platinum Award in recognition of its contribution to Sri Lanka’s industrial engineering and coconut processing machinery manufacturing sector.

The recognition reflects the company’s growing reputation for delivering durable, export-quality machinery solutions that support the efficiency and sustainability of the coconut processing industry both locally and internationally.

Based in Wariyapola, Diamond Engineers has established itself as a specialised manufacturer of coconut husk processing and coconut fibre machinery, catering to the evolving requirements of the coconut-based manufacturing sector. The company’s product portfolio includes Coconut Husk Chipping Machines, Coco Coir Machines, Coconut Husk Separator Machines, Wastage Machines, and Sieve Machines designed for continuous industrial-scale operations.

Over the years, the company has successfully expanded its footprint beyond Sri Lanka by exporting machinery to countries including India, Indonesia, Madagascar, the Philippines, Kenya, Brazil, Thailand, and the Maldives, further strengthening Sri Lanka’s presence within the global industrial machinery market.

Industry observers noted that the company’s success has been driven by its strong emphasis on engineering precision, operational reliability, low-maintenance functionality, and customised manufacturing solutions tailored to customer requirements.In addition to supporting coconut processors in improving productivity and operational efficiency, Diamond Engineers has also contributed towards promoting value addition within Sri Lanka’s coconut industry through innovative machinery solutions that minimise wastage and enhance processing capacity.
