Cancer care at five hospitals at risk over stalled tender

Colombo, Nov. 11 (Daily Mirror) - Cancer treatment services at five hospitals across the country are at risk of being disrupted due to an administrative crisis within the Ministry of Health, following a delay in the procurement of CT Simulator machines funded by the Asian Development Bank (ADB).

Consultant Physician Dr. Chamal Sanjeewa, President of the Medical Professionals' Alliance for Medical and Civil Rights, has raised concerns that the tender process for procuring the machines has come to an indefinite standstill due to alleged obstruction by certain Ministry officials.

The CT Simulator machines are intended for hospitals in Anuradhapura, Badulla, Kurunegala, Ratnapura and Hambantota.

Dr. Sanjeewa said the model selected for the five hospitals was the same as the one currently facing acceptance and operational issues at Apeksha Hospital in Maharagama.

He warned that the delay could severely affect provincial cancer treatment services and undermine the significant investment made in radiation therapy equipment.

Modern Linear Accelerator (Linac) machines have already been installed at hospitals in Ratnapura, Badulla and Hambantota, while similar machines are expected to be delivered to Anuradhapura and Kurunegala in the coming months.

However, cancer patients cannot receive targeted radiation therapy safely and effectively without the corresponding CT Simulator machines, he said.

A CT Simulator is used to create a precise three-dimensional map of a patient's body, enabling doctors to identify the exact area that requires radiation treatment and plan the therapy accurately.

Without these machines, the newly installed Linac equipment cannot be used to provide the intended radiation treatment.

Dr. Sanjeewa urged the Ministry of Health to immediately resolve the issues surrounding the tender process, warning that thousands of cancer patients in rural areas could be forced to seek treatment in the costly private sector if the situation remains unresolved.
