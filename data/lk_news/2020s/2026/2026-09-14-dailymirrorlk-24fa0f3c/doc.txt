Hayleys Mobility celebrates 1,000 new journeys across SL

Hayleys Mobility Limited, the mobility segment of Hayleys Fentons Limited, has marked the delivery of its 1,000th vehicle, a milestone achieved within its first 9 months of operation.

The milestone represents 1,000 customers, families and businesses beginning new journeys with Hayleys Mobility. Through its growing portfolio of OMODA and JAECOO, KAIYI, SRM, CF MOTO and King Long vehicles, the Company offers electric, hybrid and petrol-powered passenger and commercial vehicles tailored to Sri Lanka’s evolving mobility requirements.

This growth has been underpinned by an expanding island-wide branch and service network, bringing sales and after-sales support closer to customers across the country, alongside strategic financing partnerships with more than 20 leading financial institutions, making new vehicle ownership more accessible to a wider range of Sri Lankans.

The achievement was celebrated at a special event held at the OMODA and JAECOO Flagship Store at Altair, bringing together customers and business partners to mark the delivery of the landmark 1,000th vehicle.

Commenting on the achievement, Hasith Prematillake, Managing Director of Hayleys Fentons Limited said, “Reaching 1,000 vehicles within such a short span demonstrates the power of trust. Customers have responded with confidence because Hayleys Mobility is backed by the century-long engineering heritage of Hayleys Fentons and the 148-year legacy of the Hayleys Group. When a customer chooses a vehicle from us, they also choose an organisation that will stand behind that vehicle for years to come.”

Reflecting on the vision behind Hayleys Mobility, Roshani Dharmaratne, Executive Director of Hayleys Mobility Limited stated, “We began this journey with one clear aspiration: to help make brand-new vehicle ownership a reality for more Sri Lankans. Today, that aspiration has 1,000 stories behind it.
