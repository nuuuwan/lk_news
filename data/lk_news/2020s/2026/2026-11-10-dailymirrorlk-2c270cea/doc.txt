Egg prices may rise to Rs. 47 by December: Producers

Colombo, Nov. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Egg prices could increase to between Rs. 45 and Rs. 47 by December due to rising production costs and growing demand, Chairman of the Sri Lanka Livestock Producers Association Ajith Gunasekara said.

He said the gradual increase in egg prices was mainly driven by a nearly 20 per cent rise in the cost of raw materials used in egg production over the past few months.

The current cost of producing an egg is between Rs. 32 and Rs. 34, while the farm-gate price stood at Rs. 35 and the market price at Rs. 40 yesterday (9), he said.

Gunasekara warned that prices could rise further as some poultry farmers were considering selling their laying hens for meat to recover losses caused by increasing production costs.

He also pointed out that rising demand from tourist hotels ahead of December could create a supply shortage, putting further pressure on prices.

Meanwhile, the association has urged the Government to reduce taxes on eggs and chicken to make these essential protein sources more affordable to the public.

Gunasekara said taxes amounting to between Rs. 9 and Rs. 11 were imposed on each egg, while around 25 per cent of the prices of chicken and eggs was collected as tax.

The association called on the Government to remove taxes on essential food items such as eggs and chicken, arguing that they were among the most accessible sources of protein for the public.
