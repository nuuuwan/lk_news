Up-country train services resume after derailment

Colombo, Oct. 5 (Daily Mirror) - Train services on the Up-country Line have resumed as normal after railway authorities cleared an obstruction caused by a train derailment earlier today.

Railway authorities said re-railing operations of the train that derailed between the Gangoda and Kadigamuwa railway stations had been completed, allowing train operations along the line to resume.

The train operating from Peradeniya Junction to Colombo Fort, scheduled to depart at 4.40 a.m., derailed after soil and rocks reportedly fell onto the railway track following heavy rainfall.

The incident caused delays to trains operating in both directions along the affected section of the Main Line.

Authorities had earlier warned that the affected train could face delays or cancellation.
