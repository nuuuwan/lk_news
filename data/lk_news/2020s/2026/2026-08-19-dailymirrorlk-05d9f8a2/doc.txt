Colombo land value growth slows to 5.9% in first half

Colombo District land values rose at a slower annual pace during the first half of 2026, although price momentum strengthened compared with the preceding six months, Central Bank data showed.

The Land Valuation Indicator (LVI) increased 5.9 percent year-on-year to 271.6 during the January-June period, sharply moderating from the 11.4 percent growth recorded in the first half of 2025.

However, the indicator rose 3.7 percent from the second half of 2025, accelerating from the 2.1 percent semi-annual increase recorded during that period.

Residential land continued to record the strongest annual increase, rising 6.7 percent to an index value of 284.9. The residential sub-index advanced 4.3 percent from the previous six months, compared with a 2.3 percent semi-annual increase in the second half of 2025.

Commercial land values grew 5.7 percent year-on-year and 3.2 percent on a semi-annual basis, taking the index to 268.9.

Industrial land values rose 5.2 percent from a year earlier and 3.6 percent from the preceding six months to reach 261.0. Its semi-annual growth accelerated from 1.6 percent in the second half of last year.

The LVI, compiled using per-perch bare land prices obtained from the Government Valuation Department, covers all 13 Divisional Secretariat divisions in the Colombo District.

Separate indicators are calculated for residential, commercial and industrial land, with the overall LVI representing the simple average of the three categories. The index uses the first half of 2017 as its base period.
