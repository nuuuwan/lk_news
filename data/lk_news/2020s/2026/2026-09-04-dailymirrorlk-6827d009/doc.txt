Asalanka to lead Sri Lanka for England T20Is as Mendis sits out

Colombo, Sep. 4 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka will be without Kusal Mendis and Binura Fernando for the upcoming T20I series against England, as both players continue rehabilitation following injuries sustained during the Lanka Premier League 2026.

Mendis is recovering from a right hamstring injury, while Fernando is undergoing rehabilitation for a back injury.

Following the latest medical updates, the Sri Lanka Cricket Selection Panel has made two changes to the T20I squad. Pavan Rathnayake has been named as Mendis’ replacement, while Dilshan Madushanka has been brought into the squad in place of Binura Fernando.

With Mendis unavailable for the series, Charith Asalanka will captain Sri Lanka in the T20I matches against England.
