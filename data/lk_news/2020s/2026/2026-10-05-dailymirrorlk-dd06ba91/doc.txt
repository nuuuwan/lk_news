Hayleys Mobility-Siyapatha Finance offer flexible vehicle financing

From left: Hayleys Mobility Chief Executive Officer Rajieve Fernando, Hayleys Mobility Executive Director Roshani Dharmaratne, Hayleys Fentons Managing Director Hasith Prematillake, Siyapatha Finance Chief Executive Officer Mathisha Hewavitharana, Siyapatha Finance Chief Operating Officer Rajeev De Silva and Siyapatha Finance Chief Marketing Officer Hisham Ziard

Hayleys Mobility Limited has entered into a strategic partnership with Siyapatha Finance PLC through the signing of a memorandum of understanding, providing the customers with greater access to flexible and tailored vehicle financing solutions across its growing passenger and commercial mobility portfolio.

The partnership combines Hayleys Mobility’s expanding range of mobility solutions with Siyapatha Finance’s financial expertise and islandwide reach, enabling the customers to access convenient leasing options designed to make vehicle ownership more accessible.

Siyapatha Finance Chief Executive Officer Mathisha Hewawitharana said, “At Siyapatha Finance, we are committed to delivering flexible financial solutions that evolve with the changing needs and aspirations of our customers. Our partnership with Hayleys Mobility brings together two organisations with a shared vision of delivering service excellence, enhancing customer value and creating greater opportunities for Sri Lankan consumers.”

Hayleys Fentons Managing Director Hasith Prematillake said, “Hayleys Mobility is building a comprehensive mobility ecosystem supported by globally recognised vehicle brands, engineering expertise, charging infrastructure and dependable after-sales support. Through our partnership with Siyapatha Finance, we are strengthening the financing options available to the customers, enabling greater flexibility and convenience in vehicle ownership.”

Hayleys Mobility Executive Director Roshani Dharmaratne stated, “Our portfolio brings together OMODA and JAECOO, KAIYI and SRM four-wheelers, alongside CFMOTO and ZEEHO two-wheelers, enabling us to cater to a diverse range of personal and commercial mobility requirements. This breadth of choice allows us to serve the customers across different lifestyles, business needs and stages of their mobility journey, while continuing to deliver on our promise of ‘One Brand for Every Journey’.”
