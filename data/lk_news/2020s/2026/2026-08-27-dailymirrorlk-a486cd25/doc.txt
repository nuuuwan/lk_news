Devotees observe Nikini Poya at Kelaniya

Devotees offer prayers and participate in religious observances at the historic Kelaniya Rajamaha Viharaya on Nikini Poya Day in Kelaniya today (27). According to Buddhist tradition, the First Dhamma Sangāyana (Buddhist Council) was held on Nikini Full Moon Poya Day, three months after the Buddha’s passing, to preserve and perpetuate his teachings. It was also on this auspicious day that Ananda, the Buddha’s chief attendant and guardian of the Dhamma, attained Arahantship. (Pix by Pradeep Pathirana)
