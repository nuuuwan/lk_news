Public invited to name Dehiwala Zoo’s baby hippo

Colombo, Sep. 2 (Daily Mirror) - Animal lovers have been given an opportunity to suggest a name for a baby Nile hippopotamus recently born at the Dehiwala National Zoo.

The zoo has invited the public to submit suitable names for the newborn through an online form.

Those wishing to participate can suggest a name by filling in the required information through the designated online form.

The initiative is aimed at encouraging the public, particularly animal lovers, to take part in naming the newest addition to the zoo.
