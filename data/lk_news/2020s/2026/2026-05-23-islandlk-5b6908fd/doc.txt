SLT-MOBITEL launches Lanka’s first pocket-sized 5G mobile router

SLT-MOBITEL last week introduced Sri Lanka’s first TRCSL-approved pocket-sized 5G mobile router, marking a new phase in the country’s push toward high-speed, mobile-first digital connectivity.

The device, designed for increasingly data-intensive lifestyles, aims to support seamless streaming, gaming, content creation, remote work and online learning on the move, the company said.

Positioned as a next-generation connectivity solution, the router is powered by Wi-Fi 6 Plus technology and delivers speeds of up to 3,600 Mbps. SLT-MOBITEL said the device offers lower latency for gaming, faster downloads and stable performance even in high-density usage environments.

The compact router supports up to 32 simultaneous connections, making it suitable for households, study groups, travel teams and content creators requiring shared high-speed access.

It also features a smart OLED touchscreen that allows users to monitor data usage and connected devices in real time. Weighing about 220 grammes and measuring just 12 millimetres in thickness, the device is designed for portability.

A 7,000mAh battery provides up to 10 hours of usage and includes reverse charging capability, allowing it to function as a power bank for other devices.

Security features include parental controls, encrypted connectivity, firewall protection, VPN support and safe browsing tools. The device can also be managed via the AI Life App, which enables users to control network settings, guest access and performance monitoring through a smartphone interface.

SLT-MOBITEL said the launch reflects its commitment to advancing Sri Lanka’s digital infrastructure and delivering global-standard technology locally.

The company said the 5G mobile router would enable users to work, learn, play and create without interruption, supporting the country’s transition toward a more connected digital economy.
