Indian actress Pooja Hegde arrives in Sri Lanka for Colombo event

Colombo, Nov. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Popular Indian film actress Pooja Hegde arrived at the Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA), Katunayake, on October 11 for a short visit to Sri Lanka and to attend an opening ceremony in Colombo.

The actress, who enjoys considerable popularity in the Indian film industry and music scene, arrived from Hyderabad, India, at 2.02 p.m. aboard IndiGo flight 6E-1181.

Organisers of her Sri Lankan visit have also arranged a meet-and-greet session on November 11, providing an opportunity for fans who have registered for the event to meet the actress and take photographs with her.
