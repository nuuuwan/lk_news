Seylan Bank honoured with dual wins

Triumphs with dual recognition as Sri Lanka’s Best Bank for Customer Experience and Best Bank for SMEs

Seylan Bank has been honoured with dual wins at Euromoney Awards for Excellence 2026, a prestigious recognition, as Sri Lanka’s Best Bank for Customer Experience and Best Bank for SMEs, two of the most coveted country titles.

Euromoney is a leading international financial publication and market intelligence provider focused on global banking, capital markets and institutional finance.

Recognition from Euromoney reaffirms Seylan Bank’s commitment towards superior customer experience and small and medium enterprise (SME) empowerment.

Having previously won the Best Bank for Customer Experience at Euromoney Awards 2025, Seylan Bank has retained the title for a second consecutive year, reaffirming the dependability of its specialist support and digital platforms to help individuals and businesses amplify growth and revenue.

The twofold Euromoney accolades demonstrate international recognition of consistent care for bank customers and the depth of the bank’s dedicated support for the SMEs islandwide, validating years of investment in digital innovation and accessible banking.

Director and CEO Ramesh Jayasekara said, “The dual recognition is a proud milestone for Seylan Bank, reaffirming our drive towards more meaningful and customer‑centric banking services. Being named Sri Lanka’s Best Bank for Customer Experience for the second consecutive year and the award for Best Bank for SMEs, validates our continued investment in people and systems that deliver tangible value.”

Seylan Bank’s SME award is a significant achievement for a bank firmly established as a catalyst for SME growth in Sri Lanka. The recognition is driven by substantial efforts to empower the SME sector, resulting in the growth of Seylan’s SME loan portfolio.
