BestWeb.LK 2026 crowns best websites

LK Domain Registry CEO and Domain Registrar Prof. Gihan Dias

LK Domain Registry marked 16 years of the BestWeb.LK Awards, Sri Lanka’s national web competition and benchmark for digital excellence, at a grand award ceremony held at Monarch Imperial recently.

The event was graced by the presence of the chief guest Secretary to Prime Minister Pradeep Saputhanthri, who presented the Gold, Silver and Bronze awards for the Best Overall Websites, while several distinguished guests were also present at the occasion.

LK Domain Registry was represented by Chairman Indika De Zoysa and Director, CEO and Domain Registrar Prof. Gihan Dias, together with several other members of the organisation.

During the event, a total of 106 awards were presented across 24 categories, recognising the outstanding achievements in Sri Lanka’s digital landscape. The Overall Gold Award was won by homelandsskyline.lk, while parliament.lk was presented with the Overall Silver and jatholdings.lk received the Overall Bronze.

In recognition of their outstanding talent in web development, 3CS was awarded Best Web Developer – Gold, followed by Web Lankan (Pvt.) Ltd with Silver and Shanika Rajapaksha with Bronze.
