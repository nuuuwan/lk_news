National Development Bank reshuffles audit, risk and two other committees as Mudalige exits board

National Development Bank PLC (NDB) said Independent Non-Executive Director Sujeewa Mudalige resigned for personal reasons, effective November 12 and separately announced the changes to four board committees from the same date.

In a filing to the Colombo Stock Exchange, the bank said that it would disclose Mudalige’s shareholding as at the date of his resignation in due course.Under the revised committee line-up, Nandika Buddhipala chairs the Board Audit Committee, with Sriyan Cooray and Bernard Sinniah as members.  Hasitha Premaratne chairs the Integrated Risk Management Committee, alongside Sanjaya Mohottala and Kushan D’Alwis.

D’Alwis chairs the Nominations and Governance Committee, whose members also include Buddhipala and Cooray. Kasturi Chellaraja chairs the Human Resources and Remuneration Committee, with Sinniah, Cooray and Premaratne as members.

All four committees are chaired by independent non-executive directors and all three members of the Nominations and Governance Committee are independent, according to the filing.
