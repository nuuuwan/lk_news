SLFP still a recognised political party, Election Commission clarifies

The Sri Lanka Freedom Party (SLFP) continues to be recognised as a political party, although a dispute over the party’s secretary position remains unresolved, the National Election Commission has clarified.

The clarification was issued following reports suggesting that the SLFP was no longer recognised as a registered political party.

According to the Commission, the SLFP has retained its status as a recognised political party but has been placed under the category of parties with a disputed secretary position since 2025.

The situation arose following a disagreement between two rival factions within the party over who holds the position of secretary.

The Commission said it had summoned representatives of both factions for discussions on November 6 following requests to intervene and resolve the dispute.

At the meeting, both parties agreed that the matter should remain unresolved until the Western Province Civil Appellate High Court delivers its judgment in the case currently before it.
