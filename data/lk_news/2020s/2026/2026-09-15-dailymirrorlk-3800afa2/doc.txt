Rumesh Tharanga returns home as javelins damaged during flight

Colombo, Sep 15 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lankan javelin star Rumesh Tharanga Pathirage returned home early this morning following a historic victory at the World Ultimate Championships in Budapest, Hungary, where he claimed the gold medal in the men’s javelin throw with a stunning 91.09-metre effort.

Pathirage, who entered the competition as the Commonwealth champion, Diamond League champion and world number one, made a slow start with an opening throw of 75.89 metres, leaving him in fifth place. He responded strongly in the second round with an 85.69-metre throw to move into third before taking the lead with 87.11 metres in the third round.

The Sri Lankan star then produced his best effort of the competition in the final round, launching the javelin 91.09 metres to secure the championship title. His victory came against a field featuring some of the world's leading throwers, including Olympic champion Arshad Nadeem of Pakistan, world champion Keshorn Walcott, two-time world champion Anderson Peters and European champion Julian Weber.

The triumph earned Pathirage a prize of USD 150,000, equivalent to nearly LKR 50 million, adding another major achievement to an outstanding season in which he has established himself as one of the world's leading javelin throwers.

Speaking following his return, Pathirage expressed his gratitude for the achievement and said he now has three major targets remaining for Sri Lanka — the Asian Games, the World Championships and the Olympic Games. He is also expected to launch a book on September 17 documenting his journey and achievements in athletics.

However, the champion's homecoming was also marked by an unfortunate setback. According to reports, four of his specialised competition javelins were damaged during his return flight from Hungary, and a formal complaint has been lodged with the airline.

Despite the damage to equipment that he had used extensively in competition, Pathirage maintained a positive attitude, describing the incident as an unfortunate mistake by the airline. He said he remains focused on his future preparations and the challenges ahead as he continues his pursuit of further international success for Sri Lanka.
