Anojan moved to new cell after altercation at Dammam Prison

Colombo, Oct. 7 (Daily Mirror) - Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Minister Vijitha Herath today informed Parliament that the Sri Lankan Government is taking steps to appeal the death sentence imposed on Sri Lankan national Anojan Sivarasa in Saudi Arabia.

The Minister said the legal proceedings concerning Sivarasa are ongoing before the judicial authorities in Saudi Arabia, with the Court of Appeal delivering its verdict on September 17, 2026.

He said efforts are currently underway to prepare and submit an appeal before the Supreme Court of Saudi Arabia, with the intervention of the Sri Lankan Government. The appeal is being pursued with the assistance of a legal team from Sri Lanka and Saudi legal consultants.

Meanwhile, officials from the Sri Lankan Embassy have visited Dammam Prison, where Sivarasa is being detained, on two occasions. During their latest visit on October 6, embassy officials met the prison warden and the prison doctor to inquire about an incident in which Sivarasa had reportedly sustained injuries during an altercation with another inmate.

According to prison authorities, the scratches and marks on Sivarasa’s face resulted from a physical altercation that followed a verbal dispute between him and another inmate sharing the same cell.

The prison doctor reportedly confirmed that no knives, tools or other weapons were used in the incident and that the injuries were caused by the other inmate’s fingernails.

Prison authorities also assured the Sri Lankan Embassy that there is currently no identified threat to Sivarasa’s life and that necessary measures have been taken to ensure his safety.

Sivarasa has since been transferred to a different cell within Dammam Prison, the Minister said.

Herath assured Parliament that the Sri Lankan Government remains committed to taking all possible steps within the legal framework of Saudi Arabia to seek relief for Sivarasa.
