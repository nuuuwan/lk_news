Online Police Clearance certificate system restored

The online application system for Police Clearance Certificates has been restored following a technical breakdown, the Sri Lanka Police Media Division announced today (October 16).

According to a statement issued by the Police Media Division, the system failure had prevented applicants from obtaining police clearance reports.

With the system now restored, applicants can resume submitting their applications online from today.

The public has been advised to visit the official Sri Lanka Police website, www.police.lk⁠￼, to submit applications.
