Sri Lanka, India sign defence MoUs

Sri Lanka and India signed three Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs) on defence cooperation following a meeting between President Anura Kumara Dissanayake and visiting Indian Defence Minister Rajnath Singh in Colombo.

The agreements cover cooperation between the National Cadet Corps of Sri Lanka and India, institutional ties between the National Defence College of Sri Lanka and India’s National Defence College in New Delhi, and maintenance support for air-defence systems operated by the Sri Lanka Air Force.
