Fitch upgrades Sri Lanka Insurance Corporation General to ‘AA-(lka)’

Sri Lanka Insurance Corporation General Limited (SLICGL) achieved a key financial milestone as Fitch Ratings upgraded its National Insurer Financial Strength (IFS) Rating to ‘AA-(lka)’, from ‘A+(lka)’, Outlook Stable, on November 19, 2026.

As Sri Lanka’s leading general insurer, SLICGL holds the highest Fitch IFS rating among the rated non-life insurers islandwide.

SLICGL’s elevation highlights clear credit differentiation and places the company ahead of other Fitch-rated general insurers in Sri Lanka, which are rated ‘A+(lka)’ and below. The rating is backed by the company’s profile, capitalisation and financial performance.

The rating action follows Fitch Ratings’ comprehensive recalibration of its Sri Lankan national rating scale, which was triggered due to the upgrade of Sri Lanka’s Long-Term Local-Currency Issuer Default Rating to ‘B-’, from ‘CCC+’, with a Stable Outlook, on September 22, 2026.

National ratings highlight an issuer’s creditworthiness relative to other issuers in the country.  In addition to the capital strength, the upgraded rating represents SLICGL’s market leadership and a ‘Favourable’ company profile.

As the largest non-life insurer in Sri Lanka since 2024, SLICGL commands a 20 percent market share of non-life gross written premium as of 2025.

Fitch Ratings’ assessment further acknowledges the company’s long operating history, leading franchise and broad distribution network and solid profitability, with an average return on equity of 12 percent over 2024 and 2025.

SLICGL was the first general insurer in Sri Lanka to hit a Rs. 30 billion topline and has further strengthened its no one position in general insurance in 2026.

In year-to-date 3Q 2026 (IASL 2026) SLICGL has strengthened its position in motor with its iconic Motor Plus brand as well and strengthened its non-motor position becoming no one in non-motor in the latest quarter 3Q.

According to Fitch, SLICGL’s capitalisation as a rating strength. Its risk-based capital ratio of 265 percent at the end of the first half of 2026, after staying above 250 percent over the past two years, is stronger than the Fitch-rated domestic non-life peers.

Moreover, the results compare favourably with those peers over the same period, even though the Cyclone Ditwah-related claims weighed on 2025. For individual policyholders and corporate clients, an ‘AA-(lka)’ rating signals a resilient position within Sri Lanka’s general insurance sector.

Overall, the milestone reaffirms SLICGL’s commitment to safeguarding the policyholders, with financial stability for Sri Lankan businesses and individuals remaining a continued focus.
