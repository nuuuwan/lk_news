Indian police dismantle international cyber fraud ring, rescue 164 foreigners

Delhi police said they rescued 164 foreign nationals who were allegedly held against their will and forced to commit online fraud, foreign media reported.

In a statement, police said they had dismantled a “major international cyber fraud network” allegedly involved in human trafficking, illegal confinement and forcing foreign nationals to carry out online scams.

Police said the 92 Indonesians and 72 Malaysians were rescued from premises on the outskirts of New Delhi that were allegedly used to run the operation. A Malaysian national was arrested in connection with the case.

“Further investigation is underway to identify and apprehend other persons involved in this international criminal network,” police said.

Police said the foreign nationals had been brought to India under various pretexts, including promises of jobs and lucrative opportunities. Soon after arriving, they were allegedly “confined and compelled to participate in cyber fraud activities.”

Investigators are working to establish the full extent of the international network, determine the roles of those involved, trace the proceeds of the alleged crimes, identify victims and take legal action against anyone found to be involved, police said.
