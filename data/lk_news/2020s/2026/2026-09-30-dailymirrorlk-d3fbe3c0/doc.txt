Investors on wait-and-see mode ahead of policy decision

The secondary market yesterday was characterized by limited activity levels and thin volumes as investors were on the sidelines, eagerly anticipating the impending monetary policy decision, set to be announced today. The overall market exhibited some selling pressure, however, mixed sentiment kept the yield curve broadly unchanged.

Amongst the trades the occurred today, starting at the short end, the 15.10.2030 and the 01.02.2031 maturities traded from 11.35% to 11.40%. Moving along the belly, the 15.12.2032 maturity traded at 11.80%, followed by the 01.11.2033 maturity at 11.95% and the 15.10.2034 maturity, which was seen changing hands from 12.05% to12.10%.

On the external front, the LKR depreciated marginally against the USD, standing at LKR 330.79/USD, compared to LKR 330.26/USD seen earlier. Market liquidity in the banking system contracted to LKR 369.35bn compared to LKR 384.12bn recorded previously.
