One killed over a brawl after cattle destroy vegetable cultivation

Colombo, July 20 (Daily Mirror) - A person has been killed over a brawl in Hungama after a brawl he had with another person after his cattle destroyed the vegetable cultivation belonging to the latter.

The victim had actually visited the residence of the suspect to pay compensation to him after his cattle destroyed the long bean cultivation belonging to the latter who is to be arrested. The victim was hacked to death by the suspect and his brother.

The police have launched an operation to arrest the two suspects.

The victim had passed away after he was admitted to the hospital. An argument which arose during a discussion had eventually turned into a physical clash during which the murder had taken place .
