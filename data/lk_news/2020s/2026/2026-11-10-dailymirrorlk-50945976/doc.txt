Parties’ views sought on Provincial Council polls

Colombo, Nov. 10 (Daily Mirror) - The Parliamentary Select Committee appointed to examine the electoral system for Provincial Council elections has sought the views and proposals of several political parties on the matter.

The committee met recently in Parliament under the chairmanship of Minister Vijitha Herath to discuss the electoral system that should be adopted for conducting the elections and related recommendations.

Representatives of the Eelam People's Revolutionary Liberation Front (EPRLF), Eros Democratic Front and Eelam People's Democratic Party (EPDP) presented their respective party proposals at the meeting.

K. Premachandran represented the EPRLF, Ramasamy Rajendran represented the Eros Democratic Front, while Shanmuganathan Sanjeevan represented the EPDP.

The discussions focused on the electoral system to be adopted for Provincial Council elections, as well as ensuring the representation of women and youth.

Deputy Ministers Munir Mulaffer, Attorney-at-Law Sunil Watagala and Arun Hemachandra also attended the meeting, along with Select Committee members and MPs Dharmapriya Wijesinghe, Samanmali Gunasinghe and Lakshman Nipuna Arachchi.
