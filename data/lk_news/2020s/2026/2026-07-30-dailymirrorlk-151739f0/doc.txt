Two held over Bellanwila shooting under 90-day detention order

Colombo, July 30 (Daily Mirror) - Two suspects arrested in connection with the fatal shooting of a restaurant owner near the Bellanwila walking path have been handed over to the Western Province South Crime Investigation Division for further investigations under a 90-day detention order.

The suspects, who were arrested by the Nawagamuwa Police while allegedly attempting to flee the area, have been placed under detention for extensive interrogation following a court order.

Police identified the suspects as a former Sri Lanka Army soldier from the Gangeyaya area in Kottepitiya, Bakamuna, and another individual from the Heerawiya area in Orubendi Siyambalawa, Bakamuna.

The shooting took place on July 25, when an unidentified group travelling in a blue three-wheeler opened fire on a restaurant owner from Dehiwala Road, Boralesgamuwa. The victim was shot in front of his restaurant located near the Bellanwila walking path within the Boralesgamuwa Police Division.

Following the shooting, the assailants fled towards Dehiwala. Police immediately established roadblocks at several locations and launched search operations to apprehend those involved.

During the operation, Nawagamuwa Police intercepted the two suspects while they were travelling on a motorcycle and took them into custody.

The suspects were later produced before court, which granted police approval to detain and question them for 90 days.
