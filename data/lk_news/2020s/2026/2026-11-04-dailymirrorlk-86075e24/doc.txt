Police to arrest mahout over alleged assault on safari elephant in Sigiriya

Colombo, Nov. 4 (Daily Mirror) - The Sigiriya Police have assured that action will be taken against a mahout accused of brutally assaulting an elephant named Rani, used for tourist safaris in the Sigiriya area, following a video of the incident circulating on social media.

The Animal Welfare Collective (AWC) said the video showed the mahout beating the elephant while attempting to force it to take tourists on a safari.

Following the incident, a complaint was lodged with the Sigiriya Police.

According to the AWC, the Sigiriya Police confirmed that a complaint had been received and assured that the suspect would be arrested and produced before court.

The organisation called for stricter government regulations on elephant safaris and the use of elephants for tourism, stressing the need for continuous monitoring of their healthcare, food, water, rest and the conduct of their handlers.

It also said the use of bullhooks and clubs to control elephants for tourism purposes was unacceptable and could damage Sri Lanka's tourism image.

The AWC urged authorities to take strict legal action against those responsible for animal cruelty.
