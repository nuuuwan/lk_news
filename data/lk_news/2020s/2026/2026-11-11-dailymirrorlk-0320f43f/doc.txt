Anojan Sivarasa completely freed; return arranged

Colombo, Nov. 11 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lankan national Anojan Sivarasa, who was sentenced to death in Saudi Arabia over a Facebook post deemed offensive, has been completely cleared of the case and will be brought back to Sri Lanka, Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Minister Vijitha Herath said.

Minister Herath said all necessary arrangements would be made to facilitate Anojan's return following his release from all legal proceedings and penalties in Saudi Arabia.

Anojan's relatives visited the Ministry of Foreign Affairs yesterday to express their gratitude to President Anura Kumara Dissanayake, the Foreign Ministry and other State institutions and officials who contributed to securing his release.

The Minister said his release was made possible through the guidance of President Dissanayake, diplomatic interventions by the Foreign Ministry and the cooperation of the Saudi Arabian Government and judiciary.

He also acknowledged the efforts of diplomatic missions and officials of both countries, as well as the legal team that travelled to Saudi Arabia to assist in the case.

On September 17, 2026, a Saudi Arabian appellate court in the Eastern Province sentenced Anojan to death over charges relating to a Facebook post considered offensive.

However, following diplomatic and other interventions, the court overturned the death sentence on October 14.

Further diplomatic efforts were made to secure his release from the remaining penalties and legal proceedings.

On November 9, the Saudi Arabian Public Prosecution decided not to pursue the case further, paving the way for his complete release.
