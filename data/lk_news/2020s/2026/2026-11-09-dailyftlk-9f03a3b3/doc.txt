SENOK celebrates 47 years of enterprise, innovation and nation building

SENOK is marking its 47th anniversary this year, celebrating nearly five decades of Sri Lankan enterprise and growth across industries that have played an important role in the country’s development.

From a travel company established in 1979, SENOK has grown into one of Sri Lanka’s largest diversified privately owned business groups, today encompassing more than 40 companies with interests spanning construction and heavy machinery, automobiles and commercial vehicles, renewable energy, manufacturing and aviation, with operations extending beyond Sri Lanka into international markets.

SENOK was founded in 1979 by Noel Selvanayagam with the establishment of Senok Travels Ltd. From its beginnings in the travel industry, the business soon expanded into construction machinery and began the diversification that would shape the Group over the decades that followed. As SENOK grew, Managing Director Gerard Fernando became a key force in its development, playing a central role in building and leading many of the Group’s principal businesses, operations and major projects.

Construction and heavy machinery became one of the foundations of the Group’s growth. Machinery supplied and supported by SENOK has been deployed across roads, irrigation schemes, hydropower projects, precast construction projects, harbours, airports and other major infrastructure throughout Sri Lanka. Today, SENOK represents internationally recognised brands including Kobelco, JCB, HAMM and Airman, supported by extensive sales, service, spare parts and technical capabilities.

The Group subsequently established a significant presence in automobiles and commercial vehicles. Its current portfolio includes CHANGAN, DEEPAL, NEVO, GWM, FOTON and EICHER, spanning passenger vehicles, SUVs, pickups, commercial vehicles and new-energy mobility. Through Western Automobile Assembly (WAA) in Kuliyapitiya, SENOK has also invested in local vehicle assembly, contributing to local value addition, employment, technical knowledge transfer and skills development.

SENOK was also the pioneer of wind power development in Sri Lanka, helping introduce commercial wind generation to the country long before renewable energy became a national priority. Its energy interests have since expanded into hydro and solar power, with capabilities covering project development, engineering, construction, commissioning, operation and maintenance.

Aviation represents another distinctive chapter in the Group’s history. In 2012, SENOK expanded into aviation with the establishment of Senok Air, operating Airbus AS350 B3 multi-role helicopters across specialised aviation services including passenger transport, aerial filming, mapping and survey work and medical evacuations. Supported by its own engineering and maintenance capabilities and an experienced flying crew, Senok Air has developed a specialised helicopter operation serving both commercial and critical-response requirements across Sri Lanka.

Beyond Sri Lanka, SENOK has established a growing international presence, including in the Maldives and East and West Africa, extending its experience and capabilities in construction, machinery, commercial vehicles and other sectors into overseas markets.

While much of SENOK’s growth has taken place away from the public eye, its businesses have been closely connected with Sri Lanka’s development for decades – helping build infrastructure, pioneering renewable energy, introducing international technologies, developing local manufacturing capability and creating skilled employment.

SENOK said as it enters its 48th year, it does so with the same willingness to enter new industries, take on ambitious projects and build businesses for the long term that has defined the Group since 1979.
