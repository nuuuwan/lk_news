SLT group delivers 55% profit growth in second quarter

Sri Lanka Telecom PLC’s (SLT) group profit rose 55 percent year-on-year (YoY) in the second quarter of 2026, supported by the higher revenue, stronger operating earnings and a sharp increase in foreign exchange gains.

The group profit attributable to the shareholders climbed to Rs.3.53 billion in the three months ended June 30, from Rs.2.27 billion a year earlier, according to the interim financial statements filed with the Colombo Stock Exchange.

Revenue grew 12 percent to Rs.30.52 billion, while the direct costs rose at a slower 8 percent to Rs.15.46 billion. This lifted the gross profit by 15 percent to Rs.15.06 billion.

The operating profit advanced 30 percent to Rs.5.03 billion, despite the administrative expenses increasing 13 percent to Rs.6.91 billion. The sales and marketing costs were broadly unchanged at Rs.3.12 billion.

The quarterly earnings also received a sizeable boost from the foreign exchange gains, which surged to Rs.732 million, from Rs.52 million in the corresponding period. The finance costs declined 4 percent to Rs.1.73 billion, while the interest income increased 21 percent to Rs.389 million. However, other income recorded a negative Rs.83 million, compared with the income of Rs.510 million a year earlier, partly offsetting the improvement in the core operations and foreign exchange gains. Group profit before tax rose 48 percent to Rs.4.37 billion, while the income tax expenses increased 23 percent to Rs.838 million.

At company level, profit nearly doubled to Rs.2.57 billion, from Rs.1.30 billion, supported by an 11 percent increase in revenue to Rs.19.59 billion and a 44 percent rise in the operating profit to Rs.3.21 billion. For the first six months of 2026, the SLT group’s attributable profit increased 54 percent to Rs.6.60 billion, as revenue rose 11 percent to Rs.61.31 billion. The group generated Rs.21.56 billion in net cash from the operating activities during the period, up from Rs.16.33 billion a year earlier.
