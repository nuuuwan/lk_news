LCB Finance relocates Matara branch

Chairman Dushmantha Thotawatte , CEO Leelananda and Regional Manager Indika Pushpakumara opening the cash counter of the branch

Lanka Credit and Business Finance PLC relocated its Matara Branch at 3/1/2, Nupe Junction, Galle Road, Matara on 27 January 2026.

This relocation was made with the intention of delivering a better service to its existing and prospective customers in and around Matara, a city with an illustrious past, famous for bringing distinguished businessmen and industrial entrepreneurs, who have established themselves across our country.

The opening ceremony was graced by, a distinguish gathering consisting of the Galle Co-operative Hospital and Matara Co-operative Hospital Chairman Charles Gamage, Matara Rural Bank Chairman Chaminda, UDA and GA Gampaha former Secretary Sathyananda Subasinghe, businessmen and officials from the co-operative sector, members of Sanasa Societies, and potential customers, along with the Chairman, the Directors and staff of LCB Finance.

The Chairman Dushmantha Thotawatte and CEO/ Executive Director K.G. Leelananda addressed the gathering. Leelananda described the services provided by the company emphasising on the targeted sectors, women empowerment, financing self-employed projects, agriculture and the provision of financial services to the co-operative sectors and Sanasa. Commenting on the deposit products he stated that the company offered user friendly deposit products from kids to senior citizens with attractive rates, and Gold Loans to meet immediate cash needs of the general public.

The Chairman, Dushmantha  Thotawatte stated that the finance and business operations of the company are conducted under the prudent directions of the company, safeguarding the funds of depositors and that of the shareholders. He invited the public to create mutually beneficial business relationships with the Matara Branch and enhance the profitability of the business operations and living conditions.
