DIMO expands Bosch Diesel Service Network with Kurunegala facility

DIMO has expanded its specialized after-sales network with the opening of a new DIMO Diesel Lab branch in Kurunegala, bringing fuel injection pump repair and diagnostic services of the Bosch diesel service network closer to customers in the North Western Province and surrounding regions.

Using Bosch diagnostic equipment, genuine spare parts, and internationally recognized repair standards, the new facility operates as part of Bosch Diesel Service Workshop network, providing diesel injection pump repairs and diesel injector repairs. The workshop brings together trained technicians who deliver accurate diagnostics and reliable repair solutions for diesel-powered vehicles and equipment.

Commenting on the expansion, Mahesh Karunaratne, Chief Operating Officer - Automotive Engineering Solutions of DIMO said, “A vital aspect of the value we provide to our customers is after-sales service. The DIMO Diesel Lab’s opening in Kurunegala broadens our nationwide service network and provides customers in this significant economic region with quicker access to specialized diesel fuel injection knowledge supported by Bosch’s global standards. By making world-class diesel fuel pump repair solutions more accessible, we are helping customers keep their operations moving efficiently while fuelling the dreams and aspirations of communities and businesses that depend on reliable transport, agriculture, and industrial machinery.”

The new facility was officially inaugurated in the presence of Gahanath Pandithage, Group Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer of DIMO, together with representatives from Bosch and members of DIMO’s management and staff.
