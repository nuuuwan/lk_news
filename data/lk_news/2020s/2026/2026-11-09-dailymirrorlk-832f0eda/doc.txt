Floods in Colombo: CMC to take legal action against illegal constructions

Colombo, Nov. 9 (Daily Mirror) - The Colombo Municipal Council (CMC) has warned that legal action will be taken against unauthorised constructions obstructing drainage systems and causing parts of Colombo to flood.

According to the CMC, several unauthorised constructions identified along Sri Wickrama Road in Mattakkuliya and Bimelwatte Road have already been removed.

It said steps have been taken to issue notices to other locations where similar unauthorised constructions have been identified.

The CMC is taking action to address obstructions to drainage systems that contribute to flooding in the city.
