’She used her voice for women’: Minister pays tribute to Nayomi Chan

Colombo, Nov. 11 (Daily Mirror) - Minister of Women and Child Affairs Saroja Savithri Paulraj has expressed her condolences following the death of popular social media personality Nayomi Chan, who reportedly died due to complications during childbirth.

In a statement, the Minister said she was deeply saddened by the news, highlighting Nayomi Chan’s efforts to use her social media influence to make a positive contribution to society, particularly for women, girls and young people.

She noted that Nayomi Chan had used her platform for more than personal fame and recognition, raising awareness about important social issues.

Recalling a video shared by Nayomi Chan, the Minister said she remembered how she had spoken in simple yet meaningful terms about the importance of women's safety and society's responsibility to protect them.

Minister Paulraj also extended her heartfelt sympathies to Nayomi Chan’s family, wishing them strength and courage to cope with their loss.
