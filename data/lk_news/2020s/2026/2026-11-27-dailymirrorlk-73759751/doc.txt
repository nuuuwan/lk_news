Appeal Court postpones order in Suresh Sallay’s writ application

Colombo, Nov. 27 (Daily Mirror) - The Court of Appeal today deferred until January 28, 2027, its decision on whether to proceed with a writ application filed by former State Intelligence Service (SIS) Director Major General Suresh Sallay, challenging his arrest and detention under the Prevention of Terrorism Act (PTA).

Sallay has sought urgent judicial intervention against his arrest and continued detention in connection with investigations into the Easter Sunday attacks.

The application was scheduled to be taken up for order by the Court of Appeal today. However, Justice Priyantha Fernando, a member of the bench, announced that the order would instead be delivered on January 28 next year.

Through the writ application filed through his lawyers, Sallay is seeking interim relief to suspend the operation of the detention order issued on him under the PTA. He has also sought an order staying further proceedings before the Colombo Fort Magistrate’s Court in connection with the investigations.

The petition names several high-ranking officials as respondents, including Inspector General of Police Priyantha Weerasuriya, Criminal Investigation Department (CID) Director Shani Abeysekera, the Colombo Fort Magistrate and the Attorney General.

President’s Counsel Sanjeeva Jayawardhena appeared for the petitioner while Deputy Solicitor General Suharshi Herath appeared for the Attorney General.
