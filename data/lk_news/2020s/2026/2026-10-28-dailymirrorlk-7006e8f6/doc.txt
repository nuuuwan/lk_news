Suspect arrested over deaths of two wild elephants

Colombo, Oct. 28 (Daily Mirror) - A suspect has been arrested in connection with the deaths of two wild elephants allegedly electrocuted by an unprotected electric wire in the Aluthgama area of Rambewa, Anuradhapura.

The suspect has been identified as Baiyage Gunadasa, the owner of the land where the incident reportedly occurred.

He was arrested by the Department of Wildlife Conservation on the evening of October 27 and is scheduled to be produced before the Medawachchiya Magistrate’s Court.
