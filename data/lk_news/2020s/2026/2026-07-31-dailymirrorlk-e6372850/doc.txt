Heat advisory issued as public urged to stay hydrated

Colombo, July 31 (Daily Mirror) - The Department of Meteorology has issued a warm weather advisory for residents in the Eastern Province and the districts of Kilinochchi, Mullaittivu, Vavuniya, Polonnaruwa and Monaragala.

According to the department, the heat index, which indicates the temperature felt by the human body, is expected to rise to the “Caution” level at some locations in these areas during the daytime tomorrow (August 1).

The Heat Index Forecast is calculated using relative humidity and maximum temperature to determine the level of heat experienced by the human body, the department said.

The public has been advised to stay hydrated as prolonged exposure to direct sunlight and outdoor activities could result in fatigue, while continued strenuous activities may lead to heat cramps.

Residents in the affected areas have also been requested to limit strenuous outdoor activities during periods of high temperatures.
