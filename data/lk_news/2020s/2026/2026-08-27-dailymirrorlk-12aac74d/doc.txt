El Niño preparedness: Don’t leave out  the poor

People in low income settlements who are at the receiving end of many hardships need to be looked after during a crisis such as El Niño. Even though the El Niño has not yet been established,  it is important to ensure that these people have continued access to fresh drinking water for instance. With Sri Lanka’s reservoirs becoming vulnerable to dry spells it is important to consider the plight of urban populations as well. Many low income settlements in Colombo for instance depend on pipe-borne water. But if the water board decides to limit the distribution of water during a prolonged dry spell, these people will also be affected. On the other hand,  they live in dire conditions with no water storage facilities. Regular water distribution is required to ensure that these people are safe. It is not only for drinking purposes that people use water. They need water to do cooking, cleaning, washing and to use the toilet. Therefore the government needs to consider these people when drawing plans to prepare for an impending El Niño.
