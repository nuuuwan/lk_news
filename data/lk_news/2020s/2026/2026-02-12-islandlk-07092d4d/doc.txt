Professor Dale Pinto visits H Connect International, reinforces focus on future-ready accounting talent

H Connect International (A Hirdaramani Group Venture), a leading global Business Process Management (BPM) and Outsourcing company, was honoured to welcome Professor Dale Pinto FCPA, President and Chair of the Board of CPA Australia, for an official visit. He was accompanied by the CPA Australia delegation Jatin Sharma – Regional Manager, Middle East & South Asia, Don Poh – Business Development Manager – Emerging Markets, Global Growth Strategy, and Pramudi Nanayakkara – Business Development Manager – Sri Lanka Liaison.

The visit marks a significant milestone in H Connect International’s strategic partnership with CPA Australia, reflecting the company’s position as the first CPA Australia Recognized Employer Partner (REP) in Sri Lanka. The alliance is designed to support professional development and cultivate a workforce capable of meeting global standards in finance, accounting and operational services.
