CBSL introduces new rules on vehicle valuations for loans and leases

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - The Central Bank of Sri Lanka (CBSL) has introduced new regulations governing the valuation of motor vehicles when granting loans and leasing facilities, aiming to prevent financial institutions from circumventing existing Loan-to-Value (LTV) limits.

The new regulations were issued under Central Bank Directions No. 03 of 2026 on November 9 and came into effect on November 10, 2026.

The directions apply to licensed banks, licensed finance companies and registered finance leasing establishments that provide credit facilities for motor vehicle purchases.

Under the revised rules, the value of a brand-new, unregistered vehicle must be determined based on the final payable price indicated in the tax invoice issued by the authorised agent.

Meanwhile, the valuation of reconditioned, unregistered vehicles must be based on the final payable price stated in the dealer’s tax invoice or the value considered at the time of Customs clearance, as applicable.
