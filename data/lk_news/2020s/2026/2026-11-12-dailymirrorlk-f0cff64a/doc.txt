Three monks arrested following tensions over Sacred City Plan

Colombo, Nov. 12 (Daily Mirror) - Ven. Balangoda Kassapa Thero and two other monks who were staging a fast-unto-death protest near the historic Ruwanweli Maha Seya in Anuradhapura have been taken into police custody following a tense confrontation.

The arrests followed a clash between the protesting monks and a group led by Rajanganaye Saddharathana, who was recently defrocked after a disciplinary inquiry by the Ramanna Chapter.

During the confrontation, Rajanganaye Saddharathana sustained head injuries after allegedly being struck with a club.

Police subsequently arrived at the scene and attempted to dismantle the protest hut erected by the monks for their hunger strike. The move led to further confrontation and heightened tensions before the three monks were taken into custody.

The monks launched the hunger strike to oppose the proposed Anuradhapura Sacred City Development Plan and several other demands.
