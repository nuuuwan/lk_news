Fishing community warned of floating containers on southern seas

Colombo, Aug. 3 (Daily Mirror) - The Fisheries Department has warned all fishermen including multi-day fishing trawlers navigating the southern seas to remain extremely vigilant of the containers that fell into the sea two days ago.

Director General of the Fisheries and Aquatic Resources Department said only two containers out of the 17 empty containers that fell into the sea off the Coast of Colombo two days ago have been recovered so far.

He said it has been observed that the remaining 15 containers are highly likely to drift towards the southern coastal waters, including Kosgoda, Galle, Rathgama and Matara, due to prevailing ocean currents.

The Director General said all fishermen, including those on multi-day trawlers must remain extremely vigilant as collisions with these floating containers could cause severe accidents to fishing vessels.

The Director General requested that if any such containers are spotted, the relevant authorities be informed immediately through the Fisheries Department's radio communication centres.
