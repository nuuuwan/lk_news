Video of Israeli tourist pushing Sri Lankan guide into sea sparks outrage

A video showing an Israeli tourist pushing a Sri Lankan tour guide off a boat and into the sea has gone viral on social media.

The footage, shared by Russian media outlet RT, shows a group of Israeli tourists travelling on a boat in Sri Lanka when one of them suddenly pushes the male tour guide into the water while he is drinking.

The incident has sparked anger online, with many questioning the behaviour of the tourists and their treatment of the local guide.

Israeli journalist and social activist Daniel Amram, who brought the video to public attention, later shared the tourists’ explanation of the incident.

According to Amram, the tourists claimed it was a joke and said the tour guide knew about the prank and was involved in it.

They also insisted that there was no intention to disrespect or humiliate the guide and that he was not injured.

“It was a joke, the worker is fine, it wasn’t meant to be mean,” the tourists reportedly said.
