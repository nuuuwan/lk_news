LAKARCADE appointed as national entity member of World Crafts Council

LAKARCADE, Sri Lanka’s premier destination for authentic gifts and souvenirs, has been officially appointed as a National Entity Member of the World Crafts Council AISBL (WCC-International), following a rigorous review and formal endorsement by the WCC Board.

This prestigious membership marks a significant milestone for LAKARCADE in its mission to preserve Sri Lanka’s cultural heritage and promote handmade excellence globally. As a National Entity Member, the organisation joins an elite international network of craft practitioners, designers, cultural institutions, and advocates dedicated to advancing traditional and contemporary crafts worldwide.

Anil Koswatte, CEO & Managing Director of LAKARCADE, described the elevation as a landmark achievement for both the organisation and Sri Lanka’s craft industry, reinforcing their commitment to uplifting artisan communities and ensuring rural craftsmanship is recognised within a global framework.

Girija Sudhakaran, Deputy Secretary General & Treasurer of WCC AISBL, officially welcomed LAKARCADE, noting that the application was approved following a comprehensive evaluation process and commending their alignment with the Council’s mission.

Through this membership, LAKARCADE will preserve traditional craft techniques, collaborate on programs empowering local artisans and promoting sustainable livelihoods, and represent Sri Lanka at international forums, exhibitions, and knowledge-sharing platforms.

The appointment follows LAKARCADE’s recent participation at the 2nd Edition of the World Crafts Forum in Kuwait City (February 1–3, 2026), themed “Connecting the World, Shaping the Future,” which focused on crafts’ role in the creative economy and socio-economic resilience.
