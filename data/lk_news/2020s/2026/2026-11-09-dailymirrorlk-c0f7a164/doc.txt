Construction industry in limbo over rise in material cost

Colombo, Nov. 9 (Daily Mirror) - The National Construction Workers Union (NCWU) has criticised the Government and construction material manufacturers over rising prices, warning that continued increases could push the construction industry towards collapse.

Addressing a media briefing, NCWU General Secretary Supun Abeysekara said the price of a bag of cement had increased by nearly Rs. 300 over the past two to three months.

He said a bag of cement, which previously sold for around Rs. 1,900, was now being retailed at Rs. 2,250, while the Maximum Retail Price printed on some bags had reached Rs. 2,400.

Carrying a bag of cement to highlight the issue, Abeysekara accused manufacturers of gradually increasing prices, placing an additional financial burden on consumers and contractors.

He said the construction industry was already struggling with a severe shortage of skilled workers, many of whom had migrated overseas, including to Romania and Israel, in search of employment.

The continuing increases in the prices of construction materials, including aluminium and sand, were further aggravating the situation, he said.

Abeysekara warned that nearly 1.5 million people dependent on the local construction industry could be affected by the crisis.

He urged the relevant Minister and authorities to intervene, introduce effective price controls and establish regulations to prevent unfair pricing practices and safeguard the construction sector.
