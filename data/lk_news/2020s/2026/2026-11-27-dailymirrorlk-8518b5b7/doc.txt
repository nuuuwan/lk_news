Lacklustre trading keeps yields broadly anchored

The secondary market yesterday withstood a lacklustre day of trading, characterized by subdued investor participation and low trading volumes.

The yield curve remained largely anchored while modest buying interest was seen on select mid-tenor maturities. Moreover, the PDMO had fully rejected the bids received for the T-Bill auction that was held on Wednesday, which was set to raise a total of LKR 80.0bn.

Among the traded maturities, 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 traded between 11.30% to 11.35% while 01.12.2031 was seen changing hands at 11.44%. Furtheralong the curve, 15.12.2032 traded at 11.60% and in the 2034 segment, 15.06.2034 and 15.10.2034 both traded from 12.10% to 12.05%. Finally, 15.08.2036 changed hands at 12.29%.

On the external front, the LKR depreciated against the USD, standing at LKR 332.58/USD compared to LKR 332.55/USD recorded previously. Market liquidity in the banking system expanded to LKR 461.75bn compared to LKR 440.31bn seen previously.
