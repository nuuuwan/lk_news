Majestic Electric transforms BOC Head Office with modern facade lighting system

The iconic Bank of Ceylon (BOC) Head Office building has been transformed with a state-of-the-art facade lighting system, enhancing its architectural presence and reinforcing its status as one of Sri Lanka’s most recognisable corporate landmarks.

Majestic Electric Co. Ltd., successfully completed the project, which involved designing, supplying, and installing an advanced architectural lighting solution with the product and technical support provided by the LPA Team.

The new facade lighting system has been carefully designed to highlight the building’s unique architectural features while providing an elegant and visually striking night time appearance.

Utilising modern LED lighting technology, the system delivers exceptional energy efficiency, reliability, and flexibility. The lighting design allows for dynamic color-changing effects and customised lighting scenes, enabling the building to commemorate national celebrations, special events, and corporate milestones.

Majestic Electric Co. CEO Jayamal Gunaratne said:  “This project represents a significant achievement in architectural lighting design. Our objective was to create a visually appealing lighting solution that enhances the identity of the BOC Head Office while maintaining energy efficiency and long-term sustainability.”

The BOC Head Office facade lighting project stands as a testament to innovation, technical excellence, and professional project execution, further elevating Colombo’s night time skyline.

Established during the same era as the Bank of Ceylon, Majestic Electric has played a significant role in Sri Lanka’s electrical and infrastructure development. The company was instrumental in supporting the Bank of Ceylon’s expansion, undertaking electrical installations from its very first branch to its extensive island wide branch network, including the setup of the BOC’s initial ATM networks. In 1953, Majestic Electric also made history by becoming the first company to introduce façade lighting in Sri Lanka, transforming the Colombo Harbour in celebration of the arrival of Her Majesty Queen Elizabeth II. These milestones reflect Majestic Electric’s longstanding commitment to innovation, excellence, and national development.

Since 1940, Majestic Electric Co. Ltd., has been one of Sri Lanka’s leading providers of professional Electrical Engineering and Lighting Solutions, with expertise in architectural, commercial, industrial, and outdoor lighting projects. With a legacy spanning more than eight decades, the company has consistently delivered innovative, energy-efficient, and sustainable lighting solutions for some of the country’s most prominent landmark developments.
