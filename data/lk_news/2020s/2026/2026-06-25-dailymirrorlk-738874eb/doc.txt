Rohitha Rajapaksa appears before Central Crime Investigation Bureau

Colombo, June 25 (Daily Mirror) - Rohitha Rajapaksa, the son of former President Mahinda Rajapaksa, appeared before the Colombo Central Crime Investigation Bureau (CCIB) today to record a statement in connection with an ongoing investigation.

Police said the investigation relates to an alleged investment of Rs. 19 million in two institutions in the Mirihana area.
