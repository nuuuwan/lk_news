Marks and Spencer donates to ‘Rebuilding Sri Lanka’ Fund

Marks and Spencer has donated nearly Rs. 4.7 million to the Government’s Rebuilding Sri Lanka Fund, the President’s Media Division said.

The cheque was handed over at the Presidential Secretariat by Marks and Spencer General Manager Rifat Arman and Regional Manager Shabry Aher to the Secretary to the President Dr. Nandika Sanath Kumanayake.

Several senior officials representing Marks and Spencer were also present on the occasion.
