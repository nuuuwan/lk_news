Gnanasara Thera absent in court: Decision on warrant at noon

Colombo, Sep. 29 (Daily Mirror) - The Court of Appeal is due to announce at noon today whether or not an arrest warrant will be issued on Ven. Galagodaaththe Gnanasara Thera over his failure to appear before court in connection with the contempt of court proceedings.

The matter was taken up before a Court of Appeal two-judge Bench comprising Justices Mayadunne Corea and Lakmali Karunanayake, which is considering an application concerning the implementation of the Supreme Court judgment that invalidated the Presidential pardon granted to Gnanasara Thera.

The Attorney General has filed a motion seeking directions from the Court of Appeal to give effect to the Supreme Court judgment, which annulled the Presidential pardon granted to the Thera by former President Maithripala Sirisena. Gnanasara Thera was not present in court. However, the Thera was represented by President’s Counsel Anura Meddegoda.

Gnanasara Thera had originally been sentenced by the Court of Appeal to six years’ imprisonment for contempt of court over his conduct during proceedings at the Homagama Magistrate’s Court. The Supreme Court subsequently upheld the conviction.
