Softlogic Life puts Sri Lanka on the map as Asia’s benchmark for Digital and AI insurance

Softlogic Life emerged as one of the most recognised insurers in Asia at the 30th Asia Insurance Industry Awards (AIIA) 2026, winning both ‘Digital Insurer of the Year’ and ‘Technology and AI Initiative of the Year’.

Softlogic Life was the only organisation shortlisted amongst the top 3 in four categories which also included ‘Best Life Insurer’ and ‘Best Health Insurer’ in Asia, placing a Sri Lankan insurer at the forefront of a field that included some of the region’s largest insurance groups.

The awards were presented on 2 November in Singapore. Organised by Asia Insurance Review, the milestone 30th edition attracted almost 185 entries across 16 categories, from which 49 finalists were shortlisted. Softlogic Life featured in four award categories and converted 50 percent of its nominations into wins.

Softlogic Life was named Digital Insurer of the Year ahead of Asia’s leading insurers such as Muang Thai Life Assurance of Thailand and Go Digit General Insurance of India. It secured Technology and AI Initiative of the Year from a shortlist that included HSBC Life Singapore, Techcom Life of Vietnam and Go Digit General Insurance.

The Company was also a finalist for Life Insurance and Health Insurance Company of the Year, alongside Asia’s Fubon Life Insurance, HSBC Life and Nan Shan Life Insurance.

Commenting on the achievement, Softlogic Life Managing Director Iftikar Ahamed said, “This recognition validates something we have believed for many years: digital transformation creates value only when it fundamentally improves the experience of insurance.”

Softlogic Life Chief Information Officer Amal Darmapriya said, “Behind every service delivered faster than everyone else is a technology foundation built to perform at scale. We have invested in secure, cloud-ready infrastructure, connected data and an architecture that allows AI to move from a single use case to hundreds of live workflows across the business.”

Softlogic Life EVP/ Chief Digital Innovation Officer Saranga Wijayarathne added, “Deploying more than 300 AI agents and workflows is not only a technology milestone; it is a cultural one. Every agent represents a team that chose to rethink how its work gets done, from underwriting and claims to servicing and finance. The biggest shift has been in mindset.” The AIIA judging process is independently scrutinized by KPMG and includes scoring by a panel of senior insurance  leaders from across the region.
