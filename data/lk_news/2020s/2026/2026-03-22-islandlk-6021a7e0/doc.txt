Bottled Water Industry seeks MRP Suspension

The Sri Lanka Bottled Water Association (SLBWA) has urged President Anura Kumara Dissanayake to suspend the gazette mandating maximum retail prices for bottled water, warning that small producers are struggling to survive.

In a letter to authorities, the association cited soaring fuel, electricity, and PET plastic costs, along with global supply disruptions, as making the April 2025 price caps unsustainable. The gazette currently sets prices from Rs. 70 for 500ml to Rs. 350 for 5-litre bottles.

The SLBWA warned that continued losses could threaten the supply of safe drinking water and requested an urgent review of the pricing framework.
