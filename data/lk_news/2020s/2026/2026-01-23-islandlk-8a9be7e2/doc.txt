CINEC celebrates 1,800 graduates and announces Open Day 2026

CINEC Campus Sri Lanka, one of the pioneering non-state institutions in the country, recently hosted its 2025 Graduation Ceremony at BMICH, recognising over 1,800 graduates from across its academic departments. Delivered through multiple sessions to ensure smooth coordination for students and families, the event reinforced CINEC’s role as a leading provider of higher education in the country. Building on this milestone, CINEC will hold its Open Day 2026 on 24 January 2026 at its Malabe campus, starting at 9am, offering prospective students structured guidance on programme options, career pathways, admissions, and facilities – underscoring the institution’s commitment to expanding access to quality tertiary education.

The Graduation Ceremony marked one of CINEC’s largest convocations to date, reflecting the scale of its academic ecosystem across maritime sciences, engineering, computing, management, aviation, health sciences, education, and industrial disciplines.

Speaking with Senior Consultant, CINEC Campus Pvt Ltd – Admiral Thisara Smarasinghe, noted: “Our graduation and Open Day reflect the scale, capability, and forward direction of CINEC. We continue to invest in facilities, industry-driven programmes, and student support structures that enable Sri Lankan students to pursue competitive higher-education and career opportunities both locally and internationally.”
