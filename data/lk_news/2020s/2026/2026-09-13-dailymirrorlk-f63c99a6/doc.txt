Calls for Speaker to resign pending corruption investigations

Colombo, Sep. 13 (Daily Mirror) - The Bribery or Corruption Commission should expedite investigations into allegations against Speaker of Parliament Dr. Jagath Wickramanayake to ensure an impartial inquiry, Chairman of the Doctors' Trade Union Alliance for Medical and Civil Rights Dr. Chamal Sanjeewa said.

He made the remarks after submitting a complaint to the Commission, claiming that several investigations had already been initiated against the Speaker.

He alleged that, as the subject of ongoing investigations, the Speaker should not continue to hold office, particularly because the position carries responsibilities that could potentially create a conflict of interest in relation to the investigations.

Referring to statements recently made in Parliament by MP Dayasiri Jayasekara, Dr. Sanjeewa claimed that the Speaker had requested the Bribery Commission to postpone investigations concerning him.

He said that, under the provisions of the Anti-Corruption Act and parliamentary privileges, steps should be considered to prevent any person under investigation from influencing officials, witnesses or other matters connected to an inquiry.

Dr. Sanjeewa also pointed to the Speaker's role as Chairman of the Constitutional Council, which is involved in making appointments to independent commissions, including the Bribery Commission.

He therefore argued that the Speaker should step aside to ensure that any investigations involving him could be conducted independently and without a potential conflict of interest.

Meanwhile, the Bribery Commission has reportedly indicated that investigations would be conducted into allegations concerning the Speaker following complaints submitted by a former Deputy Secretary General of Parliament and several opposition MPs.

The allegations have not been independently established, and the Speaker has not been found guilty of any offence in relation to them.
