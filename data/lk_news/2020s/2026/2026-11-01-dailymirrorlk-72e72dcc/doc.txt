2,819 nurses appointed, 76 promoted; 13,000 more to be recruited

Colombo, Nov. 1 (Daily Mirror) - The Government has appointed 2,819 trainee nurses as Grade III nursing officers to the Sri Lankan health service, while 76 Grade I nursing officers have been promoted to the Special Grade.

The appointment letters were presented at a ceremony held at Temple Trees this morning (1), under the patronage of President Anura Kumara Dissanayake.

The newly appointed nursing officers belong to the 2020 student nursing cohort, who successfully completed the three-year Higher Diploma course at 16 nursing colleges under the Ministry of Health and Mass Media.

They will be assigned to hospitals and healthcare institutions administered by both the central government and provincial councils across the country.

Meanwhile, the 76 nursing officers promoted to the Special Grade belong to the Hospital Services category.

Addressing the ceremony, Health and Mass Media Minister Dr Nalinda Jayatissa said steps had been expedited to fill vacancies in the nursing service to address staff shortages in hospitals and improve the quality of healthcare provided to the public.

He said the Ministry had paid special attention to filling vacancies in the nursing service since 2017.

Under the health policy and strategic plans for 2025–2035, staffing requirements had been assessed using scientific data, he said.

Accordingly, arrangements had been made to recruit 13,000 personnel to the nursing service over the three-year period from 2027 to 2029, subject to approval from the Department of Management Services of the Ministry of Finance.

Deputy Health Minister Dr Hansaka Wijemuni, Ministry Secretary Dr Anil Jasinghe and several senior ministry officials were also present.
