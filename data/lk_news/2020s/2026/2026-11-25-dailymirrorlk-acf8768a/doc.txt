Suspect remanded over Facebook posts targeting Jayaratne Group director

Colombo, Nov. 25 (Daily Mirror) - A suspect, Brian Samarasinghe, arrested over allegedly publishing manipulated audio and photographic content on Facebook to defame a female director of the Jayaratne Group, one of Sri Lanka’s leading funeral service providers, and members of her family, has been remanded until December 3.

Colombo Additional Magistrate Nuwan Kaushalya ordered the suspect,  a resident of Nugegoda who claims to be a medical doctor, to be remanded until December 3 when he was produced before the court.

The arrest followed an investigation by the Police Bureau for the Investigation of Abuse of Children and Women into a complaint lodged by the company director.

A senior police officer attached to the Bureau said the suspect had been arrested over allegations of sexual harassment under Section 345 of the Penal Code, along with alleged offences under Sections 486 and 285 of the Code.

The court rejected the suspect’s bail application after considering the submissions made on behalf of the complainant.

The court was informed that the video in question had caused serious harm to the complainant and her family.

It was further submitted that the mental trauma, distress, reputational damage and sexual harassment allegedly caused to the victims should not be treated lightly.

Accordingly, the magistrate ordered that the suspect be remanded until December 3.
