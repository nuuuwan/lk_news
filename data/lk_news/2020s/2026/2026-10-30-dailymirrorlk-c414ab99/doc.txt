Prime Group becomes Most Respected Real Estate Organisation

Prime Group has been recognised among Sri Lanka’s Most Respected Entities 2026 by LMD, securing the prestigious Sector Winner – Most Respected Real Estate Organisation accolade, alongside recognition for business expansion, aspirational workplace and employee well-being.

The group also earned accolades across business innovation, corporate culture, corporate dynamism, corporate sustainability, ethical outlook, leadership profile, national perspective, strategic vision and quality consciousness, reflecting the breadth of its achievements and its continued commitment to excellence.

With over three decades of experience, Prime Group has played a significant role in shaping Sri Lanka’s real estate landscape through a diverse portfolio of land, housing and apartment developments. With a presence spanning 18 districts, more than 10,000 land projects and over 70 housing and apartment developments, the group continues to create opportunities for the customers to own property, invest in their future and embrace new lifestyles.

For Prime Group, being named Sri Lanka’s Most Respected Real Estate Organisation goes beyond an industry accolade. It reflects the trust built with generations of customers and the group’s commitment to creating lasting value through developments that respond to the evolving aspirations.

This vision is reflected in a portfolio that spans diverse property segments and distinctive lifestyles. From Prime Marina at Port City Colombo, a landmark waterfront development advancing Sri Lanka’s real estate offering on the international stage, to YOLO Kiribathgoda and Venezia Negombo, the group continues to explore new dimensions of contemporary living.

Its premium developments, including Elizabeth Colombo 7, Monvie Colombo 5 and The Golf Colombo 8 further reflect its presence across different lifestyle and residential segments.

Prime Group Chief Executive Officer Ruminda Randeniya said, “Being recognised as Sri Lanka’s Most Respected Real Estate Organisation is a testament to the trust our customers have placed in us over the years and the dedication of our people. As we continue to expand our portfolio and explore new opportunities, our vision remains to create exceptional value for our customers while elevating Sri Lanka’s real estate industry on the global stage.”
