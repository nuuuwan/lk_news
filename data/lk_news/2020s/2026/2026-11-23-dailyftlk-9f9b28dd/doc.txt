Esna Holdings acquires controlling 60% stake in Paragon Ceylon

Esna Holdings Ltd., has acquired a controlling 60.24% stake in Paragon Ceylon PLC for Rs. 93.4 million.

The company bought 602,555 ordinary voting shares in Paragon Ceylon on 20 November, 2026, at Rs. 155 per share, according to CT Smith Securities (Pvt) Ltd. The broker made the disclosure on behalf of its client under Rule 36 of the Company Take-overs and Mergers Code 1995, as amended in 2003.

Following the acquisition, Esna Holdings holds 60.24% of the voting rights in Paragon Ceylon.

Paragon Ceylon closed Friday up Rs. 65.50 at Rs. 524.75. The company reported net assets of Rs. 1.06 a share. CT Holdings was the top shareholder with a 22.21% as of end-September 2026, followed by J.T. Ratnanather (9.51%), B.A.J. Ratnasabapathy 4.88% and Cyril Gardiner Ltd., (4.75%) with a public float of 94.52%).
