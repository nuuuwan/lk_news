Balangoda Kassapa Thera and six others remanded

Colombo, Nov. 13 (Daily Mirror) - Ven. Balangoda Kassapa Thera and six others have been remanded until November 26 by the Anuradhapura Magistrate’s Court following a confrontation at a protest site near the historic Ruwanweli Maha Seya.

The group, comprising four Buddhist monks and three laypersons, had been staging a fast-unto-death protest near the Ruwanweli Maha Seya despite previous court orders to vacate the area.

The Magistrate also ordered that all seven suspects undergo psychiatric evaluations and medical tests to determine whether they had consumed narcotics.

The arrests followed a clash on Thursday (12) between the group led by Ven. Balangoda Kassapa Thera and another faction led by Rajanganaye Saddharathana.

Police took the seven individuals into custody following the confrontation, during which Rajanganaye Saddharathana reportedly sustained head injuries.
