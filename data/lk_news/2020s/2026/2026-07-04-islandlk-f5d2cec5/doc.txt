IChemC Annual Sessions 2026 inaugurated with focus on scientific excellence

The Annual Sessions 2026 of the Institute of Chemistry Ceylon (IChemC) commenced on June 26 with a formal inauguration ceremony attended by academics, researchers, students and invited guests.

The proceedings began with the arrival of guests and the singing of the National Anthem, followed by the welcome address and presidential speech delivered by IChemC President Prof. Emeritus H.M.K.K. Pathirana.

The ceremony also featured the presentation of awards, prizes, scholarships and certificates recognising outstanding achievements in chemistry Olympiads, BSc and GIC examinations, research activities and inter-university competitions.

Addressing the gathering, Guest of Honour Udesha Bopitiya and Chief Guest Prof. Emeritus A.P. de Silva highlighted the importance of scientific excellence, research innovation and the role of chemistry in national development.

Prof. de Silva stressed the need to strengthen scientific inquiry and encourage young researchers to contribute to emerging global challenges through innovation and interdisciplinary collaboration.

The inauguration concluded with the vote of thanks delivered by IChemC President-Elect Prof. Emeritus Chandani Perera, who expressed appreciation to the organisers, guests, award recipients and participants for contributing to a successful event.

Organisers said the Annual Sessions 2026 reaffirm the Institute’s commitment to promoting academic excellence, advancing research and nurturing the next generation of chemists in Sri Lanka.
