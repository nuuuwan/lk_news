Heavy showers, severe lightning expected in several provinces

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Heavy rainfall exceeding 100mm, accompanied by severe lightning, is expected in several parts of the country during the evening and night, the Department of Meteorology has warned.

The Western, Sabaragamuwa, Central, North-Central, North-Western and Uva provinces are likely to experience heavy showers exceeding 100mm in some areas.

The Department also warned of an increased likelihood of severe lightning associated with thundershowers at several locations.

Meanwhile, sea areas could experience strong gusty winds reaching 60–70 kmph, along with very rough sea conditions temporarily during thundershowers.
