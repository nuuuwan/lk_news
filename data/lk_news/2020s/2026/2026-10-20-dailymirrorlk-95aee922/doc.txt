SriLankan Airlines controlled by hackers: MP

Colombo, Oct. 20 (Daily Mirror) - The national carrier, Srilankan Airlines has been taken over by hackers, MP Dayasiri Jayasekera told Parliament today.

He said the hackers have already notified that they have taken control of the Srilankan Airlines software system.

"Even the emails of Srilankan Airlines employees have been hacked," he said.

MP Jayasekera urged the government to accept the offer made by the US government to get the system released from the hackers.
