Fabindia expands footprint in Sri Lanka opening second store at One Galle Face Mall

Fabindia, India’s lifestyle brand, marked another milestone in its Sri Lankan journey with the opening of its second store at the One Galle Face Mall, Colombo 02.

The expansion, in partnership with The Swadeshi Industrial Works PLC, reflects the brand’s growing presence in Sri Lanka and its commitment to bringing authentic craftsmanship, contemporary elegance and conscious living to discerning Sri Lankan consumers.

Following the launch of its flagship store at Havelock City Mall in August 2025, Fabindia has continued to strengthen its relationship with Sri Lankan customers who appreciate natural fabrics, handcrafted products, sustainable fashion and timeless designs.

The opening of its second store, just over a year after entering the Sri Lankan market, represents an important step in the brand’s expansion strategy and reflects its confidence in the country’s evolving retail landscape.

Speaking on the brand’s continued expansion in Sri Lanka, Chulodhara Samarasinghe, Deputy Chairperson/Managing Director of The Swadeshi Industrial Works PLC, highlighted the significance of this milestone and the growing appreciation for Fabindia’s unique lifestyle offering among Sri Lankan consumers.
