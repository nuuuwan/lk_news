New benchmark in pageant industry

In what is being hailed as an extraordinary first for Sri Lanka, top model, mentor, and pageant authority Chulpadmendra Kumarapathirana has set a new benchmark in the industry by serving as a finale judge for four prestigious national pageants, in just 30 days.

Known for her professionalism, integrity, and unwavering commitment to fairness, Chulpadmendra has become one of the most trusted names in pageantry — respected by national directors in Sri Lanka and beyond.

In July, she took the judges’ chair at Miss Universe Sri Lanka 2026 and Mister Sri Lanka 2026 – both under the leadership of National Director Nirmal Bandara.

She also served as a Finale Judge for Miss Universe Maldives 2026, strengthening her growing reputation within the Miss Universe family.

She will also sit as a Finale Judge for Pearls of Sri Lanka, led by National Director Dr. Asanka Weerasingha.

The winners from these platforms will go on to represent Sri Lanka (and the Maldives) on some of the world’s biggest international stages.

To be entrusted with four major national finals, in a single month, is unprecedented. It speaks volumes about the faith organisers place in Chulpadmendra’s experience, credibility, and her fair, impartial approach.

But this isn’t just about trophies and sashes. Chulpadmendra is also one of Sri Lanka’s top models and the founder of her own modelling school, where she mentors the next generation with the same values she brings to the judging table: discipline, dignity, and authenticity.

Widely recognised for promoting professionalism and fairness in the pageant industry

Chulpadmendra Kumarapathirana’s historic run is more than a personal milestone. It’s a proud moment for the entire regional pageant community.

In an industry often clouded by bias and speculation, she is redefining what excellence looks like — transparent, ethical, and inspiring.

One woman. Four stages. A new standard for Sri Lankan pageantry.

Chulpadmendra is also involved in Universal Woman Sri Lanka 2026, as National Director.

Universal Woman is a global platform that celebrates women of purpose, leadership, diversity, and positive impact.
