DMT reintroduces online appointment system from November 2

Colombo, Oct 31, (Daily Mirror) - The Department of Motor Traffic (DMT) will reintroduce its online appointment booking system for services at its Narahenpita office from November 2, 2026.

The facility will enable members of the public to book appointments online for new vehicle registrations, vehicle ownership transfers and technical-related matters.

The Department has requested the public to make appointments through the online system before visiting the office.

The move is aimed at reducing long queues, particularly among members of the public who arrive at the Department early in the morning to obtain its services.
