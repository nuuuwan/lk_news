Leopard rescued from snare in Norwood

Colombo, Nov. 13 (Daily Mirror) - A three-and-a-half-year-old female leopard caught in a snare at Athad Estate in Norwood was rescued by wildlife officers and taken to the Randenigala Veterinary Unit for treatment yesterday (12).

The leopard was found trapped in a snare at the estate, which belongs to the Bagawantalawa Plantation Company, within the Norwood Divisional Secretariat Division.

Following information received, officers from the Nallathanniya Wildlife Office launched a rescue operation. They cleared the area of onlookers to prevent the animal from becoming agitated.

A team comprising a veterinarian from the Randenigala Veterinary Unit and wildlife officers subsequently freed the leopard from the snare and transported it to the veterinary unit for treatment.

Chief Veterinary Officer at Randenigala, Dr. Akalanka Pinidiya, said the leopard was feeding normally.

He said the animal would remain under medical care for a few days before being released back into its natural habitat.
