GS Evo Motors launches all-new JMEV EWIND

From left: GS Evo Motors Chief Executive Officer Priyantha Perera and GS Evo Motors Chairman Sunil Wettasinghe introduce latest JMEV Ewind Electric SUV to local market

GS Evo Motors Limited, the authorised distributor of JMEV electric vehicles in Sri Lanka, has announced the official launch of JMEV EWIND, a next-generation compact electric SUV engineered to deliver exceptional performance, intelligent technology, premium comfort and uncompromising safety.

The arrival of JMEV EWIND marks another significant milestone in Sri Lanka’s electric mobility journey, offering motorists an advanced electric SUV that combines striking design with cutting-edge engineering and sustainable driving.

Designed to meet the evolving needs of today’s drivers, JMEV EWIND features a sleek aerodynamic exterior highlighted by penetrating LED daytime running lights, trapezoidal chain-inspired LED tail lamps, stylish 19-inch alloy wheels and a bold, contemporary silhouette that commands attention on every road.

Inside, EWIND offers a refined and spacious cabin equipped with a panoramic moonroof with a stylish retractable curtain, ultra-thin suspended instrument panel, D-shaped multifunction steering wheel, multi-colour ambient lighting, premium interior finishes and an eight-way electrically adjustable driver’s seat, four-way electrically adjustable passenger seat, creating a driving experience that is both luxurious and practical.

Powered by advanced electric vehicle technology, JMEV EWIND is available in single-motor front-wheel drive configurations, delivering up to 108 kW of maximum power output and 210 Nm of maximum torque and 0-100 km/h acceleration in just 8.9 seconds, offering exhilarating performance while maintaining outstanding efficiency.

Depending on the selected battery configuration, EWIND offers a driving range of up to 610 kilometres (NEDC), battery capacity of 70.38(kWh), providing confidence for both daily commuting and long-distance travel.

Safety remains a cornerstone of EWIND’s design. Built on a high-strength steel-aluminium lightweight body structure, the vehicle features a five-star safety design philosophy, six airbags, ABS, EBA, EBD, Electronic Stability Programme (ESP), Forward Collision Warning (FCW), Blind Spot Detection (BSD), Door Open Alert (DOA), Lane Keeping Assist (LKA), Hill Start Assist (HSA) and a comprehensive suite of intelligent safety technologies that deliver peace of mind on every journey.

Eight years or 150,000Km battery warranty and five years or 150,000KM for vehicle warranty will be provided. Further, GS EVO Motors will be providing first five labour and material free services.

EWIND also introduces an impressive range of intelligent features, including PEPS smart keyless entry and start, full speed range adaptive cruise control, dual-screen digital cockpit, 360-degree panoramic camera system, Level 2 Advanced Driver Assistance Systems (ADAS), OTA (Over-the-Air) software updates and inductive touch window controls.

GS Evo Motors Chief Executive Officer Priyantha Perera said, “The launch of JMEV EWIND represents more than the introduction of a new vehicle—it reflects our vision of making world-class electric mobility accessible to Sri Lankan motorists. EWIND combines outstanding performance, intelligent technology, premium comfort and exceptional safety, setting a new benchmark in Sri Lanka’s electric SUV market. At GS Evo Motors, we remain committed to supporting the country’s transition towards a cleaner and more sustainable automotive future.”

JMEV EWIND is now available for viewing and test drives at the GS Evo Motors showroom, where the customers can experience the future of electric driving firsthand.
