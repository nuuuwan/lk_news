Japan confirms construction work of BIA 2nd terminal to begin before November

Colombo, August 14 (Daily Mirror)- Japan has confirmed that the construction work of the second passenger terminal at the Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) will commence before November this year.

The confirmation was given during a discussion between the Minister of Ports, Civil Aviation and Energy Anura Karunathilaka and a delegation led by JICA Senior Vice President Hara Shohei and JICA Chief Representative Honzu Shigeo this morning at the Ministry premises.

The Ministry said in a statement that as a result of the discussions held by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake with the Japanese government to restart this project, which had been initiated and halted in the past, the Japanese government had agreed to restart it.

Subsequently, several rounds of discussions were held between the Minister of Ports and Civil Aviation, the Japanese Ambassador to Sri Lanka, and JICA officials.

Following the decision to provide the additional loan facility required for this project, those related tasks have also been completed by now.

Discussions with the construction company selected according to the accepted tender procedure are also in their final stages.

The Minister emphasised that the government also has a need to accelerate the BIA Terminal 2 project. He stated that the government is making every effort to create a favorable environment to welcome more tourists and investors to Sri Lanka.

During the discussion, views were also expressed on obtaining support to establish a Vessel Traffic Management System to manage the increasing ship traffic at the Port of Colombo. It was also decided to submit this through the Department of External Resources.
