Ranil Wickremesinghe’s case postponed till December 9 for AG’s advice

Colombo, Nov 11 (Daily Mirror) - Colombo Fort Acting Magistrate Kinsley Hettiarachchi today ordered that the case against former President Ranil Wickremesinghe and former Presidential Secretary Saman Ekanayake, who were produced before the court over allegations of misusing State funds, be postponed till December 9 to obtain the Attorney General’s advice on the legal action to be taken against them.
