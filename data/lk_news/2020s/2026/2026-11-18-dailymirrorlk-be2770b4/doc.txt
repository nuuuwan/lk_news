Ananda Palitha arrested over complaint lodged by another trade union leader: Minister

Colombo, Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) trade unionist Ananda Palitha was arrested following a complaint made by Nidahas Sevaka Sangamaya trade union leader Ranjith Hettiarachchi, Minister of Public Security Ananda Wijepala told Parliament today.

"Palitha has been stealing fuel valued at more than Rs. 3.1 millon. There was no political motivation behind this arrest," he said.

MP Dayasiri Jayasekera, who responded, said some directors of the Ceylon Petroleum Corporation too have been stealing fuel.
