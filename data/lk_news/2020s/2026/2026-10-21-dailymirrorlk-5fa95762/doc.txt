Sri Lanka hosts 69th IPA World Congress for first time in Asia

Colombo, Oct. 21 (Daily Mirror) - The 69th World Congress of the International Police Association (IPA) began at Cinnamon Lakeside Colombo today (21), with Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Vijitha Herath attending as the Chief Guest.

The Congress, held from October 20 to 25, brings together more than 400 delegates from 69 countries. This is the first time the IPA World Congress is being held in an Asian country.

The International Police Association is a non-governmental organisation with more than 600,000 members worldwide. It promotes social, cultural and professional friendship among serving and retired police officers under its motto, “Servo per Amikeco”, meaning “Service Through Friendship”.

IPA World President Martin Hoffmann leads the global organisation, while the Sri Lanka Section is headed by Inspector General of Police, Attorney-at-Law Priyantha Weerasuriya.

Addressing the gathering, Minister Vijitha Herath welcomed the delegates on behalf of the Government and the people of Sri Lanka. He highlighted the importance of international cooperation in tackling cross-border crime, organised criminal networks and emerging law enforcement challenges.

He also invited the delegates to experience Sri Lanka’s natural beauty, cultural heritage, hospitality and local traditions during their stay.

Following the Congress, the IPA Friendship Week will be held from October 25 to 31, with delegates taking part in a tour programme covering Sri Lanka’s cultural heritage, ancient cities, historical sites, natural landscapes, cuisine and traditions.
