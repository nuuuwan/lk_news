Demolition of Ahangama beachfront businesses makes no sense: SJB

Colombo, Aug. 19 (Daily Mirror) - SJB National Youth Organizer Chamith Wijesundera has criticised the demolition of beachfront businesses in Ahangama, saying the move makes little sense while Sri Lanka’s economy remains fragile.

In a post on X, Wijesundera said tourism is one of the country’s main sources of foreign exchange and employment, and urged the Government to focus on economic recovery, investment and job creation.

“Demolishing Ahangama beachfront businesses is the last thing Sri Lanka needs right now. Tourism is one of our main sources of Dollars and jobs. Destroying these spots when the economy is still fragile makes no sense,” he said.

He called on the Government to focus on what he described as the country’s real priorities — recovery, investment and jobs.
