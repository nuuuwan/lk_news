Investors adopt wait-and-see stance ahead of policy decision

The secondary market endured a quiet trading day, marked by moderate volumes and investor participation. Investors embraced a wait and see approach yesterday, ahead of the monetary policy decision set to be announced today.

At the short end of the curve, 15.06.2029 and 15.12.2029 traded at 10.65% and 10.75% respectively. Further along the curve, 01.03.2030 traded at 10.95% while 01.07.2030 and 15.10.2030 traded higher at 11.00% and 11.05% respectively. At the belly of the curve, 15.06.2034 and 15.10.2034 both traded at 11.80%. Finally, the 15.08.2036 maturity was seen changing hands at 12.05%.

On the external front, the LKR remained stable against the USD, standing at LKR 332.76/USD. Market liquidity in the banking system expanded to LKR 451.08bn compared to LKR 443.99bn seen previously.
