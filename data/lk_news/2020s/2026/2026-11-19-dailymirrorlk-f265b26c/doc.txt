Foreign remittances projected to reach record US$ 9 Billion this year: Vijitha Herath

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - Foreign worker remittances to Sri Lanka are projected to hit an all-time high of US$ 9 billion by the end of this year, Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment, and Tourism Vijitha Herath informed Parliament today.

Addressing the House, the Minister noted that while Sri Lanka recorded US$ 8.1 billion last year, this year’s inflows had already surpassed that total by November 15.

He expressed confidence that total remittances will reach the US$ 9 billion milestone by the end of December.

Minister Herath attributed the steady surge in inflows to renewed confidence among migrant workers in the current administration, noting that expatriates continue to remit funds through official channels due to their trust in the government, unlike in the past.

Highlighting new welfare measures introduced to recognize the vital economic contribution of migrant workers, the Minister announced that the government will roll out a low-interest housing loan scheme for overseas employees. Additionally, he noted that Budget 2027 has waived dual citizenship fees for the spouses and children of Sri Lankan expatriates.
