LAUGFS slashes 12.5kg gas price to Rs. 5,995

Colombo, Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - LAUGFS Gas PLC has announced a Rs. 745 reduction in the price of a 12.5kg domestic LPG cylinder, bringing the price down from Rs. 6,740 to Rs. 5,995 with effect from midnight today (November 17).

The company said the decision was taken following a request by Trade Minister Wasantha Samarasinghe and considering the financial difficulties faced by consumers.

The price of a 5kg domestic LPG cylinder has also been reduced to Rs. 2,398.

LAUGFS Gas said it had absorbed rising import costs, including increases in Saudi Aramco contract prices and freight charges caused by the ongoing conflict in the Middle East, without increasing prices for three consecutive months.

However, continued financial losses had later forced the company to increase the price of a 12.5kg cylinder by Rs. 1,775.

Despite the latest price reduction, the company said it would continue to incur losses but had decided to absorb the additional costs to provide relief to consumers.

Customers seeking information on district-wise prices and product availability can contact the LAUGFS Gas hotline on 1345.
