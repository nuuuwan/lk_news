Tyre House launches LIVFAST batteries

Tyre House Trading (Pvt.) Ltd, in a strategic partnership with Livguard Technologies (Pvt.) Ltd, India, officially unveiled the LIVFAST battery range to the Sri Lankan market at a distinguished launch ceremony held at Shangri-La Hotel, Colombo.

The landmark event marked LIVFAST’s formal entry into the local market, aimed at delivering high-performance, durable and reliable energy solutions to consumers nationwide.

The launch was spearheaded by Livguard Technologies India Vice President Shayan Dev Mukherjee and Livguard Technologies India Assistant General Manager Khushboo Sethi, alongside Tyre House Group Working Director Rohan Peiris and Tyre House Trading Sales Manager Pushpakumara.

The executive leadership of Tyre House Group was also present, including Tyre House Group Chairman Lesley Fonseka, Managing Director Sunil Fonseka, Group Director Gayan Fonseka, Directress Kanthi Thalagala and Working Director Rohan Peiris, underscoring the organisation’s commitment to expanding its automotive solution offerings.

With this launch, Tyre House Trading reinforces its position in the automotive sector by distributing the LIVFAST batteries through its extensive islandwide dealer network.
