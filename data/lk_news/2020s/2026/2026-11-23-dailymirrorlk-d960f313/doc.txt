New Kelani Bridge ramps face temporary closure

Colombo, Nov. 23 (Daily Mirror) - The entry and exit ramps at the Ingurukade Interchange will be temporarily closed at several intervals from tonight (23) until December 1 to facilitate construction work on the merging section connecting the New Kelani Bridge (NKB) and the Port Access Elevated Highway (PAEH), the Road Development Authority (RDA) said.

Accordingly, both the exit ramp of the New Kelani Bridge for vehicles travelling from Katunayake and Orugodawatta towards the Colombo Port and Ingurukade Junction, and the entry ramp for vehicles travelling from the Port and Ingurukade Junction towards Katunayake and Borella, will be closed from 9.00 p.m. today until 5.00 a.m. on November 25, including Poya Day tomorrow (24).

From November 25 to 27, the exit ramp for vehicles travelling from Katunayake and Orugodawatta towards the Colombo Port and Ingurukade Junction will be closed nightly from 9.00 p.m. to 5.00 a.m. the following day.

Both ramps will again be closed from 9.00 p.m. on November 28 until 5.00 a.m. on December 1.

During the closure periods, motorists travelling from Katunayake or Kandy towards Ingurukade Junction and the Colombo Port are advised to use the Peliyagoda or Orugodawatta exits.

Vehicles travelling from Orugodawatta towards the Colombo Port and Ingurukade Junction should use the ground-level road at the New Kelani Bridge.

Meanwhile, motorists travelling from the Colombo Port and Ingurukade Junction towards Katunayake are advised to use the ground-level road at the New Kelani Bridge and enter the highway through the Peliyagoda entry ramp. Those travelling towards Borella should also use the ground-level road at the New Kelani Bridge.

The RDA has requested motorists to follow temporary traffic signals, directional signs and instructions issued by police and authorised officers during the construction period.
