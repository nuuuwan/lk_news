Driver injured as police open fire on van in Gampaha

Gampaha, Sep 13 (Daily Mirror) - A van driver was injured after police opened fire at a vehicle suspected of transporting narcotics in the Asgiriya area of Gampaha last night (12), police said.

The incident occurred during a special operation launched by officers from the Weliweriya Police Station based on intelligence received by the Western Province Intelligence Division regarding a narcotics distribution network operating in the Gampaha area.

Police officers covertly followed the suspected van as it traveled toward Udugampola. The driver later stopped near Malwatta Junction on the Udugampola road.

When officers got out of their jeep to inspect the vehicle, the driver allegedly maneuvered the van back and forth several times, damaging several passing vehicles. The suspect then reportedly rammed the police jeep in an attempt to hit the officers and flee the scene.

Police subsequently opened fire at the van to stop it. The driver sustained gunshot injuries and was arrested before being admitted to Gampaha Hospital under police guard.

A search of the van reportedly uncovered 15 grams of heroin and 9 grams of ‘Ice’, a street name for methamphetamine.

The Weliweriya Police are conducting further investigations into the incident.
