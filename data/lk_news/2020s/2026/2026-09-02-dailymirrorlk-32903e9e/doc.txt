PM holds bilateral talks with Bhutanese counterpart

Colombo, September 02 (Daily Mirror)- Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya held bilateral talks with Bhutanese Prime Minister Dasho Tshering Tobgay in Thimphu during her visit to Bhutan to deliver the keynote address at the inaugural Global Conscious Food Systems Summit 2026.

The discussions focused on strengthening the longstanding and friendly relations between Sri Lanka and Bhutan and expanding bilateral cooperation, the Prime Minister’s Media Division said.

The two leaders exchanged views on several areas of mutual interest, including education, health, trade, tourism, air connectivity and people-to-people ties.

They also discussed closer cooperation to address climate change, combat deforestation, protect the environment and promote sustainable development.

Both leaders reaffirmed their commitment to deepening bilateral friendship and expanding mutually beneficial cooperation in areas of common interest.
