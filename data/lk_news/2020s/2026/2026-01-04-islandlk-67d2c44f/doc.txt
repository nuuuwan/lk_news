Top Australian influencer arrives to showcase Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka Tourism has welcomed award-winning Australian content creator Luana Ostling for an exclusive 10-day luxury tour aimed at highlighting the island as a premier travel destination for 2026.

Recognized as Australia’s Best Content Creator in 2025, Ostling boasts over one million followers. Her itinerary, organized by the Sri Lanka Tourism Promotion Bureau (SLTPB), covers iconic locations including Nuwara Eliya, Yala, Mirissa, and Galle to showcase the island’s diverse appeal – from wildlife safaris and whale watching to cultural heritage and luxury resorts.

“Luana’s visit is a strategic part of our campaign to reactivate international travel and present Sri Lanka as a safe, breathtaking, and resilient destination,” said SLTPB Chairperson Priantha Fernando.

The tour, supported by hospitality partners like Jetwing Hotels and Marriott, emphasizes Sri Lanka as an ideal locale for romance, adventure, and wellness. Ostling’s content will target key markets in Australia and the UK, aiming to restore traveler confidence and inspire visits in the coming year.

Her journey, which began on December 26, concluded on January 4, with content showcasing the island’s recovery and readiness to welcome global tourists.
