Harsha de Silva’s remark on Presidential advisors triggers verbal battle in Parliament

Colombo, Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - The recent remark by MP Harsha de Silva on  Presidential advisors triggered a verbal battle in Parliament today, with both MP de Silva and Leader of the House Bimal Ratnayake exchanging verbal attacks.

The battle broke out when MP Lakmali Hemachandra raised a question and said Duminda Hulangamuwa has not worked as a Presidential advisor both under former Minister Ranil Wickremesinghe and incumbent President Anura Kumara Dissanayake.

"We wonder whether MP De Silva accepts he had made a mistake," Hemachandra said.

Dr. de Silva who responded, said he never used the word advisor.

"The Sinhala meaning for advisor is upadeshaka. I never used the word upadeshaka but the word   Osthar. I don't even know the meaning of the word Osthar. I have not made a mistake," he said.

Minister Ratnayake who responded said MP de Silva has displayed what kind of politics he is following.

"One has to tender an apology if he has said something wrong or stands by his statement. We can see what kind of politics the MP is following," Leader of the House said.

This created a verbal battle and both members were heard shouting at each other.

"One cannot behave like children," Deputy Speaker Rizwy Salih who was at the chair was heard saying.
