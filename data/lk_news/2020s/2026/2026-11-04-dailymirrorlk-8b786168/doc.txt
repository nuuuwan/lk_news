Main line train services resume after fallen tree cleared in Polgahawela

Colombo, Nov. 4 (Daily Mirror) - Train services on the main railway line have returned to normal after a fallen tree obstructed the railway track near Polgahawela yesterday afternoon.

The tree fell onto a train travelling from Colombo Fort to Rambukkana as it approached Polgahawela Railway Station, causing disruptions to train operations.

The obstruction has since been cleared, and train services on the main line are now operating as usual.
