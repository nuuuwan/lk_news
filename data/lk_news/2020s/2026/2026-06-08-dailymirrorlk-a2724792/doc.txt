Judiciary will decide on Sallay: Fr. Cyril Gamini

Colombo, June 8 (Daily Mirror) - The Judiciary will decide the fate of former State Intelligence Service (SIS) chief Suresh Sallay, Director of Communications Fr. Cyril Gamini Fernando said today.

"A motion has been filed against the harassment of Sallay, and the Judiciary should decide on his fate," Fr. Fernando told a briefing.
