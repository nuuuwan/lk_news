Zoomtech Solutions launch new Meari CCTV camera models

Zoomtech Solutions (Pvt) Ltd. officially launched three new Meari CCTV camera models S1+, S2+ and G1 at a ceremony held at the Ramada Hotel Colombo recently.

Zoomtech Solutions, a CCTV and security solutions company, continues to expand its footprint under the leadership of its Founder and Managing Director S. D. M. Musthaq Mulaffer.

As the Authorised National Distributor of Hangzhou Meari Technology Co., Ltd., China, Zoomtech Solutions welcomed the company’s Head of Sales for South Asia Nafees Hossain as the Chief Guest at the launch ceremony. The new Meari CCTV camera models were officially unveiled at the event.

Zoomtech Solutions currently operates three showrooms in Colombo 11, including locations on Maliban Street and 1st Cross Street, as well as a branch on Galle Road, Mount Lavinia. Also, Meari Office in Colombo, Sri Lanka.

S. D. M. Mulaffer, CEO of Zoomtech Solutions (Pvt) Ltd., dealers, staff members, and industry representatives were present at the product launch ceremony.
