Indices close on a mixed note

The ASPI edged up as a result of price gains in counters such as Ceylinco Holdings, Haycarb and Dipped Products with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.3 Bn. Meanwhile, the S&P SL20 edged down. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Sampath Bank, Hatton National Bank and Hayleys. Mixed interest was observed in Commercial Credit & Finance, Haycarb and Sierra Cables whilst retail interest was noted in Browns Investments, Industrial Asphalts and Asia Siyaka Commodities. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

The Banking sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Sampath Bank and Hatton National Bank) whilst the sector index edged down by 0.02%. The share price of Sampath Bank closed flat at Rs 138.50. The share price of Hatton National Bank closed flat at Rs 380.00.

Materials sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover whilst the sector index increased by 0.87%.

Browns Investments, Asia Siyaka Commodities and Commercial Credit & Finance were also included amongst

the top turnover contributors. The share price of Browns Investments moved down by Rs 0.40 (7.02%) to close at Rs 5.30. The share price of Asia Siyaka Commodities recorded a gain of Rs 0.70 (4.61%) to close at Rs 15.90. The share price of Commercial Credit & Finance declined by Rs 4.75 (4.43%) to close at Rs 102.50.
