Youth arrested for whipping pony near Lake Gregory

Colombo, Aug. 28 (Daily Mirror) - A 26-year-old man has been arrested by Nuwara Eliya Police for allegedly assaulting a pony used for tourist rides near Lake Gregory in Nuwara Eliya.

Police launched an investigation after a video circulated on social media showing a man allegedly subjecting a pony to an inhumane assault.

The ponies are commonly used for rides around Lake Gregory, a popular tourist attraction visited by both local and foreign tourists.

The suspect, a resident of Nuwara Eliya, is due to be produced before the Nuwara Eliya Magistrate’s Court on August 28.
