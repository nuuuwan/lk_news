Hayleys Mobility unveils South Asia’s largest OMODA & JAECOO showroom, elevating premium new-energy vehicle experiences in Colombo

Hayleys Mobility Limited has announced the opening of South Asia’s largest OMODA & JAECOO (OJ) showroom flagship showroom at No. 127/1, Sir James Pieris Mawatha, Colombo 2.

The landmark facility marks a major milestone in transforming how Sri Lankans discover, experience and transition to premium, sustainable mobility solutions, reflecting Sri Lanka’s significant advancement in the region’s automotive landscape.

As the exclusive authorised distributor of OJ in Sri Lanka, Hayleys Mobility continues to accelerate the country’s transition towards a smarter, cleaner, and future-ready transportation ecosystem. Backed by a strategic partnership with the Chery Group, the company caters to diverse mobility needs and delivers next-generation vehicles complemented by robust charging infrastructure, aligned with its brand promise, “One Brand for Every Journey”.

Dedicated exclusively to the OJ portfolio, the flagship OJ showroom presents a curated portfolio of premium electric, plug-in hybrid and ICE vehicles. Customers can view and explore models such as the JAECOO J8, J7 PHEV, J6 and J5, alongside the OMODA C9 PHEV, C5 and E5.
