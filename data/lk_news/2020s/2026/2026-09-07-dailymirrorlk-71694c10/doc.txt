SriLankan Airlines cancels Colombo-Jakarta flights

Colombo, Sep. 7 (Daily Mirror) - SriLankan Airlines has cancelled its scheduled Colombo-Jakarta services today (07) due to volcanic ash from Indonesia’s Mount Anak Krakatoa affecting airspace in the region.

The airline said flights UL 364, operating from Colombo to Jakarta, and UL 365, from Jakarta to Colombo, were cancelled as a precautionary measure following the spread of volcanic ash.

Indonesian aviation authorities have also suspended operations at several airports, including Jakarta’s Soekarno-Hatta International Airport, after volcanic ash and silica-laden rock fragments spread across the area, creating potential risks to aircraft engines and flight control systems.

Authorities have further restricted flights through the western air corridor towards Sri Lanka, affecting international air traffic in the region.

The disruption follows the diversion of two SriLankan Airlines flights last night. Flights operating from Sydney and Melbourne were rerouted to avoid the affected airspace.
