Home remedies for tired eyes …

I guess all of us are experiencing it … yes, tired eyes, and I continue to get readers asking me for suggestions.

I’ve touched on this subject before, tired eyes, but I need to oblige my readers … again, and again!

Pop 02 metal spoons in the fridge for 15 minutes. Press the curved side gently on closed eyelids. The cold constricts blood vessels and de-puff and soothes strain.

Chill thick cucumber slices (rounds) and then lie down 10 minutes with them on your eyes. Cucumber is 96% water and has antioxidants. It hydrates, cools, and reduces dark circles.

Use cooled green tea bags. Keep cooled green tea bags for 10-15 minutes on closed eyes. First squeeze the excess water, so it doesn’t drip.

Whole milk has lactic acid and fats that soothe and moisturise. Dip cotton pads in chilled milk, press on eyes for 07-10 minutes. Great for those with dry, irritated eyes from screen time.

Use pure aloe vera gel, straight from the plant, chilled. Dab a tiny amount around the orbital bone, not on lids. It cools, hydrates, and the vitamins help with dark circles. Patch test first for sensitive skin.

Raw potato has natural bleaching enzymes and is anti-inflammatory. Keep thin chilled slices, on closed eyes, for 10 minutes. Helps with puffiness and dark circles.

Rose water is a natural astringent and smells amazing. Soak cotton pads, chill them, then rest them on eyes for 10 minutes. Refreshes and reduces redness after long hours at the laptop.

No rubbing eyes, and remember the 20-20-20 rule; every 20 minutes of screen time, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds.
