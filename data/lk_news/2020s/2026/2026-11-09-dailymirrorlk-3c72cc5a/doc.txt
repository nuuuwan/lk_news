Former President Gotabaya Rajapaksa arrives at CCIB

Colombo, Nov. 9 (Daily Mirror) - Former President Gotabaya Rajapaksa arrived at the Central Crime Investigation Bureau (CCIB) today (November 9) to provide a statement in connection with an ongoing investigation involving his former private secretary, Sugeeshwara Bandara.

Bandara, who is also the leader of the New People’s Front, is currently in remand custody.
