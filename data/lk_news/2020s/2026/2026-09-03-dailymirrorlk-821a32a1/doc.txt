Indices close in green

The ASPI closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Haycarb, Royal Ceramics and Dipped Products with the turnover crossing Rs. 2.4bn.

A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Renuka Foods, Dipped Products and John Keells Holdings.

Mixed interest was observed in Vallibel Finance, Haycarb and Alumex whilst retail interest was noted in UB Finance Company, Browns Investments and Softlogic Capital. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net buyers.

Food, Beverage & Tobacco sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Renuka Foods) whilst the sector index gained 0.85%. The share price of Renuka Foods decreased by Rs. 0.60 (2.26%) to close at Rs. 25.90.

Materials sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Dipped Products and Haycarb) whilst the sector index increased by 2.93%. The share price of Dipped Products moved up by Rs. 3.50 (6.02%) to close at Rs. 61.60. The share price of Haycarb appreciated by Rs. 12.50 (6.16%) to close at Rs. 215.50.

Vallibel Finance and John Keells Holdings were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Vallibel Finance lost Rs. 4.20 (4.62%) to close at Rs. 86.80. The share price of John Keells Holdings closed flat at Rs. 19.50.
