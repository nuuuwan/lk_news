Appeals open for 2026 Grade 5 Scholarship Exam answer sheets

Colombo, Sep. 7 (Daily Mirror) - The Department of Examinations has called for online appeals regarding the 2026 Grade 5 Scholarship Examination, conducted to select students for schools and award scholarships.

Accordingly, appeals for the re-examination of answer sheets will be accepted online from September 7 to September 21, 2026.

The appeal must be submitted by the Principal of the school attended by the examination candidate by logging into the SCHOOL LOGIN section of the Department of Examinations website using the username and password issued to the respective school.

The notice was issued by Commissioner General of Examinations A.K.S. Indika Kumari Liyanage on September 7, 2026.
