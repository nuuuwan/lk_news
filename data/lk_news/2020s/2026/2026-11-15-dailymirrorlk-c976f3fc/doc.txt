Former TNA MP M.K. Sivajilingam dies at 69

Jaffna, Nov 15 (Daily Mirror) - Former Tamil National Alliance (TNA) Parliamentarian M.K. Sivajilingam has passed away at the age of 69.

Sivajilingam, who had been suffering from an illness for some time, died last night (14), while serving as Chairman of the Valvettithurai Urban Council.

Born in Valvettithurai in the Vadamarachchi region, he began his political career through the Tamil Eelam Liberation Organization (TELO) and played an active role in the formation of the TNA, which brought together several Tamil political parties.

He represented the Jaffna District in Parliament after being elected at the 2001 and 2004 General Elections.

He later contested the 2010 Presidential Election as an independent candidate and was elected to the Northern Provincial Council in 2013.

His final rites are scheduled to be held in Valvettithurai, Jaffna.
