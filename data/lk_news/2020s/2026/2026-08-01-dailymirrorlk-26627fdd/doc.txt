Tuition classes, seminars banned ahead of grade 5, A/L exams

Colombo, August 1 (Daily Mirror) - The Examinations Department has imposed a ban on tuition classes, seminars and other examination-related activities ahead of the 2026 Grade 5 Scholarship and G.C.E. Advanced Level (A/L) examinations.

According to the Department, the restrictions will come into effect from 12.00 midnight on August 4 for the G.C.E. (A/L) Examination and from 12.00 midnight on August 5 for the Grade 5 Scholarship Examination, remaining in force until the completion of the respective examinations.

The 2026 G.C.E. (A/L) Examination will be held from August 10 to September 5 at 2,532 examination centres across the country, while the Grade 5 Scholarship Examination is scheduled for August 9 at 2,723 examination centres.

Issued under Section 22 of the Public Examinations Act, the regulations prohibit the conducting of tuition classes, thematic lectures, seminars and workshops for examination candidates. They also ban the printing and distribution of predicted question papers, as well as the publication, possession or circulation of posters, banners, brochures, handouts or other printed and electronic materials containing examination questions or similar questions.

The Department warned that any individual, institution or organisation violating these regulations will be committing an offence under the Public Examinations Act and may face legal action.

Members of the public have been requested to report any violations to the nearest police station, Police Headquarters, or the Department of Examinations through its hotline (1911) or the School Examinations Organisation and Results Branch.
