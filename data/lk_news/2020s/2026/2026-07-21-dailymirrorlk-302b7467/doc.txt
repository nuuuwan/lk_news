Five dead in Homagama factory fire

Colombo, July 21 (Daily Mirror) - Five people have died following a fire that broke out at a helmet manufacturing factory in the Katuwana Industrial Zone in Homagama this morning (July 21).

The fire erupted at the factory located within the Katuwana Industrial Estate, and fire-fighting teams are continuing efforts to bring the blaze under control.

Further details regarding the identities of the deceased and the cause of the fire are yet to be confirmed.
