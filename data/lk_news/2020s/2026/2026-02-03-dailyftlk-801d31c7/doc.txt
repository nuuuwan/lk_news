Marian Candappa no more

The remains of Marian J. Rodrigo Candappa, née Jurie, relict of the late Modestus Candappa, will lie at Lanka Florists, Punchi Borella Junction today (3) from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. Cremation will take place at the Old Crematorium General Cemetery Kanatte at 4 p.m.

Candappa was an active member of the Colombo Chetty Association, the Parish of Kotahena, and the Lions Club. Her working life was spent with Virakesari and Wijeya Newspapers.
