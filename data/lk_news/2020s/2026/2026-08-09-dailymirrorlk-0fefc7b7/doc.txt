Three Hettipola motorcycle dealers fined for overcharging

Colombo, Aug. 9 (Daily Mirror) - Three motorcycle dealers in Hettipola have been fined by the Hettipola Magistrate’s Court for selling motorcycles above the official dealer prices and failing to display prices, following raids conducted by the Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA).

The CAA said it had received complaints that certain dealers were taking advantage of limited motorcycle stocks and high demand by purchasing motorcycles and reselling them to consumers at inflated prices.

A licensed official motorcycle dealer had also informed the CAA that a waiting-list system had been introduced to ensure fair access to motorcycles amid the shortage. However, concerns had been raised that some individuals were purchasing motorcycles under different names and subsequently reselling them at higher prices.

Following the complaints, CAA officers conducted special inspections at motorcycle shops in the Hettipola area on July 27, 2026.

At Malji Auto City on Kurunegala Road, a motorcycle with an official dealer price of Rs. 790,000 was sold for Rs. 850,000, an excess of Rs. 60,000.

The business was also charged with failing to display motorcycle prices. A fine of Rs. 10,000 was imposed on each of the two charges.

Pradeep Motors on Chilaw Road was fined Rs. 10,000 on each of two charges after selling a motorcycle for Rs. 845,000 against the official dealer price of Rs. 790,000 and failing to display prices.

Meanwhile, Lucky Enterprises in Hettipola was fined Rs. 5,000 for failing to display the prices of motorcycles offered for sale.

The CAA urged consumers to check motorcycle prices carefully before making purchases and to report dealers who charge unfairly high prices or fail to display prices by calling the CAA hotline 1977 during office hours.
