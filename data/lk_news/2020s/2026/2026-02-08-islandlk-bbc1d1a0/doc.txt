A milestone in maternity care: Joseph Fraser Hospital’s medically supported water birth

Joseph Fraser Hospital recently marked an important milestone in its maternity services with the successful delivery of a baby through a medically supported water birth, reaffirming its commitment to personalised care, informed maternal choice, and the highest standards of clinical safety.

Water births at Joseph Fraser Hospital are offered within a closely monitored clinical environment and conducted in strict accordance with medical protocols. Each delivery is fully supported by the hospital’s upgraded Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU), Blood Bank, and High Dependency Unit (HDU), ensuring immediate specialist care for both mother and newborn when required.

This delivery was overseen by Professor Hemantha Senanayake, Emeritus Professor of University of Colombo who incorporated additional safety measures, including enhanced perineal support, contributing to improved clinical outcomes while preserving a calm and empowering birthing experience for the mother.

Commenting on the achievement, Professor Hemantha Senanayake said that water birth offers women an empowering and deeply personal experience when supported by appropriate clinical governance. Dr. Dilrukshi Sayyakra, Head of Medical Services, added that the hospital’s approach focuses on providing informed, flexible birthing options without compromising maternal or neonatal safety.
