LOLC Finance strengthens regional presence

Geli Oya Branch was officially inaugurated with a ceremonial ribbon-cutting attended by LOLC Finance Head of Gold Loan Nishantha Jayasekara, Head of Alternate Financial Services Shiraz Refai, Regional Manager Janaka Karunarathne and Branch Manager Ruwan Bandara

Opening ceremony of relocated Galenbidunuwewa branch was graced by LOLC Finance Head of Credit Upul Samarasinghe and Chief Digital Officer Hasala Thilekaratne

Further expanding its islandwide footprint, LOLC Finance PLC inaugurated its new Gelioya branch recently, at No. 67/1, Karamada, Gelioya.

Strategically located in an area experiencing growing commercial and community activity, the branch is expected to enhance access to a comprehensive range of financial services for individuals, entrepreneurs and small and medium-scale enterprises.

The ceremonial opening was attended by Head of Gold Loan Nishantha Jayasekara and Head of Alternate Financial Services Shiraz Refai together with the members of the LOLC Finance team, customers and distinguished invitees from the area.

In a further step towards enhancing customer convenience, LOLC Finance PLC also relocated its Galenbidunuwewa branch to a new premises on Seeppukulama Road, Galenbidunuwewa, recently.

The relocation is aimed at providing improved accessibility while continuing to offer a wide range of financial solutions tailored to the needs of individuals, entrepreneurs and businesses in the region.

The opening ceremony of the relocated branch was attended by LOLC Finance Head of Credit Upul Samarasinghe and Chief Digital Officer Hasala Thilekaratne, alongside the members of the LOLC Finance team, customers and well-wishers from the area.

With a rapidly expanding network of over 200 branches across Sri Lanka, LOLC Finance PLC continues to play a significant role in promoting financial inclusion and empowering communities through accessible and innovative financial services. Backed by one of the strongest fixed deposit bases in the industry, the company has earned sustained trust and confidence from its customers over the years.
