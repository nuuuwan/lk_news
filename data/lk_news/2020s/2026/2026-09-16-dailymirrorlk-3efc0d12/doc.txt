Veterinary surgeon attacked by leopard

Colombo, Sep. 16 (Daily Mirror) - The wildlife veterinary surgeon who attempted to save a hill country leopard caught in a wire trap had a harrowing experience this morning when it sprang at him causing him Injuries.

When Dr. Akalanka Pinideniya from Randenigala veterinary hospital approached the trap and attempted to tranquilise the leopard it broke away from the trap, sprang at him and ran into the jungle. The veterinary surgeon received minor injuries.

He said the leopard was a well grown animal and that it had not received serious injuries when caught in the trap. It was in perfect health and had the strength to break away from the trap.
