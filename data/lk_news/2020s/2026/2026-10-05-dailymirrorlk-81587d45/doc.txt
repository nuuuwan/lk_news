No-confidence motion against Speaker to be handed over on Friday

Colombo, Oct. 5 (Daily Mirror) - The Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) parliamentary group has decided to hand over the no-confidence motion against Speaker Jagath Wickramaratne on Friday, October 9, 2026, MP Ajith P. Perera told the media today.

According to MP Perera, there are 49 allegations against the Speaker.
