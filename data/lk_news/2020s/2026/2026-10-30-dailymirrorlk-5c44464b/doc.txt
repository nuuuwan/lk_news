They are trying to kill him’: Imran Khan’s son issues urgent warning over father’s life

Imran Khan’s son says his father had previously warned that he could suffer a similar fate to former Egyptian president Mohamed Morsi, who died during a court hearing in 2019

Imran Khan’s son has said no one has heard from his father since August, when the former Pakistani prime minister claimed he was being tortured.

Sulaiman Khan, his son from his first marriage to British heiress Jemima Goldsmith, alleged that his father’s life was in danger and blamed Pakistan’s army chief General Asim Munir for his continued incarceration.

Mr Khan, 74, is serving a prison sentence at Rawalpindi’s notorious Adiala Jail following convictions on corruption and bribery charges. His family has repeatedly raised concerns about his treatment in prison and deteriorating health.

The former prime minister and Pakistan’s former national cricket captain, who faces charges in more than 100 cases, has been held in solitary confinement. He has denied all charges against him, describing them as politically motivated.

Sulaiman Khan said the family and his father’s lawyers had not heard from him in months.
