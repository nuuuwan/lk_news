Sri Lanka to feature in exclusive Four Seasons private jet experience

Travel Weekly - Four Seasons has a new around-the-world Private Jet Experience for 2028 called “Coastlines & Culture,” which travels from San Francisco to the English countryside, with stops in Asia, Africa, the Middle East and the South of France along the way.

Four Seasons Private Jet Experiences use a custom-configured Airbus A321neo-LR that seats just 48 travelers. The experiences are operated and sold by TCS World Travel, and the aircraft itself is operated by Titan Airways Limited.

The new “Coastlines & Culture” journey runs May 6–26, 2028, and goes like this: Launching from San Francisco, guests cross the Pacific to Seoul, where they’ll experience Korea’s spiritual traditions through an immersive visit to Jingwansa Temple. Next stop is Hangzhou. There, guests join tea masters for a private experience at Meijuawu Tea Plantation and spend time at the centuries-old Jinshan Temple before a day trip to Shanghai.

Guests then travel to Sri Lanka to visit a private cinnamon estate. From there, the route continues to Zanzibar, where guests stay at the highly anticipated Four Seasons Resort Zanzibar and Swahili heritage and longstanding spice traditions offer a window into the island’s history. Next comes Sharm El Sheikh, where the Sinai desert meets the Red Sea, and the stay includes a private yacht charter along the coastline.

From the Red Sea, guests make their way to Cap-Ferrat for a stay at one of the French Riviera’s most iconic hotels, Grand-Hôtel du Cap-Ferrat, A Four Seasons Hotel. A privately chartered yacht excursion to Cannes offers a relaxed way to explore the coast before the itinerary’s final chapter in Hampshire. There, the English countryside sets the scene for a closer look at Britain’s royal history, including exclusive access to Hampton Court Palace.

A dedicated journey team includes a concierge, executive chef and journey physician.

Several Four Seasons properties will appear on Private Jet itineraries for the first time: Four Seasons Hotel San Francisco, Four Seasons Hotel Hangzhou at West Lake, Four Seasons Resort Sharm El Sheikh, Four Seasons Hotel Hampshire and the soon-to-open Four Seasons Resort Zanzibar.

A select number of seats remain on the following itineraries for those wishing to travel in 2027:

International Intrigue (March 3–23, 2027): Journey from cultural capitals and remote island escapes to the natural wonders of the African savanna.

New World Icons (March 26–April 14, 2027): Discover cultural landmarks, dramatic landscapes and restorative escapes across Asia, Europe and the Americas.

World of Adventures (Aug. 10–Sept. 2, 2027): Now welcoming families, this journey explores extraordinary natural wonders across four continents, from the Seychelles to the Galápagos and beyond.

Ancient Explorer (Sept. 3–25, 2027): Trace centuries of history and culture, from the Great Barrier Reef and Easter Island to the Pyramids of Giza.
