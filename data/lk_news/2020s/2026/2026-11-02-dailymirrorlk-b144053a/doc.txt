CSE holds near 20,000 as trading activity remains subdued

The Colombo Stock Exchange closed lower for a second consecutive session on 2 November 2026, with both benchmark indices weakening as early buying interest faded through the day.

The ASPI declined 62.78 points, or 0.31%, to 20,079.55, while the S&P SL20 fell 44.54 points, or 0.78%, to 5,701.86. The ASPI briefly moved towards the 20,180 to 20,190 region before selling pressure returned, leaving the index close to the key 20,000 psychological level.

Market turnover remained subdued at LKR 921.22 million, compared with the YTD daily average of LKR 3.41 billion. The Banks sector led activity, while Sampath Bank recorded the highest individual counter turnover at approximately LKR 319.42 million.

Crossings accounted for around 18% of total equity turnover, with all three block transactions recorded in Sampath Bank for a combined value of LKR 161.58 million.

Market breadth remained negative, with 74 gainers against 118 decliners, resulting in an advance-decline ratio of 0.63. Foreign activity was broadly neutral, with a marginal net outflow of approximately LKR 0.95 million.

Dialog Axiata was the largest negative contributor to the ASPI, followed by Commercial Bank, John Keells Holdings, Distilleries and LAMB.N. GREG.N provided the strongest positive contribution.

The combination of a second consecutive decline, weak breadth and sub-LKR 1 billion turnover indicates that short-term momentum remains soft. However, the ASPI continues to hold above 20,000, making this level an important near-term area to watch for signs of renewed buying support.
