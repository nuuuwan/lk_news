Royal Ceramics Lanka secures ‘Global Performance Excellence Award’

Royal Ceramics Lanka PLC (Rocell), Sri Lanka’s manufacturer of Tiles, Bathware, and Accessory solutions, has won the Global Performance Excellence Award (GPEA) 2026 – Best in Class.

Conferred by the Asia Pacific Quality Organization (APQO), the GPEA is an international recognition of performance and business excellence across the Asia-Pacific region. The award evaluates organizations against rigorous criteria, including leadership, strategic planning, customer focus, measurement, analysis, knowledge management, workforce engagement, and operational processes.

Complementing this, the International Best Practice Gold Award specifically honours Rocell’s engineering ingenuity in reusing waste heat from kilns by installing an advanced heat recovery system—a testament to the company’s commitment to sustainability and innovation.

Commenting on this stellar international achievement, Aravinda Perera Managing Director of Royal Ceramics Lanka PLC stated, “Being honoured with the GPEA 2026 – Best in Class award and the APQO International Best Practice Gold Award is a proud validation of our team’s unwavering commitment to excellence. At Rocell, we have always strived to push the boundaries of design, innovation, and quality, proving that a Sri Lankan brand can stand shoulder-to-shoulder with the best in the world. This award belongs to every member of the Rocell family whose dedication has made our vision of global excellence a reality.”

Over the years, Rocell has transformed the local landscape by introducing world-class lifestyle solutions, maintaining rigorous quality and environmental standards, and delivering exceptional customer experiences through its island-wide showroom network.
