University lecturers to strike tomorrow, FUTA says

Colombo, Sep. 8 (Daily Mirror) - University lecturers have decided to launch a one-day strike tomorrow (9), the Federation of University Teachers’ Associations (FUTA) said.

FUTA Secretary Senior Lecturer Charudatta Ilangasinghe said the union had been engaged in trade union action throughout the past week over the serious problems facing Sri Lanka’s state university system.

He said the shortage of lecturers and the lack of suitably qualified academic staff had become a major concern.

As a result, a situation has arisen where students who sat for the 2025 G.C.E. Advanced Level examination cannot be admitted to state universities, he said.

Ilangasinghe also said that although the President had announced plans to establish 50 new state universities, priority should be given to maintaining and improving the quality of the existing 17 universities.

He said FUTA was awaiting discussions with the relevant authorities and said that a permanent solution was needed to resolve the issues facing the state university system.
