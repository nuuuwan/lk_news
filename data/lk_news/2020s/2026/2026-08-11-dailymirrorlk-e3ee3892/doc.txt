CSE snaps four-day winning streak as indices edge lower

The Colombo Stock Exchange closed marginally lower on Tuesday, 11 August 2026, bringing an end to the ASPI’s four-session winning streak as buying momentum weakened during the day.

The All Share Price Index declined by 14.67 points, or 0.07%, to close at 21,402.80, while the S&P Sri Lanka 20 Index slipped by 2.36 points, or 0.04%, to end the session at 5,983.14.

The ASPI opened on a positive note and moved above the 21,450 level during early trading before gradually losing momentum. The index slipped below 21,400 around midday, although a modest late-session recovery helped limit the overall decline. The S&P SL20 followed a similarly volatile trajectory and also closed marginally lower.

Total market turnover stood at LKR 1.52 billion, with approximately 47.14 million shares changing hands during the session.

The Capital Goods sector dominated market activity, generating LKR 315.12 million in turnover from approximately 9.39 million shares. Hemas Holdings recorded the highest turnover within the sector, contributing LKR 86.07 million from around 2.77 million shares.

Crossing transactions accounted for approximately LKR 438.26 million, representing around 29% of total equity turnover. Dialog Axiata recorded the largest combined crossing activity, generating LKR 130.83 million from approximately 2.95 million shares.

Foreign investors remained net sellers, recording a net foreign outflow of LKR 356.19 million, continuing the recent trend of foreign selling pressure.

Market breadth turned slightly negative, with 95 gainers against 104 decliners, resulting in an advance-to-decline ratio of 0.91 and indicating mild selling pressure across the broader market.

Hatton National Bank was the strongest positive contributor to the ASPI, adding 8.47 points to the index. Richard Pieris, Melstacorp, Ceylinco Insurance non-voting and Carson Cumberbatch also supported the market.

Ceylinco Insurance recorded the largest negative contribution, reducing the ASPI by 7.86 points. Dialog Axiata, John Keells Holdings, SANASA Development Bank and C T Holdings also weighed on the index.

Among notable trades, Dialog Axiata recorded the highest individual counter turnover of the day at LKR 264.34 million, with approximately 5.97 million shares traded. The counter closed at LKR 44.30.

Overall, the session reflected a mild consolidation following four consecutive days of gains. While the decline in both benchmark indices was limited, weaker market breadth and continued foreign selling suggested a more cautious tone among investors.
