Bribery Commission reveals charges against Johnston

Colombo, Sep. 4 (Daily Mirror) - Former Minister of Co-operatives and Internal Trade Johnston Fernando and Design Point (Pvt) Ltd. owner Patabendigige Hemantha Lalith de Silva were arrested this morning by officers of the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) over an alleged corruption case involving the procurement of chairs for the Mahinda Rajapaksa Theatre in Polgolla.

According to CIABOC, the investigation relates to a decision to purchase the chairs from Design Point (Pvt) Ltd., despite another institution offering the lowest price while meeting the required standards.

The commission said the alleged arrangement caused a financial loss of Rs. 7,973,114.50 to the Central Engineering Consultancy Bureau (CECB) and resulted in an undue benefit being provided to an external party.

Johnston Fernando was arrested at 9:25 a.m., while Hemantha Lalith de Silva was arrested at 9:30 a.m. today.

CIABOC said Fernando was arrested on charges of corruption, while de Silva was arrested for aiding and abetting the alleged offence.

Both suspects are due to be produced before the Colombo Chief Magistrate’s Court.
