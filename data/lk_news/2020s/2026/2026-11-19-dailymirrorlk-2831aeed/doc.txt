Indices close in red

Indices closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Commercial Bank, Hatton National Bank and Dialog Axiata with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.9 Bn. Crossings were witnessed in Wealthtrust Securities Limited, Aitken Spence and Hatton National Bank nonvoting accounting for 51.7% of the turnover. Mixed interest was observed in Dipped Products, Kotagala Plantations and Sampath Bank whilst retail interest was noted in TAJ Rights, Browns Investments and Ceylon Land & Equity. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

Diversified Financials sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Wealthtrust Securities Limited) whilst the sector index lost 0.84%. The share price of Wealthtrust Securities Limited decreased by Rs 0.90 (7.69%) to close at Rs 10.80.

Capital Goods sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Aitken Spence) whilst the sector index decreased by 1.28%. The share price of Aitken Spence closed flat at Rs 150.00.

Hatton National Bank nonvoting, Dipped Products and Kotagala Plantations were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Hatton National Bank nonvoting edged down by Rs 0.25 (0.07%) to close at Rs 341.25. The share price of Dipped Products recorded a loss of Rs 2.30 (3.21%) to close at Rs 69.30. The share price of Kotagala Plantations closed flat at Rs 8.00.
