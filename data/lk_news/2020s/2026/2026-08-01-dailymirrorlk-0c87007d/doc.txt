Metro bus service to expand to Jaffna, Kandy by year-end: Minister

Colombo, August 1 (Daily Mirror) - Transport Minister Bimal Ratnayake says the government's Metro bus service will be expanded to Jaffna and Kandy before the end of this year, with a fresh fleet of buses set to enter service in the coming weeks.

The Minister said 121 Metro buses have been acquired from China and several other countries, and are currently undergoing legal procedures, including registration, before being deployed.

He said the buses are expected to be put into public service by the end of August once the necessary documentation is completed.

Ratnayake added that several new routes have already been identified for the Metro bus service, with details expected to be announced in the coming days.

He also revealed that Sri Lanka is due to receive an additional 50 Metro buses from Japan before the end of the year, enabling the service to expand beyond Colombo.

"We hope to launch the Metro bus service in Jaffna and Kandy by the end of this year. Metro depots will also be established in both districts," he said.

The Minister said the government's long-term objective is to encourage commuters to shift from private vehicles to public transport in order to reduce traffic congestion, fuel consumption and environmental pollution.

He further noted that passenger information on Metro buses will be displayed in Sinhala, Tamil and English, while the government aims to increase the Metro bus fleet to more than 500 by 2030.
