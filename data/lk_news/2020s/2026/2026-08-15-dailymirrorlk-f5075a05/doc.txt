Search and rescue launched after Navy craft accident

Colombo, August 15 (Daily Mirror) - A search and rescue operation has been launched after a Sri Lanka Navy fast attack craft encountered distress in the seas off Angulana while on a routine patrol.

It was reported that a group of naval personnel who were on board the craft were safely rescued and subsequently admitted to hospital for medical evaluation.

Further search and rescue operation underway by the Navy, the Sri Lanka Air Force, Police and local residents.
