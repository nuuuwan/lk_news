Severe lightning warning for entire island

Colombo, Oct. 14 (Daily Mirror) - A severe lightning warning has been issued for all provinces of Sri Lanka, with thundershowers expected in most parts of the island.

The warning is valid until 1.00 a.m. tomorrow (October 15) and covers all provinces of the country.

According to the Department of Meteorology, temporary, localised strong winds may also occur during thundershowers, the advisory said.

As such, the public has been urged to take adequate precautions to minimise damage and risks associated with lightning activity.
