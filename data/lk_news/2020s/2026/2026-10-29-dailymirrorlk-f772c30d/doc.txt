Rathnayake lights up Lahore but England have the last word

Colombo, Oct 29 (Daily Mirror) - Liam Dawson claimed four wickets as England defeated Sri Lanka by 55 runs in the final group match of the One-Day International tri-series at the Gaddafi Stadium in Lahore.

Chasing a formidable target of 387, Sri Lanka were bowled out for 331, despite a century from Pavan Rathnayake and a strong contribution from Kusal Mendis.

England, batting first, posted 386 for five in their 50 overs, powered by a career-best 167 off 132 balls from opener Ben Duckett.

Duckett shared a 163-run opening partnership with Tom Banton, who scored 74, laying the foundation for England’s imposing total. Contributions from Harry Brook, Will Jacks and Jordan Cox, who struck a quickfire 43, further strengthened the innings.

Sri Lanka began their chase positively, with Mendis scoring 89 to keep the side in contention. Rathnayake then produced an outstanding innings of 118 off 77 balls, keeping Sri Lanka within reach of the target as the required run rate increased.

However, Dawson changed the course of the match with figures of four for 49. His dismissal of Rathnayake in the 45th over triggered a collapse in the Sri Lankan innings.

Sri Lanka lost their final five wickets for just 16 runs, ending their chase at 331.

The victory gave England their first win of the tournament and ended a six-match losing streak in ODIs in Pakistan.

Despite the win, England failed to qualify for the final, while Sri Lanka advanced to face hosts Pakistan in the tri-series final on Sunday.
