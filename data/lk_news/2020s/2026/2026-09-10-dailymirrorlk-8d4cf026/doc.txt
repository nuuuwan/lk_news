18 Air Force athletes join Sri Lanka contingent for 20th Asian Games

Colombo, Sep. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka’s official sports contingent is scheduled to depart next Sunday (13) for the 20th Asian Games, which will begin on 19 September in Nagoya, Japan, with 18 athletes from the Sri Lanka Air Force (SLAF) among the 156 athletes representing the country.

The Sri Lankan contingent will compete in 25 sports at the Games.

Group Captain Eranda Geeganage of the Sri Lanka Air Force will serve as the Chef de Mission of the Sri Lankan delegation. He is currently the Secretary of the Air Force Sports Council and President of the Sri Lanka Squash Association. He is also a member of the International Military Sports Council and has made notable contributions to sports both locally and internationally.

World champion javelin thrower and Air Force athlete Sergeant Rumesh Tharanga will lead the entire Sri Lankan sports contingent.

Air Force pentathlete Flying Officer Isiwarana de Silva has been given the honour of carrying the Sri Lankan national flag at both the opening and closing ceremonies of the Asian Games.
