Ambewela celebrates double victory at Global Business Excellence Awards 2026

Sri Lanka’s premium fresh milk brand Ambewela has secured two important recognitions at the Global Business Excellence Awards 2026, being named Most Iconic Brand and Best Dairy Brand.

The dual recognition marks a milestone for Ambewela, celebrating both its excellence within the dairy category and the enduring trust it has earned among Sri Lankan consumers over the past 2 decades. The awards were presented on 6 October at the BMICH Exhibition Centre in Colombo 07. Organized by London Business Consultancy, United Kingdom, the Global Business Excellence Awards recognise achievements and excellence across industries. The programme received institutional recognition from DEC under the Ministry of Industry, Sri Lanka, with business focused research and academic review by the SITC Campus Business Faculty.

Being named Best Dairy Brand recognizes Ambewela’s strength and excellence within the dairy category, while Most Iconic Brand reflects the lasting connection the brand has established with Sri Lankan consumers.

Ambewela dairy products is a household name synonymous with freshness and quality. Originating from Sri Lanka’s lushest highland pastures, each Ambewela product is the result of a meticulous process that ensures the highest quality dairy delights, from farm to family. The product range, which includes fresh milk, cheese, and yoghurt, consistently offers superior value and quality, making it one of Sri Lanka’s most beloved brands.

The international recognition also strengthens Ambewela’s credibility and premium positioning, reinforcing its reputation as a leading Sri Lankan dairy brand on a wider platform. As Ambewela celebrates this significant achievement, the company said it remains committed to providing premium quality, unadulterated milk the consumers & nation deserves, from farm to familywhile contributing to the growth of Sri Lanka’s dairy industry.
