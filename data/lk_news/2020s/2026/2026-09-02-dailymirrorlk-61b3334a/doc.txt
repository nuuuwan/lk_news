PM Harini meets Bhutanese Prime Minister

The Prime Minister Dr. Harini Amarasuriya, who is visiting Bhutan to participate as the Keynote Speaker at the inaugural Global Conscious Food Systems Summit 2026, held a  bilateral meeting with Dasho Tshering Tobgay, Prime Minister of Bhutan in Thimphu.

The meeting focused on further strengthening the longstanding, close and friendly bilateral partnership between Sri Lanka and Bhutan and elevating cooperation between the two countries. The two leaders exchanged views on a wide range of areas of mutual interest, including education, health, trade, tourism, air-connectivity, and people-to-people contacts.
