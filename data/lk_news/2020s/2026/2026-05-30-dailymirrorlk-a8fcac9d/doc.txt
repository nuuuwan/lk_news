Fuel prices increased from midnight

The Ceylon Petroleum Corporation (Ceypetco) announced that fuel prices will be increased with effect from midnight today under its monthly fuel price revision.

Accordingly, the price of Auto Diesel has been increased by Rs. 15 per litre, raising the new price to Rs. 407, while Super Diesel has been increased by Rs. 20 per litre to Rs. 478.

The price of Octane 92 Petrol has been increased by Rs. 24 per litre to Rs. 434, while Octane 95 Petrol has been increased by Rs. 25 per litre to Rs. 495.

Meanwhile, the price of Kerosene has been increased by Rs. 20 per litre, bringing the new price to Rs. 285.

Ceypetco said the revised prices will come into effect from midnight today.
