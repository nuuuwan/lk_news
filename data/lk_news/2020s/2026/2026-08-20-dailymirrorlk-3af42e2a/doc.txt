14 Lamborghinis routed through Sri Lanka

Fourteen Lamborghini luxury vehicles shipped from Italy have been routed through Sri Lanka amid disruptions to global shipping caused by the closure of the Strait of Hormuz. The vehicles arrived at Hambantota Port before being transported in seven 40-foot containers to Bandaranaike International Airport, Katunayake, for onward air transport to Dubai and Doha, Qatar.
