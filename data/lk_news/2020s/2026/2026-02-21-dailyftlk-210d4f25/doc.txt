Abans Environmental Services sets new ethical benchmark as Sri Lanka’s first facility services provider to achieve SEDEX/SMETA

In a significant milestone for Sri Lanka’s facility services industry, Abans Environmental Services Ltd., has become the first company in the sector to achieve SEDEX membership and align with the globally recognised SMETA audit framework, setting a new benchmark for ethical, transparent, and sustainable business practices.

The SMETA verification was independently conducted by Control Union, a globally respected certification and verification body, providing strong third-party assurance of the company’s compliance with international standards covering labour practices, health and safety, environmental responsibility, and business ethics.

This achievement places Abans Environmental Services at the forefront of responsible business conduct in Sri Lanka’s facility services sector. By embracing SEDEX/SMETA, the company goes beyond compliance—demonstrating leadership in uplifting industry standards and championing ethical practices across operations and supply chains.

Established in 1985 and operating as part of the diversified Abans Group, Abans Environmental Services has built a reputation for reliability, professionalism, and operational excellence. For nearly four decades, the company has supported organisations across Sri Lanka by delivering integrated janitorial and facility management solutions that contribute to cleaner, healthier, and safer environments.

Its comprehensive service portfolio includes professional janitorial and cleaning services, tea services, pest control, and specialised cleaning solutions to commercial, industrial, institutional, and public-sector clients. With a skilled workforce and strong operational systems, the company continues to set high benchmarks in service quality, safety, and sustainability.

SEDEX (Supplier Ethical Data Exchange) is a globally recognised platform enabling organisations to manage and improve ethical performance across operations and supply chains. SMETA, one of the world’s most widely used ethical audit methodologies, evaluates critical areas such as labour standards, workplace safety, environmental management, and ethical business conduct.

By aligning with SEDEX/SMETA, Abans Environmental Services reinforces its commitment to transparency, accountability, employee welfare, and responsible sourcing, while aligning with evolving global ESG and sustainability expectations.

This milestone strengthens the company’s governance framework, enhances workplace safety, promotes fair labour practices, and builds greater confidence among clients, partners, and stakeholders—both locally and internationally.

Abans Environmental Services Managing Director Phiroze Pestonjee said: “Our SEDEX membership and SMETA verification represent a significant step forward in strengthening ethical labour practices, sustainability, and transparency across our operations. This milestone reflects our long-standing commitment to responsible business conduct while continuing to deliver reliable, high-quality, and environmentally conscious services to our clients.”

Abans Environmental Services Ltd., will continue to embed SEDEX/SMETA principles across its operations and supply chain, reinforcing ethical practices, sustainability, and operational excellence. By adopting global standards and driving responsible business practices within Sri Lanka, the company remains focused on long-term growth and creating lasting value for its clients, employees, and the wider industrial community.
