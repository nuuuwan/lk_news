Allianz reaches highest-ever brand value of US$ 30.7bn

Allianz has once again been recognised as the world’s most valuable insurance brand, securing this distinction for the eighth consecutive year in Interbrand’s Best Global Brands 2026 ranking.

The Allianz brand reached a record value of US$ 30.7 billion, increasing by 9 percent year-on-year and advancing to 26th position among the world’s most valuable brands across all industries.

The achievement reflects Allianz’s continued financial strength, customer-focused approach, and commitment to building trust in an increasingly complex and interconnected world. The Group’s brand growth is supported by strong business performance, including a record operating profit of EUR 9.4 billion in the first half of 2026, alongside sustained investments in innovation, customer experience, and brand management.

Through initiatives including the Allianz 3am Report, Allianz School for Life, Power of Unity, and Sense of Community, Allianz continues to support financial literacy, deepen understanding of customer needs, and strengthen connections within communities around the world.

Sport remains a powerful platform through which Allianz brings people together while promoting confidence, safety, and opportunity. In 2026, Allianz played a key role in supporting the safe and successful delivery of the Olympic and Paralympic Winter Games Milano Cortina 2026 as the Worldwide Insurance Partner of the Olympic and Paralympic Movements. The event welcomed more than 5.4 million spectators and reached an estimated global audience of 2.6 billion viewers.

The year also marked significant milestones in Allianz’s sporting partnerships. Allianz celebrated 20 years of partnership with the Paralympic Movement and announced a multi-year global partnership with world-leading tennis player Jannik Sinner. These developments build on a long-standing heritage of supporting sport around the world, including more than 25 years of partnership with FC Bayern München, support for hundreds of local sports organisations across Allianz markets, and a global network of Allianz-branded venues that welcome millions of visitors each year.

The latest Interbrand recognition underscores the strength of the Allianz brand as it continues to evolve alongside changing customer expectations and advances in technology. By consistently delivering on its promise of protection, confidence, and care, Allianz remains one of the world’s most trusted and valuable financial services brands.

The Interbrand Best Global Brands Ranking is widely regarded as one of the world’s leading assessments of brand value, evaluating brands based on financial performance, the influence of the brand on purchasing decisions, and overall brand strength.
