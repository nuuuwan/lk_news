Nawaloka Hospitals PLC, Colombo successfully performs open heart surgery on a child

Specialised paediatric heart assessments and surgeries are available at Nawaloka Hospitals PLC Colombo, bringing advanced cardiac treatment closer to children and families across Sri Lanka

Nawaloka Hospitals PLC, Colombo has successfully performed an open paediatric heart surgery on a child this week, marking another important achievement in its commitment to advancing specialised cardiac services in Sri Lanka.

The surgery was performed by a specialised medical team led by Dr. M. S. Mugunthan, MBBS (SL), MD (Col), MRCS (Eng), MRCSEd, FCSSL, Consultant Cardiothoracic Surgeon. The child is now reported to be in good health following the successful procedure.

This achievement highlights the availability of specialised paediatric cardiac surgical expertise, advanced operating theatre facilities and multidisciplinary medical support at Nawaloka Hospitals PLC, Colombo.

Paediatric heart conditions – Assessments and surgeries at Nawaloka Hospitals PLC, Colombo

Congenital heart conditions can affect children from birth. While some children may not show obvious symptoms, others may require specialist assessment, monitoring or surgical treatment.

Some children may have a heart condition without showing noticeable symptoms. Therefore, medical assessment is important whenever a heart condition is suspected or previously diagnosed.

Parents do not need to wait until their child’s condition becomes serious. If your child has been diagnosed with a heart condition, is experiencing concerning symptoms or has been advised to undergo further cardiac investigations, visit Nawaloka Hospitals PLC, Colombo directly for a specialist consultation and assessment. The medical team will evaluate the child’s condition, arrange the necessary investigations and determine the most appropriate next course of action, keeping the child’s health and well-being as the priority.

Whether the child requires monitoring, further investigations, medical management or surgery, the treatment plan will be determined following an individual medical assessment. Supported by cardiac specialists, advanced operating theatres, critical care facilities and multidisciplinary medical teams, Nawaloka Hospitals PLC, Colombo provides support throughout the child’s treatment journey, from initial assessment to surgery, recovery and follow-up consultations.

Financial assistance through the Government of Sri Lanka’s Suraksha Student Insurance Scheme may be available for eligible schoolchildren requiring approved medical treatment, subject to the scheme’s terms and conditions.

Nawaloka Hospitals PLC, Colombo staff can guide parents regarding the relevant insurance arrangements, required documentation and applicable procedures. Parents, referring doctors and families seeking a second opinion are welcome to visit Nawaloka Hospitals PLC, Colombo for paediatric cardiac assessments and specialist consultations.

Early medical assessment can help identify potential heart conditions and determine appropriate treatment options. Parents are encouraged to seek professional medical advice whenever they have concerns about their child’s heart health.

This successful surgery represents more than a medical achievement. It brings renewed hope to families and reinforces Nawaloka Hospitals PLC Colombo’s commitment to making specialized paediatric cardiac assessments and surgical treatment more accessible to children across Sri Lanka.
