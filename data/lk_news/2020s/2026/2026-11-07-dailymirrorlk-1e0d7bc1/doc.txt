Arpico welcomes festive season with cake mixing ceremony

Arpico Supercentre welcomed the festive season with its Christmas Cake Mixing Ceremony 2026.

The event was graced by members of Arpico’s management committee, which joined in the traditional cake mixing ceremony and extended their warmest Christmas wishes to all valued customers and their families.

This year’s cake mixing ceremony sets the stage for another exciting festive season, featuring special Christmas promotions, seasonal offerings, and engaging customer experiences across Arpico Supercentre outlets.
