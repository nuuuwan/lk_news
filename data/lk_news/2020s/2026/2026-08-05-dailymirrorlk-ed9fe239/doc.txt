Sri Lanka to add 132 km to expressway network by 2030

Colombo, Aug. 5 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka’s national expressway network will expand by an additional 132 kilometers by the end of 2030 following the completion of four major infrastructure projects, according to Transport Minister Bimal Rathnayake.

Among the four initiatives contributing to this expansion, construction is currently underway on two crucial segments of the Central Expressway. These include the 37-kilometer Stage 1 stretch from Kadawatha to Mirigama and the 33-kilometer Stage 3 stretch from Pothuhera to Galagedara. Both sections are actively progressing to enhance connectivity across the country’s central corridor.

In addition to the ongoing work, the Kurunegala–Galewela road project—the first phase of the Kurunegala–Dambulla Expressway—is scheduled to commence before the end of 2026. The 38-kilometer section has an estimated cost of Rs. 233 billion. It will be executed as an accelerated development project divided into 12 contract packages, awarded through a transparent and competitive tendering process that utilizes the services of domestic construction companies. Furthermore, construction on the 24-kilometer Ruwanpura Expressway is also slated to begin within this year.

" For the first time, these expressway projects will be financed using the Sri Lankan government's own domestic funds. The initiatives are being implemented in strict adherence to the government’s strategic development policy framework to ensure sustainable national infrastructure growth," the minister said.
