Two Russians faces near death situation in the sea off Hiikaduwa

Colombo, Nov. 23 (Daily Mirror) - Two Russian tourists were rescued by police lifesaving officers after being caught in strong sea currents while bathing at Hikkaduwa Beach during the weekend

According to police, the incident occurred on the when the couple, aged 38 and 40, were swept out to sea while enjoying a bath. .

Two police Constables Nasurdeen and Kumara, attached to the Police Lifesaving Unit and stationed along the beach, responded to the emergency and successfully rescued both tourists.
