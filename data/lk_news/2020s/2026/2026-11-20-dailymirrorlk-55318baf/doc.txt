Opposition MP’s adjournment motion calling for release of Imran Khan rejected

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - The adjournment motion calling for the release of former Pakistan Prime Minister Imran Khan has been rejected as the government has stated it is not ready to discuss in Parliament matters which affect bilateral ties with other nations.

Deputy Minister of External Affairs Arun Hemachandra told Parliament that the motion was rejected as the government is not ready to discuss matters which affect bilateral ties.

He said MP Ajith P. Perera who was to move the motion is free to talk of the situation pertaining to the former Pakistan Prime Minister whenever he speaks in Parliament.

"There is no necessity for a motion to talk of matters pertaining to other countries," he said.

The Deputy Minister made these remarks when MP Perera questioned as to why his motion was rejected.
