Navy rescues four fishermen stranded at sea amid Cyclone Arnab

Colombo, Nov. 3 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Navy safely rescued four fishermen who had been stranded at sea following the impact of Cyclone Arnab and brought them to Hambantota Port this morning (November 3).

The fishermen were rescued by the Navy's Offshore Patrol Vessel (OPV) SLNS Sindurala and subsequently transferred to Hambantota General Hospital for further medical treatment.

The four fishermen had departed from Kudawella Fisheries Harbour on October 21 aboard the multi-day fishing trawler Eagle (IMUL-A-0022-TLE). However, communication with the vessel was lost on October 23.

The boat's owner reported the trawler missing to the Department of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (DFAR) on October 27, following which the department sought the Navy's assistance through the Maritime Rescue Coordination Centre (MRCC) Colombo.

The Navy launched a joint search and rescue operation on October 27 in accordance with international maritime conventions, including the United Nations Convention on the Law of the Sea (UNCLOS), the International Convention for the Safety of Life at Sea (SOLAS) and the International Convention on Maritime Search and Rescue.

The operation involved the Sri Lanka Coast Guard, Sri Lanka Air Force, local fishing vessels and merchant ships operating in the region, with SLNS Sindurala deployed for the search.

Following coordinated communications among fishing and maritime communities, the missing trawler and its four crew members were located.

SLNS Sindurala reached the vessel on November 2 and safely took all four fishermen aboard. They were provided with initial medical assistance and humanitarian support before being brought ashore.

Meanwhile, a separate coordinated search and rescue operation is continuing for three other fishermen who have not returned after departing from Chalai on October 26.
