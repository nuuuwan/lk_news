NPP to slow down President’s islandwide rallies

Colombo, Sep. 16 (Daily Mirror) - The Government has decided to space out the series of islandwide public rallies scheduled to be attended by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake, Agriculture Minister K.D. Lal Kantha said today.

Speaking to reporters, he said the National People’s Power (NPP) had initially planned to hold two rallies in each district, with the participation of the President, to mark the completion of two years of the Government’s tenure.

However, the NPP has now decided to revise the schedule and increase the intervals between the rallies, the Minister said.

“We had initially planned to hold the rallies at short intervals. However, following the revision of the schedule, some rallies may be held later than originally planned. All the rallies will be held as scheduled, but with longer intervals between them instead of being held according to a tight timetable,” he said.

President Dissanayake began the series of public rallies on September 6 in Kalutara, followed by rallies in Anuradhapura and Polonnaruwa.
