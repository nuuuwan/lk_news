Autodoc 360 relocates to reinforce commitment to premium auto care

Autodoc 360 (Pvt) Ltd, a leading name in premium automotive service and repair, has officially shifted operations to No. 378/2/C, Koswatta Road, Kalapaluwawa, Rajagiriya, a new, larger, and more advanced facility, marking a significant milestone in the company’s growth journey in Sri Lanka.

The relocation reflects Autodoc 360’s steady expansion and rising demand among owners of luxury and performance vehicles. The new premises have been purpose-designed to accommodate advanced diagnostic bays, precision repair zones, and a significantly enhanced parts inventory—ensuring faster turnaround times and superior service quality for customers.

According to the management, relocation was driven by the need to support high-end European vehicles, especially BMW and Mercedes-Benz, which require specialised infrastructure, tooling, and technical expertise. The new workshop allows the team to operate with manufacturer-grade accuracy while maintaining international workshop standards.

A key strength of Autodoc 360 lies in its technical backbone. The workshop is staffed by technicians from authorised dealership backgrounds, each with 10 to 15+ years of hands-on experience with luxury vehicles. The facility is equipped with German OEM-grade tools and diagnostic systems that meet European workshop standards, ensuring precise fault detection and manufacturer-accurate repairs rather than generic trial-and-error solutions. On the customer-facing side, Head Service Advisor Ranil Alwis brings several years of experience at authorised BMW workshops and deep expertise in German and European automotive technology, particularly BMW and Mercedes-Benz standards. His ability to accurately assess issues, coordinate manufacturer-aligned repair processes, and communicate technical matters clearly and reassuringly makes him an integral part of Autodoc 360’s service delivery.

Beyond infrastructure, Autodoc 360 differentiates itself through its specialised focus on BMW and Mercedes-Benz vehicles, offering end-to-end maintenance and repair under one roof. Services include complete maintenance and mechanical repairs, brake and suspension systems, steering repairs, breakdown assistance, and full air-conditioning overhauls—all executed using approved procedures and genuine or globally recognised replacement parts.

A major highlight is the company’s extensive parts inventory, covering multiple premium brands with long-term warranties. Customers have access to internationally trusted names such as Brembo, Bilstein, as well as genuine BMW and Mercedes-Benz spares. In addition, Autodoc 360 proudly represents Düsseldorf, a globally recognised automotive parts brand known for its wide product range, consistent quality, and strong international presence.

The company is led by a diverse and experienced leadership team, combining local market understanding with international exposure. The Sri Lankan partners include Jehan Amaratunga and Hasith Senarathne, while the Indian partners are Jaitegh Singh Chandhok and Anuj Singh Chandhok. Together, the directors bring a shared vision of delivering authorised-workshop level quality with greater transparency, flexibility, and value.

With its relocation to a state-of-the-art facility and a strong emphasis on precision, authenticity, and warranty-backed parts, Autodoc 360 Pvt Ltd continues to set new benchmarks in luxury automotive care, positioning itself as a trusted destination for discerning BMW and Mercedes-Benz owners across Sri Lanka.
