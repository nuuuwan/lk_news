Deputy HC in South India meets top Microsoft official, discusses AI collaboration with Sri Lanka

Microsoft Data and Artificial Intelligence (AI) Director Cecil Sunder met Deputy High Commissioner of Sri Lanka for Southern India Ganesanathan Geathiswaran at his office on 13 January.

During the meeting, the discussions focused on strengthening cooperation between Sri Lanka and Microsoft, particularly in the areas of data analytics and AI. The discussions also covered potential collaborations in education, skills development, and technology-driven solutions that could support Sri Lanka’s development priorities.

Special attention was given to the revision of AI curricula and the introduction of structured AI skilling programs, with an emphasis on building digital capacity among students, teachers, and young professionals. It was also proposed to focus these initiatives in Sri Lanka, with the aim of empowering youth through technology, enhancing employability, and supporting inclusive regional development.

Sunder also expressed his intention to visit Sri Lanka in the near future to further explore these collaborative initiatives.

The Deputy High Commission of Sri Lanka for Southern India extends its sincere appreciation to Sunder for his generous financial contribution towards the Sri Lanka Flood Relief Fund. His support during this critical time is deeply appreciated.
