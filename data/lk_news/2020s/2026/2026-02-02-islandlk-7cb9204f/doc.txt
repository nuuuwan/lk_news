Won’t be zooming to Canada

Monique Wille, the well-known radio personality and singer who’s been making waves in the scene here, was eagerly looking forward to her very first overseas assignment, as a solo artiste.

It was mentioned that she was to be feature at a Valentine’s Dinner Dance, scheduled for 07th February, in Canada, along with Elvis Tribute singer Johan Cigan, but, unfortunately, due to unavoidable circumstances, she won’t be zooming to Canada.

Monique has certainly left her mark in the music scene, performing with the late Sunil Perera and the Gypsies, and is now focusing on her solo career, as a singer, as well as exploring new creative avenues.

Yes, she is a part of Gold FM, one of Sri Lanka’s top English radio stations, and is on the air from 8.00 pm to midnight, from Sunday to Thursday.

Monique helped usher in the New Year at a five-star venue, in Colombo, along with the band Hector Dias and D Major.

She was also seen in action, as a solo artiste, at quite a few events, during the month of January.
