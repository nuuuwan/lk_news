28th Presidential Export Awards on December 8

The 28th Presidential Export Awards 2026, organised by the Sri Lanka Export Development Board (EDB), will be held on Tuesday, December 8, 2026, at the Bandaranaike Memorial International Conference Hall (BMICH), under the patronage of President Anura Kumara Dissanayake.

A media briefing on the event was held yesterday at the EDB. It was attended by EDB Chairman and Chief Executive Officer Mangala Wijesinghe, Director General Erandika Dissanayake, Additional Director General Development Kumuduni Mudalige and Director General Finance and Administration Roshan Jayasinghe.

Representing this year’s partner organisations were Bank of Ceylon Senior Deputy General Manager Rohana Kumara, Commercial Bank Assistant General Manager Dilan Rajapaksa, Sri Lanka Tea Board Chairman R.K. Obeysekera and Sri Lanka Export Credit Insurance Corporation Chairman and Managing Director Prof. A. Anura Shantharachchi, along with the other officials.

The Presidential Export Awards programme was launched by the EDB in 1981 and to date, 27 award ceremonies have been organised.

Approximately 1,500 guests, including the Cabinet Ministers, diplomatic officials, high level government officials and exporters, are expected to participate in the 28th Presidential Export Awards ceremony.

This year’s awards ceremony is held to recognise Sri Lanka’s best exporters for the financial year 2025/2026. A total of 117 awards will be presented under two main categories, comprising 15 overall awards and 102 product and service sectoral awards.

Additionally, merit awards will be presented to the qualified sectors as determined by the panel of judges, taking into consideration the applicants’ performance as well as the export contribution of the relevant sectors to the national economy.

The selection of sectoral awards is based not only on the export revenue but also on several other criteria, including export market diversification, employment generation, growth in export earnings, repatriation of export earnings to the country, environmental sustainability and corporate social responsibility and value addition.

Demonstrating the export community’s enthusiasm for this prestigious award, a large number of applications were received by the EDB this year. The initial evaluation of applications was conducted by the Technical Committee and the selection of award winners was made by a distinguished panel of judges headed by a retired judge of the Supreme Court.

Ernst & Young (EY) serves as the Knowledge Partner for the 28th Presidential Export Awards ceremony.

In line with the new government’s policy framework, new strategies have been implemented to expand Sri Lanka’s exports and it is expected that Sri Lanka’s exports will increase significantly as a result.

Accordingly, the government considers the initiatives taken by the exporters in areas such as market diversification, product diversification, innovation, value addition, introducing local brands to the world, sustainability of exports and development of regional value chains as important for achieving its targets.

The Presidential Export Awards ceremony presents the winners with a trophy or plaque and certificate and the award recipients are entitled to use the Presidential Export Awards logo as a marketing tool for a period of three years.

The 28th Presidential Export Awards ceremony is organised by the EDB under the supervision of Wijesinghe, under the guidance of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development Minister Sunil Handunneththi and on the advice of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development Deputy Minister Chathuranga Abeysinghe and ministry Secretary Thilaka Jayasundara.

Bank of Ceylon has joined hands with the EDB as the Platinum Partner, while Commercial Bank serves as the Gold Partner. The Sri Lanka Tea Board and Sri Lanka Export Credit Insurance Corporation serve as Silver Partners for the 28th Presidential Export Awards ceremony, which is held to promote and encourage exports from Sri Lanka.
