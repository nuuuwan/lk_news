Special information system launched for 2026 Kandy Esala Perahera

Colombo, Aug. 17 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Police has introduced a special information management system to help the public easily access essential information during the 2026 Kandy Esala Perahera.

The annual Kandy Esala Perahera will commence on August 18 and continue until August 28, with special security and traffic arrangements in place throughout the festival.

Police said the special programme includes roadblocks, security duties, vehicle and person checks, tourist assistance and vehicle parking arrangements.
