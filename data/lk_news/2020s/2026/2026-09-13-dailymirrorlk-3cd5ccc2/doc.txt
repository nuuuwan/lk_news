Heavy rains affect over 17,000 people across the country

Colombo, Sep. 13 (Daily Mirror) - More than 17,000 people have been affected by adverse weather conditions reported across the country, with 10 deaths and nine injuries reported following heavy rainfall, according to the Disaster Management Centre (DMC).

The DMC said a total of 17,054 people had been affected by the adverse weather conditions, while 4,360 people had been directly impacted by climate-related incidents.

The heavy rains have also caused considerable damage to property, with 847 houses partially damaged and four houses completely destroyed.

Meanwhile, eight families comprising 25 people have been relocated to safe locations as authorities continue to respond to the situation.

The DMC said authorities were continuing to monitor the weather conditions and provide assistance to affected communities.
