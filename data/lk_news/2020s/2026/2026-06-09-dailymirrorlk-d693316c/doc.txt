14-hour water cut in Galle tomorrow

Colombo, June 9 (Daily Mirror) - The National Water Supply and Drainage Board (NWSDB) has announced a 14-hour water cut in the Galle Municipal Council area tomorrow (10) to facilitate essential maintenance work.

According to a statement issued by the NWSDB, the water supply will be suspended from 9.00 a.m. until 11.00 p.m. as maintenance activities are carried out at the Wakwella Water Treatment Plant.
