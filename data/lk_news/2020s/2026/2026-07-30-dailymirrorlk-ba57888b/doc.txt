Rathna Sri Wijesinghe steps down as National Library Board chairman

Colombo, July 30 (Daily Mirror) - Veteran lyricist Rathna Sri Wijesinghe has resigned from his position as Chairman of the National Library and Documentation Services Board.

Wijesinghe, a highly respected figure in Sri Lankan literature and music, said the decision to resign was based solely on personal reasons, particularly due to age, and not because of any loss of confidence in the President or the government.
