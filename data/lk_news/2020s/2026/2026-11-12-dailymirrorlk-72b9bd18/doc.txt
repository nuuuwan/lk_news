WindForce September-quarter profit rises 5%

WindForce PLC’s group profit after tax rose about 5 percent year-on-year to Rs.985 million in the September quarter, as the higher direct costs limited the benefit of a 17 percent rise in revenue.

The group revenue increased to about Rs.2.99 billion, from Rs.2.56 billion in the three months ended September 30.

The gross profit rose about 13 percent to Rs.1.75 billion, while the gross margin narrowed to about 58.5 percent, from 60.8 percent.  The quarterly figures were calculated by subtracting the June-quarter results from WindForce’s rounded first-half figures.

For the six months ended September 30, the group revenue rose 20 percent to Rs.5.61 billion and gross profit increased 12 percent to Rs.3.21 billion. Profit after tax rose 5 percent to Rs.1.86 billion, from Rs.1.77 billion a year earlier.

WindForce estimated that the restrictions on how much renewable power it could supply to the grid cost it Rs.193.57 million in first-half revenue.

The Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka has approved a compensation formula and directed the National System Operator to pay the generators based on their applicable tariffs. WindForce said discussions were continuing and that the timing and recovery of compensation remained uncertain.

During the quarter, WindForce secured Rs.22.74 billion and the rupee equivalent of US $ 33 million in syndicated loans and bank guarantees for its 100 MW Rividhanavi solar project in Siyambalanduwa.

It also commissioned a 5 MW floating solar plant at the Dambulu Oya Reservoir and a 10 MW battery storage facility in Anuradhapura in September.

Four more battery facilities began operating in October, taking its operational storage capacity to 50 MW, with 200 MWh of energy storage.
