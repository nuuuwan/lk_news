Sri Lanka Embassy in Doha recovers over Rs. 80 million in compensation

Colombo, Aug. 18 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Embassy in Doha has recovered compensation amounting to more than Rs. 80.8 million on behalf of the next of kin (NoK) of Sri Lankan nationals who died in Qatar during the first seven months of 2026.

The Embassy recovered a total of SLR 80,886,895.32 in death compensation between January and July, despite challenges arising from the tense regional situation.

Of the total amount, SLR 13,229,128 was paid directly to the respective next of kin through the Embassy’s efforts, while SLR 67,657,767.32 was recovered and transferred to the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism for disbursement to the relevant beneficiaries.

The recovered amount also included compensation from cases that had remained pending since 2023.

Sri Lankan Ambassador to Qatar Sithara Khan and Minister Dharmasiri Wijewardane coordinated with the relevant companies, the Consular Affairs Division of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism, the Qatari authorities and the legal firm working with the Embassy to facilitate the recovery of the compensation.

The Embassy had recovered a total of SLR 197,719,710.36 in compensation during 2025 and facilitated the disbursement of the funds to the respective next of kin in Sri Lanka.
