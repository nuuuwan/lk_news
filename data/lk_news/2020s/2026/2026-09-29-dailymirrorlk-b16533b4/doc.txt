US Army Pacific Deputy Commander arrives in Sri Lanka

Colombo, Sep. 29 (Daily Mirror) - Lieutenant General Joel “JB” Vowell, Deputy Commanding General of the U.S. Army Pacific (USARPAC), arrived in Colombo today (29) for a three-day official visit aimed at strengthening military cooperation between the United States and Sri Lanka.

His visit, scheduled from September 29 to October 1, marks a significant milestone in bilateral military relations, as he is the highest-ranking U.S. Army officer to visit Sri Lanka.

During the visit, Lieutenant General Vowell is scheduled to meet senior Sri Lankan military officials to discuss opportunities to strengthen interoperability, military readiness and defence cooperation.

Discussions will focus on upcoming joint military exercises, capacity-building programmes, and cooperation in humanitarian assistance and disaster response (HADR).
