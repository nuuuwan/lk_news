Flight ban above Parliamentary complex to be relaxed

Colombo, Nov. 13 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake yesterday announced that the government is looking towards lifting the ban on flying large aircraft over the Parliamentary complex so that large planes could operate from the Ratmalana Airport.

This is line with the government policy of developing Ratmalana International Airport for pilot training, domestic, business and regional aviation.

"To ensure aviation safety, a project of Rs. 6,000 million to rehabilitate the existing runway at Ratmalana and extend it from 1,400 metres to 1,771 metres will commence in 2027 with funds from AASL." the President announced.

The President had a few moments of humour with the Opposition MPs just after he announced the budget proposal. "It is not possible to develop Ratmalana without lifting the flight ban over the Parliamentary complex. I hope you won't become worried about it. There is nothing to fear," he said amidst jeering of Opposition MPs.

Also the President announced the plan to select an investor for the Mattala Airport next year.

"The Mattala International Airport, which was constructed with a loan of USD 190 million and commenced flight operations in 2013, has today recorded an accumulated loss of over Rs. 70,000 million. Moreover, this loss grows annually. Therefore, Expressions of Interest (EOI) have already been called for and concluded for the development of the areas of airport operations, data centres, food and catering services, and aircraft services and maintenance jointly with international companies. Steps are being taken to call for Requests for Proposals (RFP) from the selected companies. We expect to select new investors for this by next year.
