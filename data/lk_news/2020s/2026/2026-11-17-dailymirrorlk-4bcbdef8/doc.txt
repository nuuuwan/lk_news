Cyclonic Storm ‘Akvan’ intensifies over Bay of Bengal

Cyclonic Storm ‘Akvan’ has intensified over the Bay of Bengal and is expected to develop into a Severe Cyclonic Storm today, the Department of Meteorology said.

The system, which was earlier identified as a Deep Depression, was located around 640 kilometres from Trincomalee at 11.30 p.m. yesterday (16).

It is expected to move north-northwestwards and intensify into a Severe Cyclonic Storm over the west-central Bay of Bengal by 5.30 p.m. today.

The system is likely to further intensify into a Very Severe Cyclonic Storm by 5.30 a.m. on November 19 before moving northwestwards.

According to the Department, the storm is expected to cross the Andhra Pradesh and Yanam coasts between Nellore and Tuni around 11.30 a.m. on November 20 as a Very Severe Cyclonic Storm.

Meanwhile, several spells of showers are expected in the Western, Sabaragamuwa and North-Western provinces and in the Kandy, Nuwara Eliya, Galle and Matara districts today.

Showers or thundershowers may occur at a few places in the Uva Province and in the Ampara and Hambantota districts after 2.00 p.m.

Mainly fair weather is expected in other parts of the country, while misty conditions may prevail in several areas during the early hours of the morning.

The public has been advised to take precautions against temporary localised strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.
