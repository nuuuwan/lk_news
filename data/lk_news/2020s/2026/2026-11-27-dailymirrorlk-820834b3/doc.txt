IRD warns public over fake weekend service notice

Colombo, Nov. 27 (Daily Mirror) - The Inland Revenue Department (IRD) has warned the public not to be misled by a fake announcement claiming that all its regional offices will remain open tomorrow (28) and Sunday (29) to issue PIN and Taxpayer Identification Numbers (TIN).

Commissioner General of the Department said the announcement circulating among the public had not been issued by the department and was false.

The Department said there was suspicion that a group had deliberately prepared and circulated the fake notice to mislead the public ahead of the November 30 deadline for filing income tax returns for the 2024/2025 assessment year.

The department has requested the relevant authorities to investigate the incident and take necessary action against those responsible.

The public has been urged to obtain accurate information through the IRD's official website or its 1944 hotline, and not to rely on unofficial announcements.
