UNP urges government to recommence WB aided Colombo City Flood Mitigation Project

The United National Party (UNP) today urged the government to recommence the World Bank (WB) assisted Colombo City Flood Mitigation Project to prevent flash floods in the city.

"In 2018, under the UNP, the Metro Colombo Urban Development Project was launched, which included a USD 104 million flood-mitigation project funded by the World Bank. However, due to the bankruptcy crisis of 2022, the completion of this project was not possible. The government must immediately prioritise this project to reduce the risk of future flooding," Dinouk Colombage of the Working Committe said in a statement.

"The flash floods last evening (6) in Colombo clearly demonstrated the government’s inability to prepare for natural disasters. There has been continual warnings from both domestic and international Meteorologist Departments that Sri Lanka would receive higher than average rainfall during this year’s monsoon season due to the growing impact of El Niño. However, the authorities have once again demonstrated their inability to respond to emergency situations. The citizens of Colombo were not just inconvenienced by the flash floods, but more importantly many motorists faced situations which endangered their lives. One of the main contributions to the flooding of the city was the failure by the Colombo Municipal Council in ensuring the maintenance of the sewage drains and canals around the city. One of the key responsibilities of the CMC is the maintenance of the Municipalitie's drainage system. The Mayor and the CMC must immediately explain the reason behind the failure to uphold this infrastructure."
