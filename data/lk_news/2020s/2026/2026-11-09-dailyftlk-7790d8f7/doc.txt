Global Brands Awards and ‘Global Business Forum’ at Temple Trees

Key event brings together international delegates, distinguished personalities and entrepreneurs

Colombo became a meeting point for international business engagement as the Global Business Forum 2026 – Season 3, Sri Lanka Chapter, was held on 31 October at Temple Trees, Colombo.

The international gathering brought together business leaders, entrepreneurs, distinguished personalities and delegates, creating a platform for professional engagement, international networking and recognition of business achievements.

Organised by ANYELP GROUP, founded by Zakir Hussain, the program focused on strengthening connections between international business communities while highlighting the importance of innovation, leadership, technology and sustainable business development.

Indian actor Mohammed Ali and Nobel Peace Prize laureate Professor Mohan Munasinghe were among the prominent personalities associated with the gathering.

A central feature of the event was the Global Brands Awards 2026 – Sri Lanka Chapter, which recognised selected entrepreneurs, business organisations and professionals for their achievements and contributions to their respective industries.

The awards initiative aims to highlight individuals and organisations demonstrating leadership, innovation, professional excellence and commitment to business development.
