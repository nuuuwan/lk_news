Kurunegala-Galewela expressway work moves ahead with 12 contracts

Colombo, Nov. 17 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet of Ministers has approved the award of 12 contract packages for the construction of the first phase of the fourth section of the Central Expressway, covering the 38.5-kilometre stretch from Kurunegala to Galewela.

The road section, extending from Kurunegala (km 78+000) to Galewela (km 116+500), forms part of the fourth section of the Central Expressway project from Kurunegala to Dambulla.

The construction of this phase was approved by the Cabinet at its meeting held on June 30, 2026.

Bids were invited under the National Competitive Procurement System to select contractors for the project, which has been divided into 12 contract packages.

Following an evaluation of the bids received, the Cabinet approved the proposals submitted by the Minister of Transport, Highways and Urban Development to award the contracts to the lowest responsive bidders.

The awards were made based on the recommendations of the High Level Procurement Committee.
