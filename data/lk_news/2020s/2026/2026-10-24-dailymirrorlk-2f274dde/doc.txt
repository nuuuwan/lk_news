Four Thai women arrested in Wellawatte brothel raid

Colombo, October 24 (Daily Mirror)- Four Thai women were arrested during a police raid on a brothel operating under the guise of a massage parlour in Wellawatte yesterday evening, Police said.

The raid was conducted in the Marine Drive area of the Wellawatte police division based on information received by a team of officers from the Wellawatte Police Station.

A woman suspected of managing the establishment was also arrested, along with four Thai women aged between 35 and 43, who were allegedly involved in prostitution.
