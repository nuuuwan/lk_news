Bubbles & Beyond wins Golden Business Excellence Award 2025

Sri Lankan luxury wellness brand Bubbles & Beyond has been named the winner of the Excellence in Luxury Ayurvedic & Artisanal Skincare Innovation of the Year – 2025 at the Golden Business Excellence Awards. The ceremony was held recently at the City of Dreams, Cinnamon Life.

Founded in 2023 by Dr. Wathsadani Wijayasundara, a medical doctor with a deep-rooted Ayurvedic heritage, the brand distinguishes itself through clinically informed formulations inspired by traditional botanical wisdom. Each product is handcrafted in small batches, merging medical precision with ancestral knowledge to transform daily self-care into a ritual of purity and purpose.

In just two years, Bubbles & Beyond has emerged as a premium artisanal label celebrated for its authenticity and innovation. As global demand rises for science-backed natural skincare, this national recognition solidifies the brand’s position as a leader bridging Sri Lankan heritage with contemporary luxury.
