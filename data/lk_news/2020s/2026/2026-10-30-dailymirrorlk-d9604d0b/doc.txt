Bread price increased by Rs. 5 from midnight

Colombo, October 30 (Daily Mirror) - The price of a 450-gram loaf of bread will be increased by Rs. 5 with effect from midnight today (30), the All Ceylon Bakery Owners’ Association (ACBOA) announced.

ACBOA President M.K. Jayawardena said the Association had also decided to increase the prices of other bakery products by Rs. 5.

The revised prices of bread and other bakery products will come into effect from midnight today, he added.
