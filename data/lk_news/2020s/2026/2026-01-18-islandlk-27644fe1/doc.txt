Pelwatte Dairy wins ‘Best Dairy Products Brand of the Year 2025’ at People’s Pinnacle Awards

Pelwatte Dairy has been honored with the ‘Best Dairy Products Brand of the Year 2025’ award at the prestigious People’s Pinnacle Awards 2025. This significant achievement reflects the strong trust, loyalty, and love shown by consumers across the country.

The People’s Pinnacle Awards celebrate brands that truly resonate with the public, making this recognition especially meaningful as it is driven by the voice of the people. The award stands as a testament to Pelwatte Dairy’s unwavering commitment to quality, innovation, and excellence across its entire range of dairy products.

Pelwatte Dairy extends its sincere gratitude to its valued customers for their continued

support and confidence in the brand. This milestone would not have been possible without the dedication and hard work of the Pelwatte Dairy team and the strong partnerships with

local dairy farmers who form the backbone of the company’s success.
