Singer Sri Lanka honoured for ethical business practices

Singer Sri Lanka PLC has been awarded the Gold Award in the Trading Companies category at an awards ceremony organized by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Sri Lanka, recognizing the pinnacle of corporate reporting achievement in the country.

The Gold Award is a powerful endorsement of Singer’s robust corporate governance framework and its unwavering commitment to transparency, accountability, and ethical business practices. The accolade highlights the company’s leadership in sustainability and responsible retailing, from strengthening community initiatives to embedding sustainable operations across its island-wide network.

Mahesh Wijewardene, Group Managing Director, stated: “This Gold Award is a reflection of the disciplined governance culture we have embedded across the Singer Group. Transparency and accountability are not compliance requirements for us—they are core principles that guide every strategic decision we make.”

As a market leader in Sri Lanka’s retail and trading sector, the win reinforces Singer’s ability to integrate sound financial management with innovative trading excellence. With this latest milestone, Singer continues to strengthen its position as one of the country’s most respected organizations, setting new benchmarks in corporate accountability and sustainable growth.
