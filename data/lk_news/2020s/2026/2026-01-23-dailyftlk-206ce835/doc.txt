Infinity Gift Card partners with Singer Sri Lanka

IGC Director Priyantha Herath (third from left) exchanging the partnership agreement with Singer Sri Lanka PLC Managing Director Mahesh Wijewardene in the presence of Singer Sri Lanka Manager Legal (Corporate and Conveyancing) Deshani Thabrew, IGC Head of Business Diyana Buharie, Singer Sri Lanka COO Vajira Tennakoon, General Manager Marketing (White Goods) Piyum Jayathilake and Assistant Accountant Retail Operation Milinda Weththasinghe

Offers Singer Sri Lanka e-gift vouchers to gift-givers and recipients via Infinity Gift Card platform

Infinity Gift Card Ltd. (IGC), a leading digital fintech e-commerce platform, has partnered with Singer (Sri Lanka) PLC (SSL) a market leader in Sri Lanka’s consumer durable industries, enabling their customers to access a wider selection of gift cards to enjoy the exceptional gifting experience online and in-store.

Singer Sri Lanka Managing Director Mahesh Wijewardene said: “With the daily updates on new technologies, people are being more educated and user friendly with digital platforms. Therefore, gifting digital gift cards at various occasions will be the future of the gifting tradition. And SSL is excited to partner with IGC to explore new ventures that would mutually benefit”.

Whilst IGC assures the eGift Card Sender a joyous gifting experience, the recipient is offered the choice to choose from their most loved brands ensuring seamless gift card redemption journey.

IGC Director Priyantha Herath said: “This partnership marks a great opportunity for customers to experience the joy of gifting Singer products through the Infinity Gift Cards platform. With this collaboration, gifting has become easier and more convenient than ever. We are delighted to onboard one of Sri Lanka’s largest and most trusted brands to the Infinity platform.”
