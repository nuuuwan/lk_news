Week ends in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Ceylinco Holdings, John Keells Holdings and Royal Ceramics with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.5 Bn. A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Sampath Bank, Access Engineering and Hatton National Bank. Mixed interest was observed in Commercial Bank, Ceylon Cold Stores and Lanka Milk Foods whilst retail interest was noted in Cargills Bank, LVL Energy Fund and Teejay Lanka. Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers.

The Banking sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Sampath Bank, Commercial Bank and Hatton National Bank) whilst the sector index edged down by 0.11%. The share price of Sampath Bank decreased by Rs 0.50 (0.35%) to close at Rs 142.00. The share price of Commercial Bank gained Rs 0.25 (0.12%) to close at Rs 208.75. The share price of Hatton National Bank edged down by Rs 0.25 (0.06%) to close at Rs 400.00.

Capital Goods sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Access Engineering) whilst the sector index decreased by 0.22%. The share price of Access Engineering recorded a gain of Rs 0.30 (0.39%) to close at Rs 78.20.

Ceylon Cold Stores was also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Ceylon Cold Stores moved down by Rs 1.00 (0.76%) to close at Rs 130.00.
