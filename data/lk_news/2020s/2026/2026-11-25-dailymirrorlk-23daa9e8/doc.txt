Kathina ceremony in Maryland

Sri Lankan Buddhist devotees participate in the annual Kathina Robe Offering Ceremony at the Maryland International Buddhist Temple in the United States on November 22. The ceremony was held under the patronage of Venerable Katugastota Uparathana Nayaka Thero, Chief Judicial Sanghanayake of the United States and Chief Incumbent of the temple. It was attended by Venerable Maharagama Dhammasiri Thero, other Sri Lankan and resident monks, Acting Sri Lankan Ambassador to the US Maduka Wickramarachchi and a large gathering of Sri Lankan devotees living in Maryland. The traditional Kathina robe and other offerings were presented to the Maha Sangha as part of the annual religious observance. Pix by Sisira Wijesinghe from Washington.
