C.W. Mackie appoints Asoka Piyadigama as Executive Director – Finance

C.W. Mackie PLC has announced the appointment of Asoka Piyadigama as its Executive Director – Finance, effective 1 August 2026.

A Fellow Member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Sri Lanka (FCA) and a Member of CPA Australia, Piyadigama has worked at the intersection of finance, strategy, and enterprise growth, with expertise spanning corporate finance, treasury management, risk management, performance optimisation, mergers and acquisitions, capital allocation, and regulatory stewardship.

His international experience includes exposure to the FMCG sector in Australia, where he held roles with Bega Cheese Limited and Riviana Foods Pty Ltd. Furthermore, having partnered closely with Boards and executive leadership teams, he has consistently contributed towards building resilient organisations and unlocking sustainable value.

Prior to joining C.W. Mackie PLC, Piyadigama served as the Chief Executive Officer of CA Sri Lanka and held the position of Chief Financial Officer at several listed companies, and with senior roles at Carson Cumberbatch PLC, NDB Housing Finance PLC and Lankem Ceylon PLC,during which he played a pivotal role in enhancing financial discipline, advancing governance practices, improving operational effectiveness, and supporting strategic growth agendas.He began his career in audit with KPMG Sri Lanka and Deloitte Fiji.

Prior to taking up this latest appointment, Piyadigama served as an Independent Non-Executive Director of York Arcade Holdings PLC, C.M. Holdings PLC, and ACME Printing and Packaging PLC. He also served on several board committees within the CFLB Group, including Audit, Related Party Transactions, Remuneration, and Governance Committees. In addition, he provides strategic finance advisory services to international business operations of a BPO.
