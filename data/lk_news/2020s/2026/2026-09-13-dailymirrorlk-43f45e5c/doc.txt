FitsAir suspends Colombo-Dubai flights until November

Colombo, Sep. 13 (Daily Mirror) - FitsAir has temporarily suspended its Colombo-Dubai flight service with effect from September 6, 2026, citing the evolving situation in the Middle East region.

The airline said it is closely monitoring developments in the region and assessing their impact on its flight operations.

According to the current schedule, FitsAir expects to resume flights between Colombo and Dubai from November 19, 2026.

However, the airline said the proposed resumption date would depend on the prevailing situation and any future developments in the region.

FitsAir expressed regret over the disruption and inconvenience caused to passengers and thanked them for their patience and understanding during the temporary suspension.
