Veteran singer Nanda Malini dies at 82

Colombo, Aug. 20 (Daily Mirror) - Veteran Sri Lankan vocalist and one of the country’s most celebrated singers, Nanda Malini, has passed away at the age of 82.

Sri Lanka Singers Association (SLSA) President Keerthi Pasquel, who said that arrangements for her final rites will be announced later.

Nanda Malini was widely regarded as one of Sri Lanka’s greatest vocalists, with a career spanning several decades. Her powerful voice and distinctive style made her a beloved figure in Sinhala music.

Her contribution to Sri Lankan music, particularly Sinhala film and classical music, has left a lasting legacy.
