‘Medical advice for the elderly’ launched

Author Champika Deepani Ranasinghe presents her book “Medical Advice for the Elderly,” Sri Lanka’s first publication focusing on diseases affecting the elderly, featuring medical advice and insights from specialist Dr. Seethira Seneviratne. The book, the 31st publication in the Sadeepa – A Book Series for a Healthy Life, was launched today at the BMICH Lavender Hall in Colombo. Pix by Pradeep Dilrukshana
