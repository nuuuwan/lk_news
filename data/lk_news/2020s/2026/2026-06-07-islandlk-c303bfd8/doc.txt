Reshan Liyanage wins Global CEO ‘45 Under 45’ Platinum Award for hospitality leadership

Reshan Liyanage, Executive Director and CEO of Regal Réseau Hotel & Spa under Regal Holdings, has received the Global CEO “45 Under 45 – Leadership Excellence Platinum Award 2026.” The award, presented at a ceremony in Colombo, recognizes his leadership, entrepreneurial vision, and contributions to advancing Sri Lanka’s hospitality industry.

Under Liyanage’s leadership, the Negombo-based property has gained recognition for luxury accommodations, personalized service, and innovative concepts such as The Sky Track Lounge & Bar. The hotel recently earned the Agoda Golden Circle Award 2025 for guest satisfaction and service quality. Regal Holdings has also expanded its hospitality portfolio with the launch of Regal Rivera Villa, a luxury boutique villa targeting exclusive traveler segments.

Liyanage attributed the achievement to his team’s dedication and reaffirmed Regal Holdings’ commitment to innovation, service excellence, and promoting Sri Lankan hospitality globally. Beyond hospitality, Regal Holdings operates Sri Lanka’s first and only ISO 9001:2015 certified filling station, highlighting the group’s broader focus on operational excellence and customer satisfaction across sectors.
