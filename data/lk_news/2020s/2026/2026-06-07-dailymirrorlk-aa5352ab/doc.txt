“Miss Polski” beauty pageant arrive

A delegation of 45 members, including 24 finalists of the Miss Polski national beauty pageant, arrived at Katunayake Airport early morning aboard Turkish Airlines flight TK-1130 via Istanbul.

The group will take part in a special international promotional stage of the competition being hosted in Sri Lanka for the first time.

The Sri Lankan stage of the competition will run for 10 days in Sigiriya, with additional promotional shoots and cultural experiences planned across some of the country’s most scenic locations such as Sigiriya, Pasikuda, Knuckles / Neela Valley, Minneriya National Park, Dambulla and Colombo.

Each location will showcase Sri Lanka’s cultural heritage, natural beauty, and wildlife diversity to an international audience. Pix by T.K.G Kapila
