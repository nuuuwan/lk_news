CPC reduces prices of furnace oil and naphtha

Colombo, Sep. 4 (Daily Mirror) - The Ceylon Petroleum Corporation (CPC) has decided to reduce the prices of furnace oil and naphtha, with the new prices coming into effect from midnight today (04).

CPC Managing Director Mayura Netthikumara said the price of a litre of furnace oil has been reduced by Rs. 38, from Rs. 248 to Rs. 210.

Meanwhile, the price of a litre of naphtha has been reduced by Rs. 35, from Rs. 210 to Rs. 175.

He said the prices of furnace oil and naphtha will be revised on the fifth day of every month.
