Viral video raises questions over police conduct

Colombo, August 14 (Daily Mirror) - A video circulating on social media has raised questions over police conduct during a routine vehicle check near the Wariyapola Police Station.

According to the video, a man was travelling by motorcycle with his daughter from Kurunegala towards Padeniya at around 1:00 a.m. on August 8, 2026, when a group of police officers signalled them to stop while displaying a firearm.

“On 08-08-2026, at around 1:00 a.m., I was travelling by motorcycle from Kurunegala towards Padeniya with my daughter. Near the Wariyapola Police area, several police officers stopped us by showing weapons and signalling us to stop.  At that time, my motorcycle was travelling at around 45 km/h, possibly a maximum of around 50 km/h. When I saw the signal to stop, I was already slowing down and preparing to stop. I personally was not very frightened at the time. However, when I looked at my daughter, I realised that she was very frightened after seeing the weapons. My question is: Is it normal or legally permitted for police officers to display weapons when stopping a vehicle for a routine traffic check?.  My concern is that such an action could cause a rider to panic and fall from the motorcycle, collide with another vehicle, or even become frightened and continue without stopping. I am not posting this to accuse anyone. I simply want to know what the proper legal and normal police procedure is in a situation like this.”, according to the post he shared on social media.
