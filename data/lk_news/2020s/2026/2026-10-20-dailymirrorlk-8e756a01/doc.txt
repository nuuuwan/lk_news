Litro says islandwide gas distribution running smoothly

Colombo, Oct. 20 (Daily Mirror) - Litro Gas Lanka Limited said that liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) distribution is being expedited and proceeding smoothly across the country.

The company said the vessel Francois arrived in Sri Lanka on October 18, carrying 3,900 metric tons of gas, and that unloading operations are currently underway.

Meanwhile, the fourth gas-carrying vessel, Corinthian, is scheduled to arrive on October 22 with a further 3,900 metric tons of gas.

Litro Gas said the arrival of these shipments would help stabilise supplies across the island and ensure regular distribution to the market without interruptions or shortages.
