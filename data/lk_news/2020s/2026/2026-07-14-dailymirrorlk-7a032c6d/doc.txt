MBAPPÉ vs YAMAL! World Cup Final Spot on the Line

July.14 (Mirror Sports) - The FIFA World Cup 2026 reaches its biggest showdown yet as France and Spain clash in a blockbuster semi-final with a place in the final at stake. Kylian Mbappé's free-scoring France face Lamine Yamal's European champions in a battle between two of football's brightest stars and leading title contenders.

France and Spain meet in the first FIFA World Cup 2026 semi-final at Dallas Stadium in Arlington, Texas, with kick-off scheduled for 12:30 a.m. SLST on Wednesday, July 15.

Led by Golden Boot frontrunner Kylian Mbappé, France have powered into the last four after victories over Norway, Senegal, Iraq, Sweden, Paraguay and Morocco. Spain, inspired by teenage sensation Lamine Yamal, have defeated Saudi Arabia, Uruguay, Austria, Portugal and Belgium to continue their impressive march toward the title.

The semi-final brings together two of the tournament's strongest sides. France boast one of the competition's most explosive attacks, while Spain have built their campaign on disciplined defending, midfield dominance and clinical finishing.

The winners will advance to the FIFA World Cup 2026 final, where they will face either Argentina or England, setting the stage for another unforgettable chapter in World Cup history.
