FACETS Sri Lanka 2027: 34th edition of a dazzling legacy

From left: EDB Chairman Mangala Wijesinghe, SLGJA Vice President Operations and COO Shezan Mansoor, SLGJA President Akram Cassim, FACETS Sri Lanka 2027 Chairman Shezmin Mansoor, National Gem and Jewellery Authority Chairman Prof. S.P. Chaminda and Cinnamon Life - City of Dreams Hotel Manager Roshan George

FACETS 2027 Chairman Shezmin Mansoor announces dates for next three years

The 34th edition of FACETS Sri Lanka, organised by the Sri Lanka Gem and Jewellery Association (SLGJA), will take place from January 4 to 6, 2027, at the iconic Cinnamon Life at City of Dreams Colombo.

The landmark event will bring together over one hundred exhibitors representing the finest gem and jewellery companies in the country. Buyers from the USA, Europe, the Middle East, India, China, Thailand and other key markets will converge in Colombo to experience Sri Lanka’s unmatched craftsmanship and gemstone excellence.

A press conference to announce the 34th edition brought together key stakeholders from across the public and private sectors, with several distinguished guests sharing their views on the significance of the exhibition and the continued growth of Sri Lanka’s gem and jewellery industry. Among them were National Gem and Jewellery Authority Chairman (NGJA) Prof. S.P. Chaminda, Export Development Board (EDB) Chairman Mangala Wijesinghe and Cinnamon Life at City of Dreams Hotel Manager Roshan George, who reaffirmed their respective organisations’ commitment to supporting Sri Lanka’s premier international gem and jewellery exhibition.

The 34th edition of FACETS Sri Lanka will include dedicated sections that showcase the island’s world‑renowned sapphires and contemporary luxury jewellery, while a series of special events — including Sapphire Night, Story Corner, Power Breakfast, Mine Tour and Port City Tour — will create opportunities for networking, knowledge‑sharing and collaboration.

The success of FACETS Sri Lanka continues to be strengthened by the support of the EDB and NGJA. As long-standing partners of the exhibition, the EDB plays a key role in connecting Sri Lanka’s gem and jewellery industry with international buyers and markets, while the NGJA works to uphold the industry standards and support the sector’s sustainable growth. Together with the SLGJA, they have helped establish FACETS as the country’s premier international gem and jewellery exhibition.

The SLGJA unveiled the FACETS 2027 theme and promotional teaser video during the media conference, while also announcing the event dates for the next three years, reinforcing the exhibition’s role as a cornerstone of Sri Lanka’s gem and jewellery calendar.  FACETS Sri Lanka 2028 will be held from January 5 to 7, FACETS Sri Lanka 2029 from January 4 to 6 and FACETS Sri Lanka 2030 from January 6 to 8.

Speaking on the significance of the event, SLGJA President Akram Cassim remarked, “FACETS 2027 represents the very best of Sri Lanka. It brings together our centuries‑old gemstone heritage, our skilled artisans, our entrepreneurs and our spirit of innovation, reminding the world that Sri Lanka is not only the source of exceptional gemstones but also a trusted destination for excellence in the global gem

FACETS Sri Lanka 2027 Chairman Shezmin Mansoor added, “FACETS 2027 shines a spotlight on the journey of Sri Lanka’s greatest natural treasures. More than an exhibition, FACETS is a celebration of our heritage, our artisans and the pursuit of perfection that has established Sri Lanka as a trusted leader in the global gem and jewellery industry. We invite you to discover nature’s rarest treasures, its people and the purpose behind every facet. FACETS is where the world comes together to craft the future and to celebrate excellence.”

Echoing this sentiment, SLGJA Vice President Operations and COO Shezan Mansoor stated, “As the country’s premier gem and jewellery show, this event is more than an exhibition — it is a catalyst for growth, innovation and collaboration. By bringing together leading sponsors, valued partners, industry stakeholders and international buyers, we are creating a platform that unlocks new opportunities for businesses, strengthens Sri Lanka’s position in the global gem and jewellery market and showcases the exceptional craftsmanship and heritage of our industry. We look forward to welcoming participants to an event that will inspire meaningful partnerships and drive the future of the sector.”
