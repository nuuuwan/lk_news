SL makes landmark appearance as Guest Country of Honour at 9th China International Import Expo 2026

Sri Lanka marked a significant milestone in its international trade promotion and economic diplomacy by participating as a Guest Country of Honour at the 9th China International Import Expo (CIIE 2026), held from 5 to 10 November 2026 at the National Exhibition and Convention Center in Shanghai, China.

This prestigious recognition highlighted Sri Lanka’s growing international presence and provided an important platform to promote its export excellence, investment opportunities, national identity and rich cultural heritage.

Organised by the Sri Lanka Export Development Board (EDB), under the Ministry of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development, in collaboration with the Embassy of Sri Lanka in Beijing and the Consulate General of Sri Lanka in Shanghai, the participation reflected a coordinated national effort to strengthen bilateral trade and investment relations with China. Sri Lanka’s recognition as a Guest Country of Honour, alongside Canada, was particularly significant as the country has participated continuously in CIIE since its inauguration in 2018.

Under the national promotional theme “Your Vital Island,” Sri Lanka presented its unique strengths through a dedicated 252-square-metre National Country Pavilion, featuring five key institutions: the EDB, Sri Lanka Tea Board, Coconut Development Authority, National Gem and Jewellery Authority and Sri Lanka Handicrafts Board (Laksala). The pavilion showcased internationally recognised products, including Ceylon Tea, Ceylon Cinnamon and other spices, precious gemstones and jewellery, coconut-based products, coir products and traditional handicrafts. A major highlight was the visit of Chinese Vice Premier H.E. Ding Xuexiang to the Sri Lanka Pavilion, which further enhanced the country’s international visibility and the significance of its participation.

In addition to the national pavilion, 16 Sri Lankan private-sector companies participated in the Enterprise Pavilion, representing the Food and Agriculture and Gems and Jewellery sectors. Their participation provided valuable opportunities to connect with Chinese and international buyers, identify potential distributors, establish business relationships and explore new market opportunities. A high-level Sri Lankan delegation led by Economic Development Deputy Minister Nishantha Jayaweera further strengthened the country’s engagement with Chinese government representatives, business leaders and potential investors.

Sri Lanka also showcased its cultural heritage at the CIIE Culture Night held on 6 November 2026, attracting approximately 650 distinguished guests. The event featured traditional cultural performances by Chandana Wickramasinghe and the Dancers’ Guild, including the traditional “Ukusa Vannama.” The program celebrated the longstanding friendship between Sri Lanka and China while highlighting the importance of cultural diplomacy and people-to-people connections alongside economic cooperation.

An important objective of Sri Lanka’s participation was to promote Sri Lanka Expo 2027 among Chinese buyers, importers, distributors and investors, encouraging them to visit Sri Lanka, explore its export capabilities and establish long-term business partnerships. With bilateral trade between Sri Lanka and China reaching approximately $5.2 billion in 2025, including Sri Lankan exports of approximately $278.58 million, CIIE offered a valuable opportunity to further diversify exports and expand market access.

SLEDB said Sri Lanka’s participation as Guest Country of Honour at CIIE 2026 represents more than a prestigious international exhibition achievement. It demonstrates the country’s commitment to expanding global trade partnerships, attracting investment and creating new opportunities for Sri Lankan enterprises. Through its strong institutional representation, private-sector participation and cultural showcase, Sri Lanka delivered a compelling message to the international business community: Discover Sri Lanka, Source from Sri Lanka, Invest in Sri Lanka and join Sri Lanka Expo 2027 to build mutually beneficial partnerships.
