Rajnath Singh meets Sri Lankan cricket legends

Colombo, Sep. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Indian Defence Minister Rajnath Singh met Sri Lankan cricket legends Aravinda de Silva, Sanath Jayasuriya and Chaminda Vaas during his visit to Colombo.

"Delighted to interact with the Sri Lankan cricketing legends,  Aravinda De Silva, Sanath Jayasuriya and Chaminda Vaas in Colombo. They have been admired by millions in India for their exceptional performances," Sharing photographs of the meeting on X, Singh said.
