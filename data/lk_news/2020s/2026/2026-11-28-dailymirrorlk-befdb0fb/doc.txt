CINEC Campus holds Convocation 2026

CINEC Campus held its annual Convocation Ceremony on 10th and 11th of November at the Bandaranayake Memorial International Conference Hall (BMICH).

The event celebrated the graduation of over 1,800 students across seven distinct faculties and one department, marking a significant milestone in the institution’s 36-year history.

Students were conferred degrees from seven main faculties and one department namely, Faculty of Engineering, Faculty of Business and Management Sciences, Faculty of Computing, Faculty of Health Sciences, Faculty of Marine Engineering, Faculty of Maritime Sciences, Faculty of Humanities and Education and Department of Aircraft Maintenance Engineering.

This diverse grouping highlights CINEC’s capability to produce top-tier professionals with globally recognized qualification for critical sectors ranging from engineering, management, maritime and aviation to healthcare and IT. Representatives from affiliated universities in the United Kingdom and China attended the ceremony to celebrate the success of their joint programs. The event also saw the participation of key stakeholders from various sectors, including: The Ministry of Education, Sri Lanka Ports Authority, Pearson UK, CILT Sri Lanka and many other industry leaders and affiliates.

Presiding over the ceremony was Capt. Ajith Peiris – President/ CEO of CINEC Campus along with, Senior Professor and Chair Sampath Amaratunga – Deputy President / Deputy CEO of CINEC Campus.
