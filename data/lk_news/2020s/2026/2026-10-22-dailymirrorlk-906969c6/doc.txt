A09 road near Alawathugoda to close temporarily tonight

Colombo, October 22 (Daily Mirror) - The Kandy–Jaffna A09 road near Kurugoda 07 Junction in the Alawathugoda Police Division will be temporarily closed from 10 p.m. today (Oct. 22) until 2 a.m. tomorrow (Oct. 23) to remove the trailer of a prime mover involved in a fatal road accident, police said.

Police have requested motorists to use alternative routes during the closure.

Vehicles travelling from Kandy towards Matale can use Katugastota, Madawala, Wattegama, Ukuwela and Elwala to rejoin the A09 road.

Heavy vehicles travelling from Matale towards Kandy can use Elwala, Ukuwela, Wattegama, Madawala and Katugastota.

Light vehicles travelling from Matale towards Kandy can use Alawathugoda, Owissa, Poojapitiya and Ambatenna to reach Katugastota before proceeding to Kandy.

The Police Media Division has urged motorists to follow the alternative routes during the temporary closure.
