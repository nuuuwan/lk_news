Home Lands strikes Gold in digital space

Home Lands Skyline has been recognised with two prestigious Gold awards at BestWeb.LK Awards 2026, winning Best Corporate Website - Gold Winner and the highly coveted Overall Best Website - Gold Winner.

The achievement marks a significant milestone for Home Lands, recognising the strength of its digital presence and its commitment to delivering world-class experiences both online and offline. This year’s competition attracted 500+ applications, with 350 shortlisted and 106 awards presented across 24 categories.

For Home Lands, these awards represent more than recognition of its website. As Sri Lanka’s number one, most trusted and most innovative real estate brand, establishing a strong digital identity has always been an important part of the brand’s journey.

The Home Lands Skyline website has been developed as a comprehensive platform for the investors and prospective homeowners to explore the company’s extensive portfolio, discover developments and access the information they need when considering a property investment.

With an intuitive layout, seamless navigation and visually engaging presentation, the website brings the scale, quality and lifestyle proposition of Home Lands to life digitally.

Winning the Best Corporate Website - Gold is a meaningful recognition of how Home Lands represents itself as a leading corporate brand in the digital space. Securing the Overall Best Website - Gold Winner, selected from the winners across the competition’s categories, makes the achievement even more significant and reinforces the strength of the platform as a whole.

LK Domain Registry Chairman Indika De Zoysa said, “This is a celebration of the wonderful work that organisations, individuals and educational institutes have done to establish their presence on the web as well as all those who have chosen the .LK domain to build and establish their brands online.”

For Home Lands, being part of this celebration of Sri Lanka’s digital achievements is a true honour. Successfully establishing Home Lands Skyline on the .LK domain represents more than a digital address; it reflects the brand’s commitment to building a strong presence within Sri Lanka’s digital identity.
