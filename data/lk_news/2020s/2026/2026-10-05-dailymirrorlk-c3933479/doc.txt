Sri Lanka, ADB discuss development priorities for 2026–2029

Colombo, Oct. 5 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka has held discussions with the Asian Development Bank (ADB) on development priorities and projects to be implemented during the 2026–2029 period, with emphasis on making effective use of limited financial and technical resources.

The discussion was held at the Presidential Secretariat and co-chaired by Secretary to the President Dr. Nandika Sanath Kumanayake and ADB Country Director for Sri Lanka Shannon Cowlin.

The meeting was held as a preparatory discussion ahead of the ADB programme review scheduled for October 5 and 6, which will focus on the successful implementation of the bank’s annual programme for 2027.

Officials paid particular attention to identifying development projects that should be prioritised for inclusion in the ADB’s longer-term project plans.

The discussions also focused on strengthening government ownership of programmes funded by the ADB, improving coordination between government procedures and ADB processes, and ensuring the effective use of concessional financing and technical assistance.

Officials also discussed measures needed to overcome challenges in accelerating Sri Lanka’s development and achieving fiscal targets within the required timeframe.

Additional Secretary to the President W. D. R. Krishantha, Senior Assistant Secretary to the President Dr. Danushka Perera and senior ADB officials also participated in the discussion.
