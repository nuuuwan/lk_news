Fuel price revision possible by end September: Energy Minister

Colombo, Sep. 14 (Daily Mirror) - A fuel price revision could be introduced by the end of September, after taking into consideration the rise in global fuel prices and the prevailing situation in the Middle East, Energy Minister Anura Karunathilaka said today (14).

He made the remarks in response to questions from journalists regarding fuel shortages reported by several fuel distribution companies.

The Minister said that although fuel prices in the international market have increased, fuel is currently being sold in Sri Lanka at comparatively lower prices.

Several private fuel distribution companies have informed the Government that continuing operations under the current circumstances has become challenging, he said.

He added that the Government would hold discussions with representatives of the companies before arriving at an appropriate decision on the matter.

The Minister said several options were being considered to address the fuel shortage and rising prices, including Government intervention to provide a fuel subsidy, as was done previously, or taking a decision on fuel prices.
