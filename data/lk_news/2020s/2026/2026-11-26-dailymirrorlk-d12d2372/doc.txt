Indian Deputy High Commissioner meets Mahinda Rajapaksa

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Indian Deputy High Commissioner to Sri Lanka Maitreyi Kulkarni met former President Mahinda Rajapaksa at his residence in Colombo yesterday (25), with discussions focusing on bilateral relations between the two countries.

The meeting provided an opportunity for a cordial exchange of views on Sri Lanka-India relations, while highlighting the importance of such engagements in further strengthening the friendship between the two nations.

Rajapaksa noted that the longstanding historical, cultural and religious ties between Sri Lanka and India had evolved into broader cooperation over the years, contributing to the mutual benefit and well-being of both countries.

He also recalled with appreciation the assistance extended by the Indian Government following the devastation caused by Cyclone Ditwah in Sri Lanka last year, stating that it reflected the strong friendship and brotherly ties between the two countries.
