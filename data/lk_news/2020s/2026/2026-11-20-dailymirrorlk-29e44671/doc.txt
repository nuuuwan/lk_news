Breathing difficulties send 36 students to hospital

Colombo, Nov. 20 (Daily Mirror) - Thirty-six students of St. Joseph’s National College in Maskeliya were admitted to the Maskeliya Divisional Hospital today (20) after developing breathing difficulties while attending classes.

The incident occurred shortly after 8.45 a.m., when 96 students from the school's primary section had breakfast. Shortly afterwards, 36 students reportedly experienced breathing difficulties while in their classrooms and were rushed to the hospital for treatment.

Hospital sources said 30 of the students had been discharged after receiving treatment, while five others, comprising two boys and three girls, remained under inpatient care.
