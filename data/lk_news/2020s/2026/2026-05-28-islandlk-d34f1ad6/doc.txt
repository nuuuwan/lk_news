Over 200 models and 50+ designers audition for Sri Lanka’s first Women’s Fashion Week

The Ceylon Chamber of Women Entrepreneurs (CCWE) held its Model & Designer Open Call at the City of Dreams CMB, Colombo, in a landmark step toward Sri Lanka’s first-ever fashion week dedicated exclusively to women entrepreneurs. The auditions drew more than 200 models including a wave of fresh faces making their industry debut alongside over 50 designers eager to claim a spot on the runway.

Two separate panels of expert judges assessed the participants. The Designer Panel comprised Prof. (Mrs) Samudrika Wijayapala, Ms. Kanchana Perera, Jagjit Silva, Kalum Udawaththa, Roshan Peris and Nilushi Pawanya. The Model Panel featured Dannielle Kerkoven Fernando, Sanjeewani Embuldeniya, Dineth De Silva, Manoji Wadugodapitiya, Kamil Hewavitharana, and Svitlana Melnyk (Lana) bringing a combined depth of local and international fashion expertise to the auditions. Celebrated choreographer Gihan Dassanayake, who will lead the runway choreography at the main event, was also in attendance.

“This Open Call is an invitation to every woman who has dared to dream. The response today is a testament to the incredible talent across Sri Lanka. CCWE Fashion Week 2026 will be a platform that transforms aspirations into opportunity.”
