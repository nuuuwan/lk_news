Casons Rent A Car unveils new luxury fleet for premium travel

Casons Rent A Car has announced a major investment in a fully owned fleet of brand-new luxury vehicles, marking a first for Sri Lanka’s premium mobility sector. The enhanced offering features carefully handpicked models, including the Toyota Land Cruiser LC 300, Range Rover Sport, Toyota Alphard Executive Lounge, Mercedes-Benz Sprinter, and a luxury Higer 50-seater.

“These vehicles were purchased recently, are unmarked, and are fully owned by us,” a company official confirmed. “We do not borrow fleets or depend on third-party vehicles. This ensures the highest standards of safety, reliability, and service consistency.”

By retaining full ownership, Casons said it maintains strict operational control, professional maintenance, and guaranteed vehicle availability without last-minute substitutions. The fleet is available for short-term rentals, corporate assignments, and exclusive travel requirements.

“With this addition, we are well-positioned to handle mega projects, official delegations, foreign missions, and upscale events,” the official added. “Our clients deserve more than transportation – they deserve confidence, comfort, and complete peace of mind.”

The investment strengthens Casons’ position as a leader in Sri Lanka’s high-end travel sector.
