No one can stop our September 12 campaign - Namal

Colombo, Sep. 10 (Daily Mirror) - No one can stop our campaign, which is to be launched against the government starting September 12, SLPP MP Namal Rajapaksa said today.

“We asked for a certain playground for our rally in Anuradhapura, but we were denied. This suppression cannot deter our efforts,” the MP told Parliament.
