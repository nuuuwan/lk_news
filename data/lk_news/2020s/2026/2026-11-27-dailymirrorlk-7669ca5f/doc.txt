Why was section of ‘Matara Ambula’ restaurant in Jaffna demolished?

Colombo, Nov. 27 (Daily Mirror) - In response to a viral social media video showing the demolition of a section of the “Matara Ambula” restaurant in Jaffna, Sri Lanka Police have clarified that the action was carried out in accordance with a court order over an unauthorised construction.

In a statement, Police said the restaurant, operated by a Sinhalese family, had been functioning for approximately five years from a rented building at No. 103, Main Street, Jaffna.

According to Police, a section of the building had been constructed unlawfully on land belonging to the Road Development Authority (RDA), obstructing the main road.

The RDA lodged a complaint with the Jaffna Municipal Council on May 2, 2025, after its initial instructions to remove the unauthorised structure were ignored.

Following the complaint, the Municipal Commissioner issued two notices to the building owner directing the removal of the unauthorised construction. However, the owner failed to take action.

On July 9, 2026, the Jaffna Magistrate’s Court ordered the owner to remove the unauthorised section, authorising the Municipal Council to carry out the demolition if the order was not complied with.

Police said that, following a further notice, Municipal Council officials removed the unauthorised section on November 25 in accordance with the court order.
