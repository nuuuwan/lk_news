Siddhalepa Ayurveda products and health resort honoured with prestigious GlobalHealth Sri Lanka Awards 2026

Siddhalepa Ayurveda product range and Siddhalepa Ayurveda Health Resort have been honoured with two prestigious accolades at the inaugural GlobalHealth Asia-Pacific Sri Lanka Conference and Awards 2026. Siddhalepa Ayurveda products received the “Excellence in Herbal Wellness and Functional Nutrition in Sri Lanka” award, while Siddhalepa Ayurveda Health Resort was recognised as the “Holistic Health and Wellness Service Provider of the Year in Sri Lanka.”

The awards were presented during the GlobalHealth Sri Lanka Awards Gala held at ITC Ratnadipa, Colombo, on 21 July 2026. The event brought together regional healthcare leaders, policymakers, clinical experts, and wellness professionals to celebrate excellence, ethics, and innovation across the healthcare and wellness sectors through a rigorous nomination-only evaluation process led by an esteemed international and local jury.

GlobalHealth Asia-Pacific is a premier regional platform dedicated to connecting healthcare providers, aesthetic centres, wellness destinations, and policymakers across more than 14 countries. The GlobalHealth Sri Lanka Awards celebrate organisations that uphold the highest standards of professional practice, ethical governance, service excellence, and patient-centred innovation.

The recognition awarded to Siddhalepa Ayurveda products celebrates more than nine decades of pioneering authentic Ayurvedic healthcare through herbal products that have become synonymous with trust, quality, and natural healing. Built upon Sri Lanka’s rich indigenous Ayurvedic knowledge and refined through generations of expertise, research, and innovation, the Siddhalepa Ayurveda product range has brought the benefits of authentic Ayurveda into the daily lives of consumers in Sri Lanka and across the world.

Combining time-honoured Ayurvedic wisdom with modern scientific research, stringent quality assurance, and responsible manufacturing practices, Siddhalepa Ayurveda products have continually evolved to address the changing health and well-being needs of consumers while preserving the integrity of traditional Ayurveda. This commitment has enabled the Siddhalepa Ayurveda product range to become one of Sri Lanka’s most recognised Ayurvedic offerings.

From its origins in Sri Lanka over 90 years ago, Siddhalepa Ayurveda products have grown into a globally recognised representation of Sri Lankan Ayurveda. Trusted by generations and embraced internationally, the product range continues to carry Sri Lanka’s healing heritage beyond its shores, offering natural healthcare solutions that have become part of everyday lifestyles around the world.

Complementing this achievement, Siddhalepa Ayurveda Health Resort was honoured with the “Holistic Health and Wellness Service Provider of the Year in Sri Lanka” award. The recognition acknowledges the resort’s commitment to delivering authentic Ayurvedic healing experiences through personalised treatments, therapeutic programs, and holistic care that combines traditional practices with modern hospitality standards. Building on Siddhalepa’s decades of expertise in developing authentic Ayurvedic products, the Health Resort puts this accumulated knowledge and experience into practice through customised wellness solutions, applying time-tested Ayurvedic principles to create meaningful healing journeys for guests.

Commenting on the achievement, Siddhalepa Group Chairman and Managing Director Asoka Hettigoda said, “These recognitions reaffirm our commitment to preserving the authenticity of Sri Lankan Ayurveda while ensuring its benefits continue to reach people in meaningful ways. Through our herbal healthcare products, we have taken Sri Lanka’s Ayurvedic heritage to consumers across the world, while Siddhalepa Ayurveda Health Resort continues to offer authentic healing experiences that showcase the true value of Ayurveda. We are honoured to receive these awards on behalf of our dedicated team and the generations of customers who have placed their trust in Siddhalepa.”

These two recognitions reinforce Siddhalepa’s enduring commitment to advancing authentic Ayurveda, with its internationally recognised product range continuing to carry Sri Lanka’s Ayurvedic heritage to consumers around the world, while Siddhalepa Ayurveda Health Resort showcases the country’s rich tradition of holistic healing through authentic Ayurveda experiences.
