Rs. 1 billion allocated to upgrade fishermen’s mobile app: Deputy Minister

Colombo, Nov. 13 (Daily Mirror) - The government has allocated Rs. 1,000 million in the 2027 Budget to upgrade the dedicated mobile application developed for fishermen, Deputy Minister of Fisheries, Aquatic and Ocean Resources Rathna Gamage announced today.

Speaking during the Budget debate, he said more than 100,000 fishermen are currently using the application, dismissing criticism from opposition politicians over the government's failure to introduce the promised platform.

The Deputy Minister noted that the application currently provides data covering only three to four days, highlighting the need for further improvements to enhance its functionality and services.

He added that the government is currently in discussions with several foreign companies and local service providers regarding the proposed upgrade.
