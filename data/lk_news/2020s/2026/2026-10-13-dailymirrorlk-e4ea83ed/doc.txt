Polythene Abuse by Big Pharmacies!

Some pharmacies issue sealed leak proof 10 strip medicines packed in a  ziplock polythene envelope size cover.  These  strips are dry, factory-sealed, foil-packed tablets. They will NEVER leak. They do not need waterproofing again.

Yet most big pharmacies in Sri Lanka have put EACH strip into a separate CELLPHONE ZIPLOCK polythene bag. 8 various meds strips = 8 polythene covers!

4. The GLOBAL standard - In UK, Australia, they use paper envelopes only.

This is not about patient safety. This is pure laziness and huge boost to the polythene business. One pharmacy chain dispenses 10,000 prescriptions a day. If they use 2-3 ziplocks per patient, that is 30,000 polythene bags PER DAY into our drains, rivers and sea polluting the country. For NO reason!

My request to the Government / CEA is to ban  this environmental disaster. Please BAN the use of ziplock / cellophane polythene for non-leaking, dry, sealed tablets. Make PAPER ENVELOPES mandatory, just like you banned lunch sheets and free poly bags in shopping malls. This is a silent environmental disaster happening at  pharmacies all over the country.
