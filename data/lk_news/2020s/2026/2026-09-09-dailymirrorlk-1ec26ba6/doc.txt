Bread weight under watch: CAA raids 118 outlets and bakeries

Colombo, Sep. 9 (Daily Mirror) - The Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA) has launched a special islandwide inspection programme to detect underweight bread and ensure consumers receive the correct weight for the price they pay.

The inspections were conducted from September 1 to 3, with 118 retail outlets and bakeries inspected to determine whether bread was sold at the required weight and whether its weight and price were clearly displayed.

According to initial findings, underweight bread was detected at 54 of the shops and bakeries where weight information was available. The weight of bread was not properly displayed at 76 establishments, while prices were not properly displayed at 65 establishments.

The CAA also traced the supply chain to identify bakeries supplying underweight bread, resulting in inspections being carried out at 81 bakeries.

The authority said the investigations would not stop at retail outlets. Where underweight bread was detected, officials traced the product back to the bakery or manufacturer and inspected its production process, including the weight of bread produced and its distribution to other outlets.

Where bakeries were found to be producing underweight bread, their owners were instructed to immediately rectify the production process and ensure that only bread of the correct weight was released to the market.

The required weight of a full loaf of bread is 450 grammes, while a half loaf should weigh 225 grammes.

The CAA said the inspections are aimed not only at taking legal action against violators but also at ensuring consumers receive the correct weight for the price paid, maintaining a fair market for law-abiding traders and correcting the production of underweight bread at the source.

The authority said special raids, bakery inspections and supply chain investigations would continue across the country.

Consumers can lodge complaints regarding the weight, price or other trade irregularities involving bread by calling the CAA hotline 1977 during office hours.
