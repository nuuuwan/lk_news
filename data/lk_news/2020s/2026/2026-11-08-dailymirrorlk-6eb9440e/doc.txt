Monks defy court order, continue Satyagraha near Ruwanweli Seya

A group of Buddhist monks and laypeople continued their Satyagraha near the Ruwanweli Seya in Anuradhapura yesterday, despite a court order directing them to vacate the sacred areas, including the Ruwanweli Seya and Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi.

The protest, led by Ven. Balangoda Kassapa Thera, began on Friday (7) morning against the proposed Anuradhapura Sacred City Plan.

Tensions escalated yesterday afternoon when the protesters attempted to construct an elevated structure at the site. A confrontation reportedly broke out between the group and police officers who arrived to prevent the construction.

Following a report by the Anuradhapura Headquarters Chief Inspector of Police, the Anuradhapura Magistrate's Court ordered the protesters to leave the sacred areas, including the Ruwanweli Seya and Jaya Sri Maha Bodhi.

The court also directed that no damage be caused to archaeological monuments within the sacred area and ordered the removal of temporary tents erected at the site.

However, the protesting monks and laypeople defied the order and remained at the location overnight.

Ven. Balangoda Kassapa Thera said the Satyagraha would continue until the Government withdraws the Gazette notification relating to the Anuradhapura Sacred City Plan.
