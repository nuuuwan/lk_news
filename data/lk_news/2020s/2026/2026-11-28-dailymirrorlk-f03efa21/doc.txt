KAL CEO Athukorala meets King Charles III

Aravinda Athukorala being introduced to King Charles III by Lord Marland, Chairman of CWEIC

Aravinda Athukorala, Founder and CEO of ATM software company KAL, had the honour of meeting King Charles III at a Commonwealth business event organised by the Commonwealth Enterprise and Investment Council (CWEIC) in Antigua and Barbuda, alongside the Commonwealth Heads of Government Meeting (CHOGM).

During the meeting, Aravinda had the opportunity to discuss KAL’s international operations and contributions to the financial technology sector, including mission-critical support for banking infrastructure and financial services across Commonwealth markets. Aravinda was accompanied by Kader Baadoud, KAL’s deputy CEO.

Founded by Aravinda in 1989, KAL is a leading provider of ATM software, with solutions in over 100 countries, powering upwards of 350,000 ATMs. As part of its continued international expansion, KAL recently established a new office in Sri Lanka, registered within the Colombo Port City framework.

Aravinda and Kader’s participation highlighted the growing international reach of technology businesses with links to Sri Lanka and the opportunities for greater collaboration between the country’s technology sector and the wider Commonwealth business community.
