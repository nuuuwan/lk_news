Colombo-Kandy main road closed following earth and rock slide

Colombo, Nov. 10 (Daily Mirror) - The Colombo-Kandy main road has been closed at Ganethenna due to an earth and rock slide in the Kadugannawa area.

The falling rocks and soil have also damaged a motorcycle parked near the road.

Motorists are advised to avoid the affected stretch of the road until further notice.
