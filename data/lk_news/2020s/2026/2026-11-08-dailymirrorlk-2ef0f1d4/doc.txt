Doctors to protest on Nov. 10 as Development Officers launch sick-note campaign tomorrow

Two major groups of public sector employees are preparing to step up trade union action over unresolved demands, with doctors planning a protest in Colombo on November 10 and Development Officers across the country set to report sick leave tomorrow (9).

The Government Medical Officers’ Association (GMOA) announced that it would stage a protest outside the Colombo National Hospital on November 10, demanding solutions to doctors’ salary and allowance issues and the shortage of medical professionals in the country.

The GMOA said it had repeatedly requested discussions with the Ministry of Health and other relevant authorities to resolve long-standing concerns but had yet to receive a satisfactory response.

The association said the cancellation of tax-concession vehicle import permits following the 2022 economic crisis, together with increased taxation on doctors’ earnings and work-related allowances, had placed additional financial pressure on medical professionals.

It also pointed out that one-third of doctors’ overtime and holiday allowances had been suspended under the 2025 public sector salary revision. The Government had reportedly promised to restore these payments once the country’s economic situation improved.

Raising concerns over staff shortages, the GMOA said fewer than 25,000 doctors were currently in active service under the Ministry of Health.

It said no scientific, evidence-based revision of approved staff cadre numbers across the health sector had been carried out since 2015, despite a significant expansion of healthcare services, institutions and units.

According to the association, the failure to revise staff numbers had increased the workload of healthcare workers and threatened the quality of healthcare services.

The GMOA said it had submitted proposals to the Government last August, seeking solutions through the 2027 Budget, but no response had been received despite repeated requests for discussions.

The association had also staged protests near several major hospitals during the past week.

The November 10 demonstration will demand solutions to doctors' salary and allowance issues, a revision of approved staff cadre numbers to address shortages and an end to measures it claims are aimed at suppressing legitimate trade union demands.

Meanwhile, Development Officers across the island have decided to launch a trade union action by reporting sick leave tomorrow (9), over several unresolved demands.

General Secretary of the Joint Development Officers’ Association Dhammika Munasinghe said their demands had been brought to the attention of the authorities on several occasions, but no action had been taken to address them.

Speaking at a media briefing in Colombo today (8), he confirmed that the planned trade union action would go ahead tomorrow.
