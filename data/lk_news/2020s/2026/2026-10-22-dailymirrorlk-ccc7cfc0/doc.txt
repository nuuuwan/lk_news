SriLankan extends Saudi flight suspension until November 1

SriLankan Airlines has announced that the suspension of all flights between Colombo and Dammam, and Colombo and Riyadh, will remain in effect until and including November 1, 2026, due to the prevailing security situation in the region.

The airline stated that the safety and security of passengers and crew remain its highest priority. Flight services will resume once conditions have been assessed as suitable for commercial operations.

Passengers seeking further information can contact 1979 within Sri Lanka or +94 11 777 1979 for international inquiries. They can also contact the airline via WhatsApp at +94 74 444 1979 for chat-only assistance, reach out to their travel agents, or visit the official website at www.srilankan.com.
