HNB elevates home loan solutions in partnership with Groundworth

HNB PLC, the leading private bank in Sri Lanka announced a strategic partnership with Groundworth Pvt Ltd through the signing of a MoU to promote home loan financing for a landmark mixed development project in Colombo.

The MoU valid until 31 December 2026 brings together two organizations recognized for quality credibility and long term vision. Through this partnership HNB will extend access to competitive and customer centric home loan facilities while Groundworth will offer homebuyers opportunities within premium developments that have consistently demonstrated strong market demand and value appreciation.

Speaking on the partnership HNB’s Senior Vice President/ Head of Retail banking, Kanchana Karunagama said “As a bank that has consistently led the evolution of home financing in Sri Lanka we remain focused on delivering solutions that place clients at the center of every decision. This partnership with Groundworth shows our commitment to providing structured transparent and value driven home loan experiences supported by expert insight and a deep understanding of client needs. A home represents long term security and progress and we are proud to support our clients at every stage of that journey.”
