Jaffna MC members protest against commissioner’s interference

Aug. 11 (Daily Mirror) - Jaffna Municipal Council members today staged a protest, accusing the Municipal Commissioner and other government officials of continuously obstructing the activities of the council’s elected representatives.

The symbolic protest was jointly organized with the participation of representatives from all political parties represented in the 45-member Jaffna Municipal Council.

Representatives of the Ilankai Tamil Arasu Kadchi (ITAK), National People’s Power (NPP), Eelam People’s Democratic Party (EPDP), Tamil National People’s Front (TNPF), Democratic Tamil National Alliance (DTNA), United National Party (UNP), and Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) participated in the protest and expressed their opposition.

Council members expressed concern that the alleged improper actions by officials have created a difficult situation in which elected representatives are unable to properly fulfil the basic needs of the people or effectively reach out to them.

They also alleged that officials are deliberately causing delays in essential services and development activities that should be delivered to the public through the Municipal Council, while attempting to interfere with and undermine the powers and responsibilities of elected representatives.
