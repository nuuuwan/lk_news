Petro Packaging earns international food grade certification

Petform group, a pioneer in packaging industry, was awarded with international food grade certification for its sister company Petro Packaging Pvt Ltd on this 30th January 2026.

The event was hosted at SGS office in Vauxhall Street Colombo 2. SGS granted the international food grade certification on FSSC 22000, HACCP and GMP for Petro Packaging Pvt Ltd.

Petform Group chairman P. Mathytharan, HR & Admin director T.Mathytharan and Director N.Sureshkumar were awarded the certificates by the representatives of SGS Dilusha Premasiri – Business Development Manager and.Manula Munasinghe – Assistant manager. On behalf of Petform Group L.Nagulan –,AGM -Sales & Operation V.Sishanth, AGM – Finance & Admin and Wasantha Darmasiri – Group Quality Assurance Manager were also present.
