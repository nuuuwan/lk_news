Final respects for Nanda Malini

People pay their last respects to legendary singer Visharada Dr. Nanda Malini as her remains lie in state at the Nanda Malini Music Ashram in Nugegoda on August 21, 2026. Nanda Malini passed away at the age of 82 at her home in Nawala on August 20. Her final rites are scheduled to be held at the General Cemetery in Borella on August 22. Pix by Pradeep Pathirana
