Colombo stocks trade lower amid currency pressure and rising oil prices

Trading at the Colombo Stock Exchange yesterday moved on a bearish note as concerns over economic weakness, currency depreciation, and rising global oil prices weighed on investor sentiment, market analysts said. Against this backdrop, both benchmark indices moved lower during trading.

All Share Price Index down by 509.02 points while S and P SL20 down by 96.38 points. Turnover stood at Rs 3.80 billion with two crossings. Those crossings were reported in JKH crossed 30 million shares to the tune of Rs 602.2 million and its share price traded at Rs 19.90 and Haylays 110,000 shares crossed to the tune of Rs 25.9 million and its share price traded at Rs 235.

In the retail market top seven companies that have mainly contributed to the turnover were Haylays Rs 155 million (703,000 shares traded), JKH Rs 132.2 million (6.6 million shares traded), Access Engineering Rs 124 million (1.7 million shares traded), Softlogic Capital Rs 116.5 million (9.4 million shares traded), HNB (Non-Voting) Rs 104 million (312,000 shares traded), Sampath Bank Rs 100 (702,000 shares traded) and Janashakthi Rs 96 million (7.3 million shares traded). During the day 175.7 million shares volumes changed hands in 41000 transactions.

JKH was the top performer at the market while banking sector especially HNB and Sampath Bank  performed well. Further, construction sector especially Access Engineering played a prominent role on the floor.

Amana Takaful announced that its 2026 Rights Issue of shares was oversubscribed. The company offered 52,854,857 shares to raise over Ra 1.004 billion, receiving subscriptions for 51,627,687 rights shares along with applications for an additional 36,373,622 shares.

Yesterday the Central Bank announced the US Dollar rate as against rupee. The telegraphic transfer rate for the dollar was Rs 334.50 buying, Rs 343.50 selling, while bond yields were quoted higher and wider, dealers said. There was no spot quote available for the rupee.
