HRCSL summons three police officers over suspect’s death in Homagama

Colombo, Oct. 28 (Daily Mirror) - The Human Rights Commission of Sri Lanka (HRCSL) has summoned the Officer-in-Charge (OIC) of the Homagama Police Station and two other police officers to appear before the commission tomorrow (29) in connection with the death of a man reported to have died inside a police cell.

HRCSL Commissioner Attorney-at-Law Nimal G. Punchihewa said the officers had been directed to appear before the commission as part of its ongoing investigation into the incident.

The commission has also taken steps to record statements from several family members of the deceased.
