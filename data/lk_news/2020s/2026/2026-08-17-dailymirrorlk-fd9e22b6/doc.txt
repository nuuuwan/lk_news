Govt. aims to make vehicle ownership accessible to every citizen: Vijitha Herath

Colombo, Aug. 17 (Daily Mirror) - The Government aims to create an environment where every citizen can afford to own and use a vehicle, Minister Vijitha Herath said.

He said the temporary tax restrictions imposed on vehicle imports would be removed as soon as the situation arising from the conflict in the Middle East comes to an end.

The Minister made these remarks while participating as the chief guest at a vehicle exhibition and parade organised by the Sri Lanka Vehicle Importers Association.

The Minister said vehicle imports had been completely suspended two years ago, adding that the Government had managed to resume imports within a short period as the economy strengthened, allowing the importation of any type of vehicle without restrictions.
