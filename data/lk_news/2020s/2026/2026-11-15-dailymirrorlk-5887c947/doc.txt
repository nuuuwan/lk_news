Red warning issued over strong winds and rough seas

Colombo, Nov. 15 (Daily Mirror) - The Department of Meteorology has issued a red warning advising naval and fishing communities to avoid venturing into deep-sea areas due to an approaching weather system in the Bay of Bengal, east of Sri Lanka.

The warning covers deep-sea areas bounded by 05°N–15°N and 80°E–90°E, as well as coastal waters extending from Kankesanthurai to Hambantota via Trincomalee and Pottuvil.

According to the Department of Meteorology, as of 5.30 a.m. today (November 15), the weather system was located approximately 600 kilometres northeast of Trincomalee.

The system is expected to move northwestwards while remaining well away from Sri Lanka’s eastern coast. It is likely to intensify into a Deep Depression by tomorrow morning (November 16), before developing into a Cyclonic Storm over the southern and adjoining west-central Bay of Bengal by tomorrow night.

Further intensification into a Severe Cyclonic Storm is expected over the west-central Bay of Bengal by November 17.

Naval personnel and fishing communities operating in the affected sea areas have been urged to move to safer locations and refrain from entering the warned areas until further notice.

The Department has also advised all concerned to remain vigilant and closely follow future weather forecasts and advisories issued in connection with the developing system.
