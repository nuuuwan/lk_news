Rs. 3.2 billion allocated for flood mitigation projects in Colombo and suburbs

Nov 12 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake has announced a series of flood mitigation projects targeting Colombo and its suburbs, with Rs. 3.2 billion allocated under Budget 2027 to improve urban drainage systems and rehabilitate canals.

Presenting the Budget in Parliament today (12), the President said recurring floods in Colombo were largely due to inadequate maintenance of major canals and insufficient capacity in drainage systems to carry rainwater.

Several projects are scheduled to commence in 2027, including the rehabilitation of the St. Sebastian Canal and Kirulapana Canal, along with improvements to stormwater drainage systems in Piliyandala and Kesbewa.

The Government will also launch the Mudun Ela project to address flooding in Wattala, Kelaniya and Biyagama, while continuing ongoing flood mitigation projects in Kolonnawa and Oliyumulla.

The development of a section of the Hamilton Canal is also included in the wider flood prevention programme.

The allocation will support the restoration of identified canals in Colombo and its suburban areas, as well as improvements to the stormwater drainage system along the Piliyandala-Kesbewa by-road.

Flood prevention measures will also be extended to several other districts.

In the Kalutara District, the Government plans to improve the Bindunu Ela canal system in Wadduwa and Molligoda.

Drainage improvement projects are also planned for Gampaha, Ratnapura, Dikwella, Beruwala and Rathgama.

Meanwhile, ongoing projects along the Malwathu Oya are expected to help address flooding in the Mannar District.

Preliminary work on the Mundeni Aru project, which aims to address flooding in Ampara and Batticaloa, is also expected to commence.
