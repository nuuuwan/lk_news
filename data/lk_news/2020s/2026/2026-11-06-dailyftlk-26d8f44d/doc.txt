Mini Incubation Centre to empower MSMEs in Meerigama

Science and Technology Minster Professor Chrishantha Abeysena at the inauguration of first Mini Incubation Centre in Meerigama

The Science and Technology Ministry has launched its programme to establish a network of Mini Incubation Centres aimed at empowering micro and small-scale entrepreneurs.

Marking the commencement of this initiative, the first centre was officially inaugurated this wek at the Vidatha Resource Centre located within the Meerigama Divisional Secretariat premises, under the patronage of the Science and Technology Minster Professor Chrishantha Abeysena.

The program aims to provide essential technological facilities and support to micro and small-scale entrepreneurs who operate their businesses with limited resources and infrastructure.

Under the first phase of the initiative, 25 selected Vidatha Resource Centres will be developed into Mini Incubation Centres, covering all districts across the country. The program is expected to benefit approximately 2,500 entrepreneurs. An allocation of Rs. 100.9 million has been made for this initiative in 2026.

The first centre, inaugurated in Meerigama, will provide entrepreneurs engaged in food production with access to modern technological equipment and facilities required for hygienic food preparation, packaging and labelling. Through these facilities, the initiative aims to enhance the quality and standards of locally manufactured products while creating greater opportunities for entrepreneurs to access and expand their presence in the market.

The occasion was attended by Gampaha District MP Chaminda Lalith Kumara, officials of the Ministry of Science and Technology, the Meerigama Divisional Secretariat, Gampaha District Vidatha, entrepreneurs and other distinguished invitees.
