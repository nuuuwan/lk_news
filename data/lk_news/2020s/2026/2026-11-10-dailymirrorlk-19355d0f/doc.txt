DMT warns public: Beware of brokers near its premises

Colombo, Nov. 10 (Daily Mirror) - The Department of Motor Traffic (DMT) has warned the public against dealing with brokers operating near its premises, cautioning that people could lose money and important documents by falling victim to misleading practices.

In a special notice issued today (10), the DMT said facilities had already been introduced to make it easier for the public to obtain its services without relying on intermediaries.

Appointments for same-day services can be booked online or over the telephone, while general vehicle transfer documents can be submitted at District Secretariats and Divisional Secretariats, in addition to the DMT head office in Narahenpita.

However, the department said some brokers had been approaching members of the public outside its premises and misleading them into obtaining services without following official procedures.

The DMT urged the public to follow only the department's official procedures and seek advice directly from authorised officers.

It also advised people to avoid dealing with brokers operating near the department's premises, warning that doing so could result in the loss of money and important documents.
