Chamal Rajapaksa appears before Bribery Commission

Colombo, Sep. 8 (Daily Mirror) - Former MP Chamal Rajapaksa arrived at the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) this morning (08) to provide a statement in connection with an ongoing investigation into alleged financial irregularities surrounding the procurement of Airbus aircraft for SriLankan Airlines.
