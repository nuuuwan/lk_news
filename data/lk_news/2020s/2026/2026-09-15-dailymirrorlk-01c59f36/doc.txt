39 new health projects planned for 2027

Colombo, Sep. 15 (Daily Mirror) - The Government has approved 39 new development projects for 2027 aimed at improving public health services and addressing infrastructure deficiencies in hospitals and other government health institutions.

The projects are part of a broader programme to meet the growing healthcare needs of the public by upgrading facilities and developing modern infrastructure in line with current health service standards.

The National Planning Department evaluated 41 project proposals submitted during 2025 and 2026 and recommended that they be initiated and implemented in 2027.

The 41 proposals have subsequently been included in the Public Investment Programme for 2027-2031.

Considering the readiness to implement 39 of these projects, provisions have been planned for them in the 2027 Budget.

Accordingly, Cabinet approval has been granted for the proposal submitted by the Health and Mass Media Minister to implement the 39 new projects from 2027.
