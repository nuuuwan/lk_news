SLTB to introduce card payment system for bus travel soon

Colombo, Aug. 6 (Daily Mirror) - The time is coming soon for passengers to pay their bus fares by card, Transport and Highways Deputy Minister Prasanna Gunasena said.

He said the latest measures taken by the Sri Lanka Transport Board (SLTB) aim to improve the quality of bus services, provide greater convenience to passengers, and create positive competition with the private sector.

The Deputy Minister said the introduction of modern payment methods is among the steps being taken to transform public transport services and enhance the travel experience of commuters.
