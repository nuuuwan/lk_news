No difference between bombing and shooting: Madduma Bandara

Colombo, Oct. 14 (Daily Mirror) - Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) General Secretary Ranjith Madduma Bandara said today that there was no difference between bombing and shooting, referring to a recent comment by the Mahanayake of the Malwathu Chapter, Most Venerable Thibbotuwawe Sri Siddhartha Sumangala Thera, regarding the attack on the historic Sri Dalada Maligawa.

"Though bombing and shooting are two different words, but both are similar, as they are violent acts," Madduma Bandara told a media briefing.

"We thank the Mahanayake Thera for explaining the truth. However, we request a fresh probe into it and other attacks such as the bombing of the Kataragama Perahera where 13 people were killed," he added.
