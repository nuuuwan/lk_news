Valentine’s Day … with the King of Romance

Valentine’s Day is about expressing love and affection, and people often exchange gifts, go on dates, or spend quality time with loved ones.

The special occasion is typically celebrated with flowers, chocolates, and gifts, especially in our part of the world.

In Melbourne, Australia, Derrick J (Derrick Junkeer), known as the King of Romance, has worked out a very special Valentine’s Day event … to make it memorable for all those who check out this scene.

Derrick J says he plans to make this Valentine’s Day truly unforgettable with a free, sparkling gift for the ladies!

Says Derrick J: “Don’t wait until the last minute—plan ahead and show her how much she means to you with a gift that shines as brightly as your relationship. After all, the best surprises are the ones that come from the heart and sparkle just as much as love itself!”

Music and entertainment at ‘A Valentine Night of Rhythm and Romance’ will be provided by Derrick J himself and the ‘Dynamic Duo.’

In fact, Derrick J. is very much in the news these days as his New Year’s Eve event, at Cathies Lane Receptions, proved to be a resounding success, with over 600 guests in attendance, all of whom enjoyed the music and atmosphere and the delicious food.
