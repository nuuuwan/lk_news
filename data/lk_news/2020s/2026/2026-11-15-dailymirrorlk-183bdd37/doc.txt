Budget is a story without benefits for people, Kavinda Jayawardena says

Colombo, Nov. 15 (Daily Mirror) - The Government's latest Budget is nothing more than a collection of words without any meaningful benefits for the people, Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) MP Kavinda Jayawardena said.

Criticising the Budget, Jayawardena said it lacked substance and failed to provide relief to ordinary people.

"Usually, when we hear a Budget, we expect proposals that affect people's salaries, expenses and daily lives. But what is this Budget? It is like a novel or a story. There is nothing substantial in it, and the people have received nothing," he said.

Questioning the Government's claims of having saved public funds, Jayawardena said the money had been collected through taxes paid by the people and should have been used to improve their living standards.

"We saved money. How did we save it? From the tax money paid by the people. Was that money collected to ensure their prosperity and comfort? Only 50 per cent has been given back to the people," he alleged.

He accused the Government of failing to deliver the positive changes it had promised after coming to power, adding that the public had received little good news from the administration.

Jayawardena said the Budget had failed to address the expectations and economic difficulties of ordinary people.
