Namal can’t be compared to a beggar: Herath hits back at Gammanpila

Colombo, Sep 9 (Daily Mirror) - Foreign Minister Vijitha Herath who briefed the Cabinet weekly briefing today rejected former MP Udaya Gammanpila’s comparison of the alleged payment received by SLPP National Organiser and MP Namal Rajapaksa as giving money to a beggar.

Minister Herath said accepting a large sum of money in connection with a political matter could not be compared to giving money to a beggar.

“If that is the case, then Namal Rajapaksa must be a beggar,” Herath said, adding that such a comparison would not be accepted by the people.

He said there was a clear difference between giving money to a beggar and giving money to a politician.

“Anyone in this country understands how politicians have gained money. If Gammanpila compares this incident to giving money to a beggar, then the former MP’s reasoning needs to be questioned,” he said.

Herath said the younger generation was supportive of legal action against those accused of corruption and fraud.

“Gen Z launched the Aragalaya to free the country from fraudsters. Now Gen Z is confident that corruption can be eliminated from the country,” he said.

He also stressed that the case against Namal Rajapaksa was not initiated by the present Government, but had been filed during the administration of former President Ranil Wickremesinghe.

Meanwhile, speaking to the media recently, former MP Udaya Gammanpila questioned as to whether a payment allegedly made by businessman Nimal Perera to Namal Rajapaksa could automatically be considered a commission.

“Let’s say Nimal Perera gave Namal US$800,000. How does that make it a commission?” Gammanpila asked, commenting on Rajapaksa’s arrest and subsequent remand.

He argued that if accepting donations could be considered illegal, then even a beggar on the road could face legal consequences for accepting donations.
