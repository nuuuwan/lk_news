ComBank becomes only SL institution among Asia-Pacific’s top debit card issuers

Commercial Bank of Ceylon has become the only Sri Lankan institution to be ranked by The Nilson Report.

The Bank secured a top 50 position among the largest debit card issuers in the Asia-Pacific region based on debit card spend volumes in 2025, in the latest edition of the Nilson Report.This milestone builds on Commercial Bank’s longstanding position as Sri Lanka’s undisputed market leader in cumulative debit card spend, commanding the country’s highest active card usage.

The achievement also represents an important endorsement of the Bank’s leadership in Sri Lanka’s payment industry and the success of its strategy to accelerate the adoption of digital payments, while placing Sri Lanka’s growing participation in the global digital payments landscape firmly on the regional map.

Commenting on the significance of the recognition, Sanath Manatunge, Managing Director/CEO of Commercial Bank said, “This achievement carries special meaning because it demonstrates that Sri Lanka can compete on the strength of its digital payment capabilities, in a region led by some of the world’s largest economies and banking institutions. It is a testament to the confidence our customers place in Commercial Bank, the progress we have made in advancing digital payments and our commitment to making modern payment experiences accessible, secure and rewarding. More importantly, it represents a milestone for Sri Lanka’s payment industry, showing that our country’s digital payment ecosystem can make its mark well beyond our shores.”

Published in the October 2026 edition (Issue 1317) of The Nilson Report, the ranking recognises Commercial Bank’s annual debit card purchase volume of US$ 1.19 billion, equivalent to approximately Rs. 396 billion at current exchange rates. The volume was generated by a portfolio of 3.799 million debit cards, with an average annual purchase value of US$ 314 (Rs 104,562) percard.The ranking is based on the actual purchase volumes and transaction activity generated by debit cards, rather than the number of cards issued or ATM cash withdrawals. Therefore, itreflects the extent to which Commercial Bank’s debit cards are actively used for purchasing goods and services through physical point-of-sale (POS) terminals, cross-border transactions and e-commerce platforms.

Commercial Bank’s inclusion in the ranking reflects the effectiveness of its sustained efforts to move customers from cash and ATM withdrawals towards card payments.
