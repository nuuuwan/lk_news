Bank impersonation scams hit Kandy: Over 12 complaints

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - More than 12 complaints have been lodged with the Kandy Police in recent days over a financial scam in which fraudsters impersonate officials of leading banks and financial institutions to steal money from customers' accounts.

An 80-year-old victim had Rs. 7 million fraudulently withdrawn from a bank account, police investigations have revealed.

The scammers reportedly use telephone calls, SMS messages and WhatsApp to deceive customers, sometimes making the official logos of banks appear on recipients' mobile phone screens to make their communications look genuine.

Using this tactic, they attempt to obtain confidential banking information, including account details, passwords and One-Time Passwords (OTPs), either through conversations or links sent via messages.

Police have warned the public not to disclose passwords or OTPs to anyone, stressing that legitimate banks do not request such confidential information through telephone calls or text messages.

They have also urged customers not to trust calls or messages solely because a bank's official logo appears on their mobile phone screens and to exercise caution when responding to suspicious communications.
