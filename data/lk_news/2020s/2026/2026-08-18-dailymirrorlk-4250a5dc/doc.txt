DFCC Pragathi to strengthen support for MSMEs

DFCC Bank has launched DFCC Pragathi, a unified micro, small and medium enterprise (MSME) proposition that brings together specialised financial solutions, business advisory services and capacity-building initiatives under one brand, making it easier for the entrepreneurs to access the support they need to start, grow and scale their businesses.

Building on nearly a decade of experience in serving the MSME sector, DFCC Pragathi represents the next evolution of the bank’s dedicated MSME business, originally established in 2016. Since then, DFCC Bank has partnered with more than 10,000 entrepreneurs across Sri Lanka, supporting businesses in sectors including agriculture, dairy farming, manufacturing and value-added agricultural industries such as tea, cinnamon, pepper, banana, turmeric, ginger and paddy cultivation.

Designed to go beyond traditional lending, DFCC Pragathi offers an integrated ecosystem of financial and non-financial support. The proposition combines tailored lending solutions, value chain financing, financial literacy programmes, business advisory services and capacity-building initiatives, enabling the entrepreneurs to access practical guidance alongside the financial support needed to build resilient and sustainable businesses.

The launch also marked the introduction of DFCC Aloka - Pragathi, a specialised financing proposition designed to support the women entrepreneurs within the MSME sector. Reflecting this commitment, seven women entrepreneurs received funding through the DFCC Aloka - Pragathi loan scheme at the launch event, reinforcing the bank’s efforts to expand financial access and create greater opportunities for women-led enterprises.

DFCC Bank Vice President and Head of Business Banking Pradeep Alwis said, “Running a business today requires more than access to finance. Entrepreneurs also need practical guidance, trusted partnerships and banking solutions that evolve alongside their businesses. DFCC Pragathi brings these elements together through one integrated proposition, enabling us to better support the MSMEs at every stage of their growth while contributing to one of the most important sectors of Sri Lanka’s economy. Through DFCC Aloka - Pragathi, we are also strengthening our commitment to supporting women entrepreneurs and helping create a more inclusive business landscape.”
