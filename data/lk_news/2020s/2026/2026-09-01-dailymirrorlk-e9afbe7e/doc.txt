Mixed signals in secondary market

The secondary market signalled a mixed sentiment yesterday, amidst moderate trading volumes and limited market activity.

The yield curve remained anchored compared to the closure of the previous week.

Over the 2028 segment, 15.12.2028 traded at 10.10 percent. Moving ahead, 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 traded within the range of 10.45 percent - 10.55 percent, while 01.02.2031 changed hands at 10.60 percent.

To the belly-end of the curve, 15.10.2034 traded between 11.55 percent - 11.45 percent and 15.03.2035 was dealt at 11.60 percent. Towards the long-end, 15.08.2036 changed hands between 11.80 percent - 11.75 percent and 01.07.2037 was traded within the range of 11.92 percent - 11.77 percent.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee appreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.328.01/US dollar, compared to Rs.328.61/US dollar seen earlier. Market liquidity in the banking system expanded to Rs.347.33 billion, from Rs.338.13 billion recorded previously.
