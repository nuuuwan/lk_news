Veepee Graphics Lanka opens high-tech  stall at Print & Trade Fair Exhibition

Veepee Graphics Lanka Director Nalani Madhushani, Secretary to the Ministry of Industries Thilaka Jayasundara, Chairman of the Sri Lanka United National Business Alliance Tania Abeysundara, and Sri Lanka Association of Printers President Eng. Janaka Rathnakumara participating in the ribbon-cutting ceremony

​Veepee Graphics Lanka officially opened its stall at the Sri Lanka Print & Trade Fair Exhibition 2026 at the BMICH.

The stall was inaugurated with a ribbon-cutting ceremony and the lighting of the traditional oil lamp by Veepee Graphics Lanka Director Nalani Madhushani, Secretary to the Ministry of Industries Thilaka Jayasundara, and Chairman of the Sri Lanka United National Business Alliance Tania Abeysundara. Sri Lanka Association of Printers President Eng. Janaka Rathnakumara also participated in the occasion.

The exhibition, recognized as Sri Lanka’s premier and largest printing, packaging, labelling, signage, and graphic communications event, runs from June 19 to 21, serving as a prime platform for the company to showcase its cutting-edge innovations in the flexible packaging sector.

​Operating as a proud extension of Bangalore-based Veepee Graphic Solutions, Veepee Graphics Lanka has established itself as a trusted name synonymous with quality, precision, and technological excellence.

The strategic initiative to expand into Sri Lanka was positioned by astute entrepreneur P. Jaichandra. Today, the organization is further strengthened by its future-ready leadership, with Natasha Jaichandra representing the next generation. As one of the youngest directors in the flexible packaging industry, she brings youthful energy, technical acumen, and a progressive vision that seamlessly blends tradition with modern innovation.

​Veepee Graphics Lanka remains at the forefront of color innovation through its implementation of Extended Color Gamut technology. This revolutionary screening technology enables superior color consistency, expanded design possibilities, and enhanced print performance, setting new benchmarks in print quality across the region.

​​Commenting on the occasion and the company’s continuous commitment to the industry, Director Nalani Madhushani noted the organization’s dedication to uplifting local standards. “At Veepee, excellence is not merely a goal, it is a tradition, a responsibility, and a promise that continues to shape the future of print,” she stated. Beyond commercial success, Veepee Graphics Lanka takes immense pride in contributing to the economic progress of Sri Lanka by empowering local businesses to grow, compete, and thrive in a dynamic global marketplace.
