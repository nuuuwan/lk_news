SLIM National Sales Awards 2026 LOLC Insurance celebrates 8 winners

Front row (from left): Dilshani Shashikala (Merit Award – Sales Supervisor Category, Life Insurance), Khemanthi Peiris (Bronze Award – Other Sales Support Manager and Above Category – Life Insurance) and Suresh Madushanka (Bronze Award – Sales Executive Category, Life Insurance) Back row (from left): Kaushalya Abeygunarathna (Silver Award – Territory Manager Category, General Insurance), Palika Dilrukshi (Merit Award – Front Liner Category, Life Insurance), Hasara Sankalpa (Bronze Award – Territory Manager Category, Life Insurance), Manmadhanayaki Eliyathamby (Merit Award – Sales Supervisor Category, Life Insurance) and Yashodha Sandamali (Gold Award – Sales Executive Category, Life Insurance)

Eight sales professionals from LOLC Insurance were recognised at SLIM National Sales Awards 2026, securing accolades across multiple categories in recognition of their performance and professional excellence.

At the 2026 Awards, Yashodha Sandamali secured a Gold award in the Sales Executive Category – Life Insurance, while Suresh Madushanka received a Bronze award in the same category.

Kaushalya Abeygunarathna won a Silver award in the Territory Manager Category – General Insurance, while Hasara Sankalpa received a Bronze award in the Territory Manager Category – Life Insurance.

Khemanthi Peiris earned a Bronze award in the Other Sales Support Manager and Above Category, recognising her performance among the nominees from across industries. Meanwhile, Dilshani Shashikala and Manmadhanayaki Eliyathamby received Merit awards in the Sales Supervisor Category – Life Insurance, while Palika Dilrukshi was recognised with a Merit award in the Front Liner Category – Life Insurance.

LOLC General Insurance Chairman and Principal Officer and LOLC Life Assurance Director Kithsiri Gunawardena said, “At LOLC Insurance, our success is built on the strength of our people and the trust they establish with our customers. Investing in their development and recognising their achievements are not only a celebration of individual excellence but also an acknowledgement of the capabilities and commitment that drive our organisation forward.”

LOLC Life Assurance Chief Operating Officer and Principal Officer Jayantha Kalinga added, “We are delighted to see eight of our sales professionals recognised at SLIM National Sales Awards 2026. This achievement reflects their dedication, the relationships they build with customers and their commitment to serving them well.”
