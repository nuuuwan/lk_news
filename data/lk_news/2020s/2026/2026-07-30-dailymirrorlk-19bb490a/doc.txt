Suwa Seriya to launch motorcycle emergency medical service

Colombo, July 30 (Daily Mirror) - The 1990 Suwa Seriya Foundation has decided to introduce a motorcycle-based emergency medical service aimed at reaching patients requiring urgent medical attention more quickly and efficiently.

According to the foundation, the new service will initially target urban areas, including Colombo, where heavy traffic congestion and road obstructions often delay ambulance response times.

The main objective of the programme is to provide immediate first aid and basic medical assistance to patients before an ambulance arrives, the foundation said.

Under the initiative, specially trained medical officers will be deployed, while motorcycles equipped with essential medicines and medical equipment will be used to respond to emergencies.

The foundation said the motorcycle emergency service is planned to be launched as a pilot project in Colombo and other urban areas before the end of this year.
