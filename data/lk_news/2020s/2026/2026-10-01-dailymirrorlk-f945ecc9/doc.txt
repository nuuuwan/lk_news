The Executive Centre expands Sri Lankan footprint

To offer more than 150+ workstations, bringing TEC’s signature hospitality-led workspace experience to one of Colombo’s most prestigious business addresses

The Executive Centre (TEC), a premium flexible workspace provider, is set to further strengthen its presence in Sri Lanka with the launch of a new, state-of-the-art centre at Level 33, East Tower, World Trade Centre Colombo, scheduled to open on December 1, 2026.

Located within one of Colombo’s most prestigious commercial addresses, the new centre spans approximately 8,900 sq. ft. and offers over 150 workstations, reinforcing TEC’s commitment to meeting the growing demand for premium, flexible workspaces in Sri Lanka. The expansion builds on the success of TEC’s existing centres in Colombo, where the company has consistently served multinational corporations, financial institutions and high-growth enterprises seeking world-class workplace solutions.

Designed in line with TEC’s global standards, the new centre combines sophisticated interiors with thoughtfully curated amenities to create a seamless workplace experience. Members will have access to elegantly designed private offices, collaborative coworking spaces, premium meeting rooms, phone booths and hospitality-inspired member lounges, complemented by TEC’s signature personalised service and enterprise-grade technology.

As organisations increasingly prioritise workplace quality, flexibility and employee experience, TEC’s investment in WTC Colombo offers businesses the agility to scale rapidly without compromising on corporate prestige.

TEC Regional Managing Director Manish Khedia said, “Sri Lanka remains a highly attractive market for TEC and this expansion reflects our long-term confidence in its economic resilience and business landscape. As modern enterprises pivot toward premium workspaces that offer flexibility without compromising on quality, we remain committed to delivering best-in-class environments that support business growth, collaboration and productivity. Our new centre at WTC Colombo further strengthens our presence in the market while offering businesses access to an internationally recognised workspace experience backed by exceptional service and hospitality.”

Over the past three decades, TEC has built a reputation as a trusted partner to multinational corporations and growth-focused enterprises, delivering premium work environments backed by consistent service, operational excellence, and global scale. Today, TEC Group operates across 38 cities in 15 markets, supporting businesses at every stage of expansion.
