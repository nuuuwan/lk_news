SLT‑Mobitel recognised at SLASSCOM National Ingenuity Awards

SLT-Mobitel The Embryo team with award and certificate received at SLASSCOM National Ingenuity Awards 2026

Securing national recognition, SLT‑Mobitel’s innovation arm The Embryo’s SenseGrid solution was named National First Runner‑Up in the Best Innovative Product in Manufacturing Category, at the prestigious SLASSCOM National Ingenuity Awards 2026, held recently.

SenseGrid is an enterprise‑grade Industrial IoT solution designed to transform manufacturing and industrial operations through intelligent monitoring, automation and AI‑powered analytics.

The award, accepted by The Embryo team, reaffirms SLT‑Mobitel’s dedication to nurturing innovation and building solutions that are not only technologically advanced but also nationally relevant, commercially strong and closely aligned with Sri Lanka’s digital agenda.   The achievement highlights SLT‑Mobitel’s commitment to driving industry‑focused digital revolution and highlights the real impact of SenseGrid in reshaping the manufacturing operations.

Harnessing the power of Industrial IoT and artificial intelligence, SenseGrid delivers sensor‑based monitoring, real‑time visibility, predictive insights and data‑driven decision‑making, helping industries to reduce downtime, boost efficiency and confidently advance towards Industry 4.0.

Already deployed in practical industrial scenarios with partners such as DHL and DSI, SenseGrid has proven its ability to transition from prototype, to support machine condition monitoring, warehouse temperature control, environmental visibility and operational alerting.
