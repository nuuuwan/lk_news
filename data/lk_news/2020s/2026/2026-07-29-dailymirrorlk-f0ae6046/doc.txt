BASL special general meeting begins amid large lawyer turnout

Colombo, July 29 (Daily MIrror) - The Bar Association of Sri Lanka (BASL) Special General Meeting of its General Membership commenced a short while ago at the BASL Head Office in Hulftsdorp, with a large number of lawyers in attendance.

The government’s proposed constitutional amendment seeking to extend the retirement age of judges will be discussed at the Special General Meeting.
