Siyapatha Finance strengthens employee commitment to sustainability

Reinforcing its commitment to building a strong internal culture of sustainability, Siyapatha Finance PLC recently organised ‘Echoes of Earth – A Journey into Nature’, an experiential environmental learning programme tailored for its Sustainability Task Team.

The team comprises representatives from the company’s islandwide branch network and various departments, creating a broad internal platform to drive sustainability awareness and engagement across the organisation.

Held recently at the Beddagana Wetland Park, Kotte, the programme provided the employees with an opportunity to step beyond the conventional workplace setting and engage directly with nature, developing a deeper understanding of biodiversity, environmental conservation and the critical importance of protecting natural ecosystems.

Designed as more than a nature-based retreat, the initiative forms a key pillar of Siyapatha Finance’s ongoing efforts to foster employee ownership of sustainability.

Siyapatha Finance Chief Executive Officer Mathisha Hewavitharana stated, “True sustainability cannot simply be mandated from the top down; it must be lived, understood and embraced by every individual within our organisation. Through initiatives like ‘Echoes of Earth’, we are empowering our sustainability champions to step out of the office and experience first-hand the delicate balance of our natural ecosystems. By grounding our people in experiential learning and connecting their daily perspectives to broader environmental outcomes, we are building a conscious corporate culture where sustainable thinking is seamlessly woven into the fabric of our business and everyday professional lives.”

The guided nature experience was facilitated by Biodiversity Sri Lanka, led by Dr. Chamitha De Alwis and his team, whose expert insights enriched the programme with practical perspectives on biodiversity and the value of Sri Lanka’s natural habitats.

During the wetland exploration, the participants gained practical insights into wetland ecosystem functions and ecological importance, biodiversity, species diversity and interdependence of local flora and fauna, vital role wetlands play in supporting urban wildlife and natural habitats and direct relationship between human activities, corporate operations and environmental stewardship.
