CEAT opens premium Shop-in-Shop outlet at Olympic Tyre House, Colombo 6

CEAT Kelani Holdings has expanded its network of premium retail outlets with the opening of a new Shop-in-Shop (SIS) facility at Olympic Tyre House located at No. 255, Havelock Road, Colombo 6, strengthening the brand’s presence in one of the capital’s busiest commercial areas.

Strategically located opposite Havelock City Mall, the new outlet offers vehicle owners convenient access to CEAT’s full range of tyre solutions in a modern, customer-friendly retail environment. The facility provides an extensive range of CEAT tyres at attractive discounts alongside a comprehensive portfolio of tyre-related services including nitrogen and air inflation, wheel balancing and wheel alignment. A dedicated customer lounge has also been incorporated into the outlet to provide added comfort while vehicles are being attended to.
