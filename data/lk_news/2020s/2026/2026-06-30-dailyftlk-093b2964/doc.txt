CPC reduces Auto Diesel and Petrol 92 prices

The Ceylon Petroleum Corporation (CPC) yesterday announced a reduction in the retail prices of Auto Diesel and 92 Octane Petrol with effect from midnight.

Accordingly, the price of Auto Diesel has been reduced by Rs. 25 per litre to Rs. 382, while 92 Octane Petrol has been cut by Rs. 20 per litre to Rs. 414.

However, it said the prices of Super Diesel, 95 Octane Petrol, and Kerosene will remain unchanged at Rs. 478, Rs. 478, and Rs. 285 per litre, respectively.
