EDB unveils flagship statistical publication ‘Export Performance Indicators 2024’

At the official launch of the ‘Export Performance Indicators 2024’ were (from left): EDB Acting Additional Director General (Development) Kumudinie Mudalige, Chairman and CEO Mangala Wijesinghe, Digital Economy Deputy Minister Eng. Eranga Weeraratne, Industry and Entrepreneurship Development Minister Sunil Handunnetti and Deputy Minister Chathuranga Abeysinghe, Central Bank Governor Dr. Nandalal Weerasinghe, Chief Adviser to the President on Digital Economy Dr. Hans Wijayasuriya, and Treasury Secretary Dr. Harshana Suriyapperuma

The Export Development Board (EDB) has launched its flagship annual publication ‘Export Performance Indicators 2024’.

The latest edition provides statistical information related to Sri Lanka’s external trade and its contribution to major economic indicators such as trade balance.  GDP and individual sectorial involvement for the year 2024 and five preceding years.

This volume is the 38th Volume of this publication, and its previous editions have been constantly used as a comprehensive and trustworthy reference by exporters, researchers and policy makers.

The data compiled in the ‘Export Performance Indicators 2024’, is presented along with the trade statistics for the last 05 years along with a comprehensive analysis of Sri Lanka’s export trade, products and services, from the year 2020 up to the year 2024, including the export regions per each product, their market growth and projected export forecast.
