Hayleys Solar powers Kelaniya University’s Faculty of Computing & Technology

Aerial view of the 120 kW solar PV system installed at the Kelaniya University

Renewable energy solutions provider Hayleys Solar has successfully completed a solar PV installation for the Faculty of Computing & Technology at the University of Kelaniya, supporting the institution’s commitment to sustainable energy adoption and long-term energy efficiency.

The installation provides the Faculty with access to clean, renewable energy, helping reduce its reliance on conventional electricity sources while contributing to lower long-term energy costs and reduced carbon emissions. The solar system also powers the University’s newly constructed auditorium, further extending the benefits of renewable energy across the institution’s facilities.

The project was secured through a competitive government tender process, with Hayleys Solar successfully fulfilling the required eligibility criteria, including technical qualifications, financial capacity and relevant industry experience. The successful completion of the project reflects the company’s expertise in delivering reliable and high-quality solar solutions for large-scale institutional requirements. The installation forms part of Hayleys Solar’s continued efforts to support the adoption of renewable energy across Sri Lanka’s public and institutional sectors. By enabling educational institutions to integrate clean energy into their operations, such initiatives contribute to the country’s broader renewable energy ambitions while allowing institutions to manage their energy expenditure more effectively.
