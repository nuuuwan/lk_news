Why watching videos while driving is dangerous?

Colombo, August 7 (Daily Mirror) - Police have urged motorists to avoid watching cooking videos or any other entertainment while driving, warning that even a few seconds of distraction can lead to fatal consequences.

In a public safety message, Police said that taking one's eyes off the road to watch a video could result in drivers losing crucial moments needed to avoid a vehicle, pedestrian or any other obstacle.

"Even if your attention is diverted from the road for just a few seconds because of a video you are watching, you lose valuable time to react and avoid danger," Police said.

They reminded drivers that they have a responsibility to protect not only their own lives but also the lives of passengers, pedestrians and other road users.

Police advised motorists to leave videos and other forms of entertainment until after completing their journey, emphasising that while videos can be watched later, "a life lost cannot be brought back."

Urging all road users to exercise caution, Police called on drivers to remain fully focused behind the wheel and drive safely to reach their destinations without incident.
