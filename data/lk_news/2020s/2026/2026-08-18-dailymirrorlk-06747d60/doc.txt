Rs. 5,000 allowance for over 5,000 special-needs students

Colombo, Aug. 18 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet has approved a proposal to provide a monthly allowance of Rs. 5,000 to students with special educational needs from low-income families.

The 2026 Budget proposed the allowance for eligible students studying in schools across the country. Applications were subsequently invited to assess the eligibility of low-income students with special educational needs.

Accordingly, 5,530 students have been identified as eligible to receive the allowance.

The Cabinet approved the proposal submitted by Prime Minister and Minister of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education to allocate funds to provide the monthly allowance to all eligible students.
