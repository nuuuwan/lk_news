Ather introduces ‘Rizta Z’ to Sri Lanka at Colombo Motor Show

Evolution Auto (Pvt) Ltd, the authorised distributor of Ather Energy in Sri Lanka, launched the Ather Rizta Z at the Colombo Motor Show 2026.

The smart executive scooter brings together technology, safety, comfort and everyday convenience. To mark the launch, customers can purchase the scooter at a special introductory price of Rs. 799,900, a reduction of Rs. 50,000 from its standard retail price of Rs. 849,900.

The offer is available for a limited period only. Visitors to the Colombo Motor Show, which took place from 20 to 23 November, saw the Rizta Z at the Ather booth and experienced it through test rides on the dedicated track.

The Rizta Z comes equipped with features designed to make everyday riding more convenient and controlled across different road and traffic conditions. Its 7-inch TFT touchscreen comes with integrated Google Maps, allowing riders to access navigation directly from the dashboard. Magic Twist uses regenerative braking to help riders slow down through throttle control, offering greater control in stop-and-go traffic. AutoHold helps keep the scooter stationary on inclines, making uphill starts easier, while SkidControl helps manage traction on slippery surfaces, particularly during rainy conditions.

Comfort and practicality are central to the Rizta Z’s design. It features the largest seat in its class, along with a wide pillion backrest, offering a comfortable riding experience for both the rider and passenger. The scooter provides a combined storage capacity of 56 litres, comprising 34 litres of underseat storage and an additional 22-litre front storage compartment. This gives riders convenient space for work essentials, shopping and other everyday items. These features make the Rizta Z a practical choice for working professionals and daily commuters looking for comfort and convenience in their everyday journeys.

Commenting on the launch, Zahran Ziyawudeen, Chief Executive Officer of Ather Sri Lanka and Evolution Auto (Pvt) Ltd, said, “We are pleased to bring the Rizta Z to Sri Lanka. Today’s customers look for more than just a means of getting around. They want a scooter that is comfortable, practical and equipped with features that make everyday journeys easier. The Rizta Z brings these together with Ather’s technology and safety features. We also understand that confidence in the vehicle needs to be matched by confidence in the ownership experience. Through our network of Experience Centres and Service Centres, along with Ather Grid’s charging infrastructure, we are working to provide customers with the support they need as they make the shift to electric mobility.”
