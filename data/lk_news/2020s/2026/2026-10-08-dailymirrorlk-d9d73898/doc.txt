Subdued secondary market activity amid selling pressure

The secondary market remained relatively subdued yesterday, with limited activity and moderate volumes.

The overall flow was tilted towards selling, although some buying interest was observed in the 2028 maturity.

Among the trades recorded yesterday, the 15.10.2028 maturity traded at 10.55 percent. Further along the curve, the 01.08.2030, 15.10.2030 and 01.02.2031 maturities traded within a range of 11.25 percent-11.40 percent.

Yesterday, the PDMO concluded its weekly T-bill auction, raising Rs.80.0 billion, in line with the total amount offered. For the three-month T-bill, Rs.35.0 billion was initially offered, while Rs.44.1 billion was accepted. The weighted average yield rate (WAYR) increased by 2bps to 9.27 percent. For the six-month T-bill, the PDMO accepted Rs.29.5 billion against an offered amount of Rs.25.0 billion, with the WAYR rising by 3bps to 9.44 percent. For the 12-month T-bill, Rs.6.4 billion was accepted, which was below the offered amount, while the WAYR remained unchanged at 9.95 percent.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee depreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.330.87/US dollar, compared to Rs.330.62/US dollar seen earlier. Market liquidity in the banking system expanded to Rs.390.68 billion, compared to Rs.353.98 billion recorded previously.
