Jaffna-born hip-hop artist SVDP arrives in Colombo for TEDxColombo

COLOMBO, Sri Lanka (Daily Mirror) - Award-winning hip-hop artist and songwriter SVDP has arrived in Colombo ahead of TEDxColombo. Born in Jaffna and raised in Toronto, SVDP draws on his Sri Lankan Tamil roots and personal experiences to explore identity, culture and resilience through music and visual storytelling.

With more than 200 million streams worldwide, SVDP has toured internationally and earned recognition across music and film. His appearance at TEDxColombo brings him back to Sri Lanka, where his story began.

Now in its 14th edition, TEDxColombo’s flagship event will take place under the theme “The Fractured Paradox.” This year’s programme explores how fractures in our lives, communities and institutions can open up new perspectives and opportunities for change. Since its launch in 2009, TEDxColombo has brought Sri Lankan voices and stories to a global audience through the TED platform.
