Yields remain anchored at weekly T-bill auction

The secondary market reflected low volumes and activity, while the yield curve stood broadly anchored.

At the weekly T-bill auction, the weighted average yield rate (WAYR) remained unchanged across all three-month, six-month and 12-month maturities.

Among the traded maturities, 15.12.2029 traded at 10.70 percent. Within the 2030 segment, 15.10.2030 changed hands at 11.00 percent. Further along the curve, 01.12.2031 traded at 11.10 percent, while 15.12.2032 was seen trading from 11.44 percent to 11.50 percent.

At the belly of the yield curve, 15.01.2033 traded between 11.47 percent to 11.50 percent, while 01.11.2033 changed hands at 11.60 percent. Moving ahead, 15.10.2034 changed hands at 11.78 percent. Finally, 15.08.2036 was seen trading higher between 12.03 percent-11.98 percent.

The PDMO concluded its weekly T-bill auction yesterday, raising Rs.80.2 billion, below the initial offer amount of Rs.90.0 billion. For the three-month T-bill, Rs.45.0 billion was initially offered, while a higher amount of Rs.63.6 billion was accepted.

The WAYR stood flat at 9.35 percent. For the six-month T-bill, the PDMO accepted only Rs.4.6 billion against an offered amount of Rs.25.0 billion, with the WAYR remaining unchanged at 9.43 percent.

For the 12-month T-bill, nearly Rs.12.0 billion was accepted, which was below the offer of Rs.20.0 billion, while the WAYR remained steady at 9.95 percent.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee slightly depreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.332.76/US dollar, compared to Rs.332.74/US dollar seen previously. Market liquidity in the banking system contracted to Rs.443.99 billion, compared to Rs.447.79 billion seen previously.
