PAL to be removed on newsprint and solar storage batteries from January 2027

Colombo, Nov. 12 (Daily Mirror) - The Ports and Airports Development Levy (PAL) imposed on imported newsprint and batteries used for solar energy storage will be removed with effect from January 1, 2027, under the 2027 Budget proposals.

The proposal aims to provide tax relief on the importation of newsprint and batteries used in solar energy storage.
