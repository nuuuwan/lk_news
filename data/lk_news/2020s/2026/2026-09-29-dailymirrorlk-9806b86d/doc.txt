New shrine hall consecrated

A meritorious ceremony to unveil and consecrate the newly constructed shrine hall at the historic Yodhakandiya Purana Naga Maha Viharaya in Tissamaharama was held with the participation of devotees. The ceremony, dedicated to the longevity of the Sri Sambuddha Sasana, paying homage to the Supreme Buddha and fostering faith and devotion among devotees, was held under the guidance of the Chief Incumbent of the temple, Senior Lecturer Ven. Mahawewa Mahinda Nayaka Thera. Pix by Nisal Baduge
