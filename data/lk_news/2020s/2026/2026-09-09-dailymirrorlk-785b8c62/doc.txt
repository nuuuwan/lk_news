India, Sri Lanka reaffirm commitment to regional security, development

Colombo, Sep. 9 (Daily Mirror) - India and Sri Lanka have reaffirmed their commitment to work together for the development of both countries and the well-being of their people, while strengthening cooperation on regional security, peace and prosperity.

The commitment was reaffirmed during a meeting held in Colombo today between Indian leader [name] and Sri Lankan President Anura Kumara Dissanayake.

During the meeting, the Indian leader conveyed Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s greetings to President Dissanayake and congratulated him on the completion of two years of his government.

Describing India and Sri Lanka as “civilized twins, close neighbors and maritime partners,” the Indian leader said both countries had also reaffirmed their resolve to work together to ensure the security, safety, peace and prosperity of the region.
