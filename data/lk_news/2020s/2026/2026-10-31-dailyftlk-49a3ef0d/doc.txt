EDB conducts AI workshop for SME exporters in Kandy with support of Habib Bank

The Sri Lanka Export Development Board (EDB) has successfully conducted a workshop on the practical application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) for Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (SMEs) engaged in export businesses in the Kandy region, with the sponsorship support of Habib Bank.

The workshop was held at the Grand Kandyan Hotel, Kandy, bringing together 55 participants, including SME exporters and entrepreneurs. The program provided participants with practical knowledge on how AI can be effectively utilised to enhance business operations, productivity, innovation and competitiveness in international markets.

The session focused on practical applications of AI relevant to exporters, including market research, identification of potential international buyers, digital marketing, content creation, customer engagement, business process automation and other export-related activities.

Bluechip Tech Ltd., and Bluechip Technologies Asia Founder and CEO Uditha Bandara, served as the resource person for the workshop. He shared his expertise and practical experience on the application of AI and emerging digital technologies, providing participants with valuable insights into how AI-powered tools can be incorporated into their day-to-day business activities.

The interactive session also demonstrated how AI tools can help SMEs reduce the time and cost associated with routine business activities, improve decision-making and enhance their ability to identify and access new international market opportunities. Participants had the opportunity to engage in discussions and gain practical insights into the effective use of AI for business growth and export development.

The program forms part of the EDB’s ongoing efforts to strengthen the digital capabilities of Sri Lankan SMEs and encourage exporters to adopt emerging technologies to improve productivity, innovation and competitiveness in global markets.

The EDB appreciates Habib Bank for its valuable sponsorship and support in successfully conducting the program in Kandy. The EDB also extends its appreciation to Bluechip Tech Ltd., and Bluechip Technologies Asia Founder and CEO Uditha Bandara for sharing his expertise and contributing to the capacity-building of Sri Lankan SME exporters through the workshop.
