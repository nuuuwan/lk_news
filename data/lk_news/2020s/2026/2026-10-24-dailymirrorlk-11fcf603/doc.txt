Multilac upgrades CMC’s Day Care Centre for Children’s Day

Representatives of Multilac and Colombo Municipal Council at the upgraded CMC Day Care Centre

On Children’s Day, Multilac marked the completion of facility improvements at the Colombo Municipal Council Day Care Centre through a corporate social responsibility initiative undertaken with the Council.

The project enhanced the centre’s facilities, providing a safer, more comfortable and child-friendly environment. Multilac donated essential items supporting the children’s comfort, hygiene, safety and recreational needs, along with paint used to refresh the interior, desks and chairs.

The Multilac team also completed a colourful exterior mural, bringing renewed warmth, colour and vibrancy to the premises. To commemorate the occasion, the children presented a special programme expressing their appreciation to Multilac.

Representatives of Multilac, together with the Deputy Mayor of the Colombo Municipal Council, ceremonially opened the upgraded centre. The initiative reflects Multilac’s continued commitment to child welfare and meaningful community development.
