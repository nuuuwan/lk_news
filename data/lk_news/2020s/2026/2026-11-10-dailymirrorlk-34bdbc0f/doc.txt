People’s Bank facilitates digitalization of CP stamp duty payment process

In line with government’s National Digital Economy Strategy 2025–2030, People’s Bank has extended digital banking facilities to support the Central Provincial Revenue Department in digitalizing the stamp duty payment process, marking an important step towards modernizing public revenue collection services.

The digitalized process was officially inaugurated by the Governor of the Central Province, Dr. Harsha Suranga Fernando.

The occasion was attended by Central Provincial Chief Secretary H.G.M.A. Premasinghe, Central Provincial Revenue Commissioner K.G.P.J. Katupulla, and representatives of People’s Bank, including Assistant Regional Manager S.B.S.K. Priyadarshana and Chief Manager of Kandy Branch Prasanna Karunarathne. Officials from the Digitalization Department, Revenue Department and Central Provincial Council were also present at the event.
