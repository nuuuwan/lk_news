Sri Lanka and China reaffirm ties at Chinese New Year meeting

A cordial meeting between Chinese Ambassador Qi Zhenhong and Labour Minister and Economic Development Deputy Minister Dr. Anil Jayantha Fernando was held yesterday (16) in conjunction with the Chinese Spring Festival.

Both parties reflected on the longstanding friendship between Sri Lanka and China, highlighting the historical ties and shared cultural values that continue to underpin bilateral relations.

Both sides reaffirmed their commitment to further strengthening diplomatic and economic engagement in the year ahead.
