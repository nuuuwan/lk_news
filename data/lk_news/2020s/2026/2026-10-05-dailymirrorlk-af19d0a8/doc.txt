High-speed internet rollout reaches over 3,600 secondary schools

Colombo, Oct. 5 (Daily Mirror) - The national programme to provide high-speed fibre internet facilities to all government secondary schools across the country is progressing successfully, with more than 3,600 schools already connected or currently being connected.

The programme is being jointly implemented by the Telecommunications Regulatory Commission of Sri Lanka (TRCSL) under its “Gamata Sannivedanaya” project and the Ministry of Education, Higher Education and Vocational Education.

According to the programme, high-speed fibre connections are being provided to 6,225 government secondary schools islandwide.

Of these, 1,264 schools had fibre connections before the programme commenced, while 2,345 schools have received new connections under the project. Fibre connections are currently being provided to a further 2,616 schools.

The Western Province has recorded 285 schools receiving connections, while 275 schools in the Central Province, 300 in the Southern Province, 172 in the Northern Province, 347 in the Eastern Province, 334 in the North Western Province, 153 in the North Central Province, 176 in the Uva Province and 303 in the Sabaragamuwa Province have been connected under the programme.

The Government has allocated Rs. 150 million through the 2026 Budget to provide unlimited internet packages to these schools at a monthly cost of Rs. 5,130 per school.

Meanwhile, arrangements have also been made to extend high-speed internet facilities to all 3,832 government primary schools across the country during 2027 and 2028.

The programme aims to make digital education a reality by ensuring equal access to modern information technology for students, while reducing the digital divide between rural and urban areas.
