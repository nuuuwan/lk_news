EDB invites potential and SME exporters to strategic workshop on risk management and incoterms

The Sri Lanka Export Development Board (EDB) will organise a customer-centric specialised training program under the theme “Risk Management, Strategies and Incoterms to Win Competitiveness in International Trade Beyond Borders” tomorrow, 18 February from 9:30 a.m. to 3:30 p.m.

The workshop aims to enhance the export expertise of potential exporters and small and medium-scale entrepreneurs.

The program will be conducted by expert export trainer Dinesh De Silva and will be delivered with explanations in both Sinhala and English. It will include practical exercises based on real-life industry scenarios.

The workshop will be held at the 3rd floor auditorium of the NDB-EDB Building located at No. 42, Nawam Mawatha, Colombo 02.

Participants will gain insights into key subject areas including strategies for establishing and negotiating orders, building buyer confidence and credibility, product knowledge and effective labelling and packaging, strategic pricing for competitiveness, managing business risks, practical application of Incoterms in trade, and utilising international payment terms for secure transactions.

Those interested may contact the Trade Facilitation Division at 071 6993554 or 011 2300 705-11 for further information and registration. As only a limited number of participants can be accommodated, participation will be granted on a first-come, first-served basis.
