El Niño ‘100% certain’, Sri Lanka must strengthen disaster preparedness: Sajith

Colombo, Sep. 14 (Daily Mirror) - Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa has called for greater disaster preparedness, saying El Niño has become a “100% certainty” according to international climate organisations.

Premadasa, who is also Co-Chairman of the Parliamentary Caucus on Climate Change, said Sri Lanka must shift its focus from responding to disasters after they occur to preparedness and mitigation based on early warnings.

“Sri Lanka has traditionally focused on responding after a disaster strikes. There must be a paradigm shift towards mitigation and preparedness whenever an early warning is issued about an impending disaster,” he said.
