Diabetics urged to undergo annual eye examinations to prevent blindness

Colombo, Nov. 15 (Daily Mirror) - Diabetic patients have been urged to undergo eye examinations at least once a year to detect eye complications early and prevent permanent vision loss.

The warning was issued at a media conference held yesterday (13) at the National Eye Hospital auditorium to mark World Diabetes Day, which falls today (14).

Held jointly by the National Eye Hospital and the Sri Lanka Institute of Ophthalmologists under the theme “Let's Prevent Blindness Caused by Diabetes”, the event highlighted the importance of early detection and timely treatment of diabetes-related eye conditions.

President of the Sri Lanka Institute of Ophthalmologists, Dr. K. R. Dayawansa, said diabetic patients were at risk of losing their sight permanently without experiencing any early warning symptoms.

He said regular eye examinations by an ophthalmologist could help identify the risk of vision loss before symptoms appeared and allow patients to receive timely treatment.

He stressed that every diabetic patient should undergo a comprehensive eye examination at least once a year.

Dr. Dayawansa also expressed concern that many people who become blind or require hospitalisation due to diabetes are between 35 and 50 years of age and are often the main breadwinners of their families.

He said preventing diabetes-related blindness required not only medical intervention but also proper attention and control by patients.

The media conference also highlighted the need for people over 30 to undergo diabetes screening at least once a year.

Those with a family history of diabetes, obesity or a history of diabetes during pregnancy were advised to undergo screening every six months or annually, depending on their risk.

Dr. Ravi Jayawardena, Consultant Ophthalmologist at the National Eye Hospital, Dr. Deepani Wewelwala, Consultant Ophthalmologist, and Dr. P. Sriharanathan, President of the Sri Lanka Vitreo-Retina Specialists Association, also addressed the event.

National Eye Hospital Director Dr. Buddhika Kurukulasuriya, eye surgeons, doctors, nurses and other health staff were present.
