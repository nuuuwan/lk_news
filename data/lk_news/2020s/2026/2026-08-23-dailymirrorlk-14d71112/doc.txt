People’s Bank introduces Legacy 65 anniversary investment plan

To commemorate its 65th anniversary, People’s Bank has launched the Legacy 65 anniversary investment plan, offering the customers an exclusive opportunity to grow their savings while celebrating the bank’s remarkable journey of serving the nation for over six decades.

Introduced as a special anniversary promotion, the Legacy 65 investment plan is available for 65 days from July 1 to September 3, 2026, offering an attractive 6 percent annual interest rate.

The customers can invest in plans valued at Rs.25,000, Rs.50,000, Rs.100,000, Rs.500,000 and Rs.1,000,000, enabling the individuals with diverse financial goals to benefit from this limited-time investment opportunity.

People’s Bank Deputy General Manager Retail Banking A.U.A. Anzar said, “For 65 years, People’s Bank has remained committed to empowering the financial aspirations of Sri Lankans. The Legacy 65 anniversary investment plan is a tribute to the enduring trust our customers have placed in us and reflects our continued commitment to providing rewarding financial solutions that create long-term value. We invite our customers to celebrate this milestone with us by taking advantage of this exclusive anniversary investment offer.”

People’s Bank proudly celebrated its 65th anniversary on July 1, 2026, marking six and a half decades of dedicated service to the nation. Since its establishment in 1961, the bank has played a pioneering role in expanding access to formal banking services, promoting financial inclusion and supporting Sri Lanka’s economic and social development. Today, the bank continues to strengthen this proud legacy through innovative digital banking solutions and customer-centric financial services, reaching communities across every part of the island.

The customers are invited to visit their nearest People’s Bank branch to learn more about the Legacy 65 anniversary investment plan and enjoy this exclusive anniversary offer before the promotion concludes on September 3, 2026.
