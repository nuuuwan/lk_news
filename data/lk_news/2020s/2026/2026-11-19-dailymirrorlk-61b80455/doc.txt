Naval personnel awarded lifesaving medals for Kala Oya bus rescue

Three Sri Lanka Navy personnel have received the Commonwealth Lifesaving Service Medal and the Sri Lanka Lifesaving Hero Medal for rescuing 69 civilians trapped inside a passenger bus during Cyclone Ditwah in 2025.

The rescue operation was carried out in the Kala Oya area after severe weather conditions caused by the cyclone left the passengers stranded inside the bus.

The three sailors recognised for their bravery are Fleet Chief Petty Officer SAEL Senavirathna, Leading Seaman BKA Prasad and Able Seaman STD Abeyrathna, all attached to the Navy's Rapid Action Boat Squadron (RABS).

The medals and certificates were presented at a ceremony held yesterday (19) at the official residence of the British High Commissioner in Colombo.

The event was jointly organised by the Royal Life Saving Society Commonwealth and the Sri Lanka Life Saving Association.

British High Commissioner to Sri Lanka Andrew Patrick and Australian High Commissioner Matthew Duckworth attended the ceremony and presented the awards to the three sailors.

Director General of the Sri Lanka Coast Guard Rear Admiral Ruwan Rupasena, Director General Operations Rear Admiral Chaminda Karunasena, President of the Sri Lanka Life Saving Association Athula Senarathna and Chief Executive Officer Asanka Nanayakkara were also present.
