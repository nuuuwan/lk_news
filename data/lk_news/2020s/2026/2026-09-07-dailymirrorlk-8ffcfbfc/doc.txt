Colombo-Kandy train service to resume next month: Deputy Minister

Colombo, Sep. 7 (Daily Mirror) - The railway service between Peradeniya and Colombo, which was disrupted after being severely damaged by Cyclone Ditwa, is expected to resume in October, Deputy Minister of Transport and Highways Dr. Prasanna Gunasena said.

He said reconstruction work on the severely flood-damaged section of the Main Line between Rambukkana and Kadugannawa is scheduled to be completed by September 15.

The Deputy Minister said 97 locations along the Upcountry railway line were severely affected by landslides following Cyclone Ditwa.

He said construction work on the Rambukkana-Kadugannawa section has already been completed at several locations, while work at two remaining locations is progressing rapidly.

Thilina Swarnamal, site engineer of the company undertaking the railway reconstruction, said the work on the Rambukkana-Kadugannawa section has reached its final stage and is expected to be fully completed by September 15.

Meanwhile, construction work on the Peradeniya Black Bridge, also known as Devil's Bridge, has faced another setback after rising water levels in the Mahaweli River submerged construction machinery.

However, Deputy Minister Gunasena said work on the bridge would be completed soon, enabling train services between Colombo and Kandy to resume shortly.
