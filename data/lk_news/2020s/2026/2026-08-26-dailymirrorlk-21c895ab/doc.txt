Sathis Abeywickrama recognised as ‘Flexo Avenger’ at Asia Summit

Sathis Abeywickrama, Managing Director of Flexiprint (Pvt) Ltd, was recognized as a ‘Flexo Avenger’ at the Flexo Summit Asia 2026 held in Bengaluru, India, recently.

The ‘Flexo Avenger’ is a special recognition conferred on individuals who have made a significant impact on the development of Flexographic printing. He was featured and recognized at the Summit’s “Flexo Avengers: Converters who Changed the Game” session alongside other leading personalities from the region.

“Being recognized as a Flexo Avenger is indeed an honour, and it is also an acknowledgement and recognition of our continued commitment, innovation, and customer focus over a long but successful journey of over four decades. This journey has been marked by significant milestones and goes back to the time when specialized packaging required by our export industry, including tea bag tags and tags, were sourced from overseas,” said Sathis Abeywickrama.

Having entered the printing industry in 1978 and established PrintUSA in 1989, Abeywickrama founded Flexiprint in 1994 to fulfill the requirement for Sri Lanka’s important and booming tea export industry in terms of supplying tea bag tags, tea envelopes, tagged pyramid mesh tea bags and specialty labels.
