TVET CareerOne platform handed over

Deputy Minister of Vocational Education Nalin Hewage is seen attending the final handover ceremony of the TVET CareerOne Digital Platform Project, implemented by the Ministry of Education in collaboration with the Tertiary and Vocational Education Commission (TVEC), with funding from the Korea International Cooperation Agency (KOICA). The platform aims to provide career guidance, job information and employment-related services to vocational training students and industry professionals.
