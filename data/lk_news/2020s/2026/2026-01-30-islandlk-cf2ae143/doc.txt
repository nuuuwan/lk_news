Hayleys Mobility ushering in a new era of premium sustainable mobility

Sri Lanka’s automotive landscape took a decisive step towards cleaner, smarter mobility as Hayleys Mobility Limited, the mobility arm of Hayleys Fentons Limited, officially unveiled OJ (OMODA & JAECOO) on 27th January 2026 at The Kingsbury Colombo.

As the exclusive authorised distributor, Hayleys Mobility introduces OJ to address growing demands from urban and corporate customers seeking premium vehicles with reduced environmental impact and advanced connectivity.

OMODA & JAECOO are premium brands under the Chery Group, China’s No. 01 passenger vehicle exporter for 23 years and ranked 233rd on the 2025 Fortune Global 500. Through this partnership, customers in Sri Lanka will benefit from manufacturer-backed warranties, a nationwide after-sales service network, and the trust associated with the Hayleys Group’s 148-year legacy.

The initial OJ portfolio features a versatile mix of Plug-in Hybrid (PHEV), Hybrid Electric Vehicle (HEV), and advanced Internal Combustion Engine (ICE) models. The JAECOO range includes the flagship J8, J7 PHEV, J6, J5 combining off-road capability with refined luxury, while the OMODA range comprises the C9 PHEV, the stylish C5, and the fully electric E5, designed for future-focused, tech-savvy drivers.

“OJ strengthens our long-term capability to support cleaner mobility while introducing globally benchmarked vehicles recognised across key markets, including the United Kingdom, Australia, the Middle East, Europe and South America.”
