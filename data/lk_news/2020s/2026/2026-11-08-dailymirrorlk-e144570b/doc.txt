Businessman dies after fall from Slave Island apartment complex

Colombo, Nov 08 (Daily Mirror) - A 59-year-old businessman died after falling from an apartment complex in Kompannavidiya (Slave Island) this morning (08), Police said.

The incident occurred at around 10.55 am, with the deceased identified as a resident of Battaramulla.

Police have launched an investigation to determine whether the man accidentally fell from the building or died by suicide.

The Kompannavidiya Police are conducting further investigations into the incident.
