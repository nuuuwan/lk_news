Vasudeva says Mahinda Kahandagama has no connection with his party

Colombo, Oct. 6 (Daily Mirror) - Former Parliamentarian Vasudeva Nanayakkara has said that Mahinda Kahandagama, who has been linked to the Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP), has no current connection with his political party.

Responding to claims regarding Kahandagama’s political affiliation, Nanayakkara said Kahandagama had been associated with his party around 25 years ago, when he contested a local government election, but had left the party a long time ago.

“He is not in our party. He was with us about 25 years ago, when he contested the Urban Council election. Now, he is with the ‘Pohottuwa’ (SLPP),” Nanayakkara said.

He said Kahandagama’s name did not appear on any of the party’s nomination lists and that he neither participated in nor received invitations to attend the party’s meetings or activities.

Nanayakkara also alleged that Kahandagama had joined forces with the SLPP group involved in confronting activists of the 2022 ‘Aragalaya’ at Galle Face.

“As I told the General Secretary, they joined the ‘Pohottuwa’ wave and went to attack those activists at Galle Face. That is the reality,” he said.

He said confusion over Kahandagama’s affiliation had arisen because the SLPP side claimed that, during a period of political alliance, Kahandagama had contested under a common symbol associated with Nanayakkara’s party.

Nanayakkara said that association was only temporary and dated back to the period when the Pathikavedika organisation was being formed.

“His connection with us was only for a very brief period. Since then, he has had absolutely no connection with our party,” he said.
