Gotabaya gives nearly two-hour statement over Sugeeshwara’s probe

Colombo, Nov.9 (Daily Mirror) - Former President Gotabaya Rajapaksa has given a nearly two-hour statement to the Central Crimes Investigation Bureau (CCIB) in connection with an ongoing investigation into allegations against New People's Front leader Sugeeshwara Bandara.

Rajapaksa left the CCIB after recording his statement regarding Bandara, who is currently in remand custody.
