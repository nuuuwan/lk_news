Flood-hit O/L students allowed to arrange alternative practical exam dates

Colombo, Oct. 27 (Daily Mirror) - Students in Sri Lanka’s Sabaragamuwa Province who are unable to reach their examination centres for the G.C.E. Ordinary Level (O/L) aesthetic practical examinations today due to flooding can arrange to sit the examinations on another date.

Commissioner General of Examinations A. K. S. Indika Kumari Liyanage said affected students need not worry if they are unable to attend their practical examinations because of the prevailing flood situation.

Students who miss their examinations due to flooding can visit their respective examination centres once the flood situation has subsided to arrange a date for a make-up practical examination.

The measure is intended to assist students who face difficulties travelling to their examination centres due to floods.
