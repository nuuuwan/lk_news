Minister calls for stronger child protection after Knuckles incident

Colombo, Nov. 15 (Daily Mirror) - The Minister of Women and Child Affairs, Saroja Savitri Paulraj, has called for stronger child protection measures following the discovery of a 15-year-old girl with a newborn baby in the Knuckles area after an unsafe delivery.

Describing the incident as extremely shocking, the Minister said she was relieved that both the minor girl and the newborn were now safe.

She said relevant government departments had been instructed to take immediate and continuous action to ensure the physical and mental well-being of both children and safeguard their future.

In a special statement issued on November 14, the Minister said the incident should not be treated as an isolated case, pointing out that economic, social and political challenges were making children increasingly vulnerable in different ways.

She also identified the gradual breakdown of communication and mutual understanding between parents and children as a contributing factor to such incidents.

"This has created a situation where children are becoming insecure even in the environments that should be safest for them," she said.

The Minister urged parents to build an environment in which children have the confidence to believe that they are safe with their parents, regardless of the circumstances.

She said creating a safe family environment and encouraging open communication could help prevent similar traumatic incidents.

Paulraj also said the need for government institutions and other parties dealing with vulnerable children to become more accessible, approachable and trustworthy.

Meanwhile, she said the Ministry was working towards introducing a new Alternative Care Policy from 2027, with budgetary allocations already being made to implement the policy.

She said the proposed policy was intended to provide a formal solution for children who become vulnerable or lack a safe environment.

Highlighting that child protection and the safeguarding of children's rights were the responsibility of the entire society, the Minister urged the public to be more sensitive and attentive to the safety and welfare of children.
