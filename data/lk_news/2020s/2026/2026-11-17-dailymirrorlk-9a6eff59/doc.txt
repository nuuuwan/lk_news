Israel job portal opening on November 20

Colombo, November 17 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment (SLBFE) has warned the public against false social media reports claiming that an online job portal for employment in Israel will open on November 20.

The Bureau said the circulating claims were entirely false and appeared to be an attempt to mislead the public and spread misinformation.

It said recruitment for jobs in Israel is carried out exclusively through an online system, with all relevant information communicated through the Bureau's official social media platforms, website and mass media.

The SLBFE urged the public not to fall victim to such false claims.

It also warned that its investigation division would take action against individuals spreading misinformation and creating unrest in society.
