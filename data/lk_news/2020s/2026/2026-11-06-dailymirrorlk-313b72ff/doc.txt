Gampaha Magistrate who ordered contempt action against IGP transferred

Colombo, November 06 (Daily Mirror)-Gampaha Magistrate Bandula Gunaratne has been transferred to the Judges' Training Institute with immediate effect, following his request to initiate contempt of court proceedings against Inspector General of Police (IGP) over an alleged violation of court orders.

The transfer was ordered by Judicial Service Commission (JSC) Secretary Prasanna Alwis.

The development follows a request made by Magistrate Gunaratne to the President of the Court of Appeal to initiate contempt proceedings against the IGP, alleging that he had failed to comply with a court order and had brought the judiciary into disrepute.

The Magistrate had earlier directed the IGP to issue a circular instructing police officers not to institute frivolous legal proceedings against innocent members of the public.

However, following the alleged failure to comply with the order, the Magistrate sought legal action against the IGP.

Meanwhile, the IGP had lodged a complaint with the JSC against Magistrate Gunaratne, prompting the Commission to call for his observations.

The transfer order was issued shortly after the Magistrate submitted his observations to the Commission.
