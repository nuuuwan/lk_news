Rs. 4 billion allocated for industrial estate development

Colombo, Nov. 12 (Daily Mirror) - The Government has allocated Rs. 4 billion for the development of industrial estates across the country, including the Valachchenai Industrial Estate, while further development work will be carried out at several other industrial zones under the 2027 Budget.

The allocation will also cover feasibility studies and preliminary plans for the proposed Puliyankulam Industrial Estate and infrastructure improvements at existing industrial estates.

Development work will continue at industrial estates in Ingiriya, Dambulla, Raigama-Millaniya and Katunayake.

Meanwhile, Rs. 750 million has been allocated to promote exports, improve competitiveness and support high-value exports. The funds will also be used to organise Sri Lanka Expo 2027 in January and implement priorities identified under the National Export Development Plan.

The Government also plans to conduct a feasibility study to establish a knowledge and experience centre to strengthen links between universities and industries, promote research and development, and support the commercialisation of new products and processes.

In another initiative, production at the Kurunchantivu Salt Pan, where operations have remained suspended, will be resumed using funds from the National Salt Limited. The move is expected to generate employment opportunities in the Northern Province.

The General Treasury has allocated Rs. 236 million for 2026 to construct access roads and provide the necessary infrastructure for the project.

The Government also plans to amend the National Gem and Jewellery Authority Act No. 50 of 1993 to introduce legal provisions required for issuing licences to the jewellery industry.

Meanwhile, steps are being taken to acquire land for an International Gem and Jewellery Trade Hub, which will bring gem and jewellery trading, export facilities, laboratory services and licensing services under one roof. The University of Moratuwa is expected to conduct a preliminary feasibility study for the project.

A further Rs. 1,860 million has been allocated to construct a building in Narahenpita to relocate the Ministry of Industries, currently housed in Kollupitiya, together with all institutions under its purview, to a single location.
