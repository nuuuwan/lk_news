500kg Christmas cake mixture at Gregory Lake

Hotel staff, chefs and tourists join hands to prepare a 500kg Christmas cake mixture at Gregory Lake in Nuwara Eliya on October 23. The initiative was organised by the Araliya Hotel Group, comprising Araliya Green Hills, Araliya Green City and Araliya Red, ahead of the festive season. Tourists will have the opportunity to taste the Christmas cake at midnight on December 24. Pix by Ranjith Rajapaksa
