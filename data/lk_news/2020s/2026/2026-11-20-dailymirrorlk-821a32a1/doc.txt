Indices close in green

The ASPI closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Commercial Bank, Hatton National Bank and Sri Lanka Telecom with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.7 bn.

A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Hatton National Bank, Dialog Axiata and Swisstek.

Mixed interest was observed in Dipped Products, Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka and Sampath Bank whilst retail interest was noted in TAJ Rights, Softlogic Finance and UB Finance Company.

Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net sellers. The Banking sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Hatton National Bank and Sampath Bank) whilst the sector index gained 0.95%.

The share price of Hatton National Bank increased by Rs. 3.75 (0.95%) to close at Rs. 397.00. The share price of Sampath Bank appreciated by Rs. 0.50 (0.33%) to close at Rs. 150.00.

Materials sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Dipped Products) whilst the sector index increased by 0.77%. The share price of Dipped Products moved up by Rs. 0.40 (0.58%) to close at Rs. 69.70.

Dialog Axiata and Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Dialog Axiata closed flat at Rs. 49.00. The share price of Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka recorded a gain of Rs. 3.25 (1.89%) to close at Rs. 175.50.
