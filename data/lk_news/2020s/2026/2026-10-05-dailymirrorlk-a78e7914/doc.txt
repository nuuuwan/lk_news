Sampath Bank-KOKO introduce Merchants Credit Facility

Sampath Bank Chief Business Intelligence Officer Darshin Pathinayake (second from left) exchanges agreement with KOKO Regional CEO Michael Sathasivam (second from right) in the presence of Sampath Bank Deputy General Manager Consumer and SME Banking Asanka Liyanage (first from left) and KOKO Head Dinuki Karunatilake (first from right)

Sampath Bank PLC and KOKO have partnered to introduce the KOKO Merchants Credit Facility, creating a more convenient pathway to business financing for the eligible merchants within KOKO’s growing ecosystem.

The initiative extends KOKO’s role beyond payments and customer financing by connecting the merchants with Sampath Bank’s financing capabilities, helping them access funding to purchase and replenish inventory, manage working capital and cash flow, respond to seasonal demand and invest in business expansion.

The facility is designed around a streamlined assessment process that can leverage relevant information from a merchant’s existing relationship and transaction activities with KOKO, helping reduce the time and effort involved in navigating traditional financing processes.

The partnership reflects a shared commitment to supporting Sri Lankan businesses by combining KOKO’s digital merchant ecosystem with Sampath Bank’s established financial services expertise. The collaboration reinforces the role of both organisations in creating practical opportunities for merchants to strengthen their operations, pursue new opportunities and focus more time on growing their businesses.

Credit facilities remain subject to Sampath Bank’s eligibility criteria, credit assessment, applicable terms and conditions and final approval.
