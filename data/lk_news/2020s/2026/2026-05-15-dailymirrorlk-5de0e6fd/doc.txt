Another faulty financial transaction hits government

Colombo, May 15 (Daily Mirror)- Ports, Civil Aviation and Energy Minister Aruna Karunathilleke confirmed to the Daily Mirror today that another transaction error had occurred in the payment of 74,000 UAE dirhams to a SriLankan Airlines service provider.

The Minister said that the erroneous transaction had occurred in February this year and investigations are now ongoing.
