Casons Taxi rebrands as The Taxi Company, expands service portfolio

Casons Taxi has officially rebranded as The Taxi Company, marking a new growth phase with a refreshed identity and digital upgrades effective January 10, 2026. The company assures customers that the change is purely cosmetic, with rates, billing, and service delivery remaining unchanged.

Founded in 2011 after extensive market research by CEO Zufer Ahamed, the company has grown from a 10-vehicle fleet into a comprehensive mobility solutions provider. While approximately 80% of its operations cater to corporate clients, its services are diverse.

These include corporate staff transport, hotel and airport transfers, scheduled pick-ups, and baggage transport, both locally and internationally via global partners. It also supports the hospitality sector with logistics and food delivery for leading hotels.

Beyond passenger transport, the company provides driver-operated pickup trucks and utility vehicles for sectors like telecom, solar, and construction. It offers relocation services with manpower and truck rentals, and has a dedicated event transportation division for coordinated group movements.

Ahamed emphasized that the rebrand reflects evolution, not disruption. The company is strengthening its digital ecosystem, launching a new website (thetaxi.lk), testing a driver app, and developing a corporate portal and future customer app.

The Taxi Company’s service excellence was recently recognized with the Excellence in Collaboration and Service Award at the Huawei South Asia Supplier Convention 2024.
