Long tenures attract modest buying interest

Market activity remained moderate yesterday, with investor interest evident across the curve.

Modest buying at the long end prompted a downward adjustment of around 10-15bps, while the remainder of the curve remained broadly unchanged.

At the short end of the curve 01.05.2028 and 15.10.2028 traded at 10.10 percent and 10.20 percent, respectively while 15.10.2029 and 15.12.2029 changed hands between 10.55 percent to 10.60 percent.

In the 2030 segment, 01.03.2030, 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 traded within the 10.70 percent-10.80 percent range.

Further along the yield curve 01.02.2031 traded between 10.85 percent to 10.92 percent while 01.07.2032, 01.10.2032 and 15.12.2032 were seen changing hands within the 11.30 percent to 11.45 percent range.

Towards the belly end of the curve 15.01.2033 and 01.11.2033 traded at 11.40 percent and 11.44 percent-11.58 percent, respectively while 15.10.2034 changed hands between 11.72 percent to 11.67 percent.

Finally, 15.06.2036 traded within 12.15 percent to 12.10 percent and 01.07.2037 changed hands from 12.20 percent to 12.15 percent.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee appreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.333.60/US dollar, compared to Rs.334.33/US dollar seen earlier.

Market liquidity in the banking system contracted to Rs.310.69 billion, from Rs.330.91 billion recorded previously.
