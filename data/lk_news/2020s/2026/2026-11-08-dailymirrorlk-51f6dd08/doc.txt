Namal accuses Govt. of raising fuel taxes beyond previous levels

Sri Lanka Podujana Peramuna (SLPP) National Organiser and MP Namal Rajapaksa has accused the Government of increasing fuel taxes beyond previous levels, warning that the burden was ultimately being passed on to the public amid rising living costs.

Speaking during a discussion with a group of MPs in Parliament, Rajapaksa said that although the Government had provided limited relief through fuel price adjustments, increased taxation continued to place pressure on consumers.

He said the Government had promised to supply fuel based on landed import costs and eliminate commissions, while also reducing the existing fuel tax.

“Instead, they have increased fuel taxes beyond what they originally were, leaving the public to suffer the consequences,” he said.

Rajapaksa pointed out that the rising prices of milk powder and other essential food items had further increased the cost of living, while salaries remained unchanged.

He also challenged Government ministers' claims that there were no fuel or gas queues, saying people in both rural and urban areas continued to report shortages.

Questioning the Government's response to the economic difficulties, he asked whether imposing additional taxes and conducting investigations against alleged wrongdoers were its only solutions.

He further accused the Government of attempting to suppress the Opposition through what he described as fabricated evidence, adding that the public was also facing increasing pressure.

Rajapaksa said the Government had failed over the past two-and-a-half years to introduce an effective programme to ease the cost of living, reduce education expenses for schoolchildren and strengthen household economies.

He also claimed that inflation had exceeded 8%, with the financial burden on households continuing to increase.
