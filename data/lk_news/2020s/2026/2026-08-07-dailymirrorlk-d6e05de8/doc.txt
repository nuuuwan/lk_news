Massive fraud involving Aswesuma

Colombo, Aug. 8 (Daily Mirror) - Special Investigations Unit of the Hatton Police Division has initiated investigations of a fraud amounting to Rs.16.2 million of Aswesuma funds in Norwood Divisional Secretariat in Nuwara Eliya.

Two female officers involved in the fraud had been arrested earlier by the Hatton Police and remanded till August 11.

Divisional Secretary Sameera Gamlath on August 06 complained to police that the involvement of the fraud has increased to Rs.16.2 million and three more officers are involved.

The fraud had come to light when Nuwara Eliya District Secretary Thushari Thennakoon initiated a probe into the alleged fraud in response to complaints received from Aswesuma beneficiaries.

A team of audit officers from the District Secretariat and the Ministry of Finance had audited the Aswesuma payments for 2023-2025 to detect the fraud.

They are continuing investigations and recording statements of Grama Niladharies and the officers of the Divisional Secretariat.

Meanwhile, Nuwara Eliya District Parliamentarian and Chairman of the District Coordinating Committee Manjula Suraweera Arachchi said another team of audit officers would be appointed to probe Aswesuma and Samurdhi payments and the payment of Rs.5,000 relief allowance during the Covid-19 pandemic in the ten Divisional Secretariats in the district.
