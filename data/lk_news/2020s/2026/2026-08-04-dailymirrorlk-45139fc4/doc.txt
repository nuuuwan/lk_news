Appeal Court orders resumption of Johnston Fernando corruption trial

The Court of Appeal has overturned a Colombo High Court order that discharged former Minister Johnston Fernando and two former senior officials of the Cooperative Wholesale Establishment (CWE) from a corruption case, directing that the trial be resumed without further delay.

Delivering its judgment, a five-judge bench of the Court of Appeal held that the High Court had erred in upholding a preliminary objection raised by the defence and dismissing the indictment filed by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC).

Accordingly, the Court of Appeal ordered the Colombo High Court to restore the case to its trial roll and proceed with the hearing.

The judgment was delivered by Justice P. Kumararatnam, with Justices Damith Thotawatta, K.M.S. Dissanayake, K.P. Fernando and Annalingam Premashanker concurring.

The case relates to allegations that Johnston Fernando, during his tenure as Minister of Cooperative and Internal Trade, unlawfully deployed employees of the Cooperative Wholesale Establishment for duties unrelated to their official responsibilities, resulting in the State bearing the cost of their salaries and overtime payments.

Former CWE Chairman Eraj Fernando and former Acting Director Mohideen Mohamed Zakeer have been indicted for allegedly aiding and abetting the offence. The three accused also face a conspiracy charge under the Bribery Act.

This relates with a CIABOC investigation launched in 2015 following an anonymous complaint alleging corrupt activities within the CWE during the period from 2010 to 2014.

On May 19, 2023, the Colombo High Court upheld a preliminary objection by the defence, finding that the investigation had allegedly been initiated without a properly constituted Bribery Commission. On that basis, the court ruled the investigation invalid and discharged the accused.
