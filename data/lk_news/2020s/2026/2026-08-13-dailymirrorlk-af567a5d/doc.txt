Education Ministry probes Physics paper mix-up at Ambalantota A/L exam centre

Colombo, Aug. 13 (Daily Mirror) - The Ministry of Education has launched an investigation following the discovery of six packets containing tomorrow’s (14) G.C.E. Advanced Level Physics Paper I at an examination coordination centre in Ambalantota.

The packets were found inside a question paper container designated for subjects scheduled to be held today, including Music, Dancing and Agriculture. The discovery was made when examination officials inspected the containers.

Commissioner General of Examinations Indika Kumari said that the affected Physics question paper packets had not been opened or compromised.

The G.C.E. Advanced Level examinations are currently being conducted at examination centres across the island. Authorities are now conducting an inquiry to determine how the Physics question paper packets came to be placed in the wrong container at the Ambalantota coordination centre.
