Govt. has increased several taxes despite President’s claims, says Harsha de Silva

Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) MP Harsha de Silva has accused the Government of increasing several taxes over the past year, including taxes on software exports and digital services, while also doubling taxes on electric vehicles.

Questioning President Anura Kumara Dissanayake’s recent claim that the Government had not increased any taxes, de Silva said the administration had introduced several new taxes.

“As Chairman of the Committee on Public Finance, I know exactly how many taxes this administration has increased over the past year. They have even introduced entirely new taxes,” he said.

He pointed out that taxes had been imposed on software exports and digital services, while taxes on electric vehicles had been doubled.

He also questioned the information provided to the President regarding the Government's tax policies.

De Silva said the Government now had room to reduce taxes as tax revenue had exceeded expectations, mainly due to taxes collected from vehicle imports.

He said Government revenue had increased to more than Rs. 5 trillion.

However, he questioned the Government's claims about achieving a primary account surplus, saying it was partly due to the failure to spend money allocated for development projects.

Referring to the Prabhashara project in Dambulla, he said that although around Rs. 1.5 trillion had been allocated for capital expenditure this year, only Rs. 300 billion had been spent by June.

He said even if expenditure had increased to Rs. 600 billion, a substantial amount of allocated funds remained unused.

He also cited delays in installing a solar power system, saying that although funds had been allocated, the project had been dragging on for months.

“Taxes are collected not simply to keep money in the Treasury. They are collected to be spent on development and public services,” he said.
