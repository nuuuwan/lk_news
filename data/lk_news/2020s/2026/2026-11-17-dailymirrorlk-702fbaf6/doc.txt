Galadari to unveil Radisson Blu branding on November 22

Galadari Hotels (Lanka) PLC yesterday disclosed its plans to reopen the refurbished Colombo hotel in December, under the Radisson Blu brand, with a brand launch scheduled for November 22.

The property would operate as Radisson Blu Hotel Galadari Colombo, the company said in a filing to the Colombo Stock Exchange.

It said preparations were underway and that it would issue a further disclosure once the opening date was confirmed.
