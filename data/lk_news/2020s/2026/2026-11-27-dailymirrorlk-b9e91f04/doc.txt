JAT launches Fortex Professional and Heatblock20

JAT Holdings PLC expanded its paints portfolio with the launch of JAT Fortex Professional and JAT Heatblock20, at a trade event held recently.

Hosted under the theme ‘Beyond Walls’, the event brought together a select group of the group’s top-performing dealers from its island-wide distribution network.

The launch follows JAT’s efforts to develop a more comprehensive paints portfolio, extending its offering beyond conventional wall paints to serve a wider range of consumer requirements.

The introduction of the Fortex Professional range strengthens the group’s presence in established protective and decorative coating categories, while Heatblock20 brings advanced thermal-control technology to the Sri Lankan market.  Both developments are guided by JAT’s focus on identifying practical customer needs and providing technically sound solutions.

JAT Holdings Director Sales and Marketing Dilshan Rodrigo stated, “Fortex Professional strengthens our portfolio across the surfaces customers need to protect and finish, while Heatblock20 introduces advanced technology to address the growing demand for more functional coating solutions.”

The newly introduced Fortex Professional range comprises four products covering corrosion protection, enamel finishing, roofing and flooring. Fortex Anticorrosive Paint is a protective undercoat formulated to help prevent corrosion on steel and metal surfaces, while Fortex Enamel Paint delivers a durable, high-gloss finish. Fortex Roofing Paint provides enhanced weather protection and surface durability, while Fortex Floor Paint offers strong adhesion and enhanced scrub resistance for cement and concrete surfaces.

The range addresses an important area of portfolio development for JAT, allowing the group to cater to more of the surface protection and finishing requirements associated with residential and commercial properties. For its dealer network, the addition provides greater product coverage across established paint categories.

JAT Holdings Director R&D Ajith Bandara stated, “Heatblock20 works through two heat-shielding mechanisms. Specialised pigments reflect incoming sunlight, including near-infrared energy, while hollow ceramic microspheres within the coating slow the transfer of remaining heat. Together, these help limit heat build-up on exposed surfaces and reduce the amount of heat transferred into a building.”

Bandara was also joined by Director International Sales and Technical Wasantha Gunaratne for an expert discussion at the launch, covering the coating’s application requirements and its potential within the local market.
