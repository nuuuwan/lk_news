WealthTrust’s public float rises to 18.81% after Iconic Trust share sale

WealthTrust Securities PLC’s public float rose to 18.81 percent from 16.36 percent after Iconic Trust (Private) Limited sold 30 million ordinary voting shares on November 18, the company said in a filing to the Colombo Stock Exchange.

The shares were previously excluded from WealthTrust’s public holding. They counted towards the public float after being acquired by investors classified as public shareholders under CSE listing rules.

The 2.45-percentage-point increase from the last reported level on September 30 exceeded the CSE’s two-percentage-point threshold for disclosure. WealthTrust said it remained in compliance with the exchange’s minimum public holding requirement.
