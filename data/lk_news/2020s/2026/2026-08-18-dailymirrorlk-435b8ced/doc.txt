Indices close on a mixed note

The ASPI edged down as a result of price losses in counters such as Richard Pieris & Company, R I L Property and Colombo Dockyard with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.9 Bn. Meanwhile, the S&P SL20 closed in green. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Melstacorp, Aitken Spence and Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka. Mixed interest was observed in Dialog Axiata, Sierra Cables and Sampath Bank whilst retail interest was noted in Browns Investments, UB Finance Company and HNB Finance. Furthermore, foreigners closed as net sellers.

Food, Beverage & Tobacco sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Melstacorp) whilst the sector index lost 0.52%. The share price of Melstacorp increased by Rs 1.25 (0.66%) to close at Rs 192.00.

Capital Goods sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Sierra Cables) whilst the sector index edged up by 0.12%. The share price of Sierra Cables closed flat at Rs 33.00.

Dialog Axiata, Sampath Bank and Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Dialog Axiata gained Rs 0.90 (1.91%) to close at Rs 48.00. The share price of Sampath Bank moved up by Rs 1.75 (1.25%) to close at Rs 141.75. The share price of Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka appreciated by Rs 3.25 (2.03%) to close at Rs 163.50.
