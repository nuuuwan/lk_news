CIC quarterly profit rises 20% as tax charge falls

CIC Holdings PLC’s quarterly profit attributable to the shareholders rose 20.3 percent, as a lower tax bill lifted the earnings, even as the higher costs squeezed the margins and the operating profit declined.

The attributable profit rose to Rs.1.66 billion in the three months ended September 30, from Rs.1.38 billion a year earlier, according to the group’s unaudited interim results.

The earnings per share increased to 87 cents, from 73 cents. The group profit, including the non-controlling interests, rose 14.1 percent to Rs.1.98 billion. Revenue increased 3.4 percent to Rs.20.89 billion but the cost of sales rose 5.2 percent. That pushed the gross profit down 1.2 percent to Rs.5.68 billion and narrowed the gross margin to 27.2 percent, from 28.5 percent.

The higher selling and administrative expenses also weighed on the operating profit, which fell 7.3 percent to Rs.2.66 billion.

The net finance costs climbed 35.4 percent to Rs.712.3 million. A more than twofold increase in CIC’s share of profit from the equity-accounted investees, to Rs.590.5 million, helped offset the higher finance costs and weaker operating result.

The pretax profit was broadly flat at Rs.2.59 billion, while the income tax charge fell 29.4 percent to Rs.606.2 million.

For the six months ended September 30, the attributable profit rose 21.7 percent to Rs.3.17 billion on revenue of Rs.42.91 billion, up 3.1 percent.

The agri solutions, the group’s largest segment by revenue, recorded a 12.4 percent decline in sales and a 27.2 percent drop in segment profit. The segment profit rose 47.4 percent in industrial solutions, 30.9 percent in livestock solutions and 10.0 percent in health and personal care.

As of September 30, CIC’s three largest voting shareholders were Paints & General Industries Ltd with 53.31 percent, the Employees’ Provident Fund with 9.06 percent and Sampath Bank PLC/Chacra Capital Holdings (Private) Ltd with 4.49 percent. The same three led the non-voting register, with the Employees’ Provident Fund holding 12.70 percent, Sampath Bank/Chacra Capital 7.07 percent and Paints & General Industries 3.32 percent.
