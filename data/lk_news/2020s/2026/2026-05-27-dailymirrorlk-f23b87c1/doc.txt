Evolution Auto and EVO Charge launch fast charging station in Peliyagoda

Reinforcing its commitment to advancing electric mobility in Sri Lanka, Evolution Auto, in collaboration with EVO Charge, has officially launched a 60kW dual-gun DC fast charging station at the Evolution Auto After Sales Complex in Peliyagoda.

By introducing high-capacity fast charging to an area that did not previously have such facilities, this strategic initiative represents a significant milestone in the development of the country’s EV infrastructure.

Two vehicles can now be charged at once thanks to the recently installed charger, which greatly improves EV users’ convenience and speeds up turnaround times. The facility is built to accommodate both individual and commercial users, such as ride-hailing drivers and corporate fleets. It runs 24 hours a day in a safe, professionally managed setting.

To mark the launch, Evolution Auto is also offering a special introductory promotion. Evolution Auto customers can enjoy their first charge completely free from 20th May to 21st June, while all other EV owners will receive 50 percent off charging during the same promotional period.

The availability of reliable and easily accessible charging infrastructure continues to be a crucial enabler as EV adoption continues to pick up momentum throughout Sri Lanka. In order to close this gap, Evolution Auto’s most recent installation provides EV users with a useful way to navigate urban mobility, especially in high-traffic areas like Peliyagoda.

Speaking on the launch, Virann De Zoysa, Group CEO at Evolution Auto, stated: “The launch of this 60kW dual-gun fast charging station demonstrates our ongoing dedication to assisting Sri Lanka in its shift to electric mobility. Accelerating EV adoption requires dependable, fast, and accessible charging infrastructure, and Peliyagoda is a key strategic location where this requirement was apparent. With this initiative, we hope to help the country’s EV ecosystem grow while providing our customers more convenience and confidence in their EV ownership experience.”

The Peliyagoda charging station is located inside Evolution Auto’s After Sales Complex, providing plenty of room, accessibility, and security, all of which improve the user experience. EV drivers can depend on reliable charging support at any time of day thanks to its 24/7 availability.
