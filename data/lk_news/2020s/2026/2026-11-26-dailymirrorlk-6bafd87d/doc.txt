Girl dies after falling from moving cab in Norwood

Norwood, Nov 26 (Daily Mirror) - A 13-year-old girl died after falling from the rear of a moving cab in the Venture Upper Division area of Norwood yesterday evening (25).

The incident occurred while the cab was travelling from Norwood towards the Venture 50-Acre area. The girl, a resident of the 50-Acre area, sustained critical injuries in the fall.

She was admitted to the Dickoya Base Hospital but later succumbed to her injuries.

Police have arrested the cab driver in connection with the incident.
