WCIC extends women entrepreneur awards deadline to October 31

The Women’s Chamber of Industry and Commerce (WCIC) has extended the deadline for entries to its Prathibhabhisheka Women Entrepreneur Awards 2026 to October 31, inviting women-owned businesses to apply.

Applications are open under the Start-up, Micro, Small, Medium and Large business categories. The programme recognises business excellence, innovation, resilience and leadership, alongside entrepreneurs’ contributions to economic growth.

Entrants will also be considered for special awards and the Woman Entrepreneur of the Year 2026 title. The programme includes provincial recognition for entrepreneurs across Sri Lanka and an international category for women entrepreneurs from the SAARC region.

The previous edition attracted more than 100 applications from businesses across all nine provinces, according to WCIC. A total of 47 awards were presented across overall, category, provincial and special recognition categories.

Application forms can be downloaded in applicants’ preferred language from the WCIC website or requested from the WCIC office. Completed applications, together with the required supporting documentation, should be submitted by October 31.
