Chennai hosts Port City’s first Indian investor forum

Colombo, Nov. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Over 200 business leaders, investors, policymakers and industry representatives from India and Sri Lanka gathered at the Colombo Port City Investor Forum – Chennai 2026 in Chennai, organised by the Deputy High Commission of Sri Lanka in Chennai in collaboration with Port City Colombo.

Marking Port City Colombo’s first investor forum in India, the event provided a platform to explore investment opportunities and strengthen cross-border business partnerships in real estate, financial services, logistics, IT and technology.

The programme commenced with an address by   Deputy High Commissioner   for Southern India, Dr. Ganesanathan Geathiswaran, who highlighted the longstanding economic and cultural ties between the two countries and the potential to further strengthen bilateral trade, investment and business cooperation.

Delivering the keynote address, the Chairman of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission, Harsha Amarasekera, PC, highlighted Port City Colombo’s investor-friendly statutory framework, regulatory certainty and safeguards, positioning Sri Lanka as a strategic partner for Indian businesses seeking regional expansion.

The Deputy Managing Director of CHEC Port City Colombo (Pvt) Ltd, Thulci Aluwihare, presented the investment opportunities offered by Port City Colombo, highlighting its status as Sri Lanka’s only foreign currency-designated Special Economic Zone, with facilities including a single-window approval mechanism, 100% foreign ownership and transactions in 16 foreign currencies, including the Indian rupee.

A panel discussion featuring the Chief Executive Officer of TVS Vehicle Mobility Solutions, Madhu Raghunath; the Senior Director and Head of Centre of Excellence, Strategic Consulting and Value & Risk Advisory, India and Sri Lanka, JLL, Robin S; the Chairman of the Colombo Port City Economic Commission, Harsha Amarasekera; and the Deputy Managing Director of CHEC Port City Colombo (Pvt) Ltd, Thulci Aluwihare, explored sector-specific investment opportunities, regulatory facilitation and prospects for strengthening India–Sri Lanka business partnerships.

The forum highlighted the potential for Indian businesses to leverage Sri Lanka’s strategic location, investment framework and regional connectivity to expand their operations and access wider markets.

The event was coordinated by the First Secretary (Commercial) of the Deputy High Commission of Sri Lanka in Chennai, A.M. Heshani Kaushalya, under the guidance of the Deputy High Commissioner.
