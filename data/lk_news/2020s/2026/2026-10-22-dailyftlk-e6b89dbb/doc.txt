Chathuranga to attend NDTV World Summit 2026 on Sri Lanka’s economic transformation

Industry and Entrepreneurship Development Deputy Minister Chathuranga Abeysinghe, is scheduled to participate in a dialogue on Sri Lanka’s economic recovery, stability, and future economic trajectory at the NDTV World Summit 2026 on 22 and 23 October in New Delhi.

Organised by NDTV - a leading media network operating in India since 1988 with an international reach in television and digital media-the summit will bring together political leaders, diplomats, policymakers, and business leaders. The theme for this year’s summit is “A World Between Orders.”

The Deputy Minister will participate in a session titled “Sri Lanka’s Economic Reset: Recovery, Resilience and the Road Ahead,” scheduled for 5:00 p.m. on 22 October. During the session, he will share insights on Sri Lanka’s economic recovery and future steps to further strengthen the economy.

Participation in this international summit will serve as a vital opportunity to exchange views with the international community regarding Sri Lanka’s current economic transformation, economic reforms, and future development prospects.

Elected to Parliament in 2024 representing the Colombo District, Abeysinghe was appointed as the Deputy Minister of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development in November 2024. With professional expertise in finance, data analytics, and leveraging technology for business decision-making, he actively works toward the development of industry and entrepreneurship in Sri Lanka.

The NDTV media network also published a special feature article on 19 October 2026, profiling the Deputy Minister ahead of his participation in the summit.
