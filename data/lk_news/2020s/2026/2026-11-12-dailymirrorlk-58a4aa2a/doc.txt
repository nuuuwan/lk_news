Books and printed material to be exempted from VAT under 2027 Budget

Colombo, Nov. 12 (Daily Mirror) - Books and several categories of printed material, including exercise books, dictionaries, encyclopedias and children's picture books, will be exempted from Value Added Tax (VAT) under proposed amendments to the VAT Act, President Anura Kumara Dissanayake announced in the 2027 Budget.

The proposed exemption will apply to both the import and supply of books and printed materials classified under specified Harmonized System (HS) codes used for customs purposes.

The exempted items include exercise books, printed books, dictionaries and encyclopedias, children's picture, drawing and colouring books, printed matter in single sheets, music books and printed music in other forms.

The proposal will be implemented through amendments to the Value Added Tax Act No. 14 of 2002.
