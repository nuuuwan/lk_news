CHEC-Home Lands inaugurate Central Park Boulevard construction at PCC

China Harbour Engineering Company (CHEC), in partnership with Home Lands, has officially inaugurated construction of Central Park Boulevard, a landmark 40-storey twin-tower development at Port City Colombo (PCC).

Held recently, the ceremony followed the successful completion of piling work in just 88 days, significantly ahead of the originally planned six-month schedule.

The inauguration marks another important milestone for CHEC, a globally recognised engineering and construction company that played a pivotal role in developing PCC. Entrusted with the construction of Central Park Boulevard, CHEC brings its international engineering expertise and extensive experience in large-scale infrastructure development to the project, strengthening its continued contribution to Sri Lanka’s evolving urban landscape.

The ceremony was attended by approximately 200 guests, including government representatives, project partners, investors, customers and media. Key dignitaries included Home Lands Chairman Nalin Herath, CHEC South and Mainland Southeast Asia Managing Director Wang Gang and CHEC Port City Colombo (Pvt.) Ltd Managing Director Xiong Hongfeng.

The event also reflected the strong partnership between CHEC and Home Lands, bringing together stakeholders from Sri Lanka and China in support of this landmark development.

Herath said, “The construction inauguration of Central Park Boulevard represents a significant moment for Home Lands and our vision for the future of Sri Lanka’s real estate sector. Partnering with CHEC brings world-class engineering expertise to this landmark development, strengthening our confidence in delivering a project of exceptional quality. Completing the piling work in just 88 days demonstrates the commitment and efficiency of the teams involved. We remain firmly focused on maintaining this momentum and delivering Central Park Boulevard within the targeted 36-month timeframe.”

Gang said, “Central Park Boulevard represents an important continuation of CHEC’s involvement in the development of PCC. Having contributed to the creation of this landmark urban development, we are pleased to bring our engineering expertise to another significant project within it. The completion of piling work in just 88 days reflects the efficiency and technical capabilities of the project team. Working closely with Home Lands, we remain committed to maintaining high standards of construction quality, safety and project delivery.”
