Severe lightning advisory issued for Ampara and Batticaloa

Colombo, Oct. 27 (Daily Mirror) - The Meteorology Department has issued a severe lightning advisory for the Ampara and Batticaloa districts, warning that thundershowers accompanied by severe lightning are likely at several locations.

The department also warned that temporary, localised strong winds may occur during thundershowers and urged the public to take adequate precautions to minimise potential damage caused by lightning.

The public has been advised to seek shelter indoors during thunderstorms and avoid taking shelter under trees.

People have also been urged to stay away from open areas, including paddy fields, tea plantations and open water bodies, during thunderstorms.

The department further advised the public to avoid using wired telephones and electrical appliances connected to power sources during thunderstorms.

The use of open vehicles, including bicycles, tractors and boats, should also be avoided during such conditions.

The public has been warned to remain vigilant for fallen trees and power lines following thunderstorms.

Those requiring emergency assistance have been advised to contact their local disaster management authorities.

The department urged residents in the affected districts to remain cautious and follow safety precautions throughout the period of severe lightning activity.
