Secondary market sees mixed interest amid moderate activity

The secondary market saw mild buying interest yesterday amidst overall mixed sentiment, with the volumes holding firm at moderate levels.

Against this backdrop, the yield curve saw a minor downward push.

Among the maturities traded, the 15.09.2029 maturity traded at 10.75 percent, followed by the 15.05.2030 and 15.10.2030 maturities, which traded at a range of 11.00 percent to 11.05 percent.

The 01.02.2031 maturity traded at 11.10 percent, followed by the 15.12.2032 maturity, which was seen changing hands at 11.45 percent.

Moving on to the 2033 bracket, the 15.01.2033 maturity traded at 11.55 percent and the 01.06.2033 maturity traded at 11.70 percent.

Lastly, within the 2034 category, the maturities dated 15.06.2034 and 15.10.2034 traded from 11.80 percent to 11.85 percent.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee depreciated against the US dollar, standing at Rs.332.62/US dollar, compared to Rs.332.50/US dollar seen previously. Liquidity in the banking system contracted to Rs.464.15 billion, from Rs.475.40 billion seen previously.
