Grade five scholarship exam re-scrutiny results released

Colombo, Oct 17 (Daily Mirror)- The Department of Examinations has released the re-scrutiny results of the Grade Five Scholarship Examination today (17).

According to the department, candidates can access their revised results by visiting the official website of the Sri Lanka Department of Examinations, www.doenets.lk, or www.results.exams.gov.lk, and entering their examination index numbers.

The department said candidates who require further information or have inquiries regarding the results can contact the School Examinations Organisation and Results Branch on 011 278 4208, 011 278 4537 or 011 278 5922.
