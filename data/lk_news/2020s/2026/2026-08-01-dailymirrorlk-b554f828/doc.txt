Man injured in Rathmalana shooting

Colombo, August 1 (Daily Mirror) - A man was injured in a shooting incident reported on Kandawala Road in Rathmalana this afternoon.

According to the police, two unidentified gunmen who arrived on a motorcycle opened fire at an individual inside a three-wheeler before fleeing the scene.

The injured victim was admitted to the Colombo South Teaching Hospital in Kalubowila for treatment.
