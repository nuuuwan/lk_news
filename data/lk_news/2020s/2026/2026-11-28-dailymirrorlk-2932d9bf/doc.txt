Speaker appoints special select committee to probe July 1983 riots

Speaker Jagath Wickramaratne appointed a special select committee headed by Minister Saroja Paulraj to probe the July 1983 riots and to look into ways and means to ensure communal harmony.

Others in the committee include Minister Harshana Nanayakkara, Eranga Gunasekara, Mano Ganesan, A. Adeikalanathan, G. Srinesan, Thanura Dissanayake, Suranga Ratnayake, Hiruni Wijesinghe, Muneer Mulaffer and Krishnan Kalaichelvi.
