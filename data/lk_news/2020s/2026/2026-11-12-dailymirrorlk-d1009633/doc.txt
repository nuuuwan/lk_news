13-hour water cut in several Gampaha and Kelaniya areas on Saturday

Colombo, Nov. 12 (Daily Mirror) - Water supply to several areas in the Gampaha and Kelaniya districts will be suspended for 13 hours and 30 minutes on Saturday (November 14) due to essential maintenance work, the National Water Supply and Drainage Board (NWSDB) announced.

The water cut will be in effect from 8.30 a.m. to 10 p.m. due to maintenance work on the power system supplying the Biyagama Water Treatment Plant.

The affected areas include Peliyagoda, Wattala-Mabola, Ja-Ela and Katunayake-Seeduwa Urban Council areas.

Water supply will also be interrupted in the Kelaniya, Wattala, Biyagama, Mahara, Dompe and Ja-Ela Pradeshiya Sabha areas, as well as parts of the Katana, Minuwangoda and Gampaha Pradeshiya Sabha areas.

The NWSDB has advised consumers in the affected areas to use stored water sparingly during the interruption.

Consumers can contact the Board's hotline on 1939 for further information.
