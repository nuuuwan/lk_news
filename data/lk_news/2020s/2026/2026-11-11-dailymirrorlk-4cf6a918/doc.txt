Public warned against travelling abroad for employment on visit visas

Colombo, Nov. 11 (Daily Mirror) - The Ministry of Foreign Employment and Tourism has warned the public against travelling overseas for work on visit visas, noting that a large number of Sri Lankan nationals are currently facing severe hardships in countries such as Malaysia, Dubai, and Oman after falling prey to false promises of employment.

In an official advisory, the Ministry urged prospective migrant workers to seek foreign employment strictly through proper legal procedures to avoid putting their lives and futures at risk.

Authorities highlighted that working while on a visit visa violates local laws in host countries, exposing individuals to serious legal trouble, arrest, detention, and deportation.

Furthermore, those who travel through unauthorized channels risk failing to receive their promised jobs, having their wages withheld, or being paid significantly less than originally agreed upon.

The Ministry also cautioned that individuals using visit visas for work are highly vulnerable to fraud and human trafficking, which can lead to the loss of their passports, severe financial loss, and acute mental distress.

Job seekers are specifically advised not to be misled by deceptive promises such as “Go on a Visit Visa and we will change it to a Work Visa.” or “You can find a job after you go there.”

To ensure their safety and legal protection, the Ministry strongly advises all citizens travelling abroad for employment to register with the Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment (SLBFE) and depart strictly through established legal channels.
