Paytm Founder Sharma receives award at FinTech Festival

Vijay Shekhar Sharma, Founder and CEO of Paytm, one of India’s digital payments and financial technology companies, received the Excellence in AI-Powered Financial Inclusion Award 2025 at the India Pavilion during the Singapore FinTech Festival (SFF) 2026.

The award was presented on 19 November at Singapore EXPO by Rajkumar Kanagasingam, Convener of the Asian Digital Finance Forum & Awards, who also serves as Founding President of the Fintech Association of Sri Lanka (FASL) and Industry Fellow at the Global FinTech Institute (GFI), Singapore.

The recognition was originally announced at the Asian Digital Finance Forum & Awards 2025, held on 23 October 2025 at Port City Colombo, Sri Lanka. The award recognised Paytm’s contribution to expanding digital payments, supporting merchants and facilitating access to financial services through technology-driven innovation.
