Opposition leaders meet Amarapura Mahanayake

The Mahanayake of the Amarapura Chapter, Ven. Karagoda Uyangoda Maithri Murthi Thero, was visited by leaders of the People's United Opposition, including former ministers G.L. Peiris, Vajira Abeywardena, Nimal Siripala de Silva, Sagala Ratnayake and Lasantha Alagiyawanna, at Devuram Vehera in Pannipitiya today. Pix by Pradeep Pathirana
