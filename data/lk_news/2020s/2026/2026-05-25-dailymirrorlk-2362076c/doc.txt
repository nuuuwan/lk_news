Jabra-ZILLIONe introduce Evolve3 headset series and PanaCast video solutions

Jabra, a global leader in professional audio and video solutions, announced an expanded portfolio of intelligent collaboration solutions designed to support the evolving needs of hybrid work environments.

The announcement includes the new Jabra Evolve3 headset series, Jabra PanaCast Room Kits video solution and the new Jabra PanaCast U30 video bar, bringing together AI-driven personal audio, intelligent video for large meeting rooms and simplified deployment for small rooms to create more inclusive and productive meeting experiences across personal calls and meeting workspaces.

From individual focus and mobility to scalable room-based video collaboration, Jabra’s latest solutions are engineered to support clear communication, professional-grade performance and seamless deployment to create value in organisations.

Available through ZILLIONe, the sole authorised distributor for Jabra in Sri Lanka and the Maldives, the new portfolio further expands access to enterprise-grade collaboration solutions for organisations across the region.

The Jabra Evolve3 series is the company’s most advanced professional headset portfolio, purpose-built for hybrid professionals who move between offices, home and on-the-go environments. Available in over-the-ear (Jabra Evolve3 85) and on-the-ear (Jabra Evolve3 75) variants, the series introduces a discreet boomless design powered by next-generation audio intelligence and a design you want to wear.

Powered by Jabra ClearVoice and deep neural network technology trained on more than 60 million real-world sentences, the Evolve3 series isolates the user’s voice from surrounding noise without the need for a visible boom arm. Adaptive Active Noise Cancellation adjusts in real time to the environment and headset fits, helping reduce distractions during calls and while listening.

Jabra also introduced the new Jabra PanaCast U30, a USB video bar built for bring-your-own-device (BYOD) collaboration in small meeting spaces and huddle rooms for up to six people. Combining intelligent video technology, professional audio performance and simplified deployment in a compact device, PanaCast U30 provides an easy and affordable way to equip more small rooms for high-quality hybrid meetings.

Designed for a simple plug-and-play BYOD experience, the users can walk into a room, connect their own device with a single USB-C cable and start meetings instantly using platforms such as Microsoft Teams and Zoom. The system also includes on-screen guidance to help users quickly begin meetings without additional support.

PanaCast U30 delivers a 120-degree field of view optimised for small spaces, ensuring participants remain visible even in compact room layouts. Intelligent Zoom, Virtual Director and Dynamic Composition automatically adjust the framing as conversations evolve, while a built-in speaker and six microphones deliver full-duplex, room-filling audio.

Complementing PanaCast U30, the Jabra PanaCast 55 VBS and Jabra PanaCast Room Kits extend intelligent collaboration into mid-sized and large meeting rooms. Designed for scalable deployment and flexible configurations, the solutions combine panoramic video technology, extension audio support and intelligent multi-camera capabilities to deliver a consistent and immersive collaboration experience across different room sizes and layouts. The portfolio includes the Jabra PanaCast 55 VBS video bar, the Jabra PanaCast SpeakerMic extension microphone and Room Kit configurations supporting one, three or five-camera setups powered by intelligent speaker tracking.

Built around Jabra’s panoramic video technology and powered by multi-camera intelligence, the solution dynamically frames speakers and captures the full room for a more immersive front-row meeting experience. Integrated audio and extension microphone support ensure remote participants clearly hear discussions across seating positions.

“From individual workspaces to full meeting rooms, organisations across APAC need collaboration technology that is reliable, adaptable and built to scale,” said Jabra Vice President and Managing Director South Asia and Channels APAC Peter Jayaseelan.

“With the Evolve3 headset series, as well as the modern video solutions PanaCast Room Kits and PanaCast U30, Jabra is delivering an integrated ecosystem designed to simplify hybrid collaboration while maintaining professional-grade audio and video experiences.”

Products supplied through ZILLIONe are backed by a two-year warranty, verified authenticity through direct sourcing from Jabra and access to authorised after-sales service support to ensure continued product reliability and assistance.
