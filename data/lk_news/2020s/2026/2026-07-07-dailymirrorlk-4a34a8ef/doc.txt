Women’s T20 World Cup 2026 Rewrites Record Books

July.07 (Mirror Sports) - Australia’s seventh ICC Women’s T20 World Cup title capped a record-breaking 2026 tournament, with players and teams setting new milestones throughout the biggest edition in the event’s history.

Featuring 12 teams for the first time, the tournament saw several records rewritten both on and off the field. Australia defeated hosts England in front of a record crowd at Lord’s to extend their dominance with a seventh world title, while the competition established new benchmarks across multiple categories, making the 10th edition one of the most memorable in Women’s T20 World Cup history.
