Mirror Missions: Can You Stop the Next Dengue Surge in Sri Lanka?

How would you respond if dengue cases began rising in your district?

Mirror Missions: The Dengue Response Challenge is an interactive Daily Mirror experience that puts you in charge of a simulated community response in Sri Lanka.

Across six decisions, you will have to balance dengue prevention, public trust, limited resources, medical warning signs, and misinformation.

Every choice has a consequence. Your decisions will affect projected dengue cases, mosquito breeding risk, community confidence, and the resources available to your response team. There are no obvious answers, and the final result will reveal what you handled well and what you could have done differently.

Dengue prevention does not depend on one decision or one organisation. It requires early action, accurate information, responsible medical guidance and continued participation from households, schools, workplaces and local authorities.

Your Mirror Mission result represents a simulated scenario, but the prevention lessons are real.

Removing standing water, recognising dengue warning signs, challenging dangerous misinformation and seeking qualified medical care at the right time can help protect individuals and communities.

Learn more through Daily Mirror's Sri Lanka dengue live tracker and its guide to dengue symptoms, warning signs and prevention. You can also replay the challenge to explore how different decisions could produce a different outcome.
