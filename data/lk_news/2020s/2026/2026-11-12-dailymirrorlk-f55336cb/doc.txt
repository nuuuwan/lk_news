Budget 2027: Salary increases and allowance hikes for public sector employees

Nov 12 (Daily Mirror) - The government has proposed a series of salary and allowance increases for public sector employees under Budget 2027, extending financial relief to several categories of government workers and pensioners.

President Anura Kumara Dissanayake announced that Rs. 110 billion has been allocated to implement the final phase of the public sector salary revision introduced in 2025.

Doctors: DAT allowance to increase from Rs. 70,000 to Rs. 95,000.

Nurses and paramedical staff: Special duty allowance to double from Rs. 6,000 to Rs. 12,000.

University academics: Monthly academic allowance to increase by Rs. 25,000.

Police officers: An additional monthly allowance of Rs. 10,000 for food and accommodation.

Prison officers: Risk allowance to double from Rs. 8,000 to Rs. 16,000.

Grama Niladharis: Travelling allowance to increase from Rs. 1,600 to Rs. 4,000.

Executive officers: Eligible transport allowance to increase from Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 100,000.

Pensioners: An additional interim allowance of Rs. 2,500 per month for those who retired between 2020 and 2024.

Most of these allowance increases are scheduled to take effect in April or July 2027. However, some increases will be introduced in two phases, with the remaining payments scheduled for 2028.
