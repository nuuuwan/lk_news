Continuing investor optimism over Q3 corporate results helps retain CSE’s glow

Both CSE indices moved upwards yesterday; the All Share Price Index went up by 7.45 positive points while the S and P SL20 rose by 3.04 points amid continuing investor optimism over favourable Q3 corporate results.

Turnover stood at Rs 8.32 billion with 17 crossings. Top seven crossings that mainly contributed to the turnover were: Renuka Foods 10 million shares crossed to the tune of Rs 700 million; its shares sold at Rs 70, Laugfs Gas 6.35 million shares crossed for Rs 476 million; its shares traded at Rs 75, Pan Asia Bank two million shares crossed for Rs 129 million; its shares traded at Rs 61.50, CT Holdings 155,000 shares crossed for Rs 101 million; its shares fetched Rs 650.

HNB 200,000 shares crossed to the tune of Rs 86 million; its shares traded at Rs 430, Sanasa Developments Bank 1.2 million shares crossed for Rs 84 million; its shares traded at Rs. 70 and Royal Ceramics 1.5 million shares crossed to the tune of Rs 74.4 million; its shares traded at Rs 49.20.

In the retail market top seven companies that mainly contributed to the turnover were; Colombo Dockyard Rs 762 million (5.4 million shares traded), HNB Rs 463 million (1.1 million shares traded), Renuka Agri Rs 389 million (24.6 million shares traded), United Motors Rs 358 million (9.1 million shares traded), Hayleys Rs 295 million (1.3 million shares traded), Laugfs Gas Rs 241 million (3.2 million shares traded) and Sierra Cables Rs 233 million (6.3 million shares traded). During the day 187 million share volumes changed hands in 47269 transactions.

It is said that banking sector counters, especially HNB, performed well while the agriculture sector also performed well, especially Renuka agri.

Yesterday the rupee was quoted at Rs 309.70/85 to the US dollar in the spot market, weaker from Rs 309.72/80 the previous day, having depreciated in recent weeks, dealers said, while bond yields dropped on shorter tenors.
