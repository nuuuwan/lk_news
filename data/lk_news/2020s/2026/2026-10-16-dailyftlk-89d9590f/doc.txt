Zydus-Sunshine JV  to invest over $ 20 m  in Horana pharma plant

Commercial operations targeted for 2028, subject to project timelines and regulatory approvals

Initial production to focus on oral solid dosage medicines for domestic market, supporting local manufacturing and supply-chain resilience

Over 80% of workforce is expected to comprise Sri Lankan nationals, with specialised training in pharmaceutical production and quality assurance

Indian life sciences group Zydus Lifesciences Ltd., and Sri Lanka’s Sunshine Holdings PLC are partnering to invest over $ 20 million in a pharmaceutical manufacturing facility in Horana, in a move aimed at expanding local medicine production and reducing reliance on imports.

The proposed joint venture, Zydus Sunshine Lifesciences Ltd., signed an agreement with the Board of Investment of Sri Lanka (BOI) to establish the facility within the BOI zone in Horana. The project will be developed on a site of nearly four acres, with commercial operations targeted for 2028, subject to project timelines and regulatory approvals.

The investment brings together Zydus’ international pharmaceutical manufacturing expertise and Sunshine Healthcare’s established presence in Sri Lanka’s healthcare market, with the initial phase focused on producing oral solid dosage medicines for domestic consumption.

Local production is expected to strengthen the country’s pharmaceutical manufacturing base, improve medicine supply-chain resilience and support access to quality medicines. The investment also comes amid continued efforts to reduce Sri Lanka’s dependence on imported pharmaceuticals and build domestic capacity in essential healthcare products.

Beyond manufacturing capacity, the venture is expected to facilitate technology transfer and develop local expertise in pharmaceutical production, quality assurance, and scientific and technical functions.

More than 80% of the facility’s workforce is expected to comprise Sri Lankan nationals, with specialised training and capability development planned to build skills in the high-value pharmaceutical industry.

Zydus Lifesciences operates across pharmaceuticals and consumer wellness, with an emerging medical technology business and an international presence spanning the United States, India and other markets. The group employs around 30,000 people worldwide, including 1,500 scientists engaged in research and development.

Sunshine Healthcare, the healthcare arm of Sunshine Holdings PLC, has operations spanning pharmaceuticals, medical devices, retail, manufacturing, research and development, and distribution. Its local market presence is expected to support the joint venture’s access to Sri Lanka’s healthcare sector.

The Horana facility will add to the BOI’s portfolio of manufacturing investments, with the venture seeking to combine international expertise with domestic production and workforce development.

The project’s contribution to import substitution and medicine security will depend on its eventual production capacity, product range and ability to meet domestic demand once commercial operations begin.
