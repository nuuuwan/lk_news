Residents demand probe into newly opened Chabad house in Hiriketiya

Villagers and Buddhist monks in Hiriketiya have staged a protest calling for the removal of a recently established Chabad House in the popular southern coastal area.

The protest was held about a month after the Chabad House was opened in Hiriketiya.

Protesters alleged that the facility had been established without obtaining the necessary approvals and claimed that its presence was affecting harmony within the local community.

A group of Buddhist monks and residents who participated in the protest called on the relevant authorities to investigate whether the establishment was operating in accordance with the required legal and regulatory approvals.

They further urged authorities to take appropriate action, including its removal, if it is found to have been established or operated without the necessary approvals.
