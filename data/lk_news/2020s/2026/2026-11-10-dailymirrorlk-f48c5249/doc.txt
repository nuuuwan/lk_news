Secondary market opens week on mixed sentiment

The secondary market concluded a mixed session amid moderate trading volumes and relatively subdued activity, whilst the yield curve remained broadly stable.

Despite some notable buying interest across selected maturities, the overall mixed sentiment and limited market activity kept yields largely unchanged.

Amongst the trades that were seen, starting at the short end, the 15.09.2029 maturity traded at 10.90 percent. This was followed by the 2030 segment, within which the 01.03.2030, 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 maturities traded from 11.10 percent to 11.15 percent.

Moving along the curve, the 01.12.2031 bond traded at 11.30 percent, the 15.12.2032 bond traded at 11.68 percent and within the 2033 segment, the 01.06.2033 and 01.11.2033 bonds changed hands between 11.89 percent to 11.95 percent. The 15.10.2034 maturity traded at 12.10 percent and lastly, the 15.08.2036 bond traded at 12.45 percent.

On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee depreciated against the greenback, standing at Rs.332.56/US dollar, compared to Rs.332.41/US dollar recorded earlier. Market liquidity in the banking system expanded to Rs.429.10 billion compared to Rs.422.29 billion seen previously.
