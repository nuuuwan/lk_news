Community clean-up programme in Gampaha

The “Aththama” community clean-up programme was held recently in Malwathuhiripitiya, Gampaha, focusing on managing improper waste disposal, cleaning roads and public places, maintaining recreational and fitness areas, and conducting household inspections as part of dengue prevention efforts. The programme was organised with the participation of relevant Pradeshiya Sabha members, Praja Shakthi representatives, Community Development Committee members and local residents. Many state officials, including Piyal Gunathilake, Officer-in-Charge of the Malwathuhiripitiya Police Station, were also present. Pix by Kushan Pathiraja
