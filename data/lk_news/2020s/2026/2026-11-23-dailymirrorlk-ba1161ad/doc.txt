ComBank cards bring      festive savings and reward

The festive season is here,and Commercial Bank of Ceylon PLC is giving its credit and debit cardholders plenty of reasons to celebrate, with discounts of up to 60 percent at more than 680 merchant outlets island-wide under its latest seasonal card promotion, themed ‘BetterWatch Out –You Might Just Buy It All’.

Running from 15th November to 31st December 2026, the campaign brings together an extensive range of offers across more than 15 categories, covering virtually every aspect of the festive experience, from dining and food delivery to travel, leisure, entertainment, electronics, fashion and shopping. The idea is simple: whether customers are planning a festive get-together, a holiday, a shopping spree or a special treat for themselves and their loved ones, ComBank Cards makethe season more rewarding while keeping spending in check.From choosing gifts for family and planning getaways with friends, to getting the home ready for celebrations, cardholders will find offers to suit every festive occasion. Commenting on the campaign, Seevali Wickramasinghe,  Chief Manager of the Card Centre at Commercial Bank of Ceylon PLC, said, “At the heart of the ComBank Cards Seasonal Campaign is a deep appreciation of our customers. We understand the sentiments of our valued cardholders, which is why we have designed a promotional lineup that truly rewards their loyalty. By delivering highly relevant, lifestyle-focused offers, we are not merely facilitating transactions,we are actively contributing to the joy, ease, and prosperity of their seasonal celebrations.”
