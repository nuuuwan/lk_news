HNB FINANCE takes financial inclusion drive to 26 towns

Glimpses of HNB FINANCE’s ‘Diyunuwata Maga – Nagara Charika’ roadshow

HNB FINANCE PLC launched a nationwide roadshow titled ‘HNB FINANCE Diyunuwata Maga – Nagara Charika’ (Road to Progress – Town Tour) to bring financial services closer to communities, strengthen customer engagement and promote financial inclusion across Sri Lanka.

The initiative covered 26 towns over 13 days, providing residents and businesses with opportunities to interact directly with HNB FINANCE representatives and learn about financial products and services suited to their needs.

The roadshow formed part of HNB FINANCE’s efforts to improve financial accessibility by extending customer engagement beyond its branch network. Company representatives visited town centres and other public locations to provide information on available financial solutions, respond to customer inquiries and better understand the financial requirements of local communities.

HNB FINANCE Chief Manager Corporate Marketing Communications and Digital Business Madhura Wijeratne said, “At HNB FINANCE, we believe financial services should be easily accessible to everyone.”
