Woman jailed for 14 years for mariticide

Colombo, Aug. 5 (Daily Mirror) - A woman has been sentenced to 14 years' rigorous imprisonment after being found guilty of culpable homicide not amounting to murder over the killing of her husband in Rideemaliyedda in 2012.

The Badulla High Court, presided over by Judge Jeyaram Trosky, convicted Thisswalangu Mudiyanselage Niluka Damayanthi, who had originally been indicted on a charge of murder, of the lesser offence of culpable homicide not amounting to murder.

The court heard that on August 25, 2012, Damayanthi, who was 24 years old at the time, hacked her husband, Herath Mudiyanselage Premaratne, 26, to death with an axe.

In addition to the prison sentence, the court imposed a fine of Rs. 25,000 and ordered the accused to pay Rs. 1 million in compensation to the deceased's parents.

At the time of the incident, the accused was employed as a garment factory worker, while her husband was a manual labourer. The couple had a four-year-old daughter.

During the trial it transpired that that her husband often harrassed her under the influence of liqour and inveigled her to abnormal sexual acts.

On the day of the incident she had assaulted him with an axe when he was fast asleep, causing his instant death and hid the body in a nearby brick kiln.

The following morning she left home for employment as usual but her mother in law who recovered the body had informed Rideemaliyedda police.

State Counsel Dinithi Amarasiri prosecued. Rideemaliyedda police gave evidence for the prosecution.
