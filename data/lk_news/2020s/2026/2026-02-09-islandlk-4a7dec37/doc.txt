Excitement awaits fans in Australia and New Zealand

Last Tuesday, 03rd January, I had a morning call from Piyal Perera, of the Gypsies, thanking The Island newspaper for the article ‘Gypsies … flying high.’

What surprised him, he said, was that even before he could read the Gypsies article, in the newspaper, some of his fans had sent the link and the story to him, via social media!

Referring to the Gypsies trip to Australia and New Zealand, he said of the four shows, in Australia, three venues are already sold out, Perth, Melbourne and Adelaide, and Sydney, too, would be on the sold out list before long, he added.

In New Zealand, they will be performing in Auckland, Wellington and Christchurch.

“It will be our first ever performance in Christchurch and we are looking forward to it with excitement and great expectation.

“In fact, we are certain this tour to Australia and New Zealand will be extra special for us and we plan to create all the excitement on stage for our numerous fans who are eagerly awaiting our arrival.”
