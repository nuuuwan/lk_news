Three special flights bring nearly 550 Polish tourists to Sri Lanka

Colombo, Nov 06 (Daily Mirror) - Nearly 550 tourists from Poland arrived in Sri Lanka aboard three special charter flights operated by Polish airline Enter Air at the Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) in Katunayake this morning (6), marking the start of the 2026/2027 European winter tourist season.

The three Boeing 737 aircraft arrived from Katowice, Poznań and Warsaw, with the flights landing at 6.51 a.m., 7.40 a.m. and 9.40 a.m., respectively.

A special welcome ceremony featuring traditional Sri Lankan cultural performances was organised at the airport to greet the visiting tourists.

According to the Ministry of Ports and Civil Aviation, the special flight operations will continue until April 2, 2027, with flights from the three Polish cities scheduled to arrive every 10 days.

A total of 42 flight rotations are planned during the period.

Poland has remained an important European tourism market for Sri Lanka, with visitors attracted by the country's beaches, historical sites, wildlife parks, hill country and tea plantations.

Destinations such as Sigiriya, Dambulla, Kandy, Nuwara Eliya and Yala are among the popular attractions for Polish tourists.

The November-to-April period is a key tourism season for Sri Lanka, coinciding with winter in Europe, and the special charter services are expected to further increase arrivals from European markets.

The additional tourist arrivals are also expected to benefit the hotel industry, transport services, tour guides, restaurants and small-scale tourism businesses while contributing to foreign exchange earnings.

Charter flights between Poland and Sri Lanka during the European winter season have been operating for nearly 15 years, and the 42 rotations planned for the upcoming season are expected to further strengthen tourism links between the two countries, the Ministry said.
