Sri Lanka’s first automated book vending machine launched

The Book Studio Pvt Ltd has introduced Sri Lanka’s first fully automated book vending machine, a pioneering move that blends technology with reading access.

The self-service unit allows readers to instantly purchase books at high-traffic locations, transforming how literature is discovered and bought. The initiative aligns with the company’s belief that “everywhere can be a reading space,” targeting busy modern lifestyles.

Mr. Kushan Gunasekera, CEO & Director of The Book Studio, called the launch a milestone in the company’s journey toward a more well-read Sri Lanka. “By leveraging automated technology, we are making the act of discovering a new book as seamless and effortless as possible,” he said.

Two new flagship outlets are set to open in the Southern and Northern regions with contemporary design and immersive customer experiences.
