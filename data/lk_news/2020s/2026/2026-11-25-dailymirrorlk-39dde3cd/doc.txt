ComBank rewards Transact More & Win Big campaign winners

From left: Commercial Bank Digital Banking Unit senior management representatives present an iPhone Duo, the first monthly grand prize in Transact More & Win Big campaign, to the winner and her family

iPhone 18, Galaxy Z Fold8 up for grabs at upcoming monthly draws

Commercial Bank of Ceylon PLC is accelerating its ‘Digital by Default’ strategy with a high-value tech giveaway this festive season.

The first batch of winners in the bank’s year-end digital banking promotion has received an exciting array of prizes, highlighted by the selection of the grand winner of the Apple iPhone Duo, a first-of-its-kind giveaway by a bank in Sri Lanka, as the bank steps up its efforts to encourage the customers to make greater use of digital payments. Launched on October 1, under the theme ‘Transact More and Win Big’, this three-month promotion is designed to reward the existing users of ComBank Digital for making more frequent transactions through the bank’s digital banking platform.

The first monthly draw, covering October, rewarded 20 lucky customers with an array of premium tech and lifestyle prizes, including Apple iPhone Duo, JBL Go 5 ultra-portable speakers, Dialog Air Fiber connections and Dialog TV connections.

The excitement continues with two monthly draws remaining, offering the ComBank Digital users further chances to win attractive tech prizes.  The customers continuing to transact through the Digital Banking platform will be eligible for the November draw featuring a grand prize of a Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 and the ultimate December draw, which will give away a brand-new iPhone 18.

Open to the existing ComBank Digital customers, the promotion rewards the customers who successfully complete a minimum of five eligible transactions during each promotional month.

Every qualifying transaction must have a minimum value of Rs.500 and the eligible transactions include fund transfers to Commercial Bank and other banks as well as bill payments.  Twenty winners are selected each month via a raffle draw from the pool of qualifying customers. By consistently maintaining the required transaction frequency, the customers can secure their entry into every monthly draw until the promotion concludes on December 31, 2026.
