Toastmasters unite for Conference of Transformation, Inspiration and Progress

Ovation 2026 – Annual Conference of District 82 Toastmasters International

District 82 Toastmasters International concluded its flagship annual conference, Ovation 2026, on 16th and 17 May at Shangri-La Colombo.

Themed “Tides of Transformation,” the two-day event brought together communicators, leaders, professionals, entrepreneurs, educators, and change-makers from across Sri Lanka and the wider region, marking what many attendees described as one of the most energising gatherings the district has seen in recent years.

Recognised as one of the highest-performing Toastmasters districts globally, District 82 represents Sri Lanka, the Maldives, and the British Indian Ocean Territory. Ovation 2026, chaired by DTM Mario de Silva, served as the district’s premier platform for celebrating excellence in communication, personal growth, and leadership.

The conference opened with a keynote from of Rotary International Past President K.R. Ravindran, who spoke on character-driven leadership and the importance of integrity in today’s world. The program continued with impactful sessions from Rasini Bandara on resilience and mental strength, and Michelle de Silva on authenticity and purposeful leadership. A panel discussion titled “The Human Touch in a Digital Age,” featuring Sanali Kaushalya, Mevan Peiris, and Sanjaya Elvitigala, moderated by DTM Gayathri Liyanage, explored what it means to lead with empathy in an increasingly technology-driven world.

Day Two brought a much-anticipated conversation with iconic Sri Lankan music duo Bathiya and Santhush, moderated by DTM Kalpana de Silva, on personal branding, reinvention, and staying relevant in a rapidly changing world. The conference closed on a high note with a keynote by Stephanie Siriwardhana titled “Every Tide – A Chance to Rise,” encouraging participants to embrace transformation and opportunity in both their personal and professional lives.

One of the most anticipated segments of Ovation 2026 was the District 82 International Speech Contest and Table Topics Contest Finals, showcasing the finest speakers in the district. In the Table Topics Contest — which tests the ability to deliver structured, impromptu responses — Dinal Edirisinghe took first place, followed by Farhaz Farouk in second and Rishyenthan Dheenabandth in third. In the International Speech Contest, Ravi Uduwela claimed the top prize, with Sanara Premaratne finishing second and Ashinsanie Weerasinghe third. Ravi Uduwela now advances to the Regional Quarterfinals and gets one step closer to the stage of the World Championship of Public Speaking.

The conference also marked the installation of the incoming District 82 leadership team for the 2026/27 Toastmasters year. District Director Bhathiya Hettiarachchi, DTM; Program Quality Director Piyakara Jayaratne, DTM; and Club Growth Director Hingston Lovell, DTM were formally installed in a ceremony conducted by Past International President Balraj Arunasalam. Throughout the event, outstanding clubs, leaders, and members were recognised for their contributions to educational excellence, mentorship, and service to the Toastmasters movement.

Addressing the gathering, outgoing District Director Niroshan Nadarajah, DTM, reflected on a year defined by collective effort and purpose. “Transformation does not happen in isolation. It happens when people choose to show up, to learn, and to lift each other. That is what District 82 has always stood for, and what Ovation 2026 has once again demonstrated beautifully,” he said.

The conference concluded with fellowship and networking, reinforcing Toastmasters International’s enduring mission of building confident communicators and effective leaders, and the lasting impact the movement continues to create across communities and industries in Sri Lanka and beyond.

Toastmasters is a global nonprofit that helps individuals improve their public speaking, communication, and leadership skills through structured learning pathways, speaking opportunities, and constructive feedback. Anyone interested in joining can find a local club through the official Toastmasters International website. New members are always welcome.

The conference was powered by Home Lands, with support from a strong lineup of corporate partners including Janatha Steels, Nestlé, Maliban Biscuit Manufactories, A J Medichem International, New Anthoney’s Farms, Jayes Investment, and Zorro Tapes.
