Public Service Engineers strike today over Budget 2027 snub

Public service engineers across the country will stage a symbolic one-day strike today (November 13) in protest against the failure to address their demands in the 2027 Budget, the Public Service Engineers Association said.

The association said the strike was launched after the government failed to reinstate the Field Attendance and Transport (FAT) Allowance, which had been granted to officers of the Sri Lanka Engineering Service since 2012 but was reduced in 2024.

It said the government had previously promised to restore the allowance through the 2027 Budget proposals.

However, the association expressed disappointment that the Budget had completely overlooked public service engineering officers, despite plans to implement several major development projects next year.

The association said its Executive Committee, at a meeting held on November 12, decided to launch an islandwide token strike by reporting sick leave today as a mark of protest.

It warned that the symbolic strike was only an initial step and that more stringent and continuous trade union action would follow if the government failed to provide an early and satisfactory solution to the demand.

The association also warned that the government would have to take full responsibility for any disruption to development projects resulting from future trade union action.
