Ravi Karunanayake appears before bribery commission

Colombo, August 31 (Daily Mirror): Parliamentarian Ravi Karunanayake appeared before the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption (CIABOC) today (31) to provide a statement in connection with an ongoing investigation.

The investigation concerns allegations that three employees recruited for a special project of the National Lotteries Board were allegedly deployed for political activities, causing a financial loss to the government.

Karunanayake had previously been instructed to appear before CIABOC to provide a statement regarding the matter. However, after he failed to appear, a team of CIABOC officials had recently visited his residence.

The officials were unable to meet the parliamentarian as he was not at home at the time. His lawyers subsequently requested that another date be granted for him to provide the statement, citing arrangements related to his daughter’s wedding.

Accordingly, Karunanayake appeared before CIABOC today, as instructed in the previous notice, to provide his statement in connection with the investigation.
