Lethargic institutions vs. Right to Information letters

Local governments -- there are some 325 municipalities, urban councils, Pradeshiya sabhas & 9 Provincial Councils spending around Rs. 6 billion in 2026. There are many SLAS officers at 6 different levels, for  e.g. Secretary,  Commissioner,  Deputies & Assistants in many departments. Yet,  very poor performance is very poor when it comes to serving the community.

The office telephone exchanges do not answer calls, letters to them are not acknowledged & left with clerks  (management assistants). Governors are missing in the organisation charts. The Regional & Local Government Division of the Public Service Ministry in Colombo exercises no authority over the provincial administration. Many functions accepted as delegated can be shared with that ministry if it has initiative.

Keep on sending  Right to Information letters & ask why to make them RTIs.  Address to Local Government  Secretary or department head as Information Officer -- acknowledgement is due within 7 working days after its receipt. The reply is due 14 working days thereafter. Send weekly reminders. Thereafter write Appeal letter to LG Secretary as Designated Officer. If there is no acknowledgement after 7 days,  warn the default will be reported to RTI Commission. Inform your council/sabha member (after their election). Also, people can fForm non-political Rate Payers Associations.
