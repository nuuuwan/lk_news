VAT calculation on film tickets to be revised under 2027 Budget

Colombo, Nov. 12 (Daily Mirror) - The basis for calculating Value Added Tax (VAT) on cinema tickets will be revised, with the tax liability of film producers, distributors, the National Film Corporation and cinema operators determined according to their respective shares of ticket revenue.

Under the proposed revision announced by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake in the 2027 Budget, VAT will be calculated on the ticket value after deducting Entertainment Tax but before adding VAT.

The change aims to establish a revised basis for calculating VAT liability among the parties involved in the film industry.
