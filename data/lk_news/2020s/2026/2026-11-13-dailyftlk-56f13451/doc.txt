LAUGFS Gas to resume cylinder deliveries following arrival of more than 3,000 tons of LPG

LAUGFS Gas PLC said yesterday that Gas Goodwill has arrived in Sri Lanka carrying more than 3,000 metric tons of liquefied petroleum gas (LPG). The shipment will enable the company to resume cylinder deliveries to households and businesses in phases following the recent supply disruption.

Once the cargo is unloaded, Laugfs Gas will fill cylinders and dispatch them through its distributor and dealer network. Customers can expect availability to improve progressively as deliveries reach their local outlets.

Laugfs Gas said it recognises the disruption customers have faced in securing refills and thanks them for their patience. “Customers can check stock with their nearest authorized Laugfs Gas dealer or call 1345 before visiting an outlet,” the company said.
