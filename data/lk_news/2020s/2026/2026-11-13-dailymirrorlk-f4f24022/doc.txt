High Court to deliver order on CIABOC revision petition against Namal’s bail on Nov. 18

Colombo, Nov. 13 (Daily Mirror) - Colombo High Court Judge Lal Bandara Ranasinghe today ordered that its decision on the revision petition filed by the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption, seeking to revoke the bail granted to MP Namal Rajapaksa by the Colombo Magistrate’s Court on November 3, 2026, in connection with the Krrish deal case and remand him again, would be delivered on November 18.

Appearing on behalf of the CIABOC, Assistant Director Thanuja Bandara requested the court to issue an interim order suspending the operation of the bail order granted to MP Namal Rajapaksa by the Colombo Magistrate’s Court on November 3, 2026, in connection with the Krrish deal case.

She further requested the court, in its final order on the petition, to set aside the bail order granted to the respondent, Namal Rajapaksa, and remand him until the conclusion of the case.

Meanwhile, the High Court fixed November 25 for consideration of a request made by President’s Counsel Ali Sabry, appearing for Namal Rajapaksa, seeking the dismissal of the revision petition filed by the CIABOC challenging the bail order granted to him by the Colombo Magistrate’s Court.
