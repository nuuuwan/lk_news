2025 O/L results expected by mid-June

Colombo, May 25 (Daily Mirror) - The Department of Examinations says the results of the 2025 G.C.E. Ordinary Level Examination are expected to be released by mid-June.

Responding to social media speculation that the results would be released within days, Commissioner General of Examinations Indika Kumari Liyanage, said that no exact date has yet been finalized.

However, she confirmed that preparations are underway to issue the results by mid-June.

The 2025 G.C.E. O/L examination was conducted from February 17 to 26, with 451,463 candidates sitting for the exam.
