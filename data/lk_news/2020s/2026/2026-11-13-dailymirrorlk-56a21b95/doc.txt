Sajith opens budget debate instead of Harsha de Silva who did it previously

Colombo, Nov. 14 (Daily Mirror) - Instead of Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) MP Dr Harsha de Silva, who opened the budget debate on the last two occasions under the current government, Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa opened it yesterday and spoke for 45 minutes.

Asked why senior Opposition MPs were not seen in action on the opening day of the debate, Chief Opposition Whip Gayantha Karunatilleka told the Daily Mirror that many senior Opposition MPs would take part in the debate after Monday.

"Many MPs in the Opposition did not want to speak during the first few days of the second reading debate. They wanted time only after Monday," he said.

The other Opposition MPs scheduled to speak yesterday were Sujeewa Senasinghe, Dilith Jayaweera, Jeevan Thondaman, Chithral Fernando and Shanakiyan Rasamanickam.

Mr Premadasa was allocated 45 minutes, while MP Senasinghe was allocated 30 minutes. MPs Thondaman, Rasamanickam, Fernando and Jayaweera were each allocated 18 minutes.

It was also revealed that a smaller number of Opposition MPs would participate in the debate this time, allowing more speaking time for each member. In contrast, the ruling party has allocated time among a larger number of MPs to accommodate all its members.

Prime Minister Harini Amarasuriya, who opened the debate on behalf of the Government, questioned why the Opposition Leader had kicked off the debate yesterday rather than MP Harsha de Silva.

"Dr De Silva used to open the second reading debate on the Budget in past years. We wonder why the tradition has changed today. Also, we wonder whether all Opposition Samagi Jana Balawegaya MPs hold the same ideology as the Opposition Leader," she said.

Responding, MP Senasinghe said there was nothing wrong with the Opposition Leader starting the debate, as he had made an effective speech.

"It is the Government which has become helpless today and not us," he said.
