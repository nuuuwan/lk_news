Hayleys Agriculture launches grain & food processing machinery business at ProFood expo

Hayleys Agriculture Holdings Limited launched its new Hayleys Grain & Food Processing Machinery business at ProFood, ProPack & AgBiz 2026, held from 21 to 23 August at the BMICH, Colombo, marking a significant expansion of its portfolio into specialised machinery and technology solutions for Sri Lanka’s grain and food processing industries.

Hayleys Agriculture used the ProFood, ProPack & AgBiz 2026 platform to introduce the new business to industry stakeholders, alongside a range of advanced solutions designed to support greater efficiency, quality, hygiene and food safety across grain and food processing operations.

The Hayleys Grain & Food Processing Machinery portfolio includes grain drying and storage systems,high-quality rice milling machinery, specialised food processing equipment, food metal detection systems, hygiene solutions, online process control systems and other processing technologies designed to meet the evolving requirements of the Food and Beverage Industry.

The launch represents a further step in Hayleys Agriculture’s evolution from its strong foundations in agricultural inputs towards becoming a broader, solutions-led organisation across the agricultural and food value chains, with an increasing focus on mechanisation, technology and value addition. Another key highlight of the exhibition was the unveiling of the revamped Govi Aruna range from Sunfrost (Pvt) Ltd, alongside Island Magic Peanut Spread. Featuring a refreshed visual identity and brand proposition, the Govi Aruna range continues to build relevance around convenient, quality products suited to everyday Sri Lankan cooking. Visitors were able to experience the versatility of the portfolio through live tasting sessions and interactive chef demonstrations, featuring familiar Sri Lankan favourites alongside contemporary food creations. The activation provided consumers with a first-hand understanding of the products, ingredients and their different applications.
