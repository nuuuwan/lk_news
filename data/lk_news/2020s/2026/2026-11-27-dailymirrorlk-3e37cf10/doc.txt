Commercial Credit & Finance secures US $ 6mn GGSF facility from Incofin

Commercial Credit & Finance PLC, a leading financial institution in Sri Lanka, has secured a US $ 6 million loan facility from global impact investment firm Incofin Investment Management, under the Global Gender-Smart Fund (GGSF) transaction.

A defining feature of this landmark agreement is its exclusive focus on female economic empowerment. The entire US $ 6 million facility is dedicated solely to the women borrowers.

By channelling this capital directly into women-led micro-enterprises and small and medium enterprises, Commercial Credit aims to stimulate grassroots economic growth, create sustainable employment opportunities and champion the women who form the backbone of Sri Lanka’s economy.

The agreement was finalised following the legal and compliance reviews, demonstrating the governance, transparency and operational strength of Commercial Credit & Finance.

The facility underscores Incofin’s confidence in Commercial Credit’s team and its long-term vision for the Sri Lankan financial sector.

“This US $ 6 million GGSF facility from Incofin is a transformative step in our mission to drive financial inclusion, specifically for the women of Sri Lanka,” stated Commercial Credit & Finance Executive Director and Chief Operating Officer Rajiv Chitty.

“By dedicating these funds entirely to the women borrowers, we are providing vital financial support to the female entrepreneurs who are driving our nation’s economic recovery. We are proud to align with an impact-driven partner like Incofin, which shares our vision for sustainable and inclusive empowerment.”

Incofin Investment Management, known for its focus on rural microfinance and sustainable agriculture in emerging markets, partners with institutions that demonstrate strong social performance alongside financial sustainability. This loan agreement further solidifies the collaborative relationship between global impact investors and resilient Sri Lankan financial institutions.
