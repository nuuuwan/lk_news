Kandy toilets are notorious

Public toilets in Kandy are few and far between – mostly being located in a cluster around the market. People ‘caught short’ up in D.S. Senanayake Street have a half mile to walk. When out shopping, people have to note closely the location of the nearest toilet to avoid being horribly embarrassed.

On arrival at the public toilets, shoppers are greeted by a stifling, choking smell of urine so thick and overwhelming that you can cut the air with a knife. But this must be ignored due to the pressing need in hand.

This unsanitary state of affairs is the result of many months of neglect by the authorities, who, one can suppose,  are waiting for a sympathetic, generous benefactor who is willing to donate funds to make a fresh start, so to speak.
