Chamara Sampath pooh-poohs President’s Mannar Basin oil and gas exploration plan

Nov 15 (Daily Mirror) - Opposition MP Chamara Sampath Dassanayake dismissed President Anura Kumara Dissanayake’s budgetary proposal to pursue offshore oil and gas exploration in the Mannar Basin as unrealistic.

​Speaking during today's budget debate, Dassanayake noted that successive governments have mooted the proposal since 1975 without it ever seeing the light of day.

​He pointed out that the current rulers, while in the opposition, had criticized the idea of upstream petroleum development during the presidency of Mahinda Rajapaksa.

​"We won't stand in your way if you try, but you will never extract oil and gas from the Mannar Basin during the remainder of your term," he said.
