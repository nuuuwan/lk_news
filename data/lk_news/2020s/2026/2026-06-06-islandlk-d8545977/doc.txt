SLCGE presents proposal for apparel design hub to ministry

The Sri Lanka Chamber of Garment Exporters (SLCGE) has presented a proposal to establish a pioneering Design Hub aimed at strengthening the country’s apparel export sector, during discussions with the Ministry of Industry and Entrepreneurship Development.

A high-level SLCGE delegation led by President Nishantha Bakmeege met Deputy Minister Chathuranga Abeysinghe on May 28 and formally outlined the initiative, which is expected to be the first centralized platform of its kind in Sri Lanka’s apparel industry.

The proposed Design Hub is designed to showcase the craftsmanship, innovation and product portfolios of small and medium-scale apparel manufacturers under a single national-level facility. It is intended to function as a one-stop destination for international buyers, enabling direct engagement with certified SLCGE member companies.

According to the Chamber, the initiative seeks to remove traditional market access barriers, strengthen direct business linkages and expand export opportunities for SME manufacturers, while enhancing the visibility and competitiveness of the local apparel sector globally.

During the discussions, the Deputy Minister expressed support for the proposal, noting its alignment with national priorities for industrial transformation and SME development. The talks were described as constructive and forward-looking.

Both sides agreed to continue technical discussions on implementation frameworks, with the initiative viewed as a potential milestone for Sri Lanka’s apparel industry and export growth strategy.
