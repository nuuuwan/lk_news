Sri Lanka holds pre-bid meeting ahead of Domestic Dollar Bond issue

ECONOMYNEXT – Sri Lanka’s Public Debt Management Office (PDMO) has held a pre-bid meeting with designated agents ahead of the planned issuance of a new Domestic Dollar Bond (DDB) 2026, a US dollar-denominated debt instrument aimed at diversifying government funding sources and deepening the local government securities market.

The PDMO, under the Ministry of Finance, said the DDB 2026 will be offered in the domestic market through 15 designated agents comprising primary dealers and licensed commercial banks.

A pre-bid meeting was held on October 16, with all 15 designated agents and the paying agent participating, the ministry said.

According to the PDMO, the meeting was held to provide clarification on the operational and regulatory aspects of the upcoming bond issuance.

Officials from the Central Bank of Sri Lanka’s Foreign Exchange Department, as well as the Fiscal Policy Department and Treasury Operations Department of the General Treasury, were present.

The government said the bond issue forms part of broader efforts to diversify its financing base while supporting the development of the domestic market for government securities. (Colombo/Oct19/2026)
