Inmate dies after being gored by deer at Weerawila Open Prison Camp

Colombo, Nov. 9 (Daily Mirror) - An inmate undergoing rehabilitation at the Weerawila Open Prison Camp died after being gored by a deer while carrying out cleaning activities within the camp premises, police said.

The incident occurred yesterday evening (November 8), when the inmate was reportedly attacked by a deer roaming freely within the prison camp.

He was rushed to Debarawewa Hospital for treatment but later succumbed to his injuries.

The deceased has been identified as a 30-year-old resident of Warapalana.

His body is currently lying at the Debarawewa Hospital mortuary, with the post-mortem examination scheduled for today (9).

The Weerawila Police have launched an investigation into the incident.
