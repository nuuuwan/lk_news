Colombo–Kandy road closed between Kadugannawa and Ganethenna

Colombo, Aug. 3 (Daily Mirror) - The Colombo–Kandy main road has been temporarily closed between Kadugannawa and Ganethenna due to the risk of rocks sliding onto the roadway, Police said.

As such, motorists have been urged to use alternative routes until further notice as a precautionary measure.

Police also advised drivers to follow traffic instructions and avoid the affected stretch until the road is declared safe for travel. Pic by Ranjith Rajapaksa
