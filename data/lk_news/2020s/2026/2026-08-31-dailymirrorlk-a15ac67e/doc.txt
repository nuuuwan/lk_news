Two Pakistanis among three arrested over 463 kg ’Ice’ haul

Colombo, August 31 (Daily Mirror) - Three more suspects, including two Pakistani nationals, have been arrested in connection with smuggling a massive stock of 'Ice' (crystal methamphetamine) hidden inside a container.

The arrests were made by the Central Crimes Investigation Bureau.

According to the police, the operation follows the seizure of 463 kilograms of 'Ice' discovered during an inspection of the suspicious container.
