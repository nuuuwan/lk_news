Grand Theft Auto VI launches to global fanfare

DW - Grand Theft Auto VI officially launched Thursday, ending a 13-year wait for one of the most anticipated games ever.

The game became playable at midnight local time, depending on location, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X, meaning gamers in Asia will get to play the game ahead of users in the US and Europe.

In New Zealand, one of the first countries worldwide where the game became available, the release prompted internet providers to prepare for a spike in internet usage at midnight.

This is due to the game's size of 212 gigabytes, more than a quarter of the average New Zealand household's usage in a month.

Rockstar Games, the company behind the GTA franchise, faces high expectationsn after 13 years of anticipation, with previous versions having become commercial and cultural hits around the world.

GTA games sold 470 million units worldwide, and the fifth installment generated $1 billion in retail sales in just three days after its release in 2013.

Take-Two Interactive, Rockstar Games' parent company, saw its shares rise by more than eight percent the day before the game's release.

The game takes place in Vice City, a fictional version of Miami.

It follows Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, a couple drawn into criminal networks while trying to secure a better life. GTA games are often criticized for including excessive violence and for glorifying crimes and drug use.

Lucia, a former prisoner, and Jason, an ex-military man, experience a series of criminal jobs that go wrong in the drug-trafficking world of Leonida, a fictional state based on Florida.

According to Rockstar, Leonida is the company's most detailed open world so far, extending beyond Vice City into smaller communities and nightlife hotspots inspired by locations in South Florida.

Music has been an intrinsic part of the GTA experience, and the latest game is expected to feature a soundtrack including artists like Tame Impala and SZA.
