‘70s Pop Hits & Baila’

After his recent trip to England where he was originally scheduled to do three shows but ended doing seven, Ravi Sebastian is in the news again.

Yes, he is in great demand, overseas, and will be off to Dubai, next month, to create the ’70s pop hits scene and baila.

The show is scheduled for Saturday, 25th July, at the Mercure Hotel Dubai Barsha Heights, from 8.00 pm to 3.00 am.

Providing the music for Rajiv to do the needful is the band Seven Notes, said to be a very popular and much in demand in that region.

The organizer of this event, ‘70s Pop Hits & Baila,’ is Niluk Uswaththa who booms away on bass for Seven Notes.

In fact, Niluk, and his wife Apeksha who, incidentally, was featured in Glam Scene in last Thursday’s The Island newspaper, were in Sri Lanka last month.

The Sri Lankans in Dubai, I’m told, are eagerly looking forward to having Rajiv in their midst as they know, for sure, it’s going to be a night to remember.

In London, Rajiv’s seven events are still the talk of the town among the Sri Lankans who took in the action.
