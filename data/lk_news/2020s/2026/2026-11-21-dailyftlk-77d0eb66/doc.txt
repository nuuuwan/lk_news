Sathya Sai Baba devotees to mark 101st Divine Advent across Colombo

The Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Seva Samithi, Colombo will hold a series of devotional programs across the city on 23 November to commemorate the 101st Divine Advent of Bhagawan Sri Sathya Sai Baba.

Celebrations will begin at the Sai Mandir, 22 Barnes Place, Colombo 7, from 5 a.m. to 8.30 a.m.

Sivananda Nilayam at Hindu Ladies’ College, 23 Rudra Mawatha, Colombo 6, will host two sessions, from 5 a.m. to 6 a.m. and from 10.30 a.m. to noon.

The day will close at the Ramakrishna Mission, 40 Ramakrishna Road, Colombo 6, from 5 p.m. to 8.45 p.m. The programs will feature bhajans (devotional songs), Nagara Sankeerthan (devotional singing in procession), Veda chanting and processions, as well as music, dance and drama performances held as a tribute to Sathya Sai Baba. The Samithi invited devotees, well-wishers and members of the public to join the celebrations, which commemorate the life, love and universal message of Sathya Sai Baba.
