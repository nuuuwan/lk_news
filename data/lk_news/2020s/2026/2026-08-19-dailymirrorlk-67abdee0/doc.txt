CSE extends decline as ASPI slips below 21,450 despite foreign buying

The Colombo Stock Exchange extended its negative momentum for a second consecutive session on Wednesday, 19 August 2026, with both benchmark indices closing lower amid continued selling pressure across the broader market.

The All Share Price Index declined by 62.50 points, or 0.29%, to close at 21,416.57, while the S&P Sri Lanka 20 Index fell by 20.52 points, or 0.34%, to end the session at 6,020.38.

The ASPI opened around the 21,500 level before gradually losing ground throughout the session. Selling pressure intensified during the afternoon, pushing the index below 21,450 before it eventually closed at 21,416.57. The S&P SL20 followed a similar downward trajectory, despite a brief recovery around midday.

Total market turnover stood at LKR 1.54 billion, with approximately 43.88 million shares changing hands during the session.

The Food, Beverage and Tobacco sector dominated market activity, generating LKR 461.58 million in turnover from approximately 12.92 million shares. Sunshine Holdings recorded the highest turnover within the sector at LKR 220.61 million.

Crossing transactions contributed approximately LKR 515.07 million, representing around 33% of total equity turnover. The largest crossing was recorded in Seylan Bank, where 2.00 million shares changed hands at LKR 98.80, generating turnover of LKR 197.60 million.

Foreign investor activity provided a positive contrast to the weaker market performance, with foreign investors turning net buyers and recording a net foreign inflow of LKR 103.20 million.

Market breadth nevertheless remained negative, with 72 securities advancing against 128 decliners, resulting in an advance-to-decline ratio of 0.56. This indicated that selling pressure remained relatively broad despite the return of foreign buying.

C T Holdings emerged as the strongest positive contributor to the ASPI, adding 10.48 points to the index. Melstacorp, Richard Pieris, SMB Finance non-voting and Hayleys also provided support.

Sampath Bank recorded the largest negative contribution, reducing the ASPI by 17.68 points. Lion Brewery, Central Finance, Bukit Darah and Dialog Axiata also weighed on the index.

Among notable trades, Sunshine Holdings recorded the highest individual counter turnover at LKR 220.61 million, followed by Seylan Bank with LKR 200.22 million and Dialog Axiata with LKR 163.07 million.

Overall, the session reflected continued near-term weakness following the previous day’s sharp decline. While the return of foreign buying was encouraging, negative market breadth and continued pressure on both benchmark indices indicated that broader investor sentiment remained cautious.
