Prime Lands Residencies delivers higher revenue growth and profitability in 1H FY2026/27

Prime Lands Residencies PLC delivered impressive financial results for the six-month period ended 30th September 2026, with revenue rising 48 percent to Rs. 7.60 billion from Rs. 5.15 billion in the corresponding period of the previous financial year.

Furthermore, profit for the period increased 10 percent to Rs. 1.03 billion, compared to Rs. 940.7 million a year earlier, reflecting continued revenue growth and an expansion in gross and operating profits. Gross profit improved 31 percent to Rs. 2.11 billion, while operating profit rose 33 percent to Rs. 1.47 billion, demonstrating the company’s ability to sustain growth at the operating level.

Profit before taxation increased by 9 percent to Rs. 1.41 billion, while earnings per share improved to Rs. 1.10 from Rs. 1.00.

Prime Residencies PLC Executive Chairperson Sandamini Perera added, “The notable percent growth in revenue demonstrates the strength of our business performance during the period under review. Improvements in gross and operating profits provide a solid foundation for the remainder of the financial year. Our focus remains on translating revenue growth into stronger earnings and maintaining the financial capacity to support the company’s continuing business objectives.”

The robust 1H results for FY2026/27 position Prime Lands Residencies on a positive earnings trajectory for the 2026/27 financial year. With revenue, gross profit and operating profit all recording year-on-year growth, the company plans to sustain and improve on its operational performance, cost management and financial discipline as it progresses through the second half of the year.

The company’s financial position also remains strong. As at 30th September 2026, total equity stood at Rs. 11.72 billion, up from Rs. 11.44 billion at 31st March 2026, even after dividends of Rs. 750 million were paid to shareholders during the half-year. Net cash generated from operating activities was Rs. 4.15 billion for the six months, and bank overdrafts were reduced to Rs. 179 million from Rs. 2.15 billion at the start of the period.

The company ensures customer interest in its real estate developments remains high by delivering high value projects as promised, for example, the luxurious 43 by the Sea project on Marine Drive; topping-off of Tower Cosmos at The Colombo Border, a 484-unit development, four months ahead of schedule, alongside the rapid advancement of The Seasons Colombo 08.

Projected revenues from the newly launched ultra - luxury development, J’adore Negombo, and other high value projects under - construction such as MON VIE Colombo 05, The Prime Colombo 09, The Golf – Colombo 08 and The Elizabeth – Colombo 07, forecast strong earnings potential for Prime Residencies PLC for the foreseeable future.
