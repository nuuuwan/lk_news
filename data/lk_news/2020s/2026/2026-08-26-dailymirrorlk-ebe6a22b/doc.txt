CBC Finance strengthens board with two industry professionals

CBC Finance PLC has appointed two financial professionals, Ananda Seneviratne and Sanjaya Bandara, as Independent Non-Executive Directors, effective 6th August 2026.

The two new Board members bring decades of combined experience across commercial banking, non-bank financial services, audit and assurance, corporate governance, risk management, and regulatory oversight.

Ananda Seneviratne brings more than 37 years of executive experience spanning the banking, non-banking financial, and corporate sectors. His career has been distinguished by senior leadership roles encompassing strategic management, corporate rehabilitation, credit oversight, risk management, and ESG integration.

He previously served as Managing Director of Siyapatha Finance PLC from 2019 to 2025 and as Director/Chief Executive Officer of Assetline Leasing Company Limited from 2009 to 2019. His extensive industry experience has also seen him provide sector-level leadership as Chairman of the Finance Houses Association of Sri Lanka (2022–2024) and Chairman of the Leasing Association of Sri Lanka (2016–2018).

Joining him on the Board is Sanjaya Bandara, with more than two decades of experience in audit, financial reporting, corporate governance, and risk management. Bandara currently serves as Partner and Head of Audit and Assurance Services at B.R. De Silva & Co., a member firm of Nexia International a position he has held since 2004. He previously served as President of CA Sri Lanka from 2022 to 2023.

His professional career includes several key governance and regulatory appointments, such as serving as a Commission Member and Chairman of the Audit Committee of the Securities and Exchange Commission of Sri Lanka, a member of the CBSL Stakeholder Engagement Consultative Committee, and a Board Member of regional accounting bodies including the Confederation of Asian and Pacific Accountants (CAPA) and the South Asian Federation of Accountants (SAFA).

Bandara also brings considerable experience within the non-bank financial services sector, having served as Chairman of Mercantile Investment and Finance PLC and as an Independent Non-Executive Director and Audit Committee Chairman across several listed and public entities.
