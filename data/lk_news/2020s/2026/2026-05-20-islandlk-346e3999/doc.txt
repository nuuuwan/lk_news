Vella earns Great Place To Work Certification Tm, marking a milestone in its people-first journey

Colombo, Sri Lanka (6 May, 2026) Vella has been officially certified as a Great Place to Work, marking a significant milestone in the Group’s ongoing journey to build a culture rooted in trust, shared purpose and collective growth. Great Place To Work® is the global authority on workplace culture, employee experience, and the leadership behaviors proven to deliver market-leading revenue, employee retention and increased innovation.

Achieved on its first attempt, the certification reflects the strength of Vella’s people culture across its diverse portfolio spanning hospitality, adventure, tea, retail, leisure and lifestyle experiences. The Group’s growing portfolio brings together some of Sri Lanka’s most distinctive destination-led brands, from the quiet luxury of 98 Acres Resort & Spa, vibrant energy of Ravana Pool Club, to the adventure appeal of Flying Ravana Adventure Park, Ella Swing and Amber Swing. Its leisure and hospitality footprint further extends to Walaa Kulu and Villa Lé Maas Tota, while Halpé Tea, Halpe Tea Tour, Ceylon Tea Supermarket, Kiri Kopi, and Ceylon Escapade strengthen the Group’s presence across tea, retail, café culture, and travel experiences. Together, these brands reflect Vella’s vision to create meaningful experiences that celebrate Sri Lanka’s natural beauty, cultural richness and evolving appeal as a world-class destination.
