SLT-Mobitel launches SL’s first next-gen 5G mobile router

SLT‑Mobitel is redefining connectivity with the introduction of Sri Lanka’s first TRCSL‑approved pocket‑sized 5G mobile router.

Designed for high-demand digital lifestyles, the device delivers ultra-fast, reliable connectivity for streaming, gaming, content creation and remote work on the move.

Whether gaming with friends, watching TV shows, creating content, attending online classes, live streaming or working on the go, the new 5G mobile router delivers ultra‑fast, reliable connectivity tailored for today’s digital lifestyle needs.

Powered by Wi‑Fi 6 Plus technology, the router delivers speeds of up to 3,600 Mbps, offering smoother streaming, lower gaming latency, faster downloads and stable performance even in crowded environments. Supporting up to 32 devices simultaneously, it is ideal for study groups, travel teams, content creators, gaming sessions and family use.

As Sri Lanka’s digital enabler, SLT‑Mobitel has consistently led the nation’s technology evolution, introducing revolutionary experiences and innovative products that redefine how people connect. The launch of the 5G mobile router further reinforces this commitment, enabling Sri Lankans to experience network superiority through a seamless, superior 5G connectivity experience while unlocking the full power and possibilities of next-generation technology. By bringing international‑grade technology to Sri Lanka, SLT-Mobitel is shaping the next-era of connectivity and accelerating the nation’s digital advancement without compromise.

The device features a smart OLED touchscreen for instant monitoring of data usage and connected devices. The sleek design, just 12mm thin and weighing only 220g, makes it effortlessly portable. A powerful 7000mAh battery provides up to 10 hours of usage, with reverse charging capability enabling the router to double as a power bank.

Security and peace of mind are built in, with parental controls, safe browsing features, secure encryption protocols, firewall protection and VPN support. Further enhancing convenience, the AI Life App supports users to manage network settings, guest access, traffic monitoring and performance optimisation directly from their smartphones.

With the launch of Sri Lanka’s first pocket-sized 5G mobile router, SLT-Mobitel is ushering in a new era of high-speed, flexible connectivity that moves with users wherever they go. It enables the next generation to create, play, learn and work without interruption, powered by reliable performance and advanced 5G capability designed to keep pace with today’s digital lifestyles.
