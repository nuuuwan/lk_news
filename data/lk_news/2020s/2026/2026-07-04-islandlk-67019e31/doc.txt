Prof. Fazeeha Azmi becomes Lanka’s first woman envoy to Iran

Professor Fazeeha Azmi has assumed duties as Sri Lanka’s Ambassador-designate to Iran, becoming the country’s first woman to be appointed to the post.She formally took up her duties at the Sri Lankan Embassy in Tehran on June 30.

Addressing embassy staff, Prof. Azmi outlined the Government’s foreign policy priorities and underscored the importance of strengthening Sri Lanka’s longstanding relations with Iran, as well as with Azerbaijan and Turkmenistan, to which she is also concurrently accredited.

She identified trade, employment, education, pharmaceuticals, tourism and cultural cooperation as key areas for expanding bilateral engagement, while calling for renewed efforts to attract Iranian tourists to Sri Lanka in collaboration with the country’s outbound travel industry.

Prior to her diplomatic appointment, Prof. Azmi served as Professor of Geography at the University of Peradeniya, with over two decades of academic and research experience in migration, tourism, post-war development, urban heritage and poverty studies.

A graduate of the University of Peradeniya, she obtained her M.Phil. and Ph.D. from the Norwegian University of Science and Technology. She has also held academic positions in Sri Lanka, India, Norway, Fiji and Canada.
