Russia agrees to assist Sri Lanka in bringing crime suspects back

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka and Russia have signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to strengthen bilateral legal cooperation, with Moscow agreeing to assist Sri Lanka in securing the return of individuals wanted over criminal activities who have fled to Russia.

The agreement was signed today (26) by Attorney General President’s Counsel Parinda Ranasinghe and Prosecutor General of the Russian Federation Aleksander Gutsan during a meeting at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.

Discussions focused on strengthening longstanding cooperation between the two countries and enhancing collaboration on bilateral legal matters.

During the meeting, Gutsan assured that Russia would continue to assist Sri Lanka in tracing and securing the return of individuals involved in criminal activities in Sri Lanka who had fled to Russia, as well as locating their financial assets and properties held there.

He said such assistance would be extended in accordance with the provisions of Sri Lanka’s Proceeds of Crime Act.

The Russian Prosecutor General also appreciated the cooperation extended by Sri Lanka in judicial matters and other areas of mutual interest.

Senior officials from the Russian Federation and representatives of the Attorney General’s Department of Sri Lanka attended the meeting.
