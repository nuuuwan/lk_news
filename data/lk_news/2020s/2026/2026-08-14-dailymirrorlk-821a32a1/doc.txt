Indices close in green

The ASPI closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Dialog Axiata, Cargills and DFCC Bank with the turnover crossing Rs. 2.8bn.

A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in Sampath Bank, Cargills and Seylan Bank nonvoting.

Mixed interest was observed in Sierra Cables, Dialog Axiata and Agstar whilst retail interest was noted in Softlogic Holdings, Renuka Agri Foods and Asia Siyaka Commodities. Furthermore, foreigners closed as net sellers.

The Banking sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to Sampath Bank) whilst the sector index gained 0.77%. The share price of Sampath Bank increased by Rs. 0.75 (0.55%) to close at Rs. 137.75.

Capital Goods sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Sierra Cables and John Keells Holdings) whilst the sector index edged up by 0.02%. The share price of Sierra Cables gained Rs. 0.90 (2.80%) to close at Rs. 33.10. The share price of John Keells Holdings closed flat at Rs. 20.00.

Cargills and Dialog Axiata were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Cargills recorded a gain of Rs. 26.75 (4.04%) to close at Rs. 688.75. The share price of Dialog Axiata appreciated by Rs. 1.20 (2.67%) to close at Rs. 46.20.
