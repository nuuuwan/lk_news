KIU University tops ministry-led review in higher education

KIU University has set a new benchmark in Sri Lankan higher education by achieving an 85.86% score in the latest Ministry-led Periodic Institutional Review, the highest among non-state universities. This accomplishment is notable as KIU, a relatively young institution, outperformed many long-established universities across six key pillars including governance, academic planning, and quality assurance.

At the programme level, KIU’s BSc (Hons) in Biomedical Science earned an outstanding 93.7%, among Sri Lanka’s highest-rated programmes, while its Psychology degree scored 83.78%. Programmes in Accounting, Marketing, and HRM also exceeded national standards.

Accredited by the Ministry and recognised by the University Grants Commission (UGC), KIU aligns its offerings with the Sri Lanka Qualifications Framework. The university’s graduates have secured overseas postgraduate scholarships and demonstrated strong employability.
