ISMM to hold National Supply Chain Day celebrations on 24 October

The Institute of Supply and Materials Management (ISMM) in collaboration with the Ministry of Industries is taking the lead to organise the National Supply Chain Day Celebrations 2026 on October 24th at Monarch Imperial, Sri Jayewardenepura Kotte.

President ISMM Lilantha Subasinghe addressing a media briefing last week said this year’s National Supply Chain Day celebrations will be divided into two main segments, which are the morning session with three main seminars and the evening gala awards ceremony.

The morning session, from 8.30am to 5.00pm will feature the National Supply Chain Day Seminar under the theme: “Geopolitics, Technology and the Future of Global Supply Chains”. This seminar includes a Technical Session, a CEO Forum and an Industry Forum, NextGen Supply Chain Leaders Forum with international supply chain leaders, experts and professionals to explore the latest trends, best practices and technological innovations in SCM.

The Industry Forum under the theme “Sri Lanka’s Supply Chain Transformation: Challenges, Opportunities and Competitiveness,” will be carried by Director Supply Chain Unilever Sri Lanka and Head of Supply Chain - Unilever Sri Lanka, Myanmar, Cambodia and Laos Damith Abeyrathne.

The second session is the CEO Forum under the theme “Navigating Global Uncertainty: Building Resilient Supply Chains in a Fragmented Market.” This session will be chaired by Chief Executive Officer Siam City Cement (Lanka) Limited – Insee Cement Thusith Gunawarnasuriya.

The third session is the technical session, which will focus on “From Digitalization to Intelligence: Harnessing AI and Emerging Technologies for Next-Generation Supply Chain Management” and will be chaired by Deputy Chief Transformation Officer SLT Mobitel Ishari Siriwardane.

The NextGen Supply Chain Leaders Forum under the theme “Focused on engaging and empowering the next generation of supply chain professionals, fostering innovation, leadership, and future-ready capabilities within the industry” will be, chaired by Director Supply Chain Nestle Lanka Limited Kanchana Fernando.

The evening session, starting at 6.30pm at the same venue, will host the National Supply Chain Excellence Awards 2026 followed by the 54th Anniversary Banquet Dinner of ISMM.

Vice President ISMM Prof. Sanath Divakara said the awards ceremony celebrates outstanding contributions to Supply Chain management at both the individual and organizational levels, recognizing excellence across four enterprise sizes: Extra Large, Large, Medium and Small. Awards will be presented under Manufacturing, Service and Trading Sectors, along with accolades for the “Best Supply Chain Practicing Organization of the Year” and the “Best Supply Chain Professional of the Year.”

The other attendees of the media briefing included Vice President ISMM Dimuka Hemantha, Vice President ISMM and Chairman National Supply Chain Day and 53rd Anniversary Celebrations Committee Ranil Abeysekera, General Secretary ISMM Sameera Landekumbura, Treasurer ISMM Kavinda Jayathilaka and Chairman ISMM Marketing Committee Dilshan Silva.
