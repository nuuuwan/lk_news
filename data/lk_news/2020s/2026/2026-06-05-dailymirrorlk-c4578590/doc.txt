Creative Information Technologies opens new premium showroom at Unity Plaza

Lahiru Amarakoon (National Sales Manager), S. Suresh (Chairman), Ranjan Ramanayake (Actor), Surendra Ranawaka (General Manager - Sales & Marketing), Suranjith Swaris (Consultant/ Director of Business Development & Sales Operations)

Actor Ranjan Ramanayake cutting the ribbon to open the new showroom

Creative Information Technologies (Pvt) Ltd announced the opening of its newest showroom at Unity Plaza on 25 May, further strengthening its customer presence within Sri Lanka’s premier ICT and technology marketplace.

The new showroom marks another significant milestone in the company’s continuous journey of delivering innovative and world-class technology solutions to customers across the country. Recognized as a trusted name in Sri Lanka’s ICT sector since 2008, Creative Information Technologies has built a strong reputation through its commitment to quality, customer satisfaction, and technology-driven solutions.

Creative Information Technologies is the authorized distributor and agent for internationally recognized brands including Amida, HAVIT, and PNY, while also offering HP, Dell, Asus, MSI, and other leading global technology brands through its expanding retail and distribution network. The company provides a comprehensive range of ICT products and services including laptops, desktops, printer consumables, gaming accessories, CCTV solutions, networking systems, storage devices, and mobile accessories.

Speaking on the expansion, Subramanium Suresh, Chairman of Creative Information Technologies said the new showroom reflects the company’s long-term vision of becoming the preferred and trusted partner in connecting people with technology.

“At Creative Information Technologies, our vision is to become the preferred and trusted supplier connecting people with technology. The opening of our new showroom is aligned with our mission of improving lives by making world-class ICT products and solutions more accessible to customers across Sri Lanka, while continuing to deliver innovation, reliability, and service excellence,” he added.

The company’s vision is “to be the preferred and trusted supplier to connect people with technology,” while its mission focuses on improving lives through wider access to world-class ICT products and services.
