Veepee Graphics Lanka sets new benchmarks in pre-press and packaging industry

Veepee Graphics Lanka, the Sri Lankan arm of Veepee Graphic Solutions, Bangalore, has emerged as a leading force in the country’s pre-press and packaging industry, combining decades of expertise with advanced technology and innovation.

With a legacy built on quality, precision and technological excellence, the company has established itself as a trusted partner for local and international brands seeking world-class printing solutions.

The success of Veepee Graphics Lanka has been driven by the vision and industry expertise of entrepreneur and flexography specialist P. Jaichandra, whose decades of experience have played a key role in shaping the organisation’s growth. The company is also strengthened by next-generation leadership through Natasha Jaichandra, daughter of P. Jaichandra and Veepee Graphics Lanka Director Nalani Madhushani.

Veepee Graphics Lanka has built a strong reputation through its team of skilled prepress and premedia professionals, supported by state-of-the-art machinery, advanced software platforms, artificial intelligence-driven workflows and automation technologies.

The company is at the forefront of colour innovation through its adoption of Extended Colour Gamut (ECG) technology, enabling enhanced colour consistency, greater design flexibility and improved print performance.

Demonstrating its commitment to innovation, Veepee Graphics Lanka became one of the first trade shops in Asia to adopt advanced screening technologies, setting new standards in print quality.

The company’s excellence has received international recognition, including accolades at the FPLMA Awards in Australia. Its leadership has also been acknowledged, with Director Nalani Madhushani recently receiving the Factory Person of the Year Award under PrintWeek Asia’s “Women to Watch” segment.

Through its contribution to the printing and packaging sector, Veepee Graphics Lanka continues to support economic growth by helping businesses enhance their competitiveness through innovative and reliable solutions.
