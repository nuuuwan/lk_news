Meat shops, casinos and race bookies to close during Vesak week

Colombo, May 27 (Daily Mirror) - The Public Administration, Provincial Councils and Local Government Ministry has announced that meat shops, slaughterhouses, race bookies, casinos and clubs will be closed during the State Vesak festival week.

According to an official statement, the closures will be in effect from today (27) until June 2, in line with observances for Vesak.

The ministry further said that the slaughter of animals for meat, the sale of meat at butcher shops and supermarkets, and the operation of race bookies, casinos and clubs will be strictly prohibited islandwide on May 30, May 31 and June 1.

Authorities said the measures have been introduced to facilitate religious observances and maintain the sanctity of the Vesak period across the country.
