24 illegal online gambling websites blocked

Colombo, August 5 (Daily Mirror) - Access to 24 illegal online gambling and betting websites operating without valid licences in Sri Lanka has been blocked with immediate effect, the Finance, Planning and Economic Development Ministry said.

The move was taken following a notification issued by the Gambling Regulatory Authority, an agency under the Ministry, to the Telecommunications Regulatory Commission of Sri Lanka (TRCSL) under the provisions of the Gambling Regulatory Authority Act, No. 17 of 2025.

Accordingly, the TRCSL has directed all telecommunications service providers to restrict access to the identified websites from within Sri Lanka.

The Gambling Regulatory Authority has identified operating or promoting online gambling services without a valid licence issued under Sections 15, 16 and 18 of the Act as an offence under Section 44.

The Ministry urged the public to refrain from participating in or investing in illegal online gambling activities, warning of potential financial risks.

It said further measures would be taken in collaboration with relevant regulatory authorities to strengthen gambling regulation, curb illegal online gambling operations and safeguard the financial security of the public.
