Elderly giraffe dies at Dehiwala Zoo

Colombo, Aug. 19 (Daily Mirror) - An elderly female giraffe at the Dehiwala National Zoological Gardens died last night, the Department of National Zoological Gardens said.

The giraffe was approaching 30 years of age, which officials described as a remarkably long lifespan. While giraffes in the wild generally have shorter lifespans, animals under professional care can live longer due to access to consistent nutrition, veterinary attention and protection from threats.

Officials said the giraffe had not suffered from any previously reported critical illness. It was reported that the animal's death was likely due to natural causes associated with advanced age. A post-mortem examination is scheduled to be conducted today, August 19, to determine the exact cause of death.

The Department of National Zoological Gardens said the animal had received comprehensive care throughout its life, including a balanced diet, clean living conditions and regular veterinary monitoring.

Meanwhile, as part of its ongoing animal welfare, biodiversity conservation and zoological programmes, the department plans to introduce two new pairs of giraffes to the Dehiwala Zoo before the end of this year.
