Deputy Health Minister invites investors to build pharmaceutical export hub in  Sri Lanka

Health and Mass Media Deputy Minister Dr. Hansaka Wijemuni has extended an open invitation to both local and international investors to utilise Sri Lanka as a strategic hub for pharmaceutical manufacturing, eyeing expansion into regional and global markets.

Speaking at the Annual General Meeting of the Sri Lanka Pharmaceutical Manufacturers’ Association, Dr. Wijemuni emphasised that establishing a robust local manufacturing footprint presents lucrative opportunities well beyond merely fulfilling the domestic healthcare needs.

“I would like to invite more local and international investors to consider Sri Lanka as a destination for pharmaceutical manufacturing,” he stated.

Dr. Wijemuni stressed that the government authorities are focused on striking a critical balance between protecting the public funds and fostering private industry growth.

“Our objective is clear: we must safeguard public investment while also creating a fair, predictable environment for genuine investors,” he affirmed.

He further highlighted that a sustainable local manufacturing sector must ultimately thrive on market competitiveness rather than relying entirely on protectionist measures.

Dr. Wijemuni called on the industry to continuously improve operational efficiency, embrace innovation and adopt global best practices.  Outlining a mutually beneficial vision for the healthcare sector, he remarked, “Patients deserve medicine of the highest quality. The government deserves value for public expenditure. Manufacturers deserve a transparent, consistent and stable policy environment that rewards quality and efficiency.”
