Yoshitha trial adjourned to  26 Nov. as accused cites ill health

The Colombo High Court yesterday postponed further proceedings in the money laundering case against Yoshitha Rajapaksa to 26 November, after the accused failed to appear citing a back ailment.

High Court Judge Udesh Ranatunga fixed the new date for the trial to resume.

Rajapaksa is charged under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act with acquiring assets through unlawful means.

President’s Counsel Sampath Mendis, appearing for the accused, told Court his client was unwell with a back-related condition and could not attend. He tendered a medical certificate in support.

Deputy Solicitor General Janaka Bandara, appearing for the prosecution, said the State had hoped to wrap up its case yesterday. Only three prosecution witnesses remain, he said, and all three would be summoned for the next hearing.

The Attorney General filed the case, alleging Rajapaksa violated the money laundering law by spending over Rs. 730 million to buy five parcels of land in Dehiwala and Ratmalana.
