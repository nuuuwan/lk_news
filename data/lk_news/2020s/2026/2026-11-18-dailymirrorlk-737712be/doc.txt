Air quality turns unhealthy in several areas; CEA advises N95 masks

Colombo, Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - The Central Environmental Authority (CEA) has advised sensitive individuals to wear N95 face masks as air quality in several parts of the country deteriorated to unhealthy levels yesterday (18).

According to air quality readings recorded at 9.16 a.m. on Wednesday, November 18, unhealthy air quality levels were reported in Battaramulla, Kilinochchi, Mannar, Mullaitivu, Chilaw, Jaffna, Anuradhapura, Akkaraipattu and Nilaveli Beach in Trincomalee.

The CEA said increased levels of suspended particulate matter, including PM10 and PM2.5, had been detected in several areas due to wind currents carrying pollutants from outside Sri Lanka.

The authority warned that the prevailing air quality conditions could affect sensitive individuals and were expected to continue over the coming days.

It said it was continuously monitoring the situation and urged the public to follow health advice issued by the Ministry of Health.

Air quality updates are available through the National Air Quality Website at aq.cea.lk.
