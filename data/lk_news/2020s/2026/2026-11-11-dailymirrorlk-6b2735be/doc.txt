No electricity tariff revision for final quarter of 2026

Colombo, November 11 (Daily Mirror) - The Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka (PUCSL) has decided not to revise electricity tariffs for the final quarter of 2026, despite an increase in the cost of supplying electricity.

Accordingly, the electricity tariff rates in effect during the third quarter of 2026 will remain unchanged until the end of the year.

The Commission said the cost data initially submitted by the National System Operator (NSO) indicated a 2.88 per cent increase in the cost of supplying electricity during the final quarter.

The NSO submitted the initial cost estimates on October 6, although the submission was due before August 17, 2026. Updated cost data was subsequently submitted on November 9.

The Commission said it had considered the updated cost data and views and suggestions received from stakeholders during the mandatory public consultation before reaching its decision.

The consultation covered estimated electricity generation costs and the generation mix, energy costs, fuel prices, generation capacity costs, battery storage system capacity costs, adjustments to past revenue surpluses or deficits, excess revenue of the Electricity Distribution Company and the proposed percentage of tariff revision.

Under Section 29 of the Sri Lanka Electricity Act No. 36 of 2024, the NSO is required to submit estimated electricity supply costs for future periods to the PUCSL for review. The Commission is responsible for determining electricity tariffs after examining the cost data and obtaining public views.

The public consultation was conducted in accordance with Section 17 of the Public Utilities Commission of Sri Lanka Act No. 35 of 2002 and Section 29 of the Sri Lanka Electricity Act No. 36 of 2024.

Following consideration of the submissions, the Commission decided that no tariff revision was necessary for the final quarter of 2026.
