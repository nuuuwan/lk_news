Jayaweera, Indian envoy discuss economy and politics

MP Dilith Jayaweera (right) with Indian High Commissioner Santosh Jha

Sarvajana Balaya Party Leader and MP Dilith Jayaweera has held talks with Indian High Commissioner Santosh Jha on the economy, current political developments and bilateral cooperation between Sri Lanka and India.

The meeting took place at Jayaweera’s office, the MP said in a social media post. “I was pleased to welcome the High Commissioner of India, Santosh Jha, to my office. We had a valuable discussion on the economy, current political developments, bilateral cooperation between Sri Lanka and India and a number of issues of mutual concern,” Jayaweera said.
