Sureshni … going solo

Singer Sureshni Wanigasuriya continues to be seen and heard but is now pursuing a fresh chapter in her music journey.

Known for her soft, expressive voice, she’s now performing as a solo artiste.

She also mentioned that if the requirement is for a duo or trio, then they perform under her previous name of ‘Europa.’

Sureshni is seen in action quite frequently, these days, but she has no contract at the moment that would see her doing her thing, on a regular basis, as a solo artiste, duo or trio.

Sureshni’s repertoire include English, Sinhala, and Hindi songs, and Gospel songs, as well, and she has performed with many leading local bands, including Aquarius and Sohan & The X-Periments.

Yes, her voice has touched many hearts, and there’s more music ahead.
