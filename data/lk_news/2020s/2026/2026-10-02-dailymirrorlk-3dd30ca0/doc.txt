Determine whether attack near courts was aimed at RW

Colombo, Oct. 2 (Daily Mirror) - The United National Party (UNP) yesterday called for a free and fair investigation into Wednesday’s incident outside the Magistrate’s Court, where a cameraman allegedly attacked a security officer providing protection to former President Ranil Wickremesinghe.

The party said law enforcement authorities should determine the motive behind the attack.

UNP Chairman Wajira Abeywardena, rejecting Deputy Minister Sunil Watagala’s remarks that the incident was minor and not serious, said a free and fair investigation was essential to establish what had prompted the attack.

“We feel that the Deputy Minister’s comments are an indication that a proper investigation will not be conducted into the incident. This is a serious incident as it is the first time we have seen a security officer being attacked. The motive behind the attack should be determined,” he told a media briefing.

“A weapon, regardless of what it is, can be used in different ways. It can be used for self-defence or to kill someone. Therefore, it is not appropriate to come out with different interpretations about it,” he added.

Former Minister Rajitha Senaratne said the incident raised questions as to whether it could have been a rehearsal for a more serious attack targeting former President Wickremesinghe.

“We heard the Deputy Minister saying Wednesday’s incident was a minor one. One wonders what he meant by serious attacks. Does he refer to the insurgencies in 1971 and 1988/89 as serious attacks?” Dr. Senaratne questioned.

The security officer injured in Wednesday’s attack was admitted to hospital that evening.
