Indices close on a mixed note

The ASPI closed in green as a result of price gains in counters such as Royal Ceramics, Dialog Axiata and Prime Lands Residencies with the turnover crossing Rs. 3.8 Bn. Meanwhile, the S&P SL20 edged down. High net worth & institutional investor participation was noted in R I L Property, Dialog Axiata and John Keells Holdings accounting for 55.1% of the turnover. Mixed interest was

observed in Commercial Credit & Finance, Ceylon Cold Stores and Sampath Bank whilst retail interest was noted

in HNB Finance, Browns Investments and Beruwala Resorts. Furthermore, foreigners remained active closing as net sellers.

Retailing sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to RIL Property PLC) whilst the sector index gained 1.02%. The share price of RIL Property PLC increased by Rs 0.50 (1.97%) to close at Rs 25.90. Telecommunication Services sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Dialog Axiata) whilst the sector index increased by 1.77%. The share price

of Dialog Axiata gained Rs 0.40 (0.90%) to close at Rs 44.70. John Keells Holdings, Commercial Credit & Finance and C T Holdings were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of John Keells Holdings moved down by Rs 0.10 (0.50%) to close at Rs 20.00. The share price of Commercial Credit & Finance recorded a loss of Rs 0.75 (0.69%) to close at Rs 108.50. The share price of C T Holdings declined by Rs 8.00 (1.57%) to close at Rs 500.25.
