Two Sri Lankans killed in road accident in South Korea; repatriation efforts underway

Two Sri Lankan nationals have died following a road accident in Hwaseong-si, South Korea, on November 26, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism confirmed.

The Sri Lankan Embassy in Seoul said it was informed by the Hwaseong Seobu Police Station on November 27 that the accident had occurred at around 9.00 p.m. on November 26 in Jangan-myeon, Hwaseong-si.

The deceased have been identified as 39-year-old Dehipitiya Vidanelage Anil Kumara Wijerathne, a resident of Ranwala, Godakawela, and 31-year-old Maheshwaran Prasanthan, a resident of Halgranoya, Walapane.

The Embassy said it was making arrangements to repatriate the remains of the two deceased Sri Lankans to the country.
