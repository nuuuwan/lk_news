Health Ministry still silent over Ratnapura patient’s death

Colombo, Aug. 11 (Daily Mirror) - After over a month, the Health Ministry has yet to respond to a complaint seeking an independent investigation into the death of a 21-year-old woman at the Ratnapura General Hospital, amid allegations of possible medical negligence.

The woman’s father has urged the Health Minister to immediately launch an independent inquiry into his daughter’s death after she was admitted to Ward 13 of the hospital on July 3 with pneumonia.

According to his complaint, the patient’s condition deteriorated despite repeated requests from the family for medical attention. He alleged that her complaints of severe chest pain, mouth bleeding, persistent diarrhoea and worsening breathing difficulties were not adequately addressed.

He claimed that her oxygen saturation had dropped to 85% on admission and that, although oxygen was administered, the treating consultant was not promptly informed and no urgent escalation of care was initiated.

The patient later suffered a cardiac arrest in the ward and was transferred to the Intensive Care Unit, where she subsequently died.

The father has called for a comprehensive investigation into the treatment provided, including a review of medical records, nursing notes, observation charts, medication records and investigation results, as well as statements from the medical and nursing staff involved.

He said the Judicial Medical Officer had given pneumonia as the provisional cause of death, pending histopathological examination.

Ratnapura Teaching Hospital Director Dr A.D.H. Jayathilake earlier confirmed that the hospital had received the complaint and said information was being gathered and the circumstances surrounding the death were being investigated.

However, sources told the Daily Mirror that although discussions regarding the matter have begun at the hospital level, the Health Ministry is yet to respond to the allegations or announce any action regarding an independent investigation.
