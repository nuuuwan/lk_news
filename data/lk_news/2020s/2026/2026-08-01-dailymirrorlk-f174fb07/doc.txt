NPP decision-making process unclear, says Lal Wijenayake

Colombo, August 1 (Daily Mirror) - Senior lawyer Lal Wijenayake has questioned the National People's Power's (NPP) internal decision-making process, saying he is accustomed to the consultative tradition of leftist politics but is uncertain how decisions are taken within the ruling coalition.

Speaking to the Daily Mirror, Wijenayake, a long-time member of the Lanka Sama Samaja Party (LSSP), said he had spent nearly 50 years in a political culture where major decisions were preceded by extensive discussions.

"I have been in the Lanka Sama Samaja Party for about 50 years. I am therefore used to a system where decisions are taken after wide-ranging discussions with everyone's views taken into account," he said.

Referring to the government's proposal to extend the retirement age of superior court judges, he said he was unsure whether the decision had been discussed or approved by the relevant decision-making bodies of the NPP.

He said the matter was later discussed at a meeting of the National Operations Committee, where President Anura Kumara Dissanayake explained the rationale behind the proposal. However, Wijenayake maintained that he was not aware whether the Leadership Council or the Executive Committee had formally endorsed the move before it was announced.

Wijenayake said his remarks were intended to encourage broader internal discussion and collective decision-making, rather than to criticise the government.
