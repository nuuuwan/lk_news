October tourism earnings outpace arrivals

Sri Lanka’s tourism earnings grew faster than tourist arrivals in October, rising 3.5 percent to US$ 192.7 million from US$ 186.1 million a year earlier, while arrivals increased 2.1 percent, according to Central Bank and Sri Lanka Tourism Development Authority (SLTDA) data.

As a result, earnings per tourist rose 1.4 percent to about US$ 1,143 from roughly US$ 1,127.

That contrasts with the year so far. January-October earnings fell 8.3 percent to US$ 2,437.9 million from US$ 2,659.0 million, a far steeper decline than the 1.5 percent drop in arrivals to 1,862,304, pulling average earnings per visitor down 6.9 percent to about US$ 1,309. The Central Bank notes that the SLTDA revised its methodology for estimating monthly tourism earnings in May 2026.

October’s 168,625 arrivals were the highest ever for the month, surpassing the 2018 record of 153,123, and marked the first year-on-year gain since May.

India drove the increase, with arrivals surging 29.3 percent to 62,211 or 36.9 percent of the total. China rose 3.0 percent and Australia 7.7 percent, while the United Kingdom, Germany and Russia fell 4.1 percent, 17.7 percent and 41.3 percent, respectively. Excluding India, arrivals fell 9.1 percent. Asia and the Pacific accounted for 61.9 percent of visitors and Europe 31.9 percent.

Meanwhile, the sector needs a further US$ 1.76 billion in November and December, or about US$ 881 million a month, more than four times October’s earnings,to reach the revised US$ 4.2 billion target announced by the Tourism Promotion Bureau in August.
