Fishermen warned of developing low-pressure system

Colombo, Nov. 10 (Daily Mirror) - A low-pressure area is likely to form over the Southeast Bay of Bengal around November 13, the Department of Meteorology said.

The system is expected to move northwestwards and strengthen into a well-marked low-pressure area within the next 24 hours.

Winds of 40–50 kmph, with gusts of up to 60 kmph, are expected over the Andaman Sea and Southeast Bay of Bengal on November 14 and 15.

The Department has advised naval and fishing communities operating in these sea areas, particularly multi-day fishing boats, to remain alert and follow future weather updates and warnings.
