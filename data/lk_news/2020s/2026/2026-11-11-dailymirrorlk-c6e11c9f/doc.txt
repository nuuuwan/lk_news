Sri Lanka reach 404/4 as De Silva and Kusal Mendis hit centuries

No.11 (Mirror Sports) - Sri Lanka moved to 404/4 at stumps on Day 3 of the first Test against Pakistan in Rawalpindi, with Dhananjaya de Silva and Kusal Mendis scoring centuries before bad light brought play to an early close.

Sri Lanka continued their impressive batting performance against Pakistan, reaching 404/4 at stumps on the third day of the first Test in Rawalpindi on Wednesday, with only 30 runs remaining to wipe out the hosts' first-innings lead.

Dhananjaya de Silva remained unbeaten on 167, while Kusal Mendis was not out on 117 as the pair kept Sri Lanka firmly in contention with an unbeaten partnership.

Kamindu Mendis contributed 58 runs, while Lahiru Udara added 42 to Sri Lanka's total.

However, poor light forced an early end to the day's play, leaving Sri Lanka just 30 runs behind Pakistan's first-innings total of 434/9 declared.

For Pakistan, debutants Saqib Khan and Muhammad Abbas claimed two wickets each.

Pakistan had declared their first innings at 434/9 on the second day, with Saud Shakeel top-scoring with 93. Irfan Minhas (52), Tayyab Arif (57), Muhammad Taha (51) and Babar Azam (50) also made valuable contributions.
