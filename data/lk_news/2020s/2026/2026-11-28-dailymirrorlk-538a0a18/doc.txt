Secondary market trading remains muted amid stable yields

The secondary market witnessed another subdued trading session yesterday, with activity and volumes remaining moderate. Consequently, the overall yield curve remained broadly unchanged.

Among the actively traded maturities, shorter-tenor bonds saw the 01.05.2028 maturity trading at 10.60%, while the 15.09.2029 and 15.10.2029 maturities recorded yields of 10.95%. In the 2030 segment, the 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 maturities traded between 11.30% to 11.25%.

Further along the curve, both the 01.10.2032 and 15.12.2032 maturities traded at 11.60%, while the 15.01.2033 and 01.06.2033 maturities recorded yields of 11.80% and 11.85%, respectively.

On the external front, the LKR depreciated against the USD, standing at LKR 332.99/USD compared to LKR 332.58/USD recorded previously. Market liquidity in the banking system expanded to LKR 472.44bn compared to LKR 461.75bn seen previously.
