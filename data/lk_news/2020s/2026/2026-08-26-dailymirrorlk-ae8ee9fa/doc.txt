Probe underway into viral video from Ambalangoda Police Station

Police have launched an investigation into a video circulating on social media showing an incident that allegedly took place inside the Ambalangoda Police Station. The inquiry was ordered by Inspector General of Police (IGP).

According to police, the investigation is being conducted under the supervision of the Deputy Inspector General of Police in charge of the Galle Division and is being led by a senior police officer serving outside the Elpitiya Police Division, under which the Ambalangoda Police Station operates.

The video shows a person behaving aggressively inside the police station while claiming that his father had been assaulted by police officers. The footage has since been widely circulated on social media.

Police said the elderly man seen in the video had been arrested by officers from the Ambalangoda Police Station on the night of August 24 after he was allegedly found intoxicated and behaving disorderly on a road. He was subsequently taken to the police station.

According to police, the man attempted to flee at the time of his arrest but fell while trying to escape. Police said the bleeding injuries visible in the video were sustained as a result of the fall.

The man’s son has alleged that his father was severely assaulted by police officers after being taken into custody on the night of August 24. He claimed that his father was stopped about 100 metres from their home and taken to the police station after he was unable to produce his National Identity Card. According to the son, his father offered to take the officers to their nearby home to produce the identity card, but was instead verbally abused and assaulted. He further alleged that his father was assaulted inside the police station, threatened with a firearm and prevented from obtaining immediate medical treatment.

The man was taken to Balapitiya Hospital by Ambalangoda police officers on August 25, where he was examined by a Judicial Medical Officer. He was subsequently produced before the Additional Magistrate of Balapitiya.

The Additional Magistrate ordered the man’s release on bail. The investigation into the incident is continuing.
