Around 30 houses submerged as Hatton Oya overflows

Colombo, Nov. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Around 30 houses in the Pilihatara area of Hatton were submerged after the Hatton Oya overflowed yesterday (9), forcing residents to flee their homes.

The affected residents were rescued with the assistance of neighbours and other local residents.

Residents said the area had been repeatedly affected by flooding due to a blockage in a canal that flows into the Hatton Oya, causing water to overflow into nearby houses.

They said rainwater mixed with mud had risen to a height of around three to four feet inside their homes, damaging household goods and other personal belongings.

The residents urged the authorities to take immediate action to clear the blocked canal and prevent further flooding.
