Mahindra Ideal Finance partners with Toyota Lanka on vehicle financing

Toyota Lanka Chairman Masaaki Kawabata and Mahindra Ideal Finance Managing Director Mufaddal Choonia with the signed MOU

Mahindra Ideal Finance PLC (MIFL) has signed an MOU with Toyota Lanka (Private) Limited, the exclusive dealer and distributor of Toyota and HINO vehicles in Sri Lanka, to deliver dedicated vehicle financing solutions to Toyota Lanka customers island-wide.

Under the partnership, customers purchasing vehicles from Toyota Lanka will be able to access financing through MIFL’s Leasing, Ideal Draft and Vehicle Loan Scheme, at competitive interest rates and processing fees.

MIFL operates a network of 38 branches across Sri Lanka, with a growing focus on vehicle and asset financing. The partnership extends that reach into Toyota Lanka’s dealer network, placing structured financing directly where customers make their purchase decisions.

Signed at Toyota Lanka’s head office in Wattala, the MOU was formalised by MIFL Managing Director & CEO Mufaddal Choonia and Chief Operating Officer Rohitha Bandusena, and Toyota Lanka Managing Director & CEO Manohara Atukorala.

“This partnership with Toyota Lanka is part of the deliberate direction we are taking as a business, deepening our presence in vehicle financing and standing closer to where our customers make their biggest purchase decisions. Accessibility and reliability remain at the core of what we do, and this collaboration extends that commitment to Toyota Lanka’s customer base,” said MIFL Managing Director & CEO, Mufaddal Choonia.

Toyota Lanka Managing Director Atukorala said, “At Toyota Lanka, our vision of ‘Mobility for All’ is centred on making mobility and vehicle ownership more accessible to people across Sri Lanka. This partnership with MIFL is another important step towards that vision, enabling us to reach more customers and provide them with a trusted and accessible financing option.”
