‘Bus Lalith’ remanded until December 4

Colombo, Nov. 19 (Daily Mirror) - Organised crime figure and alleged drug trafficker Lalith Kannangara, alias “Bus Lalith”, was further remanded until December 4 by the Avissawella Magistrate's Court today (19).

Avissawella Magistrate Pamoda Jayasekara issued the order after the suspect was produced before the court.
