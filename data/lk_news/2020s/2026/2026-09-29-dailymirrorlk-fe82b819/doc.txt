SC orders Fowzie to pay Rs.6.6m over misuse of government vehicle with public apology

Colombo, Sep. 29 (Daily Mirror) - The Supreme Court today ordered former Minister of Disaster Management A.H.M. Fowzie to pay Rs. 6.6 million to the Consolidated Fund after holding that he had violated the fundamental right to equality guaranteed under Article 12(1) of the Constitution by misusing a government vehicle for his personal benefit.

The Court also directed Fowzie to publish a public apology in three newspapers over the misuse of public property. The publication is to include a photograph of the vehicle.

A three-judge Bench comprising Chief Justice Preethi Padman Surasena and Justices Janak De Silva and Arjuna Obeyesekere made the determination in a Fundamental Rights petition filed by former Chairman of the Ceylon Chamber of Commerce Chandra Jayaratne.

Delivering the judgment, Justice Janak De Silva directed Fowzie to pay Rs.1,664,334.93 to the Consolidated Fund within one month as compensation for expenses incurred in connection with the vehicle. He was further ordered to pay Rs.5 million as exemplary compensation in respect of acts of bribery and corruption established before the Court.
