Haiti PM praises Sri Lankan troops in anti-gang operation

Haiti Prime Minister Alix Didier Fils-Aimé has praised Sri Lankan troops serving with the international Gang Suppression Force (GSF) for their role in a joint anti-gang operation with the Haitian National Police (PNH).

The pre-dawn operation on 15 November targeted strongholds of the armed Kokorat San Ras gang in Tibwadòm and Carnifice, in the Gros-Morne area of the Artibonite region. Security forces cleared and secured a church and a school reportedly occupied by gang members.

The joint forces recovered firearms, ammunition and motorcycles, and dismantled barricades and trenches that had blocked National Road 5. The road has reportedly been reopened after being inaccessible for several years.

Fils-Aimé commended the operation in a post on X, acknowledging the PNH, the GSF and the Sri Lankan personnel.

Sri Lankan troops with the GSF are deployed at operational hubs including Port-au-Prince, Gonaïves and Estrella. Their duties include armoured vehicle patrols, protecting key supply routes and supporting local authorities in operations against armed gangs.
