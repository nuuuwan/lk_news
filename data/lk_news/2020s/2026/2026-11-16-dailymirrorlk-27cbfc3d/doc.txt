Welikanda visually impaired couple deprived of their Aswasuma

Welikanda, Nov 16 (Daily Mirror) - A visually impaired couple in Welikanda, who walk nearly 40 kilometres a day selling lottery tickets to support their two children, staged a silent protest outside their home today after their monthly Rs. 5,000 Aswesuma allowance had been abruptly withdrawn.

K. Jagath Prasanna Kumara, 44, and his wife P.W. Sugandhika Manel, 41, placed a protest sign on the wall in front of their house, hoping to draw the attention of the President and the authorities to their plight.

The couple, both parents of two school-aged children aged twelve and seven, walk roughly forty kilometres a day and sell about two hundred lottery tickets to make ends meet.

According to Jagath Prasanna, they had been receiving the Rs. 5,000 maintenance allowance for about a year until a village committee deprived them of it around two months ago. They raised the matter with the Welikanda Divisional Secretariat and the political authority in the area, but say no solution has been forthcoming, prompting today's silent protest.
