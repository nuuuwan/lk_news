Toyota Lanka expands Gampaha facility

Toyota Lanka (Pvt.) Ltd recently opened the expanded Toyota Lanka Gampaha facility, now bringing together brand-new Toyota vehicle sales, Toyota genuine parts and Tyre Zone under one roof.

The expansion provides the customers in the region with greater convenience and easier access to a wider range of Toyota Lanka’s products and mobility solutions.

The grand opening ceremony was held in the presence of Toyota Lanka Chairman Masaaki Kawabata and Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer Manohara Atukorala.

The occasion was also attended by distinguished personalities from the Gampaha area, business partners, financial partners, customers and other invited guests.

Atukorala highlighted that Toyota Lanka’s relationship with its customers goes beyond the purchase of a vehicle, with a continued focus on supporting them throughout their ownership journey.

He emphasised that the expansion in Gampaha is another important step in bringing Toyota Lanka closer to the Gampaha community and supporting the customers throughout their mobility journey.

The expanded Gampaha facility also reflects Toyota Lanka’s continuous efforts to strengthen its islandwide branch network and move closer to its customers. By developing its regional presence and infrastructure, Toyota Lanka is working towards expanding its network to 30 facilities by end-2026 financial year, further improving access to Toyota products, services and mobility solutions across the country.

Through this continued expansion, Toyota Lanka remains committed to enhancing customer convenience and delivering dependable mobility solutions across Sri Lanka.
