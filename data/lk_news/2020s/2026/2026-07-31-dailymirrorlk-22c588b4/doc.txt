Jaffna journalists protest demanding justice for murdered media personnel

July 31 (Daily Mirror) - Journalists in Jaffna staged a protest today demanding justice for murdered journalists and calling for impartial and credible investigations into the killings of media personnel, attacks on journalists, and violence against media institutions.

The protest was held on Friday in front of the Jaffna District Secretariat.

The demonstration coincided with the two-day visit of Dr. Kaushalya Ariyarathna, Deputy Minister of Mass Media, who is participating in several official events in Jaffna. As part of the visit, the Deputy Minister was scheduled to attend a seminar for journalists at the Jaffna District Secretariat.

In protest over the continued lack of justice and to demand proper investigations into crimes committed against journalists, members of the Jaffna Press Club boycotted the Deputy Minister's event and instead participated in the demonstration.
