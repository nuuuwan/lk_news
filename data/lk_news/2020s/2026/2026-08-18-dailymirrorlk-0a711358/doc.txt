Tourism activities at Nildiya Pokuna to come under Wildlife Department regulation

Colombo, Aug. 18 (Daily Mirror) - Tourism activities at Nildiya Pokuna, located within the Ravana Ella Sanctuary, are to be formally regulated and conducted under the supervision of the Wildlife Conservation Department.

Nildiya Pokuna, situated inside an internal cave complex in the Karandagolla Grama Niladhari Division of the Ella Divisional Secretariat in the Badulla District, is a rare natural water source known for its unique geographical features.

The site has become a popular destination among both local and foreign tourists. However, authorities have identified the need to properly monitor and regulate tourism activities to ensure the conservation of this sensitive natural resource.

A technical committee comprising university lecturers and representatives of relevant government institutions was appointed to study the matter and provide recommendations. The committee subsequently submitted its report on the management of tourism activities at the site.

Based on the committee's recommendations, regulations were drafted to formally regulate tourism activities at Nildiya Pokuna. The regulations have also been reviewed and confirmed by the Legal Draftsman.

Accordingly, Cabinet approval has been granted for a proposal submitted by the Minister of Environment to publish the relevant regulations in the Gazette under the provisions of the Wildlife and Flora Ordinance.

The move is aimed at allowing tourism activities to continue at Nildiya Pokuna while ensuring the protection and long-term conservation of the unique natural environment.
