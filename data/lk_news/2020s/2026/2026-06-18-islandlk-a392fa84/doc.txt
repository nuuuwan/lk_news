AI Catalyst Symposium 2026 urges shift from ambition to execution

Sri Lankan businesses must move beyond artificial intelligence pilot projects and focus on delivering tangible returns, delegates were told at the AI Catalyst Symposium Sri Lanka 2026, held in Colombo.The event, hosted by NeenOpal in partnership with Amazon Web Services (AWS), brought together business leaders, technology executives, and AI practitioners under the theme “From AI Ambition to AI Execution.”

While AI adoption has become a strategic priority across industries, many organisations continue to struggle with scaling initiatives beyond the experimentation phase, speakers noted. The symposium stressed that the real measure of success lies not in adoption alone, but in measurable outcomes – including operational efficiency, improved decision-making, enhanced customer experiences, revenue growth, and cost optimisation.

A recurring message from the sessions was that realising return on investment from AI requires more than technology spending. Experts called for a cultural shift, upskilling of employees, and alignment of leadership, processes, and business objectives around AI-driven transformation. Enterprises that adapt their operating models are significantly better positioned to unlock long-term value, they said.

The symposium also explored emerging trends such as Agentic AI, enterprise automation, AI governance, and workforce readiness, while showcasing local examples of successful AI implementation.

As Sri Lanka eyes a stronger foothold in the digital economy, the consensus was clear: organisations that combine strategic AI investments with strong leadership, cultural readiness, and a clear focus on business outcomes will be best placed to realise meaningful ROI and secure a lasting competitive advantage.
