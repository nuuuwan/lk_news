CJ being blackmailed: Opposition MP

Colombo, Sep. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Chief Justice Preethi Padman Surasena is being blackmailed by powerful persons in the government, Opposition MP Amila Prasad told Parliament today.

He said the Chief Justice is going to be dealt with and will be sent to jail if he fails to come up with the judgments which the government wants.

The MP said both the CJ and his son will be sent to jail if he fails to deliver what the government wants.

"This is the threat which the Chief Justice is faced with today. It is essential to save him from this threat," he said.
