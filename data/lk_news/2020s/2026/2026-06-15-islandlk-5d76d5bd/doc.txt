Stock market benchmark index rises in the wake of M-E peace deal news

At the time of closure yesterday the CSE benchmark index rose 3.43 percent following news of a US-Iran peace deal, brokers said.

‘The positive sentiment was seen across all sectors due to the peace deal between the US and Iran and also the oil price dropped in the world market, market analysts said.

The All Share Price Index rose 743.22 points to close at 22,380; while the S&P SL20 rose 3.28 percent, or 197.47 points, at 6,224.

The top seven crossings were: Amana Takaful 7.5 million shares crossed to the tune of Rs 173 million and its shares traded at Rs 23, Asiri Surgical Hospitals 9.2 million shares crossed to the tune of Rs 169 million; its shares traded at Rs 18.50, HNB 250,000 shares crossed for Rs 101 million and its shares sold at Rs 404.

Aitken Spence 190,000 shares crossed to the tune of Rs 98 million; its shares traded at Rs 143, Distilleries 1.7 million shares crossed to the tune of Rs 97 million; its shares traded at Rs 57.50, Ceylon Land Equity 4.4 million shares crossed for Rs 46 million; its shares sold at Rs 10.50 and JKH two million shares crossed for Rs 41 million; its shares sold at Rs 20.40.

In the retail market companies that mainly contributed to the turnover were; Asiri Surgical Rs 274 million (15 million shares traded), Prime Lands Residencies Rs 198 million (4.2 million shares traded),Commercial Bank Rs 147 million(700,000 shares traded) Access Engineering Rs 132 million (1.7 million shares traded), Softlogic Capital Rs 109 million (7.7 million shares traded), CCS Rs 80 million (550,000 shares traded) and JKH Rs 74 million (3.6 million shares traded). During the day 206 million share volumes changed hands in 34511 transactions.

Banking sector counters, especially Amana Takaful, HNB and Commercial Bank performed well while in the manufacturing sector, especially JKH, also actively participated.

Top positive contributors to the ASPI were Sampath Bank (up Rs 5.50 at 145.25), Commercial Bank (up Rs 7 at 209.25), JKH (up 50 cents at Rs 20.50 ), Melstacorp (up Rs 5.25 at 185.50) and Dialog Axiata (up Rs 1.70 at 45.60).

Senkadagala Finance announced a dividend of Rs 2.8 a share. The stock closed up Rs 16.550 at 417.50.

Yesterday the rupee was quoted at Rs 332.00/334.00 to the US dollar in the spot market on Monday, stronger from Rs 335.50/336.00 Friday, while bond yields dropped drastically as the market rallied after Pakistan announced a deal between the US and Iran, dealers said.

The telegraphic transfer rates for Sri Lanka’s rupee against the US dollar were; 327.5000 buying, 336.50 selling; the euro was 377.6064 selling, 391.5234 buying; and the pound was 439.3154 buying, 453.3610 selling.
