Government to reduce taxes further, says President

President Anura Kumara Dissanayake has announced that the government plans to introduce further reductions in certain taxes in the upcoming Budget, while ensuring that state revenue targets are maintained.

Speaking on the country’s economic performance, the President said the government had achieved its revenue targets without introducing any new taxes since assuming office.

“We have not imposed any new taxes since coming to power. In fact, we have significantly reduced some existing taxes. We will reduce certain taxes further in the upcoming Budget,” he said.

The President emphasised that the proposed tax reductions would be implemented without compromising the government’s revenue targets.

He noted that previous administrations had frequently failed to achieve their projected revenue targets, with actual collections often reaching only around 90% of the expected amounts.

However, he said the current government had managed to exceed its revenue expectations while maintaining a primary budget surplus.

According to the President, maintaining a primary budget surplus is essential to reducing the country’s debt burden over time and strengthening its economic stability.

He also acknowledged the economic challenges faced by the public, particularly the impact of rising inflation on household expenses.

The President noted that increasing prices of goods and services had placed additional financial pressure on households, underscoring the need to provide relief while maintaining fiscal discipline.
