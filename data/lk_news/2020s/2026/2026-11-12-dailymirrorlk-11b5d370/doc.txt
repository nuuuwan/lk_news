Ditwah triggered landslides a possibility during the coming days : NBRO

Colombo, Nov. 12 (Daily Mirror) - Landslides could be triggered in areas which had become unstable following the Ditwah cyclone, The Nation Building Research Organisation ( NBRO) warned yesterday.

NBRO Geologist Wasantha Senadeera speaking at a media briefing said that landslide warnings had been issued for Nagoda in Galle, Dehiowita, Deraniyagala , Bulathkohupitiya and Yatiyantota divisions in Kegalle, Eheliyagoda and Ayagama in the Ratnapura District, Ella in Badulla, Paathadumbara in Kandy, Wellawaya in Moneragala District and Norwood, and Hanguranketha in the Nuwara Eliya.

He said sand erosion could also take place and one might able to see sand mixed with water flowing .

Director Forecast Department of Meteorology Merril Mendis said the disturbance developing over the Bay of Bengal which is expected to turn into a deep depression which will affect Sri Lanka. " The Depression is expected to travel parallel to the Eastern coast of Sri Lanka and this could trigger effect Sri Lanka. Heavy rains can be expected in the Western, North Central, Central and Sabaragamuwa provinces after November 13" he said.

He said Sri Lanka will generally experience wet weather till January next year. " There will be a dry spell from January to April due to El Nino " he added.

General public were advised to heed warnings issued by the Department of Meteorology, NBRO and Disaster Management Centre(DMC). The public is requested to refrain from going on excursions and to avoid bathing in rivers. The DMC revealed that 11 persons had drowned during the last fortnight in Sri Lanka due to them bathing in high risk areas.
