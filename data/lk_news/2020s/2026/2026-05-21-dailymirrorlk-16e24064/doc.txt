Indian Air Force Chief meets Sri Lanka Navy Commander

Air Chief Marshal Amar Preet Singh, the Chief of the Air Staff of Indian Air Force met with the Commander of the Sri Lanka Navy, Vice Admiral Kanchana Banagoda at the Sri Lanka Navy Headquarters on 21 May 26.

Upon his arrival at the Navy Headquarters, the visiting Indian Air Force Chief was received by the Commander Western Naval Area. Subsequently, a ceremonial Guard of Honour was accorded to Air Chief Marshal Singh, in keeping with long-standing naval traditions. Following the ceremonial welcome, the Commander of the Sri Lanka Navy introduced the Navy Chief of Staff and the Board of Management to the Indian Air Force Chief.

During the official interaction that ensued, the Indian Air Force Chief and the Commander of the Navy engaged in a productive discussion on matters of bilateral importance. To mark the significance of this official visit, mementos were also exchanged between the two military dignitaries.
