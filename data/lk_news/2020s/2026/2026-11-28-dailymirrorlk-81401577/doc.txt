Vijitha Herath meets Indian PM Modi in New Delhi

Foreign Minister Vijitha Herath met Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi in New Delhi alongside foreign ministers and heads of delegation from member states of the Indian Ocean Rim Association (IORA).

The meeting took place on the sidelines of the 25th Meeting of the IORA Council of Ministers, Herath said in a post on X.

The meeting brought together representatives of IORA member states to engage with the Indian Prime Minister.
