SLT-MOBITEL launches ‘Lankakonect’ app to support Sri Lankans overseas

SLT-MOBITEL, in partnership with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment, & Tourism, has officially launched the Lankakonect mobile application—a digital complaint management platform designed for Sri Lankans living and working abroad.

Developed for the Ministry’s Consular Affairs Division and the Sri Lanka Bureau of Foreign Employment (SLBFE), the app enables overseas citizens and migrant workers to register, submit, and track grievances in Sinhala, Tamil, and English. Issues covered include employment contract violations, abuse, unpaid wages, and locating loved ones. The platform eliminates long waits and unnecessary travel, allowing complaints to be raised anytime, anywhere—particularly benefiting vulnerable domestic workers who can escalate urgent concerns immediately.

Upon lodging a complaint, officials from SLBFE and the Consular Affairs Division can access the system online and begin resolution processes. Users can track their complaints in real time through the full lifecycle, while management gains a holistic view of progress. The International Labour Organization (ILO) supported the initiative as a Technical Partner, and the project builds on prior digital efforts by the Kuwait Embassy.

The launch comes as Sri Lankan overseas workers contributed a record USD 8.07 billion in remittances in 2025, underscoring their vital role in the national economy. The app honours these contributions while keeping citizens connected to their motherland.

Through Lankakonect, SLT-MOBITEL reaffirms its commitment to the government’s vision of a fully digitised Sri Lanka, using technology to serve its people and strengthen ties between embassies and expatriate communities worldwide.
