HNB Life marks 25 years with CSE opening bell ceremony

HNB Life PLC marked its 25th anniversary with a special market opening bell-ringing ceremony at the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE), celebrating its journey in protecting the lives and financial aspirations of Sri Lankans.

The ceremony brought together HNB Life’s Board of Directors, Executive Committee, management and distinguished guests, along with CSE Chairman Dimuthu Abeyesekera, Chief Executive Officer Rajeeva Bandaranaike and senior officials of the exchange.

Established 25 years ago as HNB Assurance, the company has evolved into HNB Life, reflecting its renewed focus on life insurance and its commitment to helping Sri Lankans secure their financial futures.

Chairman of HNB Life and HNB General Insurance Stuart Chapman said the milestone represented the trust placed in the company by generations of customers, employees, shareholders and business partners.

“Twenty-five years is far more than a milestone. It represents the confidence that generations of customers, employees, shareholders and business partners have placed in HNB Life,” he said, adding that the company remains committed to building a stronger and future-ready organization.

HNB Life Executive Director and Chief Executive Officer Lasitha Wimalaratne said the anniversary celebrated the contribution of customers, employees, Life Planners, parent company Hatton National Bank and other partners who supported the company’s growth.

CSE Chief Executive Officer Rajeeva Bandaranaike congratulated HNB Life on reaching the milestone, noting its contribution to the insurance sector and Sri Lanka’s capital market.
