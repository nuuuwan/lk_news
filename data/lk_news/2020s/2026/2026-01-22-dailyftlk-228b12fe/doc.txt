Home Lands rewards high performers with brand-new vehicles

Home Lands, Sri Lanka’s number one and most trusted real estate developer, has always believed that its greatest strength lies in its people. The dedication, passion, and commitment of the Home Lands team are what drive the company forward, and this year too, the organisation celebrated those who consistently go above and beyond.  Recently, at the luxurious Canterbury Golf City premises, Home Lands honored 25 high-performing employees with brand-new vehicles as a token of appreciation for their hard work and loyalty. The vehicles included BYD SEALION 6 luxury SUVs, high-end Toyota Cabs, and Toyota Raize SUVs, recognising each individual’s exceptional contribution to the company’s growth.

Home Lands Group Chairman and Managing Director Nalin Herath said: “Our team is our biggest asset. Every achievement at Home Lands is made possible because of our people. Recognising their dedication and rewarding their hard work is at the heart of our culture.”

Among the recipients, the Top Sales Performer of 2025, Hiran Yatanwala was especially recognised for exceeding 100 units in sales and was awarded a BYD SEALION 6 luxury SUV for his remarkable achievement.

From completed projects like Canterbury Golf City, Piliyandala - Kahathuduwa, to ongoing developments such as Pentara Residencies, the success of Home Lands has always been built on teamwork and collaboration. By celebrating the efforts of its employees, Home Lands not only rewards excellence but also inspires continued commitment, growth, and a shared vision for the future.

At Home Lands, every team member matters and every achievement is a reflection of the collective power of its people.

Home Lands has achieved multiple wins at the PropertyGuru Asia Property Awards, including consecutive Best Developer – Sri Lanka in 2021, 2024, and 2025. In a historic milestone, Home Lands became the first Sri Lankan developer to win the Best Lifestyle Developer – Asia 2025 Award at the PropertyGuru Asia Property Awards Grand Finale, underscoring its visionary approach to lifestyle-led development and positioning Sri Lankan real estate prominently on the global stage.

With a global footprint and established offices in Australia and Dubai, UAE, Home Lands has achieved full integration through its unique business model, operating as a conglomerate of companies, including its own construction company which has obtained the highest CS2 grading from the Construction Industry Development Authority of Sri Lanka (CIDA). Guided by a strong set of values, Home Lands continues to drive trust, innovation, and excellence in the Real Estate Industry.
