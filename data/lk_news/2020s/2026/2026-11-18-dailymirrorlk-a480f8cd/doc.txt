Hotels, restaurants to benefit from Tourism Levy removal: Minister

Colombo, Nov. 18 (Daily Mirror) - The Government has proposed removing the Tourism Development Levy (TDL) imposed on the tourism industry under the 2027 Budget, in a move aimed at reducing the tax burden on the sector and supporting its growth, Minister of Foreign Affairs, Foreign Employment and Tourism Vijitha Herath said.

He made these remarks yesterday (Nov. 17) while attending the 61st anniversary celebrations of The Hotels Association of Sri Lanka (THASL) as the Chief Guest.

The Minister said the tourism industry had previously been subjected to double taxation, adding that removing this burden would provide relief to small and medium-scale hotel operators, newly established restaurants and other businesses operating in the sector.

He acknowledged that the tourism industry had faced both internal and external challenges but stressed that the Government remained committed to developing it into a leading contributor to the national economy through a long-term strategic plan.

Highlighting the private sector's role in sustaining and expanding tourism, Herath said the Government's responsibility was to establish the necessary policies and legal and regulatory framework to facilitate the industry's growth.

He also reaffirmed the Government's readiness to provide the necessary support to strengthen the sector, while emphasising that the commitment and active participation of all stakeholders were essential for its continued development.

Deputy Minister of Tourism Prof. Ruwan Ranasinghe, ambassadors and representatives of government and private sector institutions associated with the hotel industry were also present at the event.
