Pure nostalgia magic

‘Rhythm of 70s – Legends Revisited’ went live at The Arena at Pickering Casino Resort, Scarborough, on Saturday, June 20, 2026.

And this is the lineup that lit up the stage: Annesley Malawana, Corrine Almeida, Indrani Perera and Pavan Malawarachchi, backed by the CAN Naada band.

The show was billed as “stepping back into the golden era of Sri Lankan music”.

The whole idea was to recreate the magic of those iconic ’70s melodies that shaped a generation. Think timeless hits, unforgettable rhythms, and that spirit of the golden era.

Whether people grew up on those songs, or just love timeless music, it promised a memorable evening of nostalgia, culture, and live entertainment and it turned out to be just that, with the veteran Annesley Malawana leading the way, with a mesmerising performance

They say the auditorium was full of people singing along and reliving their youth.

With graceful stage presence and nostalgic emotion, the songs carried the audience back to a golden era of Sri Lankan music — where melody, poetry, and rhythm blended into pure magic.
