Two new GWM models launched

David Pieris Automobiles (Pvt) Ltd. has expanded its Great Wall Motor (GWM) range in Sri Lanka with the launch of two new models, the fully electric GWM ORA 5 and the Haval Jolion MAX plug-in hybrid electric vehicle (PHEV). The launch was held at the GWM Experience Centre operated by DPA at Access Towers, Union Place, Colombo 02.
