Female inmates stage rooftop protest following deadly clash at Negombo Prison

Colombo, July 5 (Daily Mirror) - A tense situation unfolded at the Negombo Prison on Sunday (05) as female and male inmates staged separate rooftop protests following a deadly clash between two groups of prisoners that left two inmates dead and dozens injured.

The unrest began after the violent confrontation inside the prison, prompting a group of female inmates from the women's ward to climb onto the roof in protest. A separate group of male inmates later joined the demonstration by occupying the rooftops of other prison buildings.

The clash claimed the lives of two inmates, while 34 others were injured and admitted to hospital. Two of the injured prisoners, who are in critical condition, have been transferred to the National Hospital of Sri Lanka for specialised treatment.

Authorities have deployed a large contingent of Police and Police Special Task Force personnel around the prison to bring the situation under control and prevent further violence.
