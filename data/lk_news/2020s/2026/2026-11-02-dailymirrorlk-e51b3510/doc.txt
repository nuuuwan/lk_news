Four arrested over murder of missing 24-year-old man in Colombo

Colombo, Nov. 2 (Daily Mirror) - Four suspects have been arrested in connection with the murder of a 24-year-old man from Wellampitiya who had been reported missing since October 14, 2025, police said.

The initial investigation was launched by the Grandpass Police following a complaint regarding the man's disappearance. Further investigations were subsequently taken over by the Colombo Crimes Division (CCD).

Investigations revealed that the missing man had consumed alcohol with several individuals at a vehicle repair shop in Kambikotuwawatta, within the Grandpass Police Division, on October 11, 2025. Police also found that those who had been drinking with him had fled the area following his disappearance.

On October 30, 2026, CCD officers arrested two suspects near the Mabola Kalu Bridge and in the Mahara area. Based on information obtained during questioning, two additional suspects were arrested in Uswetakeiyawa on the same day.

During subsequent interrogations, investigators uncovered that the victim had allegedly been murdered inside a vehicle repair shop in the Grandpass Police Division. His body had reportedly been burnt in the Papiliyawala area, within the Kirindiwela Police Division, while the remaining body parts were allegedly dumped into the Kelani River from the Ranwala Bridge in Kosgama.

The findings were reported to the Pugoda Magistrate's Court on November 1, 2026. An on-site inspection was subsequently conducted with the participation of the Pugoda Magistrate, the Judicial Medical Officer of Wathupitiwala Hospital and officers from the Gampaha Scene of Crime Unit.

During the inspection, police recovered a motorcycle ignition key, a protective talisman (Suraya), several bone fragments and a burnt piece of tent fabric suspected to have been used to transport the victim's body. These items were taken into police custody as evidence.

A separate inspection was also conducted on November 1 by the Avissawella Magistrate at the Kelani River near the Ranwala Bridge. Scene of Crime officers from Seethawaka carried out investigations at the location with the assistance of Sri Lanka Navy divers.

The facts uncovered during the investigation were also reported to the Colombo Chief Magistrate's Court. All four suspects have been remanded until November 2, 2026.

The suspects, aged 29, 32, 37 and 46, are residents of Wellampitiya and Agalawatta.

The Colombo Crimes Division is conducting further investigations into the incident.
