Fairfirst named as SL’s Best Travel Insurance Company

Fairfirst Insurance has been recognised as the Best Travel Insurance Company in Sri Lanka at World Economic Magazine Awards 2026.

The award honours the organisations that demonstrate sustained excellence, innovation and customer value across industries and is presented following an independent evaluation process that considers factors such as innovation, market leadership, service excellence, business performance and customer impact.

Winning this accolade from this awarding body for the first time marks an important milestone for Fairfirst, reflecting the company’s continued focus on creating meaningful customer experiences and delivering dependable protection that meets the evolving needs of modern travellers.

At a time when travel has become increasingly dynamic and unpredictable, this achievement reinforces Fairfirst’s commitment to continuously enhancing its products, services and customer experience, ensuring travellers can embark on their journeys with greater confidence and peace of mind.

Fairfirst Insurance Chief Executive Officer Ravishankar Wickneswaran said, “We are deeply grateful to our customers for placing their trust in us to protect their journeys around the world. This recognition belongs to our customers and equally to our employees and business partners whose dedication, expertise and support make achievements such as this possible.

As part of the Fairfax group, we are guided by a strong culture of customer focus, integrity and long-term value creation. This award encourages us to continue raising the bar and delivering travel insurance solutions that provide confidence and peace of mind whenever our customers travel.”

For Fairfirst, this recognition is not merely a celebration of what has been achieved but a motivation to continue improving and innovating. As customer expectations continue to evolve, the company remains committed to setting higher benchmarks, strengthening its products and service standards and delivering protection that gives travellers the confidence to explore the world, knowing they are backed by a trusted partner every step of the way.
