Ceylon Automobile Importers Association-Singer Finance offer exclusive benefits

The Ceylon Automobile Importers Association (CAIA) announced a special collaboration with Singer Finance, bringing exciting financial benefits and exclusive offers to the customers during CAIA Week.

As part of this partnership, Singer Finance will introduce special interest rates and attractive financing facilities exclusively for the customers during CAIA Week.

This initiative is designed to make vehicle purchases and automotive financing more convenient, affordable and rewarding for the customers.

Through this collaboration, the customers can look forward to special rates, flexible financing options and additional benefits when obtaining financing for their vehicle purchases through participating CAIA members.

CAIA Week will provide the customers with an excellent opportunity to explore a wide range of vehicles offered by leading automobile importers while taking advantage of these exclusive financial offers from Singer Finance.

The partnership between the CAIA and Singer Finance reflects a shared commitment to creating greater value for the customers and supporting the growth of Sri Lanka’s automotive industry.
