Sri Lanka rupee at 332.30/40 to US dollar spot, bond yields steady

ECONOMYNEXT – Sri Lanka’s rupee was quoted at 332.30/40 to the US dollar in the spot market on Monday, weaker from 332.10/20 Friday, while bond yields were broadly steady, dealers said.

A bond maturing on 01.08.2030 was quoted at 11.45/53 percent, down from 11.45/55 percent.

A bond maturing on 15.10.2030 was quoted at 11.50/58 percent.

A bond maturing on 01.02.2031 was quoted at 11.70/75 percent, down from 11.73/78 percent.

A bond maturing on 01.12.2031 was quoted at 11.70/73 percent, down from 11.80/90 percent.

A bond maturing on 15.01.2033 was quoted at 12.10/20 percent, up from 11.90/12.05 percent.

A bond maturing on 15.11.2033 was quoted at 12.25/35 percent.

A bond maturing on 15.06.2034 was quoted flat at 12.35/45 percent.

The telegraphic transfer rate for the dollar was 327.65 buying, 336.65 selling; the euro was 365.1652 buying, 378.9460 selling; pound was 437.5588 buying, 451.6672 selling.

On the Colombo Stock Exchange the All Share Price Index was down 0.02 percent, or 4.73 points, at 19,680; while the S&P SL20 was up 0.14 percent, 7.58 points, at 5,621. (Colombo/Oct26/2026)
