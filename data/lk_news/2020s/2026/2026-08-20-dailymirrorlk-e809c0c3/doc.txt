Blue Water Hotel denies involvement after dogs found dead near premises

Colombo, Aug. 20 (Daily Mirror) - The management of The Blue Water Hotel & Spa has denied any involvement in the deaths of four dogs found outside the hotel’s boundary wall near the staff entrance.

In an official statement, the hotel said the location where the deceased animals were discovered is completely outside the property and that the dogs had never been on hotel premises or under the care of its management or staff.

According to the hotel, its employees were the first to discover the deceased dogs and immediately informed the Police and the Public Health Inspector (PHI). The management said it is providing full cooperation to the ongoing investigations.

The incident has sparked allegations on social media that several stray dogs roaming the road near the hotel were allegedly poisoned and killed on the night of August 19.

The hotel expressed concern over the incident and said it shares the community’s interest in determining what happened. It urged the public to rely on verified information and avoid speculation while authorities investigate.

The management said it remains committed to transparency and will provide further updates once official findings are made available.

The hotel also emphasized that animal welfare and maintaining public trust remain among its key priorities.
