Ajith Fernando appointed Chairman of CAL’s treasury arm

Capital Alliance PLC (CAL), the primary dealer and treasury business of the Capital Alliance Group, has named its founder and former chief executive, Ajith Fernando, as the new Chairman of its Board of Directors, according to a stock filing.

He takes the helm following the resignation of D.A. de Zoysa on August 21, 2026, who stepped down from the board with immediate effect.

Speaking to Mirror Business, Fernando explained that Zoysa will now on the parent enterprise, Capital Alliance Holdings PLC, where he remains as Chairman. He added that he stepped in to take over the chairmanship of the treasury business following the resignation, as he was the most senior member in the board available to assume the role.

Fernando, who founded the investment banking group in 2000, served as the Managing Director and Chief Executive of the treasury arm until stepping down in April 2025.
