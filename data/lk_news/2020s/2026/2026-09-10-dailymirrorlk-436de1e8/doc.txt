Sri Lanka secures international cooperation through UNCCD summit

Colombo, Sep. 10 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka has secured several opportunities for international cooperation in environmental protection, agriculture and trade following the participation of Environment Minister Dr. Dammika Patabendi at the Summit of Heads of State of the United Nations Convention to Combat Desertification (UNCCD) in Mongolia.

The Minister’s official visit was the first occasion in which a Sri Lankan Environment Minister participated in the summit, which brings together representatives of 196 countries and 197 parties, including the European Union.

Speaking at a special press conference held at the Ministry of Environment today (10), Minister Patabendi said Sri Lanka had used the summit to promote its environmental and climate priorities while strengthening cooperation with other countries.

Addressing a special session held on August 26 to mark Water Day, the Minister shared Sri Lanka’s experiences and priorities in sustainable water management and environmental protection with the international community.

The visit also provided an opportunity for bilateral discussions with the Ministers of Mongolia, Iran and Fiji on expanding cooperation in several fields.

During discussions with Mongolia, the two countries agreed to cooperate towards achieving the Land Degradation Neutrality (LDN) target by 2030, while also exploring the exchange of technical expertise on sustainable land and water management and wildlife conservation.

Both sides also highlighted the importance of strengthening cooperation among developing countries in the Asia-Pacific region.

Meanwhile, discussions with Iran focused on expanding agricultural cooperation between the two countries, which share 64 years of diplomatic relations.

The Iranian side expressed appreciation for humanitarian assistance provided by Sri Lanka during periods of crisis and proposed establishing a joint agricultural technical committee comprising experts from both countries to facilitate the exchange of agricultural technology and innovation.

Sri Lanka and Iran also agreed to take steps to communicate to the Sri Lankan Agriculture Minister details concerning Sri Lanka’s contribution to the development of agricultural infrastructure in Iran.

Discussions with Fiji focused on agriculture, waterways, the sugar industry and opportunities for value addition.

Fiji expressed interest in benefiting from Sri Lanka’s longstanding expertise in value-added tea, coconut and cocoa-based products. The Fijian side also agreed to share international market opportunities and approaches related to the sugar industry.

The Environment Ministry said the visit had provided Sri Lanka with an opportunity to strengthen its environmental and climate agenda internationally while developing new partnerships in agriculture, trade and other economic sectors.
