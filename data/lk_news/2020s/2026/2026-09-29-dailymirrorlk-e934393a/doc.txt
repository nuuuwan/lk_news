Pan Asia Bank plans Rs.4bn Tier 2 debenture issue

Pan Asia Banking Corporation PLC is gearing to raise up to Rs.4 billion through a Basel III-compliant Tier 2 debenture issue, as the lender moves to strengthen its supplementary capital base.

The bank’s board of directors on September 28 approved the issuance of up to 40 million listed, rated, unsecured, subordinated and redeemable debentures, at a par value of Rs.100 each.  The proposed issue will have a five-year tenure. The debentures will also carry a non-viability conversion feature, a key Basel III mechanism, under which the qualifying Tier 2 instruments can be converted, in accordance with the applicable regulatory provisions, if a bank reaches the point of non-viability. At full subscription, the issue will raise Rs.4 billion, providing Pan Asia Bank with additional Tier 2 capital and supporting its capital position as it expands its balance sheet and lending operations. The bank said the proposed issue remains subject to the necessary regulatory approvals. It intends to list the debentures on the Colombo Stock Exchange.
