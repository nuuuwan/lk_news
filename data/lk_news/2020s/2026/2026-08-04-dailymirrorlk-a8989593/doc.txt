Draft Bill to extend judges’ retirement age sent for Gazette publication: Minister

Colombo, August 4 (Daily Mirror)- The draft Bill proposing to extend the retirement age of judges by two years has been forwarded to the Department of Government Printing for publication in the Government Gazette, Justice Minister Harshana Nanayakkara told Parliament today.

The Minister said the Attorney General granted legal clearance for the draft legislation on July 31. It was subsequently approved by the Cabinet of Ministers before being sent to be published in the Government gazette.

Responding to a question from Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa, Minister Nanayakkara said the draft Bill would be tabled in Parliament one week after its publication in the Government Gazette.

He said the entire legislative process is expected to take at least three weeks, but noted that it could be delayed further if the Bill is challenged before the Supreme Court.
