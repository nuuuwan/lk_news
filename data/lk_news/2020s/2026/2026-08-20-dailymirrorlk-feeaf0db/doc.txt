Another container scam : MP

Colombo, Aug. 20 (Daily Mirror) - A fresh container scam has allegedly taken place where a red labelled container containing undeclared items has been released, SLPP MP D.V. Chanaka stated in Parliament yesterday.

Customs authorities have allowed the clearance of a container with a large number of items including vehicle spare parts. A total of 365 items had been imported illegally while 47 items have disappeared. it is alleged that 122 vehicle seats had been in the container. Three customs teams had been assigned with the examination of the consignment however two teams have failed to detect the contraband. and only one team had spotted seven items,” the MP said.

“The allegation on this scam is directed at a former senior official at customs who is currently appointed as secretary to the President,” he added.

Furthermore he said a report on the scam has been sent to the secretary Ministry of Finance on January 19 this year. He said the report has gone missing since then. The MP said President Anura Kumara Dissanayake should make a public statement on it.

“Scams such as these are the reason as to why the President and the government wants the judiciary under their control. No action has been taken against those who are responsible for the scam pertaining to 323 containers and those involved in the procurement of inferior quality coal. The government wants the judiciary controlled to avoid court cases,” the MP alleged.
