Kanro Interiors recognized with Platinum Award

Kanro Interiors has been honoured with a Platinum Award in recognition of its outstanding contribution to Sri Lanka’s interior construction and corporate fit-out industry.

The recognition highlights the company’s commitment to quality workmanship, innovative design concepts, timely project execution, and customer-focused service delivery across a wide range of corporate interior projects.

Established in 2011 under the leadership of Current Managing Director Janaka Rodrigo, Kanro Interiors has steadily grown into a specialized interior construction company catering to Sri Lanka’s evolving corporate sector. The company provides integrated solutions including office interior construction, office furniture, partition systems, ceiling installations, carpeting, flooring structures, blinds, and complete turnkey projects.

Over the years, Kanro Interiors has successfully delivered projects for several leading public and private sector institutions in Sri Lanka. Its project portfolio includes interior developments for the Vocational Training Authority of Sri Lanka, National Institute of Business Management, Cipla Pharmaceuticals, Wurth Lanka, Sri Lanka Youth Corps, CIC Group, and Volume Lanka.

The company has also collaborated with several well-known organizations including Nestle Lanka, Browns, AgstarPlc, Keells, ADIC Sri Lanka, Majestic Pride Casino, Hemas Logistics, AMW & Assetline Leasing, Sri Lanka Mobitel, etc, reflecting its strong reputation within Sri Lanka’s corporate interior sector.
