Two fishermen killed in Trincomalee train accident

Colombo, Nov. 23 (Daily Mirror) - Two fishermen were killed and another person was critically injured after being struck by a train near the Cod Bay Fisheries Harbour in China Bay, Trincomalee last night (22), police said.

The accident occurred when three people who were on the railway track were hit by a train travelling from Trincomalee to Colombo Fort.

One of the victims died at the scene, while the other two were rushed to Trincomalee Hospital with critical injuries. One of them later succumbed to his injuries while receiving treatment.

The deceased have been identified as fishermen aged 48 and 58, residents of the Matara and Devinuwara areas.

Their bodies have been placed at the Trincomalee Hospital mortuary.

China Bay Police are conducting further investigations into the accident.
