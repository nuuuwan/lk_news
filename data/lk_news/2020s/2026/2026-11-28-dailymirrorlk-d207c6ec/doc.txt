Sri Lanka’s expressway revenue exceeds Rs. 27.5 billion in 22 months

Sri Lanka’s expressway network generated a total revenue of Rs. 27,581.4 million between January 2025 and October 31, 2026, Transport, Highways and Urban Development Minister Bimal Rathnayake said today (28).

Making a statement in Parliament, the Minister said the Southern Expressway, which runs from Kottawa to Mattala, generated the highest revenue of Rs. 11,486 million during the period.

The Central Expressway section between Mirigama and Kurunegala generated Rs. 7,282 million, while the Colombo-Katunayake Expressway earned Rs. 2,560.05 million.

Meanwhile, the Outer Circular Expressway generated a revenue of Rs. 6,251 million during the same period.

The Minister said the combined revenue from the Southern, Central and Colombo-Katunayake expressways amounted to Rs. 21,328.05 million.

Accordingly, the total revenue generated by the country’s expressway network stood at Rs. 27,581.4 million by October 31, 2026.
