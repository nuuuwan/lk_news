Jaffna Trade Fair 2027 media briefing held

The media briefing for the 17th edition of the Jaffna International Trade Fair (JITF) 2027 was held in Colombo. The exhibition, the largest multi-trade exhibition in Northern Sri Lanka, will be held on January 29, 30 and 31, 2027, at the Muttraweli Grounds. Pix by Kithsiri De Mel
