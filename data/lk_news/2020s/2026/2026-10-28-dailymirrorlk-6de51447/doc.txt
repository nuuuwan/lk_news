DFCC Bank launches Student File

DFCC Bank has launched the DFCC Student File, bringing together tuition fee payments, education financing, foreign exchange services and selected travel benefits to help students and their families manage the financial arrangements associated with overseas education.

Receiving a university admission is an important milestone but it also begins a demanding period of financial planning. Families may need to arrange tuition payments, secure foreign currency, finance part of the cost and prepare for travel, often within strict timelines. DFCC Student File provides a single point through which the customers can access these services.

The proposition includes tuition fee payment facilities, education loans at preferential interest rates and preferential foreign currency exchange rates. The customers can transact in major currencies when making education-related payments to institutions across overseas study destinations.

DFCC Bank Vice President and Head of Products and Propositions, Retail and Business Banking Dr. Asitha Pinnaduwa said, “An overseas admission begins a second process for the family, involving payments, financing, foreign currency and travel arrangements. The difficulty is often not one requirement but having to coordinate several of them within a limited period. DFCC Student File brings the principal financial services together so that the students and parents can approach this stage with greater clarity and prepare for what comes next.”

The proposition also provides access to selected student airfare deals and travel packages, supporting the practical arrangements required before departure.

The eligible customers can access exclusive discounts and zero percent easy payment plans through DFCC Credit Cards for selected education expenses, travel insurance, laptops and other devices. These options allow qualifying costs to be settled over instalments, helping families manage some of the expenses associated with preparing for overseas study.

Facilities, preferential rates, offers and payment plans are subject to applicable eligibility criteria, credit approval, availability and terms and conditions.

DFCC Student File expands DFCC Bank’s support for customers at important stages of their lives by connecting banking services with the practical requirements surrounding an overseas education.
