Great Place To Work Sri Lanka announces ‘10 Great Workplaces for Parents 2026’

Great Place To Work Sri Lanka announced the winners of the 10 Great Workplaces for Parents 2026, recognizing organizations that are creating exceptional workplace experiences for employees with parental responsibilities while fostering cultures that enable them to thrive both professionally and personally. The 2026 recognition is based on one of Sri Lanka’s most comprehensive workplace culture assessments, capturing feedback from over 75,000 employees across 130 plus  organizations, including 35,000 plus working parents, achieving a 87 percent average response-rate. The evaluation combines employee insights with a rigorous review of workplace policies, practices, and programmes that support parents.

Speaking on the recognition, Kshanika Ratnayaka, Great Place To Work Sri Lanka said, “Parents play a vital role in today’s workforce, bringing valuable perspectives, resilience, and leadership capabilities to organizations. The organizations recognized this year have gone beyond offering benefits to create cultures where parents feel trusted, supported, and empowered to succeed. These organizations demonstrate that when workplaces enable employees to thrive at work and at home, both people and businesses flourish.”

This year’s recognized organizations represent a diverse range of industries, demonstrating a strong commitment to creating supportive and inclusive workplaces for employees with parental responsibilities. The honorees are AB Mauri Lanka (Pvt) Ltd, Advantis Express (Pvt) Ltd, AmSafe Bridport (Pvt) Ltd, Dijital Team, EFL 3PL, Hayleys Plantations, HNB Life PLC, Ideal Motors (Pvt) Ltd, Marriott International, and Oxford College of Business (Pvt) Ltd.
