Government facing no threat: President

Colombo, Sep. 15 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake said yesterday the government is not facing any threat and that the public rallies being held across the country are aimed at building a strong people’s movement to support the country’s development.

Speaking at a public rally in Kahatagasdigiliya, the President said the responsibility for developing the country lies not only with the government but also with the people.

“The government must take action to develop the country, while the people too have a major responsibility in this effort. We need to build a strong public movement for this purpose, and that is what we are doing now. There is no threat to the government,” he said.

Dissanayake stressed that Sri Lanka’s progress requires economic, political and social transformation.

He also said the National People’s Power (NPP) government had brought an end to family-based politics, insisting that there should be no room for such a system in the country.

“For 76 years, several elite families governed this country. We must put an end to family politics. It is an outdated practice, and the NPP has stopped it,” he said.

The President also called on the public to change their attitudes towards politicians accused of misappropriating public property. He said people should support legal action against those responsible rather than defend them.

Referring to a gathering in Anuradhapura, he said a small group had assembled to defend individuals accused of wrongdoing and had argued that they should not be imprisoned.

Dissanayake further claimed that opposition groups were engaged in a do-or-die game to overthrow the government. He said those who got away with wrongdoing under successive governments for decades were now attempting to regain protection.

“They have been caught now and have no protection. They want to topple the government. It is not the people who want to bring down this government, but politicians who stole public property, drug traffickers and members of the underworld. The people are with the NPP,” he said.
