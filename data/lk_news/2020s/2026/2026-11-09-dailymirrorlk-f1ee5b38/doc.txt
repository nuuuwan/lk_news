Another atmospheric disturbance developing “ Met. Dept.

Colombo, Nov. 9 (Daily Mirror) - Another atmospheric disturbance is developing over the South East of Bay of Bengal, the Department of Meteorology said yesterday.

It is expected to convert into a low pressure system by November 15 , Department of Meteorology Duty forecaster Udeni Weerasinghe told media.

The fishing and naval communities were requested to be vigilant and be attentive to forests which the department would come out with during the coming days.

Sowers or thunder showers were expected in some parts of the country. Mist was forecasted during early hours during the coming days in the Western, Sabaragamuwa, Central and Uva provinces.

The highest rainfall of 110.8 mm was recorded from Awissawella while a rainfall of100mm was recorded from Deranagala. Some rains were reported from Hydro cachmet areas as well. Accordingly 55mm of rain was recorded from Kukuleganga, 36.4mm from Rantambe and 31.6mm from Randenigala.

Meanwhile, showers or thundershowers are also expected over several surrounding sea areas. Winds are expected to blow at speeds of 20 to 30 kmph, but could temporarily strengthen to 60–70 kmph during thundershowers. Flood gates of around 15 reservoirs were at spill level according to the Department of Irrigation.

The Department advised naval and fishing communities to remain vigilant and take necessary precautions when operating in these sea areas.
