Janashakthi Life deepens community engagement in Jaffna

Janashakthi Life extended its community engagement efforts to Jaffna during the Nallur Festival, connecting with over 7,000 individuals and families across the region through a 10-day initiative focused on creating greater awareness around life protection and financial security, as well as providing employment opportunities.

The first five days of the initiative featured a door-to-door community outreach programme across Chunnakam, Sanganai, Navarkuzhi, Kokkuvil and Gurunagar.

Janashakthi Life representatives engaged directly with residents, creating meaningful conversations around life protection and understanding the needs of individuals and families within these communities.

The following five days saw Janashakthi Life establish a dedicated activation stall in the vicinity of the Nallur Temple, creating an accessible touchpoint for the many pilgrims and visitors who gathered for the Nallur Festival.

The representatives were on hand to provide personalised guidance on life insurance and protection solutions, helping the visitors explore options suited to their individual circumstances and family needs.

As part of Janashakthi Life’s 32nd anniversary celebrations and Insurance Month, the company took the message of life protection closer to the people of Jaffna. The initiative was centred around the Nallur Festival, one of the Northern province’s most significant cultural celebrations, with the Nallur Kandaswamy Kovil holding deep social importance for people living in Northern peninsula.

Recognising Nallur’s deep significance to the people of Jaffna, Janashakthi Life saw the occasion as a meaningful opportunity to engage with the community and bring life protection closer to individuals and families.

Janashakthi Insurance Director and CEO Ravi Liyanage said, “At Janashakthi Life, our role extends beyond providing insurance. It is about being present in the communities we serve and helping them celebrate their cultural beliefs while also initiating conversations around bringing protection into their lives. Initiatives such as these reflect our continued commitment to reaching communities across the country and helping more individuals and families plan secure futures.”
