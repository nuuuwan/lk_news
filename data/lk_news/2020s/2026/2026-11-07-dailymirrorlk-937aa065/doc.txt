Future Life Investments recognises outstanding employee performance

Future Life Investments, which marks a decade of operations in Sri Lanka’s microfinance sector, recently held its employee convention to recognise top performers in the first half of the 2026/27 financial year. Attended by more than 900 employees, the event rewarded high achievers with awards, certificates, overseas trips and new motor vehicles.

The convention, held under the leadership of the company’s senior management, honoured achievements at both individual and branch levels. Selected members of the ‘FLI Eagles’ were also honoured for their performance. The initiative underscored the company’s commitment to rewarding employee dedication, efficiency and contribution towards organisational goals.

The event also featured a series of sessions designed to support employees’ professional and personal development. These sessions focused on helping staff identify challenges in the workplace and in their personal lives, explore innovative approaches to problem-solving, and foster a corporate culture built on teamwork and collaboration.

Guided by its theme for the year, ‘Beyond Limits, with Confidence’, Future Life Investments continues to work towards its objectives for the 2026/27 financial year. The company currently operates a network of 63 branches across Sri Lanka, providing financial services and guidance to more than 150,000 women members. Through these services, the company aims to improve members’ living standards and contribute to the economic progress of their families.
