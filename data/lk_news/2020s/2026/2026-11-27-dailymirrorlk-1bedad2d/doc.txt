Janashakthi Life declares Rs. 5.4bn interim dividend

Janashakthi Life has declared an interim dividend of Rs. 7.95 per share, amounting to approximately Rs. 5.4 billion.

The dividend is to be distributed from accumulated retained earnings, reflecting a considered approach to returning surplus capital to shareholders while preserving the financial strength required to meet policyholder commitments and support the Company’s long-term business objectives.

Janashakthi Life’s strong capital position provides a clear foundation for this distribution. As of September 2026, the Company’s Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) stood at 372 percent, significantly exceeding the statutory minimum requirement of 120 percent. Following the dividend distribution, the CAR is expected to remain at 230 percent, above both the regulatory enforcement threshold of 160 percent and the Company’s internal limit of 200 percent.

Further reinforcing its financial standing, Janashakthi Life holds an A- Insurance Financial Strength rating with a Stable Outlook, assigned by Lanka Rating Agency.
