CSE slips as turnover falls below LKR 1 bn

The Colombo Stock Exchange returned to negative territory on 2 October 2026, with both benchmark indices closing lower as selling pressure strengthened during the afternoon session.

The ASPI declined 68.26 points, or 0.33%, to 20,812.64, while the S&P SL20 fell 18.15 points, or 0.31%, to 5,883.50. The ASPI briefly approached the 20,800 level before a modest late recovery limited the decline.

Market turnover remained subdued at LKR 873.38 million, with just 29.94 million shares traded. The Banks sector led activity with LKR 244.40 million in turnover, while SING.N recorded the highest individual counter turnover at LKR 157.29 million.

Crossings accounted for a sizeable 42% of total turnover, led by SING.N with approximately LKR 156.35 million. Sampath Bank, Lanka IOC and NDB also recorded notable block transactions.

Market breadth remained negative, with 78 gainers against 116 decliners, resulting in an advance-decline ratio of 0.67. Foreign selling also remained significant, with investors recording a net outflow of approximately LKR 140.21 million.

Carson Cumberbatch was the largest negative contributor to the ASPI, followed by Commercial Bank, SINS.N, LOLC Finance and Bukit Darah. Lion Brewery provided the strongest positive contribution.

The combination of sub-LKR 1 billion turnover, weak market breadth, high crossing concentration and continued foreign selling indicates that broader investor participation remains cautious. With the ASPI again approaching the 20,800 area, investors are likely to watch whether buying support strengthens around current levels when trading resumes.
