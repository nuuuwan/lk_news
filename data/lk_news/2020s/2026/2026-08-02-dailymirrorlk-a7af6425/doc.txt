Mahara Prison situation brought under control; 104 inmates transferred

Colombo, Aug. 2 (Daily Mirror) - The unrest that erupted inside the Mahara Prison on Saturday afternoon has been brought under control through a joint operation involving prison officials, the Sri Lanka Police, the Police Special Task Force (STF) and the Tri-Forces, the Department of Prisons said.

According to Prisons Commissioner (Operations) and Media Spokesman A.C. Gajanayake, a total of 104 inmates have been transferred to the Dumbara, Welikada, Boossa, Kalutara, Badulla, Batticaloa and Polonnaruwa prisons following the incident.

He added that a list containing the names of the transferred inmates has been displayed near the roadblock outside the Mahara Prison.

Meanwhile, the Department of Prisons said that inmate visitation at Mahara Prison will resume after Monday and Tuesday, as repair work is currently being carried out at the prison.
