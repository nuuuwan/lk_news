ISMM marks 54 years of excellence  with National Supply Chain Awards 2026

The Institute of Supply and Materials Management (ISMM) celebrated its 54th anniversary by hosting Sri Lanka's premier supply chain event, the National Supply Chain Day 2026 and National Supply Chain Excellence Awards 2026, at Monarch Imperial, Sri Jayewardenepura Kotte, on 24 October 2026.

As the apex professional body for supply chain and materials management in Sri Lanka, ISMM brought together leading professionals, academics, policymakers, and industry leaders to recognise excellence and discuss the future of supply chains in an increasingly interconnected world.

The day's proceedings commenced with a highly engaging seminar under the theme "Geopolitics, Technology and the Future of Global Supply Chains." The Chief Guest was Sri Lanka Accreditation Board for Conformity Assessment Chairman AVM Prasanna Ranasinghe (Rtd), Guest of Honour was Ceylon Biscuits Ltd., Director/CEO Nalin Karunaratne, the special guest Organisation of Professional Associations (OPA) President and ISMM Immediate Past President and Capital Maharaja Group Industrial Hub Director Supply Chain Jayantha Gallehewa, and the Keynote Speaker Exterminators PLC Group Managing Director and Dreamron Group of Companies Managing Director Dr. Kishu Gomes and ISMM President Lilantha Subasinghe.

The grand Awards Ceremony in the evening was graced by High Commissioner of New Zealand to Sri Lanka, Pakistan, Maldives and Bangladesh David Pine, as a Chief Guest together with Industrial Development Board Chairman Yasas Hewage as a Guest of Honour, and ISMM President Lilantha Subasinghe.

The National Supply Chain Excellence Awards recognised outstanding achievements by organisations and professionals who have demonstrated leadership, innovation, operational excellence, and best practices in supply chain management across manufacturing, trading, and service sectors. Awards were presented under the categories of Best Supply Chain Practicing Organisation, Best Supply Chain Professional, and Special Commendations for Emerging Excellence.

The National Supply Chain Excellence Awards have established themselves as a significant national platform for recognising outstanding achievements and contributions to the advancement of Supply Chain Management in Sri Lanka. The awards aim to encourage best practices, operational excellence, innovation, efficiency, resilience, and professional development across industries.

This year, the awards received more than 128 nominations, reflecting the growing recognition and enthusiasm of organisations and professionals towards supply chain excellence. Following the evaluation process, 54 organisations and 9 individual professionals were recognised for demonstrating exceptional achievements and contributions to the field.

A total of 63 National Awards were presented under two main award titles:

n National Award of Excellence – Best Supply Chain Practicing Organisation 2026

n National Award of Excellence – Best Supply Chain Professional 2026

The organisational awards recognise outstanding supply chain practices across four business scales: Extra Large, Large, Medium, and Small, with categories covering Manufacturing, Service, and Trading sectors, as applicable. The Best Supply Chain Professional award recognises individual excellence under two categories: Corporate and Business.
