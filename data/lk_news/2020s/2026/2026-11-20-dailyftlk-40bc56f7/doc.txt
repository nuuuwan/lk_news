Eight EPDP members sentenced to death for 2008 murder

The Batticaloa High Court yesterday sentenced eight members of the Eelam People’s Democratic Party (EPDP) to death for the abduction and murder of a man in Eravur, Batticaloa, in 2008, during the civil war.

The court found the eight guilty of unlawful assembly and the murder of Devadas Suresh Kumar. It acquitted and released a ninth accused.

Suresh Kumar was abducted on 19 June, 2008, reportedly taken away in a van by a group of EPDP members attached to a camp in Chenkalady. According to evidence presented at the trial, he was murdered and buried, with a banana sapling planted over the burial site.

Eravur Police arrested nine EPDP members and filed proceedings in the Batticaloa Magistrate’s Court. Investigators also recovered the van allegedly used in the abduction. The case was transferred to the High Court in 2016.

In addition to the death sentences, the eight were given six months’ simple imprisonment under Section 140 of the Penal Code for being members of an unlawful assembly with the common object of committing murder. They were then taken to Batticaloa Prison.
