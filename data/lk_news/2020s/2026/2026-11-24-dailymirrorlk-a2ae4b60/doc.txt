AIA shines at 17th National Forum for Life Insurance Advisors

AIA Insurance Lanka celebrates the notable achievements of its Wealth Planners who received national recognitionat the National Forum for Life Insurance Advisors (NAFLIA) Life Insurance Industry Sales Awards 2025, organised by the Insurance Association of Sri Lanka (IASL).

Demonstrating AIA’s commitment to nurturing excellence and professionalism within the life insurance industry, AIA representatives secured accolades across both national and company categories at the prestigious awards ceremony.

G. Ravi Kumar Perera, Senior District Wealth Planners’ Manager of ADO – Panadura City, received the Silver Award for Best Supervisor (National Level) and the Silver Award for Best Supervisor (Company Category – Large).

Adding to AIA’s success, Sanjeewani Somarathna, District Wealth Planning Manager of Area Development Office – Kandy, was recognised with the Bronze Award for Best Advisor (National Level) and the Bronze Award for Best Advisor (Company Category – Large).

Chamilka Sarangi, Insurance Advisor of Matara Metro RDO 1, received the Silver Award for Fast Starter Advisor (Company Category – Large). These achievements highlight outstanding leadershipand commitment to developing high-performing advisors while supporting the growth of the industry.

Director/Chief Executive Officer of AIA Insurance Lanka, Chathuri Munaweera extended her congratulations to the winners, stating, “Seeing our Wealth Planners and agency leaders honoured at NAFLIA 2026 is a proud moment for AIA. This recognition reflects the high standards of professionalism and customer focus that sit at the heart of our culture. We congratulate all those recognised and look forward to seeing them continue to set new benchmarks of excellence for the industry.”

Commenting on the achievement, AIA Insurance Lanka Chief Agency Officer (CAO), Dimuthu Piyasena said,”These awards are a testament to thecommitment, expertise, and hard workthat our agency force brings to their work every day. We are immensely proud of our Wealth Planners for earning recognition on a national stage. Their achievements reflect not only individual excellence, but also the strength of our agency force and ourcommitment to helping Sri Lankans live Healthier, Longer, Better Lives.”
