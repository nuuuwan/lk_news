Underworld figure ‘Konda Ranji’ arrested at BIA

Colombo, September 09 (Daily Mirror) - Organised crime figure Ranjith Kumara, alias “Konda Ranji,” has been arrested by the Criminal Investigation Department (CID) following his deportation from Dubai.

Kumara was taken into CID custody immediately after arriving at Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA) in Katunayake, police said.

They said investigations are underway into his alleged involvement in several criminal activities, including drug trafficking operations.

“Konda Ranji” was reportedly arrested by security authorities in Dubai on January 9, 2026, while allegedly attempting to flee to Italy. His wife and children were also detained during the operation but were later released by the United Arab Emirates authorities.

Sri Lankan law enforcement authorities have identified Kumara as a key suspect wanted in connection with several high-profile criminal investigations. These reportedly include the murder of the coordinating secretary to former Minister Mangala Samaraweera.
