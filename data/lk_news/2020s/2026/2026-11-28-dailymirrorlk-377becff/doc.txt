Keells brings global taste closer to home

With an expanding international food range, Keells is turning the grocery trip into an opportunity for discovery, bringing together ingredients, flavours and brands from across the world and making them more accessible to Sri Lankan households.

From spices and curry pastes to pasta, sauces and cooking essentials, the range invites shoppers to look beyond the familiar and bring something new to the table.

For those drawn to Indian cooking, Keells offers flavours spanning North and South India. Suhana brings seasonings and cooking essentials inspired by favourites such as butter chicken, paneer tikka masala and biryani, while Shakti adds South Indian spices and specialty pappadams.

Kanakwan brings Thai cooking essentials including red, green, Massaman and Penang curry preparations, sauces and seasonings. Flying Goose adds its Sriracha range, while Thai Choice introduces rice papers, sambal oelek and noodles. Together, they make it easier to bring Thai flavours into everyday meals.

Italy takes centre stage in the international selection. De Cecco, exclusively available at Keells, offers a wider selection of pastas, sauces and gluten-free options. Directly imported Basso olive oils add another layer to the Italian offering, while the Campo D’Oro range of sauces, pestos and olives further expands the selection.

The Italian experience was also brought to life through Keells’ collaboration with the Italian Trade Agency and the Embassy of Italy in Colombo for the Week of Italian Cuisine in the World 2026.

From 13 to 19 November, the initiative featured dedicated Italian food corners and tasting activities across 11 selected Keells Supermarkets, giving shoppers across Sri Lanka the opportunity to discover and taste Italian flavours for themselves.

What makes the range appealing is that these are not products meant to sit untouched on a shelf. They are ingredients waiting to become something. A Thai curry paste can turn an evening into something different. A packet of pasta can become a family dinner. A spice blend can inspire a recipe never made before.

For families, it is an opportunity to introduce children to flavours. For professionals, it is a way to make weekday cooking more interesting. For experienced home cooks, it is an invitation to experiment, mix cuisines and discover something new.

Ultimately, this is about more than bringing products from different countries onto Sri Lankan shelves. It is about bringing the world a little closer to home. Because sometimes, discovering a new culture does not require a passport. Sometimes, all it takes is a new flavour, a new recipe and the curiosity to try something different. At Keells, we want to make those little discoveries part of everyday life and keep life fresh along the way.
