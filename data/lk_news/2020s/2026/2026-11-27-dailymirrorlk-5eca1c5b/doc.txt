Sri Lanka appoints first female Honorary Consul in Türkiye

Sri Lanka has appointed entrepreneur Elif Yücelik as its new Honorary Consul in Erzurum, Türkiye, marking the first time a woman has been appointed to the position in the country.

Sri Lanka’s Ambassador to Türkiye, Niluka Kadurugamuwa, presented the Commission of Appointment to Yücelik at the Sri Lankan Embassy in Ankara on November 26, according to a statement issued by the Embassy on November 27.

Yücelik has been appointed for a three-year term, effective October 22, 2026, with consular jurisdiction covering the Eastern Anatolian Region.

Following the presentation of the appointment, Ambassador Kadurugamuwa briefed Yücelik on her responsibilities and emphasised her role in further strengthening bilateral relations between Sri Lanka and Türkiye.

The Ambassador highlighted the importance of promoting trade, investment, tourism and people-to-people contacts between the two countries.
