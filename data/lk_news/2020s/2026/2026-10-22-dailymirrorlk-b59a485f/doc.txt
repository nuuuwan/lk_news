Hemas Hospitals brings new global standard of MRI technology

From left: Director Medical Services and Quality Dr. Malith Atapattu, Hemas Capital Hospital Director General Manager Dr. Pradeep Edward and Hemas Hospitals and Diagnostics Chain Managing Director Dr. Lakith Peiris at the ribbon-cutting ceremony to mark the introduction of Siemens Healthineers MAGNETOM Sola 1.5T MRI system at Hemas Capital Hospital, Thalawathugoda

Sri Lanka’s first MRI with Siemens Healthineers’ Deep Resolve AI technology sets a new national benchmark in diagnostic imaging, bringing a level of capability not previously available in the country and marking another step forward in advancing Sri Lanka’s healthcare standards.

Hemas Hospitals has brought a new class of MRI technology to Sri Lanka with the introduction of Siemens Healthineers MAGNETOM Sola 1.5T at Hemas Capital Hospital, Thalawathugoda.

Considered a global benchmark in advanced, patient-centric MRI imaging, the system gives Sri Lankan patients access to capabilities at the forefront of modern diagnostic imaging.

The system is the first MRI in Sri Lanka to feature Siemens Healthineers’ Deep Resolve AI-based image reconstruction technology, introducing a combination of artificial intelligence, advanced image quality, speed and patient comfort not previously available in the country.

This is more than an incremental upgrade in MRI capability. It represents a significant technological leap and establishes a new national benchmark for diagnostic imaging in Sri Lanka.

Hemas Hospitals and Diagnostics Chain Managing Director Dr. Lakith Peiris said, “This is a significant advancement not only for Hemas Hospitals but for MRI imaging in Sri Lanka. We are bringing into the country a level of capability that has not previously been available here and giving our patients access to technology that sits at the forefront of modern diagnostic imaging.

Hemas Capital Hospital Director General Manager Dr. Pradeep Edward said, “What makes this technology different is not simply what the machine can do but what that means for the patient. It enables us to combine highly advanced imaging with faster examinations and a more comfortable overall experience.”
