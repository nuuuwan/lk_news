Colombo–Kandy main road at Kadugannawa reopened after rockfall

Colombo, August 04 (Daily Mirror) - The Colombo–Kandy main road, which was temporarily closed following a rockfall in the Kadugannawa area, has been reopened to traffic, police said.

The road was closed after a rockfall occurred yesterday (3) in the Lower Kadugannawa area along the Colombo–Kandy main road due to adverse weather conditions. As a precautionary measure, police temporarily closed the stretch of road between Ganethenna and Kadugannawa.

Police said the road was reopened to traffic from 6.00 a.m. today after weather conditions improved and following recommendations issued by the National Building Research Organisation (NBRO).
