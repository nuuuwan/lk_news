SJB mulls no-confidence motion against Speaker

Colombo, September 26 (Daily Mirror)- The Samagi Jana Balawegaya (SJB) is considering bringing a no-confidence motion against Speaker Jagath Wickramaratne, SJB General Secretary Ranjith Madduma Bandara said.

Speaking to Daily Mirror, Madduma Bandara confirmed that the party is contemplating such a move, adding that the motion would be submitted at an appropriate time.

However, he did not disclose when the motion would be formally handed over to Parliament.
