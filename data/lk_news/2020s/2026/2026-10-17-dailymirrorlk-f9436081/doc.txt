12 injured in bus accident on Puttalam-Anuradhapura Road

Puttalam, Oct 17 (Daily Mirror) - Twelve people were injured when a luxury bus traveling from Colombo to Anuradhapura veered off the road and overturned due to adverse weather conditions near the Mee Oya bridge along the Puttalam-Anuradhapura road within the Puttalam police division last night.

Among the injured are seven women, a young girl, and four men, all of whom sustained minor injuries and have been admitted to the Puttalam Hospital. The bus driver and conductor are also among the injured.

The Puttalam Police are conducting further investigations into the incident.
