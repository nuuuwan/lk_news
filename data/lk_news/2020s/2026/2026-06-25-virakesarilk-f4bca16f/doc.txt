Teen Arrested at BIA Over Rs.352M Hashish

An 18-year-old Canadian woman was arrested by Customs officers at Bandaranaike International Airport (BIA), Katunayake, for allegedly attempting to smuggle 35.265 kilograms of hashish into Sri Lanka.

The drugs, concealed inside two suitcases, have an estimated street value of over Rs.352 million.

She arrived on Tuesday night aboard an Emirates flight from Toronto via Dubai.

Customs Narcotics Control Unit officers discovered the contraband during a detailed inspection prompted by suspicious behaviour.

Preliminary investigations are underway, and the suspect along with the seized drugs will be handed over to the Police Narcotics Bureau for further investigations and legal action.
