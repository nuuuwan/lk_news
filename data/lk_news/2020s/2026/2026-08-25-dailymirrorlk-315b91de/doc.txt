Court fines Jaffna restaurant Rs. 950,000 for overcharging on bottled water

Aug. 25 (Daily Mirror) - A popular vegetarian restaurant in the Nallur area of Jaffna has been fined Rs. 950,000 for selling bottled drinking water at an excessive price.

Based on the information received by the Jaffna District Consumer Affairs Authority that the restaurant was selling bottled drinking water at an excessively high price, an inspection was carried out. The inspection confirmed that the bottles were being sold above the permitted price, following which legal action was taken against the restaurant.

Following the inspection, legal proceedings were instituted against the restaurant at the Jaffna Additional Magistrate’s Court. During the hearing held today, Tuesday, the court imposed a fine of Rs. 950,000 on the restaurant owner. The court further ordered that, if the fine is not paid, the owner must serve six months in prison.

It is also noteworthy that the same restaurant had previously been fined by the court for selling drinking water at an excessive price. The latest case marks a repeat offence involving the same violation.
