14-year-old undergoes successful heart transplant at Colombo National Hospital

Colombo, Nov. 28 (Daily Mirror) - A 14-year-old boy has undergone a successful heart transplant at the Colombo National Hospital after receiving a heart donated by a 19-year-old who was declared brain-dead following an accident.

The transplant was performed on November 26 by a specialist medical team led by cardiothoracic surgeon Dr. Anil Abeywickrama.

The boy, who had been receiving treatment at the hospital’s cardiac unit, was suffering from severe heart failure and was reportedly at risk of dying within days without an urgent transplant.

The donor, who had been receiving treatment at Gampaha Hospital following an accident, was declared brain-dead. His family subsequently consented to donating his heart to save another patient’s life.

The organ had to be transported urgently from Gampaha to Colombo to facilitate the life-saving operation. However, an initial plan to transport the heart by helicopter had to be abandoned due to adverse weather conditions and heavy rainfall in the Gampaha area.

Police then stepped in to facilitate its transportation by road, temporarily restricting traffic along the main route from Gampaha to Colombo and providing a special escort to ensure the heart reached the Colombo National Hospital without delay.

The operation reportedly lasted more than seven hours, beginning at around 5.00 p.m. and concluding around midnight.

The recipient was reported to be in good condition following the surgery.

The latest procedure is reportedly the third heart transplant to be performed in Sri Lanka. The country’s first heart transplant was carried out at the Kandy National Hospital several years ago.
