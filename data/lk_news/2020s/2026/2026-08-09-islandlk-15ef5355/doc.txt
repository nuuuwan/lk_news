InsureMe Insurance Brokers PLC celebrates 10 years of ‘Insurance Made Easy’

InsureMe Insurance Brokers PLC marked its 10th anniversary with “The Decade Celebration” at Cinnamon Grand Colombo, gathering directors, staff, clients, and partners to reflect on a journey defined by innovation, trust, and growth.

Founded in 2016 as a tech-driven start-up, InsureMe has risen to become one of Sri Lanka’s leading insurance brokers, serving individuals and corporations through a wide portfolio of personal and business solutions. Its digital platform, InsureMe.lk, enables customers to compare policies, purchase coverage, and manage needs online, delivering on its core promise: “Insurance Made Easy.”

The company’s commitment to transparency and convenience has simplified what many once found a complex process, offering expert guidance alongside multi-insurer comparisons. In 2025, InsureMe achieved two major milestones: listing on the Colombo Stock Exchange, reinforcing governance and sustainable growth, and securing a Reinsurance Broking Licence from the IRCSL, expanding its capacity to deliver comprehensive risk solutions for insurers and corporate clients.
