Hashish worth Rs. 181.9 mn seized at Peliyagoda customs warehouse

Colombo, Nov. 27 (Daily Mirror) - Sri Lanka Customs has seized 18.193 kilogrammes of hashish valued at Rs. 181.93 million from a customs warehouse in Peliyagoda.

The narcotics were concealed inside three parcels sent to Sri Lanka from Malaysia, Customs said.

Officers of the Customs Narcotics Control Unit detected the suspicious parcels during a special inspection and detained them in an attempt to apprehend those who would arrive to collect the consignments.

However, as no one arrived to claim the parcels within the stipulated period, Customs officers opened and inspected them in the presence of Police Narcotics Bureau (PNB) officers.

The inspection uncovered 18.193 kilogrammes of hashish, with Customs estimating the street value of the consignment at Rs. 181,930,000.

Following preliminary investigations, the seized narcotics were handed over to the PNB for further investigations and legal proceedings.
