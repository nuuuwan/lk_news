SLT-MOBITEL marks double success at National Business Excellence Awards 2026

SLT-MOBITEL has once again demonstrated its leadership in business excellence by securing two prestigious accolades at the National Business Excellence Awards 2026, organized by the National Chamber of Commerce of Sri Lanka and held recently at the Shangri-La Colombo.

The company was recognized as Runner-up in the Infrastructure & Utilities Sector Category and Runner-up in the Excellence in Performance Management Criteria, reaffirming its commitment to operational excellence, strategic leadership, innovation, and a high-performance culture. This recognition reflects the company’s continuous efforts to create exceptional value for customers while advancing sustainable growth through digital transformation, innovation, and world-class business practices, further strengthening its position as a trusted technology leader in Sri Lanka.

The National Business Excellence Awards follows a comprehensive evaluation process through a multi-stage assessment conducted by an independent Technical and Financial Evaluation Committee, followed by a final review by an independent panel of judges.
