India, Sri Lanka discuss defence cooperation

Colombo, Sep. 10 (Daily Mirror) - A high-level Indian defence delegation met Sri Lanka’s Deputy Minister of Defence, Major General Aruna Jayasekara (Retd), at the Ministry of Defence yesterday, during Indian Defence Minister Rajnath Singh’s official visit to Sri Lanka.

The delegation included Air Marshal Ashutosh Dixit, Vice Chief of the Air Staff; Vice Admiral Ajay Kochhar, Vice Chief of the Naval Staff; Lieutenant General Sandeep Jain, Vice Chief of the Army Staff; and officials from the Indian High Commission.

During the meeting, the two sides discussed ways to further strengthen the longstanding defence cooperation between Sri Lanka and India. Discussions also focused on regional and maritime security, emerging security challenges, disaster preparedness and response capabilities, as well as training and capacity-building initiatives.

Deputy Defence Minister Major General Aruna Jayasekara expressed appreciation for India’s continued support and assistance to Sri Lanka, particularly the assistance provided during the Cyclone Ditwah disaster.

He said India’s support during the disaster further reflected the longstanding friendship, solidarity and close ties between the two countries.
