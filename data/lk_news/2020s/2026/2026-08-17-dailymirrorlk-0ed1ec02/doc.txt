Two women appointed as NTC scheduler officers for first time

Colombo, Aug. 17 (Daily Mirror) - Two women have been appointed as scheduler officers of the National Transport Commission (NTC) for the first time in the Commission’s history, among 18 new officers recruited for the positions.

An appointment ceremony was held to present letters of appointment to the newly recruited scheduler officers under the guidance of the Ministry of Transport, Highways and Urban Development.

Deputy Minister of Transport and Highways Prasanna Gunasena attended the ceremony, along with NTC Chairman P.A. Chandrapala, the Director General, members of the Board and other officials.

The appointments are expected to strengthen the NTC’s scheduling operations and support the efficient management of public transport services.
