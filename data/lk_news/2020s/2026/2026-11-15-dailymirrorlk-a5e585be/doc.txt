Did Bing Chun really fail safety tests? The company finally responds

Nov 15 (Daily Mirror) - Bing Chun Sri Lanka has clarified that recent laboratory findings concerning vanilla and chocolate ice cream sold at its Kompannaweediya (Slave Island) outlet related to prescribed compositional standards, and did not expressly establish that the products were contaminated, harmful to health or unfit for human consumption.

In an official public statement issued in response to media reports on proceedings before the Maligakanda Magistrate’s Court, the company said the matter concerned minimum fat content and total solids requirements under Sri Lanka’s Food (Standards) Regulations.

“It is important to distinguish between a product's compliance with prescribed compositional standards and a determination that a product is unsafe or unfit for human consumption,” the company said.

Clarification Regarding Recent Media Reports and Our Commitment to Quality and Regulatory Compliance

To our valued customers, the general public, and the relevant regulatory authorities of Sri Lanka,

Bing Chun Sri Lanka wishes to address the recent media reports concerning the proceedings before the Maligakanda Magistrate's Court relating to certain products sold at the Bing Chun Kompannaweediya (Slave Island) outlet.

We recognise the concerns expressed by our valued customers and members of the public. We sincerely regret any concern or uncertainty arising from this matter and wish to provide the following clarification.

The matter concerned the prescribed compositional standards applicable to two products marketed as vanilla ice cream and chocolate ice cream, particularly the minimum requirements relating to fat content and total solids under the relevant Food (Standards) Regulations of Sri Lanka.

It is important to distinguish between a product's compliance with prescribed compositional standards and a determination that a product is unsafe or unfit for human consumption.

The laboratory findings relating to this matter concerned the aforesaid compositional requirements. The reports did not expressly conclude that the products were contaminated, poisonous, harmful to human health or unfit for human consumption.

Accordingly, we respectfully request that the public and media distinguish between the actual nature of the regulatory findings and broader allegations concerning consumer health and safety.

Bing Chun fully respects the laws, regulations and regulatory authorities of Sri Lanka.

We acknowledge the importance of complying with the prescribed food standards applicable to products marketed and sold within the country.

We also respect the judicial process and the decisions of the competent courts.

In response to this matter, we are reviewing the relevant product specifications, descriptions and applicable regulatory requirements to ensure that appropriate corrective measures are implemented.

We remain committed to cooperating with the relevant regulatory authorities and addressing matters brought to our attention in accordance with Sri Lankan law.

The confidence and trust of Sri Lankan consumers are of the utmost importance to Bing Chun.

We recognise our responsibility to ensure that products offered under our brand comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Accordingly, we are taking steps to review the relevant product formulations, quality specifications and product descriptions.

Where necessary, appropriate adjustments will be made to ensure that our products are accurately described and comply with the applicable regulatory framework.

We remain committed to maintaining consistent product quality and providing consumers with clear and accurate information.

As part of our commitment to continuous improvement, we are arranging further assessments of the relevant products through appropriate testing procedures.

We will review the findings and implement any necessary corrective measures in accordance with the applicable requirements.

We are also strengthening our internal procedures for monitoring product specifications and regulatory compliance across our operations.

These measures are intended to reinforce consumer confidence and maintain consistent standards throughout the Bing Chun network.

We respect the role of the media in informing the public and recognise the importance of transparent and responsible reporting.

At the same time, we respectfully emphasise that noncompliance with a prescribed compositional standard should not automatically be represented as proof that a product is dangerous to human health.

We therefore encourage accurate and balanced reporting that reflects the actual nature of the findings and avoids creating unnecessary public concern through unsupported conclusions.

Bing Chun reserves its legal rights in respect of any false, misleading or defamatory statements that may cause unwarranted damage to its reputation.

Bing Chun values the trust and support extended by Sri Lankan consumers.

We remain committed to operating responsibly, respecting the country's laws and regulations, and continuously improving our products and quality assurance procedures.

We sincerely appreciate the concerns expressed by our customers and the guidance provided by the relevant regulatory authorities.

We assure our valued consumers that we will continue taking appropriate measures to maintain regulatory compliance, product quality and consumer confidence.
