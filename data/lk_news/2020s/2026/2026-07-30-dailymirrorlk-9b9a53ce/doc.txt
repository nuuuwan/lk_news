CAA cracks down on sale of non-SLS certified motorcycle helmets

Colombo, July 30 (Daily Mirror) - The Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA) has intensified its islandwide crackdown on the manufacture and sale of motorcycle helmets that do not carry the mandatory Sri Lanka Standards (SLS) certification mark, seizing hundreds of non-compliant helmets and halting the sale of thousands more.

The CAA said a special enforcement operation carried out on Monday (27) led to 98 raids across the country, during which 431 helmets without the SLS 517 Product Certification Mark were seized. Authorities also prohibited the sale of 3,520 helmets manufactured at two factories during periods when they did not possess valid SLS licences.

Under Direction No. 59, published in Gazette Extraordinary No. 1923/65 of July 16, 2015, all motorcycle helmets manufactured in or imported into Sri Lanka are required by law to carry the SLS 517 Product Certification Mark. The manufacture, importation, distribution, storage, display or sale of helmets without the certification mark is an offence.

The CAA said that from January to June this year, it had instituted 53 legal cases, seized 87 non-compliant helmets and concluded 39 cases, while 14 cases remain pending before courts.

Overall, the authority has carried out 151 raids and enforcement actions so far this year, covering more than 4,038 helmets.

The CAA urged manufacturers to ensure helmets are produced only with valid SLS licences and warned importers, distributors, wholesalers, retailers, mobile traders and online sellers against displaying or selling helmets that do not bear the mandatory SLS certification mark.
