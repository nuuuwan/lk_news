Yala strengthens water supply for wildlife due to dry weather

Colombo, Aug. 17 (Daily Mirror) - The Wildlife Conservation Department has taken steps to ensure that wild animals in the Yala National Park have access to adequate water during the current dry weather conditions.

According to officials, water required by animals is being supplied to the park daily, with three bowsers currently being used for the purpose.

The department has also taken steps to generate electricity through solar panels and provide water to small ponds established at six locations within the park.

Officials said the capacity of these facilities will be increased according to requirements to ensure a continuous water supply for wildlife during dry periods.

The measures were discussed during a visit to Yala National Park yesterday (16) by Environment Minister Dr. Dammika Patabendi, who met senior officials of the Department of Wildlife Conservation.

The minister was briefed on the existing water facilities and other conservation-related requirements within the park.

The provision of water is particularly important during prolonged dry conditions, when natural water sources can become limited and wildlife may be forced to travel greater distances in search of water.

The department's efforts to expand water facilities and use solar power are also expected to support more sustainable management of the national park.

The proposed improvements form part of a broader plan to develop Yala's physical and human resources while maintaining the park's wildlife conservation objectives.
