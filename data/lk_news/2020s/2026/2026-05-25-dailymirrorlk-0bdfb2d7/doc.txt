KTM Peliyagoda brings premium motorcycles closer to riders

Naalaka Madugalle along with Lakmal de Silva, Akila Elabodage cutting the ribbon to open KTM Peliyagoda.

Naalaka Madugalle handing over the bike key to one of first customers

Further strengthening the presence of KTM motorcycles in Sri Lanka following their reintroduction by David Pieris Motor Company (Private) Limited (DPMC), the latest showroom, KTM Peliyagoda, was recently opened at No. 389, Negombo Road.

Naalaka Madugalle, Director and Chief Operating Officer, David Pieris Motor Company (Private) Limited along with Lakmal de Silva, Chief Officer – Vehicle Sales, Akila Elabodage, Divisional Manager – Vehicle Sales declared opened the new dealer showroom.

The new state-of-the-art showroom offers motorcycle enthusiasts the opportunity to experience KTM’s globally renowned range of performance motorcycles, including the Duke 160, Duke 250 and Adventure 390 X models.

DPMC officially reintroduced KTM motorcycles to Sri Lanka earlier this year after a five-year hiatus, once again enabling local riders to experience one of the world’s leading high-performance motorcycle brands.

Globally recognised as Europe’s No. 1 racing motorcycle brand, KTM has established a dominant presence in premier international racing arenas including MotoGP, the Dakar Rally, motocross championships and leading global racing circuits. The brand also enjoys strong popularity across regional markets such as India and Nepal, while maintaining a footprint in more than 80 countries worldwide. KTM’s rich racing heritage, engineering excellence and consistent track performance have earned the trust of professional riders and motorcycle enthusiasts around the globe.

Renowned for their aggressive styling, bold design language and racing-inspired engineering, KTM motorcycles are powered by high-performance liquid-cooled engines paired with 6-speed transmission systems. The motorcycles also feature lightweight split trellis frames and advanced WP APEX USD suspension systems, delivering superior handling, stability and acceleration.

Selected KTM models are equipped with advanced rider-focused technologies including dual-disc dual-channel ABS with four-piston radial-mounted callipers, ride modes with Supermoto ABS, ride-by-wire systems, slipper clutches, Quickshifter+ functionality and modern TFT colour displays with multiple smart features, ensuring enhanced safety, control and riding exhilaration.

As the sole authorised distributor for KTM motorcycles in Sri Lanka, DPMC has already initiated plans to establish an extensive islandwide showroom and service network, with KTM Peliyagoda marking another important milestone in this expansion journey.
