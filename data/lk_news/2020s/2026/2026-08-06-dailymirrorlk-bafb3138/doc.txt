Third school term ends tomorrow

Colombo, Aug. 6 (Daily Mirror) - The first phase of the third school term for Sinhala and Tamil government and government-approved private schools will officially conclude tomorrow (August 7), the Ministry of Education announced.

Meanwhile, the first phase of the third term for Muslim schools is scheduled to end on September 2.

The upcoming school vacation aligns with the 2026 G.C.E. Advanced Level Examination, which will run from August 10 to September 5.

According to the Ministry, all schools across the island will resume classes for the second phase of the third term on September 7, with the term continuing until December 4.
