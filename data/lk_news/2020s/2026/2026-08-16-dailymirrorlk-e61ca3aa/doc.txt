Bathing incident in Ma Oya leaves one dead, one missing

Colombo, Aug. 16 (Daily Mirror) - A 19-year-old youth has died while another remains missing after three young men got into difficulty while bathing in the Ma Oya in the Madurupitiya area of Meerigama yesterday (15) afternoon.

Police said the three youths had gone to the Ma Oya to bathe when they encountered difficulties in the water.

Two of them were rescued by area residents and admitted to Dambadeniya Hospital. One of the youths, who was in critical condition, later succumbed to his injuries.

The deceased was a 19-year-old resident of the Meerigama area.

A search operation is underway to locate the missing youth, with Meerigama Police, officers from the Police Life Saving Unit and local residents taking part in the operation.
