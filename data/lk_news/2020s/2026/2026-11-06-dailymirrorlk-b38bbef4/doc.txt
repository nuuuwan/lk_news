Janaki Siriwardena further remanded in Krrish case

Former director of Krrish Group Janaki Siriwardena, who is in remand custody over the alleged Krrish financial transaction, was further remanded till November 20 by Colombo Chief Magistrate Asanga S. Bodaragama.

Making further submissions on behalf of the Commission to Investigate Allegations of Bribery or Corruption, Assistant Director of Legal Tanuja Bandara informed court that statements had been recorded from several individuals in connection with the incident, including the Colombo Divisional Secretary and a former Land Commissioner.

She further said that additional statements were yet to be recorded.

She also informed the court that a letter bearing the signature of the fourth suspect had been sent to the Government Analyst for handwriting examination. However, the document submitted was only a photocopy, and the Government Analyst had informed the Commission that the original document was required to conduct the examination.

The original document is currently filed in connection with another case pending before Courtroom No. 7 of the Colombo High Court.

As the Commission is not a party to that case, it is unable to obtain the original document directly, she said.

She requested court to issue an order directing that the original document be released to the Commission, stating that it could be forwarded to the Government Analyst within one day and returned to court thereafter.

Having considered the submissions, the Chief Magistrate advised the CIABOC to submit a written report clearly setting out the grounds for its request.

Meanwhile, the counsel appearing for the third suspect, former Krrish Group director Janaki Siriwardena, informed court that a bail application would be made on her behalf.

The Chief Magistrate directed the counsel to file a motion on November 13 to make the bail application.
