Yield curve eases following T-Bond auction

Following the T-Bond auction held yesterday, the yields eased in the secondary market, adjusting the yield curve downward. The market saw buying interest with moderate trading volumes.

At the T-Bond auction held yesterday, the PDMO successfully raised the full amount offered. Of the total accepted, Rs. 90.0bn was raised through the 2031 maturity, Rs. 80.0bn through the 2034 maturity, Rs. 50.0bn through the 2036 maturity, and Rs. 30.0bn through the 2037 maturity, at weighted average yields of 11.90%, 12.42%, and 12.91% and 13.01% respectively.

Over the 2030 segment, 01.07.2030, 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 traded between 11.60% - 11.55%. Moving ahead, 01.02.2031 and 15.10.2034 maturities traded within the range of 11.80% - 11.70% and 12.40% -12.25% respectively. Additionally, 15.08.2036 and 01.07.2037 traded between 12.80% - 12.65% and 12.90% - 12.75% respectively.

On the external front, the LKR appreciated against the USD, standing at Rs. 336.18/USD, compared to Rs. 336.25/USD seen earlier. Overnight liquidity in the banking system expanded to Rs. 195.00bn from Rs. 185.96bn recorded previously.
