PDMO concludes T-bond auction; 2030 tenure takes spotlight

The T-bond auction took centre stage yesterday, with investor interest in the secondary market primarily concentrated within auction-bound tenures. While the overall investor activity appeared limited, the trading volumes remained moderate. Prior to the T-bond auction, activity clustered around the 2030 segment. Consequently, driven by the selling sentiment, 01.08.2030 and 15.10.2030 traded between 10.75 percent and 10.80 percent.

The PDMO concluded the T-bond auction, raising a total of Rs.150.0 billion. Bulk of this, amounting to Rs.70.0 billion, was raised through a 2030 maturity, bearing a coupon of 10.00 percent and a weighted average yield of 10.83 percent.

Rs.50.0 billion and Rs.30.0 billion were raised via the 2034 and 2037 maturities, which had coupon rates of 11.70 percent and 10.75 percent, respectively.  The weighted average yield recorded for the 2034 maturity was 11.96 percent while that of the 2037 maturity stood at 12.08 percent.

Modest investor activity was evident following the conclusion of the T-bond auction, though investor interest continued to remain within auction-bound tenors. The overall sentiment turned mixed, with 01.08.2030 trading at 10.85 percent and 15.10.2030 trading higher at 10.90 percent while 01.07.2037 was seen changing hands at 12.00 percent. On the external front, the Sri Lankan rupee appreciated marginally against the US dollar, standing at Rs.328.60/US dollar, compared to Rs.328.75/US dollar seen earlier.

Market liquidity in the banking system remained stable at Rs.366.11 billion, compared to Rs.366.20 billion recorded previously.
