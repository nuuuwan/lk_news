Laugfs Supermarkets opens 44th outlet in Mount Lavinia

Laugfs Supermarkets marked another milestone in its expansion journey, with the opening of its 44th supermarket at No. 355, Galle Road, Mount Lavinia.

Strategically located along one of Sri Lanka’s busiest commercial corridors, the new outlet offers the customers a convenient 24-hour shopping experience, complemented by a fresh bakery, hot food section and a wide range of groceries, fresh produce and household essentials.

The opening ceremony was attended by the group chairman, group executive vice chairman, acting group managing director/group executive director, director/group chief people officer, director/group chief supply chain officer and members of the senior management team, reaffirming the group’s commitment to strengthening its retail presence across the country.

Sector Managing Director and CEO Retail Niroshan De Silva emphasised that delivering an exceptional customer experience remains the foundation of the brand’s continued growth.

“As we continue to expand our footprint across Sri Lanka, our greatest priority is to ensure every customer enjoys a welcoming, convenient and memorable shopping experience. This new outlet brings us closer to the Mount Lavinia community and we remain committed to providing quality products, excellent service and a shopping environment that encourages the customers to return time and again,” he said.

The new Mount Lavinia outlet reflects Laugfs Supermarkets’ ongoing strategy of making modern retail more accessible to Sri Lankan communities while continuously enhancing convenience, product variety and service excellence. Operating around the clock, the supermarket is designed to meet the evolving lifestyles of today’s consumers by providing a seamless shopping experience at any hour of the day.

With a steadily expanding network and a strong focus on customer satisfaction, Laugfs Supermarkets continues to reinforce its position as one of Sri Lanka’s leading modern retail chains, bringing quality, convenience and value closer to the customers across the island.
