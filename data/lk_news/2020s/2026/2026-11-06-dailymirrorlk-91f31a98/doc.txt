Kosala Senadheera appointed new Gampaha Chief Magistrate

Colombo, November 06 (Daily Mirror)- Kosala Senadheera has been appointed as the new Chief Magistrate of Gampaha by the Judicial Service Commission (JSC).

The appointment letter, signed by JSC Secretary Prasanna Alwis, was reportedly issued today (06).

Accordingly, Senadheera is scheduled to assume duties as the Chief Magistrate of Gampaha from next Monday (09).

New appointment follows the transfer of incumbent Magistrate Bandula Gunaratne to the Judges’ Training Institute with immediate effect.

The development follows a request made by Magistrate Gunaratne to the President of the Court of Appeal to initiate contempt of court proceedings against the IGP, alleging that the latter had failed to comply with a court order and had brought the judiciary into disrepute.
