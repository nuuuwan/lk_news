Sri Lanka and Russia forge path to enhanced tourism ties at Moscow Forum

The 6th Let’s Travel International Tourism Forum in Moscow has emerged as a watershed moment for Sri Lanka’s tourism diplomacy, with high-level discussions on direct flights, simplified visas, and regional market expansion taking centre stage.

Deputy Minister of Tourism Prof. Ruwan Ranasinghe led the Sri Lankan delegation in pivotal meetings with Russian Deputy Minister of Economic Development Dmitry Vakhrukov, where discussions on destination promotion, familiarisation tours, and improved air connectivity addressed critical logistical barriers to increasing tourist arrivals.

Speaking at two high-level panel sessions on “Direct flights, simplified visas and what this means for the industry” and “Tourism of national importance: The strategic potential of international cooperation,” the Deputy Minister emphasised Sri Lanka’s commitment to facilitating seamless travel and building enduring partnerships. His comments underscored Sri Lanka’s strategic push to become a more accessible year-round destination for Russian travellers.
