SAARC Cultural Centre in Matara to be handed over to Ocean University

Colombo, Nov. 26 (Daily Mirror) - Plans are underway to transfer the SAARC Cultural Centre in Navimana, Matara, to the Ocean University of Sri Lanka following Cabinet approval, with officials holding discussions on its future development.

A special meeting was held at the Presidential Secretariat on November 20 to discuss how the centre's existing buildings could be used and what development activities should be carried out.

The meeting was chaired by Senior Additional Secretary to the President G.M.R.D. Aponso and Deputy Minister of Vocational Education Nalin Hewage.

Discussions focused on the current condition of the centre, the use of its completed buildings and plans to develop the facility for educational and training purposes.

Speaking at the meeting, Aponso stressed the need to implement the project systematically under a national plan.

Officials proposed conducting a feasibility study to assess the condition and suitability of the existing buildings. Discussions will also be held with the Sri Lanka Army to obtain information and recommendations on future development work.

It was agreed to hold another meeting within the next few weeks to decide on the project's future plans and the programmes to be implemented.

The initiative is expected to enable the Ocean University of Sri Lanka to make effective use of the Matara facility for its educational and training activities.

Vice Chancellor of the Ocean University of Sri Lanka Professor Nalin Prasanna Ratnayake, officials from the relevant ministries, the Matara District Secretariat and the Sri Lanka Army also attended the meeting.
