SLID and SEC collaborate for stronger corporate leadership

The Chairman of the Sri Lanka Institute of Directors (SLID), Dinesh Weerakkody, and CEO Anitra Perera called on SEC Chairman Senior Prof. D.B.P.H. Dissabandara to discuss opportunities for enhanced collaboration between SLID and the Securities and Exchange Commission of Sri Lanka.

The discussion focused on strengthening director bench strength, elevating boardroom capabilities, and advancing capacity-building initiatives to support stronger corporate governance standards in Sri Lanka.

The meeting also highlighted the upcoming Sri Lanka National Corporate Directors Summit 2026, which will bring together leading corporate directors from Sri Lanka and international governance experts to share insights, global practices, and perspectives on building future-ready boards. The Summit aims to contribute towards strengthening investor confidence, enhancing governance excellence, and supporting Sri Lanka’s ambition to build a more competitive and resilient corporate sector.
