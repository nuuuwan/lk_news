Double-whammy for suspected murderer

Colombo, Nov. 28 (Daily Mirror) - A 45-year-old mother of one was hacked to death with a sharp weapon at her home in the Galmaduwa area within the Eppawala Police Division early yesterday.

The suspect, who allegedly committed the murder, is reported to have swallowed poison before his motorbike collided with a wild elephant. He was admitted to hospital, police said.

The victim has been identified as Gayani Iroma Dissanayake (45), a mother of a 13-year-old daughter. She was employed at the Ipalogama office of the Coconut Development Board.

Police suspect that a financial dispute or an alleged illicit relationship may have led to the murder.

The victim, separated from her husband, was residing with her daughter at No. 74, Galmaduwa, Mahailluppallama.

According to police investigations, the woman had got up at around 4.00 a.m. to prepare breakfast for her daughter, who attends a private school in Anuradhapura. As she came out of the kitchen, the suspect, who had allegedly been hiding behind the house, attacked her with a machete, inflicting fatal injuries to her head and neck. She died at the scene.

After the attack, the suspect allegedly consumed poison and fled towards Mahailluppallama on a motorcycle.

Near the multi-purpose building in Mahailluppallama, his motorcycle collided with a wild elephant, causing an accident. As he had consumed poison, he reportedly vomited at the scene.

He was rushed to the Eppawala District Hospital, where police arrested him. The suspect is a pawn broker, police said.

He was later transferred to the Anuradhapura Teaching Hospital for further treatment under police protection.

Police also recovered the machete believed to have been used in the murder from a canal near the route taken by the suspect. It had allegedly been thrown away approximately 300 metres from the victim's house.

Investigations have further revealed that the suspect had allegedly threatened to kill the woman on previous occasions.

Eppawala Police are conducting further investigations into the incident. Police crime scene officers and the Magistrate are expected to visit the scene for further investigations.
