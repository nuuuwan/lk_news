Union Bank’s Dilshan Rodrigo appointed to LankaPay board

Union Bank announced the appointment of its Director and Chief Executive Officer Dilshan Rodrigo to the board of directors of LankaPay, Sri Lanka’s national payment network.

This appointment reflects Rodrigo’s extensive experience and leadership in the banking and financial services sector as well as his commitment to advancing innovation and digital transformation within the industry.  As a Director of LankaPay, Rodrigo will contribute to strengthening Sri Lanka’s national payments infrastructure and supporting the continued development of secure, efficient and inclusive digital payment solutions.

His appointment further reinforces Union Bank’s commitment to driving progress and fostering collaboration across the country’s financial ecosystem.
