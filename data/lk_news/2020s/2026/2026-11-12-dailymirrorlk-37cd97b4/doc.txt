Navy intercepts fishing trawler smuggling suspected narcotics in Southern waters

Colombo, Nov. 12 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lanka Navy has intercepted a local multi-day fishing trawler suspected of smuggling a large consignment of narcotics during a special operation conducted on the high seas off the southern coast.

According to the Navy, the intercepted vessel and the suspects aboard are currently being escorted ashore for comprehensive investigations, with further details regarding the haul expected to be released in due course.

The latest interception follows a similar high-seas operation conducted south of Sri Lanka on November 5, during which naval personnel seized a local multi-day trawler, apprehended five suspects, and recovered over 132 kilograms of methamphetamine (Ice) and more than 57 kilograms of heroin.

Under the national anti-drug initiative, ‘A Nation United’, the Sri Lanka Navy has seized more than 2,773 kilograms of Ice and over 2,130 kilograms of heroin—with a combined street value exceeding Rs. 114 billion—and handed them over for onward legal action.

In 2026 alone, naval operations have resulted in the seizure of illicit narcotics valued at over Rs. 73 billion.
