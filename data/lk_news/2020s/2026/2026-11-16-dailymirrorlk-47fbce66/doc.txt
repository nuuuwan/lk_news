Is the ADB loan allocated for health being used as a weapon?

The Chairman of the Medical and Civil Rights Doctors' Trade Union Alliance, Specialist Dr. Chamal Sanjeewa, has called for an investigation into whether there is any bias in the tender process for cancer treatment equipment worth crores to be acquired for cancer hospitals with financial assistance from the Asian Development Bank (ADB).

While tenders for some companies are proceeding without any hindrance, he alleges that tenders won by competing companies are being deliberately delayed.

Accordingly, the tenders for dosimetry equipment for 05 hospitals and 02 linear accelerators for the Maharagama Apeksha Hospital are proceeding without hindrance, and a 17-year-old model has been recommended for one of the units, he says.

Dr. Sanjeewa also said that despite another company winning the tender to provide CT simulators for 05 hospitals, it has been challenged and that the tender evaluation for the Galle Karapitiya Hospital has been completely stalled.

Pointing out that the delay in the delivery of these essential equipment for cancer treatment is causing great injustice to patients, he urges the health authorities and the ADB to immediately remove the conflicting members of the Technical Evaluation Committee and conduct a fair evaluation.
