Govt allocates Rs. 34 Million for former presidents in 2027 Budget: Minister

Colombo, November 21 (Daily Mirror)- The government has allocated Rs. 34 million for former Presidents under the President’s expenditure head in the 2027 Budget, Minister Ananda Wijepala said in Parliament today.

The Minister also revealed that Rs. 6,500 million has been allocated for the Clean Sri Lanka programme, making it the largest expenditure under the President’s allocations.

He further stated that, unlike in the past, the President had not allocated funds for family members, friends or political parties.
