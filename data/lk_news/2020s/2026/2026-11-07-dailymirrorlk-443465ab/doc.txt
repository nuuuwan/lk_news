Culinary Art Food Expo 2026 to kick-off on November 27

Group photograph of sponsors and members of Chefs’ Guild of Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka’s food and hospitality industries will take centre stage at the 23rd edition of the Culinary Art Food Expo (CAFE 2026), bringing together industry professionals, businesses and emerging culinary talent at one of the country’s leading events dedicated to the sector.

Scheduled to be held from 27 to 29 November at the Bandaranaike Memorial International Conference Hall (BMICH), Colombo, the exhibition is expected to attract more than 15,000 visitors, with over 200 exhibition stalls showcasing products, services and technologies across the food and hospitality value chain. The awards ceremony will take place on 30 November.

Organised by the Chefs’ Guild of Lanka (CGL), a member of the World Association of Chefs’ Societies (Worldchefs), in collaboration with CDC Events & Travels (Pvt) Ltd, CAFE 2026 will draw chefs, hoteliers, food manufacturers, distributors, hospitality professionals and industry leaders.

A major feature of this year’s event will be its international culinary competition, with more than 3,000 participants, nearly 4,000 food entries and over 40 local and international judges expected. Categories will include live cooking, pastry and bakery, chocolate showpieces, cake decorating, fruit and vegetable carving, and mixology, providing a platform for culinary professionals and hospitality students to demonstrate their skills.

The programme will also introduce two concurrent events: the World Young Chefs Club Sri Lanka Junior Chefs Culinary Championship 2026 and the World Chefs Global Butchery Competition 2026 - Sri Lanka. These initiatives are expected to create further opportunities for young and emerging culinary professionals to develop their capabilities and gain exposure.

The event comes at an important time for Sri Lanka’s international culinary engagement. The country will compete in the Culinary World Cup 2026 in Luxembourg from 21 to 25 November, while the Chefs’ Guild of Lanka is also pursuing a bid to host the Worldchefs Congress in 2030. According to the organisers, Sri Lankan culinary teams have previously represented the country at the Culinary World Cup and the Culinary Olympics, winning multiple gold medals.

Beyond the competitions, CAFE 2026 will provide a platform for business engagement across the sector. Exhibits will include food and beverages, processed foods, spices, bakery products, tea and coffee, kitchen and catering equipment, food-processing technology, packaging and hospitality solutions. Product launches, demonstrations and business-to-business networking are among the opportunities planned for exhibitors.

Leading food and beverage companies supporting the event as Gold Sponsors include Cargills (Ceylon) PLC, Maliban Biscuit Manufactories (Pvt) Ltd, C.W. Mackie PLC (Sunquick), Tastyy (Pvt) Ltd, CBL Food Solutions, International Distillers Ltd, Pelwatte Dairy Industries Ltd, Pyramid Wilmar (Pvt) Ltd, Prima Ceylon (Pvt) Ltd, Silvermill Naturals (Pvt) Ltd and Dilmah Ceylon Tea Company PLC.
