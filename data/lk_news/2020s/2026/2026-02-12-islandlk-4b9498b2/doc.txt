Profit-takings return bourse to negative territory

The CSE yesterday set off on a negative note and by mid-session turned positive in a strong manner but dipped into negativity due to profit-takings latterly.The All Share Price Index went down by 3.17 points, while the S and P SL20 declined by 4.47 points. Turnover stood at Rs 4.47 billion with 10 crossings.

Top seven companies that had crossings during the day were; Renuka Foods 4.8 million shares crossed to the tune of Rs 236 million and its shares traded at Rs 70, Commercial Bank 386165 shares crossed to the tune of Rs 83 million; its shares sold at Rs 228, Colombo Dockyards 450,000 shares crossed for Rs 57.5 million; its shares traded at Rs 150, Dialog Axiata 1.5 million shares crossed for Rs 48.7 million; its shares traded at Rs 32.50, Access Engineering 180,000 shares crossed for Rs 22.4 million; its shares sold at Rs 70 and LMF 300,000 shares crossed for Rs 24.3 million; its shares traded at Rs 81.

In the retail market top seven companies that mainly contributed to the turnover were; Cargills Bank Rs 854 million (95.7 million shares traded), LMF Rs 297 million (3.7 million shares traded),Colombo Dockyard Rs 240 million (1.4 million shares traded), Softlogic Capital Rs 120 million (15.5 million shares traded), HNB Rs 105 million (243893 shares traded), Sampath Bank Rs 104 million (666,855 shares traded) and LB Finance Rs 98 million (600,000 shares traded). During the day 224 million shares volumes changed hands in 36253 transactions.

It it said that mixed reactions were noted throughout the day. The banking and finance sector performed well, especially Cargills Bank and HNB. FMCG sector also performed well, especially Renuka Foods.

Yesterday the rupee was quoted at Rs 309.38/45 to the US dollar in the spot market, from Rs 309.35/40 the previous day, dealers said, while bond yields were broadly steady from the previous close.
