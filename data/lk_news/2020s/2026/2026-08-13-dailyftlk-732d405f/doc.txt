Singer Finance joins hands with Ceylon Automobile Importers Association to promote responsible motoring

Singer Finance has come on board as the ‘Main Sponsor’ of the ‘Ceylon Automobile Importers Motor Rally 2026’, reaffirming its commitment to supporting Sri Lanka’s automotive industry while promoting responsible motoring and accessible vehicle

Organised by the Ceylon Automobile Importers Association, the Motor Rally brings together Sri Lanka’s leading vehicle importers to celebrate the country’s passion for automobiles while creating greater awareness of road safety, responsible driving practices and the positive contribution of the automotive sector to the national economy.

True to its role as ‘Your Number 1 Leasing Partner’, Singer Finance extends its involvement beyond event sponsorship by delivering trusted financial solutions that empower customers and drive the growth of the country’s mobility ecosystem.

Singer Finance Managing Director Thushan Amarasuriya said, “SINGER FINANCE is delighted to partner with the Ceylon Automobile Importers Motor Rally, an event that reflects the enduring passion for automobiles and the continued growth of Sri Lanka’s motoring community. This partnership aligns perfectly with our promise of being ‘Your Number 1 Leasing Partner’, as we continue to empower more Sri Lankans to achieve their vehicle ownership aspirations through trusted financial solutions.”

Ceylon Automobile Importers Association President Prasad Manage welcomed the partnership stating, “We are pleased to have Singer Finance join us as the Main Sponsor of the Ceylon Automobile Importers Motor Rally 2026. As the representative body of Sri Lanka’s vehicle importers, our Association works to support the sustainable growth of the industry while engaging with policymakers on matters relating to vehicle imports, regulation and industry development. Through initiatives such as the Motor Rally, we also strive to promote responsible motoring and foster greater appreciation for the automotive sector among the public. We are delighted to partner with an industry leader like SINGER FINANCE, whose commitment to responsible vehicle ownership and customer-centric financial solutions aligns closely with our vision.”

The partnership will also bring exclusive benefits to customers through a series of special promotional campaigns in collaboration with member importers of the Association, further enhancing the vehicle purchasing experience with attractive value-added offers.

Through this collaboration, Singer Finance continues to strengthen its long-standing commitment to supporting the automotive industry while helping more Sri Lankans turn their vehicle ownership aspirations into reality.
