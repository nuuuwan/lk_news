ICC World Test Championship race wide open after Bangladesh stun Australia

The latest ICC World Test Championship (WTC) standings have experienced a significant shake-up following Bangladesh’s sensational victory against table-toppers Australia in Darwin. Delivering one of the most remarkable performances in their Test history, Bangladesh secured a comprehensive nine-wicket triumph over the former world champions, shifting momentum in the ongoing 2025–27 WTC cycle.

Despite suffering their second defeat of the campaign, Australia still holds the top spot on the leaderboard. However, their points percentage (PCT) dipped from 87.50% down to 77.78% (84 points from nine matches), bringing them considerably closer to the chasing pack. South Africa closely follows in second place with a 75.00% PCT (36 points from four matches), while inaugural WTC winners New Zealand occupy third position at 72.22% (52 points from six matches).

The biggest beneficiary of the latest match result is Bangladesh, whose historic win propelled them firmly into fourth place with a 66.67% PCT (40 points from five matches). Powered by a stellar maiden Test century on Australian soil from opener Tanzid Hasan and devastating bowling spells from pacer Hasan Mahmud and spinner Mehidy Hasan Miraz, the Tigers have firmly inserted themselves into contention for a spot in the WTC Final.

Further down the table, India holds fifth place with a 48.15% PCT (52 points from nine matches), maintaining a precarious mid-table standing. Sri Lanka sits just behind in sixth place with a 41.67% PCT as they navigate their ongoing series. Meanwhile, England (24.36%), Pakistan (22.22%), and the West Indies (20.83%) occupy the bottom three positions of the current standings as the race for the 2027 WTC Final intensifies.
