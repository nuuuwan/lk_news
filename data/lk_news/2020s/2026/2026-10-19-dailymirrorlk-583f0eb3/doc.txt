New motorcycle registration services launched in five districts

Colombo, October 19 (Daily Mirror) - New motorcycle registration services commenced this morning at the Gampaha District Regional Office of the Department of Motor Traffic, with similar services being introduced in Jaffna, Hambantota, Anuradhapura and Kurunegala.

The inauguration ceremony at the Gampaha Regional Office is scheduled to be held between 9.00 a.m. and 9.30 a.m. under the patronage of the Commissioner General of Motor Traffic.

The new service is expected to enable residents in the five districts to complete motorcycle registration procedures more conveniently and efficiently through the respective regional offices, reducing the need to travel to the department’s central office.
