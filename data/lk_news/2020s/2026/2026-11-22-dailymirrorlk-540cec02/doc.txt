Workers’ protests planned for tomorrow, strikes on November 30

Colombo, Nov. 22 (Daily Mirror) - The Workers’ Struggle Centre has announced plans to hold protests in front of government institutions and workplaces tomorrow (23), followed by sector-based strikes on November 30.

The centre said one-day strikes would be held in certain sectors, while workers in other sectors would stage two-day strikes.

It warned that further trade union action would be launched if the Government failed to respond adequately to workers’ grievances.

The centre said it was prepared to mobilise workers for broader action to protect workers’ rights and oppose what it described as injustices against the people.
