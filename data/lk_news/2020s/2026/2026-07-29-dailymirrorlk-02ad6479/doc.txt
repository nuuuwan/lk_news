Private hospital fined Rs. 500,000 for overcharging on blood test

A private hospital in Vavuniya has been fined Rs. 500,000 for charging above the government-controlled price for a Full Blood Count (FBC) test.

According to the Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA), the violation was detected during a raid conducted by its investigation officers, who found that the hospital had overcharged patients for the laboratory test in breach of the regulated pricing.

Following the investigation, the Authority filed legal action against the hospital before the Vavuniya Magistrate’s Court.

After hearing the case, the Vavuniya Magistrate’s Court imposed a fine of Rs. 500,000 on the private hospital for violating the government-controlled price regulations, the CAA said.
