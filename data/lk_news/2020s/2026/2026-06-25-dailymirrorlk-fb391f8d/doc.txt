Redington-IBM host Innovation Forum Sri Lanka 2026

Bring together Sri Lanka’s enterprise leaders to accelerate AI-driven transformation

Flagship forum highlights role of secure AI, ecosystem collaboration and enterprise innovation in advancing Sri Lanka’s digital economy

Redington, in collaboration with IBM, successfully hosted IBM Innovation Forum Sri Lanka 2026 recently, at Cinnamon Life City of Dreams, bringing together senior business leaders, technology decision-makers, government representatives and ecosystem partners to discuss the future of secure, AI-driven enterprise transformation.

Held under the theme ‘Building Secure, AI-Driven and Autonomous Enterprises in Sri Lanka’, the forum provided a platform for organisations to explore how emerging technologies can accelerate innovation, strengthen resilience and deliver measurable business outcomes.

Commenting on the significance of the forum, Redington CEO ASEAN and South Asia Cem Borhan said, “Sri Lanka is entering a defining phase in its digital transformation journey. Organisations are increasingly focused on converting technology investments into measurable business outcomes. Through the IBM Innovation Forum, we are bringing together leaders from across government, industry and technology to exchange insights and enable practical pathways for AI-driven transformation. Our collaboration with IBM reflects a shared commitment to helping enterprises innovate responsibly, strengthen resilience and unlock long-term growth.”

Aligned with Sri Lanka’s National Digital Economy Strategy 2030, the forum featured discussions on enterprise AI adoption, cybersecurity, infrastructure modernisation, data protection, operational resilience and governance. Industry experts shared practical perspectives on how organisations can move beyond experimentation to operationalise AI securely and at scale.

The event featured keynote addresses from IBM India and South Asia Vice President Technology Gaurav Agarwal and IBM India and South Asia Executive Director Growth Markets, Technology Sales Siddhesh Naik, who highlighted opportunities for enterprises to leverage AI and hybrid cloud technologies to drive innovation and business value.

A key highlight of the forum was a panel discussion featuring technology leaders from DFCC Bank, SANASA Development Bank and Union Bank, who shared insights on scaling AI-driven transformation while maintaining security, compliance and operational resilience.

The forum also marked the introduction of IBM Bob, an AI-first development partner built for enterprise teams, in Sri Lanka. It works across the full software development lifecycle, from planning and coding to testing, deployment and modernisation, with the governance and security controls enterprises need.
