Construction of Jaffna Cricket Stadium to resume next week

Colombo, Nov 09 (Daily Mirror) - Construction work on the proposed cricket stadium in Mandaitivu, Jaffna, is scheduled to resume next week, Sri Lanka Cricket (SLC) said.

SLC said all necessary approvals, clearances and certificates required for the project had been obtained, while the contract-awarding process for the remaining construction work had also been completed.

Meanwhile, Minister of Youth Affairs and Sports Sunil Kumara Gamage visited the stadium site yesterday (8) to inspect its current condition and review the project's progress.

During the visit, the Minister also examined concerns raised by certain parties that the site was prone to flooding during the rainy season.

According to SLC, the inspection found those claims to be baseless.

With the required administrative procedures and approvals now completed, construction is expected to resume next week, paving the way for the development of cricket infrastructure in the Northern Province.
