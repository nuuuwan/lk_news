Medihelp Hospitals opens new facility in Kiribathgoda

Medihelp Hospitals has opened a new healthcare facility in Kiribathgoda, marking a meaningful return to where the organization first began as a diagnostic lab in 1979.

The branch offers OPD, ETU, channeling, lab diagnostics, radiology, ultrasound, and echocardiography under one roof—addressing a long-standing lack of comprehensive private healthcare in the area.

“We are proud to open a Medihelp branch in Kiribathgoda—the very place where our journey began,” said Managing Director Chandika Wijesiriwardana.

The inauguration was attended by Minister Dr. Upali Pannilage and Kelaniya Pradeshiya Sabha Chairman Dr. Sandun Yapa Karunaratna. Medihelp plans to further expand into underserved towns like Kadawatha, reinforcing its mission to make quality, accessible healthcare available to all Sri Lankans.
