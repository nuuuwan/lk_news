‘FiNAC HROptimizer’ leads as most effective remote workforce mgt. software

As fuel supply disruptions linked to the Middle East create uncertainty, remote work offers businesses a practical way to conserve fuel, reduce costs and maintain productivity

Fuel shortages and disruptions to global energy supplies can directly affect businesses and employees. For organisations that depend on daily commuting, limited fuel availability, rising transport costs and long journeys, can impact attendance, productivity and operating expenses.

In this environment, remote work is more than an employee benefit; it is a practical strategy for business continuity, fuel conservation and cost management, according to a leading IT company in Sri Lanka.

“However, allowing employees to work from home is only part of the solution. Businesses must maintain visibility into workloads, ensure accountability and deliver customer assignments on time. This is where a secure and comprehensive workflow management software platform becomes very effective,” Datamation Systems (Pvt.) Ltd Chairman and Managing Director Fazal Issadeen points out.

During the Covid pandemic, Datamation Systems faced the challenge of managing a workforce with more than 70 percent of employees working remotely. Limited visibility into employee activities and difficulties in managing distributed teams contributed to a productivity decline of nearly 50 percent.

The company implemented its in-house developed ‘FiNAC HROptimizer (FHRO)’ workflow management software across remote and office-based employees, enabling flexible working while providing management with greater visibility into performance, task progress and accountability. “The experience demonstrated how the right systems can help organisations manage distributed teams effectively.”

An employee travelling from Matara to Colombo, spending approximately three hours commuting each way, now works remotely, eliminating his daily journey, saving valuable time, reducing fuel consumption and lowering travel expenses. Similarly, an employee based in Polonnaruwa works from home without relocating to Colombo, avoiding accommodation, transportation and other associated costs.

“When applied across an organisation, these savings can be substantial. Fewer daily commutes mean less fuel consumed, reduced pressure on transport availability and lower employee personal expenses.”

Remote work can also help organisations reduce office space requirements and expenses associated with rent, electricity, maintenance, parking and administration. Using FHRO, managers can monitor workloads, identify unproductive activities and improve workforce utilisation without requiring employees to be physically present in the office.

“We witnessed employee productivity increase to more than 30 percent above pre-pandemic levels. Billable man-hours increased by 30 percent, net profitability improved by 10 percent, and the company achieved an annual return on investment equivalent to nine times its investment. FHRO by ensuring every employee contributes his fair share of work, creates a motivated team and reduces attrition,” Issadeen explains.

FHRO supports organisations across industries, particularly those with ten or more employees using desktops or laptops.

By combining remote work with measurable accountability, businesses can conserve fuel, reduce costs and maintain productivity - even during periods of energy supply uncertainty, the company says. ‘FiNAC HROptimizer’ is now effectively used by 350 employees worldwide.

Datamation’s primary infrastructure, talent pool, and headquarters are located in Colombo. This is where its teams handle day-to-day data management, digital innovation, IT, and financial outsourcing workflows. The company which began operations in 1986 presently has over 400 satisfied clients.
