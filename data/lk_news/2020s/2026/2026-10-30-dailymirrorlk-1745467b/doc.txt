NBRO extends landslide warning for four districts

The National Building Research Organisation (NBRO) has extended the Level 1 landslide warning (Yellow – Be Vigilant) issued for four districts due to prevailing rainy weather conditions.

Accordingly, the warning will remain in effect until 4:00 p.m. tomorrow (October 31), the NBRO said.

* Ratnapura District: Ayagama, Opanayake, Elapatha, Kuruwita, Pelmadulla and Ratnapura
