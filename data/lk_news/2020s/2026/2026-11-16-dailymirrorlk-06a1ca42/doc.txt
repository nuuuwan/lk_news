William and Kate play ‘gully’ cricket with Sachin Tendulkar to begin India trip

INDEPENDENT - William and Kate played street cricket with former cricketer Sachin Tendulkar in Mumbai on Monday to launch a three-day trip to India for the Earthshot Prize awards.

The royal couple were welcomed in the Ballard Estate business district by Mr Tendulkar, the former India skipper widely regarded as one of the sport’s greatest batsmen.

William tried his hand at batting, then bowled to Kate and later to Mr Tendulkar.

They played alongside schoolgirls from the Mumbai neighbourhood of Nagpada.

Mr Tendulkar supports sports, health and education initiatives for underprivileged children through the Sachin Tendulkar Foundation.

The couple were greeted by drummers and served “cutting chai”, tea in small portions, in earthen cups at a stall under a banyan tree.

Mumbai hosts the sixth Earthshot Prize ceremony on Tuesday, the first time the awards are being held in India.

William founded the prize in 2020 as a 10-year project to repair the planet, and it is now at its mid-way point. Kate is reportedly “enormously proud” of the project.

It gives £1 million each to five winners in categories that include protecting nature, cleaning the air and reviving the oceans.

Fifteen finalists were announced in September. They include a project in Argentina that uses artificial intelligence and satellite data to track wildfires across Latin America.

The visit is the Princess of Wales’s most high-profile overseas trip since undergoing cancer treatment in 2024. She is now in remission and is taking on more public engagements.

Her only significant overseas visit in that time was a two-day trip to Italy in May to promote her work on early childhood development.

The India programme also includes a ride on Mumbai’s metro and meetings with Bollywood stars, who will attend Tuesday’s ceremony, before the couple travel to Delhi on Wednesday to meet India’s prime minister, Narendra Modi.

Meanwhile, the Duke of Sussex made a surprise visit to Ukraine over the weekend to meet competitors taking part in his Invictus Games for injured and sick military veterans.

The couple last visited India in April 2016 when they also met Mr Modi.
