Sri Lanka targets UK buyers, investors ahead of Expo 2027

Sri Lanka is stepping up efforts to attract British buyers, investors and business representatives to Sri Lanka Expo 2027, with a business promotion programme in London on October 21 and 22.

The Sri Lanka Export Development Board (EDB), in collaboration with the Sri Lankan High Commission in the United Kingdom, has organised the programme to strengthen bilateral trade and investment ties and promote Sri Lankan products and services in the UK market.

The programme included a business and investment forum and a networking session on October 21, followed by bilateral business-to-business meetings on October 22. The purpose is to connect Sri Lankan representatives with British buyers, investors, chambers of commerce and trade promotion organisations.

An official Sri Lankan delegation led by Trade, Commerce, Food Security and Cooperative Development Minister Wasantha Samarasinghe left for the UK to participate in the programme. The delegation include the Industries and Entrepreneurship Development Ministry Secretary, EDB Chairman and Chief Executive Officer and an Executive Director of the Board of Investment of Sri Lanka.

The initiative aims to encourage UK companies to source goods and services from Sri Lanka, explore investment opportunities and establish long-term business partnerships. It will also focus on identifying new export markets and diversifying Sri Lanka’s export portfolio.

Sri Lanka Expo 2027 is scheduled to be held in Sri Lanka from January 14 to 17, 2027, with the UK promotion programme expected to encourage a strong British business delegation to attend the exhibition.

The EDB also aims to increase the use of preferential market access available to Sri Lankan exporters under the UK’s Developing Countries Trading Scheme (DCTS), which provides eligible developing countries with reduced or zero tariffs on a range of products, subject to applicable conditions.

The programme is expected to provide Sri Lankan businesses with opportunities to engage directly with the UK-based buyers and investors, while strengthening Sri Lanka’s position as a competitive sourcing destination and investment partner.

The initiative forms part of broader efforts to expand export opportunities, attract investment and deepen commercial relations between Sri Lanka and the UK.
