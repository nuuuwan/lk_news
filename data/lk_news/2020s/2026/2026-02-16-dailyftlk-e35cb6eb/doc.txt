California Temple donates to ‘Rebuilding Sri Lanka’ Fund

A donation of $ 21,500 from the Sun Valley Maithree Vihara in Southern California was made to the ‘Rebuilding Sri Lanka’ Fund.

The donation was raised through a fundraising program held at the temple to support fellow Sri Lankans affected by Cyclone Ditwah. This financial donation was formally handed over to the Consul General of Sri Lanka on National Independence Day under the guidance of Sun Valley Maithree Vihara in Southern California Chief Incumbent Venerable Dr. Aparakke Punyasiri Thera
