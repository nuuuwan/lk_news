Brandix launches A/L scholarships to grow apparel leaders

Brandix Corporate Campus (BCC) has launched a new scholarship framework for Advanced Level (A/L) students, aiming to build the next generation of leaders for Sri Lanka’s apparel industry.

The initiative offers three merit-based scholarships. The top award provides full tuition and a guaranteed Graduate Trainee position at Brandix for students with outstanding A/L results and provincial/national achievements in sports or co-curriculars. Two additional half-scholarships are also available.

A structured achievement-based discount framework offers benefits up to LKR 300,000, recognizing academic performance and extracurricular involvement. Family discounts for students connected to the industry are included.

The scheme applies to BCC’s flagship Advanced Diploma and Diploma programs. This talent development drive responds to the industry’s growing need for technically competent and professionally rounded future-ready professionals.
