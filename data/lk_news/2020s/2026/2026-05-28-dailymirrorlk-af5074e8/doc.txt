Over 460 Vesak Dansals registered in Colombo Municipal area

Colombo, May 28 (Daily Mirror) - A total of 464 Vesak Dansals have been registered within the Colombo Municipal Council (CMC) area as of May 27, Chief Public Health Inspector of the Colombo Municipal Council, Dehan Withanage said.

Of the registered Dansals, 91 have been identified as rice distribution Dansals.

Authorities say registration is being carried out in line with public health guidelines issued by the Epidemiology Division, with inspections to be conducted before, during, and after the events.
