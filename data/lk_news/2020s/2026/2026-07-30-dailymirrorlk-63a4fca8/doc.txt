KPMG Sri Lanka wins ACCA FINHACK Employer Edition 2026

ACCA Sri Lanka team with ACCA FINHACK EMPLOYERS EDITION 2026 winners

Sri Lanka’s leading finance professionals showcased their innovation and problem-solving skills at ACCA FINHACK Employer Edition 2026, where the key employers of the ACCA competed to solve real-world business challenges through strategic thinking and emerging technologies.

Addressing the participants, ACCA Head of South Asia Nilusha Ranasinghe highlighted the importance of continuous learning and innovation in preparing the finance professionals for the future.

ACCA Sri Lanka Member Network Panel Chairman Dr. Prasenna Balachandran encouraged the participants to develop future-ready skills and embrace lifelong learning. Judging Panel Head Mahen Muttiah praised the high quality of case analysis and strategic thinking demonstrated by the teams. He also encouraged greater use of artificial intelligence (AI) in developing business solutions, noting that AI will play an increasingly important role in the future of finance. KPMG Sri Lanka emerged as the Champion of ACCA FINHACK Employer Edition 2026, with Hayleys Advantis named First Runner-Up and WNS Global Services securing the Second Runner-Up.

The ACCA FINHACK Employer Edition continues to strengthen collaboration between ACCA and employers while developing finance professionals equipped with the skills needed to lead in an evolving business environment.
