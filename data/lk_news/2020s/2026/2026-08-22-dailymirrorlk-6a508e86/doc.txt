President pays final respects to veteran singer Nanda Malini

Colombo, August 22 (Daily Mirror) - President Anura Kumara Dissanayake paid his final respects to legendary Sri Lankan singer Visharad Dr. Nanda Malini, who passed away on August 20 at the age of 82.

The President visited the late singer’s residence in Nawala yesterday (21), where her remains have been placed for public viewing.

A large number of members of the public have also been visiting the residence to pay their final respects to the veteran artiste, whose contribution to Sri Lankan music spanned several decades.

The final rites of Dr. Nanda Malini are scheduled to be held today (22) at 5.30 p.m. at the General Cemetery in Borella.

Dr. Nanda Malini passed away at her residence in Nawala on August 20, marking the loss of one of Sri Lanka’s most celebrated voices.
