Week ends in red

The ASPI closed in red as a result of price losses in counters such as Commercial Bank, Singer Sri Lanka and LOLC Holdings with the turnover crossing Rs. 1.4bn.  A similar behaviour was witnessed in the S&P SL20. High net worth and institutional investor participation was noted in C T Holdings, Tokyo Cement Company and Hatton National Bank.

Mixed interest was observed in Janashakthi Insurance Company, Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka and Commercial Bank whilst retail interest was noted in Waskaduwa Beach Resort, Janashakthi Limited and ACME Printing & Packaging.  Foreign participation in the market activity remained at subdued levels with foreigners closing as net buyers. During the week, the ASPI and the S&P SL20 lost 0.8% and 0.6% respectively, whilst recording an average daily turnover of Rs. 1.65bn.  Food & Staples Retailing sector was the top contributor to the market turnover (due to C T Holdings) whilst the sector index gained 1.59%. The share price of C T Holdings closed flat at Rs. 529.00.

The Banking sector was the second highest contributor to the market turnover (due to Hatton National Bank and Commercial Bank) whilst the sector index decreased by 0.32%. The share price of Hatton National Bank lost Rs. 2.00 (0.51%) to close at Rs. 390.25.

The share price of Commercial Bank declined by Rs. 1.00 (0.47%) to close at Rs. 211.00.  Janashakthi Insurance Company and Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka were also included amongst the top turnover contributors. The share price of Janashakthi Insurance Company moved down by Rs. 2.00 (4.15%) to close at Rs. 46.20. The share price of Digital Mobility Solutions Lanka recorded a loss of Rs. 1.75 (0.92%) to close at Rs. 187.75.
