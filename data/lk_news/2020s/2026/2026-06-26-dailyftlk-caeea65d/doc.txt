IMF delegation reviews  Sri Lanka Customs progress

A progress review meeting of the Sri Lanka Customs Department was held yesterday, bringing together senior officials and an International Monetary Fund (IMF) delegation.

The IMF delegation included Andrew Okello, Cindy Negus, Greg Topping, Philip Griffiths, Bob Hamilton, and Maureen Kidd, who engaged with Sri Lanka Customs officials on reform milestones and next steps.

Sri Lanka Customs Spokesman said the discussions focused on ongoing reforms, operational efficiency improvements, and measures to strengthen revenue administration and compliance frameworks.
