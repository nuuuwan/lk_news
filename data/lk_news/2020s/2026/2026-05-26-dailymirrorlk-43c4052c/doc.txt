New Director General appointed to Management Services Department

Colombo, May 26 (Daily Mirror) - The Cabinet has approved the appointment of M.K.D.N. Madampe as the new Director General of the Management Services Department with immediate effect.

The appointment was approved following a proposal submitted by President Anura Kumara Dissanayake in his capacity as the Minister of Finance, Planning and Economic Development.

A committee appointed by the Secretary to the Ministry of Finance, Planning and Economic Development had been tasked with recommending a suitable officer for the vacant post after former Director General Wimal S.K. Liyanagama was appointed as the Director General of Customs.

Based on the committee’s recommendations, Ms. Madampe, a Special Grade Officer of the Sri Lanka Administrative Service currently serving as Additional Director General of the Department of Management Services, was selected for the position.

The Cabinet granted approval for the appointment at its latest meeting.
