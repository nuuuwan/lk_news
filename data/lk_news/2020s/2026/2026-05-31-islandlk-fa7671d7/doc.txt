AI’s workforce revolution takes centre stage at La Trobe Colombo Panel

On 25th April, La Trobe University Colombo, in partnership with Study Melbourne and La Trobe University Australia, hosted a successful panel discussion titled “AI and Future Employment Opportunities.” The event brought together academics, industry experts, school leaders, and education officials to explore how artificial intelligence is reshaping industries, career paths, and skill demands. Distinguished guests included Victoria’s Commissioner to South Asia, Michelle Wade, and several La Trobe academic leaders.

Key topics ranged from ethical AI use and automation trends to emerging roles in business and healthcare. Speakers emphasized the need for stronger collaboration between educators and industry to equip students for an AI-driven global job market. Professor Amalia Di Iorio highlighted the value of La Trobe’s Colombo-Australia pathway model, which combines global academic standards with local insight. The discussion reinforced the importance of digital literacy and proactive career preparation. La Trobe University Colombo offers diplomas in Business and IT, with specializations including AI, Data Science, and Cybersecurity, allowing students to start in Sri Lanka and transfer to Australia. The event successfully underscored the university’s commitment to future-ready education.
