Eight mangrove ecosystems in Puttalam to be declared Protected Reserves

Colombo, July 24 (Daily Mirror) - The Ministry of Environment has decided to designate eight mangrove ecosystems in the Puttalam District as new protected reserves this year.

Accordingly, the gazette notification declaring the reserves is scheduled to be signed on July 27, in conjunction with the International Day for the Conservation of the Mangrove Ecosystem.

The Ministry said the initiative forms part of the national programme aimed at conserving Sri Lanka’s forest ecosystems for future generations. In line with the government’s environmental policy, a new programme titled “Let the Mangroves Flourish” has also been launched to strengthen the protection and conservation of mangrove ecosystems.

Under the programme, selected mangrove ecosystems across Sri Lanka will be declared protected forest reserves to ensure their long-term conservation.

The Ministry further announced that the declaration of the eight identified mangrove ecosystems as protected reserves will coincide with World Mangrove Day, observed on July 26, 2026.

A total extent of 162.72 hectares will be gazetted as new protected reserves under the initiative, the Ministry added.
