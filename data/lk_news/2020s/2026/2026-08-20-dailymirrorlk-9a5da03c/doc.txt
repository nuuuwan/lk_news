Principal, teacher caught drunk on duty at exam center

Colombo, Aug. 20 (Daily Mirror) - The supervisor and a coordinating officer of an Advanced Level Examiniation center in Medirigiriya who had been found to be under the influence of liqour

North Central Province Director of Education S.M.W.Samarakoon said one of them was a principal of a school and the other a teacher from the Hingurakgoda Education Zone.

The Director said a team of officials had carried out an inspection on information received by the Examination Department and suspended the Supervisor and the Asst. Coordinating Officer from the duties at the examination center.

He said disciplinary action would be taken against them once findings of the ongoing preliminary investigation are completed.
