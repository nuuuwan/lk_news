Jeff Timmons arrives for charity concert

Colombo, Sep. 10 (Daily Mirror) - American musician, singer and songwriter Jeff Timmons, founder of the 98 Degrees Foundation, arrived at the Bandaranaike International Airport in Katunayake yesterday evening (9) to participate in a charity music concert in Colombo.

The concert is scheduled to be held on Sunday (13) at the Cinnamon Life Hotel in Colombo, with the proceeds received by Timmons reportedly to be directed towards providing medical treatment for children with mental health conditions in Sri Lanka.

Timmons arrived in Sri Lanka from Doha aboard Qatar Airways flight QR654, accompanied by his son.

Several local organisers of the concert were present at the airport to welcome the American musician and accompany him to Colombo.
