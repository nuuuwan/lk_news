Jacqueline Fernandez launches AI digital avatar

SWEDEN HERALD - Bollywood star Jacqueline Fernandez doesn't just want to be appreciated for her looks. Instead, she wants to project the image of a reader and a reflective person. That's why she is now launching an AI-based digital avatar.

Her fans can use it to get book recommendations, personal reflections and stories from the star's life.

The avatar was developed by AI studio Galleri5, part of the Collective Artists Network. To create the perfect Fernandez, they used voice synthesis and AI-powered conversation tools, with the aim of capturing Fernandez's personality and way of thinking, rather than just her appearance.
