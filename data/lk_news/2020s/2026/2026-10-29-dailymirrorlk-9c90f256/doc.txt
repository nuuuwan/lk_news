Sajith urges government to abolish executive presidency

Colombo, Oct. 29 (Daily Mirror) - Opposition Leader Sajith Premadasa today urged the government to fulfil the National People's Power (NPP) election pledge to abolish the executive presidency and introduce a new constitution.

In a special statement, Mr. Premadasa said the government had come to power promising to introduce a new constitution and safeguard human rights.

“This government came to power by promising that a new constitution would be introduced soon. The government also pledged to safeguard human rights. It is time to change the Constitution now,” he said.

He also urged the government to introduce a separate chapter on human rights in the Constitution.

Mr. Premadasa further warned that proposed legislation, including the Protection of the State from Terrorism Bill and the Organised Crimes Bill, could jeopardise Sri Lanka’s GSP+ trade concessions.
