‘Sumana’ becomes Pinnawala attraction

Colombo, Aug. 11 (Daily Mirror) - A majestic elephant named “Sumana” has become one of the most eye-catching attractions for visitors to the Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage.

Sumana was brought to the orphanage after being found alone in the Thirappanaya area of Maradankadawala in Anuradhapura. She is believed to have been separated from her herd and mother at a young age before being rescued by wildlife officials and handed over to the Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage.

Believed to have been born in 1993, Sumana has now grown into a majestic elephant with a well-developed pair of tusks.

From a vulnerable calf that had lost its mother and herd, Sumana has grown into a healthy and much-loved elephant, thanks to the dedicated care provided by the staff of the Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage and the Department of National Zoological Gardens.

Today, Sumana continues to attract thousands of visitors from Sri Lanka and overseas, serving as an example of the importance of elephant conservation and animal welfare.

Visitors to the Pinnawala Elephant Orphanage can see Sumana and witness her remarkable journey from a rescued calf to a majestic elephant.
