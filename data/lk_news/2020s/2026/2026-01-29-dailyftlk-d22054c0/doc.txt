Samsung awarded Superbrands Sri Lanka’s Choice 2025  in consumer electronics

Samsung team comprising Managing Director SangHwa Song (third from left) Director and Head of Consumer Electronics Sabry Ansar (third from right) and Assistant Manager Marketing Consumer Electronics Upuli Rathnayake (second from right)

Samsung has been conferred the prestigious Superbrands – Sri Lanka’s Choice 2025 award, reaffirming the brand’s position as one of the most trusted and admired technology brands in the country.

The recognition reflects strong consumer preference, sustained brand leadership, and Samsung’s continued commitment to delivering meaningful innovation to Sri Lankan homes.

Superbrands is globally recognised as one of the most independent and credible authorities on brand excellence. A Superbrand offers consumers significant emotional and physical advantages over other brands that consumers consciously or subconsciously want, recognise, and trust. The Superbrands programme identifies brands that have achieved exceptional standards in quality, reliability, consistency, and emotional connection over time.

The Superbrands selection process follows a rigorous and transparent methodology. Brands are shortlisted following extensive market research and are evaluated by an independent panel of eminent branding and business professionals, supported by consumer insights. Only a limited number of brands, selected from thousands initially considered, earn Superbrand status, making the endorsement both exclusive and highly respected.

Samsung Sri Lanka Managing Director SangHwa Song said: “This Superbrands recognition is a strong affirmation of the trust Sri Lankan consumers place in Samsung. It reflects our relentless focus on innovation, quality, and long-term value, as well as our commitment to bringing world-class technology to Sri Lanka. We are honoured to be recognised as a brand that consumers genuinely choose and believe in.”

Samsung Sri Lanka and Maldives Director and Head of Consumer Electronics Sabry Ansar said: “This Superbrands recognition is a reflection of the strong connection Samsung has built with consumers in Sri Lanka. Our focus has always been on delivering reliable, future-ready technology that adds real value to everyday life, supported by a trusted retail network, flexible payment options and strong after-sales service. This achievement reinforces our commitment to continuously raising standards across Consumer Electronics and Smart living solutions, while making global innovation accessible to our markets.”

This Superbrands recognition comes at a time when the company continues to strengthen its leadership across Consumer Electronics and Smart living solutions. Globally, Samsung has retained its position as the No.1 TV manufacturer for 19 consecutive years, driven by leadership in immersive viewing experiences and enhanced connection and security. Sri Lankan consumers benefit directly from this global expertise through access to Samsung’s latest Neo QLED, OLED, and Lifestyle TV ranges.

Samsung continues to set benchmarks with intelligent, energy-efficient, and durable products designed for modern lifestyles. The brand’s 20-year warranty on Digital Inverter Motors and Compressors stands as a testament to product reliability and long-term performance, offering consumers unmatched peace of mind and reinforcing Samsung’s confidence in its engineering excellence.

Samsung’s connected ecosystem is powered by SmartThings, an open platform that enables seamless integration across Digital Appliances. SmartThings allows users to monitor, control, and automate their homes with ease, enhancing convenience, efficiency, and energy management. Complementing this ecosystem is Samsung Knox Security, a defence-grade security platform that safeguards devices, data, and connected environments, reinforcing Samsung’s commitment to privacy and digital trust.

Samsung’s strong market presence in Sri Lanka is further supported by its extensive and trusted national distributor network, including Singer, Singhagiri, Damro, and Softlogic. This island-wide retail and service footprint ensures accessibility, reliable after-sales support, and a consistent brand experience for consumers across the country.

Ensuring global innovation is accessible locally remains central to Samsung Sri Lanka’s strategy. By providing easy payment plans in partnership with leading banks, Samsung enables more Sri Lankan consumers to experience premium global technology, reinforcing its position as a brand that understands local needs while delivering international standards.

Beyond consumer recognition, Superbrands status delivers wide-ranging benefits across the business ecosystem. It provides powerful validation for all stakeholders including customers, retailers, suppliers, investors, and employees, reaffirming that Samsung is a well-managed brand with enduring value. Inclusion in the Superbrands publication offers high-profile exposure among Sri Lanka’s top corporate leaders and marketing professionals, while reinforcing employee pride and strengthening the brand’s employer value proposition.

Samsung Sri Lanka’s leadership team, including the Managing Director and Director and Head of Consumer Electronics, attended the Superbrands Tribute Ceremony held on 12 January at Hilton, marking this milestone alongside the country’s most respected brand custodians.
