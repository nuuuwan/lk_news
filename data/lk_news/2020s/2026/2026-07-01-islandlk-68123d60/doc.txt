Sluggishness in market in the wake of slight interest rate hike

A slight increase in interest rates created a somewhat sluggish stock market yesterday among most prospective investors, market analysts said.

The All Share Price Index went down by 19.58 points while the S and P SL20 rose by 2.5 points. Turnover stood at Rs 1.07 billion with three crossings. Those crossings were: WindForce 2.5 million shares crossed to the tune of Rs 100 million; its shares traded at Rs 40, CCS 250,000 shares crossed to the tune of Rs 33.5 million; its shares traded at Rs 134 and Singhe Hospitals 1.04 million shares crossed for Rs 25 million; its shares traded at Rs 24.

In the retail market top seven companies that mainly contributed to the turnover were; Dialog Axiata Rs 247 million (5.4 million shares traded), HNB Life Rs 41.6 million (277,000 shares traded), CCS Rs 31 million (232,000 shares traded), Hayleys Fiber Rs 24 million (146,000 shares traded), Sampath Bank Rs 21 million (148,000 shares traded), Distilleries Rs 21 million (357,000 shares traded) and Commercial Bank (Non Voting) Rs 20 million (105,000 shares traded). During the day 35.2 million share volumes changed hands in 12282 transactions.

Dialog performed well while renewable energy sector’s WindForce performed well too.

Yesterday the telegraphic transfer rate for Sri Lanka’s rupee against the US dollar was Rs 331.6619 buying, Rs 341.3051 selling, Central Bank showed.

While the euro was Rs 376.0313 selling, Rs 389.9483 buying; and the pound was Rs 438.3326 buying, 452.3782 selling.

An auction of Rs. 100,000 million Treasury bills is ongoing.
