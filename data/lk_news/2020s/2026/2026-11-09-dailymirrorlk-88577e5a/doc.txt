SLIM National Sales Awards 2026 Ceylinco Life sales professionals win 6 awards

Ceylinco Life’s victorious team members at SLIM National Sales Awards

Six sales professionals from Ceylinco Life have earned national recognition for their performance, winning six awards at the 2026 National Sales Awards of the Sri Lanka Institute of Marketing (SLIM).

The awards recognise the achievements of Ceylinco Life’s sales team across three categories, with the winners representing the company’s Wennappuwa, Middeniya, Negombo and Nugegoda branches.

Lasitha Fernando of the Wennappuwa branch won a Silver award in the Territory Manager Category, while J.A. Rathnasiri of the Middeniya branch secured Silver in the Sales Executive Category and K.D.L.N. Inoka of the Negombo branch received a Merit award, also in the Sales Executive Category.

In the Frontliner Category, Rashmi Suraweera of the Negombo branch won Bronze, while Kumuduni Rathnayaka of the Nugegoda branch and Sherman Rukshan of Colombo Special Sales Branch each received Merit awards.

The SLIM National Sales Awards recognise high-performing sales professionals for their achievements and contributions to their organisations, setting benchmarks for excellence in sales at a national level.

The 2026 awards programme brought together sales professionals from 20 industry categories, with awards presented across nine designations spanning frontline sales, sales management and sales support functions.
