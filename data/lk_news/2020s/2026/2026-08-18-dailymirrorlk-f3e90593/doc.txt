Dialog doubles 2Q profit to Rs.10.1bn as operating margins widen

Dialog Axiata PLC nearly doubled its group profit in the second quarter of 2026, as the revenue growth, wider operating margins and lower net finance costs strengthened the earnings.

The group profit attributable to the shareholders rose 99.6 percent to Rs.10.11 billion in the three months ended June 30, from Rs.5.07 billion a year earlier, according to the company’s interim financial statements.

The quarterly revenue grew 9.3 percent to Rs.48.22 billion, from Rs.44.13 billion, supported by a growth in the data and home broadband revenue.

The direct costs were broadly unchanged at Rs.20.79 billion, despite the increase in revenue, lifting gross profit by 17.2 percent to Rs.27.43 billion. The gross profit margin improved to 56.9 percent, from 53.0 percent a year earlier.

The distribution and administrative expenses also declined marginally, helping the operating profit surge 45.7 percent to Rs.13.01 billion. Dialog’s operating margin expanded to 27.0 percent, from 20.2 percent.

The bottom line received a further boost from the lower financing costs and a reversal in foreign-exchange movements. The net finance costs fell 45 percent to Rs.1.38 billion, from Rs.2.52 billion, as the group recorded a foreign-exchange gain of Rs.516 million, compared with a Rs.398 million loss in the corresponding quarter. Dialog said the earnings were “supported by a strong EBITDA growth, lower net finance costs and foreign exchange gains, primarily arising from the timely execution of forward contracts against the future US dollar-denominated liabilities.”

Excluding the foreign-exchange gains, the underlying second-quarter profit after tax amounted to Rs.9.6 billion, up 76 percent from a year earlier and 4 percent from the preceding quarter, the company said.

Profit before tax climbed 81.3 percent to Rs.11.62 billion, while the income tax charge rose at a slower 12 percent to Rs.1.51 billion. The effective tax rate consequently fell to about 13 percent, from 21 percent a year earlier.

On a quarter-on-quarter basis, the group revenue increased 2 percent, earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation rose 6 percent to Rs.25.7 billion and the net profit advanced 10 percent. For the first six months, the group revenue rose 9.3 percent to Rs.95.52 billion, while the profit attributable to the shareholders more than doubled to Rs.19.30 billion, from Rs.9.20 billion.

The group invested Rs.18.5 billion in digital infrastructure during the six-month period, largely on its accelerated 5G rollout, which expanded to more than 1,000 live sites. The operating free cash flow stood at Rs.18.4 billion.

Dialog’s board approved a second interim dividend of 70 cents per share for 2026, bringing the year-to-date dividend to Rs.1.40 per share.
