SLASSCOM AI Asia Summit 2026 puts AI in practice at centre of business growth

SLASSCOM’s seventh AI Asia Summit will take place on 12 November at Cinnamon Grand, Colombo, under the theme “AI: The Multiplier – Scaling Human Potential and Enterprise Value”.

Bringing together over 400 business leaders, technology practitioners, researchers and policymakers, the summit will explore practical AI applications to translate investment into improved business performance and greater opportunities for people.

It will examine how AI can enhance human capabilities, productivity, decision-making and customer service, while enabling Sri Lanka’s technology and business services industry to undertake more complex work and strengthen its position in international markets

“Sri Lankan businesses need opportunities to learn from those already putting AI to work. We need to understand what has delivered results, what has been difficult and what it takes to make an implementation succeed.

That is the conversation we want this summit to encourage. The ambition behind ‘AI: The Multiplier’ is to help our people achieve more and enable our businesses to compete with greater confidence, here and internationally,” said Sampath Jayasundara, Chairperson of SLASSCOM.

The programme will cover AI economics, the use of Sri Lanka’s digital resources, scientific research applications and increasingly autonomous AI systems, alongside the legal, regulatory and security considerations of wider adoption.

The keynote will be delivered by Biswajit Das, Head – Specialist Sales – AI & GTM, India and South Asia, Amazon Web Services. Parallel industry sessions will explore AI applications in banking and finance for forecasting and risk management; healthcare and life sciences for diagnosis, treatment and prevention; and manufacturing and supply chains for demand forecasting and inventory management.

A masterclass on building an AI-ready culture will examine how connected information strengthens human judgement and prepares teams to work confidently with AI.
