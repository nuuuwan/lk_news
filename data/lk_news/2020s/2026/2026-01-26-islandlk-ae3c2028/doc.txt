Exclusive academic pathways launched for Sri Lankan aviation professionals

The Sri Lankan Airlines National Service Union (SLNSS) and the Lincoln Institute of Graduate Studies have announced a strategic partnership to strengthen capacity building across Sri Lanka’s aviation sector.

Driven by a shared vision, the collaboration will provide exclusive educational pathways in Management, Engineering, and Computer Sciences. These tailor-made programs are designed for working professionals, offering flexible study options and industry-relevant learning to enhance qualifications without career interruption.

The partnership focuses on creating structured academic support and recognised upskilling opportunities, enabling SLNSS members to gain qualifications that contribute directly to career advancement and meet the evolving needs of the industry.
