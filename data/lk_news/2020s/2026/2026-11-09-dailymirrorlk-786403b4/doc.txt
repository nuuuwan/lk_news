Motor Traffic Department expands one-day and general services

Colombo, Nov.9 (Daily Mirror) - The Department of Motor Traffic (DMT) has expanded its one-day and general services to provide greater convenience to the public, Commissioner General of Motor Traffic Retired Air Vice Marshal Thushara Fernando said.

Under the new arrangements, customers seeking one-day services must make appointments in advance.

Appointments are required for all one-day services, including new vehicle registrations, vehicle ownership transfers, driving licence-related services and technical issue resolution.

Customers can also book appointments through the department's official online booking system at https://dmtappointments.dmt.gov.lk.

In addition to the DMT Head Office in Narahenpita, the public can now submit general vehicle ownership transfer documents at district offices located within District Secretariats and Divisional Secretariats.

The department said the expansion of these facilities would make its services more accessible and allow the public to obtain them more efficiently.
