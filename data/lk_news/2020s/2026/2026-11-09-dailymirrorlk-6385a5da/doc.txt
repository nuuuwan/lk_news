First Capital wins SLITAD Gold for excellence in human capital development

First Capital Holdings PLC has been recognised with a Gold award by the Sri Lanka Institute of Training and Development (SLITAD) for its innovative approach to people development and its contribution to organisational learning and growth.

The recognition celebrates First Capital’s creative and innovative approach to developing its people, with initiatives that have contributed to strengthening employee capabilities while advancing learning and development practices within the organisation.

The award reflects First Capital’s continued focus on building future-ready capabilities by creating meaningful opportunities for employees to expand their skills, take on new challenges and grow in line with the evolving needs of the organisation and wider capital markets industry.

First Capital Holdings Managing Director and CEO Dilshan Wirasekera said, “The strength of any organisation is closely connected with the capabilities of its people. At First Capital, we believe that developing our people is an ongoing investment in our future. This recognition by SLITAD reflects the importance we place on creating an environment where our employees can continuously learn, develop and prepare themselves for the opportunities and challenges ahead.”

First Capital Holdings Head of Human Resources Chamara Pussella said, “We want learning at First Capital to go beyond traditional training and become part of how our people work, grow and contribute. Our focus is on creating opportunities for employees to build relevant skills, learn from one another and apply that knowledge in meaningful ways. This Gold award is an encouraging recognition of the culture of learning and development we continue to build across the organisation.”
