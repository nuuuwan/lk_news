VIASL elects 2027 office bearers

The Vehicle Importers Association of Sri Lanka (VIASL) has elected its new office bearers for 2027 at its Annual General Meeting.

The election was conducted under the leadership of former President Ranjan Peiris.

Madushan Mannapperuma was elected President of the Association, while Ushman Ali was elected Secretary. Arosha Rodrigo was elected Vice President and Anil Adhikari was re-elected Treasurer for a second consecutive term.

Meanwhile, Dammika Peiris was elected Assistant Secretary and Tissa Ranasinghe was elected Assistant Treasurer.

The Association also appointed 16 members to its Executive Committee for the new term.
