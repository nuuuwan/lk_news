Water company fined over fake SLS mark

A bottled water company in Avissawella has been fined Rs.40,000 and more than 8,500 bottles of water have been ordered destroyed, after the company was found selling water without the required SLS certification.

The Consumer Affairs Authority (CAA) took legal action against D.T.S. International (Pvt.) Ltd in Seethawaka, Avissawella and its director.

The company was accused of using a fake SLS mark and selling the water under the name ‘Aquachill’, instead of the Health Ministry-approved brand name ‘Lanka Premium’.

The CAA officers seized the bottles during a raid on August 21. The stock included 19-litre, five-litre, 1,500ml, 1,000ml and 500ml bottles.

When the case was heard at the Avissawella Magistrate’s Court on September 4, the company and its director pleaded guilty.

The court fined the company and its director Rs.40,000 and ordered the destruction of more than 8,500 bottles.

The case highlights the financial and business risks faced by the companies that fail to follow the product quality, certification and labelling rules.

The CAA advised the consumers to check the brand name, manufacturing and expiry dates and SLS mark before buying bottled water. The consumers can report suspected illegal trading practices to the CAA through the 1977 hotline during office hours.
