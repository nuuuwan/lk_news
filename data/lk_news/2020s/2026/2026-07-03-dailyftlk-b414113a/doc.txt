Dialog Air Fibre powers new era of ultra-fast Home WiFi

Dialog Axiata Head of Business, Fixed Broadband and Global Tharindra Gooneratne (third from left) presents the ultra-fast home WiFi solution to a customer. Others from left: Assistant Product Manager (5G) Fixed Business Lahiru Anjana, Product Development Manager, Fixed Business Pasindu Bandaranayake, Head of Brands, Home Sherani Silva  and Assistant Brand Manager Upekshi Perera

Dialog Axiata PLC, yesterday announced the commercial launch of Dialog Air Fibre, the country’s first 5G-powered, ultra-fast wireless broadband service, following exceptional demand during its pre-order phase.

Powered by Dialog’s rapidly expanding 5G network, now spanning over 1,000 sites nationwide, Air Fibre delivers high-speed internet without the need for physical cables. Built for modern digital lifestyles, the service seamlessly supports streaming, gaming, video conferencing, and remote work across multiple devices, and includes a built-in battery offering up to six hours of connectivity during power outages.

Air Fibre is available in three plans: 100GB at Rs. 2,795, 200GB at Rs. 3,795, and 300GB at Rs. 5,495 (tax inclusive). Dialog Air Fibre Customers also enjoy complimentary access to over 100 TV channels via Dialog Play, along with a range of digital entertainment benefits, bringing connectivity and content together in a single, compelling offering.

Head of Business, Fixed Broadband and Global Tharindra Gooneratne said:  “Dialog Air Fibre redefines how Sri Lankan households access high-speed broadband. By leveraging the power of our 5G network, we are delivering a flexible, home broadband solution that meets the evolving connectivity needs of our customers.”

Each Air Fibre connection undergoes a comprehensive onboarding and validation process, including a location assessment and installation within 48 hours of purchase at no additional cost. Customers who do not meet the required service standards following validation are eligible for a full refund, underscoring Dialog’s commitment to quality and transparency.
