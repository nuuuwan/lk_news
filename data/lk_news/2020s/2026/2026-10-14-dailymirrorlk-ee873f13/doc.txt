Indonesian Food Festival brings archipelago’s flavours to Colombo

Hilton Colombo is bringing the rich culinary heritage of Indonesia to Sri Lanka with the Indonesia Food Festival at Graze Kitchen from 8 to 17 October 2026, featuring traditional dishes prepared by two guest chefs from Jakarta.

Organised in collaboration with the Embassy of the Republic of Indonesia in Colombo, the festival features Chef Rendy of DoubleTree by Hilton Jakarta – Bintaro Jaya and Chef Shandi of DoubleTree by Hilton Jakarta – Diponegoro, who are showcasing Indonesia’s diverse culinary traditions alongside Graze Kitchen’s international buffet.

The menu features a selection of Indonesian favourites, including satay, rendang, martabak, lumpia, nasi goreng, nasi kuning, Soto Betawi and sop buntut, complemented by traditional sambals and desserts such as klepon, jenang and es teler.

The festival’s opening dinner brought together members of the diplomatic community and invited guests. The event was hosted by H.E. Mrs. Dewi Gustina Tobing, Ambassador of the Republic of Indonesia to Sri Lanka and the Maldives.

Addressing guests, the Ambassador highlighted the richness of Indonesian cuisine and the importance of cultural exchange in strengthening ties between nations. She noted the role of food in fostering mutual understanding and reinforcing the longstanding friendship between Indonesia and Sri Lanka.

The festival also highlights the role of culinary diplomacy in promoting cultural understanding, with Indonesia’s regional specialities, spices and traditional recipes offering Sri Lankan diners an introduction to the archipelago’s diverse food culture.

The Embassy of Indonesia expressed its appreciation to Hilton Colombo, its management and culinary teams, and the two guest chefs for their collaboration in presenting Indonesian cuisine to local diners and the international community.

The Indonesia Food Festival runs until 17 October at Graze Kitchen, Hilton Colombo, with dinner served from 7 p.m. to 10.30 p.m.
