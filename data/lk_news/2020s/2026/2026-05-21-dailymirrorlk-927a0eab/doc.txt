Rupee hits new low against USD

Colombo, May 21 (Daily Mirror) - The Sri Lankan Rupee has weakened further against the US Dollar, with the selling rate surpassing the Rs. 354 mark, according to the latest exchange rate report issued by the Central Bank of Sri Lanka today (20).

According to the report, the Central Bank recorded the US Dollar buying rate at Rs. 342.6353, while the selling rate stood at Rs. 354.0350.
