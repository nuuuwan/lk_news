Hemas Holdings appoints Harikrishnan Sundaram to Mobility Sector Board

Former CWIT CEO and Adani Ports executive brings 24 years of ports, maritime and logistics leadership

Hemas Holdings PLC recently announced the appointment of Harikrishnan Sundaram to the Board of Directors of its Mobility Sector, with effect from 1 November 2026.

The appointment supports the Group’s plan to grow its Mobility Sector, which covers shipping, logistics, maritime services and aviation. Hemas intends to do this through clear strategic direction, business development and the creation of new ventures.

Sundaram has built and run port infrastructure in Sri Lanka and across the region, and he has strong relationships throughout the global shipping community.

“Harikrishnan has built and operated port infrastructure at scale, including here in Colombo, and he has negotiated the kind of international partnerships we intend to pursue,” said Hemas Holdings PLC Chairman-Mobility Sector Imtiaz Esufally. “His experience in governance, cross-border trade and supply chain connectivity will be invaluable as we sharpen our strategic direction and build lasting value across the maritime and logistics ecosystem,” he added.

“Sri Lanka is well placed to become South Asia’s leading transhipment and logistics hub. Hemas has the heritage, relationships and ambition to play a meaningful part in that growth, and I look forward to helping shape it,” said Sundaram.

Sundaram has over 24 years of senior leadership experience in global ports, maritime and logistics. His main areas are strategic business development, commercial strategy, infrastructure investment and international operations. Most recently he was Business Head, Container Business at Adani Ports and Special Economic Zone Ltd. Before that he was Chief Executive Officer of Colombo West International Terminal (CWIT). He has led large infrastructure projects and business expansion across South Asia, the Middle East and East Africa.

Among his achievements, he oversaw the $ 800 million development of CWIT, Sri Lanka’s first semi-automated container terminal, which was delivered in 36 months. He has also played an active role in the joint ventures at Mundra Port with Terminal Investment Limited (TIL) and CMA Terminals.

He has served on the boards of Colombo West International Terminal, Adani Ennore Container Terminal, Adani International Container Terminal and Harbour Services Lanka. He is an active member of the Young Presidents’ Organisation (YPO) Colombo Integrated Chapter, the Indian CEO Forum Colombo and the Global Shipping and Logistics Think Tank.
