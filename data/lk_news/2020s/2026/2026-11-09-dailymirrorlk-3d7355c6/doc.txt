Hasthi Sena operation to be formalised under new policy framework

Colombo, Nov. 9 (Daily Mirror) - The Government has decided to take steps to formalise the ongoing joint operation, 'Hasthi Sena', aimed at protecting wild elephants and tuskers and reducing human-elephant conflict.

A Cabinet memorandum will be submitted to establish a formal policy and legal framework for the operation, following a special discussion held at the Ministry of Environment yesterday (9).

The meeting was chaired by Deputy Environment Minister Anton Jayakody and attended by the Ministry Secretary and senior officers of the Tri-Forces involved in the operation.

Discussions focused on expanding the operation and strengthening measures to protect elephants, particularly vulnerable tuskers and breeding herds moving through high-risk wildlife corridors and migration routes.

It was proposed that specialised military protection teams be deployed to monitor and escort solitary tuskers and elephant herds in vulnerable areas.

The meeting also stressed the need to intensify action against wildlife crimes, particularly the manufacture, distribution and use of illegal weapons and traps used to kill or injure elephants.

Intelligence-led raids and operations will be carried out to crack down on the use of hakka patas (jaw-breaking explosive devices), trap guns and illegal firearms.

The Hasthi Sena operation is being jointly conducted by the Department of Wildlife Conservation and the Tri-Forces, with the aim of strengthening elephant protection while addressing the growing human-elephant conflict.
